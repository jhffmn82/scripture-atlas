Allah's covenant

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: His covenant, a covenant, a covenant of old, covenant, their covenant

Abstract written by a model

binds Children of Israel [through belief and obedience] Allah's covenant binds those who accepted it to hear and obey and to keep their duty to Allah. The unit describes covenants made with the Children of Israel and with those who say they are Christians, and says that some broke or forgot parts of them.

Allah's covenant is in one work on this site, Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
