Chapter 1

Persecution included slaughter, captivity, burning of Jerusalem, forced sacrifices to idols, and the tearing up and burning of the books of the law. Anyone found with a book of the covenant or consenting to the law was sentenced to death, and women who had circumcised their children were put to death with their babies hung around their necks. Many refused to eat unclean food and died rather than profane the holy covenant.
