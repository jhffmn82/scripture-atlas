Abstract written by a model

Men are among the beings that multiply until there is no more room on the earth. Selected men are brought into the Vara, where they live the happiest life. Men are included among those whose deaths require the ground to lie fallow for a year. Dead men are also included in the bones and bodies whose placement on the ground incurs penalties.
