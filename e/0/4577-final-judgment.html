Final Judgment

Abstract written by a model

In Revelation, the final judgment is when the dead stand before the great white throne and are judged by their works, and Death, Hades, and those not in the book of life are cast into the lake of fire. In The Book of Mosiah, Final Judgment is the last-day judgment of all people, executed by Jesus Christ or the Redeemer.

Abstract written by a model

The final judgment is an event in which the dead, given up by the sea, Death, and Hades, stand before the great white throne and are judged by their works from opened books; Death, Hades, and those not in the book of life are cast into the lake of fire.

Chapter 20

The dead are judged before the great white throne, with books opened and the book of life, each according to his works. The sea, Death, and Hades give up their dead for this judgment. Afterward Death and Hades and all not found in the book of life are cast into the lake of fire.

Final Judgment in The Book of Mosiah

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Final Judgment first appears

Abstract written by a model

Final Judgment is the last-day judgment of all people, executed by Jesus Christ or the Redeemer. Every person, mortal and corrupt, stands before God and is judged justly according to works, good or evil. Words taught become a bright testimony at judgment. Those who live without God confess the justice of everlasting punishment and tremble beneath his all-searching eye.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Jesus Christ will stand to judge the world, and every person will be judged according to his or her works. The people taught these words will stand as a bright testimony against the people at the judgment day.

Chapter 16

The mortal and corrupt will be brought before the bar of God and judged according to their works, whether good or evil. God's judgments are just.

Chapter 27

At the last day, all people will stand to be judged by the Redeemer. Those who live without God will confess that the judgment of everlasting punishment is just upon them and will tremble beneath his all-searching eye.
