Abstract written by a model

A prayer by Habakkuk the prophet, addressed to the LORD and set to victorious music for the music director on stringed instruments. It begins with pleas for renewed work and mercy, moves through a vision of God's powerful victorious coming, then Habakkuk's fear, ending in joyful trust in the LORD.
