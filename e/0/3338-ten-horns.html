ten horns

group · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: The ten horns that you saw, The ten horns which you saw, These, first horns, ten kings, ten kings (ten horns), the horns

Abstract written by a model

In Daniel (Greek), they are ten kings arising from the fourth kingdom, with three horns plucked up before the little horn. In Revelation, they are ten kings who receive authority with the beast, give him their power, war against the Lamb, and help desolate the prostitute.

Abstract written by a model

Ten kings who have not yet received a kingdom but receive authority with the beast for one hour. Unified in one mind, they give their power to the beast and war against the Lamb. Together with the beast they hate the prostitute, desolating, stripping, devouring and burning her, fulfilling God's purpose placed in their hearts.

Chapter 17

They are ten kings who have not yet received a kingdom but receive authority with the beast for one hour. They have one mind, give their power and authority to the beast, and war against the Lamb. With the beast they will hate the prostitute, make her desolate, strip her naked, eat her flesh, and burn her with fire, because God put it in their hearts to do his purpose.
