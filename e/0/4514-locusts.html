Abstract written by a model

Locusts emerge from smoke on the earth with scorpion-like power, forbidden to harm vegetation and permitted only to torment, not kill, people lacking God's seal for five months. They resemble war horses with golden crowns, human faces, women's hair, lions' teeth, iron breastplates, noisy wings and stinging scorpion tails, ruled by Abaddon, the angel of the abyss.

Chapter 9

Locusts come out of the smoke on the earth with power like that of scorpions. They are forbidden to harm the grass, green things, or trees, and may only torment, not kill, people without God's seal for five months. They look like war horses with golden crowns, human faces, women's hair, lions' teeth, iron breastplates, noisy wings like chariots, and scorpion tails with stingers. Their king is the angel of the abyss.
