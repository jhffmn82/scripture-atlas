Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Good Mind is a benevolent divine attribute and spiritual, mental power associated with Ahura Mazda and Asha. In Yasna, Good Mind is a divine, better mind of the Lord that grounds worshippers’ words, protects Faith, aids discernment, and guides souls toward salvation. In Visparad, Good Mind is the companion with whom the male and female Bountiful Immortals dwell together. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Good Spirit creates prosperous creation, contests the Evil One for kingly Glory, and is associated with the world’s future deathlessness.

Good Mind in Yasna

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where Good Mind first appears

Abstract written by a model

Good Mind is a benevolent divine attribute and spiritual, mental power associated with Ahura Mazda and Asha. Implanted by Ahura Mazda within souls and living believers, it is a fatherly presence, a better and quiet mind, and a toiling faculty that supports Ahura Mazda as a fellow-worker. It matters most as the inward source of understanding, truth, righteous thought, worthy words, actions, strength, blessing, spiritual attainment, and advancement of the imperishable Sovereign Kingdom. It establishes and governs that kingdom with Righteous Order, gives life and vigorous might, produces blessed rewards, and blesses pure ones. It stands apart from the oppressor and is opposed by the Daêvas, rival-monarch’s teachings, and those far astray. Across the records, Good Mind is first sought for understanding, the Kingdom, and Zarathustra’s task; it then inspires sacrifices, helps the faithful know truth and serve through wise thoughts, words, and actions, and guides them toward Ahura Mazda. It protects settlements, supports the Kine and the Faith, reveals the best words, actions, wisdom, doctrine, and Mãthra, and enables those carrying out Ahura Mazda’s commandments. Finally, it is truly served, its bright and joyful grace is sought, its institutions are declared, and speakers ask it to approach so their souls may advance in good and attain wished-for recompense.

Good Mind in Yasna

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where Good Mind first appears

Abstract written by a model

Good Mind is a divine, better mind of the Lord: a worshipped presence in the living, within doctrine, and a law for Zarathustra. Invoked with Righteousness, it grounds worshippers’ words and sayings. It empowers believing natures to guard Faith, offers sheltering leadership among folk and commanders, and aids discernment and proclamation of the Faith’s truths, including against Bendva. Within the speaker’s soul it gives grace, understanding, virtue, and guidance, making holy works worthy and radiant. Worshipped with Righteousness and the Gâthas, it ultimately serves as a means by which worshippers may prosper to salvation through divine assistance.
