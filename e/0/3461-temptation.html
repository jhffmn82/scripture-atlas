temptation

topic · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: tempted, the way of escape

Abstract written by a model

In Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians, temptation is common to man, God will not allow believers to be tempted beyond what they can bear, and He provides a way of escape so they can endure it. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, temptation is something people enter by failing to watch and pray, and Jesus warns that the devil and Satan can lead them into it.
