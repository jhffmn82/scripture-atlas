Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the word of the LORD is his swift commandment and is revealed through statutes and ordinances to Jacob and Israel. In Psalms, God's word and creation demonstrate the LORD’s power in making the heavens, gathering the waters, and commanding reverence. In The Book of Isaiah, the word of the LORD accomplishes what he pleases and prospers in its mission. In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, faith brings God’s righteousness, salvation, justification apart from works, and obedience while establishing the law. In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, the commandments of the Lord direct obedience, preserve knowledge, bring blessings, and guide Nephi’s family. In The Book of Helaman, the word of the Lord exposes evil, guides people in righteousness, calls for repentance, and testifies against sin. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the words of the Lord spoken by prophets are fulfilled without delay. In The Book of Moroni, faith in Christ enables hope, salvation, spiritual knowledge, miracles, prayer, angelic ministry, and obedience.

faith in Paul’s Letter to the Romans

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where faith first appears

Abstract written by a model

Faith is presented as the means by which God's righteousness reaches all who believe. In Chapter 1, the Romans' faith is proclaimed worldwide, and the Good News brings salvation through it, with Paul aiming at the obedience of faith among nations. Chapter 3 says faith justifies apart from works, excludes boasting, and covers circumcised and uncircumcised alike. Chapter 10 says it comes by hearing God's word, though not all listened. It establishes the law rather than nullifying it.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

The Romans' faith is proclaimed throughout the whole world, and Paul says he and they will be encouraged by each other's faith. Faith is the means by which the Good News brings salvation to everyone who believes, and by which the righteous shall live. Paul's apostleship aims at the obedience of faith among all nations.

Chapter 3

Faith is the means by which the righteousness of God reaches all who believe, and by which a man is justified apart from works of the law. It excludes boasting as a law of faith, and through it God justifies both circumcised and uncircumcised.

Chapter 10

The righteousness of faith says the word is near, in the mouth and heart, which is the word of faith that is preached. Faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God. Not all listened, as Isaiah asks who has believed the report.

the commandments of the Lord in The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry

Nephi · the work’s page · read it where the commandments of the Lord first appears

Abstract written by a model

The commandments of the Lord are directives to be kept with faithfulness and diligence. They ground Lehi’s obedience and guide Nephi, his brethren, and Nephi’s seed. Keeping them brings prosperity in the land of promise, knowledge, nourishment, strength, and means to accomplish what is commanded; rebellion brings separation from the presence of the Lord and other consequences. They lead Nephi and his brothers back to Jerusalem to obtain the plates of brass, which preserve them for children. Across the records, Nephi repeatedly teaches that the Lord prepares a way to fulfill every commandment and exhorts his brethren and later seed to keep them.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The commandments of the Lord are the basis for Lehi's obedience and Nephi's promised prosperity. Keeping them leads to a land of promise, while rebellion brings separation from the presence of the Lord and other stated consequences.

Chapter 3

The commandments of the Lord require Nephi and his brothers to return to Jerusalem and obtain the records on the plates of brass. Nephi teaches that the Lord gives no commandment without preparing a way for people to accomplish it, and he urges his brothers to remain faithful in keeping the commandments.

Chapter 4

Nephi urges his brethren to be faithful and diligent in keeping the commandments of the Lord. The text teaches that Nephi’s seed will prosper in the land of promise if they keep them.

Chapter 5 .. Chapter 8

Lehi’s family preserves the commandments of the Lord through the plates of brass. Lehi exhorts Laman and Lemuel to keep the commandments after recounting his dream.

Chapter 15 .. Chapter 16

Nephi teaches that knowledge is received through faith, diligence, and keeping the commandments of the Lord. He repeatedly exhorts his brethren to keep them.

Chapter 17 .. Chapter 18

The commandments of God must be fulfilled, and those who keep them are nourished, strengthened, and provided means to accomplish what God commands. Nephi strives to keep them and exhorts his brethren to faithfulness and diligence.

word of the Lord in The Book of Helaman

The Book of Mormon · the work’s page · read it where word of the Lord first appears

Abstract written by a model

The word of the Lord is a quick and powerful word of God that can divide the devil’s cunning, snares, and wiles and lead the man of Christ in a strait and narrow course. Nephi and Lehi preach it among the people of Nephi and the Lamanites with power and authority. It commands repentance to avoid destruction, is declared by prophets including Samuel to testify of sins, and is often rejected when it condemns deeds. It is also taught by those on whom the people depend, raising fears of ignorance.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The word of God is described as quick and powerful, able to divide asunder the cunning, snares, and wiles of the devil and lead the man of Christ in a strait and narrow course.

Chapter 5

Nephi and Lehi take upon themselves to preach the word of God among the people of Nephi and the Lamanites. Their preaching is accompanied by great power and authority.

Chapter 10

The word of the Lord comes to Nephi through a voice and commands him to declare that the people must repent or be smitten unto destruction. Nephi declares this word to the people, but they refuse to hearken.

Chapter 13

The word of the Lord is declared by Samuel to testify of the people’s sins and iniquities. The people reject those who declare it when it condemns their deeds.

Chapter 16

The word was taught by those whom the people depended upon, and some feared that this dependence would keep them in ignorance.

faith in The Book of Moroni

Moroni · the work’s page · read it where faith first appears

Abstract written by a model

Faith in Christ is required for hope and salvation in the kingdom of God. Through Christ, it enables people to lay hold of good, receive answers to prayer, perform miracles, receive angelic ministry, know truth through the Holy Ghost, and do things expedient to Christ. It leads to baptism unto fulfilling the commandments and is shown through meekness.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

Faith in Christ enables people to lay hold upon every good thing, receive answers to prayer, perform miracles, and receive angelic ministry. Faith is necessary for hope and salvation, and it is shown through meekness.

Chapter 10

Faith in Christ, together with a sincere heart and real intent, enables people to know truth through the Holy Ghost. Faith is required for salvation in the kingdom of God and allows people to do all things which are expedient unto Christ.
