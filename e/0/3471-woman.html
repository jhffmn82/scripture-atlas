Abstract written by a model

Woman is portrayed in relation to man: his glory, drawn from him and made for him, yet not independent of him in the Lord. She is told to cover her head when praying or prophesying, holds authority over her head because of the angels, and has long hair as a glory and covering.

Chapter 11

Woman is described as the glory of man, made from man and for man, and is told to be covered when praying or prophesying, since being uncovered is likened to being shaved. She is said to have authority over her own head because of the angels, to be not independent of man in the Lord, and to have long hair as a glory and a covering given to her.
