disbelievers

group · 3 works · 6 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: These twain, idolaters, scoffers, the believers and the disbelievers, the disbelievers, those who disbelieve, two opponents

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 15: The Rock (Al-Hijr), the disbelievers mock the messenger, call him a madman, demand angels, reject the Reminder, and would explain an opened gate of heaven as faulty sight or sorcery. In Surah 22: The Pilgrimage (Al-Hajj), the believers and the disbelievers are two opponents contending concerning their Lord; the disbelievers receive garments of fire and other punishment. In Surah 70: The Ascending Stairways (Al-Ma'aarij), the disbelievers face an irresistible doom, stare toward Muhammad in groups, and will emerge from the graves in abasement.
