place the LORD shall choose in Deuteronomy

author unknown · 624 BC · the work’s page · read it where place the LORD shall choose first appears

Abstract written by a model

The place the LORD shall choose is the single location, within one of the tribes' territories across the Jordan, where the LORD your God causes his name to dwell. It is the only permitted site for burnt offerings and sacrifices. Chapter 12 requires all offerings, tithes, vows and firstborn animals be brought there. Chapter 14 has tithes eaten there with rejoicing, or money carried if distant. Chapter 16 adds Passover, feasts, and appearances before him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 12

The chosen place is the single location the LORD will choose among the tribes to put his name and make it dwell. All burnt offerings, sacrifices, tithes, wave offerings, vows and firstborn animals are to be brought and eaten there, not in every place the Israelites see.

Chapter 14

The place the LORD chooses is where he causes his name to dwell. The Israelites are to eat their tithe there before him and rejoice with their households. If it is too far, they carry money instead and buy what they desire when they arrive.

Chapter 16

This is the place where the LORD will cause his name to dwell. Passover must be sacrificed and eaten there rather than within the people's gates, and the feasts are kept and males appear before him there. It is also where the people rejoice together.

Abstract written by a model

David vowed to find a place for the LORD, a dwelling for the Mighty One of Jacob. The people heard of it in Ephrathah, found it in the field of Jaar, and resolve to go into his dwelling place and worship at his footstool. The LORD later declares Zion his resting place forever, where he will live because he desired it.
