Chapter 8

Samuel describes it as the king taking sons for chariots, horsemen, captains, plowing, reaping, and making weapons, and daughters as perfumers, cooks, and bakers. He will also take the best fields, vineyards, and olive groves, a tenth of seed, vineyards, and flocks, and servants, young men, and donkeys. The people will become his servants and will cry out in vain to the LORD.
