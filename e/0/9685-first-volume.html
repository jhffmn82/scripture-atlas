Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods) / Preface.

The First Volume extends from the Deity Master-of-the-August-Centre-of-Heaven to His Augustness Prince-Wave-Limit-Brave-Cormorant-Thatch-Meeting-Incompletely. It contains the beginning of the records, including the separation of Heaven and Earth and the origins of the Deities and islands.
