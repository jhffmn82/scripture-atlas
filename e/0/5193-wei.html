Wei

place · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: that State

Abstract written by a model

In The Analects of Confucius, Book IX (Tsze Han), Wei was the place from which the Master returned. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XIII: Tsze-Lu, Wei was described as having a government that was a brother to the government of Lu. In Mencius, Book V (Wan Zhang), Wei was a state in which Confucius lived with Yan Chou You; Confucius was dissatisfied there and left it.
