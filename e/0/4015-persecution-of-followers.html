Chapter 21

Jesus foretells that before the other events, his followers will be seized, persecuted, delivered to synagogues and prisons, and brought before kings and governors for his name's sake. They will be betrayed by parents, brothers, relatives, and friends, some will be put to death, and all will hate them. It will serve as a testimony, he will give them a mouth and wisdom no adversary can withstand, and by endurance they will win their lives.
