Chapter 29

The land is foretold as a place of plagues and sicknesses, all sulfur, salt, and burning, unsown, unproductive, and without grass, like Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, and Zeboiim. Future generations, foreigners, and nations will ask why the LORD did this and will be told it was because the people abandoned the covenant and served other gods, so the LORD uprooted them from it.
