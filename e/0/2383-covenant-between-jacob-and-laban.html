Chapter 31

Laban proposes a covenant to serve as a witness between himself and Jacob. They agree that Jacob will not afflict Laban's daughters or take other wives, and that neither will pass over the heap and pillar to harm the other, with God as witness and judge. Jacob swears by the fear of Isaac and then offers a sacrifice and shares a meal.
