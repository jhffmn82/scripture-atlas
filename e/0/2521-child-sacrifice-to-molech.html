Abstract written by a model

Child sacrifice to Molech is forbidden by the LORD. Offenders are punished by death through stoning by the people of the land, because it defiles his sanctuary and profanes his holy name. If people ignore it, he will cut off the offender, his family, and followers.

Chapter 20

Giving any of one's offspring to Molech is punishable by death, with the people of the land to stone the offender. The LORD says it defiles his sanctuary and profanes his holy name, and he will cut off the person. If the people hide their eyes and do not execute the offender, he will cut off the man, his family, and all who follow him.
