the church (body of Christ) in Paul’s Letter to the Ephesians

author unknown · about AD 62 · the work’s page · read it where the church (body of Christ) first appears

Abstract written by a model

The church is the body of Christ, one body with one Spirit, filled by him who fills all in all. Christ is its head and savior, and it is subject to him. Its members are built up through service until they attain unity of faith and knowledge of the Son of God, growing in love as each joint supplies. Christ loved it, gave himself up, cleansed and sanctified it, nourishes and cherishes it, and will present it to himself glorious, holy, without spot, wrinkle or defect.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

The body of Christ is one, and is built up through the work of serving until all attain unity of faith and knowledge of the Son of God. Christ the head fits and knits it together through what every joint supplies, so that it increases and builds itself up in love.

Chapter 5

The assembly is described as being subject to Christ, who is its head and savior. Christ loved it and gave himself up for her, cleansing and sanctifying her so that he might present her to himself gloriously, without spot, wrinkle or defect. The Lord nourishes and cherishes the assembly, and the readers are members of his body, of his flesh and bones.
