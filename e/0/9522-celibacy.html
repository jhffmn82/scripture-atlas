Twelfth Lecture

Harikêsa identifies celibacy as his holy bathing-place, clear and uncontaminated for the soul. In that spiritual bath, he becomes pure and removes hatred or impurity.

Sixteenth Lecture.

Celibacy is realised through ten conditions involving lodging, speech, proximity, sight, hearing, memory, food, moderation, ornaments, and the five pleasant senses. The text presents these conditions as protection against sensuality and doubts about chastity.
