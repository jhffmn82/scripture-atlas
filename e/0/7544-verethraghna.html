Verethraghna

deity · 3 works · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Verethraghna (the blow of victory), Verethraghna, made by Ahura, Verethraghna, made by Mazda

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Verethraghna is worshiped as the Ahura-made, victorious blow. In Visparad, Verethraghna is the Mazda-given, holy blow of victory and lord of ritual order, approached with praise; he furthers settlements and is associated with Victorious Ascendency. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Verethraghna appears in ten forms and grants virility, bodily strength, health, keen sight, victory, and protection while confounding or destroying hostile forces.
