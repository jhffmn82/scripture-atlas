robe of the ephod

Also called: robe, the robe, the robe of the ephod

Abstract written by a model

The robe of the ephod is a garment of woven work, all blue, with a bound head opening so it won't tear. Its skirts bear pomegranates of blue, purple, scarlet and twined linen alternating with pure gold bells, whose sound is heard so Aaron does not die entering and leaving the holy place.

Chapter 39

The robe of the ephod is made of woven work, all of blue, with a bound opening like that of a coat of mail so it will not tear. Its skirts have pomegranates of blue, purple, scarlet and twined linen alternating with pure gold bells, to minister in.
