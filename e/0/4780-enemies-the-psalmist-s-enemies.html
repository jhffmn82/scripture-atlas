Abstract written by a model

The psalmist's enemies are mighty, numerous wrongful foes who hate him without cause, mock him, give him poison and vinegar, and persecute one God has wounded. He responds by asking that their table be a snare, their habitation desolate, and that they be blotted from the book of life.

Chapter 69

The enemies hate the psalmist without cause, outnumber the hairs of his head, and are mighty. They mock him, give him poison and vinegar, and persecute one whom God has wounded. The psalmist asks that their table become a snare, their eyes be darkened, their habitation be desolate, and that they be charged with crime upon crime and blotted out of the book of life.
