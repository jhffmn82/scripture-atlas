Yakshas

group · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: The Yakshas, The Yakshas who are gifted with various virtues, The Yakshas, having remained there according to their merit

Abstract written by a model

Yakshas are virtuous beings who inhabit heaven and heavenly regions one above another, shining like great luminaries. They enjoy divine pleasures in the upper Kalpa heavens, change form at will, and remain there according to merit. At life’s end they descend and are born as men; they also pay homage to Nirgrantha.

Yakshas is in one work on this site, Uttaradhyayana Sutra. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Third Lecture.

Yakshas gifted with various virtues live in heavenly regions situated one above the other. They enjoy divine pleasures, change their form at will, remain according to their merit, and later descend to be born as men.
