Chapter 2

The man who judges others is told he is without excuse because he condemns himself by practicing the same things. He is asked whether he thinks he will escape God's judgment, or whether he despises God's goodness, forbearance, and patience. His hard and unrepentant heart is said to be treasuring up wrath for himself.
