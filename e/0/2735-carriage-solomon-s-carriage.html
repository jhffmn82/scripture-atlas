Chapter 3

The carriage is seen coming up from the wilderness like pillars of smoke, perfumed with myrrh, frankincense, and all the spices of the merchant. King Solomon made it of the wood of Lebanon, with pillars of silver, a bottom of gold, and a seat of purple. Its middle is paved with love, from the daughters of Jerusalem, and sixty mighty men surround it.
