Chapter 10

Adoni-Zedek, king of Jerusalem, feared Israel after hearing of the fall of Ai and Jericho and Gibeon's peace with Israel. He summoned the kings of Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, and Eglon to attack Gibeon, and he was among the five kings who hid in the cave at Makkedah and were later killed.
