the people

Also called: his people, people

Abstract written by a model

The people may be imposed labors upon after they have given their ruler confidence; without that confidence, they think themselves oppressed. Under the sage’s rule, the people would be established, follow their leader, become happy, resort to his dominions, and live harmoniously.
