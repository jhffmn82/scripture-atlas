man clothed in linen

Abstract written by a model

A radiant being clothed in linen, with gold-adorned thighs, a beryl body, lightning face, flaming-torch eyes, bronze arms and feet, and a multitude-like voice. In Chapter 10 he is sent to Daniel because Daniel's words were heard, explains he was withstood twenty-one days by the prince of Persia until Michael helped, and comes to reveal what will befall Daniel's people in the latter days. He strengthens Daniel and says he will return to fight the prince of Persia, after which the prince of Greece will come. In Chapter 12, above the river's waters, he raises both hands and swears by him who lives forever that the end will come in a time, times, and a half.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

The man clothed in linen appears with thighs adorned with pure gold of Uphaz, a body like beryl, a face like lightning, eyes like flaming torches, arms and feet like burnished bronze, and a voice like a multitude. He tells Daniel he was sent because Daniel's words were heard, was withstood twenty-one days by the prince of Persia until Michael helped, and has come to explain what will happen to Daniel's people in the latter days. He strengthens Daniel and says he will return to fight the prince of Persia, after which the prince of Greece will come.

Chapter 12

The man clothed in linen is above the waters of the river and is asked by one of the two others how long until the end of these wonders. He raises both hands to heaven and swears by him who lives forever that it will be a time, times, and a half, and that all will be finished when the breaking of the power of the holy people is complete.
