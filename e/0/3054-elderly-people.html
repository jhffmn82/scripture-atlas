Abstract written by a model

The aged are portrayed as honored: their gray hair brings beautiful judgment, good counsel, wisdom, and experience as a crown, with fear of the Lord as glory. The records warn that those who gather nothing in youth find nothing in old age, and that an old adulterous fool is hated.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 25

The aged are praised for beautiful judgment, knowledge of good counsel, and wisdom, with much experience as their crown and the fear of the Lord as their glory. Those who gathered nothing in youth are warned they will find nothing in old age, and an old fool who is an adulterer is hated. Walking up a sandy hill is used as an image of what a wife full of words is to a quiet man, compared with its effect on aged feet.
