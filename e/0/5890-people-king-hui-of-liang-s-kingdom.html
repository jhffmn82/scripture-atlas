Abstract written by a model

King Hui of Liang's people are moved and fed grain in bad years, yet remain no more numerous than neighboring kingdoms. They visibly suffer hunger and die of famine while the king keeps fat meat and horses. Mencius says sound management of resources, land, and education would bring prosperity.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The people are moved across the river and supplied with grain during bad years. Mencius says they would prosper if farming, fishing, forestry, livestock breeding, land use, and education were properly managed.

Chapter 4

The people have the look of hunger while fat meat is kept in the king's kitchen and fat horses are kept in his stables. Some people die of famine in the wilds because of the way government is administered.
