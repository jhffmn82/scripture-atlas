Abstract written by a model

The fool is a worldly-minded person who longs for life, wealth, pleasures, and goods. Through ignorance and carelessness, he has much wrath and greed, performs injurious, cruel, or sinful deeds, does not know law, and comes to grief. Near death and far from liberation, he remains in the whirl of pains or births and cannot reach the opposite shore.

Second Lecture, Called Conquest of the World. / First Lesson. .. Sixth Lesson.

The fool longs for life, wealth, pleasures, and worldly goods, performs cruel acts, and comes ignorantly to grief. He remains turned around in the whirl of pains and cannot reach the opposite shore.

Fifth Lecture, Called Essence of the World. / First Lesson. .. Sixth Lesson.

The fool performs cruel or sinful acts through ignorance and comes to grief. The fool may speak of final liberation while remaining in ignorance and turning in the whirlpool of births.
