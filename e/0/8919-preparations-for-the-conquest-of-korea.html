preparations for the conquest of Korea

Also called: Preparations for the Conquest of Korea

Abstract written by a model

Preparations for the conquest of Korea begin with purification and offerings to Heavenly, Earthly, mountain, river, and sea Deities. August spirits are placed on the vessel; podocarpus macrophylla ashes are put in gourds; chopsticks and leaf platters are made; an army is equipped and marshalled.
