Daêva-worshippers

Abstract written by a model

Daêva-worshippers are a group questioned over whether they ever ruled as worthy kings. They support wrongdoing with the Karpan and Usig, give the Kine to Rapine, withhold tribal wealth from Ahura Mazda’s worshippers, and fail to bring waters to righteous fields.

Yasna XLIV.

The Daêva-worshippers are questioned as to whether they have ever ruled as worthy kings. They are associated with fighting for those who act amiss, with the Karpan and Usig giving the Kine to Rapine, and with failing to bring waters to the fields for the Righteous Order.
