Surah 8: The Spoils of War (Al-Anfaal)

document · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns Allah, Muhammad and the messenger, believers, disbelievers, and the spoils of war. Allah grants believers help, guidance, protection, forgiveness, provision, and victory, while commanding obedience, steadfastness, preparation, and fair conduct in battle. The unit describes the defeat and punishment of disbelievers, rules concerning treaties, captives, and spoils, and the obligation to resist persecution. It also distinguishes the believers who strive in the cause of Allah and promises them pardon, provision, and Allah's reward.
