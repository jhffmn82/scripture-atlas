Abstract written by a model

Lodging fitness concerns whether a lodging-place suits mendicant use. It requires inspection of the lodging and floor, avoiding harmful beings, unsuitable activity, improper preparation, danger, obstruction, and excessive associations; the mendicant remains content despite discomforts.

Second Lecture, Called Begging for a Couch. / First Lesson. .. Third Lesson.

Lodging fitness depends on the absence of harmful living beings, unsuitable household activity, improper preparation, danger, obstruction, and excessive association with householders or other sectarians. The mendicant should be content with an even or uneven lodging, with or without draughts, dust, flies, gnats, dangers, or troubles, without taking offence.
