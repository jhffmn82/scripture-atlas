Abstract written by a model

A group of thirty-one kings west of the Jordan, each ruling one city from Jericho and Ai to Tirzah across varied regions and peoples. They were defeated by Joshua and the Israelites; Moses and Israel had earlier defeated kings east of the Jordan.

Chapter 12

The kings west of the Jordan numbered thirty-one in all, each listed as one king of his city, from Jericho and Ai to Tirzah. They ruled the hill country, lowland, Arabah, slopes, wilderness, and South, among the Hittite, Amorite, Canaanite, Perizzite, Hivite, and Jebusite, and were struck by Joshua and the children of Israel.
