little children

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Luke, babies and little children are brought to Jesus, and he says God's Kingdom belongs to such as these. In The Book of Moroni, little children are described as whole and unable to sin, not needing repentance or baptism, and partaking of salvation in Jesus Christ.
