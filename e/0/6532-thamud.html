Thamud

group · 8 works · 17 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: (The tribe of) Thamud, (The tribes of) Thamud, the tribe of Thamud, the tribe of) Thamud, tribe of) Thamud

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), Thamud received Salih and a clear sign but rejected the message, hamstrung the camel, and were seized by an earthquake. In Surah 11: Hud, Thamud doubted Salih's call, hamstrung Allah's camel token, and were overtaken by an awful Cry. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), Thamud rejected Salih, demanded a token, hamstrung the she-camel, became penitent, and received retribution. In Surah 51: The Winnowing Winds (Adh-Dhaariyat), Thamud rebelled against their Lord's decree and were overtaken by the thunderbolt. In Surah 54: The Moon (Al-Qamar), Thamud rejected warnings and were punished after their comrade hamstrung the she-camel. In Surah 69: The Reality (Al-Haaqqa), Thamud disbelieved in the judgment to come and were destroyed by the lightning. In Surah 89: The Dawn (Al-Fajr), Thamud were described as a people who clove the rocks in the valley and were dealt with by the Lord after their rebellion and iniquity. In Surah 91: The Sun (Ash-Shams), Thamud denied the truth in rebellious pride, hamstrung Allah's she-camel, and were doomed for their sin.
