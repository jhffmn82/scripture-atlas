Chapter 145

David vows to praise the LORD's name every day and forever, to extol him, meditate on his majesty, and declare his greatness. He says the LORD is greatly to be praised, that generations and the saints will join in it, and he closes by calling all flesh to bless the LORD's holy name forever and ever.
