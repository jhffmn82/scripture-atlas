Chapter 18

The chapter forbids uncovering the nakedness of close relatives, including mother, father's wife, sisters, granddaughters, aunts, daughter-in-law, brother's wife, and a woman and her daughter. It also forbids taking a wife in addition to her sister while the sister lives, approaching a woman during impurity, lying with a neighbor's wife, lying with a man as with a woman, and lying with an animal. These are called abominations, defilement, wickedness, and perversion, and those who do them will be cut off from their people.

Chapter 20

A long list of forbidden unions is given, including with a father's wife, a daughter-in-law, a male, a wife and her mother, an animal, a sister, a woman during her period, an aunt, an uncle's wife, and a brother's wife. Penalties include death, burning with fire, being cut off, or dying childless, and the texts calls these acts uncovering nakedness, perversion, abomination, wickedness, or impurity.
