speaker (the 'I')

person · 1 work · 19 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: I, I alone

Abstract written by a model

The speaker is listless, still, dejected, and forlorn, with no apparent desires, home, or resting place. Unlike other men, the speaker has lost everything and has a stupid, chaotic, benighted, dull, confused, and incapable mind, drifting as if at sea. Yet the speaker values the Tao as a nursing-mother.

20.

The speaker appears listless, still, dejected, forlorn, and without desires that have shown themselves. The speaker feels unlike other men, with a stupid and chaotic mind, seeming dull, confused, incapable, and without a place to rest, but values the Tao.
