Abstract written by a model

In Kojiki, Kibi was subdued and pacified by Oho-kibi-tsu-hiko and Waka-take-kibi-tsu-hiko, with Harima made the mouth of the road. In Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko), Kibi was the native country of Princess Kuro, and Emperor Nin-Toku made a progress there after following her.
