Chapter 71

The speaker takes refuge in the LORD, asks for rescue, and pleads not to be forsaken in old age when his strength fails. He recalls relying on God since the womb, calls himself a marvel to many, and resolves to hope always. He vows to declare God's righteousness, salvation, and strength to the next generation and to sing praises with harp and lyre, saying his soul has been redeemed.
