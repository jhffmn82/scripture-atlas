the mind

Also called: man's mind, mind, my mind, this mind, thy mind, wandering mind

Abstract written by a model

The mind is impure, restless, distracted by worldly occupations, and attached to worldly love, wandering under greed, deceit, pride, mammon, and false understanding. Through the Guru’s instruction and Word, it recognizes the world as a dream, is restrained and purified, fixed on God, penetrated by God's name, and gains peace and happiness.
