In 1 Chronicles, Gad serves as David's seer, delivering the LORD's offer of three punishments and relaying the angel's command to build an altar on Ornan's threshing floor, which David obeys.

Chapter 24

Gad, David's seer and a prophet, receives the LORD's word and brings David the offer of three punishments, asking for an answer to return. Later he tells David to go up and build an altar to the LORD on the threshing floor of Araunah the Jebusite.
