Chapter 7

The children of Hobaiah, Hakkoz, and Barzillai, the last named after a Gileadite Barzillai whose daughter their ancestor married, searched for their genealogical records and could not find them. They were deemed disqualified and removed from the priesthood, and the governor told them not to eat the most holy things until a priest could minister with Urim and Thummim.
