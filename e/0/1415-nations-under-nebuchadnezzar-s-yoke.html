Abstract written by a model

A collective of nations, from Egypt, Edom, Moab, Ammon, Tyre and Sidon to Elam and the Medes, is made to drink the cup of judgment. They must serve Nebuchadnezzar, his son, and his son's son for seventy years. Those who submit remain and till their land; those who refuse face sword, famine, and pestilence. Eventually, many nations and great kings will make Babylon's king their bondservant.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 25

The nations, listed from Egypt, Uz, the Philistine cities, Edom, Moab and Ammon to Tyre, Sidon, Arabia, Elam, the Medes and the kings of the north, are all made to drink the cup. They will serve Babylon seventy years and face the sword, with the slain left unlamented and unburied across the earth.

Chapter 27

The kings of Edom, Moab, Ammon, Tyre, and Sidon receive the message through their messengers in Jerusalem. All nations are told they will serve Nebuchadnezzar, his son, and his son's son; those who submit will stay in their land and till it, while those who refuse will be punished with sword, famine, and pestilence. Later, many nations and great kings will make Babylon's king their bondservant.
