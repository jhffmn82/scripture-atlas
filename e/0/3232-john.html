Chapter 16

John went up from Gazara to report Cendebaeus's actions to his father Simon, then led the army with his brother Judas, pursuing the defeated enemy to Kidron and burning the towers at Azotus before returning to Judea in peace. After Ptolemy murdered his father, John was warned at Gazara, seized the men sent to kill him and killed them. His other deeds, wars and wall-building are said to be written in the chronicles of his high priesthood, which he held after his father.
