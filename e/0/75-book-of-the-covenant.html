book of the covenant

object · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: all the LORD’s words, the book of the covenant, the book of the covenant of the Most High God, the law which Moses commanded us

Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, the book of the covenant contains all the LORD’s words, which Moses reads to the people before they agree to obey them. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, the book of the covenant is the covenant of the Most High God and the law Moses commanded as an inheritance for Jacob's assemblies.
