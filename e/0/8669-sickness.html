Fargard XX.

Sickness was driven back by Thrita and is commanded to depart. Ahura Mazda says he drives it away.

Fargard XXI.

Sickness is to be destroyed altogether by the descending waters, including the sickness made by the Gaini. It is also driven away with other diseases created by Angra Mainyu's witchcraft.
