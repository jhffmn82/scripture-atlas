Abstract written by a model

The psalmist is a speaker who waited patiently for the LORD and was rescued from a miry pit, given a firm place on a rock and a new song of praise. He delights in God's law, which is within his heart, and openly proclaims God's righteousness, faithfulness and salvation in the great assembly. Yet, beset by countless iniquities and evils and opposed by enemies, he calls himself poor and needy and pleads for the LORD, his helper and deliverer.

Chapter 40

The speaker waited patiently for the LORD, was rescued from the pit, and was given a firm place to stand and a new song of praise. He says he delights to do God's will, has proclaimed God's righteousness, faithfulness and loving kindness in the great assembly without concealing them, and now, surrounded by evils and his own iniquities, calls himself poor and needy and pleads for deliverance.
