Abstract written by a model

The Gospel According to Matthew describes Jesus being flogged, mocked, led out, and crucified between two robbers, dying amid darkness, a torn veil, an earthquake, opened tombs, and the centurion’s declaration. The Good News According to Mark describes Jesus being crucified at Golgotha between two robbers, dying after darkness falls, and being declared the Son of God by the centurion. The Gospel According to Luke describes Jesus being crucified at The Skull between two criminals, being mocked, dying amid darkness and a torn temple veil, and prompting the centurion to glorify God. The Gospel According to John describes Jesus being crucified at Golgotha between two others, receiving vinegar, declaring that it is finished, dying, and having his side pierced while his legs remain unbroken. Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa) says that Jesus son of Mary was not crucified, but that it appeared so to those who differed concerning it. The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry describes the Lamb of God being taken, judged, lifted up on the cross, and crucified for the sins of the world. The Second Book of Nephi foretells and describes the Holy One of Israel being scourged and crucified, then laid in a sepulchre for three days before resurrection. The Book of Mosiah describes Jesus Christ suffering temptation, pain, hunger, thirst, fatigue, and anguish, being scourged, crucified, and slain, yielding to the Father’s will to redeem his people.

Chapter 27

Passersby, priests, and the robbers mock him; darkness covers the land from the sixth to the ninth hour, and he dies, followed by the torn veil, an earthquake, opened tombs, and the centurion's declaration.
