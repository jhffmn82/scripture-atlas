Abstract written by a model

Ritual Order is holy, spiritual, and blessed. Its holy lords encompass creatures, festivals, seasons, chants, Gâthas, beings, places, and ritual powers, approached with praise and linked to spiritual and earthly authority. It is associated with Ahura Mazda, the Gâtha Ahunavaiti’s own ritual order, and the Ahuna-vairya as holy lord.

Visparad / Visparad I.

The holy lords of the ritual order include lords of spiritual, earthly, aquatic, terrestrial, winged, wild, and cloven-hoofed creatures. They also include the Yearly festivals, named Gâthas, ritual chants, and other beings and places addressed in the announcements and Yasna offerings.

Visparad II.

The lords of the ritual are approached with praise and are associated with spiritual and earthly authority, the water and land, animals, festivals, seasons, chants, Gâthas, and other ritual powers.

Visparad XIV (Sp. XVI).

The ritual order is associated with Ahura Mazda, who is worshipped as its holy Lord. It is also associated with the Gâtha Ahunavaiti, which is described as having its own ritual order, and with the Ahuna-vairya, which is called its holy lord.
