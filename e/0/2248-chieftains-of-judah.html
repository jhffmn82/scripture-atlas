Abstract written by a model

The chieftains of Judah resolve inwardly that Jerusalem's inhabitants are their strength through the LORD of Armies, their God. They are then portrayed as a fiery pan among wood, devouring surrounding peoples on every side.

Chapter 12

The chieftains of Judah say in their heart that the inhabitants of Jerusalem are their strength in the LORD of Armies their God. The LORD will make them like a pan of fire among wood and a flaming torch among sheaves, devouring the surrounding peoples on the right hand and on the left.
