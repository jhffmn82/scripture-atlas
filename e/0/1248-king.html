Abstract written by a model

The king is a ruler chosen by the LORD from among the people's brothers, never a foreigner. He is restricted from multiplying horses, wives, silver and gold, and from returning the people to Egypt. Upon taking the throne he writes his own copy of the law from the Levitical priests' book and reads it all his life, learning to fear the LORD and not exalt himself above his brothers, so he and his children prolong their rule in Israel.

Chapter 17

The king is to be one the LORD chooses from among the people's brothers, never a foreigner. He must not multiply horses, wives, silver, or gold, nor cause the people to return to Egypt. He must write his own copy of the law from the Levitical priests' book and read it all his life, so that he fears the LORD, keeps the law, and is not lifted up above his brothers, thereby prolonging his days and his children's in his kingdom.
