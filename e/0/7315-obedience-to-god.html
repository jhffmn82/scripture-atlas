obedience to God

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: By obeying Him, obeyeth God, obeying Him

Abstract written by a model

Obedience to God is an activity that produces wisdom and understanding and leads to salvation. It brings honour, ecstasy, virtue, freedom from punishment and transmigration, and the heart’s pleasure in His pure Name, unfolding through obedience into divine knowledge, fashioned intellect, and incomparable joy.
