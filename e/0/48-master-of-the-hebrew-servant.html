master (of the Hebrew servant)

Abstract written by a model

A master who owns a Hebrew servant under law. He must free the servant in the seventh year but keeps the wife he gave him and her children. If the servant refuses freedom, the master bores his ear with an awl at the door, binding him forever. He must also let a displeasing female servant be redeemed.

Chapter 21

The master must free the Hebrew servant in the seventh year, but keeps a wife he gave the servant and her children. If the servant refuses to leave, the master brings him to God and the door and bores his ear with an awl. A master who has married a female servant and is displeased with her must let her be redeemed and may not sell her to a foreign people.
