Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the plains of Moab are the journey's final encampment by the Jordan near Jericho, where the LORD instructs Moses about taking Canaan. In Deuteronomy, the plains of Moab are where Moses departs to ascend Mount Nebo and the children of Israel weep for him for thirty days.
