is_commanded_for seven nations of Canaan When the LORD delivers the nations up and Israel strikes them, Israel is commanded to utterly destroy them, showing no mercy and no pity. The text says the nations will be consumed little by little rather than at once, and that no one will be able to stand before Israel until they are destroyed.
