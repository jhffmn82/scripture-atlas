Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, it is the consecration of the priests through washing, clothing, anointing, offerings, blood and oil applied to the priests, lasting seven days. In Leviticus, it is the LORD-commanded ordination of Aaron and his sons, performed by Moses before the congregation through washing, clothing, anointing, and offerings, concluding with a seven-day stay at the Tent of Meeting door.

The consecration involves washing, clothing, and anointing, the sin offering bull, the burnt offering ram, and the ram of consecration with blood and oil applied to the priests. It lasts seven days, with daily atonement for the altar, and the meat and bread are eaten only by the priests, not by a stranger.

Chapter 8

The ordination is a ceremony of washing, clothing, anointing, and offerings carried out by Moses before the assembled congregation, ending with a seven-day stay at the Tent of Meeting door. Moses says it was done at the LORD's command to make atonement for them, and Aaron and his sons do all that was commanded.
