Chapter 13

Tobit foretells that Jerusalem will be built with sapphires, emeralds and precious stones, with walls, towers and battlements of pure gold. Its streets will be paved with beryl, carbuncle and stones of Ophir. Many nations will come from afar with gifts for the King of heaven, and generations will sing songs of rejoicing, while those who mourned its scourges will rejoice at seeing all its glory.
