Chapter 7

The LORD of Armies commands people to execute true judgment, show kindness and compassion to one another, not oppress the widow, fatherless, foreigner or poor, and not devise evil against a brother in the heart. These are described as the words proclaimed by the former prophets when Jerusalem was inhabited and prosperous.
