Kedar

group · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: children of Kedar, the children of the east

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, Kedar is a group whose glory and mighty archers will fail within a year. In The Book of Jeremiah, Kedar is a people of the east attacked by Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon at the LORD's command and scattered with calamity.

Kedar in The Book of Isaiah

author unknown · 739 to 680 BC · the work’s page · read it where Kedar first appears

Abstract written by a model

Kedar is a group with glory and mighty archers; the LORD declares that within a year its glory will fail and its remaining archers will be few.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 21

Kedar is told, through the Lord's word to the speaker, that within a year, counted as a contract worker would count it, all its glory will fail. The remaining number of its archers and mighty men will be few, because the LORD has spoken it.

Kedar in The Book of Jeremiah

author unknown · 629 to 579 BC · the work’s page · read it where Kedar first appears

Abstract written by a model

Kedar is a people of the east, struck by Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon. The LORD commands the attack, their tents, flocks, curtains, vessels and camels are carried off amid cries of 'Terror on every side!', and they are scattered to all winds with calamity.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 49

Kedar is described as struck by Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and the LORD commands an attack on the children of the east. Their tents, flocks, curtains, vessels and camels are to be carried off as they are met with cries of 'Terror on every side!' The LORD will scatter them to all winds and bring calamity from every side.
