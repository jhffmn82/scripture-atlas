Jesus walking on the sea

event · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: walking on the sea

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Jesus walks on the sea toward his disciples, invites Peter to come, rescues him when he sinks, and stills the wind. In The Good News According to Mark, Jesus walks on the sea toward his disciples struggling against a contrary wind, then gets into the boat and the wind ceases. In The Gospel According to John, Jesus walks on the sea toward the disciples' boat, tells them not to be afraid, and immediately brings the boat to land.
