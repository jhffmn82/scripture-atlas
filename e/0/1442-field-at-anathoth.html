Chapter 32

The field at Anathoth, in the land of Benjamin, belongs to Hanamel and is offered to Jeremiah under the right of redemption and inheritance. Jeremiah buys it for seventeen shekels of silver. It serves as a sign that fields will again be bought in the land despite the coming Chaldean conquest.
