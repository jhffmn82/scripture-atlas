Ame-oshi-tarashi-hiko

person · 1 work · 17 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: His Augustness Ame-oshi-tarashi-hiko, The elder brother His Augustness Ame-oshi-tarashi-hiko

Abstract written by a model

Ame-oshi-tarashi-hiko is a deity and the elder of two august children. He is chiefly significant as ancestor of numerous Grandees, the Dukes of Ihitaka in Ise and Ichishi, and the Rulers of the Land of Chika-tsu-Afumi.

Ame-oshi-tarashi-hiko is in one work on this site, Kojiki. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Sect. LVIII.—Emperor Ko-Sho.

His Augustness Ame-oshi-tarashi-hiko was the elder of the two august children. He was the ancestor of the Grandees of Kasuga, Ohoyake, Ahata, Wonu, Kaki-no-moto, Ichihiwi, Ohosaka, Ana, Taki, Haguri, Chita, Muza, Tsunuyama, the Dukes of Ihitaka in Ise and Ichishi, and the Rulers of the Land of Chika-tsu-Afumi.
