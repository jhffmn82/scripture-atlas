greed

topic · 3 works · 5 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: avarice, covetousness

Abstract written by a model

In Sutrakritanga (Sûtrakritâṅga), Book I, greed is a passion that defiles the soul, which a wise and pious man should conquer and remain free from. In Uttaradhyayana Sutra, greed arises from desire, ends without property, and violent desire drives theft, deceit, falsehood, and continuing misery. In Hymns of Guru Nanak, greed is a heart-burning worldly entanglement that causes the false to miss God's palace until the Guru's instruction quenches it.

Greed ceases when property is absent. When desire becomes violent, greed leads a person to take another's property, increasing deceit and falsehood without removing misery.
