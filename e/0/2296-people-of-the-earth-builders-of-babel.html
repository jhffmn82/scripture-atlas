Abstract written by a model

The people of the earth share one language, settle on a plain in Shinar, and use bricks and tar to build a city and tower at Babel to make a name and avoid scattering. The LORD confuses their language, scatters them, and building stops.

Chapter 11

Speaking one language, they travel east, settle in a plain in Shinar, and make bricks to build a city and a tower to the sky, wanting to make a name for themselves and not be scattered. The LORD confuses their language and scatters them across the earth, and they stop building the city.
