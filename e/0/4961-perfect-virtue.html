perfect virtue

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Perfect virtue consists in practising gravity, generosity of soul, sincerity, earnestness, and kindness everywhere under heaven. These qualities bring respect, followers, trust, accomplishment, and the ability to employ the services of others.
