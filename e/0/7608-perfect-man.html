Abstract written by a model

The perfect man is a person of absolute purity who unites physical frame, vitality, nature, and virtue with God. Formless and beyond objective existences, he vanquishes death, passes unharmed through solid bodies and fire, scales heights without fear, and acts without reliance on self-confidence or authority. He shares earthly food while sharing God’s happiness, remains free from care, and avoids trouble, censure, plotting, and toadying. Though he may be hidden, he benefits both natural and artificial things and enters God’s eternal harmony. In the end, he leaves no trace, transcends human limits without leaving the world, and lives with mankind unharmed and inwardly independent.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIX.

The perfect man can pass through solid bodies and fire without obstruction because he is in a condition of absolute purity. He is formless, beyond the reach of objective existences, and acts without dependence on self-confidence or authority.

CHAPTER XXIII.

The perfect man may be hidden like a corpse in a tomb while people become unartificial and without care. He shares the food of the earth but the happiness of God, avoids trouble from men and things, and acts without joining in censure, plotting, or toadying. He can devote himself profitably to the natural and artificial alike and is capable of entering the eternal harmony of God.

CHAPTER XXVI.

The perfect man leaves no trace behind and can transcend the limits of the human without withdrawing from the world. He lives in accord with mankind without suffering injury and possesses an inner independence from others and from the world’s teachings.
