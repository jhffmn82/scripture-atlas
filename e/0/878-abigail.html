Abstract written by a model

Abigail is an intelligent, beautiful woman, wife of Nabal the Carmelite, attended by five maids. She quickly sends food to David, bows, takes blame, and turns him from vengeance by speaking of his future. She tells Nabal in the morning; after his death she becomes David's wife.

Chapter 25

Abigail, Nabal's intelligent and beautiful wife, acts quickly on a young man's warning, loading donkeys with food and secretly riding out to meet David. She bows before him, takes the blame, and speaks of the LORD making David a sure house and prince over Israel, which turns him from vengeance. She tells Nabal only in the morning, and after his death she humbly accepts David's proposal and becomes his wife.
