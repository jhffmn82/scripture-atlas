Abstract written by a model

Heaven is a vast, enduring source of Nature, sincerity, appointments, nourishment, and transforming operations. It confers what is called the Nature and makes appointments that the superior man quietly awaits without murmuring. It is bountiful to beings according to their qualities: nourishing what flourishes, overthrowing what is ready to fall, and granting dignity and the throne as favors. Large, substantial, high, brilliant, far-reaching, and long-enduring, it contains the sun, moon, stars, and constellations of the zodiac, overspreading all things. It supports, contains, overshadows, and curtains all things, and is great because all are nourished together without injuring one another. Finally, it measures the ideal ruler and individual, whose sincerity may know its transforming and nurturing operations.
