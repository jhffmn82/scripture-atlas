Thrita

person · 2 works · 19 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: he, the First Healer

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Thrita prepares H(a)oma for the corporeal world and receives the blessing of fathering Urvâkhshaya and Keresâspa. In Vendidad (Vendîdâd), Thrita is the first healer and obtains remedies to drive sickness and disease from mortal bodies.

Thrita in Vendidad (Vendîdâd)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Thrita first appears

Abstract written by a model

Thrita is the first healer, foremost among the healthful, wise, happy, wealthy, glorious, and strong men of yore. He obtains a source of remedies from Khshathra-Vairya, then drives away sickness, death, pain, fever, disease, rottenness, and infection from mortal bodies.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Fargard XX.

Thrita was the first of the healthful, wise, happy, wealthy, glorious, and strong men of yore to drive back sickness and death and turn away the point of the poniard and the fire of fever from mortal bodies. He asked for and obtained a source of remedies.
