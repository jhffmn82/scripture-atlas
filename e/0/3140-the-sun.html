Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the sun is a heavenly body given a tent by God, likened to a bridegroom and a strong man, whose heat reaches everything. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, the sun is a marvelous instrument made by the Lord that gives light and looks at all things.

Abstract written by a model

The sun is a heavenly body given a tent by God, likened to a bridegroom leaving his room and a strong man rejoicing in his course, travelling from one end of the heavens to the other; its heat reaches everything.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 19

The sun has a tent set for it in the heavens by God. It is likened to a bridegroom coming out of his room and a strong man rejoicing to run his course, going out from one end of the heavens and circling to the other. Nothing is hidden from its heat.

the sun in The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus

author unknown · about 179 BC · the work’s page · read it where the sun first appears

Abstract written by a model

The sun is a marvelous instrument made by the Lord, which gives light and looks at all things. It rises bringing tidings, then at noon burns three times hotter than a furnace, drying the land and blinding eyes, its course hastened at the Lord's word.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 43

The sun is described as a marvelous instrument, the work of the Most High, that brings tidings as it rises. At noon it dries up the land, burns three times hotter than a furnace, and blinds the eyes, and the Lord hastens its course at his word.
