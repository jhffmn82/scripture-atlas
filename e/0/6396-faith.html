faith

topic · 5 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: belief, believe, faith in Allah, the faith, the religion of folk, the religion of my fathers, the right religion

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida), faith is necessary for work not to be vain, requires belief in Allah and the Last Day with righteous action, and calls believers to trust Allah and remain steadfast. In Surah 8: The Spoils of War (Al-Anfaal), faith increases when Allah's revelations are recited, true believers trust in their Lord, and Allah gives discrimination between right and wrong to those who keep their duty to Him. In Surah 12: Joseph (Yusuf), faith in Allah is presented as worship of Allah alone, rejection of partners and false names, trust in Allah's judgment, and hope in His mercy. In Surah 34: Sheba (Saba), faith is affirmed through belief in Allah, the Hereafter, and doing good works, but cannot be reached after people are seized by terror and separated from what they desire. In Surah 48: The Victory (Al-Fath), faith is increased through peace of reassurance and belief in Allah and His messenger is connected to reward and forgiveness.

faith in Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where faith first appears

Abstract written by a model

Faith is necessary for work not to be vain and requires belief in Allah and the Last Day together with righteous action. Denying faith makes one’s work vain and leads to loss in the Hereafter. Faith is connected with belief in Allah, His messengers, His revelations, and the Last Day, as well as doing right. Those who have faith are instructed to trust Allah and remain steadfast. The records begin by defining faith’s necessity and the consequence of denying it, then connect it to its beliefs and right action, ending with guidance for believers to trust Allah and persevere.

Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida) / 1 O ye who believe! Fulfil your indentures. The beast of cattle is made lawful unto you .. 5 This day are (all) good things made lawful for you. The food of those who have received the Scripture is lawful for you, and your food is lawful for them. And so are the virtuous women of the believers and the virtuous women of those who received the Scripture before you (lawful for you) when ye give them their marriage portions and live with them in honour, not in fornication, nor taking them as secret concubines. Whoso denieth the faith, his work is vain and he will be among the losers in the Hereafter.

Whoso denies the faith has vain work and will be among the losers in the Hereafter. Those who disbelieve are said to be in despair of harming the religion.

6 O ye who believe! When ye rise up for prayer, wash you faces, and your hands up to the elbows, and lightly rub your heads and (wash) your feet up to the ankles. And if ye are unclean, purify yourselves. And if ye are sick or on a journey, or one of you cometh from the closet, or ye have had contact with women, and ye find not water, then go to clean, high ground and rub your faces and your hands with some of it. Allah would not place a burden on you, but He would purify you and would perfect His grace upon you, that ye may give thanks.

Surah 48: The Victory (Al-Fath) / 1 Lo! We have given thee (O Muhammad) a signal victory, / 2 That Allah may forgive the .. 29 Muhammad is the messenger of Allah. And those with him are hard against the disbelievers and merciful among themselves. Thou (O Muhammad) seest them bowing and falling prostrate (in worship), seeking bounty from Allah and (His) acceptance. The mark of them is on their foreheads from the traces of prostration. Such is their likeness in the Torah and their likeness in the Gospel - like as sown corn that sendeth forth its shoot and strengtheneth it and riseth firm upon its stalk, delighting the sowers - that He may enrage the disbelievers with (the sight of) them. Allah hath promised, unto such of them as believe and do good works, forgiveness and immense reward.

The believers receive peace of reassurance so that they may add faith unto their faith. Faith in Allah and His messenger is presented as a condition connected with reward and forgiveness.
