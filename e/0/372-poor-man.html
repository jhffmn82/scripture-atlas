poor man

archetype · 2 works · 10 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: A poor man, humble, humble man, poor, poor men, the humble, the poor, the poor is

Abstract written by a model

In Proverbs, the poor man is valued when he walks in integrity, yet is abandoned and shunned; whoever pities him lends to the LORD and is rewarded. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, the poor man is a socially despised figure whose wisdom is ignored and who is exploited, abandoned, and rebuked.

the poor in Proverbs

author unknown · 969 to 699 BC · the work’s page · read it where the poor first appears

Abstract written by a model

The poor man who walks in integrity is valued above the perverse fool and the liar, yet he is abandoned: separated from friends, shunned by all relatives, and avoided despite pleas. Whoever pities him lends to the LORD and is rewarded.

Chapter 19

The poor man who walks in integrity is called better than a perverse fool, and better than a liar. He is separated from his friend, shunned by all his relatives, and avoided by friends even when he pursues them with pleas. Whoever has pity on him lends to the LORD and will be rewarded.

poor man in The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus

author unknown · about 179 BC · the work’s page · read it where poor man first appears

Abstract written by a model

The poor man is a figure of social contempt: he apologizes when wronged, is preyed upon and despised by the rich, and is abandoned by friends in need. When he falls he is rebuked and pushed aside; his wisdom is ignored and he is dismissed with 'Who is this?' Poverty is evil only in the ungodly's opinion.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

The poor man is wronged yet apologizes, is forsaken when in need, and is treated as a feeding ground for the rich and an abomination to them. When he falls he is pushed away even by friends and rebuked, his wisdom is not listened to, and people ask 'Who is this?' when he speaks. The text states that poverty is evil only in the opinion of the ungodly.
