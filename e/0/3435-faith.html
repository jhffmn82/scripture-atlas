faith

Also called: faith on the Lord, hearing of faith, one faith, the faith, the faith which we have had, those who are of faith

Abstract written by a model

In Paul’s Letter to the Galatians, faith brings the Spirit, makes believers children of Abraham, justifies them, and enables them to receive the promise of the Spirit. In The Book of Mosiah, faith is in Jesus Christ and king Benjamin’s teachings, brings forgiveness and spiritual transformation, and must be maintained through trials until the end of life.

faith in The Book of Mosiah

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where faith first appears

Abstract written by a model

Faith is in Jesus Christ and in what king Benjamin taught. It brings forgiveness, joy, peace of conscience, great knowledge, and changed hearts, making people sons and daughters of Christ. Alma preached faith on the Lord, the church was commanded to have one faith, and people must continue in it through testing and until the end of life.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

Faith in Jesus Christ brings forgiveness, joy, and peace of conscience. People must continue in faith concerning what they have heard about the coming of the Lord until the end of their lives.

Chapter 5

Faith in the things king Benjamin has spoken brings his people to great knowledge and exceedingly great joy. Faith on Christ’s name changes their hearts and makes them his sons and daughters.

Chapter 18

Alma taught faith on the Lord as part of the message given to the people. The church was commanded to have one faith.
