spiritual warfare (weapons against strongholds)

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: strongholds, warfare, weapons of our warfare

Abstract written by a model

Paul says that though he walks in the flesh, he does not wage war according to the flesh. The weapons of this warfare are mighty before God for throwing down strongholds, imaginations and every high thing exalted against the knowledge of God, and for bringing every thought captive to the obedience of Christ.
