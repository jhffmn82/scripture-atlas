Ashkelon

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Judges, Ashkelon is where Samson goes after the companions answer his riddle, striking thirty men and taking their plunder. In The Book of Jeremiah, Ashkelon is said to be brought to nothing and is a target against which the LORD has appointed his sword.
