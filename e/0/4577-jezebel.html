Jezebel

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: her, your woman Jezebel

Abstract written by a model

Jezebel is a woman in Thyatira who calls herself a prophetess and seduces the speaker's servants into sexual immorality and eating idol sacrifices. Given time to repent, she refuses. Judgment follows: she and her adulterous followers face great oppression, and her children will be killed with Death.
