His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites is an august male Deity, one of the Seven Divine Generations and elder brother of the Female-Who-Invites. With her, he is commanded to make, consolidate, and give birth to the drifting land; he stirs brine with the heavenly jewelled spear, makes lands, and jointly gives birth to fourteen islands and thirty-five Deities. After ritual words, he admires her, but later laments exchanging the Princess-Who-Invites for their child; his tears produce the Crying-Weeping-Female-Deity, and he cuts off the head of the Deity Shining-Elder with his ten-grasp sabre. He follows the Female-Who-Invites to the Land of Hades seeking her return so their lands may be finished, then flees her rotting body, delays pursuers with his head-dress and comb, repels them with peaches, and seals the pass with a rock. Polluted by Hades, he purifies himself at a river-mouth, producing Deities. Finally he begets three illustrious children, assigns domains, expels His-Brave-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness, and dwells at Taga.

His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Sect.

The Male-Who-Invites is one of the Seven Divine Generations. Together with the Female-Who-Invites, he is commanded to make, consolidate, and give birth to the drifting land, and he stirs the brine with the heavenly jewelled spear.

Sect.

The Male-Who-Invites speaks first after the two Deities repeat the ritual, saying, "Ah! what a fair and lovely maiden!"

Sect.

His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites lamented that he had exchanged Her Augustness the Princess-Who-Invites for their single child. As he wept while creeping round her august pillow and feet, his tears gave birth to the Crying-Weeping-Female-Deity.

Sect.

His Augustness the Male-Who-invites draws the ten-grasp sabre girded on him and cuts off the head of his child, the Deity Shining-Elder.

Sect.

His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites follows Her Augustness the Female-Who-Invites to the Land of Hades and asks her to return because the lands made by the siblings are unfinished. He looks at her rotting body, flees from her, uses his head-dress and comb to create food that delays his pursuers, and uses three peaches to repel them. He blocks the Even Pass of Hades with a thousand-draught rock and declares that a thousand and five hundred parturition-houses will be established each day if she kills a thousand people daily.

Sect.

He declares that he has come to a hideous and polluted land and decides to purify his august person. He removes his clothing and accessories, then bathes in the middle reach of a small river, producing numerous Deities.

Sect.

His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites rejoices at begetting three illustrious children and assigns each one a domain to rule. He becomes angry with His-Brave-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness and expels him.
