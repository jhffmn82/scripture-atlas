Vârengana

Also called: bird of birds, that strong bird

Abstract written by a model

Vârengana is a strong feathered bird whose feathers and bones serve as protective talismans, bringing their holder help, homage, Glory, and protection; its feather is prescribed against curses or spells through rubbing or holding.

Vârengana is in one work on this site, Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

XIV. Bahrâm Yast.

A feather of the Vârengana is prescribed as a remedy for a curse or spell. Rubbing the body with it or holding a bone or feather of the bird is said to bring help, homage, glory, and protection from attack.
