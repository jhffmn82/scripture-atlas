is_practiced_by Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra [with circumspection and without possessions] The Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra entered the state of houselessness after leaving his home and tearing out his hair. He later walked naked, accepted alms in the hollow of his hand, guarded his thoughts, words, and acts, and endured pleasant and unpleasant occurrences with equanimity.
