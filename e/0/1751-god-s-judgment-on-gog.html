Chapter 38

When Gog comes against Israel, the LORD's wrath will rise, causing a great shaking in which creatures and men tremble, mountains are thrown down, and every wall falls. He will call a sword against Gog so that every man's sword is against his brother, and will judge him with pestilence, blood, torrential rains, great hailstones, fire, and sulfur. The result is that the LORD magnifies and sanctifies himself and is known to many nations.
