Chapter 41

These eighty men arrived with shaved beards, torn clothes, and self-inflicted cuts, carrying meal offerings and frankincense to bring to the LORD's house. Ishmael met them weeping, invited them to Gedaliah, then killed them in the middle of the city and cast them into the pit; ten of them were spared after offering hidden stores of wheat, barley, oil, and honey.
