Husravah

person · 2 works · 13 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Kavi Husravah, gallant Husravah, king Husravah, the gallant Husravah, the gallant Husravah, he who united the Aryan nations into one kingdom, the lord Kavi Husravah

Abstract written by a model

In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, Husravah is a gallant king who united the Aryan nations into one kingdom, sacrifices to Drvâspa to avenge Syâvarshâna and Aghraêratha, and is asked to kill the bound Franghrasyan. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Husravah is a holy, gallant king and unifier of the Aryan nations who receives a boon to kill Franghrasyan, gains kingly Glory and other blessings, defeats enemies, and binds Frangrasyan and Keresavazda to avenge Syâvarshâna and Aghraêratha.

Husravah in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Husravah first appears

Abstract written by a model

Husravah is a holy, gallant king and unifier of the Aryan nations. After sacrificing to Ashi Vanguhi, he receives a boon to kill Franghrasyan. He gains kingly Glory, strength, victory, health, offspring, long life, and dominion; defeats enemies; and binds Frangrasyan and Keresavazda to avenge Syâvarshâna and Aghraêratha.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XVII. Ashi Yast.

Husravah is described as gallant and as having united the Aryan nations into one kingdom. He offers a sacrifice to Ashi Vanguhi and asks for the power to kill Franghrasyan behind the Kaêkasta lake, receiving that boon.

XIX. Zamyâd Yast.

Kavi Husravah receives the kingly Glory and is granted strength, victory, health, offspring, long life, and dominion. He prevails over his enemies and binds Frangrasyan and Keresavazda to avenge the deaths of Syâvarshâna and Aghraêratha.
