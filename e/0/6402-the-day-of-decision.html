the Day of Decision

event · 4 works · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: This is the Day of Decision, the Day of Decision is, the threatened Day

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 44: The Smoke (Ad-Dukhaan), the Day of Decision is the appointed term for all people, when a friend cannot avail a friend except one on whom Allah has mercy. In Surah 50: The letter Qaaf (Qaaf), the threatened Day is the day for which the trumpet is blown and on which people come forth for judgment. In Surah 77: The Emissaries (Al-Mursalaat), the Day of Decision is the appointed day when the former and latter folk are brought together and judgment is made clear; the repudiators are warned of woe, while those who kept their duty receive reward. In Surah 78: The Announcement (An-Naba), the Day of Decision is a fixed time when the trumpet is blown, people come in multitudes, the heaven opens as gates, and the hills move like a mirage.
