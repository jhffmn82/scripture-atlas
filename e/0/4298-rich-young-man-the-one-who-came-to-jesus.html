Chapter 19

The young man calls Jesus 'Good teacher' and asks what good thing he must do for eternal life. He says he has kept the commandments named by Jesus from his youth and asks what he still lacks. Told to sell his possessions, give to the poor, and follow Jesus, he goes away sad because he had great possessions.
