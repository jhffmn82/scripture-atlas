Abstract written by a model

In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, the moon marks seasons, declares times, serves as a sign for the world and feast days, names the month, wanes upon completing its course, and wonderfully increases as an instrument of the army on high. In Surah 54: The Moon (Al-Qamar), the Moon is described as having been rent in twain when the Hour drew nigh.

Abstract written by a model

The moon is a celestial object marking seasons, declaring times, and serving as a sign for the world and feast days; it names the month, wanes upon completing its course, and wonderfully increases as an instrument of the army on high.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 43

The moon marks the changing seasons, declares times, and is a sign for the world, including the feast days. It wanes when it completes its course, gives the month its name, and increases wonderfully in its changing as an instrument of the army on high.
