Abstract written by a model

Cain, a tiller of the ground and brother of Abel, offers a sacrifice the LORD does not respect, grows angry, and kills Abel. Cursed as a fugitive and wanderer yet given a protective sign, he settles in Nod east of Eden, fathers Enoch, builds a city, and founds Lamech's line.

Chapter 4

Cain, a tiller of the ground, brings an offering that the LORD does not respect, becomes angry, and kills his brother Abel in the field. He is cursed to be a fugitive and wanderer, given a protective sign, and settles in Nod, where he has a son Enoch, builds a city named for him, and becomes the ancestor of Lamech's line.
