Abstract written by a model

A divine judgment decreed by the LORD through Elijah: evil will come on Ahab, his male descendants will be swept away, and his house will match those of Jeroboam and Baasha. Dogs will devour Jezebel and Ahab's line, but Ahab's humbling postpones the evil to his son's day.

Chapter 21

The LORD declares through Elijah that he will bring evil on Ahab, sweep away his male descendants, and make his house like those of Jeroboam and Baasha. Dogs will eat Jezebel, and those of Ahab's line who die in the city or the field will be eaten by dogs and birds, though after Ahab humbles himself the evil is postponed to his son's day.
