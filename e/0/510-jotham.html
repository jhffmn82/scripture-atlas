Chapter 9

Jotham, Jerubbaal's youngest son, hides and survives the massacre. From Mount Gerizim he tells the parable of the trees and calls down fire on Abimelech and Shechem, then flees to Beer for fear of Abimelech. The text closes by saying his curse came on them.
