Amon in The Second Book of Kings

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Amon first appears

Abstract written by a model

Amon, son of Manasseh and Meshullemeth, became king of Judah at twenty-two and reigned two years in Jerusalem. Like his father, he did evil, served idols, and abandoned the LORD. His servants conspired and killed him in his own house. He was buried in the garden of Uzza, succeeded by his son Josiah.

Amon in The Second Book of Chronicles

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Amon first appears

Abstract written by a model

Amon, Manasseh's son, became king at twenty-two and reigned two years in Jerusalem. He did evil like his father, sacrificing to the engraved images Manasseh made, and never humbled himself, trespassing more and more. His servants conspired and killed him in his own house, and his son Josiah was made king.

Chapter 33

Amon, Manasseh's son, began to reign at twenty-two and reigned two years in Jerusalem. His servants conspired against him and killed him in his own house, and Josiah his son was made king in his place.
