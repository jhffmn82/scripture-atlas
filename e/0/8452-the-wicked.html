Abstract written by a model

The wicked are miscreants of the Lie who scheme with evil will, support Wrath of Rapine and the Daêvas, and pervert Faith and Insight. They withhold sacrifice, reject the holy rule, and are associated with evil words and consciences. Abjured by the speaker, they receive worse than evil, punishment, evil food, and dwelling in the Lie’s abode. The righteous wound them with justice, and they are called forth for encounter and overthrow.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XLIX.

The wicked scheme with evil will, support Wrath of Rapine and the Daêvas, and are associated with evil words and consciences. The speaker abjures the allies of the wicked, and the wicked are destined for evil food and dwelling in the Lie’s abode.

Yasna LI.

The wicked receive what is worse than evil when they withhold sacrifice and reject the holy rule. Their conscience and spirit face punishment, and they are wounded by the sword of justice.

Yasna LXI (Sp. LX).

The wicked are to be pealed forth for encounter and overthrow if their thoughts, words, and works are not congenial to the holy ritual laws.
