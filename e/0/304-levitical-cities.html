Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, it comprises forty-eight Levite cities with surrounding pasture lands, including six cities of refuge and forty-two others. In The Book of Joshua, it includes forty-eight cities with surrounding pasture lands, five of them named as cities of refuge. In 1 Chronicles, it is given to the Levites by lot from across the tribes, with pasture lands distributed among the priestly families.
