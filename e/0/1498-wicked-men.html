Chapter 5

Wicked men are found among the LORD's people, lying in wait like fowlers, setting traps and catching men. Their houses are full of deceit like a cage full of birds, and through it they have become great, rich, fat, and shining. They do not plead the cause of the fatherless or defend the rights of the needy, and the LORD asks whether he should not punish for this.
