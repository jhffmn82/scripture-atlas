Abstract written by a model

The Tao-Tê-Ching is a small later compilation, probably pieced together from recorded sayings and conversations of Lao Tzŭ. It contains many genuine sayings alongside much material he never said, and is presented as reflecting corruption of pure TAO through alchemistic research and searches for the elixir of life; calling it sacred is strained.

_Introduction._

The Tao-Tê-Ching is described as a later compilation, probably pieced together from recorded sayings and conversations of Lao Tzŭ. It contains genuine sayings attributed to Lao Tzŭ as well as material he never said, and it is said to reflect the corruption of pure TAO by alchemistic research and searches for the elixir of life. The text argues that it can only be called a sacred book by a severe stretch of courtesy.
