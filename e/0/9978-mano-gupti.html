Abstract written by a model

Mano-gupti is Gupti that restrains the mind from sensual wandering through contemplation, study, and similar activities, while preventing sinful, harmful desires and thoughts that bring misfortune, misery, or destruction to living beings.

Twenty-Fourth Lecture.

The mano-gupti prevents the mind from wandering in the forest of sensual pleasures by employing it in contemplation, study, and similar activities. It also prevents harmful desires and thoughts concerning acts that cause misery or destruction to living beings.
