The believers at Uhud were tested when some lost courage, disputed about the order, disobeyed, desired the world, and fled while Muhammad called them from behind. Allah sent security after grief, exposed the hypocrites, forgave those who turned back, and taught the believers that the disaster was connected to what they had done.
