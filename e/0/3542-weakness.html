weakness

topic · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections
Also called: weak, weaknesses

Abstract written by a model

In 2 Corinthians, Paul boasts in and takes pleasure in weaknesses for Christ’s sake, concluding that when he is weak, then he is strong. In The Book of Ether, weakness is given to the children of men so they may be humble, and the Lord promises to make weak things strong when they humble themselves and have faith.
