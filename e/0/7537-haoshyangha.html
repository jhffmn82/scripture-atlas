Haoshyangha

person · 2 works · 8 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Haoshyangha, the Paradhâta, the Paradhâta

Abstract written by a model

In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, Haoshyangha sacrifices to Drvâspa on Hara’s enclosure and gains fearlessness to overcome Mâzana’s Daêvas. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Haoshyangha offers sacrifice to Ashi Vanguhi, receives a boon to overcome and terrify the Daêvas of Mâzana, and has his Fravashi worshipped to withstand Mâzainya Daêvas, Varenya fiends, and their evil.

Haoshyangha in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

Abstract written by a model

Haoshyangha, a holy and gallant Paradhâta, offers sacrifice to Ashi Vanguhi and receives a boon to overcome and terrify the Daêvas of Mâzana; his Fravashi is worshipped to withstand Mâzainya Daêvas, Varenya fiends, and their evil.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XXVI.

Haoshyangha is described as holy and gallant. His Fravashi is worshipped to withstand the Mâzainya Daêvas, the Varenya fiends, and the evil done by the Daêvas.

XVII. Ashi Yast.

Haoshyangha offers a sacrifice to Ashi Vanguhi on the enclosure of the Hara. He asks to overcome and terrify all the Daêvas of Mâzana, and Ashi Vanguhi grants the boon.
