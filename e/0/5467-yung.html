Abstract written by a model

In The Analects of Confucius, Book V (Kung-Ye Ch'Ang), Yung is said to be truly virtuous but not ready with his tongue, and the Master says he does not know whether Yung is truly virtuous. In The Analects of Confucius, Book VI (Yung Yey), Yung is praised by Confucius for right words and portrayed as fit to occupy a prince’s place of authority.
