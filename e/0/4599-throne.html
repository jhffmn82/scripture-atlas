Abstract written by a model

The throne is set in heaven with one sitting on it, surrounded by a rainbow like an emerald. Lightnings, sounds, and thunders proceed from it, and seven lamps of fire, the seven Spirits of God, burn before it. Before it is a sea of glass like crystal, and the four living creatures are in the middle of it and around it. Twenty-four other thrones surround it, and the elders throw their crowns before it.
