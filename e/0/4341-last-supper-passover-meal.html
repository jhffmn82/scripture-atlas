Last Supper (Passover meal)

event · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the dish, the table

Abstract written by a model

A Passover meal on the first day of unleavened bread, attended by Jesus and the twelve disciples. Jesus foretells betrayal by one who dipped his hand in the dish, telling Judas 'You said it.' He gives bread as his body and the cup as his covenant blood, anticipates drinking anew in his Father's Kingdom, then they sing a hymn and go to the Mount of Olives.

Chapter 26

While reclining with the twelve, Jesus says one of them will betray him, identifying him as the one who dipped his hand in the dish, and tells Judas 'You said it.' He gives bread as his body and the cup as his blood of the new covenant poured out for many for remission of sins, and says he will not drink the fruit of the vine until he drinks it anew in his Father's Kingdom. They sing a hymn and go to the Mount of Olives.
