Chapter 16

Strange rains, hails and relentless storms with fire pursue and consume the ungodly. The fire burns hotter in water, is at times restrained to spare the creatures sent against the ungodly, and destroys the produce of an unrighteous land, while it forgets its own power to nourish the righteous and snow and ice endure it without melting.
