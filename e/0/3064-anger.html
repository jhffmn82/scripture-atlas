Abstract written by a model

In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, anger and wrath are nourished by a man who is flesh, while enmity and anger toward one's neighbor are condemned and wrath is likened to fire matching its fuel. In The Second Book of Nephi, anger describes the Lord's unturned anger, his stretched-out hand, and his use of the Assyrian against a hypocritical nation until the Assyrian is destroyed.
