construction of the temple in The First Book of Kings

author unknown · about 549 BC · the work’s page · read it where construction of the temple first appears

Abstract written by a model

The temple's construction began in Ziv of Solomon's fourth year, 480 years after Israel left Egypt. Stone was pre-cut at the quarry so no iron tool was heard on site, and work finished in Bul of the eleventh year, after seven years.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

The building began in the fourth year of Solomon's reign, in the month Ziv, 480 years after Israel left Egypt. Stone was prepared at the quarry so no hammer, ax, or iron tool was heard at the house during construction. The work was finished throughout in the month Bul of the eleventh year, taking seven years.
