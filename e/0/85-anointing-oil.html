anointing oil in Exodus

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where anointing oil first appears

Abstract written by a model

Anointing oil is a perfume compounded by a perfumer from myrrh, cinnamon, cane, cassia and a hin of olive oil. It sanctifies the Tent of Meeting, its furnishings, altars, basin, and Aaron and his sons for priesthood. It is forbidden on ordinary people's flesh and may not be imitated; offenders are cut off from their people.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 30

The oil is compounded by a perfumer from 500 shekels of liquid myrrh, 250 of cinnamon, 250 of cane, 500 of cassia, and a hin of olive oil. It is used to anoint and sanctify the Tent of Meeting, the ark, the table, the lamp stand, both altars, the basin, and Aaron and his sons. It must not be poured on ordinary people or imitated, and offenders are cut off from their people.

anointing oil in Leviticus

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where anointing oil first appears

Abstract written by a model

Anointing oil is a sanctifying substance Moses uses in Chapter 8: he anoints the tabernacle and its contents, sprinkles the altar and vessels, pours it on Aaron's head, then sprinkles oil mixed with altar blood on Aaron, his sons, and garments.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 8

Moses uses the anointing oil to anoint and sanctify the tabernacle and all in it, the altar and its vessels, the basin and its base, and Aaron's head. He later sprinkles oil mixed with altar blood on Aaron, his sons, and their garments to sanctify them.
