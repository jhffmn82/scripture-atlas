His Augustness Tachibana-no-toyo-hi

person · 1 work · 16 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Emperor Yōmei, this Heavenly Sovereign

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Tachibana-no-toyo-hi, Emperor Yōmei, was a child of Emperor Kim-Mei who succeeded Nu-na-kura-tama-shiki. A Heavenly Sovereign, he dwelt in the Palace of Ikenobe and ruled the Empire for three years. He had wives Oho-gitashi-hime, Princess Hashi-bito-no-anaho-be, and Ihime-no-ko, and seven named children. He has the august mausoleum of Emperor Yōmei.

His Augustness Tachibana-no-toyo-hi is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Sect. CLXXVI.—Emperor Kim-Mei.

His Augustness Tachibana-no-toyo-hi was one of the august children of Emperor Kim-Mei. He ruled the Empire after Nu-na-kura-tama-shiki.

Sect. CLXXVIII.—Emperor Yōmei.

Emperor Yōmei dwelt in the Palace of Ikenobe and ruled the Empire for three years. His children included King Tame, Uhe-no-miya-no-uma-ya-dono-toyo-to-mimi, King Kume, King We-kuri, King Mamuta, King Tagima, and Sugashiroko-no-iratsume.
