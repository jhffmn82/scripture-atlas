Abstract written by a model

In Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods), it concerns the command for His Augustness Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty to descend from Heaven and rule the Luxuriant Reed-Plain-Land-of-Fresh-Rice-ears, including the questioning of the Deity Prince of Saruta and the sending of divine objects and attendants with the august child. In Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods), it brought His Augustness Heaven's-Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty onto the peak of Kuzhifuru, which is Takachiho in Tsu-kushi.
