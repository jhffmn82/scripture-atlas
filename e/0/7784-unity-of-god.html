unity of God

Also called: obliterating unity of God

Abstract written by a model

The unity of God is an all-embracing state that obliterates positive and negative, right and wrong, this and that, merging them into refuge and perfect adaptation to whatever happens.

The unity of God is the all-embracing state in which positive and negative, right and wrong, this and that, are obliterated and merged. It provides refuge and perfect adaptation to whatever may happen.
