Chapter 7

The seven brothers are taken with their mother and tortured to compel them to eat swine's flesh, but they refuse, saying they are ready to die rather than transgress their ancestors' laws. Each dies in turn, expressing hope in God's compassion, resurrection, and everlasting life, and warning the king of judgment. They also say they suffer for their own sins, and the last hopes their deaths will end God's wrath on the nation.
