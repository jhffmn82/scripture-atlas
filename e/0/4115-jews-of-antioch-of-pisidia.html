Abstract written by a model

Jews of Antioch of Pisidia are a group who, after some follow Paul and Barnabas, grow jealous when crowds gather, contradict and blaspheme Paul, then incite prominent women and chief men to persecute and expel the missionaries.

Chapter 13

Some Jews and devout proselytes follow Paul and Barnabas after the first sermon, but when the crowds gather the next Sabbath, the Jews are filled with jealousy, contradict Paul, and blaspheme. They stir up the devout and prominent women and chief men to persecute the missionaries and expel them.
