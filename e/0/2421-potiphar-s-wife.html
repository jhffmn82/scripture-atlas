Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, Potiphar's wife repeatedly urges Joseph to lie with her, then falsely accuses him of attempted assault after he flees. In Surah 12: Joseph (Yusuf), the ruler's wife asks Joseph to commit an evil act, but he refuses; her attempt is exposed, and she later admits it.

Potiphar's wife in Genesis

Abstract written by a model

Potiphar's wife, married to Potiphar, repeatedly urges Joseph to lie with her. When he flees leaving his garment, she summons her household and falsely accuses him of attempted assault, keeping the garment as evidence until her husband returns.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 39

Potiphar's wife sets her eyes on Joseph and asks him to lie with her, speaking to him day by day. When he flees and leaves his garment in her hand, she calls the men of her house and then tells her husband that the Hebrew servant came in to mock her. She keeps the garment beside her until Potiphar comes home.
