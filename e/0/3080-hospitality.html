hospitality

topic · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Hospitality, entertain, go from house to house, guest, lodging

Abstract written by a model

In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, hospitality is depicted as a burdensome obligation for a dependent guest. In The Table-Talk of the Prophet Mohammad (Part 1), hospitality concerns respecting and entertaining guests while avoiding inconvenience to the host.

hospitality in The Table-Talk of the Prophet Mohammad (Part 1)

Mishkat al-Masabih · the work’s page · read it where hospitality first appears

Abstract written by a model

Hospitality requires respecting guests, being kind for one day and one night, entertaining them for three days, and not allowing a guest to remain long enough to inconvenience the host. Remembering God when entering the house and eating prevents the Devil from claiming the house and meal.
