Chapter 6

They are seen underneath the altar, having been killed for the Word of God and the testimony of the Lamb. They cry out asking how long until the Master judges and avenges their blood, and each is given a white robe and told to rest a while until their fellow servants and brothers who will also be killed complete their course.
