Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, Adam is the first man, created by God, given dominion, disobedient through eating the fruit, expelled from Eden, father of Seth and other children, and dying at 930. In 1 Chronicles, Adam is named first in the genealogy, heading the line through Seth and Enosh down to Noah. In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, Adam is the one man through whom sin and death entered the world, whose trespass brought condemnation to all and made many sinners. In Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians, Adam is the first man made from dust and earth who became a living soul; death came through him, all die in him, and humans bear his earthly image in contrast with Christ, the last Adam. In 1 Timothy, Adam is formed before Eve and is not deceived, cited as grounds for restricting women’s teaching. In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), Adam is a viceroy on earth who learns all names, dwells in the Garden with his wife, is led astray by Satan, receives his Lord’s words, and is forgiven. In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), Adam is created and honored, dwells in the Garden with his wife, disobeys, and is expelled to earth. In Surah 18: The Cave (Al-Kahf), Adam is the one before whom the angels were commanded to prostrate, in the account of Iblis’s rebellion.

and 1 more work

In Surah 20: Taa-Haa, Adam receives a covenant but forgets and lacks constancy, eats from the forbidden tree, and is chosen, forgiven, and guided by his Lord.

Adam in Genesis

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Adam first appears

Abstract written by a model

Adam is the first man, made by God in his own likeness from dust of the ground, and the name also covers male and female together as created and blessed. God gives them dominion over land animals, fish and birds, and plants for food. Adam eats the fruit his wife gives him, hides from the LORD God, and blames the woman. As a result the ground is cursed, he must labor by the sweat of his face, and he is destined to return to dust. He gains knowledge of good and evil, is clothed in animal skins made by God, and is expelled from Eden to till the ground. He fathers Seth in his own likeness, has other sons and daughters, and dies at 930.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

God makes man in his own image, male and female, on the sixth day. God blesses them, tells them to be fruitful, multiply, fill and subdue the earth and have dominion over the fish, birds and every living thing, and gives them seed-bearing herbs and fruit trees for food.

Chapter 3

Adam eats the fruit his wife gives him, then hides from the LORD God, saying he was afraid because he was naked. He blames the woman whom God gave him. He is told the ground is cursed for his sake, that he will eat with much labor, from thorns and thistles, by the sweat of his face until he returns to dust, and he is sent out of the garden to till the ground from which he was taken.

Chapter 5

Adam was made by God in God's likeness, and God created them male and female, blessed them, and named them Adam. He lived 130 years and fathered Seth in his own likeness, after his image. He then lived 800 more years, fathered other sons and daughters, and died at 930.
