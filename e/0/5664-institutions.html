Also called: The institutions of the Ruler, his institutions, them

Abstract written by a model

The Ruler’s institutions rest on character and conduct, are attested by the people, conform to Heaven and Earth, and are accepted by spiritual beings. Compared with the three kings and enduring without misgivings, they await confirmation from a sage a hundred ages later.

Chapter 29

The Ruler's institutions are rooted in his character and conduct and are attested by the masses of the people. He compares them with those of the three kings, presents them before Heaven and Earth and spiritual beings, and waits without misgivings for a sage a hundred ages later.
