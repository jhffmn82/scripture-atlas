His Grandeur Wo-hatsuse-no-waka-sazaki

Also called: Emperor Mu-Retsu, This Heavenly Monarch, Wo-hatsuse-no-waka-sazaki

Abstract written by a model

His Grandeur Wo-hatsuse-no-waka-sazaki, also called Emperor Mu-Retsu, was a son of Emperor Nin-Ken and Kasuga-no-oho-iratsume, one of their seven august children. He afterwards became a Heavenly Monarch, dwelt at the palace of Namiki at Hatsuse, ruled the Empire for eight years, had no august children, and had a mausoleum on Ihatsuki mound at Karawoka.
