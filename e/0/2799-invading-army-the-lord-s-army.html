Abstract written by a model

The LORD's army is an unparalleled, great and strong northern people, horse-like and locust-like, marching in unbroken ranks. It advances with fire before and behind, scaling walls and entering houses like thieves, turning Eden-like land into wilderness. Finally, the LORD promises to drive it into a barren land and the eastern and western seas, where its stench rises.

Chapter 2

The army is described as a great and strong people unlike any before, looking like horses, running like warriors, marching in unbroken ranks, with fire before and behind them, climbing walls and entering houses like thieves. It is the LORD's army before which he thunders, and the land is like Eden before it and a desolate wilderness behind. The LORD promises to remove the northern army into a barren land, driving it into the eastern and western seas, and its stench will rise.
