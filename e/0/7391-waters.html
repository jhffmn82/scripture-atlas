Abstract written by a model

Waters are Mazda-made, holy, good, beneficial natural entities. They are worshipped as the good and best waters, receive offerings including Haoma, Haoma-water, milk, Hadhânaêpata, Zaothras, the stone, and the iron mortar, and are included among sacred things. They figure in instruction for Mazdayasnians and are among sacred beings over which the holy man has sovereignty.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna VII.

The waters which are Mazda-made receive offerings of Haoma, Haoma-water, fresh milk, and the plant Hadhânaêpata. They are also included among the waters and sacred things offered to.

Yasna VIII.

The waters are among the sacred beings over which the holy man is to have sovereignty at his will. They are also named with the plants and Zaothras as part of the instruction of Mazdayasnians.

Yasna XVII.

The good and best waters are worshipped as Mazda-made and holy. All waters that Mazda made and that are holy are included in the worship.

Yasna XXIV.

The good waters receive Zaothras, Haoma, milk, Hadhânaêpata, Haoma-water, the stone, and the iron mortar. They are described as having the Haoma with them and as beneficial.
