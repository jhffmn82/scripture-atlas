the Mean

topic · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Mean, the path of the Mean

Abstract written by a model

The Mean is a path or course maintained by the superior man and opposed by the mean man. Most people neither walk in nor understand it: the knowing and talented go beyond it, while the stupid and worthless do not reach it. Its path is untrodden, and though men may choose it, they cannot keep to it for a full month or attain its course even amid other difficult achievements. Shun determined it from two extremes and employed it in government; Hui chose it. The superior man pursues it through learning and practice.
