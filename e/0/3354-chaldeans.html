Chaldeans in Daniel (Greek)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Chaldeans first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Chaldeans are Babylonian wise men, counted among magicians, enchanters and sorcerers, who serve the king. In Chapter 2 they address him in Syrian, ask to hear the dream, and declare only the gods can reveal it, provoking his decree to destroy all wise men. In Chapter 5 they are summoned with rewards offered but cannot read the writing.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The Chaldeans, addressing the king in the Syrian language, ask him to tell the dream so they can interpret it, and are accused of stalling and preparing lying words. They state that no one on earth can do what the king asks except the gods, whose dwelling is not with flesh. This answer provokes the king's decree to destroy all the wise men.

Chapter 5

The enchanters, Chaldeans, and soothsayers are summoned by the king and offered purple, a gold chain, and the third rulership for reading the writing. They come in but cannot read it or give its interpretation.
