Abstract written by a model

The sinner is portrayed as someone who should receive no almsgiving or help, since aiding him invites double harm. The Most High hates and avenges sinners. He stays with associates only while things go well and abandons them when they falter, so joining him earns no pity.

Chapter 12

The sinner is to be given no help, and the ungodly man is to be refused bread lest he subdue the giver with it and return twice as much evil. The Most High hates sinners and will repay them with vengeance. Anyone who goes to a sinner and shares in his sins will find no pity, because the sinner stays only while things go well and leaves when the reader falters.
