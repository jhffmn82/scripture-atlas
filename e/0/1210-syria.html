Syria

Also called: Syrians, the Syrians

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Syria oppresses Israel under Hazael, defeats Jehoahaz's forces, and is defeated by Israel three times as foretold by Elisha. In The Book of Isaiah, Syria allies with Ephraim under Rezin of Damascus and plots against Judah, but the LORD declares the plot will fail. In The Second Book of Nephi, Syria is led by Rezin from Damascus, confederate with Ephraim, and wages war against Jerusalem.

Syria in The Second Book of Kings

Abstract written by a model

Syria oppressed Israel under Hazael, and its king destroyed Jehoahaz's forces, making them like dust in threshing. Elisha foretold that Israel would strike the Syrians at Aphek, and because the king struck the ground only three times, Syria would be defeated just three times. Jehoash then struck Benhadad three times.
