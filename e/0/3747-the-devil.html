the devil

person · 5 works · 4 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: a devil, devil, evil one, him who had the power of death, power of the devil, the evil one, the power of the devil

Abstract written by a model

In Paul’s Letter to the Ephesians, the devil uses wiles against which believers must stand. In The Letter to the Hebrews, the devil has the power of death. In 1 John, the devil has been sinning from the beginning. In The Book of Jacob, the Brother of Nephi, the devil deceives Sherem through flattering speech. In The Book of Helaman, the evil one leads people to heed his words and set their hearts on worldly things.
