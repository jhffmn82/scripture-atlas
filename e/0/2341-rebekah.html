Rebekah

Abstract written by a model

Rebekah is the very beautiful daughter of Bethuel and sister of Laban. She draws water for a servant and his camels, receives gifts, is blessed by her family, agrees to go, and veils herself on seeing Isaac, becoming his wife. Initially barren, she conceives after Isaac entreats the LORD; told two nations are in her womb and the elder will serve the younger, she bears twins, Esau and Jacob, and loves Jacob. Isaac calls her his sister out of fear until Abimelech sees them. She and Isaac grieve over Esau's Hittite wives. She engineers Jacob's blessing by cooking goats and disguising him, then sends him to Laban to escape Esau, telling Isaac she is weary of life because of the daughters of Heth.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 24

Rebekah, daughter of Bethuel and a very beautiful virgin, draws water for the servant and all his camels, answering the sign he prayed for. She receives gold gifts, tells her family what happened, agrees to go with the man when asked, is blessed by her family, and on seeing Isaac covers herself with her veil before becoming his wife.

Chapter 25

Rebekah, daughter of Bethuel the Syrian and sister of Laban, married Isaac and conceived after he entreated the LORD. When the children struggled within her she inquired of the LORD and was told two nations were in her womb and the elder would serve the younger. She bore twins and loved Jacob.

Chapter 26

Rebekah is Isaac's wife, whom he calls his sister because she is beautiful and he fears being killed for her. Abimelech sees Isaac caressing her, which reveals the truth, and she and Isaac are later grieved by Esau's Hittite wives.

Chapter 27

Rebekah overhears Isaac's instructions to Esau and directs Jacob to obtain the blessing, taking on any curse herself. She cooks the goats, dresses Jacob in Esau's clothes and goat skins, and sends him to Isaac. When she learns of Esau's plan to kill Jacob, she sends Jacob to her brother Laban, and she tells Isaac she is weary of life because of the daughters of Heth.
