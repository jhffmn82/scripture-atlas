Chapter 12

These people went far astray in error, taking as gods animals that even their enemies held in dishonor, and were deceived like foolish babes. The Lord first sent mocking judgment as to unreasoning children, then tormented them through their own abominations. Those who would not heed mild correction faced deserved judgment, and through being punished by the creatures they thought gods, they recognized the true God whom they had refused to know, after which extreme condemnation came upon them.
