the sovereignty

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the Sovereignty, the True (Sovereignty)

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), the sovereignty belongs to Allah over the heavens and the earth, with no guardian or helper beside Allah. In Surah 25: The Criterion (Al-Furqaan), the Sovereignty belongs to Allah, who has no son or partner in it, and on the day of judgment the True Sovereignty belongs to the Beneficent One.
