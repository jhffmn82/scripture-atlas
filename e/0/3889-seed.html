seed

Also called: Others, a seed, every seed, seed (Alma's listeners' seed), seed (the sower's seed), seeds, seeds (sown by the farmer), some seeds, the seed

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, the seeds represent the word of the Kingdom sown in the hearts of those who hear. In The Good News According to Mark, the seed is the word sown in people, producing different results depending on where it falls. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the seed is the word planted in the heart that can grow into a tree leading to everlasting life.

Abstract written by a model

The seed is eaten by birds on the road, scorched on rocky ground for lack of root, and choked among thorns. On good ground it yields thirty, sixty, or one hundred times as much. Jesus identifies the seed with the word sown in people, and in other parables the Kingdom is likened to seed that grows by itself and to a tiny mustard seed that becomes greater than all herbs.

seed (Alma's listeners' seed) in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where seed (Alma's listeners' seed) first appears

Abstract written by a model

Alma's listeners' seed is the word planted in the heart. Accepted without unbelief, it swells, sprouts, grows, enlarges the soul, enlightens understanding, and strengthens faith. Nourished by faith, it becomes a tree leading to everlasting life; neglected or resisted, it does not grow.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 32

The word is compared to a seed planted in the heart. A good seed swells, sprouts, grows, enlarges the soul, enlightens understanding, and strengthens faith; if it is neglected or resisted, it does not grow.

Chapter 33

The seed is identified with the word that Alma's listeners are to plant in their hearts. If nourished by faith, it will become a tree leading to everlasting life.
