Mary Magdalene

person · 3 works · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Mary, Woman

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Mary Magdalene comes to the tomb, hears the angel's message, meets Jesus, takes hold of his feet, worships him, and tells the disciples. In The Good News According to Mark, Mary Magdalene, freed of seven demons by Jesus, visits the tomb, sees him first, and tells the disbelieving mourners. In The Gospel According to John, Mary Magdalene is the first witness of the risen Jesus and announces to the disciples that she has seen the Lord.

Mary Magdalene in The Gospel According to John

author unknown · AD 90 to 100 · the work’s page · read it where Mary Magdalene first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mary Magdalene is the first witness of the risen Jesus. She finds the tomb's stone removed, tells Peter and the other disciple, weeps, sees two angels, mistakes Jesus for the gardener until he names her, then announces to the disciples she has seen the Lord.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 20

Mary Magdalene goes to the tomb while it is dark, finds the stone taken away, and runs to tell Simon Peter and the other disciple. She stays weeping at the tomb, sees two angels, and meets Jesus, thinking him the gardener until he says her name and she answers 'Rabboni!' She then tells the disciples that she has seen the Lord and what he said to her.
