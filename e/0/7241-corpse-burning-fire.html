VIII.

A corpse-burning fire must be dealt with by killing the man who burns the corpse, removing the cauldron and tripod, and dispersing the fire in successive bundles. Its fire may also be brought to the Dâityô-gâtu, where it receives the reward of ten thousand fire-brands.
