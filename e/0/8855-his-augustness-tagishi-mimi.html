Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Tagishi-mimi, elder half-brother of the younger princes, weds I-suke-yori-hime after the Heavenly Sovereign’s death. He plots to kill his three younger brethren, but His Augustness Kamu-nuna-kaha-mimi kills him in the slaying of Tagishi-mimi.
