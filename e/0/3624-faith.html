Abstract written by a model

In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, faith accounts righteousness, secures the promise through grace, justifies believers, grants access to grace, and brings peace with God. In The Letter to the Hebrews, faith enables drawing near to God, sustains the righteous, and leads to the saving of the soul rather than destruction. In The Letter to the Hebrews, faith is assurance of hoped-for things and proof of unseen things, required to please God and exemplified by many faithful figures. In 1 John, faith is the victory overcoming the world through belief that Jesus is the Son of God. In Jude, the faith is the once-delivered faith for which the saints must contend and upon which they must build themselves up. In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), faith consists of believing in Allah and the revelations given to Muhammad and earlier prophets, bringing right guidance. In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan): Belief is faith in Allah and His revelations, shown through following the messenger, obedience, steadfastness, prayer, spending, and forgiveness. In Surah 10: Jonas (Yunus), faith is linked with belief in Allah, good works, trust, surrender, and Allah’s guidance and permission.

and 15 more works

In Surah 13: The Thunder (Ar-Ra'd), faith accompanies recognition of revelation, remembrance of Allah, peaceful hearts, and righteous action. In Surah 20: Taa-Haa, faith is shown by the magicians and other believers, whose good works earn high stations without injustice. In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa): A believer's good works are not rejected. Believers are saved from anguish and share in Allah's mercy. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), faith is affirmed by the wizards, believers, and those who trust Allah, while many reject it despite witnessing signs. In Surah 40: The Forgiver (Al-Ghaafir), faith in Allah is accepted by believers but rejected by disbelievers, whose faith cannot benefit them at the sight of doom. In Surah 49: The Inner Apartments (Al-Hujuraat), faith is beloved and beautified in believers’ hearts and consists of belief in Allah and His messenger with sincere striving. In Surah 58: The Pleading Woman (Al-Mujaadila), faith is written upon the hearts of those who oppose Allah’s enemies, and Allah strengthens them with a Spirit from Him. In Surah 59: The Exile (Al-Hashr), faith unites earlier and later believers, who ask Allah to forgive their brethren and remove rancour from their hearts. In Surah 60: She that is to be examined (Al-Mumtahana): Faith is examined in believing women who come as fugitives, and Allah is described as Best Aware of their faith. In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, faith brings tender mercies, blessings, revelation, deliverance, and power through diligent seeking and belief in the Son of God. In The Second Book of Nephi, faith strengthens the word of the Lord, enables seer-work, supports salvation, and centers hope in Jesus Christ. In The Book of Helaman, faith in Christ brings a change of heart, repentance, righteousness, steadfastness, truth, and refusal of violence. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, faith confirms promised signs, supports healing and blessing, purifies believers, and is tested by belief or unbelief. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record, faith without doubt enables prayer in Christ’s name and is connected with prayers for the brethren and Israel’s blessing. In The Book of Ether, faith enables knowledge of Jesus Christ, revelation, sanctification, salvation, spiritual manifestations, hope, good works, miracles, promises, strength, and divine witnesses.

faith in Paul’s Letter to the Romans

Abstract written by a model

Faith is the means by which righteousness is accounted to those who believe rather than work. In Chapter 4, Abraham's unweakened faith was counted as righteousness, and because the promise rests on faith, it accords with grace and is sure to all his offspring. In Chapter 5, faith justifies believers, gives access into grace, and brings peace with God through Jesus Christ.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

Faith is what was accounted to Abraham for righteousness, and it is likewise accounted as righteousness to the one who does not work but believes in him who justifies the ungodly. Because the promise rests on faith, it is according to grace and sure to all Abraham's offspring. Abraham's faith did not weaken, and he grew strong through faith in the face of his own body's deadness and Sarah's womb.

Chapter 5

Faith is the means by which believers are justified and through which they have access into the grace in which they stand. It leads to peace with God through Jesus Christ.
