people of the land

group · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: all the inhabitants of the land, inhabitants of the land (Judah), the people of the land

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, the people of the land avenge King Amon by killing his conspirators and install his son Josiah as king. In The Book of Jeremiah, the people of the land are among those who will face evil from the north and fight Jeremiah but not prevail.
