Abstract written by a model

Valour is nourished by Bei Gong You through not flinching from blows, thrusts, or insults, and by Meng Shi She through rising above fear. Meng Shi She's form is inferior because Zeng attends to a more important kind.

Chapter 2

Bei Gong You nourishes valour by refusing to flinch from physical blows or insults and by fearing none of the princes. Meng Shi She nourishes valour by rising superior to all fear, and Mencius says this is inferior to the more important form maintained by the philosopher Zeng.
