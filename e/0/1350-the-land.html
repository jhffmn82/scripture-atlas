Chapter 12

The land mourns and its herbs wither because of the wickedness of its inhabitants, and the animals and birds are consumed. It is made wholly desolate because no one cares, and destroyers and the LORD's sword devour it from end to end so that no flesh has peace. The people have sown wheat and reaped thorns.
