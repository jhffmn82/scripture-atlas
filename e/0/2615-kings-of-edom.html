kings of Edom in Genesis

author unknown · about 449 BC · the work’s page · read it where kings of Edom first appears

Abstract written by a model

A list of eight kings who reigned in Edom before any king ruled Israel. Each succeeded the one who died, beginning with Bela son of Beor at Dinhabah and ending with Hadar. Notable details: Hadad struck Midian in the field of Moab, and Hadar's wife was Mehetabel.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 36

Eight kings reigned in Edom before any king reigned over Israel: Bela son of Beor, Jobab son of Zerah of Bozrah, Husham of the Temanites, Hadad son of Bedad, Samlah of Masrekah, Shaul of Rehoboth by the river, Baal Hanan son of Achbor, and Hadar. Each reigned in place of the one who died; Hadad is noted as striking Midian in the field of Moab, and Hadar's wife was Mehetabel.

kings of Edom in 1 Chronicles

author unknown · 449 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where kings of Edom first appears

Abstract written by a model

A succession list of Edom's kings, each reigning in place of the one who died: Bela, Jobab, Husham, Hadad son of Bedad, Samlah, Shaul, Baal Hanan, and finally a later Hadad, whose wife was Mehetabel.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

1 Chronicles / Chapter 1

The kings who reigned in Edom were Bela son of Beor, Jobab son of Zerah, Husham, Hadad son of Bedad, Samlah, Shaul, Baal Hanan son of Achbor, and a later Hadad. Each reigned in place of the one who died, and the last Hadad's wife was Mehetabel.
