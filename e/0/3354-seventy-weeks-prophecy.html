seventy weeks prophecy

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Seventy weeks, one week, seven weeks and sixty-two weeks, the sixty-two weeks, the vision

Abstract written by a model

The seventy weeks prophecy, delivered by Gabriel in Chapter 9, is a decree upon Daniel's people and holy city. Its purposes are to end sin, reconcile iniquity, bring everlasting righteousness, seal up vision and prophecy, and anoint the most holy. It unfolds from the commandment to restore Jerusalem through seven and sixty-two weeks to the Anointed One, then a final week of covenant, ceased sacrifice, and wrath on the desolate.

Chapter 9

Gabriel says seventy weeks are decreed on Daniel's people and holy city to finish disobedience, end sin, make reconciliation for iniquity, bring in everlasting righteousness, seal up vision and prophecy, and anoint the most holy. From the going out of the commandment to restore Jerusalem to the Anointed One, the prince, are seven weeks and sixty-two weeks, and a final week involves a firm covenant, the cessation of sacrifice in its middle, and wrath poured out on the desolate.
