Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Ezekiel, repentance and return is God's call, since he has no pleasure in death; a wicked person who turns from all transgressions and does what is lawful and right shall live, unremembered sins; Israel must cast away transgressions and make a new heart and spirit. In The Book of Jacob, the Brother of Nephi, repentance is required of the filthy to prevent destruction, a cursed land, and loss to the Lamanites; Jacob urges my brethren to come with full purpose of heart and cleave unto God, leading through the narrow way to eternal life.

Chapter 18

The Lord GOD teaches that a wicked person who turns from all transgressions and does what is lawful and right shall live, and that none of those transgressions will be remembered. He calls Israel to return, cast away their transgressions, and make a new heart and a new spirit, so that iniquity will not be their ruin.
