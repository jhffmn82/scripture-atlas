Abstract written by a model

Surah 62: Friday (Al-Jumu'a), revealed at Medina, concerns Allah, His messenger, the unlettered ones, the Jews, and believers. Allah sends a messenger among the unlettered ones to recite Allah's revelations, purify them, and teach them Scripture and wisdom. The Jews are reproached for failing to apply the Law of Moses and for claiming special favor while shrinking from death. Believers are instructed to leave trading for the Friday prayer, then disperse to seek Allah's bounty and remember Allah much.
