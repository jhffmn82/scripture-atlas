Mahâvîra's descent from heaven

event · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: descended, descended from the great Vimâna, descent from heaven, he descended, he descended from heaven

Abstract written by a model

In Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra), Mahâvîra's descent from Pushpottara Vimâna occurs after twenty Sâgaropamas, ending in Kundapura in Gambûdvîpa while the moon is in Uttaraphalgunî. In Kalpa Sutra, Mahâvîra's descent from heaven occurred when the moon was in conjunction with Uttaraphalgunî, after his divine existence in Vimâna Pushpottara reached its termination.

Mahâvîra's descent from heaven in Kalpa Sutra

Abstract written by a model

Mahâvîra's descent from heaven occurred when the moon was in conjunction with Uttaraphalgunî. He descended from the Vimâna Pushpottara after his divine existence had reached its termination. Rishabha descended from the great Vimâna called Sarvârthasiddha after living there for thirty-three Sâgaropamas. His descent occurred in the dark fortnight of Ashâdha on its fourth day, when the moon was in conjunction with Uttarâshâdhâ.
