incense of sweet spices

Also called: incense, perpetual incense, strange incense, the incense of sweet spices

Abstract written by a model

Holy incense is a perpetual offering before the LORD, burned on the altar each morning and evening. It combines equal weights of sweet spices, gum resin, onycha, galbanum and pure frankincense, salted. Some is beaten small and set before the covenant in the Tent of Meeting. Private imitation is forbidden; imitators are cut off.

Chapter 30

Sweet incense is burned on the altar every morning and evening as a perpetual incense before the LORD. The holy incense is made from equal weights of sweet spices, gum resin, onycha, galbanum, and pure frankincense, seasoned with salt. Some is beaten small and placed before the covenant, and anyone who makes any like it to smell it is to be cut off.
