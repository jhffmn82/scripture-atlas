Bezalel

person · 1 work · 28 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Bezalel the son of Uri, the son of Hur

Abstract written by a model

Bezalel is a craftsman of the tribe of Judah, son of Uri and grandson of Hur. The LORD calls him by name and fills him with the Spirit of God, giving him wisdom, understanding, knowledge and skill in workmanship: work in gold, silver and bronze, stone cutting and wood carving. He also has it in his heart to teach. Early on, his lineage, calling and gifts are announced. He is then named alongside Oholiab as one who works with the wise-hearted men to whom the LORD gave wisdom for the sanctuary work, and Moses calls him to that work. He goes on to build the sanctuary furnishings: the ark, the mercy seat of pure gold, two beaten-gold cherubim, the table and its vessels, the beaten-work lamp stand with seven lamps, snuffers and dishes, and the acacia-wood altar of incense. He also makes the holy anointing oil and pure incense of sweet spices. The records close by stating that he made all that the LORD commanded Moses.

Bezalel is in one work on this site, Exodus. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 31

Bezalel is the son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, and the LORD calls him by name. He is filled with the Spirit of God, with wisdom, understanding, knowledge and skill in workmanship, including work in gold, silver and bronze, stone cutting and wood carving.

Chapter 35

Bezalel, son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, is announced as called by name by the LORD. He is filled with the Spirit of God, wisdom, understanding, knowledge, and all kinds of workmanship, including work in metals, stone cutting, and wood carving, and has it in his heart to teach.

Chapter 36

Bezalel is named with Oholiab as one who shall work with every wise-hearted man, to whom the LORD has given wisdom and understanding for the sanctuary work. Moses calls him to the work.

Chapter 37

Bezalel is the craftsman who makes the ark, mercy seat, cherubim, table with its vessels, lamp stand, and altar of incense. He also makes the holy anointing oil and the pure incense of sweet spices, after the art of the perfumer.

Chapter 38

Bezalel is identified as son of Uri, son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah. He made all that the LORD commanded Moses.
