Hindus

group · 2 works · 15 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Hindu, O Hindus, The Hindus

Abstract written by a model

In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), Hindus praise the incomparable Praised One, affirm four Veds, bathe in holy streams, worship idols, and use copious sandal incense. In Hymns of Guru Nanak, Hindus forget God and go the wrong way, following Narad’s instruction; they are described as blind, dumb, ignorant, foolish, and immersed in superstition, worshipping stones and considering ablution of the Chandal and foul.
