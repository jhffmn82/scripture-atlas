Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the LORD's coming judgment is described as his judging the earth, peoples, and world with equity, righteousness, and truth. In Psalms, the LORD's coming judgment is the reason creation is to sing, as he judges the world with righteousness and the peoples with equity. In Ecclesiastes or, The Preacher, God will bring the young man into judgment for all the things he does in following his heart and eyes. In 1 Peter, judgment comes from God, begins with the household of God, and includes judging the living and the dead. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), judgment belongs to Allah, who judges between Noah and his folk, brings the Garden near to those who ward off evil, and punishes the erring. In The Table-Talk of the Prophet Mohammad (Part 1), judgment includes accountability for murder, suicide, debt, and the conduct of judges. In The Second Book of Nephi, judgment is the holy divine reckoning before the Holy One of Israel, where all men appear after becoming immortal and are judged by their works.

judgment in The Second Book of Nephi

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where judgment first appears

Abstract written by a model

Judgment is the holy divine reckoning before the Holy One of Israel. After becoming immortal, all men appear there and are judged by their works, with the righteous and filthy remaining as they are. The Lord also judges ancients and princes for harming the poor. At the final judgment, Christ confirms his words and condemns those who reject God’s goodness and the words of the Jews, Nephi, and the Lamb of God.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 9

After becoming immortal, all men must appear before the judgment-seat of the Holy One of Israel. They are judged according to the holy judgment of God and their works, with the righteous remaining righteous and the filthy remaining filthy.

Chapter 13

The Lord stands up to plead and judge the people. He enters into judgment with the ancients and princes for eating up the vineyard and harming the poor.

Chapter 33

At the final judgment, Christ will show that the words are his, and Nephi and his readers will stand face to face before Christ's bar. Those who reject the goodness of God, the words of the Jews, Nephi's words, and the words proceeding from the Lamb of God will be condemned.
