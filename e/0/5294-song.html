Song in Mencius, Book III (Teng Wen Gong)

author unknown · 399 to 300 BC · the work’s page · read it where Song first appears

Abstract written by a model

Song is a small State, visited by the future duke Wen of Teng while traveling to Chu to see Mencius. Its ruler is reportedly beginning true royal government, though Mencius disputes this; he says genuine practice would draw people from throughout the four seas and make Qi and Chu little threat.

Mencius, Book III (Teng Wen Gong) / Part I / Chapter 1

Song is the place through which the prince, afterwards duke Wen of Teng, travels on his way to Chu. He visits Mencius there.

Chapter 5

Song is described as a small State whose ruler is said to be setting about practising true royal government. Mencius says that if Song were truly practising it, people throughout the four seas would seek its ruler, and Qi and Chu would pose little threat.
