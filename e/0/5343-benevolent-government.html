Abstract written by a model

Benevolent government, practised by duke Wen of Teng, rejects wealth-seeking and regulated taking from the people. It begins with livelihoods and land boundaries, then provides fair division, allowances, education, mutual aid, and rulers’ responsibility to govern, teach, and benefit people.

Chapter 3

Benevolent government begins by securing the people's livelihood and correctly defining land boundaries. Mencius presents it as involving fair land division, regulated allowances, education, and mutual aid.

Chapter 4

Benevolent government is associated with the prince of Teng, whose rule attracts Xu Xing and others. Mencius presents governing, teaching, and benefiting the people as responsibilities of those who rule.
