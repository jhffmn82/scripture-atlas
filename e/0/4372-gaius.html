Abstract written by a model

Gaius is the beloved recipient of the letter, praised because brothers testified to his walking in truth and to his love before the assembly. He is told he does a faithful work for brothers and strangers, is urged to send them forward worthily and to imitate good rather than evil, and is told John hopes to see him soon.
