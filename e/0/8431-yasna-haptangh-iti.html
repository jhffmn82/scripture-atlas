Yasna Haptanghâiti

ritual · 2 works · 18 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Haptanghâiti, heroic Haptanghâiti, heroic Yasna Haptanghâiti, later Yasna, that brave Yasna, the Haptanghâiti, the heroic Haptanghâiti, the holy, the later Yasna, the ritual chief

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Yasna Haptanghâiti is a supplement and portion of the Yasna, a brave holy ritual chief used in worship of the Bountiful Immortals. In Visparad, Yasna Haptanghâiti is a holy heroic Yasna that rules the ritual order, is associated with Ardvi Anâhita, and is to be pondered, memorised, and recited exactly and holily. In Visparad, the later Yasna is defined by its offering as a text that offers sacrifice to the later Yasna.

Yasna Haptanghâiti in Visparad

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Yasna Haptanghâiti first appears

Abstract written by a model

Yasna Haptanghâiti is a holy, heroic Yasna, ruling in the ritual order and associated with the water Ardvi Anâhita. It has chapters, metred lines, words, and word-structure. It is to be pondered, attended to with devotion, memorised, and recited exactly, victoriously, and holily, free of harmful malice. It inculcates good thoughts, words, and deeds. Identified with the later Yasna, it becomes holy ritual chief as it recurs.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Visparad / Visparad I.

The Yasna Haptanghâiti is celebrated and completed as holy and ruling in the ritual order. The text also associates it with the water Ardvi Anâhita.

Visparad II.

The heroic Yasna Haptanghâiti is approached in praise as holy and ruling in the ritual order.

Visparad XV (Sp. XVIII).

The Yasna Haptanghâiti is to be pondered, devotedly attended to, memorised, and recited victoriously and holily. Its recital is to be without addition or omission and is described as powerful, victory-smiting, and separate from harmful malice.

Visparad XVII (Sp. XX).

It inculcates good thoughts, words, and deeds, after which they strive.

Visparad XX (Sp. XXIII).

The heroic Haptanghâiti is identified with the later Yasna and is described as becoming the holy ritual chief as it recurs.
