wicked

Abstract written by a model

The wicked are opposed to the righteous: assigned the worst life and a long wounding. Their award reflects their actions and final state. As coadjutors of evil, they prepare its throne through lawless harm, remain in Evil Mind, and are harmed by Bountiful Spirit, receiving their portion far from Ahura Mazda’s love.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XXX.

The wicked are assigned the worst life, and a long wounding is said to be in store for them. Their fate contrasts with the blessings and salvation promised to the righteous.

Yasna XXXI.

The wicked receive an award associated with their actions and final state. Those who prepare the throne for the evil and cannot reclaim their lives except through lawless harm are questioned as coadjutors of the wicked.

Yasna XLVII.

The wicked foemen are harmed by Ahura Mazda’s Bountiful Spirit and receive their portion far from His love. They remain in the actions of the Evil Mind, and the evil person with wealth and ruling power serves the wicked.
