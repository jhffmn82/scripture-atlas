God's court

place · 3 works · 13 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: God's gate, God's mansion, God's palace, His court, His true court, Master's court, Thy court, Thy true palace, court, divine court, one court, the Master's true court, the court of God, the court of the True One
and 4 more, the great God's treasury, the true arena, the world as God's chamber, world is the True One's chamber

Abstract written by a model

In Japji, the court of God is where the elect are accepted and honoured and people are judged according to their acts by Dharmraj from the record kept by recording angels. In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), God's court is true and adjudges the real truth of acts, separating and removing those attached to mammon. In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), God's court is the True One's chamber and dwelling, where those who obey the Master's order receive the heart's desired fruit and enter through the true spiritual thread. In Hymns of Guru Nanak, God's court is an unrivalled true palace of honour, happiness, judgment, truth, and acceptance, where the worthy receive honour and emancipation's robe.

the court of God in Japji

Max Arthur Macauliffe · the work’s page · read it where the court of God first appears

Abstract written by a model

The court of God is a court where the elect are accepted and honoured, while people are judged according to their acts. Its incomparable nature cannot be fully described. Recording angels write the record, and Dharmraj judges by it; there are also fashioned knowledge, wisdom, intellect, understanding, and supernatural skill.

God's court in Asa ki War (Asa di Var)

Guru Nanak · the work’s page · read it where God's court first appears

Abstract written by a model

God's court is the True One's chamber and dwelling, seen when pride is removed. It judges both literate people and illiterate saints hereafter. Those who obey the Master's order reach it, receive the heart's desired fruit, wear honour, and enter through the true spiritual thread.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Slok II .. Pauri II

Guru Angad describes this world as the True One's chamber, with the True One's dwelling therein.

Slok XV .. Pauri XV

God's court is the place where the person who obeys the Master's order reaches, receives the fruit desired by the heart, and is clothed with a robe of honour. The true spiritual thread is described as fit for entrance into God's court.

God's court in Hymns of Guru Nanak

Guru Nanak · the work’s page · read it where God's court first appears

Abstract written by a model

God's court is an unrivalled true palace of honour, happiness, judgment, truth, and acceptance, containing three engraved destinies. False, impure, and God-separated people are denied entrance; those imbued with God's Name and the Word, carrying true merchandise and following the Guru's instruction, receive honour and emancipation's robe.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Sri Rag .. Ashtapadi

God's court is the place of honour, happiness, truth, and acceptance. Those who are false, counterfeit, impure, or separated from God are despised or denied entrance, while those imbued with God's name and the Word receive honour there.

Rag Maru Ashtapadi .. Maru ki War I

God's court is reached by carrying the true merchandise of God's Name and living according to the Guru's instruction. Those who are emancipated receive a robe of honour there.
