Chapter 7

The speaker tells his son to say to wisdom, "You are my sister," and to call understanding his relative. This kinship is meant to keep him from the strange woman and her flattering words.

Chapter 9

The fear of the LORD is called the beginning of wisdom, and the knowledge of the Holy One is understanding. Wisdom says that by her the days of her hearers will be multiplied and the years of their life increased.
