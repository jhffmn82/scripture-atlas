divine judgment

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: God's judgment, deserved judgment of God, extreme condemnation, judge, judge blamelessly, judgment, your judgment

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, God's judgment is carried out blamelessly at God's chosen time, bringing down one and lifting up another and punishing the wicked. In The Wisdom of Solomon, divine judgment is righteous and unchallengeable, proceeds gradually to allow repentance, and models how God's people should judge.

divine judgment in The Wisdom of Solomon

author unknown · about 99 BC · the work’s page · read it where divine judgment first appears

Abstract written by a model

Divine judgment is righteous and unchallengeable by any king, prince or avenger. Though God could act at once, he proceeds gently and gradually, giving chance to repent: mild correction and mockery first, then deserved, extreme condemnation for the unheeding. It models how God's people should judge.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 12

Divine judgment is portrayed as righteous, unchallengeable by any king, prince or avenger, and carried out gradually and with gentleness although the Lord has the power to act at once. It begins with mild correction and mockery and, for those who do not heed it, escalates to deserved punishment and extreme condemnation. It also serves as a model for God's people, who are to ponder his goodness when they judge.
