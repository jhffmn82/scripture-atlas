Abstract written by a model

Houses for the dead are three small structures raised in every house and borough after summer, each sized for a standing person and holding a lifeless body for two nights, three nights, or a month.

Fargard V.

The worshippers of Mazda must raise three small houses for the dead in every house and borough when summer has passed and winter has come. Each must be large enough for a standing person to extend the feet and hands without striking the skull, feet, or hands.
