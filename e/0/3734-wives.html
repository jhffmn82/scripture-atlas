Chapter 3

Wives are told to be in subjection to their own husbands so that husbands who do not obey the Word may be won without a word by their pure behavior. Their beauty is to come from the hidden person of the heart, a gentle and quiet spirit precious to God, as the holy women of the past did, and Sarah, who obeyed Abraham and called him lord, is the example.
