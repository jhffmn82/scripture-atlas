Chapter 2

At the servants' suggestion, chaste, beautiful young virgins were to be selected by local governors in all the provinces and brought to Susa, into the women's apartment under the king's chamberlain, with purification and other needs supplied. Each served twelve months of purification, six with oil of myrrh and six with spices, before going in to the king in the evening and leaving in the morning for a second women's apartment, returning only if called by name. Esther found favor beyond all of them and became queen in place of Vashti.
