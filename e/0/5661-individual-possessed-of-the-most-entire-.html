individual possessed of the most entire sincerity

person · 1 work · 13 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: he, him, individual possessing entire sincerity, man in his ideal, the individual possessing it

Abstract written by a model

An individual possessing the most entire sincerity is portrayed as large and substantial like Earth, high and brilliant like Heaven, and infinite in reach and duration. With all Heavenly virtue, quick apprehension, clear discernment, far-reaching intelligence, and all-embracing knowledge, he can regulate mankind’s enduring relations, establish humanity’s fundamental virtues, and understand Heaven and Earth’s transforming, nurturing operations. He unfolds as earnest in ideal, abyss-deep, Heaven-vast, and dependent on nothing beyond himself.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 26

An individual possessing entire sincerity becomes the co-equal of Earth through being large and substantial, the co-equal of Heaven through being high and brilliant, and infinite through reaching far and continuing long.

Chapter 32

The individual possessed of the most entire sincerity can adjust the great invariable relations of mankind, establish the great fundamental virtues of humanity, and know the transforming and nurturing operations of Heaven and Earth. He is described as earnest, deep, and vast, and as depending on nothing beyond himself.
