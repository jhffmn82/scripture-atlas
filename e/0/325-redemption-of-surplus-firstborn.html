Chapter 3

Because Israel's firstborn number two hundred seventy-three more than the Levites, the LORD commands five shekels each, by the sanctuary shekel of twenty gerahs, to redeem them. Moses collects one thousand three hundred sixty-five shekels and gives it to Aaron and his sons.
