strife

topic · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: contention, disobedience, hasty fighting, quarreling, the stoutness of the strife

Abstract written by a model

In Proverbs, strife is portrayed as worse than a feast, since quiet with a dry morsel is better; it begins like breaching a dam, so it should be stopped early; it is loved by those who love disobedience, and repetition separates friends. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, strife is a kind of contention to abstain from, since abstaining diminishes sins; passionate men kindle it, and hasty quarrels spark fires and bloodshed, burning as fuel allows.

Chapter 17

Strife is said to be worse than a feast, since a dry morsel with quietness is better than a feasting house full of it. Its beginning is likened to breaching a dam, so contention should be stopped before quarreling breaks out. Those who love disobedience love strife, and repeating a matter separates close friends.
