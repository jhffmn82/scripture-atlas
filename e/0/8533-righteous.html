Abstract written by a model

The righteous are saints who uphold Asha in community and soul and act uprightly with honour. They seek blessings, Weal, and eternal Immortality, receiving salvation and a joyful destiny. After perfection, they gather in the happy abode of the Good Mind and Ahura, while their conscience crushes the wicked at judgment.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XXX.

The righteous saints will gather in the happy abode of the Good Mind and Ahura when perfection is attained. They are described as proceeding on earth in good repute and honour.

Yasna XLV.

The righteous seek blessings, Weal, and Immortality, while their souls desire these in eternal Immortality. Their blessings contrast with the woes established for the wicked.

Yasna LI.

The righteous are those who maintain Asha in the community and in the soul and act uprightly. They receive salvation, blessings, and a joyful destiny, while their conscience crushes the wicked at judgment.
