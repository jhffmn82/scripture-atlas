Abstract written by a model

The table is a furnishing located on the north side of the tabernacle, outside the veil. It is made of acacia wood, two cubits long, a cubit wide and one and a half cubits high, overlaid with pure gold with a gold molding and hand-width rim. Four gold rings hold gold-overlaid carrying poles. Its pure gold vessels include dishes, spoons, bowls and pitchers. Chapter 25 gives instructions, including bread of the presence always set before the LORD; Chapter 37 has Bezalel make it.

Chapter 25

The table is to be made of acacia wood, two cubits long, a cubit wide, and one and a half cubits high, overlaid with pure gold and given a gold molding and a hand-width rim. It has four gold rings and gold-overlaid acacia poles for carrying, pure gold dishes, spoons, ladles, and bowls, and the bread of the presence is to be set on it before the LORD always.

Chapter 37

Bezalel makes the table of acacia wood, two cubits long, a cubit wide, and a cubit and a half high, overlaid with pure gold with a gold molding. It has a hand's-width border with its own gold molding, four gold rings near the border for the carrying poles, and gold-overlaid acacia poles. He also makes its pure gold vessels: dishes, spoons, bowls, and pitchers for pouring out.
