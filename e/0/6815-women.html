women

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: mothers, nursing mothers, their wives, wives, your women

Abstract written by a model

Women have rights similar to men’s in kindness. They observe specified waiting periods after divorce or a husband’s death, abstain from intercourse during menstruation until purified, are described as tilth for husbands, and must be treated honorably in divorce.

Women have rights similar to those of men in kindness, while divorced women must observe waiting periods. Women may be divorced before consummation under specified conditions and must be treated honorably and released kindly.
