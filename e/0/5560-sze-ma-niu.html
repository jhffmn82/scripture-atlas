Abstract written by a model

Sze-ma Niu asks the Master about perfect virtue and the superior man, probing cautious speech and freedom from anxiety or fear. He is himself anxious because, unlike other men, he has no brothers.

Sze-ma Niu is in one work on this site, The Analects of Confucius, Book XII (Yen Yuan). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
