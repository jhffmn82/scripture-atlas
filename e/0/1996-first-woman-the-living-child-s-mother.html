Chapter 3

She tells the king that she and the other woman lived in one house and gave birth three days apart, and that the other woman took her living son at midnight and put her dead child in her bosom. When the king orders the child divided, her heart yearns over her son and she begs that he be given to the other woman rather than killed. The king declares her the mother.
