Reuel is the priest of Midian and father of seven daughters, owner of a flock. After they report an Egyptian's help, he invites the man to eat bread and gives his daughter Zipporah to Moses.

Chapter 2

Reuel is the priest of Midian and father of seven daughters. When they return early from watering the flock and tell him an Egyptian helped them, he orders them to call the man to eat bread. He then gives his daughter Zipporah to Moses, who is content to dwell with him.

Jethro in Exodus

author unknown · about 1439 to 1399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Jethro first appears

Abstract written by a model

Jethro is a priest of Midian and Moses' father-in-law. After hearing of God's deliverance of Israel, he brings Zipporah and her sons to Moses, declares the LORD greater than all gods, offers sacrifices, advises Moses to share judging, and then returns to his own land.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 18

Jethro, priest of Midian and Moses' father-in-law, hears of what God did for Moses and Israel and brings Zipporah and her two sons to Moses at the Mountain of God. He rejoices, declares the LORD greater than all gods, offers a burnt offering and sacrifices, and then advises Moses to share the burden of judging. After Moses follows his advice, he departs for his own land.
