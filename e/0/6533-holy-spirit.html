Holy Spirit in The Acts of the Apostles

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Holy Spirit first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Holy Spirit appears in these records as a divine agent who speaks, directs, empowers and is given, rather than a mere force. Promised by Jesus in Chapter 1 as a baptism that would bring power to the apostles, he is also said to have spoken through David about Judas. In Chapter 2 he fills the gathered believers, who speak in other languages, and Peter teaches that Jesus received the promise from the Father and poured it out; repentant, baptized people will receive the gift. The Spirit then extends outward: Samaritan believers receive him through Peter and John's prayer and laying on of hands, Simon tries to buy this power, and the Spirit directs Philip to the eunuch and carries him away. In Chapters 10 and 11 he sends men to Peter and falls on Gentiles in Cornelius's house, amazing the circumcised believers. He fills Barnabas, informs Agabus of a famine, and in Chapter 13 directs the Antioch assembly to send out Barnabas and Saul, filling Paul against Elymas and the disciples with joy. In Chapter 19 Ephesian disciples, unfamiliar with him, receive him through Paul's hands, speak in languages and prophesy; Paul resolves in the Spirit to go to Jerusalem and Rome.
