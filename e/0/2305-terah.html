Abstract written by a model

Terah, descendant of Nahor in Shem's line, lived 205 years and fathered Abram, Nahor, and Haran. After Haran died in Ur, he led Abram, Lot, and Sarai toward Canaan, settled in Haran instead, and died there.

Chapter 11

Terah is born in the line of Shem through Nahor, lives seventy years and fathers Abram, Nahor, and Haran. After Haran dies in Ur of the Chaldees, Terah takes Abram, Lot, and Sarai toward Canaan, but they stop and live in Haran, where he dies at 205 years old.
