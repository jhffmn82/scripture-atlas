Chapter 11

Paul calls them false apostles and deceitful workers who masquerade as Christ's apostles, like Satan who masquerades as an angel of light. They are said to be servants of Satan who masquerade as servants of righteousness, and their end will be according to their works. Paul acts to cut off the opportunity of those who desire to be recognized as equal to him.
