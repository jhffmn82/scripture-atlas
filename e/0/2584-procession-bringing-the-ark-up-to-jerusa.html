Abstract written by a model

includes David [with elders and captains, joyfully] The procession carried the ark up from the house of Obed-Edom to David's city with David, the elders, and the captains over thousands, after the Levites had sanctified themselves. Seven bulls and seven rams were sacrificed, priests blew trumpets, and all Israel sounded cornets, trumpets, cymbals, stringed instruments, and harps with shouting. It ended with the ark's arrival in David's city, where Michal watched from a window.
