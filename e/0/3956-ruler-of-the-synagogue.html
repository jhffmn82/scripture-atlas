Also called: The ruler of the synagogue

Abstract written by a model

A synagogue ruler who holds that healing is not permitted on the Sabbath. Indignant at Jesus healing on that day, he told the crowd to seek healing on the six work days; Jesus called him and his companions hypocrites, and his adversaries were left disappointed.

Chapter 13

The ruler of the synagogue, indignant that Jesus healed on the Sabbath, told the crowd to come for healing on the six working days and not on the Sabbath. Jesus answered him, calling him and those with him hypocrites, and pointed out that each of them frees an ox or donkey on the Sabbath. Jesus's adversaries were disappointed afterward.
