Regulations for priests in Chapter 44: inside the inner court gates they wear only linen garments, turbans, and trousers, with no wool or anything causing sweat. Before entering the outer court they change and leave ministering garments in holy rooms, so as not to sanctify the people. Hair is neither shaved nor long; wine and marriage limits also apply.

Chapter 44

Priests entering the inner court gates must wear linen garments, linen turbans, and linen trousers, with no wool and nothing that causes sweat. They must change into other clothes and leave their ministering garments in the holy rooms before going to the outer court, so they do not sanctify the people. They may not shave their heads or let their locks grow long, may not drink wine entering the inner court, and may marry only virgins of Israel or a priest's widow.
