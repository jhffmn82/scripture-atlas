Kadesh

place · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Kadesh Barnea

Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, Kadesh is a city in the wilderness of Zin at Edom's border where the Israelites stay and Miriam dies; Moses sends messengers to Edom from there, then Israel departs for Mount Hor. In Deuteronomy, Kadesh Barnea is reached after travel through the great and terrible wilderness from Horeb; it is where Israel refuses to go up and stays many days after their defeat.
