The document presents teachings on rightful rule and moral cultivation. Yao entrusts Heaven’s succession to Shun, who passes the same charge to Yu; T’ang accepts accountability before God; and Chau restores order, rewards goodness, revives States, and gains the people through generous, sincere, active, just conduct. Tsze-chang asks Confucius how authority should govern. Confucius praises beneficence, suitable work, freedom from covetousness, dignified ease, and majesty without fierceness, while condemning uninstructed killing, sudden demands, harsh enforcement, and stingy rewards. The Master concludes that understanding Heaven’s ordinances, Propriety, and words is essential to superior character and knowing people.

CHAP. I. 1. Yao said, 'Oh! you, Shun, the Heaven-determined

Yao transfers the Heaven-determined order of succession to Shun and charges him to hold fast the due Mean. Shun gives the same charge to Yu. T'ang announces his accountability before God for his own offences and those of the people. Chau enriches the good, restores order and succession, revives States, and wins the people through generosity, sincerity, activity, and justice.

CHAP. II. 1. Tsze-chang asked Confucius, saying, 'In what way

Tsze-chang asks Confucius how a person in authority can conduct government properly. Confucius teaches that the person in authority should honour five excellent things: beneficence without great expenditure, assigning proper tasks without causing repining, pursuing desires without covetousness, maintaining dignified ease without pride, and being majestic without fierceness. Confucius identifies four bad things: killing people without instruction, suddenly demanding full work without warning, enforcing previously non-urgent orders harshly, and giving pay or rewards stingily.

The Master states that recognising the ordinances of Heaven is necessary to be a superior man. He teaches that knowing the rules of Propriety is necessary for the character to be established. He also teaches that knowing the force of words is necessary to know men.
