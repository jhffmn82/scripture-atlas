Also called: The universe, all creation, creation, the universe

Abstract written by a model

The universe is beautiful and silent, with four seasons abiding by fixed law and grounded in absolute principles. It is immeasurably great, rejects nothing, and its evolutions, deaths, births, forms, and transformations exceed human intellect. It serves as a standard of greatness, while TAO purifies, nourishes, and encompasses it and all creation. Specialists wrongly claim exclusive definition of its beauty and principles. Hui Tzŭ loves and investigates all creation endlessly, yet does not reach a conclusion in TAO.

CHAPTER XXII.

The universe is described as very beautiful and silent, with its four seasons following fixed laws. Its countless evolutions, deaths, births, forms, and transformations cannot be fully grasped by human intellect.

Kuan Chung being at the point of death, Duke Huan went to see him.

The universe is described as immeasurably great and as the standard against which greatness is considered. A person depends on what is not known in order to extend knowledge toward the apprehension of God.

CHAPTER XXXIII.

The universe is described as governed by beauty and principles that specialists wrongly claim to define exclusively. TAO is said to purify and nourish the universe and to encompass all creation.

Hui Tzŭ was a man of many ideas. His works would fill five carts. But

Creation was the subject of Hui Tzŭ's long, unending discussions and investigations. He roamed insatiably over all creation but did not reach a conclusion in TAO.
