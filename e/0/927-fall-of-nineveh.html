Abstract written by a model

fall of Nineveh in The Book of Nahum

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where fall of Nineveh first appears

Abstract written by a model

The fall of Nineveh is a foretold destruction framed by the LORD of Armies declaring he is against the city. It begins with an assault: chariots in the streets, river gates opened, the palace dissolving. The city is uncovered and carried away, its people flee and treasure is plundered, leaving it waste. Fortresses fall like ripe figs, gates stand open, fire and sword devour it, officials scatter like locusts, and its wound is incurable.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The fall is portrayed through the attacker's assault, with chariots in the streets, the opening of the river gates, and the dissolving of the palace. The city is decreed uncovered and carried away, its servants moan like doves, its people flee, and its treasure is plundered, leaving it empty, void, and waste. The LORD of Armies' declaration that he is against Nineveh frames the destruction.

Chapter 3

The fall is foretold: the city will be laid waste, its fortresses will fall like ripe figs shaken into the eater's mouth, and its gates stand open to enemies. Fire and sword will devour it, its guards and officials will flee like locusts when the sun appears, and its wound is declared incurable, with its people scattered.
