Abstract written by a model

The veil is an object first placed on Moses' face to keep Israel from seeing the fading glory's end. It stays over hearts during the reading of the old covenant, but is removed in Christ whenever someone turns to the Lord, leaving believers unveiled.

Chapter 3

The veil was put on Moses' face so the children of Israel would not look at the end of the fading glory. It remains at the reading of the old covenant and lies on the heart when Moses is read. It is taken away whenever someone turns to the Lord, and believers have unveiled faces.
