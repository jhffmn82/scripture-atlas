Frashaostra

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Frasha., Frashaostra Hvôgva, Frashaostra the Hvôgva, Frashaostra, the Hvôgva

Abstract written by a model

Frashaostra is holy, blest and foremost within Ahura Mazda’s realm. Given guardian power and headship of the Holy Order, he desires grace, offers propitiation and confessions, and seeks confirmation of his gift. He later asks Zarathustra about memorised rites and completed delivery of the Gâthas.

Frashaostra is in one work on this site, Yasna. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
