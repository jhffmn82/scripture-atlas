Chapter 21

Striking a man dead brings death, except for unintentional killing, where a place of flight is appointed, while a scheming killer is taken even from the altar. Attacking or cursing parents and kidnapping are also capital offenses. Non-fatal fights require payment for lost time and healing, and a master who kills a servant under his hand is punished, though not if the servant survives a day or two.
