Mount Ushi-darena

place · 2 works · 12 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Ushi-darena, Yazad mount, the mountain Ushi-darena

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Mount Ushi-darena is a Mazda-made mountain and Yazad mount, glorious and shining with holiness, worshipped in Yasna XVII. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Mount Ushi-darena is a Mazda-made mountain and seat of holy happiness, surrounded by waters and connected to where kingly Glory cleaves to one raised there.
