divorce

topic · 5 works · 9 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a writing of divorce, certificate of divorce, divorce (let them remember that), divorced, divorces, divorces her husband, divorces his wife, part, put away, put away his wife

Abstract written by a model

In Deuteronomy, divorce is carried out by writing a certificate, placing it in the wife's hand, and sending her from the house; remarriage to the first husband after a second marriage ends is prohibited. In The Gospel According to Matthew, divorce is permitted only for sexual immorality; whoever divorces his wife otherwise makes her an adulteress, and whoever marries a divorced woman commits adultery. In The Gospel According to Matthew, divorce is presented as contested: Moses allowed it because of hardened hearts, though it was not intended from the beginning; Jesus permits it only for sexual immorality, otherwise divorce and remarriage or marrying a divorced woman is adultery. In The Good News According to Mark, divorce is allowed by Moses because of hardness of heart, but Jesus teaches that from creation the two become one flesh, what God has joined no man should separate, and divorce followed by remarriage is adultery for either sex. In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), divorce is regulated by two pronouncements, waiting periods, fair provision, and kind retention or release; harming divorced women or obstructing remarriage is forbidden. In Surah 65: Divorce (At-Talaaq), divorce is to be conducted according to the legal period, with kindness, upright testimony, and proper provision, followed by kind reconciliation or separation when the term is reached.
