Chapter 16

The Lord GOD declares that he will judge Jerusalem as women who break wedlock and shed blood are judged. Her lovers will strip her, destroy her high places, stone her, stab her, and burn her houses, ending her prostitution and appeasing his wrath and jealousy. The judgment is followed by promises of restoration and forgiveness.
