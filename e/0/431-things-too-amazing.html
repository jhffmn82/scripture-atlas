Chapter 30

Agur says three things are too amazing for him, and four he does not understand: the way of an eagle in the air, of a serpent on a rock, of a ship in the middle of the sea, and of a man with a maiden. This is followed by the way of an adulterous woman, who eats, wipes her mouth, and claims to have done nothing wrong.
