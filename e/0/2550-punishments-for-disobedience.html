Abstract written by a model

Chapter 26 describes punishments for Israelite disobedience, rejecting statutes and breaking covenant, in escalating sevenfold stages: terror, consumption, fever, enemy rule, drought, wild animals, sword, pestilence, famine, and cannibalism. They culminate in ruined cities and sanctuaries, scattering among nations, and fainthearted exile, yet remain non-final: confession and humility lead the LORD to remember his covenant.

Chapter 26

Disobedience brings punishments in four escalating stages, each sevenfold: terror, consumption, fever, defeat, drought, wild animals, sword, pestilence, famine, and cannibalism. They culminate in destroyed high places, ruined cities and sanctuaries, scattering among the nations, and faintness of heart in exile. These punishments are not final, since confession and humility lead the LORD to remember his covenant.
