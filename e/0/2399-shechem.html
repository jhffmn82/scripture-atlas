Chapter 34

Shechem, son of Hamor the Hivite and prince of the land, takes and lies with Dinah, then loves her, speaks kindly to her, and asks his father to get her as a wife. He offers any dowry to her father and brothers, is quick to be circumcised, and is killed by the sword with his father.
