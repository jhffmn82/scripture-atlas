temple cleansing

event · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: cleanse the LORD’s house, sanctify the house of the LORD

Abstract written by a model

The temple cleansing was a sanctification of the LORD's house lasting eight days beyond its start and finishing on the sixteenth day of the first month. Priests cleansed the inner temple and brought uncleanness out; Levites carried it to the brook Kidron. Workers then reported to Hezekiah that the house, altar, table, and vessels, including Ahaz's discarded ones, were prepared.

Chapter 29

The cleansing begins on the first day of the first month, reaches the LORD's porch on the eighth day, and is finished on the sixteenth day. Priests bring out the uncleanness from the inner house to the court, and Levites carry it to the brook Kidron. The workers report to Hezekiah that the house, the altar of burnt offering, the table of show bread, and all vessels, including those Ahaz threw away, are cleansed and prepared.
