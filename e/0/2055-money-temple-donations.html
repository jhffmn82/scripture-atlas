Chapter 12

The money brought into the LORD's house included current money, the money from people's evaluations, and whatever anyone chose to bring. Jehoash first directed the priests to take it for repairs, then told them to stop taking it from their treasurers and deliver it for the repair. It was eventually weighed, put in bags, counted, and paid to the workers, while trespass and sin offering money was not brought in and belonged to the priests.
