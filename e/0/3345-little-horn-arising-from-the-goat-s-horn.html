little horn (arising from the goat's horns)

object · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: a king of fierce face, a little horn, king of fierce face

Abstract written by a model

The little horn arises from one of the goat's four notable horns and grows exceedingly great toward the south, east, and glorious land. It casts down part of the army and stars, magnifies itself against the prince of the army, takes away the continual burnt offering, throws down the sanctuary, and casts truth to the ground. Gabriel interprets it as a fierce-faced king whose mighty power is not his own, who destroys the holy people by deceit, and who, after standing against the prince of princes, is broken without human power.

Chapter 8

A little horn comes out of one of the four horns and grows exceedingly great toward the south, the east, and the glorious land. It casts down some of the army and stars and tramples them, magnifies itself to the prince of the army, takes away the continual burnt offering, throws down the sanctuary, casts truth to the ground, and prospers. Gabriel interprets it as a fierce-faced king who understands dark sentences, whose power is mighty but not his own, who destroys the mighty and the holy people through deceit, stands against the prince of princes, and is broken without human power.
