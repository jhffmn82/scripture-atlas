Yasna XXXII.

The rival leader is said to destroy and pervert the doctrines, while Zarathustra seeks to establish Ahura Mazda’s precepts. The condemned doctrines are connected with evil teachings, blasphemy, and rejection of the sacred Kine and the Sun.
