holy things of the LORD

Abstract written by a model

Holy things are offerings the children of Israel consecrate to the LORD, which priests eat as their bread, only while clean. Eating is restricted to the priest's household: purchased slaves, those born in his house, and a widowed or divorced childless daughter returned home. Strangers, foreigners living with priests, hired servants, and a daughter married to an outsider may not eat them; unwitting eating requires adding a fifth and giving the holy thing to the priest.

Chapter 22

The holy things are the offerings the children of Israel make holy to the LORD, and priests eat them as their bread. A priest may eat them only when clean, and only members of his household, such as purchased slaves, those born in his house, and a widowed or divorced childless daughter, may share. Strangers, foreigners living with the priests, hired servants, and a daughter married to an outsider may not eat them.
