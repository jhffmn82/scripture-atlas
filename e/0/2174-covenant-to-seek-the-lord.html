Chapter 15

In the third month of Asa's fifteenth year, the assembly at Jerusalem entered a covenant to seek the LORD, the God of their fathers, with all their heart and soul. Anyone who would not seek him was to be put to death, and the people swore it with a loud voice, shouting, trumpets, and cornets, after which the LORD was found by them and gave rest.
