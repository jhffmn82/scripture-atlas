those who believe and do good works

group · 3 works · 7 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 84: The Splitting Open (Al-Inshiqaaq), they receive an unfailing reward. In Surah 95: The Fig (At-Tin), they are saved from being reduced to the lowest of the low. In Surah 103: The Declining Day, Epoch (Al-Asr), they are excepted from the statement that man is in a state of loss and exhort one another to truth and endurance.
