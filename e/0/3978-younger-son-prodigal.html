Chapter 15

The younger son asks for his share of the property, travels to a far country, and wastes it with riotous living. After a famine leaves him feeding pigs and hungry, he comes to himself, resolves to return and ask to be made a hired servant, and confesses his sin to his father. His father receives him and restores him, calling him dead and alive again, lost and found.
