Abstract written by a model

Zacharias is an aged, righteous priest of Abijah's division, married to Elizabeth. Gabriel tells him in the temple that she will bear John; he doubts and is struck mute. He regains speech by naming John, then prophesies, blessing God for redemption.

Chapter 1

Zacharias, a priest of the division of Abijah, is told by Gabriel in the temple that Elizabeth will bear a son named John; he asks how he can be sure and is struck mute for not believing. He regains speech after writing 'His name is John' and then prophesies, blessing God for redemption and describing John as a prophet who will prepare the Lord's ways.
