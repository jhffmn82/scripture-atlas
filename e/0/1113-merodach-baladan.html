Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Berodach Baladan rules Babylon and sends letters and a gift to Hezekiah after hearing of his sickness. In The Book of Isaiah, Merodach-baladan sends letters and a present to Hezekiah after hearing of his recovery from sickness, and his messengers are shown all Hezekiah's treasures.
