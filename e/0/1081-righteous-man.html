Abstract written by a model

The righteous man is a figure of integrity who walks righteously, speaks blamelessly, rejects bribes and oppressive gain, and avoids evil. He is rewarded with a secure high fortress, steady bread and water, and the sight of the king and a distant land.

Chapter 33

The righteous man walks righteously, speaks blamelessly, despises gain from oppression, refuses bribes, and shuts his ears and eyes to bloodshed and evil. He will dwell on high in a fortress of rocks, with bread supplied and sure water. His eyes will see the king in his beauty and a distant land.
