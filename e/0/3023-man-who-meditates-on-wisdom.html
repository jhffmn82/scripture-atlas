Abstract written by a model

A man who meditates on wisdom is pronounced blessed. He reasons with understanding, considers her ways, gains knowledge of her secrets, then lingers at her paths, windows and doors, pitches his tent near her, and shelters his children beneath her branches from heat.

Chapter 14

He is pronounced blessed, as one who meditates on wisdom and reasons by his understanding. He considers her ways in his heart and gains knowledge of her secrets. He lies in wait on her paths, peers in at her windows, listens at her doors, pitches his tent near her, and sets his children under her shelter, resting under her branches and covered from heat.
