Surah 54: The Moon (Al-Qamar)

document · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

The Hour, the moon, warnings, and the Qur'an are described in relation to denial, punishment, and remembrance. The folk of Noah, A'ad, Thamud, the folk of Lot, and the house of Pharaoh reject warnings and suffer different punishments, while Noah, Lot, and the family of Lot receive deliverance or help. The guilty are destined for error, madness, and the Fire, whereas the righteous will dwell among gardens and rivers in the favour of a Mighty King.
