Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, Moses' mother hides her son for three months, places him in a tar-coated papyrus basket among reeds, and is hired by Pharaoh's daughter to nurse him. In Surah 28: The Stories (Al-Qasas), the mother of Moses is inspired to nurse him and cast him into the river, and Allah returns him to her and comforts her by fulfilling His promise.

Chapter 2

She is a daughter of Levi who bears a fine son and hides him three months. When she can no longer hide him, she places him in a tar-coated papyrus basket in the reeds. She is later called by her daughter and hired by Pharaoh's daughter to nurse her own child for wages.
