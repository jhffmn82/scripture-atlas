The Book of Hosea / Chapter 1

Gomer's first son is named Jezreel at the LORD's instruction. His name signals that the LORD will soon avenge the blood of Jezreel on the house of Jehu, end the kingdom of Israel, and break Israel's bow in the valley of Jezreel. The text also says great will be the day of Jezreel.
