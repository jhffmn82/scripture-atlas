Abstract written by a model

Gehazi falsely asks Naaman for a talent of silver and two changes of clothing in Elisha's name for two supposed visitors. Naaman presses two talents of silver in two bags and two changes of clothing on him, and servants carry them until Gehazi takes them and stores them in his house. Elisha's rebuke over this wealth leads to Gehazi's affliction with leprosy.
