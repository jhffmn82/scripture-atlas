justice and righteousness in The Book of Isaiah

author unknown · 739 to 680 BC · the work’s page · read it where justice and righteousness first appears

Abstract written by a model

Justice and righteousness is a theme in Isaiah. In Chapter 1 the LORD commands the people to seek justice, relieve the oppressed, and defend the fatherless and widow, laments a city now full of murderers and bribe-loving princes, and promises restoration and redemption of Zion with justice. In Chapter 42 his servant brings justice to the nations until it is set on earth.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

The Book of Isaiah / Chapter 1

The LORD commands the people to learn to do well, seek justice, relieve the oppressed, defend the fatherless, and plead for the widow. The city once full of justice, where righteousness lodged, now has murderers and princes who love bribes. The LORD promises to restore her judges and counselors so she will be called the city of righteousness, and to redeem Zion with justice and righteousness.

Chapter 42

Justice is what the LORD's servant will bring to the nations, faithfully and without being discouraged, until he has set it on the earth. The islands wait for his law. The LORD's own righteousness is also cited as the reason he magnified the law and made it honorable.
