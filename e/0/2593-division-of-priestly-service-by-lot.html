division of priestly service by lot

event · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: cast lots, divisions, drawing lots, lot, ordering in their service, their ordering in their service

Abstract written by a model

An impartial drawing of twenty-four lots divided priestly service between Eleazar's and Ithamar's lines, both having sanctuary princes. Following Aaron's ordinance, the first lot fell to Jehoiarib, the second to Jedaiah, through Maaziah twenty-fourth; Shemaiah recorded it before the king, princes, Zadok, Ahimelech, and household heads.

Chapter 24

Twenty-four lots were drawn impartially, since there were princes of the sanctuary and of God in both Eleazar's and Ithamar's lines. The first lot fell to Jehoiarib, the second to Jedaiah, and so on to Maaziah, twenty-fourth, and Shemaiah recorded them before the king and leaders.
