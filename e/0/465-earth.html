Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, earth emerges from the waters, becomes dry, receives the living creatures from the ship, and continues with the cycles of nature. In Genesis, earth begins formless and water-covered, then becomes dry land producing vegetation and living creatures under God's ordering. In Genesis, earth is corrupted and filled with wickedness and violence, so God resolves to destroy it with a flood. In The Book of Job, earth has foundations and measured width established by the LORD, while dawn shakes the wicked from it and rain nourishes its wilderness. In Psalms, earth is founded by the LORD, separated from the waters, watered, and filled with the fruit of his works. In Psalms, earth is called to praise the LORD through its creatures and peoples, while his glory is above it. In Psalms, earth belongs to the LORD with its fullness and inhabitants, founded upon the seas and established on the floods. In Psalms, earth changes, shakes, melts, and becomes the place where God makes desolations, ends wars, and is exalted.

and 10 more works

In Psalms, earth is visited, watered, enriched, and made fruitful by God, whose wonders cause its distant inhabitants to fear. In Psalms, all the earth is commanded to sing, tremble, rejoice, and await the LORD's judgment. In Psalms, earth is commanded to rejoice and trembles at the LORD's presence, who is Lord of the whole earth. In Proverbs, earth and its deeps, springs, mountains, hills, fields, and dust are described as established by the LORD with wisdom present as craftsman. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, creation is boundless, trembles at the LORD's visit, and is filled with living things and blessings. In The Book of Isaiah, earth is polluted by its inhabitants, devoured by a curse, shaken, and laid waste by the LORD. In The Book of Zephaniah, earth is threatened with being utterly swept away and devoured by the LORD's jealous fire. In 1 John, the world is a moral realm opposed to the Father, containing worldly lusts and passing away. In Revelation, earth receives fire from the censer and suffers the burning of a third of its trees and all green grass at the first trumpet. In Revelation, earth receives the dragon and his angels, is warned of woe, and helps the woman by swallowing the dragon's river.
