Also called: asterism Uttaraphalgunî, the asterism Uttaraphalgunî

Abstract written by a model

In Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra), Uttaraphalgunî is the asterism accompanying Mahâvîra’s descent from heaven, conception, transfer between wombs, birth, renunciation, and attainment of Kevala. In Kalpa Sutra, Uttaraphalgunî is an asterism joined with the moon throughout Mahâvîra’s descent, conception, transfer, birth, renunciation, and Kevala.
