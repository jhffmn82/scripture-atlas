Third Part. / Fifteenth Lecture, Called the Clauses.

The palankin Kandraprabhâ was produced by Sakra and could be carried by a thousand men. It was richly decorated with precious substances, divine designs, flowers, bells, flags, and a throne, and carried Mahâvîra from Kundapura to the park Gñâtri Shanda.
