distrust of the enemy

Abstract written by a model

Chapter 12 counsels permanent distrust of an enemy: stay careful even when he humbles himself, like someone polishing a mirror to stop tarnish. Never seat him beside you or at your right hand, lest he overthrow you; ignoring this leads to regret.

Chapter 12

The reader is told never to trust an enemy and to remain careful even if he humbles himself and walks bowed down, like one who keeps wiping a mirror to stop it tarnishing. He must not be seated beside the reader or on the right hand, lest he overthrow the reader. The reader who ignores this will at last acknowledge the speaker's words and be pricked by them.
