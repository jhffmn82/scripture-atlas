Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Kavi Vîstâspa is a heroic Kavi and holy friend who gains wisdom through Piety and Good Mind, praises Ahura Mazda, makes confessions, and establishes Sovereign Power for Maidhyô-mâh the Spitâma. In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, Kavi Vîstâspa, son of Aurvat-aspa, is a tall Naotaride Kavi and lord of swift horses who seeks victory over enemies and their conversion to the law. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Vîstâspa is a holy, valiant king who upholds the Law, defeats enemies, supports Zarathustra’s religion, and receives divine blessings and boons.

Kavi Vîstâspa in Yasna

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where Kavi Vîstâspa first appears

Abstract written by a model

Kavi Vîstâspa is a heroic Kavi and holy friend for the great cause. Through Piety and the chants of Good Mind, he receives wish and attains wisdom in accordance with Asha. A man of Âramaiti, he offers Ahura Mazda praise and propitiation, makes Yasna confessions, and establishes Sovereign Power, granting it to Maidhyô-mâh the Spitâma.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XLVI.

Kavi Vîstâspa is named by Zarathustra as a heroic holy friend suited to support the great cause. Zarathustra intends to call him and others to the attempt with the Good Mind's words.

Yasna LI.

Kavi Vîstâspa is said to have attained wisdom in the great cause through the chants of Good Mind and to have received the wisdom Ahura Mazda conceived in accordance with Asha. He is also said to have established Sovereign Power and granted it to Maidhyô-mâh the Spitâma.

Kavi Vîstâspa in Yashts (Yasts) I-XII

Abstract written by a model

Kavi Vîstâspa, son of Aurvat-aspa, is a tall Kavi, a Naotaride, and lord of the swiftest horses. Ardvi Sûra Anâhita grants him victory-related boons and is invoked to guide him toward the law. He sacrifices to Drvâspa, seeking defeat of enemies and conversion of Varedhakas and Hvyaonas.

XX.

Vîstâspa is the son of Aurvat-aspa and a Kavi who is associated with the Naotaras and the swiftest horses in the countries. Ardvi Sûra Anâhita grants him victory-related boons and is asked to help Zarathustra bring him to think, speak, and act according to the law.

IX. Gôs Yast.

Kavi Vîstâspa offers Drvâspa a sacrifice behind the waters of the river Dâitya. He asks to put several enemies and Daêva-worshippers to flight or smite them and to bring the Varedhakas and Hvyaonas to the good law.

Vîstâspa in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Vîstâspa first appears

Abstract written by a model

Vîstâspa is a holy, valiant, gallant, mighty-speared, lordly king and faithful young man who rules the country. He is defined above all by his commitment to the Law: he thinks, speaks, acts, and professes according to it; serves as the arm and support of the laws of Ahura and Zarathustra; drives the Drug before him; maintains Holiness against fiends; and makes wide room for the holy religion. He offers sacrifice to Ashi Vanguhi behind the Dâitya, seeks victory over enemies and the conversion of the Varedhakas and Hvyaonas to the good law, and receives the requested boon. He puts Asta-aurvant to flight and conquers Tãthravant, Peshana, and Aregat-aspa. Zarathustra blesses him as lord of the country with life, welfare, strength, glory, wealth, holiness, victory, and freedom from sickness and death. His blessings include royal power, swift horses, prosperity, ten sons, including priests, warriors, tillers, and one like himself, and kingly Glory made by Mazda. He is taught to uphold the Law, reject bad or unfriendly priests, sacrifice and pray properly, help the poor, and follow the right way.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

XXIII.

Vîstâspa is worshipped as a gallant, mighty-speared, lordly king who drove the Drug before him and made wide room for the holy religion. He made himself the arm and support of the law of Ahura and the law of Zarathustra.

XVII. Ashi Yast.

Kavi Vîstâspa offers a sacrifice to Ashi Vanguhi behind the waters of the river Dâitya. He asks to defeat several enemies, bring the Varedhakas and Hvyaonas to the good law, and smite large numbers among the Hvyaona nations, and Ashi Vanguhi grants the boon.

XIV.

King Vîstâspa thinks, speaks, and acts according to the Law and professes that Law. He drives the Drug before him, expands the holy religion, supports the law of Ahura and Zarathustra, and conquers its enemies.

Âfrîn Paighambar Zartûst / XXIII. Âfrîn Paighambar Zartûst.

King Vîstâspa receives Zarathustra’s blessing as lord of the country. He is wished long life, long-lived men and women, sons, beneficence, strength, glory, wealth, holiness, victory, welfare, and freedom from sickness and death.

XXIV. Vîstâsp Yast.

Vîstâspa is blessed with holiness, long life, prosperity, offspring, strength, glory, swift horses, and royal power. He is taught to support the Law of the worshippers of Mazda, reject bad or unfriendly priests, perform proper sacrifices, pray, help the poor, and follow the right way.
