Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, manna is bread given by the LORD, gathered by each person and eaten by the people for forty years. In Psalms, manna is bread of angels that God rained from the sky for Israel to eat. In The Gospel According to John, manna is the bread eaten by the Jews' fathers in the wilderness, who died after eating it.

manna in Exodus

Abstract written by a model

Manna is a bread given by the LORD, named by the house of Israel. It appears after the dew on the wilderness surface, white like coriander seed and tasting like wafers with honey. Each person gathers an omer; leftovers spoil except before the Sabbath, and it melts in the sun. An omer is preserved, and the people eat it forty years.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

Manna appears on the wilderness surface after the dew as a small round thing like frost, which the people name "Manna"; it is white like coriander seed and tastes like wafers with honey. Each person gathers an omer, leftovers breed worms and turn foul except on the Sabbath eve, and it melts in the heat of the sun. An omer-full is kept before the Testimony for generations, and the people eat it for forty years.
