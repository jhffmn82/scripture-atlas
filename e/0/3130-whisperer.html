Chapter 28

The whisperer, called double-tongued, is to be cursed because he has destroyed many who were at peace. As a slanderer he has shaken many, dispersed them from nation to nation, pulled down strong cities, overthrown great men's houses, and cast out brave women and deprived them of their labors.
