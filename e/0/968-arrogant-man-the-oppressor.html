Abstract written by a model

An arrogant, puffed-up, treacherous man, insatiable like Sheol, who gathers and plunders nations through extortion and bloodshed. He is condemned with repeated woes. The peoples mock him with a taunting proverb, the plundered rise to plunder him in return, and he finally drinks the LORD's cup, exposed and covered in disgrace.

Chapter 2

He is described as puffed up, not upright, treacherous through wine, and as enlarging his desire like Sheol, never satisfied, gathering all nations to himself. He is the target of repeated woes for extortion, evil gain, building a city with blood, and shaming his house, and the text says the peoples will take up a taunting proverb against him and his debtors and the plundered will rise and plunder him. He will also drink the cup of the LORD's right hand, be exposed, and be covered with disgrace.
