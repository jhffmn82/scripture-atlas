Abstract written by a model

Judah's disobedience is its refusal to heed Jeremiah and the prophets for twenty-three years, persisting in serving and worshipping other gods, provoking the LORD to anger with its own handiwork, which is the stated reason for coming judgment.

Chapter 25

For twenty-three years Judah did not listen to Jeremiah or the prophets calling it to return from evil, dwell in the land, and stop following other gods. It provoked the LORD to anger with the work of its hands to its own hurt, which is the stated reason for the coming judgment.
