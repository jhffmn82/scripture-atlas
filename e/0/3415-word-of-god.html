Abstract written by a model

In The Good News According to Mark: The word is what the farmer sows. Satan takes it from those by the road, those on rocky ground receive it with joy but stumble under oppression or persecution, and the cares of the age, riches, and lusts choke it among thorns. In The Good News According to Mark, the Good News announces Jesus Christ and God’s Kingdom and is preached to the whole creation. In The Acts of the Apostles: The apostles preach the Good News in Lystra and Derbe after fleeing Iconium, and make many disciples in Derbe. In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, the Good News is God’s message about his Son and his power for salvation to everyone who believes. In Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians, the Good News is Paul’s free proclamation that Christ died for sins, was buried, and was raised on the third day. In 2 Corinthians, the Good News of Christ proclaims Christ’s glory as God’s image but remains veiled to those blinded by the god of this world. In Paul’s Letter to the Galatians, the Good News of Christ is the divinely given gospel Paul preached and the Galatians received. In Paul’s Letter to the Philippians: The Good News is furthered by the Philippians' partnership, and Paul's circumstances have turned out to its progress.

and 16 more works

In Paul’s Letter to the Colossians, the Good News carries truth and hope, bears fruit, grows worldwide, and is served by Paul. In 1 Thessalonians, the Good News of God comes with power, the Holy Spirit, and assurance, and spreads through the regions and everywhere. In 2 Timothy, the Good News brings life and immortality to light and is the cause for which Timothy must endure hardship. In 2 Timothy: The addressee is told to handle the Word of Truth properly, as a workman who doesn't need to be ashamed. In The Letter to the Hebrews, the word of God is living and active, sharper than a two-edged sword, and able to discern the heart’s thoughts and intentions. In 1 Peter, the Good News is the enduring word of the Lord, preached through the Holy Spirit and examined by angels. In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, the word of God is the rod of iron that protects, leads, feeds, and delivers those who heed it. In The Second Book of Nephi, the word of the Holy One of Israel is established as a standard, given through witnesses and writings, and spread throughout the earth. In The Second Book of Nephi, the words of Christ lead people into the way, teach them what to do, and must be feasted upon toward eternal life. In The Second Book of Nephi, the words of Christ teach souls to do good and will be revealed with power and glory at the last day. In The Book of Jacob, the Brother of Nephi, the word of God is taught diligently, heals the wounded soul, exposes wickedness, and invites the pure in heart to feast upon it. In The Words of Mormon, the word of God is spoken with power and authority and will judge Mormon’s people and their brethren. In The Book of Mosiah, the word of God is taught by Alma and fellow laborers, and the sons of Mosiah seek to preach it to the Lamanites. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the word of God is freely preached, calls people to repentance, delivers from bondage, and leads believers to salvation. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the word of Christ leads people along a straight course to eternal bliss. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon’s Own Record, the word of God brings power and ensures that God’s revelations will be fulfilled despite wickedness. (The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record)

the Good News in Paul’s Letter to the Romans

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where the Good News first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Good News is God's message about his Son, promised beforehand through the prophets in the Scriptures. It is God's power for salvation for everyone who believes, Jew first and Greek, and reveals God's righteousness from faith to faith. Paul, set apart for it, first longs to preach it in Rome; later he serves it as a priest, has preached it from Jerusalem to Illyricum, and aims to reach unevangelized places before coming to Rome.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

The Good News is the message of God concerning his Son, promised beforehand through the prophets in the Scriptures. Paul is set apart for it and eager to preach it in Rome. It is the power of God for salvation for everyone who believes, for the Jew first and also the Greek, and in it God's righteousness is revealed.

Chapter 15

The Good News of God and of Christ is what Paul serves as a priest, and he has fully preached it from Jerusalem around to Illyricum. He aims to preach it where Christ was not already named. He expects to come to Rome in the fullness of its blessing.
