A census commanded by the LORD and carried out by Moses and Eleazar on the plains of Moab by the Jordan at Jericho. It counts Israelite men twenty and older able to war, by tribe and family, totaling 601,730, to guide land division; only Caleb and Joshua survive from the Sinai count.

Chapter 26

The LORD orders a census of all Israelite men twenty years old and upward who can go to war, carried out by Moses and Eleazar on the plains of Moab. It records each tribe's families and totals, giving 601,730 overall. Its purpose is tied to dividing the land, and it contains none of the men counted earlier at Sinai except Caleb and Joshua.
