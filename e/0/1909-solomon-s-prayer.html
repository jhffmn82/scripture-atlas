Solomon's prayer of dedication in The Second Book of Chronicles

author unknown · about 424 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Solomon's prayer of dedication first appears

Abstract written by a model

Solomon's dedication prayer praises the LORD as incomparable and covenant-keeping, admits heaven cannot contain him and no man is sinless, and asks him to hear prayers toward the house, including foreigners and captives, in oaths, defeat, drought, famine, plague, battle and exile, ending by pleading for his resting place.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

The prayer begins by praising the LORD as incomparable and as keeper of covenant, then asks that he hear prayers made toward the house. It lists cases such as oaths, defeat, drought, famine, plague, the foreigner, battle and exile, each asking the LORD to hear from heaven, forgive and act. It ends with a plea for the LORD to arise into his resting place and not turn away the face of his anointed.
