superior man

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

The superior man follows what is right rather than taking a fixed position for or against things. He thinks of virtue, righteousness, and the sanctions of law, avoids acting contrary to virtue, and is slow in speech but earnest in conduct.
