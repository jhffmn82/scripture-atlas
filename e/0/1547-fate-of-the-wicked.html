Chapter 15

Eliphaz says the wicked live in torment all their years, with a destroyer coming even in prosperity, and that they will not escape darkness or the sword. Their riches will not last, their branch will wither before its time, and fire will consume the tents of the godless, whose trust in emptiness brings emptiness as reward.
