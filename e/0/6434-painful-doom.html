painful doom

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba), the painful doom afflicts disbelievers, hypocrites, liars, and wrongdoing people as the threatened reward for evil conduct in the world and the Hereafter. In Surah 84: The Splitting Open (Al-Inshiqaaq), painful doom is promised to those who disbelieve, except for those who believe and do good works.
