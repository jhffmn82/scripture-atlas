Heaven

Also called: Best World, Garô Nmâna of Ahura, Heaven at the last, best life of the Saints, best life of the saints, best world of the saints, heaven, that Best World, the best life of the saints, the best world, the best world of the saints, the happy abode of the Good Mind and of Ahura

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, the best world of the saints is a shining, glorious Heaven devoted to Ahura Mazda, where glorified bodies reach the saints’ best world with Asha Vahista. In Yasna, Heaven is the best mental state and world for the holy, containing the stars, moon, and sun; after crossing the Kinvat Bridge, the holy enter it as the promised destination of glorified bodies. In Visparad, the Garô Nmâna of Ahura is worshipped as Heaven, the best world of the saints, shining and all glorious. In Visparad, Heaven is the bright, all-glorious best world belonging to the saints, identified with the Garô Nmâna of Ahura and worshipped as an ancient, first-made earthly object; the records name the best approach that leads to it. In Visparad, the Best World is the destination of a better path that is worshipped. In Afrinagan, Gahs and Miscellaneous Fragments, Heaven is the home of those who think good thoughts, speak good words, and do good deeds, and is called the best world.

Abstract written by a model

Heaven, the best mental state and world for the holy, contains the stars, moon, and sun. The better spirit wears its firm stones as a robe. After crossing the Kinvat Bridge, the holy enter it as the promised destination of glorified bodies.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Yasna XXX.

Heaven is associated with the best mental state for the holy. The better spirit clothes itself with the firm stones of heaven as its robe.

Yasna LX (Sp. LIX).

Heaven is the destination to which the glorified bodies are asked to go openly as the best world of the saints devoted to Ahura.

Yasna LXXI (Sp. LXX).

Heaven is the place into which the holy person comes after crossing the Kinvat Bridge, and the soul is promised entry there.
