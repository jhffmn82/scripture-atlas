Also called: Bagoas the eunuch

Abstract written by a model

Bagoas is a eunuch in charge of all that Holofernes has, serving him as lord and attendant who wakes him and guards his tent. In Chapter 12 he persuades Judith to dine with Holofernes, promising her honor. In Chapter 14 he finds Holofernes dead and headless, weeps, tears his garments, discovers Judith gone, and cries that a Hebrew woman has shamed Nebuchadnezzar's house.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 12

Bagoas is the eunuch who has charge over all that Holofernes has. Holofernes sends him to persuade Judith to come eat and drink with them, and he tells her not to fear and that she will be honored like the daughters of Asshur who serve in Nebuchadnezzar's palace. Judith accepts, and she had received the fleeces for her daily use from him.

Chapter 14

Bagoas goes to Holofernes' tent, knocks, and supposes Holofernes is sleeping with Judith. Getting no answer, he enters, finds Holofernes dead and headless, weeps and tears his garments, and discovers Judith's lodging empty. He cries out to the people that one Hebrew woman has shamed the house of King Nebuchadnezzar.
