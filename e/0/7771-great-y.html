Great Yü

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Yü, great Yü, the Great Yü, the great Yü

Abstract written by a model

Great Yü is a ruler who attacks Yu-hu, receives the empire from Shun, and governs it. He drains floodwaters, gives Ta-hsia, and addresses disorder, though he is called deficient in natural feeling and likened to ointment on a sore.

Great Yü is in one work on this site, Chuang Tzŭ. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
