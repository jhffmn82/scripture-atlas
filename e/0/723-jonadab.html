Jonadab in The Second Book of Samuel

author unknown · about 950 BC · the work’s page · read it where Jonadab first appears

Abstract written by a model

Jonadab, son of Shimeah and nephew of David, is Amnon's friend, called very subtle. He counsels Amnon to feign sickness to get Tamar sent, then after the killing tells David only Amnon died, per Absalom's design, and announces the sons' return.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

Jonadab, David's nephew and Amnon's friend, is described as a very subtle man. He draws out Amnon's love for Tamar and advises him to feign sickness and ask the king to send her. After the killing he tells David that only Amnon is dead, a result he says Absalom had determined since the rape, and he announces the returning sons.
