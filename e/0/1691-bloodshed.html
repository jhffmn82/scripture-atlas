bloodshed

concept · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections
Also called: blood, shedding much blood, shedding of much blood

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Ezekiel, bloodshed is the leading charge against the city, whose people shed blood through slander, bribes, and the princes' violence. In The Book of Mosiah, bloodshed is described as a destructive result of wars and contentions and as an effect of an unrighteous king.
