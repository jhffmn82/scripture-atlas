Chapter 10

After a night march from Gilgal, Joshua surprised the five Amorite kings' armies at Gibeon, where the LORD confused them and Israel killed many. The rout continued via Beth Horon to Azekah and Makkedah, aided by hailstones and the standing still of the sun and moon.
