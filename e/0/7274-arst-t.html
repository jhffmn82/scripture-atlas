Arstât

Justice · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Justice

Abstract written by a model

In Visparad, Arstât is good Justice, identified with Mazdayasnian Faith and worshipped for helping settlements advance and flourish. In Afrinagan, Gahs and Miscellaneous Fragments, Arstât causes settlements to advance and increase, receives sacrifice, and is included with Sraosha and Rashnu in the final blessing.
