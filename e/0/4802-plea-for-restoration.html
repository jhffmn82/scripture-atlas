Abstract written by a model

A plea for restoration addressed to the Chief Musician, set to the tune "The Lilies of the Covenant." Its refrain, "Turn us again... cause your face to shine, and we will be saved," recurs in verses 3, 7, and 19, the divine title growing fuller each time, alongside requests to save, look down, visit the vine, and revive.
