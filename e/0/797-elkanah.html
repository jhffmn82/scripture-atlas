Abstract written by a model

Elkanah is an Ephraimite, son of Jeroham, living at Ramathaim Zophim with two wives, Hannah and Peninnah. He yearly worships and sacrifices at Shiloh, favoring beloved Hannah with a double portion. He fathers Samuel, and after Samuel is weaned he goes up with his household to offer sacrifice and his vow.

1 Samuel / Chapter 1

Elkanah, an Ephraimite of Ramathaim Zophim, had two wives, Hannah and Peninnah, and went up yearly to worship and sacrifice at Shiloh. He gave Hannah a double portion because he loved her and asked whether he was not better to her than ten sons. He let Hannah wait until Samuel was weaned before going up, and then went with his house to offer the yearly sacrifice and his vow.
