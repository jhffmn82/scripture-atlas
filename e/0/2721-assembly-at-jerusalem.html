Chapter 10

The men of Judah and Benjamin gather on the twentieth day of the ninth month in front of God's house, trembling and soaked by heavy rain. After Ezra's exhortation they answer with a loud voice that they must do as he said, but ask for appointed times and princes, elders, and judges to handle the matter because the people are many and the weather is wet. Jonathan, Jahzeiah, Meshullam, and Shabbethai are the only ones named as opposing or helping the opposition.
