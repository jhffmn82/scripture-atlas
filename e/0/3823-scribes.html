scribes

Also called: The chief priests and the scribes, the scribes

Abstract written by a model

The scribes are a group of religious authorities who fear Jesus and, with chief priests and elders, seek to destroy him and challenge his authority, failing to answer his question about John. Jesus then questions their claim about David's son and warns against their love of honor, exploitation of widows, and pretentious prayers, declaring greater condemnation.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 11

The scribes hear Jesus' teaching in the temple and, with the chief priests, look for a way to destroy him. They join the chief priests and elders in challenging his authority and, unable to answer his question about John, say "We don't know."

Chapter 12

Jesus asks how the scribes can say the Christ is David's son, and warns against them for liking long robes, greetings, best seats, and long pretentious prayers. He says they devour widows' houses and will receive greater condemnation.
