Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Evilmerodach king of Babylon releases Jehoiachin king of Judah from prison, speaks kindly to him, sets his throne above those of the kings with him in Babylon, and gives him a continual daily allowance for life. In The Book of Jeremiah, Evilmerodach frees Jehoiachin from prison, speaks kindly to him, raises his throne above other kings' thrones, and grants him a continual daily allowance for life.
