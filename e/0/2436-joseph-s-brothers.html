In Surah 12: Joseph (Yusuf), Joseph's brethren conspire against Joseph, throw him into a pit, deceive Jacob, seek provisions in Egypt, accuse his younger brother of theft, recognize Joseph, confess their sin, and ask forgiveness.

Joseph's brothers in Genesis

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Joseph's brothers first appears

Abstract written by a model

Joseph's brothers, including Benjamin, hate and envy him over their father's favoritism and his dreams. At Dothan they plot to kill him, strip his tunic, throw him in a pit, and sell him to traders into Egypt, deceiving Jacob with a bloodied tunic. Later, terrified when Joseph reveals himself, they are reassured, reconciled, and carry the news to Jacob in Canaan.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 37

The brothers hate Joseph for their father's favoritism and for his dreams, and envy him. At Dothan they conspire to kill him, strip his tunic, throw him in a pit, and sell him to traders, then dip the tunic in goat's blood and deceive Jacob with it.

Chapter 45

The brothers are terrified and unable to answer when Joseph reveals himself, and he tells them not to be grieved or angry with themselves for having sold him. After Joseph kisses them they talk with him, receive Pharaoh's commands and Joseph's gifts, and travel to Canaan, where they tell Jacob that Joseph is alive and ruler of Egypt.

Abstract written by a model

Joseph's ten brothers are a group who go to Egypt, bow to Joseph without recognizing him, and are accused by him of spying. They protest honesty, privately admit guilt concerning Joseph, then fear finding money in their sacks and report to Jacob.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 42

The ten brothers go down to Egypt, bow to Joseph with faces to the earth, and do not recognize him. They insist they are honest men, say they are twelve brothers, and admit among themselves they are guilty concerning Joseph. On the way home they find money in a sack and tremble, then report to Jacob and are afraid when all the money bundles are found.

Joseph's brothers in Genesis

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Joseph's brothers first appears

Abstract written by a model

Joseph's brothers are a group of shepherds who worship their father's God and live in Goshen, granted by Pharaoh. They call themselves Joseph's servants. In Chapter 44 they are accused over a missing cup, found in Benjamin's sack, and fall before Joseph in distress. In Chapter 47 five of them are presented to Pharaoh, state their trade, and are given Goshen and bread during the famine. In Chapter 50, after burying their father, they fear Joseph's revenge, plead for forgiveness, and are comforted, with Joseph promising God will bring them to the promised land.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 44

The brothers were sent away with food, but were overtaken by the steward and accused of repaying good with evil. They rashly pledged that whoever had the cup would die and the rest become slaves, searched their sacks, and found the cup in Benjamin's. They tore their clothes, returned to the city, and fell on the ground before Joseph.

Chapter 47

Five of Joseph's brothers are presented to Pharaoh and say they are shepherds, as their fathers were, who have come to live as foreigners because of the famine in Canaan. Pharaoh gives them Goshen, and they, with their father's household, are provided with bread by Joseph.

Chapter 50

Joseph's brothers go up with him to bury their father and return to Egypt. Fearing Joseph will hate them and pay them back for the evil they did, they send word citing their father's command to forgive, then fall before him and call themselves his servants. Joseph comforts them and speaks kindly to them, and later he tells them God will visit them and bring them up to the promised land.
