Abstract written by a model

Foreign settlers from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath, and Sepharvaim were placed by Assyria in Samaria's cities in place of the Israelites. Initially not fearing the LORD, they suffered lion attacks, then a returned priest at Bethel taught them. They nonetheless kept making their own gods and high-place priests, continuing this mixed worship to this day.

Chapter 17

People from Babylon, Cuthah, Avva, Hamath, and Sepharvaim are placed in Samaria's cities. They did not fear the LORD at first, so lions killed some of them, and a returned priest living at Bethel taught them to fear him. They also kept making their own gods, such as Succoth Benoth, Nergal, Ashima, Nibhaz, Tartak, Adrammelech, and Anammelech, and appointed priests of the high places. They and their descendants keep this mixed worship to this day.
