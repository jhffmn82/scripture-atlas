the pit

place · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: one of the pits, pit, the depth of the pit, this pit

Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, the pit in the wilderness is empty, with no water, and is where Joseph’s brothers throw him after stripping him; Reuben intends it to keep Joseph alive, and merchants later draw Joseph out of it, so Reuben finds it empty on his return. In Surah 12: Joseph (Yusuf), Joseph’s brethren place Joseph in the depth of the pit after deciding not to kill him; a caravan finds him there, draws him out with a water bucket, and hides him as a treasure.
