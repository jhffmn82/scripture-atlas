Life

Abstract written by a model

Life is a changing condition governed by Destiny, dependent on mind, and valued as gain or loss according to circumstances. Along with death, it is powerful yet cannot affect the immortal soul or inner harmony. Human life unfolds as coming from somewhere, proceeding through mortality and unavoidable troubles, then returning through death; beginning and end follow one another without known exhaustion. Peace remains possible through TAO and recognition of the unity of all things.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER V.

Life and death are powerful but cannot affect the immortal soul. They are also among the changing conditions that revolve according to Destiny and should not disturb inner harmony.

CHAPTER XX.

Mortality brings troubles that cannot be avoided by merely appearing to be something one is not. Chuang Tzŭ says that being carried upon TAO and floating above mortality would remove such troubles.

CHAPTER XXI.

Human life is presented as a process of coming from somewhere and returning through death, with beginning and end following one another without a known exhaustion. Mortality should not destroy the peace of one who recognizes the unity of all things.

Kuan Chung being at the point of death, Duke Huan went to see him.

Life and death may each be regarded as either a gain or a loss according to circumstances. Their value is not fixed independently of the natural situation.
