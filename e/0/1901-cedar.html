Abstract written by a model

Cedar was used for the beams and planks covering the house, the timbers on which the side-room floors rested, and the boards lining the walls from floor to ceiling. The interior cedar was carved with buds and open flowers so that no stone was visible. Cedar was also used for the back part of the house forming the inner sanctuary, for the altar's covering, and for a course of beams in the inner court.
