entire creation

Abstract written by a model

A clean, holy creation encompassing spiritual and mundane realms. In creation, two spirits complete separate deeds and choose separate realms, while Âramaiti gives bodies to spiritual creations of good and evil. The entire creation is included in sacrifice, linked to the beneficent ritual and benignant Religion, prayed for goodness and prosperity against evil creation, protected by the Praises of the Offering, and determines the world’s final order.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XXX.

The two spirits completed their respective parts in the deeds of creation and chose separate realms. Âramaiti gave a body to the spiritual creations of good and evil.

Yasna XXXV.

The entire creation of the clean, the spiritual, and the mundane is included among those to whom sacrifice is offered. It is associated with the longing blessing of the beneficent ritual and the benignant Religion.

Yasna LII (Sp. LI).

The entire creation of the holy is prayed for benefits, goodness, prosperity, and sanctity. Its prosperity is set in opposition to the entire evil creation.

Yasna LV (Sp. LIV).

The entire creation of the holy and the clean is to receive protection through the Praises of the Offering.
