Abstract written by a model

Discipline is presented as proof of legitimate sonship: God treats readers as true sons, unlike earthly fathers who disciplined briefly as they saw fit. It feels grievous for now, not joyous, but is for their profit, sharing his holiness, and later yields peaceful righteousness.

Chapter 12

Discipline is explained as the sign that God treats the readers as true sons, since a son without discipline is illegitimate. Earthly fathers disciplined for a few days as seemed good to them, but God does so for the readers' profit, that they may share his holiness. Though grievous for the present, it yields the peaceful fruit of righteousness to those trained by it.
