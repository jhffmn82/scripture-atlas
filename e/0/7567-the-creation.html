Abstract written by a model

The creation belongs to and is made prosperous by the Good Spirit. It is destined to become deathless; when the world is restored, it will no longer grow old, die, decay, or rot, while the Drug and her hundredfold brood perish.

XIX. Zamyâd Yast.

The creation is made prosperous by the Good Spirit and is destined to become deathless. It will no longer grow old, die, decay, or rot when the world is restored.

XIV.

The creation will become deathless as the prosperous creation of the Good Spirit. The Drug and her hundredfold brood will perish even if she rushes on every side to kill the holy beings.
