Abstract written by a model

In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, war has a host who commences it and a guest who acts defensively; it is treated as evil-omened and like mourning, and should not be entered lightly. In Chuang Tzŭ, war is said to arise from attempts to cultivate duty towards one's neighbour as a means of ending conflict, while sincere abstention and non-aggression can spare the people death. In Mencius, Book I (Liang Hui Wang), war is associated with collecting military equipment, endangering soldiers and officers, and provoking resentment among other princes, bringing calamities.

war in The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics

Lao-Tse · the work’s page · read it where war first appears

Abstract written by a model

War has a host who commences it and a guest who acts defensively. Though arms may be beautiful, they are evil-omened instruments; war is treated like mourning, with the commander on the right. It should not be entered lightly: when weapons cross, the one who deplores it conquers.
