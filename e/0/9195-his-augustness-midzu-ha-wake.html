His Augustness Midzu-ha-wake

person · 1 work · 21 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Emperor Han-Zei, Heavenly sovereign, King's child

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Midzu-ha-wake, younger brother of Emperor Ri-Chiu and also called Emperor Han-Zei, is a Heavenly sovereign who rules the Empire from the palace of Shibakaki at Tajihi. After rejecting King Sumi-no-ye-no-naka-tsu's intentions, he is ordered to kill him, appoints Sobakari Prime Minister, kills Sobakari, reaches Chika-tsu-Asuka, performs purification and worship, and serves Emperor Ri-Chiu. He marries Lady of Tsunu and Oto-hime and has four children, including Lady of Kahi, Lady of Tsubura, King Takara, and Lady of Takabe. He dies at sixty and has a mausoleum on the Moor of Mozu.

His Augustness Midzu-ha-wake is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
