Chapter 6

Joash the Abiezrite, Gideon's father, owned the oak in Ophrah, the altar of Baal, the Asherah, and the second bull. When the men of the city demanded that Gideon be put to death, Joash challenged them to let Baal contend for himself if he were a god, and Gideon was named Jerub-Baal on that day.
