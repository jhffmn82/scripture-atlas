Abstract written by a model

Worship of the Lamb begins when the living creatures and elders fall down before him with harps and bowls of incense and sing a new song. It widens to include many angels and then every created thing, who join in ascribing blessing, honor, glory, and dominion to the one on the throne and the Lamb. It closes with the creatures' "Amen" and the elders falling down and worshiping.
