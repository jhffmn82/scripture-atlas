Râgîmatî

Abstract written by a model

Râgîmatî, a virtuous, learned king’s daughter and intended bride of Arishtanêmi, renounces after his ordination, leads others into the order, defends her vows against Rathanêmi, undertakes severe austerities, annihilates Karman, becomes a Kêvalin, and reaches highest perfection.

Twenty-Second Lecture.

Râgîmatî is the virtuous daughter of an excellent king and the intended bride of Arishtanêmi. After hearing of his ordination, she renounces, induces others to enter the order, and later defends her vows against Rathanêmi; after severe austerities she becomes a Kêvalin and reaches the highest perfection.
