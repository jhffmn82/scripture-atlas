VI. Khôrshêd Yast.

The undying, shining, swift-horsed Sun is the subject of the Yast and receives sacrifice, prayer, propitiation, glorification, libations, Haoma and meat, baresma, holy spells, speech, and deeds. When it rises, it makes the earth, waters, and holy creatures clean, and its light withstands darkness, the Daêvas, robbers and bandits, Yâtus and Pairikas, and unseen death.
