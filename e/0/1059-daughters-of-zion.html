daughters of Zion

group · 2 works · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: the daughters of Zion, the women of Zion, their scalps, women of Zion

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, the daughters of Zion are condemned as arrogant and punished by the Lord, who strips away their finery and replaces their beauty with affliction. In The Second Book of Nephi, the daughters of Zion are portrayed as haughty and punished by the Lord, who removes their beauty and washes away their filth.

Abstract written by a model

The daughters of Zion are a group condemned as arrogant, with outstretched necks, flirting eyes and jingling anklets; the Lord punishes them with sores, baldness, and stripped finery, replacing beauty with rottenness, rope, sackcloth and branding.

Chapter 3

The daughters of Zion are called arrogant, walking with outstretched necks, flirting eyes, and jingling ornaments on their feet. The Lord will bring sores and baldness on them and strip away their jewelry and finery, replacing beauty with rottenness, rope, sackcloth, and branding.

Abstract written by a model

The daughters of Zion are a haughty group marked by stretched-forth necks, wanton eyes, tinkling feet, and many ornaments. The Lord smites their heads, exposes secret parts, and removes their beauty; afterward, the Lord washes away their filth.

Chapter 13

The daughters of Zion are described as haughty, with stretched-forth necks, wanton eyes, and tinkling feet. The Lord smites their heads with a scab, discovers their secret parts, and removes their ornaments and beauty.

Chapter 14

The daughters of Zion will have their filth washed away by the Lord.
