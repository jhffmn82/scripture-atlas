Land of Kumaso

Also called: Kumaso

Abstract written by a model

Land of Kumaso is a land in the West, west of Japan. It is associated with Kumaso, the two bravoes Yamato-take is sent to slay, and later becomes the country Emperor Chiū-Ai prepares to smite before Empress Jin-Gō’s divine possession reveals a promised land farther west.

Land of Kumaso is in one work on this site, Kojiki. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
