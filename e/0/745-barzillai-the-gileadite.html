Abstract written by a model

Barzillai, an eighty-year-old great Gileadite from Rogelim, sustained David at Mahanaim, then escorts him over the Jordan. He declines David's offer of life in Jerusalem due to age, sends Chimham instead, receives David's kiss and blessing, and returns home.

Chapter 19

Barzillai, an eighty-year-old great man from Rogelim who had provided for David at Mahanaim, escorts the king over the Jordan. He declines David's offer to sustain him in Jerusalem because of his age, asks to die in his own city, and offers Chimham instead; David agrees, kisses and blesses him, and he returns home.
