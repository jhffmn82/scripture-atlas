Abstract written by a model

Comfort is a central theme in Paul's Second Letter to the Corinthians. It comes from God, who comforts Paul in affliction so he can comfort others, and it abounds through Christ. In Chapter 1 it brings the Corinthians patient endurance and steadfast hope; in Chapter 7 it overflows as joy through Titus's coming and his refreshed spirit.

Paul’s Second Letter to the Corinthians / Chapter 1

Comfort comes from God, who comforts Paul in all affliction so that he can comfort others with the same comfort. It abounds through Christ and produces in the Corinthians patient endurance of the same sufferings. Paul's hope for them is steadfast because they share in the comfort as they share in the sufferings.

Chapter 7

Comfort is a central theme: the author overflows with comfort and joy despite affliction, and God comforted him and his companions through Titus's coming. This comfort also came from the comfort Titus received in the recipients. The author and his companions rejoiced all the more because Titus's spirit was refreshed by them.
