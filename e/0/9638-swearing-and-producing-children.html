Sect.

Swearing and producing children serves as the test of sincerity between the two deities. Their actions produce deities from mist formed when the Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity and His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness crunch and blow away the sabre fragments and jewels.
