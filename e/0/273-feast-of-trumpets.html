Abstract written by a model

In Leviticus, the day of trumpets is a solemn rest and memorial of trumpet blowing, a holy convocation with no regular work and an offering made by fire brought to the LORD. In Numbers, the Feast of Trumpets is a day of blowing of trumpets held on the first day of the seventh month, with a holy convocation, no regular work, and specified burnt and sin offerings.

Feast of Trumpets in Numbers

author unknown · 1439 to 1399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Feast of Trumpets first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Feast of Trumpets is a day of blowing of trumpets held on the first day of the seventh month. It is a holy convocation with no regular work. It requires a burnt offering of one young bull, one ram, and seven male lambs, plus a male goat as a sin offering for atonement.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 29

Held on the first day of the seventh month, it is a holy convocation with no regular work, described as a day of blowing of trumpets. It requires a burnt offering of one young bull, one ram and seven male lambs with meal offerings, plus one male goat for a sin offering, in addition to the new moon and continual offerings.
