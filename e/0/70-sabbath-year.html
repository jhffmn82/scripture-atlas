Sabbath year

event · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Sabbath, Sabbath of solemn rest for the land, The Sabbath of the land, a Sabbath to the LORD, sabbatical year, seventh year, the seventh year, year of solemn rest for the land

Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, the sabbatical year is the seventh year when the land rests and lies fallow after six years of sowing and harvesting, allowing the poor and animals of the field to eat what grows, including in vineyards and olive groves. In Leviticus, the Sabbath year is a seventh-year solemn rest for the land, with no sowing, pruning, or reaping of what grows by itself; its produce feeds people, servants, strangers, and livestock, and the LORD promises the sixth year's blessing will supply the seventh.

Abstract written by a model

The Sabbath year is a seventh-year solemn rest for the land: no sowing or pruning, and no reaping of what grows by itself. Its produce feeds people, servants, strangers and livestock, and the LORD promises the sixth year's blessing will supply the seventh.

Chapter 25

The seventh year is a Sabbath of solemn rest for the land, in which fields are not sown, vineyards are not pruned, and what grows by itself is not reaped.
