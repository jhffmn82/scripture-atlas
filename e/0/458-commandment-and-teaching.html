Chapter 6

The son is told to keep his father's commandment and not forsake his mother's teaching, binding them on his heart and neck. They lead him when he walks, watch over him when he sleeps, and talk with him when he wakes. The commandment is called a lamp, the law light, and reproofs the way of life, keeping him from the immoral woman.
