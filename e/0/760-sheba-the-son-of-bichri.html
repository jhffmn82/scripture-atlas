Chapter 20

Sheba, a wicked Benjamite, blows a trumpet and calls Israel to abandon David, and the men of Israel follow him. David fears he will do more harm than Absalom did. Pursued to Abel of Beth Maacah, he is beheaded by the city's people, and his head is thrown to Joab.
