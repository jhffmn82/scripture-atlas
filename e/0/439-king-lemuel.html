Chapter 31

King Lemuel is the son whom his mother addresses as son of her womb and of her vows, and the chapter presents her words as the revelation she taught him. He is told not to give his strength to women or to drink wine, and to open his mouth, judge righteously, and serve justice to the poor and needy.
