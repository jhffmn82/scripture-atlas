benevolence

Also called: Their benevolence, benevolent, benevolent man

Abstract written by a model

Benevolence is a quality natural to the superior man, rooted in the heart and expressed in harmonious bearing and ordered conduct. It is the heart’s dwelling-place, rejects killing even one innocent person, and begins through filial affection for parents. It does not require kindness to every man, but includes cultivating affection for the virtuous. As man’s distinguishing characteristic, it arises by extending reluctance to injure others; embodied in conduct, it is called the path of duty. A benevolent ruler has no enemy under heaven.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 15

Benevolence works through filial affection for parents.

Chapter 21

Benevolence is one of the qualities belonging by nature to the superior man. It is rooted in his heart and manifests as a mild harmony in his countenance, a rich fullness in his back, and an ordered movement of his limbs.

Chapter 33

Benevolence means that putting a single innocent person to death is rejected as contrary to it, and it should be the dwelling-place of the heart.

Chapter 46

The benevolence of Yao and Shun did not show itself in acts of kindness to every man, but in earnestly cultivating affection for the virtuous.

Part II / Chapter 1 .. Chapter 38

Benevolence is the distinguishing characteristic of man and, in conduct, is called the path of duty. Extending one's feeling of reluctance to injure others produces benevolence, and a benevolent ruler has no enemy under heaven.
