Abstract written by a model

In The Good News According to Mark, believers are promised salvation if baptized, with their belief accompanied by signs including exorcism, new languages, protection from serpents and deadly drink, and healing. In The Gospel According to John, future believers are those who come to believe through the disciples’ word, for whom Jesus prays to be united so that the world may believe. In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, believers are everyone who believes, calls on the Lord, receives salvation and righteousness through Christ, and is saved without distinction between Jew and Greek. In 1 Timothy: Believers are those who know the truth and receive God's creation with thanksgiving. In 1 John, believers believe in the name of the Son of God, possess assurance of eternal life, trust that God hears their requests, know they are of God, and avoid idols. In Surah 8: The Spoils of War (Al-Anfaal), believers fear Allah, increase in faith, worship, give from their provision, trust their Lord, obey Allah and the messenger, stand firm, avoid dispute, and support fellow believers. In Surah 11: Hud, believers accept Allah’s truth and the Book of Moses, believe, do good works, humble themselves, and receive Allah’s mercy and the promise of the Garden. In Surah 18: The Cave (Al-Kahf), believers do good works, receive news of reward and Paradise, and are encouraged to remain with those who call upon Allah.

and 13 more works

In Surah 22: The Pilgrimage (Al-Hajj), believers are promised pardon, provision, the Gardens, and honor, and are commanded to worship, do good, establish worship, pay the poor-due, and hold fast to Allah. In Surah 24: The Light (An-Noor), believers are instructed to obey Allah and the messenger, worship, pay the poor-due, guard modesty, avoid slander, seek permission, and follow guidance, becoming successful through obedience. In Surah 29: The Spider (Al-Ankaboot), believers are tested, and those who believe, do good works, persevere, and trust Allah receive forgiveness and enter the Garden. In Surah 30: The Romans (Ar-Room), believers rejoice in Allah’s help and victory and are rewarded for believing and doing good works in a Garden. In Surah 38: The letter Saad (Saad), believers who do good works are distinguished from those who spread corruption and are associated with the pious and the promised Gardens of Eden. In Surah 40: The Forgiver (Al-Ghaafir), believers are prayed for by the angels, protected from ill-deeds, helped in this world and on the day of judgment, and promised forgiveness and the Gardens of Eden. In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), believers who do good works receive enduring reward, while those who remain upright receive angelic reassurance, good tidings, protection, and fulfillment of their desires. In Surah 45: Crouching (Al-Jaathiya), believers recognize Allah’s portents and believe in the revelations, and those who believe and do good works enter Allah’s mercy. In Surah 49: The Inner Apartments (Al-Hujuraat), believers are instructed in respectful conduct, verifying reports, reconciling conflict, and avoiding ridicule, insults, suspicion, spying, and backbiting. In Surah 57: The Iron (Al-Hadid), believers are called to believe in Allah and His messenger, spend in Allah’s way, seek forgiveness, and receive light and glad tidings of the Gardens. In Surah 61: The Ranks (As-Saff), believers are told to align their words with their deeds, believe in Allah and His messenger, strive with their wealth and lives, and be Allah’s helpers. In Surah 85: The Constellations (Al-Burooj), believers are persecuted by the owners of the ditch for believing in Allah, while those who believe and do good works are promised Gardens. In The City (Al-Balad), believers exhort one another to perseverance and pity and have their place on the right hand.
