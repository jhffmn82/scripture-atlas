Andrew

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Andrew was a fisherman called by Jesus to fish for men, and he immediately left his nets to follow him. In The Gospel According to John, Andrew was a disciple of John who followed Jesus, recognized him as the Messiah, and brought Simon Peter to him.
