Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, Maher Shalal Hash Baz is the symbolic name instructed by the LORD for the speaker and prophetess's son, signaling that Assyria will plunder Damascus and Samaria before the child can speak. In The Second Book of Nephi, Maher-shalal-hash-baz is the prophetess's son and the subject of the writing, whose name signifies that Damascus's riches and Samaria's spoil will be taken before he can say “My father” and “My mother.”

Chapter 8
