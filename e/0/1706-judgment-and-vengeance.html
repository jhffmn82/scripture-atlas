judgment and vengeance

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: great vengeance, judgments, my vengeance, vengeance

Abstract written by a model

Judgment takes the form of the LORD's announced punishments on each nation, each introduced by 'because' and 'therefore' and ending with the recognition that he is the LORD. Vengeance appears in two ways: Edom and the Philistines are condemned for taking vengeance, and the LORD in turn lays his own vengeance on them, with Edom's carried out by Israel.
