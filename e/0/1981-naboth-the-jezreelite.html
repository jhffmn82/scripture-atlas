Naboth the Jezreelite

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Kings, Naboth the Jezreelite owns an ancestral vineyard beside Ahab’s palace, refuses to surrender it, is falsely accused, and is stoned to death. In The Second Book of Kings, Naboth the Jezreelite’s plot of land in Jezreel is where Jehu meets and kills Joram, and where Joram’s body is thrown.

Chapter 21
