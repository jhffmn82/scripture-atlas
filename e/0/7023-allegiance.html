allegiance

event · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: oath of allegiance, their allegiance

Abstract written by a model

Allegiance is taken by believing women who pledge not to ascribe partners to Allah, steal, commit adultery, kill their children, produce devised lies, or disobey the Prophet in what is right. The Prophet is instructed to accept their allegiance and ask Allah to forgive them.
