Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, God calls the heavens and earth to judge his people, exposing Israel's hypocrisy and wickedness while promising salvation to those who offer thanksgiving. In Psalms, vengeance lets the righteous rejoice over the wicked and demonstrates that they have a reward and that God judges the earth. In Psalms, God's judgment on enemies saves the afflicted, breaks weapons, silences chariots and horses, and cuts off princes' spirits. In Ecclesiastes or, The Preacher, divine judgment covers every work, including every hidden thing, whether good or evil, motivating fear of God and obedience to his commandments. In The Wisdom of Solomon, divine judgment justly punishes the idol maker with the deed and overtakes unrighteous false swearers. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, divine judgment impartially rewards everyone according to deeds and plans, bringing vengeance upon the unrighteous and mercy to God's people. In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, God's judgment is truthful and righteous, bringing wrath upon evildoers and eternal blessings to those who do good, with secrets judged by Jesus Christ. In Revelation, God's judgment comes at its appointed hour through Babylon's fall, the torment of the beast's worshippers, and the reaping and treading of God's wrath.

and 7 more works

In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, the judgment of mankind judges the wicked and righteous according to their works, excluding the filthy from God's kingdom. In The Second Book of Nephi, judgments of God fall upon those who reject the Holy One of Israel, bringing loss, scattering, affliction, and destruction according to God's word. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, judgment follows temporal death and reunites the soul and body, with all people judged by God, Christ, and the Holy Spirit according to their mortal deeds. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the judgments of God threaten to come upon the people because of governmental slothfulness and neglect, while the righteous may be slain and the wicked judged. In The Book of Helaman, judgments of God confront wicked, disbelieving Nephites and come upon them unless they repent. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, judgment is measured by people's judgment and works, and Jesus judges the world before the Father. In The Book of Ether, the Lord executes judgment against the people to their utter destruction unless they repent of their iniquities.

Judgment follows temporal death and the reuniting of soul and body. God, Christ, and the Holy Spirit conduct it at the bar of Christ the Son.
