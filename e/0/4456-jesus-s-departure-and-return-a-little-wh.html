Jesus's departure and return ('a little while')

Abstract written by a model

Jesus's departure and return, called 'a little while', is a brief span in which the disciples will not see him and then will see him again. It is tied to his going to the Father to prepare a place for them, and it benefits them by enabling the Counselor to come. In Chapter 14 he promises to return, not leave them orphans, and speaks beforehand so they believe. In Chapter 16 they misunderstand the phrase, and he explains it through their sorrow turning to joy.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 14

Jesus tells the disciples he is going to his Father to prepare a place and that he will come again to receive them. He says he will come back to them and will not leave them orphans, and that after a little while the world will no longer see him but they will. He says he has told them beforehand so that they may believe when it happens.

Chapter 16

Jesus says that in a little while the disciples will not see him, and again in a little while they will see him, linked to his going to the Father. The disciples do not understand the phrase, and Jesus, perceiving their wish to ask, explains it through their coming sorrow and joy. He later says he came from the Father into the world and now leaves the world to go to the Father.
