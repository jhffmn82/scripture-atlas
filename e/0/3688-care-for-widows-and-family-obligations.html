A topic on supporting widows and family duties. Providing for one's own household is required, and neglect denies the faith. Relatives bear first responsibility; the assembly supports only true widows, age sixty and wife of one man, while younger widows are directed to marry, keeping the assembly unburdened.

Chapter 5

Responsibility for widows falls first on their own children, grandchildren, and believing relatives, and failure to provide for one's household is called a denial of the faith. Only widows indeed, meeting the stated criteria, are supported by the assembly, and younger widows are directed toward marriage and household life instead. The aim is that the assembly not be burdened and that none be without reproach.
