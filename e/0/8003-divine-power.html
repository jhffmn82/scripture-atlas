divine power

Abstract written by a model

Divine power produces creation, sacred books, human qualities and the elements; it causes seeing, hearing, fear, happiness and affection, so everything exists by it.

Slok III .. Pauri III

Power is attributed to God and is the cause of seeing, hearing, fear, happiness, creation, affection, virtues, vices, honour, dishonour, and the elements. Everything exists by this power.
