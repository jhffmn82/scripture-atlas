Abstract written by a model

Vanghâpara, a dog and good spirit with a prickly back and long, thin muzzle, kills thousands of evil creatures nightly until sunrise. Evil-speaking people call it Duzaka; killing it incurs severe penalties unless atoned through sacrifice to Sraosha.

Fargard XIII.

The dog Vanghâpara, also called Duzaka by evil-speaking people, has a prickly back and a long, thin muzzle. It kills thousands of the evil spirit’s creatures from midnight until sunrise, and killing it brings severe penalties unless the killer atones by offering a sacrifice to Sraosha.
