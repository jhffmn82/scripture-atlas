successor (the man who comes after the narrator)

person · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a man who has not labored for it, he, the man who comes after me

Abstract written by a model

The successor is the man who will come after the narrator and rule over all his labor, though whether he will be wise or a fool is unknown. The narrator laments that labor may pass to one who did not earn it, calling this vanity and a great evil.

Chapter 2

The successor will rule over all of the narrator's labor, and the narrator does not know whether he will be wise or a fool. The narrator notes that the king's successor can only do what was done long ago, and that a man's labor may be left to one who did not work for it, which he calls vanity and a great evil.
