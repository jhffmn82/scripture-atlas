Abstract written by a model

The doctrine and faith is the highest doctrine declared for men by Mahâvîra, known and seen with quick discernment. It is a safe refuge for those in danger and opposes sin through avoidance. It begins with control, renunciation, non-killing, and true faith; continues through chastity, self-control, abandonment of worldliness, and harmless teaching; and ends in vows, restraint, tranquility, freedom from attachment, and final liberation.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Second Lecture, Called Conquest of the World. / First Lesson. .. Sixth Lesson.

The doctrine teaches the path of control, renunciation, non-killing, and liberation. The person who adopts the true faith stands in the right place, while indiscriminate preaching is discouraged.

Sixth Lecture, Called the Cleaning. / First Lesson. .. Fifth Lesson.

The law is understood and practised by those who live in chastity, control themselves, and abandon worldliness. It is taught by the noble ones as a safe refuge and should be preached without injury to living beings.

Seventh Lecture, Called Liberation. / First Lesson. .. Eighth Lesson.

The religion declared by the Revered One teaches restraint from sinful acts, adherence to vows, control of the senses, and freedom from attachment. Those who understand and practise it become tranquil, guarded against sin, and directed toward final liberation.
