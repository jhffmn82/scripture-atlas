Yue Zheng

person · 3 works · 9 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: The disciple Yue Zheng, Yo-chAng, officer Yue Zheng, the disciple Yue Zheng

Abstract written by a model

In Mencius, Book I (Liang Hui Wang), officer Yue Zheng questions duke Ping of Lu about not visiting Meng Ke, explains that the differences in Meng Ke’s second mourning arose from poverty and wealth, and informs Meng Ke that Zang Cang prevented the duke’s visit. In Mencius, Book IV (Li Lou), Yue Zheng travels with Zi Ao to Qi, visits Mencius with his lodging-house unarranged, admits wrongdoing, and is criticized for following Zi Ao for food and drink despite learning the doctrine of the ancients. In Mencius, Book VI (Gaozi), Yue Zheng is described as lacking vigour, wisdom in council, and extensive information but loving what is good, a quality Mencius says is sufficient for governing Lu because it attracts good men and excludes calumniators, flatterers, and sycophants.
