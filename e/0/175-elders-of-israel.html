Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, seventy elders of Israel worship from a distance, go up with Moses and the others, see the God of Israel unharmed, and eat and drink. In Numbers, seventy elders of Israel are appointed by the LORD to share the people's burden and receive the Spirit taken from Moses. In The Book of Ezekiel, elders of Israel repeatedly inquire of the LORD, but he rejects their inquiry because they hold idols in their hearts.

Abstract written by a model

Elders of Israel repeatedly come and sit before the prophet to inquire of the LORD. In Chapter 14 the LORD says they hold idols in their hearts and questions being inquired of by them; in Chapter 20 he rejects their inquiry, swearing he will not be inquired of.
