Abstract written by a model

Three Jews holding appointed positions in Babylon who serve the God of Israel. Accused of refusing the king's gods and the golden image, they declared God could deliver them, yet would refuse regardless. Thrown into the furnace, they walked unharmed praising God, emerged untouched, and Nebuchadnezzar promoted them.

Chapter 3

Accused by Chaldeans of not serving the king's gods or worshiping the image, the three told Nebuchadnezzar that their God is able to deliver them, and that even if not, they would not worship the image. They were bound and thrown into the furnace, walked in the fire praising God, and sang a hymn of blessing together. They came out with no singed hair, unchanged clothing, and no smell of fire, and the king promoted them in the province of Babylon.
