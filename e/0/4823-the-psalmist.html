Chapter 94

The psalmist cries out to the LORD for vengeance and asks who will stand up for him against the wicked. He says that without the LORD's help his soul would soon have lived in silence, and that when his foot slipped the LORD's loving kindness held him up. He finds delight in the LORD's comforts and calls the LORD his high tower and rock of refuge.
