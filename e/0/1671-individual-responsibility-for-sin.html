individual responsibility for sin

Also called: The son shall not bear the iniquity of the father, The soul who sins, he shall die, iniquity

Abstract written by a model

Individual responsibility for sin holds that the soul who sins shall die, while son and father do not bear each other's iniquity. Righteousness and wickedness rest on each person alone, and the Lord GOD defends this as fair against Israel's complaints.

Chapter 18

The chapter states that the soul who sins shall die, and that the son shall not bear the father's iniquity nor the father the son's. The righteousness of the righteous and the wickedness of the wicked are each on that person alone. The Lord GOD defends this as equal and fair against Israel's complaints.
