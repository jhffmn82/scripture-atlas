Abstract written by a model

Idol makers are declared vain: their delights do not profit and their witnesses neither see nor know. The workmen are mere men, so they and their fellows will be disappointed, confounded and put to shame, with shut eyes and hearts, feeding on ashes, unable to name the lie.

Chapter 44

Idol makers are declared vain; their delights do not profit, and their witnesses neither see nor know. The LORD says they and their fellows will be disappointed and put to shame, since the workmen are mere men. Their eyes and hearts are shut so they cannot understand, and one feeds on ashes and is unable to say there is a lie in his right hand.
