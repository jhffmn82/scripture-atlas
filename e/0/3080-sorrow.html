Abstract written by a model

In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, sorrow is warned against as a harmful emotion that destroys many and brings no profit. In The Second Book of Nephi, sorrow is received from the Lord by those who kindle their own fire and walk by their sparks, and they lie down in darkness. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, sorrow arises from death, destruction, and lost kindred, unfolding through solemn sorrowful days, fasting, and prayer.

sorrow in The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where sorrow first appears

Abstract written by a model

Sorrow is a harmful emotion the reader is warned against: it has destroyed many and brings no profit, so one should not surrender to it and should remove it far away, like envy, wrath, and anxiety.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 30

The reader is told not to give his soul to sorrow or afflict himself deliberately. Sorrow is said to have destroyed many and to bring no profit, so it should be removed far away. Envy, wrath, and anxiety are likewise said to shorten life and bring old age before its time.
