Chapter 8

In Capernaum a centurion asks Jesus for help for his paralyzed, tormented servant, and says he is not worthy to have Jesus come under his roof, since a word will suffice, as he himself is a man under authority. Jesus marvels, says he has not found such great faith even in Israel, and tells him it will be done as he believed, and the servant is healed in that hour.
