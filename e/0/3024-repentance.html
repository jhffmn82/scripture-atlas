Abstract written by a model

In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, repentance and mercy involve the Lord granting a return to those who repent, comforting the hopeless, urging people to turn back, forsake sins, and pray with fewer offenses, and showing great mercy and forgiveness. In Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba), repentance is better than aversion and benefits idolaters and those turning back from disbelief and hypocrisy; it accompanies worship and payment of the poor-due, and Allah accepts it, may relent, or may pardon mixed actions.

Abstract written by a model

The topic concerns repentance and mercy: the Lord grants a return to those who repent and comforts the hopeless. Chapter 17 urges turning back, forsaking sins, and praying with fewer offenses, marvels at his great mercy and forgiveness, and notes only the living, not the dead, can praise him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 17

The Lord grants a return to those who repent and comforts those who are losing hope. The text calls people to return to the Lord, forsake sins and pray with fewer offenses, and exclaims over the greatness of his mercy and forgiveness to those who turn to him. It adds that the living, not the dead in Hades, are the ones who can give thanks and praise him.

Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba)
