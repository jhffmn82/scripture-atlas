Beth Shemesh

place · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Samuel, Beth Shemesh is the border town toward which the cows head, where the ark arrives at the field of Joshua, where a great stone remains, and which suffers a great slaughter before sending messengers to Kiriath Jearim. In The Second Book of Kings, Beth Shemesh is where Jehoash and Amaziah met face to face, Judah was defeated, and Jehoash took Amaziah captive. In The Second Book of Chronicles, Beth Shemesh is where Joash and Amaziah met face to face, Judah was defeated, and Joash took Amaziah captive.
