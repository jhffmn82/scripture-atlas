Japheth in Genesis

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Japheth first appears

Abstract written by a model

Japheth is one of Noah's three sons. He and Shem cover their father with a garment, and Noah blesses him to be enlarged, dwell in Shem's tents, and have Canaan as servant. He fathers seven sons, including Gomer and Javan, whose descendants divide the islands of the nations by language, family and nation.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 9

Japheth is named as one of Noah's sons who left the ship. With Shem, he covers his father with a garment while facing backwards, and Noah says God will enlarge Japheth, who will dwell in the tents of Shem, with Canaan as his servant.

Chapter 10

Japheth's sons were Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras. Gomer's sons and Javan's sons are also listed, and from these the islands of the nations were divided in their lands, each by language, family, and nation.
