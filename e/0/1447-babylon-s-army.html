Abstract written by a model

Babylon's army is a vast invading force under Nebuchadnezzar. It attacks Jerusalem and Judah's remaining cities, withdraws, then is commanded by the LORD to return, take and burn the city. It besieges Jerusalem, breaches it, and its princes sit in the middle gate. It is later portrayed as innumerable northern invaders delivering Egypt into its hand.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 34

The army is fighting against Jerusalem and the remaining cities of Judah, including Lachish and Azekah. It has gone away from the city, but the LORD says he will command it to return, fight against Jerusalem, take it, and burn it with fire.

Chapter 39

Nebuchadnezzar's whole army came against Jerusalem and besieged it. After the breach, the Babylonian princes, including Nergal Sharezer, Samgarnebo, and Sarsechim the Rabsaris, entered and sat in the middle gate.

Chapter 46

The invaders from the north are described as marching with axes like woodcutters and cutting down Egypt's forest, being more numerous than locusts and innumerable. Their sound is likened to a serpent, and they are the people of the north into whose hand Egypt is delivered.
