Abstract written by a model

In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, righteousness appears when the Great Tao ceases, is highest when its possessors seek to carry it out, and is lost before propriety. In The Analects of Confucius, Book IV (Le Jin), righteousness occupies the superior man's mind, leading him to follow what is right rather than pursue gain.

righteousness in The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics

Lao-Tse · the work’s page · read it where righteousness first appears

Abstract written by a model

Righteousness is a quality that comes into vogue when the Great Tao ceases and appears when benevolence is lost. In its highest form, its possessors always seek to carry it out. It is lost before propriety, after people become filial and kindly.
