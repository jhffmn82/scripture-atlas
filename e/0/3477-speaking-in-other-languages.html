Abstract written by a model

Speaking in another language is directed to God, edifying only the speaker unless interpreted. It is a sign for unbelievers, limited to two or three speakers in turn with an interpreter, silent without one, and not forbidden.

Chapter 14

Speaking in another language is described as speaking mysteries to God in the Spirit and edifying only oneself unless it is interpreted. It is a sign to unbelievers, is to be limited to two or three speakers in turn with an interpreter, and is not to be forbidden.
