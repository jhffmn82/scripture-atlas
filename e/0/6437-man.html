man

person · 3 works · 13 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: O man, he

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 82: The Cleaving (Al-Infitaar), man is questioned about what made him careless concerning his Lord and is created, fashioned, proportioned, and cast into whatever form He willed. In Surah 84: The Splitting Open (Al-Inshiqaaq), man works toward his Lord a work that he will meet in His presence, is accounted according to what he is given, and journeys from plane to plane. In Surah 100: The Chargers (Al-Aadiyaat), man is an ingrate unto his Lord, is a witness unto that, and is violent in the love of wealth.
