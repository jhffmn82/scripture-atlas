A centurion who commands soldiers and loves his dear servant. Admired for loving the Jewish nation and building their synagogue, he sends elders and friends to ask Jesus to heal the servant, declaring himself unworthy. Recognizing authority, he trusts Jesus's word, and Jesus praises his faith as unmatched in Israel.

Chapter 7

The centurion, whose servant was dear to him, sent Jewish elders and then friends to ask Jesus to heal the servant, saying he was not worthy to have Jesus come under his roof. He explained that, as a man under authority, he knew Jesus need only say the word, and Jesus said he had not found such great faith, not even in Israel. The elders said he loved the nation and had built their synagogue.
