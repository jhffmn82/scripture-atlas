coming destruction

Abstract written by a model

A destruction coming from the north country, caused by the people's refusal to listen, is directed at Jerusalem. It begins as a siege that cuts off the daughter of Zion and ruins her palaces, striking every age and relationship. The people respond with terror, sackcloth, ashes and mourning; later news announces it will make Judah's cities a desolation, a dwelling place of jackals.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Destruction looks out from the north, with siege against Jerusalem, the cutting off of the daughter of Zion, and the ruin of her palaces. It will fall on children, young men, husbands, wives, and the aged, and on fathers, sons, neighbors, and friends. The people respond with terror, sackcloth, and ashes, mourning as for an only son, since the destroyer will suddenly come.

Chapter 10

News is announced of a great commotion coming out of the north country. It is said to be aimed at making the cities of Judah a desolation and a dwelling place of jackals.
