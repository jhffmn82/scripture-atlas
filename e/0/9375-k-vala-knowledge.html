Kêvala knowledge

Also called: Kêvala, Kêvala (knowledge), full Kêvala knowledge, highest knowledge and faith, called Kêvala

Abstract written by a model

Kêvala knowledge is the highest, complete, unobstructed knowledge and faith. When full, it knows all things. Monks attain it while dying before absolute perfection and liberation; through meditation and teaching the whole Law, its possessors save themselves and others.

Kêvala knowledge is in one work on this site, Sutrakritanga, Book II. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Now the explanation of the second subject, viz. merit, is as follows:

Kêvala is the highest knowledge and faith, described as infinite, supreme, unobstructed, unimpeded, complete, and full. Monks attain it while breathing their last before obtaining absolute perfection and liberation.

Sixth Lecture, Called .

Kêvala knowledge is described as full knowledge of all things. Those who possess it, practise meditation, and teach the whole Law are said to save themselves and others.
