Chapter 15

If the whole congregation errs unwittingly, it offers a young bull as a burnt offering with its meal and drink offerings, plus a male goat as a sin offering, and the priest makes atonement so they are forgiven. An individual who sins unwittingly offers a female goat a year old and is likewise forgiven, while one who acts with a high hand despises the LORD's word and is utterly cut off.
