superior man

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Superior men, a superior man

Abstract written by a model

The superior man progresses upwards, is modest in speech but exceeds in action, and does not concern himself with plans outside his office. He is described as virtuous, wise, and bold, and as cultivating himself so as to give rest to others and all the people.
