Abstract written by a model

The ashes of the heifer are a sin offering, gathered by a clean man who becomes unclean until evening. They are stored in a clean place outside the camp and kept for the Israelite congregation, to be mixed with running water for cleansing impurity.

Chapter 19

A clean man gathers the ashes and lays them up in a clean place outside the camp, and they are called a sin offering. They are kept for use in water for cleansing impurity, and the one who gathers them is unclean until evening.
