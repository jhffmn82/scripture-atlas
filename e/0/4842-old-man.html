old man

Abstract written by a model

The old man criticizes Tsze-lu for being unaccustomed to toil and unable to distinguish the five kinds of grain. He hosts Tsze-lu overnight, kills a fowl, prepares millet, feasts him, and introduces him to his two sons; he is gone when Tsze-lu returns.
