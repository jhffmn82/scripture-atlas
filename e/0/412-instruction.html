Abstract written by a model

Instruction, which comes from the LORD and is not to be despised, is a life-or-death matter. Chapter 5 shows a son who hated instruction and regrets it at his end, and the wicked die for lacking it. Chapter 15 adds that the prudent and wise heed reproof and live, while scoffers and fools who refuse it face death and gain no understanding.

Chapter 5

Instruction is something the son is urged to heed, and its rejection is the source of the regret he is pictured voicing at his end: he hated instruction, despised reproof, and did not obey his teachers. The chapter closes by saying the wicked man will die for lack of instruction and go astray in the greatness of his folly.

Chapter 15

Reproof is heeded by the prudent and the wise, and the ear that listens to it lives and is at home among the wise. Those who hate or refuse it, such as scoffers and fools, face death, despise their own souls, and gain no understanding.
