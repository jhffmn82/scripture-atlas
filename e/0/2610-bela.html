Bela

Also called: Bela the son of Azaz

Abstract written by a model

Bela is Benjamin's firstborn son, a head of his father's house. Records list his many sons, including Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, Iri, Addar, Gera, Abihud, Abishua, Naaman, Ahoah, Shephuphan and Huram.
