Jerusalem's prostitution (idolatry)

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: prostitution, your abominations, your lewdness

Abstract written by a model

Jerusalem's prostitution is a metaphor for idolatry and unfaithfulness: she built high places on every street, offered herself to Egyptians, Assyrians, and Chaldeans, paid her lovers, was insatiable, and sacrificed sons and daughters through fire. Her filthiness and idols ground the judgment.

Chapter 16

Jerusalem's prostitution is depicted as idolatry and unfaithfulness, in which she built lofty places at every street, opened her feet to every passerby, and sought out Egyptians, Assyrians, and Chaldeans. She was insatiable and unlike ordinary prostitutes because she paid her lovers rather than being paid. It also included child sacrifice, and her filthiness and idols are given as grounds for judgment.
