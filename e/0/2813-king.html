Chapter 10

A land is called woeful when its king is a child and its princes eat in the morning, and happy when its king is the son of nobles and its princes eat in due season, for strength and not drunkenness. The text warns against cursing the king, even in one's thoughts, because a bird of the sky may carry the voice and tell the matter.
