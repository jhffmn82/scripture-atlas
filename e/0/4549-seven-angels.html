Abstract written by a model

Seven angels carry the seven last plagues. Dressed in pure, bright linen with golden sashes, they emerge from the opened heavenly temple, and one of the four living creatures gives them golden bowls full of God's wrath. Commanded by a loud voice from the temple, each pours out his bowl in turn on the earth, sea, rivers and springs, sun, the beast's throne, the Euphrates, and the air.

Chapter 15

The seven angels are seen in the sky as a great and marvelous sign, holding the seven last plagues. They come out of the opened heavenly temple clothed in pure, bright linen with golden sashes around their chests, and one of the four living creatures gives them seven golden bowls full of God's wrath.

Chapter 16

The seven angels are commanded by a loud voice from the temple to pour out the seven bowls of God's wrath on the earth. Each in turn pours out his bowl, on the earth, sea, rivers and springs, sun, the beast's throne, the Euphrates, and the air.
