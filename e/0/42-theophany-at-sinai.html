Abstract written by a model

The theophany at Sinai is the LORD's promised descent upon Mount Sinai on the third day, intended so the people would hear him speak with Moses and believe Moses forever. Announced beforehand, it arrives that morning with thunders, lightnings, thick cloud, loud trumpet, fire, smoke and a quaking mountain. The people tremble; God answers Moses by voice, while bounds and warnings keep people and priests from breaking through.

Chapter 19

The LORD announces he will come to Moses in a thick cloud so the people may hear him speak and believe Moses forever, and that he will come down in the sight of all the people on the third day. On that morning thunders, lightnings, a thick cloud, an exceedingly loud trumpet, fire, smoke and a quaking mountain accompany his descent, and the people tremble. God answers Moses by a voice, and strict bounds and warnings keep the people and priests from breaking through to gaze.
