Chapter 2

Believers are told to subject themselves to every human ordinance for the Lord's sake, whether to the king as supreme or to governors sent to punish evildoers and praise those who do well. This is God's will, since by well-doing they put to silence the ignorance of foolish men, and their freedom must not be used as a cloak for wickedness. Servants are told to submit to their masters with all respect, including the wicked ones, and all are to honor all men and the king.
