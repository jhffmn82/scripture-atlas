Chapter 5

The wife, whose husband suspects her of unfaithfulness whether or not she is actually defiled, is brought by him to the priest. She takes the oath, says "Amen, Amen," and drinks the water of bitterness. If guilty she will swell, her thigh will fall away, and she will be a curse among her people; if clean she will be free and conceive offspring. She bears her iniquity.
