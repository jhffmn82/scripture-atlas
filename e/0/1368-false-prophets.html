Abstract written by a model

False prophets are those the LORD never sent, commanded, or spoke to. They prophesy lying visions, divination, and deceit of their own heart, assuring the people of peace and no sword or famine. The LORD decrees they will themselves be consumed by sword and famine.

Chapter 14

The prophets in the land tell the people they will not see sword or famine but will have assured peace. The LORD says he did not send, command, or speak to them, and that they prophesy lying visions, divination, and the deceit of their own heart. They will be consumed by sword and famine, and the prophet and priest are said to go about the land without knowledge.
