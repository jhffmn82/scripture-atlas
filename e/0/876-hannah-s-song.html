is_spoken_by Hannah [prayer] Hannah's song exalts the LORD as holy, a rock, and a God of knowledge who weighs actions. It describes reversals in which the mighty are broken, the hungry are satisfied, the barren bears seven, and the poor are lifted from the dust. It ends by declaring that the LORD will judge the ends of the earth and strengthen his king and anointed.
