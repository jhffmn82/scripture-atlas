Abstract written by a model

The book of the covenant, also called the book of the law, was found by Hilkiah in the LORD's house. Shaphan read it before the king, and Josiah tore his clothes, recognizing the LORD's wrath because their fathers disobeyed it. Huldah confirmed its evil would come. Josiah then read it to all the people, made a covenant, kept the Passover, and removed idols as it commanded.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 22

The book of the law is found by Hilkiah in the LORD's house and read by Shaphan, first to himself and then before the king. Its words cause Josiah to tear his clothes and conclude that the LORD's wrath is kindled because their fathers did not obey what it says. Huldah confirms the evil in it will come on the place.

Chapter 23

The book of the covenant, found in the LORD's house, is read aloud by the king to all the people. Its words are confirmed by the covenant Josiah makes, and the Passover is kept as it is written in it. Josiah's removal of spirit-mediums, wizards, teraphim, and idols is also done to confirm the law written in the book Hilkiah found.
