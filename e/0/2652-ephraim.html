Ephraim in Genesis

Abstract written by a model

Ephraim, whose name means God has made me fruitful, is Joseph's younger son born in Egypt. Jacob claims him as his own, places his right hand on him, and sets him before Manasseh, foretelling greater offspring and a multitude of nations.

Chapter 48

Ephraim is Joseph's younger son, born in Egypt, whom Jacob claims as his own like Reuben and Simeon. Jacob lays his right hand on Ephraim's head, saying his offspring will become a multitude of nations and that he will be greater than Manasseh, and sets him before Manasseh.

Ephraim in 1 Chronicles

author unknown · 449 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Ephraim first appears

Abstract written by a model

Ephraim is a patriarch whose sons include Shuthelah, Ezer, Elead, and later Beriah. Ezer and Elead were killed by the men of Gath over livestock, and Ephraim mourned while his brothers comforted him. Beriah was born afterward. His line continues to Nun and Joshua, and his children settled Bethel, Gezer, Shechem, and other places.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

Ephraim's sons are traced through Shuthelah, Bered, Tahath, and others. Two of them, Ezer and Elead, were killed by the men of Gath when they came down to take livestock, and Ephraim mourned many days while his brothers came to comfort him. His line is followed down to Nun and Joshua, and his children lived in Bethel, Gezer, Shechem, and other listed places.
