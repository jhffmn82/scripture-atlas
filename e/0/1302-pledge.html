Chapter 24

Pledges are regulated: no one may take a mill or upper millstone because it takes a life in pledge, and a widow's clothing may not be taken. A lender may not enter the borrower's house to get it, and must wait outside. A poor man's pledge garment must be returned by sundown so he can sleep in it and bless the lender, which counts as righteousness.
