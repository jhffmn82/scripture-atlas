Abstract written by a model

Paul's planned itinerary: visit Corinth first, go on to Macedonia, return to Corinth, then be sent by the Corinthians to Judea. He changed the plan and did not come to Corinth, saying this was to spare them. He denies fickleness or fleshly planning, insisting his word was not 'Yes and no.'

Paul’s Second Letter to the Corinthians / Chapter 1

Paul had planned to come to the Corinthians first, pass on to Macedonia, return to them from Macedonia, and be sent on by them to Judea. He denies that changing these plans showed fickleness or fleshly planning, and says his word to them was not "Yes and no." He states he did not come to Corinth in order to spare them.
