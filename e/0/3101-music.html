Abstract written by a model

In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, music should not be interrupted by talk during performance, and concerts and melody are likened to ruby and emerald signets in gold at a pleasant wine banquet. In The Doctrine of the Mean, music, like ceremonies, may be made only by one who has proper virtue and occupies the throne.
