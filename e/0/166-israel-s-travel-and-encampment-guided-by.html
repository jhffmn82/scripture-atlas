Abstract written by a model

Israel's wilderness movement is governed by the LORD's commandment through the cloud over the tabernacle: they travel when it lifts and encamp where it remains, for a night, days, a month or a year, obediently keeping the LORD's command.

Chapter 9

Israel travels when the cloud is taken up and encamps where it remains, doing both at the LORD's commandment. The length of each stay varies, from a night to many days, a month or a year, yet Israel remains encamped as long as the cloud stays and does not travel. This obedience is described as keeping the LORD's command by Moses.
