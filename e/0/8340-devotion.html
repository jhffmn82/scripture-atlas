Abstract written by a model

In Hymns of Guru Nanak, devotion is a penance and purification of the heart requiring the Name and good acts, unfolding through remembering and praising God, serving and accepting the Guru, and practising truth, contentment, mercy, and faith to cleanse the heart and lead through the world’s dangerous ocean to God’s favour and salvation. In Hymns of Guru Nanak, God's service is required for happiness and gained through the true Guru's favour; through loving service, prayer, repeating God's name, and good works, a person crosses the ocean of the world.

devotion in Hymns of Guru Nanak

Guru Nanak · the work’s page · read it where devotion first appears

Abstract written by a model

Devotion is a penance and purification of the heart that requires the Name and good acts. It unfolds through remembering and praising God, serving and accepting the Guru, and practising truth, contentment, mercy, and faith, cleansing the heart and leading through the world’s dangerous ocean to God’s favour and salvation.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Rag Asa .. Asa Ashtapadi

Devotion consists in remembering and praising God, serving the Guru, accepting the Guru’s instruction, and practising truth, contentment, mercy, and faith. It enables people to cross the world’s dangerous ocean and obtain God’s favour and salvation.

Rag Gujari .. Gujari Ashtapadi

Worship of God is performed by meditating on the Name, cleansing the heart, and mixing good acts with devotion. Without the Name, worship is said not to exist, while sincere devotion leads toward purity and deliverance.
