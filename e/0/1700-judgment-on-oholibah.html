Abstract written by a model

A judgment issued by the LORD against Oholibah, carried out by her alienated lovers, the Babylonians, Chaldeans, and Assyrians, who come against her on every side. They strip her, mutilate her, take her children, let the remnant fall by sword, burn the rest, and a mob stones her, ending her lewdness so she knows the Lord GOD.

Chapter 23

The LORD will bring her alienated lovers, the Babylonians, Chaldeans, and Assyrians, against her on every side and commit the judgment to them. They will strip her clothes and jewels, take her labor, cut off her nose and ears, take her children, let her remnant fall by the sword, and burn the rest, and a mob will also stone her, so that she knows he is the Lord GOD.
