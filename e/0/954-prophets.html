Chapter 3

The prophets lead the LORD's people astray, proclaiming "Peace!" to those who feed them and preparing war against those who do not. Their punishment is night with no vision, darkness, and the sun going down on them. The seers and diviners will be disappointed and confounded and will cover their lips because God gives no answer. Her prophets are also said to tell fortunes for money while claiming the LORD is among them.
