Abstract written by a model

Ten identical bronze bases made by Hiram, each four cubits long and wide and three high, cast in one casting, measure, and form. Panels show lions, oxen, and cherubim; four bronze wheels like chariot wheels support each. Each carried a forty-bath basin, and five stood on each side of the house.

Chapter 7

Hiram made ten bronze bases, each four cubits long and wide and three cubits high, with panels showing lions, oxen, and cherubim, four bronze wheels like chariot wheels, and corner supports. All were cast in one casting, one measure, and one form. Each carried a bronze basin of forty baths, and five bases were set on each side of the house.
