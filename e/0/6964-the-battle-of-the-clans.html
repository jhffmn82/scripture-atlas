Abstract written by a model

The Battle of the Clans is described as a time when hosts came against the believers, who were frightened, tested, and shaken. Allah aided the believers with wind and unseen hosts, exposed the hypocrites, repulsed the disbelievers, and granted the believers victory and spoils.
