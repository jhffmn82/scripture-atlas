CHAP. XVII. Chi K'ang asked Confucius about government.

Government is described as rectification. Leading the people with correctness is presented as causing them to be correct.

CHAP. XIX. Chi K'ang asked Confucius about government,

Government should be carried on without using killing. Its leaders should show desires for what is good.
