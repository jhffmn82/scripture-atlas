In The Gospel According to Matthew, repentance is proclaimed by John, who calls for fruit worthy of repentance and administers water baptism for repentance. In The Book of Moroni, repentance is required for sin and return unto God, witnessed before baptism and bringing remission of sins and spiritual blessings.

repentance in The Book of Moroni

Moroni · the work’s page · read it where repentance first appears

Abstract written by a model

Repentance is required for sin and for return unto God, especially for those under condemnation. It is witnessed before baptism; its first fruit is baptism following faith. Through commandments it brings remission of sins, then meekness, the Holy Ghost, hope, and perfect love. Those committing iniquity must repent and confess to be forgiven; without repentance, people perish.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Repentance is witnessed before baptism, and those who commit iniquity must repent and confess or have their names blotted out. Those who repent and seek forgiveness with real intent are forgiven.

Chapter 8

Repentance is for those under condemnation and the curse of a broken law. Its first fruits are baptism, and it leads through remission of sins to meekness, the visitation of the Holy Ghost, hope, and perfect love.

Chapter 9

The people do not repent, and the writer fears that the Spirit of the Lord has ceased striving with them. He says they will perish except they repent and return unto God.
