Chapter 17

Ahithophel's counsel was to pursue David that night with twelve thousand men, come on him weary, strike only the king, and bring the people back, which pleased Absalom and the elders. Nevertheless the LORD had ordained that this good counsel be defeated so that evil would come on Absalom, and it was not followed.
