Coniah

person · 2 works · 12 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Coniah the son of Jehoiakim, Coniah the son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, Jeconiah, this man Coniah

Abstract written by a model

In 1 Chronicles, Jeconiah, son of Jehoiakim and called the captive, is listed as father of seven sons, including Shealtiel and Pedaiah. In The Book of Jeremiah, Coniah is a king of Judah, son of Jehoiakim, made king by Nebuchadnezzar, pronounced doomed, cast out with his mother to die in a foreign land, never returning home, and recorded as childless with no offspring prospering on David's throne.

Abstract written by a model

Coniah is a king of Judah, son of Jehoiakim, made king by Nebuchadnezzar. He is pronounced doomed: given into the hands of those seeking his life, cast out with his mother to die in a foreign land, never returning home, and recorded as childless, with no offspring prospering on David's throne.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 22

Coniah, son of Jehoiakim, is declared to be handed over to those seeking his life, including Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and the Chaldeans. He and his mother will be cast into a foreign land to die and never return, and he is to be recorded as childless, with none of his offspring prospering on David's throne.
