Shunammite woman

Abstract written by a model

A prominent woman of Shunem, married to an old husband, who regards Elisha as a holy man of God. She hosts him and suggests building him a roof room. Though doubtful, she bears the son he promises. When the boy dies she lays him on Elisha's bed, rides to Carmel, clings to Elisha's feet, and receives her revived son. Obeying Elisha, she lives seven years in the Philistines' land, then petitions the king, who restores her house, land, and produce.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

The prominent woman of Shunem persuades Elisha to eat at her home and suggests building him a room on the roof. After Elisha promises her a son, which she doubts, she bears him. When the boy dies she lays him on Elisha's bed, rides to Mount Carmel, seizes Elisha's feet, vows not to leave him, and finally bows and takes up her revived son.

Chapter 8

The woman obeys Elisha's word and lives in the land of the Philistines for seven years. On returning she begs the king for her house and land, and the king appoints an officer to restore all that was hers along with the produce of the fields since she left.
