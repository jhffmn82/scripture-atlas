Chapter 3

King Seleucus of Asia is said to have borne all the costs of the sacrificial services from his own revenues. After Apollonius informed him of the treasury's money, he appointed Heliodorus to remove it. Later he asked Heliodorus whom he should send to Jerusalem once more, and received a warning about God's power there.
