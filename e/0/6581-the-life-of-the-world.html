the life of the world

topic · 3 works · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: life, the life of the world is but matter of illusion, the world

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 29: The Spider (Al-Ankaboot), the world is described as the limited love between idolaters and as pastime and a game, while the home of the Hereafter is the true Life. In Surah 43: Ornaments of gold (Az-Zukhruf), the life of the world includes apportioned livelihood, social rank, wealth, and gold adornments, which are temporary provision compared with the Hereafter. In Surah 57: The Iron (Al-Hadid), the life of the world is described as play, idle talk, pageantry, boasting, and rivalry in wealth and children, with temporary and illusory attractions.
