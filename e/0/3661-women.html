women in 1 Timothy

Paul · the work’s page · read it where women first appears

Abstract written by a model

Women are instructed in Chapter 2 to adorn themselves with decent, modest clothing and good works befitting godliness, to learn quietly in full submission, and are said to be saved through childbearing if they continue in faith, love, holiness, and sobriety.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Women are told to adorn themselves in decent clothing with modesty and propriety, and with good works fitting for those professing godliness. They are to learn in quietness with full submission. The text says the woman will be saved through childbearing if they continue in faith, love, and holiness with sobriety.
