Abstract written by a model

Hoshea, son of Elah, was a king of Israel who seized the throne by conspiring against and killing Pekah, then reigned nine years in Samaria. He did evil, though less than earlier kings. He served Shalmaneser of Assyria and paid tribute, but sought help from Egypt's king So and withheld tribute, so Assyria's king seized and imprisoned him.

Hoshea is in one work on this site, The Second Book of Kings. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 15

Hoshea son of Elah conspired against Pekah son of Remaliah, attacked and killed him, and reigned in his place. This happened in the twentieth year of Jotham.

Chapter 17

Hoshea began to reign over Israel in Samaria in the twelfth year of Ahaz of Judah and ruled nine years, doing evil but not as badly as earlier kings. He became Shalmaneser's servant and paid tribute, but then sent messengers to So king of Egypt and stopped the tribute. The king of Assyria seized him and bound him in prison.
