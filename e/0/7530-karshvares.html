Karshvares

Also called: all the Karshvares, all the seven Karshvares, seven Karshvares, seven Karshvares of the earth, the seven Karshvares, the seven Karshvares of the earth

Abstract written by a model

In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, it comprises seven regions receiving Ardvi Sûra Anâhita’s waters and containing all things, which Azi Dahâka seeks unsuccessfully to empty of men. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, it comprises seven regions and divisions of the earth ruled by kings possessing Glory, which Frangrasyan attempts to seize so he can rule them all.

V. Âbân Yast.

The seven Karshvares are the regions over which the waters of Ardvi Sûra Anâhita spread. Azi Dahâka asks for a boon that would make all seven empty of men, but Ardvi Sûra Anâhita refuses it.
