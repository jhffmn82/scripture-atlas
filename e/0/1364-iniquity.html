iniquity

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: The iniquity of the house of Israel and Judah, abominations, abominations and iniquity, bloody crimes, detestable things, iniquities, perversion, rebellions, sins of the people, the iniquity of our fathers, their abominations, violence, wickedness, your abominations

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Jeremiah, it represents the people's iniquities and rebellions that testify against them and are remembered and punished by the LORD. In The Book of Ezekiel, it represents the people's abominations brought upon them as judgment, making the ornament of beauty unclean.
