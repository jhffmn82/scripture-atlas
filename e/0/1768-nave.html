Abstract written by a model

The nave is a temple chamber forty cubits long and twenty wide, with a ten-cubit entrance, six-cubit posts on each side, and squared door posts. Its face resembles the temple's; its doors bear cherubim and palm trees, with a wooden threshold on the porch outside.

Chapter 41

The guide measures the nave's posts at six cubits on each side, and its entrance at ten cubits wide, with a length of forty cubits and a width of twenty cubits. Its door posts are squared, and its face looks like that of the temple. Its doors carry cherubim and palm trees like those on the walls, with a wooden threshold on the porch's face outside.
