Abstract written by a model

The prince employs an officer to receive a visitor; after the visitor retires, the officer reports that the visitor no longer turns round. A vacant place in the prince’s palace causes passing people to change countenance, bend their legs, and strain their speech. The prince sends an unnamed attendant cooked meat, undressed meat, and a living animal, sacrifices during entertainment, visits him when ill, and summons him by order.

CHAP. III. 1. When the prince called him to employ him in the

The prince calls the officer to employ him in receiving a visitor. After the visitor retires, the officer reports to the prince that the visitor is not turning round any more.

CHAP. IV. 1. When he entered the palace gate, he seemed to

The prince has a vacant place that causes the person passing it to show a changed countenance, bending legs, and strained speech.

CHAP. XIII. 1. When the prince sent him a gift of cooked meat,

The prince sends the attendant gifts of cooked meat, undressed meat, and a living animal. He sacrifices when the attendant joins him in an entertainment, visits the attendant when he is ill, and summons him by order.
