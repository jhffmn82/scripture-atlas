Abstract written by a model

The elders of the Jews are builders of God's house at Jerusalem. Watched over by God, they are not stopped; they answer Tattenai, citing Cyrus's decree. Authorized and funded from the king's goods, they prosper through Haggai's and Zechariah's prophesying and finish the temple.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 5

The elders are watched over by God's eye, so they are not made to stop building. They answer Tattenai that they are servants of the God of heaven and earth, recount the temple's destruction and Cyrus's decree, and give their names as requested.

Chapter 6

The elders are authorized to build the house in its place and are to receive expenses from the king's goods. They build and prosper through the prophesying of Haggai and Zechariah the son of Iddo, and finish the house.
