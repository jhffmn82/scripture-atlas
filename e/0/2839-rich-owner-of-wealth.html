Abstract written by a model

A rich man whose abundance denies him sleep, unlike the laborer. His only benefit is feasting his eyes; riches may perish, leaving his son nothing, and he departs naked, eating in darkness, frustrated and wrathful, unless God grants enjoyment.

Chapter 5

The rich man cannot sleep because of his abundance, unlike the laboring man. His riches may perish by misfortune, leaving nothing in his hand for a son, and he leaves naked as he came, eating in darkness with frustration, sickness and wrath. If God gives him the power to enjoy his riches, however, that is God's gift.
