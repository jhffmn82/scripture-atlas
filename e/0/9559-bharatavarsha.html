Also called: ocean-girt Bharatavarsha, sea-girt Bharatavarsha

Abstract written by a model

In the Uttaradhyayana Sutra, Bharatavarsha is an ocean- or earth-girt kingdom abandoned by rulers who renounce worldly life. In the Kalpa Sutra, Bharatavarsha is the region within Gambûdvîpa where Kundagrâma is located and where Mahâvîra takes the form of an embryo.

Eighteenth Lecture.

Bharatavarsha is described as a kingdom or earth-girt realm abandoned by Bharata, Sagara, Maghavan, Sanatkumâra, Sânti, Kunthu, Ara, and others when they renounced worldly life.
