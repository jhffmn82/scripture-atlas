Chapter 30

A young Egyptian, servant to an Amalekite, is found in the field and given bread, water, a piece of fig cake, and two clusters of raisins, reviving him after three days and nights without food or drink. He explains that his master left him when he got sick three days earlier, and describes the raid including the burning of Ziklag. After David swears by God not to kill him or return him to his master, he leads David down to the Amalekite camp.
