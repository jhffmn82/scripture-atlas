skilful commander

Also called: A skilful (commander), commander

Abstract written by a model

A skilful commander assists by striking a decisive blow and then stopping, refusing to continue operations to assert mastery and guarding against vanity, boastfulness, and arrogance.

30. 1. He who would assist a lord of men in harmony with the Tao will

The skilful commander strikes a decisive blow and stops. He does not continue operations to assert and complete mastery, and he remains on guard against vanity, boastfulness, and arrogance.
