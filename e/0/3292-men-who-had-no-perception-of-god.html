men who had no perception of God

Abstract written by a model

Men with no perception of God are called foolish by nature: they failed to know the Sovereign Lord from the good things seen or recognize the architect from his works. They took fire, wind, stars, or water for gods. Seekers swayed by beauty earn small blame yet no excuse; worshipers of handmade things are miserable, hoping in dead things.

Chapter 13

These men are called foolish by nature because they could not know him who exists from the good things seen and did not recognize the architect from his works. They thought fire, wind, air, stars, water, or luminaries were gods; those who sought God and were swayed by beauty receive small blame, but are still not excused, and those who worshiped handmade things are called miserable, with hopes in dead things.
