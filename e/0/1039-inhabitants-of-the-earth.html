Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, all the earth saw the salvation of God and was called to make a joyful noise, sing praises, and rejoice before the LORD. In The Book of Isaiah, the inhabitants of the earth broke the everlasting covenant, transgressed laws and violated statutes, and faced judgment under a curse. In Revelation, those who dwell on the earth rejoiced over the deaths of the two witnesses and gave gifts to one another.

Abstract written by a model

The inhabitants of the earth broke the everlasting covenant, transgressed laws and violated statutes, and are found guilty under a curse. Judgment strikes every class, leaving few after burning; fear, pit and snare prevent escape, joy ends and cities desolate, while a remnant shouts the LORD's majesty.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 24

The inhabitants are scattered and found guilty for transgressing the laws, violating the statutes, and breaking the everlasting covenant. The judgment strikes every class alike, few are left, and fear, the pit, and the snare await them so that none escape. Joy, wine, and music end and cities fall into desolation, though a remnant shouts for the majesty of the LORD and the speaker laments the treacherous.
