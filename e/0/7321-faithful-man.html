faithful man

person · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: faithful, that man, the faithful man

Abstract written by a model

The faithful man is the first of men. Rashnu Razista, the male heavenly Yazatas, and the Amesha-Spentas free him from the Nasu, harmful beings, wicked men, and other threats. Protected by Sraosha, his houses are rich in good thoughts, good words, and good deeds. He separates himself from the wicked by pronouncing Ahura Mazda’s spell and drawing three, six, or nine furrows.

IV. Khordâd Yast.

The faithful man is proclaimed first of men and is freed by Rashnu Razista and the heavenly Yazatas from the Nasu, harmful beings, wicked men, and other threats. He separates from the wicked by pronouncing Ahura Mazda’s spell and drawing three, six, or nine furrows.
