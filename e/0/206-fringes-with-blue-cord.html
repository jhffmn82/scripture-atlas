Chapter 15

The LORD commands the Israelites to make fringes on the borders of their garments throughout their generations, with a blue cord on each border. The fringe is to be seen so they remember and do all the LORD's commandments, and do not follow their own hearts and eyes, and so that they may be holy to their God.
