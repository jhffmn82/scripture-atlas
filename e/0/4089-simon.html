Simon is a long-practicing sorcerer in Samaria, called the great power of God, who amazed the people. He believed, was baptized, and followed Philip, then offered the apostles money for the power to give the Holy Spirit. Peter rebuked him as bitter and in iniquity's bondage; Simon asked for prayer.

Chapter 8

Simon was a sorcerer who amazed the people of Samaria and was called the great power of God. He believed and was baptized, stayed with Philip, and was amazed by the signs. He then offered the apostles money for the power to give the Holy Spirit; Peter rebuked him, called him in the poison of bitterness and bondage of iniquity, and told him to repent, and Simon asked Peter to pray for him.
