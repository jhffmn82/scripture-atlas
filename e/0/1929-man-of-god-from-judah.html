Abstract written by a model

A prophetic messenger from Judah, sent by the LORD to Bethel, who prophesied against the altar, gave a sign, and healed Jeroboam's hand. Forbidden to eat, drink, or return the same way, he refused the king's food but was deceived by an old prophet's lie and ate. A lion killed him on the road; he was buried in the old prophet's grave.

Chapter 13

The man of God came from Judah to Bethel by the LORD's word, prophesied against the altar, gave a sign, and interceded so that Jeroboam's hand was restored. He refused the king's offer of food, citing the LORD's command, but was deceived by the old prophet's lie, went back and ate and drank, and was told his body would not reach his fathers' tomb. A lion killed him on the road, and he was buried in the old prophet's own grave with mourning.
