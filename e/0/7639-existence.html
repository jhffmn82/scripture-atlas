Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXIII.

Existence has no limitation and is paired with continuity without a starting-point, which is Time. Birth is not a beginning and death is not an end, while existence and Non-Existence are ultimately one. The text presents life and death as stages within a larger unity.
