Bai Li Xi

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

Bai Li Xi, a wise and virtuous man of Yu, did not remonstrate when Jin sought passage to attack Guo. He left before Yu’s ruin, became Qin’s chief minister under duke Mu, and made the duke distinguished throughout the kingdom; Mencius denies he sold himself to feed oxen.

Bai Li Xi is in one work on this site, Mencius, Book V (Wan Zhang). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
