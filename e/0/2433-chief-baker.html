Chapter 40

The chief baker offends Pharaoh and is imprisoned, and after seeing that the cup bearer's interpretation was good, tells Joseph his dream of three baskets of white bread with birds eating from the top one. Joseph says he will be hanged within three days, and Pharaoh hangs him on his birthday as interpreted.
