horses

Also called: the horses of the Empire, the horses of the State

Abstract written by a model

Horses of the State have straightness, curve, squareness, and roundness, but horses of the Empire outstrip them, moving as though parted with individuality. Hsü Wu Kuei presents them as levels of natural excellence, whose care requires preventing harm.

CHAPTER XXIV.

Hsü Wu Kuei uses dogs and horses to explain levels of natural excellence. The highest horses move as if they have lost their individuality and outstrip all competitors, while the boy says their care requires only that no harm come to them.
