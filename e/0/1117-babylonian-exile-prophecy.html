Babylonian exile prophecy

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: carried to Babylon, the days are coming

Abstract written by a model

A prophecy spoken by Isaiah as the LORD's word: days are coming when everything in Hezekiah's house will be carried to Babylon, leaving nothing, and his sons will become eunuchs in Babylon's king's palace. Hezekiah accepts it as good, since peace and truth will last in his days.

Chapter 39

Isaiah announces the LORD's word that days are coming when all in Hezekiah's house will be carried to Babylon, and his sons will be taken to serve as eunuchs in the king of Babylon's palace. Hezekiah accepts the word as good, noting that peace and truth will last in his own days.
