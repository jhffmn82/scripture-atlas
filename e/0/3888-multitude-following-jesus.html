A great multitude drawn from Galilee, Judea, Jerusalem, Idumaea, beyond the Jordan, and around Tyre and Sidon follows Jesus after hearing of his deeds. The diseased press to touch him, so he requests a boat; crowds grow so tight they cannot eat, and a multitude sits around him when his family arrives.

Chapter 3

A great multitude follows Jesus from Galilee, Judea, Jerusalem, Idumaea, beyond the Jordan, and around Tyre and Sidon after hearing of what he did. Those with diseases press on him to touch him, so he asks for a little boat to stay near him, and the crowds later gather so tightly that he and his followers cannot eat. A multitude sits around him when his family comes.
