atonement

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: atonement and forgiveness, forgiven, he will be forgiven, make atonement

Abstract written by a model

After the sin offering is made, the priest makes atonement for the offender or group. The text states that they "shall be forgiven" or "will be forgiven." This is stated for the congregation, the ruler, the common person, and the one who brings a lamb. Atonement is made by the priest for the guilty person concerning his sin, each time after the required offering. It results in the person being forgiven. It is made with the ram in the cases of trespass against holy things and unwitting sin.
