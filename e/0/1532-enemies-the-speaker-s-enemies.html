Chapter 3

The enemies open their mouths wide against the people and chase the speaker like a bird without cause. They cut off his life in the dungeon and cast a stone on him, and they plot against him all day long and mock him in song. The speaker says the LORD has seen their vengeance and will pay them back, giving them hardness of heart and destroying them from under the LORD's heavens.
