Chapter 50

Simon, the son of Onias and high priest, repaired the house and strengthened the temple in his life, and his days saw the foundation built to the height of the double walls and a cistern dug. He planned to save his people from ruin and fortified the city against siege. He is praised with many images of splendor, leads the altar service, pours the libation, and blesses the congregation.
