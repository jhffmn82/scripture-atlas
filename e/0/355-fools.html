Also called: a fool, a foolish man, one who is void of wisdom, people void of understanding, the fool

Abstract written by a model

In Proverbs, fools are people who gush out and feed on folly, fail to spread knowledge, and despise their father's correction and their mother. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, fools are people whose discourse shifts like the moon, whose talk is offensive, whose laughter is sinful, and whose swearing is appalling, so the reader is advised to limit time among them.

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where fools first appears

Abstract written by a model

Fools are people whose discourse shifts like the moon, unlike the godly; their talk is offensive, laughter sinful, and swearing appalling, so the reader is finally advised to limit time among them.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 27

Fools change like the moon, while the godly man's discourse is always wise. Their talk is offensive, their laughter wantonly sinful, and their swearing and strife make hair stand on end and others plug their ears; the reader is told to limit time among them.
