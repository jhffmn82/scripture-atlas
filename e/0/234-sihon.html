Sihon

Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, Sihon is the Amorite king ruling from Heshbon who had seized Moab's former land, refused Israel passage, fought them at Jahaz, and was struck down, losing his land. In Deuteronomy, Sihon is the Amorite king of Heshbon whose hardened spirit leads him to refuse Israel passage, fight them with all his people, and be struck down with his sons and people, losing all his cities. In The Book of Joshua, Sihon is an Amorite king beyond the Jordan who lived in Heshbon and ruled territory bordering Og's, until Moses and the Israelites struck him and utterly destroyed him.
