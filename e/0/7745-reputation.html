reputation

Also called: the shadow of reality

Abstract written by a model

Reputation is the shadow of reality, able to displace it and, when ill-regulated, end in notoriety. Though pursued by Tzŭ Chang and the superior man, it should arise from virtue rather than concern; Hsü Yu and the true Sage reject such concern.

CHAPTER I.

Reputation is described as the shadow of reality and is rejected by Hsü Yu and the true Sage. The chapter treats freedom from concern with reputation as part of higher understanding.

Tzŭ Chang asked Man Kou Tê,

Reputation is presented as a pursuit associated with Tzŭ Chang and with the superior man. The text says that reputation may arise as a result of virtuous conduct even when it was not sought.
