Abstract written by a model

In Sutrakritanga (Sûtrakritâṅga), Book I, water contains life and houses many beings; contact brings no perfection, use can kill beings, and it does not wash off sin. In Hymns of Guru Nanak, water is pure, generates the world, quenches thirst but cannot wash the heart, and finally destroys everything. In Mencius, Book VII (Jin Xin), water is necessary for the people’s life and so abundant that anyone requesting it at dusk receives it.
