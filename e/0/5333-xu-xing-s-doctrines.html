Abstract written by a model

Xu Xing's doctrines advocate rulers farming and preparing food with their people, division of labor, and equal market standards and prices to prevent deceit.

Xu Xing's doctrines hold that princes should farm and prepare their own food with their people. They also claim that equal standards and prices for goods would prevent deceit in the kingdom.
