great rivers and seas

place · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: great rivers and seas, rivers and seas, their, they

Abstract written by a model

Rivers and seas receive homage and tribute from all valley streams because they are lower than them, making them kings of the valley streams; their relation to the world analogizes the Tao’s.

66. 1. That whereby the rivers and seas are able to receive the homage

They receive the homage and tribute of all the valley streams because they are lower than the streams. This makes them the kings of the valley streams.
