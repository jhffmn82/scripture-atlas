CHAPTER V.

No-toes has been mutilated and walks on his heels to see Confucius, who first rebukes him for his misfortune. He leaves after Confucius's words, then asks Lao Tzŭ about Confucius's wisdom and says that Confucius's punishment by God makes him impossible to release.
