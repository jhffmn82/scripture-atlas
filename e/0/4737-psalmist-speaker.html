Abstract written by a model

The psalmist, a son of Korah, longs and thirsts for the living God like a panting deer, weeps day and night, and remembers joyfully leading crowds to God's house in festival. Now despairing, overwhelmed by waves and feeling forgotten, he calls God his rock, and repeatedly exhorts his disturbed soul to hope and praise.

Chapter 42

The psalmist, writing as one of the sons of Korah's contemplations, thirsts for God, weeps day and night, and recalls once leading a crowd to God's house in joy and praise. Remembering God from the Jordan, Hermon, and Mizar while overwhelmed by waves, he asks God why he is forgotten and exhorts his own soul to hope.
