XVII. Ashi Yast.

The sacrifice to Ashi Vanguhi includes libations, Haoma and meat, baresma, wisdom of the tongue, holy spells, words or speech, deeds, and rightly-spoken words. It is offered by several figures to request glory, prosperity, victory, protection, and other boons.
