Abstract written by a model

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Twelfth Lecture.

Various colours or forms are described as appearing in wreaths, dressed images, dolls, clothes, woodwork, plastering, paintings, jewelry, ivory-work, strings, and leaf-cutting. Monks and nuns should not deliberately seek them for the sake of pleasing the eye.
