Also called: all the people, every one, others, the people

Abstract written by a model

The people are essential to a state’s stability because they must trust their ruler. Their prosperity is tied to the prince’s: plenty prevents the prince’s want, while their want prevents his solitary enjoyment of plenty. Their conduct also follows the ruler’s example, becoming good when the ruler desires what is good.

CHAP. VII. 1. Tsze-kung asked about government. The Master

The people must have confidence in their ruler for the state to stand.

CHAP. IX. 1. The Duke Ai inquired of Yu Zo, saying, 'The year

The people are the subject of Yu Zo's proposed tithe. Yu Zo says that if they have plenty, their prince will not be left to want, but if they are in want, their prince cannot enjoy plenty alone.

CHAP. XIX. Chi K'ang asked Confucius about government,

The people will be good when rulers show desires for what is good.
