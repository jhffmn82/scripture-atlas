twelve officers (Solomon's officers)

Abstract written by a model

Solomon's twelve officers are a group with authority over all Israel, each assigned a district and responsible for supplying food to the king and his household for one month of the year. They are listed with their districts, from Ben Hur in Ephraim's hill country and Ahimaaz in Naphtali to Geber the son of Uri in Gilead, the only officer in that land. Two of them, Ben Abinadab and Ahimaaz, are married to Solomon's daughters. They also provision everyone at Solomon's table, letting nothing be lacking, and bring barley and straw for the horses.

Chapter 4

Solomon has twelve officers over all Israel, each responsible for providing food for the king and his household for one month of the year. They are named with their districts, such as Ben Hur in the hill country of Ephraim, Ahimaaz in Naphtali, and Geber in Gilead, and two of them, Ben Abinadab and Ahimaaz, are married to daughters of Solomon. They also supply food for all who come to Solomon's table, letting nothing be lacking, and bring barley and straw for the horses.
