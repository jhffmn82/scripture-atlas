profane nation (Israel)

group · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a profane nation, the people who anger me

Abstract written by a model

The profane nation is the people who anger the Lord, against whom he sends Assyria with a command to take plunder and prey and tread them down like the mire of the streets. The text compares its idols to those of the kingdoms Assyria has already found, and the Lord says he will do to Jerusalem and her idols as he did to Samaria. His anger is not turned away and his hand is stretched out still.
