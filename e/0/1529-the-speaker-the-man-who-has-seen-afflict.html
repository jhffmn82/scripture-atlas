Abstract written by a model

A speaker who has suffered affliction under the LORD's wrath is mocked by his people, dwelling in darkness, walled in, broken, and hunted by baseless enemies. His strength and expectation perish, yet recalling the LORD's mercies restores hope. He calls on the LORD from a dungeon, is told not to fear, is redeemed, and asks judgment on his enemies.

Chapter 3

The speaker describes being led into darkness, walled in, broken, and made a mark for the LORD's arrow, and he says his strength and expectation have perished. He then recalls the LORD's mercies and takes hope, declares the LORD his portion, and urges repentance. He also recounts being hunted by enemies and thrown into a dungeon, calling on the LORD's name, being told not to be afraid, and being redeemed, and he asks for judgment on his enemies.
