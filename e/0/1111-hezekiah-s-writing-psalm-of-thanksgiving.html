Abstract written by a model

authored_by Hezekiah [written after recovering from sickness] The writing records Hezekiah's thoughts when he was sick and after he recovered. It describes his fear of going into the gates of Sheol, his moaning like a dove, and his anguish. It then turns to praise: the LORD has delivered him from the pit and cast his sins behind His back, the living praise Him while Sheol cannot, and Hezekiah resolves to sing with stringed instruments in the LORD's house all his days.
