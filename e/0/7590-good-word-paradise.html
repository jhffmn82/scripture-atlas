Good-Word Paradise

Also called: Paradise, heavenly reward

Abstract written by a model

Good-Word Paradise is the second destination reached by a faithful soul after death, following Good Thoughts and preceding Good Deeds, as part of a progression toward Endless Light and the Garô-nmâna of Ahura Mazda.

Good-Word Paradise is in one work on this site, Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

The Good-Word Paradise is where the faithful person’s soul arrives after its second step.
