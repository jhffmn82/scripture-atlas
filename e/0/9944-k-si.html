Abstract written by a model

Kêsi is a young Sramana and famous disciple of Pârsva, accomplished in sciences, right conduct, and Sruta and Avadhi knowledge. Residing in Tinduka park, he arrives at Srâvastî, questions Gautama about two Laws, resolves his doubts, and sincerely adopts the five vows.

Twenty-Third Lecture.

Kêsi is a young Sramana and famous disciple of Pârsva who has mastered the sciences and right conduct, possesses Sruta and Avadhi knowledge, and arrives at Srâvastî. He questions Gautama about the two Laws and, after his doubts are resolved, adopts the Law of the five vows.
