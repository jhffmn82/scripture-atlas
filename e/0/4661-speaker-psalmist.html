The psalmist is a devoted servant of the LORD, son of his servant girl, who loves the LORD because he was heard. Surrounded by the cords of death and Sheol, he called on the LORD's name and was saved and freed from chains. He resolves to call on the LORD all his life, walk before him, take the cup of salvation, offer thanksgiving, and pay his vows publicly in Jerusalem.

Chapter 116

The psalmist declares his love for the LORD because he was heard, and recounts being surrounded by the cords of death and the pains of Sheol before calling on the LORD's name and being saved. He calls himself the LORD's servant, the son of his servant girl, freed from chains, and admits he said in haste that all people are liars. He resolves to walk before the LORD, take the cup of salvation, offer the sacrifice of thanksgiving, and pay his vows publicly in the courts of the LORD's house in Jerusalem.
