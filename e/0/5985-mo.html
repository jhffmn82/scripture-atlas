Mo

person · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Mo Di, The philosopher Mo

Abstract written by a model

In Mencius, Book III (Teng Wen Gong), Mo Di advocates spare simplicity in funerals and equal love; his widely circulating words, alongside Yang Zhu’s, prompt Mencius’s opposition over filial affection. In Mencius, Book VII (Jin Xin), Mo is a philosopher who loves all equally and would rub smooth his whole body to benefit the kingdom.
