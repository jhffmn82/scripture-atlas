Abstract written by a model

Ruler is a sovereign whose institutions rest on character and conduct. By testing them, knowing Heaven, and awaiting a sage, he reduces errors in government. From sovereignty and three important things, his movements become lasting examples, his acts laws, and his words lessons for the kingdom.

Chapter 29

The Ruler who attains sovereignty of the kingdom and possesses three important things can ensure that there are few errors under his government. His character and conduct root his institutions, and his movements, acts, and words become examples, laws, and lessons for the kingdom across ages.
