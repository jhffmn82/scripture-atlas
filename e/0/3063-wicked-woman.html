Chapter 26

A wicked woman is likened to a chafing yoke, and whoever takes hold of her is like one who grasps a scorpion. The text also condemns the jealous woman whose tongue-lashing is known to all, the drunken woman who causes great wrath and does not cover her shame, and the woman whose fornication shows in her raised eyes and eyelids. She is described as opening her mouth like a thirsty traveller and drinking from every nearby water.
