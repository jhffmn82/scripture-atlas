eighty Deities

group · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: eighty Deities his brethren

Abstract written by a model

Eighty Deities travel to Inaba seeking marriage with the Princess of Yakami. They harm the White Hare of Inaba and repeatedly try to kill their brother, Deity Great-Name-Possessor, who is restored and escapes. He then pursues, scatters, and drives them onto slopes and into rivers.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Sect.

The eighty Deities travel to Inaba because each wishes to marry the Princess of Yakami. They deceive and injure the White Hare of Inaba, and later repeatedly attempt to kill the Deity Great-Name-Possessor, but he is restored and escapes them.

Sect.

The eighty Deities are pursued and scattered by the Deity Great-Name-Possessor. He pursues them until they crouch on the august slope of every pass and are swept into every river.
