Abstract written by a model

The multiplying brood of the ungodly, their illegitimate offshoots, profits nothing. Like branches that flourish briefly, they are shaken and uprooted by winds, broken off before maturity, bearing useless, unripe fruit. Unlawfully conceived children finally witness against their parents's wickedness when investigated.

Chapter 4

The multiplying brood of the ungodly is of no profit, and their illegitimate offshoots do not take deep root or gain a sure hold. They are compared to branches that flourish briefly but are shaken and uprooted by wind, broken off before maturity, and bear useless, unripe fruit. Unlawfully conceived children also witness against their parents when investigated.
