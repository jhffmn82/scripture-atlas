Abstract written by a model

Midianite oppression was a seven-year punishment for Israel's evil, when Midian and allies raided after each sowing, destroyed crops and livestock, and drove Israel into caves, prompting cries to the LORD, a prophet's message, and Gideon's call.

Chapter 6

For seven years, as punishment for Israel's evil, Midian and its allies came up after each sowing, destroying crops and leaving no sheep, ox or donkey in Israel. It drove Israel into mountain dens and caves and brought them to cry to the LORD, which led to the prophet's message and Gideon's call.
