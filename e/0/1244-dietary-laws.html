Chapter 14

The dietary laws permit animals that part the hoof and chew the cud, fish with fins and scales, and clean birds, and forbid the listed unclean animals and birds. They also forbid eating anything that dies of itself, though it may be given or sold to a foreigner. They forbid boiling a young goat in its mother's milk, and are grounded in Israel being a holy people.
