Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the Merarites form a Levite group responsible for carrying and maintaining the tabernacle’s boards, bars, pillars, sockets, pins, cords, and court pillars. In The Book of Joshua, the Merarites, as the rest of the Levites, receive twelve cities from Zebulun, Reuben, and Gad, including Ramoth in Gilead, a city of refuge.

Merarites in Numbers

author unknown · 1439 to 1399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Merarites first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Merarites are a Levite group descending from Merari through Mahli and Mushi, forming the Mahlite and Mushite families. They number 6,200 males from a month old and camp on the tabernacle's north side under prince Zuriel son of Abihail. Their duty is the tabernacle's boards, bars, pillars, sockets, pins, cords and court pillars. Serving under Ithamar, 3,200 men aged thirty to fifty carry these items, receiving four wagons and eight oxen for their service.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The Merarites descend from Merari through Mahli and Mushi, forming the Mahlite and Mushite families, and number six thousand two hundred males. They camp on the north side under prince Zuriel son of Abihail, and are responsible for the tabernacle's boards, bars, pillars, sockets, instruments, and the court pillars with their sockets, pins, and cords.

Chapter 4

The Merarites are assigned to carry the tabernacle's boards, bars, pillars, sockets, court pillars, pins, and cords, with their instruments assigned to them by name, under the hand of Ithamar. They number 3,200 men aged thirty to fifty.
