offspring (Abram's offspring) in Genesis

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where offspring (Abram's offspring) first appears

Abstract written by a model

Abram's offspring are a promised line descending from his own body, central to a covenant. Early promises give them the land forever and numbers like the dust and stars. Then they are told they will live as foreigners in a land not theirs, serving and afflicted four hundred years, before leaving with great wealth after that nation is judged, returning in the fourth generation. Finally the covenant becomes everlasting: God will be their God, Canaan their possession, males circumcised, nations and kings arising, the line running through Isaac, and they will bless the nations.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

The LORD promises that Abram's offspring will inherit all the land Abram sees, forever. He also promises to make them as numerous as the dust of the earth, so that they could be counted only if the dust could be counted.

Chapter 15

Abram's offspring are promised to be as countless as the stars, with the heir coming from Abram's own body. They will live as foreigners in a land not theirs, serve and be afflicted for four hundred years, then come out with great wealth after the LORD judges that nation, and return in the fourth generation.

Chapter 17

God promises that Abraham's offspring after him, throughout their generations, will share in the everlasting covenant, with God being their God and the land of Canaan their everlasting possession. They are to keep the covenant by circumcising every male. Nations and kings are promised to come from Abraham, and the covenant line is specified as running through Isaac.
