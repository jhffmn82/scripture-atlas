Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Hilkiah counted the money brought into the LORD's house, found the book of the law, gave it to Shaphan, and was sent by the king to inquire of the LORD through Huldah. In The Second Book of Chronicles, Hilkiah received repair money, found the book of the LORD's law given by Moses, gave it to Shaphan, and went with others to inquire of the LORD through Huldah at Josiah's command.
