Abstract written by a model

In Deuteronomy, it is the altar to the LORD on Mount Ebal built by Israel from uncut stones for burnt and peace offerings, where the people eat and rejoice before the LORD. In The Book of Joshua, it is Joshua’s altar to the LORD, the God of Israel, built on Mount Ebal from uncut stones for burnt offerings and peace offerings.

Chapter 27

Israel is to build an altar to the LORD on Mount Ebal from uncut stones, using no iron tool on them. Burnt offerings are to be made on it and peace offerings sacrificed, with the people eating there and rejoicing before the LORD.
