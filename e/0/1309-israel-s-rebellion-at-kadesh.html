Abstract written by a model

At Kadesh Barnea, Israel refuses the command to go up, fearing fortified cities and Anakim. Despite Moses' reminders they disbelieve, and the LORD swears judgment, excluding that generation except Caleb from the promised land; they then presume to attack and are routed.

Chapter 1

Israel refuses the command to go up, murmuring that the LORD hated them and fearing the people, fortified cities, and Anakim reported by their brothers. Moses reminds them of the LORD's past care, but they do not believe, and the LORD swears judgment on that generation. They then presume to attack against his warning and are routed.
