Chapter 15

The people of Bethulia plunder the camp of Asshur and are enriched exceedingly, and villages and cities in the hill country and plain take many spoils from an exceedingly great supply. The people plunder the camp for thirty days. Holofernes' tent, silver cups, beds, bowls, and furniture are given to Judith.
