Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, the multitudes follow Jesus, receive healing and food from him, and are sent away. In The Gospel According to John, the multitude follows Jesus because of his signs, is fed by him, seeks to make him king, and asks for the bread he speaks of.

The great multitude follows Jesus because of his signs on the sick, is fed by him, and calls him the prophet who comes into the world.
