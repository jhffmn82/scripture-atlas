Abstract written by a model

Darius the Mede, son of Ahasuerus and of Median descent, receives Belshazzar's kingdom at about sixty-two and is made king over the Chaldeans. He appoints one hundred twenty governors and three presidents, planning to elevate Daniel. Tricked into signing the thirty-day decree, he is bound by the law and has Daniel cast into the den, fasting overnight. Finding Daniel alive, he rejoices, punishes the accusers, and decrees that all must fear Daniel's God.

Chapter 6

King Darius sets over the kingdom one hundred twenty local governors and three presidents, and plans to set Daniel over the whole realm. Tricked into signing the thirty-day decree, he labors until sundown to rescue Daniel but is bound by the law of the Medes and Persians, so he has Daniel cast into the den, seals it, and spends the night fasting. He rejoices at finding Daniel alive, has the accusers thrown to the lions, and decrees that all in his kingdom must tremble and fear the God of Daniel.
