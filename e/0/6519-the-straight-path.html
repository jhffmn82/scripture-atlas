the straight path

topic · 7 works · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: My straight path, The path of those whom Thou hast favoured, a plain road, a straight path, a straight road, of those who go astray, paths of peace, straight path, the (path) of those who earn Thine anger, the path of thy Lord, the right path, the way

Abstract written by a model

In The Opening (Al-Faatiha), the straight path is the way requested to be shown: the path of those whom Allah has favoured, rather than those who earn His anger or go astray. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), a straight road is the way by which Allah guides believers in Allah and the Last Day, leading them to Allah amid commands to restore deposits, judge justly, obey Allah and the messenger, and believe in the Truth. In Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida), the straight path is the path guided by Allah, from which disbelievers among the Children of Israel go astray, and toward which Allah guides people through His revelations and the paths of peace. In Surah 6: The Cattle (Al-An'aam), the straight path is Allah's path and a right religion linked to Abraham, which people are commanded to follow toward peace instead of other divisive ways. In Surah 24: The Light (An-Noor), the straight path is the path to which Allah guides whom He wills through revelations, with obedience to Allah and the messenger leading people aright. In Surah 36: Yaseen (Yaseen), the straight path is the path on which Ya Sin is sent and the right path associated with worshipping Allah rather than the devil. In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), the straight path is the path unto Allah that Muhammad is instructed to call people to take while seeking His forgiveness, affirming Allah's oneness and turning away from idolatry.

Abstract written by a model

A straight road is the way by which Allah guides believers in Allah and the Last Day. It leads to Allah. Allah guides those who do what they are exhorted to do unto it, amid commands to restore deposits, judge justly, obey Allah and the messenger, and believe in the Truth.
