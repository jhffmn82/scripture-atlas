Chapter 5

The song is sung by Deborah and Barak on the day of the victory, calling kings and princes to hear and blessing the LORD. It praises the willing leaders and people, recounts which tribes fought or stayed back, curses Meroz, blesses Jael, and ends with a prayer for the LORD's enemies to perish.
