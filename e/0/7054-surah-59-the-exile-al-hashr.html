Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns Allah, the People of the Scripture who disbelieved, the believers, the fugitives, and the hypocrites. It describes the first exile, the distribution of spoil, the loyalty and generosity of the believers, and the promises and failures of the hypocrites. It warns of punishment in the Fire and contrasts the owners of the Fire with the owners of the Garden. It glorifies Allah by recounting His names and attributes and teaches that the Qur'an would humble a mountain through fear of Allah.
