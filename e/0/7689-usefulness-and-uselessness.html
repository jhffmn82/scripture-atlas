CHAPTER I.

The chapter teaches that usefulness depends on application and perspective. What appears useless may provide safety, freedom, or an unexpectedly valuable purpose.

A certain artisan was travelling to the Ch'i State. On reaching

Uselessness is presented as a means of preserving life from cutting, conscription, public works, and other dangers. The unit says that ordinary judgments cannot properly assess means adopted to reach the unusual goal of uselessness.
