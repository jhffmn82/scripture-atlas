men who have retired to privacy from the world

group · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

The men who have retired to privacy from the world include Po-i, Shu-ch'i, Yu-chung, I-yi, Chu-chang, Hui of Liu-hsia, and Shao-lien. Confucius distinguishes among them according to whether they surrendered their wills, submitted to taint, preserved their purity, or acted according to the times.
