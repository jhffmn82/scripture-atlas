Chapter 25

She is described as the worst wickedness and malice, worse than any wound, calamity, or enemy's wrath, and the speaker would rather live with a lion and a dragon than with her. She changes her appearance and darkens her countenance like a bear, and is blamed as the beginning of sin and the cause of death. The speaker advises denying her freedom of speech and cutting her away from the husband's flesh if she does not go as directed.
