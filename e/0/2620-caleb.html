Abstract written by a model

Caleb is a son of Hezron and brother of Jerahmeel. He married Azubah, then Ephrath after Azubah died, who bore Hur. Jerioth bore Jesher, Shobab and Ardon; concubines Ephah and Maacah bore further sons. His daughter was Achsah; his line through Hur extends to Bethlehem.

Chapter 2

Caleb, son of Hezron, had children by Azubah and Jerioth, and after Azubah died he married Ephrath, who bore Hur. His concubines Ephah and Maacah bore further sons, and his daughter was Achsah. His line through Hur includes Shobal, Salma and Hareph, linked to Kiriath Jearim, Bethlehem and Beth Gader.
