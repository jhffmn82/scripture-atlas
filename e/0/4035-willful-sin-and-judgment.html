Chapter 10

If people sin willfully after receiving knowledge of the truth, no sacrifice for sins remains, only a fearful expectation of judgment and a fierceness of fire that will devour the adversaries. Whoever tramples the Son of God, counts the covenant blood an unholy thing, and insults the Spirit of grace deserves much worse punishment than one who disregarded Moses' law. The Lord says vengeance is his and he will judge his people, and it is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God.
