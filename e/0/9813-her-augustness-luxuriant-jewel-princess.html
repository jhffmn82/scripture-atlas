Her Augustness Luxuriant-Jewel-Princess

Abstract written by a model

Her Augustness Luxuriant-Jewel-Princess, daughter of the Sea-Deity, delights in His Augustness Fire-Subside and marries him. Pregnant, she comes to him and builds a cormorants’-feather parturition-hall, where she gives birth in her native crocodile shape. After Fire-Subside secretly sees her, she leaves and closes the sea-boundary, later sending him a loving song through her younger sister, Her Augustness Jewel-Good-Princess.

Sect.

Luxuriant-Jewel-Princess is the daughter of the Sea-Deity and becomes delighted when she sees Fire-Subside. She tells her father about Fire-Subside’s sigh, and she is given in marriage to him.

Sect.

Her Augustness Luxuriant-Jewel-Princess, daughter of the Sea-Deity, came to His Augustness Fire-Subside while pregnant and built a parturition-hall from cormorants' feathers. During parturition she took her native shape, which was that of a crocodile, and left after His Augustness Fire-Subside secretly looked at her. She later sent him a song through her younger sister, Her Augustness Jewel-Good-Princess, expressing her love.
