religious exercise

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: kriyâ, religious exercises, religious life

Abstract written by a model

Religious exercise uses five Samitis to practice religious life and Guptis to prevent sin; a sage mastering them crosses Samsâra, compared to traveling in a car, beyond the Circle of Births.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Twenty-Fourth Lecture.

The five Samitis are prescribed for the practice of the religious life, while the Guptis prevent everything sinful. A sage who thoroughly practices their essence will soon get beyond the Circle of Births.

Twenty-Seventh Lecture.

Religious exercise is compared to a car in which one crosses the Samsâra.
