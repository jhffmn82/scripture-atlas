Abstract written by a model

The material world, made by Ahura Mazda, gains fulfilment of its wishes and freedom from heart anxiety through the Holy Word. Tistrya brings waters, rain, clouds, and fertility and guards its life from hostile forces. It contains Mithra’s dwelling, while Sraosha protects and oversees the moving world, fighting the Mâzainya Daêvas.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

I. Ormazd Yast.

The material world is said to come to the fulfilment of its wishes and to be freed from the anxieties of the heart through the strongest part of the Holy Word. Ahura Mazda is identified as its Maker.

VIII. Tîr Yast.

The material world receives waters, rain, clouds, and fertility through Tistrya’s actions. Tistrya also protects it from Pairikas and other forces that seek to extinguish its life.

XI. Srôsh Yast Hâdhôkht.

Sraosha protects all the material world with his club uplifted. He maintains and looks over the moving world while fighting the Mâzainya Daêvas.
