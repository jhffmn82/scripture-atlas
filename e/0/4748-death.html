Death

topic · 4 works · 9 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Death under handcuffs and fetters, a pale horse, death, die, he who sat on it was Death, perish, when he dies

Abstract written by a model

Death in Psalms is described as the shepherd of the foolish, who are appointed as a flock for Sheol, over whom the upright are said to have dominion in the morning. Death in Revelation is the rider on the pale horse, given authority over one fourth of the earth to kill by sword, famine, death, and wild animals. Death in The Table-Talk of the Prophet Mohammad (Part 2) is described as a dreadful event, and people are instructed not to wish for it and to show proper respect toward the dead and graves. Death in Mencius, Book VII (Jin Xin) is described as either sustained in duties and ascribed to Heaven's appointment or occurring under handcuffs and fetters and not so ascribed.
