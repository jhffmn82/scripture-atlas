Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Samuel, Jesse presents seven sons to Samuel, reveals David tending sheep, sends for him, and later sends him to Saul with bread, wine, and a goat. In The Book of Isaiah, Jesse is named as the source of a shoot from his stock and a fruitful branch from his roots, whose root will stand as a banner sought by the nations and whose resting place will be glorious. In The Second Book of Nephi, Jesse is named as the stem from which a rod comes and the roots from which a branch grows.

Jesse in The First Book of Samuel

Abstract written by a model

Jesse is an elderly Ephrathite Bethlehemite whose youngest son is David. He presents seven sons to Samuel, none chosen by the LORD, then reveals David tending sheep, sends for him, and later sends him to Saul with bread, wine, and a goat.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

Jesse, a Bethlehemite, is called with his sons to the sacrifice and has seven of them pass before Samuel, none of whom the LORD has chosen. He then reveals the youngest, David, is keeping the sheep, sends for him, and later sends David to Saul with a donkey loaded with bread, wine, and a young goat.
