Abstract written by a model

Naniha-ne-ko-take-furu-kuma, generalissimo for the Heir Apparent, is ancestor of the Grandees of Wani. He feigns submission to deceive King Oshikuma’s forces, restores his army’s bows, then pursues and defeats the opposing army.
