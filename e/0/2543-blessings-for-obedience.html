Abstract written by a model

Blessings promised for obedience, conditioned on walking in statutes and keeping commandments: seasonal rain, abundant harvests, peaceful land without fear or sword, evil animals removed, victory over enemies, then fruitfulness, multiplication, a confirmed covenant, and the LORD dwelling and walking among them.

Chapter 26

Obedience brings rains in season, abundant crops and fruit, safe dwelling, peace, removal of evil animals, and victory over enemies, with five chasing a hundred. The LORD also promises fruitfulness, multiplication, a confirmed covenant, his tent among them, and his walking among them as their God.
