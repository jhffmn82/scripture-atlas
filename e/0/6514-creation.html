creation

topic · 6 works · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Allah's creation, His creation, We created not the heavens and the earth and all that is between them save with truth, creation and recreation, divine creation, originated creation, produceth creation, reproduceth it, the creation, the first creation, the later growth

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 13: The Thunder (Ar-Ra'd), creation is presented as Allah's work and a sign of His power. In Surah 15: The Rock (Al-Hijr), creation encompasses the heavens, earth, humanity, jinn, life, and death made by Allah. In Surah 29: The Spider (Al-Ankaboot), creation is produced and reproduced by Allah, with its origins and later growth presented for reflection. In Surah 30: The Romans (Ar-Room), creation is produced and reproduced by Allah, and returns to Him. In Surah 56: The Inevitable (Al-Waaqia), the first creation is cited as a reason for human reflection. In Surah 81: The Overthrowing (At-Takwir), creation is addressed as the recipient of the reminder and may walk straight.
