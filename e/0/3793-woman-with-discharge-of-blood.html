Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, she is made well after touching the fringe of Jesus' garment, and Jesus calls her daughter and says her faith has made her well. In The Good News According to Mark, she is healed after touching Jesus' clothes in a crowd and is called 'Daughter' and sent in peace. In The Gospel According to Luke, she is healed after touching the fringe of Jesus' cloak, and Jesus calls her 'Daughter,' credits her faith, and sends her in peace.

woman with flow of blood in The Gospel According to Luke

author unknown · AD 80 to 90 · the work’s page · read it where woman with flow of blood first appears

Abstract written by a model

A woman suffering a flow of blood for twelve years, having spent everything on physicians without healing, touches the fringe of Jesus' cloak and is healed instantly. She confesses publicly, trembling; he calls her 'Daughter,' credits her faith, and sends her in peace.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 8

A woman who had suffered a flow of blood for twelve years and spent all she had on physicians without being healed touches the fringe of Jesus' cloak from behind, and the bleeding stops immediately. When Jesus asks who touched him, she comes trembling and declares before everyone why she touched him; he calls her 'Daughter,' says her faith has made her well, and tells her to go in peace.
