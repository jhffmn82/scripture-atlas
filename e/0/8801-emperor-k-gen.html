Abstract written by a model

Emperor Kō-Gen, a Heavenly Sovereign also called His Augustness Oho-yamato-ne-ko-hiko-kuni-kuru, dwelt at Sakaki-bara and ruled the Empire. He wed Utsu-shiko-me, I-gaka-shiko-me, and Princess Haniyasu, fathering five Deities. He lived fifty-seven years and had a mausoleum at Tsurugi Pool.

Sect. XLI.—Emperor Kō-Gen.

Emperor Kō-Gen, also called His Augustness Oho-yamato-ne-ko-hiko-kuni-kuru, dwelt in the palace of Sakaki-bara at Karu and ruled the Empire. His august years were fifty-seven, and his august mausoleum was on the mound in the middle of the Pool of Tsurugi.
