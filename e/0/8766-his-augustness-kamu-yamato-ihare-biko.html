His Augustness Kamu-yamato-ihare-biko

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: the Heavenly Sovereign, the august child of the Heavenly Deity, this Heavenly Sovereign Kamu-yamato-ihare-biko

Abstract written by a model

A Heavenly Sovereign and august child of the Heavenly Deity, he leaves Himuka, progresses eastward through many places, gains the cross-sword and eight-foot crow’s guidance, defeats resistance, rules from Kashibara near Unebi, fathers children with I-suke-yori-hime, and lives 137 august years.

He leaves Himuka and progresses eastward through Tsukushi, Agi, Kibi, Kumanu, Uda, and other places. He receives the cross-sword, follows the eight-foot crow, defeats resisting forces, establishes rule at Kashibara near Unebi, and fathers children with I-suke-yori-hime.
