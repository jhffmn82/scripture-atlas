Mount Seir

place · 3 works · 10 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Seir, Seir (hill country), it, the field of Edom, the hill country of Seir, the land of Seir, you, your

Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, Seir is where Esau settled after leaving Canaan and where he became the father of the Edomites. In Deuteronomy, Mount Seir is given to Esau's descendants as a possession by the LORD, and Israel is forbidden to take its land. In The Book of Ezekiel, Mount Seir is targeted by the LORD's prophecy and sentenced to desolation for its hostility toward Israel and other offenses.

Mount Seir in The Book of Ezekiel

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Mount Seir first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mount Seir, identified with all Edom, is a mountain targeted by the LORD's prophecy. Accused of perpetual hostility toward Israel, pursuit by blood, covetousness of Israel and Judah, and insulting Israel's mountains, it is sentenced to desolation. Its cities will be laid waste and uninhabited, and its hills, valleys, and watercourses filled with the slain.

Chapter 35

Mount Seir is the target of the LORD's prophecy, accused of perpetual hostility, handing Israel over to the sword, claiming the two nations and countries as its own, and insulting and boasting against the mountains of Israel. It is sentenced to become a perpetual desolation with uninhabited cities, its travelers cut off and its hills, valleys, and watercourses filled with the slain.
