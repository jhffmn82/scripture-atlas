Chapter 3

The LORD calls Samuel three times, and each time Samuel goes to Eli, thinking Eli called, because Samuel does not yet know the LORD and the LORD's word has not yet been revealed to him. After Eli's instruction, the LORD calls a fourth time, 'Samuel! Samuel!', and Samuel answers, 'Speak; for your servant hears.'
