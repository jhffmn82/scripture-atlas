Abstract written by a model

A progression of virtues added to faith: moral excellence, knowledge, self-control, perseverance, godliness, brotherly affection, and love. When abounding, they prevent idleness and unfruitfulness in knowing Jesus Christ; lacking them brings blindness, shortsightedness, and forgetting cleansing from old sins.

Peter’s Second Letter / Chapter 1

Peter lists a progression: in faith supply moral excellence, then knowledge, self-control, perseverance, godliness, brotherly affection, and love. If these abound, they keep believers from being idle or unfruitful in the knowledge of Jesus Christ. Whoever lacks them is blind, seeing only what is near and forgetting cleansing from old sins.
