Also called: the believers, those who believe, true believers

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 10: Jonas (Yunus), believers who do good works are guided by their faith and receive good tidings, mercy, peace, the Gardens of Delight, security from fear and grief, and good tidings in the life of the world and the Hereafter. In Surah 34: Sheba (Saba), believers who do good works receive pardon, rich provision, multiplied reward, and secure dwelling in lofty halls; true believers are not led by Satan.
