Eighth Lesson.

Raw things that have not been modified by instruments, including roots, spices, fruits, shoots, berries, plants, vegetables, seeds, sprouts, and other substances, should not be accepted. Raw things are unacceptable when they are unmodified, poorly ground, contain small seeds, or are otherwise described as impure.
