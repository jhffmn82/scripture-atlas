Paggusan is a rainy-season observance, normally begun after a month and twenty nights, though earlier commencement is allowed. It unfolds through detailed rules governing monks’ and nuns’ residence, travel, alms, food, conduct, lodging, and reconciliation.

Rules for Yatis.

The Paggusan is commenced after a month and twenty nights of the rainy season have elapsed, though it may be commenced earlier but not later. During it, monks and nuns follow detailed rules concerning residence, travel, alms, food, permissions, cleanliness, speech, lodging, and reconciliation.
