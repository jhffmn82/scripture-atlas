He flees on foot to Jael's tent, asks for water, receives milk, and is killed in his sleep by a tent peg through his temples. His mother waits in vain at the window.

Chapter 4

Sisera, captain of Jabin's army, lived in Harosheth of the Gentiles and gathered his nine hundred iron chariots and his people to the river Kishon after hearing Barak had gone to Mount Tabor. His army was routed, and he abandoned his chariot and fled on foot to Jael's tent. There he asked for water, was given milk, asked Jael to deny his presence, and was killed in his sleep with a tent peg through his temples.

Chapter 5

Sisera is fought against by the stars from their courses and swept away with his forces by the river Kishon. He asks for water, receives milk, and is struck down by Jael, falling dead at her feet, while his mother waits at the window wondering why his chariot is delayed.
