falsehood

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 17: The Night Journey (Al-Israa), falsehood has vanished away and is bound to vanish, and those who follow it are described as astray and unable to find a road. In Surah 34: Sheba (Saba), falsehood will not show its face or return when the truth comes, while disbelievers describe the truth as invented falsehood or mere magic.
