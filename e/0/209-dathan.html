Chapter 16

Dathan, son of Eliab of Reuben, joins Korah's rebellion and refuses Moses' summons, saying 'We won't come up.' He accuses Moses of bringing them out of a land flowing with milk and honey to kill them and of making himself a prince. He stands at his tent door with his wife, sons, and little ones before the ground swallows them all alive.
