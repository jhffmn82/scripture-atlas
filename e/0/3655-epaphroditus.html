Abstract written by a model

Epaphroditus is Paul's brother, fellow worker and fellow soldier, and the Philippians' apostle and servant of Paul's need. He risked his life for Christ's work, supplying what they lacked in serving Paul. He fell nearly fatally ill, but God showed mercy. Longing for the Philippians, he was troubled that they had heard. Paul sends him back, asking them to receive and honor him joyfully.

Chapter 2

Epaphroditus is described by Paul as brother, fellow worker, fellow soldier, and the Philippians' apostle and servant of Paul's need. He was sick nearly to death but God had mercy on him; he longed for the Philippians and was troubled that they had heard of his illness. Paul sends him back so they may rejoice, and asks that they receive him with joy and honor him, since he risked his life for Christ's work to supply what was lacking in their service to Paul.
