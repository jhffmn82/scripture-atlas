hypocrites

group · 2 works · 16 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: the hypocrites, them

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), the hypocrites turn away from Allah's revelation and the messenger, waver between belief and disbelief, and face punishment unless they repent. In Surah 59: The Exile (Al-Hashr), the hypocrites promise to help their disbelieving brethren among the People of the Scripture but are declared liars and fear the believers more than Allah.

hypocrites in Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where hypocrites first appears

Abstract written by a model

The hypocrites are people who turn away with aversion when called to what Allah has revealed and to the messenger. Allah knows the secrets of their hearts; they are to be opposed, admonished, and addressed plainly about their souls. Believers are divided into two parties regarding them, while Allah has cast them back to disbelief because of what they earned. They desire believers to disbelieve as they do, choose disbelievers as friends instead of believers, and seek to beguile Allah. Their worship is languid and performed to be seen by people, and they are mindful of Allah only a little. They waver between believers and disbelievers, belonging fully to neither; when Allah causes them to go astray, they have no way. They receive a painful doom, are gathered with disbelievers into hell, and are in the lowest deep of the Fire with no helper. Yet those among them who repent, amend, hold fast to Allah, and make their religion pure for Allah are with the believers.
