Chapter 5

Prompted by a false rumor that Antiochus was dead, Jason attacked the city with at least a thousand men, routed those on the wall, and nearly took it while Menelaus fled to the citadel. Jason killed his own citizens without mercy. It failed to win him the government, and he fled to the Ammonites in shame.
