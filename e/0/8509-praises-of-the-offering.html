Praises of the Offering

object · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

Praises of the Offering is an offering to the bountiful Gâthas, produced by Mazda as most beneficent. It is invoked to appear with power, victory, health, healing, progress, growth, preparation, protection, beneficence, sanctity, and gifts for those who understand.
