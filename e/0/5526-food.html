CHAP. VIII. 1. He did not dislike to have his rice finely

He avoided food that was spoiled, discoloured, badly flavoured, ill-cooked, out of season, improperly cut, or served without its proper sauce. He did not eat much, did not converse while eating, and offered a little of even coarse rice and vegetable soup in sacrifice with a grave, respectful air.
