Chapter 22

Ahab is struck by an arrow between the joints of his armor and is propped up in his chariot facing the Syrians until he dies at evening, his blood running into the chariot. He is buried in Samaria, and the chariot is washed at the pool of Samaria where dogs lick his blood, as the LORD had said.
