Heaps of offerings (tithes, first fruits, cattle, sheep, dedicated things) were piled from the third to seventh month. Hezekiah, the princes and Azariah saw them, blessed the LORD, learned they were priestly surplus, and rooms were prepared in the LORD's house to store them.

Chapter 31

The offerings were laid in heaps, begun in the third month and finished in the seventh month. Hezekiah and the princes blessed the LORD and Israel on seeing them, and Azariah explained that the heaps were the surplus left after the priests had eaten their fill. Rooms were then prepared in the LORD's house to store them.
