Abstract written by a model

In Leviticus, the feast of weeks is a holy convocation dated by counting seven Sabbaths and fifty days from the wave sheaf, with offerings including two yeast-baked loaves and specified animal sacrifices. In Numbers, the Feast of Weeks is the day of the first fruits, observed with a new meal offering, a holy convocation without regular work, and specified animal sacrifices besides the continual burnt offering. In Deuteronomy, the feast of weeks is a festival beginning after seven weeks of counting, kept with a free will offering and rejoicing at the chosen place by the whole household and vulnerable members of the community.

feast of weeks (Pentecost, fifty days after the sheaf) in Leviticus

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where feast of weeks (Pentecost, fifty days after the sheaf) first appears

Abstract written by a model

The feast of weeks is a holy convocation, a statute forever with no regular work. It is dated by counting seven completed Sabbaths from the wave sheaf, then fifty days to a new meal offering. The people bring two yeast-baked loaves of fine flour as a first-fruits wave offering, along with burnt offerings of seven lambs, a young bull and two rams, a goat sin offering, and two lambs as peace offerings.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 23

The feast is dated by counting seven completed Sabbaths from the wave sheaf, then fifty days to a new meal offering. The people bring two loaves baked with yeast from two tenths of an ephah of fine flour as a wave offering of first fruits. With them they offer seven lambs, one young bull, and two rams as burnt offerings, one male goat as a sin offering, and two male lambs as peace offerings. The day is a holy convocation with no regular work, a statute forever.

Feast of Weeks in Numbers

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Feast of Weeks first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Feast of Weeks, also called the day of the first fruits, is a observance when a new meal offering is brought to the LORD. It features a holy convocation without regular work, and an offering of two young bulls, a ram, seven male lambs, and a goat for atonement, all without defect, besides the continual burnt offering.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 28

On the day of the first fruits, when a new meal offering is brought to the LORD at the feast of weeks, there is a holy convocation and no regular work. The offering is two young bulls, one ram and seven male lambs with their meal offerings, and one male goat to make atonement, besides the continual burnt offering, its meal offering and drink offerings, all without defect.

feast of weeks in Deuteronomy

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where feast of weeks first appears

Abstract written by a model

The feast of weeks is a festival that begins after counting seven weeks from first putting the sickle to standing grain. It is kept with a free will offering according to the LORD's blessing, and with rejoicing at the chosen place by the whole household, Levite, foreigner, fatherless, and widow.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

The feast of weeks comes after counting seven weeks from when the sickle is first put to the standing grain. It is kept with a free will offering given according to the LORD's blessing, and with rejoicing before him at the chosen place by the whole household, Levite, foreigner, fatherless, and widow. It is one of the three feasts at which males appear.
