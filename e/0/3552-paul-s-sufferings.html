Chapter 11

Paul lists his labors, prisons, stripes above measure and frequent deaths. These include five times receiving forty stripes minus one from the Jews, three beatings with rods, one stoning, three shipwrecks, and a night and a day in the deep. He adds many perils, hunger, thirst, cold, nakedness and daily anxiety for all the assemblies.
