Abstract written by a model

Man’s heart is revealed in speech, making lip-worship futile. He cannot sow poison and expect ambrosia, must perform God-destined work, lives by God’s gifts, and has God alone as his abiding place.

Slok XXII .. Pauri XXII

What a man has in his heart comes forth, so lip-worship is of no avail. A man who sows poison should not expect ambrosia, and a foolish man cannot perform his actions well.

Slok XXIV .. Pauri XXIV

Man must do the work that God destined for him from the beginning.
