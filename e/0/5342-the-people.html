Abstract written by a model

The people require a secure livelihood for a fixed heart; without it they fall into self-abandonment, moral deflection, depravity, and wild license, so they need fair treatment, education, mutual assistance, and protection from oppressive exactions.

Chapter 3

The people lack a fixed heart when they lack a secure livelihood, making them vulnerable to self-abandonment, moral deflection, depravity, and wild license. They should receive fair treatment, education, mutual assistance, and protection from oppressive exactions.
