Chapter 4

A woman of Samaria comes to draw water at Jacob's well and is asked by Jesus for a drink, which surprises her because Jews have no dealings with Samaritans. After he speaks of living water and tells her about her five husbands, she perceives he is a prophet, raises the question of where to worship, and hears him say he is the Messiah. She leaves her water pot, tells the people of the city to come see a man who told her everything she had done, and her testimony leads many to believe.
