lodging-place

Also called: ceded lodgings, house, lodging, lodging-place above ground, lodgings, place, underground place

Abstract written by a model

A lodging-place for religious posture must be inspected and cleaned, contain only few eggs or living beings, and avoid harmful preparation for the mendicant. It becomes unsuitable when prepared for the mendicant or when above ground, underground, householders’ space, inaccessible, dangerous, frequently visited, or sinfully prepared.

Second Lecture, Called Begging for a Couch. / First Lesson. .. Third Lesson.

A lodging-place must be inspected and cleaned, and it should not contain many eggs or living beings or require harmful preparation for the mendicant's sake. Lodging-places may be unsuitable because they are above ground, underground, used by householders, inaccessible, dangerous, frequently visited, or associated with various forms of sinful preparation.
