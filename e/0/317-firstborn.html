Abstract written by a model

The firstborn of Israel, both man and animal, belong to the LORD, made holy when he struck down Egypt's firstborn. Males from a month old number 22,273, and the Levites replace them. Firstborn men are redeemed for five shekels; cows, sheep and goats are holy, not redeemed.
