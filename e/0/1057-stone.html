Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, the cornerstone is laid in Zion by the Lord GOD as a precious, tried cornerstone of sure foundation; believers will not act hastily, and justice and righteousness become the measuring and plumb lines. In The Book of Jacob, the Brother of Nephi, the stone is the great and only sure foundation for the Jews to build on, is rejected, and becomes head of their corner. In The Book of Jacob, the Brother of Nephi, the foundation is safe for building and is associated with the rejected stone, the only sure foundation upon which the Jews can build.
