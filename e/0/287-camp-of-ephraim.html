Abstract written by a model

The camp of Ephraim, led by prince Elishama, includes Manasseh and Benjamin and encamps on the west side. Its divisions total 108,100 (Ephraim 40,500, Manasseh 32,200, Benjamin 35,400), and it sets out third in march order.
