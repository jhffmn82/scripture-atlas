sage

person · 1 work · 45 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: (ruling) sage, he, him, his, ruling sage, sage (ruler), the sage

Abstract written by a model

The sage is a ruler who governs without overt action or speech, directing people by emptying their minds, filling their bellies, weakening their wills, strengthening their bones, and keeping them without knowledge or desire. He has no personal or private ends: he puts his person last, treats it as foreign, and thereby preserves it and realises such ends. He saves every man and thing without casting anything away. When employed, he becomes Head of the Officers of government, yet uses no violent measures and harms neither men nor the departed. His manner joins opposites: he knows strength but maintains feebleness, knows white but keeps black, knows glory but loves disgrace, and displays humility. Constant excellence returns him to man's first state, embodied as the simple infant man. Seeking to be above and before men, he speaks below them and places himself behind them, so they feel neither burden nor injury; because he does not strive, none can strive with him. He wears hair cloth while carrying jade, feels difficulty over the man who dares wrong, and acts, gains merit, and refuses both possession of results and display of superiority.

2. 1. All in the world know the beauty of the beautiful, and in doing

The sage manages affairs without doing anything and conveys instructions without using speech.

3. 1. Not to value and employ men of superior ability is the way to

The sage exercises government by emptying the people's minds, filling their bellies, weakening their wills, and strengthening their bones. He keeps them without knowledge and desire and prevents those with knowledge from presuming to act on it.

7. 1. Heaven is long-enduring and earth continues long. The reason

The sage puts his own person last and treats it as foreign, yet his person is preserved. Because he has no personal and private ends, such ends are realised.

27. 1. The skilful traveller leaves no traces of his wheels or

The sage is always skilful at saving men and things, so he does not cast away any man or anything.

28.

When employed, the sage becomes the Head of all the Officers of government. In the greatest regulations, the sage employs no violent measures.

60. 1. Governing a great state is like cooking small fish.

The ruling sage does not injuriously affect the manes of the departed or hurt men.

66. 1. That whereby the rivers and seas are able to receive the homage

He wishes to be above men, so he puts himself below them in his words, and he wishes to be before them, so he places his person behind them. Although he is above and before men, they do not feel his weight or regard his position as an injury; because he does not strive, no one can strive with him.

70. 1. My words are very easy to know, and very easy to practise; but

The sage wears a poor garment of hair cloth while carrying jade in the bosom.

73. 1. He whose boldness appears in his daring (to do wrong, in

The sage feels difficulty about what to do in the case of the man who dares to do wrong.

77. 1. May not the Way (or Tao) of Heaven be compared to the (method

The ruling sage acts without claiming results as his, achieves merit without resting arrogantly in it, and does not wish to display superiority.
