Vayu

person · 1 work · 25 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: awful Vayu, great Vayu, holy Vayu, strong Vayu

Abstract written by a model

Vayu is a holy God belonging to Spenta Mainyu and the Good Spirit. Powerful beyond other creatures, he passes through two worlds as an overtaking, all-smiting worker of good, strong and golden-adorned. Worshipped by Ahura Mazda, he grants boons and protection to worthy worshippers, refuses Azi Dahâka’s request, and reveals many names.

Vayu is in one work on this site, Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

XV. Râm Yast.

Vayu is the central deity of the unit, described as holy, powerful, swift, strong, golden-adorned, and able to affect or overcome creatures in both worlds. He grants many boons to worshippers, refuses Azi Dahâka’s request, reveals numerous names for himself, and promises health and protection to those who worship him.
