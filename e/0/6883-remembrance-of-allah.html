remembrance of Allah

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: remembrance, remembrance of Me

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 20: Taa-Haa, remembrance of Allah is commanded for Moses and Aaron, Muhammad is told to glorify Allah at various times, and turning away from it is linked to a narrow life. In Surah 29: The Spider (Al-Ankaboot), remembrance of Allah is more important than the preserving effect of worship, and Allah knows what people do while they worship and remember Him.
