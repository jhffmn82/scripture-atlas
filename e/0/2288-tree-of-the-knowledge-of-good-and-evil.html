Abstract written by a model

The tree of the knowledge of good and evil stands in the middle of the garden beside the tree of life, made by the LORD God. He forbids eating or touching its fruit on pain of death. The woman sees it as good for food, a delight to the eyes, and desirable for wisdom. After the serpent claims it will open their eyes, she eats and gives to the man. God then confirms the man has become like one of us, knowing good and evil.

Chapter 2

This tree was one of the trees the LORD God made grow in the garden, standing in the middle along with the tree of life. The LORD God forbade the man to eat from it, saying that in the day he ate of it he would surely die.

Chapter 3

The tree in the middle of the garden is the one whose fruit God forbade eating or touching on pain of death. The woman sees it as good for food, a delight to the eyes, and desirable to make one wise, and its fruit is eaten by her and then the man. The serpent claims eating it will open their eyes so they know good and evil like God, and God later confirms the man has become like one of us, knowing good and evil.
