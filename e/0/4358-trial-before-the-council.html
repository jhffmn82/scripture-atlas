Abstract written by a model

A night trial of Jesus before the council at Caiaphas the high priest's house. After false testimony and two witnesses' temple claim, Jesus, silent until adjured, answers 'You have said so' and speaks of the Son of Man; the council declares him worthy of death, and some abuse him.

Chapter 26

At Caiaphas's house the chief priests, elders, and council seek false testimony; two witnesses finally claim Jesus said he could destroy the temple and rebuild it in three days. Jesus is silent until adjured by the high priest, then answers 'You have said so' and speaks of the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power. The council declares him worthy of death, and some spit on him, beat him, and slap him.
