Also called: the Samaritans, the multitudes, the people, the people of Samaria, they all

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to John, the Samaritans believe in Jesus, host him for two days, and proclaim him Savior of the world. In The Acts of the Apostles, the Samaritans listen to Philip, believe and are baptized, receive the Holy Spirit, and hear the Good News preached in their villages.

Samaritans in The Gospel According to John

author unknown · AD 90 to 100 · the work’s page · read it where Samaritans first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Samaritans live in Samaria and are shunned by Jews. Many from the city believe in Jesus through a woman's testimony, host him two days, and more believe his word, proclaiming him Savior of the world.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

Many Samaritans from the city believe in Jesus because of the woman's testimony, come out to him, and beg him to stay, and he remains two days. Many more believe because of his own word, and they tell the woman they now know for themselves that he is the Christ, the Savior of the world.

Samaritans in The Acts of the Apostles

author unknown · about AD 80 to 90 · the work’s page · read it where Samaritans first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Samaritans listened with one accord to Philip, and men and women believed and were baptized. They received the Holy Spirit when Peter and John prayed and laid hands on them, and Peter and John later preached the Good News in many Samaritan villages. Earlier they had listened to Simon the sorcerer and called him the great power of God.
