Paul’s Second Letter to the Corinthians / Chapter 1

Paul says that in Asia he and his companions were weighed down exceedingly, beyond their power, to the point of despairing of life. They felt they had received the sentence of death, which taught them to trust in God who raises the dead rather than in themselves. God delivered them from so great a death, and Paul hopes he will deliver them again.
