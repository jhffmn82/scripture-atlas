Chapter 14

Ahijah, speaking the LORD's words, charges Jeroboam with idolatry despite being exalted and given the torn kingdom, and announces that his house will be cut off and swept away like dung. He foretells the child's death on her return, a king raised up to end the house, and Israel's being uprooted and scattered beyond the River. The child's death and burial and Jeroboam's death are recorded as it begins to be fulfilled.
