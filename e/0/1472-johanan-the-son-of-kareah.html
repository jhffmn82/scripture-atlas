Johanan the son of Kareah

person · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Johanan

Abstract written by a model

Johanan, son of Kareah, is one of the captains of the forces. He first warns Gedaliah at Mizpah of Ishmael's plot and privately offers to kill Ishmael, but Gedaliah forbids it. After Ishmael's crime, Johanan pursues him, finds him at Gibeon, and recovers the captives, though Ishmael escapes. He leads the remnant to Geruth Chimham, intending to go to Egypt out of fear of the Chaldeans. He and the people ask Jeremiah to seek the LORD's guidance. Yet he calls Jeremiah a liar, disobeys the LORD's voice, and leads the remnant, with Jeremiah and Baruch, into Egypt as far as Tahpanhes.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 40

Johanan comes to Gedaliah at Mizpah with the captains and warns him of Ishmael's mission from Baalis. He privately offers to kill Ishmael secretly to protect the gathered Jews and the remnant, but Gedaliah forbids it.

Chapter 41

Johanan and the captains with him heard of Ishmael's evil, took their men, and pursued him, finding him by the great waters in Gibeon. He recovered the captives, who came over to him gladly, though Ishmael escaped. He then led the remnant to Geruth Chimham by Bethlehem, intending to enter Egypt.

Chapter 42

Johanan the son of Kareah, together with the captains and all the people, approaches Jeremiah to request a prayer for guidance. He is among those Jeremiah calls together to hear the LORD's answer.

Chapter 43

Johanan joins Azariah and the proud men in calling Jeremiah a liar. He and the captains of the forces then disobey the LORD's voice and lead the remnant of Judah, with Jeremiah and Baruch, into Egypt as far as Tahpanhes.
