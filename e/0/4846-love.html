love

topic · 4 works · 24 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: God's love, God’s love, a love, brotherly love, hates his brother, his love, love and hate of brother, love one another, loves his brother, perfect love, the love which God has for us, truth and love

Abstract written by a model

In Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians, love gives value to every gift and sacrifice, is patient and kind, and remains greatest among faith, hope, and love. In 1 Thessalonians, the believers are taught by God to show brotherly love and are exhorted to abound in it more and more. In 1 John, love of one's brother keeps a person in the light, while hatred leaves one in darkness. In 1 John, love for one another shows passage from death to life and must be demonstrated in deed and truth, following Christ's self-sacrifice. In 1 John, love originates from God, is shown in sending his Son, casts out fear, and requires loving one's brother. In 2 John, love is the commandment to love one another by walking according to God's commandments.

love in Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians

author unknown · AD 53 to 54 · the work’s page · read it where love first appears

Abstract written by a model

Love is presented as the quality that gives value to every gift and sacrifice; without it the speaker is nothing. It is patient, kind, not envious, boastful, proud, self-seeking or easily provoked, keeps no account of evil, rejoices with truth, and bears, believes, hopes and endures all things. It never fails, remains after prophecy, languages and knowledge pass away, and is greatest of faith, hope and love.

love and hate of brother in 1 John

author unknown · undated · the work’s page · read it where love and hate of brother first appears

Abstract written by a model

A contrast between loving and hating one's brother: whoever claims to be in the light yet hates his brother remains blind in darkness, not knowing his direction, while whoever loves his brother remains in the light without stumbling.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Whoever says he is in the light but hates his brother is still in darkness, walks in darkness, does not know where he is going, and has been blinded by the darkness. Whoever loves his brother remains in the light and has no occasion for stumbling in him.
