Also called: He, the lawless one, the man of sin, the son of destruction

Abstract written by a model

The man of sin, called the son of destruction, is a figure revealed after the rebellion. He opposes God, exalts himself above all called God or worshiped, and sits in God's temple, presenting himself as God. A restrainer holds him back until his own season, then is taken out of the way. His coming is by Satan's working, with power, signs, lying wonders and deception. The Lord Jesus Christ kills him with the breath of his mouth.

Chapter 2

The man of sin, the son of destruction, will be revealed after the rebellion comes, and he opposes and exalts himself against all that is called God, sitting in the temple of God and setting himself up as God. Something is restraining him until his own season, and the one who restrains is to be taken out of the way. His coming is by the working of Satan with all power, signs, lying wonders and deception, and the Lord will kill him with the breath of his mouth.
