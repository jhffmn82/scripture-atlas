Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the nations are commanded to clap their hands and shout to God with triumph, are subdued under God's people, and are ruled by God. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, the foreign nations are called to fear the Lord, recognize his power and uniqueness, and magnify his holiness. In The Book of Isaiah, the nations receive the LORD's servant as a light, bring Zion's sons and daughters, and see their kings and princes honor Zion. In The Book of Ezekiel, the nations learn that the LORD is holy, recognize his judgment, and understand Israel's captivity resulted from its iniquity.
