Diotrephes

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

Diotrephes is described as one who loves to be first among the assembly and does not accept what John and his companions say. He unjustly accuses them with wicked words, refuses to receive the brothers, and forbids and throws out of the assembly those who would; John says he will call attention to these deeds if he comes.
