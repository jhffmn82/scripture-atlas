the event

event · 5 works · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Al-Haaqqa, The Calamity, The Reality, The Reality (Al-Haaqqa), The True Promise, it, that which ye are promised, the Event, the True Promise, the promised event, this promise

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), the True Promise draws near when Gog and Magog are released and the disbelievers stare in terror. In Surah 56: The Inevitable (Al-Waaqia), the event will befall without denial, abase some, exalt others, shake the earth, and reduce the hills to scattered dust. In Surah 69: The Reality (Al-Haaqqa), The Reality is proclaimed, associated with the judgment to come and the final Event. In Surah 77: The Emissaries (Al-Mursalaat), the promised event will befall as the stars are put out, the sky is riven asunder, the mountains are blown away, and the messengers are brought to their appointed time. In Surah 101: The Calamity (Al-Qaari'a), The Calamity is announced and associated with a day when mankind will be scattered like moths and the mountains will become like carded wool.
