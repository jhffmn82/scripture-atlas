the drowning in the sea

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: We drowned those who denied Our revelations, abandoned them unto the sea, the (fate of) drowning, the Flood and drowning of Pharaoh

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 10: Jonas (Yunus), it encompassed the drowning of those who denied Allah’s revelations, including Pharaoh while pursuing the Children of Israel, whose body was preserved as a sign for those after him. In Surah 28: The Stories (Al-Qasas), it was the fate of Pharaoh and his hosts after their haughtiness and rejection of Moses, demonstrating the consequence awaiting evil-doers.
