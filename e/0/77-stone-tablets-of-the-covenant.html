Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, the two tablets of the covenant contained law and commands written by the LORD and were given to Moses on Mount Sinai. In Deuteronomy, the stone tablets of the covenant contained the ten commandments written by the LORD, were replaced after Moses broke the first set, and were placed in the ark.

stone tablets of the covenant in Deuteronomy

author unknown · 624 BC · the work’s page · read it where stone tablets of the covenant first appears

Abstract written by a model

The stone tablets of the covenant were written by the LORD's own finger with the ten commandments, containing all the words spoken on the mountain. The first set was given to Moses after forty days and nights, but he broke them upon seeing the calf. The LORD then had Moses cut two new tablets, rewrote the same words, and Moses placed them in the ark, where they remain.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 9

The LORD gave Moses two stone tablets written with God's finger, bearing all the words he had spoken from the fire on the day of the assembly, after forty days and nights. Moses threw them from his hands and broke them before the people's eyes when he saw the calf.

Chapter 10

The LORD told Moses to cut two stone tablets like the first, which Moses had broken, and he did so and carried them up the mountain. The LORD wrote on them the same words as the first tablets. Moses then put them in the ark, where they remain.
