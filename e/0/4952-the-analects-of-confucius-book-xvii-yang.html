Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns Confucius's conduct, teachings, and exchanges with figures including Yang Ho, Tsze-lu, Tsze-chang, Tsze-kung, and Tsai Wo. Confucius teaches about perfect virtue, learning, the Book of Poetry, propriety, music, mourning, righteousness, and the faults that arise when virtues are pursued without learning. He also comments on political employment, social conduct, speech, hatred of harmful character, and the persistence of a disliked character after forty.
