CHAPTER XXIV.

Wu Hou of Wei receives Hsü Wu Kuei and is told that his sensual rule oppresses his population while harming his soul. He wishes to love his people and end war through duty towards one's neighbour, but Hsü Wu Kuei says this approach will be disastrous.
