Abstract written by a model

A parable in Chapter 6 where Jesus asks why people call him 'Lord' yet disobey. Hearing and doing his words is like building on rock, so the house withstands the flood; hearing without doing is like building without foundation, so the house collapses in ruin.

Chapter 6

Jesus asks why people call him 'Lord, Lord' and do not do what he says. He likens the one who hears and does his words to a man who builds on rock, whose house withstands the flood, and the one who hears and does not do to a man who builds without a foundation, whose house falls in great ruin.
