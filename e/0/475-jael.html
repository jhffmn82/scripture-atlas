Abstract written by a model

Jael, wife of Heber the Kenite, is a woman who welcomed fleeing Sisera into her tent, gave him milk, and killed him with a tent peg and hammer while he slept, then showed his body to Barak. The following chapter celebrates her as blessed above women.

Chapter 4

Jael, wife of Heber the Kenite, welcomed the fleeing Sisera into her tent, covered him with a rug, and gave him milk. While he slept deeply, she struck a tent peg through his temples into the ground with a hammer, killing him. She then showed his body to Barak when he came pursuing.

Chapter 5

Jael, wife of Heber the Kenite, is named as a figure of the earlier days when highways were unoccupied. She is blessed above women for giving Sisera milk and butter and then striking through his head and temples with a tent peg and hammer.
