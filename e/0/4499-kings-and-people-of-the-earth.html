Abstract written by a model

The kings and people of the earth are a broad collective spanning kings, princes, commanding officers, the rich, the strong, slaves and free. First, fleeing wrath, they hide in mountain caves and rocks, begging them to fall on them and conceal them from the one on the throne and the Lamb. Later, the earth's inhabitants marvel at the first beast, worship the dragon and beast, and are deceived by the second beast into making an image and bearing its mark, except those written in the Lamb's book of life.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

The kings, princes, commanding officers, the rich, the strong, and every slave and free person hide in caves and among mountain rocks. They call on the mountains and rocks to fall on them and hide them from the face of the one on the throne and the wrath of the Lamb.

Chapter 13

The earth's inhabitants marvel at the first beast after its wound is healed and worship the dragon and the beast, asking who is like the beast and who can make war with it. All who dwell on the earth will worship the beast except those whose names are written in the book of life of the Lamb. They are deceived by the second beast's signs, urged to make an image, and made to bear the mark.
