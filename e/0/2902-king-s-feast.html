Esther (Greek) / Chapter 1

In his third year Ahasuerus made a feast for his friends, the nobles of the Persians and Medes, and the local governors, showing the wealth of his kingdom over one hundred eighty days. It was followed by a six-day banquet in the court of the king's house, richly adorned with fine linen, gold and silver couches, and costly cups. The banquet was not held according to appointed law but as the king desired.
