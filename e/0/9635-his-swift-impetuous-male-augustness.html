Abstract written by a model

In Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods), His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness is a Deity who rules the Sea-Plain, is expelled for his violence, and later protects Wondrous-Inada-Princess by slaying the eight-forked serpent of Koshi and discovering the great sword. In Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods), Crying and Weeping of His Impetuous-Male-Augustness is a Great Deity whose weeping brings omens of woe, who dwells in Nether-Distant-Land, and who commands Deity Great-Name-Possessor to rule the land.

His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness in Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods)

Futo no Yasumaro · the work’s page · read it where His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness first appears

Abstract written by a model

His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness is a Deity, born when the Great Deity the Male-Who-Invites washes his august nose. Charged to rule the Sea-Plain, he refuses it, weeps to depart for his deceased mother's Nether Distant Land, and is divinely expelled. After ascending to Heaven to take leave of his elder sister Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity, he swears with her and produces deities, becoming parent of the three female Deities born first. Claiming victory, he commits increasingly violent evil acts against her ricefields, palace, and weaving-hall. The eight hundred myriad Deities fine, mutilate, and expel him. He then kills Princess-of-Great-Food after judging her offering filth. Descending to the River Hi, he protects Wondrous-Inada-Princess by intoxicating and slaying the eight-forked serpent of Koshi, and discovers the great sword.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Sect.

He is born when the Great Deity the Male-Who-Invites washes his august nose.

Sect.

His-Brave-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness is charged to rule the Sea-Plain but does not assume that rule. He cries and weeps because he wishes to depart to the Nether Distant Land, and His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites expels him.

Sect.

He ascends to Heaven after being divinely expelled, saying that he wishes only to take leave of the Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity and depart. He swears with her and produces several deities from the curved jewels taken from her hair, head-dress, and arms.

Sect.

His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness is declared the parent of the three female Deities born first. Claiming victory, he ravages Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity's ricefields and palace and releases a flayed heavenly piebald horse into her weaving-hall.

Sect.

The eight hundred myriad Deities fine him a thousand tables, cut his beard, pull out his fingernails and toenails, and expel him. Afterward, he begs food from Princess-of-Great-Food and kills her because he believes she has offered him filth.

Sect.

He descends from Heaven to the head-waters of the River Hi, where he meets Foot-Stroking-Elder, Hand-Stroking Elder, and Wondrous-Inada-Princess. He agrees to protect Wondrous-Inada-Princess, arranges a liquor trap, intoxicates the eight-forked serpent of Koshi, and cuts it into pieces.

Crying and Weeping of His Impetuous-Male-Augustness in Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods)

Futo no Yasumaro · the work’s page · read it where Crying and Weeping of His Impetuous-Male-Augustness first appears

Abstract written by a model

Crying and Weeping of His Impetuous-Male-Augustness is a Great Deity whose intense weeping withers mountains, dries waters, and brings omens of woe. He searches Idzumo, builds and dwells in the palace of Suga, makes an august song, and appoints and names its headman. He dwells in Nether-Distant-Land, receives and tests Deity Great-Name-Possessor, then commands him to rule the land.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Sect.

His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness cries and weeps so intensely that his eight-grasp beard reaches his stomach. His weeping withers the green mountains, dries the rivers and seas, and brings forth portents of woe.

Sect.

His Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness searched in Idzumo for a place to build a palace, arrived at Suga, and built the palace of Suga there. He made an august song when clouds rose from the newly built palace. He appointed Deity Foot-Stroking-Elder as Headman of the palace and bestowed on him the name Master-of-the-Temple-of-Inada-Eight-Eared-Deity-of-Suga.

Sect.

His Impetuous-Male-Augustness receives the Deity Great-Name-Possessor and tests him by sending him into a snake-house, a centipede and wasp-house, and a burning moor. He later pursues him to the Even Pass of Hades and commands him to pursue his half-brethren and become Master-of-the-Great-Land and Spirit-of-the-Living-Land.
