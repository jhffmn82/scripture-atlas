Abstract written by a model

The virtuous man is self-controlled and nonviolent, subduing his senses and turning from unrighteousness to follow the true Law. He does not tremble at death; through discipline, he attains heavenly regions or liberation and is born as a god.

Fifth Lecture.

The virtuous control themselves and subdue their senses, and they do not tremble at the hour of death. Through discipline they can attain heavenly regions or liberation.

Seventh Lecture.

The virtuous man turns away from unrighteousness and follows the true Law. He is born as a god and cheerfully attains the state of gods.
