Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Luke, Jesus is carried up into heaven at Bethany after blessing the disciples, who worship him and return joyfully to Jerusalem. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the ascension is Jesus’s departure into heaven unto the Father, witnessed by the multitude as he is covered by a cloud and ascends.
