Abstract written by a model

The boards are the upright acacia-wood walls of the tabernacle, each ten cubits long and one and a half wide, with two tenons. Twenty stand on the south, twenty on the north, and eight on the west, including two corner boards. Each rests on two silver sockets, forty per long side and sixteen on the west. All are overlaid with gold and fitted with gold rings, and are held by acacia bars, five per side, the middle bar passing end to end.

Upright acacia boards, each ten cubits long and one and a half wide with two tenons, form the walls: twenty on the south, twenty on the north, and eight on the west including two corner boards.
