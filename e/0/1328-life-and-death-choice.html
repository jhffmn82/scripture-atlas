has_witness heaven and earth [called to witness against Israelites] The speaker sets before the Israelites life and prosperity on one side and death and evil on the other, and calls heaven and earth as witnesses. Loving and obeying the LORD leads to life, multiplication, and blessing in the land, while turning away to other gods means certain perishing. The Israelites are urged to choose life, for themselves and their descendants, by loving the LORD, obeying his voice, and clinging to him.
