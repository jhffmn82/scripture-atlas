Emperor Yū-Riyaku's mausoleum

place · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: His august mausoleum, august mausoleum of the Heavenly Sovereign Oho-hatsuse, the august mausoleum, the mausoleum

Abstract written by a model

Emperor Yū-Riyaku’s august mausoleum was at Takawashi in Tajihi in Kafuchi. Emperor Ken-Zō ordered it destroyed, but His Augustness Ohoke instead slightly excavated its side as an enduring token of insult.
