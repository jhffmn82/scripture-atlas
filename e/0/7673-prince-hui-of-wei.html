CHAPTER XXV.

Prince Hui of Wei becomes angry when prince Wei of Ch'i breaks a treaty and considers having him assassinated. He is then led to reconsider the scale and significance of his conflict through Tai Chin Jen's image of kingdoms on a snail's horns.
