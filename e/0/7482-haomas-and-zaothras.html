Haomas and Zaothras

object · 1 work · 22 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: offered Haomas and Zaothras

Abstract written by a model

Haomas and Zaothras are offerings: pungent, bounteous, holy, victorious smiters, and foes of hatred. Offered with sanctity for a blessing, they are celebrated now and in the future, being pressed with sanctity and yet to be pressed. They accompany healing virtues, Kisti, Mazda, Zarathustra, Zarathustrôtema, disciples and itinerants versed in good devices, Faith, Prayer, veracity, and the word against unbelief.

Visparad IX (Sp. X).

Haomas and Zaothras are offered, will yet be offered, and are described as smiting victoriously and being foes of hatred. They accompany healing virtues, Kisti, the remedies of Mazda, those of Zarathustra and the Zarathustrôtema, the good Mazdayasnian Faith, Prayer for blessings, good veracity, and the pious word against unbelief.
