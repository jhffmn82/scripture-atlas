Abstract written by a model

Adar is the twelfth month, when the king's letters arrived on the thirteenth, the day of killing; the following days, including the fifteenth in Susa, became rest and joy, turning the Jews' mourning into a holiday.

Chapter 9

Adar is the twelfth month, in which the king's letters arrived on the thirteenth day. The thirteenth was the day of the killing, and the fourteenth, and in Susa the fifteenth, became days of rest and joy. The whole month is described as one in which mourning turned to joy and sorrow to a holiday.
