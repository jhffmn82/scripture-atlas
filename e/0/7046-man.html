Abstract written by a model

In Surah 75: The Resurrection (Al-Qiyaama), man questions whether his bones will be assembled, wants to deny what is before him, asks when the Day of Resurrection will be, is a witness against himself despite his excuses, loves the fleeting Now, neglects the Hereafter, and will face judgment according to his trust, prayer, denial, and conduct. In Surah 103: The Declining Day, Epoch (Al-Asr), man is described as being in a state of loss.
