praise of Holiness

topic · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: recital of the praise of Holiness

Abstract written by a model

Praise of Holiness is a word of truth that glorifies Ahura Mazda, the waters, earth, cattle, plants, and all good things born of the good principle. Recited with full faith and a devoted heart, it praises these beings and things. Its value increases according to the moment of recitation, including eating, drinking Haoma, waking, sleeping, rising, dying, and cohabitation. In its greatest recital, worth all between earth and heavens, it renounces evil thoughts, words, and deeds.

XXI. Yast Fragment.

The praise of Holiness glorifies Ahura Mazda, the waters, the earth, the cattle, the plants, and all good things that are the offspring of the good principle. Its recitation is assigned increasing value when performed while eating, drinking Haoma, waking, sleeping, rising, dying, or renouncing evil thoughts, words, and deeds.
