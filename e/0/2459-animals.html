Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, animals, creeping things, and birds were to be destroyed with man, but God told Noah to bring pairs of them into the ship and keep them alive with food. In Ecclesiastes or, The Preacher, animals share the fate of humans, dying as people die, sharing one breath, and returning to dust. In Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra), Book I, animals are living beings that experience pleasure, displeasure, pain, terror, and unhappiness and are killed for sacrifices, bodily materials, or perceived danger. In Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra), animals include deer, cattle, birds, snakes, and aquatic animals, which monks and nuns should avoid injuring or disturbing. In Uttaradhyayana Sutra, creatures are to be treated kindly by ceasing to deprive, endanger, and combat them. In Uttaradhyayana Sutra, animals are movable organic beings classified by senses, kinds, habitats, durations, bodily continuance, and confinement for slaughter and consumption. In Uttaradhyayana Sutra, a wild animal lives and roams alone in forests, finding food and drink while healthy and receiving no care when sick. In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), animals are created beings whose species, forms, colours, genera, and kinds God records and creates, while caring for them and appointing Dharmraj to judge their acts.

animals in Asa ki War (Asa di Var)

Guru Nanak · the work’s page · read it where animals first appears

Abstract written by a model

Animals are created beings in all creation, living in water, dry land, and fourteen worlds. God records their names, creates their many species, forms, colours, genera, and kinds, cares for them, knows their condition, and appoints Dharmraj to judge their acts. They have no power of their own; all unfolds by God's power.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Slok II .. Pauri II

God created animals, recorded their names, and appointed Dharmraj to judge their acts.

Slok III .. Pauri III

Creatures have wonderful species, forms, colours, genera, and kinds. Their existence and characteristics arise by God's power.

Slok VIII .. Pauri VIII

Animals are among the beings created by God. God takes care of them all and knows their condition.

Slok XI .. Pauri XI

Creatures have nothing in their own power; God creates the different worlds and determines what takes place.
