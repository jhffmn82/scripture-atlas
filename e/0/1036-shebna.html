Shebna

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Shebna the scribe, disgrace of your lord’s house, this treasurer

Abstract written by a model

Shebna is a royal official, treasurer over the house and later scribe, who carved himself a high tomb in the rock. In Chapter 22 he is condemned as a disgrace to his lord's house and prophesied to be removed from office and hurled like a ball into a large country to die. In Chapter 36, as scribe, he joins Eliakim and Joah meeting Rabshakeh, stays silent, and reports to Hezekiah in torn clothes.

Shebna is in one work on this site, The Book of Isaiah. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 22

Shebna is the treasurer over the house who has cut himself a tomb on high, chiseled in the rock. He is told the LORD will hurl him away, wind him around and throw him like a ball into a large country where he will die, and that he is a disgrace to his lord's house. He will be thrust from his office and pulled down from his station.

Chapter 36

Shebna the scribe goes out with Eliakim and Joah to meet Rabshakeh and joins in asking him to speak Aramaic. He stays silent as commanded and returns to Hezekiah with torn clothes to report what was said.
