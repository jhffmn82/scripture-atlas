Heaven and Earth

place · 3 works · 10 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Heaven and earth, heaven and earth

Abstract written by a model

In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, Heaven and Earth are guided by the Tao and grow from the gate and valley spirit to send down sweet dew equally everywhere. In Chuang Tzŭ, heaven and earth are impartial to all creation and regain harmony when government ceases interfering with natural conditions. In The Doctrine of the Mean, Heaven and Earth attain happy order through Equilibrium and Harmony, join sacrifices serving God, and form a ternion with one who assists their transforming and nourishing powers.

heaven and earth in Chuang Tzŭ

Chuang Tzŭ · the work’s page · read it where heaven and earth first appears

Abstract written by a model

Heaven and earth, impartial to all creation, fall out of harmony when government interferes; irregular seasons, disturbed influences, and disorder follow, until inaction and compliance with natural conditions restore their proper relationship.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XI.

Heaven and earth fall out of harmony when government interferes, causing irregular seasons, disturbed influences, and disorder among living things. Their proper relationship is restored through inaction and compliance with natural conditions.
