Chapter 2

The lion, with his lioness and cubs, is pictured as living in a den where no one made them afraid. He tore prey in pieces for his cubs and lionesses and filled his caves and dens with the kill. The text asks where this den now is, and the LORD of Armies declares that the sword will devour the young lions and that he will cut off their prey from the earth.
