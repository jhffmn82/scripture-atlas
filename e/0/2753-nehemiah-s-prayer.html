Abstract written by a model

Nehemiah's prayer to God, day and night, on behalf of the children of Israel. After fasting, he appeals for a hearing, confesses Israel's and his father's house's sins, recalls the word to Moses, and asks for prosperity and mercy before this man.

The Book of Nehemiah / Chapter 1

Nehemiah prays after fasting, appealing to God to listen, confessing the sins of Israel and his own father's house, and recalling the word given to Moses about scattering and gathering. He closes by asking God to prosper him that day and grant him mercy in the sight of this man.
