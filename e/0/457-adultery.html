adultery

topic · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: You shall not commit adultery, adultery and lust

Abstract written by a model

In Proverbs, adultery is likened to carrying fire or walking on coals, destroys the soul when committed without understanding, brings wounds and lasting dishonor, and provokes the husband's vengeance. In The Gospel According to Matthew, adultery and lust includes lustful gazing as adultery in the heart, and Jesus teaches that avoiding such stumbling is worth losing an eye or hand rather than being cast into Gehenna.

adultery in Proverbs

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where adultery first appears

Abstract written by a model

Adultery is likened to carrying fire or walking on coals: unavoidably harmful. Committed without understanding, it destroys the soul, brings wounds and lasting dishonor, and provokes the husband's vengeance, unlike a hungry thief's forgivable theft.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Adultery is compared to scooping fire into one's lap or walking on hot coals, which cannot be done without burns. Unlike a hungry thief who is not despised, the adulterer faces destruction of his soul, unwiped reproach, and a husband's vengeance.
