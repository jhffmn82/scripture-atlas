Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Jesus is flogged, mocked, led out, and crucified at Golgotha between two robbers, dying amid darkness and other signs. In The Good News According to Mark, Jesus is crucified at Golgotha between two robbers, dies after darkness falls, and is declared the Son of God by the centurion. In The Gospel According to Luke, Jesus is crucified at The Skull between two criminals, mocked, and dies after darkness covers the land and the temple veil tears. In The Gospel According to John, Jesus is crucified at Golgotha between two others, declares "It is finished," dies, and has his side pierced while his legs remain unbroken. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), the text says that Jesus son of Mary was not crucified, but that it appeared so to those who differed concerning it.

Abstract written by a model

Jesus is flogged, mocked, and led out; Simon of Cyrene carries the cross. At Golgotha he is crucified between two robbers, his clothing divided by lot, under a 'King of the Jews' sign. Mocked by all, he dies amid darkness, a torn veil, an earthquake, opened tombs, and the centurion's declaration.

Chapter 27

After being flogged and mocked, Jesus is led out, Simon of Cyrene is compelled to carry the cross, and Jesus is crucified at Golgotha with two robbers, his clothing divided by lot and an accusation reading 'THIS IS JESUS, THE KING OF THE JEWS' above him. Passersby, priests, and the robbers mock him; darkness covers the land from the sixth to the ninth hour, and he dies, followed by the torn veil, an earthquake, opened tombs, and the centurion's declaration.
