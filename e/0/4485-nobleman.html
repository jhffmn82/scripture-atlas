Chapter 4

A nobleman whose son is sick at Capernaum goes to Jesus at Cana and begs him to come down and heal the boy, who is at the point of death. Jesus remarks that people will not believe without signs and wonders, but then says "Go your way. Your son lives." The man believes Jesus' word and goes, and when his servants meet him with news of recovery, he and his whole house believe.
