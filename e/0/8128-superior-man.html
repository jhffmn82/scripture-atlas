superior man

Also called: The superior man

Abstract written by a model

The superior man honours the left hand except in war, when the right takes precedence. He treats arms as ominous necessities, values calm over forced victory, and grieves bitterly for the multitudes he has killed.

31. 1. Now arms, however beautiful, are instruments of evil omen,

The superior man ordinarily considers the left hand the most honourable place, but in time of war he considers the right hand so. He does not regard sharp weapons as his instruments, uses them only from necessity, prizes calm and repose, and does not desire victory by force.
