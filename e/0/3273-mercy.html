mercy

topic · 3 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: divine mercy, have mercy on all men, merciful, the arm of mercy, the arms of mercy, the bowels of mercy

Abstract written by a model

In The Wisdom of Solomon, divine mercy extends to all men, overlooks human sins so they may repent, and chastens the righteous while sparing all things because they are God's. In The Book of Jacob, the Brother of Nephi, mercy is great and God's arm of mercy is extended toward Jacob's brethren in the light of the day. In The Book of Mosiah, mercy extends its arms to all mankind, but the wicked who refuse to depart or call upon the Lord lose any claim forever.

Abstract written by a model

extends_to men (humanity) [all men] God's mercy extends to all men because he has power to do all things, and he overlooks human sins so that they may repent. The text grounds this in his love for all that exists, his abhorrence of nothing he made, and his sparing of all things because they are his. The righteous were chastened in mercy, and the ungodly were not destroyed outright by a single breath.
