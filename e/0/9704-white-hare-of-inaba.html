Abstract written by a model

A hare from the Island of Oki deceives sea crocodiles to cross, but the last strips off its clothing. Harmed by eighty Deities’ instructions, it is restored with fresh water and sedge pollen by Great-Name-Possessor, becoming the Hare Deity.

The White Hare of Inaba tries to cross from the Island of Oki by deceiving the crocodiles, but the last crocodile strips off its clothing. After following the eighty Deities’ harmful instructions, it is healed by the Deity Great-Name-Possessor and becomes known as the Hare Deity.
