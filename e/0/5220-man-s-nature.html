man's nature

Also called: Man's nature, natural powers, nature, the feelings proper to humanity, the nature, the principles of our nature

Abstract written by a model

Man's nature is the nature of human beings: it naturally tends toward good and includes commiseration, shame and dislike, reverence and respect, and approving and disapproving. It begins with benevolence, righteousness, propriety, and knowledge, may be obscured by evil or neglect, and grows with proper nourishment.

Part I / Chapter 1 .. Chapter 16

Man's nature is presented as tending toward good and containing the beginnings of benevolence, righteousness, propriety, and knowledge. It can be weakened or obscured by harmful circumstances, neglect, and repeated exposure to evil, but proper nourishment allows it to grow.
