Eighth Lecture, (Called) the Pillow of Righteousness. / First Lesson. .. Fourth Lesson.

Mahâvîra stayed in workshops, assembly places, wells, shops, manufactories, sheds, travellers' halls, garden-houses, towns, burying-grounds, relinquished houses, and under trees. In these places he meditated, slept little, and endured attacks by animals, people, sounds, smells, and other disturbances.
