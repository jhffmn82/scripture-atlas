Abstract written by a model

A monk should be free from wrath, pride, deceit, greed, love, hate, quarrel, calumny, reviling, and delight in sensual things. Such freedom helps him cease acquiring gross Karman and abstain from sins. The passions include anger, pride, deceit, and greed. A monk who avoids these passions is calm and happy and is regarded as controlled and restrained.
