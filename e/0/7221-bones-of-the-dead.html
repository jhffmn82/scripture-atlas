bones of the dead

Also called: bone, bone of a dead dog, bones

Abstract written by a model

Bones of dead dogs or men must be kept in a building beyond dogs, foxes, wolves, and rain-water; if a building cannot be afforded, they are laid on a carpet and pillow under open sky. Ground placement incurs escalating penalties.

Fargard VI.

Bones of dead dogs or men placed on the ground incur penalties that increase with their size, from thirty stripes to one thousand stripes with each of the two instruments. The bones of the dead should be kept in a building beyond the reach of dogs, foxes, and wolves and where rain-water cannot stay.
