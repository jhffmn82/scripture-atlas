Abstract written by a model

Israel's watchmen are portrayed as failing guardians: blind, ignorant, mute dogs who cannot bark, dreaming and loving slumber. They are greedy shepherds without understanding, each pursuing personal gain, ending with plans to fill themselves with wine, assuming tomorrow will be as today.

Chapter 56

Israel's watchmen are described as blind, without knowledge, and like mute dogs who cannot bark, dreaming, lying down, and loving to slumber. They are also called greedy dogs and shepherds who cannot understand, each turning to his own way and gain, and they plan to fill themselves with wine and strong drink, assuming tomorrow will be as today, great beyond measure.
