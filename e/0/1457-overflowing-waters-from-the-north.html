Announced as evil and great destruction signaled by a standard and trumpet, it sweeps over the land, city and inhabitants, leaving earth waste and void, cities fleeing, and fields a wilderness, while men cry out and inhabitants wail.

Chapter 4

The LORD says he will bring evil and great destruction from the north, signaled by a standard toward Zion and the trumpet. Its effects include a hot wind that does not cleanse, destruction on destruction, the land laid waste, tents destroyed, and cities fleeing. The prophet sees the earth waste and void, mountains trembling, no man, birds fled, and the fruitful field a wilderness.

Chapter 47

Waters rise up out of the north and become an overflowing stream. They overflow the land and all that is in it, the city and those who dwell there, so that men cry out and all the inhabitants wail.
