In The First Book of Samuel, Mount Gilboa is where the men of Israel fall slain before the Philistines, Saul and his three sons die, and the Philistines find their bodies the next day. In 1 Chronicles, Mount Gilboa is where the men of Israel fall slain and where Saul and his sons die; the Philistines find their fallen bodies there the next day.
