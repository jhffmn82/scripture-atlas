Chapter 1

Elizabeth, a daughter of Aaron and wife of Zacharias, was barren and old but conceived, hiding herself five months and saying the Lord had taken away her reproach. She blesses Mary when she visits, is filled with the Holy Spirit, gives birth to a son, and insists he be called John.
