Emperor Chiū-Ai

person · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Heavenly Sovereign, Heavenly Sovereign Tarashi-naka-tsu-hiko, The Heavenly Sovereign, this Heavenly Sovereign Tarashi-naka-tsu-hiko

Abstract written by a model

Emperor Chiū-Ai is a Heavenly Sovereign who dwells at Toyora and Kashihi, rules the Empire, weds Oho-naka-tsu-hime and Empress Jin-Gō, fathers four sons, and dies aged fifty-two after refusing a divine promise concerning Korea; he is buried at Nagaye.
