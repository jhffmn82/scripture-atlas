Antioch

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of the Maccabees, Antioch is entered by Ptolemy, whose people later rise against Demetrius, are attacked and subdued by Jonathan's Jews, and are afterward controlled by Tryphon. In The Acts of the Apostles, Antioch has Gentile believers, sends relief to Judea, hosts the circumcision dispute and letter's reading, and is where Paul and Barnabas part ways.

Abstract written by a model

Antioch is a city with Gentile believers among its residents. Hellenists there believe the preached Lord Jesus; Barnabas and Saul teach for a year, and disciples are first called Christians. It sends relief to Judea, becomes the site of the circumcision dispute and letter's reading, and Paul and Barnabas part ways.

Chapter 11

In Antioch, the Hellenists hear the Lord Jesus preached and a great number believe and turn to the Lord. Barnabas and Saul teach many people there for a year, and the disciples are first called Christians in Antioch. Prophets also come there from Jerusalem, and its disciples send relief to Judea.

Chapter 15

Antioch is where the dispute over circumcision arises and where the letter is delivered and read to the gathered multitude, who rejoice. Paul and Barnabas remain there teaching, and it is from there that Paul and Barnabas part ways.
