Abstract written by a model

The five senses are foes whose desired objects cause passions and pain. Unrestricted eating strengthens their fire, while dispassion avoids harm; Gautama says he regularly vanquished them with the Self and cardinal passions.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Twenty-Third Lecture.

The five senses are counted among the ten foes. Gautama says he has regularly vanquished them along with the Self and the cardinal passions.

Thirty-Second Lecture.

The senses are strengthened by unrestricted eating, and their fires are not extinguished when a person eats as he wishes. Their objects cause pain to passionate people, but not to the dispassionate.
