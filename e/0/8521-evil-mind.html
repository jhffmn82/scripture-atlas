Evil Mind

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Worst Mind, the (personified) Worst Mind

Abstract written by a model

The Evil Mind is a personified worst mind, evil disposition, and wickedness expressed through actions. It approaches and is chosen by the Demon-gods, leading them toward Fury and pollution of mortal lives. It is the source of the Daêvas, opposed to the Good Mind, Ahura Mazda, and Righteousness; Zarathustra abjures it, while the wicked remain within it.

Evil Mind is in one work on this site, Yasna. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XXX.

The Worst Mind approached the Demon-gods so that it might be chosen. Their choice of it led them toward the Demon of Fury and the pollution of mortal lives.

Yasna XXXII.

The Evil Mind is the source from which the Daêvas are said to come. It is linked with those who confuse thoughts, abandon the Good Mind, and become estranged from Ahura Mazda and Righteousness.

Yasna XXXIII.

The Evil Mind is among the faults that Zarathustra abjures.

Yasna XLVII.

The Evil Mind is the sphere in which the wicked remain through their actions. The wicked person’s portion is far from Ahura Mazda’s love because of this alignment.
