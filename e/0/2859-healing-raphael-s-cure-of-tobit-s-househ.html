Abstract written by a model

Raphael, sent by God, heals Tobit's household. Fish bile rubbed on Tobit's eyes stings, and white films peel away. He sees Tobias, praises God's mercy after scourging, and Nineveh's people marvel while his kindred rejoice. Sarah is also healed.

Chapter 11

Tobit's blindness is cured when his eyes begin to hurt after the bile is applied, he rubs them, and the white films peel away from the corners. He sees Tobias, calls it God's mercy after scourging, and the people of Nineveh marvel when they see him go out. The event brings joy to his kindred.
