Chapter 21

A very great multitude spreads clothes and branches on the road, and those going in front and following shout 'Hosanna to the son of David!' They identify Jesus as the prophet from Nazareth of Galilee. The chief priests and Pharisees fear them and so do not seize Jesus, because the multitudes consider him a prophet.
