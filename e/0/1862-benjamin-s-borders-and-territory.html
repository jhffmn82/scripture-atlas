Abstract written by a model

Benjamin's territory is defined by its boundaries: a north border from the Jordan by Jericho through the hill country to Beth Aven, Bethel, and Ataroth Addar; the Jordan on the east; Kiriath Jearim ending the west; and a south border to the Salt Sea's north bay. Its cities include Jericho, Bethel, Gibeon, Ramah, and Jerusalem, grouped in twelve and fourteen.

Chapter 18

Benjamin's north border ran from the Jordan by Jericho through the hill country to the wilderness of Beth Aven, Luz (Bethel), and Ataroth Addar near lower Beth Horon. The west ended at Kiriath Jearim, the south ran by the spring of Nephtoah, the valley of Hinnom, En Rogel, and the stone of Bohan to the north bay of the Salt Sea, and the Jordan formed the east border. Its cities included Jericho, Bethel, Gibeon, Ramah, and the Jebusite city (Jerusalem), in groups of twelve and fourteen.
