Abstract written by a model

Two bronze pillars made by Hiram, each eighteen cubits high and twelve in circumference, topped with five-cubit molten bronze capitals decorated with nets, chains, pomegranates and lilies; set at the temple porch, right named Jachin, left Boaz.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

Hiram made two bronze pillars, each eighteen cubits high with a twelve-cubit circumference, topped with molten bronze capitals of five cubits decorated with nets, chain work, pomegranates, and lily work. They were set up at the porch of the temple, the right one named Jachin and the left one named Boaz.

Abstract written by a model

Two pillars made by Solomon stand before the LORD's house, thirty-five cubits high with five-cubit capitals. Chains and one hundred pomegranates adorn their tops. Set one on the right and one on the left, they are named Jachin and Boaz.

Chapter 3

Solomon made two pillars before the house, thirty-five cubits high, each with a capital of five cubits on top. Chains were made and put on the tops of the pillars along with one hundred pomegranates. He set them up one on the right and one on the left, naming them Jachin and Boaz.
