belief in Jesus Christ

topic · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: belief, belief in Jesus, believe, believe in me, believeth in me, the work of God, the works of God

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to John, belief in Jesus is described as the work of God that gives eternal life and lasting satisfaction, while some who see him disbelieve, many disciples leave, and Peter confesses him as the Christ, Son of the living God. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, belief in Jesus Christ is commanded by the Father and required for baptism, leading with baptism to the kingdom of God and salvation, bringing the Holy Ghost, including belief in the Father, and explaining Jesus’s choice of disciples and prayer for future believers.

belief in Jesus in The Gospel According to John

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where belief in Jesus first appears

Abstract written by a model

Belief in Jesus is described as the work of God and gives eternal life and lasting satisfaction. Some who saw him disbelieve, since coming requires the Father's gift; many disciples leave, but Peter confesses Jesus as the Christ, Son of the living God.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Jesus says the work of God is to believe in the one he sent, and that whoever believes in him will never be thirsty and has eternal life. Some of those who saw him do not believe, and he says no one can come unless the Father gives it. Many disciples leave, but Peter confesses belief that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of the living God.

belief in Jesus Christ in The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman

Mormon · the work’s page · read it where belief in Jesus Christ first appears

Abstract written by a model

Belief in Jesus Christ is commanded by the Father and required for baptism. With baptism it leads to the kingdom of God and salvation, brings the Holy Ghost, includes belief in the Father, and explains Jesus’s choice of disciples and his prayer for future believers.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

Jesus Christ teaches that the Father commands all men everywhere to believe in him. Those who believe in him and are baptized shall be saved, and those who believe in him also believe in the Father.

Chapter 19

Jesus says that he chose the disciples because of their belief in him. He asks the Father to give the Holy Ghost to those who will believe in the disciples' words because they believe in Jesus.
