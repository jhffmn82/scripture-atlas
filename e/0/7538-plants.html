plants

group · 3 works · 5 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: all the plants, bodies of plants, body of a plant, growing plants, the plants

Abstract written by a model

In Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra), Book I, plants are living bodies that are born, grow old, fall sick when cut, need food, decay, change, and are not eternal; injuring them is sinful and must be renounced. In Sutrakritanga, Book II, plants are described among the bodies in which beings are born, grow, and feed, with their roots and other parts associated with beings having manifold bodily qualities. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, plants grow from earth by never-failing springs, continue growing after Angra Mainyu’s malice is destroyed, are created by Ahura Mazda, and flourish through the Fravashis to nourish animals and people.
