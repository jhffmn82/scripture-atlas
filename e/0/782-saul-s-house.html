Abstract written by a model

Saul's house is a royal household that weakens during a long war with David's house, as its strongman Abner vows to transfer the kingdom to David. Later David shows kindness, and Mephibosheth, the remaining member, receives all Saul's holdings.

Chapter 3

Saul's house grows weaker and weaker during the long war with David's house. Abner, its strong man, vows to transfer the kingdom away from it to David.

Chapter 9

David seeks anyone left of Saul's house in order to show kindness. Ziba, a servant of the house, is found, and Mephibosheth is identified as the remaining member; everything belonging to Saul and his house is given to Mephibosheth.
