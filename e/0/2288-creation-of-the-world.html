Abstract written by a model

The world's creation unfolds over six days, each ending with evening and morning: light, sky, land and plants, sun, moon and stars, sea creatures and birds, then land animals and man; God judges it very good.

creation and provision (topic) in Psalms

author unknown · undated · the work’s page · read it where creation and provision (topic) first appears

Abstract written by a model

The psalm presents creation as the LORD's wise work and ongoing provision: springs and rain, grass for livestock, plants, wine, oil, and bread for man, and trees for birds. The moon and sun mark seasons and the cycle of night and day, in which animals prowl and man labors. All creatures depend on him for food in due season, and he renews the face of the ground by sending out his Spirit.
