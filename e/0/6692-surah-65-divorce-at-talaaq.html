Abstract written by a model

The unit concerns divorce, the conduct of men and women during the legal waiting period, and duties owed to Allah. Men are instructed to keep women in their houses during the period, provide for them, and either take them back or part from them kindly. Allah promises a way out, provision, ease after hardship, and forgiveness to those who keep their duty to Him. The unit also warns of punishment for communities that revolt against Allah's ordinance and describes believers, Allah's revelations, light, Gardens, the seven heavens, and the earth.
