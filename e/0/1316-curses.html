curses

Also called: Cursed, all these curses, cursing, the curse

Abstract written by a model

The curses are covenant penalties tied to disobedience, not listening to the LORD's voice. In chapter 27 the Levites declare twelve of them aloud, covering secret idolatry, dishonoring parents, moving a landmark, misleading the blind, withholding justice from the foreigner, fatherless, and widow, sexual offenses, secret murder, bribery to kill the innocent, and failing to uphold the law, with all the people answering 'Amen.' Chapter 28 reverses the blessings at greater length: confusion, disease, drought, defeat, loss, foreign domination, siege cannibalism, and exile, pursuing the people until destroyed, serving as a sign and wonder forever.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 27

Twelve curses are to be declared by the Levites in a loud voice, covering secret idolatry, dishonoring parents, moving a landmark, leading the blind astray, withholding justice from the foreigner, fatherless, and widow, several sexual offenses, secret murder, taking a bribe to kill the innocent, and not upholding the law. The people are to answer 'Amen' after each one.

Chapter 28

The curses are the reverse of the blessings and are set out at far greater length, running from verse 15 to the end of the chapter. They include confusion, pestilence, disease, drought, defeat, loss of wives, houses, crops, and children, foreign domination, siege cannibalism, and exile. They are said to pursue and overtake the people until they are destroyed, and to serve as a sign and wonder to their offspring forever.
