Abstract written by a model

Gog's army is a coalition of Persia, Cush, Put, Gomer with all his hordes, and Togarmah from the far north, with armored horses and horsemen, shields, and swords. They advance like a storm and cloud, but the LORD's judgment destroys them: swords turn on brothers, with rain, hailstones, fire, sulfur. Sheba, Dedan, Tarshish merchants question Gog's plunder motive.

Chapter 38

Gog's force includes horses and horsemen in full armor, with bucklers, shields, and swords, along with Persia, Cush, Put, Gomer and all his hordes, and the house of Togarmah from the far north. They will come like a storm and a cloud covering the land, but they will be struck by the LORD's judgment, with every man's sword against his brother and torrential rain, hailstones, fire, and sulfur falling on them. Sheba, Dedan, and the merchants of Tarshish are separately shown questioning whether Gog has come for plunder.
