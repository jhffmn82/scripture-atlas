Chapter 16

Twenty thousand men of war and cavalry went out under John, slept at Modin, and met Cendebaeus's large force across a brook in the plain. John crossed first, encouraging the fearful men to follow, and placed the cavalry among the infantry. At the sound of the trumpets Cendebaeus's army fled, Judas was wounded, John pursued to Kidron and burned the towers at Azotus, and about two thousand enemy fell.
