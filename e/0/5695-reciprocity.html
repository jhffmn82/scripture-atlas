Reciprocity is one word and a rule of practice for all one’s life: when one does not want an action done to oneself, one avoids doing it to other people.

Reciprocity is identified by the Master as a word that can serve as a rule of practice for all one's life. It means not doing to others what one does not want done to oneself.
