Chapter 5

Husbands are commanded to love their wives as Christ loved the assembly and gave himself up for her, and to love them as their own bodies. The text says that a husband who loves his wife loves himself, that he is head of the wife as Christ is head of the assembly, and that each must love his wife as himself.
