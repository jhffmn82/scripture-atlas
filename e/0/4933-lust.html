lust

topic · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: sensuous enjoyments, worldly desires, worldly love

Abstract written by a model

In Hymns of Guru Nanak, lust is described as a worldly desire that ruins sinners when joined with wrath, draws human beings away from the Real Thing, and prevents those absorbed in it from recognizing God’s essence. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XVI (Ke She), lust is described as a danger against which the superior man guards in youth, when his physical powers are not yet settled.
