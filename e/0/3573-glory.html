glory

topic · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 4 connections
Also called: Glory!, glory to glory, the glory of his face, the glory that surpasses

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, glory is due to the LORD, including the glory due his name, and everything in his temple says, "Glory!" In 2 Corinthians, glory characterizes the passing service of death and condemnation and the enduring, greater service of the Spirit and righteousness, transforming believers from glory to glory.
