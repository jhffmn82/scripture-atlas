Abstract written by a model

Naphtali is a tribal group whose territory is a land allotment. It received the sixth lot, with a border from Heleph to the Jordan and Hukkok, bounded by Zebulun, Asher and Judah, and held nineteen cities with villages, including fortified Ziddim, Hammath, Chinnereth and Hazor.

Chapter 19

The sixth lot came out for Naphtali, whose border ran from Heleph and the oak in Zaanannim to Lakkum and the Jordan, then west to Aznoth Tabor and Hukkok. It touched Zebulun on the south, Asher on the west and Judah at the Jordan, and had nineteen cities with villages, including the fortified cities Ziddim, Hammath, Chinnereth and Hazor.
