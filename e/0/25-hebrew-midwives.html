Abstract written by a model

Shiphrah and Puah, Hebrew midwives, feared God and defied the Egyptian king's order to kill newborn Hebrew boys. Questioned by Pharaoh, they claimed the women gave birth too quickly; God rewarded them with families.

Exodus / Chapter 1

Shiphrah and Puah were told by the king of Egypt to kill Hebrew baby boys at birth but feared God and saved the boys alive. When questioned, they told Pharaoh that Hebrew women are vigorous and give birth before the midwife arrives. God dealt well with them and gave them families.
