corpse uncleanness

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a bone of a man, a dead person, a grave, impurity, the dead, the dead body of any man, the slain, unclean, uncleanness

Abstract written by a model

Corpse uncleanness is a seven-day impurity from touching a dead person, slain person, bone, grave, or being in a tent where someone dies. It spreads to open vessels and to things the unclean person touches until evening. Purification with water on the third and seventh days is required; otherwise the tabernacle is defiled and the person is cut off from Israel.

Chapter 19

Touching a dead person, or a slain person, a bone, or a grave in the open field, or being in a tent where a man dies, makes one unclean seven days. Open vessels without a bound covering are also unclean, and what the unclean person touches becomes unclean until evening.
