Noah's folk

group · 5 works · 13 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: No-one of thy folk, The folk of Noah, his folk, his people, my own folk, my people, people, people (Noah's people), the folk of Noah, the wrongdoing folk, thy folk

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 11: Hud, Noah's folk, led by chieftains with few believers, reject, dispute, and mock Noah, and their wrongdoing ends in drowning in the Flood while he builds the ship. In The Believers (Al-Muminoon), Noah's folk reject his call, say he is only a mortal like them or a man in whom there is madness, and are overtaken by the flood and destroyed. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), Noah's folk deny the messengers, reject Noah because lowly people follow him, threaten to stone him, and are drowned after Noah and those with him are saved. In Surah 54: The Moon (Al-Qamar), the folk of Noah deny and call Noah a madman, and he is repulsed. In Surah 71: Noah (Nooh), Noah's folk are warned to serve Allah, keep their duty, obey Noah, and seek pardon, but they resist his calls, cover themselves, persist in refusal, act proudly, follow leaders, refuse to forsake their gods, disobey Noah, and lead others astray.
