Chapter 7

The speaker admits having sinned against the LORD and accepts his indignation until the LORD pleads his case. The unit then praises the LORD as a God who pardons iniquity and passes over the disobedience of the remnant, who does not keep anger forever, and who will have compassion again, tread iniquities underfoot, and cast all sins into the depths of the sea.
