Ithamar

Also called: sons of Ithamar, the sons of Ithamar

Abstract written by a model

In Leviticus, Ithamar, a surviving son of Aaron, was anointed and instructed by Moses regarding his conduct, duties, and remaining at the Tent of Meeting; Moses was angry with him and Eleazar when the sin offering goat was found burned. In 1 Chronicles, Ithamar served as priest alongside Eleazar after Nadab and Abihu died childless; his line had eight fathers' houses heads and includes Ahimelech.
