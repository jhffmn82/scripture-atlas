Chapter 7

Judas encamped at Adasa with three thousand men and prayed that God would crush the army as his angel had struck down one hundred eighty-five thousand blasphemers before. On the thirteenth of Adar the armies met, Nicanor was the first to fall, and his army threw away their weapons and fled. They were pursued a day's journey to Gazara and men from surrounding villages turned them back, so that all fell by the sword and none were left.
