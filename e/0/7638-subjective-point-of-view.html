CHAPTER XXIII.

The subjective point of view begins with life and, guided by knowledge, develops into judgments of right and wrong. People mistake their own standards for universal standards and value usefulness, success, and present circumstances according to limited perspectives. The text presents every part of a thing as capable of being a point of view and treats individual standards as fallible.
