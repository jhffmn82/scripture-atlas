Abstract written by a model

Monks during the Paggusan follow rules for residence, travel, alms, diet, permissions, cleanliness, inspection, lodging, speech, disputes, and movement. They range within a Yogana and a Krosa, collect alms at a fixed time with a teacher’s permission, eat one meal daily, and limit drinks when healthy. Longer fasts permit hot pure water. They restrict house visits, use an alms-bowl, seek permission for cures and exalted penance, and maintain three lodging-places, inspected easing spots, and three pots to support control.

Rules for Yatis.

Monks during the Paggusan must observe rules governing residence, travel, alms collection, diet, permissions, bodily cleanliness, inspection of living beings and moisture, lodging, speech, disputes, and movement. They must often seek permission from a superior before undertaking ordinary activities and must maintain practices intended to support control.
