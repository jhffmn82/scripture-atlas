Abstract written by a model

Alexander, son of Philip from Chittim, defeated Darius of the Persians and Medes and reigned in his place over Greece. He fought many battles, took strongholds, killed kings, and collected tribute until he fell sick, divided his kingdom among his servants, and died after twelve years. A king of Syria and Ptolemy's son-in-law, he was accused by Ptolemy, lost his wife, was routed, fled to Arabia, and was beheaded by Zabdiel.

Chapter 1

Alexander, son of Philip from the land of Chittim, struck Darius king of the Persians and Medes and reigned in his place over Greece. He fought many battles, took strongholds, killed kings, and collected tribute from many nations until he fell sick. He divided his kingdom among his servants while still alive, reigned twelve years, and died.

Chapter 11

King Alexander was Ptolemy's son-in-law, and Ptolemy accused him of trying to kill him and coveting his kingdom. While Alexander was in Cilicia putting down a revolt, Ptolemy took his wife away from him. Alexander fought Ptolemy, was routed, fled to Arabia, and was beheaded by Zabdiel the Arabian.
