Stephen

Abstract written by a model

Stephen is one of the chosen seven men, full of faith, power, and the Holy Spirit. He performs wonders and signs among the people and speaks with wisdom that opponents cannot withstand. Falsely accused of blasphemy against Moses, God, the holy place, and the law, he is seized and brought before the council, which sees his face like an angel's. He answers the high priest with a long review of Israel's history and accuses his hearers of resisting the Holy Spirit. He sees God's glory and Jesus at God's right hand, is stoned, entrusts his spirit to Jesus, asks forgiveness for his killers, and dies.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Stephen, described as full of faith and the Holy Spirit, is chosen as one of the seven men and is set before the apostles, who pray and lay hands on him. Full of faith and power, he performs great wonders and signs among the people, and his opponents cannot withstand his wisdom and the Spirit by which he speaks. He is seized, brought before the council, and accused of blasphemy against Moses, God, the holy place and the law, yet the council sees his face like the face of an angel.

Chapter 7

Stephen answers the high priest with a long review of Israel's history, then accuses his hearers of resisting the Holy Spirit and killing the Righteous One. Full of the Holy Spirit, he sees the glory of God and Jesus standing at God's right hand, and is stoned. He calls on the Lord Jesus to receive his spirit and asks that the sin not be held against his killers before he falls asleep.
