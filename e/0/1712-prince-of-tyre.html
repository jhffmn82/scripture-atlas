Abstract written by a model

The prince of Tyre is a wise, wealthy trader whose riches make him proud. He falsely claims to be a god and sits in God's seat, though he is only a man. He is therefore condemned to die at strangers' hands, the death of the uncircumcised.

Chapter 28

The prince of Tyre is accused of lifting up his heart, claiming 'I am a god' and sitting in God's seat, though he is only a man. Because of his wisdom and riches from trading, he is told that strangers will bring him to the pit and he will die the death of the uncircumcised.
