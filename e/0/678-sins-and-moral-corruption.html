Abstract written by a model

In The Wisdom of Solomon, moral corruption is described among the consequences of idolatry, including murder, theft, deceit, adultery, and other evils. In The Book of Hosea, sins and moral corruption are listed as the people's offenses and causes of punishment, mourning, and ruin.
