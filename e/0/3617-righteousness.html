righteousness of God in Paul’s Letter to the Romans

author unknown · about AD 57 · the work’s page · read it where righteousness of God first appears

Abstract written by a model

The righteousness of God is a righteousness revealed in the Good News and received through faith. Chapter 1 says it is revealed from faith to faith, citing that the righteous shall live by faith. Chapter 3 says it is revealed apart from the law, though the law and prophets testify to it, and comes through faith in Jesus Christ to all who believe, showing God just and justifier. Chapter 10 contrasts it with Israel's own law-based righteousness, which they did not submit to, and ties it to heart belief.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

God's righteousness is said to be revealed in the Good News from faith to faith. Paul supports this by quoting the text, 'the righteous shall live by faith.'

Chapter 3

The righteousness of God has been revealed apart from the law, though testified by the law and the prophets. It comes through faith in Jesus Christ to all who believe, and God demonstrated it through the passing over of prior sins and at the present time, so that he is himself just and the justifier of the one who has faith in Jesus.

Chapter 10

Two kinds are contrasted: Israel's own righteousness, which they sought to establish, and God's righteousness, to which they did not submit. Moses writes of the righteousness of the law, where the one who does the commands lives by them, while the righteousness of faith speaks of the near word. With the heart one believes resulting in righteousness.

righteousness in Paul’s Letter to the Philippians

author unknown · about AD 61 · the work’s page · read it where righteousness first appears

Abstract written by a model

Paul distinguishes the righteousness of the law, which he once had and found blameless, from the righteousness which is from God by faith in Christ. He wants to be found in Christ not having a righteousness of his own, but the one from God through faith.
