animals

Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, animals were first slated for destruction with man, then two of every sort were brought into Noah's ship to keep them alive, along with food. In Ecclesiastes or, The Preacher, animals share the fate of the sons of men, dying as people die, having one breath, and coming from and returning to dust, while the narrator questions whether their spirit goes downward.
