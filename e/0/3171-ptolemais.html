Also called: the cities of Ptolemais

Abstract written by a model

Ptolemais is a city contested among rulers: Demetrius offered it to the Jerusalem sanctuary, Alexander took it, wed Cleopatra and honored Jonathan there, but Tryphon's promise of it to Jonathan ended with gates shut and Jonathan seized.

Ptolemais is in one work on this site, The First Book of the Maccabees. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 10

Alexander took possession of Ptolemais in the 160th year and was received there. Demetrius offered it as a gift to the Jerusalem sanctuary. Alexander later wed Cleopatra there and met Jonathan, whom he honored.

Chapter 12

Tryphon promised to give Ptolemais to Jonathan, along with other strongholds, forces and officers. When Jonathan entered, its gates were shut and he was seized.
