Abstract written by a model

Micah of Morasheth is a prophet who receives the LORD's word during the reigns of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah of Judah, concerning Samaria and Jerusalem. He begins by lamenting judgment on both, going stripped and naked and wailing because the wound has reached Judah. He then declares himself full of power by the LORD's Spirit, and of judgment and might, to confront Jacob's disobedience and Israel's sin. He ends in personal humility: mourning a faithless land, acknowledging his own sin and the LORD's indignation, yet waiting for the God of salvation, expecting light in darkness and rising after a fall.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Micah receives the LORD's word in the days of Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, concerning Samaria and Jerusalem. He laments and wails, going stripped and naked and howling like jackals, because the wound has reached Judah. He calls on the people to shave their heads in mourning.

Chapter 3

The speaker calls the heads and rulers to listen and challenges them to know justice. He declares himself full of power by the LORD's Spirit, and of judgment and might, to declare Jacob's disobedience and Israel's sin.

Chapter 7

The speaker cries 'Misery is mine!' over a land where the godly are gone and trust is impossible even among family. He resolves to look to the LORD and wait, acknowledges that he has sinned and will bear the LORD's indignation, and expects to rise after falling and to see the LORD's righteousness. He also prays for the LORD to shepherd his people.
