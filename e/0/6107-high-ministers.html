high ministers

Also called: high ministers who are noble and relatives of the prince, high ministers who were of a different surname from the prince

Abstract written by a model

High ministers are ministers of a prince, divided by kinship to him. All remonstrate over faults; noble relatives may dethrone a prince who repeatedly ignores them, while different-surname ministers leave the State when their rejected advice is not accepted.

Chapter 6 .. Chapter 9

High ministers who are noble and relatives of the prince should remonstrate with him over great faults and may dethrone him if he repeatedly refuses to listen. High ministers of a different surname should remonstrate and then leave the State if their advice is rejected.
