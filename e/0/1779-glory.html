glory

topic · 3 works · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: glory of God, glory of the LORD, his glory, the LORD’s glory, the beauty of the glory from God, the glory of God, the glory of the God of Israel

Abstract written by a model

In Leviticus, the glory of the LORD appears to all the people after Moses and Aaron bless them. In Baruch, the glory of God is Jerusalem's beauty and the light in which God leads Israel with joy. In The Book of Ezekiel, the glory of the God of Israel approaches from the east, illuminates the earth, enters the house through the east-facing gate, and fills it.

Chapter 43

The glory of the God of Israel comes from the east with a voice like many waters, and the earth is illuminated by it. It enters the house by the east-facing gate and fills the house. Its appearance matches the earlier visions, including the one by the river Chebar.
