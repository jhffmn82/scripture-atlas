devil

person · 7 works · 25 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: Your adversary, the devil, a devil, adversary, an enemy, devils, enemy, his comrade, spirit of the devil, the devil, thine adversary, your open foe

Abstract written by a model

In 1 Peter, the devil is the believers’ adversary, prowling like a roaring lion to devour them, whom they are urged to resist steadfastly in faith. In Surah 20: Taa-Haa, the devil whispers to Adam and presents the tree of immortality and imperishable power, leading Adam and his wife to eat from it and become aware of their shame. In Surah 36: Yaseen (Yaseen), the devil is an open foe of the sons of Adam and has led a great multitude astray. In Surah 43: Ornaments of gold (Az-Zukhruf), a devil is assigned as a comrade to those who are heedless of the remembrance of the Beneficent and turns them from Allah’s way. In The Book of Mosiah, the devil exercises power over the wicked who remain carnal and rebellious, subjecting them through their desires and receiving them into endless damnation. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the devil shepherds those who reject the good shepherd, leads them to evil and death, ensnares and deceives them, and works to destroy the children of God. In The Book of Moroni, the devil continually fights against God and persuades people toward sin and evil and away from belief in Christ.

devil in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where devil first appears

Abstract written by a model

The devil is a shepherd of those who reject the good shepherd, leading followers to evil works and death. He captivates and takes captive people who harden their hearts until they know nothing of God’s mysteries. Through cunning plans, he ensnares hearts and contributes to inequality. He influences deception, including Zeezrom’s plan, and deceives Korihor by appearing as an angel and teaching him there is no God. An enemy to all righteousness, he works to destroy the children of God and may overpower those not watchful unto prayer. Those who delay repentance until death become his subjects; he enters the wicked, takes possession of their house, and leads them by his will. After the church is established throughout the land, it overcomes him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

The devil is the shepherd of those who will not hearken to the voice of the good shepherd. Those who follow the devil bring forth evil works and receive death as their wages.

Chapter 9

The devil is associated with the power and captivation according to which evil people will reap the damnation of their souls.

Chapter 12

The devil influences Zeezrom's subtle plan to lie and deceive the people. Those who harden their hearts receive less of the word until they know nothing of God's mysteries and are taken captive by the devil.

Chapter 28

The devil brings the power that contributes to the inequality of man through cunning plans designed to ensnare the hearts of men.

Chapter 30

The devil was said to have power over Korihor and to carry him about while working devices to destroy the children of God. Korihor later confessed that the devil appeared to him as an angel and taught him what to say.

Chapter 34

The devil is an enemy to all righteousness and may overpower those who are not watchful unto prayer. He possesses the bodies of those who procrastinate repentance until death and seals them as his subjects.

Chapter 40

The devil enters into the wicked and takes possession of their house. He leads them captive by his will.
