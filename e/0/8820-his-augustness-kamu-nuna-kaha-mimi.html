His Augustness Kamu-nuna-kaha-mimi

person · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Emperor Sui-Zei, His Augustness Take-nuha-kaha-mimi, His Augustness Take-nuna-kaha-mimi, The Heavenly Sovereign

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Kamu-nuna-kaha-mimi, also called His Augustness Take-nuna-kaha-mimi, is a son of His Augustness Kamu-yamato-ihare-biko and a Heavenly Sovereign. Threatened by His Augustness Tagishi-mimi, he takes a weapon from His Augustness Kamu-ya-wi-mimi and kills Tagishi-mimi, then rules the Empire. He dwells at Takawoka, weds Kaha-mata-bime, begets Shiki-tsu-hiko-tama-demi, lives forty-five years, and has a mausoleum on the Mound of Tsukida.

Sect. XLIV.—Reign of the Emperor Jim-Mu (Part I.—His Progress Eastward, and Death of His Elder Brother) .. Sect. LIV.—Emperor Jim-Mu (Part XI.—His Age and Place of Burial).

He is one of the three younger brethren threatened by His Augustness Tagishi-mimi. He takes the weapon from His Augustness Kamu-ya-wi-mimi and kills His Augustness Tagishi-mimi, after which he is called His Augustness Take-nuna-kaha-mimi and rules the Empire.

Sect. LV.—Emperor Sui-Zei.

Emperor Sui-Zei, called His Augustness Kamu-nuna-kaha-mimi, dwelt at the palace of Takawoka in Kadzuraki and ruled the Empire. He wedded Kaha-mata-bime and lived for forty-five years. His august mausoleum is on the Mound of Tsukida.
