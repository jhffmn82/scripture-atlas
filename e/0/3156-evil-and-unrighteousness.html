Chapter 7

The chapter opens by urging that doing no evil keeps evil from overtaking a person, and departing from wrong makes it turn away. Sowing on the furrows of unrighteousness leads to reaping it sevenfold. The punishment of the ungodly is described as fire and the worm, and wrath will not wait for sinners.
