Abstract written by a model

In Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra), Kundapura contained the southern brahmanical part where Mahâvîra was first conceived and the northern Kshatriya part where he was transferred to Trisalâ’s womb; Mahâvîra was born there and later departed from its northern Kshatriya part in the palankin Kandraprabhâ. In Kalpa Sutra, Kundapura was the town where the Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra was born, and King Siddhârtha ordered it decorated, cleaned, supplied with festivities, and filled with performers and celebrations for the birth.
