Abstract written by a model

Daniel's prayer, addressed to God with fasting in sackcloth and ashes, confesses Israel's sin, rebellion and ignored prophets, affirms God's righteousness, then pleads for forgiveness and prompt action on Jerusalem, resting on God's mercies, not merit.

Chapter 9

Daniel prays to the Lord, acknowledging that Israel has sinned, rebelled and ignored the prophets, while righteousness belongs to God. He asks God to turn away his wrath from Jerusalem, to hear, forgive and act without delay, basing his plea not on their righteousness but on God's great mercies and on the city and people bearing God's name.
