Frangrasyan

Also called: Frangrasyan, the Turanian, that ruffian, the Turanian ruffian Frangrasyan, the fell Turanian Frangrasyan, the most crafty Turanian Frangrasyan, the murderous robber

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Frangrasyan is a fell Turanian and murderous robber bound fast in iron fetters by H(a)oma. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Frangrasyan is a crafty Turanian ruffian who seeks the kingly Glory but fails and is ultimately defeated and bound by Kavi Husravah.

Frangrasyan in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Frangrasyan first appears

Abstract written by a model

Frangrasyan is a crafty Turanian ruffian who three times dives into sea Vouru-Kasha seeking the awful kingly Glory but fails. He threatens corn and liquors, bears Glory after Drvau and the Bull are killed, and is ultimately defeated and bound by Kavi Husravah.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

XIX. Zamyâd Yast.

Frangrasyan is a crafty Turanian ruffian who repeatedly strips himself and dives into the sea Vouru-Kasha to seize the Glory belonging to the Aryan nations and Zarathustra. He fails each time, threatens to defile corn and liquors, and is later defeated and bound by Kavi Husravah.
