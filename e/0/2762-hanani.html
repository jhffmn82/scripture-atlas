Abstract written by a model

Hanani is Nehemiah's faithful, God-fearing brother from Judah. He first arrives with men from Judah, reporting Jerusalem's ruined wall and gates and the remnant's affliction. Later, he is placed in charge of Jerusalem with Hananiah, overseeing the gates and the watches.

The Book of Nehemiah / Chapter 1

Hanani, one of Nehemiah's brothers, comes to Nehemiah with certain men out of Judah. In answer to Nehemiah's questions, they report the afflicted state of the remnant and the ruined condition of Jerusalem's wall and gates.

Chapter 7

Hanani, the narrator's brother, is put in charge of Jerusalem together with Hananiah. He is to see that the gates stay shut until the sun is hot and that watches are kept.
