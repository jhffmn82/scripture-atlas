His Augustness Oho-yamato-hiko-suki-tomo

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Emperor I-Toku, The Heavenly Sovereign, This Heavenly Sovereign

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Oho-yamato-hiko-suki-tomo, a Heavenly Sovereign, afterwards ruled the Empire from the palace of Sakahiwo at Karu. He wedded Princess Futo-ma-waka and begot Mi-ma-tsu-hiko-wake-shine and Tagishi-hiko. His reign lasted forty-five years; his mausoleum was above the Vale of Manago by Mount Unebi.

Sect. LVI.—Emperor an-Nei.

He was one of the three august children of His Augustness Shiki-tsu-hiko-tama-de-mi and Akuto-hime. He afterwards ruled the Empire.

Sect. LVII.—Emperor I-Toku.

Emperor I-Toku dwelt in the palace of Sakahiwo at Karu and ruled the Empire. He wedded Princess Futo-ma-waka and had two august children, Mi-ma-tsu-hiko-wake-shine and Tagishi-hiko. His august years were forty-five, and his mausoleum was above the Vale of Manago by Mount Unebi.
