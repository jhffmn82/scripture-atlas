rich who trust in wealth

Abstract written by a model

A group of rich people who trust in wealth and boast of riches. They cannot ransom a brother or redeem anyone from God, believe their houses last forever, and name lands after themselves, yet they die, leave wealth to others, join their fathers, and, lacking understanding, perish like animals.

Chapter 49

Those who trust in their wealth and boast of their riches cannot redeem a brother or ransom him from God. They think their houses will endure forever and name lands after themselves, yet they die, leave their wealth to others, go to the generation of their fathers, and never see the light. A man with riches but no understanding is like the animals that perish.
