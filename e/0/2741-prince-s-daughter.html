Abstract written by a model

The prince's daughter is praised in a detailed, head-to-toe address in Chapter 7. Her sandaled feet, thighs, body, waist, breasts, neck, eyes, nose, head and hair are each compared to something, such as jewels, a goblet, wheat, fawns, towers, pools and Carmel. Most notably, her hair holds the king captive in its tresses. Her stature is likened to a palm tree, and her mouth to the best wine. She is called beautiful and pleasant.

Chapter 7

The prince's daughter is addressed and praised in detail, with her feet in sandals, thighs, body, waist, breasts, neck, eyes, nose, head and hair each compared to something, such as jewels, a goblet, wheat, fawns, towers, pools and Carmel.
