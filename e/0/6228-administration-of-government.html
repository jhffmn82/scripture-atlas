administration of government

topic · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: administration, governing, government

Abstract written by a model

In Chuang Tzŭ, administration of government is described as less important than self-culture and as the surplus or dregs of TAO. In Mencius, Book IV (Li Lou), administration of government is not difficult when it avoids offending the great families.
