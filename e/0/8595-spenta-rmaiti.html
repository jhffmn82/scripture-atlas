Spenta-Ârmaiti

person · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Spenta (Ârmaiti), Spenta Ârmaiti, Spenta-Ârmaiti;, Spenta. Ârmaiti, Ârmaiti Spenta

Abstract written by a model

In Vendidad (Vendîdâd), Ârmaiti Spenta protects the faithful with Ahura Mazda, receives a man delivered by Sraosha for return at resurrection, is clad in grass partly withered by Gahi’s look, and restores the sleeper’s seed-emitting man as learned in sacred law. In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, Spenta-Ârmaiti is a good creature of Ahura Mazda, receives sacrifice, prayer, propitiation, and glorification, and helps make ruffians and enemies powerless.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Fargard XVIII.

Spenta Ârmaiti is described as being clad in grass, and Gahi’s look withers one third of that grass. The man who emits seed during sleep is delivered to Spenta Ârmaiti and is to be returned against the day of resurrection as one who knows the Gâthas, Yasna, and revealed law.

Spenta-Ârmaiti in Yashts (Yasts) I-XII

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Spenta-Ârmaiti first appears

Abstract written by a model

Spenta-Ârmaiti is a good creature of Ahura Mazda, named among holy creation. Associated with the good Râta, she receives sacrifice, prayer, propitiation, and glorification; through her eyes or help, ruffians and enemies are made powerless and their malice shattered.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

I. Ormazd Yast.

Spenta-Ârmaiti is named among the creatures of Ahura Mazda and is associated with the holy creation. Through the eyes or help of Spenta-Ârmaiti, a ruffian's power is broken and the malice of enemies is to be shattered.

II. Haptân Yast.

Spenta-Ârmaiti is called good and receives sacrifice, prayer, propitiation, and glorification. She is associated with the good Râta, made by Mazda and holy.
