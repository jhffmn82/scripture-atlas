Abstract written by a model

The neighboring kingdoms, judged by King Hui of Liang, have princes less diligent than he is, yet their people do not decrease while his kingdom’s people do not increase.

Chapter 3

The neighboring kingdoms are examined by King Hui of Liang, who finds no prince exerting his mind as he does. Their people nevertheless do not decrease, while the people of King Hui of Liang's kingdom do not increase.
