sexual intercourse

Abstract written by a model

Sexual intercourse is a sin that causes remorse, stops meditation, and threatens monks’ discipline; it is linked to the wicked, avoided by controlled men, and monks are told to abstain from it.

Fourth Lecture, Called Knowledge of Women. / First Chapter. .. Second Chapter.

Intercourse with women causes monks to cease practising meditation and may destroy their discipline like a pot of lac thrown into fire. Those attached to it are reckoned among the wicked, and a monk should avoid it.
