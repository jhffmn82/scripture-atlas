Abstract written by a model

Wan Zhang is Mencius’s questioner. Across the records, he asks about Shun, Yi Yin, Confucius, Bai Li Xi, and scholars’ dealings with princes. His questions prompt explanations of filial piety, Heaven’s mandate, righteous government, personal conduct, and proper rules for receiving support, meeting rulers, and summons.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Mencius, Book V (Wan Zhang) / Part I / Chapter 1 .. Chapter 7

Wan Zhang asks Mencius about Shun’s sorrow, marriage, treatment of Xiang, accession, succession, and Yi Yin’s introduction to Tang. His questions lead Mencius to explain filial piety, Heaven’s mandate, and righteous government.

Chapter 8 .. Chapter 9

Wan Zhang asks Mencius whether Confucius lodged with the ulcer-doctor in Wei and with the attendant Qi Huan in Qi. He also asks whether Bai Li Xi sold himself to a cattle-keeper of Jin for five rams' skins in order to gain an introduction to the duke Mu of Qin.

Chapter 6 .. Chapter 9

Wan Zhang asks Mencius about scholars accepting support from princes, refusing princely pay, avoiding interviews with princes, and the proper forms for summoning different people.
