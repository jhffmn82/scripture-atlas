Blessedness

topic · 1 work · 14 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Blessedness the good, benefits of sanctity, good Sanctity, or Blessedness, sanctity

Abstract written by a model

Blessedness is a good Sanctity closely knit with Righteous Order. Prayed for as a source of prosperity and long-lasting shelter, it brings healing, gifts, blessings, and later success, perfects and incites us, opposes evil creation, and overwhelms the Daêvas’ harmful malice.

Blessedness is in one work on this site, Yasna. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
