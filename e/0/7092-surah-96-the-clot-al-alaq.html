Abstract written by a model

Surah 96: The Clot (Al-Alaq) commands people to read in the name of Allah, who created man from a clot and taught him by the pen. It says that man becomes rebellious when he thinks himself independent, but that the return is to Allah. The unit condemns him who dissuadeth a slave from prayer and warns that Allah will seize him and summon the guards of hell, while commanding obedience, prostration, and nearness to Allah.
