Abstract written by a model

Those who lie unto Mithra are punished by Mithra: they lose strength, swiftness, eyesight, hearing, glory, vigour, and boons. Their weapons fail, homes and communities become desolate, fear, defeat, and death come upon them, and their bodies may be destroyed in battle.

X. Mihir Yast.

Men who lie unto Mithra are punished by losing strength, swiftness, eyesight, and hearing, and their weapons fail to reach their targets. Their houses and communities become desolate, and Mithra brings fear, defeat, and death upon them.

XII.

Those who lie unto Mithra receive neither strength, vigour, glory, nor any boon from him. Mithra takes strength from their arms, swiftness from their feet, eyesight from their eyes, and hearing from their ears, and their bodies may be destroyed in battle.
