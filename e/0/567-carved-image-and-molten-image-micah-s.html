Abstract written by a model

Micah's carved and molten images were made by a silversmith from two hundred pieces of silver and placed in Micah's house of gods with an ephod and teraphim. Five men identified them, and the Danites took them from Micah's house while the priest stood at the gate. They carried them to Laish, setting up the engraved image, which remained while God's house was in Shiloh.

Chapter 17

A silversmith makes a carved image and a molten image from two hundred of the pieces of silver, and they are placed in Micah's house. They form part of Micah's house of gods, along with an ephod and teraphim.

Chapter 18

Micah's ephod, teraphim, engraved image, and molten image were pointed out by the five men to the six hundred. The five men took them from Micah's house while the priest stood at the gate with the armed men. The Danites carried them to Laish and set up the engraved image for themselves, where it remained all the time God's house was in Shiloh.
