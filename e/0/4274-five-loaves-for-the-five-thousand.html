Chapter 14

With evening come, Jesus has the crowds sit on the grass, takes five loaves and two fish, looks up to heaven, blesses, breaks, and gives them to the disciples to distribute. All eat and are filled, about five thousand men plus women and children, and twelve baskets of broken pieces are gathered.
