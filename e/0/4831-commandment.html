Abstract written by a model

The commandment is described as both old, the word heard from the beginning, and new, true in him and in the readers as darkness passes and true light shines. Keeping it shows one knows him; claiming to know him without keeping it makes one a liar; keepers have God's love perfected.

Chapter 2

Keeping his commandments is how we know that we know him, and the one who claims to know him without keeping them is a liar. The writer calls his commandment both old, the word heard from the beginning, and new, true in him and in the readers because the darkness is passing and the true light already shines.
