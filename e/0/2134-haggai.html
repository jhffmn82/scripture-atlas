Chapter 1

Haggai the prophet receives the LORD's word and delivers it to Zerubbabel and Joshua. He is called the LORD's messenger, and he speaks the message 'I am with you' to the people. The people obey his words as the LORD had sent him.

Chapter 2

Haggai the prophet delivers the LORD's word to Zerubbabel, Joshua, and the remnant on the twenty-first day of the seventh month and twice on the twenty-fourth day of the ninth month. He questions the priests about the law of holiness and uncleanness and applies their answers to declare the people and their offerings unclean.
