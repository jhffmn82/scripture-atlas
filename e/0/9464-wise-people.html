Abstract written by a model

Wise people value learning, patience, humility, and good conduct; monks must not offend them openly or secretly. They face death peacefully and without injury, weigh its kinds, and choose the better one taught in the Law of Compassion.

Uttaradhyayana Sutra / First Lecture.

Wise people value learning, patience, humility, and good conduct. A monk should not do anything disagreeable to them in words or deeds, openly or secretly.

Fifth Lecture.

Wise men experience death with one's will, which is full of peace and free from injury to anyone. They weigh both kinds of death and choose the better one taught in the Law of Compassion.
