Abstract written by a model

The east gate is a gate facing east, the first gate measured in the vision. The man climbs its steps and measures its thresholds, lodges, porch, posts and opening. It is fifty cubits long, with a ten-cubit opening, three side rooms on each side, closed windows, and palm trees on each post. Later, the glory of the God of Israel arrives from the east and enters the house through it. Finally, it is shut on the six working days, opened on the Sabbath and new moon, and opened for the prince's free will offering, staying open until evening.

Chapter 40

It is the first gate measured. The man goes up its steps and measures its thresholds, lodges, porch, posts, and opening. It has three side rooms on each side of one measure, closed windows, and palm trees on each post, and is fifty cubits long and twenty-five wide in the surrounding measurements.

Chapter 43

The east gate is where the narrator is brought and where the glory of the God of Israel arrives from the east. The LORD's glory enters the house by way of this gate, which faces east.

Chapter 46

The gate is shut on the six working days and opened on the Sabbath and the new moon. It is also opened for the prince's free will offerings, and it is not shut until evening, or after he has gone out.
