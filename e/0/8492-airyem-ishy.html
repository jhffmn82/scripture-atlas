Airyemâ-Ishyô

object · 3 works · 9 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Airyemâ-ishyô, The Airyemâ-Ishyô

Abstract written by a model

In the Yasna, Airyemâ-Ishyô is a powerful, victoriously smiting holy ritual sentence opposing assaulting malice, receiving sacrifice, and standing greatest in ritual order. In the Visparad, Airyemâ-ishyô is a holy lord of the ritual order praised with the Fshûshô-mãthra and Hadhaokhdha, whose memorised recital of good thoughts, words, and deeds brings victory and other benefits. In Afrinagan, Gahs and Miscellaneous Fragments, Airyemâ-ishyô is a holy, authoritative prayer proclaimed greatest and most helpful for progress, revered and urged for use by the Saoshyants.
