Jesus's teaching

topic · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: authority, his teaching, teaching of Christ, the teaching, the teaching of Christ, these words of mine, this teaching

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Jesus's teaching, marked by authority unlike the scribes, covers not judging, asking, the golden rule, the narrow way, false prophets, and doing his words, and astonishes the multitudes. In 2 John, the teaching of Christ is the standard by which people are judged: whoever transgresses and does not remain in it does not have God, while whoever remains in it has both the Father and the Son; anyone who comes without bringing this teaching is not to be received or welcomed.
