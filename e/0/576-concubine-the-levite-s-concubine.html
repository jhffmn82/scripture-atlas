Chapter 19

The concubine had played the prostitute against the Levite and gone to her father's house in Bethlehem Judah, where she stayed four months before he came for her. At Gibeah she is pushed out to the mob, raped and abused all night, and collapses at the door with her hands on the threshold; she does not answer the Levite, and he cuts her body into twelve pieces.

Chapter 20

She is described by the Levite as having been raped by the men of Gibeah, and she is dead. Her body was cut in pieces and sent throughout the country of Israel's inheritance, which prompts the gathering of the tribes.
