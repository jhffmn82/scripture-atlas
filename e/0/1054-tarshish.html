Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, Tarshish's ships are called to howl over Tyre's ruined stronghold, its coast dwellers are told to pass over to Tarshish, and its daughter is told to pass through her land. In Jonah, Tarshish is the destination of the ship Jonah boards at Joppa to flee from the LORD's presence.
