Abstract written by a model

The Plain of the Jordan is a region Lot surveys and finds well-watered everywhere, resembling the garden of the LORD and Egypt toward Zoar, before Sodom and Gomorrah's destruction. Lot chooses it for himself, and its cities become his home.
