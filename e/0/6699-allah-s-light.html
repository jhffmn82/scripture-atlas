Allah's light

topic · 4 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: His light, Light upon light, light, no light, the Light, the light of Allah, the light revealed by Allah, the light which We have revealed

Abstract written by a model

Allah's light is likened to a lamp in a niche, shining through glass and kindled from a blessed olive tree; Allah guides whom He wills to His light and speaks to mankind in allegories in Surah 24: The Light (An-Noor). The light of Allah is something disbelievers wish to put out with their mouths, but Allah will perfect it despite their aversion in Surah 61: The Ranks (As-Saff). The light revealed by Allah is presented as something in which people are commanded to believe together with Allah and His messenger in Surah 64: Mutual Disillusion (At-Taghaabun). Light brings believers who do good works from darkness into light in Surah 65: Divorce (At-Talaaq).
