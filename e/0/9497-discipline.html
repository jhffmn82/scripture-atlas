Abstract written by a model

Discipline concerns the houseless monk and is necessary for righteousness and avoiding rejection; one seeking liberation should eagerly pursue it. It becomes virtue through self-control, truthfulness, and right conduct, but egoism, delusion, carelessness, illness, and idleness make it impossible.

Uttaradhyayana Sutra / First Lecture.

Discipline is presented as necessary for acquiring righteousness and avoiding rejection. A monk who desires liberation is urged to be eager for discipline.

Eleventh Lecture.

Discipline is impossible because of egoism, delusion, carelessness, illness, and idleness. It is called virtue when accompanied by qualities such as self-control, truthfulness, freedom from covetousness and anger, and avoidance of harmful speech and wrong conduct.
