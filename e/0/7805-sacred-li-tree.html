sacred li tree

object · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: a good-for-nothing tree, a sacred _li_ tree, a sacred tree, such a splendid piece of timber, the tree, the tree appeared to him

Abstract written by a model

The sacred li tree is a vast tree, a hundred spans in girth and ten cubits above the hilltop, able to shelter an ox, with many boat-sized branches. Its wood is useless as timber, which preserved its longevity by preventing cutting. Facing enemies, it became sacred as a means of safety, gaining use through sacredness.

The sacred li tree is large enough to shelter an ox and has many branches of boat-sized girth. It explains that its lack of usefulness protected it from being cut down and allowed it to live out its years, while becoming sacred helped it escape its enemies.
