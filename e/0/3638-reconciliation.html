reconciliation

Abstract written by a model

In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, reconciliation restores believers to God through the death and life of Jesus Christ. In Paul’s Letter to the Colossians, reconciliation brings believers redemption, forgiveness, deliverance, peace, and presentation as holy and blameless.
