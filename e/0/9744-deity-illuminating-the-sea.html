Deity illuminating the sea

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: This Deity, a Deity illuminating the sea, the Deity, the Deity who dwells on the top of Mount Mimoro

Abstract written by a model

Deity illuminating the sea is a deity who appears, offers with Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land to make land if reverently laid to rest, requires reverent worship at Yamato's green fence and the eastern mountain's top, and dwells atop Mount Mimoro.

Deity illuminating the sea is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
