word of the LORD

topic · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: By the LORD’s word, God's word and creation, his commandment, his ordinances, his statutes, his word, his work, my word, word

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the word of the LORD is his commandment that goes swiftly through the earth and is revealed with statutes and ordinances to Jacob and Israel. In Psalms, God's word and creation describe the LORD's word and breath making the heavens, gathering the sea's waters, and storing the deeps. In The Book of Isaiah, the word of the LORD accomplishes what he pleases and prospers in its mission.

God's word and creation in Psalms

author unknown · undated · the work’s page · read it where God's word and creation first appears

Abstract written by a model

The LORD's word is right and powerful: by it and his breath the heavens and their army were made, the sea's waters gathered and the deeps stored. Since he spoke and it was done, all the earth should fear him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 33

The LORD's word is described as right, and by it and the breath of his mouth the heavens and all their army were made. He gathers the sea's waters in a heap and stores the deeps. Because he spoke and it was done, and he commanded and it stood firm, all the earth is told to fear and stand in awe of him.

The LORD's word goes out from his mouth and, like rain and snow that water the earth and yield seed and bread, never returns empty but accomplishes what he pleases and prospers in its mission.
