Chapter 1

The remnant of the people, along with Zerubbabel and Joshua, obey the LORD's voice and the words of Haggai, and they fear the LORD. The LORD stirs up their spirit, and they come and work on the house of the LORD of Armies.

Chapter 2

The remnant of the people is addressed with Zerubbabel and Joshua and told to be strong, work, and not be afraid. Haggai describes the people as unclean along with the work of their hands, and notes that they suffered poor harvests from blight, mildew, and hail without turning to the LORD. The LORD promises to bless them from that day.
