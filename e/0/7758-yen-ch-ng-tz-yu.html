Yen Ch'êng Tzŭ Yu

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Tzŭ Yu, Yen Ch'êng Tzŭ

Abstract written by a model

Yen Ch'êng Tzŭ Yu is a person and friend of Tzŭ Ch'i of Nan-kuo. He questions Tzŭ Ch'i about the music of Heaven and asks Tzŭ Chi of Nan-poh about a dying important person. Under Tung Kuo Tzŭ Chi’s instruction, he progresses over nine years from simplicity and adaptability to understanding, spiritual receptivity, knowledge of God, transcendence of life and death, perfection, and questions about Destiny.

CHAPTER II.

Yen Ch'êng Tzŭ Yu finds Tzŭ Ch'i leaning on a table as though his soul and body had parted. He questions Tzŭ Ch'i about the music of Heaven and receives his explanation.

Kuan Chung being at the point of death, Duke Huan went to see him.

Yen Ch'êng Tzŭ asks Tzŭ Chi of Nan-poh how such an important person can be like dry wood in body and dead ashes in mind.

CHAPTER XXVII.

Yen Ch'êng Tzŭ Yu told Tung Kuo Tzŭ Chi that, over nine years, he became naturally simple, adaptable, understanding, intelligent, spiritually receptive, aware of God, beyond life and death, and perfect. He questioned the origins of life and death and the operation of Destiny.
