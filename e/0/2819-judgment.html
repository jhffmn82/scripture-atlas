judgment

Also called: He will judge the world with righteousness, Judgments, judge, judge Thou between us, judge the earth, judge the peoples, judge the world, judged, judgment (the LORD's coming judgment), their reckoning, to judge the earth, trial

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, judgment is the LORD's coming judgment of the earth, peoples, and world with equity, righteousness, and truth. In Psalms, judgment is the LORD's coming judgment of the earth, giving creation reason to sing as he judges the world with righteousness and the peoples with equity. In Ecclesiastes or, The Preacher, judgment is what God will bring the young man into for all he does in following his heart and eyes. In 1 Peter, judgment comes from God, begins with the household of God, and concerns the living, the dead, unbelievers, and the disobedient. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), judgment belongs to Allah, who judges between Noah and his folk, brings the Garden near to those who ward off evil, and punishes the erring. In The Table-Talk of the Prophet Mohammad (Part 1), judgment encompasses accountability for murder, suicide, debt, and judges' conduct, including a judge's reckoning before God.
