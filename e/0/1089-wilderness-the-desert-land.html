Abstract written by a model

The wilderness is a dry desert land that is promised transformation: it will rejoice and blossom like a rose, receive the glory of Lebanon, Carmel and Sharon, and gain waters, streams, pools and springs, with grass, reeds and rushes where jackals lay.

Chapter 35

The wilderness and dry land will be glad, and the desert will rejoice and blossom abundantly like a rose. It will be given the glory of Lebanon and the excellence of Carmel and Sharon. Waters and streams will break out in it, burning sand will become a pool, and the thirsty ground will become springs, with grass, reeds and rushes growing where jackals lay.
