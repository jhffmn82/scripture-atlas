Absalom's conspiracy

Also called: Absalom’s rebellion, The conspiracy, conspirators, the kingdom

Abstract written by a model

Absalom's conspiracy is a rebellion he leads, built through gate-side courting, tribal spies, and unknowing invitees from Jerusalem. It strengthens when David's counselor Ahithophel joins and people keep increasing, forcing David to flee. Absalom enters Jerusalem, receives Hushai's pledge and counsel, and publicly takes his father's concubines.

Chapter 15

The conspiracy grew through Absalom's gate-side courting of Israelites, spies sent through the tribes, and the unknowing participation of two hundred invited men from Jerusalem. It became strong when Ahithophel joined and the people increased continually with Absalom, prompting David's flight.

Chapter 16

Absalom's rebellion advances as he and the men of Israel enter Jerusalem while David is away. He receives Hushai's pledge and Ahithophel's counsel, and publicly goes in to his father's concubines to show he is abhorred by his father.
