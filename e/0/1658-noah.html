Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, Noah builds the ship, survives the flood with his family and the animals, offers burnt offerings, receives God's covenant, becomes a farmer, and dies at 950. In Genesis, Ham is one of Noah's three sons, sees his father's nakedness, tells his brothers, and fathers Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan. In 1 Chronicles, Noah is in Adam's line and is the father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, Noah is a perfect, righteous man who keeps the race alive through the flood and receives everlasting covenants. In The Book of Ezekiel, Noah is named as one of three righteous men whose righteousness would deliver only their own souls from judgment.

Noah in Genesis

author unknown · about 449 BC · the work’s page · read it where Noah first appears

Abstract written by a model

Noah is a righteous, blameless man who walks with God and finds favor with the LORD. Born to Lamech, who named him in hope of comfort from the toil of the cursed ground, he becomes father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth at age 500. Because of his righteousness, God commands him to build a ship, and he does everything he is told. At age six hundred he enters the ship with his family and the animals, and the LORD shuts him in. The flood destroys all other life, leaving only those aboard. Afterward Noah sends out a raven and a dove, learning the waters have abated when the dove returns with an olive leaf. He leaves the ship at God's command, builds an altar to the LORD, and offers burnt offerings of clean animals and birds. God blesses him and establishes a covenant with him, his sons, and his descendants. Noah then becomes a farmer and plants a vineyard, gets drunk and lies uncovered in his tent, and on waking curses Canaan, blesses the God of Shem, and asks that Japheth be enlarged. He lives 350 years after the flood and dies at 950. His sons' families become the nations that divided.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

Noah was born to Lamech, who named him in hope that he would comfort them in their work and the toil of their hands, caused by the cursed ground. At age 500 Noah became the father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

Chapter 6

Noah found favor in the LORD's eyes and is described as a righteous man, blameless among the people of his time, who walked with God. He was father of Shem, Ham, and Japheth, and he did all that God commanded him, including preparing to build the ship.

Chapter 7

Noah is told by the LORD to enter the ship with his household because of his righteousness, and he does everything the LORD commands. At age six hundred he goes into the ship with his family and the animals, and the LORD shuts him in. He and those with him are the only ones left alive after the flood destroys all other life on the earth.

Chapter 8

Noah sends out a raven and then a dove three times to learn whether the waters have abated, and he knows they have when the dove returns with an olive leaf. After removing the ship's covering and seeing dry ground, he obeys God's command to leave the ship with his family and the animals. He builds an altar to the LORD and offers burnt offerings of every clean animal and clean bird.

Chapter 9

Noah is blessed by God and receives the covenant along with his sons and descendants. He becomes a farmer, plants a vineyard, gets drunk and lies uncovered in his tent, then on waking curses Canaan, blesses the God of Shem, and asks that Japheth be enlarged. He lives 350 years after the flood and dies at age 950.

Chapter 10

Noah is named as the father whose three sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth, had sons born to them after the flood. The chapter closes by calling the listed groups the families of the sons of Noah, from whom the nations divided.

Ham in Genesis

author unknown · about 449 BC · the work’s page · read it where Ham first appears

Abstract written by a model

Ham is one of Noah's three sons and father of Canaan. He sees his father's nakedness and tells his brothers, and Noah learns what his youngest son did. Later, his sons Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan are listed, with descendants traced and the Canaanite border described.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 9

Ham is named as one of Noah's sons and as the father of Canaan. He sees his father's nakedness and tells his two brothers outside, and Noah is said to know what his youngest son had done to him.

Chapter 10

Ham's sons were Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan, and the chapter traces the descendants of each. His line is summarized as sons by their families, languages, lands, and nations, and the Canaanite border is described from Sidon toward Gerar to Gaza and toward Sodom, Gomorrah, Admah, and Zeboiim to Lasha.
