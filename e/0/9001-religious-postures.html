religious postures

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections
Also called: religious posture

Abstract written by a model

Religious postures are practices for monks or nuns, permitted only in suitably pure and safe lodging-places or couches, not householders’ lodgings or unsuitable places. They unfold through four rules governing leaning, changing position, slight movement, or complete motionlessness.

Second Lecture, Called Begging for a Couch. / First Lesson. .. Third Lesson.

Religious postures may be performed only in lodging-places and on couches that meet the required conditions of purity and safety. A monk or nun should not use householders' lodgings or unsuitable lodging-places for religious postures.

Second Part. / The Seven Lectures. / Eighth Lecture.

Religious postures are performed according to one of four rules. The rules specify whether the mendicant leans against something, changes position, moves slightly, or remains perfectly motionless.
