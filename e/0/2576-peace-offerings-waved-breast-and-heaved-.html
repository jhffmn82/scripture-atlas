Chapter 7

The breast of the peace offerings is waved as a wave offering before the LORD and belongs to Aaron and his sons. The right thigh is given to the priest as a heave offering, specifically to the one who offers the blood and fat. The LORD declares these portions given to Aaron and his sons forever from the children of Israel.
