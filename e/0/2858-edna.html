Chapter 7

Edna, Raguel's wife, hears her husband remark on Tobias's resemblance to Tobit, and weeps with the household at the news of Tobit's blindness. She prepares the other chamber, takes in her weeping daughter, and comforts her, praying that the Lord of heaven and earth give her favor.
