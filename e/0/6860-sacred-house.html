Sacred House

place · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: Ka'bah, the Sacred House

Abstract written by a model

The Sacred House is an inviolable place of worship and sacred destination. Identified as the Ka'bah, it is appointed as a standard for mankind. Those repairing to it seek their Lord’s grace and pleasure and must not be profaned. Believers are instructed not to let hostility from people who prevented their access to the inviolable place lead them to transgress. In pilgrimage, offerings are brought to the Sacred House as forfeits for certain violations.

Sacred House is in one work on this site, Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
