fasting

topic · 3 works · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Fasting, a fast of three days, fast, the fast

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, fasting should be done secretly, without the sad appearance of hypocrites, by anointing the head and washing the face so that only the Father knows. In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), fasting is prescribed for believers, with provisions for making up missed days or providing a ransom, and with eating, drinking, and intercourse permitted at night until dawn. In The Table-Talk of the Prophet Mohammad (Part 1), fasting is incomplete when accompanied by lying and slander, and moderation is recommended, including fasting three days each month while fulfilling bodily and social duties.

fasting in The Table-Talk of the Prophet Mohammad (Part 1)

Mishkat al-Masabih · 1882 · the work’s page · read it where fasting first appears

Abstract written by a model

Fasting is not considered complete when a person continues lying and slandering. The unit recommends moderation, including fasting three days each month while also resting, eating, and fulfilling duties to the body, eyes, wife, visitors, and guests.
