Bacchides

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Bacchides’

Abstract written by a model

Bacchides is a powerful, faithful friend of the king who ruled beyond the river, chosen by Demetrius to take vengeance on Israel. He first came with a great army, offered deceitful peace to Judas, camped at Bezeth, killed deserters and others in a pit, left Alcimus in charge, and returned to the king. Sent again with Alcimus, he fought Judas at Elasa, installed ungodly rulers, hunted Judas's friends, pursued Jonathan, fortified Bethsura and Gazara, and held hostages in Jerusalem. After defeat at Bethbasi, he swore peace with Jonathan, returned captives, and never re-entered Judah.

Bacchides is in one work on this site, The First Book of the Maccabees. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

Bacchides, a great man and faithful friend of the king who ruled the country beyond the river, was chosen by Demetrius to see the destruction and take vengeance on Israel. He came with a great army and sent deceitful words of peace to Judas, which were ignored. He encamped at Bezeth, seized and killed many deserters and some of the people by throwing them into a large pit, left Alcimus in charge with a force, and went back to the king.

Chapter 9

Bacchides was sent by Demetrius with Alcimus into Judah, and he fought Judas at Elasa, where he commanded the right wing. He made ungodly men rulers, hunted Judas's friends, and pursued Jonathan to the Jordan, losing about a thousand men. He fortified cities, was later defeated at Bethbasi, swore peace with Jonathan, returned the captives, and never again entered Judah's borders.
