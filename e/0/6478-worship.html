worship

topic · 13 works · 13 quoted facts · 10 connections
Also called: Establish worship, Thy worship, establish worship, make prostration, prayer, proper worship, real worship, serve, worship is this, worshippers, worshipping

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), worship is established by believers through devotion to Allah alone, including prayer, bowing, prostration, remembrance, thanks, circling the House, and paying the poor-due. In Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba), establishing worship is required alongside repentance and payment of the poor-due, and proper worship is listed among the qualities of those who tend Allah's sanctuaries. In Surah 10: Jonas (Yunus), mankind is commanded to worship Allah alone and not call upon what cannot profit or hurt. In Surah 14: Abraham (Ibrahim), worship is established by believers and is the purpose Abraham seeks for himself and some of his posterity, asking Allah to preserve him and his sons from serving idols. In Surah 19: Mary (Maryam), worship includes prayer, almsgiving, service to Allah, and steadfastness in His service, though later generations ruin it and follow lusts. In Surah 20: Taa-Haa, Moses is commanded to serve Allah and establish worship for His remembrance, while Muhammad is told to enjoin worship upon his people and remain constant in it. In Surah 22: The Pilgrimage (Al-Hajj), worship is established as a duty together with paying the poor-due, enjoining kindness, forbidding iniquity, bowing, and prostrating. In Surah 24: The Light (An-Noor), worship includes remembrance of Allah, constancy in prayer, praising Him morning and evening, establishing worship, and paying the poor-due.

and 5 more works

In Surah 27: The Ant (An-Naml), worship is commanded toward Allah alone, while the Queen of Sheba's people are criticized for worshipping the sun instead of Allah. In Surah 31: Luqman, worship is to be established, and those who establish it along with paying the poor-due and believing in the Hereafter are guided and successful. In Surah 42: Consultation (Ash-Shura), believers answer the call of their Lord and establish worship as one of the qualities of those who receive what Allah has and what is better and more lasting. In Sohila (Kirtan Sohila), worship consists of the cosmos itself, and what pleases God is identified as real worship. In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), worship of the Lord is presented as a way to find happiness and remember the Lord.
