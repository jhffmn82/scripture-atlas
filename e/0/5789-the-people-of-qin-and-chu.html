Abstract written by a model

The people of Qin and Chu lose time for ploughing and weeding because of their rulers; their parents suffer cold and hunger and families scatter. Though they serve fathers, brothers, elders, and superiors with respect, Qin and Chu troops can be opposed with sticks.
