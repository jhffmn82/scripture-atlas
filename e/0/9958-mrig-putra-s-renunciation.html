Abstract written by a model

Mrigâputra renounces power, possessions, family, and property with parental permission, enters the Sramana order, observes great vows, and lives without attachment or egoism.

Nineteenth Lecture.

Mrigâputra renounces his power, wealth, friends, wives, sons, relations, and all claims to property after obtaining his parents' permission. He then goes forth, observes the great vows, and lives without attachment or egoism.
