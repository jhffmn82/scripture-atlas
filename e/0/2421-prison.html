Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, the prison holds the king's prisoners, including Joseph, Pharaoh's cup bearer, and baker, and Joseph is put in charge of them. In Surah 12: Joseph (Yusuf), the prison holds Joseph after he is falsely accused, where he interprets dreams and later explains the king's dream.

Abstract written by a model

The prison is a place for the king's prisoners, located in the house of the captain of the guard. Joseph is bound there in custody, then given charge of all prisoners. Pharaoh's cup bearer and baker are also held there, while Joseph insists he did nothing wrong.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 39

The prison is the place where the king's prisoners are bound, and Joseph is put there in custody by his master. There Joseph is given charge of all the prisoners and is responsible for whatever they do.

Chapter 40

The prison is in the house of the captain of the guard and is the place where Joseph is bound. Pharaoh's cup bearer and baker are held there, and Joseph says he was put in its dungeon though he had done nothing wrong.
