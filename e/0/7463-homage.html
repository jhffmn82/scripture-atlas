Abstract written by a model

Homage is a ritual offering or desire directed toward the entire holy creation and its sacred powers and beings. It is associated with sacrifice, propitiation, glorification, and adoration. In celebrations, it is offered to Ahura Mazda, divine beings, Fravashis, waters, trees, Kine, Mãthra Spenta, Zarathustra, and the Bountiful Immortals, through the Avesta and ritual prescripts.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Visparad IX (Sp. X).

Homage is offered or desired toward Haomas and Zaothras, sacred powers and beings, Universal Weal and Immortality, the Kine and Kine's Soul, and the holy creation.

Visparad XI (Sp. XII).

Homage is offered in the celebrations to Ahura Mazda, divine beings, Fravashis, and the entire creation of the holy and clean. The Avesta and ritual prescripts are declared for this purpose.

Visparad XXI (Sp. XXIV).

Homage is offered earnestly to the good waters, fertile fruit-trees, Fravashis of the saints, Kine, Gaya (Maretan), Mãthra Spenta, Ahura Mazda, Zarathustra, the lofty lord, and the Bountiful Immortals. The passage also sacrifices to the hearing of spoken homage.
