warnings

Also called: My warnings, the warnings, warn, warning, warnings avail not

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 51: The Winnowing Winds (Adh-Dhaariyat), warning is presented as a duty that benefits believers, and Muhammad is told to warn as a plain warner from Allah. In Surah 54: The Moon (Al-Qamar), warnings come through news, revelation, and messengers, but many deny or doubt them, followed by repeated descriptions of punishment.
