Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Jeremiah, the people's iniquities and rebellions testify against them, and the LORD remembers and punishes their sins and wickedness. In The Book of Ezekiel, the people's abominations are brought upon them as part of the LORD's judgment. In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, the people's iniquity is the subject of Lehi's prophecy and the reason the Lord warns him to depart from Jerusalem. In The Second Book of Nephi, the people's abominations contribute to the day when the sealed things are withheld from them.
