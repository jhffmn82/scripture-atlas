Chapter 5

After news that the girl is dead, Jesus goes to the house with Peter, James, John, and the parents, puts out the ridiculing mourners, and takes the girl by the hand. She rises and walks immediately, and those present are amazed. Jesus strictly orders that no one should know of it.
