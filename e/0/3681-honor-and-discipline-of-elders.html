Abstract written by a model

requires public reproof of sinning elders [so the rest may fear] Elders who rule well receive double honor and wages, especially those who teach, and are shielded by the two-or-three-witness rule for accusations. Persistent sin is reproved publicly, and the instruction is to be carried out without prejudice or partiality. Hands are not to be laid hastily on anyone, and the text notes that some sins and good works are evident at once while others emerge later.
