Sect. XCIV.—Emperor Sei-Mu.

Emperor Sei-Mu, identified as the Heavenly Sovereign Wata-tarashi-hiko, dwelt at the palace of Taka-anaho at Shiga in Chika-tsu-Afumi and ruled the empire. He wedded Lady Oho-takara, begot King Wata-nuke, raised Take-uchi to be Prince Minister, and established rulers, boundaries, and departmental lords. He lived ninety-five years, and his mausoleum was at Tatanami near Saki.
