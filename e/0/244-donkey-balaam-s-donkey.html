Chapter 22

Balaam's donkey sees the angel and turns into the field, crushes Balaam's foot against a wall, and finally lies down under him, being struck each time. The LORD opens her mouth and she asks what she has done to deserve three blows, reminding Balaam she has carried him all his life. The angel says her turning away saved Balaam's life and that he would have spared her.
