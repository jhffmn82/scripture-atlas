Chapter 3

When the foundation was laid, priests with trumpets and Levites with cymbals praised the LORD, and the people sang antiphonally, saying he is good and his loving kindness endures forever. The great shout of joy mingled with weeping so that the two could not be told apart, and the noise was heard far away.
