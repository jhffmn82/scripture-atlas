sword of the Son of Heaven

object · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

The sword of the Son of Heaven symbolizes the empire: its lands, elements, seasons, and peoples. Made of the five elements and enclosed by the four hordes of barbarians, it operates under Yin and Yang, arbitrating punishment and reward until princes submit.
