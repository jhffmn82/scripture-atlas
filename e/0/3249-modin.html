Abstract written by a model

Modin is the city of Simon's fathers, with an altar. Mattathias lives there, kills a Jew and the king's officer enforcing sacrifice, tears down the altar, and is buried there. Later Simon buries Jonathan's bones and builds a lasting family tomb.

Chapter 2

Modin is the city where Mattathias lives and where the king's officers come to enforce sacrifice. Mattathias kills a Jew and the king's officer there and pulls down the altar, and later he is buried in his ancestors' tombs at Modin.

Chapter 13

Modin is called the city of Simon's fathers, where Simon buries Jonathan's bones. There Simon builds the elaborate tomb for his father, mother, and four brothers, which the text says remains to this day.
