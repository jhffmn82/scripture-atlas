Gorgias

person · 2 works · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: the accursed man

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of the Maccabees, Gorgias commands an army of five thousand infantry and a thousand chosen cavalry in a night march to surprise the Jews, but finds their camp empty and flees when he sees it burning. In The Second Book of the Maccabees, Gorgias is an accursed, war-experienced captain and governor of Idumaea who keeps mercenaries to fight the Jews, escapes wounded after nearly being captured by Dositheus, and suffers the rout of his troops by Judas.

Chapter 12

Gorgias, governor of Idumaea, came out with three thousand infantry and four hundred cavalry after Pentecost. Dositheus of Bacenor's company seized him by the cloak, but a Thracian horseman disabled Dositheus's shoulder and Gorgias escaped to Marisa. Judas then attacked his troops unexpectedly and put them to flight.
