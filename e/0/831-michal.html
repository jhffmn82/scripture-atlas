Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Samuel, Michal saves David from Saul and is later given to Palti. In The Second Book of Samuel, Michal despises David for dancing before the ark and remains childless until her death. In 1 Chronicles, Michal despises David in her heart as she watches him dance before the ark.

Michal in The First Book of Samuel

author unknown · about 930 to 721 BC · the work’s page · read it where Michal first appears

Abstract written by a model

Michal is Saul's daughter who loves David. Saul, hoping she will snare David, gives her to him as wife after the dowry. She then saves David, warning him, lowering him through a window, and deceiving Saul's messengers with a teraphim in the bed. Saul later gives her to Palti.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 18

Michal, Saul's daughter, loves David, and Saul is pleased by this because he intends her to be a snare for David. After David delivers the dowry, Saul gives her to him as wife.

Chapter 19

Michal, David's wife, warns David that he will be killed in the morning unless he flees, and lets him down through the window. She places the teraphim in the bed to deceive Saul's messengers, claims David is sick, and when Saul confronts her says David told her, "Let me go! Why should I kill you?"

Michal in The Second Book of Samuel

author unknown · about 950 BC · the work’s page · read it where Michal first appears

Abstract written by a model

Michal, Saul's daughter, once married to Paltiel, watches David's ark procession and despises him for dancing. She confronts him, he rebuts her, and she remains childless until her death.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Michal, daughter of Saul, watches from a window as the ark enters David's city and despises David in her heart for leaping and dancing. She confronts him, accusing him of shamelessly uncovering himself before his servants' maids. David rebuts her, and she has no child to the day of her death.
