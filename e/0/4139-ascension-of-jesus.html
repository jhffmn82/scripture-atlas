The Acts of the Apostles / Chapter 1

After speaking to the apostles, Jesus was taken up as they were looking, and a cloud received him out of their sight. While they gazed into the sky, two men in white clothing told them, 'You men of Galilee,' that Jesus would come back in the same way they saw him go. The apostles then returned to Jerusalem from the mountain called Olivet.
