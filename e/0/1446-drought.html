Chapter 14

The drought brings cracked ground, empty cisterns, disappointed plowmen, a doe forsaking her young, and wild donkeys panting with failing eyes for lack of vegetation. It is the occasion for the LORD's word to Jeremiah and the people's prayer. The chapter ends by affirming that only the LORD, not the vanities of the nations, can cause rain.
