Uriah the Hittite

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Uriah, Your servant Uriah the Hittite, her husband

Abstract written by a model

Uriah the Hittite is a loyal soldier, one of the thirty, who serves Joab. Summoned from war by David, he refuses the comfort of home while the ark, Israel, Judah and Joab's men camp in the open. Unwittingly carrying the letter ordering his death, he is killed near the city wall by the Ammonites' sword. Nathan later names David as his killer, grounding the LORD's charge.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 11

Uriah is summoned from the war by David but refuses to go down to his house, saying the ark, Israel, Judah, and Joab's men are camped in the open. He unwittingly carries the letter that orders his death and is killed in the fighting near the city wall.

Chapter 12

Uriah the Hittite is named by Nathan as the man David struck with the sword and killed with the sword of the Ammonites, after which David took his wife as his own. This is the basis of the LORD's charge against David.
