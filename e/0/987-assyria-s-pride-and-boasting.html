Abstract written by a model

claims own strength and wisdom [Assyria's boast] Assyria's king claims that all his princes are kings, that he acted by the strength of his hand and his wisdom, removed boundaries, robbed treasures, and gathered the earth like abandoned eggs with no one to chirp. The Lord says he will punish the fruit of his willful proud heart and the insolence of his arrogant looks after finishing his work on Zion and Jerusalem. The boast is answered with a fire that consumes Assyria's glory, fat ones, and forest.
