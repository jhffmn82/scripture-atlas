temple cleansing

event · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a den of robbers, a house of prayer, these things

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, temple cleansing involves Jesus driving out sellers and buyers, overturning money changers' tables and dove sellers' seats, citing that the temple is a house of prayer, calling it a den of robbers, healing the lame and blind, and provoking the chief priests and scribes. In The Gospel According to John, temple cleansing involves Jesus driving out sellers and animals, scattering money, overturning tables, and prompting the disciples to recall a zeal scripture while the Jews demand a sign.
