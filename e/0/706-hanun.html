Hanun in 1 Chronicles

author unknown · 449 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Hanun first appears

Abstract written by a model

Hanun, son of Nahash, succeeds him as Ammonite king. Heeding suspicious princes, he humiliates David's comforting messengers by shaving them and cutting their garments, then hires chariots and horsemen with a thousand talents of silver.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 19

Hanun succeeded his father Nahash as king of the Ammonites and received David's messengers of comfort. Believing his princes' warning that they were spies, he shaved them, cut off their garments at the buttocks, and sent them away. He and the Ammonites then sent a thousand talents of silver to hire chariots and horsemen.
