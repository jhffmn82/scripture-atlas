readers (the audience addressed)

Abstract written by a model

The readers are the audience addressed as holy brothers and partakers of a heavenly calling. In Chapter 3 they are told to consider Jesus, avoid an unbelieving heart and falling away, exhort one another daily, and hold confidence firm to the end to remain God's house and partakers of Christ. In Chapter 12 they are surrounded by witnesses, urged to run with perseverance, endure discipline, pursue peace and holiness, and avoid bitterness and profanity like Esau. Having come to Mount Zion and the sprinkled blood, they receive an unshakable Kingdom and must serve God with reverence.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The readers are addressed as holy brothers and partakers of a heavenly calling, told to consider Jesus. They are warned against an evil heart of unbelief and falling away, urged to exhort one another daily, and told they are partakers of Christ if they hold their confidence firm to the end.

Chapter 12

The readers are exhorted to run the race with perseverance, endure discipline as God's children, lift their drooping hands and feeble knees, and pursue peace and sanctification. They are warned against falling short of God's grace, bitterness, sexual immorality, and profanity like Esau's, and against refusing the one who speaks. They are told they have come to Mount Zion rather than the burning mountain, and are to serve God acceptably with reverence and awe, since they receive an unshakable Kingdom.
