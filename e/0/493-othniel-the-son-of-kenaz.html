Othniel the son of Kenaz

person · 2 works · 9 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Othniel, a savior to the children of Israel

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Joshua, Othniel captures Kiriath Sepher and is rewarded with Caleb's daughter Achsah as his wife. In The Book of Judges, Othniel wins Achsah as his wife by taking Kiriath Sepher, then serves as Israel's savior and judge, defeats Cushan Rishathaim, and brings forty years of rest before his death.
