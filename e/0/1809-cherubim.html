Abstract written by a model

The cherubim are four winged living creatures seen by the river Chebar, each with four faces (man, lion on the right, ox on the left, eagle), four wings, human hands under the wings, and straight calf-like feet that sparkle like burnished bronze. Eyes cover their bodies, backs, hands and wings. Their appearance is like burning coals and torches, and each has a whirling wheel beside it. They go straight forward without turning, led by the spirit. In Chapter 1 they emerge from a fiery cloud, running like flashes, with wings sounding like great waters and the voice of the Almighty. In Chapter 10 the narrator identifies them as the same creatures; they stand at the right of the house, one hands fire to the man clothed in linen, and they mount up with the wheels and stop at the east gate of the LORD's house.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Four living creatures with the likeness of a man come out of a fiery cloud; each has four faces (man, lion on the right, ox on the left, eagle) and four wings, straight feet like a calf's sparkling like burnished bronze, and human hands under the wings. They go straight forward without turning wherever the spirit goes, look like burning coals and torches with lightning, and run and return like flashes. Their wings sound like great waters and the voice of the Almighty, and they let their wings down when they stand.

Chapter 10

The cherubim stand on the right side of the house, and one cherub hands fire from between them to the man clothed in linen. Each has four faces (cherub, man, lion, eagle), four wings, and human-like hands under the wings, and the narrator identifies them with the living creature he saw by the river Chebar. They mount up from the earth with the wheels beside them and stop at the east gate of the LORD's house.
