Raphael in Tobit

author unknown · 224 to 174 BC · the work’s page · read it where Raphael first appears

Abstract written by a model

Raphael is an angel, one of the seven holy angels who present the saints' prayers before God's glory, sent by God to heal Tobit and Sarah. His tasks are to scale the white films from Tobit's eyes, give Sarah to Tobias as wife, and bind the evil spirit Asmodaeus. He first appears in disguise: Tobias does not recognize him, and he claims the name Azarias, son of Ananias the great, of Tobit's kindred. Saying he knows the way to Rages of Media well and has lodged with Gabael, he is hired at a drachma a day plus necessities and becomes Tobias's companion, addressed as Brother Azarias. He travels with Tobias to Ecbatana and presents the matter discussed on the road to Raguel. Later he takes a servant and two camels to Gabael in Rages, collects the sealed bags of money, and brings Gabael back to the wedding feast. Near the end he urges Tobias to run ahead and carry the fish's bile, and instructs him to anoint Tobit's eyes so the films fall away. Offered half of all that was brought, he reveals his identity, explains he brought the memorial of Tobit's and Sarah's prayers, and says he only appeared to eat and drink. He tells them to write everything in a book, then ascends to God and is seen no more.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Raphael is sent to heal both Tobit and Sarah. His tasks are to scale the white films from Tobit's eyes, to give Sarah to Tobias as wife, and to bind the evil spirit Asmodaeus.

Chapter 5

Raphael is identified as an angel whom Tobias does not recognize when he finds him. He agrees to go with Tobias, says he knows the way well and has lodged with Gabael, and tells Tobit he is Azarias, son of Ananias the great, of Tobit's kindred. Tobit agrees to pay him a drachma a day, and he departs with Tobias.

Chapter 7

Addressed by Tobias as Brother Azarias, he travels with Tobias to Ecbatana. At Tobias's urging he presents the matter discussed on the road to Raguel.

Chapter 9

Raphael, addressed as Brother Azarias, is told by Tobias to take a servant and two camels to Gabael in Rages of Media. He lodges with Gabael, gives him the handwriting, and receives the sealed bags of money. He then rises early and returns with Gabael to the wedding feast.

Chapter 11

Raphael urges Tobias to run ahead of his wife and prepare the house, and to carry the bile of the fish. He announces that Tobit will open his eyes and instructs Tobias to anoint them, explaining that the pricking will make Tobit rub them and the white films fall away.

Chapter 12

Raphael, who had accompanied them, is offered half of all that was brought. He reveals his identity as one of the seven holy angels who present the saints' prayers before God's glory, says he brought the memorial of Tobit's and Sarah's prayers and was with Tobit when he buried the dead, and says God sent him to heal Tobit and Sarah. He reassures them, says he only appeared to eat and drink, instructs them to write everything in a book, and ascends to God, after which they see him no more.

Azarias in Tobit

author unknown · 224 to 174 BC · the work’s page · read it where Azarias first appears

Abstract written by a model

Azarias is an angel who travels with Tobias as guide and companion, addressed as brother. In Chapter 6 he has Tobias keep the fish's heart, liver and bile, explains their uses, proposes Sarah as bride, and promises God's salvation.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Azarias, the young man's companion who is addressed as brother, commands him to grab the fish and to keep its heart, liver and bile. He explains their uses against demons and eye films, proposes that Sarah be given to Tobias, and says he will speak to Raguel. He tells Tobias how to drive off the demon in the bride-chamber and assures him that God will save them.
