Also called: bile, the bile, the bile of the fish

Abstract written by a model

Tobias is told to carry the bile of the fish in his hand as they near Nineveh. Raphael explains that anointing Tobit's eyes with it will prick them, so that he rubs them and the white films fall away. Tobias rubs it on his father's eyes, and it is the means by which the films peel away.
