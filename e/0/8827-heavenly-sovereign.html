Heavenly Sovereign

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: His Augustness Mima-ki-iri-biko-iniwe, Mima-ki-iri, This Heavenly Sovereign, this King

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Mima-ki-iri-biko-iniwe was a Heavenly Sovereign who ruled the Empire, wedded three women, and begot twelve august children in Kojiki. The Heavenly Sovereign grieved over the great pestilence, received the Great Deity the Great-Master-of-Things in an august dream, ordered rites and military action, and received reports after the campaigns in Kojiki.

Heavenly Sovereign in Kojiki

Abstract written by a model

rules Empire [during the reign in which the great pestilence arose] The Heavenly Sovereign grieved over the great pestilence and received the Great Deity the Great-Master-of-Things in an august dream. He ordered Oho-tata-ne-ko to worship the Great Deity of Great Miwa and commanded other rites, after which the country was tranquil. He also directed military action against King Take-hani-yasu and received reports after the campaigns.
