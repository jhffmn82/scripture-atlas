Chapter 71

The speaker's enemies talk about him and conspire together, watching for his soul. They say God has forsaken him and urge one another to pursue and take him because no one will rescue him. The speaker asks that these accusers be disappointed, consumed, and covered with disgrace and scorn, and states that they are disappointed and confounded.
