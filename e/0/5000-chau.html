Chau

person · 1 work · 15 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the One man

Abstract written by a model

Chau is a ruler whose greatest achievements come from generous, just restoration and care for the people. He gives gifts, enriches the good, prefers virtue to kinship, scrutinizes measures and laws, restores officers, States, families, and retired people, and gives chief importance to food, mourning, and sacrifices. His generosity, sincerity, earnest activity, and justice win the people.

Chau is in one work on this site, The Analects of Confucius, Book XX (Yao Yueh). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. I. 1. Yao said, 'Oh! you, Shun, the Heaven-determined

Chau confers great gifts, enriches the good, and says that virtuous men are more important to him than near relatives. He restores measures, laws, officers, extinguished States, broken lines of succession, and retired people, while emphasizing food, mourning duties, and sacrifices. His generosity, sincerity, earnest activity, and justice win the people and make his achievements great.
