Airyana Vaêgô

place · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

Airyana Vaêgô is the first created good land and country, lying by the good river Dâitya. Angra Mainyu brings a serpent into the river and winter with severe plagues, leaving ten winter months and two summer months. By Dâitya, Ahura Mazda gathers celestial gods, and Yima gathers excellent mortals.

Airyana Vaêgô is in one work on this site, Vendidad (Vendîdâd), Fargards I-VIII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Vendidad (Vendîdâd) / Fargard I.

Airyana Vaêgô was the first of the good lands and countries created by Ahura Mazda, by the good river Dâitya. Angra Mainyu counter-created the serpent in the river and winter there, and it has ten winter months and two summer months.

Fargard II.

Airyana Vaêgô is the place where Ahura Mazda, by the good river Dâitya, calls together a meeting of the celestial gods. It is also where Yima calls together a meeting of the excellent mortals.
