Also called: new, no new thing under the sun

Abstract written by a model

The principle from Ecclesiastes 1 that nothing is new under the sun: what has been will be again. The Preacher asks whether anything is new, answers that it already existed in earlier ages, and notes that former and later things go unremembered by those who follow.

Ecclesiastes / Chapter 1

The Preacher says that what has been is what shall be, and what has been done is what shall be done, so there is no new thing under the sun. He asks whether anything can be called new and answers that it has been long ago, in the ages before us. He adds that there is no memory of the former things, nor will there be of the latter things among those who come after.
