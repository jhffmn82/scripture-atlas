Hazael

Also called: Hazael king of Syria, a dog, your servant, who is but a dog

Abstract written by a model

Hazael begins as servant of Benhadad, sent with gifts to ask Elisha about the king's recovery. Elisha reveals Hazael will become king of Syria and do great evil to Israel. He tells Benhadad he will recover, then smothers him with a wet cloth and reigns. As king he fights Joram and Ahaziah at Ramoth Gilead, attacks Israel's borders, captures Gath, and turns toward Jerusalem until Jehoash pays tribute in holy things and gold. He oppresses Israel through Jehoahaz's days, then dies, succeeded by his son Benhadad.

Hazael is in one work on this site, The Second Book of Kings. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 8

Hazael is sent by Benhadad with forty camels' burden of gifts to ask Elisha whether the king will recover. Told by Elisha that he will be king and do great evil to Israel, he tells Benhadad he will recover, then the next day smothers him with a wet cloth and reigns in his place. He later fights Joram and Ahaziah at Ramoth Gilead.

Chapter 12

Hazael king of Syria went up, fought against Gath and took it, and set his face to go up to Jerusalem. Jehoash sent him the holy things and gold from the LORD's house and the king's house, and Hazael went away from Jerusalem.

Chapter 13

Hazael king of Syria oppressed Israel all the days of Jehoahaz, with the LORD delivering Israel into his hand. He died and his son Benhadad reigned in his place.
