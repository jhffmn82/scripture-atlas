Chapter 11

Demetrius' letter to Lasthenes confirmed the borders of Judea and added the three governments of Aphaerema, Lydda and Ramathaim, in place of the king's yearly dues. He also gave back the tithes, taxes, salt pits and crown taxes, declared the grants never to be annulled, and ordered a copy set up on the holy mountain. He later lied and treated Jonathan harshly.
