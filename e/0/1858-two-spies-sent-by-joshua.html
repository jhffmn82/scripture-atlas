Chapter 2

The two spies enter Jericho, lodge at Rahab's house, and are hidden by her from the king's pursuers. They swear to deal kindly and truly with her, set the scarlet thread and house-gathering conditions, and then hide in the mountain for three days before returning to Joshua with the report that the inhabitants melt away before them.
