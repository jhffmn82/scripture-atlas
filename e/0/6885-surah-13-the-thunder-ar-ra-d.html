Abstract written by a model

Surah 13: The Thunder (Ar-Ra'd), revealed at Medina, concerns Allah's signs, revelation, guidance, and judgment. It describes Allah's creation and ordering of the heavens, earth, sun, moon, night, day, rain, lightning, and thunder. It contrasts believers and those who disbelieve, describing the rewards of those who answer Allah's call and the punishment awaiting those who reject it. Muhammad is told to proclaim Allah's oneness and his role as a warner, while Allah's knowledge, power, forgiveness, and reckoning are affirmed.
