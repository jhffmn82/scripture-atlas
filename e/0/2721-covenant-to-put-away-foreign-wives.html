Chapter 10

Shecaniah proposes the covenant to put away all foreign wives and the children born of them according to the law and the counsel of those who tremble at God's commandment. Ezra has the chiefs of the priests, the Levites, and all Israel swear to it, and the guilty priests give their hand to put away their wives and offer a ram for their guilt.
