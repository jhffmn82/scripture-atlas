Abstract written by a model

A little horn arises among ten horns, uprooting three. With human-like eyes and a mouth speaking great things, it wars against the saints and prevails until the Ancient of Days comes. Interpreted as a king, it speaks against the Most High, wears out saints, plans to change times and law, and is finally destroyed.

Chapter 7

A little horn comes up among the ten horns, uprooting three of them, and has eyes like a man's and a mouth speaking great things. It makes war with the saints and prevails until the Ancient of Days comes. It is interpreted as a king who will put down three kings, speak against the Most High, wear out the saints, and plan to change times and law, until his dominion is taken away and destroyed.
