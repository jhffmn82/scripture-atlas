Chapter 6

Jesus says the work of God is to believe in the one he sent, and that whoever believes in him will never be thirsty and has eternal life. Some of those who saw him do not believe, and he says no one can come unless the Father gives it. Many disciples leave, but Peter confesses belief that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of the living God.
