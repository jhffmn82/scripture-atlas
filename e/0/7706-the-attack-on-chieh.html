Abstract written by a model

The attack on Chieh was undertaken by T'ang after consultations with Pien Sui, Wu Kuang, and I Yin, and it ended in Chieh's defeat. Pien Sui and Wu Kuang condemned the political and moral implications of the attack and refused the empire afterward.
