Chapter 22

The altar is a great one built beside the Jordan in the region of Canaan, which provokes Israel to prepare for war. Its builders say it is a copy of the LORD's altar, meant as a witness and not for burnt offering or sacrifice. It is named "A Witness Between Us that the LORD is God."
