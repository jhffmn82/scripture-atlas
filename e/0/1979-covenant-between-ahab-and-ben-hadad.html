Chapter 20

After Ben Hadad's servants plead for his life, Ahab calls him his brother, takes him into his chariot, and makes a covenant with him. Ben Hadad promises to restore cities his father took and to give Ahab streets in Damascus, and Ahab lets him go; a prophet later condemns this release.
