Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Joseph is Mary's husband and Jesus's legal father who obeys angelic warnings, protects the family, and settles with them in Nazareth. In The Gospel According to Luke, Joseph is Mary's husband and Jesus's father who travels to Bethlehem, witnesses events surrounding Jesus's birth and presentation, returns to Nazareth, and finds Jesus in the Jerusalem temple.

Joseph in The Gospel According to Matthew

author unknown · AD 80 to 90 · the work’s page · read it where Joseph first appears

Abstract written by a model

Joseph is a righteous man, son of Jacob and husband of Mary, whose son Jesus is born. Initially planning to quietly put Mary away when she is found pregnant, he follows an angel's dream message, takes her as wife, and names the child Jesus. Warned in dreams, he protects the family by taking them to Egypt by night, later returns after Herod's death, and, fearing Archelaus, withdraws to Galilee and settles in Nazareth.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

The Gospel According to Matthew / Chapter 1

Joseph is the son of Jacob and the husband of Mary, from whom Jesus was born. A righteous man, he intends to put Mary away secretly when she is found pregnant, but after the angel speaks to him in a dream he takes her as his wife, does not know her sexually until she gives birth, and names her firstborn son Jesus.

Chapter 2

Joseph is told by an angel in a dream to take the child and his mother and flee to Egypt, and he does so by night. After Herod's death he is told in a dream to return to the land of Israel, and, afraid of Archelaus and warned in another dream, he withdraws to Galilee and settles in Nazareth.

Joseph in The Gospel According to Luke

author unknown · AD 80 to 90 · the work’s page · read it where Joseph first appears

Abstract written by a model

Joseph, son of Heli, lives in Nazareth and belongs to David's house. He travels to Bethlehem with Mary to enroll, witnesses the shepherds' visit and temple presentation, returns to Nazareth, and later he and Mary lose and find twelve-year-old Jesus in Jerusalem's temple at Passover.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Joseph goes up from Nazareth in Galilee to Bethlehem, the city of David, because he is of David's house and family, to enroll with Mary. He is present at the shepherds' visit, the presentation at the temple, and the return to Nazareth. He and Mary lose Jesus in Jerusalem at the Passover when Jesus is twelve and find him in the temple after three days.
