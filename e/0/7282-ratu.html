Ratu

person · 4 works · 28 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Lord and regulator, Ratu from his Righteousness, Ratu's dwelling, Ratu's house, The Ratu, a Ratu, its Ratu, our) Ratu, the Ratu

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Ratu is a holy, learned, righteous lordship and authority of ritual order that speaks from Righteousness, responds to the Zaotar, commands deserving men to eat the Myazda, identifies the Zaotar as an Ahû to be revered and chosen, and is itself an Ahû to be chosen. In Yasna, Ratu is an allied peer of the master who dwells in the work-field of the Righteous Order and pastures of Thy Good Mind, rules from Righteousness, creates mental goodness and actions for Mazda, and blesses the good servant’s acquisition and reward. In Visparad, Ratu is a holy, learned priest who speaks from righteousness and answers the Zaotar’s calls by coming or fulfilling assigned priestly duties. In Afrinagan, Gahs and Miscellaneous Fragments, Ratu is a ruler who guides disciples, receives required Myazda, expels those who fail to bring it, may impose expiating deeds, and receives wood when the prescribed offering cannot be made.

Ratu in Yasna

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Ratu first appears

Abstract written by a model

Ratu is a holy, learned, righteous lordship and authority of ritual order, subject to exact regulation and ruling from Righteousness. It creates mental goodness and life’s actions done for Mazda. In the ritual dialogue, Ratu speaks from Righteousness as holy and learned, responds to the Zaotar, commands deserving men to eat the Myazda, and identifies the Zaotar as an Ahû to be revered and chosen. Ratu is itself an Ahû to be chosen.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna III.

The Ratu is a holy lord of the ritual order and is associated with exact regulation. At the conclusion, the Ratu responds to the Zaotar and is called upon to speak forth from his Righteousness, holy and learned.

Yasna VIII.

The Ratu commands deserving men to eat the Myazda. The Ratu also speaks of the Zaotar as an Ahû to be revered and chosen.

Yasna XIV (Sp. XV).

The Ratu speaks in the ritual dialogue and is described as an Ahû to be chosen. The Ratu is instructed to speak forth from his Righteousness as holy and learned.

Ratu in Yasna

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where Ratu first appears

Abstract written by a model

Ratu is an allied peer of the master, dwelling in the work-field of the Righteous Order and pastures of Thy Good Mind. Ruling from Righteousness, Ratu creates mental goodness and actions for Mazda, and speaks a blessing for the good servant’s acquisition and reward.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Yasna LIX (Sp. LVIII).

The Ratu speaks a blessing wishing the good servant of the Lord what is better than the good, acquisition of what is their own in the Zaothra, and the reward obtained by the Zaotar. The Ratu is also described as one who rules from Righteousness.
