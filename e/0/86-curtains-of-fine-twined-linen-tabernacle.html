Abstract written by a model

Ten curtains of fine twined linen in blue, purple and scarlet, decorated with cherubim by a skillful workman, each 28 by 4 cubits. They are coupled in two sets of five, with fifty blue loops and fifty gold clasps joining them, so the tabernacle becomes a unit.

Chapter 26

Ten curtains of fine twined linen, blue, purple and scarlet, with cherubim, are to be made by a skillful workman, each twenty-eight cubits long and four wide. They are joined in two sets of five using fifty blue loops and fifty gold clasps so that the tabernacle is a unit.
