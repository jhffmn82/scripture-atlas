Chapter 9

A man blind from birth receives sight after Jesus anoints his eyes with mud and he washes at Siloam, an event the text says happened on a Sabbath. It causes disputes among the neighbors and divides the Pharisees, who debate whether a sinner could do such signs. The man notes that it has never been heard of since the world began that anyone opened the eyes of someone born blind.
