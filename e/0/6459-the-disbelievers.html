the disbelievers

group · 8 works · 16 quoted facts · 10 connections
Also called: a guilty folk, disbelievers, dwellers of the Fire, evil-doers, the guilty, the owners of the Fire, the wicked, the wrong-doers, they, those who did wrong, those who disbelieve, those who disbelieved, wrong-doers, ye

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), dwellers of the Fire acknowledge their punishment, ask the dwellers of the Garden for water or provision, and are refused because Allah has forbidden these to disbelievers. In Surah 14: Abraham (Ibrahim), the disbelievers prefer worldly life to the Hereafter, debar others from Allah's way, are far astray, have works likened to windblown ashes, and face Hell and painful doom. In Surah 18: The Cave (Al-Kahf), the disbelievers reject Allah's revelations, dispute the truth, mock revelations and messengers, and face hell, punishment, and loss of their works' value. In Surah 24: The Light (An-Noor), the disbelievers' deeds are compared to a mirage and layers of darkness; they cannot escape in the land, and fire is their home. In Surah 45: Crouching (Al-Jaathiya), the disbelievers reject or mock revelations, follow falsehood, deny the Hour, and face painful doom and the Fire. In Surah 59: The Exile (Al-Hashr), the owners of the Fire are unequal to the owners of the Garden, abide in the Fire, and receive the consequence presented as the reward of evil-doers. In Surah 74: The Cloaked One (Al-Muddaththir), disbelievers face a day of anguish, question the Fire's wardens and Allah's similitude, deny the Hereafter, and are punished in the burning. In Surah 80: He frowned (Abasa), disbelievers have dust upon their faces and are veiled in darkness on that day.
