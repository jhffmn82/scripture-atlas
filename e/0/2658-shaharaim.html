Shaharaim

person · 1 work · 13 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

Shaharaim is a man with three wives, Hushim, Baara and Hodesh. After sending children away he fathered others in Moab's field. By Hodesh he had seven children, led by Jobab, head of a fathers' household; by Hushim he had Abitub and Elpaal.

Chapter 8

Shaharaim fathered children in the field of Moab after sending them away; Hushim and Baara were his wives. By Hodesh his wife he fathered Jobab, Zibia, Mesha, Malcam, Jeuz, Shachia, and Mirmah, and by Hushim he fathered Abitub and Elpaal.
