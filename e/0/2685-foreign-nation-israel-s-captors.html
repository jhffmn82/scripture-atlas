Chapter 4

God brought this nation from afar against Israel. It is described as shameless, speaking a strange language, and showing no respect for old men or pity for children. It carried off the widow's sons and daughters, and the chapter foretells that its enemy-persecutors will be destroyed and trodden upon, and that the city which received Israel's sons will be grieved in desolation, burned by fire, and inhabited by demons.
