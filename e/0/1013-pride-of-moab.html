pride of Moab

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, Moab's pride is marked by arrogance, pride, and wrath, and its boastings are declared to be nothing. In The Book of Jeremiah, Moab's pride is characterized by great loftiness and arrogance, and is given as a reason Moab will be made drunk, become a derision, and be destroyed as a people.
