Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Job, the wicked man is a godless figure whose portion from the Almighty is ruin and sudden destruction. In Proverbs, the wicked person heeds wicked lips, accepts bribes, seeks rebellion, and faces judgment, evil, trouble, and no prosperity.

wicked man in The Book of Job

author unknown · about 574 BC · the work’s page · read it where wicked man first appears

Abstract written by a model

The wicked man is a figure of the godless whose portion from the Almighty is ruin. His many children are destined for the sword and go unsatisfied with bread, and his widows do not lament. His silver and clothing pass to the just and innocent, and his house is as fragile as a moth's or a watchman's booth. He lies down rich but is suddenly gone; terrors and a storm overtake him, and men clap and hiss him from his place.

wicked person in Proverbs

author unknown · 969 to 699 BC · the work’s page · read it where wicked person first appears

Abstract written by a model

A wicked person heeds wicked lips, secretly takes bribes to pervert justice, and seeks only rebellion. The outcome is judgment: a cruel messenger will be sent against him, and evil, trouble, and no prosperity follow those repaying evil for good.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 17

The wicked person heeds wicked lips, receives bribes in secret to pervert justice, and seeks only rebellion, so a cruel messenger will be sent against him. One who repays evil for good will never see evil leave his house. Those with perverse hearts or deceitful tongues find no prosperity and fall into trouble.
