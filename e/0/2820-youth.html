Chapter 11

Youth is presented as a time to rejoice, with a cheerful heart and walking in the ways of the heart and the sight of the eyes. The unit also says that youth and the dawn of life are vanity, and that sorrow and evil should be removed.

Chapter 12

Youth is the time in which the reader is told to remember their Creator, before the evil days come and the years draw near when they will say they have no pleasure in them.
