Chapter 18

The speaker tells his son not to add reproach to good deeds or harsh words to giving. A word is said to be better than a gift, like dew relieving scorching heat, and both are found with a gracious person. A fool is ungracious and abusive, and the gift of a grudging person consumes the eyes.
