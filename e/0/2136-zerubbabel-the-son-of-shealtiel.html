Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Ezra, Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel leads the returning exiles, builds the altar, offers burnt offerings, begins the LORD's house, and refuses the adversaries' help. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, Zerubbabel is likened to a signet ring on the right hand. In The Book of Haggai, Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel is governor of Judah and the LORD of Armies' servant, who obeys, works on the LORD's house, and is promised to be like a chosen signet ring. In The Book of Zechariah, Zerubbabel lays the house's foundation and is promised to finish it by the Spirit, holding the plumb line and bringing out the capstone.

Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel in The Book of Ezra

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel first appears

Abstract written by a model

Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, is a leader of the people returning from captivity. Named first among the returnees alongside Jeshua, he builds the altar of the God of Israel with his relatives and Jeshua, offers burnt offerings, and begins work on the LORD's house under Cyrus's command. Finally, with Jeshua and the household heads, he refuses the adversaries' offer of help, insisting they alone will build.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Zerubbabel is named first among the leaders with whom the returning people came up from captivity, along with Jeshua, Nehemiah, Seraiah, and others.

Chapter 3

Zerubbabel, with his relatives and Jeshua, built the altar of the God of Israel and offered burnt offerings on it. In the second month of the second year he joined Jeshua, the priests, and the Levites in beginning the work on the LORD's house.

Chapter 4

Zerubbabel, together with Jeshua and the other heads of fathers' households, refuses the adversaries' offer to help build the temple. He says they alone will build to the LORD, the God of Israel, as King Cyrus commanded.

Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel in The Book of Haggai

Abstract written by a model

Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel, is governor of Judah and the LORD of Armies' servant. Addressed through Haggai, he obeys with Joshua and the remnant, and the LORD stirs his spirit to work on the LORD's house. He is told to be strong, and promised that when kingdoms fall he will be made like a signet ring, chosen.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Zerubbabel, governor of Judah, is addressed by the LORD's word through Haggai. He obeys the LORD's voice together with Joshua and the remnant, and the LORD stirs up his spirit so that he comes and works on the LORD's house.

Chapter 2

Zerubbabel, son of Shealtiel and governor of Judah, is told to be strong and work, since the LORD is with him. In the second word he is promised that when the LORD overthrows the kingdoms, he will be taken as the LORD's servant and made like a signet ring because the LORD has chosen him.

Zerubbabel in The Book of Zechariah

Abstract written by a model

Zerubbabel is a person who laid the house's foundation and is promised to finish it. Told the work depends on the Spirit, not might, he will see the mountain become a plain, hold the plumb line, and bring out the capstone with shouts of 'Grace, grace.'

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

Zerubbabel receives the LORD's word that the work depends on the Spirit, not might or power. The great mountain becomes a plain before him, and he will bring out the capstone amid shouts of "Grace, grace, to it!" His hands laid the foundation of the house and will also finish it, and he will hold the plumb line.
