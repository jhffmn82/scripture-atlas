Chapter 19

The second cub is made a young lion, goes up and down among the lions, learns to catch prey, and devours men. He knows their palaces, lays waste their cities, and desolates the land with his roaring. The nations attack him from the provinces, net and cage him with hooks, and bring him to the king of Babylon so his voice is no more heard on the mountains of Israel.
