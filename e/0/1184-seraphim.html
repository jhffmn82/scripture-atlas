seraphim in The Book of Isaiah

Abstract written by a model

The seraphim are six-winged beings standing above the LORD of Armies in Isaiah's vision. They cry to one another that he is holy and the earth full of his glory; then one touches the narrator's mouth with an altar's live coal, declaring his iniquity removed.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

The seraphim stand above the Lord, each with six wings: two cover the face, two cover the feet, and two are for flying. They call to one another that the LORD of Armies is holy and that the earth is full of his glory. One of them flies to the narrator with a live coal from the altar, taken with tongs, touches his mouth, and pronounces his iniquity taken away.
