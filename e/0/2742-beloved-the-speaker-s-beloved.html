Abstract written by a model

The beloved is the one to whom the woman declares she belongs, saying that his desire is toward her. She invites him to go out to the field, lodge in the villages and go early to the vineyards, where she will give him her love. She says that precious fruits, new and old, are at their doors, stored up for him.
