In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the messenger prepares the Father's way, comes suddenly to the temple, and purifies Levi's sons. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the messenger of the covenant suddenly comes to the temple.
