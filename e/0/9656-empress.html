Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods) / Preface.

The Empress is praised for obtaining Unity, illuminating the empire, nourishing the people, and extending her virtue and influence. She regretted errors in the old words and misstatements in the former chronicles, then commanded Yasumaro to record the words memorized by Hiyeda no Are.
