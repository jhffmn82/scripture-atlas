Abstract written by a model

The widow is found gathering sticks at the city gate and says she has only a handful of meal and a little oil, intending to bake a last meal for herself and her son before they die. She obeys Elijah, makes him a cake first, and her household eats for many days. When her son dies she accuses Elijah of bringing her sin to memory, but after he is revived she says she knows he is a man of God and that the LORD's word in his mouth is truth.
