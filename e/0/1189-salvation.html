Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, salvation comes to Zion with the LORD's reward and recompense, shining out like a burning lamp. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record, salvation requires repentance, belief, baptism, worthy living, working it out with fear and trembling, and enduring to the end.

salvation in The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record

Mormon · the work’s page · read it where salvation first appears

Abstract written by a model

Salvation requires repentance and is unavailable without it; it is received by those who believe and are baptized. People must work it out with fear and trembling, live worthily, and endure to the end to avoid being cast out.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

The people cannot be saved unless they come unto repentance. Belief in Christ and baptism are presented as part of what will make it well with them in the day of judgment.

Chapter 9

People are told to work out their own salvation with fear and trembling before the Lord. Those who do all things in worthiness and endure to the end will in nowise be cast out.
