Abstract written by a model

Mattathias is a priest of the sons of Joarib, son of John, living at Modin with five sons. Zealous for the law, he mourns Jerusalem's ruin and refuses the king's officers' honors, then kills an apostate Jew and the king's officer at the altar. He flees to the mountains, leads the resistance, and decides to fight on the Sabbath. He gives his sons a farewell charge, blesses them, dies in the 146th year, and is buried at Modin amid great lamentation.

Chapter 2

Mattathias, a priest of the sons of Joarib living at Modin, mourns the ruin of Jerusalem, refuses the king's officers' offer of honors, and kills an apostate Jew and the king's officer at the altar in zeal like Phinehas. He flees to the mountains, leads the resistance, decides with his friends to fight on the Sabbath, and gives a farewell charge to his sons citing Abraham, Joseph, Phinehas, Joshua, Caleb, David, Elijah, Hananiah, Azariah, Mishael and Daniel. He blesses them and dies in the 146th year, buried at Modin amid Israel's great lamentation.
