the wicked

group · 4 works · 13 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: A dishonest man, An evil man, The bloodthirsty, an accomplice of a thief, bloodthirsty, he will not learn righteousness, the unclean, those who are godless in heart, those who rose up against us, wicked, wicked men, your adversaries

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Job, wicked men reject God, are snatched away before their time, have their foundation poured out like a stream, and meet a fiery end at which the righteous rejoice. In The Book of Job, the wicked, godless in heart, lay up anger and do not cry for help when God binds them; they die in youth among the unclean, perishing by the sword without knowledge. In Psalms, the wicked are likened to wind-driven chaff; they will not stand in judgment or among the righteous, and their way will ultimately perish. In Proverbs, the wicked disregard knowledge; when they rule, people groan, and when they multiply, sin increases, while the bloodthirsty hate the upright and seek their lives. In The Book of Isaiah, the wicked fail to learn righteousness even when shown favor, act wrongfully in the land of uprightness, and are consumed as fire consumes adversaries.
