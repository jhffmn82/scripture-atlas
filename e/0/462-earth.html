world in Hymns of Guru Nanak

Guru Nanak · the work’s page · read it where world first appears

Abstract written by a model

The world is God’s created and sustained place of temporary existence, pervaded by God but filled with sin, mammon, entanglement, and desire for sweetness over bitterness. People live in it briefly, hearing and speaking of God, yet often forget the Creator, trade like transient shopkeepers, amass wealth despite death, and become intoxicated with worldly love. It is ruined by the ignorant Jogi, lying Qazi, and life-taking Brahman. Its condition unfolds through images of a frail cord that soon snaps, a terrible ocean of wandering and suffering, and transient lightning and fire. Empire, wealth, beauty, nobility, and youth rob it, while people face judgment for deeds. The Guru’s instruction, dispelling doubts, frees people from entanglements and enables them to cross the ocean; God’s name cools its fire.
