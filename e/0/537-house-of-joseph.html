Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Joshua, the children of Joseph, also called the house of Joseph, receive a northern allotment, complain that it is too small, and receive Joshua’s promise of the hill country and victory over the Canaanites. In The Book of Judges, the house of Joseph goes up against Bethel with the LORD’s help, takes the city, spares a man and his family, and subjects the Amorites to forced labor. In The Book of Zechariah, the LORD says he will save the house of Joseph and bring them back out of mercy. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the remnant of the seed of Joseph receives mercy and is brought again to knowledge of God. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the house of Joseph is the people among whom the twelve disciples are a light, and they receive their land of inheritance from the Father. In The Book of Ether, the remnant of the seed of Joseph inherits the land, is numbered among Israel, and builds the holy New Jerusalem.

children of Joseph in The Book of Joshua

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where children of Joseph first appears

Abstract written by a model

The children of Joseph, also called the house of Joseph, are a group made up of two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim, living in the north. They first receive a lot with a border from the Jordan at Jericho through the hill country to the sea. They then complain to Joshua that a single allotment is too small for a numerous people blessed by the LORD. Joshua affirms their great power and promises them the hill country and victory over the Canaanites.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

The lot came out for the children of Joseph, with a border running from the Jordan at Jericho through the hill country to Bethel, Luz, Ataroth, Beth Horon the lower and Gezer, ending at the sea. Manasseh and Ephraim, the children of Joseph, took their inheritance.

Chapter 17

The children of Joseph complained to Joshua that one lot and one part was too little for a numerous people blessed by the LORD. When Joshua told them to clear the forest, they said the hill country was not enough and the valley Canaanites had iron chariots. Joshua, addressing them as the house of Joseph (Ephraim and Manasseh), said they were numerous and powerful and would have the hill country and drive out the Canaanites.
