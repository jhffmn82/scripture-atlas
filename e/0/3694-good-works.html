good works

topic · 3 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: good work

Abstract written by a model

In Titus, good works are not the basis of salvation, but believers are instructed to be ready for, maintain, and practice them because they are good and profitable to men. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), good works are done by believers and associated with promised entry into Gardens and everlasting dwelling. In Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida), good works are associated with belief, forgiveness, immense reward, and the command to vie with one another in them.

Abstract written by a model

has_attribute good and profitable to men Good works are not the basis of salvation, since God saved the believers not by works of righteousness that they did. Yet believers are to be ready for every good work, to be careful to maintain them, and to practice them to meet necessary needs so they are not unfruitful. The text calls these things good and profitable to men.

good works in Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa)

author unknown · undated · the work’s page · read it where good works first appears

Abstract written by a model

Good works are done by believers, those who believe and do them. In the passage, believers are instructed about approaching prayer in states of drunkenness or pollution and about using clean soil if water is unavailable. Those who combine belief with good works are promised entry into Gardens with rivers, pure companions, plenteous shade, and everlasting dwelling.
