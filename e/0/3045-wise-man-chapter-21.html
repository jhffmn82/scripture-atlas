Chapter 21

The wise or prudent man commends and adds to a wise word, and his knowledge abounds like a flood with counsel like a fountain of life. His utterance is sought in the congregation, he smiles quietly, stands outside a house he has not been invited into, and is grieved at disgrace. His words are weighed in the balance, and his mouth is his heart. Instruction is to him an ornament of gold.
