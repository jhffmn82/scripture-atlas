CHAPTER XXX.

The sword of the People is represented by a dishevelled, fierce swordsman who cuts and smites opponents in personal conflict. Chuang Tzŭ compares it to a game-cock whose life is cut short and says that it is unworthy of a great prince’s sovereign power.
