Abstract written by a model

The righteous man professes knowledge of God, calls God his father, and lives a way of life unlike others', avoiding the ways of the ungodly as uncleanness. Because he reproaches their sins, they plot to torment him, test him with insult and torture, and condemn him to a shameful death to see whether God will deliver him. In the end he stands boldly before his afflicters, who are terrified and amazed at his salvation, and admit he is counted among the sons of God and has a lot among the saints.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The righteous man is targeted by the ungodly because he reproaches them for sins against the law and their training, lives differently, avoids their ways as uncleanness, calls the end of the righteous happy, and boasts that God is his father. They plan to lie in wait for him, test him with insult and torture, and condemn him to a shameful death to see if God, as his protector, will deliver him.

Chapter 5

He stands in great boldness before those who afflicted him and made his labors of no account. His persecutors are terrified at the sight of him and amazed at his salvation, and they admit they once saw his life as madness and his end as without honor, yet he is counted among the sons of God and has a lot among the saints.
