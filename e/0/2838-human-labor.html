Abstract written by a model

Human labor is portrayed as yielding no clear gain under the sun. It begins with a rhetorical question about what man gains, then calls labor and the search for understanding a heavy burden God gave humanity to be afflicted with, and ends by declaring all works vanity and chasing after wind.

Ecclesiastes / Chapter 1

The Preacher asks what man gains from all his labor in which he labors under the sun, implying no clear gain. He describes the work and the search to understand what is done under the sky as a heavy burden that God has given to the sons of men to be afflicted with. He says all the works done under the sun are vanity and a chasing after wind.
