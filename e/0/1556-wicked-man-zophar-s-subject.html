Abstract written by a model

The wicked man, described in Zophar's speech, enjoys only a brief triumph. Though his height may reach the heavens, he oppressed and forsook the poor, driven by devouring greed and restlessness. He cannot keep his gains, his children seek the poor's favor, and God's wrath strikes him down, so he perishes forever.

Chapter 20

The wicked man is described as one whose height may reach the heavens yet who perishes forever and is not found, like a dream. He oppressed and forsook the poor, devoured all, and knew no quietness, so he cannot enjoy or keep his gains; his children seek the poor's favor, and he is struck down by God's wrath, with heaven and earth against him.
