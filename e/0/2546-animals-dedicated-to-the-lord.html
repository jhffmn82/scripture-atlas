Chapter 27

Animals of a kind offered to the LORD become holy when given, and may not be altered or exchanged, since an exchanged animal and its substitute both become holy. Unclean animals are valued by the priest and may be redeemed with a fifth added, and firstborn animals cannot be dedicated because they are already the LORD's.
