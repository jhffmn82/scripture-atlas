Chapter 4

The LORD calls for abominations to be put away from his sight and for evil thoughts to be washed from the heart. He says the people's way and doings have brought the coming disaster, and that their wickedness is bitter and reaches their heart. Their rebellion against him is given as the reason for the besieging watchers and the coming desolation.
