division of the kingdom

event · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Israel rebelled against David’s house, Israel's rebellion (division of the kingdom), To your tents, Israel!, kingdom's division (Israel's sovereignty), rebelled, the sovereignty was divided

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Kings, the division of the kingdom occurred when Israel rejected Rehoboam and David's house, making Jeroboam king while Judah and Benjamin stayed with Rehoboam. In The Second Book of Chronicles, Israel's rebellion against David's house caused Israel to depart from Rehoboam, sealing the break between the kingdoms. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, the kingdom's division resulted from Solomon's folly, with a disobedient kingdom ruled out of Ephraim.
