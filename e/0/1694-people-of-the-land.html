people of the land

group · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: The people of the land

Abstract written by a model

In Leviticus, the people of the land are commanded to stone anyone who gives offspring to Molech. In The Book of Ezekiel, the people of the land are accused of oppression and robbery, troubling the poor and needy and wrongfully oppressing the foreigner.
