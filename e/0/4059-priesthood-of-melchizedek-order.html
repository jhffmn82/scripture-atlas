Chapter 7

This priesthood is the one named in the testimony 'You are a priest forever, according to the order of Melchizedek,' and it is based on the power of an endless life, not a fleshly commandment. It was established with an oath, unlike the Levitical priests, and it replaces the Aaronic order as the way to a better hope through which people draw near to God. Jesus holds it unchangeably because he lives forever.
