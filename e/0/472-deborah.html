Abstract written by a model

Deborah is a prophetess, wife of Lappidoth, who judges Israel from under her palm tree between Ramah and Bethel. She summons Barak, relays the LORD's command to attack Sisera, goes with him, and says honor will go to a woman. Later she sings with Barak, declaring she arose as a mother in Israel when rulers had ceased.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 4

Deborah, a prophetess and wife of Lappidoth, judged Israel from under her palm tree between Ramah and Bethel, and the children of Israel came to her for judgment. She called Barak, relayed the LORD's command to attack Sisera, agreed to go with him, and told him the honor would go to a woman. She later told Barak to go because the LORD had delivered Sisera into his hand.

Chapter 5

Deborah sings with Barak and says she arose as a mother in Israel when the rulers had ceased. She is called on to awake and utter a song, and the tribe of Issachar's princes are with her.
