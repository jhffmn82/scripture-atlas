Chapter 3

The mighty man, judge, prophet, elder, and other leaders are taken away. The LORD enters judgment with the elders and leaders, accusing them of eating up the vineyard, holding the plunder of the poor in their houses, and crushing and grinding the face of the poor. Those who lead the people are said to cause them to err and destroy the way of their paths.
