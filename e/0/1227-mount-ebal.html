Mount Ebal

place · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections

Abstract written by a model

In Deuteronomy, Mount Ebal is where Israel sets up law-inscribed plastered stones, builds the LORD's altar of uncut stones, and where Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali stand for the curse. In The Book of Joshua, Mount Ebal is where Joshua builds an altar to the LORD, writes a copy of Moses' law on stones, and where half of the people stand for the reading of the law.
