Canaan

person · 1 work · 14 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

Canaan, son of Ham, is cursed by Noah to be a servant of servants, specifically to Shem and Japheth. He fathers Sidon (firstborn) and Heth, and his descendants include the Jebusites, Amorites, Hivites, and other peoples.

Chapter 9
