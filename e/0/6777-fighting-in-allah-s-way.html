fighting in Allah's way

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: go forth (to fight) in the way of Allah, strive in the way of Allah

Abstract written by a model

Fighting in Allah’s way is described through believers who strive with their wealth and lives. They are ranked above those who remain sedentary and receive a great reward, degrees of rank, forgiveness, and mercy. The account also connects striving to migration for Allah’s cause: leaving home brings refuge and abundance, and death during that flight makes reward incumbent on Allah.
