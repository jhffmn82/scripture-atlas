Abstract written by a model

Fargard XV directs the faithful to support every pregnant woman or bitch, assigning responsibility by location and providing foliage bedding for a pregnant bitch until her young can fend for themselves.

Fargard XV.

The faithful must support every pregnant female. For a pregnant bitch, the person responsible is determined by the place where she lies, and in a pasture-field she is taken to rest on a litter of suitable foliage until the young dogs can defend and support themselves.
