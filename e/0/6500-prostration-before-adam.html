prostration before Adam

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Fall ye prostrate before Adam, fall down, prostrating yourselves unto him, fell prostrate, make prostration, the prostration before Adam

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), it is commanded by Allah to the angels, who all comply except Iblis. In Surah 15: The Rock (Al-Hijr), it is performed by the angels before the mortal Allah creates, while Iblis refuses.
