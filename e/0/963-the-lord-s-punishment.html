The LORD's punishment of the city for its sins: he declares it struck with a grievous wound and desolate, then foretells futility in eating, storing, sowing, and pressing olives and grapes, with savings lost to the sword, ending in the city becoming a ruin, a hissing, and a reproach.

Chapter 6

The LORD says he has struck the city with a grievous wound and made it desolate because of its sins. He foretells that they will eat without being satisfied, store up without saving, sow without reaping, tread olives without anointing with oil, and crush grapes without drinking wine, and that what they save will go to the sword. He adds that he will make them a ruin and a hissing, and they will bear reproach.
