Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the wicked are led away by the LORD together with the workers of iniquity, and the scepter of wickedness does not remain over the allotment of the righteous. In Psalms, the wicked speak peace to their neighbors while harboring mischief, disregard the LORD's works, and are broken down rather than built up. In Psalms, the wicked cannot dwell with the LORD or stand before the LORD. In Proverbs, the wicked are portrayed as people whose treasures profit nothing and whose names rot, whose fears overtake them and whose lamp is snuffed out. In Ecclesiastes or, The Preacher, the sinner fares as the good do, while one sinner destroys much good. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the wicked persist in iniquity and, unless they repent, are excluded from the righteous, cast into fire, and remain miserable until resurrection.

Abstract written by a model

The wicked are portrayed as people whose treasures profit nothing and whose names rot. Fears overtake them, hope perishes, years shorten, and violence covers their mouths. They bring shame, fall to their own wickedness, and their lamp is snuffed out. Hunger and misfortune pursue them, and their wealth is stored up for the righteous.

Chapter 10

The wicked gain nothing from their treasures, and their name will rot. What they fear overtakes them, their hope perishes, their years are shortened, and they will not dwell in the land. Violence covers their mouths, and their increase leads to sin.

Chapter 13

The wicked bring shame and disgrace, are overthrown by their own wickedness, and their lamp is snuffed out. Their bellies go hungry, misfortune pursues sinners, and a wicked messenger falls into trouble.

the wicked in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where the wicked first appears

Abstract written by a model

The wicked are those who persist in iniquity, pride, envy, persecution, costly apparel, riches, and neglect of the poor and needy, choosing evil works over good. Unless they repent, they are excluded from the righteous and cast into fire. Because of their iniquity, the devil leads them captive; after death their spirits enter outer darkness without the Spirit of the Lord, where they weep, wail, and gnash teeth. They remain miserable until resurrection, then face God’s wrath and partake of the bitter fruits of evil works.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

The wicked are those who persist in iniquity, pride, envy, persecution, costly apparel, riches, and turning their backs on the poor and needy. Their names will not be numbered among the righteous, and they will be hewn down and cast into the fire unless they repent.

Chapter 40

The spirits of the wicked are cast into outer darkness because of their iniquity and their choice of evil works. They remain in misery until resurrection and are then consigned to partake of the fruits of their evil works.
