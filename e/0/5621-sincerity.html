sincerity

topic · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Sincerity, the possessor of sincerity

Abstract written by a model

Sincerity is an irrepressibly manifest quality, compared with the subtle yet evident presence of spiritual beings. It results from intelligence and is ascribed to nature; given sincerity, intelligence is present. It effects self-completion, is the beginning and end of things, and enables the entirely sincere man to complete himself, other men, and things.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 16

The passage teaches that the outgoings of sincerity cannot be repressed. Its manifestness is compared with the evident yet subtle presence of spiritual beings.

Chapter 21

Sincerity resulting from intelligence is ascribed to nature. Given sincerity, intelligence will also be present.

Chapter 25

Sincerity effects self-completion and is the end and beginning of things. Without sincerity, there would be nothing; through it, the entirely sincere man completes himself, other men, and things.
