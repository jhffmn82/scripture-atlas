enmity and hostility

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: enmity, hostility and hate

Abstract written by a model

Abraham and those with him declared that hostility and hate had arisen between them and their folk until they believed in Allah only. The text also describes enmity between believers and some people who oppose them.
