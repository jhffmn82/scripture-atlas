right principles

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: one's principles, right principle, the right, the way of right principle

Abstract written by a model

Right principle is harmed by rigidly holding one point while ignoring many circumstances. Its presence throughout the kingdom coincides with a person's principles appearing with that person; when it disappears, both the principles and person vanish. Its manifestation does not depend on other men.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 26

Right principle is injured by holding to one point without regard for other circumstances. Such rigidity takes up one point and disregards a hundred others.

Chapter 42

Right principles prevail throughout the kingdom in one circumstance, in which a person's principles appear with the person. When right principles disappear from the kingdom, the person's principles and person must vanish together.
