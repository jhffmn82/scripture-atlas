Abstract written by a model

One is a child of The Tao and should guard the mother’s qualities. Keeping the mouth closed and nostril portals shut brings freedom from laborious exertion; opening the mouth and promoting affairs through breath brings no safety. Guarding the mother’s qualities keeps one free from peril to life’s end.

52. 1. (The Tao) which originated all under the sky is to be

One knows that he is his mother's child and can proceed to guard the qualities of the mother that belong to him. By keeping his mouth closed and shutting up the portals of his nostrils, one remains free from laborious exertion; by keeping his mouth open and promoting his affairs through breath, one has no safety.
