alliance and friendship with Rome and Sparta

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: alliance, alliance with Rome and Sparta, brotherhood and friendship, friendship, the alliance, the friendship, the friendship and the alliance

Abstract written by a model

Jonathan sent Numenius son of Antiochus and Antipater son of Jason to Rome to renew the former friendship and alliance, and told them to go also to the Spartans with his letters. Rome received them and gave letters for safe conduct, while the Spartan link rested on Arius's earlier letter claiming kinship. Rome and Sparta renewed with Simon the friendship and alliance confirmed with Judas and Jonathan, sending letters on brass tablets that were read at Jerusalem. The Spartan letter records the ambassadors Numenius and Antipater, and Simon sent Numenius to Rome with a gold shield to confirm the alliance.
