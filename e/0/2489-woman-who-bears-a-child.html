woman who bears a child

person · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: a woman, her, her who bears, she

Abstract written by a model

A woman who bears a child is ritually unclean afterward. Uncleanness lasts seven days for a male child and two weeks for a female, followed by blood of purification for thirty-three or sixty-six days respectively. Throughout, she may not touch holy things or enter the sanctuary. When the days are completed, she brings a year-old lamb for a burnt offering and a pigeon or turtledove for a sin offering to the priest, who makes atonement. If poor, she brings two turtledoves or pigeons.

Chapter 12

After bearing a child she is unclean and may not touch any holy thing or enter the sanctuary until her days of purifying are completed. When they are over she brings offerings to the priest at the door of the Tent of Meeting, and atonement is made so that she is cleansed. If she cannot afford a lamb, she may bring two turtledoves or two young pigeons.
