Abstract written by a model

A letter from consul Lucius to King Ptolemy, brought by Numenius from Rome after Jewish ambassadors renewed friendship and gave a thousand-mina gold shield. It forbids kings to fight the Jews or aid their enemies, orders troublemakers handed to Simon, and was sent to many rulers.

Chapter 15

Brought by Numenius from Rome, the letter from the consul Lucius to King Ptolemy says the Jews' ambassadors renewed the friendship and gave a gold shield of one thousand minas. It tells the kings not to fight the Jews or ally with their enemies, and to hand fugitive troublemakers to Simon, and the same message goes to many other rulers and countries.
