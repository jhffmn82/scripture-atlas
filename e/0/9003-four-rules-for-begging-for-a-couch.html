four rules for begging for a couch

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: first rule, four rules, fourth rule, second rule, third rule

Abstract written by a model

govern begging for a couch [four rules] The four rules permit a monk or nun to beg for a specified couch, to beg after inspecting it, to request it from the person whose house is occupied, or to request a couch as it is spread on the ground or a wooden plank. These rules govern when the couch may be used and require the mendicant to remain squatting or sitting if it is not obtained.
