Chapter 4

Daniel interprets the tree as Nebuchadnezzar, whose greatness has grown to the sky and whose dominion reaches the end of the earth. He declares the decree of the Most High that the king will be driven from men, live with the animals of the field, eat grass like oxen, and be wet with dew for seven times until he knows the Most High rules. He adds that the stump left with its roots means the kingdom will be restored after the king learns that the heavens rule.
