Tang

Also called: our prince, the prince

Abstract written by a model

In Mencius, Book I (Liang Hui Wang), Tang, a prince ruling a seventy-li kingdom, begins administering justice at Ge, and the people trust him and welcome his progress. In Mencius, Book II (Gong Sun Chou), Tang, sovereign of a kingdom with only seventy li, first learns from Yi Yin, employs him as minister, and becomes sovereign without difficulty. In Mencius, Book III (Teng Wen Gong), Tang aids Ge with sacrificial animals and cultivators, punishes Ge’s chief after provision-carriers are robbed and killed, consoles the people, and gains their support through his campaigns. In Mencius, Book IV (Li Lou), Tang holds fast the Mean, employs talented and virtuous men without regard to origin, and gains support as Jie’s actions drive people toward Tang. In Mencius, Book V (Wan Zhang), Tang, a prince seeking Yi Yin, repeatedly sends silk-bearing messengers, gains his counsel, and attacks Xia to save the people. In Mencius, Book VII (Jin Xin), Tang makes benevolence and righteousness his own.
