passions

Abstract written by a model

In Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra), Book I, passions include wrath, pride, deceit, greed, love, hate, and delusion, and conquering or avoiding one entails many. In Uttaradhyayana Sutra, passions ensnare people and subject them to hatred, love, and other harmful influences. In Chuang Tzŭ, human passions disturb internal economy and increase mortality, while the truly wise associate with men yet remain free from their rule. In The Analects of Confucius, Book V (Kung-Ye Ch'Ang), passions influence Shan Ch'ang and prevent him from being pronounced firm and unbending.
