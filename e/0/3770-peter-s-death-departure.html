Peter's death (departure)

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to John, Peter's future death is foretold as a death that would glorify God. In Peter’s Second Letter, Peter's death is described as the putting off of his tent and as his departure.

Abstract written by a model

Jesus foretells Peter's death: once self-dressed and free-walking, in old age Peter will stretch out his hands and be dressed and carried unwillingly by another, signifying a death glorifying God, then Jesus says "Follow me."

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 21

Jesus tells Peter that though he dressed himself and walked where he wished when young, in old age he will stretch out his hands and another will dress and carry him where he doesn't want to go. The text explains this signifies the kind of death by which Peter would glorify God, after which Jesus says, "Follow me."
