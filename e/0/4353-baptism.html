Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, baptism is performed by John in the Jordan for repentance, and Jesus is baptized by John before the heavens open. In The Second Book of Nephi, baptism by water in the name of the Holy One of Israel is commanded of all men and is the gate into the strait and narrow path. In The Book of Mosiah, baptism is a covenant and witness of willingness to serve God and keep his commandments, performed by Alma for believers including Limhi’s people. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, baptism is performed in the waters of Sidon and Mormon after belief, unto repentance and a covenant to keep God’s commandments. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, baptism is a testimony before God and the people, performed in water by immersion and preceding the Holy Ghost. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record, baptism is commanded by Jesus Christ, required for salvation, and begins with water followed by fire and the Holy Ghost.

baptism in The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman

Mormon · the work’s page · read it where baptism first appears

Abstract written by a model

Baptism is a testimony before God and the people for those who repent and receive remission of sins. It is performed in water by immersion, in the name of Jesus Christ, with authority given to Nephi and others and exercised by the twelve and disciples. Jesus teaches that believers who humble themselves and repent may be baptized; afterward they may receive bread and wine, be received and ministered to, and be called the church of Christ. Baptism is required for the kingdom of God with repentance and becoming as a little child, and it leads toward remission of sins and salvation when joined with belief and hearkening to Jesus’ words. It precedes the Holy Ghost: after Nephi and those chosen by Jesus were baptized and came out of the water, the Holy Ghost fell upon them. Those with broken hearts and contrite spirits are baptized with fire and the Holy Ghost, which sanctifies them and may enable them to stand spotless before Jesus. The command extends to Gentiles who turn from wicked ways and repent.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

9 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

Those brought unto repentance were baptized with water as a witness and testimony before God and the people. Nephi ordained men to perform this ministry.

Chapter 9

Those who come to Jesus Christ with a broken heart and a contrite spirit will be baptized with fire and the Holy Ghost. The Lamanites experienced this baptism at the time of their conversion, though they knew it not.

Chapter 11

Jesus Christ gives Nephi and others power to baptize those who repent through their words and desire to be baptized in his name. Baptism is performed in the water in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost, by immersing the person and bringing the person forth from the water.

Chapter 12

The twelve baptize believers with water, and Jesus baptizes them with fire and the Holy Ghost. Those who believe, humble themselves, and are baptized receive a remission of their sins.

Chapter 18

Those who believe and are baptized in Jesus's name are to receive the bread and wine. A person who repents and is baptized may be received and ministered to.

Chapter 19

Nephi is baptized in the water and then baptizes all those whom Jesus had chosen. After they are all baptized and come out of the water, the Holy Ghost falls upon them.

Chapter 26

The disciples baptized those who came unto them, and those baptized in the name of Jesus were filled with the Holy Ghost and called the church of Christ.

Chapter 27

Baptism in the name of Jesus is required for those who repent. Through baptism and the reception of the Holy Ghost, people may be sanctified and stand spotless before Jesus.

Chapter 30

Baptism in the name of Jesus Christ is commanded for the Gentiles after they turn from their wicked ways and repent. Through it, they may receive a remission of their sins and be filled with the Holy Ghost.

baptism in The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record

Mormon · the work’s page · read it where baptism first appears

Abstract written by a model

Baptism is commanded by Jesus Christ and required for salvation for believers. It begins with water, then fire and the Holy Ghost, following the Savior’s example. People are told to be baptized in Jesus’s name, warned against unworthy baptism, and taught that believers baptized shall be saved.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

The people are told to be baptized in the name of Jesus. Baptism is to be first with water and then with fire and with the Holy Ghost, following the example and commandment of the Savior.

Chapter 9

Those who believe and are baptized shall be saved, while those who do not believe shall be damned. People are warned not to be baptized unworthily.
