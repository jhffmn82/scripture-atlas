Abstract written by a model

The suffering servant is a figure described as the LORD's righteous servant. He grows up as a tender plant without beauty or majesty, despised and rejected, a man acquainted with disease. He is pierced for the speakers' transgressions, bears their sickness, and heals them by his wounds. Silent like a lamb led to slaughter, though free of violence or deceit, he is buried with the wicked and a rich man. Afterward he sees offspring, prolongs his days, justifies many, bears their iniquities, and intercedes for transgressors, counted among them.

Chapter 53

The servant grew up as a tender plant and a root out of dry ground, without beauty or majesty, and was despised, rejected, and acquainted with suffering. He was pierced, crushed, oppressed, and silent like a lamb, was cut off, and was buried with the wicked and a rich man though he had done no violence and spoken no deceit. He will see offspring, prolong his days, see the light and be satisfied, justify many, bear their iniquities, and receive a portion with the great as one who interceded for transgressors.
