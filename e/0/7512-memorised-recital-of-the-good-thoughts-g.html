Visparad XX (Sp. XXIII).

The memorised recital of the good thoughts, good words, and good deeds is said to acquire rewards, health, healing, progress, growth, and victorious smiting. It is used for withstanding evil thoughts, words, and deeds and for undoing treacherous thoughts, false words, and unfair deeds.
