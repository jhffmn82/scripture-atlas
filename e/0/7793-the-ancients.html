the ancients

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: ancient Sages, ancients, men of old, those of old

Abstract written by a model

The ancients cultivated TAO, putting essentials before accidentals and governing through inaction. They practised self-discipline, leniency, public spirit, restraint, and acceptance of Providence; their music, rites, government, and teachings expressed TAO. Their words and forms retain traces, yet are called only the dregs of dead Sages as later schools fragmented their purity.

CHAPTER XIII.

The ancients cultivated accidentals but did not allow them to precede essentials. Their words and forms may preserve traces of TAO, but the wheelwright says the words of dead Sages are only their dregs.

CHAPTER XXXIII.

The ancients are said to have followed TAO through self-discipline, leniency toward others, public spirit, restraint, and acceptance of Providence. Their music, rites, government, and teachings are presented as expressions of TAO, though later schools fragmented their original purity.
