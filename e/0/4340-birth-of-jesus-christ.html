Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, the birth of Jesus Christ is described as occurring after Mary's engagement to Joseph, through the Holy Spirit, fulfilling prophecy and leading Joseph to name the child Jesus. In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan), the birth of Jesus is announced to Mary as the creation of a child without a mortal touching her.
