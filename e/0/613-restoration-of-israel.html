restoration of Israel

restoration · 4 works · 11 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: heal you of your wounds, joy and gladness forever, restoration (of Israel), restore health to you, reverse the captivity, salvation and return of Israel, salvation of Israel, the days come, the joy that comes to you from God, the mercy that will soon come to you, the salvation of Israel, your salvation

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, salvation of Israel is a hoped-for deliverance wished for in the closing verses of Chapters 14 and 53 that would come out of Zion, tied to the LORD restoring and bringing back his captive people and resulting in rejoicing and gladness. In The Book of Jeremiah, restoration (of Israel) is the LORD's promise to break yokes, end foreign enslavement, heal wounds, rebuild the city and palace, bring thanksgiving, multiply and glorify the people, establish their congregation, and raise a prince from among them drawn near to the LORD. In Baruch, salvation and return of Israel is God's promised salvation of the people, delivered from enemies' hands and returned to Jerusalem with everlasting joy, while those near Zion see salvation in great glory and the sons are gathered from east to west rejoicing in God's glory. In The Book of Amos, restoration of Israel is the Lord's promise to return his people from captivity amid abundant harvests, where they rebuild ruined cities, plant vineyards and gardens, and settle permanently on their land, never again being plucked up from it.

Chapter 30

The LORD promises restoration: breaking the yoke and bonds so strangers no longer enslave them, restoring health and healing wounds, and building the city on its own hill with the palace in its own place. Thanksgiving and merry voices will come from them, they will be multiplied and glorified, their children will be as before, and their congregation established before the LORD. Their prince and ruler will come from among them and be brought near to the LORD.
