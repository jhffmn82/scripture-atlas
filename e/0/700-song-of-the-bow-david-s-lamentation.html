song of the bow (David's lamentation)

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: lamentation, the song of the bow, this lamentation

Abstract written by a model

A lamentation by David over Saul and Jonathan, written in the book of Jashar and commanded to be taught to Judah's children. It opens with the refrain "How the mighty have fallen," asks that the news be kept from Gath and Ashkelon, praises both, urges Israel's daughters to weep for Saul, and closes mourning Jonathan.

The Second Book of Samuel / Chapter 1

David sings this lamentation over Saul and Jonathan and commands that the children of Judah be taught it; it is written in the book of Jashar. It repeats the refrain "How the mighty have fallen," asks that the news not be told in Gath or Ashkelon, praises Saul and Jonathan, calls on Israel's daughters to weep for Saul, and mourns Jonathan as David's beloved brother.
