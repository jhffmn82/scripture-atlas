Abstract written by a model

The two witnesses are two olive trees and two lamp stands standing before the Lord of the earth. Empowered by God, they prophesy 1,260 days in sackcloth, torment earth's inhabitants, and wield fire from their mouths, sky-shutting, water-to-blood, and plagues. After finishing their testimony, the beast from the abyss kills them. After three and a half days God's breath enters them, they stand, and enemies watch as they ascend to heaven in a cloud.

Chapter 11

The two witnesses are given power to prophesy for 1,260 days clothed in sackcloth, and are called the two olive trees and two lamp stands standing before the Lord of the earth. They devour enemies with fire, shut the sky, turn waters to blood, and strike the earth with plagues, until the beast kills them. After three and a half days God's breath enters them, they stand, and they are called up to heaven in a cloud as their enemies watch.
