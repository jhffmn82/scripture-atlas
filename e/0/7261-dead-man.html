Fargard VII.

After death, the Drug Nasu rushes upon him. His corpse pollutes nearby people and objects, while the ground beneath it may require a year or fifty years to become itself again, depending on whether it lies on the ground or is buried.

VIII.

A corpse is being burned or roasted on a corpse-burning fire. A man who has touched a corpse can be cleansed under the stated procedures.
