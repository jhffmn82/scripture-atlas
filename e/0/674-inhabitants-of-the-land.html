inhabitants of the land

group · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: everyone who dwells in it, inhabitants of the land (Israel), the inhabitants of the land, they, you who live under siege

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Jeremiah, the inhabitants of the land, living under siege, are told to gather their wares; the LORD declares he will sling them out and distress them so they feel it. In The Book of Hosea, the inhabitants of the land lack knowledge of God, truth and goodness; the LORD charges them with cursing, lying, murder, stealing, adultery and bloodshed, and they are destroyed, practicing prostitution and idolatry, ruined without understanding.
