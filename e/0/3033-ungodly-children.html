Chapter 16

The text counsels against desiring or delighting in a multitude of unprofitable, ungodly children, and says not to trust in their life or numbers. It holds that one child can be better than a thousand, that dying childless is better than having ungodly children, and that a race of wicked men will be made desolate while a city is populated through one who has understanding.
