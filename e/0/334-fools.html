fools

Also called: Fools, a companion of fools, a fool, fools (afflicted by disobedience), mockers, the foolish

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, fools are afflicted by disobedience and iniquities, but the LORD saves, heals, and delivers them when they cry to him. In Proverbs, fools despise wisdom, instruction, and knowledge, detest turning from evil, and suffer the consequences of their careless ease and folly.

Abstract written by a model

Fools are afflicted because of their disobedience and iniquities, so that their soul abhors all food and they draw near the gates of death. When they cry to the LORD, he saves them, sending his word to heal them and deliver them from their graves. They are urged to offer sacrifices of thanksgiving and declare his deeds with singing.

fools in Proverbs

author unknown · 969 to 699 BC · the work’s page · read it where fools first appears

Abstract written by a model

Fools are portrayed as despising wisdom, instruction and knowledge, and detesting turning from evil. Chapter 1 warns their careless ease destroys them and they reap their own schemes; Chapter 13 adds that they expose folly and harm companions.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Proverbs / Chapter 1

Fools despise wisdom and instruction and hate knowledge. Wisdom says their careless ease will destroy them and that they will eat the fruit of their own way and be filled with their own schemes.

Chapter 13

A fool exposes folly, and fools detest turning from evil. A companion of fools suffers harm.
