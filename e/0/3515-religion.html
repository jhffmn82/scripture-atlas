The Letter from James / Chapter 1

James says that a person who thinks himself religious but does not bridle his tongue deceives his heart, and his religion is worthless. Pure and undefiled religion before God the Father is to visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction and to keep oneself unstained by the world.
