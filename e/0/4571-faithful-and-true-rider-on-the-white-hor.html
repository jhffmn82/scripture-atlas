Faithful and True (rider on the white horse)

Abstract written by a model

Faithful and True is a figure who appears when heaven opens, riding a white horse and judging and making war in righteousness. He has eyes like fire, many crowns, a blood-sprinkled garment, the name The Word of God, and the title KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS on his garment and thigh. A sharp sword comes from his mouth. He treads God's wine press of wrath, defeats the beast and false prophet, kills the rest with his sword, and rules the nations with an iron rod.

Chapter 19

Heaven opens and he appears on a white horse, called Faithful and True, judging and making war in righteousness. He has eyes like a flame of fire, many crowns, a name no one knows but himself, a garment sprinkled with blood, and the name The Word of God. A sharp sword comes from his mouth, he will rule the nations with an iron rod, he treads the wine press of God's wrath, and he bears the name KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS on his garment and thigh. He defeats the beast and false prophet, and kills the rest with the sword from his mouth.
