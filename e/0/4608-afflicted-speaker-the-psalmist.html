Chapter 102

The speaker cries out in distress, describing days consumed like smoke, burned bones, a withered heart, forgotten food, lonely watching like a sparrow on a housetop, and enemies who reproach him and use his name as a curse. He ends by asking God not to take him away in the middle of his days and affirms that the children of God's servants will continue.
