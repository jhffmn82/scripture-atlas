the house of Abraham

group · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

The house of Abraham is a group that received Scripture, wisdom, and a mighty kingdom from Allah. It is presented through a passage addressing believers’ conduct in prayer and purification, then promising Gardens, pure companions, and shade to those who believe and do good works; within this setting, Allah bestows these gifts on the house of Abraham.

the house of Abraham is in one work on this site, Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
