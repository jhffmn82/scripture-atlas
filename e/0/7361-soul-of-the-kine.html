Soul of the Kine

topic · 3 works · 8 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Kine's Soul, Kine's soul, Soul of the Kine (our sacred herds and folk), The Geus Urvan, soul, souls of the dead, the Kine's Soul, the Soul of the Kine, the mother Kine, the soul of the Kine of blessed gift, the souls of the dead

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Kine's Soul, belonging to saints, is worshipped with holy souls of the dead, living, and unborn, and finds satisfaction and delight in works of Righteousness. In Yasna, Soul of the Kine suffers violent assault, desolation, insolence, and thievish might, cries for strong-handed help, and is later worshipped with the Kine’s created body and worthy cattle souls. In Visparad, Kine's Soul is approached for homage and praises.

Yasna XXVI.

The souls of holy men and women, including the dead, living, and unborn, are worshipped. The unit also worships the soul of the Kine of blessed gift and the souls of the dead who are identified as the Fravashis of the saints.

Soul of the Kine in Yasna

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where Soul of the Kine first appears

Abstract written by a model

Soul of the Kine, soul of the sacred herds and folk, suffers violent assault, desolation, insolence, and thievish might. With no pasture-giver but Ahura and Asha and a powerless lord, it cries for strong-handed help. It is later worshipped with the Kine’s created body and worthy cattle souls.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XXIX.

The Soul of the Kine cries aloud about assault, violent power, desolation, insolence, and thievish might. It has no pasture-giver other than Ahura and Asha, later laments that its lord is powerless, and asks when strong-handed help will appear.

Yasna XXXIX.

The Kine's soul is worshipped together with her created body. The souls of cattle fit to live are also worshipped.
