Abstract written by a model

Heavenly-Young-Prince, son of Deity Heaven's-Earth-Spirit, is sent to Reed-Plains to pacify savage Deities. Armed with heavenly bow and arrows, he marries Princess Under-Shining, fails to report for eight years, shoots Name-Crying-Female, and is killed by the returning arrow while asleep.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Sect.

The Heavenly-Young-Prince is sent to the Central Land of Reed-Plains with heavenly feathered arrows to subdue and pacify its savage Deities. He marries Princess Under-Shining, brings back no report for eight years, shoots the pheasant the Name-Crying-Female, and dies when the returning arrow strikes him while he sleeps.
