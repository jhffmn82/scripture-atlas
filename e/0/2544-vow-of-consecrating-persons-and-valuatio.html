vow of consecrating persons and valuation

topic · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: consecrates a person to the LORD in a vow, valuation, your valuation

Abstract written by a model

Vow of consecrating persons sets sanctuary-shekel silver values by age and sex. Males aged twenty to sixty are fifty shekels, females thirty; younger and older people are valued lower, down to five and three for infants. Those too poor to pay are set before the priest, who assigns a value by ability.

Chapter 27

A person consecrated by vow is valued in shekels of silver of the sanctuary by age and sex: for a male aged twenty to sixty, fifty shekels, and thirty for a female. Younger and older people have lower values, and a person too poor to pay is set before the priest, who assigns a value according to his ability to pay.
