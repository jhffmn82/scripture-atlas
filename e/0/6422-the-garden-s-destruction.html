the garden's destruction

event · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: a visitation from thy Lord came upon it, it was as if plucked

Abstract written by a model

The garden's destruction occurred while its owners slept, after they had vowed to pluck its fruit without making an exception for Allah's will. In the morning it appeared as if it had been plucked, leading its owners to recognize that they were wrongdoers.
