final liberation

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: liberated, liberation

Abstract written by a model

Final liberation is a sought purpose, alongside birth and death, attained through self-control, renunciation, desirelessness, and cessation from action. It requires understanding and abandoning the causes of sin, property and its idea, violence, ignorance, sensuality, attachments, and injurious deeds. Those attached to sense objects or burdened by sin do not reach it. The houseless and hero advance by control and renunciation; the liberated, inactive person has no passage from birth to birth.

Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra), Book I / First Book. / First Lecture, Called Knowledge of the Weapon. / First Lesson. .. Seventh Lesson.

Final liberation is presented as one of the purposes connected with understanding and renouncing the causes of sin. Self-control and the illustrious road are associated with attaining it.

Second Lecture, Called Conquest of the World. / First Lesson. .. Sixth Lesson.

Final liberation is approached by ceasing from acts, relinquishing property and its idea, avoiding violence, and overcoming worldly obstructions. The houseless and hero proceed toward it by renunciation and control.

Fifth Lecture, Called Essence of the World. / First Lesson. .. Sixth Lesson.

Liberation is sought by abandoning ignorance, sensuality, worldly attachments, and injurious deeds. Those who are liberated and inactive have no passage from birth to birth.

Sixth Lecture, Called the Cleaning. / First Lesson. .. Fifth Lesson.

Liberation is not reached by beings attached to the objects of the senses or burdened by sinfulness. One who perfectly knows and renounces injurious acts is liberated.
