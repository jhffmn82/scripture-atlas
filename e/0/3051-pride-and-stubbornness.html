Chapter 3

The conceit of many leads them astray, and evil opinion makes their judgment slip. A stubborn heart will do badly at the end and be burdened with troubles, and one who loves danger will perish in it. The calamity of the proud has no healing because a weed of wickedness has taken root in him, and the sinner heaps sin upon sins.
