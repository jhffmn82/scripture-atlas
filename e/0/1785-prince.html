Abstract written by a model

The prince is a leader figure in the records, defined by land, worship duties, and responsibility to the people. In Chapter 45 he receives land on both sides of the holy allotment and city possession, from west to east border, and is forbidden to oppress the people. He must provide burnt, meal, sin, peace, and drink offerings at feasts, new moons, and Sabbaths, including a Passover sin offering bull for himself and the people. In Chapter 46 he enters by the east gate's porch on Sabbaths and new moons, worships at the threshold, and leaves the same way. He sits there to eat bread before the LORD, may give free will offerings, goes in and out with the people at feasts, and may not seize the people's inheritance. In Chapter 48 he is given the remainder of the land around the Levites' and city possessions, between Judah and Benjamin.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 45

The prince receives land on both sides of the holy allotment and city possession, from the west border to the east border. He must no longer oppress the people, and he is responsible for providing the offerings at feasts, new moons, and Sabbaths. At Passover he prepares a bull for sin offering for himself and the people and supplies the daily offerings.

Chapter 46

The prince enters by the porch of the east gate on the Sabbath and new moon, stands by the post, and worships at the threshold, going out the same way. He provides the prescribed offerings, may offer free will offerings through the opened east gate, goes in and out with the people at feasts, and is bound by laws forbidding him to seize the people's inheritance.

Chapter 48

The prince receives the remainder of the land on both sides of the holy offering and the city's possession, eastward and westward to the borders. His land also lies between the border of Judah and the border of Benjamin, around the Levites' and the city's possessions.
