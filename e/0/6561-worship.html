Abstract written by a model

In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), worship belongs to Allah alone, and the messengers call people to worship Him. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), worship belongs to Allah, while Abraham rejects idol worship and the condemned are questioned about what they worshipped instead of Allah.
