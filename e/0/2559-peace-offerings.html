peace offerings

Abstract written by a model

Peace offerings are a category of sacrifice governed by rules that differ by type. A thanksgiving offering comes with unleavened cakes and wafers with oil and must be eaten on the day offered. Vow or freewill offerings may be eaten that day and the next; leftovers on the third day are burned and bring iniquity. Unclean contact requires burning, and eating while unclean means being cut off. Priests receive the breast and right thigh.

Chapter 7

The peace offerings have rules by type: a thanksgiving offering is accompanied by cakes and wafers and eaten on the day it is offered, while a vow or free will offering may be eaten that day and the next. Meat left until the third day is burned, and eating it then is not accepted and brings iniquity. Meat touching an unclean thing must be burned, and anyone eating it while unclean is cut off from his people. The priests receive the breast and right thigh.
