Abstract written by a model

Prayer for all people is an exhortation, placed first, to offer petitions, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings for everyone, explicitly including kings and those in high places, so believers may lead a tranquil, quiet life in godliness and reverence, which is good and acceptable to God.

Chapter 2

The author exhorts first of all that petitions, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings be made for all men, including kings and those in high places. The stated purpose is that believers may lead a tranquil and quiet life in godliness and reverence, and it is called good and acceptable to God.
