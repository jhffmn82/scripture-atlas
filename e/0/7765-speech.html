Speech

topic · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Great speech, LANGUAGE, Language, language, small speech, speech

Abstract written by a model

Speech is differentiated by meaning but obscured by the world’s gloss; it cannot fully express TAO and may not fit categories of contraries. It can rest on weighty authority, which bars further argument, or arise as the natural overflowing of the heart. Constant speech and unspoken constancy flow like a full goblet, in accord with God, while expression remains distinct from identity.

CHAPTER II.

Speech is differentiated by meaning and becomes obscured by the gloss of the world. It cannot fully express TAO, and the unit questions whether speech itself belongs to either category of contraries.

CHAPTER XXVII.

Language may be put into other people’s mouths, based on weighty authority, or flow constantly over like a full goblet. Natural language in harmony with God embraces both positive and negative, while language and identity are not identical with their expressions.
