house of David

group · 4 works · 5 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: David's house, David’s house, House of David, the family of David’s house, the glory of David’s house, the house of the king of Judah, your own house

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Samuel, David’s house grows stronger during the long war with Saul’s house and gains an expectation that all Israel will come over to David. In The Second Book of Chronicles, David's house is rebelled against by Israel, while only the Israelites living in Judah's cities remain under Rehoboam. In The Book of Jeremiah, the house of David is commanded by the LORD to execute justice in the morning and deliver the robbed from the oppressor. In The Book of Zechariah, the house of David is elevated by the LORD to be like God and like the LORD's angel, and it receives a spirit of grace and supplication.
