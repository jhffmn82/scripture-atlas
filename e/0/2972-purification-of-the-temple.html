Also called: purification, purification of the greatest temple, the purification, the purification of the temple

Abstract written by a model

The purification of the temple is a celebrated event, kept on the twenty-fifth day of Chislev. Jerusalem's Jews announce it to Egyptian Jews, inviting them to keep a feast of tabernacles alongside; the writers urge celebrating the days, credit God with purifying the place, and list it among the history's subjects.

The Second Book of the Maccabees / Chapter 1

The Jews in Jerusalem are about to celebrate it in Chislev on the twenty-fifth day. They notify the Egyptian Jews so that they may keep a feast of tabernacles at the same time.

Chapter 2

The writers say they are about to celebrate the purification and urge the recipients to celebrate the days. God is said to have purified the place, and the purification of the greatest temple is named among the subjects of the history to be abridged.
