Chapter 31

The war is commanded by the LORD as vengeance on Midian and is fought by twelve thousand men with Phinehas, carrying the sanctuary vessels and trumpets. The Israelites kill every male, burn the cities, and return with captives and plunder, and no Israelite is reported lost.
