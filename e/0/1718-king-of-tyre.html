Abstract written by a model

The king of Tyre is the subject of a lamentation in Chapter 28. He is portrayed as an anointed cherub, perfect in beauty and wisdom, adorned with precious stones in Eden, set on God's holy mountain. Unrighteousness, pride, and violence from his commerce corrupted him. He was cast out as profane, consumed by fire from within, reduced to ashes before kings, and became a terror who will exist no more.

Chapter 28

A lamentation is raised over the king of Tyre, who is described as the seal of full measure, full of wisdom, perfect in beauty, in Eden adorned with precious stones, and an anointed covering cherub on God's holy mountain. Unrighteousness, violence from his commerce, and a heart lifted up because of his beauty led to his being cast out, laid before kings, and turned to ashes by a fire from within him, so that he becomes a terror and exists no more.
