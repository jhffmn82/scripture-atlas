Abstract written by a model

The remnant of Jacob is the lame, driven away, and afflicted whom the LORD gathers. He makes the cast-off a strong nation and reigns over them on Mount Zion forever. Among many peoples the remnant is like refreshing dew and showers independent of man; among the nations it is like a lion, treading down and tearing in pieces with no deliverer. It returns to the children of Israel, its brothers.

Chapter 4

The LORD will assemble the lame and gather the driven away, including those he has afflicted. He will make the lame a remnant and those cast far off a strong nation, over whom he will reign on Mount Zion forever.

Chapter 5

The remnant of Jacob will be among many peoples like dew from the LORD and like showers on the grass, which do not wait for man. It will also be among the nations like a lion among forest animals or a young lion among flocks of sheep, treading down and tearing in pieces with no one to deliver.
