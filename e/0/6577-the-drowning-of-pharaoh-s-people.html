the drowning of Pharaoh's people

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: drowned them every one, we drowned them in the sea

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), the drowning of Pharaoh's people was retribution for denying Allah's revelations and being heedless of them. In Surah 43: Ornaments of gold (Az-Zukhruf), the drowning of Pharaoh's people punished them after they angered Allah and made them an example for those after them.
