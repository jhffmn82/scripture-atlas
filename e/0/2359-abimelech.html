Chapter 26

Abimelech, king of the Philistines at Gerar, sees Isaac caressing Rebekah, rebukes him for calling her his sister, and commands that anyone who touches Isaac or his wife be put to death. He later tells Isaac to leave because he is mightier, then comes to Beersheba with Ahuzzath and Phicol to seek an oath and covenant, acknowledging that Isaac is blessed by the LORD.
