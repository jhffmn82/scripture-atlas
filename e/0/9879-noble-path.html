Abstract written by a model

The noble path, preached by Mahâvîra, contains all virtues and is not blamed. After conquering greed, a wise man enters this difficult route across Samsâra through study, non-injury, restraint, proper alms, and abandoning passions, leading to liberation from misery.

Eighth Lecture, Called on Exertion.

The noble path contains all virtues and is not blamed. A wise man should enter upon it after conquering greed.

Eleventh Lecture, Called the Path.

The Path taught by the wise Brâhmana, identified as Mahâvîra, is the difficult way across Samsâra. It consists in studying living beings, avoiding injury, restraining the senses, accepting only proper alms, and abandoning passions and worldly causes.
