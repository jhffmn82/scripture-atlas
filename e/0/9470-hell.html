Abstract written by a model

Hell is a place and state of misery where fools and great sinners suffer violently. It awaits those who commit cruel, unrighteous deeds or pursue pleasures after turning from the Law. Even mendicants cannot escape; Brahmadatta reaches it after rejecting Kitra’s counsel and enjoying sensual pleasures.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Fifth Lecture.

Hell is described as a place where fools who do cruel deeds suffer violently. A sinner, even if a mendicant, will not escape it.

Seventh Lecture.

Hell is the destination of the great sinner who turns away from the Law and commits unrighteousness. The sinner's pursuit of pleasures is said to gain nothing but life in hell.

Thirteenth Lecture.

Hell is where Brahmadatta sinks after refusing Kitra's counsel and continuing to enjoy the highest sensual pleasures.
