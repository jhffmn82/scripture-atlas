Abstract written by a model

In Revelation, heaven has a door that opens to a voice calling the narrator up and contains the throne, twenty-four thrones and elders, seven lamps of fire, sea of glass, and four living creatures. In The Book of Mosiah, heaven is a place above the earth where faithful commandment-keepers dwell with God in never-ending happiness.
