Abstract written by a model

The saints do good deeds, serve the renovation of the completed world, and have blessed Fravashis. Some are within and some outside the Province. Through their Fravashis and souls they receive offerings, announcements, and honors, and they and their Fravashis are worshipped.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Visparad XI (Sp. XII).

The saints receive offerings, announcements, and honors through their Fravashis and souls. The unit refers to saints of the house, saints who do good deeds, and saints whose deeds are conspicuously good.

Visparad XVI (Sp. XIX).

The saints and their blessed Fravashis are worshipped. The unit also worships the entire creation of the holy and the Fravashis of saints within and outside the Province.
