Battle of Mount Gilboa

event · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Battle at Mount Gilboa, The battle, battle, the battle

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Samuel, the Battle of Mount Gilboa pitted the Philistines against Israel, resulting in Israelite defeat, the deaths of Saul's sons, Saul's suicide, and the abandonment of Israelite cities. In 1 Chronicles, the Battle at Mount Gilboa saw the Philistines put Israel to flight, kill Saul's sons, distress Saul, and cause the Israelites to abandon their cities.

Abstract written by a model

The Battle of Mount Gilboa pitted the Philistines against Israel, whose men fled and were slain. Saul's three sons died, Saul was wounded by archers and killed himself, and his armor bearer and men perished; Israelites then abandoned their cities.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 31

In this battle the Philistines fought Israel, whose men fled and fell slain on Mount Gilboa. Saul's three sons were killed, Saul was wounded by archers and died by his own sword, and his armor bearer and all his men died that same day. The defeat caused Israelites beyond the valley and the Jordan to abandon their cities to the Philistines.
