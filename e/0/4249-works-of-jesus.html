Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Jesus' mighty works include healing the blind, lame, lepers, and deaf, raising the dead, and preaching good news to the poor. In The Gospel According to John, the works of Jesus are good deeds given by the Father, performed in the Father's name, and testify that the Father sent him. In The Book of Mosiah, the ministry of Jesus Christ unfolds through mighty miracles, including healing the sick, raising the dead, enabling the lame, blind, and deaf, curing diseases, and casting out devils.

works of Jesus in The Gospel According to John

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where works of Jesus first appears

Abstract written by a model

The works of Jesus are good deeds that originate from the Father, who gave them to him to accomplish. Done in the Father's name, they testify that the Father sent him. In chapter 10 he challenges the Jews' stoning, urging belief in the works so they know the Father is in him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

Jesus says the works he does in his Father's name testify about him, and he asks the Jews for which of his many good works from the Father they stone him. He urges them to believe the works if they will not believe him, so they may know the Father is in him and he in the Father. Others cite the opening of the eyes of the blind as evidence against the charge of demon possession.
