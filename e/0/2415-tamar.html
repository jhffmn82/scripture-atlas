Abstract written by a model

Tamar is a woman widowed when her husband Er dies. Judah sends her to live in her father's house as a widow until Shelah grows up. When he is withheld from her, she veils herself, conceives by Judah, and keeps his pledge. Condemned, she proves him the father, is declared more righteous, and bears twins Perez and Zerah.

Chapter 38

Tamar is given as wife to Er, is widowed, and is told by Judah to remain a widow in her father's house until Shelah is grown. Seeing that Shelah is grown and she was not given to him, she veils herself at Enaim, conceives by Judah, and secures his signet, cord, and staff. Condemned to burning, she proves the father with the pledge, is declared more righteous than Judah, and bears twins, Perez and Zerah.
