Abstract written by a model

In 2 Corinthians, the judgment seat of Christ is where all are revealed and recompensed for deeds done in the body. In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, judgment brings every person before God's judgment-seat, where the wicked are found unclean and unable to dwell with God. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record, judgment is the post-resurrection event at Christ's judgment-seat where every soul is judged by works and assigned an eternal state.

judgment in The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record

Mormon · the work’s page · read it where judgment first appears

Abstract written by a model

The judgment is an event after the resurrection, at the judgment-seat of Christ, when every soul of Adam’s family stands to be judged by works, good or evil. Israel and the Nephite remnant are judged by twelve chosen by Jesus. The dead become incorruptible; the guiltless dwell with God, while the filthy and righteous remain eternally in their states.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Every soul belonging to the whole human family of Adam must stand before the judgment-seat of Christ and be judged according to works, whether good or evil. The house of Israel and the remnant of the Nephites will be judged by the twelve chosen by Jesus in the respective lands.

Chapter 6

The dead will become incorruptible and stand before the judgment-seat of Christ. They will be judged according to their works, and the righteous will be blessed with their fathers.

Chapter 7

At the judgment day, the person found guiltless before Jesus Christ is given to dwell in the presence of God in his kingdom.

Chapter 9

After all people are resurrected and stand before God’s bar, the judgment of the Holy One comes upon them. The filthy, righteous, happy, and unhappy remain as they are.
