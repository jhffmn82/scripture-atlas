LORD's loving kindness and mercy

topic · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the LORD's loving kindness and mercy are abundant, crowning the speaker, directed toward those who fear him, vast as the heavens above earth, and enduring from everlasting to everlasting. In Jonah, God's mercy and compassion extend to Nineveh as he is described as gracious, merciful, slow to anger, abundant in loving kindness, and one who relents from doing harm.
