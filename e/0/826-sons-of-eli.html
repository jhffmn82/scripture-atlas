Abstract written by a model

Eli's sons, Hophni and Phinehas, are wicked men who did not know the LORD. They despise the LORD's offering, their servants seize raw meat by force, and they sleep with women serving at the Tent of Meeting, making their sin very great. They ignore their father's rebuke, and the LORD intends to kill them, with both dying in one day as the sign.

Chapter 2

Eli's sons are called wicked men who did not know the LORD. Their servants take raw meat from worshipers by force, and they sleep with the women serving at the door of the Tent of Meeting, so their sin is very great before the LORD. They ignore their father's rebuke because the LORD intends to kill them, and the sign given is that Hophni and Phinehas will both die in one day.
