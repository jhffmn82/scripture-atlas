Mizpah

place · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

Mizpah is where Gedaliah dwells as governor and where Jeremiah goes to live with him. The captains, their men and returning Jews gather there, and it is where Johanan warns of the plot against Gedaliah. Mizpah was the place where Gedaliah was governor and where Ishmael killed him, the Jews with him, and the Chaldean men of war. Ishmael also killed pilgrims in the middle of the city and carried away the people remaining there as captives.
