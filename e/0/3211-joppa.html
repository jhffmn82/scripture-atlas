Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of the Maccabees, Joppa is a seaside harbor that first shut out Jonathan, was won by his forces, and was later demanded back by Antiochus from Simon. In The Acts of the Apostles, Joppa is a seaside place near Lydda where Tabitha died and Peter raised her.

Joppa in The First Book of the Maccabees

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Joppa first appears

Abstract written by a model

Joppa is a seaside harbor, entrance for the islands of the sea. It first shut out Jonathan, whose forces won it after fighting when the frightened people opened to him. Later Antiochus demanded it back from Simon, who said it harmed the people and offered a hundred talents.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

Joppa shut out Jonathan because Apollonius had a garrison there. After fighting, the people grew afraid and opened to him, and Jonathan became master of the city.

Chapter 15

Joppa is named by Antiochus as a city of his kingdom held by Simon, and he demands its return. Simon replies that it and Gazara did great harm to the people and offers one hundred talents for them.
