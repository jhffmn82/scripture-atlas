Chapter 18

The just man does what is lawful and right: he avoids idolatry and adultery, does not rob or wrong others, restores pledges, feeds the hungry, clothes the naked, and lends without interest. He walks in the Lord's statutes and shall surely live. The text also warns that a righteous man who turns away from his righteousness and commits iniquity will die in it, and his earlier righteous deeds will not be remembered.
