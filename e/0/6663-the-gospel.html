the Gospel

object · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections
Also called: Gospel

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan), the Gospel was revealed by Allah after the Torah, and Jesus was taught both the Torah and the Gospel. In Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida), the Gospel contains guidance and a light, confirms the Torah, and directs the People of the Gospel to judge by what Allah revealed therein.
