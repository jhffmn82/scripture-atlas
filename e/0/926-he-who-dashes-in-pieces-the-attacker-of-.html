Chapter 2

He has come up against Nineveh, and his force is described with red shields, scarlet-clad valiant men, and flashing chariots in the day of his preparation. His chariots rage through the streets like torches and lightning, and he summons his picked troops, who rush to the wall and set up the protective shield. His assault leads to the city's collapse and plunder.
