In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan): is_a resting-place [evil] Hell is the destination described for those who disbelieve, and it is called an evil resting-place. In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan), the Fire is punishment and heavy doom prepared for disbelievers, while believers ask Allah to guard them from it. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), Hell is the final destination and habitation for disbelievers, wrongdoers, those opposing guidance, followers of other than the believer’s way, those choosing Satan instead of Allah, and hypocrites. In Surah 6: The Cattle (Al-An'aam): The Fire contains disbelievers and is the lasting home of the disobedient, who, set before it, wish to return and believe. In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf): The Fire is a destination for disbelievers who deny and scorn Allah's revelations, where its dwellers receive torment and are denied Garden water and provision.
