Abstract written by a model

In Surah 65: Divorce (At-Talaaq), punishment comes to communities that revolt against Allah's ordinance and His messengers, and Allah has prepared stern punishment for them. In Surah 85: The Constellations (Al-Burooj), the punishment of Allah is described as stern.
