Barnabas

person · 1 work · 15 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Joses, Son of Encouragement, a Levite, a man of Cyprus by race, our beloved Barnabas

Abstract written by a model

Barnabas, also called Joses by the apostles, is a Levite from Cyprus, a good man full of the Holy Spirit and faith, and later an apostle, prophet and teacher. He begins by selling his field and laying the money at the apostles' feet. He then supports Saul, bringing him to the apostles and vouching for him when the disciples in Jerusalem feared him. Sent by the Jerusalem assembly to Antioch, he exhorts believers, fetches Saul from Tarsus, and carries relief to Judea. Set apart by the Holy Spirit with Saul (Paul), he leads the Gentile mission through Cyprus, Antioch of Pisidia, Iconium, Lystra and Derbe, is driven out of one city, is called Jupiter by the Lystrans, and tears his clothes to stop sacrifice. They appoint elders and report back to Antioch and Jerusalem about signs and wonders among the nations. The story ends with a sharp contention with Paul over John Mark, after which Barnabas sails to Cyprus with Mark.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

Joses, called Barnabas by the apostles, meaning Son of Encouragement, is introduced as a Levite and a man of Cyprus by race. He sells a field he owns and lays the money at the apostles' feet.

Chapter 9

Barnabas took Saul, whom the disciples in Jerusalem feared, and brought him to the apostles. He told them how Saul had seen the Lord on the way, that the Lord spoke to him, and that he had preached boldly in Jesus' name at Damascus.

Chapter 11

Barnabas is sent by the Jerusalem assembly to Antioch, where he rejoices at God's grace and exhorts the believers to remain near the Lord. He is described as a good man full of the Holy Spirit and faith, fetches Saul from Tarsus, and later carries relief to Judea with him.

Chapter 13

Barnabas, one of the prophets and teachers at Antioch, is set apart with Saul for the Spirit's work and travels with him to Cyprus and Antioch of Pisidia. He joins Paul in speaking boldly to the Jews and turning to the Gentiles, and is driven out with him before they go to Iconium.

Chapter 14

Barnabas accompanies Paul in Iconium, Lystra, and Derbe, and the Lystrans call him Jupiter. He tears his clothes with Paul to stop the sacrifice, then travels with Paul to appoint elders and returns to Antioch to report what God had done.

Chapter 15

Barnabas goes with Paul to Jerusalem and reports signs and wonders among the nations. He later wants to take John Mark on a return visit, and after a sharp contention with Paul he sails to Cyprus with Mark.
