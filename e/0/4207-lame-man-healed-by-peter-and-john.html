Abstract written by a model

A man over forty, lame from birth, is carried daily to the temple's Beautiful gate to beg. Peter raises him in Jesus' name; his feet gain strength and he enters the temple walking, leaping, and praising God, recognized by the people. He later stands whole before the council, and the rulers cannot speak against it.

Chapter 3

The man, lame from his mother's womb, is carried daily to the temple door called Beautiful to ask for gifts for the needy. After Peter raises him in Jesus' name, his feet and ankle bones receive strength, and he enters the temple walking, leaping, and praising God, and the people recognize him as the one who used to sit begging.

Chapter 4

The healed man stands before the council whole, and the rulers can say nothing against it. He is said to be more than forty years old.
