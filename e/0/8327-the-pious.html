the pious

group · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: God's perfect people, The saints, godly people, holy, holy man, holy men, pious, pious man, pious men, pious people, saints, the holy man

Abstract written by a model

The pious are holy people associated with the Name through spiritual fellowship, truth, divine knowledge, and God’s love. They worship God, thirst for truth, and are freed from filth, pride, and worldly entanglement through God’s praises. Carrying on true dealings according to God’s will, they escape Death’s noose and punishment. Absorbed in the True One, they are known in God’s court, invited to God’s palace, and blended with God.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Rag Gauri .. Gauri Chhant

Saints and holy people are associated with the Name, truth, divine knowledge, and God’s love. Their minds are freed from filth, pride, and worldly entanglement, and they obtain happiness through God’s praises.

Rag Maru Ashtapadi .. Maru ki War I

Pious people carry on their dealings according to God's will, keep their true capital intact, and escape Death's noose. Their minds restrain wandering, and they are not punished by Death or forced onto the difficult road.

Rag Malar .. Malar ki War

The pious are described as those who are absorbed in the True One and are known in God's court. They are invited to God's palace and will be blended with Him.
