VIII. Tîr Yast.

The Daêva Apaosha meets Tistrya at the sea Vouru-Kasha in the shape of a dark horse and fights him. He defeats Tistrya in the first three-day battle, but Tistrya later overcomes him after receiving strength through sacrifice.
