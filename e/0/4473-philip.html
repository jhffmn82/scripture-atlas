The Gospel According to John / Chapter 1

Philip is from Bethsaida, the city of Andrew and Peter, and is found by Jesus, who says to him, "Follow me." He then finds Nathanael and tells him they have found the one of whom Moses and the prophets wrote, Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph. When Nathanael doubts, Philip answers, "Come and see."

Chapter 14

Philip asks Jesus to show them the Father, saying that would be enough for them. Jesus responds by asking whether Philip still does not know him after so long, and says that whoever has seen him has seen the Father.
