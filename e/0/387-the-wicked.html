the wicked

Also called: A wicked man, The sacrifice of the wicked, The soul of the wicked, Those who hate the righteous, all the proud, all who work wickedness, proud and wicked people, the house of the wicked, the proud, the proud and wicked, them, those who do evil, those who work wickedness, wicked

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the wicked will be killed by evil, while those who hate the righteous will be condemned. In Proverbs, the wicked are proud, violent, devious people who desire evil, show neighbors no mercy, refuse to do right, and end in ruin. In The Book of Malachi, proud and wicked people become stubble burned up on the coming day and end as ashes beneath the feet of those who fear the LORD's name.

Abstract written by a model

The wicked are proud, violent, devious people who desire evil and show neighbors no mercy. They refuse to do right, so their own violence drives them away. Their sacrifices are abominations, and they harden their faces. They end in ruin, serving as a ransom for the righteous.

Chapter 21

The wicked are described as proud, violent, devious, and desiring evil, showing no mercy to their neighbor. Their violence drives them away, their sacrifice is an abomination, and they are brought to ruin. The wicked serve as a ransom for the righteous, and a wicked man hardens his face.
