superior man

person · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: The superior man, the superior man

Abstract written by a model

The superior man is a person without anxiety or fear. He reverentially orders his own conduct, respects others, and observes propriety; thus all within the four seas are his brothers. He values substantial qualities, though ornament is also indicated as important, and he does not want ornamental accomplishments only. He seeks to perfect admirable qualities rather than bad ones. He meets friends on grounds of culture, and through friendship helps his virtue.
