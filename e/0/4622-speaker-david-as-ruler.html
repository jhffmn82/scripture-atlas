author unknown · undated · the work’s page · read it where speaker (David as ruler) first appears

Abstract written by a model

David, speaking as a ruler, commits to praising the LORD and singing of loving kindness and justice. He resolves to live blamelessly in his house, rejecting evil, faithless deeds, slander, arrogance and deceit. He favors the faithful, wanting them near him, and ends by vowing to destroy the wicked of the land every morning.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 101

The speaker vows to sing of loving kindness and justice and to walk blamelessly in his house. He pledges to set no vile thing before his eyes, silence slanderers, tolerate no arrogant person, and keep the deceitful and false from his house and sight. He favors the faithful as servants and resolves to destroy the wicked of the land every morning.
