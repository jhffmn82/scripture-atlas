Chapter 6

The armor of God is to be put on whole so believers can withstand in the evil day and stand. It comprises the belt of truth, breastplate of righteousness, feet fitted with the preparation of the Good News of peace, shield of faith that quenches the fiery darts of the evil one, helmet of salvation, and sword of the Spirit.
