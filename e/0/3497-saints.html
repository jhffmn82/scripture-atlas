Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the saints of the LORD extol him and his works, then speak of his kingdom’s glory and power, making his mighty acts known to humanity. In Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians, the saints are destined to judge the world and even angels, making them fit to settle minor everyday disputes themselves rather than before the unrighteous. In Revelation, the saints and martyrs of Jesus are warred upon and overcome by the first beast; facing captivity and death by the sword, they are marked by endurance and faith.
