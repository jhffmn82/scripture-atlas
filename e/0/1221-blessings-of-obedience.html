Chapter 7

For obeying the ordinances, the LORD promises to love, bless, and multiply Israel, and to bless their offspring, ground, grain, new wine, oil, livestock, and flocks. Israel will be blessed above all peoples, with no barrenness among people or livestock. The LORD will remove all sickness and the evil diseases of Egypt from them and lay those on all who hate them.
