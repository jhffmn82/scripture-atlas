A'ad

group · 8 works · 18 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: (The tribe of) A'ad, the folk of Hud, the tribe of A'ad, the tribe of) A'ad, tribe of) A'ad

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), A'ad receives Hud, rejects his warning, and is destroyed after refusing to abandon worship of what their fathers worshipped. In Surah 11: Hud, A'ad rejects Hud's message, denies Allah's revelations, follows froward potentates, and is destroyed and cursed in the world and on the Day of Resurrection. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), the tribe of A'ad denies the messengers, rejects Hud's warning, calls it an old fable, and is destroyed. In Surah 46: The Dunes (Al-Ahqaf), A'ad is associated with a people warned by the brother of A'ad, whose people reject the warning and are destroyed by a painful wind. In Surah 51: The Winnowing Winds (Adh-Dhaariyat), A'ad has a sign in the fatal wind sent against them, which spares nothing it reaches and makes it as dust. In Surah 54: The Moon (Al-Qamar), A'ad rejects warnings and suffers a raging wind on a day of constant calamity. In Surah 69: The Reality (Al-Haaqqa), A'ad disbelieves in the judgment to come and is destroyed by a fierce roaring wind imposed for seven long nights and eight long days. In Surah 89: The Dawn (Al-Fajr), A'ad is described as a people dealt with by the Lord after rebellion and iniquity in the lands.
