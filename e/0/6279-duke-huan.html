Duke Huan

person · 2 works · 8 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Duke Huan of Ch'i, Hsiao Poh, Huan, Your Highness, the Duke, the duke, the duke Huan, your Highness

Abstract written by a model

In Chuang Tzŭ, Duke Huan rules the Ch'i State, studies the Sages’ words, hunts, consults or travels with Kuan Chung, kills his elder brother, marries his sister-in-law, and seeks advice on entrusting state administration near Kuan Chung’s death, accepting Hsi P'êng’s recommendation. In Mencius, Book II (Gong Sun Chou), the duke Huan first learns from Guan Zhong and then employs him as his minister, becoming chief of all the princes without difficulty.

CHAPTER XIII.

Duke Huan studies the words of the Sages. When a wheelwright calls those words the dregs of the ancients, the Duke threatens him and demands an explanation.

Kuan Chung being at the point of death, Duke Huan went to see him.

Duke Huan visits Kuan Chung at the point of death and asks whom to entrust with administration of the State. He considers Pao Yü before accepting Kuan Chung's recommendation of Hsi P'êng.
