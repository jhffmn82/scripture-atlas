the rulers of Qin and Chu

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: The rulers of those States, Those rulers

Abstract written by a model

The rulers of Qin and Chu are depicted as robbing their people of time needed for farming, causing cold and hunger among parents, separating families by driving people abroad, and treating the people as though driving them into pit-falls or drowning.

Chapter 5

The rulers of Qin and Chu take their people's time so that they cannot plough and weed their fields to support their parents. Mencius compares their treatment of the people to driving them into pitfalls or drowning them.
