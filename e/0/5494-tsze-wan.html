Tsze-wan

Also called: minister Tsze-wan

Abstract written by a model

Tsze-wan is a minister who takes office three times without joy and retires three times without displeasure, each time informing the new minister about his government conduct. The Master judges him loyal, while leaving his perfect virtue uncertain.

Tsze-wan is in one work on this site, The Analects of Confucius, Book V (Kung-Ye Ch'Ang). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. XVIII. 1. Tsze-chang asked, saying, 'The minister Tsze-

Tsze-wan thrice took office without showing joy and thrice retired without showing displeasure. He informed each new minister about how he had conducted the government. The Master judged him loyal but did not know whether he was perfectly virtuous.
