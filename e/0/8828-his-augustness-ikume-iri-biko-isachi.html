His Augustness Ikume-iri-biko-isachi

person · 1 work · 13 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Emperor Sui-Nin, the Heavenly Sovereign, this Heavenly Sovereign

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Ikume-iri-biko-isachi was a Heavenly Sovereign, child of His Augustness Mima-ki-iri-biko-iniwe and Her Augustness Princess Mimatsu, who ruled Yamato from Tama-kaki palace in Shiki. He married Princess Sahaji, Princess Hibasu, and Empress Princess Saho-bime; faced the conspiracy of King Saho-biko and Princess Saho-bime; and oversaw Prince Homo-tsu-wake’s upbringing and worship at Idzumo. He had sixteen children, including successor His Augustness Oho-tarashi-hiko-oshiro-wake, constructed pools, reigned 153 years, and was buried at Sugahara.
