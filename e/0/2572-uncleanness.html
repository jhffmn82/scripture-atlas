Abstract written by a model

Uncleanness is a ritual state arising from leprosy, discharge, touching the dead, seminal emission, or a creeping thing. It lasts until evening, requires bathing before eating holy things after sundown, and approaching holy things while unclean means being cut off.

Chapter 22

Uncleanness comes from leprosy, a discharge, touching the dead, a seminal emission, or a creeping thing. An unclean person who approaches the holy things is cut off from the LORD. Contact makes a person unclean until evening, and the person must bathe before eating the holy things, after sundown.
