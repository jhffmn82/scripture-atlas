Chapter 29

Seven bulls, seven rams, seven lambs, and seven male goats are brought as a sin offering for the kingdom, the sanctuary, and Judah. The priests sprinkle the blood of the bulls, rams, and lambs on the altar, and the male goats are brought before the king and assembly, who lay their hands on them. The priests kill the goats and make atonement with their blood for all Israel, at the king's command.
