Abstract written by a model

Surah 60: She that is to be examined (Al-Mumtahana) addresses believers about relations with Allah's enemies and those who war against them on account of religion. It presents Abraham and those with him as a pattern of rejecting false worship while trusting Allah, and it permits kindness and justice toward those who do not fight or expel believers. It describes the examination and treatment of believing women who come as fugitives, and sets out conditions for women taking allegiance to the Prophet. The unit warns that kinship and children will not avail on the Day of Resurrection and refers to the Hereafter, faith, enmity, and the possibility of love between former enemies.
