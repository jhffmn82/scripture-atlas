The Book of Daniel with Greek Portions / Chapter 1

The youths were chosen from Israelite royal and noble families for having no defect, being well-favored, skillful in wisdom, and able to serve in the palace. They were to be taught Chaldean learning and language and nourished for three years before serving the king. Among them were four from Judah, and those who ate the king's delicacies looked worse than the four on vegetables.
