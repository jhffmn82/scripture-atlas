Abstract written by a model

Surah 94: The Consolation (Ash-Sharh) is the document that speaks to one whose bosom has been dilated and burden eased, assures that hardship is accompanied by ease, and instructs the addressed person to continue toiling and strive to please the Lord. Surah 94: The Consolation (Ash-Sharh) is the work revealed at Mecca that presents reassurance that hardship is accompanied by ease and instructs the addressed person to continue toiling when relieved and strive to please the Lord.
