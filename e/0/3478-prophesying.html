Abstract written by a model

Prophesying speaks to men for edification, exhortation, and consolation, edifying the assembly. It surpasses uninterpreted speaking in languages and is a sign to believers. Two or three prophets speak in turn while others discern, so all may prophesy one by one, learn, and be exhorted.

Chapter 14

Prophesying speaks to men for edification, exhortation, and consolation, and edifies the assembly, so the one who prophesies is greater than the one who speaks in languages without interpretation. It is a sign to believers, two or three prophets are to speak in turn while others discern, and all can prophesy one by one so that all may learn and be exhorted.
