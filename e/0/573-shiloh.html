Shiloh

place · 5 works · 11 quoted facts · 10 connections
Also called: my place which was in Shiloh, the tent of Shiloh

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Joshua, Shiloh is where the congregation set up the Tent of Meeting, surveyors reported to Joshua, and Joshua cast lots before the LORD to divide the remaining land. In The Book of Judges, Shiloh hosts the yearly feast of the LORD and receives the 400 virgins brought to the camp. In The First Book of Samuel, Shiloh houses the LORD's house, receives Elkanah's yearly sacrifices, and is where Eli's sons served as priests, Hannah prayed and brought Samuel, and the ark was later taken to battle before defeat was announced and Eli died at its gate. In Psalms, Shiloh's tent was where God placed his dwelling among men before abandoning it and delivering his strength and glory into the adversary's hand. In The Book of Jeremiah, Shiloh was where the LORD caused his name to dwell at first, and its destruction for the wickedness of Israel serves as a pattern for what he will do to the temple in Jerusalem.
