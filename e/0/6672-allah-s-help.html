Allah's help

support · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Allah's succour, His help, help, help from Allah, support, victory, your helper

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan), Allah's help gives believers victory at Badr and support through angels, and is presented as the source of victory. In Surah 61: The Ranks (As-Saff), help from Allah is promised as another blessing that believers love. In Surah 110: Divine Support (An-Nasr), Allah's succour comes together with the triumph, followed by mankind entering the religion of Allah in troops.
