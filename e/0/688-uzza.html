Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Samuel, Uzzah drove the new cart with his brother Ahio and died after taking hold of the ark when the cattle stumbled. In 1 Chronicles, Uzza drove the cart carrying the ark with Ahio and died after touching the ark when the oxen stumbled.
