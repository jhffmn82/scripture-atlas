Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Samuel, Araunah the Jebusite owns a threshing floor, bows to the king, freely offers it with cattle and equipment, and sells them to David for fifty silver shekels after David insists on paying. In 1 Chronicles, Ornan the Jebusite owns a threshing floor, sees the angel while threshing, offers everything free to David, and sells it to David for six hundred gold shekels after David insists on paying.

Ornan, a Jebusite with four sons, owns a threshing floor; he sees the angel while threshing, offers everything free to David, but David insists on paying, so Ornan sells it for six hundred gold shekels.

Chapter 21

Ornan is threshing wheat when he sees the angel, and his four sons hide. He bows to David and offers the threshing floor, oxen, threshing instruments and wheat free, but David insists on paying, and Ornan receives six hundred shekels of gold for the place.
