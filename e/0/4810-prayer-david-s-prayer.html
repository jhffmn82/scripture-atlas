Abstract written by a model

David's prayer is spoken aloud with his voice and takes the form of a complaint and a plea for mercy. It moves from describing his troubles and abandonment to declaring the LORD his refuge, then asks for a hearing, deliverance from persecutors, and release from prison so he may give thanks. It ends with the expectation that the righteous will surround him because the LORD will be good to him.
