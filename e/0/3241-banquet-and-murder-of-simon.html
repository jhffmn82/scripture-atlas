Chapter 16

Ptolemy received Simon and his sons Mattathias and Judas into the small stronghold of Dok, which he had built, and gave a great banquet with men hidden there. When they had drunk freely, Ptolemy and his men took their weapons, rushed in, and killed Simon, his two sons and some servants. The text condemns it as a great iniquity, evil repaid for good.
