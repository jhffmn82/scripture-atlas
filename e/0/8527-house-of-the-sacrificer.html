house of the sacrificer

Abstract written by a model

A house receives sacred blessings, wise perceptions, righteous order, divine sovereignty, welfare, holy strength, and saints’ Fravashis. It has enduring glory, abundance, offspring, and companionship, while obedience, peace, generosity, reverence, and truthful speech prevail within it.

Yasna LX (Sp. LIX).

The house is asked to receive blessings, wise perceptions, righteous order, divine sovereignty, prosperity, holy strength, and the Fravashis of the saints. Obedience, peace, generosity, reverence, and truthful speech are to prevail within it.
