Chapter 6

The offering is prescribed as five golden tumors and five golden mice, matching the number of the Philistine lords, as images of the tumors and the mice that mar the land. It is placed in a box beside the ark on the cart. The tumors are listed as one each for Ashdod, Gaza, Ashkelon, Gath, and Ekron, and the mice match all the cities of the five lords.
