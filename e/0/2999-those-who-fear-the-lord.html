Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, those who fear him receive the LORD's loving kindness, compassion, and righteousness, extended to their children's children. In Psalms, everyone who fears the LORD walks in his ways, is blessed, enjoys the labor of his hands, and is surrounded by family prosperity. In Psalms, those who fear him and hope in his loving kindness are the LORD's favored ones, in whom he takes pleasure. In Psalms, those who fear the LORD are called to praise, glorify, and stand in awe of him, and the humble among them are promised satisfaction and enduring life. In Psalms, those who fear the LORD are taught by him, enjoy peace, inherit the land, receive his friendship, and learn his covenant. In Psalms, the saints and faithful fear the LORD, take refuge in him, hope in him, receive his goodness and protection, and are urged to love him and be courageous. In Psalms, those who fear him are called saints who trust and hope in the LORD, receive his protection, and lack no good thing. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, those who fear the Lord obey him, seek his pleasure, humble themselves, trust him, receive discipline, and find favor and true judgment.

those who love the Lord in The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where those who love the Lord first appears

Abstract written by a model

Those who love and fear the Lord are a group described as honored, blessed, and greater than any prince, judge, or mighty man. They keep his ways, are filled with the law, and obtain wisdom, relying on her without being moved. The records open with their honor in contrast to commandment-breakers. They then show them inheriting joy, a crown of gladness, and an everlasting name. They end living in spirit, unafraid, with the Lord's eyes as protection, raising their souls and giving health, life, and blessing.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

Human offspring who fear the Lord have honor, in contrast to those who break the commandments. Rich, honorable, and poor alike glory in the fear of the Lord, and no prince, judge, or mighty man is greater than the one who fears him.

Chapter 15

The one who fears the Lord and has possession of the law will obtain wisdom. He will be stayed upon her and not moved, will rely on her and not be confounded, and will inherit joy, a crown of gladness, and an everlasting name. The Lord's eyes are upon him.

Chapter 34

Those who fear the Lord are said to live in spirit, because their hope is in him who saves them. They will not be afraid or cowardly, and the text calls their souls blessed, with the Lord's eyes on them as a mighty protection and strong support. He raises up their souls, enlightens their eyes, and gives health, life, and blessing.
