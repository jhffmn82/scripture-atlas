monthly offering (new moon)

event · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: In the beginnings of your months, one ram, seven male lambs a year old, the burnt offering of every month, two young bulls

Abstract written by a model

The monthly new moon offering is a burnt offering made at the start of each month: two young bulls, one ram, and seven unblemished year-old male lambs, with fine flour meal offerings mixed with oil and wine drink offerings scaled by animal. One male goat is added as a sin offering, alongside the continual burnt offering.

Chapter 28

At the beginning of each month a burnt offering is made of two young bulls, one ram and seven male lambs a year old without defect, with meal offerings of fine flour mixed with oil and the prescribed drink offerings. One male goat is also offered as a sin offering, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.
