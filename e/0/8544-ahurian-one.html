Ahurian One

deity · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Ahurian (daughter) of Ahura, Ahurian One (Ahura Mazda), Ahurian One of Ahura, Ahurian one of Ahura, Ahurian ones of Ahura

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, the Ahurian One is addressed as the recipient of the Zaothra, offered with sanctity together with Haoma and flesh. In Yasna, the Ahurian One receives offerings and Yasna, is worshipped through sacrifice and good thought, and is asked for presence, protection, healing, prosperity, offspring, assistance, and heaven. In Afrinagan, Gahs and Miscellaneous Fragments, the Ahurian One of Ahura is addressed as the water created by Mazda, receiving praises, sacrifices, adoration, and offerings.
