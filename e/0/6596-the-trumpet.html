the trumpet

object · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: the Trumpet

Abstract written by a model

In The Believers (Al-Muminoon), the trumpet is blown, ending kinship and mutual asking among people. In Surah 27: The Ant (An-Naml), the Trumpet is blown on the Day when those in the heavens and earth begin in fear and come to Allah humbled. In Surah 39: The Groups (Az-Zumar), the trumpet is blown twice, causing swooning and then raising people to stand waiting.
