Chapter 14

The LORD strikes all the peoples who fought against Jerusalem with a plague in which their flesh, eyes and tongues consume away while they stand. A great panic leads each to seize and raise his hand against his neighbor. A like plague falls on the horse, mule, camel, donkey and all animals in their camps.
