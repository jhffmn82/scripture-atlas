Chapter 2

It is commended for its works, toil, perseverance, intolerance of evil men, testing of false apostles, and hatred of the works of the Nicolaitans. It is rebuked for leaving its first love and told to remember, repent, and do the first works, or its lamp stand will be moved out of its place.
