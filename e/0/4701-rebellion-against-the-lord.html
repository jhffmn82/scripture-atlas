Chapter 2

The rebellion is the plot of the nations, peoples, kings and rulers to break apart the bonds and cast off the cords of the LORD and his Anointed. It is called vain, and the LORD answers it with laughter, derision, anger and wrath, and by setting his King on Zion. The text warns that those who persist will perish, while those who take refuge in the Son are blessed.
