those who fear the LORD's name

Abstract written by a model

Those who fear the LORD's name are a faithful group who spoke with one another and honored him. The LORD listened, and a book of memory was written before him for them. He claims them as his own possession and will spare them as a father spares a serving son. On the coming day, the sun of righteousness will arise with healing, they will leap like calves, and they will tread down the wicked into ashes.

Chapter 3

Those who feared the LORD spoke with one another, and the LORD listened and heard, and a book of memory was written before him for them and for those who honored his name. He says they shall be his own possession in the day that he makes, and he will spare them as a man spares his own son who serves him.

Chapter 4

For those who fear the LORD's name, the sun of righteousness will arise with healing in its wings. They will go out and leap like calves of the stall, and they will tread down the wicked, who will be ashes under their feet in the day the LORD makes.
