The Letter from James / Chapter 1

James tells his readers to be doers of the word and not only hearers who delude themselves. A mere hearer is like a man who looks at his natural face in a mirror and immediately forgets it, while the one who looks into the perfect law of freedom and continues as a doer will be blessed in what he does.
