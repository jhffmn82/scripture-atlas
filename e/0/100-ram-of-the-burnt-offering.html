Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, the first ram is laid hands on, killed, sprinkled with blood, cut into pieces, washed, and burned whole on the altar as a burnt offering and pleasant aroma to the LORD. In Leviticus, the ram is laid hands on, killed, sprinkled with blood, cut into pieces, washed, and burned whole on the altar as a pleasant aroma and offering made by fire to the LORD.
