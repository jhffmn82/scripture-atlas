Bountiful Spirit

Also called: Bountiful Spirit (Ahura Mazda's spirit), His bountiful spirit, Thine own, Thy bounteous spirit, bounteous spirit, bountiful Spirit, most bounteous spirit, spirit, spiritual gifts

Abstract written by a model

Bountiful Spirit is Ahura Mazda’s most bounteous spirit. It brings blessedness through words and Âramaiti’s hands, grants sovereign power, gifts, spiritual glory, Universal Weal, and Immortality to chosen saints, blesses contending throngs with certainty and peace, and harms the wicked but not saints.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XLIII.

Ahura Mazda’s bounteous spirit is associated with sovereign power, gifts, and the granting of spiritual glory. Zarathustra says he loves the Spirit, and spiritual gifts are to be given to contending throngs as blessings of certainty and peace.

Yasna XLVII.

Ahura Mazda’s Bountiful Spirit is the means by which He gives Universal Weal, Immortality, and other best gifts to His chosen saint. It brings blessedness into action and harms the wicked, but not the saints.
