Also called: rules of propriety

Abstract written by a model

Propriety governs officials’ conduct and relations between superiors and inferiors, helping preserve the State. Its richest fruit is ordering and adorning service to parents and obedience to elder brothers. It requires respect and prescribed rules; the superior man examines himself and observes it even when others are unreasonable.

Mencius, Book IV (Li Lou) / Part I / Chapter 1

Rules of propriety govern how officials take office and retire from it and how superiors and inferiors conduct themselves. When superiors do not observe them and inferiors do not learn, seditious people arise and the State quickly perishes.

Chapter 27

Propriety consists in ordering and adorning the service of one's parents and the obeying of one's elder brothers.

Part II / Chapter 1 .. Chapter 33

Propriety involves showing respect to others and following prescribed rules. The superior man examines himself for a lack of propriety and continues to observe it even when others behave unreasonably.
