the meeting with their Lord

event · 7 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: the meeting of this your day, the meeting with Allah, the meeting with your Lord, the resurrection and return to Allah, the return of all of you, the return to Allah, their return, unto Us is your return, ye will be returned

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 10: Jonas (Yunus), the resurrection and return to Allah brings all mankind back for judgment. In Surah 13: The Thunder (Ar-Ra'd), the meeting with their Lord is the certainty that the revelations establish. In Surah 29: The Spider (Al-Ankaboot), the meeting with Allah is certain and its deniers face painful doom. In Surah 30: The Romans (Ar-Room), disbelief in the meeting with their Lord leads to doom. In Surah 32: The Prostration (As-Sajda), disbelievers deny the meeting with their Lord until the guilty recognize it and ask to return. In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), people are warned not to doubt the meeting with their Lord. In Surah 88: The Overwhelming (Al-Ghaashiya), the return of the people is unto Allah.
