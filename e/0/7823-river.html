Abstract written by a model

During autumn floods, streams pour into the river, making its course turbid and swollen; individual drops make it large, it provides bird habitat, yet it remains small beside the ocean.

CHAPTER XVII.

During the autumn floods, every stream pours into the river, which swells in its turbid course until its banks are too far apart to distinguish a cow from a horse. The river is presented as small in relation to the ocean.
