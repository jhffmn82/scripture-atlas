Abstract written by a model

The hill country near Bethulia, once desolate, was possessed and resettled by the children of Israel. Its narrow ascents form the entrance into Judea, where two men at most fit, so Joakim orders them seized. Israel then shuts the passages and fortifies the hilltops near Holofernes, whose men vow to attack and make Israel prey.

Chapter 4

The ascents are the entrance into Judea, and Joakim orders them seized because the approach is narrow, with space for two men at most, making it easy to stop an invader. The children of Israel do as he commanded.

Chapter 5

The hill country is where Israel has shut up the passages and fortified the high hilltops, near where Holofernes is. Achior says Israel possessed all of it after crossing the Jordan and again settled in it after returning from dispersion, because it was desolate. Holofernes' men say they will go up against it and make Israel prey.
