Gog and Magog

group · 3 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

In Revelation, Gog and Magog are nations from the earth's four corners deceived by Satan, gathered for war, and devoured by fire from heaven. In Surah 18: The Cave (Al-Kahf), Gog and Magog spoil the land and cannot surmount or pierce the barrier built against them, which will be laid low when Allah's promise comes to pass. In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), Gog and Magog will be let loose and hasten out of every mound before the True Promise draws near.
