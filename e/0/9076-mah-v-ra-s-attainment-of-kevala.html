Abstract written by a model

In Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra), it describes Mahâvîra attaining Kevala in deep meditation and becoming an omniscient Arhat and Gina. In Kalpa Sutra, it describes Mahâvîra attaining the highest knowledge and intuition called Kevala and becoming a Gina, Arhat, and Kevalin.
