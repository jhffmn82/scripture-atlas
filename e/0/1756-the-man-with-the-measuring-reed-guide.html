the man with the measuring reed (guide)

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

The man with the measuring reed is a guide who appears bronze-like, holding a line of flax and a measuring reed, and leads Ezekiel (the narrator, called Son of man) through the temple vision. In Chapter 40 he stands in the gate, guides the speaker through the outer and inner courts, measures gates, courts, and porches, and explains the priests' rooms. In Chapter 41 he measures the nave, posts, walls, side rooms, and inner room, naming the most holy place and the wooden altar as the table before the LORD. In Chapter 42 he leads the speaker to the north rooms, explains their holiness and the priests' rules, then exits by the east gate and measures all four sides of the surrounding area. In Chapter 47 he leads Ezekiel out the northern gate to the east gate, measures a thousand cubits at a time, has him pass through the waters at each depth, and explains where they go.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 40

He appears with the look of bronze, holding a line of flax and a measuring reed, and stands in the gate. He guides the speaker through the outer and inner courts, measures every gate, court, and porch, and explains that the south-facing room is for the priests who perform the duty of the house and the north-facing room for those who perform the duty of the altar.

Chapter 41

The guide brings the narrator to the nave and measures the posts, entrances, walls, side rooms, and the building behind. He goes inward to measure the inner room and tells the narrator it is the most holy place. He also identifies the wooden altar as the table before the LORD.

Chapter 42

He brings the speaker out into the outer court toward the north and into the rooms opposite the separate place. He explains that the north and south rooms are holy and states the priests' rules. After finishing the inner house, he leads the speaker out by the east gate and measures all four sides of the surrounding area with the measuring reed.

Chapter 47

The man leads Ezekiel out by the northern gate and around to the outer east gate, then measures one thousand cubits at a time with the line in his hand. He has Ezekiel pass through the waters at each depth, asks whether he has seen this, and explains where the waters go and what they will do.
