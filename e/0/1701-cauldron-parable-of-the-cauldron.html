Abstract written by a model

The cauldron is a parable image of the bloody city, Jerusalem, whose stubborn rust never leaves it. It is filled with choice meat and bones and boiled, then set empty on coals so its filth melts away, showing no cleansing until the LORD's wrath rests.

Chapter 24

The cauldron is set on the fire with water, filled with choice pieces such as the thigh and shoulder and choice bones, and boiled well with a great pile of wood. It is then set empty on the coals so its bronze burns and its filthiness and rust are melted and consumed. It represents the bloody city, whose great rust does not leave her and who will not be cleansed until the LORD's wrath rests.
