    • “Guarding his speech and possessed of carefulness, acquiring (pure) Lêsyâ, he should wander about; he should not thatch a house for himself or for others, nor behave towards other people like a householder. (15)”Sutrakritanga (Sûtrakritâṅga), Book I · Tenth Lecture, Called Carefulness.
