superior man

person · 1 work · 14 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: A superior man, The superior man, a superior man, the superior man

Abstract written by a model

The superior man may endure want while remaining oriented toward righteousness and truth. In everything, he treats righteousness as essential: he follows propriety, expresses it humbly, and completes it sincerely. He seeks in himself, judges people beyond their words, and preserves good words regardless of their speaker. He values truth rather than food, worries about failing to attain truth rather than poverty, and is not fully known through little matters, though he may be entrusted with great concerns.
