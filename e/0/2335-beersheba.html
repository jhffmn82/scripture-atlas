Abstract written by a model

Beersheba is a wilderness place named for an oath sworn there by Abraham and Abimelech, who made a covenant. Abraham plants a tamarisk and calls on the LORD; Hagar wanders there. Later Isaac, visited by the LORD, builds an altar and digs a well called Shibah, giving the city its name.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 21

Beersheba is the wilderness where Hagar wanders after being sent away. Abraham names the place because he and Abimelech swore an oath there, and it is where they made a covenant; Abraham plants a tamarisk tree there and calls on the LORD.

Chapter 26

Isaac goes up to Beersheba, where the LORD appears to him the same night, and he builds an altar, pitches his tent, and has a well dug. After the oath with Abimelech the well is called Shibah, and the city is named Beersheba to this day.
