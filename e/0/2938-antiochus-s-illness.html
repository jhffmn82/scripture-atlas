is_inflicted_by God [fatal and invisible stroke] An incurable pain of the bowels with bitter inner torments seizes Antiochus as he finishes his boast, which the text calls just for the torments he had inflicted on others. It worsens after his chariot fall into worms, falling flesh and an intolerable stench, until he is broken in spirit and dies.
