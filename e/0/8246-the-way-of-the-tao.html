the way of the Tao

Abstract written by a model

The way of the Tao acts without thinking of acting and conducts affairs without trouble; it treats small as great, few as many, tastes without discerning flavour, and recompenses injury with kindness.

63. 1. (It is the way of the Tao) to act without (thinking of) acting;

The way of the Tao is to act without thinking of acting and to conduct affairs without feeling their trouble. It also teaches treating what is small as great, a few as many, and recompensing injury with kindness.
