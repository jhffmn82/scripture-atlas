Abstract written by a model

Jacob's present for Esau is a large gift of livestock: goats, ewes, rams, milk camels with colts, cows, bulls, donkeys and foals. Jacob has servants drive it in separate herds ahead of him, telling Esau it is Jacob's gift, to appease him. Later Jacob explains it was meant to find favor. Esau first refuses, saying he has enough, but Jacob insists and Esau accepts.

Chapter 32

Jacob takes from his possessions a present for Esau: goats, ewes, rams, milk camels with colts, cows, bulls, donkeys, and foals. He gives it to his servants in separate herds with spaces between them, instructing them to tell Esau the animals are Jacob's gift and that Jacob is behind them. Jacob intends it to appease Esau so that he might accept him, and it passes over before him.

Chapter 33

Jacob explains that the company Esau met was sent to find favor in his sight. Esau first says he has enough and refuses it, but Jacob insists, saying God has dealt graciously with him and that he has enough, and Esau takes it.
