two first spirits

group · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: primeval spirits, the two spirits, these two spirits, world's two first spirits

Abstract written by a model

The two first spirits are a pair, independent in action and opposed as better and worse in thought, word, and deed. They differ in thoughts, commands, understandings, beliefs, deeds, consciences, and souls. They come together to determine life, its absence, and the world’s final ordering, after which the more bountiful spirit speaks to the harmful spirit.
