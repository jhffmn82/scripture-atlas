Abstract written by a model

Yin Wên, an enthusiastic follower of TAO with Sung Hsing, wore a Hua Mountain-shaped cap and pursued tranquillity, opposing strife and aggression while accepting hardship to improve the lowly’s condition.

CHAPTER XXXIII.

Yin Wên became an enthusiastic follower of TAO with Sung Hsing and sought to prevent strife, end aggression, and improve the condition of the lowly. He and Sung Hsing accepted hardship while pursuing the world's claims.
