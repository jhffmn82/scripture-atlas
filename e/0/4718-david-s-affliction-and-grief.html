Abstract written by a model

David's affliction and grief: his eye, soul and body waste away, his life spent in sorrow and sighing. Strength fails through iniquity, bones waste, and he becomes a horror to acquaintances, forgotten like the dead and broken pottery, yet the LORD has seen and known.

Chapter 31

David's eye, soul, and body waste away with grief, and his life and years are spent in sorrow and sighing. His strength fails because of his iniquity and his bones are wasted. He becomes a horror to acquaintances, forgotten like a dead man and like broken pottery, yet the LORD has seen his affliction and known his soul in adversities.
