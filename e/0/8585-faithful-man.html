faithful man

Also called: faithful, the faithful man, the man, the man who is to be cleansed

Abstract written by a model

Faithful man’s soul is parted by Ashemaogha’s deadly blow, then he is cleansed through chanting, ritual materials and prayers over nine nights, and invocation of Airyaman for rejoicing and holiness.

Fargard XI.
