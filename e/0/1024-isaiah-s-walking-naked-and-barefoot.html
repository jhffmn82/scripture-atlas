Chapter 20

At the LORD's command, Isaiah loosened his sackcloth and removed his sandals, walking naked and barefoot for three years. The LORD calls it a sign and a wonder concerning Egypt and Ethiopia, foreshadowing their captives being led away by Assyria in the same state.
