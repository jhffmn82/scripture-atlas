Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the holy things of the children of Israel, including the most holy offerings from the fire, are given to Aaron and his sons by reason of the anointing, and the Levites are warned not to profane them. In The Book of Ezekiel, the most holy things, including the meal offering, the sin offering, and the trespass offering, are eaten by the priests and laid in the holy rooms, making the place holy.
