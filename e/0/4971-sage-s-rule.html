sage's rule

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: the description which has been given of a sage's rule

Abstract written by a model

The sage’s rule is described as planting the people so that they are established, leading them so that they follow, making them happy so that multitudes resort to the dominions, and stimulating them so that they become harmonious. While the sage lives he is glorious, and after his death he is bitterly lamented.

sage's rule is in one work on this site, The Analects of Confucius, Book XIX: Tsze-Chang. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
