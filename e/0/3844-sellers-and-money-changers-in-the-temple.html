Abstract written by a model

In The Good News According to Mark, money changers and sellers in the temple are thrown out by Jesus, who overturns the money changers' tables and the seats of the dove sellers. In The Gospel According to John, sellers and money changers in the temple are driven out by Jesus, who pours out the changers' money, overturns their tables, and tells the dove sellers to take their things away.
