Abstract written by a model

In Deuteronomy, exile and scattering among nations is a judgment bringing eventual restoration. In Baruch, the captivity and scattering subjects the Lord’s people to rival kingdoms before repentance and return. In The Book of Ezekiel, exile portrays Israel going into captivity, dispersed among the nations and scattered through the countries. In The Book of Ezekiel, scattering of Israel among the nations is God’s judgment followed by gathering and return. In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, the scattering of Israel disperses the house of Israel among all nations before latter-day restoration. In The Second Book of Nephi, dispersion of Israel scatters surviving Jews among all nations until they believe in Christ and are gathered. In The Second Book of Nephi, scattering and captivity afflict the seed of Lehi and the Jews for rejecting the Lord and his commandments. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the scattering of Israel results from the Gentiles’ blessing through the Holy Ghost.

exile and scattering among nations in Deuteronomy

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where exile and scattering among nations first appears

Abstract written by a model

The exile and scattering of the Israelites among the nations is a judgment the LORD brings, driving them and their king from one end of the earth to the other. In Chapter 28 it means serving wood and stone gods, becoming an astonishment, proverb and byword, and living with no rest, trembling hearts, failing eyes and constant fear. Chapter 30 turns to restoration: if they remember, return and obey with all heart and soul, the LORD releases them, gathers them, returns them to the land, circumcises their hearts, and prospers them.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 28

The LORD will scatter the Israelites among all peoples from one end of the earth to the other, and bring them and their king to a nation they have not known. There they will serve gods of wood and stone, become an astonishment, proverb, and byword, and find no rest. They will have trembling hearts, failing eyes, and constant fear for their lives.

Chapter 30

The chapter foresees the Israelites driven by the LORD among all the nations after the blessing and curse come on them. If they call these to mind, return, and obey with all their heart and soul, the LORD will release them from captivity, gather them from every place of scattering, and bring them back to the land. Restoration includes the circumcision of their hearts, increased numbers, prosperity, and the LORD's renewed rejoicing over them.

Abstract written by a model

The Lord scatters his people among surrounding nations, subjecting them to rival kingdoms as a reproach and desolation and reducing a great multitude to a few. In captivity they seek favor from captors, and as foretold through Moses, repent, know and praise the Lord, and return.

Chapter 2

The Lord has scattered the people among the surrounding nations, subjecting them to the kingdoms around them as a reproach and desolation, leaving only a few. The people ask for favor in the sight of their captors. The Lord's word through Moses foretells that in captivity they will take it to heart, know the Lord, praise him, and return from their wicked deeds.

scattering of Israel among the nations in The Book of Ezekiel

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where scattering of Israel among the nations first appears

Abstract written by a model

God scatters Israel among the nations, pouring out wrath for their bloodshed and idols and judging their deeds. Among the nations they profane his holy name, as people say they left the LORD's land. Out of respect for his name, God gathers them from all countries and returns them.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 36

God states that he poured out his wrath on Israel for their bloodshed and idols, scattering them among the nations and judging them according to their ways and deeds. Among the nations they profaned his holy name, since men said that they were the LORD's people who had left his land. This leads God to act out of respect for his name by gathering them from all the countries and bringing them back to their own land.

the scattering of Israel in The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry

Abstract written by a model

The scattering of Israel is an event in which the house of Israel is dispersed among all nations and across the earth, likened to olive-tree branches broken off. Caused through the Gentiles before latter-day restoration, it follows many hardening their hearts against the Holy One of Israel and precedes grafting in and restoration.
