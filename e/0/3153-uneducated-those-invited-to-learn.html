Abstract written by a model

The uneducated are a group invited by the speaker to live in the house of instruction. Thirsty in soul and lacking wisdom, they are urged to get wisdom without money, take her yoke, receive instruction, rejoice in the Lord's mercy, and work before the time comes.

Chapter 51

The uneducated are called by the speaker to draw near and live in the house of instruction. He asks why they lack these things and why their souls are very thirsty, and tells them to get wisdom without money, put their necks under her yoke, and receive instruction. They are urged to rejoice in the Lord's mercy, not be put to shame, and work their work before the time comes.
