Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the Spirit is taken from Moses and placed on the elders, rests on Eldad and Medad, and is wished upon all God’s people. In The Book of Isaiah, the Spirit will be poured on God’s people from on high, transforming desolation into fruitfulness. In The Gospel According to Matthew, the Holy Spirit causes Mary’s conception of Jesus and descends like a dove upon Jesus after his baptism. In The Good News According to Mark, the Holy Spirit speaks through disciples and must not be blasphemed. In The Gospel According to John, the Holy Spirit is the Counselor and Spirit of truth who remains with disciples, teaches them, convicts the world, guides them, and glorifies Jesus. In The Acts of the Apostles, the Holy Spirit empowers, directs, fills, speaks through, and is given to believers and apostles. In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, the Holy Spirit dwells in believers, gives life and adoption, leads God’s children, testifies with their spirits, and intercedes for them. In Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians, the Holy Spirit searches God’s deep things, reveals and teaches hidden wisdom, dwells in believers, and distributes spiritual gifts.

and 7 more works

In 2 Corinthians, the Holy Spirit gives life and liberty, writes on believers’ hearts, transforms them from glory to glory, and provides a more glorious ministry. In Paul’s Letter to the Galatians, the Spirit is received through faith, opposes the flesh, leads believers beyond the law, and produces fruit as they walk by him. In Paul’s Letter to the Ephesians, the Holy Spirit is the one Spirit of the body, pledges believers’ inheritance, seals them for redemption, and must not be grieved. In 1 Timothy, the Spirit expressly warns that some will fall away from the faith in later times. In Surah 16: The Bee (An-Nahl), the Spirit delivers truthful revelation from Muhammad’s Lord as confirmation, guidance, and good tidings. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), the True Spirit brings revelation to Muhammad’s heart so he may be a warner. In Surah 97: The Power, Fate (Al-Qadr), the Spirit descends during the Night of Predestination by the Lord’s permission with all decrees.

Holy Spirit in The Acts of the Apostles

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Holy Spirit first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Holy Spirit appears in these records as a divine agent who speaks, directs, empowers and is given, rather than a mere force. Promised by Jesus in Chapter 1 as a baptism that would bring power to the apostles, he is also said to have spoken through David about Judas. In Chapter 2 he fills the gathered believers, who speak in other languages, and Peter teaches that Jesus received the promise from the Father and poured it out; repentant, baptized people will receive the gift. The Spirit then extends outward: Samaritan believers receive him through Peter and John's prayer and laying on of hands, Simon tries to buy this power, and the Spirit directs Philip to the eunuch and carries him away. In Chapters 10 and 11 he sends men to Peter and falls on Gentiles in Cornelius's house, amazing the circumcised believers. He fills Barnabas, informs Agabus of a famine, and in Chapter 13 directs the Antioch assembly to send out Barnabas and Saul, filling Paul against Elymas and the disciples with joy. In Chapter 19 Ephesian disciples, unfamiliar with him, receive him through Paul's hands, speak in languages and prophesy; Paul resolves in the Spirit to go to Jerusalem and Rome.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

The Acts of the Apostles / Chapter 1

Jesus gave commandment to the apostles through the Holy Spirit, and promised that they would be baptized in the Holy Spirit not many days later. He said they would receive power when the Holy Spirit came upon them, and Peter said the Holy Spirit had spoken by David's mouth about Judas.

Chapter 2

The Holy Spirit fills all the gathered believers, who speak in other languages as the Spirit gives them ability. Peter explains that Jesus, having received the promise of the Spirit from the Father, has poured it out, and that repentant, baptized people will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.

Chapter 8

The Holy Spirit had not yet fallen on any of the Samaritan believers, who had only been baptized in the name of Christ Jesus. They received him when Peter and John prayed and laid hands on them, and Simon sought to buy this power. The Spirit also told Philip to join the eunuch's chariot and then caught Philip away after the baptism.

Chapter 10

The Spirit tells Peter that three men are seeking him and to go with them without doubting, since the Spirit has sent them. While Peter is still speaking in Cornelius's house, the Holy Spirit falls on all who hear the word. The circumcised believers with Peter are amazed that the gift was poured out on Gentiles, who speak in other languages and magnify God.

Chapter 11

The Spirit told Peter to go with the three men without discriminating, and the Holy Spirit fell on the Gentiles as Peter began to speak. Barnabas is described as full of the Holy Spirit, and Agabus indicates the coming famine by the Spirit.

Chapter 13

The Holy Spirit speaks to the Antioch assembly, directing them to separate Barnabas and Saul for the work he has called them to, and sends them out. Paul is filled with the Spirit when he confronts Elymas, and at the end the disciples are filled with joy and the Holy Spirit.

Chapter 19

The Holy Spirit is something the Ephesian disciples had not heard of. It comes on them when Paul lays hands on them, and they speak with other languages and prophesy. Paul also determines in the Spirit to travel to Jerusalem and then Rome.

Holy Spirit in Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Holy Spirit first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Holy Spirit is presented as the Spirit from God who searches all things, including God's deep things, and uniquely knows them. In Chapter 2, he reveals God's hidden wisdom, teaches the words spoken, and is received by believers so they may know what God freely gave. In Chapter 6, he dwells in believers, whose bodies are his temple, and they were justified in him. In Chapter 12, he enables confessing Jesus as Lord, produces all varied gifts, distributes them to each as he desires, and baptizes all believers into one body.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The Spirit searches all things, including the deep things of God, and is the one who knows God's things. God revealed his hidden wisdom through the Spirit, and the Spirit teaches the words the speaker uses. The Spirit is from God and was received by believers so they might know what God freely gave them; his things are spiritually discerned.

Chapter 6

The Holy Spirit is described as living in believers, whose body is his temple, and as given to them from God. Believers were also justified in the Spirit of God.
