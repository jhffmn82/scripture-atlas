peace

topic · 5 works · 2 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Peace, Peace, peace, no peace, offer you peace, security, terms of peace, the judgment of truth and peace, truth, truth and peace

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, peace is what the speaker urges others to pray for on Jerusalem's behalf and wishes within the city's walls. In Psalms, Peace is what God speaks to his people and saints, pictured as kissing righteousness. In The Book of Isaiah, peace is what the righteous enter and rest in at death, created by the LORD for those far and near, but denied to the wicked. In The Book of Zechariah, truth and peace are what the people are commanded to practice, love, and enact in judgment. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), peace is presented as the better course among believers and within marriage, and is to be accepted when offered. In Surah 49: The Inner Apartments (Al-Hujuraat), peace is to be made between fighting parties and among believing brothers, established justly and equitably.

peace in Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where peace first appears

Abstract written by a model

Peace is presented as the better course in relations among believers and within marriage. Believers are told to accept those who stand apart, do not fight, and offer peace, and not to deny the belief of someone offering peace for worldly gain. In matters concerning women, orphans, and children, the text calls for just dealing and says Allah knows every good done. When a woman and her husband fear ill treatment or desertion, they may establish peace; the text concludes that peace is better.
