Also called: Woe to you Pharisees, Woe to you lawyers

Abstract written by a model

Jesus pronounces three woes on the Pharisees, for tithing herbs but neglecting justice and love, loving the best seats and greetings, and being like hidden graves. He pronounces three on the lawyers, for burdening people, building tombs of prophets their fathers killed, and taking away the key of knowledge. The woes provoke the anger of the scribes and Pharisees.
