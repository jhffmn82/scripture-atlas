Abstract written by a model

The LORD says every container should be filled with wine and applies this to filling the inhabitants of the land, including kings, priests, prophets, and all of Jerusalem, with drunkenness. He will dash fathers and sons against one another, without pity, sparing, or compassion. Further judgment includes darkness, scattering like stubble, and the exposure of Jerusalem's shame.
