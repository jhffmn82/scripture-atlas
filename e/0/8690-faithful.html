Abstract written by a model

The faithful are Mazda worshippers, men and women. They must remain apart from cleansing grounds and unclean people during seclusion, and rejoice when cleansing from the Nasu is proper. They are promised gifts, rejoicing, desirable reward through the law, and holiness’ boon.

Fargard IX.

The faithful must be kept at a distance from the cleansing ground and from the unclean one during the periods of seclusion. The faithful are pleased when someone is properly cleansed from the Nasu.

Fargard XXI.

The faithful are promised gifts from the holy bull and are included among those for whom Airyaman is asked to come and bring rejoicing. A desirable reward and a boon for holiness are associated with them.
