Abstract written by a model

The Shunammite's son is born at the season Elisha foretold. When grown, he cries "My head!" in the field with the reapers, is carried to his mother, sits on her knees until noon and dies. After Elisha's prayer and stretching himself on him, the boy's flesh grows warm, he sneezes seven times, and opens his eyes.
