false teachers (at Ephesus)

group · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: anyone teaches a different doctrine, certain men, false teachers, people of corrupt minds and destitute of the truth, some, such, teachers of the law

Abstract written by a model

The false teachers at Ephesus are men who teach a different doctrine, devoting themselves to myths, endless genealogies, and disputes. Having missed the mark, they turn to vain talking and want to be law teachers without understanding what they affirm. Later they are described as conceited, knowing nothing, obsessed with word battles that breed envy, strife, insults, and evil suspicions, and they treat godliness as a means of gain. Timothy is told to command them to stop and to withdraw from them; some have wandered from the faith.

1 Timothy / Chapter 1

Certain men at Ephesus teach a different doctrine and pay attention to myths and endless genealogies, which cause disputes rather than God's stewardship in faith. Having missed the mark, some have turned to vain talking, desiring to be teachers of the law while understanding neither what they say nor what they affirm. Timothy is to command them not to teach this way.

Chapter 6

Those who teach a different doctrine and do not consent to sound words are called conceited and knowing nothing. They are obsessed with disputes and word battles that produce envy, strife, insults, and evil suspicions, and they suppose godliness is a means of gain. Timothy is told to withdraw from them, and some who professed falsely called knowledge have wandered from the faith.
