Chapter 44

The outer east gate of the sanctuary is shown shut. The LORD says it must stay shut and unopened, with no man entering by it, because the LORD, the God of Israel, has entered by it. Only the prince may sit in it to eat bread before the LORD, entering and leaving by the porch of the gate.
