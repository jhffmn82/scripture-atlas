Abstract written by a model

Humanity is portrayed as fleeting: like a breath and a walking shadow, busying itself in vain and heaping up wealth for unknown heirs. Rebuked by the LORD for iniquity, its wealth is consumed like a moth; every man is but breath.

Chapter 39

Every man is described as standing as a breath and walking like a shadow, busying himself in vain. He heaps up wealth without knowing who will gather it, and when the LORD rebukes him for iniquity, his wealth is consumed like a moth. The text repeats that every man is but a breath.
