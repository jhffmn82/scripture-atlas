Abstract written by a model

In Surah 8: The Spoils of War (Al-Anfaal), Allah's reward includes pardon, bountiful provision, and better than what has been taken. In Surah 64: Mutual Disillusion (At-Taghaabun), Allah's reward is immense and doubles goodly loans while bringing forgiveness.
