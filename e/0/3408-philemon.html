Philemon

Also called: brother

Abstract written by a model

Philemon is addressed as Paul's beloved fellow worker, praised for his love and faith toward the Lord Jesus and all the saints, and for refreshing the hearts of the saints. Paul appeals to him to receive Onesimus as a beloved brother rather than a slave, and to put any wrong or debt to Paul's account. Paul says Philemon owes him even his own self and expresses confidence that he will do even more than asked.
