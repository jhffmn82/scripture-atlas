Abstract written by a model

In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan), forgiveness is sought from Allah for sins and granted to those who repent, remember Allah, seek forgiveness, and do not knowingly repeat wrongs; Allah also forgave believers who turned back at Uhud. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), repentance is accepted for those who do evil in ignorance and then turn quickly to Allah, but not for those who persist until death or die as disbelievers. In Surah 11: Hud, repentance is required from people as the messengers urge them to seek Allah's forgiveness and turn to Him repentantly; Allah is described as Merciful, Loving, Nigh, and Responsive to those who repent. In Surah 25: The Criterion (Al-Furqaan), repentance is accepted from one who turns away from wrongdoing, believes, and does righteous work, truly turning toward Allah. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), repentance and forgiveness appear as Abraham asks forgiveness for himself and his father, the wizards hope Allah will forgive their sins, and repentance follows the hamstringing of the she-camel. In Surah 39: The Groups (Az-Zumar), repentance involves turning to Allah, putting away false gods, and surrendering to Him before doom arrives, with glad tidings for those who repent. In Surah 40: The Forgiver (Al-Ghaafir), repentance is accepted by Allah, and those who repent and follow His way are included in the angels' prayer for forgiveness and protection from hell's punishment. In Surah 40: The Forgiver (Al-Ghaafir), forgiveness is sought for those who believe, repent, and follow Allah's way, and Muhammad is instructed to ask forgiveness of his sin.

and 1 more work

In Surah 66: The Prohibition (At-Tahrim), sincere repentance is commanded for believers and may lead to remission of evil deeds and entry into Gardens beneath which rivers flow.

repentance in Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where repentance first appears

Abstract written by a model

Repentance is accepted for those who do evil in ignorance and then turn quickly to Allah. It is incumbent on Allah for them. The account contrasts this with those who continue ill-deeds until death attends them and then repent, and with those who die as disbelievers; neither receives the stated forgiveness, and painful doom is prepared for the latter.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

15 As for those of your women who are guilty of lewdness, call to witness four of you against them. And if they testify (to the truth of the allegation) then confine them to the houses until death take them or (until) Allah appoint for them a way (through new legislation). .. 18 The forgiveness is not for those who do ill-deeds until, when death attendeth upon one of them, he saith: Lo! I repent now; nor yet for those who die while they are disbelievers. For such We have prepared a painful doom.

Repentance is accepted for those who do evil in ignorance and then turn quickly to Allah. Those who repent only when death attends them are excluded from the stated forgiveness.
