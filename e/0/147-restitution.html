restitution

topic · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: a fifth part, add a fifth part more to it, make restitution for his guilt in full, restitution for guilt, restitution for trespass, restore it in full, restore that which he took by robbery

Abstract written by a model

In Leviticus, restitution requires returning robbed, oppressed, deposited, found, or falsely sworn goods in full, adding a fifth part, to the owner on the day of conviction. In Numbers, restitution for trespass against the LORD requires confession, full repayment plus a fifth to the one wronged or, if there is no kinsman, payment to the LORD belonging to the priest, besides the atonement ram.

Restitution obliges a guilty person to return robbed, oppressed, deposited, found, or falsely sworn goods in full, adding a fifth part, and give it to the owner on the day of conviction.

Chapter 6

Restitution requires the guilty person to restore robbed goods, things gained by oppression, a deposit, a found lost thing, or anything sworn falsely about, in full. He must add a fifth part and give it to the owner on the day he is found guilty.

Abstract written by a model

Restitution for trespass against the LORD requires confessing the sin and repaying in full plus a fifth to the one wronged; if he has no kinsman, payment goes to the LORD and belongs to the priest, besides the atonement ram.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

A man or woman who trespasses against the LORD must confess the sin and repay the guilt in full plus a fifth to the one wronged. If that person has no kinsman, the restitution goes to the LORD and belongs to the priest, in addition to the ram of atonement.
