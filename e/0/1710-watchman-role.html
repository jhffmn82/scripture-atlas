Abstract written by a model

The watchman is a role the LORD assigns to Ezekiel over the house of Israel. He must hear the LORD's word and warn the wicked and righteous; failure makes him answerable for blood, while warning that goes unheeded frees his own soul.

Chapter 3

The LORD makes Ezekiel a watchman to the house of Israel, charged to hear the word from his mouth and warn them for him. If Ezekiel fails to warn, the LORD will require the blood of the one who dies at his hand. If he warns and the person does not turn, Ezekiel has delivered his own soul.
