Âfrînagân. / I. Âfrîn[-Agân] Gahanbâr. .. III. Âfrîn[-Agân] Rapithvin.

The Rapithwina ritual requires washed hands and mortars, spread Baresman, elevated Haoma, brightly flaming fire, loud recitation of Ahuna-vairya, a Haoma-moistened tongue, and a Mãthra-bound body. Its proper performance earns the reward declared by Ahura Mazda.
