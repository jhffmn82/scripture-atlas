Chapter 34

Zedekiah made this covenant with all the people in Jerusalem, made before the LORD in the house called by his name, to free every Hebrew male and female servant. It echoes the LORD's earlier covenant at the exodus requiring release after seven years, but the people broke it by retaking their servants, which brings judgment on the covenant-breakers who had passed between the parts of the calf.
