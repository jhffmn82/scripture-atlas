avenger of blood

person · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: The avenger of blood, avenger, the avenger, the avenger of blood

Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the avenger of blood executes a murderer by putting him to death when he meets him. In Deuteronomy, the avenger of blood may pursue and kill an unintentional killer if no city of refuge exists. In The Book of Joshua, the avenger of blood cannot receive an unintentional killer whom a city of refuge protects.
