Chapter 9

The foolish woman is described as loud, undisciplined, and knowing nothing. She sits at her door on a seat in the high places of the city and calls to passersby with the same invitation as Wisdom, saying stolen water is sweet and food eaten in secret is pleasant. Her guests are in the depths of Sheol.
