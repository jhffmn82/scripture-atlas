sword, famine, and pestilence

Abstract written by a model

Sword, famine, and pestilence are the LORD's instruments of punishment on Judah. In Chapter 14 he vows to consume the people with them, false prophets who deny them will die by them, and corpses will lie unburied. In Chapter 44 they are the means that punished Jerusalem and will consume the remnant in Egypt, making them a horror, curse, and reproach.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 14

The LORD declares he will consume the people by the sword, by famine, and by pestilence. The false prophets deny that sword and famine will come, but the LORD says those prophets will die by them. Jeremiah describes the slain with the sword in the field and those sick with famine in the city, and the people's corpses will lie unburied.

Chapter 44

Sword, famine, and pestilence are the means by which the LORD says he punished Jerusalem and will punish those in Egypt. The Judah remnant there will be consumed by sword and famine from the least to the greatest, becoming an object of horror, a curse, and a reproach.
