the tree of Zaqqum

object · 3 works · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Its crop, Zaqqum, a tree, a tree called Zaqqum, the tree called Zaqqum, tree of Zaqqum

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 37: Those drawn up in Ranks (As-Saaffaat), the tree of Zaqqum is appointed as a torment for wrong-doers and springs from the heart of hell; its crop resembles the heads of devils, and the guilty must eat from it before drinking boiling water. In Surah 44: The Smoke (Ad-Dukhaan), the tree of Zaqqum is the food of the sinner. In Surah 56: The Inevitable (Al-Waaqia), the tree called Zaqqum will be eaten by the erring deniers, who will fill their bellies with it.
