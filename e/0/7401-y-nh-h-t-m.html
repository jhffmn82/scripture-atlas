Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, YêNhê hâtãm is a prominent, holy Yast recitation following sacrifice and offering, addressed to the Bountiful Immortals during Yasna, and itself receiving worship. In Yasna, YêNhê hâtãm is a prayer to be sent forth in the liturgy between heaven and earth.
