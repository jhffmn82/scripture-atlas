Chapter 8

Another angel stands over the altar with a golden censer and is given much incense to add to the prayers of all the saints. The smoke goes up before God from his hand, after which he fills the censer with altar fire and throws it on the earth, followed by thunders, sounds, lightnings, and an earthquake.
