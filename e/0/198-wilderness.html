Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the wilderness is where the people's dead bodies will fall, their children will wander for forty years, and the LORD's signs were worked. In Deuteronomy, the wilderness is where the LORD led the Israelites for forty years to humble and test them, feeding them with manna, bringing water from flint rock, and preserving their clothing and feet. In Psalms, the wilderness is where God provides for Israel by leading them like a flock, splitting rocks, and bringing streams from the rock, though Israel rebels and doubts him there. In The Book of Ezekiel, the wilderness is where the LORD gave Israel statutes, ordinances, and Sabbaths, where they rebelled, and where he promises future judgment.
