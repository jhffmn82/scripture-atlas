Nuna-kura-futo-tama-shiki

person · 1 work · 25 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Heavenly Monarch, Heavenly Sovereign, Heavenly Sovereign Nuna-kura-futo-tama-shiki, His Augustness Nuna-kura-futo-tama-shiki

Abstract written by a model

Nuna-kura-futo-tama-shiki is a Heavenly Sovereign who dwelt in the Palace of Wosada and ruled the Empire for fourteen years. He had four wives, including half-sister Toyo-mike-kashiki-ya-hime, and many children, including Hiko-hito among seventeen Kings and Queens. His august mausoleum is at Shinaga in Kafuchi.

Nuna-kura-futo-tama-shiki is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
