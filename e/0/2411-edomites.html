Abstract written by a model

The Edomites are descendants of Esau in Seir's hill country, traced through Eliphaz, Reuel, and Oholibamah's sons, with chiefs listed (Timna, Alvah, Teman, Kenaz and others), ending with chiefs by habitation.

Chapter 36

Esau is named as the father of the Edomites in the hill country of Seir. Their descent is traced through Eliphaz, Reuel, and Oholibamah's sons, with lists of chiefs ending with chiefs according to their habitations in the land of their possession.
