Abstract written by a model

Eleven goats' hair curtains, each thirty by four cubits, form a covering over the tabernacle. They are coupled in sets of five and six, joined by fifty loops on the outer edges and fifty bronze clasps. The sixth curtain is doubled at the tent front, the overhang hangs over back and sides, and rams' skins and sea cow hides go above.

Chapter 26

Eleven goats' hair curtains, each thirty cubits long and four wide, are to be made as a covering over the tabernacle. They are coupled in sets of five and six with fifty loops and fifty bronze clasps, the sixth is doubled over at the front of the tent, and the overhang hangs over the back and sides. Coverings of rams' skins dyed red and sea cow hides go above them.
