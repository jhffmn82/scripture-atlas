burning of the great palace

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

The great palace at Naniha was set on fire by King Sumi-no-ye-no-naka-tsu while Emperor Ri-Chiu was asleep. The fire was still visible from the Pass of Hanifu as the Emperor was carried toward Yamato.
