Heman

Also called: Heman the king’s seer in the words of God

Abstract written by a model

Heman is a Kohathite singer, son of Joel and descendant of Levi, who served in the song ministry alongside Asaph. Chosen and named with Jeduthun, he gave thanks to the LORD with trumpets and cymbals. Called the king's seer in the words of God, he was given fourteen sons and three daughters, who served under him in song in the LORD's house.

Chapter 6

Heman the singer is identified as a Kohathite, the son of Joel and the son of Samuel. His ancestry is traced back through Elkanah, Korah, Izhar, Kohath, and Levi to Israel. He served in the song ministry alongside his brother Asaph.

Chapter 25

Heman is called the king's seer in the words of God, to lift up the horn. God gave him fourteen sons, who are named, and three daughters, and his sons served under his hands in song in the LORD's house under the king's order.
