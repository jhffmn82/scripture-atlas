Chapter 7

Noah's household, consisting of his sons Shem, Ham, and Japheth, his wife, and the three wives of his sons, enters the ship on the same day as Noah. They are included in the LORD's command to come into the ship. They survive the flood with Noah as those who were with him in the ship.
