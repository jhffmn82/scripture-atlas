believing maids

group · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: the believing maids

Abstract written by a model

Believing maids are women who may be married by believers unable to afford free believing women. Their folk must grant permission, and they must receive portions in kindness after honorable marriage. They are expected to be honest rather than debauched or of loose conduct. If they commit lewdness after honorable marriage, they incur half the punishment prescribed for free women. The record places these rules alongside commands against forcibly inheriting or constraining women and for consorting with them in kindness.

Believing maids may be married by those unable to afford free, believing women, with permission of their folk and payment of their portions in kindness. They are to be honest, not debauched or of loose conduct, and if they commit lewdness after honorable marriage, they incur half the punishment prescribed for free women.
