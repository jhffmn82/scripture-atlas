Fargard XX.

Disease was created by Angra Mainyu through witchcraft against mortal bodies. It is commanded to depart, and Ahura Mazda says he drives it away.

Fargard XXII.

The diseases include nine, ninety, nine hundred, nine thousand, and nine times ten thousand diseases. Airyaman drives away diseases, deaths, pain, fever, rottenness, and infection created by Angra Mainyu.
