Chapter 12

In Rehoboam's fifth year, Shishak came with a huge army, took Judah's fortified cities, and reached Jerusalem, because the people had trespassed against the LORD. After the leaders humbled themselves, the LORD limited the outcome: Jerusalem was not destroyed, but Shishak carried off the temple and royal treasures and Solomon's gold shields, and Judah became his servants.
