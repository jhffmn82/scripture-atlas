Cendebaeus

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

Cendebaeus is a military commander appointed chief captain of the sea coast by Antiochus, with infantry and cavalry, and ordered to fight Judea. He comes to Jamnia, invades, takes captives, kills people, and builds up Kidron for raids. John reports to Simon, and the Jewish army meets him across a brook; his forces are routed and flee to Kidron.

Chapter 15

Cendebaeus is appointed chief captain of the sea coast by Antiochus, with infantry and cavalry. He is ordered to fight the people of Judea, and he comes to Jamnia, provokes and invades, takes captives, kills people, and builds up Kidron with cavalry and infantry for raiding.

Chapter 16

Cendebaeus's actions prompted John's report to Simon, and he met the Jewish army with a great force of infantry and cavalry across a brook. His army was put to flight, with many wounded to death and the rest fleeing to the stronghold. He had built Kidron, to which John pursued his forces.
