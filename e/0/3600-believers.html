Abstract written by a model

In The Good News According to Mark, believers are those Jesus promises salvation if baptized, with belief marked by signs including casting out demons, speaking new languages, handling serpents, surviving deadly drink, and healing the sick by laying on hands. In The Gospel According to John, future believers are those who will come to believe through the disciples’ word, for whom Jesus prays that they may be one as the Father and Son are one so the world may believe. In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, believers are everyone who believes, calls on the Lord, receives salvation, gains righteousness through Christ, is not disappointed, and has faith through hearing the preached word from those sent, without Jew-Greek distinction. In 1 Timothy, believers are those who know the truth and receive God’s creation with thanksgiving, are to be addressed by example in word, way of life, love, spirit, faith, and purity, and are especially saved by God, the Savior of all men. In 1 John, believers are those who believe in the name of the Son of God, are born of God, hold testimony in themselves, are assured of eternal life, keep believing, have boldness that God hears requests per his will, know they are of God, and are urged to avoid idols.
