Abstract written by a model

Paul announces his third visit to the Corinthians, saying he is ready to come and will not burden them. He fears finding them unprepared and being humbled by God as he mourns those who sinned. He warned them on his second visit and now writes while absent, so that if he comes again he will not spare sinners, using his authority to build up.

Chapter 12

Paul says this is the third time he is ready to come to the Corinthians and that he will not be a burden to them. He fears that when he comes he will find them not as he wishes, and that God may humble him before them as he mourns over those who have sinned.

Chapter 13

Paul says this will be his third coming to the Corinthians, after warning them during his second visit and now again in writing while absent. If he comes again he will not spare those who have sinned. He writes beforehand so he need not deal sharply when present, since his authority is for building up and not tearing down.
