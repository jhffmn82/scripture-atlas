exemptions from battle

Also called: a new house, a vineyard, fearful and faint-hearted, pledged to be married to a wife

Abstract written by a model

Chapter 20 lists four categories of men who may return home from battle: the owner of an undedicated new house, the planter of a vineyard whose fruit is unused, the man betrothed but not yet married, and the fearful and faint-hearted. Reasons: another would otherwise take the house, fruit or wife, and fear would spread to others.

Chapter 20

Four groups may return home: a man who has built a house and not dedicated it, one who has planted a vineyard and not used its fruit, one pledged to a wife and not yet married, and the fearful and faint-hearted. The reasons given are that another man would otherwise dedicate the house, use the fruit or take the wife, and that a fearful man's fear would melt his brother's heart.
