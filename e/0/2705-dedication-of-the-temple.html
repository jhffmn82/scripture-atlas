The temple's dedication is a joyful ceremony by the children of Israel, priests, Levites and the rest of the captivity. They offer 100 bulls, 200 rams, 400 lambs and 12 male goats for the tribes, then set priests and Levites in their divisions per Moses' book.

Chapter 6

The house is dedicated with joy by the children of Israel, priests, Levites and the rest of the captivity. They offer one hundred bulls, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs and twelve male goats according to the number of the tribes. They set the priests and Levites in their divisions as written in the book of Moses.
