Chapter 21

A man who opens or digs an uncovered pit must make good the loss when a bull or donkey falls in, paying the owner and keeping the dead animal. When one man's bull kills another's, the live bull and the carcass are divided, unless the bull was known to gore, in which case its owner pays bull for bull. A bull killing a servant requires thirty shekels to the master.
