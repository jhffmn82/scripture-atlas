the Truth

topic · 10 works · 8 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Truth, the Truth from our Lord, the truth, the truth thereof, truth

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 6: The Cattle (Al-An'aam), the truth comes to disbelievers but is denied, while Allah’s revelations are affirmed as truth. In Surah 8: The Spoils of War (Al-Anfaal), Allah makes the Truth triumph, brings vanity to naught, and defeats its opponents. In Surah 10: Jonas (Yunus), the Truth comes from Allah, is opposed to error and false claims, and is vindicated by His words. In Surah 17: The Night Journey (Al-Israa), Truth comes and prevails, and the Qur'an is sent down and descended with truth. In Surah 28: The Stories (Al-Qasas), the Truth comes from Allah and is affirmed as belonging to Him, while invented claims fail. In Surah 38: The letter Saad (Saad), the truth is tremendous tidings spoken by Allah that people reject but will come to know. In Surah 40: The Forgiver (Al-Ghaafir), Moses brings the Truth from Allah, which Pharaoh and his people reject. In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), the revelation is the truth, whose manifestation Allah will show through His portents.

and 2 more works

In Surah 46: The Dunes (Al-Ahqaf), the Truth reaches disbelievers through Allah’s clear revelations, which they call magic. In Surah 72: The Jinn (Al-Jinn), the Truth is conveyed by Muhammad as part of his duty from Allah.
