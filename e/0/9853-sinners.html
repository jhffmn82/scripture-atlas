Abstract written by a model

Sinners are a group who, driven by worldly desire, love, hate, passions, vices, and unrest, commit bad deeds and acquire Karman in Samsâra. Their actions lead to misery: they are sent to hell, cannot escape punishment proportionate to their deeds, and suffer pain through beatings, cutting, burning, boiling, piercing, dismemberment, and devouring, for crimes and cruelty inflicted on other beings in former lives.

Fifth Lecture, Called Description of the Hells. / First Chapter. .. Second Chapter.

Sinners who commit bad deeds are sent to hell and undergo beatings, cutting, burning, boiling, piercing, dismemberment, and devouring by animals. They suffer according to the cruelty they committed in former lives.

Eighth Lecture, Called on Exertion.

Sinners are subject to love and hate, do wrong, acquire Karman arising from passions, and commit many sins. Their exertion leads to works and ultimately to misery.
