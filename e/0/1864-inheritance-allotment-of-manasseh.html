inheritance allotment of Manasseh

topic · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Ten parts, inheritance, lot, one lot, one lot only

Abstract written by a model

Manasseh's inheritance allotment comprised Gilead and Bashan beyond the Jordan plus ten parts west of it, with Zelophehad's daughters receiving shares among their father's brothers. Gilead went to the remaining sons of Manasseh. The border ran from Asher to Michmethath before Shechem, down to the brook of Kanah, bounded by Ephraim south, Asher north, Issachar east, and the sea. It also held towns in Issachar and Asher: Beth Shean, Ibleam, Dor, Endor, Taanach, Megiddo.

Chapter 17

Manasseh's allotment included Gilead and Bashan beyond the Jordan plus ten parts west of it, with the daughters of Zelophehad receiving shares among their father's brothers. Its border ran from Asher to Michmethath near Shechem, down to the brook of Kanah, with Ephraim to the south, Asher to the north, Issachar to the east, and the sea as boundary. It also included towns in Issachar and Asher such as Beth Shean, Ibleam, Dor, Endor, Taanach and Megiddo.
