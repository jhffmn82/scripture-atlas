Abstract written by a model

The prince succeeds Duke Ding of Teng. Formerly devoted to horsemanship and sword-exercise rather than learning, he consults Mencius through Ran You after Duke Ding’s death, rejects opposition to observe three years’ mourning, dwells in a shed for five months, and exemplifies grief for officers and ministers.

Chapter 2

After Duke Ding of Teng dies, the prince asks Ran You to consult Mencius about the funeral duties. He decides to observe three years’ mourning despite opposition, then lives in the shed for five months without issuing orders or cautions and sets an example of deep grief at the interment.
