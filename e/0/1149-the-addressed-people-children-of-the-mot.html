Abstract written by a model

The addressed people, children of the mother, are a rebuked group portrayed across two chapters. First, they are told they were sold for their iniquities, with no one answering when the LORD called; those who fear him should obey the servant's voice, while those kindling their own fires will lie down in sorrow. Then they are called offspring of adulterers and prostitutes, children of disobedience and falsehood. Their sins include idol worship, offerings to smooth stones, killing children in valleys, a bed on a high mountain, and ambassadors sent far away, even degrading themselves to Sheol. Though wearied, they never say 'in vain.' The LORD declares their works will not benefit them, and the wind will carry off their idols.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 50

The addressed people are told they were sold for their iniquities, and the LORD asks to which of his creditors he sold them. They are rebuked because no one was there or answered when he came and called. The servant's voice is to be obeyed by those who fear the LORD, and those walking in darkness are urged to trust the LORD's name, while those who kindle their own fires and torches are warned they will lie down in sorrow.

Chapter 57

Addressed as sons of a sorceress and offspring of adulterers and prostitutes, they are rebuked for mocking, and called children of disobedience and falsehood. They practice idol worship among oaks, kill children in valleys, offer to smooth stones, set their bed on a high mountain, and send ambassadors far off, even degrading themselves to Sheol. Though wearied, they never say 'in vain'. The LORD says he will declare their righteousness, their works will not benefit them, and the wind will carry away the idols they gathered.
