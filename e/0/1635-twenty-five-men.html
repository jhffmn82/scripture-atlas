Abstract written by a model

A group of twenty-five men stands at the east gate door of the LORD's house, devising iniquity and giving wicked counsel in the city. They dismiss building houses, calling the city a cauldron and themselves the meat. The LORD declares he will bring them out, deliver them to strangers, and judge them by the sword at Israel's border.

Chapter 11

The twenty-five men stand at the door of the east gate, and are described as those who devise iniquity and give wicked counsel in the city. They say the time is not near to build houses and that the city is the cauldron and they are the meat. The LORD declares they will be brought out, delivered to strangers, and judged by the sword at the border of Israel.
