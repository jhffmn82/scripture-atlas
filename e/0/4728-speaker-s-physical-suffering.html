The speaker's physical suffering, attributed to foolishness and God's indignation, begins with unsound flesh and bones, then reveals loathsome wounds, burning waist, bruising and faintness, ending in failing strength, lost eyesight, daily mourning and constant pain.

Chapter 38

The speaker describes no soundness in his flesh or health in his bones, loathsome and corrupt wounds, burning in his waist, bruising, faintness, and a throbbing heart. His strength fails and the light of his eyes has left him, and he mourns all day, with pain continually before him.
