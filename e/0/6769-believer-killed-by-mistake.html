believer killed by mistake

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a believer, a believer by mistake, the victim

Abstract written by a model

A believer killed by mistake is addressed through required penance for the killer. The killer must free a believing slave and pay blood-money to the slain person’s family, unless the family remits it as charity. If the killer lacks the means, the required penance becomes fasting for two consecutive months. The record also notes that different provisions apply when the victim belongs to a hostile people or a people under covenant.

believer killed by mistake is in one work on this site, Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
