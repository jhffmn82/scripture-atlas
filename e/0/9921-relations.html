Third Lecture, Called the Knowledge of Troubles / First Chapter. .. Fourth Chapter.

Relations try to persuade a monk to return home by lamenting, recalling family duties, offering support, and promising to settle debts. Their attachment can hold him fast and lead him to abandon control.
