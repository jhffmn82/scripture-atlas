Abstract written by a model

In the Yasna, waters are worshipped in rain, pools and vats, vessels, seas, earth, springs, streams, wells, canals, and running or standing forms, and grant blessings to pious sacrificers. In Vendidad (Vendîdâd), water is a holy creation of Mazda governed by ritual separation and cleansing. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Waters flow from Ardvi Sura Anahita across the seven Karshvares and nourish animals, people, and plants.

waters in Yasna

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where waters first appears

Abstract written by a model

Waters are a natural entity worshipped in rain, pools and vats, vessels, seas, earth, springs, streams, wells, canals, and running or standing forms. They are female Ahuras of Ahura, made by Ahura Mazda, Mazda-made, good, maternal, productive, full-flowing, beautiful, and useful for bathing. They nourish the frail and needy before birth, are mother of life, and can reach sickness and misfortune with long arms. Their sounding waves, flood and ebb, and roar mark their power. Worshippers offer them inculcated words and sanctified Zaothras, seeking their favour. The waters grant splendour, glory, health, vigour, possessions, legitimate offspring, long life, and the best life of the Saints to those who sacrifice piously. Yet they are also asked to withhold benefits from evildoers and to come destructively as torments against wicked men.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XXXVIII.

The waters are worshipped in forms that are showered down, stand in pools and vats, and bear forth vessels. They are called female Ahuras of Ahura, beneficent, productive, maternal, full-flowing, useful for bathing, nourishing to the frail and needy, and capable of reaching sickness or misfortune.

Yasna LXIII (Sp. LXII). / Yasna LXIV (Sp. LXIII). / Yasna LXV (Sp. LXIV).

The waters are praised as good, Mazda-made, holy, and purifying. They are called upon to withhold their benefits from evildoers and to come as torments against those who harm settlements, bodies, disciples, or kindred. They are to receive a first petition and sanctified Zaothras delivered according to the ritual taught by the Aêthrapaiti.

Yasna LXVIII (Sp. LXVII).

The waters are worshipped as the sea Vouru-kasha and all waters upon earth, including standing, running, well, spring, rain, and canal waters. They are asked to grant splendour, glory, health, vigour, possessions, offspring, long life, and the best life of the saints.

water in Vendidad (Vendîdâd)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where water first appears

Abstract written by a model

Water is a holy creation of Mazda, governed by ritual separation and cleansing. It must stand thirty paces from the cleansing ground and fifteen from a woman with an issue of blood. Washing proceeds through three earth holes once, twice, then three times; worship drives Pairika and uncleanness away.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Fargard IX.

Water must be thirty paces from the cleansing ground. The unclean one washes at the first hole once, at the second twice, and at the third three times, while avoiding water during seclusion.

Fargard XI.

The water is cleansed by worshipping the waters in the tree, stream, and rain. Pairika and the demon of uncleanness that come upon the water are driven away.

Fargard XVI.

The water must be kept fifteen paces from the woman. Water is also used to wash her by one of three holes dug in the earth.

Waters in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Waters first appears

Abstract written by a model

Waters, created by Ahura Mazda, flow from Ardvi Sura Anahita across the seven Karshvares and rise from sea Vouru-Kasha with Mazda-made Glory. They follow Mazda-appointed paths, maintained and beautified by Fravashis, nourish animals, people, and plants, reach communities, and continue flowing after Angra Mainyu’s malice is destroyed.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XIII. Farvardîn Yast.

The waters flow through paths made by Mazda and appointed by Ahura Mazda and the Amesha-Spentas. The Fravashis maintain the waters, show them beautiful paths, and help them nourish animals, people, and plants.

XXII.

The waters come up from the sea Vouru-Kasha along with the Glory made by Mazda. The Fravashis seek and bring them to their kindred, boroughs, towns, and countries, and the waters of Ahura Mazda continue flowing when Angra Mainyu’s malice is destroyed.
