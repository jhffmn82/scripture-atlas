Chapter 41

In the seventh month, Ishmael and ten men killed Gedaliah with the sword at Mizpah after eating bread with him, and also killed the Jews and Chaldean soldiers present. No one knew of it for two days, and it led to further killings, the capture of Mizpah's people, and the remnant's fear of Chaldean reprisal.
