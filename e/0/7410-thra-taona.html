Thraêtaona

person · 4 works · 21 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Thraêtaona, the heir of the valiant Âthwya clan, Thraêtaona, the Âthwyan, the heir of the valiant Âthwya clan, the strong fiend-smiter, the Âthwyan

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Thraêtaona, born to Âthwya, is the heroic tribe member who smites the three-jawed, triple-headed, six-eyed dragon Dahâka. In Afrinagan, Gahs and Miscellaneous Fragments, Thraêtaona, the Âthwyan, is a saint and holy lord of the ritual order whose saintly Fravashi receives offerings and protects ordered sacrificers. In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, Thraêtaona, the valiant heir of the Âthwya clan, receives a boon from Ardvi Sûra Anâhita and overcomes Azi Dahâka to rescue Savanghavâk and Erenavâk. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Thraêtaona is a holy heir of the valiant Âthwya clan and a victorious man who overcomes Azi Dahâka, rescues Savanghavâk and Erenavâk, seizes the kingly Glory, kills the fiendish Drug, and bears Mazda-made Glory.

Thraêtaona in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Thraêtaona first appears

Abstract written by a model

Thraêtaona is a holy heir of the valiant Âthwya clan and a victorious man. Invoked against itch, fevers, humours, incontinency, and the Serpent’s evil, he rides Vârengana and sacrifices to Ashi Vanguhi at four-cornered Varena. Granted a boon to overcome Azi Dahâka and deliver Savanghavâk and Erenavâk, he seizes the awful kingly Glory after it leaves Yima, kills the fiendish Drug, and bears Mazda-made Glory.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

XXVI.

Thraêtaona, of the Âthwya house, is invoked against itch, hot fever, humours, cold fever, incontinency, and the evil done by the Serpent.

XVII. Ashi Yast.

Thraêtaona offers a sacrifice to Ashi Vanguhi in the four-cornered Varena. He asks to overcome Azi Dahâka and deliver Savanghavâk and Erenavâk, and Ashi Vanguhi grants the boon.

XIX. Zamyâd Yast.

Thraêtaona seizes the Glory the second time it departs from Yima. He is the heir of the valiant Âthwya clan and kills Azi Dahâka.
