taxation

Also called: multitude of taxes, taxes, the tax

Abstract written by a model

In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, taxes are described as multitudinous, consumed by superiors, and causing the people to suffer famine. In Mencius, Book VI (Gaozi), taxation is proposed as a revenue system at one-twentieth of produce.

taxation in Mencius, Book VI (Gaozi)

Mencius · the work’s page · read it where taxation first appears

Abstract written by a model

Taxation is a revenue system proposed at one-twentieth of produce. Bai Gui advocates that rate; Mencius says it suits Mo but not the Middle Kingdom, which needs fuller institutions and Yao and Shun’s arrangements.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Part II / Chapter 1 .. Chapter 13

Bai Gui proposes taking only a twentieth of the produce as taxation. Mencius says that rate may suit Mo because it lacks many institutions and officials, but that the Middle Kingdom requires a fuller system and cannot reduce taxation below the arrangements of Yao and Shun without becoming a great Mo.
