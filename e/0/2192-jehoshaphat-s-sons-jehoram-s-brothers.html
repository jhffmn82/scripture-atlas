Chapter 21

Six sons of Jehoshaphat are named besides Jehoram: Azariah, Jehiel, Zechariah, Azariah, Michael, and Shephatiah. Their father gave them silver, gold, precious things, and fortified cities in Judah. Jehoram killed them all with the sword, and Elijah's letter says they were better than he was.
