Chapter 2

The vast, flowing passion-nature is exceedingly great and strong when nourished by rectitude and left unharmed. It fills all between heaven and earth, is the mate and assistant of righteousness and reason, and is produced by accumulating righteous deeds rather than by incidental acts.
