the true religion

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: religion, the (true) religion, the religion of Abraham

Abstract written by a model

The true religion is Abraham’s upright, non-idolatrous religion, chosen by Allah for his sons and the Children of Israel. Its followers are instructed to remain surrendered to Allah. It has no compulsion: right direction is distinct from error, and rejecting false deities while believing in Allah secures an unbreakable firm handhold.

The true religion is identified with the religion of Abraham, the upright, who was not an idolater. Allah chose it for Abraham’s sons, and they were instructed not to die except as those who had surrendered to Allah.
