the speaker (the Lord GOD's servant)

Also called: his servant, me

Abstract written by a model

The speaker is the Lord GOD's servant, taught and given a tongue to sustain the weary with words. His ear is opened morning by morning, so he is not rebellious and does not turn back. He endures beating, hair-plucking, shame and spitting without hiding. Trusting the Lord GOD as helper, he sets his face like flint, confident he will not be disappointed, and challenges any accuser, since the one who justifies him is near.

Chapter 50

The speaker says the Lord GOD gave him the tongue of the taught to sustain the weary with words, and opened his ear morning by morning so that he was not rebellious and did not turn back. He gave his back to those who beat him and his cheeks to those who plucked out the hair, and did not hide from shame and spitting. He sets his face like flint, trusts that the Lord GOD will help him, and challenges any adversary or accuser to come near, since the one who justifies him is near.
