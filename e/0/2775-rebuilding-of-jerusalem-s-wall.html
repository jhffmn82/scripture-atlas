Abstract written by a model

The rebuilding of Jerusalem's wall is a communal project proposed by the narrator after secretly inspecting the wall. The people resolve to rise up and build, backed by the king's letters and timber and by God of heaven, though Sanballat, Tobiah and Geshem mock it. Work is divided into sections among priests, rulers, Levites, craftsmen and townspeople, including the Fish Gate, circling from the Sheep Gate back to it.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The narrator proposes the project after secretly inspecting the wall, and the people respond, "Let's rise up and build," strengthening their hands for the good work. It is supported by the king's letters and timber but immediately opposed by Sanballat, Tobiah and Geshem, who mock it.

Chapter 3

The wall rebuilding is carried out in assigned sections by priests, rulers, Levites, craftsmen, merchants, families, and townspeople from places such as Jericho, Tekoa, Gibeon, Mizpah, Zanoah, Beth Zur, and Keilah. Many worked across from their own houses, and the gates were built with beams, doors, bolts, and bars. The work went all the way around from the sheep gate and back to it.
