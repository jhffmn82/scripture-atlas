Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, trust in God is expressed by David's resolve to trust God when afraid, knowing God is for him and delivers his soul from death. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, trust in the Lord is a topic in Chapter 2, bringing the Lord's help and an unfailing reward to those who fear him.

trust in God in Psalms

author unknown · undated · the work’s page · read it where trust in God first appears

Abstract written by a model

David states that when he is afraid he will put his trust in God, and repeats that he has put his trust in God and will not be afraid. This trust is expressed in the question of what flesh or man can do to him. It rests on his knowledge that God is for him and on God's deliverance of his soul from death.

Chapter 2

Trust in the Lord brings his help and means one's reward will not fail. The text asks who ever trusted in the Lord and was ashamed, implying that no one was.
