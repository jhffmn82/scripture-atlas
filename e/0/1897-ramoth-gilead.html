Ramoth Gilead

place · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Kings, Ramoth Gilead is claimed by Israel but remains in the hand of the king of Syria, and Ahab and Jehoshaphat fight there, where Ahab dies. In The Second Book of Chronicles, Ramoth Gilead is the battle site where Ahab and Jehoshaphat fight the Syrians, and where Ahab is fatally wounded.
