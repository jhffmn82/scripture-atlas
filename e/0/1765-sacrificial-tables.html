Abstract written by a model

Sacrificial tables are eight tables in the gate porch, four on each side, used for slaughtering burnt, sin and trespass offerings. Four are cut stone, a cubit and a half square and one cubit high, holding slaughter instruments. Hooks a hand width long are fixed within, and offering meat lies on them.

Chapter 40

Two tables stand on each side in the gate porch, with more outside the north entry, making four on each side and eight in all for killing the burnt, sin, and trespass offerings. Four cut stone tables, a cubit and a half square and one cubit high, hold the instruments of slaughter. Hooks a hand width long are fastened within, and the meat of the offering lies on the tables.
