king's delicacies and wine

Also called: delicacies, the king’s delicacies, the wine which he drank

Abstract written by a model

The king appointed a daily portion of his delicacies and the wine he drank for the selected youths for three years. Daniel purposed not to defile himself with them, and the youths who ate them looked less fair and less fat than the four after ten days. The steward then took away the delicacies and wine of the four.
