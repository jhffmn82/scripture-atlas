judgment

Also called: controversies, judging righteously, judgment and justice, just cause, the judgment

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Chronicles, judgment and justice are carried out for the LORD faithfully and without iniquity, partiality, or bribes, with judges warned and instructed to deal courageously. In Psalms, judgment is carried out and prepared by the LORD, who judges righteously and will judge the world and peoples uprightly.

judgment and justice in The Second Book of Chronicles

author unknown · about 424 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where judgment and justice first appears

Abstract written by a model

Judgment is presented as being done for the LORD, in his fear, faithfully and with a perfect heart, free of iniquity, respect of persons, and bribes. Those judging controversies must warn people not to be guilty toward the LORD so that wrath does not come on them and their brothers, and are told to deal courageously, with Amariah the chief priest over the LORD's matters and Levites serving as officers.
