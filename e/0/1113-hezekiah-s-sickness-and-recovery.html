Hezekiah's sickness and recovery

event · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Hezekiah's illness, Hezekiah's sickness (boil), boil, he had been sick, and had recovered, sick, sick and dying, sickness, the boil

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Hezekiah's sickness and recovery is declared fatal, then healed by the LORD after his prayer and treatment with a cake of figs laid on the boil. In The Book of Isaiah, Hezekiah's sickness and recovery is a near-fatal illness reversed by the LORD after Hezekiah prays, with a fig-cake poultice aiding recovery.

Chapter 38

Hezekiah's illness brings him near death, and Isaiah first announces from the LORD that he will die. After his prayer the LORD reverses this, and Hezekiah recovers. A boil is mentioned, and Isaiah prescribes a poultice made from a cake of figs so that he will recover.
