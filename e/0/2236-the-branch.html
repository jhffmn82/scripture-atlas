the Branch

Also called: He, king (the coming King of Zion), my servant, the Branch, the man whose name is the Branch, your King

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Zechariah, the coming King of Zion is portrayed as righteous, lowly, and bearing salvation, arriving riding a donkey's colt; he speaks peace to the nations, and his dominion extends from sea to sea and from the River to the earth's ends. In The Book of Zechariah, the Branch is the LORD's servant, promised with Joshua and his fellows as a sign; he grows up from his place, builds the LORD's temple, bears glory, and sits and rules on his throne as priest, with peace counseled between both roles.

the Branch in The Book of Zechariah

author unknown · 519 to 517 BC · the work’s page · read it where the Branch first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Branch is the LORD's servant, promised in Chapter 3 with Joshua and his fellows as a sign. In Chapter 6 he grows up from his place, builds the LORD's temple, bears glory, and sits and rules on his throne as priest, with peace counseled between both roles.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The Branch is named as the LORD's servant whom the LORD says he will bring out. Joshua and his fellows are described as men who are a sign in connection with this promise.

Chapter 6

The Branch is a man who will grow up out of his place and build the LORD's temple. He will bear the glory, sit and rule on his throne, and be a priest on his throne, with the counsel of peace between them both.
