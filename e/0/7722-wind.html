Also called: The wind, Wind, breath of the universe, the wind

Abstract written by a model

The wind, without form, envies eye, moves quickly, and says that though anyone can point at or kick it, it breaks huge trees and destroys large buildings.

The walrus envies the centipede;

The wind says that anyone who can point at or kick it excels it, but that it can break huge trees and destroy large buildings. It explains that excelling in great things belongs only to the Sages.
