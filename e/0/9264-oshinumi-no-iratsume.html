Oshinumi-no-iratsume

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Princess Ihi-toyo, Queen Ihi-toyo, their aunt

Abstract written by a model

Oshinumi-no-iratsume, also called Princess Ihi-toyo, is the younger sister of Prince Ichinobe-oshiha-wake and aunt of Prince Ohoke and Prince Woke. She is found residing at the palace of Tsunusashi at Takaki in Oshinumi in Kadzuraki.

Oshinumi-no-iratsume is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
