Abstract written by a model

In Sutrakritanga, Book II, Sudharman speaks and recounts to Gambûsvâmin discourses he heard from Mahâvîra, including the Lotus and Knowledge of Food lectures. In Uttaradhyayana Sutra, Sudharman reports that he heard The Exertion in Righteousness discourse from Mahâvîra.
