Abstract written by a model

In Leviticus, the feast of booths is a perpetual seven-day statute in the seventh month, commemorating Israel's shelters after leaving Egypt. In Deuteronomy, the feast of booths is a seven-day festival at the place the LORD chooses, during which the whole household rejoices in the LORD's blessing. In The Book of Nehemiah, the Feast of Booths is a seven-day festival commanded through Moses and kept with an eighth-day solemn assembly. In The Book of Zechariah, the feast of booths is kept yearly by survivors of the nations who go up to worship the King, while nations that fail to attend receive no rain. In The Gospel According to John, the Feast of Booths is the Jewish feast during which Jesus' brothers go up, Jesus teaches in the temple, and he invites the thirsty on its greatest last day.

feast of booths in Leviticus

author unknown · 539 to 331 BC · the work’s page · read it where feast of booths first appears

Abstract written by a model

The feast of booths is a perpetual statute kept in the seventh month. It begins on the fifteenth day with a holy convocation and lasts seven days, during which people gather fruit of majestic trees, palm branches, boughs and willows, rejoice before the LORD, and native-born Israelites dwell in temporary shelters, commemorating Israel's shelters after leaving Egypt. A solemn assembly closes it on the eighth day.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 23

The feast of booths begins on the fifteenth day of the seventh month and lasts seven days, with a holy convocation on the first day and a solemn assembly on the eighth, each with no regular work. The people take the fruit of majestic trees, palm branches, boughs of thick trees, and willows of the brook, and rejoice before the LORD seven days. All native-born Israelites dwell in temporary shelters so their generations will know the LORD had Israel dwell in such shelters when he brought them out of Egypt.

feast of booths in Deuteronomy

author unknown · 624 BC · the work’s page · read it where feast of booths first appears

Abstract written by a model

The feast of booths lasts seven days, following the gathering from the threshing floor and wine press. Held at the place the LORD chooses, the whole household, Levite, foreigner, fatherless, and widow rejoice as the LORD blesses their increase; it is the third feast males attend.

Chapter 16

The feast of booths is kept seven days after the gathering in from the threshing floor and wine press. It is kept at the place the LORD chooses, with all in the household, the Levite, foreigner, fatherless, and widow rejoicing, because the LORD will bless their increase. It is the third of the feasts at which males appear.

The Feast of Booths is a seven-day festival in the seventh month, commanded by the LORD through Moses, requiring Israelites to dwell in temporary booths. Leaders found the command in the law, proclaimed it, and the people kept it with an eighth-day solemn assembly and great gladness.
