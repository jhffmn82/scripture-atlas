Also called: The servant

Abstract written by a model

A servant performs service and is defined as absorbed in love of the master. Pride, quarrelsomeness, and excessive talk prevent pleasing the master. By effacing himself and serving, he obtains honour; by following the master’s wishes, he receives greater honour and double wages. Vying with the master instead brings jealousy, loss of a large salary, and shoe-beating on the mouth.

servant is in one work on this site, Asa ki War (Asa di Var). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Slok XXII .. Pauri XXII

The servant who is proud, quarrelsome, and talkative does not please the master. A servant who effaces himself and performs service obtains honour, while one who follows the master's wishes receives greater honour and double wages.

Slok XXIII .. Pauri XXIII

A servant is called one who is absorbed in the love of the master.
