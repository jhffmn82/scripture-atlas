Chapter 3

The golden image was sixty cubits high and six cubits wide, set up by Nebuchadnezzar in the plain of Dura in the province of Babylon. Its dedication was attended by all the officials of the provinces, and all peoples were commanded to worship it at the sound of music. Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego refused to worship it.
