Abstract written by a model

A man living in the tombs, possessed by an unclean spirit, is so strong that chains and fetters cannot hold him, and he cries out and cuts himself with stones night and day. He runs to Jesus, is freed of the spirit, and is found clothed and in his right mind. Told to go home, he proclaims what the Lord did in Decapolis, and all marvel.

Chapter 5

He lives in the tombs, cannot be bound even with chains, and cries out night and day while cutting himself with stones. He runs to Jesus and bows down, is freed of the unclean spirit, and is then found sitting, clothed, and in his right mind. He begs to go with Jesus but is told to go home and tell what the Lord has done, and he proclaims it in Decapolis, where everyone marvels.
