institutions of religion

topic · 1 work · 16 quoted facts · 10 connections
Also called: Institutions of Religion, institutions established for us

Abstract written by a model

Institutions of religion are declared established, exact, and undeviating. They encompass Ahura Mazda’s associated powers and beings, including Good Mind, Righteousness, the desired Realm, Piety, Weal, Immortality, the Kine, Fire, Sraosha, Rashnu, Mithra, and Wind. They culminate in the good Mazdayasnian Religion and good, pious prayers for blessings and against belying and unbelieving words.

They are declared as established, exact, and undeviating. The unit includes institutions associated with Ahura Mazda, the Good Mind, Asha Vahista, Khshatra-vairya, Bountiful Âramaiti, Weal and Immortality, the Kine, Fire, Sraosha, Rashnu, Mithra, Wind, the good Mazdayasnian Religion, and various prayers.
