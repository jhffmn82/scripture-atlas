Brahmadatta

Also called: Excellent king, king of the Pañkâlas, powerful and glorious king, universal monarch Brahmadatta

Abstract written by a model

Brahmadatta, born of Kulanî in Kâmpilya, is the powerful king of the Pañkâlas with five beautiful palaces. Though knowing the Law, he rejects Kitra’s counsel and clings to sensual pleasures, ending in the deepest hell.

Brahmadatta is in one work on this site, Uttaradhyayana Sutra. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
