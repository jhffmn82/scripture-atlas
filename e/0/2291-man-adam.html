Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, the man is a living soul formed from the dust of the ground, placed in Eden to cultivate and keep it, forbidden to eat from the tree of knowledge, naming the animals, and receiving the woman as his wife. In The Wisdom of Solomon, the one formed first is a man created alone, guarded by wisdom, delivered from his transgression, and given strength to rule over all things.

man (Adam) in Genesis

author unknown · about 449 BC · the work’s page · read it where man (Adam) first appears

Abstract written by a model

The man is a living soul formed from the dust of the ground and placed in the garden of Eden to cultivate and keep it. Forbidden, on penalty of death, to eat from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, he names all livestock, birds and animals, finds no fitting helper among them, and receives the woman, made from his rib, as his wife.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The man was formed from the dust of the ground, became a living soul, and was put in the garden of Eden to cultivate and keep it. He was forbidden to eat from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, named all the livestock, birds, and animals, found no comparable helper among them, and received the woman made from his rib, saying she was bone of his bones and flesh of his flesh.
