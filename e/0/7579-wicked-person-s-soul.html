wicked person's soul

topic · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: soul of the faithless one, soul of the wicked, soul of the wicked man

Abstract written by a model

A wicked person’s soul remains near the skull for three nights after death, singing the Kima Gâtha and tasting suffering. At third-night dawn it encounters snow, stench, and a foul northern wind, then successively enters Evil-Thought Hell, Evil-Word Hell, Evil-Deed Hell, and Endless Darkness.

Yast XXII.

For three nights, the wicked person’s soul rushes and sits near the skull, singing the Kima Gâtha and tasting suffering. At the third-night dawn it encounters snow, stench, and a foul-scented northern wind, then enters the Evil-Thought Hell, Evil-Word Hell, Evil-Deed Hell, and Endless Darkness.
