Onesimus

person · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: my child, my own heart, slave, the faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you

Abstract written by a model

In Paul’s Letter to the Colossians, Onesimus is a faithful and beloved brother, one of the Colossians, sent with Tychicus to report what is happening where Paul is. In Paul’s Letter to Philemon, Onesimus is Paul’s child and own heart, whom Paul sends back to Philemon to be received as a beloved brother rather than a slave.

Abstract written by a model

Onesimus is called Paul's child, whom Paul became father to in his chains. He was once useless to Philemon but is now useful to both Paul and Philemon, and Paul sends him back, describing him as his own heart. Paul asks that Philemon receive him no longer as a slave but as a beloved brother, in the flesh and in the Lord.
