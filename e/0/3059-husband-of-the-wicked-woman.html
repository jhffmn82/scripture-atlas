Abstract written by a model

The husband of the wicked woman is a figure in Chapter 25 who sits among neighbors sighing bitterly at his wife's conduct. A talkative or unhappy-making wife burdens him like a sandy hill for aged feet, leaving weak knees, a saddened face and wounded heart. He is advised not to be ensnared by beauty and to cut off a wife who will not follow his direction.

Chapter 25

He is described as sitting among his neighbors and sighing bitterly when he hears of his wife's conduct. Living with a talkative wife is likened to climbing a sandy hill for aged feet, and a wife who will not make him happy leaves him with hanging hands and weak knees, a saddened countenance, and a wounded heart. He is advised not to be ensnared by a woman's beauty and to cut off a wife who will not follow his direction.
