Rezin

person · 3 works · 6 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Rezin king of Syria, Rezin the king of Syria, the king of Syria

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Rezin, king of Syria, comes up against Jerusalem with Pekah, besieges Ahaz without overcoming him, recovers Elath for Syria, drives out the Jews, and is killed when the king of Assyria takes Damascus. In The Book of Isaiah, Rezin, king of Syria, goes up with Pekah to war against Jerusalem but cannot prevail; his fierce anger is likened to the smoking tails of torches, and he is called the head of Damascus, the head of Syria. In The Second Book of Nephi, Rezin, king and head of Syria, joins Pekah against Jerusalem, and his fierce anger is likened to a smoking firebrand.
