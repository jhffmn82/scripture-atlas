Chapter 16

Each of the 250 rebels and Aaron is told to bring a censer with fire and incense before the LORD. After the fire destroys the rebels, the bronze censers are declared holy because they were offered before the LORD. They are beaten into plates as an altar covering, serving as a sign and memorial. Aaron's censer is later used to make atonement during the plague.
