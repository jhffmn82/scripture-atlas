Antichrist

group · 2 works · 8 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: They, antichrists, anyone, deceivers, he who welcomes him, many deceivers, the Antichrist, the deceiver, the spirit of the Antichrist

Abstract written by a model

In 1 John, Antichrist is a spirit and liar already present in the world, denying that Jesus is the Christ and denying the Father and the Son; many antichrists have arisen, signaling the final hour, and they came out of the readers' community without belonging to it, exposing this by their departure. In 2 John, Antichrist refers to deceivers who have gone out into the world in large numbers and do not confess that Jesus Christ came in the flesh; anyone who welcomes one shares in his evil deeds.
