Abstract written by a model

The psalmist is a servant of the LORD, who holds the LORD as his portion yet sees himself as a stranger on the earth, small and despised. He vows to keep, meditate on, and love the LORD's law, and prays for teaching and revival. Afflicted and persecuted by the proud and wicked, he does not forget the law, rises at midnight, and praises seven times daily. He hates falsehood, befriends those who fear the LORD, and ends confessing he has strayed like a lost sheep, asking to be sought.

Chapter 119

The psalmist vows to keep, meditate on, delight in, and love the LORD's law with his whole heart, and prays repeatedly for teaching, understanding, and revival. He is afflicted, despised, and persecuted by princes and the proud, yet says he does not forget the law, rises at midnight and before dawn, and praises seven times a day. He ends by confessing that he has gone astray like a lost sheep and asking the LORD to seek him.
