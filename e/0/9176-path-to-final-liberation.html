Abstract written by a model

The path to final liberation is a road followed by the wise, hero, and sage through right conduct, control, renunciation, non-killing, desirelessness, knowledge, and freedom from property and worldly ideas. Those who exert themselves cross life’s flood; attachment to sensual objects prevents arrival.

Second Lecture, Called Conquest of the World. / First Lesson. .. Sixth Lesson.

The path to final liberation consists of right conduct, control, renunciation, non-killing, desirelessness, and freedom from property and worldly ideas. The hero and sage follow this path by crossing the flood of life.

Sixth Lecture, Called the Cleaning. / First Lesson. .. Fifth Lesson.

The road to liberation is praised for those who exert themselves, forswear cruelty, and are zealous and endowed with knowledge. Those attached to sensual objects fail to reach it.
