Beatitudes

Also called: Blessed are the gentle, Blessed are the merciful, Blessed are the peacemakers, Blessed are the poor in spirit, Blessed are the pure in heart, Blessed are those who mourn

Abstract written by a model

Verses 3-10 give a series of blessings: the poor in spirit, those who mourn, the gentle, those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, the merciful, the pure in heart, the peacemakers, and the persecuted. Each is paired with a promise, such as comfort, inheriting the earth, mercy, seeing God, and being called children of God. Verses 11-12 extend the blessing to those reproached and persecuted for Jesus's sake, who are told to rejoice because their reward in heaven is great.
