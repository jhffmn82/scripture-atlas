Abstract written by a model

A proud, deceitful man is portrayed as a decoy partridge and a spy seeking weakness. He lies in wait, turning good into evil, blaming praiseworthy things, and plotting bloodshed through many tricks. The reader is warned not to bring such a man into the house, since his wicked schemes could ruin one's reputation.

Chapter 11

The proud man's heart is likened to a decoy partridge in a cage and to a spy who looks for weakness. He lies in wait to turn good into evil, to blame what is praiseworthy, and to shed blood, so the reader is warned not to bring every such man into the house, since he plans wicked things that could ruin one's reputation.
