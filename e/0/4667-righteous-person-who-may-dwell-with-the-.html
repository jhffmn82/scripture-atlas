Abstract written by a model

This person walks blamelessly, does what is right, and speaks truth in his heart. He does not slander, harm his friend, or cast slurs against his fellow man, despises a vile man, honors those who fear the LORD, and keeps an oath even when it hurts. He does not lend money for usury or take a bribe against the innocent, and he will never be shaken.
