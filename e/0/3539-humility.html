humility

topic · 3 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: Humble yourselves, everyone who exalts himself, humble, humility and exaltation, pride and humility, the humble, the proud, whoever humbles himself

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Luke, it is the principle that the self-exalted will be humbled and the self-humbled will be exalted. In The Letter from James, God resists the proud but gives grace to the humble, and readers are told to humble themselves in the Lord's sight so he will exalt them. In 1 Peter, humility is commanded of all, who are to clothe themselves with it, subject themselves to one another, and humble themselves under God's mighty hand so that he may exalt them in due time.
