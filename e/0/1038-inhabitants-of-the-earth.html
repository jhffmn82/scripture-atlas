Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, all the ends of the earth see the salvation of God and are called to make joyful noise, sing praises, and rejoice before the LORD. In The Book of Isaiah, the inhabitants of the earth break the everlasting covenant, transgress laws and statutes, and face judgment under a curse. In Revelation, those who dwell on the earth rejoice over the deaths of the two witnesses and give gifts to one another, while people from the peoples, tribes, languages, and nations watch their bodies and refuse them burial. In The Second Book of Nephi, the inhabitants of the earth will be destroyed when fully ripe in iniquity unless they repent of their wickedness and abominations. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, people of the earth fall through iniquity, many are destroyed for wickedness, and survivors are called to receive Jesus Christ, repent, and convert.

Abstract written by a model

The inhabitants of the earth broke the everlasting covenant, transgressed laws and violated statutes, and are found guilty under a curse. Judgment strikes every class, leaving few after burning; fear, pit and snare prevent escape, joy ends and cities desolate, while a remnant shouts the LORD's majesty.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 24

The inhabitants are scattered and found guilty for transgressing the laws, violating the statutes, and breaking the everlasting covenant. The judgment strikes every class alike, few are left, and fear, the pit, and the snare await them so that none escape. Joy, wine, and music end and cities fall into desolation, though a remnant shouts for the majesty of the LORD and the speaker laments the treacherous.
