vision of the sheet (Peter's vision)

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Peter's vision, This was done three times, a certain container descending to him, like a great sheet let down by four corners on the earth, a trance, a vision, the thing, the vision

Abstract written by a model

While Peter prays on the housetop around noon, he sees heaven opened and a great sheet let down by four corners, holding all kinds of four-footed animals, wild animals, reptiles, and birds. A voice tells him to rise, kill and eat, and says that what God has cleansed he must not call unclean. This happens three times before the sheet is taken up into heaven, and Peter is perplexed until Cornelius's men arrive; he later applies it to people, saying he should call no man unholy or unclean. In a trance at Joppa, Peter saw a great sheet let down from heaven by four corners, containing four-footed animals, wild animals, creeping things, and birds. A voice told him to kill and eat, and after he refused, said not to call unclean what God has cleansed. This happened three times before everything was drawn back up to heaven.
