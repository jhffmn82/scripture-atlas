Abstract written by a model

Sheshbazzar is a prince of Judah entrusted with the LORD's house vessels. Mithredath the treasurer counted them out to him at Cyrus's order, and he brought them up from Babylon to Jerusalem with the captives. Later the elders recall that Cyrus made him governor, told him to place the vessels in the temple, and that he laid the foundations of God's house.

The Book of Ezra / Chapter 1

Sheshbazzar, called the prince of Judah, was given the vessels of the LORD's house, counted out to him by Mithredath the treasurer at Cyrus's order. He brought all of them up to Jerusalem when the captives were brought up from Babylon.

Chapter 5

The elders say Cyrus made Sheshbazzar governor and delivered to him the temple vessels, telling him to place them in the temple in Jerusalem and have God's house built in its place. He came and laid the foundations of God's house.
