Abstract written by a model

The superior man is a man defined by principled conduct and character. He speaks with people when appropriate and refrains when it is inappropriate. He should use sincere, truthful words and honourable, careful actions, continually keeping and practising these principles. He is distressed by his own want of ability, rather than by men’s failure to know him, though he dislikes the thought that his name may not be mentioned after death. He is dignified without wrangling, sociable without partizanship, and correctly firm rather than merely firm.

CHAP. V. 1. Tsze-chang asked how a man should conduct

The man should conduct himself with sincere and truthful words and honourable and careful actions. He should keep these principles constantly before him and practise them.

CHAP. VII. The Master said, 'When a man may be spoken

A man may be someone who should be spoken with or someone who should not be spoken with.

CHAP. XVIII. The Master said, 'The superior man is distressed

The superior man is distressed by his want of ability. He is not distressed by men's not knowing him.

CHAP. XIX. The Master said, 'The superior man dislikes the

The superior man dislikes the thought of his name not being mentioned after his death.

CHAP. XXI. The Master said, 'The superior man is dignified,

The superior man is dignified but does not wrangle. He is sociable but not a partizan.

CHAP. XXXVI. The Master said, 'The superior man is correctly

The superior man is correctly firm and is not firm merely.
