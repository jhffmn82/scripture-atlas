assembly

group · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: assembly (Jesus's assembly), my assembly, the assembly

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Jesus says he will build his assembly on the rock identified with Peter, against which the gates of Hades will not prevail, and promises Peter the keys of the Kingdom of Heaven with binding and releasing on earth bound and released in heaven. In Paul’s Letter to the Ephesians, the assembly is the means by which God's manifold wisdom is made known to principalities and powers in the heavenly places, and God is glorified in it and in Christ Jesus to all generations.
