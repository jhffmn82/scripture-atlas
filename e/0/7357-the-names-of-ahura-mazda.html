I. Ormazd Yast.

Ahura Mazda reveals twenty names, including the One of whom questions are asked, the Herd-giver, Perfect Holiness, Understanding, Knowledge, Weal, AHURA, the Creator, and MAZDA. Reciting and pronouncing these names by day or night is said to protect a person from weapons, fiends, malice, and harm.
