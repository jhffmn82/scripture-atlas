Abstract written by a model

A figure dressed in linen who carries a writer's inkhorn and is counted among six men called executioners. He stands by the bronze altar, is told to mark the foreheads of those in Jerusalem who sigh over abominations, and reports the task done. Then he is sent between the wheels, takes fire from the cherub's hand, and goes out.

Chapter 9

The man clothed in linen, with a writer's inkhorn at his side, comes with the six men and stands beside the bronze altar. He is told to go through Jerusalem and mark the foreheads of those who sigh and cry over the abominations, and he afterward reports that he has done as commanded.

Chapter 10

He is commanded to go in between the whirling wheels, under the cherub, and fill his hands with coals of fire to scatter over the city. He goes in and stands beside a wheel, receives the fire from the cherub's hand, and goes out.
