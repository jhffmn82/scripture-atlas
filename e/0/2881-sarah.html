Abstract written by a model

Sarah is the fair and wise only daughter of Raguel and Edna, living in Ecbatana of Media in the land of her captivity. She has been given to seven husbands, all of whom perished in the bride-chamber, and her father's maidservants reproach her for it. Grieved enough to think of hanging herself, she desists to spare her father sorrow. She prays by the window, claims purity from all sin with man, and asks to be released from the earth or shown pity. Her prayer is heard: she was prepared for Tobias from the beginning, and as a kinswoman whose inheritance falls to him, she is to be his wife. She meets the travelers and brings them into the house, and her father gives her to Tobias. She is led away weeping, comforted by her mother. In the bride-chamber she joins Tobias's prayer with 'Amen' and sleeps through the night alive, to the maidservant's discovery. Raguel then gives her to Tobias with half his goods, tells her to honor her parents-in-law, and Edna commits her to Tobias's care. Arriving near Nineveh, she is welcomed by Tobit as daughter and blessed, ending her story in a new family.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Sarah, daughter of Raguel in Ecbatana of Media, is reproached by her father's maidservants for having had seven husbands die. She grows so grieved that she thinks of hanging herself, but desists because it would bring sorrow on her father, her only parent mentioned. She prays by the window, claims purity, and asks to be released from the earth or shown pity. Her prayer is heard, and she is to be given as wife to Tobias; she comes down from her upper chamber.

Chapter 6

Sarah is Raguel's only daughter, described by the angel as fair and wise, and a kinswoman whose inheritance belongs to Tobias. Tobias has heard she was given to seven men who all perished in the bride-chamber. The angel says she will be given to Tobias as wife that night, that she was prepared for him from the beginning, and that he will save her.

Chapter 7

Sarah meets the travelers, greets them, and brings them into the house. Her father gives her by the hand as wife to Tobias, and she is then led to another chamber, weeping, where her mother comforts her.

Chapter 8

Sarah, called "Sister" by Tobias, is the bride he is brought in to after supper; she is not named in this chapter. She joins his prayer, saying "Amen," and sleeps with him through the night. The maidservant finds her alive and sleeping with him, and Raguel calls her and Tobias the only begotten children of their parents.

Chapter 10

Sarah is given to Tobias by her father Raguel, together with half his goods, servants, cattle and money. Raguel tells her to honor her father-in-law and mother-in-law as her parents and kisses her, and her mother Edna commits her to Tobias in special trust, asking that he not cause her grief.

Chapter 11

Sarah, Tobit's daughter-in-law, arrives near Nineveh and is met by Tobit at the gate. He welcomes her as daughter, blesses her and her father and mother, and blesses God who brought her to them.
