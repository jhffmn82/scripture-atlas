the guilty

group · 3 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: evildoers, guilty, the erring, ye are guilty

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), the guilty do not believe in the revelation until they see the painful doom, and the revelation traverses their hearts. In Surah 32: The Prostration (As-Sajda), the guilty will hang their heads before their Lord, ask to be sent back so they can do right, and be requited by Allah. In Surah 77: The Emissaries (Al-Mursalaat), the guilty are threatened with destruction and woe and told to eat and take their ease only a little.
