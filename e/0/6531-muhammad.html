Muhammad in Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa)

author unknown · undated · the work’s page · read it where Muhammad first appears

Abstract written by a model

Muhammad is Allah’s messenger unto mankind, made a witness against people who disbelieved and disobeyed the messenger. He is to be obeyed together with Allah, and believers in Allah and the Last Day accept his decisions with full submission. He judges disputes and mankind by what Allah shows him through the Scripture revealed with truth. Allah also gives him wisdom, grace, mercy, and teaching of what he did not know. The records present Muhammad’s role in guidance, judgment, worship, and struggle. He arranges worship for believers when among them, is told to judge justly, not to plead for the treacherous, and to seek Allah’s forgiveness. He is told to fight in Allah’s way, urge believers onward, oppose people secretly planning against his instructions, and trust Allah. Although he is charged with these tasks, he bears responsibility only for himself. His authority is linked to Allah’s commands and limits: obeying Allah and His messenger brings believers among those shown favour, while disobeying the messenger and transgressing Allah’s limits leads to Fire and a shameful doom. Muhammad’s position unfolds as that of a recipient of revelation, wisdom, grace, and mercy; a judge guided by the revealed Scripture; a leader directing believers in worship and action; and a messenger whose obedience and judgments are integral to belief and accountability before Allah.
