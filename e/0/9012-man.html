Abstract written by a model

Fourth Lecture, Called Modes of Speech. / First Lesson. .. Second Lesson.

A man should not be addressed with abusive, degrading, or insulting speech if he does not answer when addressed. He may instead be addressed with respectful terms such as long-lived one, layman, pupil, faithful one, or lover of faith.
