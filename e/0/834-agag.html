Chapter 15

Agag, king of the Amalekites, is taken alive by Saul and spared along with the best of the livestock. He comes to Samuel cheerfully, saying the bitterness of death is past. Samuel tells him that as his sword made women childless, so will his mother be childless, and cuts him in pieces before the LORD in Gilgal.
