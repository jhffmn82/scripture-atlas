rich man

Also called: He, mighty man, one who is mightier and richer than yourself, the rich, the rich man

Abstract written by a model

The rich man is portrayed as an exploitative, deceptive figure who wrongs others and threatens when at fault. He uses those who are profitable, drains the owners of resources, smiles falsely, then forsakes and mocks those he has stripped. He preys on the poor, who are an abomination to him. When he falls, many helpers rally; when he speaks, all keep silent and praise him. His riches are good only if sinless.

Chapter 13

The rich man wrongs others and threatens, exploits those who are profitable to him, drains the resources of those who own something, and deceives, smiles at, and then forsakes and mocks those he has stripped. He is portrayed as the natural prey-taker of the poor, to whom the poor are an abomination. When he falls he has many helpers, and when he speaks all keep silent and praise him to the clouds. His riches are called good if they have no sin.
