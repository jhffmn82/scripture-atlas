An old man from the hill country of Ephraim, living in Gibeah, sees the travelers in the street, offers to supply all their needs, and takes them into his house. When the men of the city demand the Levite, he pleads with them not to act wickedly and offers his virgin daughter and the Levite's concubine instead.
