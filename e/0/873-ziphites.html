Abstract written by a model

The Ziphites are residents of the wilderness of Ziph who betray David by reporting his hiding place to Saul at Gibeah and offering to deliver him; Saul blesses them and sends them to confirm.

Chapter 23

The Ziphites go to Saul at Gibeah and report that David is hiding in the strongholds in the woods of the hill of Hachilah, offering to deliver him up. Saul blesses them for their compassion and sends them to learn David's hiding places more certainly.
