people guilty of lewdness

group · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the two of you who are guilty thereof, those of your women who are guilty of lewdness

Abstract written by a model

People guilty of lewdness are subject to testimony and punishment. Women may be confined after four witnesses testify, until death or new legislation provides another way. The guilty may be released or left alone if they repent and improve, while repentance at death is not accepted and disbelievers who die face painful doom.
