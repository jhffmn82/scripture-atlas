Chapter 15

The elder son, in the field, hears music and dancing and learns from a servant that his brother has returned and the fattened calf has been killed. He becomes angry, refuses to go in, and complains to his father that he has served for years and never disobeyed yet never received even a goat, while his brother who devoured the property with prostitutes got the calf. The father answers that he is always with him and that all that is his is the son's.
