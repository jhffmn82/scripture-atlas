worship

Abstract written by a model

Worship is a service of homage and praise, chiefly offered to Ahura Mazda, with praises, eternal names, supplication, raised hands, sacred metric feet, and freely-giving service. It unfolds through wise, self-humbling devotion, drawing near to the eternal ones, and support from Good Mind. Its direction expands to Righteousness, the Gâthas, Praises of the Yasna, good waters, Fravashis and souls of saints, Immortals, Blessedness, and Sraosha, through recitation, chanting, and complete offering. Its results include Blessedness, Piety, well-thought thought, well-spoken word, and well-done deed; it protects settlements and persons and defends against Daêva and evil-minded man.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XLIX, 12-L.

Sacrifice and worship are offered to Ahura Mazda together with the Righteous Order, Good Mind, and Sovereign Authority. Through them, the speaker approaches Ahura Mazda with supplication, raised hands, sacred metric feet, and freely-giving service.

Yasna LI.

Worship is performed through wise, self-humbling devotion, praise, homage, and drawing near to the eternal ones. It is supported by Good Mind and directed toward Ahura Mazda and those who have ever lived and still live.

Yasna LV (Sp. LIV).

Worship is directed toward the divine Righteousness, the Good Mind, the Gâthas, and the Praises of the Yasna, including their recitation, chanting, and complete offering.

Yasna LVI (Sp. LV).

Worship in this unit is directed toward Ahura Mazda, the good waters, the Fravashis of the saints and their souls, the good and bountiful Immortals, Blessedness, and Sraosha.

Yasna LVIII (Sp. LVII).

Worship is offered as a service whose results include Blessedness, Piety, well-thought thought, the word well spoken, and the deed well done. Settlements and persons are entrusted to it for protection, care, guarding, and oversight, and choices are exercised in it.
