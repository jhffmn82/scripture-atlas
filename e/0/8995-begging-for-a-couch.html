Abstract written by a model

is_governed_by four rules for begging for a couch [for avoidance of occasions to sin] When begging for a couch, a monk or nun should specify or inspect the requested couch and address the householder respectfully. If the couch is not obtained under the applicable rule, the mendicant should remain in a squatting or sitting posture for the night.
