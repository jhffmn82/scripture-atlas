Abstract written by a model

In Revelation, worship of God involves the living creatures proclaiming God's holiness and the elders falling down before him, giving him glory, honor, thanks, and power. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, worship of God extends beyond synagogues and weekly observance, teaching cast-out poor people that they can worship despite exclusion.
