Abstract written by a model

A ruler in Israel, coming from Bethlehem Ephrathah, whose origins are from ancient times. He shepherds in the LORD's strength and majesty, with the LORD as his God, becomes great to earth's ends, is peace itself, and delivers the people from Assyria's invasion.

Chapter 5

The ruler in Israel will come out of Bethlehem Ephrathah, and his goings out are from of old, from ancient times. He will stand and shepherd in the strength and majesty of the name of the LORD his God, and will be great to the ends of the earth. He will be peace and deliver the people from the Assyrian.
