Fish heart and liver, kept by Tobias on the angel's order, serve as a remedy against demonic affliction. In Chapter 6 the angel explains that burning them as smoke before an afflicted person makes the affliction flee. In Chapter 8 Tobias lays them on incense ashes in the bride-chamber, and the smell drives the demon to Egypt's uppermost parts.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

The heart and liver are kept by Tobias at the angel's order. The angel says that burning them as smoke before a man or woman troubled by a demon or evil spirit will make the affliction flee. He instructs Tobias to lay some on the ashes of incense in the bride-chamber so the smoke drives off the demon for good.

Chapter 8

Tobias, remembering Raphael's words, puts the heart and liver of the fish on the ashes of the incense and makes smoke with them. The smell drives the demon away into the uppermost parts of Egypt.
