Chapter 39

Potiphar, an Egyptian officer of Pharaoh and captain of the guard, buys Joseph from the Ishmaelites and sees that the LORD is with him. He makes Joseph overseer of his house and entrusts everything to him except his own food. After hearing his wife's accusation, his wrath is kindled and he puts Joseph into the king's prison.
