Chapter 9

Aaron offers a calf as a sin offering for himself and a goat as a sin offering for the people. For the calf, he puts blood on the altar's horns, burns the fat, kidneys, and liver cover on the altar, and burns the meat and skin outside the camp. The people's goat is offered in the same way as the first.
