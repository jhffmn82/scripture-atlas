Chapter 13

Fellowship with the proud, mighty, or rich is warned against through images such as touching pitch, the earthen pot against the kettle, the wolf and the lamb, and the hyena and the dog. Such relationships lead to exploitation, deception, and abandonment. If invited by a mighty man, one should be reserved, neither pressing nor standing far off, not speak as an equal, not trust his many words, and keep secrets carefully. The text says every creature naturally associates with its own kind.
