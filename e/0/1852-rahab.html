Rahab in The Book of Joshua

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Rahab first appears

Abstract written by a model

Rahab is a prostitute living in a house on Jericho's wall who believes the LORD is God in heaven and on earth. She hides two spies under flax on her roof, misleads the king's messengers, and asks the spies to swear to spare her family. She lowers them by a cord and ties a scarlet line in her window. When Jericho is destroyed, she and her father's household are brought out alive and saved, and she lives in the middle of Israel to this day.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Rahab, a prostitute in Jericho, takes in the two spies, hides them under flax on her roof, and misleads the king's messengers by saying they had already left. She declares her belief that the LORD has given Israel the land, asks the spies to swear to spare her family, lowers them by a cord from her window, and ties the scarlet line in the window as agreed.

Chapter 6

Rahab the prostitute is exempted from the devotion to destruction because she hid the messengers Joshua sent. The two spies go into her house and bring out Rahab with her father, mother, brothers, relatives and all she had, as they had sworn to her. Joshua saves her and her father's household alive, and she lives in the middle of Israel to this day.
