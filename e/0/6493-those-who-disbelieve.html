those who disbelieve

group · 2 works · 7 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: disbelievers, evil-living folk, the disbelieving folk, the guilty folk, the losers, those who believe, those who do wrong

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 16: The Bee (An-Nahl), those who disbelieve reject Allah’s revelations, deny the Hereafter, oppose guidance, and face humiliation, sealed hearts and senses, painful doom, the Fire, and loss in the Hereafter. In Surah 46: The Dunes (Al-Ahqaf), those who disbelieve turn away from warnings, reject the Truth, deny Allah’s revelations, face the Fire, lose the good things they squandered, and receive ignominy and doom.
