music and ceremonies

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Music, ceremonies, music

Abstract written by a model

In Chuang Tzŭ, music and ceremonies are important factors in the Confucian system of government, introduced by Sages to rectify its form and associated with the division of the empire; their necessity is questioned when natural instincts and Tao remain intact. In The Analects of Confucius, Book VIII (T'ai-po), Music gives the finish to a person's development, and the finish of the Kwan Tsu is magnificent and filling to the ears.
