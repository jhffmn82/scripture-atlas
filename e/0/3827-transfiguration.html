Chapter 9

Six days after the preceding teaching, Jesus is changed into another form on a high mountain before Peter, James, and John, his clothing becoming glistening, exceedingly white. Elijah and Moses appear and talk with him, and a voice from a cloud calls him the beloved Son. Afterward Jesus commands the three to tell no one until the Son of Man has risen.
