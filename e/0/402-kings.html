Abstract written by a model

Kings are portrayed as rulers who find glory in searching out matters, with hearts unsearchable like heaven and earth. Their thrones are established in righteousness by removing the wicked from their presence. The records end by advising humble approach and patience, since rulers can be persuaded.

Chapter 25

Kings are said to find glory in searching out a matter, and their hearts are called unsearchable like the heavens and the earth. Their thrones are established in righteousness when the wicked are removed from their presence. They are to be approached humbly, since it is better to be invited up than put lower before the prince, and a ruler can be persuaded by patience.
