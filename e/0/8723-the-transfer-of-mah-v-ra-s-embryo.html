Abstract written by a model

On Sakra’s command, Harinegamesi transfers Mahâvîra’s embryo at midnight under Uttaraphalgunî from Devânandâ’s womb to Trisalâ’s, exchanging it with Trisalâ’s embryo.

The transfer of Mahâvîra's embryo occurred on Sakra's command, in the middle of the night under Uttaraphalgunî. Harinegamesi moved it from Devânandâ's womb to Trisalâ's womb and exchanged it with Trisalâ's embryo.
