Fourth Lecture, Called Renunciation of Activity.

A senseless being lacks reason, consciousness, intellect, mind, and speech, yet it may cause pain, grief, damage, harm, and injury. Such beings are regarded as not abstaining from causing these harms and as instrumental in bringing about sins.
