Abstract written by a model

A fervent woman, enamoured of God’s glances, longs for the Beloved and weeps in separation. Unaccepted despite adornment and needing no worldly ornaments, she offers her head and life as sacrifice and service to God.

The fervent woman is enamoured of God's glances and longs for the Beloved. She is not accepted despite her decorations, weeps in separation, wishes to see God in dreams, and offers her head and life in service.
