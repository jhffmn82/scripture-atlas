fighting for the faith

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Fighting for the Faith, fight for religion

Abstract written by a model

Fighting for the faith is described as better than worldly life and prolonged supererogatory prayer. The Prophet instructs armies not to kill old men unable to fight, children, or women, not to steal spoils, and to maintain good relations.
