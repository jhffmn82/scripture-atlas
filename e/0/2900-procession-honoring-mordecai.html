Abstract written by a model

A procession through city streets honoring Mordecai, who rides the king's horse dressed in the king's fine linen robe. Haman devises it believing it is for himself, but the king orders it for Mordecai, and Haman carries it out, proclaiming honor for whomever the king wishes.

Chapter 6

Haman devises the procession, thinking it is for himself: the honored man is dressed in the king's robe, mounted on the horse, and proclaimed through the city streets. The king orders it done for Mordecai, and Haman carries it out, proclaiming that this is what will be done for every man the king wishes to honor.
