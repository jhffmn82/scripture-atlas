the Prophet's wives

group · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: O ye wives of the Prophet, the wives of the Prophet, thy wives, wives, wives of the Prophet, you

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 33: The Clans (Al-Ahzaab), the Prophet's wives are described as mothers of the believers and instructed concerning modesty, speech, residence, prayer, almsgiving, obedience, remembrance, conduct, relatives, access to their dwellings, and interaction from behind a curtain. In Surah 66: The Prohibition (At-Tahrim), the Prophet's wives include one who divulged a confidential fact and two who are urged to repent and warned that Allah may replace them with better wives if the Prophet divorces them.

Abstract written by a model

The Prophet's wives are described as mothers of the believers and are given instructions concerning modesty, speech, residence, prayer, almsgiving, obedience, and remembrance of revelation and wisdom. They may choose Allah, His messenger, and the abode of the Hereafter with an immense reward, or receive worldly provision and a fair release. The text also gives rules concerning their conduct, relatives, access to their dwellings, and interaction from behind a curtain.
