Abstract written by a model

In the Yasna, good Yazads are heavenly and earthly beings fit for sacrifice, homage, propitiation, and praise. In the Yasna, the Bountiful Immortals are holy, bountiful beings approached for propitiation, worship, praise, sacrifice, homage, invocation, dedication, sanctification, and priestly service. In the Yasna, the Bountiful Immortals are good Yazads who rule and dispose of all aright, receive worship and sacrifice, and grant wealth, offspring, and salvation. In Visparad, Bountiful Immortals are holy, good, powerful Yazads, also called Amesha Spenta, who are summoned, invoked, worshipped, and offered sacrifices, homage, praise, and celebrations. In Afrinagan, Gahs and Miscellaneous Fragments, the Bountiful Immortals are holy beings and guardians of the saints who receive praise, propitiation, invocation, and sacrifice.

Bountiful Immortals in Yasna

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Bountiful Immortals first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Bountiful Immortals are holy, bountiful beings: good, beautiful by name, ever-living, ever-helpful, and associated with holy Fravashis described as brilliant, lofty, devoted, and swift. They rule all aright and dispose of all aright, abiding with the Good Mind of the Lord and of His folk. Across the Yasna records, they are repeatedly approached for propitiation, worship, praise, sacrifice, homage, invocation, dedication, sanctification, and priestly service. Offerings made or presented for them include the Myazda-offering, fresh meat, Baresman with Zaothra, Haoma, milk, Hadhânaêpata, the flesh of the speaker’s body or frame, and the blessings of life or of a prospered life. They are summoned with precept, praise, grace-produced delight, and blessings; they are recipients of the Yasna itself and are sacrificed to with the blessing of the good ritual. Their Fravashis are approached with praise alongside all holy Fravashis of the heavenly Yazads. The records conclude by worshipping them with Ahura Mazda as those who rule and dispose of all aright.

Bountiful Immortals in Yasna

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where Bountiful Immortals first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Bountiful Immortals are good Yazads, male and female givers of good things, who rule and dispose of all aright. They include Ahura Mazda and are the entire body of Ahura Mazda’s holy order; Righteousness the Best is identified as one of them. They are ever living and helpful, dwell beside the pious, guard the Fravashis of saints, and save holy men and saintly women. Across the Yasna, they are worshipped alongside Ahura Mazda and receive sacrifice, homage, propitiation, praise, and the entire collection of the Yasna, Haptanghâiti. Sraosha worships them and sits among them as a companion. They are invoked to rule rightly, dispose of all aright, grant wealth and offspring, and bring salvation through their Righteousness. They seek good Yasnas, praises, sacrifices, homage, and a long continued offering of the entire self.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

11 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XXXV.

The Bountiful Immortals are worshipped alongside Ahura Mazda. They are described as ruling and disposing of all things aright.

Yasna XXXVII.

The Bountiful Immortals are worshipped. Righteousness the Best is identified as a Bountiful Immortal.

Yasna XXXIX.

The Bountiful Immortals, both male and female, are worshipped. The males are described as ever living and ever helpful, dwelling beside the pious, and the females are described likewise.

Yasna XLII.

The Bountiful Immortals are worshipped with the entire collection of the Yasna, Haptanghâiti. Sacrifice is also offered to all the Bountiful Immortals.

Yasna LII (Sp. LI).

The Bountiful Immortals are the subjects of sacrifice, homage, propitiation, and praise in the prayer.

Yasna LVII (Sp. LVI).

The Bountiful Immortals are worshipped and praised in connection with Sraosha’s first ritual acts, the Baresman, and the Gâthas. Sraosha sits among them as a companion at their meeting.

Yasna LVIII (Sp. LVII).

The Bountiful Immortals are asked to save holy men and saintly women and to rule and dispose aright. Their Righteousness is invoked as the means of salvation, and their greatness, goodness, and spiritual beauty are rendered through the owner of herds.

Yasna LX (Sp. LIX).

The Bountiful Immortals seek good Yasnas, good praises, and good sacrifices and acts of homage for salvation. Their desired offerings include a long continued offering of the entire self.

Yasna LXIII (Sp. LXII). / Yasna LXIV (Sp. LXIII). / Yasna LXV (Sp. LXIV).

The Bountiful Immortals are addressed as ruling and disposing aright. They are asked to grant wealth and offspring.

Yasna LXX (Sp. LXIX).

They are worshipped with sacrifice as those who rule aright and dispose of all aright.

Yasna LXXI (Sp. LXX).

The Bountiful Immortals are worshipped as guardians of the saints and as the entire body of Ahura Mazda’s holy order.

Bountiful Immortals in Visparad

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Bountiful Immortals first appears

Abstract written by a model

Bountiful Immortals are holy, good, powerful Yazads, also called Amesha Spenta, who rule and dispose of all aright. They are ever-living, ever-helpful, bountiful bestowers of good, named with beauty, and dwell with or arise from Good Mind. Across Visparad, they are summoned by the Zaotar, invoked with Ahura Mazda, and addressed as recipients of sacrifice, homage, propitiation, praise, offerings, preparation, Haomas, and celebrations. They are worshipped according to ritual, with the usta and the saying the Spenta, alongside the shining sun; a chant is performed for them, and they are worshipped as the best.
