Abstract written by a model

Trees are living beings born, originated, and grown in earth. They consume earth, water, fire, wind, plants, and beings; arise because of Karman; and have manifold-coloured bodies. They arise from seeds at tops, roots, knots, or stems, while beings animating them feed on earth-liquid or tree sap.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Third Lecture, Called Knowledge of Food.

Trees arise from seeds generated at their tops, roots, knots, or stems. Beings animating trees feed on the liquid substance of earth or on the sap of other trees, and their bodies have manifold colours, smells, tastes, touches, forms, and arrangements of corporeal particles.
