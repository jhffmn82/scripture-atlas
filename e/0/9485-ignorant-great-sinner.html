ignorant great sinner

person · 1 work · 14 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: An ignorant man, an ignorant, great sinner, the great sinner, the sinner, the villain

Abstract written by a model

An ignorant great sinner commits many harmful acts: killing, lying, robbery, theft, deception for plunder, pursuit of women and pleasures, drinking liquor, and eating meat. Through sinful conduct he longs for and ultimately attains hell. At death, burdened by Karman and grieving, he unwillingly goes to the world of the Asuras, a dark place, or is born in hell.

Seventh Lecture.

The ignorant great sinner kills living beings, tells lies, robs, steals, deceives, pursues women and pleasures, drinks liquor, eats meat, and commits many sins. At death, under the burden of his Karman, he is grieved and falls to the world of the Asuras, to the dark place, or is born in hell.
