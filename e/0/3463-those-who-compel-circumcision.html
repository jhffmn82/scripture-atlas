Abstract written by a model

A group accused of compelling circumcision: they seek a good impression in the flesh and avoid persecution for the cross of Christ, though circumcised themselves they do not keep the law, and they want recipients circumcised to boast in their flesh.

Chapter 6

Those who compel circumcision are said to desire a good impression in the flesh and to do this so they will not be persecuted for the cross of Christ. They are said not to keep the law themselves, and they want the recipients circumcised so they may boast in their flesh.
