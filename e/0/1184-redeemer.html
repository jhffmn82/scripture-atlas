Redeemer

person · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: A Redeemer, Redeemer (Job's Redeemer), my Redeemer

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Job, Redeemer is known by Job to live and ultimately stand upon the earth, with Job expecting to see God in his flesh with the Redeemer on his side. In The Book of Isaiah, Redeemer is promised by the LORD as one who will come to Zion to those in Jacob who turn from disobedience.
