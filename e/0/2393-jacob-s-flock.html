Abstract written by a model

Jacob tells Rachel and Leah that the flock bore speckled and streaked young according to the wages Laban named, and that God has taken Laban's livestock and given it to him. Jacob takes all his livestock, including what he gained in Paddan Aram, when he leaves, and says he bore the losses of torn animals during his years of service.
