Peter’s Second Letter / Chapter 1

Peter recounts that the apostles heard a voice come from the Majestic Glory out of heaven when they were with Jesus on the holy mountain. The voice said, "This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased." Peter cites it as eyewitness proof that their message was not cunningly devised fables.
