Abstract written by a model

Jehoahaz, son of Jehu, was an evil king of Israel who reigned seventeen years in Samaria, following Jeroboam's sins. The LORD delivered Israel to Hazael and Benhadad; he begged the LORD, who listened, though his army stayed small. He was buried in Samaria and succeeded by his son Jehoash.

Chapter 13

Jehoahaz began to reign over Israel in Samaria in the twenty-third year of Joash of Judah and reigned seventeen years. He did evil and followed the sins of Jeroboam, and the LORD delivered Israel into the hands of Hazael and Benhadad. He begged the LORD, who listened, and he was left with only fifty horsemen, ten chariots, and ten thousand footmen. He slept with his fathers, was buried in Samaria, and was succeeded by his son Joash.
