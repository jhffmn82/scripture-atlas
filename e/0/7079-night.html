Abstract written by a model

In Surah 73: The Enshrouded One (Al-Muzzammil), the night is described as a time when impression is more keen and speech more certain, during which the enshrouded one and some of those with him keep vigil while Allah measures the night and the day. In Surah 92: The Night (Al-Lail), night is described as enshrouding.
