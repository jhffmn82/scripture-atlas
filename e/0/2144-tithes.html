Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Malachi, tithes and offerings are withheld from the LORD of Armies, who calls it robbery, and he commands the whole tithe be brought to the storehouse, promising blessing, rebuke of the devourer, and restored pleasantness of Judah and Jerusalem's offering. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, tithes are owed to the Lord of Hosts, withheld as robbery, and must be brought into the storehouse.

Abstract written by a model

Tithes and offerings are withheld from the LORD of Armies, which he calls robbery bringing a curse on the nation. He commands the whole tithe be brought to the storehouse, promising blessing, rebuke of the devourer, and restored pleasantness of Judah and Jerusalem's offering.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The LORD charges that the nation robs him in tithes and offerings, bringing a curse on the whole nation. He commands that the whole tithe be brought into the storehouse so there is food in his house, promising to open the windows of heaven, pour out blessing, and rebuke the devourer so crops and vines do not fail. The offering of Judah and Jerusalem will again be pleasant to him as in ancient years.
