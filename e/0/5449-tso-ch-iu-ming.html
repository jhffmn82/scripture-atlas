Tso Ch'iu-ming

Abstract written by a model

Tso Ch'iu-ming is characterized by shame toward fine words, insinuating appearance, excessive respect, and friendliness that conceals resentment.

Tso Ch'iu-ming is in one work on this site, The Analects of Confucius, Book V (Kung-Ye Ch'Ang). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAP. XXIV. The Master said, 'Fine words, an insinuating

Tso Ch'iu-ming was ashamed of fine words, an insinuating appearance, and excessive respect. He was also ashamed of concealing resentment against a person while appearing friendly.
