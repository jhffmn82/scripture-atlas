Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, seeds fall on four kinds of ground, with different outcomes, eaten, withered, choked, or fruitful, and Jesus explains that they represent the word of the Kingdom sown in the hearts of those who hear. In The Good News According to Mark, seed is eaten by birds on the road, scorched on rocky ground for lack of root, and choked among thorns; on good ground it yields thirty, sixty, or one hundred times as much, and Jesus identifies it with the word sown in people.
