Siddhârtha

Abstract written by a model

Siddhârtha is a Kshatriya king of the Kâsyapa gotra, husband of Trisalâ and father of Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra. After Trisalâ’s fourteen dreams, he consults dream interpreters, honours them, and tells her their prediction of an exceptional son.

Siddhârtha is in one work on this site, Kalpa Sutra. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

When the Kshatriyânî Trisalâ, having seen these fourteen illustrious, great drea

He interprets Trisalâ’s fourteen dreams as foretelling a lovely son who will become a brave king, a universal emperor, or a Gina. He summons the interpreters of dreams, hears their confirmation, honours them, and tells Trisalâ the interpretation.
