Kâmpilya

Also called: town Kâmpilya

Abstract written by a model

Kâmpilya is a town where Brahmadatta is born, Sambhûta and Kitra reunite to recount actions’ rewards, and Sañgaya rules and hunts in Kêsara-park, meeting an ascetic.

Kâmpilya is in one work on this site, Uttaradhyayana Sutra. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Thirteenth Lecture.

Kâmpilya was where Brahmadatta and Kitra met again and told each other the rewards they had realized for their good and bad actions.

Eighteenth Lecture.

Kâmpilya was the town ruled by Sañgaya. Its Kêsara-park was where Sañgaya hunted and encountered the houseless ascetic.
