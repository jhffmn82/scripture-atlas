Chapter 17

After the widow's son stops breathing, Elijah carries him to the upper room, cries to the LORD, and stretches himself on the child three times, asking that his soul come into him again. The LORD listens, the child's soul returns and he revives, and Elijah gives him back to his mother, which leads her to affirm that Elijah is a man of God.
