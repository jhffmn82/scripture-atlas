sacrifice

Abstract written by a model

Sacrifice is offered with self-humbling praise and homage to Ahura Mazda, Righteousness, and guarded settlements. Through it, worshippers seek approach, rewards, and grace; Ahura Mazda receives it and grants requested gifts. Withholding sacrifice brings worse than evil, while the Immortals are asked to accept it.

Yasna XXXIV.

Sacrifice is offered to Ahura Mazda, the Righteousness, and the settlements guarded by the Good Mind. Through sacrifice and praise, the worshipper seeks to approach Ahura Mazda and receive His rewards.

Yasna LI.

Sacrifice is offered to Ahura Mazda's cause with homage and praise. The one who withholds sacrifice receives worse than evil, while the Immortals are asked to accept sacrifice and seek Ahura Mazda's grace.
