Abstract written by a model

Jews who settled in Egypt after leaving Judah, at Migdol, Tahpanhes, Memphis, and Pathros, repeat their ancestors' sins by burning incense to the queen of the sky and refusing Jeremiah's message. The LORD declares they will be consumed by sword, famine, and pestilence, with only a few escapees returning to Judah.

Chapter 44

The Jews living in Egypt, at Migdol, Tahpanhes, Memphis, and Pathros, are accused of repeating the sins of their fathers and kings. They refuse to listen to Jeremiah and insist on continuing to worship the queen of the sky, and the LORD says they will be consumed by sword and famine, with only a few escapees returning to Judah.
