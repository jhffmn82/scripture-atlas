Abstract written by a model

Demons are unclean spirits who recognize Jesus as the Christ and Son of God, and obey his authoritative commands. In Chapter 4 they cry out and are rebuked into silence. In Chapter 8 a multitude begs not to be sent to the abyss; Jesus lets them enter pigs, which drown in the lake.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

Demons come out of many people, crying out that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God. Jesus rebukes them and does not allow them to speak, because they knew he was the Christ. The onlookers marvel that he commands unclean spirits with authority and power and they obey.

Chapter 8

The many demons in the man call Jesus Son of the Most High God and beg not to be sent into the abyss. Jesus allows them to enter a herd of pigs, which rush down the steep bank into the lake and drown.
