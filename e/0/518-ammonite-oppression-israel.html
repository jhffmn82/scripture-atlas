Abstract written by a model

A war waged by the Ammonites against Israel. Because of the LORD's anger, Israel was sold to the Philistines and Ammonites, who oppressed them eighteen years, especially in Gilead, and crossed the Jordan against Judah, Benjamin and Ephraim. Israel cried out. Ammon then claimed land from the Arnon to the Jabbok; after failed diplomacy, Jephthah advanced and the LORD delivered Ammon to him, subjugating it.

Chapter 10

Because of the LORD's anger, Israel was sold into the hands of the Philistines and Ammonites, who oppressed them for eighteen years, especially those beyond the Jordan in Gilead. The Ammonites crossed the Jordan to fight Judah, Benjamin and Ephraim, leaving Israel very distressed. The oppression led Israel to cry to the LORD and ended with the Ammonites encamped in Gilead and Israel at Mizpah.

Chapter 11

The war begins when the children of Ammon make war on Israel, claiming land from the Arnon to the Jabbok and the Jordan. After diplomatic exchanges fail, Jephthah crosses Gilead, Manasseh, and Mizpah to reach Ammon, and the LORD delivers Ammon to him, resulting in a great slaughter and Ammon's subjugation.
