Chapter 23

The set feasts are defined as the LORD's own appointed times, to be proclaimed as holy convocations in their appointed season. The unit lists them in order: the Sabbath, Passover, unleavened bread, the first fruits and the fifty-day feast, trumpets, atonement, and booths. Each is to be kept with its own offerings in addition to the Sabbaths, gifts, vows, and free will offerings.
