Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Fravashi of Zarathustra Spitâma is propitiated with praise or a Yast, receives offerings of Haoma and Haoma-juice, and is explicitly worshipped. In Visparad, Fravashi of Zarathustra Spitâma receives announcement in the celebration for sacrifice, homage, propitiation, and praise.

Fravashi of Zarathustra Spitâma in Yasna

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Fravashi of Zarathustra Spitâma first appears

Abstract written by a model

Fravashi of Zarathustra Spitâma is a holy, saintly Fravashi belonging to Zarathustra Spitâma, identified with him and worshipped as holy. Across the records, it is propitiated with praise or a Yast, receives offerings of Haoma and Haoma-juice, and is finally explicitly worshipped.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna III.

The Fravashi of Spitâma Zarathustra is the object of propitiation when Haoma and Parahaoma, and later Haoma and Haoma-juice, are approached with praise or a Yast.

Yasna IV.

The holy Fravashi of Zarathustra Spitâma receives offerings and is identified as a saint.

Yasna VII.

The Fravashi of Zarathustra Spitâma receives the offering of Haoma and Haoma-juice and is identified with the saint Zarathustra Spitâma.

Yasna XIII (Sp. XIV).

The holy Fravashi of Zarathustra Spitâma is worshipped.
