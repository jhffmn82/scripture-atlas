virtue

Also called: his virtue, one's virtue

Abstract written by a model

Virtue is exalted by treating faithfulness, sincerity, and continual movement toward what is right as first principles. It is also exalted when doing what is to be done comes before success. The superior man’s virtue is further helped through friendship with his friends.
