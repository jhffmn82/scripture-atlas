Abstract written by a model

The potter is a maker of clay vessels who kneads earth and shapes them for clean and contrary uses, judging each one's purpose. He also molds a vain god from the same clay to an evil end. Though made of earth and soon to return the lent soul, he is ignorant of his Maker, has a heart of ashes, treats life as a profit game, and sins beyond all others.

Chapter 15

The potter kneads soft earth and molds vessels for clean and contrary uses from the same clay, and he alone judges what each is for. He also labors to an evil end by molding a vain god from clay, though he was himself made of earth and must soon give back the soul lent to him. He is said to have a heart of ashes, to be ignorant of his Maker, to treat life as a game for profit, and to know he sins more than anyone else.
