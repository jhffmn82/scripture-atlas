Chapter 16

Abiram, son of Eliab of Reuben, joins Korah's rebellion and, with Dathan, refuses to come when Moses summons him. He complains that Moses has not brought them into a land with fields and vineyards as an inheritance. He and his household are swallowed alive by the earth into Sheol.
