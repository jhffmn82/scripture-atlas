Abstract written by a model

The angel who talks with Zechariah is the interpreter of his visions. In Chapter 1 he promises to show what the horses are, receives kind and comforting words from the LORD, tells Zechariah to proclaim the LORD's jealousy for Jerusalem and Zion, and explains the four horns and craftsmen. In Chapter 2 he goes out and is met by another angel, who sends word about Jerusalem. In Chapter 5 he explains the ephah basket, names the woman Wickedness, confines her under the lead weight, and says the basket goes to Shinar. In Chapter 6 he explains the chariots as the four winds.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

This angel tells Zechariah he will show him what the horses are, and he is answered by the LORD with kind and comforting words. He instructs Zechariah to proclaim the LORD's jealousy for Jerusalem and Zion, and explains the four horns and the four craftsmen.

Chapter 2

The angel who talked with the narrator goes out, and another angel goes out to meet him. The other angel then tells him to run and speak to the young man with the message about Jerusalem.

Chapter 5

The angel asks the narrator what he sees, tells him to look at the appearing ephah basket, and explains what it is. He identifies the woman as Wickedness, throws her into the basket, and throws the lead weight on its mouth. He also tells the narrator that the basket is being carried to Shinar.

Chapter 6

The angel answers the narrator's question about the chariots, explaining that they are the four winds of the sky that go out from standing before the Lord of all the earth. He also tells the narrator that those who go toward the north country have quieted his spirit there.
