people who deceive themselves

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a party of them, them, they

Abstract written by a model

People who deceive themselves are treacherous and sinful, hide from men, hold secret nighttime conferences displeasing to Allah, receive worldly defense from men, and are not harmed by Muhammad. They conceal themselves from people but not from Allah.
