repose

topic · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: mental equilibrium, perfect repose, stillness, tranquillity

Abstract written by a model

Repose is a perfect, unchanging level of the universe and the Sage’s mental equilibrium, not ordinary inactivity. Established inwardly, it produces movement, attainment, inaction, potential action, happiness, long life, wisdom, and knowledge. With passivity it excludes sorrow, anxiety, foul influences, plans, and worldly disturbance; through it, ancients cultivated TAO, developing harmony and order.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIII.

Repose is the mental equilibrium of the Sage and is not ordinary inactivity. It gives rise to movement, attainment, inaction, potential action, happiness, and long life.

CHAPTER XV.

Repose accompanies passivity and excludes sorrow, anxiety, and foul influences. In repose, the Sage shares the passivity of the Yin and remains free from plans and worldly disturbance.

CHAPTER XVI.

Repose is the condition through which the ancients cultivated TAO and begat knowledge. Knowledge and repose mutually produced each other, developing harmony and order.
