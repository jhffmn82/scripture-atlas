Chapter 66

The psalmist vows to come into God's temple with burnt offerings and pay the vows he made with his lips when in distress. He invites all who fear God to hear what God has done for his soul, saying he cried to God and that God listened and heard his prayer. He says that if he had cherished sin in his heart, the Lord would not have listened, and he ends by blessing God.
