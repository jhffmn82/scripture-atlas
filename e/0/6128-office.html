office

Abstract written by a model

Office is sought only in poverty and under low circumstances. It is appropriate when government is good, principles can be practiced, reception is proper, or the state gives support. For scholars, serving while principles are ignored is shameful. Regular officeholders, including gatekeepers and watchmen, may receive princely support; Confucius answered an official summons immediately.

Part II / Chapter 1 .. Chapter 5

Office is appropriate when government is good, when principles may be practiced, when reception is proper, or when the state provides support. Holding office while one's principles are ignored is described as shameful for a scholar.

Chapter 6 .. Chapter 9

People with regular offices may receive support from the prince, including keepers of the gates and watchmen. Confucius was in office when he went immediately after being summoned on the business of his office.
