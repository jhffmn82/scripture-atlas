Pharaoh's dream of the cattle

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: dream, dreams, seven cattle, seven other cattle, the seven sleek and fat cattle, the ugly and thin cattle

Abstract written by a model

symbolizes seven years of plenty and famine [seven good cattle are plenty, seven thin cattle are famine] In this dream Pharaoh stands by the river and sees seven sleek, fat cattle come up and feed in the marsh grass, followed by seven ugly, thin cattle that eat them up and remain as ugly as before. Joseph explains that the seven good cattle are seven years of plenty and the seven thin cattle are seven years of famine.
