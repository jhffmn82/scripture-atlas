Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, Death is described as the shepherd of the foolish, who are appointed as a flock for Sheol. In Revelation, Death rides the pale horse, follows Hades, and is given authority over one fourth of the earth to kill by sword, famine, death, and wild animals.
