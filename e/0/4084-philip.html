Philip

Also called: Philip the evangelist, this man

Abstract written by a model

Philip is an evangelist and one of the seven. He goes down to Samaria, preaching Christ and performing signs and miracles, then, guided by an angel and the Spirit, explains Isaiah to an Ethiopian eunuch and baptizes him. Caught away by the Spirit, he appears at Azotus, preaches onward to Caesarea, and has four virgin daughters.

Chapter 8

Philip went down to Samaria, proclaimed Christ, and did signs, including casting out unclean spirits and healing the paralyzed and lame. Guided by an angel and the Spirit, he met the Ethiopian eunuch, explained Isaiah to him starting from the Scripture he was reading, and baptized him. The Spirit then caught him away, and he was found at Azotus, preaching in the cities until he came to Caesarea.
