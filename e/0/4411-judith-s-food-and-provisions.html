Judith's food and provisions

Abstract written by a model

Judith declines Holofernes' delicacies and wine, saying she cannot eat them lest there be an occasion of stumbling, and that provision will be made from what came with her. When Holofernes asks what will happen when it runs out, she says it will not be used up until the Lord works by her hand. She eats her food toward evening each day and at the feast eats and drinks what her servant prepared.
