partners ascribed unto Allah in Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where partners ascribed unto Allah first appears

Abstract written by a model

Ascribing partners to Allah is presented as a tremendous sin and a grave error. It is not pardoned by Allah, except insofar as He wills, while other sins may be pardoned. The account first states that those who ascribe partners have invented a tremendous sin. It then reiterates that such ascription leads one far astray, contrasting this offense with wrongdoing Allah may otherwise forgive.
