Abstract written by a model

The fool is a figure of self-destructive folly: he demolishes his own house, earns a rod through his speech, mocks atonement, and is hot-headed, reckless, and deceitful. Chapter 14 concludes by advising people to avoid him, since his lips hold no knowledge.

Chapter 14

The fool tears down his house with his own hands, brings a rod to his back by his talk, and mocks atonement for sins. He is hot headed, reckless, and quick-tempered, and his folly is deceit. The text advises staying away from him because no knowledge is found on his lips.
