Chapter 51

The speaker thanks the Lord for delivering him from death, a slanderous tongue before the king, and enemies, after finding no human help and then remembering the Lord's mercy and praying. He recounts seeking wisdom from his youth, praying for her before the temple, and gaining a good possession and much rest. He then invites the uneducated to receive instruction and rejoice in the Lord's mercy.
