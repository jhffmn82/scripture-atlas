Abstract written by a model

Ishbosheth, a son of Saul, was made king over Israel by Abner at Mahanaim at age forty and reigned two years. He feared Abner and could not answer his furious reply about Rizpah, and he complied with David's demand to take Michal from Paltiel. After hearing Abner was dead, he grew feeble-handed. His captains Rechab and Baanah killed and beheaded him on his bed at midday, and his head was buried in Abner's grave in Hebron.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Ishbosheth, Saul's son, was taken by Abner to Mahanaim and made king over Gilead, the Ashurites, Jezreel, Ephraim, Benjamin, and all Israel. He was forty years old when he began to reign and reigned two years.

Chapter 3

Ishbosheth, Saul's son, asks Abner why he went in to Saul's concubine Rizpah, and is unable to answer Abner's furious reply because he fears him. He complies with David's demand and takes Michal from her husband Paltiel.

Chapter 4

Ishbosheth, Saul's son, grows feeble-handed when he hears Abner is dead in Hebron. He is struck, killed and beheaded on his bed at midday in his own house by Rechab and Baanah, and his head is later buried in Abner's grave in Hebron.
