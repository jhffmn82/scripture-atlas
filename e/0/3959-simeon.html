Chapter 2

Simeon is a righteous and devout man in Jerusalem, looking for the consolation of Israel, with the Holy Spirit on him. It had been revealed to him that he would not see death before seeing the Lord's Christ. He comes into the temple in the Spirit, takes Jesus in his arms, blesses God, says his eyes have seen God's salvation, and tells Mary the child is appointed for the falling and rising of many in Israel and that a sword will pierce her soul.
