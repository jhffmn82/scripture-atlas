Abstract written by a model

In 1 John, the little children are forgiven, know the Father, have anointing and knowledge, overcome the evil one, reject the world's love, and remain in Christ. In 1 John, the children of God are believers who are born of God, walk in the light, do righteousness, love their brothers, test the spirits, and keep God's commandments. In Jude, the recipients are called, sanctified by God, kept for Jesus Christ, and instructed to remember the apostles' words, build up their faith, pray, remain in God's love, and show compassion. In The Fourth Book of Nephi, the people who were united and free from robbers, murderers, and divisions were called the children of Christ and heirs to the kingdom of God. (The Fourth Book of Nephi, Who Is the Son of Nephi, One of the Disciples of Jesus Christ)

children of God in 1 John

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where children of God first appears

Abstract written by a model

The children of God are believers addressed across the records of 1 John. They are called children by the Father's love, born of God, and do not commit sin because his seed remains in them. The account begins with the authors offering fellowship with the Father and the Son and the message that God is light, contrasting walking in light with darkness. It then says they are now God's children and will be like him when he is revealed, are known by doing righteousness and loving their brothers, and are not known by the world. Next they are told to test the spirits, having overcome false prophets because the one in them is greater, and to love one another, remaining in God through his Spirit with boldness for judgment. It ends by saying they are known by loving God and keeping his commandments, and the evil one does not touch them.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

1 John / Chapter 1

The readers are addressed by the authors, who declare what they have seen and heard so that the readers may share fellowship with them and with the Father and the Son. They are given the message that God is light and are shown the conditions of walking in light versus darkness.

Chapter 3

Believers are now children of God, though what they will be is not yet revealed; when he is revealed they will be like him, seeing him as he is. They are recognized by doing righteousness and loving their brothers, they are born of God and do not commit sin, and the world does not know them.

Chapter 4

The readers are told to test the spirits, and are assured that they are of God and have overcome the false prophets because greater is he who is in them than he who is in the world. They are urged to love one another, and the author says that he who knows God listens to the authors, and that believers remain in God and he in them through his Spirit, with boldness for the day of judgment.

Chapter 5

The children of God are known by loving God and keeping his commandments. Whoever loves the Father also loves the child born of him. Whoever is born of God doesn't sin but keeps himself, and the evil one doesn't touch him.
