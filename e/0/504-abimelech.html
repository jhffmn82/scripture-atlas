Abimelech

Abstract written by a model

Abimelech is a son of Gideon (Jerubbaal) by a female servant. Backed by Shechem, he hires reckless men with silver from Baal Berith's house, kills his seventy brothers, and is made king. After three years Shechem betrays him; he destroys it, then attacks Thebez, where a woman's millstone fatally wounds him and he has his armor bearer finish him.

Abimelech is in one work on this site, The Book of Judges. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 9

Abimelech, Jerubbaal's son by a female servant, gains Shechem's support, hires reckless men, kills his seventy brothers, and is made king. After three years Shechem betrays him; he defeats Gaal, destroys Shechem and burns its tower's people, then attacks Thebez. A woman's millstone breaks his skull and he has his armor bearer kill him so it won't be said a woman killed him.
