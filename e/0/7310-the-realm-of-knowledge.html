the realm of knowledge

place · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: divine knowledge

Abstract written by a model

The realm of knowledge is a realm and a tool in spiritual practice. Its resplendent divine knowledge matters most, alongside fashioned knowledge, wisdom, intellect, understanding, and supernatural skill. It begins with light and songs yielding joys, moves through incomparable beauty and fashioning, and ends beyond description.
