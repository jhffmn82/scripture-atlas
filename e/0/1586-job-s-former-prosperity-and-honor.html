Chapter 29

Job recalls a time when his steps were washed with butter and the rock poured out streams of oil for him. He had a seat at the city gate, esteem from all ranks, and the blessing of those he helped. He expected his glory to stay fresh and his bow to be renewed, his root spread to the waters, and his days to be many as the sand.
