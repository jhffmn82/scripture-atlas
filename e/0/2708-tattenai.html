Abstract written by a model

Tattenai is a governor beyond the River who, with Shetharbozenai and their companions, questions the Jews about who authorized their building and the builders' names, then writes to Darius asking for a search for Cyrus's decree. Darius's decree orders him to stay away and fund the elders, and he complies diligently.

Chapter 5

Tattenai, the governor beyond the River, comes with Shetharbozenai and their companions to ask the Jews who authorized the building and for the names of the builders. He sends a letter to Darius reporting what he saw and heard, and asks that a search be made for Cyrus's decree.

Chapter 6

Tattenai, governor beyond the River, is told in the decree to stay far away and leave the work alone, and to give expenses to the Jewish elders. He and Shetharbozenai and their companions comply with all diligence because Darius had sent the decree.
