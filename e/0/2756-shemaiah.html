Chapter 6

Shemaiah, son of Delaiah son of Mehetabel, is shut in at his home when Nehemiah visits him. He urges Nehemiah to meet in the temple and shut its doors because assassins will come at night. Nehemiah refuses and discerns that God had not sent him and that Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him.
