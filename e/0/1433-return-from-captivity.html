return from captivity

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: bring you again to the place from where I caused you to be carried away captive, causing you to return to this place, return from Babylonian captivity, the captivity, turn again your captivity

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Ezra, it is the return of those carried away by Nebuchadnezzar from Babylon to Jerusalem and Judah, ending with the people settled in their cities. In The Book of Jeremiah, it is promised by the LORD after seventy years, involving gathering the exiles and bringing them back to the place from which they were carried away.

return from Babylonian captivity in The Book of Ezra

author unknown · 399 to 299 BC · the work’s page · read it where return from Babylonian captivity first appears

Abstract written by a model

Those whom Nebuchadnezzar had carried away to Babylon went up out of captivity and returned to Jerusalem and Judah, each to his own city, with Zerubbabel and other leaders. Some who came from Tel Melah, Tel Harsha, Cherub, Addan, and Immer could not show their fathers' houses or prove they were of Israel. The return ends with the people settled in their cities.
