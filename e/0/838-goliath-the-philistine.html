Abstract written by a model

Goliath of Gath is a towering Philistine champion, six cubits and a span tall, armed with bronze helmet, mail, shin armor, javelin, and an iron-headed spear, with a shield bearer. He challenges Israel for forty days to send a man to fight, the loser's side becoming servants, and curses David. David strikes his forehead with a stone, then beheads him with his own sword.

Chapter 17

Goliath of Gath is a Philistine champion six cubits and a span tall, armed with a bronze helmet, coat of mail, shin armor, javelin, and a spear with an iron head of six hundred shekels. He challenges Israel for forty days to send a man to fight, with the loser's side becoming servants, and he curses and disdains David. David strikes him in the forehead with a stone, and then kills and beheads him with his own sword.
