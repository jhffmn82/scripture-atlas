atonement

Also called: atone, atoned, full atonement, penalty, trespass

Abstract written by a model

Atonement is required for contract-breaking, certain repeated assaults, confessed sin, and a corpse-toucher who encounters water or trees. Contract penalties increase from three hundred-fold for a word-contract to a thousand-fold for a field-contract; unatoned or refused repeated assaults bring Peshôtanu status and stripes. Confession under Mazda’s law can effect full atonement, while voluntary seed emission by those taught the law has none. The corpse-toucher’s atonement is eight hundred stripes split between two instruments.

Fargard IV.

A broken contract requires an atonement corresponding to its level, from three hundred-fold for the word-contract through a thousand-fold for the field-contract. Repeated assaults committed without atoning for preceding offenses, or committed with refusal to atone, result in Peshôtanu status and two hundred stripes with each instrument.

Fargard VIII.

There is no atonement for a man who voluntarily emits his seed when he is a professor of the law of Mazda or has been taught in it. For one who confesses sin under the law of Mazda, a full atonement is effected by means of that law.

VIII.

Atonement is required when the man who touched a corpse finds water or trees on his way. The stated atonement is four hundred stripes with the Aspahê-astra and four hundred stripes with the Sraoshô-karana.
