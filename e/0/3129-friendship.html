Abstract written by a model

In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, friendship is counseled to grow through sweet words and a gracious tongue, with friends gained by testing and guided by fear of the Lord. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XII (Yen Yuan), friendship helps virtue through faithful admonition and skillful guidance, ending when a friend proves impracticable. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XVI (Ke She), friendship is advantageous with the upright, sincere, and observant, but injurious with those of specious airs, insinuating softness, and glib speech.

Abstract written by a model

Friendship is counseled to grow through sweet words and a gracious tongue, which multiply friends; peaceful relations should be many, advisers rare, and friends gained by testing, not hurried trust, directed by fear of the Lord.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Friendship is advised to be built through sweet words and a gracious tongue, which multiply friends and courtesies. Peaceful relations should be many but advisers one in a thousand, and a friend should be gained in a time of testing without hurried trust. Whoever fears the Lord directs friendship properly.
