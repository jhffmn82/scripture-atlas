Nan Po Tzŭ K'uei

Mêng Sun Ts'ai mourns his mother's death without snivelling, heartfelt grief, or howling, yet he is considered the best mourner in the State of Lu. Confucius explains that he follows outward requirements while remaining inwardly free from disturbance and accepts life and death as natural transitions.
