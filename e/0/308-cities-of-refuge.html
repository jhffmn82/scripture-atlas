Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, six cities of refuge among the Levitical cities shelter Israelites, strangers, and foreigners who kill unintentionally until they stand before the congregation for judgment. In Deuteronomy, three cities of refuge are set apart for a man who kills his neighbor unintentionally to flee and live, with three more added if the border is enlarged. In The Book of Joshua, the cities of refuge are six designated places where a person who kills unintentionally can flee, and their elders hear the fugitive's case, take him in, and give him a place to live.

three cities of refuge in Deuteronomy

author unknown · 624 BC · the work’s page · read it where three cities of refuge first appears

Abstract written by a model

Three cities of refuge are set apart so that a man who kills his neighbor unintentionally, without prior hatred, can flee there and live, preventing innocent blood from being shed. Chapter 4 names them beyond the Jordan: Bezer for the Reubenites, Ramoth for the Gadites, Golan for the Manassites. Chapter 19 sets three cities in the middle of the land, with roads prepared and the land divided in three, and adds three more if the border is enlarged.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

Moses sets apart three cities beyond the Jordan toward the sunrise so that a man who kills his neighbor unintentionally, without prior hatred, can flee there and live. They are Bezer in the wilderness for the Reubenites, Ramoth in Gilead for the Gadites, and Golan in Bashan for the Manassites.

Chapter 19

Three cities are to be set apart in the middle of the land, with roads prepared and the land divided into three parts so every man slayer can flee to one. Three more are to be added if the border is enlarged, so that innocent blood is not shed and blood guilt does not fall on the people.

cities of refuge (for the man slayer) in The Book of Joshua

author unknown · about 600 BC · the work’s page · read it where cities of refuge (for the man slayer) first appears

Abstract written by a model

The cities of refuge are six designated places, previously commanded through Moses, where a person who kills unintentionally can flee from the avenger of blood. They are Kedesh in Naphtali, Shechem in Ephraim, Kiriath Arba (Hebron) in Judah, Bezer from Reuben, Ramoth in Gilead from Gad, and Golan in Bashan from Manasseh. They serve all the children of Israel and the alien living among them. Their elders hear the fugitive's case, take him in, and give him a place to live.
