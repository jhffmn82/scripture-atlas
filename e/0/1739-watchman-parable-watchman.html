Chapter 33

In the parable, the people of a land set a man as watchman who, on seeing the sword come, should blow the trumpet. If he warns and people ignore it, their blood is on their own heads; if he fails to warn, the LORD will require the blood at his hand. Ezekiel is told he is such a watchman for Israel.
