Chapter 42

When Job prays for his friends, the LORD restores his prosperity and gives him twice as much as before. He has fourteen thousand sheep, six thousand camels, a thousand yoke of oxen, a thousand female donkeys, seven sons and three daughters. His relatives and acquaintances eat with him, comfort him, and each give him a piece of money and a gold ring.
