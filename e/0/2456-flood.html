Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, the flood is a catastrophic deluge brought by God to destroy all flesh having the breath of life, sparing only those kept alive in the ship. In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), the Flood drowns Noah’s people who deny Allah’s revelations while Noah and those with him are saved in the ship. In Surah 11: Hud, the Flood begins when the oven gushes water, covers land with mountain-like waves, drowns Noah’s son and disbelievers, then subsides when Allah commands earth and sky. In The Believers (Al-Muminoon), the flood occurs when the oven gushes water, and those who have done wrong are drowned. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), the flood of Noah drowns Noah’s folk after Noah and those with him are saved in the laden ship. In Surah 71: Noah (Nooh), drowning causes the disbelievers to enter a Fire, where they find no helpers in place of Allah.

flood in Genesis

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where flood first appears

Abstract written by a model

The flood is a catastrophic deluge brought by God to destroy all flesh having the breath of life, sparing only those kept alive in the ship. God announces it first. It begins on the seventeenth day of the second month of Noah's six hundredth year, when the deep's fountains burst open and the sky's windows open. Rain falls forty days and nights, and the waters rise fifteen cubits above the high mountains, covering the whole earth and killing all life on dry land. Waters flood the earth for one hundred fifty days. God then sends a wind, stops the fountains and windows, and the waters recede, showing mountain tops in the tenth month. They are dried up by the first day of the first month of Noah's 601st year, and the earth is dry by the twenty-seventh day of the second month. God afterward promises it will never happen again.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

God announced that he would bring the flood of waters on the earth to destroy all flesh having the breath of life from under the sky. Everything on the earth would die, apart from those kept alive in the ship.

Chapter 7

The flood begins after seven days, when the fountains of the great deep burst open and the sky's windows open on the seventeenth day of the second month in Noah's six hundredth year. Rain falls for forty days and forty nights, and the waters rise until they cover all the high mountains by fifteen cubits. The waters flood the earth for one hundred fifty days and destroy all life on dry land except what is in the ship.

Chapter 8

The waters subside after God sends a wind over the earth and the deep's fountains and the sky's windows are stopped and the rain restrained. They recede continually, with the mountain tops visible in the tenth month, and are dried up from the earth by the first day of the first month of Noah's six hundred first year. The earth is dry by the twenty-seventh day of the second month.

Chapter 9

The flood is referred to as the event that cut off flesh by water, and God promises it will never happen again to destroy the earth. Noah is also said to live 350 years after the flood.
