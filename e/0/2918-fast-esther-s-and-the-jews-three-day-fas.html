Abstract written by a model

lasts three days [no eating or drinking, night and day] Esther tells Mordecai to assemble the Jews in Susa and have them fast for her, with no eating or drinking for three days, night and day, while she and her maidens also fast. She says that afterward she will go in to the king contrary to the law, even if she must die. Mordecai does all that she commanded, and prayers by him and Esther accompany the fast.
