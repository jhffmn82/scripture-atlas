Chapter 5

The wicked admit that their arrogance, riches, and boasting all passed away like a shadow, a rumor, a ship's wake, a bird's flight path, or an arrow's trail through air, leaving no trace. The ungodly man's hope is compared to chaff carried by wind, foam vanishing before a tempest, smoke scattered by wind, and the remembrance of a guest who stays one day.
