Abstract written by a model

In The Good News According to Mark, the loaves and fish were blessed, broken, divided, and distributed by the disciples, with twelve baskets remaining. In The Gospel According to John, the loaves and fish were taken by Jesus, distributed to the crowd, and produced twelve baskets of leftovers.
