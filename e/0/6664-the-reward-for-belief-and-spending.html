the reward for belief and spending

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: a great reward, a rich reward, their reward

Abstract written by a model

Those who believe in Allah and Allah's messengers, give alms, and spend in Allah's way receive doubled reward, a rich reward, light, and mercy. Those who spent and fought before the victory have a greater rank than those who did so afterward, though Allah promises good to each.
