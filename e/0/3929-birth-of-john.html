Abstract written by a model

John's birth is foretold by Gabriel to Zacharias, and Elizabeth conceives afterward. When he is born, neighbors and relatives rejoice at the Lord's mercy, and on the eighth day Elizabeth and Zacharias both insist on the name John, which frees Zacharias's tongue and causes fear and talk throughout the hill country of Judea.
