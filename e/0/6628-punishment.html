punishment

topic · 4 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Our might, doom, dreadful doom, painful doom, punished, the anguish, the torment of eternity, torment

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 10: Jonas (Yunus), punishment includes boiling drink, painful doom, the Fire, disgrace, and the torment of eternity, and comes upon those who disbelieve, deny revelations, do wrong, and persist in rebellion. In Surah 17: The Night Journey (Al-Israa), punishment comes upon those who corrupt, disbelieve, follow Iblis, or reject Allah’s revelations, with repeated punishment threatened for repeated wrongdoing and punishment described in hell. In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), punishment comes upon disbelievers and wrong-doers unexpectedly, cannot be repelled, and eventually surrounds those who mock the messengers. In Surah 37: Those drawn up in Ranks (As-Saaffaat), punishment includes perpetual torment, painful doom, hell, the tree of Zaqqum, and boiling water, and is assigned to wrong-doers, deniers, and those who worship beside Allah.
