Tyre's trade and merchandise

topic · 1 work · 21 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: handiworks, merchandise, riches, wares, your merchandise

Abstract written by a model

Tyre's trade and merchandise is a catalogue of the many nations that traded with Tyre. Tarshish paid silver, iron, tin, and lead; Javan, Tubal, and Meshech supplied persons and bronze vessels; Togarmah horses and mules; Judah and Israel wheat, honey, oil, and balm; Sheba and Raamah spices, stones, and gold. Others include Dedan, Syria, Damascus, Arabia, Kedar, and Vedan. This commerce filled many peoples and enriched the kings of the earth, but it ends in ruin, when the merchandise falls into the seas on the day of Tyre's fall.

Chapter 27

Many nations traded with Tyre, including Tarshish with silver, iron, tin, and lead; Javan, Tubal, and Meshech with persons and bronze vessels; Togarmah with horses and mules; Judah and Israel with wheat, honey, oil, and balm; and Sheba and Raamah with spices, stones, and gold. This trade filled many peoples and enriched kings, but the merchandise falls into the seas in the day of Tyre's ruin.
