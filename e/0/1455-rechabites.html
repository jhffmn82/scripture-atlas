Abstract written by a model

The Rechabites are a nomadic group bound by Jonadab's commands: no wine, no houses, fields, vineyards or seed, only tents. They took refuge in Jerusalem fearing Chaldean and Syrian armies. Brought into the LORD's house, they refuse wine, and through Jeremiah the LORD promises them a continuing line.

Rechabites is in one work on this site, The Book of Jeremiah. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 35

The Rechabites, led by Jaazaniah son of Jeremiah and his brothers and sons, are brought into the LORD's house and refuse the wine offered to them. They explain that they live in tents and keep Jonadab's commands, and that they came to Jerusalem only for fear of the Chaldean and Syrian armies. The LORD promises them, through Jeremiah, a continuing line before him.
