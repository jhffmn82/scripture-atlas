Chapter 18

The merchants of the earth grew rich from Babylon's luxury, and her fall leaves no one to buy their goods, which include metals, gems, fabrics, spices, foods, animals, chariots, and people's bodies and souls. They stand far away weeping and mourning, lamenting that such great riches were made desolate in an hour. Babylon's rulers are described as their princes.
