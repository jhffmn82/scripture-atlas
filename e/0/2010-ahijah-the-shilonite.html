Abstract written by a model

Ahijah is a prophet of the LORD and his servant, living in Shiloh. He tore a garment into twelve pieces, giving Jeroboam ten, to foretell the kingdom's division from Solomon's house. Later, blind with age, he is warned by the LORD, recognizes Jeroboam's disguised wife, and pronounces judgment on Jeroboam's house and Israel; his word is fulfilled.

Chapter 11

Ahijah the prophet met Jeroboam alone in a field, tore his new garment into twelve pieces, and gave Jeroboam ten. He delivered the LORD's message that the kingdom would be torn from Solomon's house, with one tribe remaining.

Chapter 14

Ahijah is the prophet at Shiloh who earlier said Jeroboam would be king, and he is now unable to see because of his age. Warned by the LORD, he identifies Jeroboam's disguised wife and delivers the LORD's judgment on Jeroboam's house and on Israel. His word about the child is fulfilled.
