Abstract written by a model

Mining is the human pursuit of silver, gold, iron, copper, and sapphires, done in remote shafts where workers dangle far from people. It ends darkness and brings hidden things to light, yet contrasts with wisdom, which such searching cannot obtain.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 28

Mining is described as man's pursuit of silver, gold, iron, copper, sapphires, and other precious things, carried out in remote shafts where workers hang far from people and swing back and forth. It sets an end to darkness and brings hidden things out to light. It serves as a contrast to wisdom, which no such searching or wealth can obtain.
