Abstract written by a model

Weak believers are brothers for whom Christ died, lacking knowledge about idols and holding a conscience tied to them. Seeing the knowledgeable eat in an idol's temple emboldens them to eat, defiling their conscience and risking their perishing.

Chapter 8

The weak lack the knowledge about idols, and their conscience is defiled when they eat as of a thing sacrificed to an idol. They can be emboldened to eat by seeing someone with knowledge in an idol's temple and may perish as a result, though each is a brother for whom Christ died.
