Abstract written by a model

assigns_as_reason six classes of living beings [for the existence of sin] The Âkârya answers the opponent by explaining that the six classes of living beings are the cause of sins and by using the examples of a murderer, a sentient being, and senseless beings. The Âkârya also teaches that one should avoid injuring or killing living beings because they feel pain as he does.
