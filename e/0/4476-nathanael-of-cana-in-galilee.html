The Gospel According to John / Chapter 1

Nathanael is found by Philip, who tells him about Jesus of Nazareth, and he doubts that anything good can come out of Nazareth. Jesus calls him an Israelite indeed in whom is no deceit and says he saw him under the fig tree before Philip called him. Nathanael then declares Jesus the Son of God and King of Israel, and Jesus promises he will see greater things.
