Abstract written by a model

The state is to be ruled without purposeful action, though correction and craft in war are also described as means of rule. When Tao is seen throughout it, it finds good fortune. Its instruments of profit should not be shown to the people, because greater possession of such implements brings more disorder. It is a scourge when governed by wisdom and a blessing when it is not.
