Positive and Negative principles

topic · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Contraries, Positive and Negative, Positive and Negative Principles of Chinese cosmogony, Yin and Yang, affirmatives and negatives, contraries, negative, positive, positive and negative, the Yin and Yang principles, two Principles of Creation

Abstract written by a model

Positive and Negative principles arise from subjective individuality and appear as contraries, yet are identical in the identity of all things. The mind recognises them through distinctions; the Sage reconciles them by nature. Once peacefully united, they influence, act upon, and regulate each other, sometimes inharmoniously. Their harmony orders changing sounds and creation, while their interaction transforms seasons and all things between heaven and earth.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER II.

Contraries arise with individual bias and appear as affirmations and negations, subjective and objective, and other distinctions. The true Sage regards them as identical and reconciles them by the light of nature.

CHAPTER XIV.

The Yin and Yang principles are listed among the Six Influences and are associated with the ordering of the world. In the Yellow Emperor's account of perfect music, their harmony produces changing, interwoven sounds that bring creation into accord.

CHAPTER XXV.

The Positive and Negative principles influence, act upon, and regulate each other. Their interaction contributes to the alternation and transformation of the seasons and all things between heaven and earth.
