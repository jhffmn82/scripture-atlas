Chapter 11

Those of Israel other than the chosen were hardened, in accord with Scripture that God gave them a spirit of stupor, eyes that do not see, and ears that do not hear. It is described as partial and lasting until the fullness of the Gentiles has come in, after which all Israel will be saved.
