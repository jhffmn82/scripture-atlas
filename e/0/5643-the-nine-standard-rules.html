the nine standard rules

topic · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: nine standard rules, the above nine standard rules

Abstract written by a model

The nine standard rules are practices pursued by singleness: cultivating one’s character, honoring virtue and talent, caring for relatives, ministers, officers, and people, encouraging artisans, treating distant men indulgently, and cherishing princes of the states.

Chapter 20

The nine standard rules are cultivating one’s character, honoring men of virtue and talents, showing affection to relatives, respecting great ministers, treating officers kindly, treating the people as children, encouraging artisans, treating distant men indulgently, and cherishing the princes of the states.
