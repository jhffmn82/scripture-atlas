CHAPTER XIV.

During Confucius's visit to Lao Tzŭ, Lao Tzŭ questions his search for TAO and explains why TAO cannot simply be transmitted. After the visit, Confucius remains silent for three days and says he saw a Dragon whose presence left him unable to admonish him.
