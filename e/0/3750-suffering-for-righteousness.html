Chapter 3

Suffering for righteousness' sake is declared a blessing, and believers are told not to fear what others fear or be troubled. It is said to be better, if God wills, to suffer for doing right than for doing evil. Christ's own suffering is given as the model, and those who speak against believers as evildoers may be disappointed.
