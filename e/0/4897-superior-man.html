superior man

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: the superior man

Abstract written by a model

The superior man guards against lust in youth, quarrelsomeness in strength and vigor, and covetousness in old age. He also stands in awe of the ordinances of Heaven, great men, and the words of sages. The account moves from conduct across youth, strength, and old age to enduring reverence.
