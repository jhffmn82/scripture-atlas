Abstract written by a model

The red heifer is a ritual animal required to be red, spotless, and never yoked. Given to Eleazar the priest, it is killed outside the camp, its blood sprinkled seven times toward the Tent of Meeting, then burned whole with cedar wood, hyssop, and scarlet.

Chapter 19

The red heifer must be without spot or defect and never yoked. It is killed outside the camp, its blood sprinkled seven times toward the Tent of Meeting, and its skin, meat, blood, and dung are burned, with cedar wood, hyssop, and scarlet cast into the fire.
