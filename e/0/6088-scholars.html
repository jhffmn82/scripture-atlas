Abstract written by a model

Scholars may accept a prince’s grain as a present to meet necessities, but refuse support or pay without regular office. As common men in city or country, they do not seek a prince’s interview before presenting an introductory present and becoming ministers. Distinguished scholars befriend virtuous scholars; if that is insufficient, they study men of antiquity.

Chapter 6 .. Chapter 9

Scholars may accept a prince's gifts to assist their necessities but should not accept princely pay without a regular office. Scholars without office should not presume to interview princes, and those of distinguished virtue make friends with virtuous scholars and study the men of antiquity.
