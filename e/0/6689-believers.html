Abstract written by a model

In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), believers are defined by faith and sincere obedience, worship through prayer and charity, steadfastness, gratitude, and observance of Allah’s limits, receiving guidance and the Garden. In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan), the believers surrender to Allah, follow Muhammad, obey, pray, seek forgiveness, remain steadfast, enjoin right, forgive, and receive Allah’s protection and reward. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), believers are commanded to act justly, worship carefully, obey Allah and His messenger, strive in Allah’s cause, and uphold faith and justice. In Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida), believers fulfil covenants, uphold justice, worship, purify themselves, obey Allah and the messenger, observe lawful duties, and are promised forgiveness and victory. In Surah 6: The Cattle (Al-An'aam), the believers accept Allah’s revelations, do right, worship carefully, avoid wrongdoing, eat food named for Allah, and receive safety. In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf): are_successful the successful [when their scale is heavy] Believers are instructed to follow Allah, avoid evil, worship sincerely, and do good works; they inherit the Garden and receive Allah's mercy. In Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba), the believers repent, worship, give the poor-due, obey Allah and Muhammad, strive with wealth and lives, and receive mercy, victory, and the Garden. In Surah 13: The Thunder (Ar-Ra'd): Believers know that what is revealed to Muhammad from his Lord is the truth.

In Surah 14: Abraham (Ibrahim), the believers trust Allah, establish worship, spend from what they have received, and those who do good works enter Gardens. In Surah 17: The Night Journey (Al-Israa), believers who do good works are promised a great reward, and the Qur'an is described as healing and mercy for them. In Surah 19: Mary (Maryam): The believers are contrasted with those who disbelieve and are associated with better guidance and lasting good works. In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa): The believers are saved from anguish and benefit from Allah's mercy. In Surah 28: The Stories (Al-Qasas), believers trust Allah’s revelations, repent, do right, remain steadfast, repel evil with good, spend from Allah’s provision, and receive success and reward. In Surah 32: The Prostration (As-Sajda), believers prostrate upon hearing Allah’s revelations, praise Him, pray at night, spend from His provision, and receive the Gardens of Retreat. In Surah 33: The Clans (Al-Ahzaab), the believers obey Allah and His messenger, remember and glorify Allah, speak rightly, follow the Prophet, endure trials, and receive forgiveness and vast reward. In Surah 39: The Groups (Az-Zumar), believers worship Allah alone, observe their duty, remain steadfast, and receive good in this world and Allah’s unrestricted reward. In Surah 47: Muhammad, believers follow the truth, do good works, receive removal of their ill-deeds, and are commanded to help Allah, obey, remain steadfast, and spend in His way. In Surah 48: The Victory (Al-Fath), believers receive reassurance, increased faith, forgiveness, Gardens, and immense reward, while those with Muhammad are merciful among themselves. In Surah 52: The Mount (Al-Tur), believers whose descendants follow them in faith are joined with their descendants and receive the pleasures and service of Paradise. (Surah 52: The Mount (At-Tur)) In Surah 58: The Pleading Woman (Al-Mujaadila), believers avoid sinful conspiracies, pursue righteousness and piety, trust Allah, and are exalted with knowledge to high ranks. In Surah 60: She that is to be examined (Al-Mumtahana), believers are told to avoid alliance and secret friendship with Allah’s enemies, trust Allah, keep duty, and examine believing women. In Surah 62: Friday (Al-Jumu'a): Believers are commanded to hasten to the remembrance of Allah and leave their trading when the call is heard for the prayer of the day of congregation. In Surah 63: The Hypocrites (Al-Munaafiqoon), the believers possess might with Allah and His messenger and are warned to spend and remember Allah before wealth and children distract them. In Surah 66: The Prohibition (At-Tahrim), believers guard themselves and their families from the Fire, repent sincerely, and receive light and forgiveness with the Prophet. In The Table-Talk of the Prophet Mohammad (Part 1), Muslims are urged to pray, read the Korān, fast moderately, give charity, work honestly, avoid unlawful gain, repay debts, and treat people and animals well.

Abstract written by a model

Believers are a group addressed through commands, prohibitions, promises, and warnings. They are directed to act fairly in marriage and wealth: not to forcibly inherit or constrain women, to consort with them kindly, fulfill marriage portions, avoid vain squandering, and not kill one another. Their worship requires care: they must not approach prayer while drunken or polluted, and may use high clean soil when water is unavailable in specified circumstances. Those who believe and do good works are promised eternal Gardens, pure companions, and plenteous shade. They must obey Allah and the messenger, refer disputes to them, and submit to the messenger’s judgment. Obedience places them with those shown Allah’s favour. They are called to take precautions, advance in groups or together, fight for Allah’s cause, avoid intentional killing of another believer, and carefully distinguish those offering peace rather than calling them disbelievers. Those striving with wealth and lives receive higher rank and reward. Believers must be staunch witnesses for Allah in justice, even against themselves, parents, or kindred, and must not follow passion away from truth. They are commanded to believe in Allah, Muhammad, angels, scriptures, messengers, and the Last Day; to accept Allah’s messengers without distinction; and to believe in revelations sent to Muhammad and before him. They are warned against choosing disbelievers as friends in place of believers and are promised immense reward, particularly alongside prayer, poor-due, and belief in Allah and the Last Day.

It is not lawful for you forcibly to inherit the women (of your deceased kinsmen), nor (that) ye should put constraint upon them that ye may take away a part of that which ye have given them, unless they be guilty of flagrant lewdness. But consort with them in kindness, for if ye hate them it may happen that ye hate a thing wherein Allah hath placed much good. .. 28 Allah would make the burden light for you, for man was created weak.

Believers are instructed not to forcibly inherit the women of deceased kinsmen, constrain women to take back gifts, or marry prohibited relatives. They are directed to consort with women in kindness and observe the stated marriage rules.

Abstract written by a model

Believers are those who believe and are addressed with commands and promises centered on duty to Allah. They must fulfil indentures, avoid transgression, help in righteousness and pious duty, establish prayer, pay the poor-due, purify themselves for prayer, and keep just testimony as steadfast witnesses for Allah in equity. They are to trust Allah, seek the straight path and approach unto Him, strive in His way to succeed, obey Allah and the messenger, and have charge of their own souls. They may eat lawful and good food; their food is lawful for those who received the Scripture, and marriage with virtuous women is permitted under stated conditions. They are told not to forbid lawful good things, to avoid strong drink and games of chance, and to observe duties concerning oaths, pilgrimage, hunting, and testimony. They should not take as guardians those who make a jest of their religion. Allah and the Messenger Muhammad guard them, and forgiveness is promised when they do good works. In the end, they are victorious as the party of Allah.
