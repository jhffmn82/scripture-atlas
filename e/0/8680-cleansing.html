cleansing

Abstract written by a model

Cleansing is a ritual purification for one defiled by the dead. Proper cleansing delights the sun, moon, stars, fire, water, earth, cow, trees, and faithful, and earns the cleanser welfare in the blessed abode. Through prescribed words and repeated chants, it cleans the material world and drives away fiends, defilements, demons, and Angra Mainyu. House cleansing proceeds through three washings each of bodies and clothes, three Gâtha chants, sacrifice to Fire, baresma, and libations to good waters.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Fargard IX.

Proper cleansing delights the sun, moon, stars, fire, water, earth, cow, trees, and faithful. Its reward for the cleanser in the other world is the welfare of the blessed abode.

Fargard XI.

Cleansing is performed through prescribed spoken words and repeated chants. It makes the named parts of the material world clean and drives away fiends, defilements, demons, and Angra Mainyu.

Fargard XII.

Cleansing the house requires washing the bodies three times, washing the clothes three times, chanting the Gâthas three times, offering a sacrifice to the Fire, offering the bundles of baresma, and bringing libations to the good waters.
