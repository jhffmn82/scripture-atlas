Chapter 4

Eliphaz says that, according to what he has seen, those who plow iniquity and sow trouble reap the same. They perish by the breath of God and are consumed by the blast of his anger, like lions whose teeth are broken, who perish for lack of prey, and whose cubs are scattered.
