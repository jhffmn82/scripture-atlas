the wicked

group · 3 works · 9 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Evil men, The wicked, he who is perverse in his ways, him who loves violence, one who hardens his heart, one with perverse ways, wicked

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the wicked bend bows to shoot secretly in darkness at the upright in heart; the LORD hates them, and their portion is blazing coals, fire, sulfur, and scorching wind. In Proverbs, the wicked are marked by needless fear, mutual praise among law-forsakers, and ignorance of justice; their rise makes men hide, those who mislead the upright fall into their own trap, and when they perish the righteous thrive. In Surah 82: The Cleaving (Al-Infitaar), the wicked will be in hell and burn there on the Day of Judgment, never absent from it.
