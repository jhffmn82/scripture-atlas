Jacob's vow

Also called: a vow

Abstract written by a model

In Chapter 28, Jacob makes a conditional vow: if God is with him, protects him, provides bread and clothing, and returns him safely to his father's house, the LORD will be his God. He then promises that the stone pillar will be God's house and a tenth of everything.

Chapter 28

Jacob vows that if God is with him, keeps him on his way, gives him bread and clothing, and brings him back to his father's house in peace, then the LORD will be his God. The stone pillar will become God's house, and he will give God a tenth of all that he is given.
