Abstract written by a model

does_not_disturb Sun Shu Ao [because it is outside his own self] Rank and power are treated as worldly conditions that do not affect a person who has TAO. The perfect sages and Sun Shu Ao remain unmoved by obtaining or losing office, and honour is said to reside in the person rather than in a changing condition.
