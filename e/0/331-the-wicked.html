Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the wicked are those who turn away to crooked ways and will be led away by the LORD with the workers of iniquity. In Psalms, the wicked are workers of iniquity who speak peace to their neighbors while harboring mischief, and David asks that they be repaid according to their deeds. In Psalms, the wicked cannot dwell with the LORD or stand before him. In Proverbs, the wicked are portrayed as people whose treasures profit nothing and whose names rot; fears overtake them, hope perishes, years shorten, violence covers their mouths, and their wealth is stored up for the righteous. In Ecclesiastes or, The Preacher, the wicked meet the same event as the righteous, and one sinner destroys much good.

the wicked in Proverbs

author unknown · 969 to 699 BC · the work’s page · read it where the wicked first appears

Abstract written by a model

The wicked are portrayed as people whose treasures profit nothing and whose names rot. Fears overtake them, hope perishes, years shorten, and violence covers their mouths. They bring shame, fall to their own wickedness, and their lamp is snuffed out. Hunger and misfortune pursue them, and their wealth is stored up for the righteous.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

The wicked gain nothing from their treasures, and their name will rot. What they fear overtakes them, their hope perishes, their years are shortened, and they will not dwell in the land. Violence covers their mouths, and their increase leads to sin.

Chapter 13

The wicked bring shame and disgrace, are overthrown by their own wickedness, and their lamp is snuffed out. Their bellies go hungry, misfortune pursues sinners, and a wicked messenger falls into trouble.
