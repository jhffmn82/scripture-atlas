offering (for the tabernacle work)

Abstract written by a model

The offering for the tabernacle work is commanded by the LORD and taken from the children of Israel, only from those whose hearts make them willing. Chapter 25 lists its materials: metals, dyed fabrics, skins, acacia wood, oil, spices, and stones. Chapter 35 repeats the command, and the people bring it freely, women spinning and rulers bringing stones, spices, and oil.

Chapter 25

The offering is to be taken from everyone whose heart makes him willing. It consists of gold, silver, bronze, blue, purple, scarlet, fine linen, goats' hair, rams' skins dyed red, sea cow hides, acacia wood, oil for the light, spices for the anointing oil and sweet incense, onyx stones, and stones for the ephod and breastplate.

Chapter 35

The LORD commands an offering taken from the willing-hearted, consisting of metals, dyed and spun fabrics, skins, acacia wood, oil, spices, and stones. The people bring it freely, with the women spinning and the rulers bringing stones, spices, and oil, as a free will offering for the work.
