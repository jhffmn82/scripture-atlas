Hannah

Also called: Oh, my lord, the woman, your servant

Abstract written by a model

Hannah is Elkanah's beloved wife, childless and severely provoked each year by her rival Peninnah. At Shiloh she weeps, prays in bitterness, and vows to give a son to the LORD. After Eli blesses her, she bears Samuel, weans him, and brings him to Eli to fulfill the vow. She sings a prayer of rejoicing, makes Samuel a robe yearly, and the LORD later gives her three more sons and two daughters.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 Samuel / Chapter 1

Hannah, Elkanah's beloved wife, was childless and was provoked yearly by Peninnah, so she wept and would not eat. At Shiloh she prayed in bitterness of soul and vowed to give a boy to the LORD all his days with no razor on his head. After Eli blessed her she ate and was no longer sad, bore Samuel, weaned him, and brought him to Eli at Shiloh to fulfill her vow.

Chapter 2

Hannah prays a song of rejoicing in the LORD's salvation. She makes her son Samuel a little robe each year and brings it when she goes up with her husband to the yearly sacrifice. The LORD visits her, and she conceives and bears three sons and two daughters.
