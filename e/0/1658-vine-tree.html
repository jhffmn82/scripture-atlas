Chapter 15

The vine tree, and its branch among the forest trees, is shown to be worthless for any work: nothing can be made from its wood, not even a pin to hang a vessel on. It is cast into the fire for fuel, and the fire devours both ends and the middle. It serves as the comparison for the inhabitants of Jerusalem.
