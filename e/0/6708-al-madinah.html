Al-Madinah

place · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Medina

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba), Al-Madinah has townspeople who include some persistent hypocrites, and its townsfolk are not to stay behind the Messenger of Allah or prefer their lives to his. In Surah 63: The Hypocrites (Al-Munaafiqoon), Medina is the place the hypocrites say the mightier will drive out the weaker if they return.
