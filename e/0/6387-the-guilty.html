the guilty

group · 4 works · 13 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: guilty

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 54: The Moon (Al-Qamar), the guilty are in error and madness and, on the day of doom, are dragged into the Fire upon their faces. In Surah 55: The Beneficent (Ar-Rahmaan), the guilty are known by their marks, taken by the forelocks and feet, and enter hell, circling between it and fierce, boiling water. In Surah 74: The Cloaked One (Al-Muddaththir), the guilty say they did not pray or feed the wretched, disputed vainly, and denied the Day of Judgment until the Inevitable came. In Surah 83: Defrauding (Al-Mutaffifin), the guilty laugh at those who believe, wink at one another when passing them, return to their own folk jesting, and say that those who believe have gone astray.
