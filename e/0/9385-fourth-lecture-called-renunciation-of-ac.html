Fourth Lecture, Called Renunciation of Activity.

The Fourth Lecture, Called Renunciation of Activity, explains how the Self commits sins through uncontrolled activity and how a monk avoids and renounces them. It concerns the six classes of living beings, the five cardinal sins, the passions, and wrong belief.
