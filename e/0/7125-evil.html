evil

topic · 3 works · 9 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: good, good and evil, good works

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 113: The Dawn (Al-Falaq), evil comes from that which He created, intense darkness, malignant witchcraft, and the envier when he envies. In The Book of Mosiah, evil is a disposition removed by a change that makes King Benjamin's people desire to do good continually, and they are instructed to abound in good works. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, evil comes before all people, is distinguishable to those who know it, and should be resisted with words or swords against rebellion when necessary.
