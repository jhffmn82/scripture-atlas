XIII. Farvardîn Yast.

The material world is sustained through the brightness and glory of the Fravashis, who preserve its sky, waters, earth, plants, animals, and people. Without their help, the Drug would gain strength, dominion, and control of the material world.

XXVI.

The bodily world is the whole world that SAOSHYANT will benefit. ASTVAT-ERETA will make its bodily creatures rise up and withstand their destruction.
