Abstract written by a model

Jeremiah's prophetic calling began before his birth, when the LORD knew, sanctified and appointed him prophet to the nations. Though he protested being a child, he is commissioned to go and speak as commanded, set over nations and kingdoms to uproot, destroy, build and plant, and promised divine protection.

Chapter 1

Jeremiah's calling is described as predating his birth: the LORD knew and sanctified him and appointed him a prophet to the nations. Despite his protest of being a child, he is commissioned to go wherever sent and say whatever he is commanded, and is set over nations and kingdoms to uproot, tear down, destroy, overthrow, build, and plant. The LORD promises to be with him and rescue him, making him a fortified city, iron pillar, and bronze walls.
