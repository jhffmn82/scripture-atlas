Abstract written by a model

The inner gates (south, east, north) share identical measurements: fifty cubits long, twenty-five wide, with lodges, posts, arches, windows and palm trees. Arches face the outer court, each gate is reached by eight steps, and a washing room for burnt offerings stands by the posts.

Chapter 40

The south, east, and north gates of the inner court are each measured by the same measurements, with lodges, posts, arches, and windows all around, fifty cubits long and twenty-five wide. Their arches face the outer court, palm trees stand on their posts, and each is reached by eight steps.
