Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Samuel, Obed-Edom takes the ark into his house, and the LORD blesses him, his household, and all he owns. In 1 Chronicles, Obed-Edom's house receives the ark of God and is blessed, and Obed-Edom later serves as a doorkeeper and receives the south gate by lot.

Obed-Edom in 1 Chronicles

author unknown · 449 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Obed-Edom first appears

Abstract written by a model

Obed-Edom is a Gittite whose house the LORD blessed after he received the ark of God for three months, when David would not move it to David's city. Later he appears as a doorkeeper, son of Jeduthun and Hosah, blessed by God with eight sons and grandsons through Shemaiah. With sons and relatives, sixty-two able men served; the lot gave him the south gate and his sons the storehouse.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

Obed-Edom the Gittite received the ark of God into his house when David would not move it to David's city. It stayed with his family three months, and the LORD blessed his house and all that he had.

Chapter 26

Obed-Edom had eight sons, and the text says God blessed him. With his sons and relatives, sixty-two able men served as doorkeepers, and the lot gave him the south gate and his sons the storehouse.
