mighty angel

Abstract written by a model

The mighty angel is a figure seen in a vision, descending from the sky clothed with a cloud, with a rainbow on his head, a sun-like face, and fiery pillar-like feet. He stands with his right foot on the sea and left on the land, holding a little open book. He cries like a roaring lion, then raises his hand and swears by the eternal Creator that delay is over and God's mystery will be finished when the seventh angel sounds. He finally gives the book to the narrator to eat, bitter in the stomach, sweet in the mouth.

Chapter 10

The mighty angel comes down from the sky clothed with a cloud, with a rainbow on his head, a face like the sun, and feet like pillars of fire. He stands with his right foot on the sea and his left on the land, holding a little open book, and cries out like a roaring lion. He lifts his right hand and swears by the eternal Creator that there will be no more delay and that the mystery of God will be finished in the days of the seventh angel's sounding. He then gives the little book to the narrator, telling him to eat it and warning that it will be bitter in his stomach but sweet as honey in his mouth.
