cherubim (inner sanctuary) in The First Book of Kings

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where cherubim (inner sanctuary) first appears

Abstract written by a model

Two cherubim of olive wood, made by Solomon for the inner sanctuary, each ten cubits high with five-cubit wings spanning ten cubits, identical in measure and form. Overlaid with gold, their wings touch both walls and each other mid-house, covering the ark.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Solomon made two cherubim of olive wood for the inner sanctuary, each ten cubits high, with wings of five cubits each, ten cubits from tip to tip, and of one measure and form. Their outstretched wings touched the two walls and each other in the middle of the house, and they were overlaid with gold.

cherubim (in the most holy place) in The Second Book of Chronicles

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where cherubim (in the most holy place) first appears

Abstract written by a model

Two carved cherubim, overlaid with gold, stand in the most holy place. Each has five-cubit wings, reaching the wall and touching each other, spanning twenty cubits together. They stand on their feet facing the house, wings spread over the ark's place.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Two cherubim were made by carving in the most holy place and overlaid with gold. Each had wings of five cubits each, one reaching the wall and the other touching the other cherub's wing, so together they spread twenty cubits. They stood on their feet with their faces toward the house.
