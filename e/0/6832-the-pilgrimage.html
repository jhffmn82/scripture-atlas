the pilgrimage

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), the pilgrimage and visit to Makka are performed for Allah during well-known months with prescribed observances. In Surah 22: The Pilgrimage (Al-Hajj), the pilgrimage is proclaimed to mankind and its rites are described.
