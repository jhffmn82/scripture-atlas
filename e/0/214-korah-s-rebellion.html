Chapter 16

The rebellion is a challenge by Korah, Dathan, Abiram, On, and 250 princes to the authority of Moses and Aaron, claiming the whole congregation is holy. It ends in judgment, with the earth swallowing the leaders' households and fire consuming the 250 incense offerers. The aftermath includes the altar covering made from their censers as a memorial and a further complaint by the congregation that leads to a plague.
