Laws on justice and honest testimony: they forbid false reports, malicious witnessing, bribes that blind and pervert, crowd-driven injustice, partiality, denying the poor justice, and killing the innocent, require helping an enemy's animals, and ban oppressing aliens, who Israelites once were.

Chapter 23

These laws forbid spreading false reports, joining the wicked as a malicious witness, following a crowd to pervert justice, and taking bribes. They also forbid favoring a poor man in his cause, denying justice to the poor, and killing the innocent, and require returning an enemy's strayed ox or donkey and helping his fallen donkey. Aliens must not be oppressed, since the Israelites were aliens in Egypt.
