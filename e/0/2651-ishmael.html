Ishmael in Genesis

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Ishmael first appears

Abstract written by a model

Ishmael is the son of Abram (Abraham) and Hagar, announced by the LORD's angel before birth and foretold as a wild donkey among men, opposed to all his brothers. Born when Abram was eighty-six, he is circumcised at thirteen. God keeps the covenant for Isaac but blesses Ishmael, promising fruitfulness, twelve princes and a great nation. After Sarah sees him mocking, he is sent away with Hagar; God hears him in the wilderness and provides water. He grows up as an archer in the wilderness of Paran and marries a woman from Egypt, obtained by Hagar. He helps Isaac bury Abraham, fathers twelve sons beginning with Nebaioth and Kedar, who become princes of their nations, and dies at 137, gathered to his people.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

Ishmael is announced by the LORD's angel as the son Hagar will bear, named because the LORD has heard her affliction. He is foretold to be like a wild donkey among men, with his hand against every man and every man's hand against him, living opposed to all his brothers. He is born to Hagar and named by Abram when Abram is eighty-six.

Chapter 17

Ishmael is Abraham's son, for whom Abraham pleads that he might live before God. God says the covenant will not be with him but with Isaac, yet promises to bless him, make him fruitful, and make him father of twelve princes and a great nation. He is circumcised at age thirteen on the same day as Abraham.

Chapter 21

Hagar's son is seen mocking by Sarah, which leads to his being sent away with his mother. In the wilderness God hears his voice and promises to make him a great nation; he is given water from the well, grows up as an archer in the wilderness of Paran, and has a wife from Egypt.

Chapter 25

Ishmael, son of Abraham and Hagar the Egyptian, helped Isaac bury Abraham. He fathered twelve sons, listed beginning with Nebaioth and Kedar, who became twelve princes according to their nations. He died at 137 and was gathered to his people.
