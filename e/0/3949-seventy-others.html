Chapter 10

The seventy are appointed and sent two by two ahead of Jesus, as lambs among wolves, to heal the sick and announce that God's Kingdom has come near. They return with joy that even demons are subject to them in Jesus' name, and Jesus gives them authority over serpents, scorpions, and the enemy's power, urging them to rejoice that their names are written in heaven.
