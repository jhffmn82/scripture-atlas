Abstract written by a model

A lesson on lodging restrictions concerning clothing: monks and nuns beg for acceptable clothes, wear them unchanged, regulate their handling, and learn how to act when encountering thieves.

Fifth Lecture, Called Begging of Clothes. / First Lesson. .. Second Lesson.

It requires monks and nuns to beg for acceptable clothes and wear them as received, without washing or dyeing them. It regulates borrowed, exchanged, discarded, and coloured clothes and instructs mendicants how to act when encountering thieves.
