Abstract written by a model

Hiyeda no Are is an exceptionally intelligent twenty-eight-year-old retainer, able to repeat what he sees and retain what he hears. By command of the Heavenly Sovereign, he memorizes imperial genealogies and former ages’ family-held words, later recorded by Yasumaro.

Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods) / Preface.

Hiyeda no Are was a retainer whose intelligence enabled him to repeat what he saw and remember what he heard. He was commanded to memorize the genealogies of the emperors and the words of former ages, which Yasumaro later recorded.
