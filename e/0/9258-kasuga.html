Kasuga

Abstract written by a model

Kasuga was where Emperor Yū-Riyaku made a progress to wed Princess Wodo, daughter of the Grandee Satsuki of Wani. A maiden hid beside a mound when she saw the Imperial progress, and the Emperor’s song gave the mound the name Mound of the Metal Spade.
