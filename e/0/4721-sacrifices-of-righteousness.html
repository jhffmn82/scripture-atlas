Abstract written by a model

Sacrifices of righteousness are offerings God will delight in. David says God does not want mere sacrifice; the true sacrifices are a broken, contrite spirit. The psalm closes promising delight in righteous offerings, burnt offerings, and bulls on his altar.

Chapter 51

David says God does not delight in sacrifice or burnt offering, or else he would give it, and that the sacrifices of God are a broken spirit and a broken, contrite heart. The psalm ends with the promise that God will delight in sacrifices of righteousness, burnt offerings and whole burnt offerings, and that bulls will be offered on his altar.
