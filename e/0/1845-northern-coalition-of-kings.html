Chapter 11

Jabin summons Jobab of Madon and the kings of Shimron and Achshaph, along with kings of the north, hill country, Arabah, lowland and Dor, and various peoples including Canaanites, Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Jebusites and Hivites. They muster a multitude like the sand of the seashore with many horses and chariots, and are routed and destroyed by Israel.
