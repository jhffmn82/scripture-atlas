supports royal sway [when made the basis of government] Mencius says that counsel based on benevolence and righteousness could lead rulers to stop warfare and cause ministers and families to act according to those principles. He contrasts this with relations governed by profit and says a state guided by benevolence and righteousness can rise to royal sway.
