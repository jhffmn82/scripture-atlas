Sambhûta

Abstract written by a model

Sambhûta, once a Kândâla contemptuously treated in Hastinâpura, resolves to become a universal monarch. Born later as Brahmadatta in Kâmpilya, he reunites with Kitra, receives wealth, yet as king pursues sinful sensual thoughts and ultimately sinks into hell.

Sambhûta is in one work on this site, Uttaradhyayana Sutra. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Thirteenth Lecture.

Sambhûta was contemptuously treated because he was a Kândâla and formed a sinful resolution to become a universal monarch in a later birth. He was later born as Brahmadatta and met Kitra again in Kâmpilya.
