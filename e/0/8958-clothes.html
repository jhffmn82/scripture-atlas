Abstract written by a model

Clothes are classified by whether a mendicant may accept them. Acceptable clothes, begged for in the prescribed way, may be wool, silk, hemp, palm-leaves, cotton, Arkatûla, or similar materials and must be fit, strong, lasting, wearable, pleasant, and free from impurities. Appropriated, expensive, ornate, or impure clothes must not be accepted; clothes are not washed or dyed because they are not new.

Fifth Lecture, Called Begging of Clothes. / First Lesson. .. Second Lesson.

Acceptable clothes may be made of wool, silk, hemp, palm-leaves, cotton, Arkatûla, or similar materials, and must be fit, strong, lasting, wearable, pleasant, and suitable for a mendicant. Clothes containing eggs, living beings, seeds, grass, hidden valuables, or other impurities, as well as certain expensive or ornate clothes, must not be accepted.
