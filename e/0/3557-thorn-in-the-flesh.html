Chapter 12

The thorn in the flesh, described as a messenger of Satan to torment Paul, was given to him because of the exceeding greatness of the revelations, so that he would not be exalted excessively. Paul begged the Lord three times for it to depart, but it was not removed; he was told grace is sufficient.
