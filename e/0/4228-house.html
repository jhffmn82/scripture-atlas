house

Also called: his house on the rock, house on the rock, that house, the rock

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, the house on the rock was struck by rain, floods, and winds but did not fall because it was founded on the rock, representing the life of one who hears and does Jesus’s words. In The Third Book of Nephi, the house is founded on rock when its builder hears and does Jesus’s sayings, withstanding rain, floods, and winds; founded on sand when its builder hears but does not do them, it falls. (The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman)
