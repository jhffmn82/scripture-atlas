Chapter 8

The vision shows a ram, a goat, a great horn broken into four, and a little horn that desecrates the sanctuary and the continual burnt offering. A holy one says it will last 2,300 evenings and mornings before the sanctuary is cleansed. Gabriel says it belongs to the appointed time of the end and the latter time of the indignation, declares it true, and tells Daniel to seal it up because it concerns many days to come, though no one understands it.
