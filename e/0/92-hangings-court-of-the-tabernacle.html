Abstract written by a model

The tabernacle court's hangings are fine twined linen enclosing the court: 100 cubits on the south and north sides, 50 on the west, and 50 on the east split 15 cubits either side of the gate, hung on pillars with silver hooks and fillets and bronze sockets.

Chapter 27

The hangings are of fine twined linen: one hundred cubits on each of the south and north sides (twenty pillars and sockets each), fifty cubits on the west side (ten pillars and sockets), and on the east side fifty cubits in total, with fifteen cubits on each side of the gate (three pillars and sockets each). The pillars have silver hooks and fillets and bronze sockets.
