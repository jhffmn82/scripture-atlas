Abstract written by a model

A vision symbol: the male goat, which Gabriel interprets as the king of Greece. It arrives from the west over the whole earth without touching the ground, furiously attacks the ram, and grows proud. At its strength its great horn, the first king, breaks, and four lesser horns, four kingdoms, replace it.

Chapter 8

The male goat comes from the west over the whole earth without touching the ground, with a notable horn between its eyes, and attacks the ram in fury. It magnifies itself exceedingly, but when strong its great horn is broken and four notable horns grow in its place toward the four winds. Gabriel explains that the goat is the king of Greece, the great horn is the first king, and the four horns are four kingdoms that will arise from the nation but without his power.
