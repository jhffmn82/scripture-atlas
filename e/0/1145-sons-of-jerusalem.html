Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, the sons of Jerusalem first lie fainted at street heads, later hurry back as destroyers leave, gather from all around, ask for more room, are carried by nations in bosoms and on shoulders, and are saved by the LORD. In The Book of Isaiah, Zion's sons come from far away and her daughters are carried in arms as they gather to her; ships of Tarshish bring the sons with their silver and gold, and in the end her people are all righteous, inherit the land forever, and the little one becomes a thousand and the small one a strong nation. In Baruch, Jerusalem's children are gathered from the going down of the sun to its rising at the word of the Holy One, rejoicing that God has remembered them, and are brought back carried on high with glory on a royal throne.

Chapter 49

Zion's children hurry back while her destroyers leave, and they gather from all around to come to her. Born in her bereavement, they say the place is too small and ask for room to live.
