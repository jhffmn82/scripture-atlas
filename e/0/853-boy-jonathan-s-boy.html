Chapter 20

Jonathan's boy goes with him to the field and runs to find the arrows Jonathan shoots, while Jonathan shouts after him to hurry. He understands nothing of the signal, as only Jonathan and David know the matter, and he is sent back to the city carrying Jonathan's weapons.
