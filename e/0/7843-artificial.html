Also called: artificiality

Abstract written by a model

The artificial is an external condition arising from rulers’ self-created standards and controls; like halters or nose strings, it abides without and must not obliterate the natural.

CHAPTER VII.

It arises when rulers rely on self-created standards, charity, laws, regulations, and deliberate efforts instead of natural order.

CHAPTER XVII.

The artificial is represented by putting a halter on a horse's head or a string through a bullock's nose. It abides without and must not be allowed to obliterate the natural.
