Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Luke, the Scriptures are presented as Moses, the prophets, and the psalms, which testify about Christ, whose suffering, glory, and fulfillment Jesus explains to his disciples. In 2 Timothy, Scripture is described as God-breathed and profitable for teaching, reproof, correction, and instruction in righteousness, making one wise for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus and equipping believers for every good work. In Peter’s Second Letter, prophecy of Scripture is called the more sure word, a lamp in a dark place, not of private interpretation but given as holy men were moved by the Holy Spirit.

prophecy of Scripture in Peter’s Second Letter

Simon Peter · AD 64 to 68 · the work’s page · read it where prophecy of Scripture first appears

Abstract written by a model

Prophecy of Scripture, called the more sure word, is likened to a lamp shining in a dark place that readers do well to heed until the day dawns and the morning star arises in their hearts. Peter says it is not of private interpretation; it came not by man's will but from holy men moved by the Holy Spirit.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Peter’s Second Letter / Chapter 1

Prophecy is called the more sure word, which readers do well to heed as a lamp shining in a dark place until the day dawns and the morning star arises in their hearts. Peter states that no prophecy of Scripture is of private interpretation. It never came by the will of man, but holy men of God spoke, moved by the Holy Spirit.
