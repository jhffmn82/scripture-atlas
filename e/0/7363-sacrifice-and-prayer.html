sacrifice and prayer

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: prayer, sacrifice, sacrifice and invocation, sacrifice, prayer, propitiation, and glorification

Abstract written by a model

Sacrifice and prayer are offerings to Ahura Mazda, the Amesha-Spentas, Haurvatât, Tistrya, and other holy beings, made with propitiation and glorification. They include libations, Haoma, baresma, meat, spoken words, deeds, and for Tistrya a same-coloured head of cattle. They seek blessings, protection, health, prosperity, strength, and Tistrya’s timely arrival; some people are forbidden from taking part.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

I. Ormazd Yast.

Sacrifice and prayer are offered to the Amesha-Spentas, Ahura Mazda, and other holy beings with propitiation and glorification. They are also used in offerings of libations and are associated with blessings, protection, health, prosperity, and strength.

IV. Khordâd Yast.

Sacrifice is offered unto Hâvani, Haurvatât, the prosperity of the seasons, and the years. It includes libations, Haoma and meat, baresma, wisdom of the tongue, holy spells, speech, deeds, and rightly-spoken words.

VIII. Tîr Yast.

Sacrifice and prayer addressed to Tistrya by his own name give him strength and cause him to arrive at the appointed time. The proper observance includes libations, baresma, and cooking a single-coloured head of cattle, while certain people are forbidden to take part in the offerings.
