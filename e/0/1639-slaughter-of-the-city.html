Chapter 9

The six men carry out the slaughter, killing old men, young men, virgins, little children and women, beginning at the old men before the house and filling the courts with the slain. The narrator, left alive, falls on his face and cries out in protest. The LORD justifies it by the people's iniquity and bloodshed.
