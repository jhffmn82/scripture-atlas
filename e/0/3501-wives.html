Abstract written by a model

Wives are addressed in Chapter 7 as a group bound to their husbands: they must give affection, not leave them, and lack authority over their own bodies. If separated, they stay unmarried or reconcile. Married wives concern themselves with pleasing husbands; widows may remarry in the Lord, though Paul thinks staying single is happier.

Chapter 7

Wives are told to give husbands the affection owed them and not to leave them, and the wife has no authority over her own body, but the husband does. If a wife departs, she must remain unmarried or be reconciled. A widow is free to marry whomever she desires, only in the Lord, though Paul thinks she is happier if she stays as she is.
