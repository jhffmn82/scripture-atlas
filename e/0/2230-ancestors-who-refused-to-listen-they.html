Abstract written by a model

Ancestors who refused to listen turned their backs, stopped their ears and hardened their hearts like flint, rejecting the law and the prophets' words. Consequently, great wrath of the LORD fell on them, He ignored their calls, and a whirlwind scattered them among unknown nations.

Chapter 7

The ancestors refused to listen, turned their backs, stopped their ears and made their hearts as hard as flint, rejecting the law and the words sent by the LORD's Spirit through the former prophets. As a result great wrath came on them, the LORD would not listen when they called, and they were scattered by a whirlwind among nations they had not known.
