Chapter 50

Jesus, the son of Sirach Eleazar, of Jerusalem, identifies himself as the writer of this book, who poured forth wisdom out of his heart. He also expresses that two nations vex his soul and a third is no nation, naming those on the mountain of Samaria, the Philistines, and the foolish people living in Shechem.
