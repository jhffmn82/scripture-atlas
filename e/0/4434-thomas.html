Abstract written by a model

Thomas, also called Didymus, is one of the twelve disciples of Jesus. In Chapter 14 he asks how they can know the way, prompting Jesus to call himself the way, truth, and life. In Chapter 20, absent at first, he doubts the others' report until Jesus appears and he declares 'My Lord and my God!'

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 14

Thomas tells Jesus that the disciples do not know where he is going and asks how they can know the way. His question leads Jesus to say he is the way, the truth, and the life.

Chapter 20

Thomas, one of the twelve, called Didymus, is absent when Jesus first appears to the disciples. He refuses to believe their report unless he sees the nail prints and puts his finger and hand into Jesus' hands and side. When Jesus appears eight days later and invites him to touch, Thomas answers 'My Lord and my God!'
