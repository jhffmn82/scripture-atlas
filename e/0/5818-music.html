Abstract written by a model

Music, whether present-day or ancient, has the same effect on good government. The king prefers music of the present age, while Mencius stresses that shared enjoyment, especially with many, is more pleasant than enjoying it alone or with few.

Chapter 1

The king says he loves music suited to the present age rather than the music of the ancient sovereigns. Mencius says that present-day and ancient music are alike in their effect, and that music enjoyed with others or with many is more pleasant than music enjoyed alone or with a few.
