Chapter 19

The third lot came up for Zebulun, with a border running from Sarid west to Maralah and Dabbesheth, then east to Chisloth Tabor, Daberath, Japhia, Gath Hepher, Rimmon and Hannathon, ending at the valley of Iphtah El. Its inheritance was twelve cities with their villages, including Kattath, Nahalal, Shimron, Idalah and Bethlehem.
