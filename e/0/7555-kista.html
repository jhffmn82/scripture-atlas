Abstract written by a model

Kista is a holy, most right being made by Ahura Mazda and identified with the good Law. Swift, clever, renowned, cleansing, and protective, she supplies good stores, frees from dangers, and grants vital strengths and abilities.

XVI. Dîn Yast.

Kista is the most right, made by Mazda and holy, and is identified with the good Law of the worshippers of Mazda. She is praised as swift, cleansing, protective, and able to grant speed, hearing, strength, health, sturdiness, and exceptional sight.
