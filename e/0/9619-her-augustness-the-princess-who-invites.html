Her Augustness the Princess-Who-Invites in Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods)

Futo no Yasumaro · the work’s page · read it where Her Augustness the Princess-Who-Invites first appears

Abstract written by a model

Her Augustness the Princess-Who-Invites is a deity, the younger sister of His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites and one of the Seven Divine Generations. With him, she is commanded to make, consolidate, and give birth to the drifting land, stirring brine with the heavenly jewelled spear. She gives birth to Deity-of-Fire and other deities; the fire birth burns her private parts, causing sickness, divine retirement, and burial at Mount Hiba. In Hades, she has eaten of its furnace and considers returning, but after her brother looks at her, she sends pursuers and pursues him herself. Threatening to strangle a thousand people daily, she becomes the Great-Deity-of-Hades and is also called Road-Reaching-Great-Deity.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Sect.

The Female-Who-Invites is one of the Seven Divine Generations. Together with the Male-Who-Invites, she is commanded to make, consolidate, and give birth to the drifting land, and she stirs the brine with the heavenly jewelled spear.

Sect.

The Female-Who-Invites speaks after the Male-Who-Invites, saying, "Ah! what a fair and lovely youth!"

Sect.

Through giving birth to the Deity-of-Fire, her august private parts were burnt, and she sickened and lay down. She divinely retired, and His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites buried her on Mount Hiba.

Sect.

Her Augustness the Female-Who-Invites says that she has eaten of the furnace of Hades and agrees to discuss returning with the Deities of Hades. After His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites looks at her, she sends the Ugly-Female-of-Hades, the eight Thunder-Deities, and warriors to pursue him, and later pursues him herself. She threatens to strangle a thousand people of his land each day and is thereafter called the Great-Deity-of-Hades.
