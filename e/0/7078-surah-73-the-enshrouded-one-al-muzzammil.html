Abstract written by a model

Surah 73: The Enshrouded One (Al-Muzzammil) concerns commands to the enshrouded one concerning night vigil, recitation, remembrance, devotion, patience, and reliance on Allah. It warns of punishment for deniers and recalls Pharaoh's rebellion against a messenger. It presents the coming day when the earth and hills quake and the heaven is rent asunder. It concludes by easing the requirement of night vigil and instructing recitation, worship, almsgiving, good works, and seeking forgiveness.
