Abstract written by a model

Daniel's prayer is a thrice-daily act of petition, supplication and thanks to his God, kneeling toward Jerusalem with windows open, continued despite the decree; the men find him praying, grounding their accusation.

Chapter 6

Daniel prays and gives thanks to his God three times a day, kneeling in his house with windows open toward Jerusalem, as he did before the decree. The men find him making petition and supplication, which becomes the basis of the accusation against him.
