Chapter 31

The Assyrian is described as a cedar in Lebanon of high stature, beautiful and nourished by many waters, under whose shadow great nations lived. Its heart was lifted up in its height, so the LORD delivered it to the mighty one of the nations. It was cut down by foreigners and sent down to Sheol with the slain, including those who had been its arm.
