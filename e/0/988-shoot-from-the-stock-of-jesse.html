shoot (from the stock of Jesse)

Also called: A shoot, a branch, his, the root of Jesse

Abstract written by a model

The shoot is a figure from Jesse's stock, upon whom the LORD's Spirit rests with wisdom, might and fear of the LORD. He delights in that fear, judges the poor and humble with righteousness and equity, wears righteousness as a belt, strikes the wicked, stands as a banner the nations seek, and his resting place is glorious.

Chapter 11

The shoot will have the LORD's Spirit resting on him: wisdom, understanding, counsel, might, knowledge and the fear of the LORD. He will delight in the fear of the LORD, judge not by sight or hearing but with righteousness and equity for the poor and humble, strike the earth with the rod of his mouth and kill the wicked with the breath of his lips. Righteousness and faithfulness will be his belt.
