Arishtanêmi

Abstract written by a model

Arishtanêmi, Gautama’s black-skinned, powerful son of Samudravigaya and Sivâ, is a saviour and lord of ascetics, honoured by Dasârhas and linked to Vrishnis. En route to marry Râgîmatî, he sees animals for slaughter, renounces, becomes a Kêvalin, and reaches highest perfection.

Twenty-Second Lecture.

Arishtanêmi is the son of Samudravigaya and Sivâ, the saviour of the world, and the lord of ascetics. While travelling to marry Râgîmatî, he sees animals destined for slaughter, renounces, plucks out his hair, and later becomes a Kêvalin who reaches the highest perfection.
