Abstract written by a model

Natural integrity is human perfection without knowledge or evil desires; left unharmed it makes sacrificial vessels, but its destruction enables artisans to produce artificial articles and systems.

CHAPTER IX.

Natural integrity is described as the condition in which people, equally without knowledge and evil desires, remain in a state of natural perfection. Its destruction permits the production of artificial articles and systems.
