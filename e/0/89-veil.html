Abstract written by a model

The veil is a skillfully worked curtain of blue, purple, scarlet and fine twined linen decorated with cherubim. It hangs on four gold-overlaid acacia pillars set in silver sockets, separating the holy place from the most holy, near the ark of the covenant.

Chapter 26

The veil is to be made of blue, purple, scarlet and fine twined linen with cherubim, the work of a skillful workman. It hangs under the clasps on four gold-overlaid acacia pillars with gold hooks on silver sockets, and separates the holy place from the most holy.
