Abstract written by a model

Ephron the Hittite, son of Zohar and member of the children of Heth, owned the cave of Machpelah. He first offered it freely, then, as Abraham insisted on paying, named four hundred shekels of silver and received it.

Chapter 23

Ephron the Hittite, sitting among the children of Heth, was asked through them to sell the cave of Machpelah at the end of his field. He first offered to give the field and cave, then, when Abraham insisted on paying, named a price of four hundred shekels of silver, which he received.
