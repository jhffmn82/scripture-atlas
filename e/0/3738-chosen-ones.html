Abstract written by a model

In The Good News According to Mark, the chosen ones are those chosen by God whom the days of oppression are shortened for, whom false christs and prophets try to lead astray, and whom angels gather at the coming of the Son of Man. In 1 Peter, the chosen ones are the recipients of the letter: believers living as foreigners and pilgrims in the Dispersion, called to holiness, faithful conduct, and hope in Christ.

chosen ones (recipients of the letter) in 1 Peter

author unknown · AD 60 to 112 · the work’s page · read it where chosen ones (recipients of the letter) first appears

Abstract written by a model

The chosen ones are the recipients of the letter: believers living as foreigners and pilgrims in the Dispersion across Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia. They are chosen according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, redeemed by the blood of Christ rather than silver or gold, healed by his wounds, and guarded by God's power through faith, with an incorruptible, undefiled inheritance reserved in Heaven. They love Jesus Christ though unseen. The records open by describing them as rejoicing amid trials and being called to holiness, fervent love, and obedient purity. Next they are described as living stones built into a spiritual house and holy priesthood, a chosen race, royal priesthood, holy nation, and people of God who have obtained mercy. As free people who are God's bondservants, they are to keep good behavior among the nations and abstain from fleshly lusts. They are then called to bless rather than repay evil, to inherit a blessing, and to be ready to explain their hope with humility and a good conscience. Finally they are told to leave their past Gentile lifestyle and live for God's will rather than human lusts. Facing a fiery trial and insults for Christ's name, they are called blessed, as partakers of Christ's sufferings with the Spirit of glory resting on them, and are told to entrust their souls to God while doing good.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

1 Peter / Chapter 1

The chosen ones are foreigners in the Dispersion in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia. They rejoice though grieved in various trials, love Jesus Christ without having seen him, and are guarded by God's power through faith. They are told to prepare their minds, be sober, be holy in all their behavior, live in reverent fear, and love one another fervently from the heart, having purified their souls in obedience to the truth.

Chapter 2

Believers are told to put away wickedness, deceit, hypocrisies, envies and evil speaking and to long for spiritual milk so they may grow. They are built up as living stones into a spiritual house and holy priesthood, and are called a chosen race, royal priesthood, holy nation and people for God's possession who have now obtained mercy. As foreigners and pilgrims they are to abstain from fleshly lusts, keep good behavior among the nations, and live as free people who are God's bondservants, having returned to the Shepherd after going astray like sheep.

Chapter 3

Believers are urged to be like-minded, compassionate, loving, tenderhearted and courteous, and not to repay evil for evil or insult for insult but to bless, since they were called to inherit a blessing. They are to sanctify the Lord God in their hearts and always be ready to give an answer about their hope with humility, fear and a good conscience. They are told baptism, a symbol tied to the water of Noah's days, now saves them through Jesus Christ's resurrection.

Chapter 4

The believers are told to stop living for human lusts and live for God's will, having already spent enough time in the Gentiles' practices. They are commanded to be sober, love earnestly, show hospitality, and serve with their gifts as managers of God's grace. They face a fiery trial and insults for Christ's name, are called blessed, and are told to rejoice, not be ashamed, and entrust their souls to God while doing good.
