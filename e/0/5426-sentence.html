Abstract written by a model

A saying held by people concerns whether one sentence can make a country prosperous or ruin it: it cannot do so alone, but it may express a ruler’s understanding that aids prosperity or words unopposed that contribute to ruin.

CHAP. XV. 1. The Duke Ting asked whether there was a single

A single sentence is not expected by itself to make a country prosperous or ruin it. One sentence may nevertheless express an understanding that contributes to prosperity or a condition that contributes to ruin.
