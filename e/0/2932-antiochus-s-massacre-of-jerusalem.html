Abstract written by a model

Believing Judea was in revolt, Antiochus took Jerusalem by force and ordered soldiers to kill everyone in their path, including houses' occupants. Over three days eighty thousand died, forty thousand in close combat, and as many were enslaved; all ages were slain.

Chapter 5

Antiochus took the city by force and commanded his soldiers to cut down all in their way and kill those in their houses. In three days eighty thousand were destroyed, forty thousand in close combat, and no fewer were sold into slavery than were slain. Young and old, women, children, virgins and infants were killed.
