Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Job, the wicked are described as having failing eyes, no escape, and only death as their hope. In Proverbs, the wicked are defined by perverse speech, abandoning upright paths for darkness, delighting in evil, crooked ways, treachery, and the consequences of their wickedness. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, the wicked are associated with stumbling blocks, evil things created for sinners, and punishment to destruction. In Surah 80: He frowned (Abasa), the wicked are among those whose faces on that day bear dust and are veiled in darkness.

the wicked in Proverbs

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where the wicked first appears

Abstract written by a model

The wicked are a group defined by perverse speech, abandoning upright paths for darkness, delighting in evil, and crooked ways. They are treacherous and destined to be rooted out of the land. Their own wickedness brings them down; they take the righteous's place in trouble, destroy neighbors with their mouths, earn deceitful wages, expect wrath, and will not go unpunished. Trust in riches fails them, and they are ultimately repaid.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Evil men speak perverse things, forsake the paths of uprightness to walk in darkness, rejoice to do evil, and are crooked and wayward in their paths. The son is to be delivered from them, and the wicked and treacherous are said to be cut off and rooted out of the land.

Chapter 11

The wicked fall by their own wickedness and take the place of the righteous in trouble. When one dies, hope perishes, and his mouth destroys his neighbor and overthrows the city. They earn deceitful wages, expect only wrath, and will not go unpunished. The chapter says they will be repaid even more than the righteous.
