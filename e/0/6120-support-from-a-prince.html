support from a prince

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: pay, prince's help, stated support, support, the prince supports me

Abstract written by a model

A scholar may accept a prince's gift of grain or other assistance for necessities but should not accept regular princely pay without an office. Proper support is offered initially with the prince's commission and then continued through the storekeeper and master of the kitchen without repeated direct commissions.
