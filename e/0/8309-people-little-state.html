people (little state)

group · 1 work · 14 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: people, the people

Abstract written by a model

A little state with a small population values simplicity and treats death as grievous. It has boats, carriages, buff coats, and sharp weapons but does not use them. People avoid exceptional abilities, travel, weapons, and communication with neighbouring states; use knotted cords; and find coarse food, plain clothes, poor dwellings, and common ways satisfying.

80. 1. In a little state with a small population, I would so order it,

The people are made to avoid employment of exceptional abilities, travel, and the use of weapons. They return to knotted cords, value coarse food, plain clothes, poor dwellings, and simple ways, and do not communicate with the neighbouring state.
