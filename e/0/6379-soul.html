Abstract written by a model

In Surah 39: The Groups (Az-Zumar), the soul is received by Allah at death and during sleep; Allah keeps the soul for which He has ordained death and releases the others until an appointed term. In Surah 56: The Inevitable (Al-Waaqia), the soul of the dying person comes up to the throat while people look on; the Lord of the Worlds is nearer to it than they are, though they do not see. In Surah 81: The Overthrowing (At-Takwir), souls are reunited, and every soul will know what it has made ready. In Surah 82: The Cleaving (Al-Infitaar), a soul will know what it sent before and what it left behind, and no soul will have power over any other soul on that day. In Surah 91: The Sun (Ash-Shams), the soul is perfected and inspired with conscience of what is wrong and right for it; it is successful when caused to grow and fails when stunted. In The Table-Talk of the Prophet Mohammad (Part 2), the soul leaves the body at death, with the eyes following it; a believer’s soul has a musk scent, is welcomed by merciful angels and faithful souls, and reaches celestial regions, while an infidel’s soul reeks and is taken by punishing angels.
