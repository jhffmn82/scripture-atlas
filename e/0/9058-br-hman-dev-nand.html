Brâhmanî Devânandâ

person · 2 works · 9 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Devânandâ, the Brâhmanî Devânandâ

Abstract written by a model

In Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra), Devânandâ is the wife of Rishabhadatta whose womb first conceives Mahâvîra and later receives Trisalâ’s fetus. In Kalpa Sutra, Brâhmanî Devânandâ first carries Mahâvîra’s embryo, experiences the fourteen great dreams, and sees them taken from her when the embryo is removed.

Brâhmanî Devânandâ in Kalpa Sutra

Bhadrabâhu · the work’s page · read it where Brâhmanî Devânandâ first appears

Abstract written by a model

Brâhmanî Devânandâ, of the Gâlandharâyana gotra and wife of Rishabhadatta, first carries Mahâvîra’s embryo in her womb. In a state between sleeping and waking, she experiences the fourteen great dreams. On the night the embryo is removed, she sees the dreams taken from her by the Kshatriyânî Trisalâ and awakens.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Kalpa Sutra / Lives of the Ginas. / Life of Mahâvîra.

Devânandâ is a Brâhmanî of the Gâlandharâyana gotra and the wife of Rishabhadatta. She saw the fourteen great dreams and first carried Mahâvîra's embryo in the brahmanical part of Kundagrâma.

In that night in which the embryo of the Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra was removed

The Brâhmanî Devânandâ of the Gâlandharâyana gotra carries the embryo of the Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra until it is removed from her womb. While in a state between sleeping and waking, she sees the fourteen dreams being taken from her by the Kshatriyânî Trisalâ and awakens.
