Adam

person · 5 works · 22 quoted facts · 11 connections
Also called: The first man, The first man Adam, The first man is of the earth, made of dust, The man, her husband, him, husband, male and female, man, one man, one trespass, one who sinned, the man, your husband

Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, Adam is the first man, created by God, who sins, is expelled from Eden, fathers children, and dies at 930. In 1 Chronicles, Adam heads the genealogy leading through Seth and Enosh to Noah. In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, Adam is the one man whose trespass brought sin, death, and condemnation to all. In Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians, Adam is the first man made from dust, through whom death came, contrasted with Christ, the last Adam. In 1 Timothy, Adam is cited as formed before Eve and not deceived, as grounds for restricting women’s teaching.

Adam in Genesis

author unknown · about 449 BC · the work’s page · read it where Adam first appears

Abstract written by a model

Adam is the first man, made by God in his own likeness from dust of the ground, and the name also covers male and female together as created and blessed. God gives them dominion over land animals, fish and birds, and plants for food. Adam eats the fruit his wife gives him, hides from the LORD God, and blames the woman. As a result the ground is cursed, he must labor by the sweat of his face, and he is destined to return to dust. He gains knowledge of good and evil, is clothed in animal skins made by God, and is expelled from Eden to till the ground. He fathers Seth in his own likeness, has other sons and daughters, and dies at 930.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

God makes man in his own image, male and female, on the sixth day. God blesses them, tells them to be fruitful, multiply, fill and subdue the earth and have dominion over the fish, birds and every living thing, and gives them seed-bearing herbs and fruit trees for food.

Chapter 3

Adam eats the fruit his wife gives him, then hides from the LORD God, saying he was afraid because he was naked. He blames the woman whom God gave him. He is told the ground is cursed for his sake, that he will eat with much labor, from thorns and thistles, by the sweat of his face until he returns to dust, and he is sent out of the garden to till the ground from which he was taken.

Chapter 5

Adam was made by God in God's likeness, and God created them male and female, blessed them, and named them Adam. He lived 130 years and fathered Seth in his own likeness, after his image. He then lived 800 more years, fathered other sons and daughters, and died at 930.

Adam in Paul’s Letter to the Romans

author unknown · about AD 57 · the work’s page · read it where Adam first appears

Abstract written by a model

Adam is the one man through whom sin entered the world, followed by death. Though a foreshadowing of Jesus Christ, his single trespass brought condemnation to all and made many sinners, with death reigning from him until Moses.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

Adam is the one man through whom sin entered the world, followed by death through sin. Death reigned from Adam until Moses, even over those whose sins were not like his disobedience. He is described as a foreshadowing of him who was to come, and his one trespass led to condemnation for all men and made many sinners.

Abstract written by a model

Adam is the first man, made of dust and earth, who became a living soul. Death came through him and all die in him; contrasted with Christ, the last Adam, a life-giving spirit, humans bear his earthly image.
