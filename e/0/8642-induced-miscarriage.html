induced miscarriage

event · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Cause thy fruit to perish, destroy the fruit in her womb, procure her miscarriage, produce in herself the menses

Abstract written by a model

Induced miscarriage is murder when a damsel destroys the fruit in her womb. A man may order it from dread of people, and an old woman may use Banga, Shaêta, Ghnâna, Fraspâta, or another drug; the sin falls on all three.

Fargard XV.

A damsel is told not to produce menses or destroy the fruit in her womb from dread of the people. If a man orders it and an old woman uses Banga, Shaêta, Ghnâna, Fraspâta, or another drug to cause miscarriage, the sin is on the man, the damsel, and the old woman.
