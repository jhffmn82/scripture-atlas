Abstract written by a model

A servant given one talent according to his ability buries it out of fear of his hard lord. Judged wicked and slothful for not earning interest, he loses the talent and is cast into outer darkness as an unprofitable servant.

Chapter 25

The servant with one talent dug in the earth and hid his lord's money, explaining that he knew his lord was a hard man and was afraid. His lord called him wicked and slothful, said he should have deposited the money with bankers for interest, took the talent from him, and had him thrown into the outer darkness.
