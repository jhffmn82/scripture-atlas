the painful doom

event · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Our doom, a painful doom, doom, great torment, painful doom, the doom, the doom thereof

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 25: The Criterion (Al-Furqaan), the doom prepared for evil-doers is painful, with Hell's anguish doubled for persistent wrongdoers on the Day of Resurrection. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), the painful doom comes suddenly to those who refuse belief, after which they ask whether they will be reprieved. In Surah 76: Man (Al-Insaan), painful doom is prepared for evil-doers, contrasted with Allah's mercy and the rewards given to the righteous.
