Abstract written by a model

The grace of giving is a collection serving the saints. In Chapter 8, the Macedonians plead to share in it and Titus is to complete it; it is acceptable according to what one has and administered honorably before the Lord and men. In Chapter 9, the Achaians' earlier promise is to be prepared ahead as generosity, given willingly as decided in the heart. It aims at equality, supplies lack, and produces much thanksgiving to God.

Chapter 8

The grace of giving is a service to the saints that the Macedonians pleaded to share in, and that Titus is to complete among the addressees. It is acceptable according to what one has, and is administered by the writer's party to the Lord's glory and to show readiness, with care to be honorable before the Lord and before men.

Chapter 9

The gift was promised by the Achaians earlier and is to be arranged ahead of time so that it is ready as a matter of generosity, not greediness. Each person is to give as determined in the heart, not grudgingly or under compulsion. It makes up for lack among the saints and produces much thanksgiving to God.
