Harikêsa-Bala

Abstract written by a model

Harikêsa-Bala, born in a family of Svapâkas, becomes a chaste, self-controlled monk and sage. He practices mendicancy, penance, Samitis, and Guptis, owns no property, and receives food after a month’s fast. At a Brahmanical sacrifice he is mocked and beaten while begging, yet remains without hatred, accepts apology and food, and teaches true sacrifice and purity.

Twelfth Lecture

Harikêsa-Bala was born in a family of Svapâkas, became a monk and sage, subdued his senses, and observed the rules of mendicancy. While begging at a Brahmanical sacrifice, he was mocked and beaten, but he remained without hatred, accepted the Brahmanas’ apology and food, and taught them about true sacrifice and purity.
