Abstract written by a model

The presidents and local governors, accountable to King Darius, oppose Daniel. Finding no fault in him, they target his God's law, trick Darius into the thirty-day decree, accuse Daniel after seeing him pray, and are finally cast with their families into the lions' den and killed.

Chapter 6

The presidents and local governors seek an occasion against Daniel but find no fault, so they decide to target his God's law. They persuade Darius to sign the thirty-day decree, find Daniel praying, and accuse him before the king, pressing Darius that the law cannot be changed. Afterward they, with their children and wives, are cast into the den of lions and killed.
