work and self-support

Also called: don’t let him eat, eat their own bread, labor and travail, work

Abstract written by a model

Paul says he and his companions did not eat anyone's bread without paying, but labored night and day so as not to burden anyone, though they had the right to support, in order to set an example. The chapter commands that those who do not work should not eat, and that the idle should work quietly and eat their own bread.
