Chapter 23

The son is told to listen to his father who gave him life and not to despise his mother when she is old. The father of the righteous and the parent of a wise child delight in him, and the father and mother are to be glad and rejoice. The speaker's own heart is also said to rejoice when the son speaks what is right.
