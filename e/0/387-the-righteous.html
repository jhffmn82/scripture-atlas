Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the righteous who take refuge in God are loved by the LORD and love the LORD’s name. In Psalms, the righteous see God’s destruction of the wicked man, fear and laugh at him, and say that he trusted in riches and wickedness instead of making God his strength. In Psalms, the righteous rejoice on seeing vengeance and washing their feet in the wicked’s blood, and people affirm that a reward awaits them. In Proverbs, the righteous delight in justice and give generously; the Righteous One brings the wicked to ruin, while the righteous are ransomed and the upright establish their ways. In Ecclesiastes or, The Preacher, the righteous, together with the wise and their works, are in God’s hand and share the same events as the wicked. In The Wisdom of Solomon, the righteous are held in God’s hand, tested and found worthy, rest in peace with hope of immortality, and receive great good, authority, and life with God. In Habakkuk, the righteous are surrounded and swallowed up by the wicked while justice is perverted, yet they are defined as those who live by faith. In The Gospel According to Matthew, the righteous inherit the Kingdom prepared from the world’s foundation, learn that serving the least of the King’s brothers served him, and enter eternal life.
