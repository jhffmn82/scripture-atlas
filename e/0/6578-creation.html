creation

event · 8 works · 7 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Creation of Allah, We created not the heaven and the earth and all that is between them, brought you into being, created, created the earth, created the heavens and the earth, created you, new creation, the Creation of Allah, the creation of the earth and heavens, the creation of the heavens and the earth, the first creation

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 6: The Cattle (Al-An'aam), creation refers to Allah creating the heavens and the earth, people from clay, and animals and flying creatures as peoples like humanity. In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), creation refers to Allah creating the heavens and the earth, Adam, humanity, and all creation; His are both creation and commandment. In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), the creation of the heavens and the earth refers to Allah creating them and all that is between them in truth, not in play. In Surah 31: Luqman, Creation of Allah includes the heavens, the earth, hills, beasts, plants, and all that Allah has made serviceable to mankind. In Surah 35: The Originator (Faatir), creation refers to Allah creating the heavens and the earth, angels, mankind, pairs, seas, plants, animals, and cattle, and multiplying in creation what He wills. In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), the creation of the earth and heavens refers to Allah creating the earth in two Days, supplying it, and ordaining the seven heavens in two Days with power and knowledge. In Surah 43: Ornaments of gold (Az-Zukhruf), creation refers to Allah creating the heavens, the earth, all pairs, and the people who question these matters. In Surah 50: The letter Qaaf (Qaaf), creation refers to Allah creating the heavens, the earth, and all between them in six Days, without weariness, and being able to create man again.
