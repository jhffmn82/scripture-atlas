Abstract written by a model

Prince of Ch'u rules the Ch'u State and is dignified, severe, and merciless in punishment. Told that preserving Ch'u will not preserve him because he lacks TAO, he later refuses Tsê Yang; only an exceptionally practised or perfect person could influence him.

CHAPTER XXI.

The Prince of Ch'u is told that the preservation of the Ch'u State will not preserve his existence because he is without TAO. From this perspective, the Ch'us have not begun to exist even while the Fans have not begun to be destroyed.

CHAPTER XXV.

The prince of Ch'u refuses to receive Tsê Yang. He is described as severe and merciless, so that only a very practised or perfect person could influence him.
