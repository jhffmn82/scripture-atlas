Chapter 2

The dream troubles the king's spirit and takes away his sleep, and he has forgotten it. It is revealed to Daniel in a night vision and told to the king as a vision of a great image struck and destroyed by a stone that becomes a mountain filling the earth. Daniel declares the dream certain and its interpretation sure.
