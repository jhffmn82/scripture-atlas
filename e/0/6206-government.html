Mencius, Book IV (Li Lou) / Part I / Chapter 1

Government requires both virtue and laws, since virtue alone cannot exercise it and laws cannot carry themselves into practice. It should proceed according to the ways of the former kings and be guided by concern for the sufferings of men.

Chapter 4

A man's government may be unsuccessful when he is trying to rule others, and he should then examine his wisdom.
