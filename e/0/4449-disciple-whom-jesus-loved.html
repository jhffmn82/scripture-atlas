Abstract written by a model

The disciple whom Jesus loved is a witness whose testimony is declared true. He leaned on Jesus' chest at the supper. At the cross, Jesus tells him "Behold, your mother!" and he takes Mary to his own home. He outruns Peter to the tomb, enters, sees the cloths, and believes. On the beach he first recognizes Jesus: "It's the Lord!" A rumor that he would not die is corrected, since Jesus did not say so. He is identified as the one who testifies about and wrote these things.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 19

The disciple whom Jesus loved stands by the cross. Jesus tells him "Behold, your mother!" and from that hour he takes her to his own home. The text identifies him as one who has seen and testified, and whose testimony is true.

Chapter 20

The other disciple whom Jesus loved runs with Peter to the tomb and outruns him, arriving first, and stoops to look in without entering. He then enters after Peter, sees the cloths, and believes. It is noted that they did not yet know the Scripture that Jesus must rise from the dead.

Chapter 21

This disciple is the first to recognize Jesus on the beach, saying to Peter, "It's the Lord!" He is seen following Jesus and Peter, and is identified as the one who leaned on Jesus' chest at the supper. A rumor that he would not die arises, which the text corrects, and he is identified as the one who testifies about and wrote these things.
