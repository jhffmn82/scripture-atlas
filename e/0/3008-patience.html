Abstract written by a model

In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, patience is urged during humiliation and calamity, while those who lose it are warned of woe. In The Letter from James, patience is commanded until the Lord's coming and illustrated through the farmer, suffering prophets, and Job, with those who endure called blessed.

Abstract written by a model

Patience is a virtue commanded until the Lord's coming; it is illustrated by the farmer awaiting the precious fruit, then by suffering prophets and Job's perseverance, with those who endured called blessed.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

Patience is commanded until the coming of the Lord, illustrated by the farmer waiting for the early and late rain. It is further exemplified by the prophets who suffered and by Job, whose perseverance is recalled, and those who endured are called blessed.
