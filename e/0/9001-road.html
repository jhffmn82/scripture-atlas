Abstract written by a model

A road may contain living beings, seeds, grass, water, mud, walls, ditches, ramparts, or gates and pass through forest. During rainy season, avoid taking it straight when an unobstructed byway exists; avoid dangerous roads when another route is available.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Third Lecture, Called Walking. / First Lesson. .. Third Lesson.

A road containing many living beings, seeds, grass, water, or mud should not be taken straight when an unobstructed byway exists. Roads through dangerous territories, unstable governments, uncertain forests, armies, transports, or thieves should be avoided when another route is available.
