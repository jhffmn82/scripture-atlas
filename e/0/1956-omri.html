Chapter 16

Omri, captain of the army, is made king by all Israel in the camp at Gibbethon, then besieges Tirzah and ends Zimri's reign. After overcoming Tibni's faction he reigns twelve years, buys the hill Samaria from Shemer for two talents of silver and builds the city. He does evil above all before him, walks in Jeroboam's way, dies and is buried in Samaria, and Ahab succeeds him.
