Abstract written by a model

The temple's measurements are given with a reed of six cubits, each a cubit and a hand width. Chapter 40 sets the wall at one reed thick and high, gates at fifty by twenty-five cubits, and courts and gate distances at one hundred cubits. Chapter 41 then details the nave (twenty by forty), inner room (twenty by twenty), six-cubit house wall, four-cubit side rooms, and a one-hundred-cubit temple length.

Chapter 40

The man measures with a reed of six cubits, each cubit a cubit and a hand width. The wall is one reed thick and one reed high. Gate thresholds and lodges are one reed, and the gates are fifty cubits by twenty-five, with courts and gate-to-gate distances of one hundred cubits.

Chapter 41

The measurements include a nave twenty cubits wide and forty long, an inner room of twenty by twenty cubits, a house wall of six cubits, and side rooms of four cubits. The raised base is a full reed of six great cubits, and the open area is five cubits. The temple and the separate place with its building each measure one hundred cubits in length, and the east face measures one hundred cubits.
