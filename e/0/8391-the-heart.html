the heart

object · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: every heart, heart, his heart, his own heart, intellect, man's heart, mind, minds, my heart, the human heart, thy heart, understanding

Abstract written by a model

The heart contains God and God’s eternal light, yet without God’s essence and the Word it is impure, changeful, unstable, and burdened by pride, worldly love, desire, lust, and greed. Through the Guru’s instruction, favour, and meditation on the Name, it becomes fixed and pure, gains divine knowledge, happiness, truth, nectar, freedom from disease, and extinguished burning.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Sri Rag .. Ashtapadi

The heart is impure without the Word and becomes burdened by pride, worldly love, and desire. When God's name penetrates the heart, it receives happiness, truth, and freedom from disease.

Rag Gauri .. Gauri Chhant

The mind is unstable when absorbed in lust, greed, pride, and worldly desire. Through the Guru’s instruction and meditation on the Name, it becomes fixed, pure, and free from wandering thoughts.

Rag Malar .. Malar ki War

The human heart is the place where the inaccessible and illimitable God dwells. Through the Guru's favour, people find God there, receive nectar, and have their burning extinguished.
