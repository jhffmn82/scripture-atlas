Chapter 7

A Midianite tells his fellow that he dreamed a cake of barley bread tumbled into the camp and struck a tent so that it lay flat. His fellow interprets it as the sword of Gideon, with God delivering Midian and its army into his hand. Gideon overhears this, worships, and returns to rouse Israel.
