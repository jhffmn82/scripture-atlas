Also called: the adversaries of Judah and Benjamin, the people of the land

Abstract written by a model

The adversaries of Judah and Benjamin, called the people of the land, ask to join temple building, claiming they seek Israel's God and have sacrificed since Esar Haddon's days. Zerubbabel rejects them, after which they weaken Judah's hands, trouble the builders, and hire counselors to frustrate the work.

Chapter 4

They hear that the captives are building a temple and ask to join, claiming they seek the same God and have sacrificed to him since the days of Esar Haddon king of Assyria. After being refused, they are identified as the people of the land, who weaken the hands of Judah, trouble the builders, and hire counselors against them to frustrate the work.
