wheels (beside the living creatures)

Abstract written by a model

The wheels are four beryl-like wheels, each a wheel within a wheel, standing beside the living creatures (cherubim) in the vision. Their high, dreadful rims are full of eyes all around. They travel in four directions without turning. Most importantly, they go, stand and lift up together with the creatures because the spirit of the living creature is within them. Chapter 1 introduces them on the earth beside each creature; Chapter 10 repeats the description with the cherubim and names them "the whirling wheels."

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

A wheel on the earth stands beside each living creature, appearing like beryl, as a wheel within a wheel, moving in four directions without turning. Their high, dreadful rims are full of eyes all around. They go, stand and are lifted up together with the living creatures, because the spirit of the living creature is in the wheels.

Chapter 10

Four wheels, one beside each cherub, look like beryl stone, each like a wheel within a wheel, and are full of eyes all around. They go in four directions without turning, and they move, stand, and mount up with the cherubim because the spirit of the living creature is in them. They are called "the whirling wheels" in the narrator's hearing.
