Chapter 13

The inhabitants are told to wail, and their hands will be feeble and their hearts will melt. They will be in pain like a woman in labor, with faces of flame, and will flee like hunted gazelles to their own people and lands. Those found or captured will be killed by the sword, their infants dashed in pieces, their houses ransacked, and their wives raped.
