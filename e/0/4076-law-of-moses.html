law of Moses

topic · 4 works · 20 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: law, law hath become dead, law shall be fulfilled, law was given, living revelations, the custom of Moses, the customs which Moses delivered to us, the law, the law and the prophets, the law of Moses, the law which was given unto Moses

Abstract written by a model

In The Acts of the Apostles, the law of Moses is read in synagogues every Sabbath, while the council declines to impose it on Gentile believers. In The Second Book of Nephi, the law of Moses is given by God to typify and prove Christ’s coming, is kept until Christ fulfills it, and then becomes dead to Nephi’s people. In The Book of Jacob, the Brother of Nephi, the law of Moses is kept by Jacob’s people, sanctifies them for righteousness, and points souls to Christ. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the law of Moses is fulfilled completely in Jesus, has an end in him, and is to be remembered.

the law of Moses in The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman

Mormon · the work’s page · read it where the law of Moses first appears

Abstract written by a model

The law of Moses is a law containing statutes and judgments, commanded to Moses in Horeb for all Israel. Some wrongly teach that it need no longer be observed, but it must be fulfilled in every whit, without loss of a jot or tittle until all is fulfilled. Jesus did not come to destroy it or the prophets; the law is fulfilled completely in him and has an end in him, while old things are done away and all things become new. Yet it is to be remembered.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman / Chapter 1

Some people preach that it is no longer expedient to observe the law of Moses, but they err. They are converted and learn that the law must be fulfilled in every whit, with not one jot or tittle passing away until all is fulfilled.

Chapter 12

Jesus says that he did not come to destroy the law or the prophets but to fulfill them. The law is fulfilled in him, and old things are done away while all things become new.

Chapter 15

The law given unto Moses is fulfilled in Jesus and has an end in him. Jesus says he does not destroy the prophets or the things that are yet to come.

Chapter 25

The law of Moses is to be remembered. It was commanded to Moses in Horeb for all Israel, with statutes and judgments.
