Abstract written by a model

promises burnt offering of whatever first comes out of his house [to the LORD, if he delivers Ammon] Jephthah vows that if the LORD delivers Ammon into his hand, whatever comes out of his house doors to meet him on his peaceful return will be the LORD's and he will offer it as a burnt offering. The LORD grants the victory, his daughter is the first to come out, and Jephthah says he cannot go back on what he has said to the LORD; he does with her according to the vow.
