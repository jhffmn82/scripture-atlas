Toyo-mike-kashiki-ya-hime

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

Toyo-mike-kashiki-ya-hime, an august child of Emperor Kim-Mei, became Empress Sui-Ko and ruled the Empire after His Augustness Tachibana-no-toyo-hi. Wife and half-sister of Nuna-kura-futo-tama-shiki, she bore eight children, dwelt at the Palace of Wohorida, and ruled for thirty seven years.

Toyo-mike-kashiki-ya-hime is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
