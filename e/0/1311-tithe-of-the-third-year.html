Chapter 26

In the third year, called the year of tithing, the people finish tithing all their increase and give it to the Levite, the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow, so that they may eat within the gates and be filled. The giver then declares before the LORD that he has put away the holy things, not eaten of it in mourning, not removed it while unclean, and not given of it for the dead.
