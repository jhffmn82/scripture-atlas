Abstract written by a model

Israel's punishment is the LORD's judgment on Jacob and Israel for refusing to walk in his ways and disobeying his law. He gives them to robbers, leaving them plundered, snared, imprisoned, and undelivered. His fierce anger sets them ablaze, yet they neither understand nor take it to heart.

Chapter 42

Israel is described as a robbed and plundered people, snared in holes, hidden in prisons, and made captives with no one to deliver them or say 'Restore them!' The LORD gave Jacob and Israel to the robbers because they would not walk in his ways and disobeyed his law. He poured the fierceness of his anger and the strength of battle on him, which set him on fire all around, yet he did not know or take it to heart.
