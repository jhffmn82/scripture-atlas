Abstract written by a model

Raguel keeps the wedding feast for fourteen days. Before it ends he swears that Tobias must not leave until all fourteen days are fulfilled, after which Tobias may take half of Raguel's goods and go in safety to his father. The wedding feast is the occasion Tobias wants Gabael to attend, and he stays because Raguel has sworn that he must not depart. Raphael and Gabael rise early and arrive at the feast together, and Tobias blesses his wife.
