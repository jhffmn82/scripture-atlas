Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, water comes abundantly from the rock for the congregation and their livestock, and Israel promises Edom to pay for any water they drink along the highway. In The Wisdom of Solomon, water comes from flinty, hard stone to the righteous when they thirst and call on God, healing their thirst.
