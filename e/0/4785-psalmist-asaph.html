Chapter 77

The psalmist cries to God day and night, stretches out his hand without tiring, and refuses comfort, groaning at the thought of God and being too troubled to speak. He recalls his song in the night, asks searching questions about God's favor, and then resolves to remember and meditate on the LORD's deeds and wonders of old.
