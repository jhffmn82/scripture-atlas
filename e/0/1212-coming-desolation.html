Coming desolation is a judgment answering 'how long?': it lasts until cities and houses stand empty and the land is utterly waste, the LORD having removed men far away. Even a surviving tenth is consumed, leaving a stump, the holy seed.

Chapter 6

In answer to 'how long?', the Lord says it will last until cities are waste without inhabitant, houses without man, and the land utterly waste. The LORD will have removed men far away and the forsaken places will be many within the land. Even a surviving tenth will be consumed, like a terebinth or oak whose stump remains, and the holy seed is that stump.
