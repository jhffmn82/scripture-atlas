guidance

topic · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Our paths, guide me, the Way, the straight path, the way

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 10: Jonas (Yunus), guidance leads to the truth and a straight path, benefiting those who follow it and leaving those who err to harm only themselves. In Surah 29: The Spider (Al-Ankaboot), guidance leads those who strive in Allah to His paths, while Satan's deception debarred A'ad and Thamud from the Way. In Surah 37: Those drawn up in Ranks (As-Saaffaat), guidance is shown through Allah guiding those who turn to Him and showing Moses and Aaron the right path.
