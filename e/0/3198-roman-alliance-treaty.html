Abstract written by a model

A treaty of alliance between the Romans and the Jews, inscribed on bronze tables and sent to Jerusalem as a memorial of peace. Each side pledges willing military aid in war and withholds supplies, food, weapons, money, and ships from the other's enemies. Both may add or remove terms by mutual agreement, with changes ratified.

Chapter 8

The treaty, written on bronze tables and sent to Jerusalem as a memorial, binds Rome and the Jews to help each other in war and to give no supplies, food, weapons, money, or ships to each other's enemies. Both parties may add to or remove terms by mutual decision, and such changes are ratified.
