worldly love

topic · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: love of everything, love of mammon, such love as the worldly feel, worldly love and superstition

Abstract written by a model

Worldly love is a destructive stream of fire and worldly sin that binds people to desires, family, possessions, wealth, and sensual pleasures. It intoxicates the mind, creates anxiety, error, transmigration, and the pain of birth and death, causing people to forget God’s Name and waste human life. It begins in attachment and pursuit, deepens into entanglement and suffering, and departs through God’s favour, the Guru’s instruction, the Guru's Word, and God's Name.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Rag Asa .. Asa Ashtapadi

Worldly love binds people to family, possessions, desires, sin, and transmigration. It causes people to forget God’s Name, waste their human lives, and suffer, but it departs through God’s favour and the Guru’s instruction.

Rag Gujari .. Gujari Ashtapadi

Worldly love intoxicates the mind and causes people to pursue wealth, while saturating them with the pain of birth and death. Being imbued with the Guru's Word and God's Name frees a person from worldly love.

Ramkali .. Ramkali ki War I

Worldly love attaches people to gold, woman, wealth, houses, and sensual pleasures. It intoxicates the mind, causes anxiety and entanglement, and must be overcome through the Guru's instruction.
