Korah

Also called: Korah, the son of Izhar, the son of Kohath, the son of Levi, Korahites, Korah’s men

Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, Korah leads a challenge against Moses and Aaron, seeking priesthood, and is swallowed alive by the earth with his household. In Surah 28: The Stories (Al-Qasas), Korah oppresses Moses’ folk, exults in his immense wealth, refuses admonition, and is swallowed by the earth with his dwelling-place.

Abstract written by a model

Korah, a Levite son of Izhar, leads a challenge against Moses and Aaron claiming the whole congregation is holy; rebuked for seeking priesthood, he assembles the people and is swallowed alive by the earth with his household.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

Korah, son of Izhar and a descendant of Levi, leads a challenge against Moses and Aaron, saying the whole congregation is holy. Moses rebukes him for seeking the priesthood in addition to his Levite service. Korah assembles the congregation at the Tent of Meeting and is swallowed alive by the earth with his household and goods.
