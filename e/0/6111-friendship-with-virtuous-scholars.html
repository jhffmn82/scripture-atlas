friendship with virtuous scholars

Also called: be on terms of friendship, friendship, intercourse, make friends

Abstract written by a model

Friendship with virtuous scholars is grounded in respect, expressed through presents, and rejects presumptions from age, station, or relatives. It begins with friendship based on virtue, extends to virtuous scholars in one’s village, State, or kingdom, and culminates in studying the poems, books, and histories of antiquity.

Part II / Chapter 1 .. Chapter 5

Friendship should be maintained without presumption based on age, station, or relatives. It is friendship with a person's virtue and does not admit assumptions of superiority.

Chapter 6 .. Chapter 9

A scholar distinguished by virtue in a village, State, or kingdom makes friends with the virtuous scholars of that place. A scholar who remains unsatisfied ascends further by studying the poems, books, and histories of the men of antiquity.
