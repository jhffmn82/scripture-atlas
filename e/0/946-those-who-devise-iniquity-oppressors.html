those who devise iniquity (oppressors)

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: these people, they, those who devise iniquity, you

Abstract written by a model

Oppressors who plan evil on their beds and carry it out at morning light because they have the power. They covet and seize fields and houses, oppressing a man, his house and his heritage, including the LORD's people. They have no one dividing land by lot in the LORD's assembly. The LORD declares disaster against them, and they will be mocked with a lament.
