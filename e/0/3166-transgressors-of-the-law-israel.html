Abstract written by a model

Transgressors of the law arose from Israel, persuading many to covenant with the Gentiles, blaming separation for evils. With the king's authorization they adopted Gentile ordinances, built a Jerusalem gymnasium, became uncircumcised, forsook the holy covenant, and sold themselves to evil. Later, a sinful nation of such transgressors was placed in the city of David citadel.

Chapter 1

Transgressors of the law came out of Israel and persuaded many to make a covenant with the Gentiles, believing separation had brought evils. Some went to the king, who authorized them to follow Gentile ordinances, so they built a gymnasium in Jerusalem, made themselves uncircumcised, forsook the holy covenant, and sold themselves to do evil. Later a sinful nation of transgressors of the law was placed in the city of David citadel.
