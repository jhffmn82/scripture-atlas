Abstract written by a model

A bronze basin with a bronze base, made from mirrors, set between the Tent of Meeting and the altar of burnt offering and filled with water. Aaron and his sons must wash their hands and feet in it before entering the Tent or approaching the altar, or they die. This is a statute forever; the basin is also anointed with holy oil.

Chapter 30

The basin and its base are to be made of bronze and set between the Tent of Meeting and the altar, filled with water. Aaron and his sons wash their hands and feet in it before entering the Tent or approaching the altar, as a statute forever. The basin and its base are also anointed with the holy oil.
