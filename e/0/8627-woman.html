Abstract written by a model

Woman is described as rich in children, milk, seed, fat, marrow, and offspring. Intercourse with her during bleeding, or while pregnant if harm leads to her death, makes a man a Peshôtanu; later she is made pure and strong.

Fargard XV.

Intercourse with a woman who has an issue of blood is one of the five sins that makes a man a Peshôtanu. Intercourse with a woman quick with child makes a man a Peshôtanu if mischief follows and she dies.

Fargard XXI.

The woman is made pure in body and strength and is made rich in children and milk. She is also described as rich in seed, fat, marrow, and offspring.
