eight-forked serpent of Koshi

object · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: eight-forked serpent, the serpent

Abstract written by a model

An eight-forked serpent from Koshi has one body, eight heads and tails, akakagachi-like eyes, and moss, chamaecyparis, and cryptomerias growing on it. Spanning eight valleys and hills, with a bloody, inflamed belly, it devours eight young girls yearly before drinking liquor, sleeping, and being cut into pieces.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Sect.

It has eight heads and eight tails, eyes like akakagachi, and a body covered with moss, chamaecyparis, and cryptomerias. Its length extends over eight valleys and eight hills, and its belly is constantly bloody and inflamed. It drinks from eight vats of liquor, becomes intoxicated and sleeps, and is then cut into pieces by His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness.
