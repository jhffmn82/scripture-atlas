Sadducees

group · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Some Sadducees, Some of the Sadducees, the Sadducees

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, the Sadducees deny the resurrection, join the Pharisees in testing Jesus, and question him about a woman married to seven brothers. In The Good News According to Mark, the Sadducees assert that there is no resurrection and question Jesus about a woman who married seven brothers. In The Gospel According to Luke, the Sadducees deny the resurrection and question Jesus about whose wife a woman married to seven brothers will be in the resurrection.

Sadducees in The Gospel According to Matthew

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Sadducees first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Sadducees are a group who deny the resurrection. They first join the Pharisees to test Jesus with a demand for a sign, earning rebuke and a warning about their teaching as yeast; later their seven-brothers question is answered by Jesus, silencing them.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

The Sadducees came with the Pharisees to test Jesus, asking for a sign from heaven. Jesus rebuked them as part of an evil and adulterous generation and warned his disciples to beware of their teaching, called yeast.

Chapter 22

The Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, ask Jesus about a woman who married seven brothers in turn. Jesus tells them they are mistaken, not knowing the Scriptures or the power of God, and the Pharisees hear that he has silenced them.
