Abstract written by a model

The man who fears the LORD is blessed, delighting greatly in the commandments and being gracious, merciful, and righteous. He lends and gives to the poor, and has mighty offspring and wealth. Trusting the LORD with a steadfast heart, he is never shaken and fears no evil news. He ends unafraid of adversaries, remembered forever, his horn exalted with honor.

Chapter 112

The man who fears the LORD is called blessed, delights greatly in the LORD's commandments, and is gracious, merciful, and righteous. He deals graciously, lends, and gives to the poor. His offspring will be mighty, wealth and riches are in his house, and his righteousness endures forever. He will never be shaken, will be remembered forever, will not fear evil news, will not fear when he sees his adversaries, and will have his horn exalted with honor.
