Guru Ram Das in So Dar and So Purukh (the Rahiras)

The Sikh Religion · the work’s page · read it where Guru Ram Das first appears

Abstract written by a model

Guru Ram Das, associated with Rag Gujari, teaches servants, the true Guru, and saints to supplicate and meditate on God, seek enlightenment through His Name, and serve Him. The company of saints reveals God’s attributes; pleasing God brings happiness, emancipation, fearlessness, and absorption in the Supreme Being within every heart.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Sodar

Guru Ram Das urges servants of God and the true Guru to supplicate Him and seek enlightenment with God’s Name. He teaches that the company of the saints reveals God’s attributes and brings the relish of His Name.

So Purukh

Guru Ram Das praises God as the true Creator, the giver and enjoyer, and the one Supreme Being contained in every heart. He teaches that those who meditate on and serve God become happy, emancipated, fearless, and absorbed in Him. He also teaches that only those saints who please God are truly good saints.
