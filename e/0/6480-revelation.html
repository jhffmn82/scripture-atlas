revelation

topic · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Inspiration, inspire, inspired, inspired in thee, inspireth, revelations, that which is inspired, the inspiration

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 10: Jonas (Yunus), revelation warns mankind, brings good tidings to believers, provides exhortation, healing, guidance, and mercy, and Muhammad is commanded to recite and follow it. In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), revelation is given to messengers, declares that there is only one Allah, and warns and instructs Muhammad. In Surah 42: Consultation (Ash-Shura), revelation is a means by which Allah speaks to mortals and inspires Muhammad with a Spirit of His command.
