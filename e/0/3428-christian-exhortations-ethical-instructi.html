Chapter 5

The chapter gives many instructions: encourage and build each other up, respect and honor those who labor among them, and be at peace. It also tells them to admonish the disorderly, encourage the faint-hearted, support the weak, and be patient toward all. Further commands include not returning evil for evil, always rejoicing, praying without ceasing, giving thanks in everything, not quenching the Spirit, not despising prophecies, testing all things, holding to the good, and abstaining from every form of evil.
