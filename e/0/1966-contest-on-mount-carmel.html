Chapter 18

Elijah proposes that each side prepare a bull on wood with no fire and that the God who answers by fire be God, and the people agree. The prophets of Baal call from morning to the evening offering without answer. Elijah then offers his bull on a soaked altar, the LORD's fire falls, and the people acknowledge the LORD as God.
