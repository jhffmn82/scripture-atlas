Tzŭ Chang asked Man Kou Tê,

Tzŭ Chang argues that practising virtue inspires confidence and leads to reputation, wealth, and a proper place in society. He maintains that duty towards one's neighbour is the true key and challenges Man Kou Tê's rejection of moral distinctions.
