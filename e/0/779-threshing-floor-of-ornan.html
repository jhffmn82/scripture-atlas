Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Samuel, the threshing floor of Araunah is where the LORD's angel stands, and David buys it with the oxen for fifty shekels of silver as the site for an altar. In 1 Chronicles, the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite is where the LORD's angel stands, and David buys it for six hundred shekels of gold as the site for an altar to stop the plague.
