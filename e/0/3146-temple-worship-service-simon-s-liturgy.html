Abstract written by a model

Simon finishes the service at the altars, receives the portions from the priests' hands, and pours the cup of the grape at the foot of the altar. The sons of Aaron then sound trumpets, the people fall in worship, the singers sing, and the people pray until the service is finished. Simon then goes down, lifts his hands over the congregation, and bows a second time to declare the blessing from the Most High.
