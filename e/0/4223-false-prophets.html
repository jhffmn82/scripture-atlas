false prophets

group · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: ravening wolves, sheep’s clothing

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, false prophets are warned against as disguised sheep who are inwardly ravening wolves and are known by their fruits. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, false prophets are described as wearing sheep’s clothing, being inwardly ravening wolves, and being revealed by their fruits.
