Abstract written by a model

has_power_over Egypt [its treasures of gold, silver and precious things] The king of the north is repeatedly in conflict with the king of the south, being attacked by the southern king's successor, who carries off gods and treasures to Egypt, then returning with greater armies, taking a well-fortified city and standing in the glorious land. He gives the daughter of women to corrupt, but she does not stand for him. At the time of the end he comes like a whirlwind with chariots, horsemen and ships, overflows the countries and Egypt, is troubled by news from the east and north, and pitches his palace tents between the sea and the glorious holy mountain before coming to his end with no one to help him.
