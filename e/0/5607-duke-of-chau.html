Abstract written by a model

The duke of Chau completes the virtuous course of Wan and Wu, gives King T'ai and King Chi the title of king, and extends royal sacrificial rules to all ranks. He establishes burial, sacrifice, and mourning rules, expressing far-reaching filial piety by carrying out forefathers’ wishes and undertakings.

Chapter 18

The duke of Chau completed the virtuous course of Wan and Wu. He gave the title of king to T'ai and Chi, extended royal sacrificial rules through the kingdom, and established rules for burial, sacrifice, and mourning.

Chapter 19

The duke of Chau is presented as having far-reaching filial piety. His filial piety is expressed through carrying out the wishes of forefathers and carrying forward their undertakings.
