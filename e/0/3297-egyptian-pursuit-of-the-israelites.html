Chapter 19

After having sped the Israelites eagerly on their way, the Egyptians changed their minds and pursued them while still in the midst of mourning. God foreknew this, and the pursuit was drawn on by the doom they deserved, so that they would complete their punishment and meet a strange death, while his people traveled by a marvelous road.
