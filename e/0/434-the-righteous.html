Abstract written by a model

The righteous are people who walk a path likened to dawning light growing toward perfect day, contrasting the wicked's darkness. They hate lies, are guarded by righteousness, shine brightly, and are rewarded with prosperity, satisfaction, and the sinner's stored wealth.
