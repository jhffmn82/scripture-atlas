wine in The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus

author unknown · about 179 BC · the work’s page · read it where wine first appears

Abstract written by a model

Wine is a substance whose value depends on how it is drunk. In season and moderation it is life-giving, created to gladden men with joy of heart and gladness of soul. Drunk excessively it brings bitterness, rage, conflict, weakened strength and wounds, destroying many and testing the proud's hearts like a furnace tests steel.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 31

Wine has destroyed many, and it tests hearts in the quarreling of the proud as a furnace tests steel. Drunk in season and moderation it is as good as life, created to make men glad, joy of heart and gladness of soul. Drunk excessively it brings bitterness of soul, provocation, conflict, rage, diminished strength and wounds, and one should not rebuke or distress a neighbor at a wine banquet.
