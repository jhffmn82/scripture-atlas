fatal winters

Abstract written by a model

Fatal winters are a work of the Daêvas and a counter-creation of Angra Mainyu, foretold by Ahura Mazda. They come upon the material world with fierce, foul frost and deep snow, bringing winter’s worst plagues and causing all three sorts of beasts to perish.

Vendidad (Vendîdâd) / Fargard I.

Winter is described as a counter-creation of Angra Mainyu and a work of the Daêvas. In Airyana Vaêgô, there are ten winter months and two summer months, and winter falls with the worst of its plagues.

Fargard II.

The fatal winters are foretold by Ahura Mazda as coming upon the material world. They bring fierce, foul frost and deep snow, causing the three sorts of beasts to perish.
