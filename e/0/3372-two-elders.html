Abstract written by a model

Two elders, appointed judges that year, frequent Joakim's house where litigants come. Lustful toward Susanna, they spy on her, ambush her in the garden, and falsely accuse her when refused. Daniel questions them separately, exposes contradictory testimony, and they are executed under the law of Moses.

Chapter 13

The two elders, appointed judges that year, become inflamed with lust for Susanna, secretly watch her, and hide in the garden to ambush her. When she refuses, they falsely accuse her of lying with a young man, and the assembly believes them and condemns her. Daniel separates and questions them, they name different trees, and the assembly puts them to death according to the law of Moses.
