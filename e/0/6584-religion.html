religion

topic · 6 works · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Al-Islam, al-Islam, making religion pure, my religion, pure religion, religion pure for Him, the religion, their religion, this your religion, true religion, your religion

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida), religion is al-Islam, which Allah has perfected, completed His favour through, and chosen for believers. In Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida), al-Islam is identified as the religion Allah has chosen after perfecting the religion and completing His favour. In The Believers (Al-Muminoon), the religion is one religion, and Allah is its Lord; mankind breaks it into sects. In Surah 39: The Groups (Az-Zumar), pure religion belongs to Allah alone, and those who worship others while claiming they bring them near to Allah are judged concerning their differences. In Surah 40: The Forgiver (Al-Ghaafir), religion is to be made pure for Allah alone, while Pharaoh claims to fear that Moses will alter the people's religion. In Surah 61: The Ranks (As-Saff), Al-Islam is what a person is summoned unto, but Allah does not guide wrongdoing folk. In Surah 98: The Evidence (Al-Bayyina), religion is to be kept pure for Allah, with service to Allah, establishment of worship, and payment of the poor-due; this is described as true religion.
