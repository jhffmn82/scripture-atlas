Musalman

group · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: A Musalman, Muhammadan, Musalmans, The Musalmans

Abstract written by a model

In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), Musalmans praise and study Shariat, while God’s servants serve Him to behold Him; their ashes become burning, weeping vessels and bricks. In Hymns of Guru Nanak, Musalman is one who cleanses impurity and requires a guru or priest for acceptance.
