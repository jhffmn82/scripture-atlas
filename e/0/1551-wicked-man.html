Abstract written by a model

The wicked man, portrayed in Chapter 15, is a proud oppressor who defies God with a stiff neck. He is fat yet not wealthy, suffers pain and terrors all his days, wanders for bread, and lives in desolate cities. His substance fails, and the breath of God's mouth destroys him, leaving him in darkness.

Chapter 15

The wicked man is portrayed as an oppressor who stretches out his hand against God, behaves proudly against the Almighty, and runs at him with a stiff neck. He is said to writhe in pain all his days, hear terrors, wander for bread, and see his wealth, branches, and fruit fail.
