Abstract written by a model

A woman is portrayed as a young bride and wife whose whole life is directed toward God as her Spouse and Beloved. Separated from Him, she is dishonoured, sleepless, impatient, and unable to find satisfaction in worldly ornaments, food, or pleasures. She seeks the love of the one God, belongs to Him exclusively, and offers her soul and body to Him. Through the Guru’s word, God’s Name, truthfulness, obedience, and love, she becomes dyed with God’s Name, absorbs herself in God, and experiences happiness, divine companionship, and peace. Her heart, body, and soul are fulfilled by God; she possesses nectar-filled tanks and ambrosial fruit in her own home. Her conduct matters: falsehood and lack of merit cause her Spouse to forget her, while pleasing Him brings honour. The account moves from longing separation to meeting and union with the Bridegroom through God’s favour, culminating in lasting honour, happiness, and peace.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Sri Rag .. Ashtapadi

The virtuous woman practices truth, loves her Spouse, obeys Him, and is decorated with the Guru's word and God's name. She is united with God, does not become a widow, and receives honour and happiness.

Rag Gauri .. Gauri Chhant

The bride suffers sleepless longing and dishonor without her beloved Lord, and worldly ornaments, food, and pleasures cannot satisfy her. She becomes happy and fulfilled when she meets and unites with the Bridegroom through the Name and God’s favor.

Asa Chhant

The young woman seeks the love of the one God, offers her soul and body to her Beloved, and finds happiness through the Word. When God favours her, she treasures the Word, sings a true song of rejoicing, and enjoys divine companionship.

Rag Dhanasari .. Dhanasari Chhant

The woman is described as seeking honour in her Spouse's house by pleasing Him. If she speaks falsely, she is forgotten by her Spouse and experiences painful nights.

Rag Maru Ashtapadi .. Maru ki War I

The devotee woman separated from God suffers impatience and longing, but the woman who remembers the Beloved and finds Him through the Guru obtains peace. She is compared to a wife whose true Husband never forsakes those imbued with love of the True One.
