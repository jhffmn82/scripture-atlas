Son of Heaven

person · 4 works · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: son of Heaven, the Son of Heaven

Abstract written by a model

In The Analects of Confucius, Book XVI (Ke She), the son of Heaven is the source from which ceremonies, music, and punitive military expeditions proceed when good government prevails. In The Great Learning, the Son of Heaven must consider cultivation of the person the root of everything besides. In The Doctrine of the Mean, the Son of Heaven alone holds authority over ceremonies, measures, and written characters. In Mencius, Book V (Wan Zhang), the Son of Heaven appointed an officer to administer Xiang’s state and pay its revenues to him, preventing Xiang from oppressing the people while allowing him to retain honour and wealth.
