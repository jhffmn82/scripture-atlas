Chapter 7

The mother watches all seven sons die in one day and bears it with courage because of her hope in the Lord, and she is called marvelous and worthy of honorable memory. She exhorts each son in the language of her fathers, saying the Creator who shaped all things will mercifully give back their spirit and life. Urged by the king to persuade the youngest, she instead tells him to accept death so she may receive him again with his brothers; she dies last, after her sons.
