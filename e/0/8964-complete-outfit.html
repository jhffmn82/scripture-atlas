complete outfit

Abstract written by a model

A monk or nun should wear the complete outfit when entering a householder’s abode, entering or leaving out-of-door places for religious practices or study, and wandering from village to village. They should not perform these actions with the complete outfit when strong rain, widespread mist, high wind with much dust, or many flying insects are present.
