Abstract written by a model

The daughters of Jerusalem are a chorus located in Jerusalem, addressed by the bride. In Chapter 3 they are adjured not to awaken love prematurely and are linked to Solomon's love-paved carriage. In Chapter 5 she adjures them to tell her beloved she is faint with love; they ask how he excels, prompting her description.

Chapter 3

They are adjured by the speaker, by the roes and hinds of the field, not to stir up or awaken love until it so desires. They are also named as those from whom the middle of Solomon's carriage is paved with love.

Chapter 5

The daughters of Jerusalem are adjured by the bride to tell her beloved, should they find him, that she is faint with love. They respond by asking how her beloved is better than any other, prompting her extended description of him.
