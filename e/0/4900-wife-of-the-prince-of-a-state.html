CHAP. XIV. The wife of the prince of a state is called by him

She is called FU ZAN by the prince of a state. She calls herself HSIAO T'UNG; the people of the State call her CHUN FU ZAN, and to the people of other States she is called K'WA HSIAO CHUN.
