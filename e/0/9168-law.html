Abstract written by a model

The law is pure, unchangeable, and eternal, applying throughout the world to every kind of person. Taught by noble ones and known by sages, it calls for religious life while youth, health, and perceptions endure, through renunciation, control, non-killing, and freedom from possessions. The self-possessed, wise, just, chaste, careful, and zealous understand it correctly. They follow it to overcome Mâra and sinfulness, learn restraint and repentance, avoid harming living beings, and ultimately reach final liberation.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Second Lecture, Called Conquest of the World. / First Lesson. .. Sixth Lesson.

Religious life should be entered while youth, health, and the perceptions remain unimpaired. It requires renunciation, control, non-killing, and freedom from worldly possessions.

Third Lecture, Called Hot and Cold. / First Lesson. .. Fourth Lesson.

The law is understood by the self-possessed, wise, just, and chaste person who correctly understands the world. The wise follow the law and use it to overcome Mâra and sinfulness.

Fourth Lecture, Called Righteousness. / First Lesson. .. Fourth Lesson.

The pure, unchangeable, eternal law teaches that living beings should not be harmed. It is declared to be true among the zealous and not zealous, faithful and not faithful, cruel and not cruel, and among those with or without worldly weakness.

Fifth Lecture, Called Essence of the World. / First Lesson. .. Sixth Lesson.

The law is understood by the careful and zealous person who knows pain and pleasure and follows the teaching of the noble ones. Through the law, one learns restraint, repentance, and the way toward liberation.
