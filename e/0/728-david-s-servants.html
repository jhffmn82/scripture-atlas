David's servants

group · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: David's men, David’s, David’s servants, comforters, his men, his servants, messengers, servants, the elders of his house, the men, them

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Samuel, David's servants were envoys sent to comfort Hanun among the Ammonites, whom Hanun humiliated by half-shaving their beards and cutting their garments; David told them to wait at Jericho until regrowth. In 1 Chronicles, David's servants went to Hanun in the land of the Ammonites to comfort him over his father's death, were shaved and had their garments cut off at the buttocks, and were sent away humiliated; David told them to stay at Jericho until their beards had grown and then return.
