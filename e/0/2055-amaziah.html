Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Amaziah was king of Judah, son of Joash and Jehoaddin, who reigned twenty-nine years, did right in the LORD's eyes, defeated Edom, was defeated by Jehoash of Israel, and was killed by conspirators at Lachish. In The Second Book of Chronicles, Amaziah was king of Judah, son of Joash and Jehoaddan, who reigned twenty-nine years, defeated Seir, worshiped its gods, lost to Israel, fled to Lachish, and was killed by conspirators.

Amaziah in The Second Book of Kings

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Amaziah first appears

Abstract written by a model

Amaziah was a king of Judah, son of Joash and Jehoaddin, who began reigning at twenty-five and ruled twenty-nine years in Jerusalem. He did right in the LORD's eyes, though not like David, and killed his father's murderers while sparing their children. He struck ten thousand Edomites and captured Sela, but provoked Jehoash of Israel and was defeated at Beth Shemesh. Conspirators killed him at Lachish; he was buried in Jerusalem and Azariah succeeded him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 14

Amaziah began to reign at twenty-five and reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem, doing right but not like David, and he killed his father's murderers while sparing their children. He struck ten thousand Edomites, then provoked Jehoash of Israel, ignored his warning, was defeated and captured at Beth Shemesh. He outlived Jehoash by fifteen years, fled a conspiracy to Lachish, was killed there, and was buried in Jerusalem; Azariah succeeded him.

Abstract written by a model

Amaziah, son of Joash and Jehoaddan, became king of Judah at twenty-five and reigned twenty-nine years in Jerusalem, doing right but without a perfect heart. After defeating Seir he worshiped its gods, ignored a prophet, challenged Israel and lost, then fled a conspiracy to Lachish, was killed, and was buried with his fathers.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 25

Amaziah became king of Judah at twenty-five and reigned twenty-nine years, doing right but not with a perfect heart. He defeated Seir's children, then worshiped their gods, ignored a prophet, challenged Joash of Israel, and was defeated and taken to Jerusalem. He later fled a conspiracy to Lachish and was killed there.
