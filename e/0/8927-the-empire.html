the Empire

polity · 3 works · 21 quoted facts · 42 connections
Also called: Empire, Heaven's sun, The Central Land of Reed-Plains, country, land, people, people of the Empire, the Central Land of Reed-Plains, the empire, the realm

Abstract written by a model

In Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods), the Empire is pacified through imperial accession, governed by Heavenly Sovereigns, and purified and enlarged so its country’s basis and monarchy’s foundation endure. In Kojiki, the Central Land of Reed-Plains is pacified by divine direction, later ruled from the palace of Izakaha at Kasuga, and brought to peace after pestilence and military disturbances end. In Kojiki, the Empire is a realm whose government His Augustness Kamu-yamato-ihare-biko establishes by going east, after which it is ruled from Kashibara and unfolds through successive rulers. In Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko), Empire is repeatedly ruled by successive emperors and august rulers, whose people and officials reject King Karu of Kinashi and support His Augustness Anaho, while succession passes through Princes Ohoke and Woke and later Emperor Kei-Tai. In Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko), the Empire turns against King Karu of Kinashi and turns towards His Augustness Anaho, then is ruled in succession by later emperors and empresses.

Abstract written by a model

Empire is a polity repeatedly ruled by successive emperors and august rulers. It is ruled in turn by Emperor Nin-Toku and later rulers, Emperor Han-Zei, Emperor in-Giyō, His Augustness Shiraka-no-oho-yamato-ne-ko, Emperor Mu-Retsu, Emperor Kei-Tai, Emperor Kan-an, and Emperor Sen-Kuwa. Its people and officials reject King Karu of Kinashi and support His Augustness Anaho after misconduct. Following Shiraka-no-oho-yamato-ne-ko’s death, Princes Ohoke and Woke contend over succession, with Woke ruling first. Emperor Mu-Retsu rules eight years; after his death, the Empire is presented to Ohodo, and Emperor Kei-Tai rules from Tamaho at Ihare.
