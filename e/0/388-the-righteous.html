Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the righteous who take refuge in God love the LORD and his name. In Psalms, the righteous see God's destruction of the wicked, fear and laugh at him, and explain that he trusted in riches and wickedness instead of God. In Psalms, the righteous rejoice at vengeance against the wicked, and people affirm that a reward awaits them. In Proverbs, the righteous delight in justice, give generously, are ransomed from the wicked, and establish their ways. In Ecclesiastes or, The Preacher, the righteous and their works are in God's hand and share the same events as the wicked. In The Wisdom of Solomon, the righteous are held in God's hand, tested and found worthy, rest in peace with hope of immortality, and receive great good, authority, and life with God. In Habakkuk, the righteous are surrounded and swallowed by the wicked, while they are defined as those who live by faith. In The Gospel According to Matthew, the righteous inherit the Kingdom, learn that serving the least of the King's brothers served him, and enter eternal life.

and 11 more works

In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), the righteous are among those favored by Allah, alongside those who obey Allah and the messenger. In Surah 19: Mary (Maryam), the righteous are gathered as a goodly company, rescued from Hell, and made heirs of the Garden. In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), the righteous are guided, brought into Allah's mercy, promised inheritance of the earth, and protected from Hell. In Surah 25: The Criterion (Al-Furqaan), the righteous are promised the Garden and a better home, while repentant believers have their evil deeds changed to good. In Surah 25: The Criterion (Al-Furqaan), the faithful slaves of the Beneficent walk modestly, worship at night, avoid sin and excess, accept revelations, and seek comfort in their families. In Surah 38: The letter Saad (Saad), the righteous are promised Gardens of Eden, abundant fruit, cool drink, modest companions, and unfailing provision. In Surah 54: The Moon (Al-Qamar), the righteous dwell among gardens and rivers in the favour of a Mighty King. In Surah 76: Man (Al-Insaan), the righteous drink cups mixed with Kafur and Zanjabil, feed the needy, orphan, and prisoner for Allah's sake, and receive protection, Paradise, silk, joy, and pure drink. In Surah 82: The Cleaving (Al-Infitaar), the righteous are in delight. In Surah 83: Defrauding (Al-Mutaffifin), the righteous have their record in Illiyin and enjoy pure wine mixed with water from Tasnim. In Surah 92: The Night (Al-Lail), the righteous give their wealth seeking their Lord's purpose, are far removed from the Fire, and become content.
