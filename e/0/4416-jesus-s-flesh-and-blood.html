Jesus's flesh and blood

topic · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Jesus's flesh, flesh, his blood, his flesh, my blood, my flesh, the flesh of the Son of Man

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to John, Jesus's flesh and blood are described as true food and drink that give eternal life to those who eat and drink them. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, Jesus's flesh is partaken of in the gospel sacrament by worthy participants, while unworthy participants eat damnation to their souls.

Jesus's flesh and blood in The Gospel According to John

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Jesus's flesh and blood first appears

Abstract written by a model

Jesus's flesh and blood are described as true food and drink that give eternal life to those who eat and drink them. Jesus says the bread he gives for the world's life is his flesh; the Jews question how, and many disciples find it hard.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Jesus says the bread he will give for the life of the world is his flesh, and that unless people eat his flesh and drink his blood they have no life in themselves. He calls his flesh food indeed and his blood drink indeed, and says those who eat and drink live in him and he in them. The Jews ask how he can give his flesh, and many disciples find it a hard saying.
