Yamashiro

Also called: land of Yamashiro

Abstract written by a model

In Kojiki, Yamashiro was the land where King Take-hani-yasu dwelt and raised an army to confront the Imperial forces at the River Wakara. In Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko), Yamashiro was the place to which the Empress withdrew by ascending the River Tsutsuki, where Emperor Nin-Toku later progressed after sending Toriyama and Kuchiko, and from which Kings Ohoke and Woke later fled to Karibawi before crossing the River Kusuba toward Harima.

Abstract written by a model

Yamashiro is a place with a river, Tsutsuki, and a boar-herd. The Empress withdrew there by ascending its river; Emperor Nin-Toku sent Toriyama and Kuchiko after her and later progressed there. Later, fleeing Kings Ohoke and Woke reached Karibawi, lost provisions, and crossed the River Kusuba toward Harima.

Kings Ohoke and Woke reached Karibawi in Yamashiro while fleeing and were confronted by an old tattooed man who seized their provisions. They then fled across the River Kusuba toward Harima.
