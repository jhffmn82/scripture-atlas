Emperor Ri-Chiu

person · 1 work · 15 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Heavenly Sovereign, His Augustness Iza-ho-wake, Sovereign

Abstract written by a model

Emperor Ri-Chiu is a Heavenly Sovereign who originally dwelt at the palace of Naniha, was secretly carried into Yamato after King Sumi-no-ye-no-naka-tsu burned that palace, and later ruled the Empire from Wakasakura at Ihare. He met Midzu-ha-wake at the temple of the Deity of Iso-no-kami, wed Princess Kuro, and had King Oshiha of Ichinobe, King Mima, and Awomi-no-iratsume. During his reign he made appointments, granted names, and established the Ihare Clan. He died at sixty-four and was buried at Mozu.

Emperor Ri-Chiu is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
