Abstract written by a model

The foolish shepherd is a worthless shepherd the LORD raises up in the land. He neglects the flock, neither visiting, seeking, healing nor feeding it, while eating the fat sheep and tearing their hoofs. Woe is pronounced on him: judgment comes as a withered arm and blinded right eye.

Chapter 11

The narrator is told to take the equipment of a foolish shepherd. The LORD says He will raise up such a shepherd, who will neither visit the cut off, seek the scattered, heal the broken, nor feed the sound, but will eat the fat sheep and tear their hoofs. Woe is pronounced on him as a worthless shepherd who leaves the flock, with his arm to wither and his right eye to be blinded.
