Jason immediately shifted his own race to the Greek way of life, setting aside royal ordinances favorable to the Jews and introducing new customs forbidden by the law. The text calls it an extreme of hellenization and an advance of foreign religion. Priests despised their fathers' honors and valued Greek prestige, and the text says calamity followed.
