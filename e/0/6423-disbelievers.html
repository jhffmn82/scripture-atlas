disbelievers

group · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: the disbelievers, those who disbelieve

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 68: The Pen (Al-Qalam), the disbelievers try to disconcert Muhammad with their eyes, call him mad, and have their claim contradicted because the Reminder is for creation. In Surah 86: The Morning Star (At-Taariq), the disbelievers plot against Muhammad and are given a respite before being dealt with gently for a while.
