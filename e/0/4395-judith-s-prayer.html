Chapter 9

The prayer recalls Simeon's vengeance, affirms God's foreknowledge and his care for the weak, and describes the Assyrian threat. It petitions God to break the Assyrians through the deceit of her lips and the hand of a woman. It ends by asking that every nation and tribe know that the Lord is God and Israel's only protector.
