His Augustness Yamato-take

person · 1 work · 17 quoted facts · 11 connections
Also called: His Augustness Yamato-take's, King Yamato-wo-guna, Yamato-take, Yamato-take's, [Prince Wo-usu], [Yamato-take], [the Prince], [the dead prince], the august child, the dead prince

Abstract written by a model

In Kojiki, His Augustness Yamato-take is Heir Apparent, subdues various peoples and deities, marries Princess Oto-tachibana, has children, dies after illness, becomes a white dotterel, and is buried in the August-Mausoleum of the White-Bird. In Kojiki, Yamato-take is the name by which His Augustness Wo-usu is praised after killing the Kumaso bravoes; he later campaigns in the East, dies after illness, and becomes a white dotterel.

His Augustness Yamato-take in Kojiki

Basil Hall Chamberlain · the work’s page · read it where His Augustness Yamato-take first appears

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Yamato-take, also called Yamato-wo-guna, is Heir Apparent and an august child of Heavenly Sovereign Oho-tarashi-hiko-oshiro-wake. He subdues the Kumaso and Idzumo bravoes, rulers of Sagamu, and the East’s savage Deities and unsubmissive people, using the Herb-Quelling-Sabre and august bag. He weds Princess Oto-tachibana and has several children by her and other wives. After illness following Mount Ibuki, he dies at Nobe, becomes a white dotterel, and is buried in the August-Mausoleum of the White-Bird.
