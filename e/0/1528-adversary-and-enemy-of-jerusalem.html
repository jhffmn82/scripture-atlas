Abstract written by a model

Jerusalem's adversaries and enemies are a hostile group who prosper and are exalted, with the Lord having raised their horn and given them Zion's palaces. They mock her desolations, rejoice in her trouble, and claim to have swallowed her up. They enter the sanctuary the LORD forbade to nations, and breach her gates, which even the kings of the earth thought impossible. Pursuing swifter than eagles, they hunt her people through mountains, wilderness and streets, while Jerusalem asks that their wickedness come before the LORD.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Jerusalem's adversaries have become the head and prosper, mocking her desolations, spreading their hand on her pleasant things, and entering her sanctuary, which the LORD had forbidden to the nations. They are glad of her trouble, and Jerusalem asks that her enemies be made like her and that their wickedness come before the LORD.

Chapter 2

The enemy is given the walls of Zion's palaces by the Lord and makes a noise in the Lord's house as on a solemn assembly day. Enemies open their mouths, hiss, gnash their teeth, and claim to have swallowed Zion up; the Lord has caused them to rejoice and has exalted the horn of her adversaries.

Chapter 4

The adversary and enemy entered the gates of Jerusalem, which the kings of the earth and the world's inhabitants did not believe could happen. Their pursuers were swifter than eagles, chased the people on the mountains, ambushed them in the wilderness, and hunted their steps so that they could not go in the streets.
