coming judgment and desolation

event · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: my anger and my wrath, the land will become a waste

Abstract written by a model

The LORD announces coming judgment on Judah: his unquenched anger and wrath will be poured out on this place, on people, animals, trees and crops. He will cast Judah out of his sight, leave unburied corpses as food for birds and animals, and end all mirth and gladness, leaving the land a waste.

Chapter 7

The LORD announces that his anger and wrath will be poured out on this place, on man, animal, trees, and the fruit of the ground, burning without being quenched. He says he will cast Judah out of his sight, leave corpses as food for birds and animals with no one to frighten them away, and end the voices of mirth, gladness, bridegroom, and bride in Judah's cities and Jerusalem's streets, as the land becomes a waste.
