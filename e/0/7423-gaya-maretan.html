Gaya Maretan

person · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Gaya Maretan (the first created), Gaya Maretan (the life-man), Gaya Maretan the holy, holy Gaya Maretan

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Gaya Maretan is a holy first created being whose holy Fravashi is worshipped and praised and who begins the succession extending to Saoshyant. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Gaya Maretan is the first listener to Ahura Mazda’s thought and teaching and is worshipped as the source from whom Ahura formed the Aryan nations.
