Abstract written by a model

A self-exalting king acts by his own will, magnifies himself above every god, and speaks marvelous things against the God of gods. He prospers until the indignation is accomplished, ignores his fathers' gods, and honors an unknown god of fortresses with treasures. With that foreign god's help he conquers fortresses, elevates his supporters, and divides the land for a price.

Chapter 11

The self-exalting king does according to his will, magnifies himself above every god, and speaks marvelous things against the God of gods. He prospers until the indignation is accomplished, disregards his fathers' gods and the desire of women, and honors a god of fortresses unknown to his fathers with gold, silver and precious things. With a foreign god's help he deals with strong fortresses, increases the glory of those who acknowledge him, makes them rule over many, and divides the land for a price.
