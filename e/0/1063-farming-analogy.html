Chapter 28

The farming analogy shows a plower who does not plow continually but levels the soil and plants dill, cumin, wheat, barley and spelt each in its place. Dill and cumin are beaten with a stick and rod, and bread flour is not ground endlessly, because God instructs the farmer in right judgment, which comes from the LORD of Armies' counsel and wisdom.
