Abstract written by a model

Chapter 2 describes four successive kingdoms symbolized by an image's metals: Nebuchadnezzar's head of gold, an inferior second, a bronze third ruling all the earth, and a fourth strong as iron, crushing everything, which finally becomes divided, partly strong and partly brittle, with iron mixed with clay.

Chapter 2

The four kingdoms are represented by the gold, silver, bronze, and iron parts of the image. The first is Nebuchadnezzar's as head of gold, the second is inferior to it, the third is of bronze and rules over all the earth, and the fourth is strong as iron, breaking and crushing. The fourth ends up divided with feet of iron mixed with clay, partly strong and partly brittle.
