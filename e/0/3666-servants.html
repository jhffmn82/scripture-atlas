Abstract written by a model

Servants are a group defined by requirements: reverent, not double-tongued, wine-addicted or greedy for money, holding the faith's mystery in a pure conscience, husbands of one wife, ruling households well. They are tested first and serve only if blameless; their wives must also be worthy. Those who serve well gain good standing and boldness in faith.

Chapter 3

Servants must be reverent, not double-tongued, not addicted to much wine, not greedy for money, and must hold the mystery of the faith in a pure conscience. They are to be tested first and serve only if blameless. Their wives must be reverent, not slanderers, temperate, and faithful in all things. Servants must be husbands of one wife and rule their children and houses well, and those who serve well gain a good standing and great boldness in the faith in Christ Jesus.
