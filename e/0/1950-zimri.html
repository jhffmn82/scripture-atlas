Chapter 16

Zimri, captain of half of Elah's chariots, conspires against Elah, kills him in Tirzah, and reigns in his place. He destroys all of Baasha's house, but reigns only seven days; when Tirzah is besieged by Omri, he burns the king's house over himself and dies for his sins.
