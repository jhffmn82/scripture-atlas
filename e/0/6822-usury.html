usury

topic · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: interest

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), usury is forbidden and unfruitful; trade is permitted, Allah blights it, commands believers to abandon it, and warns unrepentant people of war from Allah and His messenger. In Surah 30: The Romans (Ar-Room), usury given to increase another person's property has no increase with Allah.
