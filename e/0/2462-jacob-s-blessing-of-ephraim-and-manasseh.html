Jacob's blessing of Ephraim and Manasseh

Abstract written by a model

Jacob blesses Joseph's sons Ephraim and Manasseh, invoking the God of Abraham and Isaac and asking that they become a multitude. He deliberately crosses his hands, right on younger Ephraim, and over Joseph's protest declares Ephraim greater. He establishes the blessing formula 'God make you as Ephraim and as Manasseh,' placing Ephraim first.

Chapter 48

Jacob blesses Joseph and the two lads, invoking the God of Abraham and Isaac and asking that his name and theirs be named on the boys and that they grow into a multitude. He deliberately crosses his hands, putting his right on the younger Ephraim, and over Joseph's protest says Ephraim will be the greater. He declares that Israel will bless in their name, saying 'God make you as Ephraim and as Manasseh,' and sets Ephraim before Manasseh.
