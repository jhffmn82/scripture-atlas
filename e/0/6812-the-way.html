the way

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a way, a way unto his Lord, the right road, the road

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 25: The Criterion (Al-Furqaan), the way is the path toward Allah, which Muhammad invites whoever wills to choose, while those who reject it are astray and farther from the right road. In Surah 76: Man (Al-Insaan), the way is shown to man, who may be grateful or disbelieving, and whoever wills may choose a way unto his Lord, though people do not will unless Allah wills.
