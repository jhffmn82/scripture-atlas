holy mountain (the LORD's holy mountain)

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the LORD’s hill is the holy place that only a person of clean hands and a pure heart may approach. In The Book of Isaiah, the LORD's holy mountain is a place free of hurt or destruction, full of knowledge of the LORD, where the LORD of Armies hosts a feast for all peoples and his hand rests. In The Second Book of Nephi, the holy mountain is free from harm, where no one hurts or destroys.
