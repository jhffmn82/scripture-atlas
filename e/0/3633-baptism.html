baptism

event · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: baptized, baptized into Christ Jesus, be baptized, to be baptized

Abstract written by a model

In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, baptism unites believers with Christ into his death, burying them with him so that, as Christ was raised, they may walk in newness of life. In The Book of Helaman, baptism was sought by those who believed Samuel's words and came repenting and confessing their sins. In The Book of Ether, baptism is commanded in Jesus Christ’s name and leads to salvation when accompanied by belief.
