Abstract written by a model

symbolizes prince in Jerusalem [the burden concerns him and the house of Israel] The sign act is Ezekiel's moving out in the sight of the people, with baggage by day, a wall dug through in the evening, and his face covered. He does as commanded, and the LORD explains in the morning that it means, 'I am your sign. As I have done, so will it be done to them.' It foretells the exile of the prince and the people.
