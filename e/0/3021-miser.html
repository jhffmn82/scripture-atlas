Abstract written by a model

The miser is an evil, stingy person unsuited to riches. He denies himself bread and enjoyment, hoarding goods that others will later revel in. His covetous eye is never satisfied, and wicked injustice dries up his soul, leaving him grudging even to himself.

Chapter 14

The miser is called stingy, evil, and grudging even to himself; riches are not appropriate for him, and he does not enjoy his possessions. He gathers by denying himself, so others will revel in his goods, and he begrudges bread so that it is lacking at his table. His covetous eye is never satisfied, and wicked injustice dries up his soul.
