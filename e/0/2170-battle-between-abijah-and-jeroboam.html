Chapter 13

The battle pitted Abijah's 400,000 chosen men against Jeroboam's 800,000, with Jeroboam's ambush placing Judah between two fronts. After Judah cried to the LORD, the priests sounded trumpets, and Judah shouted, God struck Israel, and 500,000 Israelites fell in a great slaughter. Judah prevailed because they relied on the LORD, and Abijah then took Bethel, Jeshanah, and Ephron.
