Yasna LXII (Sp. LXI).

The requested offspring is to be sure of foot, wakeful, quick to rise, helpful to nurture or reclaim, legitimate, orderly in men's meetings, influential in assemblies, powerful, skilled, and able to redeem others from oppression. Such offspring is also asked to advance the speaker's line, Vîs, Zantu, and province, and to deliver orders to the Province as firm and righteous rulers.
