Chapter 7

The LORD warns that if Solomon and the people turn away, forsake his statutes, and serve other gods, he will pluck them up by the roots from their land and cast the house out of his sight. The house will become a proverb and byword, and passersby will ask why, and be told the people abandoned the LORD who brought them out of Egypt and so received this evil.
