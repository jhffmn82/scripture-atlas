Sutrakritanga, Book II / First Lecture, Called the Lotus.

The third doctrine teaches that all things have the Self as their cause and object, are produced and manifested by it, and are bound up in it. Its adherents reject the twelve Aṅgas as false and remain trapped in misery and pleasures and amusements.
