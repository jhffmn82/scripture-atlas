government

topic · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 7 connections

Abstract written by a model

Government requires sufficient food, military equipment, and the people’s confidence in their ruler. It depends on prince, minister, father, and son properly fulfilling their named roles. It is carried forward by keeping its affairs in mind without weariness and practising them with undeviating consistency.

Government requires sufficient food, sufficient military equipment, and the confidence of the people in their ruler.

CHAP. XI. 1. The Duke Ching, of Ch'i, asked Confucius about

Government exists when each person properly fulfills the role named for him: prince, minister, father, or son.

CHAP. XIV. Tsze-chang asked about government. The Master

Government is discussed in relation to keeping its affairs before the mind without weariness and practising them with undeviating consistency.
