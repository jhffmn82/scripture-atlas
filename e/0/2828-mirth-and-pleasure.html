Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The narrator tests himself with mirth and pleasure, cheering his flesh with wine, and calls laughter foolishness and asks what mirth accomplishes. He withholds his heart from no joy and takes it as his portion from his labor, but concludes this too is vanity. Later he says enjoyment in one's labor is good and from God's hand.

Chapter 8

The narrator commends mirth, since a man has nothing better under the sun than to eat, drink, and be joyful. This will accompany him in his labor all the days of the life God has given him.
