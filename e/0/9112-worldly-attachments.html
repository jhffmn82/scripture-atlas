Abstract written by a model

Worldly attachments concern small or great sentient or nonsentient things and may cause great danger for some; abandoning them and contemplating the world’s course avoids this danger.

Worldly attachments may concern little or much, small or great, sentient or nonsentient things. The person who abandons them and contemplates the course of the world avoids the danger they bring.
