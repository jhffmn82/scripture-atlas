Abstract written by a model

Hiram is a skilled bronze worker brought from Tyre by King Solomon, son of a Naphtali widow and a Tyrian bronze-working father. Filled with wisdom, understanding and skill, he made the two pillars, molten sea, ten bases and basins, pots and shovels, finishing all his work for Solomon in the LORD's house.

Chapter 7

Hiram, brought from Tyre by King Solomon, is the son of a widow of the tribe of Naphtali and of a Tyrian father who worked in bronze. He was filled with wisdom, understanding, and skill, and made the two pillars, the molten sea, the ten bases and basins, and the pots, shovels, and basins. He finished all the work he did for Solomon in the LORD's house.
