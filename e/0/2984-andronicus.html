Chapter 4

Andronicus, a man of high rank, was left as deputy by the king when he went to settle the revolt of Tarsus and Mallus. Menelaus gave him stolen gold vessels and asked him to kill Onias, and he gave Onias his right hand with oaths, drew him from the sanctuary, and killed him. The king later stripped him, led him through the city, and put him to death where he had committed the outrage.
