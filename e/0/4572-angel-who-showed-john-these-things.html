Chapter 22

The angel shows John the river of the water of life and the tree of life, and tells him the words are faithful and true. When John falls at his feet to worship, the angel forbids it, saying he is a fellow bondservant with John, the prophets, and those who keep the book's words, and commands John to worship God. Jesus says he sent this angel to testify to the assemblies.
