Holy Word

Also called: Holy Word (itself), Word, conversation (with God) on the Holy Word, holy Word, that word, the Holy Word, the Word incarnate, the word of truth

Abstract written by a model

Holy Word is the strongest, most victorious, glorious, effective, fiend-smiting, and best-healing part of religious knowledge. Its part consists of Ahura Mazda’s names. It heals the faithful and enables conversation with God through revelation. As the best-healing remedy, it is true, progress-making, discerning, healthful, wise, happy, and more powerful in destruction than all other creatures.

Holy Word is in one work on this site, Yashts (Yasts) I-XII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

I. Ormazd Yast.

The Holy Word is described as having a strongest, most victorious, most glorious, most effective, most fiend-smiting, and best-healing part. Ahura Mazda says that his names constitute this part.

III. Ardibehist Yast.

The Holy Word is one of the means by which healing may be performed. Healing with the Holy Word is described as the best-healing of all remedies.

XII. Rashn Yast.

The most glorious Holy Word is declared to be true, progress-making, fit to discern, healthful, wise, happy, and more powerful to destroy than all other creatures.
