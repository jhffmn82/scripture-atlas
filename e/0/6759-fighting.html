fighting

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: So fight, do battle, fight, fighting was prescribed, make war, wage not war, war

Abstract written by a model

Fighting is prescribed for believers in the cause of Allah. It is presented as a means to benefit oppressed people and to oppose those who fight for idols or make war against believers. It follows an earlier instruction for some people to withhold their hands, establish worship, and pay the poordue. The account distinguishes enemies by conduct: those who hold aloof, do not wage war, and offer peace are not to be opposed; those who repeatedly return to hostility, neither hold aloof nor offer peace, may be fought. Believers are urged to take precautions and advance in groups or all together.

Fighting is prescribed after some people had been told to withhold their hands, establish worship, and pay the poordue. It is presented as being for the cause of Allah, for oppressed people, and against those who fight for idols.
