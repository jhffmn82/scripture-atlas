betrayal of Jesus

event · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: betray him, betray me, betrayed

Abstract written by a model

In The Good News According to Mark, betrayal of Jesus is foretold by Jesus and carried out by Judas, who leads an armed crowd to Gethsemane and identifies Jesus with a kiss, leading to his arrest. In The Gospel According to John, betrayal of Jesus fulfills Scripture as Judas is identified as the disciple who will betray Jesus and leaves after receiving the dipped bread.
