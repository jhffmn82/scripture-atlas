Abstract written by a model

The serpent is an animal of the field made by the LORD God, noted as more subtle than any other. In Chapter 3 it questions the woman about God's command, tells her she won't die and will become like God, and she says it deceived her. The LORD God then curses it above all livestock and animals: it must go on its belly and eat dust, with hostility set between its offspring and the woman's.

Chapter 3

The serpent, described as more subtle than any animal of the field, questions the woman about God's command and tells her she won't really die, and that her eyes will be opened and she will be like God. When confronted, the woman says it deceived her. The LORD God curses it above all livestock and animals, condemning it to go on its belly and eat dust, and sets hostility between its offspring and the woman's offspring, who will bruise its head while it bruises his heel.
