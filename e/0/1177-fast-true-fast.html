Abstract written by a model

The true fast is the one the LORD chooses, set against the people's rejected ritual fasting of sackcloth and ashes mixed with pleasure-seeking and oppression. It means releasing wickedness's bonds, freeing the oppressed, feeding the hungry, sheltering the poor, and clothing the naked.

Chapter 58

The people's fast, with bowed heads, sackcloth, and ashes, is rejected because it coexists with pleasure-seeking, oppression, and strife. The LORD states that the fast he has chosen is to release the bonds of wickedness, undo the yoke, let the oppressed go free, feed the hungry, shelter the poor, and clothe the naked.
