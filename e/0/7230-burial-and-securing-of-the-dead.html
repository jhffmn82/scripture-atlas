burial and securing of the dead

Also called: erect a building, fasten the corpse, lay the bodies of the dead, lay the bones of the dead

Abstract written by a model

takes_place_on high summits [where corpse-eating dogs and corpse-eating birds are always known to be] The dead should be placed on high summits and fastened by the feet and hair with brass, stones, or lead so dogs and birds cannot carry bones to water or trees. Bones should then be kept in a suitable building, or, if that cannot be afforded, the dead man may be laid on the ground on a carpet and pillow, clothed with the light of heaven and beholding the sun.
