Abstract written by a model

A deceitful schemer who winks while plotting evil; he flatters sweetly in person, then twists words into traps afterward. Those who know him avoid him, and the Lord hates him.

Chapter 27

He winks the eye and contrives evil, and those who know him keep their distance. He speaks sweetly and admires your words when present, but afterward twists his speech and sets a trap in your words; the speaker hates him and says the Lord will hate him.
