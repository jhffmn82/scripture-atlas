Fargard XIII.

A sacrifice to Sraosha can atone for killing the dog Vanghâpara. After a water dog is killed, a sacrifice for three days and three nights with fire, tied baresma, and uplifted Haoma is required before the land’s sweetness, fatness, health, and growth return.
