Chapter 17

The lawless men supposed they held a holy nation in their power, but they became prisoners of darkness, bound in the fetters of a long night and exiled from eternal providence. Their magic arts and boasted understanding proved powerless, and even ordinary sounds and creatures paralyzed them with terror. Farmers, shepherds, and laborers among them were all bound by one chain of darkness.
