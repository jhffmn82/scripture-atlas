the owners of the Garden

group · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: rightful owners of the Garden

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 11: Hud, the rightful owners of the Garden are believers who do good works, humble themselves before their Lord, and abide permanently in the Garden. In Surah 59: The Exile (Al-Hashr), the owners of the Garden are described as not equal to the owners of the Fire and as the victorious.
