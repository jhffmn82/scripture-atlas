Chapter 2

The sons of the prophets at Bethel and Jericho tell Elisha that the LORD will take his master away that day, and fifty of them watch from a distance at the Jordan. After seeing Elisha part the river they declare that Elijah's spirit rests on him and bow to him. They then urge him until he allows them to send fifty men to search for Elijah, who find nothing in three days.
