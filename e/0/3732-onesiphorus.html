Abstract written by a model

Onesiphorus is a loyal supporter of Paul: he often refreshed him, served at Ephesus, was unashamed of Paul's chain, and diligently sought him out in Rome. Paul prays for mercy on his house and for him on that day.

2 Timothy / Chapter 1

Paul asks the Lord to grant mercy to the house of Onesiphorus, who often refreshed Paul and was not ashamed of his chain. When in Rome he diligently sought Paul and found him, and Paul prays he will find the Lord's mercy in that day, noting Timothy knows well his service at Ephesus.
