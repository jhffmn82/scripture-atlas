borders of the land

Abstract written by a model

The borders of the land are described on four sides. The west is the great sea. The north runs from the great sea to Mount Hor, the entrance of Hamath, Zedad, Ziphron and Hazar Enan. The east runs from Hazar Enan through Shepham, Riblah and the sea of Chinnereth, down the Jordan to the Salt Sea. The south runs from Zin by Edom, past Akrabbim, Kadesh Barnea, Hazar Addar and Azmon, to the brook of Egypt and the sea.

Chapter 34

The south border runs from the wilderness of Zin by Edom and the end of the Salt Sea, past the ascent of Akrabbim, Kadesh Barnea, Hazar Addar, and Azmon to the brook of Egypt and the sea. The west border is the great sea, the north border runs from the great sea to Mount Hor, the entrance of Hamath, Zedad, Ziphron, and Hazar Enan, and the east border runs from Hazar Enan through Shepham, Riblah, and the sea of Chinnereth, down the Jordan to the Salt Sea.
