Hukairya

Also called: highest Hukairya, the height Hukairya

Abstract written by a model

Hukairya is a golden height with deep precipices, from which Ardvi Sûra Anâhita runs and leaps toward Vouru-Kasha, descending with great force and Glory; Yima Khshaêta offers sacrifice to her there.

Hukairya is in one work on this site, Yashts (Yasts) I-XII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
