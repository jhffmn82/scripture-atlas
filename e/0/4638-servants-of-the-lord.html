Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the servants of the LORD are called to serve and praise him, urged to praise his name now and forever and to stand by night in his house, lifting their hands in the sanctuary in praise. In Psalms, those who fear the LORD are described as his servants who stand in his house and courts, urged to trust him as help and shield, promised blessing for small and great and their children, and called to praise him with his compassion.

Abstract written by a model

The servants of the LORD are a group called to serve and praise him. In one psalm they are urged to praise his name now and forever; in another they stand by night in his house, lifting their hands in the sanctuary in praise.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 113

The servants of the LORD are called on to praise the LORD's name. The psalm opens with this command and frames their praise as fitting from this time forward and forever.

Chapter 134

The servants of the LORD are addressed directly and called to praise him. They are described as standing by night in the LORD's house, and are told to lift up their hands in the sanctuary and praise the LORD.

Abstract written by a model

Those who fear the LORD are a group of his servants who stand in his house and courts. They are urged to trust him as help and shield, promised blessing for small and great and their children, then called to praise him, with his compassion.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 115

Those who fear the LORD are told to trust in him, as he is their help and shield. He will bless them, both small and great, and the speaker wishes that he would increase them and their children.

Chapter 135

The servants of the LORD, who stand in the LORD's house and in the courts of God's house, are called to praise the LORD and his name. The LORD is said to have compassion on his servants when he judges his people.
