cloud (over the tabernacle)

object · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: The cloud of the LORD, appearance of fire, cloud, cloud (of the LORD), the cloud

Abstract written by a model

The cloud is a guiding presence over the tabernacle (Tent of the Testimony). It covers it from the day it is raised, appearing as fire at night, continually. Its lifting signals Israel to travel; where it rests, they camp, for a night, days, a month, or a year. It lifts on the twentieth day of the second month of the second year, then stays in the wilderness of Paran, over the people by day.

Chapter 9

The cloud covers the tabernacle from the day it is raised, and at evening it has the appearance of fire until morning, continually. When it is taken up from over the Tent Israel travels, and where it remains Israel encamps. It stays sometimes a few days, sometimes one night, and sometimes two days, a month, or a year.

Chapter 10

The cloud is taken up from over the tabernacle of the covenant on the twentieth day of the second month of the second year, the signal for Israel to set out. It then stays in the wilderness of Paran. It is over the people by day when they set forward from the camp.
