Abstract written by a model

In Leviticus, they fall before the Israelites by the sword when Israel obeys, but under the curses they eat the people's seed, rule over them, receive them into their hands, and inhabit their desolated land, while scattered survivors flee and perish in their lands. In Deuteronomy, they may have horses, chariots, and greater numbers, but the LORD goes with Israel to fight against them and save Israel. In The Book of Isaiah, they are disappointed and confounded, fall, perish, and become as nothing, so that Israel searches for them and finds none.
