Tzŭ Chang asked Man Kou Tê,

Wu Yoh says that the mean man pursues wealth while the superior man pursues reputation, though setting aside both activities produces the same result. He urges people to follow the laws of heaven, avoid self-conscious good deeds, and walk in the way of TAO.
