Chapter 7

Achan, son of Carmi, son of Zabdi, son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took some of the devoted things. He was singled out by the LORD's selection and confessed that he coveted and took a Babylonian robe, two hundred shekels of silver, and a fifty-shekel wedge of gold, hiding them in his tent. He was stoned and burned with his sons, daughters, livestock, tent, and all he had in the valley of Achor.
