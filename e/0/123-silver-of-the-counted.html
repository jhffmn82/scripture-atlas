silver of the counted

Also called: The one hundred talents of silver, The silver of those who were counted of the congregation, silver

Abstract written by a model

Silver collected from the counted men of the congregation totaled one hundred talents and 1,775 shekels by the sanctuary shekel, at a beka (half shekel) a head from each man twenty and older, 603,550 men. The hundred talents were cast into one hundred sockets for the sanctuary and veil; the 1,775 shekels made hooks, capitals and fillets for the pillars.

Chapter 38

Silver from those counted in the congregation came to one hundred talents and 1,775 shekels, at a beka (half shekel) a head from each man aged twenty and up, 603,550 men in all. The hundred talents were cast into one hundred sockets, one talent each, for the sanctuary and the veil, and the 1,775 shekels made hooks, capital overlays, and fillets for the pillars.
