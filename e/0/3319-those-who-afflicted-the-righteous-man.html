Chapter 5

They see the righteous man, are troubled with terrible fear, and groan in repentance, confessing that they derided him, thought him mad, and went astray from the way of truth. They say their arrogance, riches, and boasting profited nothing, that they traveled the paths of lawlessness and destruction without knowing the Lord's way, and that they were consumed in their wickedness.
