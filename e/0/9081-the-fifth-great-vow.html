Abstract written by a model

The fifth great vow renounces attachment to all things, whether living or lifeless, and forbids forming, causing, or consenting to attachment. It unfolds through five clauses requiring non-attachment to agreeable and disagreeable sounds, forms, smells, tastes, and touches.

The Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra endowed with the highest knowledge and intuition

The fifth great vow renounces attachments to living or lifeless things and forbids forming, causing, or consenting to such attachments. Its five clauses teach non-attachment to agreeable or disagreeable sounds, forms, smells, tastes, and touches.
