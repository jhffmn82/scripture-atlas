Chapter 14

The two armies set the battle in array in the valley of Zephathah at Mareshah, after Asa's prayer for help. The LORD strikes the Ethiopians, who flee, and Asa's army pursues them to Gerar, where they are destroyed. Judah then plunders the cities around Gerar and returns to Jerusalem with much booty.
