Abstract written by a model

The altars of the Baals, incense altars, Asherah poles, engraved images, and molten images were broken down, cut down, and ground to dust under Josiah's direction. The dust was scattered on the graves of those who had sacrificed to them. This extended throughout Israel, and Josiah took away all abominations from the lands of the children of Israel.
