Revelation / Chapter 1

The seven assemblies in Asia are the recipients of John's greeting and of the book he is told to write. They are named as Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia, and Laodicea. The mystery is explained: the seven lamp stands are the seven assemblies, and the seven stars in Christ's right hand are their angels.
