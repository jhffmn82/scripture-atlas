Abstract written by a model

The wicked man is said to face a fall: his strength is shortened and famished, his own counsel casts him down, and terrors chase him. He is devoured by the firstborn of death, rooted out of his tent, and brought to the king of terrors. His memory perishes, he has no name in the street, he is driven from light into darkness and out of the world, and he leaves no son or grandson. Those who come after are astonished at his day.
