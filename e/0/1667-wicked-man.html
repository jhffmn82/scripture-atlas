Also called: that wicked man, the wicked, the wicked man

Abstract written by a model

The wicked man is a figure who stands under threat of death for his iniquity yet is offered a path to life. Across both chapters, the Lord takes no pleasure in the wicked's death and calls them to turn. If he turns from all sins, keeps the statutes, does what is lawful and right, restores pledges and returns robbed goods, he will surely live and his transgressions will not be remembered. If unwarned, he dies in iniquity, and the watchman is accountable.

Chapter 18

The wicked man who turns from all his sins, keeps the Lord's statutes, and does what is lawful and right shall surely live and not die, and his transgressions will not be remembered against him. The Lord GOD says he takes no pleasure in the death of the wicked but would rather they return from their way and live.

Chapter 33

The wicked who are not warned will die in their iniquity, with the watchman held accountable for their blood. The LORD takes no pleasure in their death and calls them to turn. A wicked person who turns, restores the pledge, returns robbed goods, and walks in the statutes of life will surely live, and his sins will not be remembered.
