Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Job, weather shows God's power through rain, clouds, storms, and thunderings that judge people and provide food. In Psalms, weather describes the LORD's control over clouds, rain, snow, frost, hail, wind, and cold. In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, weather brings healing and fertility through rain that follows vapours and clouds after Tistrya is properly worshipped.

rain and clouds (God's weather) in The Book of Job

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where rain and clouds (God's weather) first appears

Abstract written by a model

God draws up the drops of water, which distill as rain from his vapor, and the skies pour it down abundantly on man. No one can understand the spreading of the clouds or the thunderings of his pavilion. By these means he judges the people and gives food in abundance, and the noise of the storm tells about him, as the livestock also do.

Abstract written by a model

The LORD covers the sky with clouds, prepares rain for the earth, and makes grass grow on the mountains. He gives snow like wool, scatters frost like ashes, and hurls down hail like pebbles, so that no one can stand before his cold. He then melts them by sending out his word and causing his wind to blow so that the waters flow.
