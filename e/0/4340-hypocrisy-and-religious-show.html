hypocrisy and religious show

Abstract written by a model

characterizes scribes and the Pharisees [outwardly righteous, inwardly hypocrisy and iniquity] Jesus describes the scribes and Pharisees as acting to be seen by men: broad phylacteries, enlarged fringes, places of honor, salutations, and titles like 'Rabbi'. He compares them to cleaned cups and whitened tombs that look good outside but are full of extortion, uncleanness, hypocrisy, and iniquity within. He contrasts this with humility, saying whoever exalts himself will be humbled and whoever humbles himself will be exalted.
