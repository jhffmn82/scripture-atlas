Assyrian siege of Jerusalem

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Assyrian invasion of Jerusalem, fight against Jerusalem, invaded, remain under siege

Abstract written by a model

The Second Book of Chronicles describes the Assyrian siege of Jerusalem, ending when the LORD's angel destroys the Assyrian forces and Sennacherib withdraws in shame. The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, describes the Assyrian invasion of Jerusalem, ending when the LORD's angel strikes and utterly destroys the Assyrian camp.
