Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, the altar receives blood, fat, and offerings, is cleansed and sanctified over seven days, and is used for daily lamb offerings. In Leviticus, the altar at the Tent of Meeting's door is where the priest sprinkles sacrificial blood for atonement.

has_status most holy [after seven days of atonement and sanctification] The altar receives the bull's blood on its horns and the rams' blood sprinkled around it, and the fat and offerings are burned on it.
