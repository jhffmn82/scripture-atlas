Also called: a monk, learned monk, such a (monk), such a monk, true monk

Abstract written by a model

A monk is a non-killer who renounces possessions, relations, pleasures, worldly work, and household life. He lives chastely through non-killing and non-acquisition, avoids love, hatred, passions, wrong belief, and the five cardinal sins, and refuses improperly obtained food and material gain from teaching. Controlled, restrained, careful, wise, calm, and happy, he possesses Samitis, preaches the Law solely to annihilate Karman, and seeks Liberation.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

I say: here in the East, West, North, and South there are some men, viz. Âryas,

A monk renounces possessions, relations, pleasures, amusements, worldly occupations, and the life of a householder. He avoids killing, acquiring property, sinful actions, passions, improperly obtained food, and teaching for material gain, while practising control, chastity, and the Law.

Fourth Lecture, Called Renunciation of Activity.

A monk abstains from the five cardinal sins, the passions, and wrong belief. He does not clean his teeth with a tooth-brush or accept collyrium, emetics, and perfumes; he is controlled, restrained, careful, thoroughly wise, calm, and happy.
