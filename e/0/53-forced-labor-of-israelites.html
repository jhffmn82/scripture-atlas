Abstract written by a model

The forced labor of Israelites is hard service imposed by the Egyptians, consisting of mortar, brick, and field work that made their lives bitter. Taskmasters afflicted them with burdens, building Pithom and Raamses for Pharaoh, yet the people multiplied. Their bondage outlasted the king's death, and their groaning reached God, who heard. He promised to bring them out and redeem them, though cruel bondage kept them from heeding Moses.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Exodus / Chapter 1

Taskmasters were set over the Israelites to afflict them with burdens, and they built the storage cities Pithom and Raamses for Pharaoh. The Egyptians ruthlessly made them serve in mortar, brick, and all kinds of field work, making their lives bitter. The oppression did not stop their growth, as they multiplied and spread out all the more.

Chapter 2

The Israelites groan under bondage that continues past the death of the king of Egypt. Their cry because of it comes up to God, who hears it.

Chapter 6

The Egyptians keep the children of Israel in bondage, and the LORD has heard their groaning. He promises to bring them out from under the burdens of the Egyptians, rid them of the bondage, and redeem them with an outstretched arm and great judgments. The cruel bondage, with anguish of spirit, keeps the people from listening to Moses.
