Chapter 5

Gamaliel, a Pharisee and teacher of the law honored by all the people, stood in the council and had the apostles put out for a little while. He cited Theudas and Judas of Galilee, whose movements came to nothing after they perished, and urged leaving the apostles alone, since a work of God could not be overthrown. The council agreed with him.
