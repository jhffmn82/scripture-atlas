Joseph's dreams

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a dream, another dream, dream, his dreams, my dream, my dream of old, the dream of Joseph, the dreams which he dreamed about them, this dream, thy vision, yet another dream

Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, Joseph dreams that his brothers' sheaves bow to his sheaf, and then that the sun, moon, and eleven stars bow to him; his brothers hate him all the more and his father rebukes him, though Jacob keeps the saying in mind; the brothers later mock him as "this dreamer" and say they will see what becomes of his dreams. In Surah 12: Joseph (Yusuf), Joseph dreams of eleven planets and the sun and the moon prostrating before him; Jacob tells him not to disclose the vision to his brethren, and the dream is later fulfilled when Joseph's family comes before him.
