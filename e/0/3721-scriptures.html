Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Luke, the Scriptures concerning Jesus include the law of Moses, the prophets, and the psalms, which testify that the Christ would suffer and enter his glory. In 2 Timothy, Scripture is described as God-breathed, profitable for teaching, reproof, correction, and instruction in righteousness, making believers wise for salvation and equipped for every good work. In Peter’s Second Letter, prophecy of Scripture is likened to a lamp in a dark place, is said not to be of private interpretation, and is attributed to holy men moved by the Holy Spirit. In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, scriptures are likened to Nephi’s people for their profit and learning, and Nephi reads from them to persuade them to believe in the Lord their Redeemer. In The Second Book of Nephi, Nephi delights in the scriptures, continually ponders them, and writes them for the learning and profit of his children. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, holy scriptures contain records teaching about the fall of man, worship, prayer, the Son of God, mercy, commandments, transgression, and redemption, but the people cast them into a fire. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the scriptures are given by the Father to Jesus for future generations and expounded to a multitude. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record, the scriptures teach that God is unchangeable and the same yesterday, today, and forever.

Peter’s Second Letter / Chapter 1

Prophecy is called the more sure word, which readers do well to heed as a lamp shining in a dark place until the day dawns and the morning star arises in their hearts. Peter states that no prophecy of Scripture is of private interpretation. It never came by the will of man, but holy men of God spoke, moved by the Holy Spirit.

Abstract written by a model

Holy scriptures are records containing the fall of man and teaching worship, prayer, the Son of God, mercy, commandments, transgression, and redemption. Some seek and expound them diligently, beginning with Adam’s creation; they testify through prophets of old. The people finally cast these records into fire to destroy them.
