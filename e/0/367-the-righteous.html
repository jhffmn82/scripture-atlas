the righteous

Abstract written by a model

In Proverbs, the righteous find safety by running to the LORD's name, withstand calamity, and are warned not to attack the wicked's home. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, the righteous man's sacrifice is acceptable and unforgotten, enriching the altar with a sweet fragrance before the Most High. In The Book of Ezekiel, the righteous man will live if he takes warning and does not sin, but will die if he turns from righteousness and commits iniquity.

righteous (the LORD's righteous) in Proverbs

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where righteous (the LORD's righteous) first appears

Abstract written by a model

The LORD's righteous are a group, spoken of as a man, who find safety by running to the LORD's name, a strong tower, unlike the rich whose wealth is imagined protection. Later, the wicked are warned not to attack his home; he falls seven times yet rises, while they fall to calamity.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 18

The righteous run to the LORD's name, a strong tower, and are safe. This contrasts with the rich man, whose wealth is only an unscalable wall in his own imagination.

Chapter 24

The righteous man is warned against by the wicked, who are told not to lie in wait against his habitation or destroy his resting place. The text says he falls seven times and rises up again, in contrast to the wicked, who are overthrown by calamity.
