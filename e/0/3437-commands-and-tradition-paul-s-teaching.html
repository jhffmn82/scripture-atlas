commands and tradition (Paul's teaching)

Also called: every letter, our word in this letter, the things we command, the tradition which they received from us, this letter

Abstract written by a model

Paul commands in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ that the brothers withdraw from anyone not walking after the tradition they received from him. This teaching includes the earlier rule that anyone unwilling to work should not eat, and disobedience to the word in this letter is to be noted and met with withdrawal of company.
