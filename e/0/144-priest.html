priest in Leviticus

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where priest first appears

Abstract written by a model

The priest is an officiant who judges and restores cleanness. In Chapter 14 he goes outside the camp to examine a leper, directs the two-bird ritual, sprinkles, and pronounces the person clean. On the eighth day he offers sacrifices, applies blood and oil, and makes atonement. He also inspects a mildewed house, orders it emptied, and pronounces it clean or demolished.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 14

The priest goes out of the camp to examine the leper, directs the two-bird ritual, sprinkles, and pronounces the person clean. On the eighth day he offers the sacrifices, puts blood and oil on the person's right ear, thumb, and big toe, and makes atonement. He also inspects the mildewed house, orders it emptied, shuts it up for seven days, directs the removal of stones and scraping, and pronounces it clean or orders it torn down.

the priest in Leviticus

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where the priest first appears

Abstract written by a model

The priest is an officiant who makes atonement for guilty persons so they are forgiven. He handles bird sin offerings and altar blood, burns a memorial handful of flour, keeps the remainder as a meal offering, and receives restitution plus a fifth.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

The priest makes atonement for the guilty person in each case, after which the person is forgiven. He offers the birds, wringing off the head of the sin offering without severing it and handling the blood at the altar. He burns a handful of the flour as a memorial portion, keeps the remainder as a meal offering, and receives the restitution plus a fifth.
