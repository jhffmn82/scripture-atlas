Caesarea

Abstract written by a model

Caesarea is where Paul is kept in custody and where Festus returns after his stay in Jerusalem. Festus sits on the judgment seat there to hear Paul. King Agrippa and Bernice arrive there, and Paul is brought before Agrippa and the others there.
