Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the righteous hold an allotment that wickedness's scepter will not rule, keep their hands from evil, and are included in the psalmist's request that the LORD do good to the upright. In Psalms, the righteous are watched over by the LORD, who hears their cry, delivers them from all troubles despite many afflictions, and protects their bones so none is broken. In Psalms, the righteous are told to trust the LORD, do good, wait patiently, cease from anger, and depart from evil; the LORD makes their righteousness shine, upholds them, satisfies them in famine, and rescues them from the wicked. In Psalms, the righteous, the upright in heart, are glad in the LORD, take refuge in him, and are finally called to praise him. In Psalms, the righteous are called to be glad and rejoice with gladness before God, in contrast to the wicked who perish at his presence. In Psalms, the righteous are told to hate evil, follow judgment, and be glad in the LORD and give thanks to his holy Name, while the LORD preserves their souls, delivers them from the wicked, and sows light and gladness for them. In Proverbs, the righteous care about justice for the poor, thrive, cause people to rejoice, sing with gladness, see the wicked fall, and are detested by dishonest people while detesting them in turn.
