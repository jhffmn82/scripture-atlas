priesthood ordination

event · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: an everlasting priesthood, anoint them, and consecrate them, and sanctify them, priesthood, priesthood (Aaron's), priest’s office, the priest’s office

Abstract written by a model

Priesthood ordination is the perpetual statute by which Aaron and his sons are set apart to minister to the LORD as priests. It begins with clothing in holy garments, anointing, consecration and sanctification, with linen pants required so they do not bear iniquity and die. It continues with their lasting portion of the wave offering and succession by Aaron's sons. It concludes with washing, clothing and anointing that grant an everlasting priesthood throughout their generations.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 28

Aaron and his sons are to be clothed in the holy garments, anointed, consecrated, and sanctified so that they may minister to the LORD in the priest's office. The linen pants are required whenever they enter the Tent of Meeting or approach the altar, so that they do not bear iniquity and die. This is stated as a statute forever for Aaron and his offspring.

Chapter 29

Aaron and his sons are to hold the priesthood by a perpetual statute. Their portion of the wave offering from the children of Israel is theirs forever, and the priesthood continues through the sons who succeed Aaron in the holy garments.

Chapter 40

The LORD commands that Aaron and his sons be washed, clothed, and anointed to minister as priests. Their anointing is to give them an everlasting priesthood throughout their generations.
