eternal life

topic · 4 works · 7 quoted facts · 10 connections
Also called: death, glory, honor, and incorruptibility, glory, honor, and peace, have it abundantly, life, newness of life, the Word of life, the life, the life, the eternal life, the promise, this life

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, eternal life is inherited by those who leave family or lands for Jesus and follow him. In The Gospel According to John, eternal life is received by believers in Jesus, consists in knowing God and Jesus, and is given by Jesus to those entrusted to him by the Father. In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, eternal life is God’s free gift in Christ Jesus, resulting from freedom from sin and service to God. In 1 John, eternal life is the life with the Father revealed in Jesus Christ, given to believers so they may know they have it.

eternal life in The Gospel According to John

author unknown · AD 90 to 100 · the work’s page · read it where eternal life first appears

Abstract written by a model

Eternal life is a gift received by whoever believes in Jesus, the Son, and is defined as knowing the only true God and Jesus Christ. It begins with a promise in Chapter 3 that believers will not perish, tied to the Son being lifted up. Chapter 6 adds food that remains, being raised at the last day, and Jesus' words of eternal life. Chapter 17 defines it and says Jesus gives it to those the Father gave him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Eternal life is promised to whoever believes in the Son, who will not perish. It is linked to the Son being lifted up as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness. John adds that one who believes in the Son has eternal life, while one who disobeys won't see life.

Chapter 6

Jesus says the food that remains leads to eternal life and that the Son of Man will give it. He says everyone who sees the Son and believes has eternal life and will be raised at the last day, and that eating this bread means living forever. Peter says Jesus has the words of eternal life.

Chapter 17

Eternal life is defined as knowing the only true God and Jesus Christ whom he sent. Jesus says that, having been given authority over all flesh, he will give eternal life to all whom the Father has given him.

eternal life in 1 John

author unknown · undated · the work’s page · read it where eternal life first appears

Abstract written by a model

Eternal life is the life that existed from the beginning with the Father, was revealed to the authors, and was heard, seen and touched. God gave it to believers, and it is in his Son, Jesus Christ: whoever has the Son has the life. It was written so believers may know they have it.
