Chapter 32

Reuben and Gad ask Moses, Eleazar, and the princes for the eastern land as a possession and not to be brought over the Jordan. After Moses's rebuke, the request is accepted on the condition that their armed men fight alongside Israel until the land is subdued, and the tribes agree. The land is then given to them, along with half of Manasseh.
