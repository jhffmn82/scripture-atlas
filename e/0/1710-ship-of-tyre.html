Abstract written by a model

Tyre is depicted as a magnificent ship built from materials gathered from many lands: Senir cypress planks, Lebanon cedar mast, Bashan oak oars, Kittim ivory benches, Egyptian embroidered linen sail, Elishah awning. Its rowers steer it into great waters, where the east wind wrecks it.

Chapter 27

Tyre is depicted as a ship whose planks are cypress from Senir, mast is Lebanon cedar, oars are Bashan oak, benches are ivory inlaid in cypress, and sail is embroidered Egyptian linen with blue and purple awning from Elishah. Its rowers bring it into great waters, where the east wind breaks it in the heart of the seas.
