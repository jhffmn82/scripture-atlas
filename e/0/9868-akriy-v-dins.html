Akriyâvâdins

topic · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Akriyâvâda, Akriyâvâdin

Abstract written by a model

Akriyâvâdins is a heretical creed among doctrines: it denies Karman and transmission of the soul’s action to future moments. Its contradictory adherents fail to understand truth, are countered by a valiant opinion, and may cause rebirth in the Circle of Births.

Akriyâvâdins is in one work on this site, Sutrakritanga (Sûtrakritâṅga), Book I. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
