Also called: an Avaoirista

Abstract written by a model

An Avaoirista is the act of coming upon a man to smite him. Penalties begin at ten stripes with each implement, rise to ninety by the sixth occurrence, and reach two hundred when a seventh occurrence is unatoned or atonement is refused.

Fargard IV.

An Avaoirista occurs when a man comes upon another to smite him. Its penalties rise from ten stripes with each instrument on the first occurrence to ninety on the sixth; a seventh unatoned occurrence or refusal to atone makes the offender a Peshôtanu liable to two hundred stripes with each instrument.
