Chapter 24

A sheaf forgotten in the field must not be fetched again, olive boughs must not be gone over a second time, and the vineyard must not be gleaned after the harvest. All of it is left for the foreigner, the fatherless, and the widow. The LORD promises to bless the work of the owner's hands for this.
