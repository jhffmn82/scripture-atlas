darkness

topic · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: blackness, hidden darkness, light and darkness, the darkness, the light, true light

Abstract written by a model

In 1 John, darkness is absent from God, and those who claim fellowship with God while walking in darkness lie and do not tell the truth. In The Second Book of Nephi, darkness covers the heavens, marks walking without light, and brings sorrow until the Messiah brings Joseph’s branch and Israel from darkness unto light.
