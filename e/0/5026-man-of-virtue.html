man of virtue

Also called: The man of virtue, art of virtue, perfect virtue, perfectly virtuous, virtue

Abstract written by a model

The man of virtue results from an equal blending of accomplishments and solid qualities. His perfect virtue follows the Constant Mean, though such practice has long been rare among the people. He first prioritizes overcoming difficulty and treats success afterward. Virtue unfolds through establishing and enlarging oneself and others, and through judging others by what is near in oneself.
