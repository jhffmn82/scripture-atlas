Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, the sign is given by the LORD as a virgin conceiving and bearing a son called Immanuel, whose early growth is tied to the forsaking of the land of the two kings. In The Second Book of Nephi, the sign from the Lord concerns a virgin conceiving, bearing a child, and naming him Immanuel.
