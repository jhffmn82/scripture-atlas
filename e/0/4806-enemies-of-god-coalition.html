Abstract written by a model

A coalition of God's haters, united as one mind, conspires against Israel to destroy it as a nation. It comprises Edom, Ishmaelites, Moab, Hagrites, Gebal, Ammon, Amalek, Philistia, Tyre and Assyria; the speaker finally prays they be confounded, made like chaff, and perish.

Chapter 83

The enemies, who hate God, form an alliance with one mind against Him and His people. The coalition includes Edom, the Ishmaelites, Moab, the Hagrites, Gebal, Ammon, Amalek, Philistia, the inhabitants of Tyre, and Assyria, which helps the children of Lot. The speaker asks that they be made like tumbleweed and chaff, confounded, and perish.
