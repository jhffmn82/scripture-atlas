royal sway

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: the royal sway

Abstract written by a model

Royal sway depends on loving and protecting the people. Mencius says King Xuan of Qi can attain it by extending kindness, instituting benevolent government, and sharing pleasure with the people.

royal sway is in one work on this site, Mencius, Book I (Liang Hui Wang). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 7

Royal sway is attained through the love and protection of the people. Mencius says King Xuan of Qi is capable of it if he extends his existing kindness and institutes benevolent government.

Chapter 1

Mencius says that royal sway awaits the king if he makes pleasure a thing common to the people and himself.
