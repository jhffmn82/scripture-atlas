Year of Jubilee

event · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Jubilee, That fiftieth year, fiftieth year, jubilee, the Jubilee, the Year of Jubilee

Abstract written by a model

The Year of Jubilee is a holy observance in the fiftieth year, after seven Sabbaths of years. A trumpet on the Day of Atonement opens it, proclaiming liberty. Each person returns to property and family, and sowing and reaping are forbidden. Land prices and dedicated-field valuations are reduced by years remaining until it. Sold land and indentured Israelites are released, and a dedicated field returns to its original owner or passes to the priests.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 25

The fiftieth year, following seven Sabbaths of years (forty-nine years), is made holy by a trumpet blast on the Day of Atonement. Liberty is proclaimed, and each person returns to his property and family, with no sowing or reaping. Prices of land are set by the number of years remaining until it, and sold land and indentured Israelites are released in it.

Chapter 27

The Year of Jubilee determines the value of dedicated fields, with the valuation reduced according to the years remaining after it. A bought field dedicated to the LORD returns in the Jubilee to its original owner, while an unredeemed or sold field becomes holy and owned by the priests.
