Abstract written by a model

Gayaghôsha, a famous Brâhmana and houseless monk, practices the yamas, subdues his senses, and wanders. In Benares he fasts, calmly endures refusal of alms, teaches true sacrifice, Brâhmanahood, and liberation, and urges Vigayaghôsha to enter the order. Through self-control and penance, he annihilates Karman and reaches highest perfection.

Twenty-Fifth Lecture.

Gayaghôsha is a famous Brâhmana who performs the yamas, subdues his senses, and wanders from village to village. At Benares he refuses to be angered when denied alms, teaches Vigayaghôsha and the assembled priests about true Brâhmanahood and liberation, and urges Vigayaghôsha to enter the order. He later annihilates his Karman through self-control and penance and reaches the highest perfection.
