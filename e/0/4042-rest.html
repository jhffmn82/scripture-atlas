Abstract written by a model

In The Letter to the Hebrews, rest is God's Sabbath rest, entered by believers through faith and cessation from their own works. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, rest of the Lord their God is entered by people made pure and spotless through repentance.

rest (God's rest) in The Letter to the Hebrews

Abstract written by a model

God's rest is a Sabbath rest for God's people. It was sworn in wrath to be denied the disobedient wilderness generation, who could not enter through unbelief. Joshua did not provide it, so it remains available; believers enter through belief, resting from their own works as God rested from his.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

God's rest is what he swore in wrath that the disobedient wilderness generation would not enter. The chapter states they were unable to enter because of unbelief.

Chapter 4

God's rest is something the believing enter, although God's works were finished from the foundation of the world and he rested on the seventh day. Joshua did not give it, so a Sabbath rest remains for the people of God, and whoever enters it rests from their own works as God did from his.
