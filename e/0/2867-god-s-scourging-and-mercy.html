Chapter 13

The prayer states that God scourges and shows mercy, leading down to the grave and bringing up again. He will scourge Israel for iniquities and Jerusalem for the works of its sons, and then will show mercy again, gathering the scattered and having mercy on the sons of the righteous. Turning to him with the whole heart and soul is said to bring him turning to them.
