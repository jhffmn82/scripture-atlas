Chapter 20

Jeremiah's familiar friends defame him, saying "Terror on every side! Denounce, and we will denounce him," and watch for his fall hoping to prevail and take revenge. Jeremiah asserts that the LORD will make his persecutors stumble, disappoint them, and bring them everlasting dishonor, and he asks to see the LORD's vengeance on them.
