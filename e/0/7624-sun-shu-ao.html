Sun Shu Ao

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Sun Shu Ao stays emotionally steady through three appointments and dismissals, treating rank and power as outside himself. At a banquet he stands quietly in repose; the men of Ying throw down their arms. His inaction, rather than speech, is presented as effective from office to banquet.

CHAPTER XXI.

Sun Shu Ao remains emotionally steady after being appointed and dismissed from office three times. He regards gaining and losing office as outside his own self and says that concern with rank leaves no leisure for troubling over it.

Kuan Chung being at the point of death, Duke Huan went to see him.

Sun Shu Ao stands quietly in repose at the banquet, and the men of Ying throw down their arms. His inaction is presented as more effective than speech.
