Abstract written by a model

Seven kinsmen of Raguel to whom Sarah was given in marriage; each was killed by Asmodaeus on entering her room before consummation, leading maidservants to accuse Sarah and causing her reproach, despair, and later Raguel's admission.

Chapter 3
