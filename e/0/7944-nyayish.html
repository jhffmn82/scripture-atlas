Nyayish

document · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

The document comprises five Nyâyis hymns and prayers centered on Ahura Mazda and sacred beings. The Khôrshêd Nyâyis honors the Sun, Mithra, Tistrya, and Ahura Mazda, affirms good thoughts, words, and deeds, rejects evil, repeats Ashem Vohû, and identifies the worshipper as Zarathustra’s follower. The Mihir Nyâyis offers prayer to Ahura Mazda, the Amesha-Spentas, Fravashis, Vayu, and especially Mithra, portrayed as powerful, vigilant, truthful, and lord of wide pastures and countries. The Mâh Nyâyis praises the Moon, the Bull, Waters, healing and glorious gods, seeking strength, victory, welfare, children, brightness, glory, and a blissful abode. The Âbân Nyâyis recounts Ahura Mazda directing Spitama Zarathustra to sacrifice to Ardvi Sûra Anâhita, the holy water-spring whose river serves the seven Karshvares and purifies seed and wombs. Finally, the Âtas Nyâyis venerates Âtar, Ahura Mazda’s son, requesting welfare, knowledge, courage, offspring, prosperity, and fellowship with the holy Ones for proper worshippers.

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Nyayish / I. Khôrshêd Nyâyis.

This unit is a hymn to the Sun, Ahura Mazda, and other holy beings. It praises good thoughts, words, and deeds, rejects evil ones, and offers sacrifice and prayer to the Amesha-Spentas and other sacred entities. It invokes Mithra, Tistrya, the Sun, and Ahura Mazda for help, health, glory, and a blessed abode. It also includes repeated recitations of Ashem Vohû and identifies the worshipper as a follower of Zarathustra.

II. Mihir Nyâyis.

This unit is a prayer and liturgical offering to Ahura Mazda, the Amesha-Spentas, Mithra, and other holy beings. It asks that the prayer reach Ahura Mazda, the Amesha-Spentas, the Fravashis of the holy Ones, and Vayu. It praises good thoughts, words, and deeds, and seeks health, holiness, brightness, and glory. Much of the unit concerns sacrifice and prayer to Mithra, who is described as powerful, watchful, truth-speaking, and lord of wide pastures and all countries.

III. Mâh Nyâyis.

This unit is a series of praises, sacrifices, and prayers concerning Ahura Mazda and the Moon that keeps in it the seed of the Bull. It also concerns the Amesha-Spentas, the Bull, the gods of full Glory and healing, and the Waters. The speaker asks for strength, victory, welfare, abundant male children, and blessings of brightness, glory, and a blissful abode for those who offer sacrifice.

IV. Âbân Nyâyis.

This unit concerns Ahura Mazda’s praise and worship of the good waters and the holy water-spring Ardvi Sûra Anâhita. Ahura Mazda tells Spitama Zarathustra to offer sacrifices to Ardvi Sûra Anâhita, who purifies male seed and female wombs and whose river supplies waters across the seven Karshvares. The text describes offerings made to Ardvi Sûra Anâhita with libations, Haoma, meat, baresma, holy spells, words, deeds, and rightly-spoken words. It asks for brightness, glory, and a blissful abode for the man who worships properly.

V. Âtas Nyâyis

This unit concerns prayers and offerings to Ahura Mazda and Âtar, the son of Ahura Mazda. Âtar is praised as beneficent, powerful, healing, and worthy of sacrifice and invocation. Worshippers ask Âtar for welfare, knowledge, courage, offspring, prosperity, and a place among the holy Ones. Âtar blesses those who bring him properly prepared sacred wood and other offerings, while he watches what passers-by bring.
