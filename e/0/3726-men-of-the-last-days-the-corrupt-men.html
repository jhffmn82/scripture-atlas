men of the last days (the corrupt men)

group · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: evil men, evil men and impostors, impostors, men, these, these also

Abstract written by a model

The corrupt men of the last days are lovers of self, money, and pleasure rather than God, boastful, arrogant, disobedient, and unforgiving. They keep a form of godliness while denying its power, creep into houses and captivate gullible women, and oppose the truth as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses. Corrupted in mind and rejected concerning the faith, they will advance no further because their folly will be evident, yet they grow worse, deceiving and being deceived.

Chapter 3

They are described as lovers of self, money, and pleasure rather than God, boastful, arrogant, disobedient, unforgiving, and holding a form of godliness while denying its power. Some creep into houses and captivate gullible women, are always learning but never reach the truth, and oppose the truth like Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses. They will go no further, as their folly will be evident to all, yet evil men and impostors will grow worse, deceiving and being deceived.
