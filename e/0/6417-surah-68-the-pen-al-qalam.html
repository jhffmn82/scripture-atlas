Surah 68: The Pen (Al-Qalam)

document · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

The unit concerns Muhammad, whom Allah describes as not mad and as possessing a tremendous nature, with an unfailing reward promised to him. It warns Muhammad against obeying the rejecters and describes their slander, wrongdoing, and denial of Allah's revelations. It recounts how the owners of the garden lost its fruit after resolving to exclude the needy, then regretted their wrongdoing and hoped for better from their Lord. It contrasts those who keep from evil, who will have gardens of bliss, with the guilty, and warns of the punishment of the Hereafter and the Day of Judgment.
