Heir Apparent

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: His Augustness Oho-tomo-wake, Oho-tomo-wake, the Heir Apparent, this Heir Apparent

Abstract written by a model

In Kojiki, Oho-tomo-wake, also called Homuda-wake, is the child of Emperor Chiū-Ai and Empress Jin-Gō and Heir Apparent, destined before birth to rule countries and born with an elbow-pad-like arm protuberance. In Kojiki, the Heir Apparent, child of Empress Jin-Gō, is placed in a mourning-vessel en route to Yamato, travels with Noble Take-uchi, dwells temporarily at Tsunuga, and exchanges names with Great Deity Izasa-wake.
