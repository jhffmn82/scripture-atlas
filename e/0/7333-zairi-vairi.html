Zairi-vairi

Abstract written by a model

Zairi-vairi is a mounted fighter who offers a sacrifice to Ardvi Sûra Anâhita and receives the boon of overcoming Peshô-Kangha, Humâyaka, and the wicked Aregat-aspa. He is later named as one of the Aryan opponents whom Aregat-aspa and Vandaremaini fail to conquer.
