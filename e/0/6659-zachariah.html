Zachariah

person · 4 works · 10 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: O Zachariah, Zacharias, his father, his father Zacharias

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Luke, Zacharias is an aged priest who is told Elizabeth will bear John, doubts, becomes mute, regains speech by naming John, and prophesies. In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan), Zachariah becomes Mary’s guardian, finds food provided for her, prays for offspring, receives tidings of John, and is given a three-day sign of silence. In Surah 19: Mary (Maryam), Zachariah asks Allah for a successor, receives tidings of John, and is made unable to speak for three nights. In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), Zachariah asks Allah not to leave him childless, and Allah grants him John and adjusts his wife to bear a child.

Zacharias in The Gospel According to Luke

author unknown · AD 80 to 90 · the work’s page · read it where Zacharias first appears

Abstract written by a model

Zacharias is an aged, righteous priest of Abijah's division, married to Elizabeth. Gabriel tells him in the temple that she will bear John; he doubts and is struck mute. He regains speech by naming John, then prophesies, blessing God for redemption.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Zacharias, a priest of the division of Abijah, is told by Gabriel in the temple that Elizabeth will bear a son named John; he asks how he can be sure and is struck mute for not believing. He regains speech after writing 'His name is John' and then prophesies, blessing God for redemption and describing John as a prophet who will prepare the Lord's ways.
