CHAPTER XI.

The Spirit of the Clouds worries that heaven and earth are out of harmony and seeks advice on nourishing living beings. The Vital Principle tells him that government is the source of the disorder and teaches him to feed the people with his heart, rest in inaction, and release intelligence.
