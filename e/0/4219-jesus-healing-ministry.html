Jesus' healing ministry spans Galilee, where he heals every disease and sickness, including demon-possession, epilepsy, and paralysis. His fame spreads into Syria, and the sick are brought to him, drawing great multitudes from many regions to follow him.

Chapter 4

Jesus goes about all Galilee healing every disease and sickness among the people, and the report of him spreads into all Syria. People bring him the sick, those afflicted with various diseases and torments, the demon-possessed, epileptics, and paralytics, and he heals them, while great multitudes from Galilee, Decapolis, Jerusalem, Judea, and beyond the Jordan follow him.
