Abstract written by a model

A group of strangers and wicked nations, called the terrible of the nations, whom the LORD brings as instruments of judgment. First they plunder and profane the ornament, houses and secret place; later they are sent against Tyre's prince, defiling his brightness and bringing him to a violent death.

Chapter 7

The LORD gives the ornament into the hands of strangers as prey and to the wicked of the earth as plunder, and they profane it. He will bring the worst of the nations to possess the people's houses, and robbers will enter and profane his secret place.

Chapter 28

The LORD says he will bring strangers, 'the terrible of the nations,' against the prince of Tyre. They will draw swords against the beauty of his wisdom, defile his brightness, and bring him down to a violent death.
