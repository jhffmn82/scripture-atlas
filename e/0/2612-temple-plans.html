Plans for the temple, the LORD's house, which David gives to Solomon. They cover the porch, rooms, treasuries, courts, priestly and Levite divisions, and vessels, with gold and silver specified by weight. David says the pattern came in writing from the LORD's hand, partly by the Spirit.

Chapter 28

David gives Solomon the plans covering the porch, rooms, treasuries, courts, the divisions of priests and Levites, and the vessels of service. They specify gold and silver by weight for vessels, lamp stands, tables of show bread, forks, basins, cups, bowls, the altar of incense, and the chariot with the cherubim covering the ark. David says he was made to understand all the works of this pattern in writing from the LORD's hand, and some of the plans came by the Spirit.
