The daily continual burnt offering consists of two year-old lambs offered on the altar, one each morning and evening. Each lamb is accompanied by fine flour mixed with beaten oil and a wine drink offering. It is kept throughout the generations at the Tent of Meeting door, where the LORD meets Israel.

Chapter 29

Two year-old lambs are to be offered on the altar every day, one in the morning and one at evening. Each is accompanied by a tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a fourth of a hin of beaten oil, and a fourth of a hin of wine as a drink offering. It is a continual offering throughout the generations at the tent door, where the LORD will meet and speak with Israel.
