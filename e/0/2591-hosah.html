Abstract written by a model

Hosah, a descendant of Merari, had four named sons: Shimri, appointed chief by his father though not firstborn, then Hilkiah, Tebaliah, and Zechariah. Sons and brothers totaled thirteen. With Shuppim he received the western lot by the gate of Shallecheth at the ascending causeway.
