Peter's denial

event · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: denied, denied it, deny, deny me three times

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Peter denies being Jesus' companion three times in the high priest's court, then remembers Jesus' prediction and weeps bitterly. In The Good News According to Mark, Peter denies knowing Jesus three times in the high priest's courtyard, fulfilling Jesus' prediction. In The Gospel According to Luke, Peter denies knowing Jesus three times in the high priest's courtyard, and the fulfilled prediction leads him to weep bitterly.
