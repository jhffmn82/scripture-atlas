Abstract written by a model

REV. AUBREY MOORE is a tutor at Keble and Magdalen Colleges, Oxford, and an honorary canon of Christ Church. In an appended note, he compares Chuang Tzŭ with Western philosophy while cautioning against Western preconceptions.
