art of preserving life

Abstract written by a model

consists_in inaction [by which there is nothing that cannot be accomplished] The art of preserving life consists in keeping all in ONE, losing nothing, knowing when to stop and how much is enough, leaving others alone, attending to oneself, and remaining without cares and excessive knowledge. It is illustrated by the child, whose constitutional harmony allows it to act, move, and adapt without conscious calculation. Lao Tzŭ later connects it with freedom from trouble, repose, and unconscious adaptation to circumstances.
