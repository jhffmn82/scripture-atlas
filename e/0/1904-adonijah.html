Abstract written by a model

Adonijah, handsome son of King David and Haggith, born after Absalom, exalts himself as king with backing from Joab and Abiathar. When Solomon is anointed, his guests flee and he clings to the altar in fear. Solomon spares him conditionally, but after Adonijah asks for Abishag through Bathsheba, Solomon deems it a threat and has Benaiah kill him.

Chapter 1

Adonijah, son of Haggith, exalts himself, saying "I will be king," and gathers chariots, horsemen, and support from Joab and Abiathar. He holds a feast for his brothers and the men of Judah, but when Solomon is anointed his guests flee, and he grabs the altar's horns in fear. Solomon spares him conditionally, saying he will be safe if he shows himself worthy, and sends him home.

Chapter 2

Adonijah, who says the kingdom had been his, asks Bathsheba to request Abishag the Shunammite as his wife. Solomon treats the request as speaking against his own life and has Benaiah kill him.
