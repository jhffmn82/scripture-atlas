Abstract written by a model

The recipients are the believers at Colossae, described as saints and faithful brothers in Christ who learned the grace of God in truth from Epaphras. They are praised for faith in Christ Jesus, love toward all the saints, and hope laid up in the heavens. Once alienated and enemies in mind through evil deeds, they have been delivered into the Kingdom of the Son and reconciled through Christ's death, with forgiveness of sins, to be presented holy and blameless if they remain grounded and steadfast. Paul prays they be filled with knowledge of God's will, walk worthily, and bear fruit. They are urged to walk in Christ, rooted and established, and warned against philosophy, observances, angel worship, and ordinances, since they were circumcised, buried and raised with Christ, made alive, and died to the elemental spirits of the world. Having been raised with Christ, their life is hidden with him in God. They are to seek things above, put to death earthly vices, put off the old man, put on the new man and its virtues, and walk in love, thankfulness, and teaching. Finally, they receive instructions on prayer, wise conduct toward outsiders, greetings, reading the letter from Laodicea, and Archippus's ministry.

recipients of the letter (Colossians) in Paul’s Letter to the Colossians

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

The believers at Colossae are praised for their faith in Christ Jesus and love toward all the saints, having learned the grace of God in truth from Epaphras. They were once alienated and enemies in mind through evil deeds but are now reconciled through Christ's death, to be presented holy, without defect and blameless, if they continue grounded and steadfast in the faith. Paul prays that they be filled with knowledge of God's will, walk worthily, and bear fruit.

Chapter 2

The recipients are urged to walk in Christ, rooted, built up, established in the faith, and abounding in thanksgiving. They were circumcised in Christ, buried and raised with him in baptism, and made alive after being dead in trespasses. They are warned against philosophy, judgment over observances, angel worship, and submitting to ordinances although they died with Christ from the elemental spirits of the world.

Chapter 3

The readers are told they were raised with Christ, died, and have their life hidden with Christ in God, and will be revealed with him in glory. They are commanded to seek things above, put to death earthly vices, put off the old man, put on the new man and the Christian virtues, and walk in love. They are also to be thankful, let Christ's word dwell in them, teach and admonish one another with songs, and do all in the Lord Jesus' name.

Chapter 4

The recipients are exhorted to treat servants justly if they are masters, to continue steadfastly in prayer with thanksgiving, to walk wisely toward outsiders and to speak graciously. They are told to expect Tychicus and Onesimus, to greet the brothers in Laodicea with Nymphas and the assembly in his house, to read this letter also to the Laodiceans and read the one from Laodicea, and to tell Archippus to fulfill his ministry.
