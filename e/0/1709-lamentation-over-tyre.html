Chapter 27

The lamentation is commissioned by the LORD in verse 2 and takes the form of a long description of Tyre as a splendid ship and trading power. It also anticipates the mourners' own lament: 'Who is there like Tyre, like her who is brought to silence in the middle of the sea?'
