saints

Also called: his people, his saints, the humble, the saints, them, themselves, they

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the saints gather in the assembly of the new song, rejoice and sing for joy on their beds, and execute vengeance and punishments on nations. In 2 Corinthians, the saints receive the giving service that supplies their lack and glorify God for Achaia's obedience and generosity. In The Second Book of Nephi, the saints are persecuted and slain by the wicked, but the righteous who heed prophets, protect them, and look forward to Christ preserve them. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the saints are killed and shed blood through wickedness, and their blood cries against wicked people.

saints in Psalms

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where saints first appears

Abstract written by a model

The saints are the people of the LORD, a group honored by God. They gather in the assembly of the new song, rejoicing and singing for joy on their beds. With high praises in their mouths and two-edged swords in their hands, they execute vengeance and punishments on nations, binding kings with chains and nobles with iron fetters.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 149

The saints form the assembly in which the new song is sung, and they are told to rejoice in honor and sing for joy on their beds. They are to have high praises of God in their mouths and a two-edged sword in their hands to execute vengeance, punishments, and the written judgment. The text says all his saints have this honor.
