The Analects of Confucius, Book VIII (T'ai-po)

document · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

The unit presents teachings of Confucius and the philosopher Tsang on virtuous conduct, learning, propriety, government, and personal humility. It describes how the people respond to the conduct of those in high stations and what conditions accompany good or bad government. It praises Shun, Yu, Yao, King Wan, and King Wu for their governance and virtue, while emphasizing the importance of perfect virtue, sincerity, and endurance. The unit concludes that Yu displayed filial piety, elegance in sacrifice, and great labor for the empire despite his simple way of living.
