Abstract written by a model

A heap of stones in the mountain of Gilead, gathered by Jacob's relatives beside his pillar after a shared meal. Named Jegar Sahadutha by Laban, Galeed by Jacob, and Mizpah, it witnesses that neither man will cross it to harm the other.

Chapter 31

Jacob's relatives gather stones into a heap beside the pillar Jacob sets up, and they eat there. Laban calls it Jegar Sahadutha and Jacob calls it Galeed, and it is also named Mizpah. It stands as a witness that neither man will pass over it to harm the other.
