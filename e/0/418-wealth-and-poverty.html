Chapter 28

Wealth gained by excessive interest is gathered for one who pities the poor, and a person eager to be rich will not go unpunished. Working one's land brings abundant food, while chasing fantasies brings poverty, and the stingy man hurrying after riches does not know poverty awaits him.
