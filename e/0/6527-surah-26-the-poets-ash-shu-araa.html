Abstract written by a model

Surah 26 concerns revelation, Muhammad, and the warnings delivered through the stories of Moses, Abraham, Noah, Hud, Salih, Lot, and Shu'eyb. Moses confronts Pharaoh, his wizards believe, the Children of Israel escape, and the sea parts before Pharaoh's people are drowned. Abraham rejects idols, while the peoples of Noah, A'ad, Thamud, Lot, and Midian deny their messengers and receive punishment. The unit affirms Allah's power, mercy, judgment, resurrection, and revelation, and concludes by distinguishing false poets from believers who do good works and remember Allah.
