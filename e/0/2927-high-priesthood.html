high priesthood

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: the high priesthood, the office

Abstract written by a model

The office was taken from Onias by his brother Jason through a promise of silver, then taken from Jason by Menelaus, who outbid him by three hundred talents. Menelaus left his brother Lysimachus as deputy in it. The text calls Jason an ungodly man and not a high priest, and Menelaus kept the office through the covetous dealings of those in power.
