Abstract written by a model

King Hui of Liang's kingdom contains great officers and people, governed through moving people and conveying grain in bad years. It is endangered when profit is prioritized over righteousness; benevolence and righteousness should guide it, yet its people do not increase while animals consume human food.

Chapter 1

The kingdom would be endangered if its rulers and people prioritized profit and competed to seize it from one another. The discussion concerns how benevolence and righteousness should guide the kingdom.

Chapter 3

The kingdom is governed by King Hui of Liang, who moves people and conveys grain during bad years. Its people do not increase despite these efforts, and its animals consume food that should support humans.
