Nazirite vow

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Nazirite, No razor shall come on his head, a Nazirite, a Nazirite to God, his separation, his vow of separation, law of the Nazirite, separation, special vow, the law of the Nazirite, the vow of a Nazirite, vow

Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the Nazirite vow is a special vow of separation to the LORD for a set period, requiring abstinence from grape products, uncut hair, avoidance of the dead, and observance according to the vow made. In The Book of Judges, the Nazirite vow is a consecration to God from the womb to death, forbidding a razor on the head and involving abstinence from wine, strong drink, uncleanness, and vine products.

Nazirite vow in The Book of Judges

author unknown · about 1000 BC · the work’s page · read it where Nazirite vow first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Nazirite vow is a consecration to God from the womb to death, forbidding a razor on the head. An angel announces it for the promised child, with his mother avoiding wine, strong drink, uncleanness and vine products. Later Samson reveals he has kept it, and says shaving would remove his strength.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

The angel declared that the promised child would be a Nazirite to God from the womb to the day of his death. This meant no razor on his head, and his mother was to drink no wine or strong drink and eat nothing unclean, nor anything that comes of the vine.

Chapter 16

Samson tells Delilah that no razor has ever come on his head because he has been a Nazirite to God from his mother's womb. He says that if he is shaved his strength will leave him and he will become like any other man.
