Chapter 25

Fitly spoken words are like apples of gold in settings of silver, and a wise reproof is like a gold earring to an obedient ear. Faithful messengers refresh like cold snow at harvest, and patience and a soft tongue can persuade and break the bone. Restraint is urged, in eating honey, seeking honor, and governing one's spirit, along with kindness to an enemy who is hungry or thirsty, for which the LORD will reward.
