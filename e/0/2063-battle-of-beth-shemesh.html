Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, it was the battle at Beth Shemesh in which Amaziah and Jehoash faced each other, Judah was defeated by Israel, Amaziah was captured, and Jerusalem's wall and treasures were taken. In The Second Book of Chronicles, it was the battle at Beth Shemesh in which Amaziah's challenge drew Joash of Israel to battle, Judah was defeated, and every man fled to his tent.
