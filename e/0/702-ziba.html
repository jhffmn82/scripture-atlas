Abstract written by a model

Ziba is a servant of Saul's house with fifteen sons and twenty servants. He tells David about Jonathan's lame son, and David orders his household to till Saul's land for Mephibosheth, making them Mephibosheth's servants. Later, Ziba meets the fleeing David with donkeys and provisions, says Mephibosheth is staying in Jerusalem hoping to regain his father's kingdom, and receives all Mephibosheth's property from David, bowing and asking for favor.

Chapter 9

Ziba is a servant of Saul's house who tells David that Jonathan still has a lame son and where he is. David commands him, with his fifteen sons and twenty servants, to till Saul's land for Mephibosheth, and Ziba agrees to do as the king commands. His household becomes servants to Mephibosheth.

Chapter 16

Ziba, servant of Mephibosheth, meets David with saddled donkeys, bread, raisins, summer fruit and wine. He tells David that Mephibosheth is staying in Jerusalem expecting Israel to restore his father's kingdom, and David grants him all that belonged to Mephibosheth; Ziba bows and asks for favor.
