divine knowledge

Also called: Divine knowledge

Abstract written by a model

Divine knowledge is the highest duty: difficult to seek or speak of, gained by God’s grace rather than words, skill, or orders. Without it, people are consumed by the minister of death. It requires a guru, restrains the mind, and washes away impurity.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Slok IV .. Pauri IV

Divine knowledge is not sought in mere words, and speaking about it is difficult. A person obtains it by God's grace, not through skill or orders.

Slok V .. Pauri V

Those who are devoid of divine knowledge are robbed and consumed by the minister of death.

Slok XII .. Pauri XII

Divine knowledge controls and restrains the mind. The text says that divine knowledge cannot exist without a guru.

Slok XVIII .. Pauri XVIII

Divine knowledge washes away impurity rather than physical washing.
