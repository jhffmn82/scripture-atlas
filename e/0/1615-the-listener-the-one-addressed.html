Abstract written by a model

The listener is a man addressed in counsel, asked who will answer him and urged not to despise the Almighty's chastening. He is promised God's deliverance in six troubles and seven, redemption from famine and war, peace with the animals and stones, a peaceful tent missing nothing, great offspring like grass, and finally a grave reached in full age like a shock of grain in season, all for his good.

Chapter 5

The listener is asked whether anyone will answer him and to which of the holy ones he will turn. He is told not to despise the chastening of the Almighty and is promised deliverance in six troubles and in seven, redemption in famine and war, and hiding from the scourge of the tongue. He is also promised peace with the animals, a peaceful tent, great offspring, and a grave reached in full age, and is urged to hear and know it for his good.
