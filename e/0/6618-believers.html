believers

Also called: Our believing slaves, believing slaves, those who believe, those who believed, those who put faith

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 27: The Ant (An-Naml), believers establish worship, pay the poor-due, are sure of the Hereafter, receive guidance and mercy, and those who ward off evil are saved. In Surah 37: Those drawn up in Ranks (As-Saaffaat), believers put faith in Allah and His sent warners, and the believing slaves who are single-minded are promised provision, honor, and reward.
