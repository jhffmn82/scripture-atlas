Chapter 28

Hananiah, a prophet from Gibeon, spoke in the LORD's house before the priests and people, claiming the LORD had broken Babylon's yoke and would return the vessels and captives within two years. He broke the bar from Jeremiah's neck, and was told by Jeremiah that the LORD had not sent him and that he made the people trust a lie. He died that same year in the seventh month.
