Kinvat Bridge

place · 3 works · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Judge's Bridge, Kinvad bridge, Kinvat bridge, Kinvat-bridge, the Kinvad bridge, the Kinvad bridge made by Mazda, the holy bridge made by Mazda, the powerful Kinvat-bridge, the veritable Judgment Bridge, this (temptation-)bridge of earth

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Kinvat Bridge is the bridge and place of judgment crossed by souls. In Vendidad (Vendîdâd), the Kinvad bridge is the holy bridge made by Mazda where souls are asked for the reward of their worldly goods. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Kinvat-bridge is a powerful, well-kept bridge crossed by the soul of man in perfect holiness.

Kinvat Bridge in Yasna

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where Kinvat Bridge first appears

Abstract written by a model

Kinvat Bridge is a bridge and place of judgment crossed by souls. Zarathustra leads the righteous there, and souls cross it in holiness after prescribed acts and speech. At the bridge, conscience crushes the wicked spirit; the wicked miss the path, fall into the Lie’s abode, and struggle to pollute Asha’s paths.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XLVI.

The Judge's Bridge is the place toward which Zarathustra will lead the righteous with those who follow him. The wicked approach it but miss their path and fall into the Lie's abode.

Yasna LI.

The Kinvat Bridge is the place of judgment where the righteous man's conscience crushes the wicked man's spirit. The wicked spirit struggles to reach and pollute Asha's paths there.

Yasna LXXI (Sp. LXX).

The Kinvat Bridge is crossed by the soul in holiness when the person has acted and spoken as prescribed.
