Jesus sends the twelve out two by two with authority over unclean spirits, allowing only a staff, sandals and one tunic, no bread or money. They stay in one house per place, preach repentance, cast out demons, heal the sick, then report back.

Chapter 6

Jesus called the twelve and sent them out two by two with authority over unclean spirits, instructing them to take only a staff, wear sandals, and stay in one house in each place. They were to shake off the dust against places that refused them. They preached repentance, cast out many demons, and anointed and healed many sick people, then gathered to tell Jesus all they had done and taught.
