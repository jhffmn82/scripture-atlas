Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Jehoiada was the priest who led action for the LORD's house, gathered captains, crowned Joash, had Athaliah killed, appointed officers, instructed Jehoash, and established a chest for temple repair funds. In The Second Book of Chronicles, Jehoiada was the chief priest who crowned and anointed the king's son, had Athaliah killed, ended Baal worship, guided King Joash, chose his wives, oversaw temple repairs, and was buried among the kings for his good toward Israel and God.

Jehoiada in The Second Book of Kings

author unknown · 560 to 539 BC · the work’s page · read it where Jehoiada first appears

Abstract written by a model

Jehoiada is a priest who leads decisive action for the LORD's house. He gathers captains, covenants with them, crowns Joash, has Athaliah killed, and appoints officers over the house. He then instructs Jehoash, who does right while instructed. When priests fail to repair the house, Jehoiada bores a hole in a chest's lid and sets it beside the altar.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

Jehoiada the priest gathered the captains, made a covenant with them, showed them the king's son, and commanded how the watches were to be set. He supplied David's spears and shields, crowned Joash, ordered Athaliah removed and killed outside the LORD's house, made covenants between the LORD, the king and the people, and appointed officers over the LORD's house.

Chapter 12

Jehoiada the priest instructed Jehoash, who did right in the LORD's eyes all the days of that instruction. When the priests stopped taking money but did not repair the house, Jehoiada took a chest, bored a hole in its lid, and set it beside the altar on the right side as one enters the LORD's house.

Jehoiada in The Second Book of Chronicles

author unknown · about 424 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Jehoiada first appears

Abstract written by a model

Jehoiada was a chief priest who, in the seventh year, covenanted with the captains and Levites, had the king's son crowned and anointed, had Athaliah killed, and ended Baal worship, restoring the temple's offerings and gatekeepers. He then guided King Joash, chose his two wives, and oversaw temple repair funds. He died at 130 and was buried among the kings in David's city for his good toward Israel and God. Afterward Joash killed his son Zechariah, and Joash's servants killed Joash.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 23

Jehoiada the priest strengthened himself in the seventh year, made a covenant with the captains of hundreds, and directed the Levites and people in guarding the king's son and the temple. He had the king crowned and anointed with his sons, ordered Athaliah removed and killed outside the LORD's house, and made a covenant between himself, the people, and the king. He then destroyed Baal worship's presence, restored the temple's offerings and gatekeepers, and brought the king to the throne.

Chapter 24

Jehoiada the priest, called the chief, guided Joash, took two wives for him, and with the king oversaw the money used to restore the LORD's house. He died full of days at 130 and was buried in David's city among the kings for his good toward Israel, God, and his house. His son Zechariah was later killed by Joash, and Joash's servants killed the king for the blood of Jehoiada's sons.
