Abstract written by a model

Men are of ten kinds. Those born into families with fields, houses, gold, cattle, slaves, and servants may possess friends and relations and be of good family, healthy, wise, noble, famous, and powerful. After enjoying unrivalled human pleasures, through pure religious merit acquired in a former life, they obtain true knowledge.
