Abstract written by a model

In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, learning involves seeking from day to day to increase knowledge. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XVI (Ke She), learning enables some people to gain possession of knowledge and allows those who are dull and stupid to achieve learning; failure to learn places dull and stupid people in the lowest class.
