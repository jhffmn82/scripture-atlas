Abstract written by a model

Jesus' sheep are his followers who recognize his voice, are called by name, and follow him while fleeing strangers. They receive eternal life and cannot be snatched from his or the Father's hand. Other sheep will join them as one flock; unbelieving Jews are not his sheep.

Chapter 10

The sheep listen to the shepherd's voice, are called by name, and follow him but flee strangers. Jesus says his sheep hear his voice and follow him, are given eternal life, will never perish, and cannot be snatched from his or the Father's hand. He has other sheep not of this fold who will become one flock with one shepherd, and he tells the Jews they do not believe because they are not of his sheep.
