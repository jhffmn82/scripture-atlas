Chapter 27

Isaac's blessing gives Jacob the dew of the sky, the fatness of the earth, grain and new wine, service from peoples and nations, and lordship over his brothers. It also declares cursed those who curse him and blessed those who bless him. It cannot be reversed once given, leaving Esau only a lesser blessing and provoking his hatred of Jacob.
