Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Kings, Solomon's wisdom is presented as a God-given gift whose breadth and fame surpass those of other rulers and sages. In The Second Book of Chronicles, Solomon's wisdom is presented as a God-given gift that enables him to answer the queen of Sheba and surpass kings in wisdom and riches.

Solomon's wisdom in The First Book of Kings

author unknown · about 549 BC · the work’s page · read it where Solomon's wisdom first appears

Abstract written by a model

Solomon's wisdom is a gift from God, put in his heart, with abundant understanding and breadth of mind like the sand on the seashore. It surpasses the wisdom of the children of the east, of Egypt, and of Ethan, Heman, Calcol, and Darda. Chapter 4 shows its scope: three thousand proverbs, one thousand five songs, and knowledge of trees, animals, birds, creeping things, and fish. Its fame spreads among surrounding nations, and kings send people to hear him. In Chapter 10 the queen of Sheba tests him, finds nothing hidden from him, and says the reality exceeds the report. All the earth seeks him, and he exceeds all kings in wisdom.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

God gives Solomon abundant wisdom, understanding, and breadth of mind like the sand on the seashore. It excels that of all the children of the east and of Egypt, and he is wiser than Ethan, Heman, Calcol, and Darda. He speaks three thousand proverbs, composes one thousand five songs, and speaks of trees, animals, birds, creeping things, and fish, so that people sent by kings of all nations come to hear him.

Chapter 10

Solomon's wisdom drew the queen of Sheba to test him, and he answered every question with nothing hidden from him. She said the reality exceeded the report she had heard, and called his men happy for hearing it. All the earth sought his presence to hear the wisdom God had put in his heart, and he exceeded all kings in wisdom.

Solomon’s wisdom in The Second Book of Chronicles

author unknown · about 424 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Solomon’s wisdom first appears

Abstract written by a model

Solomon's wisdom is a God-given gift, put in his heart by the LORD. It enabled him to answer all the queen of Sheba's questions; she found its greatness exceeded its fame, and kings of the earth sought him, as he surpassed them in wisdom and riches.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 9

Solomon's wisdom let him answer all the queen of Sheba's questions, and she said half of its greatness had not been told her and that he exceeded its fame. All the kings of the earth sought his presence to hear the wisdom God had put in his heart, and he exceeded them all in wisdom and riches.
