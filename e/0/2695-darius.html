Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Darius orders the archives searched and, after the scroll of Cyrus's decree is found, issues his own decree confirming it. He commands the governors beyond the River to leave the work alone and fund it, and threatens those who alter the decree. His sixth year is when the house is finished.
