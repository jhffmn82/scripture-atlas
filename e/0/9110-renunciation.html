Abstract written by a model

Renunciation is preached by professors conversant with pain and practiced by wise, unattached people who know karman. It means leaving the world, possessions, and passions; living as a mendicant; and bearing disagreeable feelings to give up sinfulness and attain liberation. Mahâvîra exemplifies it by abandoning robe, shelter, food, treatment, and attachments, wandering naked and houseless.

Fourth Lecture, Called Righteousness. / First Lesson. .. Fourth Lesson.

Renunciation is preached by all the professors conversant with pain. Thoroughly knowing karman, the wise and unattached practice renunciation.

Sixth Lecture, Called the Cleaning. / First Lesson. .. Fifth Lesson.

Renunciation involves leaving the world, abandoning possessions and passions, living as a mendicant, and bearing all disagreeable feelings. It is taught as the way to give up sinfulness and attain liberation.

Eighth Lecture, (Called) the Pillow of Righteousness. / First Lesson. .. Fourth Lesson.

Renunciation required Mahâvîra to give up his robe, possessions, shelter, delicious food, medical treatment, and attachment to householders and worldly pleasures. He wandered as a naked, houseless sage and did not answer those who questioned or insulted him except when necessary.
