Rages of Media is the destination where the money is to be collected. Tobias asks his companion whether he knows those places well, and Raphael answers that he knows the way well.

Rages of Media is the place where Gabael is, and Tobias directs Raphael to go there to receive the money. Raphael travels there, lodges with Gabael, and collects the bags before the return journey to the wedding feast.
