the Lecture

Also called: Surah 43: Ornaments of gold (Az-Zukhruf), a Lecture, a Lecture in Arabic, a Reminder, an Arab, it, the Lecture in Arabic, the Truth, this Qur'an

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), the Lecture in Arabic has verses expounded for knowledgeable people and serves as guidance and healing for believers. In Surah 42: Consultation (Ash-Shura), the Lecture is inspired by Allah in Arabic so Muhammad can warn people and call them toward Allah's guidance and the right path. In Surah 43: Ornaments of gold (Az-Zukhruf), Surah 43 concerns Allah's revelation, the rejection of prophets, and the consequences awaiting believers and rejecters. In Surah 43: Ornaments of gold (Az-Zukhruf), the text presents Allah's Arabic revelation as clear, sublime, and decisive.

Abstract written by a model

Surah 43: Ornaments of gold (Az-Zukhruf) concerns Allah's revelation, the rejection of prophets, and the consequences awaiting believers and rejecters. It recounts how Muhammad, Moses, and Jesus delivered Allah's guidance, while their opponents mocked or disputed it. The unit contrasts the temporary wealth of the life of the world with the Garden for the believers and hell for the guilty. It affirms Allah's power over creation, resurrection, judgment, and the Hour.
