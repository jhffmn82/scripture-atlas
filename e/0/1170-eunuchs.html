Chapter 56

Eunuchs are told not to say, "Behold, I am a dry tree." To those who keep the LORD's Sabbaths, choose what pleases him, and hold fast his covenant, he promises a memorial and a name within his house and walls, better than sons and daughters, an everlasting name that will not be cut off.
