Chapter 8

Noah sends the dove out to see whether the waters have abated, and the first time she finds no place to rest her foot and returns, so Noah takes her back into the ship. Seven days later she returns at evening with a freshly plucked olive leaf in her mouth. After another seven days Noah sends her out again and she does not return.
