Abstract written by a model

Abraham makes his servant swear by the LORD, with his hand under Abraham's thigh, not to take a Canaanite wife for Isaac but to find one among Abraham's relatives. The oath releases the servant if the woman is unwilling, though he must not bring Isaac back; the servant repeats its terms to Rebekah's family.
