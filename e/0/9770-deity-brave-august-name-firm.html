Sect.

Deity Brave-August-Name-Firm came bearing a thousand-draught rock and demanded a trial of strength with Brave-Awful-Possessing-Male-Deity. After his hand was first turned into an icicle and then a sword-blade, his hand was crushed, he fled, and he agreed to obey his father's command and yield the Central Land of Reed-Plains.
