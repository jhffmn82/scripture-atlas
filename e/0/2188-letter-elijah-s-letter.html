Chapter 21

The letter from Elijah came to Jehoram in the name of the LORD, the God of David. It charged him with walking in Israel's kings' ways, leading Judah into prostitution, and slaying his better brothers. It foretold a great plague on his people, children, wives, and possessions, and a bowel disease that would make his bowels fall out.
