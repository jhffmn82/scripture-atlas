King Hiko-imasu

person · 1 work · 17 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Hiko-imasu

Abstract written by a model

King Hiko-imasu, a Deity and child of Emperor Kai-Kuwa and Oke-tsu-hime, wed four women and fathered eleven Kings, including Ohō-mata, Wo-mata, Noble Shibumi, Tatatsu-michi-no-ushi, and Ma-wata. Through these marriages and children, his descendants became ancestors of numerous Dukes, Lords, Chiefs, and regional rulers.

Sect. LXII.—Emperor Kai-Kuwa.

King Hiko-imasu was the child of Emperor Kai-Kuwa and Her Augustness Oke-tsu-hime. He fathered eleven Kings through several marriages, and his descendants became ancestors of numerous Dukes, Lords, Chiefs, and regional rulers.
