Chapter 3

Ehud, a left-handed Benjamite, was raised up by the LORD as a savior and sent Israel's tribute to Eglon. He made a two-edged sword, hid it on his right thigh, and killed Eglon alone in the upper room under pretense of a message from God, then locked the doors and escaped to Seirah. He blew a trumpet in Ephraim and led Israel to take the Jordan fords and defeat Moab.
