Abstract written by a model

David's promised offspring, raised up by the LORD after David sleeps with his fathers. He will build a house for the LORD's name, with the LORD as his father and an eternally established throne. Iniquity brings chastening with men's rod, yet lovingkindness never departs, unlike with Saul.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 7

The LORD promises that after David sleeps with his fathers he will set up an offspring from David's own body and establish his kingdom. This son will build a house for the LORD's name, and the LORD will be his father. If he commits iniquity he will be chastened with the rod of men, but the LORD's loving kindness will not depart from him.

Immanuel in The Book of Isaiah

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Immanuel first appears

Abstract written by a model

Immanuel is a child, the son a virgin will conceive and bear. In chapter 7 he is a sign: before he knows to refuse evil and choose good, the land of the two kings Ahaz abhors is forsaken, and he eats butter and honey. In chapter 9 he is born and given, bearing names Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, and Prince of Peace. Government rests on his shoulders, and he reigns on David's throne forever with justice and righteousness.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

Immanuel is the name to be given to the son the virgin will conceive and bear. Before he knows to refuse evil and choose good, the land whose two kings Ahaz abhors will be forsaken, and he will eat butter and honey.

Chapter 9

A child is born and a son is given to us, and the government will be on his shoulders. He is named Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, and Prince of Peace, and his government on David's throne will increase without end, upheld with justice and righteousness forever.
