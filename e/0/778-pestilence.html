Abstract written by a model

A pestilence sent by the LORD on Israel, chosen by David from three options, kills seventy thousand from Dan to Beersheba, is halted before Jerusalem's destruction, and is removed after David's altar offerings.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 24

The pestilence is one of three options the LORD offers, and David chooses it. It falls on Israel from morning until the appointed time and kills seventy thousand from Dan to Beersheba. It is halted before Jerusalem is destroyed and removed after David's sacrifices.
