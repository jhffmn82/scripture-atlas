Sirozahs (Sîrôzahs)

document · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

This unit presents Sîrôzah I and II, prayers and sacrifices addressed to Ahura Mazda, the Amesha-Spentas, and many other divine beings. Ahura Mazda is invoked as bright and glorious and as the Maker. The Amesha-Spentas are repeatedly invoked or sacrificed to as beneficent and all-ruling, and each is associated with particular virtues or sacred powers. The text also names waters, plants, stars, winds, Fravashis, mountains, laws, and other beings honored in the prayers.
