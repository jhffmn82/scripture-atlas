Mammon

Also called: mammon, the unrighteous mammon, unrighteous mammon

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Luke, Mammon is called unrighteous, and Jesus urges making friends by means of it while saying no one can serve both God and Mammon. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, Mammon is a master that cannot be served together with God.
