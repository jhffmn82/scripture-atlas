camel of Allah

animal · 4 works · 8 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: camel, her, she-camel, she-camel of Allah, the camel, the camel of Allah, the she-camel

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), the camel of Allah is a sign for Thamud and may feed in Allah's earth, but they hamstring her and bring punishment upon themselves. In Surah 11: Hud, the camel of Allah is given to Thamud as a token to feed in Allah's earth; when Thamud hamstring her, they bring upon themselves threatened punishment. In Surah 54: The Moon (Al-Qamar), the she-camel is sent as a test for Thamud, with water to be shared, but Thamud's comrade takes and hamstrings her. In Surah 91: The Sun (Ash-Shams), the she-camel of Allah is to be allowed to drink, but Thamud deny Allah's messenger and hamstring her.
