Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Zarathustrian Faith receives offerings through opposition to evil and good deeds, thoughts, words, labour, and admonition, and is described as Ahura's Religion. In Yasna, holy Faith is sought for hallowing and understanding, furthers Righteous Order, advances lands in Asha, makes actions just, and guides the enlightened man's will, creed, words, and actions.

holy Faith in Yasna

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where holy Faith first appears

Abstract written by a model

Holy Faith is the best holy faith of Ahura Mazda's people, sought for hallowing and understanding. Taught by the beneficent kingdom's lord, it furthers Righteous Order, advances lands in Asha, and makes actions just. The enlightened man closely follows it, aligning will, creed, words, and actions.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XLIV.

The Faith of Ahura Mazda's people is something the speaker seeks to hallow and understand. It is taught by the beneficent kingdom's lord and is said to advance the speaker's lands in Asha and make actions just through Piety Âramaiti.

Yasna XLVIII.

The holy Faith is to be followed closely by the enlightened man, whose struggle and effort are devoted to it. His will, wish, creed, fealty, words, and actions must be consistent with it.
