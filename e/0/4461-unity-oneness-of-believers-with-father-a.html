Abstract written by a model

Unity is the oneness of believers with the Father and Son, modeled on the Father-Son oneness. In Chapter 17 Jesus prays that the disciples, then all future believers, be one and perfected, so the world may believe the Father sent him.

Chapter 17

Jesus prays that the disciples be one as he and the Father are one, with the Father in him and he in them. He extends this to all future believers, that they be one in the Father and Son and be perfected into one. The purpose is that the world may believe and know that the Father sent him and loved them as he loved Jesus.
