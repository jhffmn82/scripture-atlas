Abstract written by a model

The generation of men of war who left Egypt were all circumcised, yet disobeyed the LORD by not heeding his voice. They were consumed in the wilderness over forty years, and the LORD swore they would not see the promised land; their children replaced them.

Chapter 5

All the males and men of war who came out of Egypt had been circumcised but died in the wilderness after forty years of walking. They were consumed because they did not listen to the LORD's voice, and the LORD swore they would not see the land promised to their fathers, a land flowing with milk and honey. Their children were raised up in their place.
