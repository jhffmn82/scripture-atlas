mourning of Moab

Abstract written by a model

Moab's mourning is a nationwide lament: people go up to Bayith, Dibon, and the high places to weep over Nebo and Medeba, with heads shaved, beards cut, and sackcloth worn in streets and on housetops. The wailing echoes through Heshbon, Elealeh, Jahaz, and to Moab's borders, and the speaker's heart cries out.

Chapter 15

Moab goes up to Bayith, Dibon, and the high places to weep and wails over Nebo and Medeba, with every head bald and every beard cut off. In the streets and on the housetops people wear sackcloth and weep abundantly. Heshbon, Elealeh, and Jahaz echo with the cry, which goes around Moab's borders to Eglaim and Beer Elim, and the speaker's own heart cries out for Moab.
