Surah 58: The Pleading Woman (Al-Mujaadila)

document · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns Allah's hearing and judgment, the pleading woman, Muhammad, and rulings about wives who are repudiated by comparison to mothers. It addresses secret conferences, conspiracy, alms before conference with Allah's messenger, oaths, and relations with those opposed to Allah and His messenger. It describes the fate of disbelievers and the devil's party, and the success of believers and Allah's party. It also describes the Day of Resurrection, Hell, the Fire, and the Gardens.
