Abstract written by a model

In Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods), Land of Idzumo contains Ifuya-Pass, Miho, the little shore of Inasa, and the shore of Tagishi; His Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness sought a palace there, Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land dwelt at Miho and had an heavenly august abode at Tagishi, and the Deity departed from it to the Land of Yamato. In Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods), the land of Idzumo was made and consolidated conjointly by the Great-Name-Possessor and the Little-Prince-the-Renowned-Deity. In Kojiki, Idzumo was where Prince Homu-chi-wake worshipped the Great Deity of Idzumo and where the ancestor of its rulers prepared a temporary palace and food for the prince.

Abstract written by a model

Land of Idzumo is a land containing Ifuya-Pass, Miho, the little shore of Inasa, and the shore of Tagishi. His Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness sought a palace there, while Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land dwelt at Miho and had an heavenly august abode at Tagishi. It is the place from which the Deity departs to the Land of Yamato.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Sect.

Idzumo was the land where His Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness searched for a place to build a palace. The august song says that the eight-fold fence of Idzumo makes an eight-fold fence for the spouses to retire within.

Idzumo is the place from which the Deity is about to depart.

Sect.
