Yellow Emperor

person · 1 work · 18 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Divine Teacher, Huang Ti, The Yellow Emperor, the Emperor, the Yellow Emperor, your Majesty

Abstract written by a model

The Yellow Emperor is a ruler, doubter, and being associated with TAO. He first seeks to govern heaven and earth, control the Two Powers, secure harvests, and feed his people, but Kuang Ch'êng Tzŭ rebukes this interference. He resigns the Throne, withdraws into solitude, and learns to preserve himself through perfect TAO. He teaches that TAO cannot be rightly known through conceptual knowledge, thoughts, resting, following, or pursuit. Traveling with six companions to TAO on Chü-tz'ŭ Mountain, he loses the road, questions a boy about government, and prostrates himself before him as Divine Teacher. He fights Ch'ih Yu, gives Hsien-ch'ih, rules nineteen years, obtains TAO, and soars to heaven.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XI.

The Yellow Emperor initially seeks to govern heaven and earth, control the Two Powers, secure harvests, and feed his people. After Kuang Ch'êng Tzŭ rebukes his interference, he resigns the Throne, withdraws into solitude, and learns about preserving the self through perfect TAO.

CHAPTER XXII.

The Yellow Emperor teaches that TAO is approached and attained through no thoughts, no resting, no following, and no pursuit. He says that those who understand TAO do not speak about it, and that knowledge of it makes Knowledge and himself wholly wrong.

CHAPTER XXIV.

The Yellow Emperor travels with six named companions to see TAO upon Chü-tz'ŭ Mountain. After losing the road, he questions the boy about governing the empire and finally prostrates himself before him as Divine Teacher.
