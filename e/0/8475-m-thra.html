Mãthra

topic · 2 works · 12 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Mãthra of Welfare and Immortality, Mãthra of Welfare and of Immortality, Mãthra word, Mãthra-word, Mãthras, every Mãthra, inspired Word-of-reason, sacred word of reason, the Mãthra, the entire bounteous Mãthra, the inspired Word-of-reason

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Mãthra is a word, teaching, incarnate word of reason, and offering of fatness that proclaims beneficent power, guides the speaker through Righteousness, and is linked to Welfare, Immortality, holiness, and a flourishing Realm. In Yasna, Mãthra is an inspired Word-of-reason opposing the Daêvas, bounteous and worshipped with its complete, long descent. In Yasna, the Mãthra-word belongs to Ahura Mazda; cheered servants speak it, and its speaker leads people toward weal and bliss. In Visparad, Mãthra is a sacred word of reason that receives sacrifice and is especially possessed by Zarathustra.

Mãthra in Yasna

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where Mãthra first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mãthra is a word, teaching, incarnate word of reason, and offering of fatness. Declared by the All-wise One as the best and world's first teaching, it proclaims beneficent power, guides the speaker through Righteousness, and is linked to Welfare, Immortality, holiness, and a flourishing Realm. It is declared from Ahura Mazda; disobedience ends in woe, while Sraosha embodies and holds first place in it.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XXXI.

The Mãthra of Welfare and Immortality is the best word that the All-wise One declares. It proclaims beneficent power and is associated with a Realm that flourishes in holiness to the Great Creator's glory.

Yasna XLV.

The Mãthra is the teaching that the speaker declares from Ahura Mazda. Those who do not fulfil and obey it are said to meet the end of life in woe.

Yasna LVII (Sp. LVI).

The Mãthra is identified as Sraosha’s body. Sraosha is also described as possessing the first place in the Mãthra.
