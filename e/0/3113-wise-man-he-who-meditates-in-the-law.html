wise man (he who meditates in the law)

person · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: he, he who has applied his soul and meditates in the law of the Most High

Abstract written by a model

The wise man is one who meditates in the law of the Most High, applying his soul to it and glorying in the covenant of the Lord. He seeks the wisdom of the ancients, serves among great men, and travels foreign nations, learning good and evil. He prays early and asks pardon; if the Lord wills, he pours forth wisdom. Nations and the congregation praise him, and his name endures across generations.

Chapter 39

He applies his soul to the law, seeks out the wisdom of the ancients, prophecies, proverbs and parables, serves among great men, and travels through foreign lands having learned good and evil. He rises early to pray to the Lord and asks pardon for his sins, and if the Lord wills he is filled with understanding and pours forth wisdom. His name and memory will live from generation to generation, and he will leave a greater name than a thousand.
