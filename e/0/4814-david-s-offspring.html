Chapter 89

The LORD promises that David's offspring will endure forever and his throne last like the days of heaven, the sun, and the moon. If his children forsake the law and break the statutes, they will be punished with the rod and stripes, but the LORD will not completely remove his loving kindness or let his faithfulness fail.
