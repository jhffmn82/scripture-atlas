Part II / Chapter 1 .. Chapter 38

Mencius says that the Spring and Autumn contains no righteous wars, though one war may be better than another. Correction belongs to supreme authority punishing subjects, while hostile States cannot correct one another; rulers who love benevolence can overcome enemies without relying on warfare.
