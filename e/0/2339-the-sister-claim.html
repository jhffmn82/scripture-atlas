the sister claim

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: He is my brother

Abstract written by a model

The sister claim is Abraham's statement that Sarah is his sister, echoed by Sarah at his request. It is true only in part, as she is his father's daughter, not his mother's. It leads Abimelech to take her; after Abraham explains his fear, Sarah is restored and Abraham enriched.

Chapter 20

Abraham's statement that Sarah is his sister, which she echoes by calling him her brother, leads Abimelech to take her. Abraham explains that he feared being killed for his wife and that she is truly his father's daughter, and that he had asked her to say it everywhere they traveled. Abimelech calls it a great sin brought on him and his kingdom, yet it ends with Sarah restored and Abraham enriched.
