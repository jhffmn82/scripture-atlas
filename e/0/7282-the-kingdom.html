Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, the Kingdom is a good, holy Realm belonging to Ahura Mazda and sought by worshippers for blessings and power. In Visparad, the Kingdom is to belong to Him who rules the best. In Visparad, Vohu-khshathra is a good kingdom receiving sacrifice and encompassing rewards, health, healing, progress, growth, and victory. In Visparad, Khshathra-vairya is the desired kingdom honored through sacrifice. In Afrinagan, Gahs and Miscellaneous Fragments, Khshathra-vairya is named with the Good Mind and Righteousness the Best and invoked against the Drug(k).

the Kingdom in Yasna

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where the Kingdom first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Kingdom, also called a Realm, is good, holy, mighty, and shining with splendour like the sun. It belongs to Ahura Mazda and is associated with good rulers, true saints, and those who follow Righteous Order. Requested from Ahura and Asha for disciples, it begins by giving peaceful home life and quiet happiness against fearful ravages. It is then sought as power to smite the Lie-demon, with headship and vigour given to faithful servants. Worshippers seek it for ever, as Ahura Mazda’s desired holy Realm, through which He gives highest blessings to the right-living poor.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XXIX.

The Sovereign Kingdom is requested from Ahura and Asha for the disciples. Through the Good Mind, it bestows peaceful home life and quiet happiness against fearful ravages.

Yasna XXXI.

The Kingdom is requested as a mighty power by which the Lie-demon may be smitten. Ahura Mazda gives its headship and vigorous might to those who follow Righteous Order and serve Him faithfully.

Yasna XLI.

Ahura Mazda's good Kingdom is something the worshippers seek to attain for ever. They ask that Ahura Mazda be a good King over them.

Yasna XLIII.

Ahura Mazda’s Realm is the desired place declared to be within His domain. It is associated with the preparation of Ahura Mazda’s Kingdom and is described as a holy Realm that shines with splendour like the sun.

Yasna LIII.

The Kingdom belongs to Ahura Mazda. Through the Kingdom, Ahura Mazda bestows the highest blessings on the right-living poor.
