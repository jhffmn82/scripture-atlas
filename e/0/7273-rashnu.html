In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, Rashnu Razista administers justice, smites thieves, destroys bandits, watches men worldwide, and receives praise, invocation, offerings, and blessings.

Rashnu Razista in Yashts (Yasts) I-XII

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Rashnu Razista first appears

Abstract written by a model

Rashnu Razista is a holy, tall, strong, true, just, beneficent, knowledgeable being associated with Sraosha. He administers justice, smites thieves, destroys bandits, and watches men worldwide. Praised and invoked, his friendship is sought for a prepared var and offerings; sacrifice, prayer, strength, and vigour are blessed for him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

XII. Rashn Yast.

Rashnu Razista is praised and invoked as strong, tall, true, beneficent, knowing, discerning, fore-knowing, far-seeing, and the best doer of justice and smiter of thieves. His friendship is invoked toward the var prepared and its associated offerings, wherever he is located. A sacrifice, prayer, strength, and vigour are blessed for him.
