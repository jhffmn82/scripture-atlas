Chapter 9

Moses sprinkles handfuls of furnace ashes toward the sky before Pharaoh, and they become small dust over Egypt and boils and blisters on man and animal. The magicians cannot stand before Moses because of the boils, which afflict them and all the Egyptians. The LORD then hardens Pharaoh's heart.
