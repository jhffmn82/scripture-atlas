Abstract written by a model

The ocean receives streams without overflowing and is constantly drained without becoming empty. Superior to rivers and brooks in size and stability, it takes shape from the universe yet remains small beside it.

CHAPTER XVII.

The ocean receives all streams without overflowing and is constantly drained without becoming empty. It is described as immeasurably superior to rivers and brooks, yet still insignificant in comparison with the universe.
