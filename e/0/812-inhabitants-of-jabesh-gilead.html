inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead in The First Book of Samuel

author unknown · about 930 to 721 BC · the work’s page · read it where inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead first appears

Abstract written by a model

The inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead are a community whose elders, facing Nahash's demand to lose their right eyes, sent messengers across Israel, were promised rescue by the next day, and were delivered. Later, hearing of Saul's fate, their valiant men marched overnight, took the bodies from Beth Shan's wall, burned them, buried the bones under a tamarisk, and fasted seven days.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

The men of Jabesh offer to serve Nahash, then learn the terms include losing their right eyes, so the elders send messengers across Israel. When promised rescue by the next day, they are glad and tell Nahash they will come out to him, and they are then delivered.

Chapter 31

The inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul. All their valiant men went through the night, took the bodies of Saul and his sons from the wall of Beth Shan, and burned them in Jabesh. They buried the bones under the tamarisk tree and fasted seven days.
