Abstract written by a model

Drink has well-flavoured and astringent parts. A monk or nun should consume all received drink without rejection, offer any excess to nearby agreeable fellow-ascetics, and follow seven rules, with an exception for lightly cleaned water used to water flour or sesamum.

Ninth Lesson.

A monk or nun should consume all received drink, whether well flavoured or astringent, and reject nothing. If the quantity is more than sufficient, it should be offered to agreeable fellow-ascetics living nearby.

Eleventh Lesson.

Drink is governed by seven rules corresponding to the seven rules for begging food. One specified exception permits a monk or nun to accept water used for watering flour or sesamum when it requires little cleaning or removal of impure articles.
