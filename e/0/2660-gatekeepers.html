Also called: The gatekeepers, chosen to be gatekeepers, four chief gatekeepers

Abstract written by a model

The gatekeepers are a group of 212 men chosen to guard the gates of God's house, led by chief Shallum with Akkub, Talmon, and Ahiman. Ordained by David and Samuel in an office of trust, they were stationed on four sides, relieved every seven days by brothers from villages, opened the house each morning, and four chief Levites oversaw the rooms and treasuries.

Chapter 9

The gatekeepers Shallum (chief), Akkub, Talmon, Ahiman, and their brothers were Korahites who previously served at the king's gate eastward. Totaling 212, they were ordained by David and Samuel, guarded the gates on four sides, were relieved by brothers coming every seven days, and opened the house each morning.
