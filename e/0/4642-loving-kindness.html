In Psalms, the LORD's loving kindness is great toward us and gives nations and peoples reason to praise him. In Psalms, the LORD's loving kindness is in the heavens, is precious, provides refuge under his wings, and is asked to continue toward those who know him. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Lord's everlasting kindness remains with the woman, enduring beyond mountains and hills. (The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman)
