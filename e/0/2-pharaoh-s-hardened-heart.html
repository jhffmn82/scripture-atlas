Pharaoh's hardened heart

Abstract written by a model

Pharaoh's hardened heart is a recurring condition in which the LORD foretells and causes Pharaoh's stubbornness. It follows the serpent sign and blood plague, then the boils. After the hail, Pharaoh hardens his own heart, and his servants do too. Throughout, he does not listen and does not release Israel, as the LORD spoke through Moses.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

The LORD says beforehand that he will harden Pharaoh's heart, and Pharaoh's heart is hardened after the serpent sign and again after the blood plague. Each time he does not listen, as the LORD had spoken, and the LORD calls his heart stubborn.

Chapter 9

After the boils the LORD hardens Pharaoh's heart and he does not listen. After the hail stops, Pharaoh hardens his own heart, and his servants harden theirs. The chapter closes saying his heart was hardened and he did not let Israel go, just as the LORD had spoken through Moses.
