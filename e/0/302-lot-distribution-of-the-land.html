In Numbers, the division of the land by lot assigned the inheritance to nine tribes and the half-tribe. In The Book of Joshua, the lot distribution of the land completed the division among the tribes.

lot distribution of the land in The Book of Joshua

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where lot distribution of the land first appears

Abstract written by a model

A lot distribution of the land, conducted by Eleazar the priest, Joshua and the heads of the fathers' houses at Shiloh before the LORD, at the Tent of Meeting's door. Lots fell in order to Simeon, Zebulun, Issachar, Asher, Naphtali and Dan, completing the division.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 19

The lots for the remaining tribes were cast in order: second Simeon, third Zebulun, fourth Issachar, fifth Asher, sixth Naphtali and seventh Dan. Eleazar the priest, Joshua and the heads of the fathers' houses distributed the inheritances by lot at Shiloh before the LORD, at the door of the Tent of Meeting, and so finished dividing the land.
