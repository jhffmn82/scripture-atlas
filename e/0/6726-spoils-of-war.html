spoils of war

object · 2 works · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: spoil, what ye have won, whatever ye take as spoils of war

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 8: The Spoils of War (Al-Anfaal), spoils of war belong to Allah and the messenger, with a fifth assigned for Allah, the messenger, the kinsman, orphans, the needy, and the wayfarer; what believers win is made lawful and good for them, with a command to keep their duty to Allah. In Surah 59: The Exile (Al-Hashr), spoil is assigned for Allah, His messenger, near of kin, orphans, the needy, and the wayfarer, so that it does not become a commodity among the rich; believers are told to take what the messenger gives them and abstain from what he forbids.
