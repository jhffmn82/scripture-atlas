Abstract written by a model

The transfiguration is an event on a mountain where Jesus, praying with Peter, John and James, is changed in appearance; Moses and Elijah appear speaking of his departure, a cloud and a voice call him beloved Son, and the three stay silent.

Chapter 9

About eight days after his sayings, Jesus goes up the mountain with Peter, John and James to pray, and his face changes and his clothing becomes white and dazzling. Moses and Elijah appear in glory and speak of his departure at Jerusalem, a cloud overshadows them, and a voice names him the beloved Son. Afterwards the three tell no one what they saw.
