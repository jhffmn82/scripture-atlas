Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, the priests are instructed to collect donor money and repair the house, while threshold-keeping priests put offerings into the chest. In 1 Chronicles, the priests serve God's house, are descended from Eleazar and Ithamar, and are divided into twenty-four orders. In The Second Book of Chronicles, the priests serve according to the divisions appointed by Solomon and David's ordinance and follow the king's commandment. In The Book of Nehemiah, the priests serve as temple servants, with some dwelling in Jerusalem and others in Judah's cities, and they purify themselves and participate in the wall's dedication. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, the priests serve as ministers of the Lord and are to be revered and given their commanded portions. In The Book of Joel, the priests serve as the LORD's ministers of the altar and are commanded to mourn when offerings are cut off.

priests in The Second Book of Kings

author unknown · 560 to 539 BC · the work’s page · read it where priests first appears

Abstract written by a model

The priests are a group instructed by Jehoash to collect donor money and repair the house, but by his twenty-third year they had not. They then agreed to take no more money and not repair it. Threshold-keeping priests put offerings into the chest; trespass and sin offering money stayed theirs.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 12

The priests were told by Jehoash to take the money from their donors and repair the damage to the house, but by his twenty-third year they had not done so. They then consented to take no more money from the people and not repair the house. The priests who kept the threshold put the money brought into the LORD's house into the chest, and the money for trespass and sin offerings remained the priests'.

priests in 1 Chronicles

author unknown · 449 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where priests first appears

Abstract written by a model

The priests are a group serving God's house. Named members include Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, Jachin, and Azariah, its ruler, with 1,760 able kinsmen, heads of fathers' houses. Descended from Eleazar and Ithamar, they were divided by lot into twenty-four orders, sixteen and eight, each serving its appointed time per Aaron's ordinance.

Chapter 9

The priests named include Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, Jachin, Azariah (ruler of God's house), Adaiah, and Maasai. Their brothers, heads of fathers' houses, numbered 1,760 very able men for the service of God's house, and some priests' sons prepared the mixing of the spices.

Chapter 24

The priests, descended from Eleazar and Ithamar, were divided into twenty-four orders by lot, sixteen from Eleazar's houses and eight from Ithamar's. Each order had its time to come into the LORD's house according to Aaron's ordinance.

priests in The Book of Nehemiah

author unknown · about 399 to 299 BC · the work’s page · read it where priests first appears

Abstract written by a model

The priests are a group of temple servants, some dwelling in Jerusalem and others in Judah's cities. Chapter 11 names leaders such as Jedaiah, Jachin, and Seraiah, with their brothers counted and Zabdiel overseeing. Chapter 12 lists those who returned with Zerubbabel and Jeshua. At the dedication they purified themselves, the people, gates, and wall, and sounded trumpets while Judah rejoiced.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

Priests who lived in Jerusalem included Jedaiah, Jachin, Seraiah (ruler of God's house), Adaiah, and Amashsai, with brothers numbering 822, 242, and 128. Their overseer was Zabdiel, and other priests remained in the cities of Judah.

Chapter 12

The priests who went up with Zerubbabel and Jeshua are listed by name, along with the heads of their fathers' households in the days of Joiakim. At the dedication they purified themselves, and priests' sons blew trumpets in the procession and stood with trumpets in God's house. Judah rejoiced for the priests and Levites who served, and the Levites set apart a portion for the sons of Aaron.
