Chapter 27

Tyre's rowers came from Sidon and Arvad, its pilots were its own wise men, and the old men of Gebal repaired seams. Its army included men of Persia, Lud, Put, and Arvad. When the ship falls, the mariners and pilots come ashore, cry bitterly, cast dust on their heads, wear sackcloth, and mourn for Tyre.
