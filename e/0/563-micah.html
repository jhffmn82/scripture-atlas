Abstract written by a model

Micah is a man of the hill country of Ephraim. He confesses to his mother that he took her eleven hundred pieces of silver and returns it. He then keeps a house of gods holding a carved image, a molten image, an ephod, and teraphim. He first consecrates one of his sons as priest, then hires a wandering young Levite, believing the LORD will do him good. Later the Danites take the images and the priest. Micah protests that he is left with nothing, but they warn him of angry men and he sees their strength, so he goes home.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 17

Micah, a man of the hill country of Ephraim, admits to his mother that he took her eleven hundred pieces of silver and returns it. He keeps a house of gods with an image, an ephod, and teraphim, and first consecrates one of his sons as priest. He then hires a wandering Levite as his priest, believing that the LORD will now do him good.

Chapter 18

Micah had hired the young Levite as his priest and kept an ephod, teraphim, a carved image, and a molten image in his house. When the Danites took the images and the priest, he protested that they had taken his gods and the priest, leaving him with nothing. After the Danites warned that angry men might take his life and his household's, and he saw they were too strong, he went back home.
