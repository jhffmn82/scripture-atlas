Abstract written by a model

A deity born from His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness's breath, His Augustness Prince-Lord-of-Heaven is chiefly identified as ancestor of numerous rulers, chiefs, suzerains, departmental suzerains, and territorial lords across several lands.
