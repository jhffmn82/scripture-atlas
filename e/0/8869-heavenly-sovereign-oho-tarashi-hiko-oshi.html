Heavenly Sovereign Oho-tarashi-hiko-oshiro-wake

person · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: The Heavenly Sovereign, The Heavenly Sovereign Oho-tarashi-hiko-oshiro-wake, this Heavenly Sovereign

Abstract written by a model

A Heavenly Sovereign dwelling at Hishiro in Makimuku who rules the Land of the Eight Great Islands. He marries Elder Lady of Inabi and Princess Ka-guro, fathers numerous children including Wo-usu and Yamato-take, lives one hundred and thirty-seven years, and is buried above Yamanobe road.

He dwells in the palace of Hishiro at Makimuku and rules the Empire. He has numerous children by several women, including His Augustness Wo-usu, His Augustness Yamato-take, and Prince I-ho-ki-no-iri, and his august years are one hundred and thirty-seven.
