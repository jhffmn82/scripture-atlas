Abstract written by a model

Oho-yamato-tarashi-hiko-kuni-oshi-bito, a deity and younger brother of Ame-oshi-tarashi-hiko, later ruled the Empire from the palace of Akidzushima at Muro in Kadzuraki. He wed Princess Oshika, begot children including Oho-kibi-no-moro-susumi, lived 123 years, and had a mausoleum at Mound of Tamade.

Sect. LVIII.—Emperor Ko-Sho.

His Augustness Oho-yamato-tarashi-hiko-kuni-oshi-bito was the younger of the two august children. He was the one who afterwards ruled the Empire.

Sect. LIX.—Emperor Koan.

Oho-yamato-tarashi-hiko-kuni-oshi-bito dwelt at the palace of Akidzushima at Muro in Kadzuraki and ruled the Empire. He wedded his niece, Her Augustness Princess Oshika, and begot Oho-kibi-no-moro-susumi and Oho-yamato-ne-ko-hiko-futo-ni.
