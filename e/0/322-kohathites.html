Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the Kohathites are a Levite family division responsible for carrying the most holy tabernacle objects on their shoulders under Eleazar’s oversight. In The Book of Joshua, the Kohathites receive the first lot and twenty-three cities, including thirteen for the children of Aaron and ten for the rest of the Kohathites.

Kohathites in Numbers

author unknown · 1439 to 1399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Kohathites first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Kohathites are a Levite family division, sons of Levi descending from Kohath through Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel. They number 8,600 males and camp on the tabernacle's south side under prince Elizaphan. Their duty is the ark, table, lamp stand, altars, and sanctuary vessels, under Eleazar's oversight. They carry the most holy things on their shoulders, without wagons, after Aaron and his sons cover them, and must not touch or see them. 2,750 men aged thirty to fifty serve.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The Kohathites descend from Kohath through Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel, and number eight thousand six hundred males. They camp on the south side under prince Elizaphan son of Uzziel, and are responsible for the ark, table, lamp stand, altars, sanctuary vessels, and screen, under Eleazar's oversight.

Chapter 4

The Kohathites are assigned to carry the most holy things of the Tent of Meeting after Aaron and his sons have covered them, but they must not touch or see the sanctuary lest they die. They number 2,750 men aged thirty to fifty.
