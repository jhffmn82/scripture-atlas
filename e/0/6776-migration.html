migration

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: forsaketh his home, migrated, migrated therein, migrateth

Abstract written by a model

Migration is undertaken for Allah’s cause. Those oppressed in the land are urged to migrate when able, and migrants are promised refuge and abundance in the earth. Whoever leaves home as a fugitive unto Allah and His messenger but is overtaken by death has a reward incumbent on Allah, who is Forgiving and Merciful.
