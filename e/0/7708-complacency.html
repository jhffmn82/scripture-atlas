Complacency

Abstract written by a model

A scholar separated from humanity and the world, Complacency defines happiness as contentment, urging the wise to seek sufficiency and reject excess because wealth brings confusion, misery, disease, shame, unhappiness, and fear.

Tzŭ Chang asked Man Kou Tê,

Complacency says that the wise man seeks only what is sufficient and rejects excess without calling this either greed or honesty. Complacency describes excessive wealth as bringing confusion, misery, disease, shame, unhappiness, and fear, and states that happiness is found in contentment.
