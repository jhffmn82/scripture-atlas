Kriyâvâdins

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Kriyâvâda

Abstract written by a model

A heretical creed with doctrines among varied opinions: it teaches three ways of sin, misery from one’s works, and liberation through right knowledge, conduct, and understanding.

Kriyâvâdins is in one work on this site, Sutrakritanga (Sûtrakritâṅga), Book I. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Twelfth Lecture, Called the Creed.

It teaches that misery is produced by one's own works and that right knowledge and conduct lead to liberation. He who understands the self, the world, Karman, misery, and its annihilation is entitled to expound it.
