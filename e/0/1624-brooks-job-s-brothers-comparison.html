Chapter 6

Job compares his brothers' deceitful dealing to brooks that are dark with ice and snow but vanish in the dry season and are consumed in the heat. Caravans that travel beside them turn away, go up into the waste, and perish. The caravans of Tema and the companies of Sheba looked and waited for them, and were distressed and confounded because they had been confident.
