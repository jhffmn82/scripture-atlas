Abstract written by a model

A man follows and can enlarge principles, though principles do not enlarge him. Knowledge may attain gains, but without sufficient virtue he loses them; with knowledge and virtue, he must govern with dignity to earn respect. He may also move people contrary to propriety.

CHAP. XXVIII. The Master said, 'A man can enlarge the

A man can enlarge the principles which he follows. The principles do not enlarge the man.

CHAP. XXXII. 1. The Master said, 'When a man's knowledge is

A man who has sufficient knowledge to attain but insufficient virtue to hold what he gains will lose it again. If he has knowledge and virtue but cannot govern with dignity, the people will not respect him.
