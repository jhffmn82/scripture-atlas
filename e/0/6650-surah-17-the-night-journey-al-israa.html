Surah 17: The Night Journey (Al-Israa)

document · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns Allah’s signs, commandments, mercy, and punishments, including the Night Journey of His servant from the Inviolable Place of Worship to the Far distant place of worship. It recounts what happened to Moses, the Children of Israel, Iblis, and mankind, and describes guidance, worship, resurrection, the Hereafter, reward, and hell. It teaches believers to worship Allah alone, honor parents, protect life and wealth, keep covenants, avoid adultery and wrongdoing, and speak kindly. It presents the Qur'an as guidance, healing, and mercy, and declares that Truth has come while falsehood is bound to vanish.
