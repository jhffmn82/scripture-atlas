Abstract written by a model

The king, a royal son, is the figure for whom justice and righteousness are requested. He judges God's people and the poor rightly, saves the needy, and breaks the oppressor. His dominion spans sea to sea, with tribute and gifts from distant kings and service from all nations. The account closes with his name enduring forever and all nations calling him blessed.

Chapter 72

The king is the royal son for whom justice and righteousness are requested. He is pictured as judging the people and the poor rightly, breaking the oppressor, ruling from sea to sea, receiving homage and tribute from enemies and kings, and being prayed for and blessed. His name is said to endure forever, and all nations will call him blessed.
