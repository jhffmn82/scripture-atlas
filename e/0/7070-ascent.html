Ascent

Also called: the Ascent, what the Ascent is

Abstract written by a model

The Ascent is not attempted by man. It consists of freeing a slave, feeding in the day of hunger, helping an orphan near of kin or a poor wretch in misery, and belonging to those who believe and exhort one another to perseverance and pity.
