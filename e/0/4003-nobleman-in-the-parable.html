Abstract written by a model

A nobleman in a parable, called exacting by his third servant, who travels to a far country to receive a kingdom. He entrusts ten servants with ten minas, is hated by citizens who send an envoy opposing him, then returns, rewards the faithful servants with cities, judges the third by his words, and orders enemies killed.

Chapter 19

The nobleman goes to a far country to receive a kingdom, gives ten servants ten minas to do business with, and is hated by his citizens, who send an envoy saying they don't want him to reign. On returning he rewards the first two servants with authority over ten and five cities, judges the third by his own words, gives that servant's mina to the one with ten, and commands his enemies be killed before him.
