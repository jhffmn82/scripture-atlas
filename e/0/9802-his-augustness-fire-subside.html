His Augustness Fire-Subside

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Fire-Subside, also called His Augustness Heaven's-Sun-Height-Prince-Great-Rice-ears-Lord-Ears, is the third-born prince, younger brother of His Augustness Fire-Shine, whose luck is on the mountains. During their exchange of luck, he loses Fire-Shine’s fish-hook and journeys to the Ocean-Possessor’s palace. Identified as Heaven's-Sun-Height, he lives in the sea deity's realm for three years, marries Her Augustness Luxuriant-Jewel-Princess, retrieves the hook, and receives tide-controlling jewels. He gives back the hook and overcomes Fire-Shine’s attack by drowning, then saving him. Though forbidden to watch Luxuriant-Jewel-Princess give birth, he peeps, sees her crocodile form, flees, and later answers her song with remembrance.

His Augustness Fire-Subside is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Sect.

His Augustness Fire-Subside is the third child born during the delivery in the burning hall. He is also called His Augustness Heaven's-Sun-Height-Prince-Great-Rice-ears-Lord-Ears.

Sect.

His Augustness Fire-Subside is a prince whose luck is on the mountains, where he catches things rough of hair and soft of hair. After losing his elder brother’s fish-hook, he travels to the palace of the Ocean-Possessor, marries Luxuriant-Jewel-Princess, retrieves the hook, and receives instructions and tide-controlling jewels.

Sect.

His Augustness Fire-Subside gives the fish-hook to his elder brother according to the Sea-Deity's instructions. When his elder brother attacks, His Augustness Fire-Subside uses the tide-flowing jewel to drown him and the tide-ebbing jewel to save him.

Sect.

His Augustness Fire-Subside was told not to look at Her Augustness Luxuriant-Jewel-Princess during parturition, but he stealthily peeped at her. After seeing her crocodile form, he became terrified and fled. He later received songs from Her Augustness Luxuriant-Jewel-Princess and replied with one expressing that he would not forget her.
