Also called: Ahura Mazda's true saints, Thy saints, Thy true saints, offering saints, the blessed, the saints

Abstract written by a model

Saints are souls, men and women worshipped wherever born. With good consciences, they conquer the Daêvas, bring wicked converts in penitence, and receive the Realm through Good Mind. Unharmed by Ahura Mazda’s Bountiful Spirit, they are associated with wise perceptions, sacred blessings, guileless characteristics, and due recognition; their Fravashis aid better men.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XXXIX.

The souls of saints, both men and women, are worshipped wherever they were born. Their good consciences have conquered, are conquering, or will conquer the Daêvas.

Yasna XLVII.

The saints are not harmed by Ahura Mazda’s Bountiful Spirit. The chosen saint receives Ahura Mazda’s best gifts, while a feeble man can give support to Ahura Mazda’s saint.

Yasna LX (Sp. LIX).

The saints are associated with wise perceptions, sacred blessings, guileless characteristics, and recognition of what is due. Their Fravashis are asked to come to the house and aid the better men.
