Abstract written by a model

The rich are addressed as a class whose wealth is corrupted and who fade like grass. They withhold laborers' wages by fraud, live in luxury, and condemn and murder the nonresisting righteous one, so they are told to weep over coming miseries.

Chapter 5

The rich are told to weep and howl over miseries coming on them. They are accused of withholding wages by fraud, living in luxury and pleasure, and condemning and murdering the righteous one, who does not resist them.
