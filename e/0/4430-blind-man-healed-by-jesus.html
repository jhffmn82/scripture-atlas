Abstract written by a model

A beggar blind from birth is healed after washing at Siloam as Jesus directed. He affirms his identity to doubting neighbors, then under repeated Pharisee questioning calls Jesus a prophet, says he knows only that he now sees, and argues Jesus is from God. Expelled by the Pharisees, he comes to believe in Jesus as Lord and worships him.

Chapter 9

The man, blind from birth, is healed after washing at Siloam as Jesus directed, and he identifies himself to doubting neighbors as the one who begged. Under repeated questioning by the Pharisees he calls Jesus a prophet, says he does not know whether Jesus is a sinner but knows he now sees, and argues that Jesus must be from God. He is thrown out, then believes in Jesus as Lord and worships him.
