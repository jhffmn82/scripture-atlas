Abstract written by a model

Oholiab, son of Ahisamach of the tribe of Dan, is a skilled engraver, workman and embroiderer in blue, purple, scarlet and fine linen. The LORD appoints him to work with Bezalel, filling him with wisdom of heart for engraving, embroidery and weaving. Across the chapters he is first appointed (31), named with Bezalel as a teacher (35), called by Moses to labor with every wise-hearted man on the sanctuary according to the LORD's command (36), and finally shown working alongside Bezalel (38).

Oholiab is in one work on this site, Exodus. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Oholiab in Exodus

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 31

Oholiab, son of Ahisamach of the tribe of Dan, is appointed by the LORD to work with Bezalel. He is mentioned alongside the other wise-hearted people in whom the LORD has put wisdom to make all that was commanded.

Chapter 35

Oholiab, son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan, is named alongside Bezalel as one who can teach. He is filled with wisdom of heart for all kinds of workmanship, including engraving, skilled work, embroidery, and weaving.

Chapter 36

Oholiab is named with Bezalel as one who shall work with every wise-hearted man in the service of the sanctuary according to all the LORD commanded. Moses calls him to the work.

Chapter 38

Oholiab, son of Ahisamach of the tribe of Dan, worked alongside Bezalel. He is described as an engraver, a skillful workman, and an embroiderer in blue, purple, scarlet, and fine linen.
