applies_to mankind [one entrance into life and a common departure] The narrator describes himself as a descendant of the first-formed man, formed as flesh over ten months in his mother's womb, born drawing the common air and uttering the same first cry as everyone. He was nursed in swaddling clothes, and he concludes that no king had a different beginning, since all men share one entrance into life and a common departure.
