Abstract written by a model

Ziklag is a city that Achish gives David when he asks for a place in the country; it thereafter belongs to the kings of Judah. The Amalekites raid and burn it, David and his men find it in ruins on the third day, and after victory David returns and sends plunder to Judah's towns.

Chapter 27

Ziklag is given to David by Achish when David asks for a city in the country rather than living in the royal city. The text notes that it belongs to the kings of Judah to this day.

Chapter 30

Ziklag is struck and burned with fire by the Amalekites, who raid it and the South. David and his men arrive on the third day to find it in ruins, and after the victory David returns there and sends plunder from it to Judah's towns.
