Abstract written by a model

The people are a community afflicted by famine and the king's tribute, forced to mortgage fields, vineyards and houses and to put their sons and daughters into debt bondage. They cry out over their hardship. Former governors had burdened them with bread, wine and silver, though Nehemiah did not demand the governor's pay. After the nobles' promise, the assembly says Amen, praises the LORD, and keeps the promise.

Chapter 5

The people, together with their wives, raised a great cry about their hardship, saying they had mortgaged their land for grain and for the king's tribute and that their children were in bondage. After the nobles' promise, all the assembly said Amen and praised the LORD, and the people did according to the promise. Nehemiah also notes that former governors burdened them with bread, wine, and forty shekels of silver, while he did not demand the governor's pay because the bondage was heavy on them.
