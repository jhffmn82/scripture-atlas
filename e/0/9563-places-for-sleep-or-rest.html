Abstract written by a model

Places for sleep or rest should not be frequented by women, cattle, or eunuchs. Occupying such places may create doubt about a Nirgrantha's chastity or lead to sensual desire, remorse, rule-breaking, passion, illness, or abandonment of the faith.
