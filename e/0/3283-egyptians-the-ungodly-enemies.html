Abstract written by a model

The Egyptians, ungodly enemies, worshiped irrational reptiles and vermin through senseless imaginings, so God sent such creatures to punish them, judging them with wrath. Their river turned to clotted blood for the decree to slay babies; distressed, they heard others benefited, recognized the Lord, and marveled at their unusual thirst.

Chapter 11

The ungodly enemies saw their river turn to clotted blood as rebuke for the decree to slay babies, and they were distressed with double grief, groaning at the memory of past events. When they heard that the others benefited through their own punishments, they recognized the Lord, stopped mocking the one once thrown out and exposed, and marveled at a thirst unlike that of the righteous. Because they worshiped irrational reptiles and vermin, a multitude of irrational creatures was sent to punish them.
