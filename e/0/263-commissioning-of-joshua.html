Chapter 27

At the LORD's command, Moses took Joshua, set him before Eleazar the priest and the whole congregation, laid his hands on him, and commissioned him in their sight. This gave Joshua authority so that the congregation would obey him, with Eleazar to inquire for him by the Urim.
