Abstract written by a model

David's preparations of building materials for the LORD's house: abundant iron for nails and couplings, bronze without weight, countless cedar from Sidonians and Tyre, vast gold and silver, plus timber, stone and many skilled workmen. The records list the materials, then end with Solomon being told he may add to them.

Chapter 22

David prepared iron for nails and couplings, bronze without weight, and cedar without number brought by the Sidonians and people of Tyre. He also prepared one hundred thousand talents of gold, one million talents of silver, and timber and stone, with skilled workmen in abundance. Solomon is told he may add to them.
