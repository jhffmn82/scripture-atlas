identity of all things

Also called: ONE, all things are ONE

Abstract written by a model

The identity of all things is understood by the true Sage as all things being one, bringing distinctions such as beam and pillar, beauty and ugliness, construction and destruction together through the standpoint of TAO.

CHAPTER II.

The identity of all things means that distinctions such as beam and pillar, ugliness and beauty, greatness and wickedness, construction and destruction, are brought together into one. The truly intelligent understand this by transferring themselves into the position of the things viewed.
