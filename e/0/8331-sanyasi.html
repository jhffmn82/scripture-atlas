Abstract written by a model

Sanyasi is a renunciant who serves Guru, remembers and loves the one God’s Name, subdues passions, and is satisfied; hypocrisy instead brings dress, begging, hoarding, and ignorance.

Rag Maru Ashtapadi .. Maru ki War I

The true Sanyasi thinks of the one God under the Guru's instruction, loves His Name, and is satisfied with it. A hypocritical Sanyasi merely wears religious dress, begs, hoards money, and remains ignorant of the Word.
