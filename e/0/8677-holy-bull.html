Abstract written by a model

Fargard XXI.

The holy bull is hailed as beneficent and as one who makes increase and growth. He bestows gifts upon the excellent faithful and the faithful yet unborn, but is killed by the Gahi, the ungodly Ashemaogha, and the wicked tyrant.
