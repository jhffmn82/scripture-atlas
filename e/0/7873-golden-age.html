Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chiang Lü Mien went to see Chi Ch'ê,

During the Golden Age, rulers served as mere beacons and people lived freely like wild deer. People were upright, loving, truthful, honest, and unconstrained without consciously recognizing duty, charity, loyalty, good faith, or obligation.
