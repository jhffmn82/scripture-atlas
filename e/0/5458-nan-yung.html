Abstract written by a model

Nan Yung is praised by The Master as fit to remain in office under good government and able to avoid punishment and disgrace under bad government; The Master gives him his elder brother’s daughter as wife.

CHAP. I. 1. The Master said of Kung-ye Ch'ang that he might

Nan Yung was said by The Master to remain in office if the country were well governed and to escape punishment and disgrace if it were ill-governed. The Master gave him the daughter of his own elder brother to wife.
