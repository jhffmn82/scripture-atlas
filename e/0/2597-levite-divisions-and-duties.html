Chapter 23

David divided the Levites by the sons of Levi: Gershon, Kohath, and Merari, listing family heads under each, and assigned 24,000 to oversee the work, 6,000 as officers and judges, 4,000 as doorkeepers, and 4,000 as musicians. Their duties included service in the courts and rooms, purifying holy things, preparing show bread and offerings, standing morning and evening to thank and praise, and offering burnt offerings at the set times.
