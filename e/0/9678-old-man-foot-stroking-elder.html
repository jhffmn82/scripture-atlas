Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Sect.

He identifies himself as an Earthly Deity and a child of the Deity Great-Mountain-Possessor. He explains that the eight-forked serpent of Koshi has devoured seven of his eight daughters and agrees to offer Wondrous-Inada-Princess to His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness.

Sect.

Deity Foot-Stroking-Elder was appointed by His Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness as Headman of the palace. He was also given the name Master-of-the-Temple-of-Inada-Eight-Eared-Deity-of-Suga.
