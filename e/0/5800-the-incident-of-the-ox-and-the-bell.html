Chapter 7

The incident of the ox and the bell concerns King Xuan of Qi ordering an ox to be released because he could not bear its frightened appearance before consecration of a bell, while instructing that it be changed for a sheep. Mencius interprets the act as evidence of compassion rather than unwillingness to bear the expense.
