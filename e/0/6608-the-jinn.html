the Jinn

group · 3 works · 12 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Jinn, a company of the Jinn, among us, certain of the jinn, jinn, the Jinn who had listened to the Qur'an, the jinn

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 46: The Dunes (Al-Ahqaf), certain of the jinn listen to the Qur'an, return to their people as warners, affirm its guidance, and urge their people to respond to Allah’s summoner. In Surah 51: The Winnowing Winds (Adh-Dhaariyat), the jinn are created by Allah only so that they might worship Him. In Surah 72: The Jinn (Al-Jinn), the Jinn hear a marvellous Qur'an, believe in it, affirm that Allah has no partner, include righteous and unrighteous groups, and acknowledge that they cannot escape Him.
