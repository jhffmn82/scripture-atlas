Abstract written by a model

New Jerusalem is a holy city seen coming down out of heaven from God, prepared like a bride and identified as the Lamb's wife. It is square, walled with twelve gates, and made of pure gold like clear glass, including its street. It has no temple, sun, or moon, because God and the Lamb are its temple and light. Its gates are never shut, yet only those written in the Lamb's book of life enter; the wicked are excluded.

Chapter 21

New Jerusalem, the holy city, is seen coming down out of heaven from God, prepared like a bride adorned for her husband, and is identified as the bride, the Lamb's wife. It is square, with equal length, width, and height, a jasper wall with twelve gates of pearl and twelve foundations of precious stones, and pure gold like clear glass for the city and its street. It has no temple, sun, or moon, since God and the Lamb are its temple and light, its gates are never shut, and only those written in the Lamb's book of life may enter.
