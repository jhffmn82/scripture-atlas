kingdom

Also called: it, kingdom o'er, spirit-like thing

Abstract written by a model

The kingdom is a spirit-like thing. It cannot be obtained by active doing: attempts to win it destroy it, and attempts to hold it lose it. It may be entrusted to an administrator who loves and honours it as his own person. When Tao is employed throughout it, men thrive all around.
