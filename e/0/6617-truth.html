truth

topic · 15 works · 26 quoted facts · 14 connections
Also called: True, True One, Truest of the true, Truth, medicine, the True, the Truth, the Truth from our Lord, the real truth, the truth, the truth thereof, true

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 6: The Cattle (Al-An'aam), the truth comes to disbelievers through Allah’s revelations but is denied as false or magical. In Surah 8: The Spoils of War (Al-Anfaal), Allah makes the Truth triumph, nullifies vanity, and defeats its opponents. In Surah 10: Jonas (Yunus), truth comes from Allah and opposes error, conjecture, invented claims, and magic. In Surah 17: The Night Journey (Al-Israa), Truth comes and prevails, and the Qur’an is sent down with truth. In Surah 28: The Stories (Al-Qasas), the Truth comes from Allah, though some reject it and turn to invented claims. In Surah 38: The letter Saad (Saad), the truth is tremendous tidings spoken by Allah that people will come to know. In Surah 40: The Forgiver (Al-Ghaafir), Moses brings the Truth from Allah, but Pharaoh and his people reject it. In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), the revelation is the truth whose reality Allah will make manifest through His signs.

and 7 more works

In Surah 46: The Dunes (Al-Ahqaf), the Truth reaches disbelievers through Allah’s revelations, which they call magic. In Surah 72: The Jinn (Al-Jinn), Muhammad conveys the Truth as part of his duty from Allah. In Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra), Book I, truth is followed constantly and enables the wise to destroy karman. In Uttaradhyayana Sutra, Truth is knowledge of reality that dispels ignorance and makes one wise. In Japji, truth is a virtue associated with God, His Name, contentment, divine knowledge, and following the Commander’s will. In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), truth dwells in the true Guru’s heart, comes from the true Lord, purifies, and is scarce in the age. In Hymns of Guru Nanak, truth is the central practice for pleasing God, attaining honour, and reaching the True One.

truth in Hymns of Guru Nanak

Guru Nanak · the work’s page · read it where truth first appears

Abstract written by a model

Truth is presented as superior to every other practice and as the central way to please God, recognize God, enter God’s court, obtain honour, and reach the True One. It is bestowed by God, learned through the Guru and the true Name, and obtained by practising it. Truth purifies a person, serves as acceptable ablution for a body defiled by falsehood, and makes one true when held in the heart. It requires abandoning falsehood, greed, pride, and sin; false pride is condemned. Truth also appears as fasting and modest spiritual conduct, and it ultimately prevails when falsehood ends.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Sri Rag .. Ashtapadi

Truth is the condition for pleasing God, entering God's court, and obtaining honour. The text teaches that truth is superior to all other practices and that the practice of truth purifies man.

Rag Asa .. Asa Ashtapadi

Truth is granted by God and is learned through the Guru and the true Name. Practising truth and abandoning falsehood, greed, pride, and sin leads to God’s love, spiritual honour, and acceptance.

Rag Wadhans .. Wadhans ki War

Truth is presented as the acceptable ablution for a body defiled by falsehood. When truth is in the heart, a person becomes true and obtains the True One.

Ramkali .. Ramkali ki War I

Truth is obtained through practicing truth, and false pride is condemned. Truth shall ultimately prevail when falsehood ends.

Sarang ki War

Truth is presented as a form of fasting and as part of the right spiritual way. Those without truth and modesty are said to receive no assistance hereafter.
