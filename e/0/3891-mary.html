Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Mary is Jesus Christ's mother, becomes pregnant by the Holy Spirit while engaged to Joseph, bears her firstborn son, and travels with him to Egypt, Israel, and Nazareth. In The Good News According to Mark, Jesus's mother comes with his brothers, sends word calling him, and is included by Jesus among those who do God's will. In The Gospel According to Luke, Mary is a virgin of Nazareth who becomes Jesus's mother, visits Elizabeth, gives birth in Bethlehem, ponders the shepherds' sayings, hears Simeon's prophecy, searches for Jesus, and sits at his feet as Martha's sister. In The Gospel According to John, Mary, Jesus's mother, tells him about the lack of wine at Cana, instructs the servants to obey him, travels with his family and disciples, and is entrusted to the disciple at the cross. In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan), Mary is protected from Satan, accepted and given goodly growth, chosen and purified by Allah, and given tidings of Jesus the Messiah. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), Mary is the subject of a tremendous calumny. In Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida), Mary is a saintly woman and mother of the Messiah who eats earthly food and is not a god beside Allah. In Surah 19: Mary (Maryam), Mary withdraws from her people, receives the Spirit in human likeness, conceives Jesus, gives birth in hardship, and returns carrying him.

and 2 more works

In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), Mary, who was chaste, receives Allah's Spirit, and she and her son become a token for all peoples. In Surah 66: The Prohibition (At-Tahrim), Mary, daughter of 'Imran, is obedient to Allah, chaste, receives Allah's Spirit, and believes in Allah's words and scriptures.

Mary in The Gospel According to Luke

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Mary first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mary is a virgin of Nazareth, betrothed to Joseph, who becomes the mother of Jesus. In Chapter 1, Gabriel tells her she has found favor with God; she accepts, visits Elizabeth, is called blessed, and sings praise. In Chapter 2 she travels to Bethlehem, gives birth to her firstborn, ponders the shepherds' sayings, hears Simeon say a sword will pierce her soul, and searches three days for the twelve-year-old Jesus. In Chapter 10, as Martha's sister, she sits at Jesus' feet and is said to have chosen the good part.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Mary, a virgin of Nazareth pledged to Joseph of David's house, is told by Gabriel she has found favor with God and will bear Jesus. She accepts, saying 'let it be done to me according to your word,' visits Elizabeth, is called blessed and the mother of Elizabeth's Lord, and sings praise to God before staying about three months and returning home.

Chapter 2

Mary, pledged to Joseph and pregnant, goes with him to Bethlehem to enroll and gives birth to her firstborn son there. She keeps the shepherds' sayings, pondering them in her heart, and Simeon tells her a sword will pierce through her own soul. When Jesus is twelve she and Joseph search for him for three days, she questions him in the temple, and she keeps all these sayings in her heart.

Chapter 10

Mary is Martha's sister and sits at Jesus' feet hearing his word while Martha serves. Jesus says she has chosen the good part, which will not be taken away from her.

Chapter 2

Jesus' mother is at the wedding in Cana and tells Jesus, "They have no wine." After his reply that his hour has not yet come, she tells the servants to do whatever he says. She later goes down to Capernaum with Jesus, his brothers, and his disciples.

Chapter 19

Jesus' mother stands by the cross with her sister, Mary the wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene. Jesus says to her, "Woman, behold, your son!" and from that hour the disciple takes her into his own home.
