shepherd's dog

animal · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

The shepherd’s dog, one of Ahura Mazda’s two dogs, guards the fold by circling it against thieves and wolves. Together with the house dog it is necessary for every house. It is strongly protected: mistreatment, bad food, exclusion, striking, or killing bring severe guilt or punishment.

Fargard XIII.

The shepherd’s dog goes around the fold watching for thieves and wolves and is protected from being struck or killed. Bad food given to it incurs the guilt associated with mistreating a high-ranking master, and its death carries eight hundred stripes with each of two instruments. It and the house dog must not be kept away when passing a faithful person’s house.
