Abstract written by a model

A city addressed by the LORD's voice, where wisdom fears his name yet dishonest commerce thrives: wicked treasures, short ephah, false weights. It follows Omri's and Ahab's ways, speaks lies, and is threatened with ruin and hissing.

Chapter 6

The LORD's voice calls to the city, and wisdom is said to fear his name. The city is charged with treasures of wickedness, a short ephah, dishonest scales, and deceitful weights, and its inhabitants speak lies and are threatened with becoming a ruin and a hissing.
