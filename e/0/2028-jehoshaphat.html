Abstract written by a model

Jehoshaphat is a king of Judah and father of Jehoash. He allies with Jehoram against Moab, pledging his people and horses, asks for a prophet of the LORD, and Elisha helps only out of respect for him.

Jehoshaphat is in one work on this site, The Second Book of Kings. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 3

Jehoshaphat king of Judah agreed to join Jehoram against Moab, saying his people and horses were as Jehoram's. He asked for a prophet of the LORD, and Elisha said he would not even look at Jehoram were it not for respect for Jehoshaphat's presence.
