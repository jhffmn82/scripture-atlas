Baruch / Chapter 1

The exiles gathered money according to every man's ability and sent it to Joakim the high priest and the priests in Jerusalem. It was meant to buy burnt offerings, sin offerings, and incense and to prepare an oblation for the altar of the Lord, and was sent at the time the vessels of the house of the Lord were returned to Judah.
