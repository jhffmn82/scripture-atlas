jinn

group · 3 works · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: assembly of the jinn, company of jinn and men, jinni, the jinn

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 6: The Cattle (Al-An'aam), the jinn are described as created beings, some made partners with Allah, with devils among them seducing people; jinn and humankind are gathered and questioned, and the disobedient belong in Fire. In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), the jinn are included among those who may beguile people, and disbelievers will ask for their jinn and human tempters to be placed beneath their feet on the Day of Resurrection. In Surah 55: The Beneficent (Ar-Rahmaan), the jinn were created from smokeless fire, addressed with men as dependents, warned they cannot escape the heavens and earth without sanction, and told the guilty will be identified and punished.
