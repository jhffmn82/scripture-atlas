woman with a discharge

Also called: If a woman has a discharge, a woman, a woman has a discharge, her who has her period, her who is unclean

Abstract written by a model

A woman with a bloody discharge is impure seven days, making her bedding, seats, and anyone touching them unclean. If discharge outlasts her period, she stays unclean throughout. After cleansing she waits seven days, then on the eighth brings two birds, a sin and burnt offering, so the priest atones for her.

Chapter 15

A woman with a bloody discharge is impure seven days, and whatever she lies or sits on is unclean, as is anyone who touches her or those things. If her discharge continues beyond her period, she remains unclean for all those days, and once cleansed she counts seven days and on the eighth brings two birds to the priest.
