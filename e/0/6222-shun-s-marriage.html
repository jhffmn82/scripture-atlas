Abstract written by a model

In Mencius, Book IV (Li Lou), Shun's marriage took place without his informing his parents because he feared having no posterity. In Mencius, Book V (Wan Zhang), Shun's marriage took place without informing his parents because doing so would have prevented him from marrying.
