Chapter 33

The text says no king is saved by a great army and no mighty man by great strength, and a horse is a vain hope for safety. Instead the LORD delivers the souls of those who fear him and hope in his loving kindness from death and keeps them alive in famine. The people state that they have waited for him and trusted his holy name, and they pray for his loving kindness on them.
