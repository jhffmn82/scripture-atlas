Chapter 9

The men of Shechem follow Abimelech because he is their brother, fund his killers from the house of Baal Berith, and make him king. After God sends an evil spirit, they betray him with ambushes and trust Gaal. Abimelech kills them, and about a thousand die in the burned stronghold.
