An idolater is a man or woman who breaks the Lord's covenant by serving other gods, sun, moon, or stars. Once reported, the matter is investigated; if certain, the person is stoned at the gates to remove evil from the people.

Chapter 17

A man or woman who transgresses the covenant by serving and worshiping other gods, or the sun, moon, or stars, is to be investigated once the matter is reported. If the charge is true and certain, that person is brought out to the gates and stoned to death. This is done to remove the evil from among the people.
