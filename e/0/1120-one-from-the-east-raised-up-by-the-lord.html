one from the east (raised up by the LORD)

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

A figure raised up by the LORD from the east (also described as from the north, the rising of the sun) and called in righteousness. The LORD hands nations and kings over to him; his sword and bow make them like dust and stubble, and he treads rulers like clay. He calls on the LORD's name. Later he is loved by the LORD, acts against Babylon with his arm against the Chaldeans, and is brought and made prosperous.

Chapter 41

The LORD asks who raised up this one from the east and called him in righteousness, and says he hands nations and kings over to him. His sword and bow make them like dust and driven stubble, and he pursues them and passes on safely by a way he had not gone. Later the LORD says he raised up one from the north, from the rising of the sun, who calls on his name and treads rulers like mortar or clay.

Chapter 48

He whom the LORD loves is said to do what he likes to Babylon, with his arm against the Chaldeans. The LORD says he has spoken, called him, and brought him, and that this man will make his way prosperous.
