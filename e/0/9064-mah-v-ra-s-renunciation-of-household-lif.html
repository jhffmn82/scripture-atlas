Mahâvîra's renunciation of household life

Abstract written by a model

Mahâvîra’s renunciation of household life follows thirty years among householders and a year of giving away wealth. In Gñâtri Shanda under Uttaraphalgunî, he leaves Kundapura, removes ornaments, plucks out five handfuls of hair, honors liberated spirits, and adopts holy conduct.

Third Part. / Fifteenth Lecture, Called the Clauses.

After living thirty years among householders and giving away his property for a year, Mahâvîra resolved to retire from the world. He left Kundapura in the palankin Kandraprabhâ, removed his ornaments, plucked out his hair in five handfuls, paid obeisance to liberated spirits, and adopted holy conduct.
