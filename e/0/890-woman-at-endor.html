Chapter 28

The woman at Endor, who has a familiar spirit, is sought out by the disguised Saul. She fears for her life because Saul had cut off such practitioners, but after he swears an oath she brings up Samuel, and cries out on seeing him, recognizing the visitor as Saul. Afterward she urges the collapsed Saul to eat, kills her fattened calf, and bakes unleavened bread for him and his servants.
