It was levied from surviving Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites and Jebusites, not Israelites, overseen by 550 chief officers.

Chapter 9

Solomon conscripts forced labor to build the LORD's house, his own house, Millo, Jerusalem's wall, Hazor, Megiddo and Gezer, along with storage cities and cities for chariots and horsemen. He raises a levy of bondservants from the surviving Amorites, Hittites, Perizzites, Hivites and Jebusites, not from the Israelites, and 550 chief officers oversee the work.
