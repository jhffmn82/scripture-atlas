Gabriel in Daniel (Greek)

author unknown · about 164 BC · the work’s page · read it where Gabriel first appears

Abstract written by a model

Gabriel is a man-like being who serves as interpreter and giver of wisdom and understanding to Daniel. In Chapter 8, a voice between the banks of the Ulai commands him to explain the vision; he touches and steadies the fallen Daniel, explains the ram, goat, horns and fierce-faced king, and says the vision is true but sealed. In Chapter 9, he flies swiftly at the evening offering, instructs Daniel, and delivers the seventy weeks message.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 8

Gabriel is named when a man's voice between the banks of the Ulai commands him to make Daniel understand the vision. He comes near Daniel, tells him the vision belongs to the time of the end, touches him and sets him upright when he falls asleep, and explains the ram, the goat, the horns, and the fierce-faced king. He ends by telling Daniel that the vision of the evenings and mornings is true but must be sealed up for many days to come.

Chapter 9

Gabriel, the man Daniel had seen in the vision at the beginning, flies swiftly to touch Daniel at the time of the evening offering. He instructs Daniel, explains that the commandment went out at the start of Daniel's petitions, and delivers the seventy weeks message.

Gabriel in The Gospel According to Luke

author unknown · AD 80 to 90 · the work’s page · read it where Gabriel first appears

Abstract written by a model

Gabriel is an angel who stands in God's presence; he announces John's birth to Zacharias, muting him for unbelief, then is sent to Mary in Nazareth to announce Jesus's conception.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Gabriel identifies himself as one who stands in the presence of God. He announces John's birth to Zacharias and makes him mute for his unbelief, then in the sixth month is sent to Mary in Nazareth to announce that she will conceive Jesus.
