Cyrus's proclamation

event · 2 works · 8 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Cyrus's decree, a decree, a proclamation, decree, decree to build the house, proclamation of Cyrus, the decree of Cyrus

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Chronicles, the proclamation of Cyrus is issued throughout his kingdom orally and in writing, inviting God's people to go up and build a house for the LORD in Jerusalem. In The Book of Ezra, Cyrus's proclamation invites God's people to go up to Jerusalem and build the house of the LORD, and directs others to support them. In The Book of Ezra, the decree orders the house of God built at Jerusalem and provides for its expenses and the restoration of its vessels.

decree to build the house in The Book of Ezra

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where decree to build the house first appears

Abstract written by a model

A decree issued in Cyrus's first year orders the house of God built at Jerusalem, sixty cubits high and wide, with its expenses paid from the king's house and the gold and silver vessels of God's house restored. In Chapter 5 the Jewish elders cite it, and Tattenai's letter asks that Babylon's treasure house be searched. In Chapter 6 the decree is found in a scroll at Achmetha in Media, Darius confirms it, and the building is completed according to it.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

The Jewish elders claim that Cyrus made a decree in his first year to build God's house. Tattenai asks who gave the decree, and his letter requests that Babylon's treasure house be searched to verify whether Cyrus made it. Its existence is not yet confirmed in this unit.

Chapter 6

The decree is found in a scroll at Achmetha in the province of Media. It specifies a house sixty cubits high and wide, with three courses of great stones and a course of new timber, paid for by the king's house, and the return of the gold and silver vessels. Darius confirms it and the building is completed according to it.
