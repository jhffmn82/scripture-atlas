day of atonement in Leviticus

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where day of atonement first appears

Abstract written by a model

The day of atonement falls on the tenth day of the seventh month and is a holy convocation, a Sabbath of solemn rest, and a statute forever, kept from evening to evening. Its purpose is to make atonement before the LORD, cleansing the children of Israel from all their sins. The people, native-born and stranger alike, must afflict their souls, do no work, and offer an offering made by fire. Whoever does not deny himself is cut off from his people, and whoever works is destroyed by the LORD. Chapter 16 sets the day and its cleansing; Chapter 23 adds the penalties.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

The text sets this day as the tenth day of the seventh month, a statute forever and a Sabbath of solemn rest. The people are to afflict their souls and do no work, native-born and stranger alike, because on this day atonement is made to cleanse them from all their sins before the LORD.

Chapter 23

The tenth day of the seventh month is the day of atonement, a holy convocation to make atonement for the people before the LORD. The people must afflict or deny themselves, do no kind of work, and offer an offering made by fire. Whoever does not deny himself is cut off from his people, and whoever works is destroyed by the LORD. It is a Sabbath of solemn rest kept from evening to evening, a statute forever.

Day of Atonement in Numbers

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Day of Atonement first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Day of Atonement is a holy convocation on the tenth day of the seventh month. People must afflict their souls and do no work. Required offerings are a young bull, a ram, seven male lambs, and a male goat as a sin offering, in addition to the atonement sin offering and continual burnt offering.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 29

On the tenth day of the seventh month there is a holy convocation on which the people are to afflict their souls and do no kind of work. The offerings are one young bull, one ram, seven male lambs and a male goat for a sin offering, in addition to the sin offering of atonement and the continual burnt offering.
