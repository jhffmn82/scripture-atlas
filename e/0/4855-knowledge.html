knowledge

topic · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

In The Table-Talk of the Prophet Mohammad (Part 2), knowledge suffers from forgetfulness and is lost by sharing it with the unworthy; pursuing it leads toward Paradise, and learned people surpass mere worshippers. In The Analects of Confucius, Book II (Wei Chang), knowledge consists in recognizing what one knows and admitting what one does not know; it also involves hearing and seeing much, setting aside doubtful or perilous points, and speaking and acting cautiously.
