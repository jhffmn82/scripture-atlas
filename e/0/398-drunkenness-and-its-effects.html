Abstract written by a model

Drunkenness, paired with gluttony, is a warning topic: it brings poverty and rags through drowsiness, then woe, strife, bruises and bloodshot eyes. The drinker sees strange things, feels confused like a sleeper at sea, ignores blows, and wakes only to seek more drink.

Chapter 23

Drunkenness and gluttony lead to poverty, with drowsiness clothing the drinker in rags. The drunkard is marked by woe, sorrow, strife, complaints, needless bruises, and bloodshot eyes. He sees strange things, imagines confusing things, and is like someone asleep in the middle of the sea. He is insensible to blows and wakes only to look for more drink.
