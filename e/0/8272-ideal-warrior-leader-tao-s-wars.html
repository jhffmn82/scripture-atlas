ideal warrior-leader (Tao's wars)

person · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

The ideal warrior-leader in Tao's wars avoids martial display and rage, fights with good will, vanquishes foes while keeping apart from them, and humbly practices his art. He fulfills men's wishes, unites them by bending their wills, and gains power through never contending.

68.

The ideal warrior-leader avoids martial display and rage, vanquishes while keeping apart from no foes, and humbly practices his art. He fulfills men's wishes, bends their wills so they unite with him, and gains power through not contending.
