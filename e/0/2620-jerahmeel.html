Chapter 2

Jerahmeel was Hezron's firstborn, and his sons were Ram, Bunah, Oren, Ozem and Ahijah. He had another wife, Atarah, who was the mother of Onam. His line includes Onam's descendants and Sheshan's line, and his brother was Caleb.
