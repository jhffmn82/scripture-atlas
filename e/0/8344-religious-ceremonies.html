religious ceremonies

Also called: All religious acts, ceremonial acts, religious acts, religious ceremony, similar religious acts

Abstract written by a model

Religious ceremonies are entanglements tied to hopes and desires when done without devotion and understanding. Without meditation or knowledge of God, they are worthless, empty, or vain, and are inferior to God’s Name. Purifications, austerities, pilgrimages, ablutions, and decorations are rejected unless they lead to God; repetition of the Name is the exception.

Rag Sorath .. Sorath Ashtapadi

Religious ceremonies, hopes, and desires are called entanglements when performed without devotion and understanding. The text says they are worthless without meditation on the Supreme Being, and that the Name is superior to ablutions, alms, and similar acts.

Rag Prabhati .. Prabhati Ashtapadi

They are said to be empty when performed without knowledge of God, and in Prabhati Ashtapadi they are called vain except for repetition of the Name. Purifications, austerities, devotion, penance, pilgrimages, ablutions, ceremonies, and decorations are rejected when they do not lead to God.
