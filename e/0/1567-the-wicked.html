Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Job, the wicked who oppress the poor remove landmarks, seize property, harm the vulnerable, murder, steal, commit adultery, and rebel against light. In Psalms, the wicked are an arrogant group who prey on the helpless, sneer at adversaries, condemn the LORD, and ambush and murder the innocent. In Psalms, the wicked oppress the weak and poor and are the ones from whose hand the needy are to be delivered.

the wicked who oppress the poor in The Book of Job

author unknown · about 574 BC · the work’s page · read it where the wicked who oppress the poor first appears

Abstract written by a model

The wicked oppress the poor and vulnerable: they remove landmarks, seize flocks, donkeys and a widow's ox, push the needy aside, and also murder, steal and commit adultery under cover of darkness. They rebel against light. Though some are briefly preserved and exalted, they are cursed, taken by Sheol, forgotten, and cut off like grain.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 24

They remove landmarks, violently take flocks, drive away the donkey of the fatherless, take the widow's ox as a pledge, and pluck the fatherless from the breast. Others among them murder, steal, commit adultery, and dig into houses under cover of darkness. The speaker says they are like foam on the water, cursed, taken by Sheol, forgotten, and broken like a tree, though some are for a time preserved and exalted before being cut off like tops of grain.

the wicked in Psalms

author unknown · undated · the work’s page · read it where the wicked first appears

Abstract written by a model

The wicked are an arrogant group who prey on the helpless, sneering at adversaries and condemning the LORD while leaving no room for God in their thoughts. Prosperous and certain they will never be shaken or held to account, they ambush and murder the innocent like a lion. Yet they are caught in their own nets, and the speaker asks the LORD to break their arm until no wickedness remains.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

The wicked arrogantly hunt the weak, boast of their cravings, condemn the LORD, and have no room in their thoughts for God. They speak cursing, deceit, and oppression, lie in ambush to murder the innocent, and believe they will never be shaken or called to account. The speaker notes they are caught in their own schemes and asks that the LORD break their arm and seek out their wickedness until none is found.
