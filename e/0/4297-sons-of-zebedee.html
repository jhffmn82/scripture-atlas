Chapter 20

The two sons come with their mother, and Jesus asks if they can drink his cup and be baptized with his baptism; they say they are able. Jesus says they will indeed drink his cup, but the seats at his right and left are for those prepared by his Father, and the other ten are indignant with the two brothers.
