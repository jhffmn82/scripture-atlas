Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the righteous take refuge in and love the LORD. In Psalms, the righteous see God's destruction of the wicked, fear and laugh at him, and declare that he trusted in riches and wickedness instead of God. In Psalms, the righteous rejoice at God's vengeance on the wicked and affirm that a reward awaits them. In Proverbs, the righteous delight in justice and give generously, while the Righteous One brings the wicked to ruin and the upright establish their ways. In Ecclesiastes or, The Preacher, the righteous, together with the wise and their works, are in God's hand and share the same fate as the wicked. In The Wisdom of Solomon, the righteous are held in God's hand, tested, found worthy, and receive immortality, great good, judgment, and life with God. In Habakkuk, the righteous are surrounded and swallowed up by the wicked but live by faith. In The Gospel According to Matthew, the righteous inherit the Kingdom by serving the King's least brothers and enter eternal life.
