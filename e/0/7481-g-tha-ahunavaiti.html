Gâtha Ahunavaiti

topic · 1 work · 17 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

Gâtha Ahunavaiti is a holy Gâtha, ruling in ritual order. Announced and completed as holy, it is mentioned with women who bring forth many sons of many talents and with a chant whose Ahû and Ratu come before it in the Yasna. It is approached with praise, alongside a chant having Alva and Ratu. It is then worshipped and sacrificed to in all its forms: measures, word-structure, Zand, questions, counter-questions, words, metric feet, chapters, recitals, and offerings. It is actively recited and used in worship, possessing wisdom, clearness, loving intention, sovereignty, ritual order, and an acquired boon.

Gâtha Ahunavaiti is in one work on this site, Visparad. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Visparad / Visparad I.

The Gâtha Ahunavaiti is announced and completed as holy and ruling in the ritual order. It is mentioned together with women who bring forth many sons of many talents and with a chant that has its Ahû and Ratu before it in the Yasna.

Visparad II.

The Gâtha Ahunavaiti is approached with praise. A chant associated with it is described as having the Alva and the Ratu.

Visparad XIV (Sp. XVI).

The Gâtha Ahunavaiti is worshipped and sacrificed to with its measures, word-structure, Zand, questions and counter-questions, words, and metric feet. It is described as well-recited and being recited, well-worshipped and being used in worship, and as having its own wisdom, clearness, loving intention, sovereignty, ritual order, and acquired boon. Its chapters, metrical lines, words, word-structure, heard-recital, memorised recital, continuous offering, and steadfast offering are also worshipped.
