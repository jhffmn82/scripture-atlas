Abstract written by a model

Clothing is a covering for infirm persons in a Mazdean house. Touched by a corpse, it is defiled; cleansing may permit use for a sick woman or unfit man, never worshippers of Mazda.

Fargard VII.

Clothing touched by a corpse can be cleansed with gômêz, earth, and water if it is not defiled with seed, sweat, dirt, or vomit. Cleansed clothing cannot be used by priests, warriors, or husbandmen, but may cover a sick woman or an unfit man.
