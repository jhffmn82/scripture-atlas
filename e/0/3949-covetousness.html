Abstract written by a model

is_warned_against_by Jesus (the speaker) [life is not abundance of possessions] Jesus tells the crowd to beware of and keep themselves from covetousness, because a man's life does not consist of the abundance of his possessions. The parable of the rich man who stores up goods illustrates its folly. It is contrasted with selling possessions, giving to the needy, and storing treasure in the heavens.
