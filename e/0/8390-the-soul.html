the soul

topic · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: conscious soul, her soul, my soul, soul, soul from a strange land, souls, spiritual ignorance, thy soul

Abstract written by a model

The soul is an immortal, conscious being dwelling within the heart and originating from a strange land. At bodily death it leaves the body and departs the world, yet does not die when spiritual ignorance ends. Separated from God, it suffers and becomes entangled in death through pride, mammon, counterfeit conduct, and worldly attachment. The Guru, the Word, and God's name bring understanding, satisfaction, and deliverance.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Sri Rag .. Ashtapadi

The soul suffers when separated from God and becomes entangled through pride, mammon, and counterfeit conduct. The Guru, the Word, and God's name bring the soul understanding, satisfaction, and deliverance.

Rag Gauri .. Gauri Chhant

The soul does not die when spiritual ignorance and the body perish, because it is contained in the One who is everywhere. The soul is warned against supporting theft, slander, false witness, and other evil deeds.

Asa Chhant

The soul is described as coming from a strange land and becoming entangled in Death's net through worldly attachment. It is urged to serve God, dispel anxiety, and remember that rivers separated in their course may meet again only by chance.
