Abstract written by a model

Surah 64: Mutual Disillusion concerns Allah's sovereignty, knowledge, creation, and judgment of believers and disbelievers. It warns that disbelievers denied the messengers and resurrection, while believers are urged to trust Allah, obey, spend, and guard against greed. On the Day of Assembling, believers who do right receive forgiveness and the Gardens, while those who deny the revelations face the Fire. Wives, children, wealth, and worldly temptation are described as tests, while Allah's reward is immense.
