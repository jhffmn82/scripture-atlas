Chapter 10

The Jews observed eight days of gladness in the manner of the feast of tabernacles, recalling how they had recently wandered in mountains and caves like wild animals. They carried leafy wands, branches, and palm fronds and sang thanksgiving hymns. A public statute and decree ordained that the whole nation keep these days every year.
