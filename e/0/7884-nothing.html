Abstract written by a model

Nothing recovers the Yellow Emperor's magic pearl after Intelligence, Sight, and Speech fail, showing spirituality through negation. One with Non-Existence, it is the Portal of God and the origin from which all things sprang; the Sage abides in its formless state.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XII.

Nothing successfully recovers the Yellow Emperor's lost magic pearl after Intelligence, Sight, and Speech fail. The text teaches that true spirituality is found in a state of negation rather than through knowledge, sight, or speech.

CHAPTER XXIII.

Non-Existence is described as the Portal of God and as the source from which all things sprang. Existence could not make existence exist, and Non-Existence and Nothing are one. The Sage's abiding-place is connected with this formless state.
