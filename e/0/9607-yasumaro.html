Abstract written by a model

Court Noble Yasumaro, following the Imperial Decree, records ancient words learned by heart by Hiyeda no Are, compiles Kojiki in three volumes, explains its writing, and presents it to the Empress.

Yasumaro records the ancient words learned by heart by Hiyeda no Are, following the Imperial Decree. He explains the combined use of phonetic and ideographic writing and reverently presents Three Volumes.
