king Tai

Also called: Gu Gong Tan Fu, he, him

Abstract written by a model

King Tai is a king, described as fond of beauty and loving the lady of Jiang; together they chose a settlement site. He formerly dwelt in Bin, where he served the barbarians of the north with gifts, yet their repeated incursions continued. Regarded by the people of Pin as a benevolent man, he left Bin to preserve his people when the barbarians sought his territory. After crossing mountain Liang, he built a town and established his residence at the foot of mount Qi, where he dwelt.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

king Tai is described as being fond of beauty and loving his wife. With the lady of Jiang, he chose the site for their settlement.

Chapter 14

He formerly dwelt in Bin, but repeated incursions by the barbarians of the north compelled him to leave. He settled at the foot of mount Qi out of necessity rather than because he had selected the situation.

Chapter 15

King Tai dwelt in Bin and tried serving the barbarians of the north with skins and silks, dogs and horses, and pearls and gems, but still suffered from them. He concluded that they wanted his territory, then left Bin and established himself at the foot of mount Qi.
