Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, this people have unclean lips and hear without understanding, see without perceiving, and have unresponsive hearts, ears, and eyes; they will be removed far away, and even a remaining tenth consumed. In The Second Book of Nephi, the people of unclean lips are the people among whom the speaker dwells; they hear without understanding, see without perceiving, and have unresponsive hearts, ears, and eyes lest they be converted and healed.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

This people is described by the narrator as having unclean lips. They are the audience of the message to hear but not understand and to see but not perceive, with hearts made fat, ears heavy and eyes shut so that they do not turn and be healed. The Lord says they will be removed far away, and even a remaining tenth will be consumed.
