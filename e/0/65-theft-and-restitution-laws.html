Chapter 22

The chapter fixes restitution for theft, for animals that graze in another's field, for fires that spread, and for goods lost while kept or borrowed by a neighbor. Penalties range from double to five times the value, and disputes are brought before God. The laws also cover seduction of a virgin, who must be given a dowry and married or paid for, and forbid charging interest to the poor and keeping a pledged garment overnight.
