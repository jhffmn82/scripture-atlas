Abstract written by a model

The Romans are portrayed as valiant men who befriend those who rely on them and destroy opponents. They conquered the Gauls, defeated Philip and Perseus, and captured Antiochus alive, giving his lands to Eumenes. They hold the Greeks in bondage and can make and depose kings, yet none wears a crown or purple. A 320-man senate and one yearly ruler govern them. They welcome the Jewish embassy, agree to an alliance, and warn Demetrius.

Chapter 8

The Romans are described as valiant men who befriend those who join them, destroy those who oppose them, and make and depose kings, yet none wears a crown or purple. They are governed by a senate of three hundred twenty men and one yearly ruler, and they welcome the Jewish embassy, agree to the alliance, and warn Demetrius to stop oppressing the Jews.
