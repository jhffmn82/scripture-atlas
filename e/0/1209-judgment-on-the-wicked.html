Abstract written by a model

targets iniquities of the fathers [together with their own] The LORD says it is written before him that he will not keep silence but will repay into their bosom their own iniquities and their fathers' together. Those who forsake him are destined to the sword and bow down to the slaughter, because they did not answer when called and chose what he did not delight in. They will hunger, thirst, be disappointed, cry for sorrow and wail for anguish of spirit, and the Lord GOD will kill them.
