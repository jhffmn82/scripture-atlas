Abstract written by a model

Chapter 9

The people bend their tongues like bows for falsehood, and brothers supplant and neighbors slander. Friends deceive each other, and one speaks peaceably while plotting ambush in his heart. Through deceit they refuse to know the LORD, and this is a reason the LORD gives for punishing them.
