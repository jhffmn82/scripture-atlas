Abstract written by a model

The council is the Jerusalem body of rulers, elders and scribes, including the senate of Israel, convened by the high priest. It confronts the apostles over preaching and healing in Jesus' name. First it questions Peter and John, threatens them, and releases them. Later it seeks the imprisoned apostles, resolves to kill them, yields to Gamaliel's counsel, then beats them, repeats the command, and lets them go.

Chapter 4

The rulers, elders, and scribes gather in Jerusalem the next morning and question Peter and John about the power or name behind the healing. Unable to deny the notable miracle, they confer privately, decide to threaten the apostles to stop the matter spreading, and command them not to speak or teach in Jesus' name. They release them, finding no way to punish them because of the people.

Chapter 5

The council was called together by the high priest with all the senate of Israel, and sent to the prison for the apostles, who were not found there. It heard Peter's answer and was determined to kill them, but agreed with Gamaliel's counsel. It summoned the apostles, beat them, commanded them not to speak in the name of Jesus, and let them go.
