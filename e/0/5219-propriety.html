propriety

topic · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: highest (sense of) propriety, the proprieties

Abstract written by a model

In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, propriety is an attenuated form of leal-heartedness and good faith, yet the commencement of disorder; when righteousness is lost, it appears, and those showing it most highly seek response, then bare the arm and march up when men do not respond. In Mencius, Book VI (Gaozi), propriety is a principle arising from reverence and respect, furnished in people rather than infused from outside.
