Emperor Kai-Kuwa

Abstract written by a model

Emperor Kai-Kuwa, a Heavenly Sovereign, dwelt at Izakaha in Kasuga and ruled the Empire. He wed four women, fathered five Deities, four Kings and one Queen, and after sixty-three august years was succeeded by Mima-kiri-biko-iniwe. His mausoleum stood atop Izakaha hill.

Sect. LXII.—Emperor Kai-Kuwa.

Emperor Kai-Kuwa, also called His Augustness Waka-yamato-ne-ko-hiko-ōho-bibi, dwelt in the palace of Izakaha at Kasuga and ruled the Empire. He fathered five august children, including Mima-kiri-biko-iniwe, King Hiko-imasu, and one Queen, and his august years were sixty-three; his mausoleum was at the top of the hill of Izakaha.
