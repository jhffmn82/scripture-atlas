Chapter 27

The centurion finds this ship at Myra sailing for Italy and puts Paul and the others on board. It sails slowly past Cnidus and Crete, leaves Fair Havens for Phoenix, and is caught by the Euroclydon wind and driven along. It is reinforced with cables, lightened of cargo, tackle and wheat, and finally runs aground with its bow stuck fast while the stern breaks up in the waves.
