Abstract written by a model

Perfection is an existent goal of the religious path. Monks obtain absolute perfection after attaining Kêvala; it accompanies enlightenment, deliverance, final beatitude, and the ending of all misery. It is not reached through unworthy, impure, harmful conduct, and has a place exclusively reserved for its attainments.
