the Truth

topic · 4 works · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: My guidance, The right direction, guidance, guidance from their Lord, guiding, the guidance of Allah, the guiding of them, the truth, the truth’s sake, truth

Abstract written by a model

In 2 John, truth is the basis of the elder’s love, remains in those who share that love, and is reflected in some children walking in it. In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), guidance comes from Allah through revelation, leads followers to the right way, and brings success and freedom from fear and grief. In The Believers (Al-Muminoon), the Truth is brought by Allah as the message that should guide people to the straight path, though many reject it. In Surah 39: The Groups (Az-Zumar), the truth is revealed in Scripture and brought by believers, while those who deny it face hell.

guidance in Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara)

author unknown · undated · the work’s page · read it where guidance first appears

Abstract written by a model

Guidance comes from Allah through revelation and is itself Allah’s Guidance, distinct from error. Allah makes revelations clear, calls followers to the right way, and guides whom He wills to a straight path. Following it brings success, freedom from fear and grief, and movement from darkness into light; purchasing error instead brings no prosperity, while false deities lead disbelievers into darkness.
