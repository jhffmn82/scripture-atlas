parents

group · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Your parents, his parents, parent

Abstract written by a model

Parents are heirs in inheritance provisions. When their child dies leaving a son, each parent receives one-sixth after any legacy or debt. If there is no son and the parents are heirs, the mother receives one-third; if there are brethren, her share is one-sixth. The provision is presented as an injunction from Allah, who is Knower and Wise, alongside a warning that disobeying Allah and His messenger and transgressing limits leads to Fire and shameful doom.
