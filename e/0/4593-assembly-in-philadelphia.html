Abstract written by a model

The assembly in Philadelphia is a church with little power that kept the speaker's word and did not deny his name. He rewards it with an open door no one can shut, humbles those of Satan's synagogue before it, keeps it from the hour of testing, and urges it to hold fast so no one takes its crown.

Chapter 3

The assembly in Philadelphia has a little power, kept the speaker's word, and did not deny his name, so he sets before it an open door no one can shut. He will make some of the synagogue of Satan, who say they are Jews but lie, come and worship before its feet and know he loved it. Because it kept his command to endure, he will keep it from the hour of testing, and it is told to hold firmly what it has so no one takes its crown.
