Abstract written by a model

In Surah 99: The Earthquake (Az-Zalzala), mankind’s deeds are shown, and whoever does good or ill weighing an atom sees it. In Yasna, deeds are praised and chosen by worshippers as best for both worlds when joined with good thoughts and words.
