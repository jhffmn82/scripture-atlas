Yasna LXII (Sp. LXI).

The man who worships the Fire in verity and truth approaches it with wood in hand, Baresman ready, flesh in hand, and a mortar. Salvation is promised to him, and the Fire blesses him if he brings properly prepared wood or other sacred materials.
