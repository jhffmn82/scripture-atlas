Abstract written by a model

Settlements are guarded by Good Mind, advanced by Mazda and victory, and urged to remain uncorrupted. Endangered by evil rulers, they thrive through mental goodness and Ahura Mazda’s favor; those who further Piety’s settlements with zeal receive Mazda’s presence and joyful grace, while offerings protect present and saving settlements.

Yasna XLVI.

The settlements are endangered by evil rulers and may be delivered to harm. Those who further the settlements of Piety with energy and zeal receive Ahura Mazda's presence and joyful grace.

Yasna LV (Sp. LIV).

Mazda helps the settlements advance, and the Praises of the Offering are associated with protecting present settlements and those that shall bring salvation.
