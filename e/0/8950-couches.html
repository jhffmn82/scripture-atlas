Abstract written by a model

A couch may be accepted by a monk or nun only if free of eggs and living beings, light, movable, and well tied. Heavy, immovable, badly tied couches are unacceptable; one with few living beings may be returned after inspection, sweeping, and drying.

Second Lecture, Called Begging for a Couch. / First Lesson. .. Third Lesson.

A couch may be accepted only when it is free from eggs and living beings, light, movable, and well tied. A couch that is heavy, immovable, or badly tied should not be accepted, and a couch containing few living beings may be returned only after careful inspection, sweeping, and drying.
