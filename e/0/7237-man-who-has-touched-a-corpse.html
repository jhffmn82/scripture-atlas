man who has touched a corpse

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: one who has touched the corpse of a man

Abstract written by a model

A man who has touched a corpse must seek cleansing. He washes with gômêz, rubs dry, runs Hâthra until meeting or overtaking a man, and cries out for cleansing. If no man cleanses him after reaching habitation, he cleanses himself with gômêz and water. Finding water on the way incurs four hundred stripes with each instrument; refusing penalty and atonement leads him to inhabit the mansion of the Drug.

VIII.

A man who has touched a corpse without wilful sin must wash with gômêz, rub himself dry, run specified distances, and call out that he wishes to be made clean. If no one cleanses him, he may cleanse himself with gômêz and water, but he incurs penalties when he finds water or trees on the way.
