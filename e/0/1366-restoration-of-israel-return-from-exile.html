Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Jeremiah, the LORD restores Israel and Judah from exile, gathers them from across the earth, brings them to their ancestral land, and rebuilds their city. In The Book of Ezekiel, the LORD gathers Israel from the countries where they are scattered, brings them into the land of Israel, and sanctifies them before the nations. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the Lord gathers the remnant of Jacob and the dispersed house of Israel from across the earth to knowledge of him and their inherited lands.

restoration of Israel (return from exile) in The Book of Jeremiah

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where restoration of Israel (return from exile) first appears

Abstract written by a model

The LORD promises to restore Israel and Judah from exile, an act of deliverance so great it will surpass the Exodus in memory. Recompense for sin comes first. He then reverses their captivity, sends fishermen and hunters to gather them from the north country and the ends of the earth, and brings them to the land given their fathers, while their oppressors go into captivity. The gathering includes the blind, lame, and women with child, weeping but led by rivers in a straight way. They sing on Zion, enjoy grain, wine, and oil, and see mourning turned to joy. The city is rebuilt and made holy, never again plucked up.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

The LORD promises that the days are coming when Israel's return from the north and all the countries of dispersion will replace the Exodus as the defining act of deliverance. He will send many fishermen and hunters to gather them from every mountain, hill and cleft of the rocks and bring them into the land he gave their fathers, though the recompense for their sin comes first.

Chapter 30

The LORD promises to reverse the captivity of his people Israel and Judah and bring them back to the land he gave their fathers. He will save them and their offspring from the land of their captivity, and will reverse the captivity of Jacob's tents. Those who enslave them will themselves go into captivity.

Chapter 31

The LORD promises to bring Israel back from the north country and the ends of the earth, including the blind, lame, and women with child, weeping but led by rivers in a straight way. They will sing on Zion, enjoy grain, wine, and oil, and see mourning turned to joy, while the city is rebuilt from the tower of Hananel onward and the whole valley and fields to the Kidron become holy and never again plucked up.

Abstract written by a model

Gathering of Israel is the Lord’s gathering of the remnant seed of Jacob and dispersed house of Israel from long dispersion across the earth. From east, west, south, and north, they are brought to knowledge of the Lord and to their inherited lands, including Jerusalem and the New Jerusalem, with repentant people assisting.
