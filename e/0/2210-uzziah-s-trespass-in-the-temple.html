Uzziah's trespass in the temple

Abstract written by a model

Uzziah, grown strong and proud, corruptly enters the LORD's temple to burn incense on the altar. Azariah and eighty priests oppose him, saying it belongs to Aaron's consecrated sons; he persists in anger, is struck with leprosy, and loses his role.

Chapter 26

After becoming strong, Uzziah's heart is lifted up, and he acts corruptly by entering the LORD's temple to burn incense on the altar of incense. The priests tell him this is not for him but for the consecrated sons of Aaron, and his persistence in anger is followed by leprosy, ending his role in the LORD's house.
