great filial piety

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: filial piety, flial piety

Abstract written by a model

Great filial piety is filial service to parents, exemplified by Zengzi: it nourishes their will, completely fulfills what service requires, brings them delight in goodness, and thereby establishes proper duties throughout the kingdom.
