Chapter 6

Men of the synagogue called The Libertines, and of the Cyrenians, Alexandrians, and those of Cilicia and Asia, dispute with Stephen but cannot withstand his wisdom and Spirit. They secretly induce men to claim Stephen spoke blasphemy, stir up the people, elders, and scribes, and seize him and bring him to the council.
