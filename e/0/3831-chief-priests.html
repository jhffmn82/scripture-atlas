Abstract written by a model

The chief priests are a group hostile to Jesus, driven by fear of him and of the crowd's astonishment at his teaching. In the temple they seek to destroy him and, with scribes and elders, question his authority while dodging his question for fear of the people. They plot to seize and kill him by deception, welcome Judas's offer of betrayal, and send a multitude to arrest him. They bind and deliver him to Pilate, stir the crowd to demand Barabbas, and mock Jesus at the cross.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

The chief priests hear Jesus' words in the temple and seek a way to destroy him, fearing him because of the crowd's astonishment. Later, with scribes and elders, they ask by what authority he acts, and they will not answer his question about John's baptism for fear of the people.

Chapter 14

The chief priests and scribes seek to seize Jesus by deception and kill him, but not during the feast. They are glad when Judas offers to deliver him and promise him money, then send a multitude to arrest him and gather with the elders and scribes to seek testimony against him.

Chapter 15

The chief priests, with the elders, scribes and council, bind Jesus and deliver him to Pilate, accusing him of many things. They stir up the multitude to ask for Barabbas instead. At the cross they mock Jesus, saying he saved others but cannot save himself.
