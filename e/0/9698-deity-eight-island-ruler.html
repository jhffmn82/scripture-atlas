Abstract written by a model

Deity Eight-Island Ruler is a deity born to unnamed parents, counted among seventeen generations, who marries several consorts and parents Great-Harvest Deity, August-Spirit-of-Food, and Deity Fuha-no-moji-Ku-nu-su-nu.

Deity Eight-Island Ruler is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Sect.

The Deity Eight-Island Ruler was begotten by the procreation of the unnamed parents in the thalamo. He wedded Princess-Falling-Like-the-Flowers-of-the-Trees, daughter of the Deity Great-Mountain-Possessor, and begot the Deity Fuha-no-moji-Ku-nu-su-nu.

Sect.

The text states that from the Deity Eight-Island-Ruler down to Deity Toho-tsu-yama-zaki-tarashi are called the Deities of seventeen generations.
