Chapter 7

David sits before the LORD and asks who he is that he has been brought this far, then praises God as great and without equal. He recalls God's redemption of Israel from Egypt, and asks that the word spoken about his house be confirmed forever and that his house be blessed. He says he has found it in his heart to pray because God revealed the promise to him.
