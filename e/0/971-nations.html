nations

Also called: the nations, the peoples

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the nations are described as raging and plotting a vain thing, promised to the LORD's son as an inheritance, and destined to be broken with a rod of iron and dashed in pieces like a potter's vessel. In Habakkuk, the nations tremble when the LORD looks at them, and he threshes them in anger as he marches through the land in wrath.
