Abstract written by a model

In Chapter 8's vision, a two-horned ram, the higher horn rising last, pushes westward, northward and southward. Nothing can stand before it, and it acts as it wills and magnifies itself. A male goat then breaks its horns and tramples it, with no deliverer. Gabriel explains the ram is the kings of Media and Persia.

Chapter 8

The ram has two horns, one higher than the other with the higher coming up last, and pushes westward, northward, and southward so that no animal can stand before it, and it does as it wills and magnifies itself. The male goat breaks its two horns, casts it to the ground, and tramples it with no one to deliver it. Gabriel explains that the ram is the kings of Media and Persia.
