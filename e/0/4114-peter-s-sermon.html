Abstract written by a model

In Cornelius's house Peter opens by saying God shows no favoritism and accepts in every nation the one who fears him and works righteousness. He recounts the gospel of Jesus: his anointing, ministry of doing good and healing, death on a tree, resurrection on the third day, appearance to chosen witnesses, and appointment as Judge. He closes by saying all the prophets testify that everyone who believes in Jesus's name will receive remission of sins, and the Holy Spirit falls on the hearers while he is still speaking.
