XVII. Ashi Yast.

The courtezan is condemned in Ashi Vanguhi’s wailings for destroying her fruit and for bearing a child conceived by a stranger and presenting it to her husband. Ashi Vanguhi says that libations sent by the courtezan who produces untimely issues will not be accepted.
