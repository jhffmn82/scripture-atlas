Jabesh Gilead

place · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Jabesh, all Jabesh Gilead, inhabitants of Jabesh Gilead

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Judges, Jabesh Gilead alone sent no one to the Mizpah assembly, and the congregation's army struck its inhabitants with the sword, sparing only four hundred young virgins. In The First Book of Samuel, Jabesh Gilead was besieged by Nahash the Ammonite, obtained seven days to seek help, and was rescued by Saul and Israel; later, its valiant men responded to Saul's death by bringing his and his sons' bodies there, burning them, and burying their bones under the tamarisk tree. In 1 Chronicles, Jabesh Gilead heard what the Philistines did to Saul, and its valiant men took the bodies of Saul and his sons, brought them to Jabesh, and buried their bones under the oak there; the people fasted seven days.
