Emperor Kei-Tai

person · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: Heavenly Monarch, His Augustness Ohodo, Ohodo, this Heavenly Monarch, this august reign

Abstract written by a model

Emperor Kei-Tai, also called His Augustness Ohodo, was the fifth descendant of Heavenly Monarch Homuda. After Emperor Mu-Retsu’s death, he was sent for from Afumi, married Tashiraka, and received the Empire. Dwelling at Palace of Tamaho at Ihare, he ruled for forty-three years and had nineteen children.

Emperor Kei-Tai is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Sect. CLXXII—Emperor Mu-Retsu.

Ohodo, the fifth descendant of the Heavenly Monarch Homuda, was sent for from the land of Afumi after Emperor Mu-Retsu's death. He was married to Tashiraka and presented with the Empire.

Sect. CLXXIII.—Emperor Kei-Tai.

Emperor Kei-Tai, also called His Augustness Ohodo, dwelt at the Palace of Tamaho at Ihare and ruled the Empire. His reign lasted forty-three years, and his august mausoleum was at Awi in Mishima.
