Abstract written by a model

Perfection is taught by Ginas and reached through compassion, Sânti, or renunciation, self-restraint, austerities, and freedom from defilement and Karman; Gautama reaches it after cutting off love and hatred, attaining safety and perfect happiness.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Tenth Lecture.

Perfection is reached by Gautama after he cuts off love and hatred. It is associated with the world of safety and perfect happiness.

Eighteenth Lecture.

Perfection is reached by those who renounce the world, practise self-restraint or austerities, and become free from defilement and Karman. The unit says that several named kings and Gaya with thousands of kings reached perfection.
