Abstract written by a model

The pillar of cloud is the form in which the LORD leads Israel by day and never departs from the people. At the Red Sea crossing it moves behind Israel, with the angel of God, separating them from Egypt's camp; it is dark yet gives light by night, and the LORD looks through it to confuse the Egyptian army. Later it descends to the Tent of Meeting door whenever Moses enters, as the LORD speaks with him and the people worship.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

The pillar of cloud is the form in which the LORD goes before Israel by day to lead them on their way. It does not depart from before the people.

Chapter 14

The pillar of cloud, with the angel of God, moves from before Israel's camp to stand behind them, coming between the camp of Egypt and the camp of Israel. It is cloud and darkness yet gives light by night, so that neither camp comes near the other all night. In the morning watch the LORD looks out through the pillar of fire and cloud on the Egyptian army and confuses it.

Chapter 33

The pillar of cloud descends and stands at the door of the Tent of Meeting whenever Moses enters, and the LORD speaks with Moses. All the people see it and rise to worship, each at their own tent door.
