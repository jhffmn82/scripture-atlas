Abstract written by a model

In The Acts of the Apostles, the Way is the community of the Lord's disciples centered in Jerusalem, which grows, spreads across Judea, Galilee, and Samaria, is first called Christians in Antioch, and sends relief to Judean brothers. In The Acts of the Apostles, the Way is the religious movement Paul once persecuted, later described as a sect and defended by him as the path of service to his fathers' God according to the law and prophets.

the Way (disciples of the Lord) in The Acts of the Apostles

author unknown · about AD 80 to 90 · the work’s page · read it where the Way (disciples of the Lord) first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Way is the community of the Lord's disciples, numbering about one hundred twenty and centered in Jerusalem. It grows steadily: the multitude chooses seven men, and many priests obey the faith. Saul first persecutes followers, then is received after conversion, while assemblies across Judea, Galilee, and Samaria enjoy peace and multiply. In Antioch they are first called Christians, and they send relief to Judean brothers.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

The disciples are multiplying in number, and the whole multitude is pleased with the twelve's proposal and chooses the seven men. Their number greatly multiplies in Jerusalem, and a great company of priests becomes obedient to the faith.

Chapter 9

Saul sought to bind followers of the Way, men or women, and bring them to Jerusalem. After his conversion the disciples at Damascus received him, while those in Jerusalem initially feared him. The assemblies throughout Judea, Galilee, and Samaria had peace, were built up, and multiplied in the fear of the Lord and the comfort of the Holy Spirit.

Chapter 11

The disciples are first called Christians in Antioch. As each has means, they determine to send relief to the brothers in Judea.

the Way in The Acts of the Apostles

author unknown · about AD 80 to 90 · the work’s page · read it where the Way first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Way is a religious movement Paul once persecuted to the death, imprisoning men and women. In Chapter 19 some in the synagogue speak evil of it, sparking a disturbance and riot. Later, Paul's accusers call it a sect, while he confesses serving his fathers' God according to it, believing the law and prophets.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 19

Some in the synagogue are hardened and speak evil of the Way before the multitude, so Paul separates the disciples. A no small disturbance arises concerning the Way, which leads into Demetrius's complaint and the riot.

Chapter 22

The Way is the movement Paul says he persecuted to the death, binding and imprisoning both men and women. The high priest and council are cited as witnesses to his actions against it.

Chapter 24

The Way is called a sect by Paul's accusers, but Paul confesses that he serves the God of his fathers according to it, believing all that is in the law and the prophets. Felix is said to have more exact knowledge concerning it.
