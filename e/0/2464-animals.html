animals

Also called: The animals, animals (creatures to be saved), birds, creatures, creeping things, every creeping thing of the ground, every living thing, genera, livestock, species, the animal, the other

Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, animals are initially included in the LORD's plan for destruction, then preserved when Noah brings pairs of birds, livestock, and creeping things into the ship with food. In Ecclesiastes or, The Preacher, animals share the fate of humans, dying, sharing one breath, and returning to dust while the narrator questions their spirit's destination. In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), animals are created beings whose existence, diversity, care, and judgment unfold by God's power.

animals in Asa ki War (Asa di Var)

Guru Nanak · the work’s page · read it where animals first appears

Abstract written by a model

Animals are created beings in all creation, living in water, dry land, and fourteen worlds. God records their names, creates their many species, forms, colours, genera, and kinds, cares for them, knows their condition, and appoints Dharmraj to judge their acts. They have no power of their own; all unfolds by God's power.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Slok II .. Pauri II

God created animals, recorded their names, and appointed Dharmraj to judge their acts.

Slok III .. Pauri III

Creatures have wonderful species, forms, colours, genera, and kinds. Their existence and characteristics arise by God's power.

Slok VIII .. Pauri VIII

Animals are among the beings created by God. God takes care of them all and knows their condition.

Slok XI .. Pauri XI

Creatures have nothing in their own power; God creates the different worlds and determines what takes place.
