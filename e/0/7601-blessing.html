Âfrîn Paighambar Zartûst / XXIII. Âfrîn Paighambar Zartûst.

The blessing wishes Vîstâspa to resemble various exemplary figures in beneficence, fiend-smiting, strength, arms, glory, instruction, might, leadership, beauty, wealth, holiness, speed, radiance, heat, piercing rays, truth, conquest, welfare, and freedom from sickness and death. It concludes with a wish that the blessing come true and lead to the bright, all-happy, blissful abode of the holy Ones.
