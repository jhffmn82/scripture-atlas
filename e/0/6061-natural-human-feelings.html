natural human feelings

Abstract written by a model

Natural human feelings include desire for pleasure and ease, aversion to injuring others, and reluctance toward wrongs; extending what one cannot bear or will not do into action produces benevolence and righteousness.

Part II / Chapter 1 .. Chapter 38

Natural human feelings include desires for sensory pleasure and ease, as well as aversion to injuring others and reluctance to commit certain wrongs. When these feelings are fully extended, they produce benevolence and righteousness.
