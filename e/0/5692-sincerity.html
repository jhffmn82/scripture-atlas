sincerity

topic · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 8 connections

Abstract written by a model

Sincerity is the way of Heaven and the defining quality of the superior man, whose words and actions respect each other and avoid excess. It grounds obedience to parents, truthfulness with friends, and confidence from the sovereign. It is attained by choosing what is good and holding it firmly through study, inquiry, reflection, discrimination, earnest practice, and cultivating goodness. Complete sincerity develops one’s own nature and those of men, animals, and things, assisting Heaven and Earth. It progresses from apparent to manifest to brilliant, affects others, foreknows events, and through ceaselessness produces changes and accomplishes its ends.

sincerity in The Doctrine of the Mean

James Legge · about 449 BC · the work’s page · read it where sincerity first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

Entire sincerity marks the superior man when his words respect his actions and his actions respect his words. He is careful in speaking and does not permit excess in his words.

Chapter 20

Sincerity is the way of Heaven and the foundation of obedience to parents, truthfulness with friends, and confidence from the sovereign. Its attainment requires choosing what is good and holding it firmly, through study, inquiry, reflection, discrimination, and earnest practice.

Chapter 22

The most complete sincerity enables a person to give full development to their own nature and then to the natures of other men, animals, and things.

Chapter 23

Sincerity is attained through cultivating the shoots of goodness. It becomes apparent, manifest, and brilliant, and its brilliance affects others.

Chapter 24

Most entire sincerity is characterized by the ability to foreknow. Through it, good and evil events can be anticipated.

Chapter 26

Entire sincerity includes ceaselessness. Without display or movement, it becomes manifested, produces changes, and accomplishes its ends.
