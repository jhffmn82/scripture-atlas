Abstract written by a model

CHAP. V. 1. Tsze-chang asked how a man should conduct

Conduct consisting of sincere and truthful words and honourable and careful actions may be practised among the rude tribes of the South or the North. Without these qualities, conduct will not be appreciated even in a man's neighborhood.
