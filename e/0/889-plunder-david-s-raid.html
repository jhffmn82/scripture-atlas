The Amalekites' plunder from the Philistines' land and Judah is recovered by David, with nothing lacking, small or great. David also takes the flocks and herds the Amalekites drove before their other livestock and calls them David's plunder. He shares it equally among all his men and sends part to the elders of Judah and many towns.
