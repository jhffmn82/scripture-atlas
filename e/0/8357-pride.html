pride

topic · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: egotism, selfishness, the filth of pride, worldly love

Abstract written by a model

Pride is an inner force that makes the heart impure, sustains worldly attachment, blocks spiritual understanding, and causes false claims, suffering, transmigration, and subjection to death. It arises from beauty, wealth, status, and religious display, affecting kings and religious figures as well as others. Worldly offerings do not remove it. Under the Guru’s instruction, meditation on God’s Name and the Guru’s Word dispel or remove pride, bringing genuineness and supreme dignity. It is ultimately to be forgotten, subdued, and made to depart through the Word.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Sri Rag .. Ashtapadi

Pride makes the heart impure, causes man to wander in worldly attachment, and prevents spiritual understanding. The Guru's instruction and the Word remove pride.

Majh ki War

Pride causes people to claim what is not truly theirs and to suffer for their arrogance. When pride is dispelled through the Guru's instruction, a person meditates on God's Name and is accepted as genuine.

Rag Gauri .. Gauri Chhant

Pride causes kings and religious figures to suffer, wander in transmigration, and remain subject to death. Through the Guru’s Word, pride is removed and supreme dignity is obtained.

Ramkali .. Ramkali ki War I

Pride arises from beauty, wealth, status, and religious display and causes suffering and error. It departs through the Word and the Guru's instruction.

Rag Basant

Pride is to be forgotten and subdued, while mental pride is to be dispelled through meditation on God.
