Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, treasure is what one values, with listeners told to store it in heaven because earthly treasure is vulnerable to loss and the heart follows its location. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, treasures laid up in heaven are secure from the corruption and theft facing earthly treasures, and a person's heart follows the person's treasure.

Abstract written by a model

Treasure is what one values; earthly treasure is vulnerable to moth, rust and thieves, so listeners are told to store it in heaven, safe from loss, because the heart follows the treasure's location.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Listeners are told not to lay up treasures on earth, where moth and rust consume and thieves steal, but to lay up treasures in heaven, where these cannot destroy or take them. The text says where one's treasure is, there the heart will be also.
