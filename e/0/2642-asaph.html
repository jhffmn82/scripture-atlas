Asaph

Abstract written by a model

Asaph, son of Berechiah and brother of Heman, is a Levite singer whose lineage traces back to Levi through Gershom. He stood at Heman's right hand in the service of song. David made him chief of the appointed Levites, who sound cymbals and minister continually before God's ark. He prophesied at the king's order, and his four sons served under him; the first lot fell to Joseph.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

Asaph, the son of Berechiah, is called Heman's brother and is said to have stood on his right hand in the service of song. His ancestry is traced through Shimea, Zerah, Jahath, and Gershom back to Levi.

Chapter 16

Asaph is the chief of the appointed Levites and sounds aloud with cymbals. David ordains the giving of thanks through him and his brothers, and leaves them before the ark to minister continually as each day's work requires.

Chapter 25

Asaph had four sons named: Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, and Asharelah, who served under his hand. Asaph prophesied at the order of the king, and the first lot fell to Joseph of his sons.
