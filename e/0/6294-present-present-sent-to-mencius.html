present (present sent to Mencius) in Mencius, Book II (Gong Sun Chou)

Mencius · about 299 BC · the work’s page · read it where present (present sent to Mencius) first appears

Abstract written by a model

A gift of 2,400 taels of fine silver is sent to Mencius in Qi, said to fund arms for protection, yet counts as a bribe if unsolicited.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Presents were sent to Mencius in Qi, Song, and Xue. Their stated purposes were travelling expenses in Song and procuring arms in Xue.
