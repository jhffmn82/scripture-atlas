morning and evening lambs (continual burnt offering)

Abstract written by a model

The continual burnt offering consists of one unblemished year-old male lamb offered each morning and another at evening. Each is accompanied by a tenth ephah of fine flour mixed with a fourth hin of beaten oil, plus a fourth hin drink offering poured out in the holy place.

Chapter 28

One male lamb a year old without defect is offered in the morning and the other at evening. Each is accompanied by one tenth of an ephah of fine flour mixed with a fourth of a hin of beaten oil, and a drink offering of a fourth of a hin of strong drink, poured out in the holy place.
