The body is the church as the body of Christ: one body with many members, formed by baptism in one Spirit of Jews, Greeks, bond or free. God composed it without division, so members care for one another, suffering and rejoicing together.

Chapter 12

The body is one yet has many members, and all who were baptized in one Spirit, Jews or Greeks, bond or free, form it. It is not a single member but many, and God composed it so there is no division and the members care for one another. When one member suffers all suffer, and when one is honored all rejoice.
