Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Kings, Ahaziah son of Ahab reigns over Israel in Samaria for two years, does evil, and serves Baal. In The Second Book of Kings, Ahaziah is king of Israel in Samaria, also recorded as king of Judah; he falls ill, sends messengers to Baal Zebub and captains to Elijah, receives Elijah’s prophecy, and dies as foretold.

Ahaziah in The Second Book of Kings

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Ahaziah first appears

Abstract written by a model

Ahaziah was a king of Israel in Samaria, also recorded as king of Judah. He fell through the lattice of his upper room and became sick, then sent messengers to Baal Zebub to ask whether he would recover. He sent three captains with fifty men each to Elijah; two companies were consumed by fire. Elijah told him he would not leave his bed. He died as the LORD said, and Jehoram succeeded him because he had no son.

1 section; each heading opens the text

The Second Book of Kings / Chapter 1

Ahaziah falls through the lattice in his upper room in Samaria and becomes sick, then sends messengers to inquire of Baal Zebub whether he will recover. He sends three captains with fifty men each to Elijah, and the first two companies are consumed by fire. Elijah tells him he will not leave his bed, and he dies according to the LORD's word, with Jehoram reigning in his place because he had no son.
