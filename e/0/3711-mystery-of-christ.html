Abstract written by a model

In Paul’s Letter to the Ephesians, the mystery of Christ was revealed to Paul and God's holy apostles and prophets, showing that Gentiles are fellow heirs, members of the body, and partakers of the promise. In Paul’s Letter to the Colossians, the mystery of Christ was revealed to God's saints as Christ in the Gentiles, the hope of glory, whom Paul and his companions proclaim and teach.

mystery of Christ in Paul’s Letter to the Ephesians

author unknown · about AD 62 · the work’s page · read it where mystery of Christ first appears

Abstract written by a model

The mystery of Christ, hidden in God for ages and unknown to earlier generations, was made known to Paul by revelation. It is now revealed in the Spirit to God's holy apostles and prophets. Its content: Gentiles are fellow heirs, members of the body, and partakers of the promise.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The mystery of Christ was made known to Paul by revelation, and he had written of it before in few words so readers could perceive his understanding of it. It was not made known to earlier generations but has now been revealed to God's holy apostles and prophets in the Spirit. Its content is that the Gentiles are fellow heirs, members of the body, and partakers of the promise, and it had been hidden for ages in God.

Abstract written by a model

The mystery was hidden for ages and generations, then revealed to God's saints; its glorious riches are made known among the Gentiles as Christ in you, the hope of glory, whom Paul and companions proclaim, teaching everyone in wisdom.

Chapter 1

The mystery was hidden for ages and generations but has now been revealed to God's saints. God was pleased to make known its glorious riches among the Gentiles, and it is identified as Christ in you, the hope of glory. Paul and his companions proclaim Christ, teaching every man in all wisdom.
