Inviolable Place of Worship

place · 5 works · 7 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Allah's sanctuaries, the Inviolable Place of Worship

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), the Inviolable Place of Worship is a sanctuary and sacred place in Makka, serving as the qiblah toward which Muhammad and believers turn in prayer, and is governed by pilgrimage obligations and restrictions on fighting unless opponents attack first. In Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba), the Inviolable Place of Worship is the site of a treaty with certain idolaters, is not to be approached by idolaters after the stated year, and is tended by believers. In Surah 17: The Night Journey (Al-Israa), the Inviolable Place of Worship is the starting place from which Allah carries His servant by night. In Surah 22: The Pilgrimage (Al-Hajj), the Inviolable Place of Worship is appointed for mankind, including its dwellers and nomads, and those who disbelieve, bar people from it, or seek wrongful partiality there are threatened with a painful doom. In Surah 48: The Victory (Al-Fath), the Inviolable Place of Worship is the place from which disbelievers debarred Muhammad and the believers, and which Allah promised they would enter secure if He wills.
