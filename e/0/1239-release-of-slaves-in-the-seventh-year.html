Chapter 15

In the seventh year a Hebrew servant who has served six years must be let go free, and the master must not send him away empty. The command is grounded in remembering that Israel was a slave in Egypt and the LORD redeemed them. The master should not find it hard, because the LORD will bless him in all he does.
