Eleazar

Abstract written by a model

Eleazar is a ninety-year-old principal scribe of noble countenance, resolutely loyal to God's laws. Forced to eat swine's flesh, he spits it out and walks willingly to the torture instrument. He refuses his acquaintances' offer to feign eating, fearing to mislead the young and to escape God. He dies under blows, suffering bodily but gladly in soul, leaving an example of virtue.

Chapter 6

Eleazar, a principal scribe of advanced age (ninety years), is compelled to eat swine's flesh but spits it out and goes to the instrument of torture willingly. He refuses the offer of his acquaintances to pretend to eat, saying deception would lead the young astray and that he cannot escape God either way. He dies under the blows, saying he suffers in body but gladly in soul out of fear of the Lord, leaving an example of nobleness and virtue.
