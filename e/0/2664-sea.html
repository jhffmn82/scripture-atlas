Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the sea flees when it sees the Exodus from Egypt. In Psalms, the sea and great waters writhe and convulse at seeing God pass through it by unknown paths. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, the sea is calmed by the Lord, planted with islands, sailed with danger, and filled with wondrous living things and huge creatures. In Jonah, the sea receives the storm sent by the LORD, grows increasingly violent, and ceases raging after Jonah is thrown into it. In The Gospel According to Matthew, the sea is where the disciples' boat is distressed by waves, where Jesus and Peter walk on the waters, and where the wind ceases after Jesus enters the boat. In The Good News According to Mark, the sea is where Jesus teaches from a boat and calms a storm by commanding it, astonishing his disciples. In Revelation, the sea is struck by judgment, with parts turned to blood and its creatures and ships destroyed, then becoming entirely blood. In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), the sea is crossed by the Children of Israel as Allah rescues them and Pharaoh's folk drown.

and 7 more works

In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), the sea contains the drowning of Pharaoh and his people as divine retribution for denying Allah's revelations. In Surah 20: Taa-Haa, the sea becomes a dry path for the Children of Israel and covers Pharaoh and his hosts. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), the sea parts when Moses strikes it with his staff, allowing Moses and his companions to escape. In Surah 28: The Stories (Al-Qasas), the sea is where Allah abandons Pharaoh and his hosts after seizing them, making their drowning an example and consequence for evil-doers. In Surah 82: The Cleaving (Al-Infitaar), the seas are poured forth. In Mencius, Book VII (Jin Xin), the sea makes other waters difficult to contemplate for one who has considered it. In Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods), the sea contains the lost fish-hook, the savoury august road, the palace of the Ocean-Possessor, fishes, and crocodiles.
