Mencius, Book II (Gong Sun Chou) / Chapter 1

Royal dignity is described by Mencius as easy for Qi to attain under present conditions. He contrasts this with the greater difficulty king Wen faced because of his small territory and limited initial resources.

Chapter 5

A ruler who truly practises the five things and has no enemy in the kingdom will attain the royal dignity.
