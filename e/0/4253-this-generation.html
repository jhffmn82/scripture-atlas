Abstract written by a model

This generation is likened by Jesus to children in marketplaces whose companions refuse to dance or mourn. It rejects John the Baptizer as demon-possessed for fasting, then rejects the Son of Man as a glutton and drunkard, friend of sinners; judgment is to come upon it.

Chapter 11

Jesus compares this generation to children in the marketplaces who complain that their companions neither danced to the flute nor lamented at mourning. It rejected John for his fasting, saying he had a demon, and rejected the Son of Man for eating and drinking, calling him a glutton and drunkard and a friend of tax collectors and sinners.
