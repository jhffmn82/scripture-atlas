Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, the Red Sea lies before Israel's camp at Pihahiroth, is parted for Israel to cross on dry land, and returns to drown Pharaoh's army. In Psalms, the Red Sea is where God rebuked the waters, dried and divided them for rebellious Israel to pass through, and covered Pharaoh's army. In The Wisdom of Solomon, the Red Sea is where wisdom brought the holy people through much water and drowned their enemies, then yielded a dry highway and grassy plain for God's people to pass over.

Red Sea in Exodus

author unknown · about 1439 to 1399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Red Sea first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Red Sea is a body of water that receives locusts driven by a west wind. It lies before Israel's camp at Pihahiroth. Its waters are driven back by an east wind, letting Israel cross on dry land as walls stand either side. The waters then return, drowning Pharaoh's army, and Moses leads Israel onward.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 14

The sea lies before Israel's camp at Pihahiroth, between Migdol and Baal Zephon. A strong east wind drives it back all night to make dry land, with waters standing as walls on either side, and it returns to its strength in the morning when Moses stretches out his hand. It covers Pharaoh's army and none of them remain.

Chapter 15

The Red Sea is where Pharaoh's army is drowned, its waters piled up like a heap by the blast of the LORD's nostrils so that Israel walks through on dry land. The waters then return over the Egyptians. Moses afterwards leads Israel onward from it.

Red Sea in Psalms

author unknown · undated · the work’s page · read it where Red Sea first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Red Sea is a place where God performed an awesome work: rebuked, it dried up and was divided, letting rebellious Israel pass through its middle as through a desert, while its waters covered and overthrew Pharaoh's army, leaving none of their adversaries.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 106

At the Red Sea the fathers were rebellious, not understanding God's wonders. God rebuked it and it dried up, leading them through the depths as through a desert, and its waters covered their adversaries so that none was left. The Red Sea is also named among the sites of God's awesome works.

Chapter 136

The Red Sea is divided apart by God so that Israel can pass through its middle. Pharaoh and his army are overthrown in it.

Red sea in The Wisdom of Solomon

author unknown · about 99 BC · the work’s page · read it where Red sea first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Red sea is a body of water where wisdom brought the holy people through much water and drowned their enemies, casting them up from the deep. It then yielded an unhindered dry highway and grassy plain, letting God's people pass over.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

Wisdom brought the holy people over the Red sea and led them through much water. She drowned their enemies in it and cast them up from the bottom of the deep.

Chapter 19

The Red sea yielded an unhindered highway of dry land rising out of the water, and a grassy plain out of the violent surge. By this way God's people passed over.
