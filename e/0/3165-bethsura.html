Bethsura

place · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of the Maccabees, Bethsura is a city on Judea's borders, fortified by the Jews; the king's army attacks, its people make peace and leave for lack of Sabbath-year food, and the king garrisons it. In The Second Book of the Maccabees, Bethsura is a strong Jewish fortress that the king attacked; he advanced, was turned back, failed, and was defeated. Judas sent necessary supplies to those inside, and the king later negotiated with them a second time, giving his hand and taking theirs.
