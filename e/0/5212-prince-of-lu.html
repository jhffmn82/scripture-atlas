prince of Lu

Also called: The prince of Lu, our prince, the prince

Abstract written by a model

In Mencius, Book VI (Gaozi), the prince of Lu considers appointing Shen army commander and entrusting Lu’s administration to Yue Zheng; Mencius opposes war with an uninstructed people but welcomes Yue Zheng’s love of good. In Mencius, Book VII (Jin Xin), the prince of Lu goes to Song and calls out at the Die Shi gate; the gatekeeper recognizes that his voice closely resembles that of their prince because of the correspondence of their positions.
