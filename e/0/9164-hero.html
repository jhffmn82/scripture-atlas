Abstract written by a model

The hero is a person defined by disciplined self-control and knowledge. He endures the hardships of control, crosses life’s flood, liberates the fettered, and avoids attachment, discontent, lust, violence, pleasures, and bad works. Grounded in knowledge, he conquers wrath, pride, deceit, greed, and other passions, destroys sorrow, and remains careful and restrained. Along with other heroes, he follows the difficult road toward final liberation, where there is no return.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Second Lecture, Called Conquest of the World. / First Lesson. .. Sixth Lesson.

The hero is not careless, attached, discontented, or lustful, and does not tolerate the hardships of control. He crosses the flood of life, liberates the bound, and is not polluted by the sin of killing.

Third Lecture, Called Hot and Cold. / First Lesson. .. Fourth Lesson.

The hero guards himself, abstains from pleasures and bad works, and is grounded in knowledge. He conquers wrath, pride, deceit, and greed, abstains from killing, and destroys sorrow.

Fourth Lecture, Called Righteousness. / First Lesson. .. Fourth Lesson.

The heroes are careful, guarded, endowed with knowledge, and always restrained. Their road is difficult because they go where there is no return, and they subdue blood and flesh.
