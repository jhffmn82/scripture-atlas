Joseph of Arimathaea

person · 4 works · 10 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Joseph, a man named Joseph, a rich man from Arimathaea named Joseph

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Joseph of Arimathaea, a rich disciple of Jesus, asks Pilate for Jesus' body, wraps it in clean linen, lays it in his own new rock-cut tomb, and seals it with a large stone. In The Good News According to Mark, Joseph of Arimathaea, a prominent council member seeking God's Kingdom, boldly asks Pilate for Jesus' body, buys linen, wraps him, lays him in a rock-cut tomb, and rolls a stone against the door. In The Gospel According to Luke, Joseph of Arimathaea is a good, righteous council member awaiting God's Kingdom who did not consent to the council's deed; he asks Pilate for Jesus' body, wraps it in linen, and lays it in an unused tomb. In The Gospel According to John, Joseph of Arimathaea, a disciple of Jesus secretly for fear of the Jews, asks Pilate for Jesus' body; Pilate gives permission, and Joseph takes the body away. With Nicodemus he binds it in linen cloths with spices and lays it in the tomb.
