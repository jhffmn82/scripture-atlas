the promise of Allah

topic · 4 works · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Allah's promise, His promise, a promise of Allah, promise of Allah, the promise of my Lord

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 18: The Cave (Al-Kahf), the promise of Allah is true, including the Hour and the final events involving the barrier, as shown by the disclosure of the People of the Cave. In Surah 30: The Romans (Ar-Room), Allah's promise is true and cannot fail, and Muhammad is told to remain patient because it will come to pass. In Surah 35: The Originator (Faatir), the promise of Allah is true, and mankind is warned not to let worldly life or the beguiler deceive it regarding that promise. In Surah 39: The Groups (Az-Zumar), the promise of Allah is that those who keep their duty to Him will receive lofty halls with rivers beneath them, and Allah does not fail His promise.
