Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Keresâspa smites a poisonous dragon that swallowed men and horses, then flees when it springs from boiling water. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Keresâspa withstands destructive forces and enemies, seizes kingly Glory after it leaves Yima, and kills Srvara, Gandarewa, hostile broods, Snâvidhaka, and other enemies.

Keresâspa in Yasna

Abstract written by a model

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Yasna IX.

Keresâspa is the son of Thrita and a youth of great ascendant, ringlet-headed, and bludgeon-bearing. He smote a poisonous green horny dragon that swallowed men and horses, though he fled when the dragon sprang up from the boiling water after he cooked meat on its back.

Keresâspa in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Keresâspa first appears

Abstract written by a model

Keresâspa is a Sâma, club-bearer with plaited hair, manly-hearted and exceptionally strong. Invoked against destructive forces, he withstands a brigand and evil, seizes kingly Glory after it leaves Yima, and with Manly Courage kills Srvara, Gandarewa, hostile broods, Snâvidhaka, and other enemies.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XXVI.

Keresâspa, the Sâma and club-bearer with plaited hair, is invoked against dreadful armed forces, a destructive brigand, and a merciless man-slayer.

XIX. Zamyâd Yast.

Keresâspa seizes the Glory the third time it departs from Yima. With Manly Courage, he kills Srvara, Gandarewa, several hostile broods, and other enemies, including Snâvidhaka.
