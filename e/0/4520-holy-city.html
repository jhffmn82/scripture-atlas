Abstract written by a model

The holy city is first trodden underfoot by the nations for forty-two months. It is then shown containing the river of life and the throne of God and the Lamb, with no curse or night. Commandment-keepers enter its gates, wrongdoers stay outside, and anyone removing the book's words loses his part.
