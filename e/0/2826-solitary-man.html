Abstract written by a model

A solitary man with no son or brother labors endlessly, his eyes never satisfied by wealth. He questions for whom he toils, denying himself enjoyment; this is called vanity and misery, and he has no one to help him when he falls.

Chapter 4

The solitary man has neither son nor brother, and there is no end to his labor. His eyes are never satisfied with wealth, and he asks for whom he labors and deprives his soul of enjoyment, a miserable business and vanity. He has no one to lift him if he falls and cannot keep warm alone.
