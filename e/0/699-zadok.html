Also called: Zadok the priest, Zadok the priest and his brothers the priests, Zadok the son of Ahitub

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Samuel, Zadok is a priest, son of Ahitub and father of Ahimaaz, who brings the ark with the Levites, returns it when told, and receives Hushai's reports. In 1 Chronicles, Zadok is a priest descended from Eleazar who serves at the LORD's tabernacle, offers daily burnt offerings, helps David divide the priestly orders, and is anointed at Solomon's accession.

Abstract written by a model

Zadok, son of Ahitub and father of Ahimaaz, is a priest David calls a seer. He brings the ark with the Levites, is told to carry it back, and is named a recipient of Hushai's reports.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 15

Zadok came with the Levites bearing the ark of the covenant, but David told him to carry it back into the city. David called him a seer, told him to return in peace with his son Ahimaaz and Jonathan son of Abiathar, and designated him with Abiathar as recipient of Hushai's reports.

Zadok in 1 Chronicles

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Zadok first appears

Abstract written by a model

Zadok is a priest, son of Ahitub, descended from Eleazar. Early he is a young mighty man of valor with twenty-two captains. He serves before the LORD's tabernacle at Gibeon, offering daily burnt offerings, helps David divide the priestly orders, and is anointed at Solomon's accession.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 24

Zadok, a priest of the sons of Eleazar, helped David divide the priests into their orders. He was present when Shemaiah recorded the lots and when the other Levites cast lots.
