Abstract written by a model

Princes destroy records of the Zhou arrangement because they consider it injurious to themselves, and are compared to robbers in taking from their people. Yet they may receive properly presented gifts and should assist people, honor worthy scholars, summon appropriately, and heed repeated remonstrance from high ministers.

Part II / Chapter 1 .. Chapter 5

The princes disliked the Zhou arrangement because they considered it injurious to themselves and destroyed its records. They are also said to take from their people as a robber does, though their gifts may be received when presented with propriety.

Chapter 6 .. Chapter 9

Princes ought to assist people in their necessities and properly honor scholars of virtue and talents. They must use appropriate forms when summoning people and should listen to repeated remonstrance from high ministers.
