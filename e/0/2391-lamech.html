Lamech

Abstract written by a model

Lamech is a man appearing in two chapters. In the first, a descendant of Cain, he takes wives Adah and Zillah, fathers several children, and boasts of slaying a young man, claiming seventy-seven-fold vengeance. In the second, born to Methuselah, he fathers Noah at 182, hopes he will bring comfort from cursed toil, and dies at 777.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 4

Lamech, Cain's descendant through Enoch, Irad, Mehujael, and Methushael, takes two wives, Adah and Zillah, and is father of Jabal, Jubal, Tubal Cain, and a daughter Naamah. He tells his wives he has slain a young man for wounding him and claims that if Cain is avenged seven times, he will be avenged seventy-seven times.

Chapter 5

Lamech was born to Methuselah and fathered a son at 182, naming him Noah. He said Noah would comfort them in their work and toil caused by the ground the LORD cursed. He lived 595 more years with other sons and daughters and died at 777.
