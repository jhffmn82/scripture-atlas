Chapter 14

The story is a tale Joab put in the woman's mouth: a widow's two sons fought in the field, one killed the other, and the family demands the killer's death, which would quench her last coal and leave her husband no name or remainder. It leads the king to promise protection to the surviving son. The woman then applies it to the king's own situation with his banished son, and the king sees Joab behind it.
