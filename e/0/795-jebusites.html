Jebusites

group · 3 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Jebusite, The inhabitants of Jebus, the Jebusite, the Jebusites, the inhabitants of the land

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Joshua, the Jebusites inhabit Jerusalem; Judah cannot drive them out, and they live with the children of Judah there. In The Second Book of Samuel, the Jebusites inhabit Jerusalem and taunt David that the blind and lame will keep him out, but he takes their stronghold of Zion. In 1 Chronicles, the Jebusites inhabit the land at Jebus and tell David he will not enter, but he takes the stronghold of Zion.
