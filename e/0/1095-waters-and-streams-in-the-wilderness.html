Chapter 41

The LORD promises to open rivers on the bare heights and springs in the valleys, and to make the wilderness a pool of water and dry land springs, in answer to the poor and needy who seek water. He will plant cedar, acacia, myrtle, oil trees, cypress, pine and box in the desert, so that all may see and understand that the LORD's hand has done this and the Holy One of Israel created it.
