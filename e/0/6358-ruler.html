Abstract written by a model

A ruler honours talented and virtuous people, employs the able, and governs markets and husbandmen with limited exactions. He levies ground-rent on shops, does not tax goods, inspects frontier-passers, and does not exact further taxes, an idler’s fine, or a hamlet’s cloth quota. By truly practising five things, he is regarded as a parent, has no enemy in the kingdom, becomes Heaven’s minister, and attains royal dignity.

Chapter 5

A ruler should fill offices with distinguished individuals, limit or avoid specified taxes and charges, and require husbandmen to give mutual aid on the public field. If he practises these five things, neighbouring kingdoms will look up to him as a parent, he will have no enemy in the kingdom, and he will attain the royal dignity.
