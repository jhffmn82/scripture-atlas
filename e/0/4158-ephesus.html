Ephesus

Abstract written by a model

Ephesus is a city described as temple keeper of the great goddess Artemis and of an image said to have fallen from Zeus. Paul arrives, leaves Priscilla and Aquila, reasons in the synagogue, and promises to return. Apollos is instructed there. Paul later teaches about two years, all Asia hears the word, and Jews and Greeks magnify Jesus' name.

Chapter 18

Paul comes to Ephesus, leaves Priscilla and Aquila there, and reasons with the Jews in the synagogue. He declines their request to stay longer, saying he must keep the coming feast in Jerusalem but will return if God wills. Apollos later arrives there and is instructed more accurately by Priscilla and Aquila.

Chapter 19

Ephesus is where Paul finds the disciples and teaches for about two years, so that all in Asia hear the word. Its people, Jews and Greeks, fear and magnify the name of the Lord Jesus after the exorcists' failure. The town clerk describes the city as temple keeper of the great goddess Artemis and of the image which fell from Zeus.
