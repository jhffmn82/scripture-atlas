Abstract written by a model

The author exhorts the elders as a fellow elder, a witness of Christ's sufferings, and one who will share in the glory to be revealed. He says he wrote briefly through Silvanus, whom he considers a faithful brother, to testify that this is the true grace of God. He sends greetings from the woman in Babylon and from Mark, whom he calls his son, and he closes with a blessing of peace.
