Chapter 31

The text counsels considering one's neighbor's feelings, being discreet, eating like a human being what is set before one, being first to stop for manners' sake, and not reaching out before others. A very little suffices the well-mannered man, and moderate eating brings healthy sleep, early rising and clear wits; one forced to eat should rise in the middle of it. People bless the one liberal with food, while the city murmurs against the stingy one.
