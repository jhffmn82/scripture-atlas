Tai Gong Wang

Also called: Tai Gong

Abstract written by a model

In Mencius, Book IV (Li Lou), Tai Gong avoids Zhou and lives on the eastern sea coast, then hears of king Wen’s rise and follows him, drawn by the chief of the West’s care for the old. In Mencius, Book VII (Jin Xin), Tai Gong Wang avoids Zhou while dwelling on the eastern sea coast, then hears of king Wen’s rise and chooses to follow him, valuing the chief of the West’s care for the old.
