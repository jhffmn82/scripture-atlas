silver, gold, and vessels offering

Abstract written by a model

A holy free will offering to God for the house of God, given by the king, his counselors, his princes, and all Israel present. It consisted of 650 talents of silver, 100 talents of silver vessels, 100 talents of gold, twenty gold bowls weighing 1,000 darics, and two vessels of fine bright bronze. In Chapter 7 the king and counselors freely give silver and gold, which Ezra is to carry, along with other offerings, to buy altar offerings; treasurers beyond the River may supply more. In Chapter 8 the gifts are weighed at Ahava, guarded by priests and Levites, then weighed again in Jerusalem into Meremoth's hand, counted and recorded.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

The king and his counselors freely offered silver and gold to the God of Israel, and Ezra was to carry it along with whatever he found in the province of Babylon and the free will offerings of the people and priests. It was to buy offerings for the altar, and what remained could be used as Ezra and his brothers saw fit according to God's will. The treasurers beyond the River were also to supply up to one hundred talents of silver on Ezra's request.

Chapter 8

The offering from the king, his counselors, his princes, and all Israel present was weighed out at Ahava: 650 talents of silver, 100 talents of silver vessels, 100 talents of gold, twenty gold bowls weighing 1,000 darics, and two vessels of fine bright bronze. The priests and Levites guarded it as a holy free will offering until it was weighed in Jerusalem on the fourth day into Meremoth's hand, with everything counted and the weight written down.
