Abstract written by a model

A hostile group of adversaries who attack God and his people. They roar in the midst of his assembly, set up their standards, hack down the carved work with axes and hammers, burn and profane the sanctuary and all places of worship, resolve in their hearts to crush God's people completely, and reproach and mock his name continually, their tumult rising unceasingly.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 74

The adversaries roared in the middle of God's assembly and set up their standards as signs. They behaved like men with axes cutting through a thicket, broke down the carved work with hatchet and hammers, burned the sanctuary, and profaned the dwelling place of God's Name. They said in their heart, "We will crush them completely," and burned all the places of worship in the land. The speaker says the enemy reproaches and blasphemes God's name and that foolish people mock him all day, and asks God to remember the tumult of those who rise up against him, which ascends continually.
