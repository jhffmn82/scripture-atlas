Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the Feast of Tabernacles is a seven-day feast to the LORD beginning on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, with prescribed offerings and prohibited regular work. In The Second Book of the Maccabees, the feast of tabernacles is to be kept by the Egyptian Jews in Chislev and linked to the temple's purification and the fire remembered from Nehemiah's time.

Feast of Tabernacles in Numbers

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Feast of Tabernacles first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Feast of Tabernacles is a seven-day feast to the LORD beginning on the fifteenth day of the seventh month with a holy convocation, when regular work is prohibited. Each day requires two rams, fourteen male lambs, and one male goat as a sin offering. The bull offerings decrease daily, from thirteen on the first day down to seven on the seventh day.

Chapter 29

Beginning on the fifteenth day of the seventh month with a holy convocation and no regular work, this is a feast to the LORD lasting seven days. Each day requires two rams, fourteen lambs and a goat for a sin offering, with bulls decreasing from thirteen on the first day to seven on the seventh.
