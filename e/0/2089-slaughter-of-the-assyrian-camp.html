Chapter 19

In one night the LORD's angel strikes 185,000 in the Assyrian camp, and when men rise early in the morning they find all were dead bodies. It fulfills the promise that the Assyrian king would not take Jerusalem, and Sennacherib then departs and goes home to Nineveh.
