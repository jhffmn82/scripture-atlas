Abstract written by a model

The flesh is portrayed as a realm whose mind is death, hostile to God, unsubmissive to His law, and unable to please Him; living after it leads to death, the law was weakened through it, yet God condemned sin in it.

Chapter 8

The flesh is presented as the realm whose mind is death and is hostile toward God, not subject to God's law and unable to be. Those in the flesh cannot please God, and living after the flesh leads to death. The law was weak through the flesh, and God condemned sin in the flesh.
