Abstract written by a model

The fourteen great dreams are fourteen auspicious dreams, numbered among the thirty great dreams in dream-books and associated with mothers of universal monarchs or Arhats. They are seen by Kshatriyânî Trisalâ and include, in order, an elephant, bull, lion, Srî, flower garland, moon, sun, flag, vase, Lotus Lake, milk-ocean, celestial abode, jewel heap, and fire. They are described as illustrious, beautiful, lucky, blest, and fortunate. A parallel account says Devânandâ saw the same dreams between sleeping and waking. The dreams of Kshatriyânî Trisalâ portend the birth of a son who will become a universal emperor or a Gina.

Kalpa Sutra / Lives of the Ginas. / Life of Mahâvîra.

The fourteen great dreams were seen by Devânandâ while between sleeping and waking. They were an elephant, bull, lion, the anointing of Srî, a garland, moon, sun, flag, vase, lotus lake, ocean, celestial abode, heap of jewels, and flame.
