Abstract written by a model

Apostolic right to financial support is held by Barnabas, Paul, and Cephas, and exercised over the Corinthians by others, though Paul has it more. Paul defends it with examples of soldiers, planters, shepherds, the ox in Moses' law, and temple servants, and the Lord's ordinance, yet he and Barnabas did not use it.

Chapter 9

Paul argues that apostles have the right to eat and drink, to take a believing wife, and to refrain from other work, supported by the examples of soldiers, vineyard planters, shepherds, the ox in the law of Moses, and temple servants. He says the Lord ordained that proclaimers of the Good News live from it. Paul says he and Barnabas nevertheless did not use this right, and that he is not writing to ask for it in his own case.
