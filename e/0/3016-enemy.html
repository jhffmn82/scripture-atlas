Abstract written by a model

The enemy is a deceitful figure whose wickedness is like corrosion in copper. He is not hidden in adversity, where he waits, grieves at prosperity, speaks sweetly and weeps while plotting a pit, seeks blood given opportunity, feigns help to trip the reader, and mocks. He should be kept away, lest he take the reader's place.

Chapter 12

The enemy is portrayed as deceitful: his wickedness is like corrosion in copper, and he speaks sweetly and weeps while planning to throw the reader into a pit. He is found waiting in adversity, pretends to help in order to trip the reader, and shakes his head, claps his hands, whispers, and changes his countenance. He should not be set near the reader or on the right hand, lest he take the reader's place.
