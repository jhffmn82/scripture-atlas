great works and possessions (the narrator's)

Abstract written by a model

The narrator's great works and possessions: houses, vineyards, gardens, pools, servants, herds, silver, gold, singers and instruments, surpassing all before him in Jerusalem. Reviewing them, he finds everything vanity with no profit under the sun, and hates his labor because he must leave it to his successor.

Chapter 2

The narrator makes houses, vineyards, gardens, parks, fruit trees, and pools, and acquires servants, herds, flocks, silver, gold, the treasure of kings, singers, and musical instruments, surpassing all before him in Jerusalem. On reviewing them he finds all vanity with no profit under the sun. He hates the labor behind them because he must leave it to the man who comes after him.
