the handover of the empire

event · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: handed over the empire, offered to resign, offered to resign in favour of, offered to resign the empire, resign the empire, resignation of the empire

Abstract written by a model

Resignation of the empire is repeatedly offered by Yao, Shun, and T'ang but is declined by people who prioritize preservation of life, inner virtue, or adherence to TAO. The refusals show that ruling is inferior to maintaining one's life and principles.
