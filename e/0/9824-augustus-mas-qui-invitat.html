Augustus Mas-Qui-Invitat

Also called: Mas-Qui-Invitat

Abstract written by a model

A male deity descended from Heaven on an island, with a younger sister and heavenly structures. He proposes procreation, directs their ritual circling, reproves her speech, and helps produce Hirudinem and the Island of Aha.

Sect.

Augustus Mas-Qui-Invitat asks Augusta Femina-Qui-Invitat about her body and proposes inserting his superfluous part into her undeveloped part to procreate regions. He directs their circling of the heavenly august pillar, reproves Augusta Femina-Qui-Invitat for speaking first, and helps produce Hirudinem and the Island of Aha.
