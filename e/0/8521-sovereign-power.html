Sovereign Power

Also called: His Sovereign Power, Kingdom of (Thy Good Mind), Kingdom of Welfare, Power, Sovereign Power (Ahura Mazda's power), That Sovereign Power, Thy (holy) kingdom, Thy Kingdom, Thy Kingdom (are 'helps to our progress'), Thy Sovereign Power, Thy nature, ruling power, the power of Thine attributes, this established Sovereign Power

Abstract written by a model

Sovereign Power is a beneficent spiritual power and gift given by Ahura Mazda through holy deeds, words, and sacrifice. It holds priority among Yazads and serves as rule and defense for the faithful. Invoked with Good Mind and Sanctity for salvation, it is sought to grant Zarathustra strength, establish welfare with Righteous Order and Âramaiti, preserve desired wealth, and provide the best rule and defense.

Sovereign Power is in one work on this site, Yasna. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XXXIII.

Sovereign Power is invoked with the Good Mind and Sanctity as a means of salvation and is asked to grant Zarathustra power and strength.

Yasna XXXIV.

The Kingdom of Welfare is one of the gifts given by Ahura Mazda through holy deeds, words, and sacrifice. Ahura Mazda’s Kingdom is sought as the sphere in which the Righteous Order, Good Mind, and Âramaiti establish welfare.

Yasna XXXVII.

Sovereign Power is associated with Ahura Mazda and is worshipped for its beneficence and greatness. It is also worshipped as belonging to the Lord.

Yasna LI.

Sovereign Power is sought as the best rule and defense, established through Good Mind. It is requested for the preservation of desired wealth and is attributed to Kavi Vîstâspa's gift.
