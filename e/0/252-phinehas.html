Phinehas

person · 3 works · 11 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Phinehas the priest, Phinehas the son of Eleazar, Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest

Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, Phinehas spears Zimri and Cozbi, ending the plague, and receives a covenant of peace and everlasting priesthood for his offspring. In The Book of Joshua, Phinehas leads ten princes to Gilead to accuse the eastern tribes of rebellion, accepts their explanation, and reports back to Israel. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, Phinehas is honored for his zeal, atoning for Israel and receiving leadership of the sanctuary with priesthood forever for his offspring.

Phinehas in Numbers

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Phinehas first appears

Abstract written by a model

Phinehas, priest Eleazar's son and Aaron's grandson, stands from the congregation and spears Zimri and Cozbi, ending the plague. The LORD, citing his jealousy, credits him with atoning for Israel and grants him a covenant of peace and everlasting priesthood for his offspring.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 25

Phinehas, son of Eleazar and grandson of Aaron the priest, rose from the congregation, took a spear, and thrust through Zimri and Cozbi in the pavilion, stopping the plague. The LORD said he was jealous with his jealousy and made atonement for Israel, and gave him a covenant of peace and an everlasting priesthood for him and his offspring.

Phinehas in The Book of Joshua

Abstract written by a model

Phinehas, a priest and son of Eleazar, holds a hill in Ephraim's hill country. He leads ten princes to Gilead to accuse the eastern tribes of rebellion, is satisfied by their explanation, affirms the LORD is among them, and reports back to Israel.

Chapter 22

Phinehas son of Eleazar the priest leads the delegation of ten princes to Gilead to charge the eastern tribes with rebellion. After hearing their explanation he is pleased and declares that the LORD is among them and that they have delivered Israel from the LORD's hand. He returns to Canaan and reports to the children of Israel.

Abstract written by a model

Phinehas, son of Eleazar, is honored as third in glory for zeal in the fear of the Lord. He stood fast when the people turned away and atoned for Israel, so a covenant of peace made him leader of the sanctuary, with priesthood forever for his offspring.

Chapter 45

Phinehas the son of Eleazar is named third in glory, because he was zealous in the fear of the Lord, stood fast when the people turned away, and made atonement for Israel.
