Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Samuel, it is the LORD's angry appearance in a storm that scatters David's enemies and delivers him from many waters. In Psalms, it is the LORD's descent amid a storm that reveals the channels of waters and the foundations of the world.

Abstract written by a model

In answer to David's cry, the LORD appears in anger amid a storm: earth shakes, fire and smoke issue from him, he rides a cherub on the wind and thunders; arrows and lightning scatter enemies, and he draws David from many waters.

Chapter 22

In response to David's cry, the LORD appears in a storm: the earth shakes, smoke and consuming fire come from his mouth, he rides a cherub on the wind, and he thunders from heaven. His arrows and lightning scatter enemies, the sea's channels and the world's foundations are laid bare at his rebuke, and he draws David out of many waters.
