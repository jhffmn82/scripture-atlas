Abstract written by a model

Walking is a disciplined monk’s practice, pure in cause, time, road, and effort: grounded in knowledge, faith, and right conduct; done by day; avoiding bad ways and sense objects; and maintained through attentive focus on each step, body, and path.

Twenty-Fourth Lecture.

Walking should be pure in respect to cause, time, road, and effort. A monk should look with his eyes, attend to the space of a yuga, remain attentive as long as he walks, and focus on his walk and body while avoiding the objects of sense.
