Chapter 7

The unit promises a day to build walls and extend boundaries, when people will come from Assyria, the cities of Egypt, the River, and from sea to sea and mountain to mountain. It also expects the LORD to show marvelous things as in the exodus from Egypt, with the nations ashamed, silenced, and coming trembling to the LORD in fear.
