Chapter 36

The scroll records all the LORD's words against Israel, Judah, and the nations, written by Baruch from Jeremiah's mouth. It is read publicly, read to the princes, stored in Elishama's room, then read to the king and burned. A second scroll replaces it, with the same words plus many similar ones.
