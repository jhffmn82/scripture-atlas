Bairagis

group · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Bairagi

Abstract written by a model

Bairagis are devotees defined not by outward identity but by abandoning desires, treasuring the Word, fearing God, serving the Guru, and loving God alone. They may remain at home or beg alms to fix love on God, becoming perfect, content with the Name, and absorbed in divine pleasure.

Bairagis is in one work on this site, Hymns of Guru Nanak. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Rag Sorath .. Sorath Ashtapadi

A Bairagi is not merely one who adopts an outward identity but one who abandons desire, treasures the Word, fears God, serves the Guru, and remains absorbed in divine pleasure. The perfect Bairagi is pleasing to God, content with the Name, and naturally fortunate.
