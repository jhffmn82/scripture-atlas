judgment on Tyre and Sidon

Abstract written by a model

The LORD pronounces judgment on Tyre's prince and king through foreign swords, death, a devouring fire, and ashes, and on Sidon through pestilence, bloodshed, and the sword. The purpose stated is that they will know he is the LORD and that he is sanctified, and the judgments also end the scorn Israel suffered from those around them.
