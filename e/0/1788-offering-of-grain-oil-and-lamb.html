Chapter 45

The people of the land are to give a sixth of an ephah from a homer of wheat and of barley, a tenth of a bath of oil from the cor, and one lamb from every two hundred of the flock. These serve for meal, burnt, and peace offerings to make atonement, and are given for the prince.
