Abstract written by a model

False teachers are portrayed as covetous deceivers who secretly introduce destructive heresies, deny the Master, and exploit readers with deceptive words. They despise authority, slander dignitaries, follow Balaam's way, and entice people escaping error, promising liberty while enslaved to corruption. Their immoral ways cause the way of truth to be maligned. Their destruction is swift and certain, and the blackness of darkness is reserved for them forever.

Chapter 2

False teachers will secretly bring in destructive heresies, deny the Master who bought them, and exploit people in covetousness with deceptive words, leading many to follow their immoral ways. They are described as daring, self-willed, despising authority, reveling in deceit, and following the way of Balaam, and they promise liberty while being bondservants of corruption. Their destruction is certain, and the blackness of darkness is reserved for them, with their last state worse than the first, like a dog returning to its vomit.
