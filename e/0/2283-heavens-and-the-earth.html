Abstract written by a model

The heavens and the earth are an account of creation by God. It begins with a formless, empty, dark earth under God's Spirit, then is declared finished with its vast array. Before plants or man existed, a mist watered the ground; the LORD God formed man, trees, animals, and woman.

The heavens, the earth, and all their vast array are described as finished, and the text calls the account the history of the generations of the heavens and the earth when they were created. Before plants appeared and before there was a man to till the ground, a mist watered the ground, and the LORD God then formed man, the garden's trees, the animals, and the woman.
