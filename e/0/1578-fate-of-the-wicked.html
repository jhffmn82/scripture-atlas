fate of the wicked

Abstract written by a model

In Job 27, Job describes the wicked's fate: God cuts off the godless without hope and may ignore their cry. Their children die by sword and hunger, survivors go unmourned, wealth passes to the just, and terrors, storm, and the east wind sweep them away while men clap and hiss.

Chapter 27

Job says the godless have no hope when God cuts them off and takes their life, and God may not hear their cry or delight them. Their children face the sword and hunger, their survivors are buried in death without widows' lament, and their wealth passes to the just and innocent. They are swept away suddenly by terrors, a storm, and the east wind, and men clap and hiss at them.
