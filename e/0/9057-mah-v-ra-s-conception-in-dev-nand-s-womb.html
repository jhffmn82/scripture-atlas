Third Part. / Fifteenth Lecture, Called the Clauses.

After descending from heaven, Mahâvîra took the form of an embryo in the womb of Devânandâ, wife of the Brâhmana Rishabhadatta. He knew that he was to descend and that he had descended, but not the infinitesimally small moment when he was descending.
