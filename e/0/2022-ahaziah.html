Ahaziah in The Second Book of Kings

author unknown · 560 to 539 BC · the work’s page · read it where Ahaziah first appears

Abstract written by a model

Ahaziah, son of Jehoram and Athaliah and son-in-law of Ahab's house, became king of Judah at twenty-two and reigned one year in Jerusalem, doing evil in Ahab's house's way. He joined Joram against Hazael at Ramoth Gilead, then visited wounded Joram in Jezreel. When Jehu came, he fled, was struck in his chariot, died at Megiddo, and was buried in David's city, Jerusalem.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 8

Ahaziah, son of Jehoram and Athaliah, becomes king of Judah in the twelfth year of Joram of Israel at age twenty-two and reigns one year in Jerusalem. He walks in the way of Ahab's house and does evil, goes with Joram to war against Hazael at Ramoth Gilead, and visits the wounded Joram in Jezreel.

Chapter 9

Ahaziah king of Judah goes to Jezreel to see Joram and rides out with him to meet Jehu. He flees by the garden house, is struck in his chariot at the ascent of Gur by Ibleam, and dies at Megiddo. His servants carry him to Jerusalem and bury him in David's city.

Ahaziah the son of Jehoram king of Judah in The Second Book of Chronicles

author unknown · about 424 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Ahaziah the son of Jehoram king of Judah first appears

Abstract written by a model

Ahaziah, youngest son of Jehoram and Athaliah, became king of Judah at forty-two and reigned one year in Jerusalem. Guided by his mother and the house of Ahab, he did evil in the LORD's sight. He joined Jehoram of Israel against Hazael and visited the wounded Jehoram in Jezreel. Caught hiding in Samaria, he was killed by Jehu and buried as Jehoshaphat's son.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 22

Ahaziah was made king by the inhabitants of Jerusalem at forty-two years old and reigned one year, walking in the evil ways of Ahab's house under his mother's counsel. He joined Jehoram of Israel against Hazael of Syria, visited the wounded Jehoram in Jezreel, and his destruction was of God. He was caught hiding in Samaria, brought to Jehu, killed, and buried because he was the son of Jehoshaphat.
