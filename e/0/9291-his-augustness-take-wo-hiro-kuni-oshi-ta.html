His Augustness Take-wo-hiro-kuni-oshi-tate

person · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: Emperor Sen-Kuwa, this Heavenly Sovereign

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Take-wo-hiro-kuni-oshi-tate, Emperor Sen-Kuwa, child of Emperor Kei-Tai, dwelt at Ihorinu in Hinokuma and ruled the Empire. He had two wives and five august children, three by Tachi-bana-no-naka-tsu-hime and two by Kafuchi-no-waku-go-hime.

His Augustness Take-wo-hiro-kuni-oshi-tate is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
