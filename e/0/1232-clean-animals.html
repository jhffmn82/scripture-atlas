Abstract written by a model

Clean animals are those with split hooves that chew the cud, such as ox, sheep, goat, and deer; the records also permit water creatures with fins and scales, and all clean birds, as food.

Chapter 14

Clean animals may be eaten: those that part the hoof, have it split in two, and chew the cud, such as the ox, sheep, goat, deer, gazelle, roebuck, wild goat, ibex, antelope, and chamois. Of the waters, whatever has fins and scales is allowed, and all clean birds may be eaten.
