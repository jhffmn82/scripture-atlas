Chapter 14

Chedorlaomer, king of Elam, was served for twelve years by the five kings, who rebelled in the thirteenth year. In the fourteenth year he and his allied kings struck the Rephaim, Zuzim, Emim, Horites, Amalekites, and Amorites, then defeated the five kings in the valley of Siddim. He and the kings with him were afterward slaughtered by Abram's pursuit.
