Abstract written by a model

The Vêdas chiefly concern agnihôtra; mere knowledge neither makes a true Brâhmana nor grants liberation, and reliance on sacrifices causes sin.

Twenty-Fifth Lecture.

The most essential subject in the Vêdas is said to be the agnihôtra. Gayaghôsha also teaches that mere knowledge of the Vêdas does not make a person a true Brâhmana or provide liberation.
