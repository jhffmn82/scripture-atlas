Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Prayer for blessings is a timely, good, pious, and beneficent prayer offered with sanctity among sacred sacrifices. In Yasna, the good and pious Prayer for blessings is sent between heaven and earth in liturgies for blessings and to encounter and displace evil powers and beings. In Visparad, the good and pious prayer for blessings is associated with offered Haomas and Zaothras and included in ritual praise and celebration.

Prayer for blessings in Yasna

Abstract written by a model

Prayer for blessings is a timely, good, pious, and beneficent prayer offered with sanctity among sacred sacrifices. In the due course of ritual, it approaches as an offering and is presented as the prayer of the holy lord of ritual order.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna VII.

The Prayer for blessings is described as good and pious and is offered with sanctity. It is included among the sacred things sacrificed to, along with the Ahuna-vairya and other offerings.

Yasna XXIV.

The timely prayer for blessings approaches in the due course of the ritual and is presented as an offering. It is also described as the prayer of the holy lord of the ritual order.

good and pious Prayer for blessings in Visparad

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where good and pious Prayer for blessings first appears

Abstract written by a model

A good, pious prayer for blessings and benediction, associated with offered Haomas and Zaothras. Included in ritual praise and celebration, it is uttered at a fitting moment, following the order of prayers and linked to victory, sanctification, and souls’ well-being.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Visparad IX (Sp. X).

The pious and beneficent Prayer for blessings is associated with offered Haomas and Zaothras. A well-timed prayer for blessings is also included in the ritual praise.

Visparad XI (Sp. XII).

The prayer for blessings is uttered at the fitting and well-timed moment and is presented in the celebration. It is associated with the order of the prayers, victory, sanctification, and the well-being of souls.
