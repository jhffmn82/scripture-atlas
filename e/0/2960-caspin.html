Caspin was a strong, walled, fortified city with a mixed population beside a two-furlong lake. Its people trusted their defenses and blasphemed against Judas's company; Judas invoked God and captured it in a slaughter that made the lake seem bloody.

Chapter 12

Caspin was a strong city with earthworks and walls and a mixed population. Its people trusted their walls and provisions and blasphemed against Judas's company. Judas called on God, who had brought down Jericho, and took the city with unspeakable slaughter that made the adjoining lake seem filled with blood.
