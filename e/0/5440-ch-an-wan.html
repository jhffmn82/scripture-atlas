Abstract written by a model

Ch'an Wan owns forty horses but abandons them and leaves after Ch'ui kills Ch'i's prince. In two other States, he sees people like Ch'ui and leaves again. The Master assigns him purity, while not judging him perfectly virtuous.

Ch'an Wan owned forty horses but abandoned them and left the country after Ch'ui killed the prince of Ch'i. In two other States, he made the same comparison to Ch'ui and left, and the Master judged him pure but not known to be perfectly virtuous.
