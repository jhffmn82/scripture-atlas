Chapter 16

Ptolemy son of Abubus was governor of the plain of Jericho, rich in silver and gold, and son-in-law of the high priest. His heart was lifted up and he planned to master the country, so he deceitfully received Simon and his sons at Dok, and killed Simon and two sons at a banquet. He then wrote to the king for forces, sent men to kill John at Gazara, summoned the captains of thousands with promises of gifts, and sent others to take Jerusalem and the temple hill; the text calls his deed a great iniquity repaying evil for good.
