Chapter 13

Tamar, Absalom's beautiful sister, is sent by David to Amnon's house to make cakes for him. She refuses Amnon's advances, warns of shame and folly, and suggests he ask the king, but he overpowers her and rapes her. After he casts her out and she pleads against it, she puts ashes on her head, tears her robe, and cries aloud, then remains desolate in Absalom's house.
