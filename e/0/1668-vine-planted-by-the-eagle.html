Chapter 17

The vine grows from seed planted by the eagle beside many waters, becoming a spreading vine of low stature whose branches turn toward the first eagle. It then bends its roots and branches toward the second eagle, and the LORD asks whether it will prosper, saying it will be pulled up and wither utterly when the east wind touches it.
