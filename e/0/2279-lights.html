Abstract written by a model

Lights are celestial bodies God makes on the fourth day, along with the stars. Set in the sky's expanse, they give light to earth, mark seasons, days and years, and divide light from darkness; the greater rules day, the lesser night.

On the fourth day God makes the two great lights, the greater to rule the day and the lesser to rule the night, and also the stars. He sets them in the expanse of the sky to give light to the earth, mark seasons, days and years, and divide light from darkness.
