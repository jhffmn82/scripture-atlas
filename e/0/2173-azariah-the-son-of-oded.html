Chapter 15

Azariah, on whom the Spirit of God came, went out to meet Asa and addressed him and all Judah and Benjamin. He told them the LORD is with those who are with him and can be found by those who seek him, but forsakes those who forsake him, and urged Asa to be strong because his work would be rewarded.
