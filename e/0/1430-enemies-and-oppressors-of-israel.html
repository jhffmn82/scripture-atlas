will_become plunder and prey [reversal of their fortunes] Strangers who have made Israel bondservants will do so no more, as the LORD breaks their yoke. All who devour, plunder, and prey on Israel will be devoured, plundered, and made prey, and all adversaries will go into captivity. The LORD says he will punish all who oppress them and make a full end of the nations where he scattered Israel, and his wrath will burst on the head of the wicked.
