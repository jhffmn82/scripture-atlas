Abstract written by a model

The authors of the letter are a group of witnesses who heard, saw, and touched the Word of life. They have fellowship with God, his Son, and Jesus Christ, declare what they witnessed to readers so they may share fellowship, and include themselves in statements on sin.

1 John / Chapter 1

The authors testify to having heard, seen, and touched the Word of life, and they declare it to the readers. They write so that the readers may have fellowship with them and so that their own joy may be fulfilled. They include themselves in the statements about sin, saying 'we' when speaking of deception and confession.
