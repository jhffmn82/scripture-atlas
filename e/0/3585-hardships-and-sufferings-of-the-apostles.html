Abstract written by a model

The ministry's sufferings are listed as great endurance, afflictions, hardships, distresses, beatings, imprisonments, riots, labors, watchings, and fastings. They are part of how the apostles commend themselves as servants of God, alongside glory and dishonor, evil and good report, and being punished yet not killed. They are paired with paradoxes of sorrow yet rejoicing and poverty yet making many rich.
