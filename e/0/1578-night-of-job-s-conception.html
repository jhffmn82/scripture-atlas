night of Job's conception

Abstract written by a model

The night Job was conceived, announced as a boy's conception, is cursed by Job in Chapter 3: he wishes it seized by darkness and barren, blaming it for not shutting his mother's womb or hiding trouble.

Chapter 3

Job curses this night, asking that thick darkness seize it, that it not be counted among the days or months, and that it be barren with no joyful voice. He blames it for not shutting the doors of his mother's womb and not hiding trouble from his eyes.
