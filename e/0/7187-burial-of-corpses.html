Abstract written by a model

Burial of a dog’s or man’s corpse requires disinterment within half a year or increasing stripes; if left into the second year, professors or students of Mazda’s law receive no payment, atonement, or cleansing.

Fargard III.

A person who buries the corpse of a dog or a man must disinter it within the stated period or receive increasing stripes. Burial left until the second year has no payment, atonement, or cleansing when committed by a professor or student of the law of Mazda.
