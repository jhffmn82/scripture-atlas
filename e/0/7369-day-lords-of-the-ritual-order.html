Day-lords of the ritual order

Also called: Day-lords, day-lords, the Day-lords, the day-lords, the lords of the days during their duration, those of the days during daylight

Abstract written by a model

Day-lords are holy lords approached in worship, governing the day and receiving offerings; the ritual order unfolds through Hâvani, Sâvanghi, Vîsya, Rapithwina, Uzayêirina, Aiwisrûthrima, and Ushahina.

Yasna IV.

The Day-lords of the ritual order receive offerings for sacrifice, homage, propitiation, and praise. They include Hâvani, Sâvanghi, Vîsya, Rapithwina, Uzayêirina, Aiwisrûthrima, and Ushahina.
