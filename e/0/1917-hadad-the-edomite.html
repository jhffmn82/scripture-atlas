Abstract written by a model

Hadad, an Edomite royal offspring, fled to Egypt as a child after Joab struck Edom's males. Favored by Pharaoh, he married Tahpenes's sister and fathered Genubath. Hearing David and Joab were dead, he sought to return home, and the LORD raised him as Solomon's adversary.

Chapter 11

Hadad, of the king's offspring in Edom, fled to Egypt as a child after Joab struck every male in Edom. He found favor with Pharaoh, married the sister of Tahpenes the queen, and had a son, Genubath. After hearing that David and Joab were dead, he asked to return to his own country, and the LORD raised him up as an adversary to Solomon.
