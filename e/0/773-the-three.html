the three

group · 2 works · 0 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: The three mighty men, Three, Three of the thirty chief men, the two, three mighty men

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Samuel, three of the thirty chief men come to David at the cave of Adullam, break through the Philistine army, draw water from the well of Bethlehem, and bring it to David, who pours it out to the LORD; Eleazar is named among the three, Abishai is their chief but is not counted as one of them, and Benaiah does not attain to the three. In 1 Chronicles, the three mighty men break through the Philistine army to draw water from the well of Bethlehem and bring it to David, who will not drink it; Abishai is chief of the three but not included among them, and Benaiah gains a name among them but does not attain to them, while Jashobeam and Eleazar are named as among the leading mighty men.
