Chapter 5

The person who sins becomes guilty by failing to testify, touching uncleanness unknowingly, swearing rashly, or trespassing unwittingly against holy things or commands. He must confess, bring an offering suited to his means, and make restitution where holy things are involved. Through the priest's atonement he is forgiven.
