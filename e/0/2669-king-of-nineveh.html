Chapter 3

The king of Nineveh hears the news, leaves his throne, takes off his royal robe, covers himself with sackcloth, and sits in ashes. With his nobles he decrees a fast for all people and animals, calls for crying mightily to God and turning from evil and violence, and voices hope that God may relent.
