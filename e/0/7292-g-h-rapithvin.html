Gâh Rapithvin

event · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Rapithwina, Rapithwina, the holy lord of the ritual order, Rapithwina-ratu

Abstract written by a model

Gâh Rapithvin is a holy lord and ritual time within the ritual order, associated with Frâdat-fshu and Zantuma. It includes five Gâthas, each holy and ruling in that order. Its blessing and office unfold through ritual acts with washed hands, Baresman, Haoma, fire, and spoken Ahuna-vairya, binding tongue and body to Mãthra. It is devoted to Rapithwina, associated lords, Asha Vahista, and Ahura Mazda's Fire, while invoking Gâthas, words, waters, lands, plants, Fravashis, and the Bountiful Immortals' assembly.

Gâh Rapithvin is in one work on this site, Afrinagan, Gahs and Miscellaneous Fragments. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Rapithwina is the ritual time and holy lord of the ritual order named in the text. Its blessing and office are recited with washed hands and mortars, spread Baresman, lifted Haoma, flaming fire, loudly intoned Ahuna-vairya, a Haoma-moistened tongue, and a Mãthra-bound body.

The Gâhs. / I. The Gâh Hâvan. .. V. Gâh Ushahin.

Gâh Rapithvin is devoted to Rapithwina, Frâdat-fshu, Zantuma, Asha Vahista, and Ahura Mazda's Fire. It invokes the Gâthas, correctly spoken words, waters, lands, plants, Fravashis, and the assembly held by the Bountiful Immortals.
