The ruler cultivates character through duty, serves parents, acquires knowledge of men, and knows Heaven. As sovereign, he governs kingdom, states, and families by nine standard rules, honoring virtue, treating people kindly, and cherishing princes.

Chapter 20

The ruler must cultivate his own character, serve his parents, acquire knowledge of men, and know Heaven. He governs through the nine standard rules, including honoring virtue, treating the people kindly, and cherishing the princes of the states.
