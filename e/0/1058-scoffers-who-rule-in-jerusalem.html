Abstract written by a model

Scoffers ruling in Jerusalem claim a covenant with death and agreement with Sheol, taking refuge in lies. They are told to hear the LORD, who says hail and waters will sweep away their refuge, annul their covenant, and warns against scoffing lest their bonds tighten.
