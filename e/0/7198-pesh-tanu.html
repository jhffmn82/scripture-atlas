penalty · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: a Peshôtanu

Abstract written by a model

In Vendidad (Vendîdâd), Fargards I-VIII, Peshôtanu is the status assigned when specified assaults are repeated without atonement or when the offender refuses to atone, carrying two hundred stripes with the Aspahê-astra and two hundred with the Sraoshô-karana. In Vendidad (Vendîdâd), Peshôtanu is a penalty status arising from five specified unconfessed, unatoned sins and resulting in two hundred stripes with the Aspahê-astra.
