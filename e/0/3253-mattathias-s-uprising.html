Chapter 2

The uprising begins when Mattathias kills the apostate and the king's officer at Modin and flees with his sons. After the Sabbath massacre, they resolve to fight on the Sabbath, are joined by the Hasidaeans and fugitives, and raise an army that strikes sinners and lawless men and pulls down altars. Their work prospers, and the sinner is never allowed to triumph.
