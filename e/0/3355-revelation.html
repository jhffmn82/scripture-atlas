Chapter 10

In the third year of Cyrus king of Persia, a revelation is revealed to Daniel, described as true and as a great warfare. Daniel understands it and has understanding of the vision. The man clothed in linen says he has come to make Daniel understand what will happen to his people in the latter days, and that the vision is yet for many days.
