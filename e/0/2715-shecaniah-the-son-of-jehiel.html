Chapter 10

Shecaniah, one of the sons of Elam, answers Ezra by admitting that the people have trespassed by marrying foreign women, yet says there is hope for Israel. He proposes a covenant to put away all the wives and their children according to the law, and urges Ezra to arise and act, promising the people's support.
