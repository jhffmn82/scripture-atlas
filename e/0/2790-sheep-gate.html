Abstract written by a model

The sheep gate is a gate of Jerusalem, built by Eliashib the high priest and his fellow priests, who sanctified it as far as the towers of Hammeah and Hananel and set up its doors. It marks where the repair account closes, with goldsmiths and merchants working nearby. Later, Nehemiah ordered gates shut over the Sabbath, and Levites were set to guard them.

Chapter 3

The sheep gate was built by Eliashib and the priests, who sanctified it and set up its doors. It is also where the chapter's account of repairs closes, with the goldsmiths and merchants working between the ascent of the corner and the sheep gate.

Chapter 13

Nehemiah commanded that the gates be shut as they grew dark before the Sabbath and not opened until it ended, with his servants set over them. Later the Levites, after purifying themselves, were commanded to keep the gates.
