rain and clouds (God's weather) in The Book of Job

author unknown · about 574 BC · the work’s page · read it where rain and clouds (God's weather) first appears

Abstract written by a model

God draws up the drops of water, which distill as rain from his vapor, and the skies pour it down abundantly on man. No one can understand the spreading of the clouds or the thunderings of his pavilion. By these means he judges the people and gives food in abundance, and the noise of the storm tells about him, as the livestock also do.

The LORD covers the sky with clouds, prepares rain for the earth, and makes grass grow on the mountains. He gives snow like wool, scatters frost like ashes, and hurls down hail like pebbles, so that no one can stand before his cold.
