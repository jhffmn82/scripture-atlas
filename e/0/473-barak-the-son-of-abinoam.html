Chapter 4

Barak son of Abinoam of Kedesh Naphtali was summoned by Deborah and told to lead ten thousand men to Mount Tabor. He would go only if she went with him, gathered Zebulun and Naphtali, and went down from Tabor to pursue Sisera's army to Harosheth of the Gentiles. When he reached Jael's tent, he found Sisera already dead.

Chapter 5

Barak the son of Abinoam joins Deborah in singing on that day. He is told to arise and lead away his captives, and Issachar is described as being with him as they rushed into the valley at his feet.
