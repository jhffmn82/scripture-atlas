Abstract written by a model

Abimelech is the king of Gerar, with Phicol as his army captain. He takes Sarah, is warned by God in a dream, and pleads the integrity of his heart. He restores her, rebukes Abraham, and gives him livestock, servants, and silver. After Abraham prays, God heals his household. Later he and Phicol seek Abraham's oath, and they make a covenant before he returns to the Philistines' land.

Chapter 20

Abimelech, king of Gerar, takes Sarah, is warned by God in a dream, and pleads that he acted in the integrity of his heart. He obeys by restoring Sarah, rebukes Abraham, gives him sheep, cattle, servants, a thousand pieces of silver, and the freedom to dwell in his land. After Abraham prays, God heals him, his wife, and his female servants, and they bear children.

Chapter 21

Abimelech, with Phicol the captain of his army, asks Abraham to swear not to deal falsely with him or his descendants, noting that God is with Abraham. He says he did not know who seized the well, and makes a covenant with Abraham before returning to the land of the Philistines.
