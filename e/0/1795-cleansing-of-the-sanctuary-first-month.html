cleansing of the sanctuary (first month)

event · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: cleanse the sanctuary, make atonement for the house

Abstract written by a model

A ritual to cleanse the sanctuary, held on the first day of the first month. A priest takes a young bull without defect and puts its sin-offering blood on the house's door posts, the altar ledge's four corners, and the inner court gate posts. The rite is repeated on the seventh day for those who err or are simple, making atonement for the house.

Chapter 45

On the first day of the first month a young bull without defect is taken to cleanse the sanctuary. The priest puts its blood on the house's door posts, the altar ledge's four corners, and the inner court gate posts, and the rite is repeated on the seventh day for those who err or are simple, to make atonement for the house.
