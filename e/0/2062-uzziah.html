Azariah in The Second Book of Kings

author unknown · 560 to 539 BC · the work’s page · read it where Azariah first appears

Abstract written by a model

Azariah, son of Amaziah and Jecoliah of Jerusalem, was made king of Judah at sixteen and reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem. He built Elath and restored it to Judah and did right in the LORD's eyes. Struck with leprosy until death, he lived apart while his son Jotham managed the household and succeeded him; he was buried in the City of David.

Azariah son of Amaziah began to reign in Judah at sixteen and reigned fifty-two years in Jerusalem. He did right in the LORD's eyes but did not remove the high places. Uzziah in The Second Book of Chronicles

author unknown · about 424 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Uzziah first appears

Abstract written by a model

Uzziah is a king of Judah, son of Amaziah and Jechiliah of Jerusalem, who becomes king at sixteen and reigns fifty-two years. He does right in the LORD's eyes and prospers while seeking God, winning military victories, receiving Ammonite tribute, rebuilding Eloth, fortifying Jerusalem, farming extensively, and equipping a large army. His fame reaches Egypt. Once strong, his heart is lifted up; he enters the temple to burn incense, angers at the priests' rebuke, and is struck with leprosy. He lives in a separate house, cut off from the temple, until death. Buried in the kings' burial field, he is succeeded by Jotham; Isaiah chronicles his reign.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 26

Uzziah is made king at sixteen by the people of Judah in place of his father Amaziah, reigns fifty-two years, and prospers while he seeks God, becoming strong through military victories, fortifications, farming and a large equipped army. When strong, his heart is lifted up and he enters the temple to burn incense, becomes angry at the priests' rebuke, and is struck with leprosy. He lives in a separate house, cut off from the LORD's house, until his death, and is buried with his fathers in the kings' burial field, with Jotham reigning in his place.
