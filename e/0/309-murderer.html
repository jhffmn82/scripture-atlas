murderer

Also called: The murderer, a murderer, any man, he who struck him, intentional murderer

Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the murderer is one who kills with an iron, stone, or wooden weapon, or through hatred, ambush, or hostile striking, and must be executed by the avenger of blood on multiple witnesses' testimony, with no ransom sparing him. In Deuteronomy, the intentional murderer hates his neighbor, lies in wait, strikes him mortally, and is handed by his city's elders to the avenger of blood to die, without protection from a city of refuge or pity.

Abstract written by a model

A murderer is one who kills with an iron, stone, or wooden weapon, or through hatred, ambush, or hostile striking. He must be executed by the avenger of blood on the testimony of multiple witnesses, and no ransom may spare him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 35

A murderer is one who kills with an iron instrument, a stone, or a wooden weapon, or by shoving out of hatred, lying in wait, or hostile striking. He must surely be put to death by the avenger of blood, on the testimony of witnesses, and no ransom may be taken for his life.
