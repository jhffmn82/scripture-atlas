census ransom

Abstract written by a model

The census ransom is a half-shekel payment, by the sanctuary shekel, made by every man aged twenty and up when the people are counted. Rich and poor pay equally. It atones for souls, prevents plague, and is appointed for the Tent of Meeting's service as a memorial.

Chapter 30

When the people are counted, each man aged twenty and up gives half a shekel by the sanctuary shekel (twenty gerahs) as a ransom for his soul, so that no plague comes. The rich do not give more or the poor less. The money is appointed for the service of the Tent of Meeting as a memorial and atonement.
