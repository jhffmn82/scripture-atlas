Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, Sodom is a wicked city whose inhabitants and king are involved in rebellion, Lot's capture and rescue, demands against Lot's guests, and its destruction by sulfur and fire. In The Book of Ezekiel, Sodom is Jerusalem's younger sister, removed for pride, prosperity, neglect of the poor, arrogance, and abomination, but later destined for restoration.

Sodom in Genesis

author unknown · about 449 BC · the work’s page · read it where Sodom first appears

Abstract written by a model

Sodom is a city of the plain in the valley of Siddim, whose inhabitants are described as exceedingly wicked sinners against the LORD. Lot moves his tent there. Its king, with other kings, rebelled against Chedorlaomer and was defeated; its goods were taken and Lot carried off, until Abram recovered them. Later the cry against Sodom and Gomorrah is great and their sin grievous, and the LORD agrees to spare it for as few as ten righteous. Its men surround Lot's house demanding to have sex with his guests. The angels announce the outcry has grown too great, and Sodom is overthrown with sulfur and fire from the sky, along with Gomorrah, the plain, and all inhabitants.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

Lot moves his tent as far as Sodom, one of the cities of the plain. The text describes the men of Sodom as exceedingly wicked and sinners against the LORD, and mentions that the LORD would later destroy Sodom and Gomorrah.

Chapter 14

Sodom's king and the kings of Gomorrah, Admah, Zeboiim, and Bela rebelled against Chedorlaomer and were defeated in the valley of Siddim. Its goods and food were taken by the victors, and Lot, who lived there, was carried off. Its goods and people were later recovered by Abram, though its king let Abram keep the goods.

Chapter 18

The cry of Sodom and Gomorrah is said to be great and their sin very grievous. The LORD says he will go down to see whether their deeds are as bad as the reports. Men go toward the city, and the LORD agrees to spare it for the sake of as few as ten righteous.

Chapter 19

Sodom is the city where Lot lives and where its men surround his house demanding to have sex with his guests. The angels say the outcry against it has grown so great before the LORD that it will be destroyed. It is overthrown with sulfur and fire from the sky, with Gomorrah, the whole plain, and all its inhabitants.

Sodom in The Book of Ezekiel

author unknown · about 592 to 570 BC · the work’s page · read it where Sodom first appears

Abstract written by a model

Sodom is Jerusalem's younger sister, whose pride, fullness of bread, prosperous ease and neglect of the poor and needy, with arrogance and abomination, led the Lord GOD to remove it; Jerusalem did worse, yet Sodom's captivity will be reversed and it will be restored.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

Sodom is Jerusalem's younger sister, dwelling at her right hand with her daughters. Her iniquity was pride, fullness of bread, prosperous ease, and not strengthening the poor and needy; she was arrogant, committed abomination, and was taken away. Jerusalem has done worse than Sodom, whose captivity will be reversed and who will return to her former estate.
