Chapter 38

Snow and hail are described as stored in the LORD's storehouses, reserved for times of trouble, battle, and war. Lightning, the east wind, floods, thunderstorms, rain, dew, ice, frost, and clouds are all presented as things Job cannot direct, count, or explain. Rain is said to fall even where no man lives, satisfying desolate ground and making grass grow.
