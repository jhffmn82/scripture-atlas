Chapter 5

Bethesda is a pool in Jerusalem by the sheep gate, called this in Hebrew, with five porches. A great multitude of sick, blind, lame and paralyzed people lay there waiting for the water to move, since the first one in after an angel stirred it was healed. It is the place where Jesus heals the man who had been sick for thirty-eight years.
