Abstract written by a model

A group who meditate on God as the truest of the true. They perform real worship, become contented, reject evil, do good and honest deeds, live on little, break worldly entanglements, and ultimately find God.

Slok VII .. Pauri VII

People who have meditated on God as the truest of the true have performed real worship and become contented. They have refrained from evil, done good deeds, practised honesty, lived on little corn and water, broken the entanglements of the world, and found God.
