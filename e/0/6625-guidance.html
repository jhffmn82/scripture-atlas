guidance

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan), guidance comes from Allah, the Scripture, and the clear revelations; those who surrender are rightly guided, while Allah does not guide wrongdoing people or those who disbelieve after clear proofs. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), Allah's guidance is manifested to the messenger's opponents before they oppose him; opposing the messenger after that manifestation leads to following another way and exposure to hell. In Surah 6: The Cattle (Al-An'aam), guidance belongs to Allah, who places whom He wills on a straight path; Muhammad and the believers are ordered to follow Allah’s guidance and call people to it. In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), guidance comes through Allah’s revelations and messengers, and the people of the Garden acknowledge that they could not have been guided without Allah. In Surah 13: The Thunder (Ar-Ra'd), Allah guides unto Himself all who turn unto Him and sends whom He wills astray; those whom Allah sends astray have no guide. In Surah 17: The Night Journey (Al-Israa), guidance is provided by the Scripture and the Qur'an, which guides to the straightest way; whoever follows guidance does so for the good of his own soul. In Surah 18: The Cave (Al-Kahf), guidance comes from Allah, and the one whom Allah guides is rightly led; people who turn away from the revelations or follow their own lust may not be led aright. In Surah 20: Taa-Haa, Allah promises that whoever follows His guidance will not go astray or come to grief; guidance is connected with following revelation and walking aright.

and 9 more works

In Surah 27: The Ant (An-Naml), guidance comes through the Qur'an, which is described as guidance and mercy for believers; those who follow the right way benefit their own souls, while Muhammad is commanded only to warn those who go astray. In Surah 28: The Stories (Al-Qasas), guidance comes from Allah, and no one can guide whom Allah has not chosen to guide; following lust without guidance is the farthest error. In Surah 39: The Groups (Az-Zumar), guidance comes from Allah through revelation and remembrance; those whom Allah guides cannot be misled, while those whom Allah sends astray have no guide. In Surah 40: The Forgiver (Al-Ghaafir), Allah gives guidance, and Moses is given guidance before the Children of Israel inherit the Scripture as a guide and reminder; whoever Allah sends astray has no guide, while the believing man of Pharaoh's family calls his people to the way of right conduct. In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), guidance is given by Allah, but Thamud preferred blindness to it; the revelation is guidance and healing for those who believe, while those who reject it are described as deaf and blind. In Surah 47: Muhammad, those who turn back after guidance has been manifested are seduced by Satan, while those who walk aright receive increased guidance and protection against evil. In Surah 53: The Star (An-Najm), the guidance from their Lord has come to those who follow guesswork and desire as a revealed correction to their claims. In Surah 92: The Night (Al-Lail), guidance belongs to Allah to give. In Surah 96: The Clot (Al-Alaq), guidance of Allah is something on which a person may rely or which he may deny, and it is associated with enjoining piety.
