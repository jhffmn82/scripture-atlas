Abstract written by a model

In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, men in general fear what is to be feared, seek rest, peace, and ease, avoid hurt, and contrast with the speaker’s bright, intelligent engagement. In Mencius, Book IV (Li Lou), men are described as ready with their tongues when they have not been reproved.
