Abstract written by a model

The prophets are a group condemned as profane, speaking visions from their own hearts rather than the LORD. Unsent and absent from his council, those of Samaria and Jerusalem prophesy by Baal, commit adultery, lie, and strengthen evildoers; the LORD opposes them, vowing wormwood and poisoned water.

Chapter 23

The prophets of Samaria and Jerusalem are condemned: those in Samaria prophesied by Baal, and those in Jerusalem commit adultery, walk in lies, strengthen evildoers, and have become like Sodom and Gomorrah. The LORD says he did not send them, that they have not stood in his council, and that he is against them for stealing his words and prophesying lying dreams; he will feed them wormwood and poisoned water.
