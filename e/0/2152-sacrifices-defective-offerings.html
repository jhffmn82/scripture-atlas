Abstract written by a model

Offerings of blind, lame, and sick animals are called evil, and the LORD asks whether a governor would accept such gifts. He rejects them, contrasting them with the pure offering made to his name among the nations. He curses the deceiver who vows a male from his flock but sacrifices a defective thing.
