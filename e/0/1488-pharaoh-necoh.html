Pharaoh Necoh

person · 3 works · 7 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Neco, Neco king of Egypt, Pharaoh, Pharaoh Necoh king of Egypt, Pharaoh king of Egypt, The king of Egypt, king of Egypt (Neco), you king of Judah

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Pharaoh Necoh marches against Assyria's king, kills Josiah at Megiddo, imprisons Jehoahaz at Riblah and takes him to Egypt, imposes silver-and-gold tribute on Judah, and installs Eliakim as king, renaming him Jehoiakim. In The Second Book of Chronicles, king of Egypt (Neco) marches against Carchemish by the Euphrates, sends envoys telling Josiah God commanded his haste and warning him not to oppose God; Josiah refuses to turn back and fights him at Megiddo. In The Book of Jeremiah, Pharaoh Necoh is a king of Egypt whose army camped by the Euphrates at Carchemish; later Egyptians dismiss him as a noise who missed his time, and the LORD vows to punish him and his followers.

Chapter 35

Neco king of Egypt goes up to fight against Carchemish by the Euphrates.
