Aryan nations

Abstract written by a model

In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, Aryan countries depend on Tistrya for fertility, rain, and flowing waters and receive protection through sacrifice, prayer, and invocation. In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, the Aryan people possess Glory, include those born and not yet born, and are united by Husravah into one kingdom. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, the Aryan nations form a united kingdom associated with Husravah and the Glory, honor Verethraghna, and receive protection for their lands, animals, welfare, and Law.

Abstract written by a model

Aryan countries depend on Tistrya for fertility, rain, and flowing waters; through sacrifice, prayer, and invocation, they receive rain and protection from hostile hordes, plagues, leprosy, venomous plants, and enemy weapons.

VIII. Tîr Yast.

The Aryan countries depend on Tistrya for fertility, rain, and flowing waters. If they perform the proper sacrifice and invocation, hostile hordes, plagues, leprosy, venomous plants, and enemy weapons will not enter them.

V. Âbân Yast.

The Aryan people are associated with the Glory in the middle of Vouru-Kasha, which Frangrasyan seeks but is not granted. They are also mentioned as including those born and those not yet born, and as people whose enemies are defeated in requests made to Ardvi Sûra Anâhita.

Aryan nations in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Aryan nations first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Aryan nations are a united kingdom associated with Husravah and the Glory. They are instructed to honor Verethraghna with libations, baresma, cattle, sacrifice, and prayer; proper observance protects their countries from hostile forces, disease, and weapons. The Glory preserves their lands, animals, welfare, and faithful Law against Frangrasyan.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XIV. Bahrâm Yast.

The Aryan nations are told to bring libations, tie bundles of baresma, and cook a single-coloured head of cattle for Verethraghna. If the sacrifice and prayer are properly performed, hostile hordes, plagues, leprosy, venomous plants, and enemy weapons will not enter their countries.

XIX. Zamyâd Yast.

The Aryan nations, born and unborn, are associated with the Glory that Frangrasyan tries to seize. The Glory protects them and helps preserve their lands, animals, welfare, and faithful Law.
