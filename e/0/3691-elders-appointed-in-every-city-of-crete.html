Abstract written by a model

Elders are officeholders Titus must appoint in every city of Crete under Paul's direction. They must be blameless, husband of one wife, with believing, well-behaved children, and free of anger, drunkenness, violence, greed. Hospitable, sober, holy, self-controlled, they hold the faithful word to exhort in sound doctrine and convict contradictors.

Letter to Titus / Chapter 1

Elders are to be appointed by Titus in every city, following Paul's direction. They must be blameless, the husband of one wife, with believing children not accused of loose or unruly behavior. As overseers and God's stewards, they must not be self-pleasing, easily angered, given to wine, violent, or greedy, but hospitable, lovers of good, sober minded, fair, holy, and self-controlled, holding to the faithful word so as to exhort in sound doctrine and convict those who contradict.
