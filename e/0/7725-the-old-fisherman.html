CHAPTER XXXI.

The old fisherman listens to Confucius's song, questions Tzŭ Lu about him, and criticizes Confucius's occupation. He teaches Confucius about proper social functions, the eight blemishes, the four obstacles to business, original purity, and TAO before departing by boat.
