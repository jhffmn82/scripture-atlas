the two Deities

group · 1 work · 16 quoted facts · 7 connections

Abstract written by a model

The two Deities judge their first children unsuccessful, consult the Heavenly Deities, descend and amend their words, and repeat their ritual with the Male-Who-Invites speaking first. They then give birth to the Island of Ahaji, eight islands, and six additional islands, including Great-Yamato-the-Luxuriant-Island-of-the-Dragon-Fly.

Sect.
