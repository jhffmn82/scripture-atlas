Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Micah, the remnant of Jacob is the lame, driven away, and afflicted whom the LORD gathers, makes a strong nation, reigns over on Mount Zion forever, and returns to the children of Israel. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the remnant of the house of Jacob is a remnant of Israel among the Gentiles that receives the land, gains power over adversaries, and assists repentant Gentiles and Israel in building New Jerusalem and gathering the dispersed people. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record, the seed of Jacob is a remnant scattered among the Gentiles because of unbelief and idolatry that receives the gospel and may go among the Gentiles as a lion if they do not repent.

remnant of Jacob in The Book of Micah

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where remnant of Jacob first appears

Abstract written by a model

The remnant of Jacob is the lame, driven away, and afflicted whom the LORD gathers. He makes the cast-off a strong nation and reigns over them on Mount Zion forever. Among many peoples the remnant is like refreshing dew and showers independent of man; among the nations it is like a lion, treading down and tearing in pieces with no deliverer. It returns to the children of Israel, its brothers.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

The LORD will assemble the lame and gather the driven away, including those he has afflicted. He will make the lame a remnant and those cast far off a strong nation, over whom he will reign on Mount Zion forever.

Chapter 5

The remnant of Jacob will be among many peoples like dew from the LORD and like showers on the grass, which do not wait for man. It will also be among the nations like a lion among forest animals or a young lion among flocks of sheep, treading down and tearing in pieces with no one to deliver.

The remnant of the house of Jacob is a remnant of the house of Israel among the Gentiles, dwelling as a lion among beasts and sheep. It receives this land from the Father, goes forth with power over adversaries, whose enemies are cut off, and assists repentant Gentiles and the house of Israel to build New Jerusalem and gather the dispersed people.

Abstract written by a model

The seed of Jacob is a remnant scattered among the Gentiles because of unbelief and idolatry, becoming dark, filthy, and loathsome. Numbered among the people of the first covenant, it receives the gospel and may go among the Gentiles as a lion if they do not repent.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

The seed of Jacob will be scattered and become a dark, filthy, and loathsome people because of unbelief and idolatry. A remnant of the seed of Jacob may later go among the Gentiles as a lion if they do not repent.

Chapter 7

The people are told that they are a remnant of the seed of Jacob. Therefore, they are numbered among the people of the first covenant.
