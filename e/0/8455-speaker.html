Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, the speaker, identified as Ahura Mazda’s foremost servant, hates Daêva-worshippers and repeatedly asks Ahura Mazda for truth about creation, righteousness, wickedness, and protection to increase in goodness. In Yasna, the speaker, identified as the supplicant, approaches Ahura Mazda with Yasnas, prayer, worship, sacrifice, and praises, seeking grace, protection, assistance, righteous lands, sacred privilege, success, and the Home-of-song.

Yasna XLIV.

The speaker repeatedly asks Ahura Mazda to tell him aright about creation, worship, righteousness, wickedness, and the fate of opposing forces. He identifies himself as Ahura Mazda's foremost servant and asks how to increase in goodness, attain perfection, and receive protection and blessing.
