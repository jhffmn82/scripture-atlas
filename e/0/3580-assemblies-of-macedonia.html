Abstract written by a model

The assemblies of Macedonia, enduring severe affliction and deep poverty, nonetheless gave with abundant joy, willingly and beyond their means. They begged to share in the collection for the saints, having first given themselves to the Lord and to the writer's party.

Chapter 8

The assemblies of Macedonia, in a severe ordeal of affliction and deep poverty, gave with abundant joy, according to and beyond their power, of their own accord. They begged for the fellowship in the service to the saints, and first gave themselves to the Lord and to the writer's party through the will of God.
