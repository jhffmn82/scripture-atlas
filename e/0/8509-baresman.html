Baresman

Also called: Baresman plants, Thy Baresman, branch for the Baresman, the Baresman, this Baresman, this Baresman spread with sanctity, this branch for the Baresman, this plant for the Baresman, this plant of the Baresman

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Baresman is provided with Zaothra, girdle, binding, sanctity, and wood-billets; approached with worship, praise, prayers, recital, and blessings; brought with Zaothra to its appointed place at Hâvani and used in approaching holy Yazads; bound and spread with sanctity for propitiation of the Bountiful Immortals; and finally spread with punctilious sanctity and presented as an offering with its plant, prayers, recollection, wood-billets, perfume, and other Mazda-made objects. In Yasna, Baresman is sacrificed to with Zaothra and girdle, its band, and is spread with sanctity. In Visparad, Baresman is a holy ritual chief and implement with Zaothra and a girdle, spread with sanctity, and used with Zaothra as the speaker approaches and praises entities.

Baresman in Yasna

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Baresman first appears

Abstract written by a model

Baresman is a ritual plant bundle and ritual object, provided with Zaothra, girdle, binding, sanctity, and wood-billets; it contains stars, moon, and sun. It is approached with worship, praise, prayers, recital, and blessings, then brought with Zaothra to its appointed place at Hâvani and used in approaching holy Yazads. Its binding and spreading are performed with sanctity for propitiation of the Bountiful Immortals. Finally it is spread with punctilious sanctity and presented as an offering with its plant, prayers, recollection, wood-billets, perfume, and other Mazda-made objects.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna II.

The speaker desires to approach the Baresman with worship and praise, together with the Zaothra and conjoined with it. The Baresman is described as provided with its Zaothra and girdle and spread with sanctity.

Yasna III.

The Baresman is brought to its appointed place with the Zaothra at the time of Hâvani. It is approached with praise, its binding and spreading being performed with sanctity for the propitiation of the Bountiful Immortals.

Yasna XIV (Sp. XV).

The Baresman is used together with the Zaothra in the speaker's approach to the holy Yazads.

Yasna XXII.

The Baresman is brought together with the Zaothra for worship. Its plant is approached with praise, prayers for blessings, memorised recital, the fulfilment of the Mazdayasnian law, and the heard recital of the Gâthas.

Yasna XXIV.

The Baresman is spread with punctilious or scrupulous sanctity and is presented as an offering. Its plant is also presented along with prayers, recollection, wood-billets, perfume, and other Mazda-made objects.
