Abstract written by a model

The plunder is a large war booty of 675,000 sheep, 72,000 cattle, 61,000 donkeys and 32,000 persons, beyond what soldiers took themselves. It is split into two halves, warriors and congregation; one in five hundred of the warriors' half goes to the LORD, one in fifty of the congregation's to the Levites.

Chapter 31

The plunder includes 675,000 sheep, 72,000 cattle, 61,000 donkeys, and 32,000 persons, and it is divided into two equal halves. From the soldiers' half one in five hundred goes to Eleazar as the LORD's offering, and from the congregation's half one in fifty goes to the Levites. The men of war also took booty for themselves.
