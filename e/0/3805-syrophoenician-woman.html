Chapter 7

A Greek woman, a Syrophoenician by race, falls at Jesus' feet in a house near Tyre and begs him to cast a demon out of her little daughter. When Jesus says the children should be filled first and bread not thrown to dogs, she answers that even the dogs under the table eat the children's crumbs. For this saying, he tells her to go her way, and she returns home to find her daughter healed.
