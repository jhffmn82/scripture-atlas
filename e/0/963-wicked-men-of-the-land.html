wicked men of the land

group · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: The best of them, The most upright, The powerful man, The ruler and judge, They, a friend, a neighbor, every man

Abstract written by a model

The wicked men of the land are marked by ungodliness, with no godly or upright person left. They lie in wait for blood, hunt one another with nets, and diligently do evil, while rulers and judges demand bribes. Even their best are like thorn hedges. Their day of visitation and confusion arrives, leaving the land desolate.

Chapter 7

The wicked are described as lying in wait for blood, hunting one another with nets, and doing evil diligently. The ruler and judge ask for bribes, the powerful dictate their evil desires, and even the best among them are like a brier or thorn hedge. Their day of visitation and confusion has come, and the land will be desolate because of the fruit of their doings.
