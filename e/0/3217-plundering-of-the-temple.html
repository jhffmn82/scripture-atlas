Chapter 1

After defeating Egypt in the 143rd year, Antiochus went up against Israel and Jerusalem with a great multitude and entered the sanctuary. He took the golden altar, lampstand, table, cups, bowls, censers, veil, crowns, silver, gold, precious vessels, and hidden treasures, and peeled the gold decoration off the front of the temple. He then went to his own land after making a great slaughter and speaking arrogantly, and great mourning came upon Israel.
