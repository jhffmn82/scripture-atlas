Abstract written by a model

The twelve disciples are the apostles Jesus names and sends out, giving them authority over unclean spirits and disease. They preach that the Kingdom of Heaven is at hand, travelling without money or provisions, like sheep among wolves. They are warned of hatred, trials and betrayal, and promised the Father's Spirit will speak through them. Later they protest the anointing, prepare the Passover, and each asks if he is the betrayer. They sleep in Gethsemane, and all flee at Jesus' arrest despite vowing otherwise.

Chapter 10

The twelve are named: Simon called Peter, Andrew, James son of Zebedee, John, Philip, Bartholomew, Thomas, Matthew the tax collector, James son of Alphaeus, Lebbaeus called Thaddaeus, Simon the Zealot, and Judas Iscariot, who betrayed him. They are given authority over unclean spirits and every disease and are sent out to preach, heal, and cast out demons without money, bag, extra coats, sandals, or staff. They are told to expect hatred, trials, and betrayal, to speak openly without fear, and to flee persecution from city to city.

Chapter 26

The disciples protest the anointing as waste, prepare the Passover as Jesus commands, and each asks whether he is the betrayer. They sleep while Jesus prays in Gethsemane, and all leave him and flee when he is arrested, despite their claims they would not stumble.
