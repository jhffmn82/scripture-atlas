Also called: Zaothras, full offering of Zaothras, the Zaothra, these Zaothras, this Zaothra

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Zaothra is a ritual offering closely joined to the Baresman, used in worship and presented with sanctity among the offerings. In Yasna, Zaothra is a sanctified ceremonial offering for propitiation, invoked for health, healing, progress, growth, merit, renown, equanimity, and victory. In Visparad, Zaothra is a ritual offering performed while approaching and praising numerous lords, festivals, Gâthas, divine beings, people, and places.

Zaothra in Yasna

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Zaothra first appears

Abstract written by a model

Zaothra is a ritual offering, closely joined to the Baresman in worship. It is used to approach Ahura Mazda, Mithra, the holy Yazads, and other holy beings with praise, and accompanies the Baresman to its appointed place at Hâvani. As an offering for beneficent or good waters, it has Haoma, fresh milk, and Hadhânaêpata with it. The Zaothras are lifted up and presented with sanctity among the offerings.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna II.

The speaker desires to approach the Zaothra with worship and praise, both separately and conjointly with the Baresman. The Zaothra is used in approaching Ahura Mazda, Mithra, the lords of the ritual order, and many other holy beings and things.

Yasna III.

The Zaothra accompanies the Baresman when it is brought to its appointed place at Hâvani.

Yasna XIV (Sp. XV).

The Zaothra is used with the Baresman as the speaker approaches the holy Yazads with praise.

Yasna XXII.

The Zaothras are approached with praise as offerings for the beneficent waters. They have Haoma, milk, and Hadhânaêpata with them and are lifted up with sanctity.

Yasna XXIV.

Zaothras are presented to Ahura Mazda and to the good waters having the Haoma with them. They are included among the offerings made with sanctity.

Zaothra in Yasna

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where Zaothra first appears

Abstract written by a model

Zaothra is a sanctified ceremonial offering for propitiation. Offered to the Ahurian One, it unfolds with Haoma, flesh, milk, and Hadhânaêpata lifted with sacred regularity, then is invoked for health, healing, progress, growth, merit, renown, equanimity, and victory.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna LXVI (Sp. LXV).

The Zaothra is offered with sanctity. It is offered together with the Haoma and the flesh, and with the Hadhânaêpata lifted up with sacred regularity.

Yasna LXVIII (Sp. LXVII).

The Zaothra is offered to the Ahurian One with Haoma, milk, and Hadhânaêpata. It is invoked for health, healing, progress, growth, ceremonial merit, good renown, equanimity, and victory.
