the supreme triumph

topic · 5 works · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Their reward, a reward, comfort, reward, salvation and reward, supreme triumph

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba), the supreme triumph is promised to those who receive the Garden and rejoice in giving their lives and wealth for Allah's way. In Surah 37: Those drawn up in Ranks (As-Saaffaat), salvation and reward describe Allah saving His believing slaves and rewarding the good with provision, honor, peace, and the Gardens of delight. In Surah 44: The Smoke (Ad-Dukhaan), the supreme triumph is the bounty of Allah for the salvation of the people of duty from the doom of hell. In Surah 76: Man (Al-Insaan), reward is given to the righteous for what they endured, including a Garden, silk attire, and pure drink. In Surah 98: The Evidence (Al-Bayyina), Their reward is the eternal Gardens of Eden granted to those who believe and do good works.
