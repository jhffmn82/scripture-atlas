Abstract written by a model

The deep is a sea-realm that wisdom denies containing. It is described as bursting from a womb, then shut behind doors, clothed in clouds and thick darkness, and given a boundary its proud waves cannot cross. It has springs, and its surface freezes like stone.

Chapter 38

The sea is said to have broken out as from a womb, and the LORD shut it up with doors, made clouds its garment, and wrapped it in thick darkness. He set its bound and bars and told it, 'You may come here, but no further,' so its proud waves are stopped. Job is asked whether he has entered the springs of the sea, and the surface of the deep is said to freeze hard like stone.
