still-born child

Abstract written by a model

A still-born child whose birth makes the woman who bears it unclean. During and after delivery, it requires her separation in an enclosure, with prescribed distances from fire, water, baresma, and the faithful, followed by stated purification periods.

Fargard V.

A still-born child is treated as requiring special separation and purification procedures for the woman who bears it. The text directs that the woman be established in an enclosure and kept apart for the stated periods.

Fargard VII.

Its birth causes the woman with child to be treated as unclean and placed in a separate enclosure according to prescribed distances from fire, water, baresma, and the faithful.
