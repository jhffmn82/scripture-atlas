Esther (Greek) / Chapter 1

On the seventh day of the banquet, Vashti refused to come with the chamberlains sent to bring her to the king, leaving him grieved and angry. The king asked his friends and the Persian and Median princes for a legal judgment, and they reported on what was proper to do about her failure to do what the king commanded.
