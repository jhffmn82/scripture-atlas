the wind

event · 3 works · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a raging wind, a wind, fatal wind against A'ad, raging wind, the fatal wind against them

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 46: The Dunes (Al-Ahqaf), the wind came as a painful torment by command of its Lord and destroyed all things, leaving only the dwellings of the guilty people visible by morning. In Surah 51: The Winnowing Winds (Adh-Dhaariyat), the fatal wind was sent against A'ad, sparing nothing it reached and turning it to dust. In Surah 54: The Moon (Al-Qamar), the raging wind was let loose upon A'ad and swept men away as though they were uprooted trunks of palm-trees.
