superior man

Also called: A superior man, The superior man

Abstract written by a model

The superior man helps the distressed without adding to the wealth of the rich. He may be sent to a well and imposed upon, yet cannot be sent down into it or fooled. He extensively studies learning and observes rules of propriety, thereby not overstepping what is right.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAP. III. 1. Tsze-hwa being employed on a mission to Ch'i,

The superior man is described as helping the distressed without adding to the wealth of the rich.

CHAP. XXIV. Tsai Wo asked, saying, 'A benevolent man,

The superior man may be made to go to the well and may be imposed upon, but he cannot be made to go down into it or be fooled.

CHAP. XXV. The Master said, 'The superior man, extensively

The superior man extensively studies all learning and keeps himself under the restraint of the rules of propriety. Through this, he may not overstep what is right.
