The narrator's group travels with Paul from Cos through Rhodes, Patara, Tyre, Ptolemais, and Caesarea, staying with disciples and with Philip the evangelist. They beg Paul with the local people not to go up to Jerusalem, then cease, saying 'The Lord's will be done,' and go with him to Jerusalem, where the brothers receive them gladly and they meet James.
