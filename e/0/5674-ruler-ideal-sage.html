ruler (ideal sage)

person · 1 work · 32 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: the equal of Heaven

Abstract written by a model

The ideal ruler possesses every sagely quality: clear, far-reaching intelligence; vast knowledge; magnanimity, generosity, firmness, and discrimination. Self-adjusted and grave, he never swerves from the Mean and commands reverence. Like Heaven in vastness and an abyss in depth, he sends forth virtues in season; they spread through the Middle Kingdom and to barbarous tribes, where all honor and love him as the equal of Heaven.

Chapter 31

The ideal ruler possesses sagely qualities, including discernment, intelligence, knowledge, generosity, endurance, reverence, and discrimination. His virtues spread throughout the Middle Kingdom and to barbarous tribes, and all who have blood and breath honor and love him; he is called the equal of Heaven.
