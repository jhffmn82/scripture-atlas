Chapter 3

Onias explained that the treasury contained deposits of widows and orphans and some money belonging to Hyrcanus son of Tobias, a man in very high place, totaling four hundred talents of silver and two hundred of gold. The priests and people prayed that God, who gave the law concerning deposits, would keep them safe for those who deposited them. The text ends with the treasury kept, not confiscated.
