Abstract written by a model

A man is considered in an inheritance ruling when he dies childless and has a sister. His sister receives half of his heritage. Conversely, if she dies childless, he would inherit from her. The ruling appears within a pronouncement that explains shares for sisters and other distant kindred so people do not err.
