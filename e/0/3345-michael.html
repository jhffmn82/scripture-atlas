Abstract written by a model

In Daniel (Greek), Michael is a chief and great prince who protects Daniel's people, helps the speaker against the princes of Persia and Greece, and stands up at the time of trouble so the people are delivered. In Revelation, Michael leads his angels against the dragon in the heavenly war, prevails, and casts the dragon's forces out of heaven.

Michael in Daniel (Greek)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Michael first appears

Abstract written by a model

Michael is a chief and great prince, the protector of Daniel's people. In Chapter 10 he helps the speaker after the prince of Persia withstands him for twenty-one days, and alone supports him against the princes of Persia and Greece. In Chapter 12 he stands up at the time of trouble, and the people are delivered.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

Michael is described as one of the chief princes who came to help the speaker after the prince of the kingdom of Persia withstood him for twenty-one days. He is called Daniel's prince and is said to be the only one who supports the speaker against the princes of Persia and Greece.

Chapter 12

Michael is called the great prince who stands for the children of Daniel's people. He will stand up at the time of trouble, and at that time the people will be delivered.
