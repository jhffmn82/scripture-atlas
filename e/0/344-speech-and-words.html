Chapter 12

Speech can destroy or heal. The wicked's words lie in wait for blood, while the speech of the upright rescues them. Rash words pierce like a sword, but the wise tongue heals. Truth's lips are established forever, a lying tongue is only momentary, and a kind word makes an anxious heart glad. A man is satisfied with good by the fruit of his mouth, but an evil man is trapped by the sinfulness of his lips.
