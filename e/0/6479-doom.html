Abstract written by a model

In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), hell forever is the punishment for intentional killing of a believer, accompanied by Allah's wrath, curse, and an awful doom. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), a painful doom is the punishment for those who were scornful and proud. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), Allah prepares a shameful doom for disbelievers who disbelieve in Allah and His messengers and seek a way between belief and disbelief. In Surah 14: Abraham (Ibrahim), an awful doom awaits the disbelievers who reject Allah and turn people away from His way. In Surah 22: The Pilgrimage (Al-Hajj), the Doom of Allah will overpower mankind when the Hour is seen, making them appear drunken though they are not. In Surah 29: The Spider (Al-Ankaboot), Allah's doom has a fixed appointed term and will come suddenly upon those who deny, overwhelming the disbelievers from above and beneath their feet. In Surah 37: Those drawn up in Ranks (As-Saaffaat), the Doom is tasted by those who deny the truth, worship beside Allah, and follow error, while single-minded slaves of Allah are saved from it. In Surah 39: The Groups (Az-Zumar), doom comes upon those who deny, scorn, and do wrong, and people are warned to repent before it comes suddenly.

and 7 more works

In Surah 42: Consultation (Ash-Shura), the doom awaits disbelievers, wrong-doers, and those who reject or dispute Allah's revelations. In Surah 46: The Dunes (Al-Ahqaf), the Day of Doom is a punishment for disbelief, arrogance, transgression, and rejection of Allah's warnings. In Surah 52: The Mount (At-Tur), the doom of Allah will surely come to pass, and none can ward it off. In Surah 58: The Pleading Woman (Al-Mujaadila), a shameful doom is prepared for those who oppose Allah and His messenger and use oaths to turn people from Allah's way. In Surah 70: The Ascending Stairways (Al-Ma'aarij), the Lord's doom is a punishment before which none can feel secure, and those who fear it are among the described worshippers. In Surah 70: The Ascending Stairways (Al-Ma'aarij), the doom is about to fall upon the disbelievers, and none can repel it. In Surah 77: The Emissaries (Al-Mursalaat), the doom is the punishment that the repudiators used to deny, and they are told to depart toward it.
