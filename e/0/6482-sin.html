Abstract written by a model

In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), those who do evil in ignorance may receive forgiveness if they quickly turn in repentance to Allah, but evil deeds followed by repentance at death do not receive the stated forgiveness. In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), sin includes indecency, wrongful oppression, associating partners with Allah, denying revelations, and corrupting the earth, and people are punished for what they earn. In Surah 40: The Forgiver (Al-Ghaafir), Allah is the Forgiver of sin, earlier peoples are seized for their sins, and every soul is requited for what it has earned. In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), evil deeds are not equal to good deeds, are held against the person who commits them, and are to be repelled with what is better. In Surah 42: Consultation (Ash-Shura), those who believe and trust in their Lord shun the worst of sins and indecencies, and Allah forgives much.
