remnant of Judah in The Book of Jeremiah

author unknown · 629 to 579 BC · the work’s page · read it where remnant of Judah first appears

Abstract written by a model

The remnant of Judah is a small surviving group, returned from the nations where they had been driven and living in the land. They ask the LORD for direction and swear obedience. The LORD tells them to stay, promising to build and plant them, and warns that Egypt means death. Jeremiah calls them deceitful. Johanan and the captains then lead everyone, including men, women, children and king's daughters, into Egypt, reaching Tahpanhes in disobedience.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 42

The remnant, left a few out of many, asks for the LORD's direction and swears to obey whether the answer is good or bad. The LORD then tells them to stay in the land, promising to build and plant them, but warns that going to Egypt will bring death and that they will see this place no more. Jeremiah says they have been deceitful and have not obeyed in anything he was sent to tell them.

Chapter 43

The remnant of Judah had returned from all the nations where they had been driven to live in Judah. Johanan and the captains take them, the men, women, children, and king's daughters, into Egypt in disobedience to the LORD. The people include those Nebuzaradan had left with Gedaliah, and they reach Tahpanhes.
