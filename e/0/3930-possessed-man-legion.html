Chapter 8

A man from the city of the Gadarenes, long possessed by demons, wore no clothes and lived in the tombs, and had broken chains and fetters. He falls before Jesus, names himself Legion, and is freed when the demons leave; people then find him sitting at Jesus' feet, clothed and in his right mind. He begs to go with Jesus but is sent home to declare what God has done, and he proclaims throughout the city what Jesus did for him.
