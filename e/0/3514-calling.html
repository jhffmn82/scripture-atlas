Abstract written by a model

The calling is a teaching that each person should remain in the condition in which God called them, whether circumcised or not, bondservant or free. Keeping God's commandments matters most; a bondservant may take an opportunity for freedom. Paul says this rule applies in all assemblies.

Chapter 7

Each person is to walk as God has called him and stay in the calling in which he was called, whether circumcised or uncircumcised, bondservant or free. Circumcision and uncircumcision are nothing compared with keeping God's commandments. A bondservant may take an opportunity to become free, and Paul states that this rule applies in all the assemblies.
