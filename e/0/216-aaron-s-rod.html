Abstract written by a model

Aaron's rod, inscribed with Aaron's name to represent Levi's house, is laid up before the LORD overnight among others, then sprouts, buds, blossoms and bears ripe almonds. It is placed back before the covenant as a token against the children of rebellion.

Chapter 17

Aaron's rod, representing the house of Levi, is laid up among the others before the LORD and by the next day has sprouted, budded, produced blossoms, and borne ripe almonds. The LORD has it put back before the covenant to be kept as a token against the children of rebellion.
