Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Job, a tree is portrayed as having hope that man lacks: even if cut down it sprouts again, and even with an old root and dead stock, the scent of water makes it bud like a plant. In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, trees are soft and brittle in early growth, dry and withered at death; a strong tree fills outstretched arms and invites the feller.

Chapter 14

The tree is presented as having hope that man lacks: if cut down it will sprout again, and its tender branch will not cease. Even when its root grows old in the earth and its stock dies in the ground, the scent of water makes it bud and put out boughs like a plant.
