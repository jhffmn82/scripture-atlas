Midian

place · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), Midian receives Shu'eyb, who calls them to serve Allah, give full measure and weight, avoid corruption, and not obstruct Allah's path. In Surah 11: Hud, Midian is warned by Shu'eyb to serve Allah, use full measure and weight, and avoid corruption; the unjust are seized by the awful Cry and destroyed. In Surah 28: The Stories (Al-Qasas), Midian is where Moses turns after fleeing Pharaoh, finds two women at the water, helps them, is welcomed by their father, and remains for a term.
