Karpans

Also called: Karpans’, the Karpans

Abstract written by a model

The Karpans are a group aligned with Grehma, the Kavis, and those desiring the Demon of lies. Inspired by polluted drunken joy and angry zeal, they crush the faithful and bring woe, but Zarathustra will drive out their disciples.

Yasna XXXII.

The Karpans are aligned with Grehma, the Kavis, and the Throne of those who desire the Demon of lies. Zarathustra says he will drive away the Karpans’ disciples.

Yasna XLVIII.

The Karpans use angry zeal to crush the faithful, and their inspiration supports the evil rule of the tyrants of the provinces. Their polluted drunken joy is to be driven away.
