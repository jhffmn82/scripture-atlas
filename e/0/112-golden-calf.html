Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, it was a molded gold calf Aaron made from the people's golden earrings, which the people hailed as the gods who brought them out of Egypt and made offerings to, before Moses destroyed it and made the Israelites drink it. In Deuteronomy, it was a molten calf made by Israel after turning from the LORD's way, which Moses destroyed by burning it, grinding it to fine dust, and throwing the dust into the mountain brook. In Psalms, it was a calf the fathers made in Horeb and worshiped as a molten image, exchanging their glory for the image of a bull that eats grass and forgetting God their Savior.
