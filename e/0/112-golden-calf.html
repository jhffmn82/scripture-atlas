Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, the golden calf is a molded gold calf Aaron makes from the people's golden earrings, which the people hail as the gods who brought them out of Egypt and to which they make offerings before Moses destroys it and makes the Israelites drink its powder. In Deuteronomy, the molten calf is made by Israel after turning from the LORD's way, and Moses burns it, grinds it to fine dust, and throws the dust into the mountain brook. In Psalms, the calf made at Horeb is worshiped as a molten image, and the fathers exchange their glory for the image of a bull that eats grass, forgetting God their Savior. In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), the calf is chosen by the Children of Israel for worship while Moses is away, causing them to wrong themselves until Moses tells them to turn in penitence to their Creator. In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), the golden calf is a saffron-hued calf made from ornaments that produces a lowing sound but cannot speak or guide people, and its worshipers face terror and humiliation unless they repent and believe. In Surah 20: Taa-Haa, the calf is saffron-colored and gives forth a lowing sound, but it cannot return a saying or possess harm or benefit; Moses burns it and scatters its dust over the sea.
