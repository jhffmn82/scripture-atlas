Chapter 47

Joseph buys all the land of Egypt for Pharaoh as each Egyptian sells his field because of the famine, with the exception of the priests' land, since the priests had a portion from Pharaoh. The people are moved to the cities and given seed, with a fifth of the harvests owed to Pharaoh and four parts kept for seed and food, a statute Joseph made.
