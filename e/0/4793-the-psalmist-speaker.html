Abstract written by a model

The psalmist, attributed to David, is God's servant, consumed by zeal for God's house. He suffers hatred without cause, reproach for God's sake, alienation from his brothers, and mockery as the drunkards' song. Weary and dry-throated from crying out, he is given poison and vinegar and finds no comforters. He prays for deliverance, asks judgment on enemies, and vows thankful praise.

Chapter 69

The psalmist cries out that he is sinking, weary, and with a dry throat, and that he has borne reproach and shame for God's sake and become a stranger to his brothers. He is mocked in the gate, given poison and vinegar, and finds no comforters. He prays for deliverance, asks for judgment on his enemies, and vows to praise God with a song and thanksgiving, which he says will please the LORD more than an ox or bull.
