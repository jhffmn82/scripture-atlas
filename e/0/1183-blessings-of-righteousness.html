Chapter 58

The LORD promises that obedience will bring light breaking out like morning, quick healing, righteousness going before, and his glory as rear guard. He will answer when they call, guide them continually, satisfy them in dry places, strengthen their bones, and make them like a watered garden. They will rebuild old waste places and be called Repairer of the Breach, Restorer of Paths with Dwellings.
