Abstract written by a model

Ebedmelech is an Ethiopian eunuch in the king's house who trusts the LORD. He tells the king at Benjamin's gate that Jeremiah will starve in the dungeon, then leads thirty men to lift Jeremiah out with rags and cords. Later the LORD promises him deliverance from the sword and from those he fears, because of his trust.

Chapter 38

Ebedmelech, an Ethiopian eunuch in the king's house, goes to the king at Benjamin's gate and says Jeremiah will die of famine in the dungeon. Commanded to take thirty men, he lets down rags and worn-out garments by cords for Jeremiah's armpits and lifts him out.

Chapter 39

Ebedmelech the Ethiopian is the recipient of a message the LORD told Jeremiah to deliver. He is promised deliverance on the day the LORD's words come on the city, that he will not be given into the hand of those he fears or fall by the sword, because he has put his trust in the LORD.
