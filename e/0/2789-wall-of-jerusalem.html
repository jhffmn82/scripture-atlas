Abstract written by a model

The wall of Jerusalem is a structure whose condition changes across the Book of Nehemiah. It first appears broken down, its gates burned with fire, which moves Nehemiah to grief and prayer. He inspects it by night, finding a place where his animal could not pass, and urges the people to rebuild so they will not be disgraced. Despite mockery, the wall is joined together to half its height, and the filled breaches provoke enemy plotting. It is then completed with no breach left on the twenty-fifth of Elul, in fifty-two days, though the gate doors are not yet set up. Its completion frightens surrounding nations, who perceive God as the doer. Finally, priests and Levites purify it, and the princes of Judah and two thanksgiving companies go up on it for the dedication, passing its many gates and towers.

The Book of Nehemiah / Chapter 1

The wall of Jerusalem is reported to be broken down, and its gates are burned with fire. This news from Hanani and the men of Judah prompts Nehemiah's grief and prayer.

Chapter 2

The narrator inspects the broken-down wall at night, going by the valley gate, the jackal's well, the dung gate, the spring gate and the king's pool, where there was no room for his animal to pass. He then urges the people to build it up so they will not be disgraced.

Chapter 4

The wall is mocked as flimsy but is built up until it is joined together to half its height. The breaches begin to be filled, which provokes the enemies to plot against the city.

Chapter 6

The wall is reported to be built with no breach left, though the doors have not yet been set in the gates. It is finished on the twenty-fifth of Elul, in fifty-two days. Its completion frightens the surrounding nations, who perceive it was accomplished by God.

Chapter 12

The wall was purified by the priests and Levites before the dedication. The princes of Judah and the two thanksgiving companies went up on it, passing gates and towers including the dung gate, spring gate, water gate, gate of Ephraim, old gate, fish gate, sheep gate, tower of the furnaces, tower of Hananel, and tower of Hammeah, ending at the gate of the guard.
