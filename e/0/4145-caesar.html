Caesar

person · 4 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Caesar's, Caesar’s, Caesar’s judgment seat, my lord, the emperor

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Caesar's image and inscription are on the denarius shown to Jesus, and Jesus says to give Caesar the things that are Caesar's. In The Good News According to Mark, Caesar's image and inscription are on the denarius that Jesus asks to see, and Jesus says to give Caesar the things that are Caesar's. In The Gospel According to Luke, Caesar's image and inscription are on the denarius that Jesus has the spies show him, and Jesus says to give Caesar the things that are Caesar's. In The Acts of the Apostles, Caesar is the authority to whom Paul appeals, and Paul says he is standing before Caesar's judgment seat; Festus answers that to Caesar he shall go, Paul denies having sinned against Caesar, and Festus refers to Caesar as his lord.
