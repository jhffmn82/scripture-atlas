Zhou dynasty

group · 4 works · 8 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Zhou, Zhou dynasties, our dynasty of Zhou, the Zhou dynasty

Abstract written by a model

In Mencius, Book II (Gong Sun Chou), Zhou had existed for more than seven hundred years since its commencement and was the place where Mencius spent the night after leaving Qi. In Mencius, Book III (Teng Wen Gong), the Zhou dynasty is the dynasty whose sovereign the people of Shang said they would serve to be made happy, and whose men of station were met by men of station of Shang bearing black and yellow silks. In Mencius, Book IV (Li Lou), Zhou loses the people and their hearts, driving them to Tang and Wu, and ultimately loses the throne. In Mencius, Book V (Wan Zhang), Zhou established five degrees of rank and six degrees of dignity with assigned territories and emoluments, and received from Yin a rule concerning the punishment of robbers.
