Abstract written by a model

Rules on uncleanness and purification: touching or carrying carcasses brings uncleanness until evening, with clothes washed. Vessels go into water and are clean by evening; earthen vessels and ovens are broken. Soaked food or drink becomes unclean, while springs, cisterns and dry sowing seed are exceptions.

Chapter 11

Uncleanness is contracted by touching or carrying carcasses and lasts until evening, with washing of clothes required for carrying or eating them. Contaminated vessels used for work must be put into water and are clean again by evening, while earthen vessels and ovens must be broken. Food or drink that has been soaked in water or held in contaminated vessels becomes unclean.
