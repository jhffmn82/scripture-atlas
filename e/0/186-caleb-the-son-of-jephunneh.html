Caleb the son of Jephunneh

person · 5 works · 32 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: Caleb, Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenizzite, my servant Caleb

Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, Caleb the son of Jephunneh urged Israel to trust the LORD and was promised entry into the land. In Deuteronomy, Caleb the son of Jephunneh was promised the land he had trodden and its inheritance by his children. In The Book of Joshua, Caleb the son of Jephunneh received Hebron, drove out Anak's sons, and gave springs to his daughter Achsah. In The Book of Judges, Caleb offered his daughter Achsah to Othniel, received Hebron, and drove out Anak's sons. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, Caleb the son of Jephunneh stood with Joshua, entered the hill country, and saw his offspring inherit it.

Caleb the son of Jephunneh in Numbers

author unknown · 1439 to 1399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Caleb the son of Jephunneh first appears

Abstract written by a model

Caleb, son of Jephunneh of the tribe of Judah, is one of the twelve spies and a prince representing Judah. After the spies' report he stills the people and urges them to possess the land, saying Israel is well able to overcome it. He tears his clothes and, with Joshua, urges trust in the LORD rather than fear. The LORD names him his servant, with another spirit who followed fully, and promises he will enter the land, his offspring possessing it. He survives the plague that kills the other spies.

Chapter 14

Caleb, a spy, tears his clothes and with Joshua urges the people to trust the LORD and not fear the people of the land. He survives while the other spies die by plague.

Caleb the son of Jephunneh in The Book of Joshua

author unknown · about 600 BC · the work’s page · read it where Caleb the son of Jephunneh first appears

Abstract written by a model

Caleb, a Kenizzite counted among the children of Judah, is a man who wholly followed the LORD. Sent at forty from Kadesh Barnea to spy out the land, he reported faithfully while his brothers disheartened the people, earning Moses' promise of the land he walked on. At eighty-five, kept alive by the LORD and still strong for war, he came to Joshua at Gilgal and asked for the hill country. Joshua blessed him and gave him Hebron within Judah's portion. He drove out Anak's three sons, Sheshai, Ahiman and Talmai, offered his daughter Achsah to whoever took Kiriath Sepher, and gave her upper and lower springs when she asked.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 14

Caleb came to Joshua at Gilgal with the children of Judah and recalled how he was sent at forty to spy out the land, brought back word as it was in his heart, and wholly followed the LORD while his brothers made the people's hearts melt. Now eighty-five and as strong as before, he asked for the hill country and received Hebron as his inheritance from Joshua, who blessed him.

Chapter 15

Caleb is given a portion among the children of Judah, Kiriath Arba (Hebron), according to the LORD's commandment to Joshua. He drove out Sheshai, Ahiman and Talmai, the three sons of Anak, and offered his daughter Achsah as wife to whoever took Kiriath Sepher. When Achsah came and asked for a blessing, he gave her the upper and lower springs.
