Fourth Lecture, Called Modes of Speech. / First Lesson. .. Second Lesson.

A woman should not be addressed with abusive or degrading speech if she does not answer when addressed. She may instead be addressed with respectful terms such as long-lived one, sister, madam, lady, lay-sister, pupil, faithful one, or lover of faith.
