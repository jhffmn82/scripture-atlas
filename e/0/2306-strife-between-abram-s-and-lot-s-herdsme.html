Chapter 13

Strife arises between the herdsmen of Abram's livestock and those of Lot's because the land cannot support both men's great possessions together. The Canaanites and Perizzites were living in the land at the time. Abram cites it as the reason for proposing that he and Lot separate, and the separation follows.
