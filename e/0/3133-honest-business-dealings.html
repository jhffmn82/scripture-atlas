Abstract written by a model

In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, honest business dealings provide guidance on exact scales and weights, fair reckoning, bargaining, and recording transactions. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), justice and fair dealing command full measure, true weighing, and no wronging of mankind in their goods.

Abstract written by a model

Guidance on honest business dealings: don't be ashamed of exact scales and weights, fair reckoning with partners and travellers, or bargaining with merchants; hand over goods by number and weight, and record all giving and receiving in writing.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 42

The reader is told not to be ashamed of exact scales and weights, fair reckoning with partners and travellers, or bargaining with merchants. Whatever is handed over should be by number and weight, with all giving and receiving put in writing.
