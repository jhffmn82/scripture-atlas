Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Chronicles, Mount Seir was spared by Israel during the exodus from Egypt, then came against Judah and was struck by the LORD's ambushers while Ammon and Moab killed and destroyed them. In The Second Book of Chronicles, Seir's children were struck down by Amaziah, with survivors thrown from a rock and their gods taken by Amaziah.
