Emperor Ō-Jin

person · 1 work · 18 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Heavenly Sovereign, His Augustness Homuda-wake, Homuda, Homuda-wake, the Heavenly Sovereign, this Heavenly Sovereign

Abstract written by a model

Emperor Ō-Jin, also called Homuda-wake or Heavenly Sovereign Homuda, is the Heir Apparent Oho-tomo-wake whose future rule was foreknown in the womb. He rules the Empire from the palace of Akira at Karushima, marries several queens and princesses, and has twenty-six children. His Augustness Oho-sazaki is identified as his successor, and Ō-Jin grants Princess Kaminaga to him. The account proceeds through Ō-Jin’s genealogies, marriages, descendants, deeds, succession arrangements, intoxication by great august liquor, death, age, and burial.
