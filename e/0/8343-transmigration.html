transmigration

topic · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: disease of transmigration, eighty-four lakhs of species, pain of transmigration

Abstract written by a model

Transmigration is a disease and state of repeated birth and death, suffered when humanity or man forgets God’s Name, goes astray, and remains in spiritual ignorance. It is linked with darkness, doubt, worldly attachment, sin, evil actions, mammon, and ignorance of the Word, including the effects of good and evil acts across life’s phases. It unfolds as repeated existence until release comes through service to the True Guru, the Guru’s instruction and favour, divine knowledge, humility, God’s love and favouring glance, remembrance and repetition of the Name, and union or absorption in God.

transmigration in Hymns of Guru Nanak

Guru Nanak · the work’s page · read it where transmigration first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Rag Gujari .. Gujari Ashtapadi

Transmigration is associated with darkness, doubt, and the disease that ruins the world. Repeating God's name, seeking the true Guru's protection, and obtaining divine knowledge are presented as means of escaping it.

Rag Suhi .. Suhi ki War

Transmigration follows when man is led astray by the various phases of life and by the effects of good and evil acts. It ends through God's favouring glance and union with God.

Ramkali .. Ramkali ki War I

Transmigration follows from forgetfulness of the Name, worldly attachment, sin, and failure to follow the Guru's instruction. Holy people who know God and those who receive the Guru's favour can end transmigration and become absorbed in God.

Rag Maru Ashtapadi .. Maru ki War I

Transmigration results from forgetting the Name, evil actions, attachment to mammon, and ignorance of the Word. It ends through the Guru's instruction, God's love, humility, and remembrance of the Name.
