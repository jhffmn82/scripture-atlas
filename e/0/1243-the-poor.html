Abstract written by a model

In Deuteronomy, the poor brother is to receive an open hand and a sufficient loan, and must not be refused because the year of release is near. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, the poor are owed generosity and must not be deprived, delayed, provoked, rejected, or turned away.

Abstract written by a model

The poor brother is to receive an open hand and a loan sufficient for his need, and must not be refused because the year of release is near. If refused, he may cry to the LORD against the lender, and it would be sin to the lender. The text says the poor will never cease out of the land.
