Hâvani

time · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Hâvani at his time, at the time of Hâvani, our Hâvani

Abstract written by a model

Hâvani is both a holy lord of the ritual order and a ritual time. It receives sacrifice, homage, propitiation, praise, and requests for forgiveness. At Hâvani, the Baresman and Zaothra are brought to their appointed place, and ritual lords are approached. Thirty and three lords are arranged nearest around it; its services were inculcated by Mazda and pronounced by Zarathustra.

Hâvani is in one work on this site, Yasna. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna / Yasna I.

Hâvani is the holy lord of the ritual order and receives sacrifice, homage, propitiation, and praise. The speaker asks forgiveness if he has offended Hâvani and identifies Hâvani as the one around whom the thirty and three lords of the ritual order are arranged.

Yasna III.

Hâvani is the time at which the Baresman and Zaothra are brought to their appointed place. Hâvani is also approached as a holy lord of the ritual order and is named among the ritual lords whose services were inculcated by Mazda and pronounced by Zarathustra.

Yasna VII.

Hâvani is named as a holy lord of the ritual order and as the time at which certain lords of the ritual order are approached. Thirty and three lords approach nearest round about Hâvani.
