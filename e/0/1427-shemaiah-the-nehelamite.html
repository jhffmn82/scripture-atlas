Chapter 29

Shemaiah sends letters in his own name to Zephaniah the priest and the priests in Jerusalem, urging them to put Jeremiah in the stocks and shackles as a madman posing as a prophet. The LORD declares that Shemaiah prophesied without being sent and led the people to trust a lie. He and his offspring will be punished, none will remain among the people, and he will not see the good the LORD will do.
