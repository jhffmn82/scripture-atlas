spiritual gifts in Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where spiritual gifts first appears

Abstract written by a model

Spiritual gifts are varied manifestations of the one Spirit, given to each person for the profit of all. Listed kinds include the word of wisdom, word of knowledge, faith, healings, miracles, prophecy, discerning of spirits, languages and interpretation of languages. Chapter 12 explains that not all have the same gifts and urges earnest desire for the best, with a more excellent way to come. Chapter 14 stresses desiring gifts, especially prophesying, to build up the assembly.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 12

Gifts come in various kinds, with the same Spirit behind them, and each person receives a manifestation of the Spirit for the profit of all. They include the word of wisdom, word of knowledge, faith, healings, miracles, prophecy, discerning of spirits, languages and interpretation. Not all have the same gifts, and the readers are urged to earnestly desire the best gifts, with a most excellent way yet to be shown.

Chapter 14

The believers are told to earnestly desire spiritual gifts, especially prophesying. Since they are zealous for them, they should use them to abound in building up the assembly.

gifts of God in The Book of Moroni

Moroni · the work’s page · read it where gifts of God first appears

Abstract written by a model

The gifts of God are many, come from the same God, and are given by the Spirit of God to profit people. They include wisdom, knowledge, great faith, healing, miracles, prophecy, angels and ministering spirits, tongues, and interpretation of languages. Distributed individually according to the Spirit of Christ’s will, they continue as long as the world stands, except according to the unbelief of the children of men.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

The gifts of God are many and come from the same God through different ways of administration. They include wisdom, knowledge, faith, healing, miracles, prophecy, the beholding of angels and ministering spirits, tongues, and interpretation of languages and tongues; they continue as long as the world stands except according to unbelief.
