Saul's death

event · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Saul died, died, fell on it

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Samuel, Saul's death occurs at Mount Gilboa when he falls on his own sword after his armor bearer refuses to thrust him through. In 1 Chronicles, Saul's death is caused by Saul's trespass, as he falls on his own sword and dies with his three sons and all his house together.

occurs_at Mount Gilboa Saul, badly pressed by archers, asked his armor bearer to thrust him through so the uncircumcised could not abuse him. The armor bearer refused, so Saul fell on his own sword and died, and the armor bearer followed him in death. The Philistines then beheaded and stripped his body and fastened it to the wall of Beth Shan, and men of Jabesh Gilead later burned it and buried the bones.
