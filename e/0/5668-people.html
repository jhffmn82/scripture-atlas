Abstract written by a model

The people rarely practice perfect virtue and have long had few who follow the Mean. They fast and purify themselves before sacrifices, then attend them. They follow only institutions, regulations, and rules that have sufficient attestation, honor, or credence. An ideal ruler inspires universal reverence, belief, pleasure, honor, and love; his lack of rewards stimulates virtue more than hatchets and battle-axes awe them.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The people are described as having long had few members who could practice the virtue according to the Mean.

Chapter 29

The people provide sufficient attestation for the Ruler's institutions. They do not follow regulations or rules that lack attestation, honor, or credence.

Chapter 31

The people revere the ideal ruler when they see him, believe him when he speaks, and are pleased with his actions. People throughout the lands reached by ships, carriages, human strength, the heavens, the earth, the sun, the moon, frost, and dew honor and love him.
