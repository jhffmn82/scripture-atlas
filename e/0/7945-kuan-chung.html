Kuan Chung being at the point of death, Duke Huan went to see him.

Kuan Chung advises Duke Huan that Pao Yü should not administer the State because his purity and intolerance of wrongdoing would bring him into conflict with the prince and people. He recommends Hsi P'êng instead.
