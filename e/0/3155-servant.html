Abstract written by a model

A servant, owed liberty if wise, is given bread, discipline and work. Kept busy to prevent idleness and escape; an evil one faces yoke, whip and fetters, yet is treated like oneself, bought with blood, and may flee if ill treated.

Chapter 33

The slave is to be kept at work, since idleness teaches mischief and idle hands lead him to seek liberty. An evil slave faces yoke, whip, racks and heavy fetters if he disobeys, yet he is also to be treated like oneself, having been bought with blood, and may run away if ill treated.
