Chapter 6

This people is described by the narrator as having unclean lips. They are the audience of the message to hear but not understand and to see but not perceive, with hearts made fat, ears heavy and eyes shut so that they do not turn and be healed. The Lord says they will be removed far away, and even a remaining tenth will be consumed.
