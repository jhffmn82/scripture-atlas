time

Also called: Time, continuous flow

Abstract written by a model

In Uttaradhyayana Sutra, time is described as a formless thing existing in the place of time and as a continuous flow without beginning or end, though each individual thing has both. In Chuang Tzŭ, time is described as endless, without a starting-point, unable to be arrested, and unfolding through unrecalled past years and continual new beginnings.

Abstract written by a model

Time is a formless thing existing in the place of time. As a continuous flow it has no beginning or end, but each individual thing has both.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Thirty-Sixth Lecture.

Time is one of the ten kinds of formless things and exists in what is called the place of time. Regarded as a continuous flow, it is without beginning and end; with regard to an individual thing, it has a beginning and an end.
