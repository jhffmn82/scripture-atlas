Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Job, the poor and needy whom Job helped are delivered, and Job is a father to the needy. In Proverbs, the poor are valued for integrity, while oppressing them is condemned and giving to them brings no lack. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, the lowly are set in place of rulers by the Lord.

Abstract written by a model

The poor are a group valued for integrity, judged better than perverse rich people, and the understanding poor see through the rich. Oppressing them is condemned; giving to them brings no lack, while ignoring them brings many curses.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 28

A poor person who walks in integrity is called better than a perverse rich one, and a poor person with understanding sees through the rich man. Oppression of the poor is condemned, and one who gives to the poor has no lack while one who closes his eyes to them will have many curses.
