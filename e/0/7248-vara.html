Vara

place · 1 work · 13 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Vara which Yima made, a Vara, the Vara

Abstract written by a model

Vara is a square enclosure made by Yima as an abode for men and a fold for flocks. It holds flowing water, selected men and women, cattle, trees, fruit, birds, dwellings, and seeds, all of the finest kinds. It excludes Angra Mainyu’s brands, divides into parts with nine, six, and three streets, and is sealed with a golden ring, with a door, self-shining window, and lights.

Vara is in one work on this site, Vendidad (Vendîdâd), Fargards I-VIII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Fargard II.

The Vara is a square enclosure built by Yima as an abode for men and a fold for flocks. It contains waters, birds, dwellings, selected seeds of living beings and plants, nine streets, a golden seal, a door, and a self-shining window.
