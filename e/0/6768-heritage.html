heritage

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 10 connections
Also called: inheritance, inherited, the heritage, the inheritance

Abstract written by a model

Heritage is a legal share left by parents and near kindred and allotted to men and women, whether little or much. Kinsfolk, orphans, and the needy who are present when it is divided are to receive something. Its central matter is the prescribed distribution among children, parents, spouses, and certain distant heirs, with legacies and debts settled first. For children, a male receives the equivalent of two females; one woman receives half, while more than two women receive two-thirds. Parents may receive sixths, while the mother may receive a third or a sixth according to the stated heirs. The pronouncement concerning distant kindred concludes with the childless-man case: a sister receives half, two sisters receive two-thirds, and where there are brethren, men and women, the male receives the equivalent of two females.

Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa) / 1 O mankind! Be careful of your duty to your Lord Who created you from a single soul and .. 10 Lo! Those who devour the wealth of orphans wrongfully, they do but swallow fire into their bellies, and they will be exposed to burning flame.

Heritage left by parents and near kindred is divided into legal shares for men and women, whether the amount is little or much. Kinsfolk, orphans, and the needy present at the division are to receive something from it.

11 Allah chargeth you concerning (the provision for) your children: to the male the equivalent of the portion of two females, and if there be women more than two, then theirs is two-thirds of the inheritance, and if there be one (only) then the half. And to each of his parents a sixth of the inheritance, if he have a son; and if he have no son and his parents are his heirs, then to his mother appertaineth the third; and if he have brethren, then to his mother appertaineth the sixth, after any legacy he may have bequeathed, or debt (hath been paid). Your parents and your children: Ye know not which of them is nearer unto you in usefulness. It is an injunction from Allah. Lo! Allah is Knower, Wise. .. 14 And whoso disobeyeth Allah and His messenger and transgresseth His limits, He will make him enter Fire, where he will dwell for ever; his will be a shameful doom.

Inheritance is divided among children, parents, spouses, and certain distant heirs according to prescribed shares. Legacies and debts are settled before the relevant shares are distributed.

170 O mankind! The messenger hath come unto you with the Truth from your Lord. Therefor believe; (it is) better for you. But if ye disbelieve, still, lo! unto Allah belongeth whatsoever is in the heavens and the earth. Allah is ever Knower, Wise. .. 176 They ask thee for a pronouncement. Say: Allah hath pronounced for you concerning distant kindred. If a man die childless and he have a sister, hers is half the heritage, and he would have inherited from her had she died childless. And if there be two sisters, then theirs are two-thirds of the heritage, and if they be brethren, men and women, unto the male is the equivalent of the share of two females. Allah expoundeth unto you, so that ye err not. Allah is Knower of all things.

The heritage is divided according to the pronouncement concerning distant kindred: a sister receives half, two sisters receive two-thirds, and the male receives the equivalent of the share of two females.
