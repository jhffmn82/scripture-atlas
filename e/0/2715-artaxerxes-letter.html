A royal decree authored by Artaxerxes and addressed to Ezra the priest and scribe. It permits willing Israelites, priests, and Levites to accompany him, sends him to inquire about Judah and Jerusalem, orders supplies from treasurers, exempts temple workers from taxes, appoints magistrates and judges, and prescribes penalties for disobedience.

Chapter 7

The letter, given to Ezra the priest and scribe, decrees that willing Israelites, priests, and Levites may go with him and that he is sent by the king and his seven counselors to inquire about Judah and Jerusalem. It directs the carrying and use of silver and gold, orders the treasurers beyond the River to supply Ezra up to set amounts of silver, wheat, wine, oil, and unlimited salt, and exempts temple workers from taxes. It also instructs Ezra to appoint magistrates and judges and prescribes death, banishment, confiscation, or imprisonment for those who disobey the law of God and the king.
