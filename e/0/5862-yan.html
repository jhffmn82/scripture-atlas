Yan

place · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Yen, another country of ten thousand chariots, it, the country

Abstract written by a model

In Mencius, Book I (Liang Hui Wang), Yan is a kingdom and State conquered and occupied by Qi after its ruler tyrannized its people; Qi considered popular approval, then dismantled Yan’s ancestral temple and removed its precious vessels. In Mencius, Book II (Gong Sun Chou), Yan was treated by Mencius as a case in which Zi Kuai had no right to give it away and Zi Zhi had no right to receive it; the people of Qi smote Yan, which Mencius questioned by asking how one Yan could smite another Yan.
