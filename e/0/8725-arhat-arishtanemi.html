Arhat Arishtanemi

person · 1 work · 20 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Arishtanemi, The Arhat Arishtanemi

Abstract written by a model

Arhat Arishtanemi descended from Aparâgita into Gambûdvîpa and Bharatavarsha, took embryonic form in Queen Sivâ, and was born in Sauripura as King Samudravigaya’s son. After a life lasting a thousand years, he renounced at Revatîka with a thousand persons, followed by gods, men, and Asuras. He obtained Kevala, led eighteen Ganas and eighteen Ganadharas with large communities, then died on mount Girnâr after a month’s fast and attained final liberation.

Life of Arishtanemi.

The Arhat Arishtanemi descended from the Vimâna Aparâgita, was born in Sauripura, renounced the world with a thousand persons, and attained Kevala under a Vetasa tree on mount Girnâr. He had eighteen Ganas and eighteen Ganadharas, along with large communities of Sramanas, nuns, lay votaries, and sages.
