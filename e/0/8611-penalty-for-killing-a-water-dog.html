penalty for killing a water dog

topic · 1 work · 32 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: This is the atonement, atonement, penalty, the deed that he has done, the penalty

Abstract written by a model

The penalty for killing a water dog is an atonement made up of punishments, offerings, destruction of harmful creatures, repairs, and gifts. It begins with ten thousand stripes with the Aspahê-astra and ten thousand with the Sraoshô-karana. It requires ten thousand loads each of hard and soft wood for the fire of Ahura Mazda, consecrated baresma, Zaothra libations, and the killing or removal of many creatures and unclean holes. It then requires fire implements, silver, gold, running water, arable land, a house, beds, a virgin maid, cattle, whelps, bridges, repaired stables, and cleansed dogs. It ends with meat, bread, strong drink, and wine sufficient for twice nine godly men, all prescribed as the atonement and penalty for the deed.

Fargard XIV.

The penalty includes ten thousand stripes with the Aspahê-astra and ten thousand with the Sraoshô-karana, along with many prescribed atonements. These include offerings to the fire of Ahura Mazda, killing harmful creatures, filling holes, giving implements, providing land and buildings, and other acts.
