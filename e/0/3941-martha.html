Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Luke, Martha receives Jesus into her house, grows distracted with serving, asks him to make Mary help, and is told only one thing is needed. In The Gospel According to John, Martha serves the supper at Bethany, sends word to Jesus when Lazarus falls sick, meets him, confesses him as Christ and God's Son, calls Mary secretly, and objects at the tomb until reminded about belief.

Chapter 10

Martha receives Jesus into her house in a village. Distracted with much serving, she asks Jesus to tell her sister to help her, and Jesus replies that she is anxious and troubled about many things when only one thing is needed.

Martha in The Gospel According to John

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Martha first appears

Abstract written by a model

Martha is a woman, sister of Mary and Lazarus, who serves the supper at Bethany. When Lazarus falls sick she sends word to Jesus, meets him, confesses him as Christ and God's Son, calls Mary secretly, and objects at the tomb until reminded about belief.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

Martha, sister of Lazarus, sends word to Jesus about his sickness and goes out to meet him when he comes, telling him Lazarus would not have died had he been there. She confesses that he is the Christ, God's Son, who comes into the world, then secretly calls Mary. At the tomb she objects that there will be a stench after four days, and Jesus reminds her that belief means seeing God's glory.
