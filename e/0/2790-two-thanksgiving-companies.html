Chapter 12

Nehemiah appointed two great companies to give thanks and go in procession on the wall. One went to the right toward the dung gate, led by Hoshaiah with half of the princes of Judah, priests with trumpets, and Ezra the scribe before them. The other went to meet them with Nehemiah and half the people, and both companies then stood in God's house.
