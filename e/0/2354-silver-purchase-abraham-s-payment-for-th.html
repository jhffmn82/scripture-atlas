Chapter 23

Ephron said the land was worth four hundred shekels of silver between him and Abraham, and Abraham weighed out that amount according to the current merchants' standard. This was done in the hearing of the children of Heth, and the field, cave, and trees were then deeded to Abraham before all who went in at the city gate.
