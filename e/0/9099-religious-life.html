religious life

Also called: a religious life

Abstract written by a model

Religious life is entered after seeing suffering, to avoid misery and seek a pious end and liberation. It is led carefully through restraint, circumspection, truth, guarded conduct, obedience, repentance, detachment, meditation, renunciation, and endurance. It unfolds from recognizing sinful action’s danger to sustained disciplined practice, exemplified by Mahâvîra’s years without cold water and in difficult resting-places.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Third Lecture, Called Hot and Cold. / First Lesson. .. Fourth Lesson.

Religious life is entered by one who sees living beings suffering and understands the danger of sinful action. It is led with restraint, circumspection, truth, and guarded conduct.

Fifth Lecture, Called Essence of the World. / First Lesson. .. Sixth Lesson.

The religious life is chosen by those who seek a pious end and liberation. It requires carefulness, restraint, obedience, repentance, and freedom from worldly attachment.

Eighth Lecture, (Called) the Pillow of Righteousness. / First Lesson. .. Fourth Lesson.

Religious life is presented as a course of meditation, restraint, renunciation, and endurance. Mahâvîra led it for more than two years without using cold water and for thirteen years in difficult resting-places.
