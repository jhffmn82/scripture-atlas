Chapter 33

If an angel, an interpreter, one among a thousand, shows a man what is right, God is gracious, says to deliver him from the pit, and declares he has found a ransom. The man's flesh becomes fresher than a child's, he returns to the days of his youth, prays and is favored, sees God's face with joy, and has his righteousness restored. He then sings before men confessing his sin and says that God has redeemed his soul and his life will see the light.
