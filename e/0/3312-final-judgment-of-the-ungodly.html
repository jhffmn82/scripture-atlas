Abstract written by a model

A judgment scene for the ungodly: they arrive in coward fear as their sins are counted, and their lawless deeds convict them to their face. The Lord then dashes them speechless down, leaves them waste and anguished, their memory perishing, a dishonored carcass in lasting reproach.

Chapter 4

The ungodly will come with coward fear when their sins are counted, and their lawless deeds will convict them to their face. The Lord will dash them speechless to the ground, shake them from their foundations, leave them utterly waste and in anguish, and their memory will perish.
