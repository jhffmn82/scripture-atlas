Chapter 2

The princes are used as examples in discussions of fear, insult, and service. Bo Yi, Yi Yin, and other sages are described as capable of bringing all the princes to attend their court and obtaining the throne without unrighteous acts or killing an innocent person.

Chapter 9

Some princes sent proper messages and offered gifts to Bo Yi, but he would not receive them. Hui of Liu Xia served an impure prince.
