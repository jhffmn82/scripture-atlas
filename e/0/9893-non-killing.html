non-killing

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: not to kill anything

Abstract written by a model

Non-killing is the quintessence of wisdom and reciprocity’s legitimate conclusion. Because beings are subject to pain, it requires ceasing to injure or kill every living being, as presented first in the doctrine and again on the path.

non-killing is in one work on this site, Sutrakritanga (Sûtrakritâṅga), Book I. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
