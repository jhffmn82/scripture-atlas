King Saho-biko

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: His Augustness Saho-biko, Saho-biko, mine elder brother, the elder brother

Abstract written by a model

King Saho-biko, child of King Hiko-imasu and Saho-no-oho-kurami-tome and elder brother of Princess Saho-bime, conspired with her against Emperor Sui-Nin to rule the Empire. He made an eight times tempered stiletto for the plot, was slain by Emperor Sui-Nin with his sister, and became ancestor of Kusaka and Kahi rulers.

King Saho-biko is in one work on this site, Kojiki. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
