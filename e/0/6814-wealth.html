wealth

topic · 3 works · 6 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: bounty, property

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), wealth is given and spent for Allah’s cause, while wrongful consumption of property is warned against. In Surah 63: The Hypocrites (Al-Munaafiqoon), wealth is warned against as a distraction from remembrance of Allah, and believers are told to spend it before death. In Surah 100: The Chargers (Al-Aadiyaat), wealth is described as violently loved by man.
