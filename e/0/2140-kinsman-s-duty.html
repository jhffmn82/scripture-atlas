kinsman's duty

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: the duty of a kinsman, the kinsman's duty, the part of a kinsman

Abstract written by a model

Ruth appeals to Boaz as a near kinsman, and he affirms that he is one but that a nearer kinsman has the first claim. Boaz says that if the nearer kinsman performs the duty, good, but if he will not, Boaz will do it, swearing by the LORD's life. Naomi expects Boaz to settle the matter that same day.
