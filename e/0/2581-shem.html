Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, Shem is one of Noah's three sons, helps cover his father's nakedness, receives Noah's blessing, and fathers several sons and descendants. In 1 Chronicles, Shem is a father of sons including Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, and Aram, with the line through Arpachshad leading to Terah and Abram, called Abraham.

Shem in Genesis

author unknown · about 449 BC · the work’s page · read it where Shem first appears

Abstract written by a model

Shem is one of Noah's three sons and elder brother of Japheth. He leaves the ship, helps cover his father's nakedness while walking backwards, and receives Noah's blessing of the LORD as his God. He fathers Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad (at age 100), Lud and Aram, and is father of all Eber's children, with descendants listed.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 9

Shem is named as one of Noah's sons who left the ship. Along with Japheth, he covers his father's nakedness with a garment, walking in backwards so as not to see it, and Noah blesses the LORD as the God of Shem and says Japheth will dwell in Shem's tents.

Chapter 10

Shem is called the elder brother of Japheth and the father of all the children of Eber. His sons were Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, and Aram, and his descendants are listed with their families, languages, lands, and nations.

Abstract written by a model

Shem is a father of sons Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Aram and others; the line through Arpachshad runs down to Terah and Abram, called Abraham.

1 Chronicles / Chapter 1

Shem's sons are listed as Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, Aram, Uz, Hul, Gether, and Meshech. The line through Arpachshad, Shelah, Eber, Peleg, Reu, Serug, Nahor and Terah leads to Abram, also called Abraham.
