Life of Arishtanemi.

Arishtanemi took the form of an embryo in the womb of queen Sivâ, wife of king Samudravigaya, in Sauripura. After nine months and seven and a half days, Sivâ gave birth to a perfectly healthy boy named Arishtanemi.
