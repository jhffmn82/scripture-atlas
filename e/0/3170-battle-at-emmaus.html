Chapter 4

Gorgias' night march finds Judas' camp empty, and Judas' three thousand appear in the plain at daybreak and defeat the Gentile army, pursuing it as far as Gazara, Idumaea, Azotus, and Jamnia. About three thousand fall, the camp is burned and plundered, and Gorgias' men flee to the land of the Philistines.
