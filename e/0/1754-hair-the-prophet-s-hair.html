Chapter 5

The hair shaved from the prophet's head and beard is weighed and divided into thirds as a sign for Jerusalem's people. One third is burned in the city, one third is struck with the sword, and one third is scattered to the wind. A small number of strands is bound in his robe, and some of those are cast into the fire, from which fire will spread to all the house of Israel.
