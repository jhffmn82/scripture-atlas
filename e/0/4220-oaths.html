oaths in The Gospel According to Matthew

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where oaths first appears

Abstract written by a model

Oaths are a teaching of Jesus: swearing is forbidden entirely. After recalling not to make false vows but keep vows to the Lord, he bans swearing by heaven, earth, Jerusalem, or one's head, requiring plain Yes and No.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

Jesus recalls the instruction not to make false vows but to perform vows to the Lord. He tells his hearers not to swear at all, whether by heaven, earth, Jerusalem, or their own head. Their 'Yes' should be 'Yes' and their 'No' 'No,' since anything more is of the evil one.
