congregations of saints

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: saints, the saints

Abstract written by a model

Congregations of saints are groups of saints who are ever happy, rejoicing with the True One in their hearts. They obtain salvation through hearing God's Name, which frees them from sorrow and sin. They ponder God's attributes and divine knowledge and sing God with love. They dwell in the true realm, within realms where divine knowledge, beauty, incomparable fashioning, force, and greatness are described. God dwells in the true realm, looks on its denizens with favour, and renders them happy. The account begins with their happiness and salvation through hearing God's Name, then places them amid divine knowledge and loving song, and ends with their dwelling and rejoicing in the true realm under God's favour.

Japji .. 36 In the realm of knowledge the light of divine knowledge is resplendent. There are heard songs from which millions of joys and pleasures proceed. Beauty is the attribute of the realm of happiness. There things are fashioned in an incomparable manner. What is done there cannot be described. Whoever endeavoureth to describe it shall afterwards repent. There are fashioned knowledge, wisdom, intellect, and understanding; And there too is fashioned the skill of demigods and men of supernatural power.
