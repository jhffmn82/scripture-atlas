Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, Reuben is Jacob and Leah's firstborn who opposes the plot to kill Joseph but is condemned by Jacob for defiling his father's bed. In 1 Chronicles, Reuben is Israel's firstborn who loses his birthright to Joseph's sons after defiling his father's couch.

Reuben in Genesis

author unknown · about 449 BC · the work’s page · read it where Reuben first appears

Abstract written by a model

Reuben is Leah's son and Jacob's firstborn, with sons including Hanoch. He lies with his father's concubine Bilhah, and Israel hears of it. Later he opposes his brothers' plot to kill Joseph, urging them to throw him in a pit so he can restore him to his father; finding the pit empty, he tears his clothes in distress. Finally Jacob calls him his might and strength's beginning, but because he defiled his father's bed, declares him unstable and unable to excel.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 35

Reuben goes and lies with Bilhah, his father's concubine, and Israel hears of it. He is listed as Jacob's firstborn and a son of Leah.

Chapter 37

Reuben hears the plot to kill Joseph and objects, urging them to shed no blood and to throw him into the pit instead, intending to restore him to his father. When he returns and finds the pit empty, he tears his clothes and says to his brothers, "The child is no more; and I, where will I go?"

Chapter 49

Jacob calls Reuben his firstborn, his might and the beginning of his strength, excelling in dignity and power. Because Reuben went up to his father's bed and defiled it, Jacob says he is boiling over like water and will not excel.

Reuben in 1 Chronicles

author unknown · 449 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Reuben first appears

Abstract written by a model

Reuben, firstborn of Israel, defiled his father's couch and so lost his birthright to Joseph's sons; consequently his genealogy isn't listed by birthright, though his sons Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi are named.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

Reuben is identified as the firstborn of Israel who lost his birthright to the sons of Joseph because he defiled his father's couch. His genealogy is therefore not listed according to the birthright, and his sons are named as Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.
