Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Jeremiah, Elam is condemned to have its bow broken, its people scattered, and its king and princes destroyed, with its captivity later reversed. In The Book of Ezekiel, Elam is a slain, uncircumcised multitude in Sheol, bearing shame among those in the pit.

Elam in The Book of Jeremiah

author unknown · 629 to 579 BC · the work’s page · read it where Elam first appears

Abstract written by a model

Elam, a people and land whose chief strength is its bow, is condemned in a word to Jeremiah: the LORD will break its bow, scatter its people to every nation, destroy its king and princes, then reverse its captivity in the latter days.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 49

In the word given to Jeremiah at the start of Zedekiah's reign, the LORD says he will break Elam's bow, scatter its people to the four winds, and send the sword after them until they are consumed. He will set his throne in Elam and destroy its king and princes. In the latter days he will reverse Elam's captivity.
