Chapter 5

Judgment comes as a stripped and wasted vineyard, desolate houses, meager harvests, famine and thirst, captivity, and Sheol swallowing the proud. The people are consumed like stubble in fire, the mountains tremble, dead bodies lie as refuse in the streets, and the LORD's anger is not turned away. The chapter closes with invasion and a land of darkness and distress.
