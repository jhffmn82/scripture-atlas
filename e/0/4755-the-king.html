Abstract written by a model

The king is praised as the most excellent of the sons of men, with grace on his lips and blessed by God forever. His throne endures forever and ever, ruled by an equitable scepter. God anointed him with the oil of gladness above his fellows because he loves righteousness and hates wickedness. He is fragrant with myrrh, aloes, and cassia, dwells in ivory palaces, and is attended by honorable women, with the queen at his right hand. He rides victoriously against enemies, and his sons will become princes in all the earth.

Chapter 45

The king is praised as the most excellent of the sons of men, with grace on his lips, and is called to ride on victoriously for truth, humility, and righteousness with sharp arrows that make the nations fall. His throne is declared forever and ever, and God, his God, has anointed him with the oil of gladness above his fellows because he loved righteousness and hated wickedness. He is attended by honorable women, will receive the princess and her companions, and is promised sons who will be princes in all the earth and a name remembered in all generations.
