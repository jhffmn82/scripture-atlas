Abstract written by a model

The mountains of Israel, with hills, watercourses and valleys, are addressed by the Lord GOD in prophecy. In Chapter 6 they face judgment: a sword comes and the slain lie among idols on the high places. In Chapter 36, having borne the nations' mockery and shame, they are promised restoration: branches, fruit, tilling, inhabitants, and an end to bereaving their nation, becoming Israel's inheritance. In Chapter 39 they are the site where Gog and his hordes fall, and where the great sacrifice feeds birds and animals.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

The mountains of Israel, along with the hills, watercourses, and valleys, are addressed by the prophecy and told to hear the word of the Lord GOD. A sword will come on them, and the slain will lie among the idols on every high hill and on all the mountaintops.

Chapter 36

The mountains of Israel are addressed by the prophet and told to hear the LORD's word, having been mocked by the enemy and made a prey and derision. The Lord GOD promises they will shoot out branches and yield fruit, be tilled and sown, be inhabited by multiplied men and animals, and never again devour or bereave their nation or bear the shame of the nations.

Chapter 39

The mountains of Israel are the place where the Lord GOD brings Gog and where Gog and his hordes fall. They are also the site of the great sacrifice to which the birds and animals are invited to eat flesh and drink blood.
