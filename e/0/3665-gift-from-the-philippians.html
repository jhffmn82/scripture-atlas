Chapter 4

The gift was brought by Epaphroditus and received by Paul, who calls it a sweet-smelling fragrance and an acceptable, well-pleasing sacrifice to God. Paul says he does not seek the gift itself but the fruit that increases to the Philippians' account, and he notes they had also sent aid more than once in Thessalonica.
