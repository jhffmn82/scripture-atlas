Chapter 12

The prince is the subject of the burden the LORD explains. He will carry his baggage on his shoulder in the dark, go out through a hole dug in the wall, and cover his face so he will not see the land. The LORD will catch him in a net and snare and bring him to Babylon, the land of the Chaldeans, where he will die without seeing it. His helpers and bands will be scattered to every wind.
