religious life

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: that inseparable from a religious life, thirteenth kind of acquiring Karman

Abstract written by a model

Religious life is the self-controlled, chaste practice of monks: avoiding harm, speaking gently, carefully receiving alms, and guarding mind, speech, body, and senses. It leads toward freedom from misery, while subtle actions acquire îryapathika Karman, experienced and destroyed within three moments.

Second Lecture, Called on Activity.

Religious life involves controlling oneself, avoiding harm to living creatures, using gentle and righteous speech, receiving alms carefully, guarding mind, speech, and body, controlling the senses, and leading a chaste life. Even a monk’s subtle movements produce îryapathika Karman that is acquired, experienced, and destroyed within three moments.
