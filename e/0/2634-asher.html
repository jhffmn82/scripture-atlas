Abstract written by a model

Asher is a patriarch whose sons Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi and Beriah, and sister Serah, begin the line. His descendants become heads of fathers' houses, mighty men of valor, listed by genealogy as 26,000 war-ready fighting men.

Chapter 7

Asher's sons were Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, and Beriah, and Serah was their sister. His descendants, through Beriah, Heber, Japhlet, Shemer, Helem, and others, are listed as heads of fathers' houses and choice mighty men of valor, numbering twenty-six thousand men for war.
