Abstract written by a model

Sodar and So Purukh present hymns by Guru Nanak, Guru Ram Das, and Guru Arjan praising God as eternal, supreme Creator, sovereign, and present in every heart. All beings and worlds sing His greatness, while He sustains creatures and determines events. Remembering and meditating on God’s true Name brings life, satisfaction, happiness, fearlessness, and liberation; forgetting it through worldly attachment brings misery and spiritual loss. The hymns urge service, the true Guru’s protection, saintly company, and using human life to meet God and cross the terrible ocean.

Sodar

Sodar presents hymns by Guru Nanak, Guru Ram Das, and Guru Arjan concerning God, His greatness, His Name, creation, and devotion. Guru Nanak describes all beings and worlds singing God’s praises and says that God is eternal, sovereign, and beyond description. The hymns teach that remembering the true Name gives life and satisfaction, while forgetting it brings spiritual loss. Guru Ram Das and Guru Arjan emphasize the protection of the true Guru, the company of the saints, and God’s provision for all creatures.

So Purukh

This unit concerns God as the pure, infinite, and supreme Creator who pervades every heart, sustains all creatures, and determines what comes to pass. Guru Ram Das teaches that those who meditate on God, serve Him, and receive His kindness gain happiness, freedom from fear, and absorption in God's name. Guru Nanak confesses human weakness and warns that worldly love causes humanity to drown in forgetfulness and misery. Guru Arjan urges humanity to use the human body to meet God, join the saints, repeat God's name, and prepare to cross the terrible ocean.
