Abstract written by a model

A contest at the pool of Gibeon, proposed by Abner, pitted twelve young men for Benjamin and Ishbosheth against twelve of David's servants. Each seized his opponent and stabbed him, so all fell together, naming the place Helkath Hazzurim. A very severe battle followed, in which David's servants beat Abner and Israel.

Chapter 2

Abner proposed that young men compete before them at the pool of Gibeon, and twelve for Benjamin and Ishbosheth faced twelve of David's servants. Each seized his opponent and thrust a sword into his side, so all fell together, and the place was named Helkath Hazzurim. A very severe battle followed that day, in which Abner and the men of Israel were beaten by David's servants.
