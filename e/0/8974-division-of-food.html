Abstract written by a model

Division of food is an impartial, equal distribution: a monk or nun avoids selecting disproportionately large, preferred vegetables or conspicuous, savoury, delicious, nice, or big things, without eagerness, desire, greed, or covetousness.

Fifth Lesson.

The division of food must be impartial. The monk or nun must not select too great a portion, vegetables, conspicuous, savoury, delicious, nice, or big things, and must not be eager, desirous, greedy, or covetous.
