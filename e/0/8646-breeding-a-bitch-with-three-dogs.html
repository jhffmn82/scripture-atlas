Abstract written by a model

A rite for breeding a bitch to produce strong offspring: worshippers of Mazda isolate her in an earth hole, keep three dogs apart, and let them approach her one after another under watch.

Fargard XV.

To have a bitch covered so that the offspring is strong, worshippers of Mazda dig a hole in the earth, keep the bitch far from children and Fire, and let three dogs approach her one after another while watching her.
