unruly men (deceivers in Crete)

Abstract written by a model

Unruly men in Crete are many: vain talkers and deceivers, especially of the circumcision. Their minds and consciences are defiled and unbelieving. They overthrow whole houses by teaching wrongly for dishonest gain, so their mouths must be stopped. They claim to know God yet deny him by deeds, being abominable, disobedient, and unfit for good work.

Letter to Titus / Chapter 1

Unruly men are described as many in number, vain talkers and deceivers, especially those of the circumcision. Their mouths must be stopped because they overthrow whole houses, teaching what they ought not for dishonest gain. They profess to know God but deny him by their deeds, and are called abominable, disobedient, and unfit for any good work.
