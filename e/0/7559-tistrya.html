Tistrya

Abstract written by a model

In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, Tistrya brings waters, rain, fertility, health, and protection, grants boons, and defeats forces threatening the waters. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Tistrya's movement brings increase, multiplying horses, flocks, and virtuous offspring. In Nyayish, Tistrya is honored as a bright, glorious star through sacrifices and rains.

Tistrya in Yashts (Yasts) I-XII

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Tistrya first appears

Abstract written by a model

Tistrya is a bright, glorious star and powerful seed of the waters, bringing waters, rain, fertility, health, and protection to the material world. A lord of a thousand boons, he grants male children, oxen, and horses to those offering libations with Haoma and holy meat. Traveling from the shining east by a watery way, he appears successively as a fifteen-year-old man, a golden-horned bull, and a white horse. At Vouru-Kasha he fights and overcomes Daêva Apaosha, drives back Pairikas, binds Duzyâirya, boils the sea, and sends vapours, clouds, rain, and waters toward the seven Karshvares.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

VIII. Tîr Yast.

Tistrya is the bright and glorious star who brings waters, rain, fertility, health, and protection to the material world. He battles and defeats the Daêva Apaosha, makes the sea Vouru-Kasha boil, and sends vapours, clouds, rain, and waters toward the seven Karshvares.

Tistrya in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

Abstract written by a model

Tistrya is a bright, glorious star whose movement brings increase, multiplying horses, flocks, and virtuous offspring, and is hailed with Mazda’s strong wind and Aryas’ Glory.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

XVIII. Âstâd Yast.

Tistrya is the bright, glorious star whose movement is associated with the multiplication of horses, flocks, and virtuous offspring. He is also associated with the bringing of increase and is hailed alongside the strong wind made by Mazda and the Glory of the Aryas.
