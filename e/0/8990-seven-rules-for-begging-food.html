Abstract written by a model

The seven rules govern accepting begged food, considering the mendicant’s hand and vessel, required cleaning, the giver’s wet hands, food from the ground, usable portions, and others’ wishes.

Eleventh Lesson.

The seven rules for begging food govern the acceptance of food according to the condition of the mendicant's hand and vessel, the amount of cleaning required, the moisture on the giver's hands, whether the food was taken from the ground, and whether only part of it is usable or wanted by others.
