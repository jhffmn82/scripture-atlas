Mih Tzŭ

person · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Mih, Mih Tzŭ himself

Abstract written by a model

Mih Tzŭ, a poor, simple, well-meaning man of genius and follower of TAO, taught universal love and beneficence without contention. He wrote Against Music and Economy, rejected music, singing, and elaborate mourning, and advocated toil and self-mortification. Though judged partly right, his excessive practice was condemned as unlike the true Sage’s TAO.

CHAPTER XXXIII.

Mih Tzŭ taught universal love and beneficence without contention, rejected singing and elaborate mourning, and advocated toil and self-mortification. His theory was considered well-meaning and right in part, but his practice was excessive and unlike the TAO of the true Sage.
