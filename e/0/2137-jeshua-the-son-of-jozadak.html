Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Ezra, Jeshua, son of Jozadak, is a priest who helps build the altar, offer burnt offerings, and oversee the house's work. In The Book of Haggai, Joshua, son of Jehozadak, is the high priest who obeys the LORD and joins the work on the house. In The Book of Zechariah, Joshua the son of Jehozadak is the high priest cleansed, commissioned, and crowned in visions concerning the LORD's house and the Branch.

Joshua the son of Jehozadak in The Book of Zechariah

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Joshua the son of Jehozadak first appears

Abstract written by a model

Joshua is a high priest who first appears standing before the LORD's angel in filthy garments, opposed by Satan yet called a burning stick plucked from the fire. His garments are replaced with rich clothing and a clean turban, and he is promised authority to judge and keep the LORD's house if he walks in the LORD's ways. He and his fellows are a sign. Later, crowns of silver and gold from the exiles Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah are to be set on his head, with a message about the Branch.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Joshua stands before the LORD's angel in filthy garments while Satan opposes him, and is called a burning stick plucked out of the fire. His garments are exchanged for rich clothing and a clean turban, and he is told that if he walks in the LORD's ways and follows his instructions he will judge and keep the LORD's house and courts. He and his fellows are described as men who are a sign.

Chapter 6

Joshua, the high priest, is to have crowns of silver and gold set on his head, taken from the exiles Heldai, Tobijah, and Jedaiah. He is to be told the message about the man called the Branch.
