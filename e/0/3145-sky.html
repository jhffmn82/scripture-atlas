sky

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the heavens are called to praise the LORD along with angels, his army, the sun, moon, shining stars, and the waters above them. In Psalms, the heavens are described as the work of the LORD's fingers, containing the moon and the stars which he ordained. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, the clear sky is called the pride of the heavenly heights and the appearance of heaven in the spectacle of its glory. In Revelation, heaven is the setting of the signs of the woman and the dragon and of the war in which the dragon and his angels lose their place.
