the devil

person · 4 works · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Your adversary, the devil, a devil, adversary, an enemy, devil, devils, his comrade, your open foe

Abstract written by a model

In 1 Peter, the devil is the believers' adversary, likened to a roaring lion prowling to devour, whom believers are urged to resist steadfastly in faith. In Surah 20: Taa-Haa, the devil whispers to Adam about the tree of immortality and power, leading Adam and his wife to eat from it and become aware of their shame. In Surah 36: Yaseen (Yaseen), the devil is described as an open foe of the sons of Adam who has led a great multitude astray. In Surah 43: Ornaments of gold (Az-Zukhruf), devils turn people from Allah's way through companions assigned to those who are dim to the remembrance of the Beneficent.
