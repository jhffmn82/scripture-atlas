guidance

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a right path, better guidance, the right path

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 43: Ornaments of gold (Az-Zukhruf), guidance comes through Allah's revelations and messengers, and Muhammad is told that he is on a right path. In Surah 48: The Victory (Al-Fath), guidance leads Muhammad and is promised to the believers alongside the religion of truth.
