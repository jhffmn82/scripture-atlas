Also called: the priest

Abstract written by a model

In Leviticus, the priest assesses values for vows, dedicated animals, unclean animals, houses, and fields; his evaluation stands, adjusts field value by years remaining to the Jubilee, and unredeemed fields become priestly property. In Deuteronomy, the priest serving in those days receives the worshipper's profession that he has come into the promised land, takes the basket from the worshipper's hand, and sets it down before the altar of the LORD.

priest in Leviticus

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where priest first appears

Abstract written by a model

The priest is an official who assesses values for vows, dedicated animals, unclean animals, houses, and fields; his evaluation stands, adjusts field value by years remaining to the Jubilee, and unredeemed fields become priestly property.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 27

The priest assesses the value of the poor person's vow, unclean animals, houses, and fields, and his evaluation stands. He reckons the reduced value of fields by the years remaining to the Jubilee, and fields not redeemed come to be owned by the priests.
