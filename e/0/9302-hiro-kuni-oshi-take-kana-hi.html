Sect. CXXXIV.—Emperor Kan-an.

Hiro-kuni-oshi-take-kana-hi was Emperor Kan-an and a Heavenly Monarch. He dwelt in the Palace of Kanahashi at Magari, ruled the Empire, had no august children, and had his august grave at the village of Takaya in Furuchi in Kafuchi.
