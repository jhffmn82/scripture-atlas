Abstract written by a model

A corrupt, arrogant group who invert good and evil, consider themselves wise, and acquit the guilty for bribes while denying the innocent justice. Having rejected the LORD of Armies' law and despised the Holy One's word, they are condemned with woes; their root will rot and their blossom turn to dust.

Chapter 5

Woes are pronounced on those who draw iniquity with cords of falsehood, mock the Holy One's work, call evil good and good evil, and are wise in their own eyes. Also condemned are those who acquit the guilty for a bribe and deny justice to the innocent. Because they rejected the law of the LORD of Armies and despised the word of the Holy One of Israel, their root will rot and their blossom go up as dust.
