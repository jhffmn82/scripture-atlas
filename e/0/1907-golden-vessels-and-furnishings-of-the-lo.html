Abstract written by a model

Golden vessels and furnishings made by Solomon for the LORD's house, all of pure gold. They begin with the altar and show-bread table, then ten lamp stands, five per side before the inner sanctuary, plus flowers, lamps, tongs, cups, basins, spoons, fire pans, and door hinges.

Chapter 7

Solomon made the golden altar, the gold table for the show bread, ten lamp stands of pure gold (five on each side before the inner sanctuary), and gold flowers, lamps, and tongs. He also made cups, snuffers, basins, spoons, and fire pans of pure gold, and gold hinges for the doors of the most holy place and the temple.
