Heavenly-Alarming-Female

person · 1 work · 14 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Heavenly-Alarming Female, Heavenly-Alarming-Female-Deity, Her Augustness Heavenly-Alarming-Female, Her Augustness the Heavenly-Alarming-Female

Abstract written by a model

Heavenly-Alarming-Female is a female Deity, a delicate yet deity-conquering Her Augustness, one of five chiefs of companies and ancestress of the Duchesses of Saru. Wearing heavenly clubmoss as a sash, a heavenly spindle-tree head-dress, and bamboo-grass as a posy, she performs before the Heavenly Rock-Dwelling as if possessed. The performance shakes the Plain of High Heaven and makes the eight hundred myriad Deities laugh, causing Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity to open the door slightly; she claims greater illustriousness. She questions a radiant Deity, descends among the five chiefs, escorts Deity Prince of Saruta back, bears his august name, gathers fishes for the august son of the Heavenly Deities, and cuts the silent bèche-de-mer’s mouth.

Heavenly-Alarming-Female is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Sect.

The Heavenly-Alarming Female hangs heavenly clubmoss around herself, wears a heavenly spindle-tree as a head-dress, binds bamboo-grass in a posy, and performs before the door as if possessed by a Deity. Her performance makes the Plain of High Heaven shake and causes the eight hundred myriad Deities to laugh, leading the Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity to open the door slightly.

Sect.

The Heavenly-Alarming-Female-Deity is commanded to question the Deity whose refulgence reaches from the Plain of High Heaven to the Central Land of Reed-Plains. She asks who he is and later descends among the five chiefs of companies.

Sect.

The Heavenly-Alarming-Female is charged with respectfully escorting the Great Deity Prince of Saruta back. She is also charged with bearing his august name and respectfully serving the speaker.

Sect.

The Heavenly-Alarming-Female escorts the Deity Prince of Saruta back. She gathers the fishes, asks them to serve the august son of the Heavenly Deities, and slits the mouth of the bèche-de-mer when it gives no reply.
