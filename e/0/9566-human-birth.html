Abstract written by a model

Human birth is a difficult, paramount and rare chance for a living being, attained in due time through cessation of Karman. Even after becoming human, becoming an Ârya, possessing five senses, receiving, believing, and practising the best Law remain rare.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Third Lecture.

Human birth is difficult to obtain. By the cessation of Karman, living beings may in due time become men.

Tenth Lecture.

Human birth is described as a rare chance in the long course of time for a living being. Even after human birth, becoming an Ârya, possessing all five organs of sense, receiving instruction in the best Law, believing it, and practising it are each described as rare.
