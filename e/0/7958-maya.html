mammon in Hymns of Guru Nanak

Guru Nanak · the work’s page · read it where mammon first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mammon is the evil, beloved worldly attachment to wealth, desire, and possessions. Spread as a net, it bewitches and deludes humanity, drawing the mind in every direction. It produces covetousness, greed, pride, superstition, false or counterfeit conduct, anxiety, unhappiness, and forgetfulness of the Name; it ruins the virtuous woman separated from her Spouse and absorbs one whose heart is filthy and hard. Those who make it their friend cannot recognize the real treasure within, obtain the water of life, or reach God's mansion. Attached people wander in the ocean of the world, face Death's gate and transmigration, and finally die and blend with dust. Its attraction is ultimately exposed, set aside, and must be abandoned with its entanglements.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Sri Rag .. Ashtapadi

Mammon ruins the woman separated from her Spouse and leads man into pride, worldly love, wandering, and counterfeit conduct. Those under mammon fail to recognize that the real treasure is within and suffer in the ocean of the world.

Rag Asa .. Asa Ashtapadi

Mammon represents the evil way of attachment to wealth, desire, and worldly possessions. Those who pursue mammon forget the Name, become greedy and proud, and are ultimately plundered and deprived of spiritual benefit.

Asa Chhant

Mammon is loved by the world and creates entanglement and mental anxiety. Its attraction is ultimately exposed and set aside.

Rag Sorath .. Sorath Ashtapadi

Mammon causes greed, worldly attachment, error, and spiritual ruin. The text teaches that devotion to mammon cannot obtain the water of life and that humanity must abandon mammon and its entanglements.

Rag Suhi .. Suhi ki War

Mammon causes people to become absorbed in superstition, false pride, and worldly attachment. Those who make mammon their friend are lost and ultimately die and blend with dust.

Ramkali .. Ramkali ki War I

Mammon draws the mind in every direction and causes people to forget the Name. Those tinctured with mammon do not reach God's mansion.
