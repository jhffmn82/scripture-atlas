divine judgment

topic · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 0 connections
Also called: In the judgment of the Lord, judgment of God, judgment of the Lord, my judgment, the judgment of the Lord

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Job, it is presented as the LORD's judgment that Job challenges and is asked to answer for. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, it is described as the Lord judging each man according to his works, with examples and limits illustrating its scope.

divine judgment in The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus

author unknown · about 179 BC · the work’s page · read it where divine judgment first appears

Abstract written by a model

has_boundaries_set his works [determined from their making] The Lord judges a man according to his works, and each man will receive according to his works. Past examples include the giants, Lot's neighbors, the people of perdition, and the six hundred thousand footmen. The text rejects the idea that anyone can hide from this judgment or go unremembered, and notes that the Lord's covenant and righteous works are hard to comprehend and seem afar off.
