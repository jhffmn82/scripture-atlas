oppression

Also called: all the oppressions that are done under the sun, oppressed, oppressions, oppressors

Abstract written by a model

In Ecclesiastes or, The Preacher, oppression occurs under the sun and leads the speaker to judge the dead and those never born as better off than the living. In The Second Book of Nephi, oppression affects the people through conflict among individuals and neighbors and through oppression by children.
