Her Augustness Princess Okinaga-tarashi

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Empress, Empress Jin-Gō, Her Augustness Okinaga-tarashi, Princess Okinaga-tarashi, This Empress

Abstract written by a model

Empress Jin-Gō, Princess Okinaga-tarashi, is divinely possessed and promised Korea for her unborn child. She crosses to Korea, gains Shiragi and Kudara’s submission, subdues Yamato, gives birth in Tsukushi, is mother to named children, and dies aged one hundred, buried at Tatanami.
