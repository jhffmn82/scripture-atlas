Twenty-Second Lecture.

Vasudêva is a powerful king of Sauryapura and the father of Râma and Kêsava. He gives Arishtanêmi his blessings after Arishtanêmi plucks out his hair, and later says to Râgîmatî that she should cross the ocean of Samsâra.
