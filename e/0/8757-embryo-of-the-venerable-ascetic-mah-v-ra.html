embryo of the Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra (in Trisalâ's womb)

object · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: embryo, embryo (of the Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra), fruit of my womb, her child, the embryo, the embryo of Trisalâ, the embryo of the Kshatriyânî Trisalâ

Abstract written by a model

Mahâvîra’s embryo is first placed in Devânandâ’s womb, then removed during a transfer and placed in Trisalâ’s womb. Trisalâ carries it during pregnancy; the embryo remains motionless compassionately to spare her distress.
