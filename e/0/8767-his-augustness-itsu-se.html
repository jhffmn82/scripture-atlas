His Augustness Itsu-se

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Itsu-se, elder brother of His Augustness Kamu-yamato-ihare-biko, joins the eastward progress, is wounded in the hand by Nagasune-biko of Tomi, dies valiantly at the river-mouth of Wo, and is buried at Mount Kama in Ki.
