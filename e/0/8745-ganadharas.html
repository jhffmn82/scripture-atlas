Ganadharas

group · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: all these eleven Ganadharas, eleven Ganadharas, the Ganadharas

Abstract written by a model

Ganadharas were eleven disciples of the Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra, knowing twelve Aṅgas, fourteen Pûrvas, and the Siddhânta of the Ganins. After commencing Paggusan, they died following a month’s fast without water and left no descendants.

List of the Sthaviras.

The Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra had eleven Ganadharas, who knew the twelve Aṅgas, the fourteen Pûrvas, and the whole Siddhânta of the Ganins. They died after fasting for a month without drinking water, and the text states that the other Ganadharas left no descendants.
