hospitality toward traveling brothers

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: a faithful work, send them forward on their journey

Abstract written by a model

John commends Gaius for his faithful work and love toward brothers and strangers and urges him to send them forward in a way worthy of God. He says such people ought to be received so that believers may be fellow workers for the truth, in contrast to Diotrephes, who refuses them and expels those who welcome them.
