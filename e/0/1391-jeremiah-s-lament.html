Chapter 20

Jeremiah laments that the LORD has overpowered him, that he is mocked daily, and that his proclamations of violence and destruction bring reproach. He says he cannot keep silent because the LORD's word burns like fire in his bones. The lament closes with curses on the day of his birth and on the man who announced it, and the question of why he came from the womb to see labor, sorrow, and shame.
