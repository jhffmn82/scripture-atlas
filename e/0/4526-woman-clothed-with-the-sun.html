Abstract written by a model

A woman appearing as a great heavenly sign, clothed with the sun, the moon under her feet, and a crown of twelve stars. She cries out in labor and bears a son. She then flees to a place God prepared in the wilderness, nourished 1,260 days, aided by eagle wings and the earth, and has further offspring.

Chapter 12

She appears as a great sign in heaven, clothed with the sun, with the moon under her feet and a crown of twelve stars, and cries out in labor. After she bears a son, she flees to a place God prepared in the wilderness, where she is nourished for 1,260 days, a time, times, and half a time. She is given two wings of the great eagle and is helped by the earth, which swallows the river the serpent spews after her.
