Abstract written by a model

A mango has peel, rind, and sap. For monks or nuns, it may be taken only when free from eggs and living beings and nibbled by animals or injured; mangoes covered with eggs or living beings, or uninjured and untouched, are impure and prohibited.

Seventh Lecture, Called Regulation of Possession. / First Lesson. .. Second Lesson.

A monk or nun may take a mango only when it is free from eggs and living beings and has been nibbled by animals or injured. A mango that is covered with eggs or living beings, or is uninjured and untouched by animals, is considered impure and must not be taken.
