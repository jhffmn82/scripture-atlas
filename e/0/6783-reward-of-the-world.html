reward of the world

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: reward of the world and the Hereafter

Abstract written by a model

The reward of the world belongs to Allah and is linked with the Hereafter. The record says those who desire it should know it is with Allah, who is ever Hearer and Seer, within a passage urging just treatment of female orphans and weak children.

reward of the world is in one work on this site, Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
