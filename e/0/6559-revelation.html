revelation

topic · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: The revelation, a fresh reminder, a revelation, revealed, revelations, that which is revealed

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), revelation comes from Allah as a Reminder and warning, telling mankind of His oneness, judgment, punishment, and mercy. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), revelation comes from the Lord of the Worlds through the True Spirit upon Muhammad's heart in plain Arabic speech and is found in the Scriptures of earlier peoples. In Surah 39: The Groups (Az-Zumar), revelation comes from Allah, commands pure worship, warns of doom, offers guidance, and calls people to repentance before punishment arrives.
