Abstract written by a model

A young Levite from Bethlehem Judah who leaves his city seeking a place to live and reaches Micah's house in Ephraim. Micah hires him as father and priest for silver, clothing, and food, and consecrates him. When five Danite men recognize him, he tells them their way is before the LORD. Invited to be priest to a whole tribe, he gladly takes the ephod, teraphim, and image and goes with them. He is identified as Jonathan, son of Gershom, son of Moses, serving the Danites until the captivity.

Chapter 17

A young Levite from Bethlehem Judah, of the family of Judah, leaves the city to find a place to live and comes to Micah's house in Ephraim. Micah invites him to be a father and priest for ten pieces of silver per year, a suit of clothing, and food. The Levite is content, is treated as one of Micah's sons, and is consecrated as his priest.

Chapter 18

The young Levite was recognized by the five men, who asked how he came to be there. He explained that Micah had hired him, and told them their way was before the LORD. When the Danites invited him to be priest to a tribe rather than one man's house, his heart was glad and he took the ephod, teraphim, and engraved image and went with them. He is identified as Jonathan, son of Gershom, son of Moses, who with his sons served as priests to the Danites until the captivity of the land.
