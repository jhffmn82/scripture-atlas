those who believe and do good works

group · 4 works · 11 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: a believer, believers, the believers who do good works, those who believe, those who believe in Allah, and hold fast unto Him, those who believed and did good works

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), those who believe and do good works are brought into Gardens of Paradise beneath which rivers flow, abiding there forever without being wronged. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), those who believed and did good works are promised their wages in full together with an additional gift from Allah’s bounty. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), the believers who do good works are excepted from the condemnation of the poets because they believe, remember Allah much, and vindicate themselves after being wronged. In Surah 35: The Originator (Faatir), believers who do good works are promised forgiveness and a great reward, including imperishable gain, full wages, and increase from Allah’s grace. In Surah 98: The Evidence (Al-Bayyina), those who believe and do good works are described as the best of created beings and receive reward with their Lord in Gardens of Eden.
