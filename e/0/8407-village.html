Yasna LXVIII (Sp. LXVII).

The village from which the Zaothras come is the subject of a benediction for a safe, quiet, joyful, and long abode. Every Mazdayasnian village is likewise given a prayer for safety, joy, longevity, succour, salutations of salvation, and praises.
