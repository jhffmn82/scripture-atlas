Abstract written by a model

Love is described as better than wine, both by the Beloved speaking of her lover's love and by the Friends who say they will praise it more than wine. The virgins love the lover because his name is like oil poured out, and the Beloved says they are right to love him. The Beloved addresses him as the one whom her soul loves, and the Lover calls her "my love."
