Abstract written by a model

A first-person speaker who has loved wisdom since youth and sought her as a bride, expecting counsel, comfort, glory, honor, rule, and immortality. Describing himself as a clever child with a good soul and undefiled body, he concludes wisdom must be given by God, and so prays to the Lord wholeheartedly.

Chapter 8

The speaker says he loved wisdom from his youth, sought her as a bride, and resolved to live with her as a source of counsel and comfort. He anticipates glory, honor among elders, admiration from rulers, rule over peoples, and immortality through her. He describes himself as a clever child with a good soul who came into an undefiled body, and concludes that he can have wisdom only if God gives her, so he pleads with the Lord with his whole heart.
