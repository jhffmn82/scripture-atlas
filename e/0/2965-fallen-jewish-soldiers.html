Chapter 12

Some Jews fell in the battle against Gorgias. When Judas's men gathered the bodies to bring them to their ancestors' tombs, they found idol tokens from Jamnia under each one's garments. The army prayed that the sin be blotted out, and an offering was sent to Jerusalem on their behalf.
