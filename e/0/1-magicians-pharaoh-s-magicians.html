Abstract written by a model

Wise men and sorcerers summoned by Pharaoh who use enchantments to imitate signs: they match rods-to-serpents, water-to-blood and frogs, though Aaron's rod swallows theirs, and they fail at lice, leaving Pharaoh hardened.

Chapter 7
