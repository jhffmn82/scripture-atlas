the folk of Lot

group · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: The folk of Lot, his people

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 11: Hud, the folk of Lot practiced abominations, rejected Lot's appeal to fear Allah, were overthrown, and had stones of clay rained upon them. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), the folk of Lot denied the messengers, pursued conduct Lot hated, threatened to expel him, and were destroyed after rejecting his warning. In Surah 54: The Moon (Al-Qamar), the folk of Lot rejected warnings, doubted Lot's warnings, sought his guests for an ill purpose, and were punished by a storm of stones except for Lot's family.
