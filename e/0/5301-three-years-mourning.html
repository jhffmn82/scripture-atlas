Also called: the three years' mourning

Abstract written by a model

Three years’ mourning is equally prescribed by the three dynasties and binding from sovereign to people; Mencius says the prince resolves to observe its coarse garment and congee despite opposition.

Chapter 2

Mencius says that three years’ mourning, along with the coarse garment and eating of congee, was prescribed by the three dynasties and binding on all. The prince determines to observe it despite opposition.
