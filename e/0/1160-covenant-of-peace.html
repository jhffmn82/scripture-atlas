Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, the covenant of peace is described as unremovable from the afflicted woman, more lasting than mountains. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the Lord's covenant of peace is described as not being removed from the woman.
