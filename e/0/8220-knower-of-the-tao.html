knower of the Tao

person · 1 work · 15 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: (Such an one), He (who knows it), He who knows (the Tao), the noblest man under heaven

Abstract written by a model

A person who knows Tao and, by disposition, does not speak of it. He shuts his mouth and nostril-portals, tempers sharpness and brightness, unravels complications, and joins others’ obscurity. Entering Mysterious Agreement, he transcends familiarity, distance, profit, injury, nobility, and meanness, becoming the noblest man under heaven.

56. 1. He who knows (the Tao) does not (care to) speak (about it); he

The knower of the Tao keeps his mouth shut and closes the portals of his nostrils. He blunts his sharp points, unravels the complications of things, attempers his brightness, and brings himself into agreement with the obscurity of others; he is beyond familiarity, distance, profit, injury, nobility, and meanness.
