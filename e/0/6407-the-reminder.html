the Reminder

religious text · 7 works · 5 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: My reminder, This is a blessed Reminder, a Reminder, a new reminder, a reminder, reminder, the reminder, this pronouncement

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 15: The Rock (Al-Hijr), the Reminder is revealed by Allah, who declares that He guards it, though the guilty do not believe in it. In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), the Reminder comes from Allah, is rejected by many, is given to Moses and Aaron, and is described as a blessed Reminder. In Surah 38: The letter Saad (Saad), the Reminder is disputed by disbelievers, presented as a revealed blessing for reflection, and described as a reminder for all peoples. In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), the Reminder is rejected by disbelievers and described as an unassailable Scripture that falsehood cannot approach. In Surah 65: Divorce (At-Talaaq), the Reminder is sent down by Allah as a messenger reciting His revelations. In Surah 68: The Pen (Al-Qalam), the Reminder is heard by disbelievers, who call Muhammad mad, and is described as a reminder to creation. In Surah 73: The Enshrouded One (Al-Muzzammil), the Reminder identifies the passage and calls whoever wills to choose a way unto his Lord.
