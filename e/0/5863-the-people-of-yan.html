Abstract written by a model

The people of Yan welcomed King Xuan’s host with baskets of rice and vessels of congee, hoping to escape fire and water and gain liberation from Yan’s ruler. Their hopes failed as they suffered killings, confinement, and destruction of their ancestral temple. Mencius warned that, if water became deeper and fire fiercer, they would make another revolution.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

The people of Yan brought baskets of rice and vessels of congee to meet king Xuan’s host because they hoped to escape out of fire and water. Mencius said they would revolt again if their situation became more oppressive.

Chapter 11

The people of Yan initially believed that King Xuan’s forces would deliver them from oppression and brought baskets of rice and vessels of congee to meet the army. They then suffered the killing of their fathers and elder brothers, the confinement of their sons and younger brothers, and the destruction of their ancestral temple.
