Immortality

concept · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Ameretatât, eternal Immortality

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Ameretatât is a Bountiful Immortal guarding plants and wood, offered meat and flesh for propitiation while Immortality is requested from Mazda. In Yasna, eternal Immortality is an eternal gift identified with Welfare and requested from the Yazads and Ahura Mazda.
