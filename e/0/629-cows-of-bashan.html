Chapter 4

The cows of Bashan, on the mountain of Samaria, are accused of oppressing the poor and crushing the needy while telling their husbands to bring them drinks. They are told that days will come when they are taken away with hooks, and they will go out through breaks in the wall, each straight ahead, and cast themselves into Harmon.
