blessing and curse

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: All these blessings, a blessing and a curse, blessings, the blessing, the blessing and the curse, the curse, these curses

Abstract written by a model

Blessing and curse is the paired choice Moses sets before the Israelites, depending on whether they listen to the LORD's voice and keep his commandments or go after other gods. In Chapter 11 the blessing is to be set on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal once they enter the land. Chapter 28 describes the blessings in detail: prosperity in city and field, offspring, livestock, rain, victory over enemies, and lending to nations. Chapter 30 returns to the pair, says the curses fall on the Israelites' enemies, and closes by framing them as life and death.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

Moses sets before the Israelites a blessing for listening to the LORD's commandments and a curse for not listening and going after other gods. When they enter the land, the blessing is to be set on Mount Gerizim and the curse on Mount Ebal. These mountains are described as beyond the Jordan, in the land of the Canaanites near Gilgal and the oaks of Moreh.

Chapter 28

The blessings come upon and overtake those who listen to the LORD, covering city and field, offspring, ground, livestock, basket and kneading trough, and coming in and going out. They include victory over enemies, abundant prosperity, rain in season, and lending to many nations without borrowing. They are conditioned on keeping the commandments and not turning to other gods.

Chapter 30

The blessing and the curse are set before the Israelites, who are expected to call them to mind among the nations after they have come upon them. The LORD will put all the curses on the Israelites' enemies and on those who hate and persecuted them. In the closing appeal, the blessing and the curse are paired with life and death as the choice the Israelites face.
