Sect.

Brave-Awful-Possessing-Male-Deity was offered by his father, Deity Heaven-Point-Blade-Extended, and sent with Deity Heavenly-Bird-Boat to obtain the Central Land of Reed-Plains. He defeated Deity Brave-August-Name-Firm in a trial of strength, secured Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land's agreement to yield the land, and reported to Heaven that he had subdued and pacified it.
