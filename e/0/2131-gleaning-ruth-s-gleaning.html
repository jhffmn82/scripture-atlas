Chapter 2

Ruth proposes to glean among the ears of grain after someone who will show her favor, and begins after the reapers. Boaz extends it by letting her glean even among the sheaves and having extra grain pulled out for her. She gleans until evening, beats out about an ephah of barley, and continues through the barley and wheat harvests.
