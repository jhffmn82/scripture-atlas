expanse

Also called: The expanse, The heavens, an expanse in the middle of the waters, heavens, sky, the end of the heavens, the expanse, the expanse of the sky, the open expanse of the sky, the sky

Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, the expanse divides the waters above from the waters below, is called sky, holds the lights, and is where birds fly. In Psalms, the expanse declares God's glory, shows his handiwork, pours out speech and knowledge, reaches all the earth, and holds a tent for the sun.
