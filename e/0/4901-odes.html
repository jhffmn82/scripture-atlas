Abstract written by a model

In The Analects of Confucius, Book XVI (Ke She), the Odes are presented as something Po-yu's father requires him to study to become fit to converse with. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XVII (Yang Ho), the Odes serve to stimulate the mind, support self-contemplation, teach sociability, regulate resentment, and teach duties toward one's father and prince.
