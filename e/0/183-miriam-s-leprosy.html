Chapter 12

Miriam's leprosy appeared, as white as snow, when the cloud departed from over the Tent after the LORD's anger burned against her and Aaron. Aaron begged that she not be as one dead, and Moses asked for her healing. The LORD set a term of seven days shut outside the camp, after which she would be brought in again.
