Balaam's parables

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: his parable, parable

Abstract written by a model

Balaam delivers two main parables, each opened with the formula that the man whose eyes are open speaks. The first blesses Israel's tents, prosperity, king, and strength, while the second looks to the latter days, with a star and scepter from Jacob conquering Moab, Edom, and Seir. Three shorter parables follow, announcing destruction for Amalek, the Kenite, and Asshur and Eber, the last of which says ships from Kittim will afflict them.
