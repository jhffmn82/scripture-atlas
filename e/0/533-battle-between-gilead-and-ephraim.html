Chapter 12

After Ephraim threatens Jephthah, he gathers the men of Gilead and fights Ephraim, who are struck down. The Gileadites hold the Jordan fords and kill fugitives who fail the 'Shibboleth' test. Forty-two thousand of Ephraim fall.
