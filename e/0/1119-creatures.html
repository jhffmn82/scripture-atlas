Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, living creatures enter the ship by pairs according to their kinds, while all flesh with the breath of life dies in the flood except those with Noah. In The Book of Isaiah, grass symbolizes all flesh and the people, who wither and fade when the LORD's breath blows on them, unlike the enduring word of God. In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, creatures live through the One, the Tao, and without that life they would pass away.
