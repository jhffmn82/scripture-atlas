Abstract written by a model

Firstborn is the law that the LORD claims every firstborn that opens the womb, human and animal, as his. Male animals belong to him; firstborn donkeys are redeemed with a lamb or have necks broken; human firstborn sons are redeemed, commemorating Egypt's firstborn killed.

Chapter 13

The LORD claims all firstborn that open the womb, of man and animal, as his, and the males are to be set apart to him. Firstborn donkeys are redeemed with a lamb or have their necks broken, and human firstborn sons are redeemed, in memory of the LORD killing Egypt's firstborn.
