Abstract written by a model

Virtue is equipment whose completeness withstands a corrupt age. Honored and delighted in, it secures perfect satisfaction through cultivation of personal character; it is weakened by conduct only resembling sincerity, purity, or disinterestedness, requiring true standards to expose corrupt appearances.

Chapter 9

Virtue is to be honored and delighted in as a means of securing perfect satisfaction. The men of antiquity cultivated their personal character when their wishes were not realized.

Part II / Chapter 1 .. Chapter 38

Virtue is weakened by conduct that merely resembles sincerity, purity, or disinterestedness while agreeing with corrupt customs. The doctrines of Yao and Shun and the true standard of virtue are needed to expose such appearances.
