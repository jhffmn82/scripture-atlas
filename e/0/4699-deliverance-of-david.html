Chapter 18

The LORD sent from on high, took David, and drew him out of many waters, delivering him from his strong enemies who were too mighty for him. He brought David into a large place because he delighted in him, and the LORD was David's support in his day of calamity. The song ends affirming that the LORD gives great deliverance to his king, David, and his offspring forever.
