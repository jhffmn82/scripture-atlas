Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Joshua, Gibeon is a great city with mighty men and Hivite inhabitants who made peace with Israel; Joshua came to relieve it when five Amorite kings besieged it. In The Second Book of Chronicles, Gibeon is the site of the high place where Solomon and the assembly go, where Solomon offers a thousand burnt offerings and God appears to him.

Chapter 10
