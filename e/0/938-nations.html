Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the nations will remember and turn to the LORD, and all their relatives will worship before him, including the rich and those who go down to the dust. In The Book of Zephaniah, the nations and kingdoms will be gathered by the LORD for indignation and fierce anger, after which the peoples will call on his name and serve him shoulder to shoulder.
