Jehoash in The Second Book of Kings

author unknown · 560 to 539 BC · the work’s page · read it where Jehoash first appears

Abstract written by a model

Jehoash (also Joash), son of Ahaziah and Zibiah of Beersheba, was hidden in the LORD's house for six years after a slaughter, then crowned king at age seven in the seventh year of Jehu. He reigned forty years in Jerusalem, doing right while the priest Jehoiada instructed him. He ordered temple repairs and confronted the priests when they lagged, but paid Hazael with holy things and gold. His servants struck him down at Millo; he was buried in David's city, succeeded by Amaziah.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

Joash, son of Ahaziah, was stolen away from the slaughter and hidden in the LORD's house for six years. In the seventh year he was shown to the captains, crowned, given the covenant, anointed, and made king, and he sat on the throne at age seven (called Jehoash).

Chapter 12

Jehoash began to reign in the seventh year of Jehu and reigned forty years in Jerusalem; his mother was Zibiah of Beersheba. He did right all the days Jehoiada instructed him, ordered the money for repairs, and confronted the priests in his twenty-third year when the damage was still unrepaired. He bought off Hazael with the holy things and gold, and was later struck down by his servants at the house of Millo and buried in David's city, with his son Amaziah succeeding him.

Joash in The Second Book of Chronicles

author unknown · about 424 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Joash first appears

Abstract written by a model

Joash was a king's son of David's line, the son of Ahaziah of Judah. As an infant he was rescued by Jehoshabeath from the slaughter of the king's sons and hidden with his nurse for six years in God's house while Athaliah reigned. Jehoiada and his sons then crowned, covenanted, and anointed him amid rejoicing, guarded by Levites, and set him on the throne. He became king at seven and reigned forty years in Jerusalem; his mother was Zibiah of Beersheba. He did right in the LORD's eyes all the days of Jehoiada, who chose his two wives, and he commissioned temple repair. After Jehoiada died he heeded the princes, forsook the LORD, and ordered Zechariah stoned. The Syrians wounded him, and his servants killed him on his bed for the blood of Jehoiada's sons. He was buried in David's city, not the kings' tombs, and his son Amaziah succeeded him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 22

Joash was rescued by Jehoshabeath from among the king's sons who were being slain. He was hidden with his nurse, first in the bedroom and then in God's house, for six years while Athaliah reigned.

Chapter 23

The king's son was brought out, crowned, given the covenant, made king, and anointed by Jehoiada and his sons, while the people cried 'Long live the king!' He stood by his pillar at the entrance amid captains, trumpeters, and rejoicing people, and was guarded by the Levites. He was then brought down from the LORD's house through the upper gate and set on the throne of the kingdom.

Chapter 24

Joash began to reign at seven and reigned forty years in Jerusalem, doing right all the days of Jehoiada. He commissioned the temple repair, but after Jehoiada's death he listened to the princes, abandoned the LORD, and ordered Zechariah killed. The Syrians wounded him, his servants killed him on his bed, and he was buried in David's city but not in the kings' tombs; Amaziah his son reigned after him.
