owner of the bull

Abstract written by a model

A bull's owner bears liability only if the animal was known to gore and he failed to restrain it. Then he faces death, though ransom may replace it; a servant's death costs thirty silver shekels to the master, and a slain animal requires bull-for-bull replacement.

Chapter 21

An owner is not held responsible when his bull gores someone to death without prior habit. If the bull had a known habit and he did not keep it in, he is put to death, though a ransom may be imposed for his life. He must pay bull for bull and keep the dead animal if his habitually goring bull kills another's.
