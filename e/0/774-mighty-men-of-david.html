Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Samuel, the mighty men of David are listed by name with their leaders, total, and notable deeds. In 1 Chronicles, the mighty men of David are listed as those who supported David in his kingdom and helped make him king, with their origins and deeds.

mighty men of David in The Second Book of Samuel

author unknown · about 950 BC · the work’s page · read it where mighty men of David first appears

Abstract written by a model

The mighty men are listed by name, led by Josheb Basshebeth, chief of the captains, who killed eight hundred at one time. They include the three, Abishai, Benaiah, and the thirty, ending with Uriah the Hittite and a total of thirty-seven. Their deeds include victories over the Philistines, an Egyptian, and a lion.
