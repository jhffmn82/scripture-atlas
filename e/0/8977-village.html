Abstract written by a model

A village is assessed as a place for monks or nuns to stay or travel through. Its key value is providing religious-practice or study space, adequate necessities, and acceptable alms. During the rainy season, they should remain in a suitably equipped village; during the cold season, they should not remain where facilities and means of existence are lacking. They must not go there for entertainment.

Third Lesson.

A monk or nun should not resolve to go to a village or scot-free town for the sake of an entertainment. They may wander from village to village only with the complete outfit, subject to the stated restrictions during dangerous weather and conditions.

Third Lecture, Called Walking. / First Lesson. .. Third Lesson.

A monk or a nun should remain during the rainy season in a village that provides suitable space for religious practices or study and adequate necessities. They should not remain during the cold season in a village where such facilities and means of existence are lacking.
