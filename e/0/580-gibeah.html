Abstract written by a model

Gibeah is a Benjamite city whose inhabitants refuse the Levite's party shelter until an Ephraimite old man takes them in, and whose men assault the concubine. Israel assembles against it; its seven hundred chosen men fight alongside Benjamin, but ambushers rush the city, strike it with the sword, and burn it entirely.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 19

Gibeah, which belongs to Benjamin, is where the Levite's party stops at sundown after passing by Jebus, and no one there takes them in until an Ephraimite old man does. Its inhabitants are Benjamites, and the men of the city commit the assault on the concubine there.

Chapter 20

Gibeah, a city of Benjamin, is the place where the Levite was attacked and is the target of Israel's assembly and assault. Its seven hundred chosen men fight with Benjamin, and on the third day the ambushers rush the city, strike it with the sword and set it on fire, so that the whole city goes up in smoke.
