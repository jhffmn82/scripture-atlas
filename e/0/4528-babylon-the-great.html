Abstract written by a model

Babylon the great is a symbolic great and strong city, portrayed also as a woman, whose name is written on her forehead. She is spiritually called Sodom and Egypt and is the place where the Lord was crucified. She reigns over the kings of the earth, sits on the waters, on a beast, and on seven mountains, dressed in purple and scarlet, adorned with gold, precious stones and pearls, and holding a golden cup full of abominations. Most important is her guilt: she made the nations drink the wine of her sexual immorality, deceived them with sorcery, enriched merchants through her luxury, and is drunk with the blood of saints, martyrs, prophets and all the slain. She glorifies herself and says in her heart she sits a queen and will see no mourning. The records unfold from the great city in which the witnesses' bodies lie and an earthquake topples a tenth of it, to an angel's declaration that she has fallen. At the seventh bowl she is remembered before God and given the cup of his wrath. She becomes a habitation of demons and a prison of unclean spirits, is repaid double, burned and made desolate in one hour. God judges her and avenges his servants, and her smoke goes up forever and ever.

Babylon the great in Revelation

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

The great city, spiritually called Sodom and Egypt and the place where the Lord was crucified, is where the witnesses' dead bodies lie in the street. A great earthquake there makes a tenth of it fall and kills seven thousand people.

Chapter 14

A second angel declares that Babylon the great has fallen. She is said to have made all the nations drink the wine of the wrath of her sexual immorality.

Chapter 16

When the seventh bowl is poured out, Babylon the great is remembered in the sight of God. She is to be given the cup of the wine of the fierceness of his wrath.

Chapter 18

Babylon the great is declared fallen and has become a habitation of demons and a prison of every unclean spirit and hated bird. She is accused of making the nations drink the wine of her immorality, enriching merchants, deceiving nations with sorcery, and bearing the blood of prophets, saints, and all the slain. She boasts that she sits a queen and will see no mourning, yet she is to be repaid double, burned, and made desolate in one hour.

Chapter 19

She is said to have corrupted the earth with her sexual immorality and to have shed the blood of God's servants. God has judged her and avenged his servants, and her smoke goes up forever and ever.
