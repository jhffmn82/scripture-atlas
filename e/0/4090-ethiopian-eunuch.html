Chapter 8

The Ethiopian eunuch was a man of great authority under Candace, queen of the Ethiopians, who was over all her treasure. Returning from worship in Jerusalem, he was reading Isaiah in his chariot and asked Philip to explain it. After hearing about Jesus he was baptized and went on his way rejoicing.
