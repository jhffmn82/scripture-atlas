Abstract written by a model

Paul bows before the Father and prays for the readers: that they be strengthened with power through the Spirit in the inner person, that Christ dwell in their hearts through faith, rooted in love, that they grasp Christ's surpassing love with all the saints, and finally be filled with all the fullness of God.

Chapter 3

Paul bows his knees to the Father and asks that, according to the riches of his glory, the readers be strengthened with power through his Spirit in the inner person. He prays that Christ dwell in their hearts through faith, that they be rooted and grounded in love, and that they comprehend with all the saints the width, length, height and depth. He also prays that they know Christ's love, which surpasses knowledge, and be filled with all the fullness of God.
