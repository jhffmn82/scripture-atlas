vessel

Also called: a vessel, from a vessel, to a vessel, with a vessel

Abstract written by a model

A vessel is necessary for everyone’s existence, involved from conception and birth through marriage, friendship, and worldly passage. It gives birth to kings; when one vessel dies, another is sought, continuing the vessel’s cycle.
