Abstract written by a model

The king is a ruler who rejoices in the LORD's strength and salvation and trusts in him. The LORD grants his request for life forever, sets a fine gold crown on him, and gives glory, honor and majesty. Through the Most High's loving kindness he is unmoved, made most blessed forever and glad in God's presence.

Chapter 21

The king rejoices in the LORD's strength and salvation, and receives his heart's desire, a crown of fine gold, honor and majesty, and life of length forever and ever. He trusts in the LORD and, through the loving kindness of the Most High, will not be moved. He is made most blessed forever and glad in the LORD's presence.
