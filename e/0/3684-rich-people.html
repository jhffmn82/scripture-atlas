Chapter 6

Those who are rich in the present age are to be charged not to be arrogant or set their hope on the uncertainty of riches, but on the living God. They are to do good, be rich in good works, and be ready to share. In doing so they lay up a good foundation for the time to come and lay hold of eternal life.
