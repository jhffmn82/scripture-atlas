Chapter 9

The land is burned up and empty, with no one passing through, no livestock, and birds and animals fled. The LORD promises wormwood, poisoned water, scattering, and the sword, and death enters windows and palaces, with corpses lying like dung on the open field. The cause given is forsaking his law and following the Baals, and the punishment extends to the nations and to Israel.
