reward

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a great reward, a vast reward, an immense reward, his reward, immense reward, the reward thereof, their wages

Abstract written by a model

Reward is a vast, immense benefit bestowed by Allah. An immense reward from Allah's presence is promised to those who do what they are exhorted to do. Allah promises good to both those who strive and those who remain behind, while granting the strivers a greater reward. For one who leaves home as a fugitive unto Allah and His messenger and dies before reaching the destination, the reward becomes incumbent on Allah. Believers who believe in Allah and His messengers without making distinction between them are to receive an immense reward and their wages from Allah.
