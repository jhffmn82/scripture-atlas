Abstract written by a model

Achsah is Caleb's daughter, offered as wife to whoever captures Kiriath Sepher and given to Othniel after he takes it. Arriving, she prompts a field request, then asks Caleb for a blessing and water, and he gives her upper and lower springs.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 15

Achsah is Caleb's daughter, offered as wife to whoever takes Kiriath Sepher, and she is given to Othniel after he takes it. When she came, she had him ask her father for a field, got off her donkey, and asked Caleb for a blessing and springs of water because he had set her in the land of the South. He gave her the upper springs and the lower springs.
