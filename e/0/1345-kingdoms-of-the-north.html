Chapter 1

The LORD says he will call all the families of the northern kingdoms, whom Jeremiah's vision of a boiling cauldron tipping from the north foreshadows. They will come and set their thrones at Jerusalem's gates, against its walls, and against all Judah's cities, as the means by which evil breaks out on the land.
