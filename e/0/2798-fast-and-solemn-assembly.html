Abstract written by a model

A fast and solemn assembly, commanded in the Book of Joel, summons the elders and all the land's inhabitants to gather at the LORD's house and cry out. The LORD urges turning to him wholeheartedly, with fasting, weeping, and mourning, tearing hearts rather than garments, since he is gracious and merciful. It culminates with the priests weeping between porch and altar, begging him to spare his people.

The Book of Joel / Chapter 1

Joel commands that a fast be sanctified and a solemn assembly called. The elders and all inhabitants of the land are to gather at the LORD's house and cry to the LORD.

Chapter 2

The LORD calls for turning to him with all the heart, with fasting, weeping, and mourning, and says to tear the heart rather than garments. The motive given is that he is gracious, merciful, slow to anger, abundant in loving kindness, and relents from calamity. A fast is sanctified, a solemn assembly is called, and the priests weep between the porch and the altar, asking him to spare his people.
