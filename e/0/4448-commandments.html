commandments

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: commandments of Christ, his commandments, my commandments, my word, my words

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to John, commandments are kept by Jesus' disciples as a sign of their love for him. In The Book of Moroni, commandments of Christ are given by Jesus Christ to those who partake of the bread, govern the administration of his flesh and blood, and are witnessed as willing to be kept by participants.
