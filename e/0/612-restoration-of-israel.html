Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, salvation of Israel is the hoped-for deliverance from Zion, tied to the LORD restoring and bringing back his captive people with rejoicing and gladness. In The Book of Jeremiah, restoration of Israel is the LORD's promise to end foreign enslavement, heal wounds, rebuild the city, multiply the people, and establish a prince near the LORD. In Baruch, salvation and return of Israel is God's promised deliverance from enemies and return to Jerusalem with everlasting joy, gathering the sons from east to west. In The Book of Amos, restoration of Israel is the Lord's promise to return his people from captivity, with abundant harvests and permanent settlement on their land. In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, the restoration of the Jews will occur in the latter days, after which they will no longer be confounded or scattered. In The Second Book of Nephi, restoration of Israel is God's work to restore his people from a lost and fallen state, gather them to their lands, and convince them of the true Messiah.

The LORD promises Israel's restoration: breaking yokes and ending foreign enslavement, healing wounds, rebuilding the city and palace in place, bringing thanksgiving, multiplying and glorifying the people, establishing their congregation, and raising a prince from among them, drawn near to the LORD.

Chapter 30

The LORD promises restoration: breaking the yoke and bonds so strangers no longer enslave them, restoring health and healing wounds, and building the city on its own hill with the palace in its own place. Thanksgiving and merry voices will come from them, they will be multiplied and glorified, their children will be as before, and their congregation established before the LORD. Their prince and ruler will come from among them and be brought near to the LORD.

The Lord promises that days are coming when harvests will be so abundant that the plowman overtakes the reaper and sweet wine drips from the mountains. He says he will plant them on their land and they will no more be plucked up from it.

restoration of Israel in The Second Book of Nephi

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where restoration of Israel first appears

Abstract written by a model

Restoration of Israel is God’s second work to restore his people from a lost and fallen state upon the earth and to their lands of inheritance. It unfolds through work among all nations, gathering from long dispersion and the isles of the sea, words convincing them of the true Messiah, Gentile assistance, belief that Christ is the Christ, and much restoration by one mighty among Joseph’s descendants.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

One mighty among Joseph's descendants will work much restoration unto the house of Israel and the seed of Joseph's brethren.

Chapter 10

When the Jews believe that Christ is the Christ, they will be restored in the flesh to the lands of their inheritance. They will be gathered in from their long dispersion by the assistance of the Gentiles.

Chapter 25

The Lord sets his hand again to restore his people from their lost and fallen state. This restoration includes bringing forth words to convince them of the true Messiah.

Chapter 30

The restoration of his people upon the earth will be brought about through the Lord God’s work among all nations, kindreds, tongues, and people.
