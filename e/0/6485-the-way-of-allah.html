the way of Allah

topic · 9 works · 10 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: Allah's cause, Allah's way, His cause, His way, a right path, cause of Allah, the cause of Allah, the path of Allah, the right path, the right path purposefully, way of Allah

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), believers fight for the way of Allah, giving up worldly life for the Hereafter, while those who hinder others from it are far astray. In Surah 8: The Spoils of War (Al-Anfaal), disbelievers spend wealth to debar people from the way of Allah, while believers strive for Allah's cause and receive full repayment for what they spend. In Surah 8: The Spoils of War (Al-Anfaal), believers leave their homes and strive with their wealth and lives for the cause of Allah, and those who help them are promised pardon and bountiful provision. In Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba), believers are commanded to strive in the way of Allah with their wealth and lives, while some obstruct it for worldly gain. In Surah 14: Abraham (Ibrahim), the way of Allah is the path to which Scripture brings mankind, though disbelievers debar people from it and would have it crooked. In Surah 38: The letter Saad (Saad), David is commanded to judge rightly and not follow desire away from the way of Allah, while those who wander from it face an awful doom. In Surah 42: Consultation (Ash-Shura), Muhammad guides toward the right path, which is the path of Allah, to whom all things ultimately reach. In Surah 47: Muhammad, those who turn people from the way of Allah or away from it have their actions made vain, while those who spend in it receive Allah's help.

and 2 more works

In Surah 61: The Ranks (As-Saff), those who battle for Allah's cause in ranks are loved by Allah, and believers are told to strive for it with their wealth and lives. In Surah 72: The Jinn (Al-Jinn), those among the Jinn who surrender to Allah take the right path purposefully, and idolaters who tread it receive abundant water as a test.

the way of Allah in Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa)

author unknown · undated · the work’s page · read it where the way of Allah first appears

Abstract written by a model

The way of Allah is a cause for which believers fight, taking precautions and advancing in groups or all together. It calls people to give up the life of this world for the Hereafter; those who fight are promised a vast reward whether slain or victorious. In contrast, those who disbelieve, do wrong, and hinder others from the way of Allah are far astray, without forgiveness or guidance to a road.
