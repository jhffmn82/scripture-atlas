Abstract written by a model

A sage from the south, Wên Po Hsüeh Tzŭ visits Lu on journeys to and from Ch'i, initially refusing a Lu man for lacking human-heart knowledge, then twice meeting him and lamenting his rigid, admonishing conduct.

CHAPTER XXI.

Wên Po Hsüeh Tzŭ initially refuses to meet a man of Lu because he considers the people of the Middle Kingdom skilled in ceremonies but lacking knowledge of the human heart. After meeting him twice, he sighs because the man behaves according to rigid forms while criticizing and admonishing him like a son and father.
