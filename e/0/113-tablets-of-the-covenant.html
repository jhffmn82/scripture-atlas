tablets of the covenant

object · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: The tablets, tablets, the tablets, two tablets of the covenant

Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, the tablets of the covenant are engraved by God, carried down the mountain by Moses, and broken by him in anger at the calf and dancing. In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), the tablets contain lessons and explanations, are cast down and later taken up by Moses, and bear guidance and mercy for those who fear their Lord.
