Abstract written by a model

The readers are believers addressed in Paul's Letter to the Ephesians. In Chapter 1 they are blessed, chosen to be holy, and given redemption, forgiveness and an inheritance in Christ, existing for the praise of God's glory, with the Holy Spirit as pledge. In Chapter 4 they are called to one hope and members of one body, urged to walk worthily, grow to maturity, put away the old man, put on the new man, and replace falsehood and malice with truth, kindness and forgiveness.

Paul’s Letter to the Ephesians / Chapter 1

Believers, referred to as "us" and "we," were blessed with every spiritual blessing, chosen to be holy and without defect in love, and given redemption, forgiveness, and an inheritance in Christ. They were foreordained to be to the praise of God's glory, and the Holy Spirit is a pledge of their inheritance.

Chapter 4

The readers are urged to walk worthily of their calling with humility, patience, and love, and to grow into maturity rather than be tossed about by false doctrine. They are told to put away the old man, be renewed in mind, put on the new man, and abandon falsehood, theft, corrupt speech, bitterness, and malice in favor of truth, honest labor, kindness, and forgiveness.
