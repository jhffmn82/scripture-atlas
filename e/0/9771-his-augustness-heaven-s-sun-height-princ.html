Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Heaven's-Sun-Height-Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty is an august child of the Heavenly Deities, commanded by Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity and High-Integrating-Deity to govern Central Land of Reed-Plains and rule Luxuriant Reed-Plain-Land-of-Fresh-Rice-ears. He agrees, receives the mirror, Herb-Quelling-Great-Sword, and several deities, leaves the Heavenly Rock-Seat, and descends through the clouds onto Kuzhifuru. He seeks marriage with Divine-Princess-of-Ata, rejects Princess-Long-as-the-Rocks, and weds Princess-Blossoming-Brilliantly-Like-the-Flowers-of-the-Trees for one night. When she says she is pregnant, he doubts the child is his and calls it an Earthly Deity's child.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Sect.

The august child of the Heavenly Deities was designated to govern the Central Land of Reed-Plains. Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land agreed to yield the land according to the command of the august child of the Heavenly Deities.

Sect.

His Augustness Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty is charged to rule the Luxuriant Reed-Plain-Land-of-Fresh-Rice-ears and agrees to descend from Heaven. He receives the mirror, the Herb-Quelling-Great-Sword, and several deities, and is instructed to regard the mirror as the august spirit of the commanding deities.

Sect.

His Augustness Heaven's-Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty left the Heavenly Rock-Seat, parted the heavenly clouds, and descended from Heaven onto the peak of Kuzhifuru, which is Takachiho in Tsu-kushi.

Sect.

He met Divine-Princess-of-Ata and asked to marry her. He rejected Princess-Long-as-the-Rocks because she was very hideous, kept only Princess-Blossoming-Brilliantly-Like-the-Flowers-of-the-Trees, and thereby caused the consequence declared by Deity-Great-Mountain-Possessor.

Sect.

His Augustness Prince Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty is told by Princess-Blossoming-Brilliantly-Like-the-Flowers-of-the-Trees that she is pregnant after one night. He doubts that the unborn child is his and says it must be the child of an Earthly Deity.
