Doeg the Edomite

person · 2 works · 15 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Doeg, a certain man of the servants of Saul, deceitful tongue, mighty man

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Samuel, Doeg the Edomite informs Saul that David visited Ahimelech at Nob, kills eighty-five priests, and destroys Nob. In Psalms, Doeg the Edomite is addressed as a boastful mighty man who loves evil, lying, and devouring words, wields a deceitful tongue, trusts in riches rather than God, and is declared to be destroyed forever.

Doeg the Edomite in The First Book of Samuel

author unknown · about 930 to 721 BC · the work’s page · read it where Doeg the Edomite first appears

Abstract written by a model

Doeg, an Edomite and Saul's chief herdsman, informs Saul that David visited Ahimelech at Nob; when Saul's guards refuse, Doeg kills eighty-five priests and destroys Nob. David had foreseen his betrayal.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 22

Doeg, standing among Saul's servants, told Saul he had seen David come to Ahimelech at Nob. When Saul's guards refused to kill the priests, Doeg obeyed the order, killing eighty-five linen-ephod-wearing priests and then striking down the city of Nob. David later said he had known Doeg would tell Saul.

Doeg the Edomite in Psalms

author unknown · undated · the work’s page · read it where Doeg the Edomite first appears

Abstract written by a model

Doeg the Edomite is the man who told Saul that David had come to Ahimelech's house. The psalm addresses him as a boastful 'mighty man' who loves evil, lying and devouring words, wields a deceitful tongue, and trusts in his riches rather than God. It ends by declaring God will destroy him forever, uprooting him from the land of the living.

Chapter 52

Doeg the Edomite is named in the superscription as the one who came and told Saul that David had come to Ahimelech's house. The psalm's address to a boastful, deceitful 'mighty man' with a destructive tongue is set against this event, and the man is said to face destruction by God.
