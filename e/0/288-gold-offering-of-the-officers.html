Chapter 31

The captains of thousands and hundreds bring gold ornaments, armlets, bracelets, signet rings, earrings, and necklaces as an offering to make atonement for their souls. It totals 16,750 shekels, and Moses and Eleazar bring it into the Tent of Meeting as a memorial for Israel before the LORD.
