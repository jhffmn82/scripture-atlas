Abstract written by a model

Confucius's teaching identifies four bad things: cruelty, oppression, injury, and acting as a mere official, illustrated by harsh, sudden demands and stingy rewards.

CHAP. II. 1. Tsze-chang asked Confucius, saying, 'In what way

The four bad things are killing people without instruction, suddenly requiring the full tale of work without warning, enforcing orders harshly after first presenting them without urgency, and giving pay or rewards stingily.
