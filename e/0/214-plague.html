Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the plague kills 14,700 after the congregation complains, until Aaron's incense atonement stops it. In The Wisdom of Solomon, the wilderness plague destroys a multitude in the wilderness until the blameless man intervenes and stops it.

plague in Numbers

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where plague first appears

Abstract written by a model

A plague sent by the LORD's wrath after the congregation complained that Moses and Aaron killed the LORD's people; it kills 14,700, beyond Korah's dead, until Aaron's incense atonement between dead and living stops it.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

A plague begins among the congregation after they complain that Moses and Aaron killed the LORD's people, because wrath has gone out from the LORD. It kills 14,700, in addition to those who died in the matter of Korah. It stops when Aaron makes atonement with incense, standing between the dead and the living.
