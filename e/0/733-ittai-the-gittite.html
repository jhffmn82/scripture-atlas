Abstract written by a model

Ittai, a Gittite from Gath described by David as a foreigner and exile, refuses David's offer to turn back, swears loyalty in life or death, crosses over with his men and families, and commands a third of David's people.

Ittai the Gittite is in one work on this site, The Second Book of Samuel. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 15

David urged Ittai, a foreigner and exile who had come only recently, to return and take his brothers back. Ittai swore by the LORD and the king's life to stay with David in death or life, and he passed over with all his men and little ones.
