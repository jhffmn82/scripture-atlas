persecution

topic · 3 works · 5 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: persecute, persecute you, persecuted, persecuted for my name’s sake, persecuting, persecutions, persecutions which were heaped upon them

Abstract written by a model

In 2 Timothy, persecution affects all who desire to live godly in Christ Jesus, and the speaker recounts persecutions and sufferings at Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra from which the Lord delivered him. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, persecution afflicts humble church members, is inflicted by outsiders amid contention, and is later committed by some church members against nonbelievers, while humble followers of God continue suffering. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, persecution suffered for Jesus’s name brings blessedness, the kingdom of heaven, joy, and a great heavenly reward.

persecution in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where persecution first appears

Abstract written by a model

Persecution concerns affliction of humble church members, initially by outsiders using words and arising amid contention. Later, some church members persecute nonbelievers according to their will, while humble followers of God continue suffering from the remainder of the people.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

People who do not belong to the church of God persecute its members with words because of their humility and free sharing of the word of God. Some church members also contend with adversaries and one another, causing affliction and trial.

Chapter 4

Members of the church began to persecute those who did not believe according to their will and pleasure. Humble followers of God also suffered persecutions from the remainder of the people.
