Chapter 9

A ram for Aaron and a calf and lamb for the people are commanded as burnt offerings, and Aaron kills his burnt offering with his sons delivering the blood to sprinkle around the altar. He burns the pieces and head, the washed innards and legs, and offers the people's burnt offering according to the ordinance. Fire from the LORD later consumes the burnt offering on the altar.
