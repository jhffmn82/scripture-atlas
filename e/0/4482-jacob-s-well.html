Abstract written by a model

Jacob's well is a deep well at Sychar in Samaria, near ground Jacob gave Joseph. Jacob gave it to the woman's people and drank from it with his children and livestock. Jesus, tired, sits by it, and his conversation with the Samaritan woman occurs there.

Chapter 4

Jacob's well is at Sychar in Samaria, near the parcel of ground Jacob gave to his son Joseph. Jesus sits by it, tired from his journey, around the sixth hour, and the conversation with the Samaritan woman takes place there. The woman notes it is deep and that Jacob gave it, and that he, his children, and his livestock drank from it.
