Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the daughters of Zelophehad argued for and received their father's inheritance and later married within their tribe to preserve it. In The Book of Joshua, the daughters of Zelophehad received an inheritance among their father's brothers in Manasseh.

daughters of Zelophehad in Numbers

author unknown · 1439 to 1399 BC · the work’s page · read it where daughters of Zelophehad first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah are the five daughters of Zelophehad. They came before Moses, Eleazar, the princes and the congregation, arguing their father's name should not be lost for lack of a son, and the LORD declared they speak right, granting them their father's inheritance. Later, concern arose that marriage might transfer it, so they were commanded to marry within their tribe and married their father's brothers' sons in Manasseh.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 27

The daughters of Zelophehad, named Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Tirzah, came before Moses, Eleazar, the princes and the congregation at the door of the Tent of Meeting. They argued that their father's name should not be lost for lack of a son and asked for a possession among their father's brothers, and the LORD declared that they speak right.

Chapter 36

The daughters, named Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah, are the subject of the concern about their inheritance being transferred through marriage. They are commanded to marry within the family of their father's tribe, and they do as the LORD commanded by marrying their father's brothers' sons.
