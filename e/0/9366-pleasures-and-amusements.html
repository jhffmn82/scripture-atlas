pleasures and amusements

topic · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: amusements, dear pleasures, pleasures, sensual lust

Abstract written by a model

Pleasures and amusements are represented by mud and include sounds, colours, smells, tastes, and feelings of touch. Men become stuck in them, unable to return to worldly life or get beyond them. They cannot help or save from illness or suffering; the wise consider them alien and give them up.

Sutrakritanga, Book II / First Lecture, Called the Lotus.

Pleasures and amusements are represented by the mud. The four men become stuck in them and cannot return to worldly life or get beyond them.

I say: here in the East, West, North, and South there are some men, viz. Âryas,

Pleasures and amusements cannot help or save a person from illness or suffering. They may be forsaken by a person or forsake him, and a wise person considers them alien and gives them up.
