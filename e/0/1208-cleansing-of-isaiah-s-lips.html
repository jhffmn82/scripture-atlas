Chapter 6

A seraph flies to the narrator with a live coal taken with tongs from the altar. He touches the narrator's mouth with it and says that it has touched his lips, his iniquity is taken away, and his sin forgiven. This answers the narrator's cry that he is a man of unclean lips.
