Idol worshipers are described as straying from knowledge of God, living in ignorant war yet calling evils peace. They practice madness, lying prophecy, unrighteousness and false oaths, trusting lifeless idols for safety, and because they thought evil of God and despised holiness, just judgment will pursue them.

Chapter 14

Worshipers of idols are said to go astray in the knowledge of God, live in a great war of ignorance, and call a multitude of evils peace. They make merry to madness, prophesy lies, live unrighteously and lightly swear false oaths, trusting lifeless idols to spare them from harm. Because they thought evil of God and despised holiness, the just doom will pursue them.
