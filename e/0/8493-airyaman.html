Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Airyaman is described as the desired friend and peers-man, asked to draw near in grace to Zarathustra's instructed men and women. In Vendidad (Vendîdâd), Airyaman is invoked as a much-desired holy healer to bring rejoicing and drive away disease, death, and evil.

Airyaman in Vendidad (Vendîdâd)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Airyaman first appears

Abstract written by a model

Airyaman is a much-desired holy healer invoked for the faithful and the men and women of Zarathustra to bring rejoicing. He is called upon to smite or drive away all manner of disease, sickness, and death, as well as Yâtus, Pairikas, and wicked Gainis. In Fargards XX and XXI, the call emphasizes rejoicing and protection from these harms. In Fargard XXII, after receiving Ahura Mazda’s message through Nairyô-sangha, Airyaman comes with nine stallions, camels, bulls, head of small cattle, twigs, and furrows, then drives the evils away. He is associated with the mountain and forest of the holy questions, toward which he eagerly sets off.

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Fargard XX.

Airyaman is called upon to come for the men and women of Zarathustra and the faithful to rejoice. He is also called upon to smite diseases, deaths, the Yâtus, Pairikas, and wicked Gainis.

Fargard XXI.

Airyaman is called to come for the men and women of Zarathustra and the faithful to rejoice. He is asked to smite all manner of diseases and deaths, Yâtus, Pairikas, and wicked Gainis.

Fargard XXII.

Airyaman is called the much-desired. After receiving Ahura Mazda's message through Nairyô-sangha, Airyaman comes with nine stallions, nine camels, nine bulls, nine head of small cattle, nine twigs, and nine furrows, and drives away diseases, deaths, Yâtus, Pairikas, and wicked Gainis.
