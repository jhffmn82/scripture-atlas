Abstract written by a model

In Proverbs, speech marks character: the righteous mouth is a spring of life and source of wisdom, while perverse, slanderous, or excessive speech brings harm. In Ecclesiastes or, The Preacher, speech and words are taught to be restrained before God, since many words bring vanity and the mouth must not cause sin.

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where speech and words first appears

Abstract written by a model

A teaching on restraint in speech before God: words should be few, since many words bring vanity like many dreams, and the mouth must not cause sin, such as protesting a vow was a mistake.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

One should not be rash with the mouth or hasty in heart before God, and words should be few. Many words bring vanities like many dreams, and the mouth should not lead one into sin, such as protesting to the messenger that a vow was a mistake.
