Abstract written by a model

In Paul’s Letter to the Galatians, it is the promise to Abraham and his offspring on which the inheritance rests, making believers heirs according to promise. In The Letter to the Hebrews, it is God's promise and oath to Abraham that gives heirs who fled for refuge strong encouragement to hold the hope set before them.

Abstract written by a model

God promised Abraham blessing and multiplication, then swore by himself since none greater existed. Promise and oath, two immutable things in which God cannot lie, give heirs who fled for refuge strong encouragement to hold the hope set before them.

God promised Abraham blessing and multiplication and swore by himself since he could swear by no one greater. The promise and oath are two immutable things in which God cannot lie, giving strong encouragement to those who have fled for refuge to take hold of the hope set before them.
