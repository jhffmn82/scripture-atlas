Chapter 3

The serpent is cursed above all livestock and animals, to go on its belly and eat dust, with hostility between its offspring and the woman's. The woman is given greatly multiplied pain in childbirth, desire for her husband, and his rule over her. Adam faces cursed ground, laborious eating with thorns and thistles, bread by the sweat of his face, and return to dust, and the man is driven out of the garden away from the tree of life.
