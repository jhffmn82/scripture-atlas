Abstract written by a model

The inner sanctuary, the most holy place at the back of the house, is a twenty-cubit cube prepared to hold the ark of the LORD's covenant. Built of cedar and overlaid with pure gold, it is fronted with gold chains and entered through carved, gold-overlaid olive-wood doors.

Chapter 6

The inner sanctuary was prepared at the back of the house, twenty cubits in length, width, and height, to hold the ark of the LORD's covenant. It was built with cedar boards, overlaid with pure gold, and fronted with gold chains. Its entrance had olive-wood doors carved with cherubim, palm trees, and open flowers and overlaid with gold.
