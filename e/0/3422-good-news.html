Abstract written by a model

In The Good News According to Mark, the word is what the farmer sows, producing fruit in those who hear and accept it. In The Good News According to Mark, the Good News is the message about Jesus Christ, the Son of God, preached by Jesus and his disciples. In The Acts of the Apostles, the Good News is preached in Lystra and Derbe and calls people to turn from vain things to the living God. In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, the Good News is God’s message about his Son, God's power for salvation, and what Paul preaches and serves. In Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians, the Good News is the message Paul preaches, whose core is Christ’s death for sins, burial, and resurrection. In 2 Corinthians, the Good News of Christ is the message of Christ’s glory as God’s image, veiled to those who are dying. In Paul’s Letter to the Galatians, the Good News of Christ is the divinely given gospel Paul preached and the Galatians received. In Paul’s Letter to the Philippians, the Good News is advanced through partnership, defended by Paul, proclaimed by others, and worthy of the believers’ way of life.

and 6 more works

In Paul’s Letter to the Colossians, the Good News is the word of truth and hope that bears fruit and grows throughout the world. In 1 Thessalonians, the Good News of God is a powerful message received with joy in affliction and spread throughout the regions. In 2 Timothy, the Good News is what Timothy must endure hardship for and through which life and immortality were brought to light. In 2 Timothy, the Word of Truth is to be handled properly, contrasted with those who err concerning the truth. In The Letter to the Hebrews, the word of God is living and active, piercing and discerning the heart’s thoughts and intentions. In 1 Peter, the Good News is the enduring word of the Lord, announced by the prophets and preached through the Holy Spirit.

Abstract written by a model

The Good News is the message Paul preaches, which the Lord ordained should support those who proclaim it. In Chapter 9, Paul preaches under necessity, declines support, offers it free, and acts for its sake to share in it. In Chapter 15, it is what the brothers received and are saved by if they hold firm. Its core is that Christ died for sins, was buried, and was raised on the third day, confirmed by many appearances.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 9

The Good News is what Paul preaches under necessity, and he says the Lord ordained that those who proclaim it should live from it. Paul declines support so as to cause no hindrance to it and presents it without charge. He does all things for its sake, so that he may be a joint partaker of it.

Chapter 15

The Good News is what the speaker preached and the brothers received, by which they are saved if they hold firmly to it. Its core, delivered first of all, is that Christ died for our sins, was buried, and was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures, as confirmed by many appearances. The speaker says that whether it is he or the others, so they preach and so the brothers believed.

Abstract written by a model

The Good News of Christ is the gospel Paul preached and the Galatians received, which he insists is the only one and not according to man. Chapter 2 shows Paul entrusted with it for the uncircumcised, affirmed by Jerusalem leaders, defended against false brothers, and violated by Peter's conduct.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Paul’s Letter to the Galatians / Chapter 1

The Good News of Christ is the gospel Paul preached and the Galatians received, and he says there is no other. He declares that it is not according to man, that he neither received it from man nor was taught it, and that anyone preaching something different is to be cursed.

Chapter 2

Paul preaches the Good News among the Gentiles and laid it before the Jerusalem leaders, who added nothing to him. He was entrusted with it for the uncircumcised, as Peter was for the circumcised. Paul did not yield to the false brothers so that the truth of the Good News might continue with the Galatians, and he says Peter and the others did not walk uprightly according to it.
