Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Zechariah, the LORD's shepherd, the man close to the LORD of Armies, is targeted when the LORD calls the sword to awake against him and commands that he be struck, scattering the sheep. In The Gospel According to John, the shepherd in Jesus' parable enters the sheepfold by the door, is admitted by the gatekeeper, calls his sheep by name, and leads them out ahead.

author unknown · AD 90 to 100 · the work’s page · read it where shepherd (in the parable) first appears

Abstract written by a model

The shepherd in Jesus' parable enters the sheep fold by the door, is admitted by the gatekeeper, calls his sheep by name and leads them out ahead. He contrasts with thieves and hired hands; Jesus later identifies himself as the good shepherd.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

In the parable the shepherd enters by the door, is opened to by the gatekeeper, calls his own sheep by name, leads them out and goes before them. Jesus contrasts him with the thief and robber who climb in another way. Jesus later identifies himself as the good shepherd, distinct from the hired hand who flees from the wolf.
