the skilful masters (of the Tao)

group · 1 work · 17 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: The skilful masters (of the Tao), They, They who preserve this method of the Tao, the skilful masters

Abstract written by a model

The skilful masters of the Tao are masters with subtle, exquisite penetration of the Tao’s mysteries, deep beyond men’s knowledge. They appear cautious, irresolute, grave, evanescent, unpretentious, vacant, and dull. By preserving the Tao’s method and not seeking fullness of self, they remain worn rather than new and complete.
