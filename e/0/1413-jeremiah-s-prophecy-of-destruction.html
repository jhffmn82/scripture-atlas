Abstract written by a model

Jeremiah's conditional prophecy of destruction warns that if the people ignore the LORD's law and prophets, the LORD's house will become like Shiloh and Jerusalem a curse to all nations. It provokes demands for his death, yet allows the LORD to relent if they amend; elders cite Micah's similar prophecy.
