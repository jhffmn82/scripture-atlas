Abstract written by a model

Kitra, born to a merchant family in Purimatâla, enters the order after hearing the Law, becomes a monk indifferent to sensual pleasures, advises Brahmadatta to abandon them, practices excellent conduct, penance, and highest self-control, and reaches perfection.

Kitra is in one work on this site, Uttaradhyayana Sutra. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Thirteenth Lecture.

Kitra was born in Purimatâla in a merchant family and entered the order after hearing the Law. He advised Brahmadatta to abandon sensual pleasures, practiced excellent conduct and penance, and reached the highest place of perfection.
