Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Nazareth is the Galilean city where Joseph, Mary, and the child settle after returning from Egypt. In The Gospel According to Luke, Nazareth is the Galilean city where Jesus grows up and later faces rejection in its synagogue.

Nazareth in The Gospel According to Luke

author unknown · AD 80 to 90 · the work’s page · read it where Nazareth first appears

Abstract written by a model

Nazareth is a city in Galilee built on a hill with a cliff brow. Joseph leaves it for Bethlehem, and the family returns there after Jerusalem. Jesus grows up there, obedient to his parents. Later he reads Isaiah in its synagogue; townspeople doubt him, grow wrathful, and lead him to the brow to throw him off, but he walks away.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Nazareth in Galilee is the city Joseph leaves to go to Bethlehem for the enrollment. After fulfilling the law's requirements in Jerusalem, the family returns there as their own city. Jesus grows up there and returns to it after the Passover visit at age twelve, being subject to his parents.

Chapter 4

Nazareth is the town where Jesus was brought up and where he reads from Isaiah in the synagogue on the Sabbath. Its people doubt him as Joseph's son, grow wrathful at his words, and lead him to the brow of the hill on which the city is built to throw him off. He passes through them and goes his way.
