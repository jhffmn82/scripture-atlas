Surah 66: The Prohibition (At-Tahrim)

document · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns Allah's guidance to the Prophet and believers about lawful matters, repentance, protection, and conduct toward disbelievers and hypocrites. It recounts the Prophet's disclosure of a confidential matter to one of his wives and warns two wives to repent and not aid against Muhammad. It commands believers to guard themselves and their families from the Fire and promises sincere penitents Gardens with flowing rivers. It gives examples through the wives of Noah and Lot, the wife of Pharaoh, and Mary, daughter of 'Imran.
