Abstract written by a model

In The Acts of the Apostles, Macedonia is the region from which a man in Paul’s vision begs him to come and help, leading the company to preach there. In 2 Corinthians, Macedonia is the region whose people Paul has told about Achaia’s readiness, and some of whom may come with him.
