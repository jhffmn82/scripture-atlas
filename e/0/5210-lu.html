Lu

Also called: Lu State, State of Lu, the Lu State, the State of Lu

Abstract written by a model

In Chuang Tzŭ, Lu State is a state of Confucius and Liu Hsia Chi, where Confucius was driven out twice. In Chuang Tzŭ, Lu is a State containing Wang T'ai and many apparent scholars, setting episodes involving Wang T'ai, Shu Shan No-toes, Wên Po Hsüeh Tzŭ, Duke Ai, and a stranger; Wang T'ai’s teaching reaches the whole world, while Duke Ai tests true scholars against robe-wearers. In The Analects of Confucius, Book V (Kung-Ye Ch'Ang), Lu is presented as a place where virtuous men exist, whose presence is implied to have helped Tsze-chien acquire his character. In The Analects of Confucius, Book VI (Yung Yey), Lu would come to a State where true principles predominated by one change. In The Analects of Confucius, Book IX (Tsze Han), Lu was the place to which the Master returned. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XIII: Tsze-Lu, Lu is described as having a government that is a brother to the government of Wei. In Mencius, Book I (Liang Hui Wang), Lu is involved in a brush with Zou. In Mencius, Book VI (Gaozi), Lu is a five-hundred-li-square territory, including Nan Yang as potentially taken from Qi; though larger than Zhou Gong’s original grant, it should be governed through benevolence and perhaps diminished by a true royal ruler.
