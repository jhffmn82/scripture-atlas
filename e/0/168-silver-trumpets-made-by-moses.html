Chapter 10

Two trumpets of beaten silver are to be made, to be used for calling the congregation and for the journeying of the camps. Blowing both gathers all the congregation at the door of the Tent of Meeting, blowing one gathers the princes, and an alarm sends the east camps and then the south camps forward. They are also to be sounded as an alarm in war and blown over burnt offerings and peace offerings on days of gladness, set feasts, and the beginnings of months as a memorial before God.
