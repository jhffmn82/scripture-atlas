the sea Vouru-Kasha

place · 6 works · 13 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Vouru-Kasha, Vouru-kasha, sea Vouru-Kasha, sea Vouru-kasha, the bright sea Vouru-Kasha, the middle of the sea Vouru-Kasha, the sea Vouru-kasha

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Vouru-kasha is the sea containing the sea where the sacred Unicorn (?) stands and to which sacrifice is offered. In Visparad, sea Vouru-kasha is worshipped. In Vendidad (Vendîdâd), Fargards I-VIII, sea Vouru-kasha sends waters with wind and clouds to corpses, Dakhmas, unclean remains, and bones, after which the waters return unseen from the sea Pûitika toward the well-watered tree. In Vendidad (Vendîdâd), the sea Vouru-kasha is the holy gathering sea from which waters rise, travel through the air, descend to earth, and roll along. In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, the sea Vouru-Kasha is a powerful saltwater sea where Ardvi Sûra Anâhita flows, whose shores and middle boil, and where various mythic events occur. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, the sea Vouru-Kasha receives waters, Glory, and the Son of the Waters, while its arms form three lakes and Frangrasyan repeatedly fails to seize the Glory.

sea Vouru-Kasha in Yashts (Yasts) I-XII

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where sea Vouru-Kasha first appears

Abstract written by a model

Vouru-Kasha is a powerful, large, deep sea of salt waters. Ardvi Sûra Anâhita runs into it from Hukairya; its shores and middle boil, holding Mount Us-hindu and the tree of the eagle. Frangrasyan seeks Aryan Glory there, Keresâspa opposes Gandarewa, and Tistrya fights Apaosha, rises with Satavaêsa, and sends its waters toward material countries.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

V. Âbân Yast.

Vouru-Kasha is the sea into which Ardvi Sûra Anâhita runs from Hukairya. Its shores and middle are described as boiling over, and it is the place where Frangrasyan seeks to seize the Glory belonging to the Aryan people and where Keresâspa seeks to overcome Gandarewa.

VIII. Tîr Yast.

Vouru-Kasha is the powerful, large, deep sea of salt waters where Tistrya fights Apaosha and where Tistrya and Satavaêsa rise. Tistrya makes the sea boil and sends its waters, through vapours and clouds, toward the countries of the material world.

the sea Vouru-Kasha in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where the sea Vouru-Kasha first appears

Abstract written by a model

Vouru-Kasha is a sea whose shores and middle boil as Ardvi Sura Anahita flows into it. Its arms form Lake Husravah, Lake Vanghazdau, and Awz-dânva. Guarded by countless Fravashis, it receives the Glory after its escape; the Son of the Waters seizes it while Frangrasyan repeatedly fails.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XIII. Farvardîn Yast.

The sea Vouru-Kasha receives the powerful flow of Ardvi Sura Anahita. It is watched over by ninety thousand, nine thousand, nine hundred, and ninety-nine Fravashis of the faithful.

XIX. Zamyâd Yast.

The sea Vouru-Kasha receives the Glory after it escapes the struggle between the Good Spirit and the Evil One. The Son of the Waters seizes the Glory there, while Frangrasyan repeatedly dives into it trying unsuccessfully to seize it.
