Abstract written by a model

Issachar is a figure with four sons, Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron. His families were notable for mighty men of valor, counted by genealogy at 87,000, with army bands of 36,000 due to many wives and sons.

Chapter 7

Issachar's sons are named as Tola, Puah, Jashub, and Shimron, four in all. His families included mighty men of valor, listed by genealogy as eighty-seven thousand, and bands of the army numbered thirty-six thousand because they had many wives and sons.
