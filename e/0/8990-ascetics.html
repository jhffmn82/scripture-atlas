Ninth Lesson.

Ascetics are described as illustrious, pious, virtuous, eloquent, restrained, controlled, chaste, and having ceased from sensual intercourse. Fellow-ascetics living nearby and following the same rules may be asked to consume food or drink that is too much for a monk or nun.
