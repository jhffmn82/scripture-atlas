Chapter 2

Two chamberlains, chiefs of the body-guard, plotted to kill the king out of grief over Mordecai's promotion. Mordecai discovered it and told Esther, who declared it to the king. The king examined and hanged the two men, and a note of Mordecai's goodwill was made in the royal library.
