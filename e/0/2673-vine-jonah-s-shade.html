Chapter 4

God prepares a vine that comes up over Jonah to shade his head and relieve his discomfort, making him exceedingly glad. A worm God prepares at dawn chews it so that it withers; God notes it came up in a night and perished in a night, and Jonah did not labor for it or make it grow.
