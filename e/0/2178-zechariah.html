Abstract written by a model

Zechariah, son of Jehoiada the priest, was a prophet and ruler of God's house, descended from the Kohathites and Asaph's line. Moved by God's Spirit, he warned the people that forsaking the LORD meant being forsaken. They conspired and stoned him at the king's command; he asked the LORD to repay.

Chapter 24

Zechariah, son of Jehoiada the priest, was moved by the Spirit of God to stand above the people and warn that forsaking the LORD meant being forsaken. The people conspired against him and stoned him in the court of the LORD's house at the king's command. As he died he said, "May the LORD look at it, and repay it."
