Guru Angad

person · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

Guru Angad is presented as a teacher of the Guru’s instruction and of devotion to God. His teachings begin by portraying the Guru as essential light against profound darkness. They describe the world as the True One’s dwelling, governed by God’s order, which frees some from mammon and reveals God to the holy. He elevates repetition of the one God’s name above social and religious duties, and defines true love as absorption in God rather than worldly attachment. He further teaches service, humility, truthful speech, friendship, praise of God, and respect for God’s works. He concludes by affirming God’s ordering of creation, including animals’ birth and death.

Guru Angad is in one work on this site, Asa ki War (Asa di Var). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Slok .. Pauri I

Guru Angad teaches that even a hundred moons and a thousand suns would leave appalling darkness without the Guru.

Slok II .. Pauri II

Guru Angad teaches that the world is the True One's chamber and dwelling. He says that God acts according to His order, withdraws some from mammon, leaves others in it, and reveals Himself to those known as holy.

Slok XII .. Pauri XII

Guru Angad teaches that repeating the name of the one God is the highest duty, above the duties associated with Jogis, Brahmans, Khatris, and Sudars. He says that one who knows the secret of this is a bright God himself, and that Nanak is his slave. He also describes God as the Supreme God of souls and says that one who knows the secrets of the soul and God is a bright God himself.

Slok XXI .. Pauri XXI

Guru Angad teaches that true love is being ever absorbed in God rather than attached to worldly things. He also teaches that saluting while criticizing God's works is mistaken and vain.

Slok XXII .. Pauri XXII

Guru Angad is named as the speaker of the teachings in Slok XXII. He teaches about service, humility, speech, friendship, falsehood, and praising God.

Slok XXIV .. Pauri XXIV

Guru Angad teaches that God Himself arranges what He has made and puts it in its proper place. He says that God created animals and beholds their birth and death.
