Abstract written by a model

While Jehudi reads to the king in the winter house, Jehoiakim cuts off three or four columns at a time with a penknife and throws them into the brazier until the whole scroll is consumed. He and his servants show no fear and do not tear their garments. The act leads to the LORD's command for a new scroll and his judgment on Jehoiakim.
