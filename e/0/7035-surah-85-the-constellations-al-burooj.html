Abstract written by a model

Surah 85: The Constellations (Al-Burooj) concerns the owners of the ditch who persecuted believers by witnessing them in a fuel-fed fire. It condemns those who persecute believing men and women without repentance and promises them hell, while promising believers who do good works Gardens beneath which rivers flow. It describes Allah as the Sovereign, Witness, Forgiving, Loving, and Doer of what He wills, and mentions the hosts of Pharaoh and Thamud. It declares that the Qur'an is glorious and kept on a guarded tablet.
