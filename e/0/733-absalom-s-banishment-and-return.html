Chapter 14

Absalom's banishment in Geshur ends when the king orders Joab to bring him back and Joab brings him to Jerusalem. The return is only partial: Absalom lives in his own house and does not see the king's face for two full years. The matter is resolved when Joab carries Absalom's plea to the king, who calls Absalom in, receives his bow, and kisses him.
