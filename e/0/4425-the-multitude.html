Chapter 7

The multitude murmurs about Jesus, with some calling him a good man and others saying he leads people astray. One answer to him is that he has a demon, and many believe in him because of his signs. They are divided, some calling him the prophet, some the Christ, and others objecting that the Christ does not come from Galilee.
