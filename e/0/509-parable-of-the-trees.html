Chapter 9

In Jotham's parable the olive, fig, and vine each refuse to reign over the trees, unwilling to give up their oil, fruit, and wine. The bramble accepts, offering shade and threatening fire to devour the cedars of Lebanon. Jotham applies it to Abimelech and Shechem.
