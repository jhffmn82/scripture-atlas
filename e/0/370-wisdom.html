Abstract written by a model

In Proverbs, the fountain of wisdom is compared to a flowing brook, while the wise seek knowledge and the discerning gain understanding. In Baruch, wisdom is the fountain Israel forsook and the source of length of days and life; only God knows her and gave her to Jacob and Israel, after which she appeared on earth living with men.

wisdom in Baruch

author unknown · about 299 BC · the work’s page · read it where wisdom first appears

Abstract written by a model

Wisdom is the fountain Israel forsook, a source of length of days and life. No one found her place: not princes, giants, the sons of Agar, or merchants, and none can fetch her from heaven or sea. Only God knows her; he gave her to Jacob and Israel, and she then appeared on earth, living with men.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Wisdom is called the fountain Israel forsook; walking in God's way would have meant peace forever. No one has found her place or treasuries: not the princes, hoarders of silver, giants, nor the sons of Agar and merchants of Merran and Teman. No one can go up to heaven or across the sea to fetch her. Only God knows her, found her with his understanding, and gave her to Jacob and Israel, after which she appeared on earth and lived with men.
