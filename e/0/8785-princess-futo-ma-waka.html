Sect. LVII.—Emperor I-Toku.

Princess Futo-ma-waka, also called Princess Ihi-bi, was wedded to Emperor I-Toku. She was the ancestress of the Departmental Lords of Shiki and was the mother of Mi-ma-tsu-hiko-wake-shine and Tagishi-hiko.
