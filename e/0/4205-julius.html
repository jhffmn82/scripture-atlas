Chapter 27

Julius, a centurion of the Augustan band, takes charge of Paul and other prisoners, treats Paul kindly at Sidon, and puts them aboard a ship of Alexandria at Myra. He heeds the master and owner of the ship over Paul's warning, but later acts on Paul's advice about the sailors. Desiring to save Paul, he stops the soldiers from killing the prisoners and orders swimmers to go overboard first.
