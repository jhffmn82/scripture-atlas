Gâh Ushahin

event · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Ushahina

Abstract written by a model

Gâh Ushahin is a holy lord of the ritual order, concerned with Ushahina, Beregya, Nmânya, Sraosha, Rashnu, and Arstât; it sacrifices to Aurora, dawn, Ahura Mazda, Good Mind, Asha Vahista, Khshathra-vairya, and Âramaiti, then blesses Sraosha, Rashnu, and Arstât.

Gâh Ushahin is in one work on this site, Afrinagan, Gahs and Miscellaneous Fragments. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
