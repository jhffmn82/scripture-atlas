Sect. CLXXV.—Emperor Sen-Kuwa.

The august children of Emperor Sen-Kuwa numbered five altogether: three Kings and two Queens. They were born to him by Her Augustness Tachi-bana-no-naka-tsu-hime and Kafuchi-no-waku-go-hime.
