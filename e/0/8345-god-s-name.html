Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, God's name is revealed as the LORD, I AM WHO I AM, and an enduring memorial for all generations. In Japji, God's Name is true, pure, cleansing, and a practice through which contemplation may lead to emancipation. In So Dar and So Purukh (the Rahiras), God's name is a divine jewel whose remembrance brings life, happiness, absorption, and relief from pain. In Sohila (Kirtan Sohila), God's name supports Nanak, brings happiness through absorption, and is a profit to earn in life. In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), God's name is the supreme duty and the word that brings comfort, honour, and salvation. In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), the true Name is the Formless divine, essential to repetition, worship, sacrifice, and speech. In Hymns of Guru Nanak, true merchandise is God's name as spiritual wealth that destroys sin, brings wisdom, and leads to freedom and God's court. In Hymns of Guru Nanak, God's name is presented as true, pure, unfailing wealth and the essential means of salvation.

God's Name in Japji

Max Arthur Macauliffe · the work’s page · read it where God's Name first appears

Abstract written by a model

God's Name is presented as true, pure, and cleansing. Through love of the Name, it cleanses sin, removes sorrow and the mind's impurity, and is melted in divine love. It is also a true practice for those on whom God looks with favour. Within that practice, the Name is melted in divine love and the Word is coined in the true mint. Those who ponder the Name and depart after completing their toil may be emancipated. The account moves from the Name's truth and cleansing power, through its loving, favoured practice, to the possibility of emancipation for those who contemplate it.

God's name is a jewel obtained through God's kindness. Remembering, cherishing, serving, and repeating it brings life, happiness, absorption, and relief from pain; forgetting it leaves people without spiritual worth, while repetition prepares them to cross the terrible ocean.

God's name in Asa ki War (Asa di Var)

Guru Nanak · the work’s page · read it where God's name first appears

Abstract written by a model

God's name is the only word that truly counts, the highest duty for all, and is everywhere, like the sun in heaven. Meeting the true Guru fixes it in the heart, bringing comfort, freedom from hopes and fears, and an abiding place. Those who sow its seed depart with honour. Repeating it removes sins and leads to salvation; in the Kal age it was called Allah.

Slok XIII .. Pauri XIII

The true Name is the Formless divine. Repeating it prevents hell, while saints praise God through it and remain in everlasting joy. true merchandise in Hymns of Guru Nanak

Guru Nanak · the work’s page · read it where true merchandise first appears

Abstract written by a model

God's Name is true merchandise and unfailing spiritual wealth. Repeated with the tongue and remembered, it destroys sin, gives wisdom, knowledge, and understanding, profits humanity day and night, and cools the world's fire. Those who traffic in it gain profit, preserve their capital, receive honour and tranquilly meditate on God. Unlike worldly wealth, it accompanies people after death, brings God’s favour, and leads to freedom, happiness, and God's court.

the Name in Hymns of Guru Nanak

Guru Nanak · the work’s page · read it where the Name first appears

Abstract written by a model

God's name is presented as a true, pure, unfailing wealth: nectar, jewel, merchandise, capital, support, and object of meditation. It has infinite sweetness and value, sustains man, brings honour, purity, wisdom, devotion, contentment, peace, happiness, and access to God's court. Remembering, repeating, adoring, treasuring in the heart, and reverencing the Name, especially under the True Guru's instruction, remove sin, fear, pride, greed, doubt, sorrow, pain, disease, mourning, separation, worldly desire, worldly love, error, transmigration, rebirth, hell, and Death's net. It frees and delivers human beings, enables crossing the world, causes meeting and absorption with God, protects the soul, assists at the Last Hour, and is necessary for salvation. The Name is superior to religious ceremonies, ablutions, alms, offerings, calls to prayer, the horn of the Jogi, and other religious acts; without it, learning, dress, austerities, cleverness, and practices are vain and lead to suffering. It is to be thought of, heard, spoken of, and repeated, including on the tenth lunar day. Its culminating claim is that it is the highest and only unfailing religious act, containing six religious duties; however, selling it by writing it is condemned.

Sri Rag .. Ashtapadi

God's name is the true merchandise, pure wealth, a jewel, and the support that brings happiness, honour, purity, and deliverance. Through the Guru, God's name removes sin, fear, pride, and transmigration.

Majh ki War

God's Name is to be remembered, repeated, and adored. Through the Name a person is absorbed in happiness, receives comfort, and approaches God's court.

Rag Gauri .. Gauri Chhant

The Name is nectar, a support, and true wealth that frees human beings from worldly desires and death. Those who meditate on and repeat the Name are absorbed in God and obtain peace and happiness.

Rag Asa .. Asa Ashtapadi

The true Name is the source of honour, purification, wisdom, devotion, and spiritual wealth. It blooms in the heart, destroys sin and fear, protects from death, and brings people to God’s court and salvation.

Asa Chhant

God's name has infinite sweetness and value and brings happiness and protection from the bitterness of worldly desire. Without God's name, fools and the heedless wander in superstition, suffer, and are ruined.

Rag Gujari .. Gujari Ashtapadi

The Name is presented as essential to worship, purity, divine knowledge, peace, and deliverance. It is sought as wealth and capital for the heart, and repeating it frees people from worldly love, mourning, separation, disease, doubt, and transmigration.

Rag Wadhans .. Wadhans ki War

God's name is described as ambrosial and sweet to the soul. It brings happiness, removes sorrow, grants victory in the arena of the world, and is superior to alms-offerings and various good works.

Rag Sorath .. Sorath Ashtapadi

God's Name is the support and capital that brings happiness, purity, contentment, and freedom from doubt. Repeating it under the Guru's instruction destroys greed, protects from transmigration, and is superior to ablutions, alms, and similar religious acts.

Rag Dhanasari .. Dhanasari Chhant

God's name enables people to cross over, remain attached to God, and become absorbed in Him. Those who forget it despite reading and cleverness suffer punishment and transmigration.

Rag Suhi .. Suhi ki War

The Name is the support of the supportless and the means by which man profits spiritually and avoids wandering in error. It is superior to ritual calls and is necessary for love, salvation, and freedom from rebirth.

Lunar Days

The Name is to be repeated as offerings and ablutions on the tenth day of the lunar month.

Ramkali .. Ramkali ki War I

The Name is the highest religious work and assists a person at the last hour. Remembering the Name saves a person from error, transmigration, and hell, while forgetting it leads to suffering.

Rag Maru Ashtapadi .. Maru ki War I

The True Name brings happiness, final deliverance, emancipation, and a sight of God's court. Repeating and reverencing it gives patience, removes fear and avarice, and prevents rebirth.

Bhairo

God's name is necessary for salvation and makes the holy man obtain it. Without it, birth, learning, religious practices, religious dress, and bodily austerities are useless, while whoever repeats it is saved.

Sarang ki War

God's name gives sight to the blind. Writing God's name in order to sell it is condemned.

Rag Prabhati .. Prabhati Ashtapadi

The jewel of the Name is loved night and day, and repetition of the Name is presented as the only religious act that is not vain. The six religious duties are contained in the name of the Bright One.
