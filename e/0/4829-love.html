Abstract written by a model

In Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians, love gives value to every gift and sacrifice, is patient and kind, endures all things, never fails, and is greatest among faith, hope, and love. In 1 Thessalonians, brotherly love is shown among the believers and is something in which they are exhorted to abound more and more. In 1 John, loving one's brother keeps a person in the light, while hating one's brother leaves a person blind in darkness. In 1 John, love for one another is a sign of passing from death into life, is known through Christ laying down his life, and must be shown in deed and truth. In 1 John, love originates from God, is shown in sending his Son, casts out fear, and requires loving one's brother. In 2 John, love is the commandment to love one another and is defined as walking according to God's commandments. In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), love is found with fear of God, leads to salvation through devotion and lowliness, and remains absorbed in God while worldly attachment is rejected. In Hymns of Guru Nanak, God's love unites people with God, leads toward emancipation, removes pride, and makes people fearless, peaceful, and detached from worldly desires.

love in Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where love first appears

Abstract written by a model

Love is presented as the quality that gives value to every gift and sacrifice; without it the speaker is nothing. It is patient, kind, not envious, boastful, proud, self-seeking or easily provoked, keeps no account of evil, rejoices with truth, and bears, believes, hopes and endures all things. It never fails, remains after prophecy, languages and knowledge pass away, and is greatest of faith, hope and love.

Abstract written by a model

A contrast between loving and hating one's brother: whoever claims to be in the light yet hates his brother remains blind in darkness, not knowing his direction, while whoever loves his brother remains in the light without stumbling.

love in Asa ki War (Asa di Var)

Guru Nanak · the work’s page · read it where love first appears

Abstract written by a model

Love is found with fear of God and leads to salvation through devotion and lowliness. It moves good people to weep, laugh, or be silent, and makes them care only for the true Master. Worldly attachment is rejected; true love remains absorbed in God, as a servant in the master.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Slok V .. Pauri V

Love is found in those who have the fear of God in their hearts.

Slok XX .. Pauri XX

God's love moves good people to weep, laugh, or remain silent. It leads them to care for nothing except the true Master.

Slok XXI .. Pauri XXI

Love that attaches itself to worldly things is not true love. True love is described as being ever absorbed in God.

Slok XXIII .. Pauri XXIII

Love of the master is the state in which a servant is absorbed.

God's love in Hymns of Guru Nanak

Guru Nanak · the work’s page · read it where God's love first appears

Abstract written by a model

God's love unites people with God, brings the soul home to the True One, and leads toward emancipation. It matters because it fulfills the heart's desire, preserves honour with God, removes pride, and makes people fearless, peaceful, and detached from worldly desires. It is likened to the lotus, fish, chatrik, water, chakwi, and sun in longing and protection. Those dyed with it pray to God, exchange hearts with Him, and are not separated from their Spouse. True love preserves honour; without it, love becomes hate, and ritual that forgets the Beloved is condemned. It is a jewel filling the heart, an ornament of the virtuous woman, and food for the few on the right spiritual way.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Sri Rag .. Ashtapadi

Love for God is compared with the lotus's love for water, the fish's need for water, the chatrik's longing for rain, water's protection of milk, and the chakwi's longing for the sun. Genuine love unites the separated with God and removes pride.

Asa Chhant

Love of God brings companionship, happiness, and the fulfillment of the heart's desire. The person dyed with God's love prays to Him and exchanges hearts with Him.

Rag Wadhans .. Wadhans ki War

Love is true when it preserves a person in honour with God. Ritual is condemned when it causes a person to forget the Beloved.

Rag Suhi .. Suhi ki War

Love is the means by which God is found and brings the soul home to the True One. The jewel of love fills the heart, while the absence of true love turns love into hate.

Rag Maru Ashtapadi .. Maru ki War I

God's love makes people fearless, peaceful, and detached from worldly desires. Those imbued with it are not separated from their Spouse and are led toward emancipation.

Sarang ki War

God's love is described as food for those who follow the right spiritual way.
