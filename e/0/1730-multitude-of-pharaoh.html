Abstract written by a model

The multitude of Pharaoh is the host of followers belonging to Egypt's king. First, Pharaoh's arm is shown broken by the LORD and unhealed, with both arms to be broken before Babylon's king. Then his multitude is joined to him in the Assyrian cedar comparison, sharing his coming fall to the lower parts of the earth.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 30

Pharaoh king of Egypt has had his arm broken by the LORD, and it has not been bound up to become strong enough to hold a sword. The LORD says he is against Pharaoh and will break both his arms, so that the sword falls from his hand and he groans like a mortally wounded man before the king of Babylon.

Chapter 31

The multitude of Pharaoh is addressed together with Pharaoh and is included in the comparison to the Assyrian cedar. The closing line identifies the tree as Pharaoh and all his multitude, so they share his coming fall to the lower parts of the earth.
