Abstract written by a model

In The Good News According to Mark, Jesus's brothers come with his mother looking for him, and Jesus identifies those doing God's will as his brother and sister. In The Gospel According to John, Jesus' brothers urge him to go into Judea, do not believe in him, and go ahead of him to the feast.
