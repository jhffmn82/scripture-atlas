Holiness

topic · 3 works · 6 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Holiness against the host of the fiends, Sanctify yourselves, You shall be holy to me, be holy, beings of Holiness, holiness, holy

Abstract written by a model

In Leviticus, holiness is a state the LORD commands of Israel, pursued by avoiding defilement, keeping clean and unclean distinctions, and keeping His statutes. In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), holiness is the state of one to whom God reveals Himself. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Holiness is the best of all good and is sought, praised, obtained, declared, worshipped, and victoriously maintained against fiends.

holiness in Leviticus

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where holiness first appears

Abstract written by a model

Holiness is a state the LORD commands of Israel, required of the children of Israel because He is holy. In Chapter 11 it is pursued by avoiding defilement from creeping things and keeping clean/unclean distinctions; Chapter 20 repeats the command, adding that He set them apart as His, to keep His statutes.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

The LORD commands Israel to sanctify themselves and be holy because He is holy. Avoiding defilement by creeping things and keeping the clean and unclean distinctions is presented as the way to be holy.

Chapter 20

The LORD commands the people to sanctify themselves and be holy because he is the LORD their God and is holy. He has set them apart from the peoples to be his, and they are to keep his statutes and distinguish clean from unclean animals, birds, and creatures that teem on the ground.

Holiness in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Holiness first appears

Abstract written by a model

Holiness is the best of all good. It is sought, praised, obtained, declared, worshipped, and victoriously maintained against fiends. Holy men and women and men of the primitive law seek it; king Vîstâspa maintains it, while friends of Astvat-ereta follow the good law. It is praised through Ashem Vohû, healing Words, and a recital praising Ahura Mazda and Mazda’s good things.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

XXVI.

Holiness is sought, praised, and obtained by the holy men and women and by the men of the primitive law. Ashem Vohû declares that holiness is the best of all good.

XVIII. Âstâd Yast.

Holiness is declared to be the best of all good. It is praised and worshipped through the Ashem Vohû and through the rightly-spoken, healing, fiend-smiting Words.

XIV.

Holiness is victoriously maintained by king Vîstâspa against the host of the fiends. The friends of Astvat-ereta follow the good law, and Holiness is called the best of all good.

XXI. Yast Fragment.

Holiness is praised through a recital that also praises Ahura Mazda and the good things made by Mazda. Beings of Holiness are mentioned in comparison with the value of this recital.
