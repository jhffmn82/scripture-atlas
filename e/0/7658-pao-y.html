Pao Yü

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Pao Yü is a pure, incorruptible, good man who avoids unlike people and remembers wrongdoing; Kuan Chung says that, if employed in governing the empire, these traits would make him quarrel with Duke Huan and the people.

Pao Yü is in one work on this site, Chuang Tzŭ. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Kuan Chung being at the point of death, Duke Huan went to see him.

Pao Yü is described as pure, incorruptible, and good, but unwilling to associate with those unlike himself and unable to forget wrongdoing. Kuan Chung predicts that he would quarrel with Duke Huan and the people.
