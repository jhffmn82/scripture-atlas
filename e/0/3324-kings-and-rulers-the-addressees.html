Chapter 6

Kings, judges, rulers, and princes are told that their dominion was given by the Lord and that, as officers of his kingdom, they failed to judge rightly, keep the law, or walk in God's counsel. They face a stern, swift judgment and are urged to desire the speaker's words, learn wisdom, and honor her so that they may reign forever.
