Hud

person · 4 works · 10 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: the brother of A'ad, their brother Hud, their brother, Hud

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), Hud called A'ad to serve Allah alone, warned them of wrath, and was saved with his followers. In Surah 11: Hud, Hud called his people to serve Allah, seek forgiveness, and repent, and he and the believers were saved from harsh doom. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), Hud called the tribe of A'ad to duty and obedience, reminded them of Allah's blessings, and warned them of an awful day. In Surah 46: The Dunes (Al-Ahqaf), the brother of A'ad warned his people to serve none but Allah and feared for them the doom of a tremendous Day.
