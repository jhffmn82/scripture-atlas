Chapter 12

The men of Gilead are gathered by Jephthah and fight and strike Ephraim, after saying Ephraim's people are fugitives in the middle of Ephraim and Manasseh. They take the fords of the Jordan and test those crossing by asking whether they are Ephraimites and having them say 'Shibboleth'. Those who fail are seized and killed.
