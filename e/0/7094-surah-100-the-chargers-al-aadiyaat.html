Abstract written by a model

Surah 100: The Chargers (Al-Aadiyaat) was revealed at Mecca. It describes chargers snorting, striking sparks, scouring to a raid at dawn, raising dust, and cleaving the centre of the foe. It then concerns man, who is an ingrate unto his Lord, witnesses this, and is violent in his love of wealth. The unit warns that the contents of the graves will be poured forth, the secrets of the breasts made known, and their Lord will be perfectly informed concerning them.
