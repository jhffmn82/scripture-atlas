Abstract written by a model

After the defeat, Lysias agreed to terms recognizing the Jews' rights, and Maccabaeus accepted them. Letters follow from Lysias (dated year 148, Dioscorinthius 24), from King Antiochus to Lysias and to the Jewish senate (year 148, Xanthicus 15), and from the Roman ambassadors Quintus Memmius and Titus Manius, who consented to Lysias's grants and asked the Jews to send someone promptly about matters referred to the king.
