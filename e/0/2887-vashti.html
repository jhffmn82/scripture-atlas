Abstract written by a model

Vashti is a beautiful queen who hosted a banquet for the palace women while the king feasted. She refused his summons to be displayed for her beauty on the seventh day, so a decree barred her from the king and gave her royalty to Esther.

Esther (Greek) / Chapter 1

Vashti the queen made a banquet for the women in the palace while the king feasted. When the king, on the seventh day, sent chamberlains to bring her in to be crowned and shown to the princes and nations for her beauty, she refused to come. As a result the decree barred her from coming in to the king and gave her royalty to a better woman.
