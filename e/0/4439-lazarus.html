Abstract written by a model

Lazarus of Bethany, brother of Mary and loved by Jesus, falls sick and dies. After four days in the tomb, Jesus calls him out, still wrapped in grave cloths, and orders him freed. Later he dines with Jesus at the supper in Bethany. Crowds come to see him, and the chief priests plot to kill him because many believed in Jesus through him.

Lazarus is in one work on this site, The Gospel According to John. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

Lazarus of Bethany, whom Jesus loves, falls sick and dies, and has been in the tomb four days when Jesus arrives. At Jesus' loud call he comes out bound hand and foot in wrappings with his face wrapped in a cloth, and Jesus says to free him and let him go.

Chapter 12

Lazarus, whom Jesus raised from the dead, sits at the table with Jesus at the supper in Bethany. Crowds come partly to see him, and the chief priests conspire to put him to death too because many of the Jews believed in Jesus on account of him.
