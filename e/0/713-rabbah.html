Rabbah in The Second Book of Samuel

author unknown · about 950 BC · the work’s page · read it where Rabbah first appears

Abstract written by a model

Rabbah is the walled Ammonite royal city, the city of waters. Joab besieges it, and Uriah dies fighting near its wall. David orders the assault, then captures it: its crown is taken, plunder carried off, and its people put to labor.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

Rabbah is the city of the Ammonites that Joab and the army besiege. Uriah is killed in fighting near its wall, and David tells Joab to strengthen the battle against the city and overthrow it.

Chapter 12

Rabbah, the Ammonite city, has its royal city taken by Joab and is then captured by David with all the people. Its king's crown is taken, plunder is carried off, and its people are put to labor.
