Abstract written by a model

The yoke is described in The First Book of Kings as the heavy burden and hard service imposed by Solomon, which the people ask Rehoboam to lighten and whose increase triggers Israel’s rebellion. The heavy yoke is described in The Second Book of Chronicles as the grievous service Solomon imposed on Israel, whose proposed increase causes Israel’s break from Rehoboam.
