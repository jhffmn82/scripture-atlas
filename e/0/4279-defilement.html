Chapter 15

Jesus teaches that what enters the mouth does not defile a person, since it passes into the belly and out of the body. What proceeds out of the mouth comes from the heart and does defile, namely evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, sexual sins, thefts, false testimony, and blasphemies. Eating with unwashed hands does not defile.
