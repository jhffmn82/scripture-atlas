Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, nations that fight against Ariel attack Ariel, her stronghold, and Mount Zion; they become like fine dust and chaff, vanishing instantly like a dream leaving a hungry dreamer unsatisfied. In The Second Book of Nephi, nations of the earth become drunken with iniquity and abominations; those that fight against Zion and distress her are visited, become as an empty dream, and are ultimately brought to naught or cut off.
