In Exodus, they are summoned by Pharaoh and use enchantments to imitate signs, but fail at lice, leaving Pharaoh hardened. In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), they oppose Moses for Pharaoh's reward, then prostrate, believe in the Lord of the Worlds, and face Pharaoh's threat. In Surah 20: Taa-Haa, they make their cords and staves appear to run, accept defeat, believe in the Lord of Aaron and Moses, and refuse to prefer Pharaoh over the clear proofs. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), they throw down their cords and staves, then believe in the Lord of the Worlds and face Pharaoh's threats.

magicians (Pharaoh's magicians) in Exodus

author unknown · about 1439 to 1399 BC · the work’s page · read it where magicians (Pharaoh's magicians) first appears

Abstract written by a model

Wise men and sorcerers summoned by Pharaoh who use enchantments to imitate signs: they match rods-to-serpents, water-to-blood and frogs, though Aaron's rod swallows theirs, and they fail at lice, leaving Pharaoh hardened.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

Pharaoh's wise men, sorcerers and magicians cast down their rods, which become serpents by their enchantments, but Aaron's rod swallows theirs. They also turn water to blood with their enchantments, which leaves Pharaoh's heart hardened.
