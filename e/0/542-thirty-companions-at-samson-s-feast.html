attends wedding feast (Samson's feast) [brought to be with Samson] Thirty companions are brought to be with Samson at his feast. They cannot solve his riddle in three days, so on the seventh day they threaten Samson's wife with burning to make her get the answer. They then answer the riddle before sunset and receive the garments Samson pays from the plunder at Ashkelon.
