believing women

group · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: such women, women

Abstract written by a model

Believing women may come to believers as fugitives and are to be examined regarding their faith. If they are known to be true believers, they are not to be sent back to disbelievers, and marriage to them is permitted after giving them their dues.
