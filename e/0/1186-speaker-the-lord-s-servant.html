In The Second Book of Nephi, the speaker receives a learned tongue and awakened ear, endures abuse without hiding, and trusts the Lord’s help and justification.

Abstract written by a model

An anointed preacher, sent by the Lord GOD, is commissioned to preach good news to the humble, bind up the brokenhearted, proclaim liberty to captives, and comfort mourners. He closes by rejoicing, clothed in garments of salvation and a robe of righteousness, like a bridegroom or bride.

Chapter 61

The speaker says the LORD has anointed and sent him to preach good news to the humble, bind up the broken hearted, proclaim liberty to captives, and comfort all who mourn. He rejoices greatly in the LORD, saying he has been clothed with the garments of salvation and covered with the robe of righteousness, like a bridegroom or a bride.

speaker (the Lord's servant) in The Second Book of Nephi

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where speaker (the Lord's servant) first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Lord’s servant receives a learned tongue and awakened ear to speak timely words to Israel. Obedient and unturned, he endures smiting, hair-plucking, shame, and spitting without hiding. Trusting the Lord God’s help and justification, he sets his face like flint, remains unashamed, and smites adversaries with his mouth’s strength.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

The speaker receives the tongue of the learned and an awakened ear, and is not rebellious or turned back. He gives his back to the smiter, his cheeks to those who pluck off the hair, and his face to shame and spitting, while trusting that the Lord God will help and justify him.
