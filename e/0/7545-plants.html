Also called: all the plants, growing plants, the plants

Abstract written by a model

Plants grow from earth by never-failing springs, continue growing after Angra Mainyu’s malice is destroyed, are created by Ahura Mazda, and flourish through the Fravashis to nourish animals and people.

XIII. Farvardîn Yast.

The plants grow through the brightness and glory of the Fravashis and along paths made by Mazda. The Fravashis show them beautiful growth and help them nourish animals, people, and the Aryan nations.
