Abstract written by a model

A vine, mother of the princes, once planted by many waters, fruitful and tall with strong branches fit for rulers' scepters. It was then plucked up in fury and cast down; the east wind dried its fruit, and fire consumed its broken branches. Now it sits in a dry, thirsty wilderness, with no strong branch left to rule.

Chapter 19

The vine is planted by the waters and is fruitful and full of branches, with strong branches for the scepters of rulers and great height. It is then plucked up in fury and cast to the ground, its fruit dried by the east wind and its branches broken, withered, and consumed by fire. It is now planted in a dry and thirsty wilderness, with no strong branch left to be a scepter to rule.
