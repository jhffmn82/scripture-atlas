Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, Righteousness is personified as going before the LORD and preparing his way. In The Book of Isaiah, righteousness is the foundation of the king's reign whose work produces peace and lasting confidence. In The Gospel According to Matthew: Those who hunger and thirst for righteousness will be filled, and those persecuted for righteousness' sake are blessed. In Yasna, Righteousness is the blessing and righteous order praised, worshipped, and sought for protection and weal. In Yasna, Righteousness the Best is the Bountiful Immortal and best ritual order governing thought, word, and deed. In Yasna: A ritual prayer proclaiming Righteousness as the best blessing and its aid as weal, explained through blessedness and Mãthra, then worshipped, recited, memorised, chanted, and used sacrificially. In Yasna, Righteousness is the holy ritual truth receiving sacrifice, homage, praise, and observance. In Yasna, Righteous Order is the holy ritual order providing protection, prosperity, righteous action, and the best life.

Abstract written by a model

Righteousness is a natural quality of the superior man, rooted in the heart and expressed throughout the body. It opposes taking what one has no right to and is presented as the path of life. A poor scholar must not abandon it, since it supports perfect satisfaction, and it works through respect for elders. It begins in natural feelings that refuse wrongdoing, then unfolds by extending those feelings into sincere speech and action, proper conduct, and rejection of gain by guile. Declining a dish of rice, a plate of soup, or the kingdom of Qi may show righteousness, though these are small forms beside other moral obligations.
