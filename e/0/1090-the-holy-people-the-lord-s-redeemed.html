Abstract written by a model

The Holy People are the LORD's ransomed, redeemed ones. Across both chapters, they belong to the LORD and are recalled as those for whom he made the sea's depths a path. They walk the Holy Way and return to Zion singing, crowned with everlasting joy and gladness, while sorrow and sighing flee.

Chapter 35

The redeemed will walk on the Holy Way. The LORD's ransomed ones will return and come to Zion with singing, with everlasting joy on their heads, and they will obtain gladness and joy as sorrow and sighing flee.

Chapter 51

The redeemed are recalled as those for whom the LORD made the depths of the sea a way to pass over. The ransomed will return and come with singing to Zion, with everlasting joy on their heads, while sorrow and sighing flee away.
