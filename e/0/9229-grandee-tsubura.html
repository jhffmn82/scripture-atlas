Grandee Tsubura

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the Grandee, the Grandee Tsubura

Abstract written by a model

Grandee Tsubura, a householder with Princess Kara in service and five granaries, shelters fleeing King Ma-yowa; during Prince Oho-hatsuse’s siege, he fights until exhausted, kills Ma-yowa at his request, and cuts off his own head.

Sect. CXLIV.—Emperor an-Kō (Part I.—He Slays King Oho-Kusaka). .. Sect. CXLIX.—Emperor an-Kō (Part VI.—Flight of Princes Ohoke and Woke)].

He sheltered King Ma-yowa when the king fled after slaying Emperor an-Kō. During Prince Oho-hatsuse’s siege, he fought until his hands and arrows were exhausted, then slew Prince Ma-yowa at the prince’s request and cut off his own head.
