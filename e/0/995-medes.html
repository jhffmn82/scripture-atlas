Medes

group · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the Medes, the kings of the Medes

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, the Medes are ruthless warriors whom the LORD stirs up against Babylon's people. In The Book of Jeremiah, the Medes are represented by the kings of the Medes, whom the LORD stirs up against Babylon to destroy it in vengeance for his temple.

Abstract written by a model

The Medes are a people the LORD stirs up against Babylon's people; indifferent to silver and gold, they are ruthless warriors whose bows dash young men down and who spare neither children nor the unborn.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 13

The Medes are the people the LORD says he will stir up against Babylon's people. They will not value silver or delight in gold, and their bows will dash young men in pieces. They will show no pity on children or the fruit of the womb.
