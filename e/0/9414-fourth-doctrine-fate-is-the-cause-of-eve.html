Abstract written by a model

The fourth doctrine asserts that Fate causes everything: bodily existence, life’s changes, death, rebirth, pleasure, and pain. It denies action and personal causation, leaving adherents absorbed in pleasures and amusements.

Sutrakritanga, Book II / First Lecture, Called the Lotus.

The fourth doctrine teaches that beings undergo bodily existence, life’s vicissitudes, death, rebirth, pleasure, and pain by the will of Fate. It denies that beings cause their own or others’ experiences and leaves its adherents stuck in pleasures and amusements.
