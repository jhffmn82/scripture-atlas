Abstract written by a model

In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), the people of the Scripture are divided, with some concealing truth and opposing believers while others recognize and believe in revelation. In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan), the People of the Scripture receive Scripture, with some concealing revelations and others believing, promoting right conduct, and receiving Allah’s reward. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), the People of the Scripture are described through their disputed beliefs and earlier transgressions, and are told not to exaggerate in religion. In Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida), the People of the Scripture include Jews and Christians, receive the Torah and Gospel, and are called to believe and follow Allah’s revelations. In Surah 28: The Stories (Al-Qasas), the people of the Scripture who received Scripture before the Qur’an believe in it and receive their reward twice for steadfastness and good conduct. In Surah 57: The Iron (Al-Hadid), the People of the Scripture are told that they control none of Allah’s bounty, which is in His hand to distribute. In Surah 59: The Exile (Al-Hashr), the People of the Scripture who disbelieved are driven from their homes and ruin their houses with their own hands and those of the believers. In Surah 98: The Evidence (Al-Bayyina), the People of the Scripture cannot leave error until the clear proof comes, and those who disbelieve abide in hell.

People of the Scripture in Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan)

author unknown · AD 624 to 625 · the work’s page · read it where People of the Scripture first appears

Abstract written by a model

People of the Scripture receive the Scripture but differ after knowledge through transgression. They are warned against hiding, distorting, and concealing Allah’s revelations and against turning believers away. Though some oppose believers, others are trustworthy and steadfast: they recite revelations at night, believe in Allah and the Last Day, promote right conduct, forbid indecency, compete in good works, humble themselves before Allah, and receive His reward.

People of the Scripture in Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa)

author unknown · undated · the work’s page · read it where People of the Scripture first appears

Abstract written by a model

People of the Scripture are a religious group with desires that believed in Jesus son of Mary before his death. They ask for an actual Book to descend from heaven. Their history is presented through earlier actions: asking Moses to show Allah plainly, choosing the calf after clear proofs, breaking their covenant, disbelieving in Allah’s revelations, and saying they slew Jesus son of Mary. Though forgiveness was given after the calf, disbelief and wrongdoing are said to receive neither forgiveness nor guidance. The account closes by telling them not to exaggerate in their religion and to say nothing about Allah except the truth.

People of the Scripture in Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida)

author unknown · undated · the work’s page · read it where People of the Scripture first appears

Abstract written by a model

People of the Scripture are a group that includes Jews and Christians and that has received the Torah, the Gospel, and revelations from Allah. Their food is lawful for believers, as believers’ food is lawful for them; virtuous women among them may be married by believers with marriage portions and honourable treatment. They are called to believe, observe the Torah and Gospel, accept the Messenger’s message, and judge by what Allah has revealed. They are guided by the Scripture when they observe it. The account closes by commanding them not to exceed the truth in religion or follow the vain desires of those who went astray.
