doorkeepers

Also called: divisions of the doorkeepers

Abstract written by a model

The doorkeepers were Levite guards ministering in the LORD's house, drawn from Korahite divisions and the sons of Merari. Gates were assigned by lot, by fathers' houses, small and great alike. Posts ranged from six on the east to four daily north and south, two at the storehouse, and six on the west.

Chapter 26

The doorkeepers were organized in divisions drawn from the Korahites and the sons of Merari, with chief men serving like their brothers in the LORD's house. Guards were assigned by gate: six on the east, four a day on the north and south, two and two at the storehouse, and four at the causeway plus two at Parbar on the west.
