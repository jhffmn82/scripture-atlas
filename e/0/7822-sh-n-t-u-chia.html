CHAPTER V.

Shên T'u Chia has had his toes cut off and studies under Poh Hun Wu Jen alongside Tzŭ Ch'an. He argues that accepting Destiny and focusing on the internal frees him from concern about his mutilation and from being dragged back to externals.
