Abstract written by a model

Nabal, a very wealthy man of the house of Caleb living in Maon with holdings in Carmel, owns three thousand sheep and a thousand goats. Surly, evil and called a worthless fool, husband of Abigail, he scorns David's request, feasts drunkenly, becomes stone-like on hearing the danger, and dies ten days later struck by the LORD.

Chapter 25

Nabal is a very wealthy man of the house of Caleb with three thousand sheep and a thousand goats, described as surly and evil in his doings. He scorns David's request, holds a kingly feast and gets drunk, and when told of the danger in the morning his heart dies within him and he becomes as a stone. About ten days later the LORD strikes him and he dies.
