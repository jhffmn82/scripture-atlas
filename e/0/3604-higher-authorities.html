Abstract written by a model

Higher authorities are ordained by God, the source of all authority, and serve him for the reader's good. They are no terror to those who do good but bear the sword not in vain, avenging wrath on evildoers, and receive taxes as God's service.

Chapter 13

The higher authorities are said to exist only from God and to be ordained by him. They are described as servants of God who are not a terror to good work but to evil, who bear the sword not in vain, and who receive taxes as God's service.
