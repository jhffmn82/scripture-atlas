Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, they are to be driven out before Israel, or those left will harass Israel like pricks in its eyes and thorns in its sides. In The Wisdom of Solomon, they are former inhabitants of the holy land hated by God for detestable practices, whom God spared, judged gradually, and sent hornets against.

old inhabitants of your holy land in The Wisdom of Solomon

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where old inhabitants of your holy land first appears

Abstract written by a model

Former inhabitants of the holy land, hated by God for detestable practices including enchantments, unholy rites, child slaughter, and sacrificial banquets of human flesh and blood. Though counsel was to destroy them, God spared them, sent hornets, and judged them gradually to allow repentance, knowing their inborn evil would not change: a cursed seed from the beginning.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 12

They are described as practicing detestable enchantments and unholy rites, including merciless slaughter of children and sacrificial banquets of men's flesh and blood. The Lord's counsel was to destroy them by the hands of 'our fathers', yet he spared them as men, sent hornets ahead of the army, and judged them gradually to give them a chance to repent. The text says he knew their nature was evil and inborn and would not change, calling them a cursed seed from the beginning.
