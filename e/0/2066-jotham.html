Jotham in The Second Book of Kings

Abstract written by a model

Jotham, son of Azariah and Jerusha, was a king of Judah. He governed the household and judged the people while his father was leprous, then reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem from age twenty-five. He did right but left the high places, built the LORD's upper gate, was buried in the City of David, and was succeeded by Ahaz.

Chapter 15

Jotham was over the household and judged the people while his father Azariah was leprous. He began to reign at twenty-five in the second year of Pekah and reigned sixteen years, doing right but leaving the high places, and he built the upper gate of the LORD's house. He died and was succeeded by his son Ahaz.

Jotham in The Second Book of Chronicles

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Jotham first appears

Abstract written by a model

Jotham was a king of Judah who succeeded his father Uzziah at age twenty-five and reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. His mother was Jerushah, daughter of Zadok, and his son was Ahaz. He did right in the LORD's eyes but did not enter the temple. He built the temple's upper gate, the wall of Ophel, and cities, fortresses and towers in Judah, and defeated Ammon's king. He grew mighty by ordering his ways before God, and was buried in David's city.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 27

Jotham began to reign at twenty-five and ruled sixteen years in Jerusalem, and his mother was Jerushah daughter of Zadok. He did right in the LORD's eyes as his father Uzziah had, built the upper gate of the LORD's house, the wall of Ophel, and cities, fortresses and towers in Judah, and defeated the king of the children of Ammon. He became mighty because he ordered his ways before the LORD his God, and he was buried in David's city.
