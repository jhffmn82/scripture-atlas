Sixteenth Lecture.

The venerable Sthaviras are said to have declared ten conditions for the realisation of celibacy. Hearing and understanding these conditions enables monks to advance in self-discipline, Samvara, contemplation, and guarded conduct.

Sthaviras in Kalpa Sutra

Bhadrabâhu · the work’s page · read it where Sthaviras first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Sthaviras are a succession of teachers from Ârya-Sudharman through many named figures, whose disciples founded Ganas and Sâkhâs and preserved sacred lore. Praised for virtue and self-restraint, they follow rainy-season conduct and Paggusan restrictions on requests, hair, lodging, and behavior.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

List of the Sthaviras.

The Sthaviras are presented as a succession of teachers beginning with Ârya-Sudharman and continuing through numerous named figures. Their disciples founded Ganas and Sâkhâs, preserved sacred lore, and were praised for knowledge, conduct, virtue, penance, patience, self-restraint, and clemency.

Rules for Yatis.

The Sthaviras follow the conduct prescribed during the rainy season and are restricted during the Paggusan from asking householders about things they do not see. They also have specified rules concerning hair, lodging, and conduct.
