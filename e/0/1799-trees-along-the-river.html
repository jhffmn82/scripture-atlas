Abstract written by a model

Many food-bearing trees line both banks of the river flowing from the temple. Their leaves never wither and their fruit never fails, bearing new fruit monthly because the waters issue from the sanctuary. The fruit serves as food and the leaves bring healing.

Chapter 47

Very many trees stand on both banks of the river. Every tree for food will grow there, with leaves that do not wither and fruit that does not fail, producing new fruit every month because the waters come from the sanctuary; the fruit is for food and the leaf for healing.
