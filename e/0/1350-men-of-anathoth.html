Chapter 11

The men of Anathoth seek Jeremiah's life and tell him not to prophesy in the LORD's name, or he will die by their hand. The LORD says he will punish them: the young men will die by the sword, their sons and daughters by famine, and no remnant will remain in the year of their visitation.
