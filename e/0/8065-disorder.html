disorder

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections

Abstract written by a model

Disorder occurs in states and clans when the Great Tao ceases to be observed. It increases as people gain more implements to add to profit. Because it is easier to address before it starts, disorder should be prevented before it begins.

disorder is in one work on this site, The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
