Chapter 10

Five splendid men on horses with golden bridles appeared from heaven to the enemy when the battle grew strong. Two led the Jews and, taking Maccabaeus in their midst, covered him with their armor to guard him from wounds. They shot arrows and thunderbolts at the enemy, blinding and confusing them.
