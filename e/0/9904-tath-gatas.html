Tathâgatas

group · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the Tathâgatas, wise Tathâgatas

Abstract written by a model

Tathâgatas teach the Law through diligent teachers. Wise, supreme, and the eyes of the world, they engage in no undertakings; therefore the text asks how they could be born again and states that they are not born again.

Tathâgatas is in one work on this site, Sutrakritanga (Sûtrakritâṅga), Book I. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Thirteenth Lecture, Called the Real Truth.

The Tathâgatas are among those from whom men learn the Law through diligent teachers.

Fifteenth Lecture, Called the Yamakas.

Tathâgatas engage in no undertakings and are described as supreme and the eyes of the world. The text asks how such wise Tathâgatas could be born again.
