Chapter 17

The person of understanding is affected more deeply by a rebuke than a fool is by a hundred lashes, and wisdom is set before his face. He spares his words, is even tempered, and is thought discerning. A servant who deals wisely is said to rule over a shameful son and share the inheritance.
