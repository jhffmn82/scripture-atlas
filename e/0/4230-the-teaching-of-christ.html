the teaching of Christ

topic · 3 works · 4 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Jesus's teaching, all things, authority, his teaching, teaching of Christ, the teaching, the things Jesus taught, the things which Jesus did truly teach, the things which have been commanded me, the things which he taught, the things which they did utter, these things, these words of mine, this teaching

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Jesus's teaching covers not judging, asking, the golden rule, the narrow way, false prophets, and doing his words, and astonishes the multitudes with authority unlike the scribes. In 2 John, the teaching of Christ is the standard by which people are judged, and those who do not remain in it are not to be received or welcomed. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the things Jesus taught are brought again to the people from the Gentiles, with only a hundredth part written in the book and more teachings contained on the plates of Nephi.
