Surah 111: The Palm Fibre (Al-Masad) concerns Abu Lahab, his fate, and his wife; it describes the perishing of Abu Lahab’s power and himself, the failure of his wealth and gains to exempt him from flaming Fire, and his wife’s punishment with a halter of palm-fibre upon her neck.
