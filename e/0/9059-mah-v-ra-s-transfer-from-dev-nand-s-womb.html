Mahâvîra's transfer from Devânandâ's womb to Trisalâ's womb

Abstract written by a model

On the eighty-third day, Indra compassionately transfers Mahâvîra’s embryo from Devânandâ’s womb to Trisalâ’s, retaining clean matter and exchanging Trisalâ’s fetus into Devânandâ’s womb.

Third Part. / Fifteenth Lecture, Called the Clauses.

After eighty-two days, on the eighty-third day current, the compassionate god Indra removed Mahâvîra’s embryo from Devânandâ’s womb and lodged it in Trisalâ’s womb. The unclean matter was rejected and the clean matter retained, while Trisalâ’s fetus was placed in Devânandâ’s womb.
