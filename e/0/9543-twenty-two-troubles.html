Abstract written by a model

The twenty-two troubles are declared by the Kâsyapa and explained in due order. They span bodily hardship, unstable living conditions, abuse and refusal, illness and dirt, and contrasting experiences of respectful treatment, understanding, ignorance, and righteousness.

Second Lecture.

The twenty-two troubles include hunger, thirst, cold, heat, insects, nakedness, discontent, women, erratic life, places for study, lodging, abuse, corporal punishment, begging, refusal of alms, illness, pricking grass, dirt, respectful treatment, understanding, ignorance, and righteousness. They are declared by the Kâsyapa and explained in due order.
