required_condition without defect [sin offering for the anointed priest] A young bull without defect is the sin offering for the anointed priest and for the whole congregation. It is killed at the door of the Tent of Meeting before the LORD. Its fat is burned on the altar, and the rest of it (skin, meat, head, legs, innards, dung) is carried outside the camp to a clean place where ashes are poured out and burned on wood.
