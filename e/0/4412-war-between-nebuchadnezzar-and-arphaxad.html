Judith / Chapter 1

The war is fought on the great plain on the borders of Ragau, with Arphaxad drawing allies from the hill country, the rivers, and the plain of Arioch king of the Elymaeans, and many nations of the sons of Chelod. In the seventeenth year Nebuchadnezzar prevails, routs Arphaxad's army, takes his cities including Ecbatana, and kills Arphaxad in the mountains of Ragau.
