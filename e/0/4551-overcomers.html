the one who overcomes in Revelation

author unknown · about AD 95 · the work’s page · read it where the one who overcomes first appears

Abstract written by a model

The one who overcomes is a recipient of promised rewards given across the letters, spoken by a speaker who addresses each message. Chapter 2 promises eating from the tree of life in Paradise, safety from the second death, hidden manna, a white stone with a new name, authority over the nations to rule with a rod of iron, and the morning star, for those who keep his works to the end. Chapter 3 continues: white garments, a name kept in the book of life and confessed before the Father and his angels, becoming a pillar in God's temple, receiving the names of God and the new Jerusalem, and finally sitting with the speaker on his throne.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Those who overcome receive promises in each letter: to eat from the tree of life in the Paradise of God, to be unharmed by the second death, and to receive hidden manna and a white stone with a new name. In the last letter, the one who overcomes and keeps his works to the end is given authority over the nations to rule them with a rod of iron, and the morning star.

Chapter 3

The one who overcomes is promised, in each message, a reward from the speaker. In Sardis he will be arrayed in white and his name will not be blotted out of the book of life. In Philadelphia he will become a pillar in the temple of God, never leaving it, and receive the names of God, the new Jerusalem, and the speaker. In Laodicea he will sit with the speaker on his throne.
