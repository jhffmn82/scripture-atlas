CHAPTER XXX.

The Heir Apparent Li becomes troubled by the State of Chao’s condition and offers a thousand ounces of silver to anyone who can persuade the prince to dismiss the swordsmen. He sends messengers to Chuang Tzŭ and accompanies him into the prince’s presence.
