Twenty-Third Lecture.

The meeting of Kêsi and Gautama takes place in the Tinduka park at Srâvastî. Gautama resolves Kêsi’s doubts about the two Laws and spiritual practice, after which Kêsi adopts the Law of the five vows and knowledge and virtuous conduct are brought to eminence.
