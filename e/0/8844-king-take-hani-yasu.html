King Take-hani-yasu

Abstract written by a model

King Take-hani-yasu, the Heavenly Sovereign's half-brother and king of Yamashiro, was suspected of plotting a foul act, raised opposing armies, confronted Imperial forces, missed Hiko-kuni-buku with an arrow, and was killed by Hiko-kuni-buku.
