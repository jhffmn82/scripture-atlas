Israelites' rebellion and punishment of forty years in Numbers

author unknown · 1439 to 1399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Israelites' rebellion and punishment of forty years first appears

Abstract written by a model

The congregation complains, refuses to enter the land, and tempts the LORD ten times. The LORD sentences all counted adults twenty and older to die in the wilderness, except Caleb and Joshua. Their children wander forty years, a year per spying day. A later attempt to go up without the LORD ends in defeat.

Chapter 14

The congregation's complaining, refusal to enter the land, and tempting of the LORD ten times lead to the sentence that all counted adults from twenty years old and upward will die in the wilderness, except Caleb and Joshua. Their children will wander forty years, one year for each of the forty days of spying, bearing the people's iniquities. Their attempt to go up afterward without the LORD fails in defeat.
