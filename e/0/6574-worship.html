worship

topic · 5 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Serve Allah, serve, serve Him, worship and be dutiful to Him, worshipped, worshipped beside the Beneficent

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 6: The Cattle (Al-An'aam), people are ordered to establish worship and be dutiful to Allah, while Muhammad is forbidden to worship those called upon instead of Allah. In Surah 11: Hud, worship is due to Allah alone, as messengers call their peoples to serve and worship Him and turn away from false gods. In Surah 29: The Spider (Al-Ankaboot), worship is commanded as service to Allah alone and is said to preserve people from lewdness and iniquity. In Surah 43: Ornaments of gold (Az-Zukhruf), people are criticized for worshipping others beside Allah, while Jesus and Abraham uphold worship of Allah alone. In Surah 106: Quraysh (Quraish), Quraysh are told to worship the Lord of this House.
