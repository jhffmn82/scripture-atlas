Chapter 20

Amasa is told by David to gather the men of Judah within three days but stays longer than the appointed time. He meets Joab at the great stone in Gibeon and, not heeding the sword in Joab's hand, is struck in the body and dies. His body is moved from the highway into a field and covered with a garment.
