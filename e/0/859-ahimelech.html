Abstract written by a model

Ahimelech, son of Ahitub, is a priest at Nob. He receives a trembling, solitary David, giving him holy bread and Goliath's sword. Saul later summons him, accusing him of conspiracy with David. Ahimelech defends himself, praising David's faithfulness and claiming ignorance of any plot, yet Saul sentences him and his father's whole house to death.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 21

Ahimelech the priest met David trembling and questioned why he was alone. He gave David the holy bread after hearing that the young men were kept from women, and he told David that Goliath's sword was there and gave it to him.

Chapter 22

Ahimelech, son of Ahitub and a priest at Nob, was summoned by Saul and accused of conspiring with David by giving him bread and a sword and inquiring of God for him. He defended himself by praising David's faithfulness as the king's son-in-law and claiming he knew nothing of any plot. Saul sentenced him to death along with all his father's house.
