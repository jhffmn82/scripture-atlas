Abstract written by a model

Heaven began with Earth when three deities were born. It has the Floating Bridge of Heaven and an eight-forking road linked to passage with the Central Land of Reed-Plains. His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness ascends there, and princes are commanded to descend from it.

Heaven is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
