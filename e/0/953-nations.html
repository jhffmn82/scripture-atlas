Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, all nations are summoned to praise and extol the LORD. In Psalms, nations are targets of vengeance and punishment by the saints. In Psalms, nations rage and kingdoms are moved until God lifts his voice and ends wars. In Psalms, nations are called to sing for joy because God judges with equity. In Psalms, the nations hear the LORD's glory, marvelous works, and salvation and are told that he reigns and judges the peoples. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, nations undergo transfers of sovereignty because of injustice, violence, and greed. In The Book of Joel, nations are accused of wickedness against Israel and summoned to war and judgment in the valley of Jehoshaphat. In The Book of Micah, nations are portrayed with divided fates, including streaming to the LORD's mountain, abandoning war, assembling against Zion, and facing judgment.

and 3 more works

In The Book of Zechariah, many nations join themselves to the LORD and become his people. In The Gospel According to Matthew, all nations are gathered before the Son of Man and separated as sheep from goats. In Surah 45: Crouching (Al-Jaathiya), the nations are seen crouching and summoned to their records to be requited for their deeds.

nations in The Book of Micah

Abstract written by a model

The nations are a collective of peoples portrayed with divided fates. Many will stream to the LORD's mountain to learn his ways, be judged, and abandon war. Yet each walks in its own gods' name, and some assemble against Zion to defile her, not knowing the LORD's counsel. He gathers them like sheaves for threshing, Zion beating many in pieces. The remnant of Jacob lives among them like a lion, and the LORD executes vengeance on those who did not listen.
