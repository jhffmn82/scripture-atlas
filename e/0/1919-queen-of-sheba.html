queen of Sheba in The First Book of Kings

author unknown · about 549 BC · the work’s page · read it where queen of Sheba first appears

Abstract written by a model

The queen of Sheba travels to Jerusalem with a great caravan to test Solomon with hard questions. Overwhelmed by all she sees, she says not half was told her, praises the LORD, gives Solomon gold, spices and precious stones, receives all she desired, and returns home.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

The queen of Sheba came to Jerusalem with a very great caravan to test Solomon with hard questions, and was left with no more spirit in her after seeing all he had. She declared that not even half had been told her, praised the LORD for setting Solomon on the throne, and gave him 120 talents of gold, abundant spices, and precious stones. She received all she desired and returned to her own land with her servants.
