Abstract written by a model

In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, the church is one body in Christ whose many members have differing functions while belonging to one another. In Paul’s Letter to the Ephesians, the church is the body of Christ, with Christ as its head and savior, whose members are unified and built up through service, and whom Christ cleanses, sanctifies, nourishes, and cherishes. In The Book of Mosiah, the baptized people are called the church of Christ and are taught unity, love, one faith, and one baptism. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the church of Christ consists of believers baptized in Jesus’s name, receives the Holy Ghost, is built upon Jesus’s gospel, administers bread and wine, and faces persecution while remaining unconquered. In The Fourth Book of Nephi, Who Is the Son of Nephi, One of the Disciples of Jesus Christ, the disciples form a church of Christ throughout the surrounding lands, while its true church is later denied and persecuted by other churches. In The Book of Moroni, the church of Christ receives the administration of Christ’s flesh and blood, numbers baptized people cleansed by the Holy Ghost, nourishes them by God’s word, meets to worship and care for souls, removes the unrepentant, forgives the repentant, and consists of peaceable followers of Christ.

the church (body of Christ) in Paul’s Letter to the Ephesians

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where the church (body of Christ) first appears

Abstract written by a model

The church is the body of Christ, one body with one Spirit, filled by him who fills all in all. Christ is its head and savior, and it is subject to him. Its members are built up through service until they attain unity of faith and knowledge of the Son of God, growing in love as each joint supplies. Christ loved it, gave himself up, cleansed and sanctified it, nourishes and cherishes it, and will present it to himself glorious, holy, without spot, wrinkle or defect.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

The body of Christ is one, and is built up through the work of serving until all attain unity of faith and knowledge of the Son of God. Christ the head fits and knits it together through what every joint supplies, so that it increases and builds itself up in love.

Chapter 5

The assembly is described as being subject to Christ, who is its head and savior. Christ loved it and gave himself up for her, cleansing and sanctifying her so that he might present her to himself gloriously, without spot, wrinkle or defect. The Lord nourishes and cherishes the assembly, and the readers are members of his body, of his flesh and bones.

the church of Christ in The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman

Mormon · the work’s page · read it where the church of Christ first appears

Abstract written by a model

The church of Christ is a church established in the land of Mormon by Alma and later to be established among Gentiles who repent and hearken to Jesus Christ’s words. It consists of people who believe and are baptized in Jesus’s name; they receive the Holy Ghost and are united to it. Called in Jesus’s name and built upon his gospel, it receives the Father’s blessing and works for Jesus’s sake. It administers bread and wine and includes prayer among repentant, baptized people. A church built on men’s or the devil’s works ends and is cast into fire. Its disciples face imprisonment but cannot be held.

Jesus's church is to administer bread and wine to those who believe and are baptized in his name. Prayer is to occur in the church among people who repent and are baptized in his name.

Chapter 21

The Father will establish the church among the Gentiles if they repent, hearken to his words, and do not harden their hearts.

Chapter 26

Those baptized in the name of Jesus were called the church of Christ.

Chapter 27

The church is to be called in the name of Jesus and must be built upon his gospel. If it is built upon the works of men or the devil, its works will end and it will be cast into the fire.

Chapter 28

Those who believe the preaching of the three disciples are baptized, receive the Holy Ghost, and are united to the church of Christ. People who do not belong to the church cast the three disciples into prisons and other places, but cannot hold them.

church of Christ in The Book of Moroni

Moroni · the work’s page · read it where church of Christ first appears

Abstract written by a model

The church of Christ receives the administration of the flesh and blood of Christ, kneeling with elders or priests in prayer and partaking often of bread and wine in remembrance of the Lord Jesus. It numbers baptized people cleansed by the Holy Ghost, records and nourishes them by the good word of God, and meets often to fast, pray, and care for souls. It requires no iniquity among members, removes the unrepentant, and forgives those who sincerely repent. It consists of peaceable followers of Christ with hope to enter the Lord’s rest.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

The church receives the administration of the flesh and blood of Christ. The church kneels with the elder or priest during the prayer.

Chapter 6

The church of Christ numbers baptized people among its members, takes their names so they may be remembered and nourished by the good word of God, and meets often to care for their souls. It observes that there should be no iniquity among its members, blots out the names of those who do not repent and confess, and forgives those who repent and seek forgiveness with real intent.

Chapter 7

The church consists of peaceable followers of Christ who have hope sufficient to enter into the rest of the Lord. Those without faith in Christ are not fit to be numbered among its people.
