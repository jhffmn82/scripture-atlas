Abstract written by a model

The princes of the present day hate disgrace yet act without benevolence, and in peaceful leisure abandon themselves to pleasure and indifference, thereby bringing calamity upon themselves.

Chapter 4

The princes of the present day hate disgrace but complacently do what is not benevolent. During peaceful periods, they abandon themselves to pleasure and indolent indifference, thereby seeking calamities for themselves.
