Epaphras

Abstract written by a model

Epaphras is a faithful, beloved servant of Christ and one of the Colossians' own. He sends greetings, prays constantly that they stand perfect and complete in God's will, and, as Paul testifies, has great zeal for them and for those in Laodicea and Hierapolis.
