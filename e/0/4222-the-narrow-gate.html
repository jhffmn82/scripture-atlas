the narrow gate

place · 3 works · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: broad is the way, broad way, gate, narrow gate, narrow gate and broad way, strait gate, the gate, the strait gate, the way, the way is broad, the way which leads to death, way

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, the narrow gate and broad way are contrasting paths, with Jesus urging entry through the narrow gate leading to life rather than the broad way to destruction. In The Second Book of Nephi, the narrow gate is entered by souls who are to be reconciled unto Christ. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the strait gate is the entrance to the narrow way leading unto life, and Jesus commands people to enter through it, though few find it. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the broad way is wide, entered and traveled by many, leading toward destruction and death until night comes.
