Abstract written by a model

A monastic rule for airing or drying clothes: a monk or nun must use a secluded, repeatedly inspected and cleaned heap of ashes or bones, and must avoid bare ground, wet earth, living clay, houses, and unstable or elevated places.

Fifth Lecture, Called Begging of Clothes. / First Lesson. .. Second Lesson.

A monk or nun must air or dry clothes in a secluded spot on a repeatedly inspected and cleaned heap of ashes or bones. They must not air or dry clothes on bare ground, wet earth, living clay, unstable household objects, walls, rocks, pillars, platforms, scaffolds, second stories, or flat roofs.
