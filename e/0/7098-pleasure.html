Abstract written by a model

In Surah 102: Competition (At-Takaathur), pleasure is what people will be asked about on that day. In Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra), Book I, sensual enjoyment is difficult to reject and dear to fools; worldly attachment brings delusion, loss brings grief, and it unfolds into death and continued suffering.
