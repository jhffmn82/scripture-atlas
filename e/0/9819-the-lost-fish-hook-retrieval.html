recovers fish-hook [from the throat of the tahi] The Sea-Deity summons the fishes and learns that the tahi likely swallowed the fish-hook because it complained of something in its throat. The hook is found in the tahi’s throat, washed, and respectfully presented to His Augustness Fire-Subside.
