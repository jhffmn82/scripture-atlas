Gambûdvîpa

place · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Gambudvîpa, the continent Gambûdvîpa, the continent of Gambûdvîpa

Abstract written by a model

In Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra), Gambûdvîpa was the continent in which Mahâvîra descended, was conceived, and later renounced household life. In Kalpa Sutra, Gambûdvîpa was the continent where Mahâvîra's conception and embryo transfer took place and which contained Bharatavarsha and Kundagrâma.
