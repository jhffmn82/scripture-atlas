Abstract written by a model

The godless man is a figure who forgets God, so his hope perishes and his confidence and trust prove fragile as a spider's web. He leans on his house but it cannot stand; though he flourishes, once destroyed his place denies him and others spring up.

Chapter 8

The godless man is depicted as one who forgets God, whose hope perishes and whose confidence and trust are like a spider's web. He leans on his house but it does not stand, and though he flourishes green before the sun, if he is destroyed from his place it denies him, and others spring up from the earth in his stead.
