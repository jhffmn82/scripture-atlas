Abstract written by a model

The faithful one makes the LORD their refuge and dwelling place, loving him and knowing his name. Protected from pestilence, terror by night, arrows by day, and destruction at noon, they remain unafraid. Angels bear them up, they trample lion, cobra, and serpent, and they only witness the recompense of the wicked. The LORD promises to deliver, honor, and satisfy them with long life.

Chapter 91

The faithful one dwells in the secret place of the Most High, rests in the shadow of the Almighty, and has made the LORD their refuge and dwelling place. They need not fear terror, arrows, pestilence, or destruction; no evil or plague will come near them, and they will only look on the recompense of the wicked. Angels bear them up, they will trample the lion, cobra, young lion, and serpent, and the LORD promises to deliver, honor, and satisfy them with long life because they have set their love on him and known his name.
