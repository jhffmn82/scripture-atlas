Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, it is the event in which Jesus is tempted three times after fasting, answers each with Scripture, and is then served by angels. In The Gospel According to Luke, it is the event in which the devil tempts Jesus three times in the wilderness, and Jesus answers each temptation with Scripture.

Abstract written by a model

After fasting forty days and nights and becoming hungry, Jesus is tempted three times: to command stones to become bread, to throw himself down from the temple pinnacle, and to worship the devil for all the kingdoms of the world. He answers each with 'It is written' and a quotation from Scripture, and the devil then leaves while angels come and serve him.

Abstract written by a model

During forty days fasting in the wilderness, the devil tempts hungry Jesus three times: turning stone to bread, worshiping for kingdoms, jumping from the temple pinnacle. Jesus answers each with Scripture, and the devil departs until later.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

During forty days in the wilderness the devil tempts Jesus, who has eaten nothing and is hungry. The three temptations are to make a stone into bread, to gain all the kingdoms by worshiping the devil, and to cast himself from the temple pinnacle. Jesus answers each with Scripture, and the devil leaves until another time.
