Abstract written by a model

A square city set in the middle of a leftover strip of land for common use, dwelling and pasture. It measures 4,500 reeds per side, has 250 reeds of pasture around it, and an 18,000 circumference. Twelve gates, three per side, bear tribal names. Workers from all the tribes farm its land for food. From that day it is named 'The LORD is there.'

Chapter 48

The city lies in the middle of the five-thousand-cubit strip left over, for common use, dwelling and pasture. It measures four thousand five hundred on each side, has two hundred fifty of pasture lands around it, and eighteen thousand in circumference. It has three gates on each side named after the tribes, and from that day its name is 'The LORD is there.'
