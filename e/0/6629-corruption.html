corruption

topic · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Corruption, corruption in the earth

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 28: The Stories (Al-Qasas), corruption is associated with Pharaoh's oppression and Korah's warning not to seek it in the earth; Allah does not love corrupters, and the Hereafter belongs to those who avoid oppression and corruption. In Surah 30: The Romans (Ar-Room), corruption appears on land and sea because of what people's hands have done, and Allah makes them taste part of what they have done so that they may return.
