Iblis

person · 6 works · 19 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: O Iblis

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), Iblis refuses to prostrate before Adam, receives reprieve until resurrection, and vows to ambush believers. In Surah 15: The Rock (Al-Hijr), Iblis refuses to prostrate to Adam, is expelled and cursed, and vows to mislead humanity except Allah's devoted slaves. In Surah 17: The Night Journey (Al-Israa), Iblis refuses to prostrate before Adam, requests respite, and vows to seize most of Adam's seed. In Surah 18: The Cave (Al-Kahf), Iblis, one of the jinn, rebels by refusing to prostrate before Adam and is presented with his seed as an enemy. In Surah 20: Taa-Haa, Iblis refuses Allah's command to prostrate before Adam and is identified as an enemy of Adam and his wife. In Surah 38: The letter Saad (Saad), Iblis refuses to prostrate, is cast out and cursed, and declares that he will beguile everyone except Allah's single-minded slaves.
