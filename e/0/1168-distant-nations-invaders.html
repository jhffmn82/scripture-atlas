Chapter 5

The LORD lifts a banner and whistles for a nation from far away, and they come speedily and swiftly. They are tireless, with sharp arrows, bent bows, horses with hoofs like flint, and wheels like a whirlwind. They roar like lions, seize their prey with no one to deliver it, and bring darkness and distress to the land.
