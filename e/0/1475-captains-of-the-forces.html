Abstract written by a model

The captains of the forces and their men, who were in the fields, hear of Gedaliah's appointment and come to him at Mizpah, including Ishmael, Johanan, Jonathan, Seraiah, the sons of Ephai and Jezaniah. Gedaliah swears to them that serving the Chaldeans will go well, and Johanan with the captains later warns him of the plot. The captains of the forces join Johanan and the people in asking Jeremiah to pray to the LORD for the way they should walk. They are summoned with Johanan to hear the LORD's response.
