Chapter 20

The king's terror is likened to a lion's roar, and whoever provokes him forfeits his life. Sitting on the throne of judgment, he scatters evil with his eyes, and a wise king winnows out the wicked and drives the threshing wheel over them. Love and faithfulness keep him safe and sustain his throne.
