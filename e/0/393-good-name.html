Abstract written by a model

In Proverbs, a good name is declared more desirable than great riches, while loving favor is better than silver and gold. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, a good name is urged as worth regarding because it outlasts a thousand great treasures of gold and continues forever.
