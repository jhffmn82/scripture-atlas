Chapter 20

The elders were summoned by Paul from Ephesus to Miletus. They were told that the Holy Spirit had made them overseers to shepherd the assembly, that vicious wolves and perverse men from among themselves would arise, and that they would see Paul's face no more. They wept, fell on his neck and kissed him, and accompanied him to the ship.
