Ch'u State

place · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Ch'u, State of Ch'u, the Ch'u State, the State of Ch'u

Abstract written by a model

Ch'u State is a place distinct from Yüeh State, ruled by the prince of Ch'u. It hosts Confucius at a banquet and is where Tsê Yang seeks influence. Though its political preservation is discussed, it lacks TAO and cannot preserve the prince’s deeper existence; its prince is severe and merciless in punishment.

Ch'u State is in one work on this site, Chuang Tzŭ. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

CHAPTER XXI.

The preservation of the Ch'u State is presented as insufficient to preserve the existence of the Prince of Ch'u. Its continued political existence is contrasted with the deeper existence associated with TAO.

Kuan Chung being at the point of death, Duke Huan went to see him.

Ch'u is the place to which Confucius travels, where the prince entertains him at a banquet.

CHAPTER XXV.

The Ch'u State is where Tsê Yang seeks influence with its prince. Its prince is described as dignified, severe, and merciless in punishment.
