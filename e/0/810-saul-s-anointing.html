Chapter 10

Samuel anoints Saul privately by pouring a vial of oil on his head and kissing him, saying the LORD has anointed him prince over his inheritance. Signs follow that day, confirming it, and God gives Saul another heart. Saul keeps the matter secret from his uncle until the public choice at Mizpah.
