Ahab's house

group · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Ahab’s house, house of Ahab, the kings of Israel, your master Ahab’s house

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Ahab's house is a royal family doomed by the LORD's prophecy, and Jehu is told to strike it in vengeance for the prophets' blood. In The Second Book of Chronicles, Ahab's house served as counselors to Ahaziah, leading him to evil and destruction, and Jehu executed judgment on it.

Abstract written by a model

Ahab's house is a royal family doomed by the LORD's prophecy, compared to Jeroboam's house; Jehu is told to strike it avenging the prophets' blood, and Joram and Jezebel die.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 9

The young prophet declares that the whole house of Ahab will perish, like the houses of Jeroboam and Baasha, and that Jehu must strike it to avenge the prophets' blood. Joram, Ahab's son, and Jezebel are killed in this unit.
