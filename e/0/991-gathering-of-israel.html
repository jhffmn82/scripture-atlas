Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, it includes the recovery and return of Israel's remnant and Judah's dispersed from the four corners of the earth. In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, it is the latter-days restoration of scattered Israel, including the grafting in of natural branches and the return to Zion. In The Second Book of Nephi, it is the latter-days restoration in which Israel's outcasts and Judah's dispersed are assembled, brought home, and gathered in God's word. In The Second Book of Nephi, it is the Lord's recovery of his remnant from Assyria, Egypt, Pathros, Cush, Elam, Shinar, Hamath, and the islands of the sea.

gathering of Israel in The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry

Nephi · the work’s page · read it where gathering of Israel first appears

Abstract written by a model

The gathering of Israel is the latter-days restoration that follows the scattering of the house of Israel. After being broken off, driven out, and scattered because of wickedness, its remnants are gathered from the four quarters of the earth, including the isles of the sea. Natural branches are grafted in after unbelief and the fulness of the gospel through the Gentiles. The people return to Zion, inherit desolate places, are delivered from oppressors, and come out of captivity, obscurity, and darkness to know the Lord as Savior, Redeemer, and the true Messiah.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 9 .. Chapter 12

After the house of Israel is scattered, it is gathered together again. The remnants of the house of Israel are grafted in and come to the knowledge of the true Messiah.

Chapter 15 .. Chapter 16

The grafting in of the natural branches occurs in the latter days after the seed has dwindled in unbelief and the fulness of the gospel has come through the Gentiles.

Chapter 19

The Lord will gather all people of the house of Israel from the four quarters of the earth, including those on the isles of the sea.

Chapter 21

Israel is described as broken off, driven out, and scattered abroad because of the wickedness of the pastors of the people. Its restoration includes being gathered from far places, returning to Zion, inheriting desolate places, and being delivered from oppressors.

Chapter 22

The gathering of Israel will bring the people out of captivity, obscurity, and darkness to the lands of their inheritance. They will know the Lord as their Savior and Redeemer.
