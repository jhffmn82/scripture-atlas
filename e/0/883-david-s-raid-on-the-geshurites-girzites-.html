Abstract written by a model

David's raid strikes the Geshurites, Girzites, and Amalekites on the way to Shur and Egypt. He leaves no man or woman alive and takes sheep, cattle, donkeys, camels, and clothing. He then returns to Achish, falsely claiming he raided Judah's South, the Jerahmeelites, and Kenites.

Chapter 27

David and his men strike the land of these inhabitants, who live on the way to Shur and Egypt, saving no man or woman alive and taking sheep, cattle, donkeys, camels, and clothing. David then returns to Achish and misrepresents the raid as against the South of Judah, the Jerahmeelites, and the Kenites, killing everyone so none can tell the truth.
