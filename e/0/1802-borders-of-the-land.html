Abstract written by a model

The borders of the land are defined on four sides in Chapter 47. The north runs from the great sea via Hethlon to Zedad, Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim, Hazer Hatticon and Hazar Enon at Damascus's border; the east is the Jordan; the south runs from Tamar to Meriboth Kadesh and the brook; the west is the great sea.

Chapter 47

The north border runs from the great sea by way of Hethlon to the entrance of Zedad, and on to Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim, Hazer Hatticon, and Hazar Enon at the border of Damascus. The east side is the Jordan, between Hauran, Damascus, Gilead, and the land of Israel, to the east sea; the south runs from Tamar to the waters of Meriboth Kadesh, to the brook, to the great sea; and the west side is the great sea.
