Abstract written by a model

Corn is food: sown, it fulfills the law of Mazda and increases holiness; coming forth with barley and wheat, it destroys Daêvas; eaten, it gives strength for holiness, husbandry, and begetting children.

Fargard III.

Sowing corn is identified as the food that fulfills the law of Mazda and as an act that makes holiness and the law of Mazda grow. The growth of barley, corn, and wheat drives away and destroys the Daêvas, while a person who eats has strength for holiness, husbandry, and begetting children.
