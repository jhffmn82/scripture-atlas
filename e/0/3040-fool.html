Abstract written by a model

The fool, paired with the braggart, mistimes his speech: his parables are rejected, his pleasantries wasted. His gifts bring no profit because he expects manifold repayment, gives little, insults much, and lends only to demand back. He complains of having no friend or thanks, and ends laughed to scorn by many.

Chapter 20

The fool, along with the braggart, misses his time to speak, and his parables are rejected because he does not tell them at the proper time. His gifts bring no profit because he expects repayment many times over; he gives little, insults much, and lends today to demand it back tomorrow. He complains that he has no friend and no thanks, and he will be laughed to scorn by many.
