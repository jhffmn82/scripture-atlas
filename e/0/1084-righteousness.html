Righteousness

topic · 3 works · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: The work of righteousness, righteousness, the effect of righteousness, your righteousness

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, Righteousness is personified as kissing peace, looking down from heaven, and going before the LORD to prepare the way for his steps. In The Book of Isaiah, righteousness is the foundation of the king's reign, remaining in the fruitful field; its work is peace, and its effect is lasting quietness and confidence forever. In The Gospel According to Matthew, righteousness fills those who hunger and thirst for it, blesses those persecuted for its sake, and must exceed that of the scribes and Pharisees for entry into the Kingdom of Heaven.
