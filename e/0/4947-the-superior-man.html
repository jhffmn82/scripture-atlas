Abstract written by a model

The superior man is a person defined by nine thoughtful concerns: clear sight and distinct hearing, benign countenance, respectful demeanor, sincere speech, and reverently careful business. He asks about doubts, considers anger’s difficulties, and chooses righteousness when gain appears. He also maintains distant reserve toward his son.

CHAP. X. Confucius said, 'The superior man has nine things

The superior man seeks clear sight and distinct hearing, a benign countenance, respectful demeanor, sincere speech, and reverently careful business dealings. He questions others about doubts, considers the difficulties anger may cause, and thinks of righteousness when he sees gain to be got.

The superior man is described as maintaining a distant reserve toward his son.
