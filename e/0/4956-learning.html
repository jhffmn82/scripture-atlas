Abstract written by a model

Learning involves recognizing what has not yet been attained, remembering what has been attained, studying extensively, maintaining a firm and sincere aim, inquiring earnestly, and reflecting with self-application. The superior man learns to reach the utmost of his principles, and the student who completes learning should apply himself to be an officer.
