Abstract written by a model

A dedicated field is a possessed field consecrated to the LORD, valued by seed at fifty shekels of silver per homer of barley sowing, with the value reduced by years remaining after the Jubilee. If unredeemed or sold, it passes to the priests at the Jubilee; a bought field returns to its original seller.

Chapter 27

A dedicated field of one's possession is valued by seed, with a homer of barley sowing valued at fifty shekels of silver, and the value is reduced for years after the Jubilee. If it is not redeemed or has been sold, it becomes holy to the LORD at the Jubilee and is owned by the priests, while a bought field returns to its original owner.
