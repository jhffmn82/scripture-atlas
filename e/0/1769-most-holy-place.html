Most Holy Place

Abstract written by a model

In Leviticus, it lies within the veil, containing the mercy seat on the ark where the LORD appears in the cloud; Aaron may enter only at the prescribed time and manner, with incense and sprinkled blood, or die. In The Book of Ezekiel, it is the inner room measured by a guide after going inward: twenty cubits long and wide, located before the nave, and named so to the narrator.
