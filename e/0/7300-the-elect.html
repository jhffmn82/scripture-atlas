the elect

Also called: The elect

Abstract written by a model

The elect are acceptable, distinguished, and honoured in God's court, where they also shed lustre on kings' courts. They give their attention to the one Guru; the accompanying passage describes a realm of divine knowledge, joy, beauty, incomparable fashioning, and formed wisdom and understanding.
