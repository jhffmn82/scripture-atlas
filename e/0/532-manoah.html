Chapter 13

Manoah, a Danite man of Zorah, prayed that the man of God would return to teach them how to raise the promised child. He questioned the angel, offered him a young goat, asked his name, and then made a burnt offering to the LORD. After the angel ascended in the flame, he realized who he was and feared they would die, but his wife reassured him.
