Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, the survivors of Israel are part of Israel and are associated with the beauty and glory of the fruit of the land. In The Second Book of Nephi, the escaped of Israel are an escaped people who regard the fruit of the earth as excellent and comely.
