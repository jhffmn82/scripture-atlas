the superior man's nature

topic · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: What belongs by his nature to the superior man, his nature, the highest enjoyment of his nature

Abstract written by a model

The superior man's nature is Heaven-apportioned and includes benevolence, righteousness, propriety, and knowledge. It remains unchanged by wider action, poverty, or retirement, rooted in the heart and unfolding outward as harmony, fullness, and character in the limbs.

Chapter 21

The superior man's nature cannot be increased by a larger sphere of action or diminished by poverty and retirement. It includes benevolence, righteousness, propriety, and knowledge, which are rooted in the heart and expressed outwardly.
