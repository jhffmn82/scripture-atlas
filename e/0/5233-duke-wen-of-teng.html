Abstract written by a model

In Mencius, Book I (Liang Hui Wang), duke Wen of Teng rules and guards Teng and its people amid pressure from the large kingdoms Qi and Chu, seeking Mencius’s guidance on protecting Teng and avoiding suffering. duke Wen of Teng in Mencius, Book I (Liang Hui Wang)

Mencius · the work’s page · read it where duke Wen of Teng first appears

Abstract written by a model

Duke Wen of Teng rules and guards Teng and its people amid pressure from the large kingdoms Qi and Chu. Seeking Mencius’s guidance, he considers serving either power to protect Teng, worries over Qi’s fortification of Xue, and asks how Teng can avoid suffering. Mencius urges stronger defenses, readiness to die defending Teng, and strength in doing good, while presenting two possible courses through king Tai’s example.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

Duke Wen of Teng asks Mencius whether Teng should serve Qi or Chu. He is advised to strengthen Teng's defenses and prepare to die in its defense if attacked.

Chapter 14

He asks Mencius what course to take because the people of Qi are going to fortify Xue and the situation alarms him. Mencius tells him to be strong to do good.

Chapter 15

Duke Wen of Teng asks Mencius what course he should take so that Teng may avoid suffering from the large kingdoms on either side of it. Mencius asks him to choose between two courses illustrated by king Tai’s example.

duke Wen of Teng in Mencius, Book III (Teng Wen Gong)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where duke Wen of Teng first appears

Abstract written by a model

Duke Wen of Teng is first a prince and later a duke who rules Teng. While traveling to Chu through Song, he visits Mencius twice and learns that human excellence is attainable and small Teng can become a good State. As duke, he seeks guidance on government and land division, practices benevolent government, and grants Xu Xing a dwelling-place.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Mencius, Book III (Teng Wen Gong) / Part I / Chapter 1

Before becoming duke Wen of Teng, the prince visits Mencius while traveling to Chu by way of Song and visits him again on the return journey. He hears Mencius explain that human excellence is attainable and that Teng, though small, can become a good State.

Chapter 3

Duke Wen of Teng asks Mencius about the proper way of governing a kingdom. He chooses Bi Zhan to consult Mencius about the nine-squares system of dividing the land.

Chapter 4

Duke Wen gives Xu Xing a dwelling-place after Xu Xing asks to become one of his people.
