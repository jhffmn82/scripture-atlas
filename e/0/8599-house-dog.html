house dog

Abstract written by a model

A dog belonging to Ahura Mazda guards and supports every house, circling it against thieves and wolves and protecting household goods. Giving it bad food is grave mistreatment, and causing its death brings seven hundred stripes with each of two instruments.

Fargard XIII.

The house dog goes around the house watching for thieves and wolves and protects household goods. Bad food given to it incurs the guilt associated with mistreating a master of middle rank, and its death carries seven hundred stripes with each of two instruments.
