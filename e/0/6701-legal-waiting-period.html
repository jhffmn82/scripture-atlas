legal waiting period

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: period (of waiting), term, the period, their (legal) period, their period

Abstract written by a model

The legal waiting period is to be reckoned. It is three months for women who despair of menstruation and those who have it not, while pregnant women's period lasts until they give birth.
