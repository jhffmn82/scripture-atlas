Surah 98: The Evidence (Al-Bayyina)

document · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

The unit concerns those who disbelieve among the People of the Scripture and the idolaters, who could not leave off error until the clear proof came to them. The clear proof includes a messenger from Allah reading purified pages containing correct scriptures, after which the People of the Scripture were divided. People are ordered to serve Allah with pure religion, establish worship, and pay the poor-due. Those who disbelieve will abide in the fire of hell, while those who believe and do good works will receive reward in Gardens of Eden.
