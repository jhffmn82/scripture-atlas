cross of Christ

object · 4 works · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Christ crucified, cross, cross (Jesus' cross), his cross, the cross, the cross of Christ, the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, the stumbling block of the cross, the word of the cross

Abstract written by a model

In The Good News According to Mark, the cross is carried to Golgotha by Simon of Cyrene, whom the soldiers compel to do so, while passersby and the chief priests taunt Jesus to come down from it. In The Gospel According to John, Jesus bears his cross to Golgotha, where Pilate's title is placed on it and the Jews wish the bodies not to remain on the crosses during the Sabbath. In Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians, the cross of Christ is preached as Christ crucified and is described as foolishness to those dying and a stumbling block to Jews, yet God's power to those being saved. In Paul’s Letter to the Galatians, the cross of Christ is the sole thing the author boasts in, through which the world and he are crucified to each other.
