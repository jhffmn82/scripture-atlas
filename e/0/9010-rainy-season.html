rainy season

Also called: rain, strong and widely spread rain

Abstract written by a model

Rainy season is a four-month season when living beings and seeds arise, roads become difficult, and monks and nuns remain in one place until travel is permitted.

During it, many living beings are originated and many seeds spring up, making roads difficult to use. A monk or a nun should remain in one place during the rainy season and should travel only after the prescribed period and when conditions permit.
