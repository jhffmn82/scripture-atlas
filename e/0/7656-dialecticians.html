Abstract written by a model

Dialecticians regarded Hui Tzŭ as a great philosopher and subtle dialectician, then argued endlessly over his paradoxes. Including Huan T'uan and Kung Sun Lung, they used specious premises to impose false conclusions, winning verbal battles without convincing adversaries.

Hui Tzŭ was a man of many ideas. His works would fill five carts. But

Dialecticians regarded Hui Tzŭ as a great philosopher and subtle dialectician, and they argued with him about his paradoxes without ever getting to the end. Huan T'uan and Kung Sun Lung belonged to this class and used specious premises to impose false conclusions, winning battles in words without convincing their adversaries.
