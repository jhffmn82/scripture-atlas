the hypocrites

group · 3 works · 15 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: a party of you, hypocrisy, hypocrites, hypocritical men, hypocritical women

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba), the hypocrites are depicted as disbelieving, doubtful, false-oath-taking, mocking, excuse-making men and women who oppose right and are threatened with punishment. In Surah 33: The Clans (Al-Ahzaab), the hypocrites are described as doubting Allah's and His messenger's promises, hindering believers, fleeing battle, and facing punishment. In Surah 63: The Hypocrites (Al-Munaafiqoon), the hypocrites falsely testify that Muhammad is Allah's messenger, obstruct Allah's way, oppose forgiveness, and are described as enemies with sealed hearts.

the hypocrites in Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where the hypocrites first appears

Abstract written by a model

The hypocrites are a group of men and women marked by disbelief, doubt, false oaths, mockery, excuses, and avoidance of striving. They enjoin wrong, forbid right, withhold spending, and forget Allah. Opposed by the Prophet through striving and harshness, they are promised fire, lasting torment, punishment, and a painful doom in this world and the Hereafter.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba)

The hypocrites are described as enjoining wrong, forbidding right, withholding their hands from spending, forgetting Allah, and wavering in doubt. They mock Allah's revelations and His messenger, make excuses, and are promised fire of hell and lasting torment.

73 O Prophet! Strive against the disbelievers and the hypocrites! Be harsh with

The hypocrites are exposed for disbelief, false oaths, avoidance of striving, mockery of believers, and excuses after returning from campaigns. Allah knows some of them whom the Prophet does not know, and they face punishment and a painful doom.

the hypocrites in Surah 63: The Hypocrites (Al-Munaafiqoon)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where the hypocrites first appears

Abstract written by a model

The hypocrites come to Muhammad claiming that he is Allah's messenger, but Allah identifies their testimony as false. They use faith as a pretext to turn people from Allah's way, refuse calls to seek forgiveness, and say that the mightier will drive out the weaker from Medina. They are described as enemies, and their hearts are sealed after they believed and then disbelieved.
