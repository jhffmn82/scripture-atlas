Sixteenth Lecture.

Pleasant things include sounds, colours, smells, tastes, feelings of touch, refined food and drink, ornaments, conversation, company, and looking at women's charms. They are difficult to abandon and are compared to the poison Tâlaputa for one seeking the true Self.
