Chapter 144

The chapter pictures a prosperous people whose sons are like well-nurtured plants and whose daughters are like carved pillars adorning a palace. Their barns are full of every kind of provision, their sheep produce thousands and ten thousands, their oxen pull heavy loads, and there is no breaking in, no going away and no outcry in the streets. Such people are called happy, especially because their God is the LORD.
