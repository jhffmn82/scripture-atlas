Abstract written by a model

occurs_in theater (at Ephesus) [crowd rushed there] The city fills with confusion and the people rush into the theater, seizing Gaius and Aristarchus, Macedonians and Paul's travel companions. Paul is kept from entering by the disciples and Asiarchs, and most in the assembly do not know why they came. The crowd shouts down Alexander, a Jew, and the town clerk finally quiets it and dismisses the assembly.
