mind

Also called: mind (men's minds), minds, the mind

Abstract written by a model

In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, the mind is made mad by the chariot course, wild hunting, and rare and strange objects sought for by people. In Mencius, Book VII (Jin Xin), men's minds are injured by hunger and thirst; preventing these harms removes reason to sorrow about inequality with other men.
