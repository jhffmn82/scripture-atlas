ram of consecration

object · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Aaron’s ram of consecration, a ram of consecration, ram of consecration (second ram), the other ram, the ram of consecration, the two rams

Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, it is the second ram consecrated for Aaron and his sons, with its blood applied to them, portions waved and burned, and its meat eaten by the priests. In Leviticus, it is the second ram offered by fire to the LORD, with its blood applied to Aaron and his sons and its portions burned, given to Moses, or eaten.

is_a ram of consecration The second ram has hands laid on it and is killed. ram of consecration in Leviticus

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where ram of consecration first appears

Abstract written by a model

The ram of consecration is a second ram, presented after the burnt offering ram, offered by fire to the LORD as a pleasant aroma. Aaron and his sons lay hands on it; it is killed, its blood applied to their ear, thumb and toe and sprinkled on the altar; fat and thigh burned, breast to Moses, meat eaten.

Chapter 8

After Aaron and his sons lay their hands on it and it is killed, its blood is put on the right ear, thumb, and great toe of Aaron and his sons and sprinkled around the altar. Its fat and right thigh, with bread from the basket, are waved and burned on the altar, the breast is Moses' portion, and the meat is to be boiled and eaten at the door.
