Abstract written by a model

Nuns are mendicants governed by a third lesson on conduct. They must not seek festivals, feasts, or village entertainment; instead they obtain acceptable alms from other families, avoid doubtful or prohibited food, and reject food from houses connected to Kshatriyas, kings, messengers, or kings’ relations. They wear a complete outfit when entering homes, traveling, or religious practice and study, except in harsh outdoor conditions.

Third Lesson.

Nuns should not resolve to attend festivals preceded or followed by feasts, or go to a village for the sake of an entertainment. They should obtain acceptable alms from other families and eat them, avoid doubtful food, wear the complete outfit in specified circumstances, and reject food from the houses of Kshatriyas, kings, messengers, and relations of kings.
