Emmaus

Also called: a village named Emmaus, the village, village

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of the Maccabees, Emmaus is plain country where the Syrian army under Ptolemy, Nicanor, and Gorgias encamps, merchants and allied forces gather, and Judas's army later camps on its south side. In The Gospel According to Luke, Emmaus is a village sixty stadia from Jerusalem where two disciples travel on resurrection day, Jesus stays with them, and they recognize him at table before he vanishes.
