Sect.

The mirror that had allured the Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity from the Rock-Dwelling is joined to His Augustness Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty for his descent. He is instructed to regard the mirror exactly as the august spirit of the commanding deities and to reverence it accordingly.
