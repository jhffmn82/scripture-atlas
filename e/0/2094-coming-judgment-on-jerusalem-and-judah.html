Abstract written by a model

The LORD decrees disaster on Jerusalem and Judah because of Manasseh's abominations and leading Judah into sin. Announced through prophets, it will make hearers' ears tingle: Jerusalem measured by Samaria's line and Ahab's plumb line, wiped like an upturned dish, and the remnant given to enemies as plunder, for provoking him since Egypt.

Chapter 21

Because of Manasseh's abominations and his leading Judah into sin, the LORD announces through his prophets a disaster so severe that the ears of all who hear will tingle. He will stretch over Jerusalem the line of Samaria and the plumb line of Ahab's house, wipe it like a dish, and give the remnant of his inheritance to enemies as prey and plunder. The reason given is that the people have provoked him since the day their fathers came out of Egypt.
