meditation

topic · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: ablutions, meditate, meditate on the True One, meditation on God, meditation on the True One

Abstract written by a model

In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), meditation on the True One is practised by the truthful and devotees, whom Guru Nanak declares true; the speaker begs from them, valuing devotion over high caste. In Hymns of Guru Nanak, meditation is presented as pure ablution, a cooking square made by few, and a proper spiritual practice centered on God.
