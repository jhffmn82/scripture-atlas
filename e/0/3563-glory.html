glory

topic · 3 works · 1 quoted fact · 4 connections
Also called: Glory!, divine glory, glory to glory, greatness, the glory of his face, the glory that surpasses

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, glory is due to the LORD and is proclaimed in his temple. In 2 Corinthians, glory contrasts the passing glory of the service of death with the surpassing, enduring glory of the Spirit and righteousness. In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), divine glory is described as great because of God's attributes and acts.
