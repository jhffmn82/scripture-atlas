worship

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: worship Me, worship at the going down of the sun, worship none save Him, worship of Allah

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 17: The Night Journey (Al-Israa), worship is to be established from the going down of the sun until the dark of night, with Qur'an recited at dawn, and people are commanded to worship none save Allah and call upon Him with His beautiful names. In Surah 51: The Winnowing Winds (Adh-Dhaariyat), worship of Allah is the purpose for which Allah created the jinn and humankind, who are commanded to flee unto Allah and not set any other god along with Him.
