Miriam

person · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Miriam the prophetess

Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, Miriam, a prophetess and Aaron’s sister, took a tambourine, led the women out dancing, and answered them by repeating the song praising the LORD’s triumph. In Numbers, Miriam joined Aaron in speaking against Moses over his Cushite wife, was rebuked and struck with leprosy, shut outside the camp seven days until restored, and later died and was buried at Kadesh.
