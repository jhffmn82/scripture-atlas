Abstract written by a model

A bitch big with young must be supported until her whelps are born, with responsibility determined by where she lies. If covered by three dogs, she is watched until she produces a young one and milk; striking her afterward brings seven hundred stripes with each of two instruments.

Fargard XV.

A bitch that is big with young must be supported until the whelps are born, with responsibility assigned according to where she is lying. A bitch covered by three dogs is watched until she brings forth a young one, and striking her after she has milk brings seven hundred stripes with each of two instruments.
