Chapter 8

Thirty thousand mighty men of valor are sent out by night to lie in ambush behind the city, between Bethel and Ai on the west side; the text also says Joshua set about five thousand men in ambush there. When Joshua stretches out his javelin, they rise quickly, enter the city, take it, and set it on fire.
