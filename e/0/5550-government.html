Abstract written by a model

In The Table-Talk of the Prophet Mohammad (Part 2), government is described as a trust from God whose holder must be worthy, just, and good. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XII (Yen Yuan), government is presented as rectification through correct leadership, avoiding killing, and desiring what is good.
