Families of Habaiah, Hakkoz, and Barzillai could not prove their genealogy and were disqualified from the priesthood, barred from the most holy things until a priest with Urim and Thummim arose. The priests gave 100 garments to the treasury, then stood with Jeshua to build the altar and, at the foundation, praised the LORD with trumpets, many weeping.

Chapter 2

The priests are listed as the children of Jedaiah (973), Immer (1,052), Pashhur (1,247), and Harim (1,017). The children of Habaiah, Hakkoz, and Barzillai could not find their registration in the genealogies and were deemed disqualified and removed from the priesthood. The governor told them not to eat the most holy things until a priest stood up with Urim and Thummim. The priests gave 100 priests' garments to the treasury and afterward lived in their cities.

Chapter 3

The priests stood with Jeshua to build the altar and offer burnt offerings. When the foundation was laid they were set in their vestments with trumpets to praise the LORD according to David's directions, and many of them wept aloud.
