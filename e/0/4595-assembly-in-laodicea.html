Chapter 3

The assembly in Laodicea is neither cold nor hot but lukewarm, so the speaker says he will vomit it out of his mouth. It claims to be rich and in need of nothing, but is wretched, miserable, poor, blind, and naked. It is counseled to buy refined gold, white garments, and eye salve from him, and to be zealous and repent, since he reproves and chastens those he loves.
