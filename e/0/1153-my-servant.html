Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, my servant acts wisely, is exalted and lifted very high, has a marred appearance, cleanses many nations, and causes kings to fall silent. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the servant declares the work among the Gentiles, brings forth Jesus Christ’s words with power, is marred because of them, and is healed by Jesus Christ.

Abstract written by a model

The LORD's servant acts wisely and is exalted and lifted very high, though his appearance is marred beyond any man's, astonishing many. He cleanses many nations, and kings fall silent, understanding what they had never been told.

Chapter 52

The LORD's servant will deal wisely and be exalted, lifted up, and very high. Many were astonished at him because his appearance and form were marred more than any man's, and he will cleanse many nations while kings shut their mouths at him, seeing and understanding what they had not been told or heard.
