Abstract written by a model

The kingdom begins as the people demand a king and Saul is chosen by lot at Mizpah and acclaimed. Samuel tells the people the regulations of the kingdom and writes them in a book laid up before the LORD. Saul is accompanied by an army whose hearts God had touched, though some despise him and bring no tribute.
