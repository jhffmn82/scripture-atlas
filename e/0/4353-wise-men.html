Abstract written by a model

Wise men from the east travel to Jerusalem seeking the newborn King of the Jews after seeing his star; they find the child, worship him with gold, frankincense and myrrh, then, warned in a dream, avoid Herod and return home another way.

Chapter 2

The wise men come from the east to Jerusalem asking where the King of the Jews is born, having seen his star. They are guided to the house where the child is, worship him and offer gifts, and, warned in a dream not to return to Herod, go back to their own country another way.
