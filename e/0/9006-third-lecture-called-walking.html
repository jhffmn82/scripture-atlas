Third Lecture, Called Walking. / First Lesson. .. Third Lesson.

It teaches rules for the conduct of a monk or a nun during the rainy season, on pilgrimage, and while wandering from village to village. It describes how to avoid harm, danger, unnecessary speech, and disturbance of living beings.
