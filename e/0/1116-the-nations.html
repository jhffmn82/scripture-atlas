Abstract written by a model

The nations are portrayed as insignificant before the LORD: a drop in a bucket, dust on a balance, less than nothing; their princes and judges are reduced to nothing, withering at his breath.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 40

The nations are described as a drop in a bucket and a speck of dust on a balance, and as nothing, even less than nothing, before the LORD. Their princes and judges are brought to nothing, withering when he merely blows on them.

Abstract written by a model

The residue of the nations, with all Edom, seized Israel's land joyfully and spitefully, making it prey and derision. The Lord God, jealous, swears they will bear their shame, and those left around Israel will learn that the LORD rebuilt the ruins and replanted desolation.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 36

The residue of the nations, along with all Edom, took the land for themselves with joy and despite of soul, swallowed it up, and made it a prey and a derision. The Lord GOD speaks against them in the fire of his jealousy and swears they will bear their own shame. Those left around Israel will come to know that the LORD built the ruined places and planted what was desolate.
