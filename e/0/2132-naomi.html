Naomi

Abstract written by a model

Naomi is a woman who worships the LORD, wife of Elimelech and mother of Mahlon and Chilion. She went to Moab, where her husband and both sons died, leaving her a widow. She returned to Bethlehem, asking to be called Mara, saying she left full and came home empty. Her daughter-in-law Ruth gleans for them, and Naomi recognizes Boaz as a near kinsman. She guides Ruth to approach him at the threshing floor, then tells her to wait while he settles the matter. Boaz buys Elimelech's field from her. When Ruth's son is born, Naomi takes him to her bosom as nurse, and the neighbor women say a son is born to Naomi, turning her bereavement into restoration.

Naomi in The Book of Ruth

author unknown · about 449 to 299 BC · the work’s page · read it where Naomi first appears

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Naomi moved to Moab with her husband Elimelech and two sons, and was left bereaved when all three died. She returned to Bethlehem after hearing the LORD had provided bread, urging her daughters-in-law to turn back. Upon arrival she asked to be called Mara, saying she went out full and came home empty.

Chapter 2

Naomi lets Ruth go to glean, and receives the barley and the leftover food Ruth brings back. On hearing Boaz's name she blesses him and says he is a close relative, one of their near kinsmen. She approves of Ruth staying with his maidens so she will not be harmed in another field.

Chapter 3

Naomi, Ruth's mother-in-law, proposes seeking rest for Ruth and gives detailed instructions: wash, anoint, dress, go to Boaz at the threshing floor, and uncover his feet and lie down after he has eaten and drunk. When Ruth returns, Naomi asks how it went and hears everything. She tells Ruth to wait, because Boaz will not rest until he has settled the matter that day.

Chapter 4

Naomi, who has come back from Moab, is the one selling the parcel that was Elimelech's, and Boaz buys it from her hand. After Ruth's son is born, the women bless the LORD for giving her a restorer of life, and Naomi takes the child to her bosom and becomes his nurse. The neighbor women say "A son is born to Naomi."
