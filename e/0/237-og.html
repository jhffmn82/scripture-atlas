Og

Also called: Og king of Bashan, Og the king of Bashan, Og, the king of Bashan, him

Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, Og, king of Bashan, went out with all his people to battle Israel at Edrei, was struck along with his sons and people, and lost his land to Israel. In Deuteronomy, Og was an Amorite king of Bashan who led his people against Israel at Edrei, where the LORD delivered them into Israel’s hand; he is remembered for his iron bedstead. In The Book of Joshua, Og was an Amorite king of Bashan whom Moses and the Israelites struck and utterly destroyed.
