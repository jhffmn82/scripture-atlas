Obscurity

topic · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections
Also called: obscurity

Abstract written by a model

Obscurity is the origin and remainder of all things; after work brings distinction, withdrawing into it is presented as the way of Heaven.

Obscurity is in one work on this site, The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
