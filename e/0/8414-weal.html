Weal

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Welfare, benefit and glorious welfare, blessing, good Blessedness, helpful blessing, the complete welfare

Abstract written by a model

Weal is Ahura Mazda’s abiding, eternal gift: a saving power and blessing for the righteous, complete welfare in ritual order. It comes to those who obediently attend Ahura Mazda’s declared word, is established with Immortality, and is requested from the Yazads and Ahura Mazda for petitioners’ well-being.

Weal is in one work on this site, Yasna. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Yasna XLV.

Weal is a blessing that comes to those who give obedient attention to Ahura Mazda’s declared word. Ahura Mazda establishes it together with Immortality.

Welfare is identified with Immortality as an eternal gift. It is requested from the Yazads and from Ahura Mazda as that which most promotes the petitioners' well-being.
