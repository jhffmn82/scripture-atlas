Ramah

place · 3 works · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Samuel, Ramah was where David came to Samuel after fleeing Saul, and where Naioth was located. In The First Book of Kings, Ramah was built by Baasha to blockade Asa's Judah, but its construction stopped after Ben Hadad attacked Israel, and its materials were reused to build Geba and Mizpah. In The Second Book of Chronicles, Ramah was built by Baasha to block access to Asa's Judah, but its construction stopped after Ben Hadad attacked Israel, and its materials were reused to build Geba and Mizpah.
