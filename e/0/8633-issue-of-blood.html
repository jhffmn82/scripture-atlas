issue of blood

Abstract written by a model

An issue of blood is a condition of a woman that may be ordinary or unusual, occurring out of the ordinary course or at the usual period. Suppressing it incurs stripes, while knowing, willing intercourse with a woman who has it requires the stated atonement.

Fargard XVI.

The issue of blood may occur out of the ordinary course or at the usual period. Suppressing it incurs two hundred stripes with the Aspahê-astra and two hundred with the Sraoshô-karana.

Fargard XVIII.

An issue of blood may occur out of the ordinary course or at the usual period. Intercourse with a woman who has an issue of blood, when both parties act knowingly and willingly, requires the stated atonement.
