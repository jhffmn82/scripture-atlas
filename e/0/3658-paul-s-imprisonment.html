Chapter 1

Paul's bonds became evident to the whole palace guard and all the rest as being in Christ. They made most of the brothers more confident and bold to speak the word of God without fear, though some preach from selfish ambition thinking to add affliction to his chains. Paul trusts they will turn out to his salvation through the Philippians' prayers and the supply of the Spirit of Jesus Christ.
