Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, the sword is the LORD's weapon that comes down on Edom for judgment, filled with blood and covered with fat. In The Book of Ezekiel, the sword is the LORD's weapon drawn, sharpened, polished, and handed to a killer for slaughter and judgment.

sword (the LORD's sword) in The Book of Ezekiel

author unknown · about 592 to 570 BC · the work’s page · read it where sword (the LORD's sword) first appears

Abstract written by a model

The LORD's sword is a weapon drawn from its sheath, sharpened and polished to slaughter and flash like lightning, then handed to a killer. It is set against all gates, doubled to a third time, and will not return to its sheath for Israel, though the LORD later tells it to return for Ammon. It is identified with the Babylonian king's sword.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 21

The sword is drawn from its sheath, sharpened and polished to slaughter and flash like lightning, and given to the hand of the killer. It is set against all gates and is doubled to a third time as the sword of the fatally wounded great one. It will not return to its sheath for Israel, though the LORD later tells it to return in the case of Ammon. It is also identified with the sword of the king of Babylon coming along two ways.
