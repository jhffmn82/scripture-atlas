Abstract written by a model

The Hasidaeans were a company of mighty men of Israel devoted to the law who joined Mattathias's uprising. They were first to seek peace with Alcimus and Bacchides, trusting his oath, but sixty were killed in one day, spreading fear among the people.

Chapter 7

The Hasidaeans were the first among the children of Israel to seek peace from Alcimus and Bacchides, reasoning that a priest of Aaron's seed would do them no wrong. They trusted Alcimus's oath, but sixty of them were seized and killed in one day. This broken oath spread fear among all the people.
