Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, temple repair was the ordered repair of the damage to the LORD's house. In The Second Book of Chronicles, temple repair was the work of restoring, rebuilding, and strengthening the LORD's house.

temple repair in The Second Book of Chronicles

author unknown · about 424 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where temple repair first appears

Abstract written by a model

Joash intended to restore the LORD's house; after the Levites' delay, tax money collected in a chest funded it, and Jehoiada with the king paid workers. Masons, carpenters and metalworkers rebuilt and strengthened it, and leftover money became ministering vessels.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 24

Joash intended to restore the LORD's house, and after the Levites' delay the work was funded through the chest. Hired masons, carpenters, and metalworkers carried it forward, and the house was set up as designed and strengthened. The remaining money was made into vessels for ministering and offering, and the account of the rebuilding is said to be written in the commentary of the book of the kings.
