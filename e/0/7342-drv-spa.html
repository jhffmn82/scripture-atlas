Drvâspa

Abstract written by a model

Drvâspa is a powerful, holy maintainer made by Mazda who keeps flocks, herds, cattle, and young ones healthy, assists the faithful, yokes horses and has a sounding-wheeled chariot, then grants boons to entreating sacrificers.

Drvâspa is in one work on this site, Yashts (Yasts) I-XII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

IX. Gôs Yast.

Drvâspa is the powerful, holy maintainer made by Mazda who keeps flocks, herds, grown-up cattle, and young ones in health and assists the faithful. She grants the requested boons to those who offer sacrifices, gifts, libations, and entreaties.
