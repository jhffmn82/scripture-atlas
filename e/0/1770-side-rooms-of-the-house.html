Abstract written by a model

The side rooms are chambers ringing the house, each four cubits wide, arranged in three stories of thirty rooms and set into the house wall without penetrating it. They widen on higher levels as the walls narrow, reached by climbing through the middle level. Doors face an open area five cubits wide, north and south.

Chapter 41

The side rooms are four cubits wide all around the house, in three stories with thirty in each story, set into the house wall so as not to penetrate it. They grow wider on higher levels as the walls narrow, and one climbs through the middle level to the highest. Their doors open toward an open area five cubits wide, one door to the north and one to the south.
