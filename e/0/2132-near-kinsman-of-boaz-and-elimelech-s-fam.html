near kinsman (of Boaz and Elimelech's family)

Abstract written by a model

A kinsman nearer to Ruth than Boaz, holding first claim to perform the kinsman's duty. Boaz reveals him in Chapter 3 and offers him the chance first. In Chapter 4 at the gate he agrees to redeem the land, but balks on learning he must also buy it from Ruth, fearing for his inheritance. He cedes the right to Boaz, removing his sandal.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Boaz reveals that there is a kinsman nearer than he is. This man is to be given the chance in the morning to perform the kinsman's duty for Ruth. If he will not, Boaz will do it.

Chapter 4

The near kinsman comes to the gate at Boaz's call and first says he will redeem the land, but after Boaz adds that he must also buy it from Ruth, he says he cannot lest he endanger his own inheritance. He gives Boaz his right of redemption and takes off his sandal to formalize the transaction.
