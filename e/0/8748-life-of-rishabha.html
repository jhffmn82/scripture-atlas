Abstract written by a model

Life of Rishabha traces his descent, conception, birth, long life as prince, king, and householder, then renunciation, attainment of Kevala, death, and final liberation. His total life spans eight millions four hundred thousand years, including time inferior to perfection and as a Kevalin.

Life of Rishabha.

Rishabha lived two millions of former years as a prince, six millions three hundred thousand former years as a king, and together eight millions three hundred thousand former years as a householder. He then lived a thousand former years in a state inferior to perfection and ninety-nine thousand former years as a Kevalin, for a total life of eight millions four hundred thousand years.
