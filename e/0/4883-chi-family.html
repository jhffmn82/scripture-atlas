Chi family

group · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Chi-sun family

Abstract written by a model

In The Analects of Confucius, Book VI (Yung Yey), the Chi family is represented by its chief, who sent to ask Min Tsze-ch'ien to be governor of Pi. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XVI (Ke She), the Chi family plans to attack Chwan-yu, while Confucius warns that internal divisions, hostile movements, leavings, and separations may bring sorrow within its own court.
