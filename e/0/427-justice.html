justice

topic · 6 works · 5 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: deal fairly, do injustice, equity, justly, righteous judgment, that which is altogether just, the demands of justice, the equitable

Abstract written by a model

In Deuteronomy, justice is a commanded standard that must be followed fully, without perversion, partiality, or bribery, and pursuing it is tied to living and inheriting the land the LORD gives. In Proverbs, justice makes the land stable when practiced by the king, is cared about by the righteous for the poor, and comes from the LORD. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), justice is required in dealing with orphans, marriage, and truthful witness, without following passion and lapsing from truth. In Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida), justice requires steadfast witnessing for Allah, fair dealing despite hatred, and equitable judgment, which Allah loves. In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), justice is enjoined by Allah along with sincere worship, fair dealing, and avoidance of oppression and corruption. In The Book of Mosiah, justice denies mercy to the wicked forever, while the Son stands between people and justice, takes iniquity, and satisfies its demands.
