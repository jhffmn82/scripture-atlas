Chapter 13

After two full years Absalom has his servants strike down Amnon at a sheep shearing when his heart is merry with wine, while the other sons flee on mules. A false report says all the sons are dead until Jonadab clarifies that only Amnon was killed. It leads to Absalom's flight to Geshur for three years and David's mourning.
