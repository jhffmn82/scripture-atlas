Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Ezra, they were taken from the Jerusalem temple to Babylon, placed in the house of Nebuchadnezzar's gods, and later brought out by Cyrus and counted to Sheshbazzar for transport to Jerusalem. In The Book of Jeremiah, they had not yet been taken by Nebuchadnezzar; the LORD said they would be carried to Babylon until he visited and restored them. In Daniel (Greek), they had been taken by Nebuchadnezzar from the Jerusalem temple and were brought for Belshazzar and his guests to drink from, an offense against the Lord of heaven.

Abstract written by a model

A collection of five thousand four hundred gold and silver vessels from God's house. Nebuchadnezzar took them from the Jerusalem temple to Babylon and placed them in the house of his gods. Cyrus later brought them out and had them counted to Sheshbazzar, who brought them up to Jerusalem.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

The Book of Ezra / Chapter 1

The vessels had been brought out of Jerusalem by Nebuchadnezzar and put in the house of his gods. Cyrus brought them out and had them counted to Sheshbazzar: thirty gold platters, a thousand silver platters, twenty-nine knives, thirty gold bowls, 410 silver bowls of a second kind, and a thousand other vessels, five thousand four hundred in all of gold and silver, and Sheshbazzar brought them up to Jerusalem.

Abstract written by a model

The vessels of the LORD's house, including pillars, sea, bases, and remaining temple, king's house, and Jerusalem vessels, were not yet taken by Nebuchadnezzar. Prophets falsely promise quick return; the LORD says they will be carried to Babylon until he visits and restores them.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 27

Prophets claim the vessels of the LORD's house will shortly be brought back from Babylon, but the LORD says this is a lie. The pillars, the sea, the bases, and the remaining vessels in the temple, the king's house, and Jerusalem, which Nebuchadnezzar did not take earlier, will be carried to Babylon and stay there until the LORD visits them, then brings them up and restores them to this place.
