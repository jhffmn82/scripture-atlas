Also called: The prayer of disbelievers, pray, pray unto Me, the prayer of disbelievers, the real prayer

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 13: The Thunder (Ar-Ra'd), the real prayer belongs to Allah, while those prayed to beside Allah do not respond and the prayer of disbelievers goes astray. In Surah 40: The Forgiver (Al-Ghaafir), prayer is commanded to Allah alone, who promises to hear it, while the prayer of disbelievers is declared vain. In Surah 107: Almsgiving (Al-Maa'un), prayer is treated heedlessly by the worshippers who are warned of woe.
