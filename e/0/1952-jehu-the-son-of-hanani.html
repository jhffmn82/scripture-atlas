Jehu the son of Hanani

person · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Jehu, Jehu the prophet, Jehu the son of Hanani the seer, the prophet Jehu the son of Hanani

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Kings, Jehu the son of Hanani is a prophet who receives and delivers the LORD's word against Baasha and his house, foretelling and later fulfilling their destruction. In The Second Book of Chronicles, Jehu, seer and son of Hanani, confronts King Jehoshaphat, rebuking him for helping the wicked and loving God's haters, warning of the LORD's wrath, yet acknowledging his removal of the Asheroth and seeking God.
