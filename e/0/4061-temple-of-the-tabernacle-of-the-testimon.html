Abstract written by a model

In The Letter to the Hebrews, the greater and more perfect tabernacle is the true heavenly sanctuary pitched by the Lord, of which the earthly tabernacle is a copy and shadow, and through which Christ passed into heaven itself to appear in God's presence for us. In Revelation, the temple of the tabernacle of the testimony is God's heavenly temple, measured with its altar and worshippers, and later opened as seven angels emerge while smoke from God's glory fills it until the seven plagues finish.

Abstract written by a model

The greater, more perfect heavenly tabernacle is the true sanctuary, pitched by the Lord, not made with hands nor of this creation. Earthly tabernacle service, patterned for Moses, is only its copy and shadow. Christ passed through it into heaven itself to appear in God's presence for us.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 8

The true tabernacle is the sanctuary in which the high priest serves, and it was pitched by the Lord, not by man. The earthly priests' service is only a copy and shadow of the heavenly things, as Moses was told to make the tabernacle according to the pattern shown on the mountain.

Chapter 9

The greater and more perfect tabernacle is not made with hands and not of this creation. Christ passed through it into heaven itself, the true holy place, to appear in God's presence for us. The earthly holy places are representations or copies of it.

temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in Revelation

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where temple of the tabernacle of the testimony first appears

Abstract written by a model

The heavenly temple of the tabernacle of the testimony is God's temple. In Chapter 11 the narrator measures it, its altar and worshippers, while the outer court is left to the nations. In Chapter 15 it opens, seven angels emerge, and smoke from God's glory fills it, barring entry until the seven plagues finish.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

The narrator is told to measure God's temple, the altar, and those who worship in it. The court outside the temple is to be left unmeasured because it has been given to the nations.

Chapter 15

The temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven is opened, and the seven angels come out of it. It is then filled with smoke from God's glory and power, and no one can enter until the seven plagues are finished.
