Chapter 18

Seven tribes had not yet divided their inheritance, and Joshua urged them to stop delaying in taking possession of the land. Their portions were to be surveyed in seven parts and assigned by lot at Shiloh, with Judah staying in the south and the house of Joseph in the north.
