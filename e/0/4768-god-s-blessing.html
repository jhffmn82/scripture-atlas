God's blessing

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a state of never-ending happiness, bless, bless you, blessed, blessedness and prosperity, merciful, prosper, prospered

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, God's blessing is requested and affirmed as something God will give to the psalmist's community, alongside the earth's yielded increase, so that God's way may be known on earth and all the ends of the earth may fear him. In The Book of Mosiah, blessedness and prosperity are received in heaven by those faithful to the end, who keep God's commandments, prosper in the land, and dwell with God in never-ending happiness.
