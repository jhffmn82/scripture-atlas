Abstract written by a model

A man may die under a house, with prescribed treatment of house and body. Contact with a corpse can require gômêz then water cleansing, while specified sexual acts, covering a corpse, and voluntary seed emission incur consequences.

Fargard VIII.

A man who dies under a house or hut is removed or left while the house is treated according to the prescribed procedure. A man who touches a corpse may be purified under specified conditions, while penalties are imposed for placing clothes over a dead body or voluntarily emitting seed.
