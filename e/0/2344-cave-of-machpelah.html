In Genesis, the cave of Machpelah is a burial site in Ephron the Hittite's field where Sarah, Abraham, Isaac, Rebekah, and Leah were buried, and which Jacob desired as his burial place. In Genesis, the cave is the cave of Machpelah at the end of Ephron's field that Abraham bought with the field and where Sarah was buried.

cave of Machpelah in Genesis

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where cave of Machpelah first appears

Abstract written by a model

The cave of Machpelah is a burial site in Ephron the Hittite's field, before Mamre (Hebron) in Canaan. Abraham bought it with the field from Ephron as a burial possession. Sarah was buried there first; later Abraham, Isaac, Rebekah, and Leah were too, and Jacob named it as his own desired burial place.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 23

Machpelah is the location of Ephron's field and cave, before Mamre (that is, Hebron), in the land of Canaan. Sarah was buried in the cave of the field of Machpelah.

Chapter 49

Jacob names the cave in the field of Machpelah, before Mamre in the land of Canaan, as his desired burial place. Abraham bought it with the field from Ephron the Hittite, and Abraham and Sarah, Isaac and Rebekah, and Leah are buried there.
