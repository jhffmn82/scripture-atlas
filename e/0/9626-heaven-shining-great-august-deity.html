Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity

person · 1 work · 19 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: Heaven-Shining-Great-August Deity, Heaven-Shining. Great-August-Deity, Heavenly Shining-Great-August-Deity, The Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity, her, the Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity, the other two Deities

Abstract written by a model

Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity is a deity born when the Great Deity the Male-Who-Invites washes his left august eye. Receiving the August necklace, she is charged to rule the Plain-of-High-Heaven. When her younger sibling, His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness, approaches, she arms herself with jewels, bow, and arrows; they swear and produce children, and she claims the five male Deities born last. After his ravages culminate in the release of a flayed heavenly piebald horse in the weaving-hall, she withdraws into the Heavenly Rock-Dwelling, bringing darkness and eternal night. She emerges after seeing the mirror, restoring light to the Plain of High Heaven and the Central Land of Reed-Plains. Thereafter she exercises heavenly authority: she governs alongside other deities, convenes the eight hundred myriad Deities, sends envoys concerning the yielding of the Central Land of Reed-Plains, and commands His Augustness Heaven's-Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty to descend and rule it.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

10 sections; each heading opens the text

Sect.

She is born when the Great Deity the Male-Who-Invites washes his left august eye.

Sect.

The Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity receives the August necklace and is charged to rule the Plain-of-High-Heaven.

Sect.

She fears that His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness intends to seize her land, so she arms herself and challenges him. After hearing his explanation, she agrees to test his sincerity by swearing and producing children.

Sect.

Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity declares the division of the children, assigning the five male Deities born last to herself and the three female Deities born first to His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness. She excuses His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness's ravages before he continues them, and she is present in the weaving-hall when the flayed heavenly piebald horse is released.

Sect.

The Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity closes and fastens the door of the Heavenly Rock-Dwelling and retires, causing darkness and eternal night. She later opens the door slightly, comes forth after seeing the mirror, and her emergence restores light to the Plain of High Heaven and the Central Land of Reed-Plains.

Sect.

The Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity commands that the land be governed by Truly-Conqueror-I-Conquer-Conquering-Swift-Heavenly-Great-Great-Ears. She later joins the High-August-Producing-Wondrous-Deity in convening the eight hundred myriad Deities and sending Deity Ame-no-ho-hi.

Sect.

The Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity joins the High-August-Producing-Wondrous-Deity in asking which Deity should be sent after the Heavenly-Young-Prince fails to report.

Sect.

Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity asked which Deity should be sent on a fresh mission and charged Brave-Awful-Possessing-Male-Deity and Deity Heavenly-Bird-Boat to ask Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land about yielding the Central Land of Reed-Plains.

Sect.

Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity, together with the High-Integrity-Deity, commands His Augustness Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty to descend from Heaven and rule the Central Land of Reed-Plains. Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity also helps question the radiant Deity at the eight-forking road of Heaven and joins the mirror and other divine objects to the descent.

Sect.

Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity commanded His Augustness Heaven's-Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty to descend from Heaven.
