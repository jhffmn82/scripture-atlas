Chapter 11

The righteous are guided by integrity and delivered from trouble and death, and their way is directed by their righteousness. Their blessing exalts the city, their desire is only good, and they flourish like a green leaf. Their offspring will be delivered, they bear a tree of life, and they will be repaid on the earth.
