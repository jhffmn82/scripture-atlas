Chapter 3

Bashan was the kingdom of Og, containing sixty fortified cities in the region of Argob plus many unwalled villages. Israel took it, and it was given to the half-tribe of Manasseh; Jair named the Argob region Havvoth Jair after himself. It is also called the land of Rephaim.
