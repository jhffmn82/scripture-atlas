Abstract written by a model

The faithful person worships Mazda and lives by good thoughts, words, deeds, and religion, supporting the Law through worship, sacrifice, and aid to the poor. After death, a beautiful conscience meets him; he crosses the Paradises and reaches Endless Light.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Yast XXII.

The faithful person has good thoughts, good words, good deeds, and good religion. After death, the faithful person’s conscience appears as a beautiful maiden reflecting those qualities.

XXIV. Vîstâsp Yast.

The faithful man supports the Law, performs proper worship and sacrifice, helps the poor, and follows good thoughts, words, deeds, and religion. After death, he receives a sweet-scented wind, encounters his conscience, and proceeds through the Paradises to Endless Light.
