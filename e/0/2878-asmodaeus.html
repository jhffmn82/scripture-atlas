Abstract written by a model

Asmodaeus is an evil spirit who loves Sarah and killed her seven husbands in the bride-chamber before they lay with her. Raphael is sent to bind him because Sarah belongs to Tobias; the fish's smoke will make him flee, never returning.

Chapter 3

Asmodaeus is the evil spirit who killed Sarah's seven husbands before they had lain with her. Raphael is sent to bind him, because Sarah belongs to Tobias by inheritance.

Chapter 6

The demon loves Sarah and, as Tobias has heard, harms only those who come to her, so that her seven husbands died in the bride-chamber. The angel says it will smell the smoke of the fish's heart and liver, flee, and never come again.
