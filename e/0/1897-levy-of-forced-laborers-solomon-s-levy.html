Solomon's levy was thirty thousand forced laborers drawn from all Israel, supervised by Adoniram. They worked in rotation of ten thousand monthly in Lebanon, one month there and two at home, alongside seventy thousand burden bearers, eighty thousand stone cutters, and 3,300 officers.

Chapter 5

Solomon raised a levy of thirty thousand men out of all Israel and sent them to Lebanon in courses of ten thousand a month, one month there and two at home. Adoniram was over the men subject to forced labor. Alongside them Solomon had seventy thousand burden bearers, eighty thousand stone cutters in the mountains, and three thousand three hundred chief officers ruling the laborers.
