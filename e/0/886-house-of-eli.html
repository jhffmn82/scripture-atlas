Chapter 3

The LORD declares he will perform against Eli's house all he has spoken, from beginning to end, and will judge it forever. The cause given is the iniquity Eli knew of: his sons brought a curse on themselves and he did not restrain them. The LORD has sworn that the iniquity will not be removed by sacrifice or offering forever.
