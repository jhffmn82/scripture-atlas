Yasna IX.

The dragon Dahâka is three-jawed, triple-headed, six-eyed, and endowed with a thousand powers and mighty strength. He is a lie-demon of the Daêvas, harmful to settlements and the homes of Asha, and was made by Angra Mainyu as a mighty Drug(k) against the corporeal world.
