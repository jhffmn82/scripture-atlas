Chapter 21

The men of Israel had sworn at Mizpah not to give their daughters to Benjamin as wives, and a curse was placed on anyone who does. A great oath also called for the death of anyone who did not come up to the LORD at Mizpah. These oaths create the problem of providing wives for Benjamin and drive the actions against Jabesh Gilead and Shiloh.
