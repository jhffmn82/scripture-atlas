Lot's household

group · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: his household, the family of Lot

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), Lot's household is saved with him, except for an old woman among those who stayed behind. In Surah 54: The Moon (Al-Qamar), the family of Lot was rescued from the storm of stones in the last watch of the night as grace from the Lord.
