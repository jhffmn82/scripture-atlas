Chapter 38

Jeremiah declares that the city will surely be given into the hand of the army of the king of Babylon, who will take it. Those who remain will die by sword, famine, and pestilence, while those who go out to the Chaldeans will live. He repeats it privately to Zedekiah, offering survival and no burning if the king surrenders, and the chapter closes with Jerusalem's capture still pending.
