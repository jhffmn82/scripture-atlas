Chapter 7

Following the advice of Esau's and Moab's leaders, Holofernes besieges Bethulia, seizing the springs and occupying the nearby hills so that no one can leave. After thirty-four days the water has run out and the people demand surrender. Ozias secures a delay of five days, promising to surrender if no help comes.
