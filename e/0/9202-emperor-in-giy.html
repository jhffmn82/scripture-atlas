Emperor in-Giyō

person · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Heavenly Sovereign, His Augustness Wo-asa-dzu-ma-waku-go-no-sukune

Abstract written by a model

Emperor in-Giyō ruled the Empire from the Palace of Toho-tsu-Asuka after recovering from a long sickness through a physician from Shiragi. He established surnames and gentile names, wed Empress Osaka-no-oho-naka-tsu-hime, had several august children, lived seventy-eight years, and was buried at Naga-ye near Wega in Kafuchi.

Emperor in-Giyō is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Emperor in-Giyō ruled from the Palace of Toho-tsu-Asuka after initially declining because of a long sickness. A physician sent from Shiragi cured him, and he established surnames and gentile names for the people. He lived to seventy-eight and was buried at Naga-ye near Wega in Kafuchi.
