Abstract written by a model

An open letter sent by Sanballat through his servant, the fifth delivery, accusing Nehemiah and the Jews of plotting rebellion and kingship, citing rumors, prophets and a threat to inform the king, and demanding a meeting. Nehemiah denies it, calling it Sanballat's invention.

Chapter 6

Sanballat's servant delivers this open letter the fifth time, reporting among the nations and from Gashmu that Nehemiah and the Jews intend to rebel and that he would be king. It claims Nehemiah has appointed prophets to proclaim a king in Judah, threatens that the king will be told, and calls for a meeting to take counsel. Nehemiah answers that no such things are done and that Sanballat imagines them from his own heart.
