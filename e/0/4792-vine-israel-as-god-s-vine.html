Abstract written by a model

The vine is Israel, brought out of Egypt and planted by the LORD God of Armies after the nations were driven out. It took deep root, filled the land, covered mountains, and stretched branches to the sea and shoots to the River. Then its walls were broken down, so passersby pluck it, the boar and wild animals ravage it, and it is burned and cut down.

Chapter 80

The vine was brought out of Egypt and planted after the nations were driven out. Its walls are now broken, so passersby pluck it, the boar and wild animals ravage it, and it is burned and cut down.
