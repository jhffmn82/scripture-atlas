Myazda meat-offering

ritual · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Myazda, Myazda which is that of the Maidhyô-zaremaya, Myazda, the meat-offering, fifth Myazda, first Myazda, fourth Myazda, meat-offering, second Myazda, sixth Myazda, the Myazda meat-offering, third Myazda

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Myazda is a complete and sacred meat-offering offered with Haurvatât, Ameretatât, and blessed Kine flesh for Sraosha; the Ratu then commands deserving righteous men to eat it. In Afrinagan, Gahs and Miscellaneous Fragments, Myazda is a ritual offering for six seasonal observances, beginning with Maidhyô-zaremya; failure to bring it can bring expulsion and expiating deeds.

Yasna VII.

The Myazda meat-offering is offered with Haurvatât, Ameretatât, and the flesh of the Kine of blessed gift for the propitiation of Sraosha.

Yasna VIII.

The Myazda is a meat-offering presented with a complete and sacred offering. The Ratu commands men who have deserved it by their righteousness and correctness to eat it.
