Abstract written by a model

In Revelation, the lake of fire and sulfur is the second death and a place of eternal torment where the devil joins the beast and false prophet, Death and Hades are thrown, and anyone absent from the book of life is cast. In The Book of Jacob, the Brother of Nephi, the lake of fire and brimstone is a place of unquenchable flames, forever-ascending smoke, and endless torment for those condemned by justice. In The Book of Mosiah, hell is a lake of fire and brimstone with unquenchable flames and smoke ascending forever and ever, representing a state of misery and endless torment from which the wicked cannot return. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the lake of fire and brimstone describes the torments of those who die spiritually, with its flame ascending forever and ever.
