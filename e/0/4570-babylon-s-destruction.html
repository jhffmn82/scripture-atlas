Babylon's destruction is a sudden, fiery downfall in one day and one hour, shown by a millstone cast into the sea. It brings death, famine, and silence; heaven and the saints rejoice while kings, merchants, and sailors mourn.

Chapter 18

Babylon's destruction comes in one day and one hour, with death, mourning, famine, and burning with fire. It is illustrated by the mighty angel's millstone cast into the sea, and it silences all music, craft, light, and wedding voices in her. Heaven, the saints, apostles, and prophets are told to rejoice, while kings, merchants, and sailors lament from afar.
