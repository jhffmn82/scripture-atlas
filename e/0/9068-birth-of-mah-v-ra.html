Abstract written by a model

In the Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra), Mahâvîra's birth occurs to Kshatriyânî Trisalâ in Kundapura, marked by divine lustre, gods, showers, and auspicious ceremonies. In the Kalpa Sutra, birth of Mahâvîra occurs at king Siddhârtha's palace in Kundapura, where Trisalâ gives birth to Vardhamâna, marked by divine signs and a ten-day festival.

Mahâvîra was born to the perfectly healthy Kshatriyânî Trisalâ in Kundapura during the dark fortnight of Kaitra. At his birth, divine lustre, a great gathering of gods, showers of nectar and precious substances, and auspicious ceremonies appeared.

birth of Mahâvîra in Kalpa Sutra

Bhadrabâhu · the work’s page · read it where birth of Mahâvîra first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mahâvîra's birth is an event at king Siddhârtha's palace in Kundapura. After nine months and seven and a half days, during the first month of summer, on the fourteenth day of Kaitra's dark fortnight, Trisalâ gives birth at midnight to a healthy boy while the moon is joined with Uttaraphalgunî. Divine lustre, noise from gods and goddesses, and riches mark the birth. His parents name him Vardhamâna, and a ten-day festival celebrates it.
