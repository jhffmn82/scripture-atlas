Paul’s First Letter to the Corinthians / Chapter 1

The text says God will destroy the wisdom of the wise and has made the wisdom of this world foolish, since the world through its wisdom didn't know God. Greeks seek after wisdom, but the foolishness of God is wiser than men, and God chose the foolish things of the world to put the wise to shame.
