Judah's allotment boundaries

place · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: The lot for the tribe of the children of Judah, This is the border of the children of Judah according to their families, east border, south border, west border

Abstract written by a model

Judah's allotment is defined by four borders. The south border runs from the Salt Sea's bay through Edom's side, Zin, Kadesh Barnea and Azmon to the brook of Egypt and the sea. The east is the Salt Sea, the west the great sea, and the north runs from Beth Hoglah through Jerusalem-area valleys to Jabneel.

Chapter 15

The south border runs from the Salt Sea's bay by the ascent of Akrabbim, Zin, Kadesh Barnea, Hezron, Addar, Karka and Azmon to the brook of Egypt and the sea. The east border is the Salt Sea to the end of the Jordan, and the north border runs through Beth Hoglah, Beth Arabah, the stone of Bohan, the valley of Achor, En Shemesh, En Rogel, the valley of Hinnom, Baalah, Mount Seir, Beth Shemesh, Timnah, Ekron and Jabneel to the sea. The west border is the shore of the great sea.
