Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Mary is Jesus Christ's mother, who is found pregnant by the Holy Spirit, marries Joseph, bears Jesus, and travels with him to Egypt, Israel, and Nazareth. In The Good News According to Mark, Jesus's mother comes with his brothers and sends word calling him, prompting Jesus to define his spiritual family as those who do God's will. In The Gospel According to Luke, Mary is the virgin of Nazareth who becomes Jesus' mother and, as Martha's sister, sits at Jesus' feet. In The Gospel According to John, Mary is Jesus' mother who speaks to him at Cana and is entrusted to the disciple at the cross.

Mary in The Gospel According to Luke

author unknown · AD 80 to 90 · the work’s page · read it where Mary first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mary is a virgin of Nazareth, betrothed to Joseph, who becomes the mother of Jesus. In Chapter 1, Gabriel tells her she has found favor with God; she accepts, visits Elizabeth, is called blessed, and sings praise. In Chapter 2 she travels to Bethlehem, gives birth to her firstborn, ponders the shepherds' sayings, hears Simeon say a sword will pierce her soul, and searches three days for the twelve-year-old Jesus. In Chapter 10, as Martha's sister, she sits at Jesus' feet and is said to have chosen the good part.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Mary, a virgin of Nazareth pledged to Joseph of David's house, is told by Gabriel she has found favor with God and will bear Jesus. She accepts, saying 'let it be done to me according to your word,' visits Elizabeth, is called blessed and the mother of Elizabeth's Lord, and sings praise to God before staying about three months and returning home.

Chapter 2

Mary, pledged to Joseph and pregnant, goes with him to Bethlehem to enroll and gives birth to her firstborn son there. She keeps the shepherds' sayings, pondering them in her heart, and Simeon tells her a sword will pierce through her own soul. When Jesus is twelve she and Joseph search for him for three days, she questions him in the temple, and she keeps all these sayings in her heart.

Chapter 10

Mary is Martha's sister and sits at Jesus' feet hearing his word while Martha serves. Jesus says she has chosen the good part, which will not be taken away from her.

Mary (Jesus' mother) in The Gospel According to John

author unknown · AD 90 to 100 · the work’s page · read it where Mary (Jesus' mother) first appears

Abstract written by a model

Jesus' mother is a woman with a sister who appears at two moments. At the Cana wedding she tells Jesus they have no wine and instructs the servants to obey him, then travels to Capernaum with his family and disciples. At the cross, Jesus entrusts her to the disciple, who takes her home.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Jesus' mother is at the wedding in Cana and tells Jesus, "They have no wine." After his reply that his hour has not yet come, she tells the servants to do whatever he says. She later goes down to Capernaum with Jesus, his brothers, and his disciples.

Chapter 19

Jesus' mother stands by the cross with her sister, Mary the wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene. Jesus says to her, "Woman, behold, your son!" and from that hour the disciple takes her into his own home.
