Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Kings, Elijah wraps his face in the mantle and puts it on Elisha as he passes him plowing. In The Second Book of Kings, Elijah rolls up the mantle and strikes the Jordan with it; after it falls from him during his ascent, Elisha picks it up and strikes the Jordan with it.
