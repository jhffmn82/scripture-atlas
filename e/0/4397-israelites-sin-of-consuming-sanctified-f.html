Abstract written by a model

is_forbidden_by God [God's laws] Judith claims that, because their food and water have failed, her people mean to kill their livestock and eat what God forbade them by his laws, including first fruits of the grain and tithes of wine and oil reserved for the priests. She says they have sought permission from the elders in Jerusalem and that, once they act, they will be given over to Holofernes to be destroyed the same day.
