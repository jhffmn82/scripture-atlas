Abstract written by a model

The Spartans are a people presented as kindred of the Jews, both said to descend from Abraham. Jonathan wrote to them, recalling King Arius's earlier letter to Onias. After Jonathan's death, they grieved and wrote on brass tablets to Simon, renewing friendship and alliance, honorably receiving ambassadors Numenius and Antipater.

Chapter 12

Jonathan wrote to the Spartans as kindred, recalling a letter from their king Arius to Onias stating that Spartans and Jews descend from Abraham. Arius had proposed that their livestock and goods be held in common, and Jonathan asked for a reply.

Chapter 14

The Spartans grieved at Jonathan's death and wrote on brass tablets to Simon to renew friendship and alliance. Their letter to Simon, the elders, priests and people calls the Jews kindred, and records that they received the Jewish ambassadors Numenius and Antipater honorably and put their words in the public records.
