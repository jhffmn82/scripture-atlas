Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, it is the covenant Josiah makes before the LORD to walk after him and keep his commandments, testimonies, and statutes with all his heart and soul, confirmed by all the people. In The Second Book of Chronicles, it is a covenant renewal at the LORD's house in which Josiah gathers the elders and all people, reads them the covenant book, pledges wholehearted obedience to the LORD's commandments, and has those in Jerusalem and Benjamin stand and act according to it.
