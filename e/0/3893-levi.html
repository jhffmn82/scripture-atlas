Abstract written by a model

In The Good News According to Mark, Levi, son of Alphaeus, is a tax collector who follows Jesus after being called and hosts tax collectors and sinners at a meal. In The Gospel According to Luke, Levi, son of Melchi, is a tax collector who follows Jesus after being called, leaves everything at his tax office, and hosts a great feast in his house.
