Chapter 40

Its lodges (three on each side), posts, arches, windows, and palm trees match the measure of the east gate, and it is fifty cubits long and twenty-five wide. It is reached by seven steps, with its arches before them. A gate to the inner court faces it, one hundred cubits away.
