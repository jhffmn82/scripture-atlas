water

substance · 4 works · 5 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: a part of the waters, purifying water, the holy waters, the water which ye drink, the waters, waters

Abstract written by a model

In The Believers (Al-Muminoon), water is sent down in measure, lodged in the earth, withdrawable by Allah, and associated with the refuge of the son of Mary and his mother. In Surah 25: The Criterion (Al-Furqaan), water is sent as purifying rain to revive dead land, provide drink for beasts and humans, and separate sweet and salty seas. In Surah 56: The Inevitable (Al-Waaqia), water is drunk by humans and comes from rainclouds through the Lord of the Worlds, who could make it bitter. In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, water flows from Ardvi Sûra Anâhita across the seven Karshvares, purifies seed and wombs, provides milk, and can form a dry passage.
