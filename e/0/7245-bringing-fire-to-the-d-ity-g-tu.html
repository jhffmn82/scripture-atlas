bringing fire to the Dâityô-gâtu

Abstract written by a model

Bringing fire to the Dâityô-gâtu is an event rewarded after a man’s soul has parted from his body. Its central feature is a graded reward of fire-brands based on the fire’s source: corpse-burning fire earns ten thousand, while his own hearth earns ten. Between these are fires from excrement, cowdung, kilns, reapers, craftspeople, ovens, cauldrons, encampments, herdsmen, and fields, with specified or source-dependent amounts. The event proceeds by bringing a particular fire, then receiving the corresponding reward after death.

VIII.

Bringing fire to the Dâityô-gâtu earns a reward after the man's soul has parted from his body. The reward varies according to the fire, from ten thousand fire-brands for corpse-burning fire to ten for the fire of his own hearth.
