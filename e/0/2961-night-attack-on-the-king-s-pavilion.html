Abstract written by a model

Judas led a chosen force of the bravest young men against the king's pavilion by night, using the watchword "VICTORY IS GOD'S". They killed about two thousand men and felled the leading elephant and its rider, terrifying the army, then withdrew successfully at dawn, credited to the Lord's protection.

Chapter 13

Judas led a chosen force of the bravest young men against the king's pavilion by night with the watchword "VICTORY IS GOD'S". They killed about two thousand men and brought down the leading elephant along with the man in its tower. The army was filled with terror and the attackers departed with good success at dawn, attributed to the Lord's protection.
