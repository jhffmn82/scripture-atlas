Chapter 4

The early death of the righteous man is presented as rest rather than loss, since he was caught away lest wickedness corrupt him. Because his soul pleased the Lord, he hurried out of the midst of wickedness. The peoples see it without understanding, but it will condemn the long-lived unrighteous.
