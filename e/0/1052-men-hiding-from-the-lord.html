Chapter 2

Men are told to enter the rock and hide in the dust, and they are said to go into caves, holes of the earth, and clefts of ragged rocks. They flee from the terror of the LORD and the glory of his majesty when he arises to shake the earth mightily, casting away their idols as they go.
