two females

group · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: females

Abstract written by a model

A group of two females is defined through inheritance shares. Each female receives half the portion of a male, so together their shares equal one male's share. The record situates this rule within a pronouncement on distant kindred: when brethren include men and women, the male receives the equivalent of the share of two females. It begins with the share relation and concludes by confirming the two females' combined equivalence to one male.
