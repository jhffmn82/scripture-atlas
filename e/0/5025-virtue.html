virtue

Abstract written by a model

Virtue is presented as the basis of an excellent neighborhood and as the condition for rightly loving or hating others. The virtuous rest in virtue, cleave to it in hardship and danger, and gain neighbors through practicing it.

virtue is in one work on this site, The Analects of Confucius, Book IV (Le Jin). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
