Shu'eyb

person · 3 works · 8 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: their brother Shu'eyb

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), Shu'eyb calls for justice, warns against fraud and disbelief, rejects their religion, and seeks Allah's judgment. In Surah 11: Hud, Shu'eyb calls for worship, justice in measure and weight, forgiveness, and repentance, seeks reform, trusts Allah, and is saved with believers when punishment comes. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), Shu'eyb calls the dwellers in the wood to duty, obedience, full measure, true weighing, justice, and avoidance of corruption, while asking no wage.
