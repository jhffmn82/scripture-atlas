the heavens and the earth

place · 6 works · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: The heavens, the earth, and all their vast array, creation, heaven and earth, heaven and the earth, heavens and the earth, the earth, the earth and the heavens, the heavens, the heavens and of the earth, the sky, work of creation

Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, the heavens and the earth are an account of creation by God, beginning with a formless earth and ending with its vast array. In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), the heavens and the earth belong to Allah, whose sovereignty encompasses them. In Surah 14: Abraham (Ibrahim), the heavens and the earth are created by Allah with truth and made serviceable to mankind. In Surah 24: The Light (An-Noor), the heavens and the earth belong to Allah, and all who are in them praise Him. In Surah 27: The Ant (An-Naml), the heavens and the earth contain Allah’s signs and nothing hidden in them is absent from a clear Record. In Surah 29: The Spider (Al-Ankaboot), the heavens and the earth are created by Allah with truth as a sign for believers.

heavens and the earth in Genesis

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where heavens and the earth first appears

Abstract written by a model

The heavens and the earth are an account of creation by God. It begins with a formless, empty, dark earth under God's Spirit, then is declared finished with its vast array. Before plants or man existed, a mist watered the ground; the LORD God formed man, trees, animals, and woman.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

God creates the heavens and the earth in the beginning. The earth is at first formless and empty, with darkness over the deep and God's Spirit hovering over the waters.

Chapter 2

The heavens, the earth, and all their vast array are described as finished, and the text calls the account the history of the generations of the heavens and the earth when they were created. Before plants appeared and before there was a man to till the ground, a mist watered the ground, and the LORD God then formed man, the garden's trees, the animals, and the woman.
