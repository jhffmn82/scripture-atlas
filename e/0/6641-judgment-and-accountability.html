judgment and accountability

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: be brought to book, judged, must be questioned, requital, requited, reward, what ye used to do

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 10: Jonas (Yunus), judgment and accountability concerns Allah witnessing and recording every act, holding every soul accountable for its deeds, and judging nations and individuals fairly. In Surah 37: Those drawn up in Ranks (As-Saaffaat), judgment and accountability concerns people being assembled, stopped, and questioned about their conduct and worship, being requited only for what they did, and the guilty questioning and accusing one another before sharing the doom.
