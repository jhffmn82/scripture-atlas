Abstract written by a model

Holy men and women in Aryan, Turanian, Sairimyan, Sâini, Dâhi, and all countries, across generations, struggle for holiness and teach the Law; they are worshipped through their Fravashis, souls, spirits, consciences, and perceptions.

XXVI.

The holy men and women of many countries and generations are worshipped through their Fravashis, souls, spirits, consciences, and perceptions. They are described as struggling or having struggled for holiness and teaching the Law.
