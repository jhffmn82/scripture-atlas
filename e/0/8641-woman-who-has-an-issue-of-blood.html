woman who has an issue of blood

Also called: a woman who has an issue of blood, a woman who has an issue of blood, either out of the ordinary course or at the usual period, her

Abstract written by a model

A woman with an issue of blood is kept in a separate place of infirmity while she continues to see blood. She must remain three paces from worshippers of Mazda and fifteen paces from fire, water, and consecrated bundles of baresma, then is washed with gômêz and water.

The woman is kept in a separate building and must remain three paces from the faithful, fifteen paces from the fire and water, and fifteen paces from the consecrated bundles of baresma. She remains in the place of infirmity as long as she continues to see blood and is washed with gômêz and water.
