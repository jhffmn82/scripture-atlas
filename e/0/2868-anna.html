Anna

Abstract written by a model

Anna is the wife of Tobit, the narrator, and mother of Tobias. She earns wages weaving cloth in the women's chambers, and when her husband doubts the kid she was given, she sharply rebukes him. Her devotion centers on her son: she weeps when Tobias is sent away, valuing him above money, until Tobit reassures her. Later she watches the path for him, spots his return, runs to embrace him, and says she is ready to die, weeping together.

Anna in Tobit

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Anna, the narrator's wife, is restored to him and weaves cloth in the women's chambers, sending the work back to its owners. She is paid wages and given a kid, and when her husband doubts her and demands its return she rebukes him, asking where his alms and righteous deeds are and saying he and his works are known.

Chapter 5

Anna weeps when Tobias is sent away, calling him the staff of their hand and saying money should be as refuse compared to their child. She says what the Lord has given them is enough, and she stops weeping after Tobit assures her that Tobias will return safe with a good angel.

Chapter 11

Anna sits watching the path for her son and sees him coming, telling Tobit that Tobias approaches with the man who went with him. She runs to Tobias, falls on his neck, and says she is ready to die now that she has seen him, and they both weep.
