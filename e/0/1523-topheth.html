Topheth

place · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: The valley of Slaughter, the high places of Topheth, the valley of the son of Hinnom, ‘Topheth’

Abstract written by a model

Topheth is a place in the valley of the son of Hinnom where Judah built high places to burn their sons and daughters, something the LORD says he never commanded. In Chapter 7 he declares it will be renamed the valley of Slaughter, a burial ground until no room remains. In Chapter 19 he repeats the burial judgment, vows to make Jerusalem and its defiled royal houses like Topheth, and Jeremiah returns from Topheth, where he was sent to prophesy.

Topheth is in one work on this site, The Book of Jeremiah. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 7

Topheth, in the valley of the son of Hinnom, is where the people of Judah built high places to burn their sons and daughters in the fire, which the LORD says he did not command and which never came into his mind. He declares it will be renamed the valley of Slaughter, where they will bury the dead until there is no more room.

Chapter 19

Topheth is a place where the LORD says they will bury until there is no place left to bury. He will make Jerusalem as Topheth, and the defiled houses of Jerusalem and of the kings of Judah will be like the place of Topheth. Jeremiah comes from Topheth, where the LORD had sent him to prophesy.
