Abstract written by a model

The great multitude is an uncountable crowd drawn from every nation, tribe, people and language. They stand before the throne and the Lamb in white robes holding palm branches, proclaiming salvation. They came out of great suffering and washed their robes in the Lamb's blood. They now serve God day and night in his temple, sheltered by him and shepherded by the Lamb to life-giving waters.

Chapter 7

The great multitude is a crowd no one could count, from every nation, tribe, people, and language, standing before the throne and the Lamb in white robes with palm branches. They cry out that salvation belongs to God and the Lamb. The elder explains that they came out of the great suffering and washed their robes in the Lamb's blood. They serve God day and night in his temple and will never again hunger, thirst, or be scorched by the sun or heat.
