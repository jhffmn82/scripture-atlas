Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Judges, the lords of the Philistines promise Delilah silver to learn Samson's secret, bring the cords and money, and gather to sacrifice to Dagon before the house falls on them. In The First Book of Samuel, the princes of the Philistines distrust David and insist that Achish send him away, fearing he would become an adversary in battle.
