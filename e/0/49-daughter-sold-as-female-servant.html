Abstract written by a model

A daughter sold as a female servant, whose release differs from male servants. If her master is displeased with her, she may be redeemed but never sold to foreigners; if married to his son, she is treated as a daughter. If denied food, clothing or marital rights, she goes free without payment.

Chapter 21

A daughter sold as a female servant does not go out as male servants do. If her master is displeased with her, she is to be redeemed and cannot be sold to a foreign people; if married to his son she is treated as a daughter.
