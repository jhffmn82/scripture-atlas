III. Ardibehist Yast.

The Airyaman prayer smites the strength of the creatures of Angra Mainyu, the Yâtus, and Pairikas. It is called the greatest, fairest, firmest, most victorious, and best-healing of spells, and it drives away sickness, Death, Daêvas, and other harmful forces.
