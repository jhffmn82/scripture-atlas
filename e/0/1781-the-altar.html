It is meant for burnt offerings and blood sprinkling, and after seven days of cleansing and atonement it is consecrated.

Chapter 43

The altar is given detailed measurements: a base, a lower ledge, a greater ledge, an upper altar of four cubits, four horns, a twelve-by-twelve cubit hearth, and a fourteen-by-fourteen ledge, with its steps facing east. Its ordinances cover offering burnt offerings and sprinkling blood on it. It is to be cleansed and made atonement for over seven days, after which it is consecrated.
