Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, salvation comes from God alone, who is the psalmist's rock and salvation. In Psalms, salvation is near those who fear God, and the psalmist asks the LORD to grant it. In Psalms, the LORD's salvation is worked by his right hand and holy arm and shown to the nations. In The Acts of the Apostles, salvation through Jesus is proclaimed as remission of sins and justification for everyone who believes. In The Acts of the Apostles, the salvation of God is sent to the nations, and they will listen. In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, salvation comes to those who confess that Jesus is Lord, believe God raised him from the dead, and call on the Lord's name. In Paul’s Letter to the Ephesians, salvation through faith means believers are saved by grace as God's gift rather than by their own works. In 1 Timothy, salvation is desired by God for all people, who are to come to full knowledge of the truth.

and 5 more works

In 2 Timothy, salvation and holy calling are given in Jesus Christ according to God's purpose and grace. In Titus, salvation comes from God's kindness, love, and mercy through regeneration's washing and the Holy Spirit's renewal via Jesus Christ. In The Letter to the Hebrews, salvation was first spoken through the Lord, confirmed by those who heard, and testified by God through signs, wonders, powers, and Holy Spirit gifts. In 1 Peter, salvation is ready to be revealed in the last time and is received by believers through faith. In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), salvation is obtained through meeting the true Guru, love, devotion, humility, and repeating God's name.
