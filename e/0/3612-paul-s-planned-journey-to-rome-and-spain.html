Paul's planned journey to Spain, passing through Rome. He must first go to Jerusalem to deliver the contribution. He then hopes to visit the Roman believers, enjoy their company, and be helped onward, asking prayer that he come in joy and find rest.

Chapter 15

Paul plans to go to Spain and visit the Roman believers on the way, hoping to be helped onward by them after enjoying their company. This follows first going to Jerusalem to deliver the contribution. He asks for prayer that he may come in joy through God's will and find rest with them.
