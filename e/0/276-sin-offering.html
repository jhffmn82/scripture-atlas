sin offering

topic · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a sin offering, one male goat, one male goat for a sin offering, the sin offering

Abstract written by a model

In Leviticus, the sin offering is a sacrifice scaled to the sinner’s means, with specified animal or flour offerings and prescribed handling of the bird’s blood. In Numbers, the sin offering is one male goat required on each holy day to make atonement for the people, in addition to the continual burnt offering and, on the tenth day, the sin offering of atonement.

Abstract written by a model

The sin offering is a sacrifice scaled to the sinner's means: a female lamb or goat; if poor, two turtledoves or young pigeons; if poorer still, a tenth of an ephah of fine flour without oil or frankincense. The bird's blood is sprinkled on the altar's side and drained at its base.

Chapter 5

The sin offering is a female lamb or goat, or if the person is poor, one of two turtledoves or young pigeons, or if poorer still, a tenth of an ephah of fine flour. The flour has no oil or frankincense. The bird's blood is sprinkled on the altar's side and drained at its base.
