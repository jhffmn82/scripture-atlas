Chapter 18

The LORD's altar had been thrown down, and Elijah repairs it. He builds it with twelve stones, digs a trench around it, arranges the wood and the cut bull, and has water poured over it three times until the trench is filled. The LORD's fire then consumes the offering, wood, stones, and dust.
