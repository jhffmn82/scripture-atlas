wisdom and knowledge

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: knowledge, love of knowledge, wisdom, wisdom-tricks

Abstract written by a model

Wisdom and knowledge, developed after ceremonies, music, charity and duty, protect great thieves through government instruments and restrictions. Pursued without Tao and used in sophistical argument, they create artificial distinctions and confusion as people chase unknown things and dispute matters beyond them.

CHAPTER X.

Wisdom and knowledge are portrayed as instruments that protect great thieves, create artificial distinctions, and bring confusion to the world. The unit says that love of knowledge lies at the bottom of great confusion because people pursue what they do not know and dispute what they do not excel in.
