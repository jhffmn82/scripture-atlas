His Augustness Fire-Shine

person · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: [the elder brother], his elder brother, the elder brother

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Fire-Shine, a prince born when the fire burned most fiercely, is ancestor of the Hayabito, Dukes of Ata. Blessed with sea-luck, he catches broad- and narrow-finned things and owns the original fish-hook. After exchanging luck with his younger brother Fire-Subside, he demands the lost hook back and becomes poorer, guarding and respectfully serving Fire-Subside day and night.

His Augustness Fire-Shine is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Sect.

His Augustness Fire-Shine is the first child born in the fire, arriving when the fire is burning most fiercely. He is identified as the ancestor of the Hayabito, Dukes of Ata.

Sect.

His Augustness Fire-Shine is a prince whose luck is on the sea, where he catches things broad of fin and narrow of fin. He exchanges luck with his younger brother but later demands the original fish-hook back after Fire-Subside loses it.
