Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the righteous are targets of the wicked, but the LORD examines them and promises the upright in heart that they will see his face. In Proverbs, the righteous are portrayed as a group whose righteousness delivers them from death and brings blessing, remembrance, life, stability, fulfilled desires, the LORD's favor, flourishing, and refuge in death.

upright and righteous people in Proverbs

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where upright and righteous people first appears

Abstract written by a model

The upright and righteous are portrayed as a group whose righteousness delivers them from death. They are blessed and well remembered; their mouths are a spring of life, their labor leads to life, and their desires are granted. They stand firm forever and are never removed. They fear the LORD, walk in uprightness, enjoy good will, and their tent flourishes. The evil bow before them, and in death they have refuge.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

The righteous are blessed and remembered well, and righteousness delivers them from death. Their mouths are a spring of life, their labor leads to life, their desires are granted, and they stand firm forever and will never be removed.

Chapter 14

The upright walk in uprightness and fear the LORD, enjoy good will, and their tent will flourish. The evil bow down before the righteous, and in death the righteous has a refuge. Righteousness is said to exalt a nation.
