Abstract written by a model

In Surah 15: The Rock (Al-Hijr), hell is the promised place for those who follow Iblis, with seven gates and an appointed portion for each. In Surah 39: The Groups (Az-Zumar), hell receives disbelievers in troops after its gates are opened and becomes their hapless journey's end.
