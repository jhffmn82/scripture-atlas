Paul's farewell speech at Miletus, addressed to the elders of the assembly at Ephesus. He recounts his humble, tearful service in Asia and his teaching publicly and house to house, declares himself clean of all blood, warns that wolves will come, entrusts the elders to God, and quotes Jesus on giving.

Chapter 20

Paul's speech at Miletus recounts his service in Asia with humility, tears, and trials, his public and house-to-house teaching, and his testimony to Jews and Greeks of repentance and faith. He declares himself clean of all men's blood, warns of wolves and men who will draw disciples away, entrusts the elders to God and the word of his grace, and cites his own labor and the Lord Jesus' saying, 'It is more blessed to give than to receive.'
