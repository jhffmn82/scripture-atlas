Abstract written by a model

Godliness with contentment is a teaching presented across two chapters. Chapter 4 urges exercising oneself toward godliness, which outweighs bodily exercise, holding value in all things and promise for life now and to come. Chapter 6 calls it great gain, unlike treating godliness as profit, with contentment shown as satisfaction with food and clothing.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

Godliness is something to exercise oneself toward. It is contrasted with bodily exercise, which has some value, while godliness has value in all things and holds the promise of the life now and of that to come.

Chapter 6

Godliness with contentment is declared to be great gain, in contrast to those who treat godliness as a means of gain. Contentment is illustrated by being satisfied with food and clothing, since nothing was brought into the world and nothing can be carried out.
