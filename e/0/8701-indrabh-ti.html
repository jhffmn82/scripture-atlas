Indrabhûti

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: monk Indrabhûti

Abstract written by a model

Indrabhûti, of the Gautama gotra, was the oldest monk and disciple of the Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra, a Ganadharas who instructed five hundred Sramanas. On the night of his master’s death he cut his tie of friendship for his master and attained Kevala; he died after Mahâvira’s Nirvâna.

Indrabhûti is in one work on this site, Kalpa Sutra. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
