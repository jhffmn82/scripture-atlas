Idolatrous worshippers who choose their own ways and delight in abominations. Their sacrifices are likened to killing a man or offering pig's blood. Because they ignored the LORD's call, he chooses their delusions and brings their fears, and those eating abominable things end together.

Chapter 66

Their sacrifices are likened by the LORD to killing a man, breaking a dog's neck, offering pig's blood, and blessing an idol, because they chose their own ways and delight in abominations. The LORD will choose their delusions and bring their fears on them, since they did not answer or listen when he called. Those who sanctify themselves to go to the gardens and eat pig's meat, abominable things, and the mouse will come to an end together.
