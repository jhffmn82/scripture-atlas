Abstract written by a model

Gabael, son and brother of Gabrias, lives at Rages of Media and holds ten talents of silver Tobit left in trust. Tobit fears he may be dead, but when Raphael lodges with him and presents the handwriting, Gabael produces the sealed bags, hands them over, and travels with Raphael to the wedding feast.

Chapter 4

Gabael, called the son of Gabrias, is the man at Rages of Media with whom Tobit left money in trust. Tobit tells Tobias about this deposit of ten talents of silver.

Chapter 9

Gabael is in Rages of Media and is to hand over Tobias's money. After Raphael lodges with him and gives him the handwriting, he brings out the sealed bags and gives them to Raphael. He then rises early and goes with Raphael to the wedding feast.
