Gu Sou

person · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: father

Abstract written by a model

In Mencius, Book IV (Li Lou), Gu Sou was brought by Shun’s complete service to find delight in what was good, transforming the kingdom and establishing fathers and sons in their respective duties. In Mencius, Book V (Wan Zhang), Gu Sou was Shun’s father, whom Shun honoured and nourished with the whole kingdom despite Gu Sou’s lack of accord with him, displaying highest filial piety. In Mencius, Book VII (Jin Xin), Gu Sou was presented in a hypothetical case as having murdered a man, after which Shun would privately carry him into concealment and live with him along the sea-coast.
