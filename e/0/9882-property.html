property

Also called: personal property, possessions, possessions and relations, wealth, wealth, cattle, and relations

Abstract written by a model

Property is a possession monks should abandon: sought for liberation or clung to by men, it brings harm and increases iniquity, especially through deadly pleasure-seeking acts.

property is in one work on this site, Sutrakritanga (Sûtrakritâṅga), Book I. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Ninth Lecture, Called the Law.

Property is something a monk should abandon and should not desire when it could lead to harm. Those who cling to property increase their iniquity.
