Abstract written by a model

attempts_to_rob the Colossian recipients (you) [through philosophy and vain deceit] These unnamed people are warned against as ones who might delude the recipients with persuasive speech, rob them through philosophy and vain deceit, judge them over food and festivals, or rob them of their prize. One such person is described as dwelling in things he has not seen, vainly puffed up by his fleshly mind, and not holding firmly to the Head.
