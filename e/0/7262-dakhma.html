Dakhma

place · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Dakhmas, those Dakhmas

Abstract written by a model

The Dakhma is a place on the earth where worshippers of Mazda lay corpses, with the eyes toward the sun, for exposure. Rain falls on its exposed corpse and unclean remains. Fiends gather and feed there, bringing pollution and disease. Ahura Mazda urges people to pull down Dakhmas, and worshippers seek one when a dog or man dies under a house or hut.

Dakhma is in one work on this site, Vendidad (Vendîdâd), Fargards I-VIII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Fargard V.

The Dakhma is the place where worshippers of Mazda lay down the dead with the eyes toward the sun. It is rained on while the corpse and unclean remains are exposed, and water flows down to it.

Fargard VII.

A Dakhma is a place where corpses are laid and where fiends gather, feed, and cause pollution and disease. Ahura Mazda urges people to pull down Dakhmas, and says that doing so can remit sins.

Fargard VIII.

The worshippers of Mazda are instructed to look for a Dakhma when a dog or man dies under a house or hut.
