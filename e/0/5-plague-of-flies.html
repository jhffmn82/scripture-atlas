Chapter 8

The LORD announces swarms of flies that will fill the houses of the Egyptians and the ground, while Goshen is spared. The flies grievously corrupt the land, Pharaoh bargains, and the LORD removes them at Moses' prayer so that not one remains, but Pharaoh again refuses to let the people go.
