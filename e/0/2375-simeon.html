Abstract written by a model

Simeon is a son of Jacob and brother of Dinah and Levi, marked by violence. With Levi he kills the males of a city, including Hamor and Shechem, and Jacob rebukes them. Joseph later binds him as a hostage in Egypt until the youngest brother comes, and Jacob laments him. Finally Jacob condemns his and Levi's violent anger and declares they will be divided and scattered in Israel.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 34

Simeon, a brother of Dinah, takes his sword with Levi, comes upon the unsuspecting city on the third day, and kills all the males, including Hamor and Shechem. Jacob rebukes him along with Levi for the consequences.

Chapter 42

Simeon is taken from among the brothers by Joseph and bound before their eyes, to remain in Egypt as a hostage until the youngest brother is brought. Jacob later laments that Simeon is no more.

Chapter 49

Jacob names Simeon as a brother of Levi, with swords that are weapons of violence. He says they killed men in anger and hamstrung cattle in self-will, curses their fierce anger, and says he will divide and scatter them in Israel.
