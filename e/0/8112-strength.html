strength

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Strength, strong, the strong

Abstract written by a model

Strength is often spoiled by weakness and accompanies death. It is false when the mind makes the vital breath burn, and should be mourned; when things become strong, they grow old, contrary to the Tao. The strong is overcome by the weak, does not lead to conquest when relied upon, and has its place below.
