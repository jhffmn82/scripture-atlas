Herodias

person · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: her, her mother

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Herodias is Herod's brother Philip's wife, prompts her daughter to ask for John's head on a platter, and receives it. In The Good News According to Mark, Herodias opposes John the Baptizer and wants him dead, then instructs her daughter to request John's head and receives it.

Chapter 6
