Jehu in The Second Book of Kings

author unknown · 560 to 539 BC · the work’s page · read it where Jehu first appears

Abstract written by a model

Jehu, son of Jehoshaphat and grandson of Nimshi, is anointed king over Israel through a young prophet's message from the LORD. He drives furiously to Jezreel, kills Joram with an arrow, has Ahaziah struck, and has Jezebel thrown down and trampled. He then destroys Ahab's house, Ahaziah's brothers, and Baal's worshipers, removing Baal worship from Israel. Yet he follows Jeroboam's sins and does not wholeheartedly walk in the LORD's law. He reigns twenty-eight years from Samaria, is buried there, and is succeeded by his son Jehoahaz.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 9

Jehu is anointed king over Israel, acknowledged by the captains who spread their cloaks and blow the trumpet, and conspires against Joram. He drives furiously to Jezreel, kills Joram with an arrow, has Ahaziah pursued and struck, and has Jezebel thrown down and trampled. He then eats and drinks, orders her buried, and cites the LORD's word spoken by Elijah the Tishbite when told her body was mostly gone.

Chapter 10

Jehu writes letters to Samaria's leaders, orders the heads of Ahab's sons delivered, and kills the rest of Ahab's house, Ahaziah's brothers, and all of Baal's worshipers through a feigned sacrifice. He destroys Baal out of Israel but does not depart from the golden calves of Jeroboam or walk in the LORD's law. He reigns twenty-eight years, is buried in Samaria, and is succeeded by his son Jehoahaz.

Chapter 22

Jehu is identified as the one whom the LORD had anointed to cut off Ahab's house. While executing judgment on it, he found and killed the princes of Judah and the sons of Ahaziah's brothers who served Ahaziah, and Ahaziah was brought to him and killed.
