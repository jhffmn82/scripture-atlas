slave

Also called: a slave, servant, slaves

Abstract written by a model

In The City (Al-Balad), a slave is someone whom the Ascent requires a person to free. In The Table-Talk of the Prophet Mohammad (Part 1), slaves are described as brothers whose owners must provide them with food and clothing and not burden them beyond their power; a person who beats or slaps a slave without fault must free the slave, and ill-treatment of a slave prevents entry into Paradise.
