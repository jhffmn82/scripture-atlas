woman with child

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: woman

Abstract written by a model

A woman with child who delivers a still-born child is confined in a clean, dry enclosure of a Mazdean house, thirty paces from fire, water, and baresma and three from the faithful. She is unclean for three nights, follows prescribed food, and drinks gômêz mixed with ashes for three, six, or nine cups. After three nights she is cleaned with gômêz and water; she remains separated for nine nights and later washes her body and clothes.

Fargard V.

A woman with child who brings forth a still-born child must be placed in the cleanest and driest part of a Mazdean house, thirty paces from fire, water, and baresma and three paces from the faithful. She must drink gômêz mixed with ashes, remain on prescribed food and in confinement, and later wash her body and clothes with gômêz and water.

Fargard VII.

If she gives birth to a still-born child, she is placed in an enclosure thirty paces from the fire, water, and consecrated baresma, and three paces from the faithful. She remains there for three nights, follows a prescribed diet, and is then cleansed with gômêz and water.
