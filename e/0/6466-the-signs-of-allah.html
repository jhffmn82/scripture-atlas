the signs of Allah

topic · 5 works · 11 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Allah's signs, His signs, Our signs, Portents, a portent, a token, indications of Allah, one of the portents, portents, portents for a folk whose faith is sure, portents for a people who have sense, portents for believers, signs, the portents

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), the signs of Allah encompass creation, changing night and day, ships, rain, revived earth, beasts, winds, clouds, As-Safa, and Al-Marwah. In Surah 6: The Cattle (Al-An'aam), the portents belong to Allah, and belief will not benefit a soul after a portent arrives if it had not believed before or earned good through works. In Surah 30: The Romans (Ar-Room), Allah's signs include creation, human relationships, natural phenomena, provision, and resurrection as portents for those who reflect, know, heed, understand, or believe. In Surah 31: Luqman, the signs of Allah include the creation of the heavens and earth, the movement of night and day, the sun and moon, ships on the sea, and the raising of the dead. In Surah 45: Crouching (Al-Jaathiya), the portents of Allah appear in the heavens, earth, human creation, beasts, night and day, provision, and the ordering of the winds as signs for believers and people who reflect or have sense.
