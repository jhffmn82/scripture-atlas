Chapter 11

Rehoboam builds and fortifies fifteen cities in Judah and Benjamin, including Bethlehem, Tekoa, Lachish and Hebron. He places captains in them with stores of food, oil and wine, and puts shields and spears in each. Later he disperses some of his sons to every fortified city with abundant food.
