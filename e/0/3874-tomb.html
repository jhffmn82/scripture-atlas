Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Jesus' tomb is visited by the women, its stone is rolled away by an angel, and the women are shown that it is empty. In The Good News According to Mark, Jesus' tomb is where Joseph of Arimathaea lays his body, and the women later find its stone rolled back and hear that he has risen. In The Gospel According to Luke, Jesus' tomb is found empty, with its stone rolled away and only the strips of linen remaining. In The Gospel According to John, Jesus' tomb is found empty, with its stone taken away and linen cloths left inside; Mary sees two angels there.
