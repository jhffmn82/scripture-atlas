disorderly brothers (those who walk in rebellion)

group · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: If anyone is not willing to work, any man, anyone, every brother who walks in rebellion, him, some who walk among you in rebellion, those who are that way

Abstract written by a model

Some among the brothers are reported to walk in rebellion, not working at all but being busybodies, contrary to the tradition received from Paul. They are commanded and exhorted to work with quietness and eat their own bread, and anyone who disobeys the letter is to be noted and shunned so that he may be ashamed, while still being admonished as a brother.
