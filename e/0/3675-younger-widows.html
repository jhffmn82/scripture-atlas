Abstract written by a model

Younger widows are a group the text says should be refused enrollment as widows. It warns they may grow wanton against Christ, learn idleness, gossip and meddle from house to house, and some have already turned to Satan. It advises they marry, bear children, and rule households.

Chapter 5

Younger widows are to be refused enrollment, because they may grow wanton against Christ and desire to marry, incurring condemnation for rejecting their first pledge. They are said to learn idleness, going from house to house as gossips and busybodies, and some have already turned away after Satan. The text desires that they marry, bear children, and rule the household, giving the adversary no occasion for insult.
