Chapter 4

The census counts Levite men aged thirty to fifty who enter service in the Tent of Meeting: 2,750 Kohathites, 2,630 Gershonites, and 3,200 Merarites. Moses, Aaron, and the princes carry it out according to the LORD's commandment, for a total of 8,580.
