Abstract written by a model

People are led by a superior’s example while their affairs are diligently attended to. When the superior loves propriety, righteousness, and good faith, they become reverent, submissive, and sincere; people from all quarters come bearing children. In Wei, the people are numerous, and should first be enriched and then taught.
