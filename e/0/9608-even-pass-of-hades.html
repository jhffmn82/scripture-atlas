Even Pass of Hades

Also called: Even-Pass-of-Hades, Pass of Hades, the Even Pass of Hades

Abstract written by a model

A pass of Hades where His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites repels pursuers with three peaches and blocks the passage with a thousand-draught rock; the Great Deity pursues Great-Name-Possessor and calls commands from afar. Later called Ifuya-Pass in the Land of Idzumo.

The Even Pass of Hades is where His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites reaches the base and repels his pursuers with three peaches. He blocks it with a thousand-draught rock, and it is later called the Ifuya-Pass in the Land of Idzumo.
