Chapter 11

Creeping things that creep on the earth are an abomination and are not to be eaten, whether they go on the belly, on all fours, or have many feet. The weasel, rat, great lizards, gecko, monitor lizard, wall lizard, skink, and chameleon are named as unclean, and touching them dead brings uncleanness until evening.
