Abstract written by a model

Men of antiquity conferred benefits on the people when their wishes were realized; when not, they cultivated personal character, attended to virtue in poverty and solitude, and became illustrious, later advancing to dignity in prosperity.

Chapter 9

When the men of antiquity realized their wishes, they conferred benefits on the people. When they did not realize their wishes, they cultivated their personal character and became illustrious in the world.
