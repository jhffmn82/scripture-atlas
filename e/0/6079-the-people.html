the people in Mencius, Book VII (Jin Xin)

Mencius · the work’s page · read it where the people first appears

Abstract written by a model

The people are the most important element in a nation and can make a sovereign by being gained. They become rich through well-cultivated fields and light taxes, and virtuous when pulse and grain are abundant. They suffer under multiple exactions, but welcome benevolent correction, as shown by their pleasure at Tai Jia’s banishment and return after he became virtuous.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 23

The people may become rich when their fields are well cultivated and their taxes are light. They cannot live without water and fire, and when pulse and grain are abundant, they will be virtuous.

Chapter 31

The people were much pleased when Tai Jia was banished to Tong and were again much pleased when he was brought back after becoming virtuous.

Part II / Chapter 1 .. Chapter 38

The people are the most important element in a nation. They suffer when rulers demand multiple forms of exaction at once, and they may welcome a ruler who performs benevolent correction.
