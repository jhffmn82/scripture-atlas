Abstract written by a model

Jephthah the Gileadite is a mighty man of valor, son of Gilead and a prostitute. Driven out by his half-brothers, he lives in Tob with outlaws until the elders recall him as head and chief of Gilead. He argues Israel's case to the Ammonite king, vows to the LORD, defeats Ammon, and carries out the vow when his only daughter meets him. He then fights Ephraim, judges Israel six years, and is buried in Gilead's cities.

Chapter 11

Jephthah, son of Gilead and a prostitute, is driven out by his half-brothers, lives in Tob with outlaws, and is recalled by the elders to be head and chief of Gilead. He argues Israel's case to the Ammonite king, makes a vow to the LORD, defeats Ammon, and then, upon his only daughter coming out to meet him, tears his clothes and carries out his vow.

Chapter 12

Jephthah is threatened by the men of Ephraim for not calling them to fight Ammon, and he replies that they did not save him when he called. He gathers the men of Gilead and fights Ephraim. He judges Israel six years, then dies and is buried in the cities of Gilead.
