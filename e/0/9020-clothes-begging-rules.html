Abstract written by a model

Four rules for begging clothes cover specified cloth qualities, well-inspected clothes, under or upper garments, and unwanted left-off robes. The second lesson addresses robe inspection and prohibits borrowing a robe; acceptable pure clothes received under these rules may be accepted.

Fifth Lecture, Called Begging of Clothes. / First Lesson. .. Second Lesson.

There are four rules: begging for specified qualities of cloth, asking for well-inspected clothes, begging for an under or upper garment, and begging for a left-off robe unwanted by others. Clothes received under these rules may be accepted when they are pure and acceptable.
