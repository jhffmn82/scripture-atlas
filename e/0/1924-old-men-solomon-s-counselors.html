old men (Solomon's counselors)

group · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: old men, the old men

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Kings, the old men advised Rehoboam to serve the people and answer kindly so they would serve him forever, but he abandoned their counsel. In The Second Book of Chronicles, the old men advised Rehoboam to be kind, please the people, and speak good words so they would be his servants forever, but he abandoned their counsel.
