Chapter 36

God promises to sprinkle clean water on Israel and cleanse them from all their filthiness and idols. He will give them a new heart and new spirit, remove the stony heart, put his Spirit within them, and cause them to walk in his statutes. This is done for the sake of his holy name, and they will afterward remember their evil ways and loathe themselves.
