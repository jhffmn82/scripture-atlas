women

Also called: the women, women (whom ye marry), women a fortune, women unto whom ye have gone in, your women, your women unto whom ye have gone in

Abstract written by a model

Women are presented through legal, moral, and marital provisions. They receive marriage portions as free gifts and may freely remit part of them. They are assigned a legal inheritance share from parents and near kindred; when more than two, they collectively receive two-thirds. They may possess a fortune from their own earnings. Good women are described as obedient and as guarding in secret what Allah has guarded. The records also set out a punishment for women found guilty of lewdness: four witnesses must testify to the allegation, after which the women are confined to houses until death or until Allah appoints another way. Across the records, women appear first as recipients and holders of property, then as subjects of legal accusation and confinement, and finally within expectations of obedience and private guardianship.
