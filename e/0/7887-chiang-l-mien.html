Chiang Lü Mien went to see Chi Ch'ê,

Chiang Lü Mien tells Chi Ch'ê that he instructed the Prince of Lu to be decorous and thrifty, advance the public-spirited and loyal, and avoid partialities. He admits uncertainty about his doctrine and asks Chi Ch'ê for instruction.
