Princess-of-Great-Food

Also called: Deity Princess-of-Great-Food, the Deity Princess-of-Great-Food, the Deity who had been killed

Abstract written by a model

Princess-of-Great-Food is a deity who makes dishes from dainty things and offers them to High-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness. She takes them from her body, is killed by him, and silkworms, rice-seeds, millet, beans, and barley are born from her body.

Princess-of-Great-Food is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Sect.

She takes dainty things from her nose, mouth, and fundament, makes them into dishes, and offers them to High-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness. He kills her, and silkworms, rice-seeds, millet, small beans, barley, and large beans are born from her body.
