water

object · 3 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: purifying water, the water which ye drink

Abstract written by a model

In The Believers (Al-Muminoon), water is sent down from the sky in measure and given lodging in the earth, while Allah is able to withdraw it; it is also associated with the refuge given to the son of Mary and his mother. In Surah 25: The Criterion (Al-Furqaan), water is sent as purifying water from the sky to give life to dead land and provide drink for many beasts and human beings, while a bar is set between two seas, one sweet and palatable and the other saltish and bitter. In Surah 56: The Inevitable (Al-Waaqia), water is drunk by human beings and comes from rainclouds by the Lord of the Worlds' causing, and He could make it bitter if He willed.
