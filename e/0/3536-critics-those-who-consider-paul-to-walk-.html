Abstract written by a model

These critics consider Paul and his companions to be walking according to the flesh, and they say his letters are weighty and strong but his bodily presence is weak and his speech despised. Paul tells such a person to consider that he is in deed when present what he is in word by letters when absent. He also tells them he is ready to avenge disobedience once the Corinthians' obedience is full.
