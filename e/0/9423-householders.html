Also called: (householders), a householder

Abstract written by a model

Householders are men in a demeritorious condition with great desires, undertakings, and possessions; across East, West, North, and South, they kill living beings or acquire property directly, through others, or by consent, from love and hatred.

I say: here in the East, West, North, and South there are some men, viz. Âryas,

Householders are described as killers of living beings and acquirers of property. They may kill or acquire objects themselves, have others do so, or consent to others doing so, and they commit sins from love and hatred.
