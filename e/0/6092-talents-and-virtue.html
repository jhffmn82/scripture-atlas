Abstract written by a model

Talents and virtue concern how a prince should recognize and properly support worthy people. Bai Li Xi exemplifies their value: as chief minister of Qin, he made his prince distinguished and memorable. Extensive information may qualify someone as a teacher, but virtue and talents require appropriate promotion, not improper summons.

Talents and virtue are attributed to Bai Li Xi because he became chief minister of Qin and made his prince distinguished throughout the kingdom and worthy of being remembered by future ages.

Chapter 6 .. Chapter 9

A prince who is pleased with virtue and talents should promote or support their possessor in the proper way. A person with extensive information may be a teacher, while a person with virtue and talents should not be summoned in an inappropriate manner.
