Also called: Princes and kings, princes and kings

Abstract written by a model

Princes and kings receive from the One, the Tao, the model for all to follow. Though grand and high, they would decay without moral sway. Therefore, they call themselves Orphans, Men of small virtue, and Carriages without a nave, preferring a coarse appearance like an ordinary stone.
