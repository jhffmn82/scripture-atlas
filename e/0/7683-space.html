space

concept · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Space, great Space, the limits of space

Abstract written by a model

In Uttaradhyayana Sutra, space is a formless thing with divisions and indivisible parts, filling world and Non-world and ever without beginning and end. In Chuang Tzŭ, great Space is infinite, limitless existence, all-receptive and open to movement in every direction, beyond judgments of small or great.

Space is infinite, with no limit to movement in any direction. The wise person looks into space without treating the small as too little or the great as too much.
