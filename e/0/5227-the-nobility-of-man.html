Abstract written by a model

The nobility of man is an office-based nobility including gong, qing, and da fu; it may follow cultivating Heaven’s nobility, but pursuing it while discarding the higher nobility is extreme delusion.

Part I / Chapter 1 .. Chapter 16

The nobility of man consists of offices such as being a gong, a qing, or a da fu. It may come after the cultivation of the nobility of Heaven, but pursuing it while discarding the higher nobility is described as extreme delusion.
