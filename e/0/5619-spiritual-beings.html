Abstract written by a model

Spiritual beings are everywhere, entering all things and displaying unseen powers. They influence people’s fasting, purification, and rich sacrificial dress, appearing above and beside worshippers. Before them, the Ruler presents his institutions without doubt, a display that he knows Heaven.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

Spiritual beings display powers that people cannot see or hear, yet they enter into all things and are present everywhere. They cause people to fast, purify themselves, and dress richly for sacrifices, then seem to be above and beside their worshippers.

Chapter 29

The Ruler presents himself with his institutions before spiritual beings, and no doubts about them arise. This is described as showing that he knows Heaven.
