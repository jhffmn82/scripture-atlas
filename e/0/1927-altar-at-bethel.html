Chapter 13

The altar at Bethel is where Jeroboam stood to burn incense and is the object of the man of God's cry. It is prophesied that a son of David's house named Josiah will sacrifice the high-place priests on it and burn men's bones there. As the promised sign, it is split apart and its ashes are poured out.
