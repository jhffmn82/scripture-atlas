Sect. LVIII.—Emperor Ko-Sho.

Her Augustness Princess Yoso-taho was the younger sister of Oki-tsu-yoso, ancestor of the Chiefs of Wohari. She wedded His Augustness Mi-ma-tsu-hiko-kawe-shine and bore two august children.
