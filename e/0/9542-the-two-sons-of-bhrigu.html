Abstract written by a model

Bhrigu’s two dear bachelor sons remember former austerities, reject worldly and Vedic pursuits, become virtuous Sramanas seeking liberation, and attain perfection with their parents and a royal couple.

Fourteenth Lecture.

The two sons of Bhrigu remember their former birth, penance, and self-control. They reject Vedic study, sacrifices, sons, wealth, and pleasures, enter the monastic order, and reach the end of misery.
