Abstract written by a model

A man incurs grave sin by knowingly teaching a faithful person a foreign wrong creed, harming dogs or pregnant women, or causing a damsel’s miscarriage through an old woman and drugs. He shares the miscarriage sin with them, but if he conceives a child with a damsel, he must support her until birth.

Fargard XV.

A man makes himself a Peshôtanu by teaching a faithful person a foreign wrong creed or law, giving harmful bones or hot food to a dog, smiting or frightening a pregnant bitch so that she dies, or having intercourse with a pregnant woman when mischief follows and she dies. A man who causes a damsel's miscarriage shares the sin with the damsel and the old woman, and a man who conceives a child with a damsel must support her until the child is born.
