Abstract written by a model

A principle, affirmed by the Lord GOD against claims of unfairness, that the LORD judges each person by his own ways. Past righteousness cannot save a sinner, nor past wickedness condemn one who turns. Warnings ignored leave blood on the hearer; the watchman who warns is free. All are called to turn and live.

Chapter 33

The chapter teaches that each person is judged by his own ways: past righteousness does not save one who sins, and past wickedness does not condemn one who turns and does what is lawful and right. Those who hear a warning and ignore it bear their own blood, and a watchman who warns has delivered his soul. The LORD calls everyone to turn, turn from evil and live, and rejects the claim that his way is unfair.
