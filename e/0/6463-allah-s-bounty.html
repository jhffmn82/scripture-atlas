Allah's bounty

thing · 3 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Infinite Bounty, bounty of the Lord, the bounty, the bounty of Allah, the bounty of thy Lord

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 57: The Iron (Al-Hadid), Allah's bounty includes forgiveness, the Gardens, mercy, and rewards, and is given by Allah to whom He wills. In Surah 62: Friday (Al-Jumu'a), Allah's bounty is given to whom Allah wills, described as infinite, and sought by believers after Friday prayer. In Surah 93: The Morning Hours (Ad-Dhuhaa), the bounty of the Lord is to be spoken of.
