Chapter 12

Paul says he knows a man in Christ who fourteen years earlier was caught up into the third heaven, into Paradise, whether in or out of the body being known only to God. The man heard unspeakable words which it is not lawful for a man to utter, and Paul says he will boast on behalf of such a one.
