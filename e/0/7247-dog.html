Abstract written by a model

Dog is an animal whose death can require a year of fallow ground and purification, especially when it dies under a house or hut. Its body may incur penalties when placed on the ground. Ritual yellow or white forms are led along a corpse-bearing way to drive away Drug Nasu, and corpse-eating dogs may smite Nasu.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Fargard VI.

Dogs are included among those whose deaths require the ground to lie fallow for a year. Dead dogs are also included in the bones and bodies whose placement on the ground incurs penalties.

Fargard VIII.

A dog that dies under a house or hut is treated like a dead body requiring removal and purification procedures. A yellow dog with four eyes or a white dog with yellow ears is led three, six, or nine times along a corpse-bearing way to drive away the Drug Nasu, and corpse-eating dogs may smite the Nasu on a corpse.
