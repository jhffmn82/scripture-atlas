the soft

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: soft, softness, what is soft and tender

Abstract written by a model

The soft is a guarded secret of strength and accompanies life. It holds a place above, overcoming the hard; water exemplifies this, though people struggle to put the principle into practice.

the soft is in one work on this site, The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
