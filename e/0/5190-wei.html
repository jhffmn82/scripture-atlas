Wei

place · 4 works · 4 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: State of Wei, Wei State, that State

Abstract written by a model

In Chuang Tzŭ, Wei State is a state containing resident Ai T'ai T'o, suffering poor government as people die and corpses cover the land. In The Analects of Confucius, Book IX (Tsze Han), Wei was the place from which the Master returned. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XIII: Tsze-Lu, Wei is described as having a government that is a brother to the government of Lu. In Mencius, Book V (Wan Zhang), Wei was a state in which Confucius lived with Yan Chou You; Confucius was dissatisfied in Wei and left it.
