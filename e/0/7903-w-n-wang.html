Wên Wang

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Wên Wang, called a sage, seeks to employ the old man of Tsang in government and invokes a supposed dream to satisfy ministers and the vulgar. After the administration succeeds, he appoints the old man Grand Tutor and asks about extending government over the empire.
