Abstract written by a model

The good wife is portrayed as a great gift given to those who fear the Lord and as her husband's richest treasure. Chapter 26 describes her bringing him joy and peace, delighting him with grace, and strengthening him with knowledge, while her silence, modesty, and self-control are prized and her beauty in an orderly home is likened to the rising sun. Chapter 36 calls her a help meet and pillar of support, whose cheering beauty and kind, humble speech set her husband apart.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 26

A good wife is called a great gift given to those who fear the Lord. She gives her husband joy and peace, delights him with her grace, and strengthens him with her knowledge. Her silence, modesty, and self-control are valued above measure, and her beauty in a well-organized home is likened to the sun rising, a lamp on the holy lampstand, and golden pillars on a silver base.

Chapter 36

A wife is described as a man's richest treasure, a help meet for him and a pillar of support. A woman's beauty cheers the countenance, and if kindness and humility are on her tongue her husband is unlike other men, though one daughter is better than another.
