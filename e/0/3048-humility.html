humility in The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus

author unknown · about 179 BC · the work’s page · read it where humility first appears

Abstract written by a model

Humility is commended in Chapter 3: practiced even in daily business, it brings love from an acceptable man and favor before the Lord; the greater one is, the more he should humble himself, since the Lord is glorified by the lowly.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Humility is commended as the way to be loved by an acceptable man and to find favor before the Lord. The greater a person is, the more he should humble himself. The Lord's power is great and he is glorified by the lowly.

Christian virtues in Paul’s Letter to the Colossians

Paul · about AD 62 · the work’s page · read it where Christian virtues first appears

Abstract written by a model

Christian virtues are qualities readers are told to put on: compassion, kindness, lowliness, humility, perseverance, with mutual bearing and forgiveness as Christ forgave. Love crowns them as the bond of perfection; peace, thankfulness, and Christ's word are commended.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The readers are to put on a heart of compassion, kindness, lowliness, humility, and perseverance, bearing with and forgiving each other as Christ forgave them. Love is placed above all of these as the bond of perfection. Peace of God, thankfulness, and the word of Christ dwelling richly are also commended.
