Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Angra Mainyu is the wicked being who makes the dragon Dahâka as a mighty destructive force against the corporeal world, settlements, and Asha’s homes. In Yasna, Angra Mainyu is a wicked evil being filled with death who has evil creatures and whose encounter and displacement are sought. In Afrinagan, Gahs and Miscellaneous Fragments, Angra Mainyu is contrasted with Ahura Mazda as a ruler over realms and is commanded to be hidden beneath the earth. In Vendidad (Vendîdâd), Fargards I-VIII, Angra Mainyu is all death who counter-creates harmful beings, sins, conditions, the serpent in the river, winter, and brands on mortal bodies. In Vendidad (Vendîdâd), Angra Mainyu is the mischievous, deadly, guileful evil-doing Daêva and maker of the evil world who creates Khrafstras and afflicts mortals. In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, Angra Mainyu is a fiend and Daêva who creates Azi Dahâka and the strongest Drug against the material world and is ultimately destroyed. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Angra Mainyu is the Evil Spirit and destructive, lying Evil One who creates an evil creation and opposes the good principle.

Angra Mainyu in Vendidad (Vendîdâd)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Angra Mainyu first appears

Abstract written by a model

Angra Mainyu is a mischievous, deadly, guileful, evil-doing Daêva and ruffian, described as the maker of the evil world. He creates the evil world, makes Khrafstras, and by witchcraft brings disease, sickness, rottenness, and infection upon mortals. He opposes Zarathustra: he sends the Drug to destroy him and later urges him to renounce the good law, but Zarathustra refuses and names sacred things as weapons against Angra Mainyu and his creation. Prescribed words smite him down and drive him from houses through the whole holy world, from the defiled, and from fire, water, earth, living things, faithful people, heavenly lights, boundless light, and all good things made by Mazda. The afflictions he creates are also driven away.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Fargard X.

Angra Mainyu is driven away by the prescribed words from the house, borough, town, land, the bodies of those defiled by the dead, their leaders, and the whole holy world. The words are also said to smite down Angra Mainyu.

Fargard XI.

Angra Mainyu is called mischievous and is driven away from the fire, water, earth, cow, tree, faithful man, faithful woman, stars, moon, sun, boundless light, and all good things made by Mazda.

Fargard XIX.

Angra Mainyu sends the Drug to destroy Zarathustra and later urges him to renounce the good law of the worshippers of Mazda. Zarathustra refuses and declares that the sacred Word, the sacred mortar, the sacred cup, and the Haoma are weapons against Angra Mainyu and his creation.

Fargard XX.

Angra Mainyu created sickness, rottenness, and infection by witchcraft against the bodies of mortals.

Fargard XXI.

Angra Mainyu has created disease, rottenness, and infection against the bodies of mortals by his witchcraft. These afflictions are driven away.

Fargard XXII.

Angra Mainyu is called the ruffian and the deadly one. He looked at Ahura Mazda and wrought many diseases by his witchcraft against the bodies of mortals.

Abstract written by a model

Angra Mainyu is a fiend, Daêva, and all death: the worst-lying of all Daêvas and an enemy of the good principle. He creates Azi Dahâka and the strongest Drug against the material world to destroy the world of the good principle. Ahura Mazda’s names protect people from him; Asha-Vahista makes him flee in fear. He is afflicted and is destroyed by those who truly do God’s foremost wish.

Angra Mainyu in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Angra Mainyu first appears

Abstract written by a model

Angra Mainyu is the Evil Spirit, also named the Evil One, a destructive, lying, evil-doing being who is all death and full of death without Glory. He breaks into the creation of good holiness and makes an evil creation, including Daêvas, Drug, and Azi Dahâka, powerful fiendish forces against the material world and the good principle. His Khrafstras are called accursed, foul, and most abominable. He struggles over the Glory and is associated with lust, derision, idolatry, rejection of the poor, and other harmful acts. Yet his malice is destroyed by the Fravashis, preserving flowing waters and growing plants. At Zarathustra’s birth he flees from earth, acknowledges Zarathustra can smite and daunt him, and is smitten by Ahuna Vairya and burned by Asha-Vahista through Zarathustra. His influence is ultimately to be kept away.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

XXII.

Angra Mainyu is named as the Evil One and as a fiend who is all death. He breaks into the creation of good holiness, but the Fravashis destroy his malice so that the waters continue flowing and the plants continue growing.

XV. Râm Yast.

Angra Mainyu is described as the Evil Spirit whose creation Ahura Mazda seeks to smite. Vayu promises that Angra Mainyu will not harm the worshipping Zarathustra.

XVII. Ashi Yast.

Angra Mainyu is described as evil-doing, all death, and the creator of the strongest Drug against the material world. He flees from the earth at Zarathustra’s birth and says that Zarathustra can smite and daunt him.

XIX. Zamyâd Yast.

Angra Mainyu created Azi Dahâka as a powerful fiendish Drug against the material world and the world of the good principle. He is named as the Evil One struggling over the Glory.

XX. Vanant Yast.

Angra Mainyu is described as most abominable, and the Khrafstras are identified as his accursed and most foul creatures.

Yast XXII.

Angra Mainyu, described as the lying one, says not to question the wicked person who has just traveled the dreary way between body and soul.

XXIV. Vîstâsp Yast.

Angra Mainyu is the Evil Spirit, and his influence is to be kept away. His evil is associated with lust, derision, idolatry, rejection of the poor, and other harmful acts.
