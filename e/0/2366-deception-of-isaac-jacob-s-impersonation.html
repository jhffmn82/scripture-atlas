Chapter 27

Rebekah plans and Jacob carries out the impersonation using Esau's clothes, goat skins on his hands and neck, and the claim that the LORD gave him quick success. Isaac notes the voice is Jacob's but accepts the hairy hands and the smell of the clothing as Esau's. The deceit is exposed when Esau arrives, and Isaac says Jacob came with deceit.
