Abstract written by a model

In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, all things are produced and nourished by the Tao, harmonised by the Breath of Vacancy, completed according to their conditions, and honour the Tao through spontaneous tribute. In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, things pass through activity, return to their original state, grow strong and old, depart from the Tao, and come to an end.

all things in The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics

Lao-Tse · the work’s page · read it where all things first appears

Abstract written by a model

All things are produced and nourished by the Tao. They leave behind Obscurity, embrace Brightness going forward, and are harmonised by the Breath of Vacancy. Each receives forms according to its nature and is completed according to the circumstances of its condition. Without exception, they honour the Tao and exalt its outflowing operation through spontaneous tribute. From early life to death, they follow a pattern: supple or soft at first, then firm, strong, dry, or withered.
