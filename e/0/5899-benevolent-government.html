benevolent government

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a benevolent government, good government, good government (the kingdom's government), royal government

Abstract written by a model

Benevolent government is royal government based on love and protection of the people. It begins by enabling people to nourish the living and bury the dead without resentment, through proper resource management and education. It uses light taxes, levies, punishments, and fines; careful cultivation of fields; support for families; and teaching filial piety, fraternal respectfulness, sincerity, and truthfulness. When good government is lacking, the king does not answer what should be done. If duke Mu practices it, the people will love him, those above them, and die for their officers.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Mencius calls the condition in which people can nourish the living and mourn or bury the dead without resentment the first step of royal government. Proper resource management and education are presented as means to achieve it.

Chapter 5

Benevolent government involves sparing use of punishments and fines, light taxes and levies, careful cultivation of fields, and the cultivation of filial piety, fraternal respectfulness, sincerity, and truthfulness.

Chapter 7

Royal government is presented as government based on love and protection of the people. Mencius says it should regulate the livelihood of the people, support their families, and provide education in filial and fraternal duties.

Chapter 6

Mencius asks what should be done when the kingdom lacks good government. The king does not directly answer the question.

Chapter 12

Mencius says that if duke Mu practices benevolent government, the people will love him and all above them and will die for their officers.
