Abstract written by a model

A river flowing eastward from beneath the temple threshold, deepening every thousand cubits from ankle-deep to too deep to cross. It descends through the Arabah into the Dead Sea, healing its waters and giving life to every creature, though its swamps and marshes stay salt.

Chapter 47

The river flows eastward from the temple and deepens over each thousand cubits measured, from ankle-deep to knee-deep to waist-deep, until it is too deep to walk through. It will go down into the Arabah and into the sea, healing the waters and bringing life to every creature wherever it comes, though its swamps and marshes will remain salt.
