His Augustness Anaho

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: August Child Anaho, Emperor an-Kō, Heavenly Sovereign, Prince Anaho, The Heavenly Sovereign, august child Anaho

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Anaho, a child of Emperor in-Giyō, ruled the Empire from the palace of Anaho at Isonokami. He raised and then disbanded an army against King Karu after a promise to deliver him. Later, persuaded by Grandee of Ne’s slander, he slew King Oho-kusaka and was beheaded in sleep by King Ma-yowa.

His Augustness Anaho is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Sect. CXXXVII.—Emperor in-Giyō (Part I—Genealogies). .. Sect. CXLIII.—Emperor in-Giyō (Part VII.—Death of Prince Karu and Princess So-Tohoshi).

His Augustness Anaho was one of Emperor in-Giyō's children and later ruled the Empire. He raised an army against King Karu, but disbanded it after the Noble Oho-mahe Wo-mahe promised to secure and present King Karu.
