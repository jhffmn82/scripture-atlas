Vigayaghôsha

Abstract written by a model

Vigayaghôsha, a Vêda-versed Brâhmana and priest in Benares, initially refuses alms to Gayaghôsha, then accepts and praises his teaching, enters the monastic order, annihilates Karman through self-control and penance, and reaches the highest perfection.

Twenty-Fifth Lecture.

Vigayaghôsha is a Brâhmana versed in the Vêdas who offers a sacrifice at Benares and initially refuses alms to Gayaghôsha. After questioning Gayaghôsha and hearing his teaching, he assents to it, praises his knowledge, enters the order, and annihilates his Karman through self-control and penance.
