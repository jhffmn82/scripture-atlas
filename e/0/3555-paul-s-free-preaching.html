Abstract written by a model

Paul's free preaching of God's Good News to the Corinthians, humbling himself so they might be exalted. He accepted support from other assemblies and Macedonian brothers so he burdened no one, and vows to continue this in Achaia, denying boasters any opportunity.

Chapter 11

Paul preached God's Good News to the Corinthians free of charge, humbling himself so that they might be exalted. He took wages from other assemblies to serve them, and brothers from Macedonia supplied his need so he was no burden to anyone. He vows to continue this in the regions of Achaia, to cut off opportunity from those who want to boast.
