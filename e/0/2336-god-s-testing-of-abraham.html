Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, God's testing of Abraham commands him to offer Isaac as a burnt offering, stops him through an angel when he obeys, and is followed by an oath of blessing. In Surah 37: Those drawn up in Ranks (As-Saaffaat, the sacrifice of Abraham's son is seen in Abraham's dream, fulfilled after both surrender, with the son ransomed by a tremendous victim. Abstract written by a model

At Moriah, God tests Abraham by commanding him to offer his only son Isaac as a burnt offering; Abraham obeys fully until an angel stops him, then an oath of blessing follows.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 22

The test begins when God tells Abraham to offer his only son Isaac as a burnt offering, and Abraham obeys without withholding him. It ends when the angel stops him, declaring that God now knows Abraham fears him. The test is followed by the oath of blessing given because Abraham obeyed God's voice.
