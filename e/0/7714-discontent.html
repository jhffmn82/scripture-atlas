Discontent

Abstract written by a model

Discontent asks Complacency, seeks wealth and reputation, and argues that wealth enables good and power while pleasure makes life worthwhile beyond health alone.

Tzŭ Chang asked Man Kou Tê,

Discontent argues that wealth enables a person to do good, exert power, and borrow the courage, wisdom, and virtue of others. Discontent also maintains that pleasures are instinctively appreciated and questions whether life devoted to health without pleasure would be worthwhile.
