Abstract written by a model

Holofernes' head is the severed trophy of the enemy captain. Judith's maid places it in her food bag; at Bethulia's gate Judith shows it as proof of his death, and the people worship God. It is displayed before the assembly, making Achior faint, then hung on the city wall, alarming the Assyrians.

Chapter 13

Holofernes' head is given by Judith to her maid after it is cut off and is put in her bag of food. At the gate of Bethulia, Judith takes it from the bag and shows it to the people as proof that the enemy captain is dead. The people are amazed and worship God.

Chapter 14

Judith has the head hung on the battlement of the wall, and it is displayed in the assembly of the people, where it makes Achior faint. At morning it is hanged upon the wall, and the Assyrians' discovery that it has been taken from the body sparks their alarm.
