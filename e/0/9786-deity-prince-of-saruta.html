Deity Prince of Saruta

person · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Bottom-Touching-August-Spirit, Bubble-Bursting-August-Spirit, Great Deity Prince of Saruta, Gurgling-up-August-Spirit, Male Deity the Prince of Saruta, an Earthly Deity named the Deity Prince of Saruta, that Deity, the Deity Prince of Saruta

Abstract written by a model

The Deity Prince of Saruta is an Earthly, male Deity Prince, son of the Heavenly Deities, dwelling at Azaka. At Heaven’s eight-forking road, he offers himself as vanguard to His Augustness Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty and respectfully serves in that role. While fishing, a hirabu shell-fish catches his hand and he drowns in the brine; this leads to three names: Bottom-Touching-August-Spirit, Gurgling-up-August-Spirit, and Bubble-Bursting-August-Spirit.

The Deity Prince of Saruta dwells at Azaka and goes fishing. A hirabu shell-fish catches his hand, and he drowns in the sea, where he receives three names associated with sinking, gurgling water, and bubbles.
