Abstract written by a model

In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, all things under heaven sprang from Tao, existing and named. In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, all under heaven considers Tao most valuable because it is obtainable by seeking and may let the guilty escape guilt; yet obtaining all under heaven requires giving oneself no trouble, since taking trouble prevents possessing it. In Mencius, Book VII (Jin Xin), the feelings of filial affection for parents and respect for elders belong to all under heaven.
