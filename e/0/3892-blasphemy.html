blasphemy

topic · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: You blaspheme, blaspheme, blasphemies

Abstract written by a model

In The Good News According to Mark, blasphemy is forgivable except against the Holy Spirit, which brings eternal condemnation. In The Gospel According to John, blasphemy is the charge the Jews make against Jesus for making himself God.
