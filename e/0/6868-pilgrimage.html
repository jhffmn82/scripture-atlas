pilgrimage

event · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the pilgrimage

Abstract written by a model

Pilgrimage is a period governed by sacred duties and restrictions. While believers are on pilgrimage, game and hunting on land are forbidden. Hunting and eating fish from the sea remain permitted. The Sacred House, Sacred Month, offerings, and garlands are appointed in connection with these duties. Once believers leave the sacred territory, they may hunt again.
