Abstract written by a model

Peter's readers are a group who share a precious faith with the apostles and have received God's exceedingly great promises, having escaped worldly corruption caused by lust. In the first letter they are told to add diligently to their faith, make their calling and election sure, and heed the prophetic word. In the second letter they are stirred to remember the prophets and apostles, live in holiness and godliness, look for new heavens and a new earth, be found in peace, without defect and blameless, avoid the error of the wicked, and grow in grace and knowledge.

Peter’s Second Letter / Chapter 1

Peter's readers are those who have obtained a like precious faith with the apostles. They are told to add diligently to their faith, to make their calling and election sure so they will never stumble, and to heed the prophetic word. Peter says they already know these things and are established in the present truth.

Chapter 3

They receive a second letter meant to stir up their sincere minds, and they are told to remember the prophets' words and the apostles' commandment. They are urged to live in holiness and godliness, to be diligent to be found in peace, without defect and blameless, to beware of being carried away by the error of the wicked, and to grow in grace and knowledge.
