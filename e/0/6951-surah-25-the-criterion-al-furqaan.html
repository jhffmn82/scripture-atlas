Surah 25: The Criterion (Al-Furqaan)

document · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns Allah's revelation of the Criterion to Muhammad as a warning to the peoples and describes the objections of the disbelievers. It contrasts the fate of those who deny the Hour, reject the Qur'an, and worship other gods with the rewards promised to the righteous and the faithful slaves of the Beneficent. It recounts Allah's signs in creation, the missions of earlier messengers, and the punishment of past peoples. It also describes the qualities of Allah's faithful slaves, including humility, night worship, moderation, avoidance of falsehood, acceptance of revelations, repentance, and righteous work.
