Chapter 2

Moses looks around, sees no one, kills an Egyptian who was striking a Hebrew, and hides him in the sand. The next day a Hebrew he rebukes refers to it, and Moses realizes it is known. Pharaoh hears of it and seeks to kill Moses, who flees to Midian.
