Chapter 10

Alexander, son of Antiochus, took Ptolemais in the 160th year and reigned there. He appointed Jonathan high priest and friend, fought Demetrius, married Ptolemy's daughter Cleopatra at Ptolemais, and later honored Jonathan with a gold buckle and Ekron. He grieved and returned to Antioch when Demetrius, son of Demetrius, arrived.
