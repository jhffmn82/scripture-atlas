idolaters

group · 4 works · 11 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: a folk, a folk who know not, the idolaters, the others, them, they

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida), the idolaters are described as among mankind's most vehement in hostility toward believers. In Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba), the idolaters are addressed under treaty conditions, with some treaties retained, breakers and attackers fought, exclusion from the Inviolable Place of Worship, and protection given to protection-seekers. In Surah 37: Those drawn up in Ranks (As-Saaffaat), the idolaters reject the call that there is no Allah save Allah, mock the messenger, cling to other gods, deny the truth, follow their fathers in error, and face questioning and doom. In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), the idolaters are warned for withholding the poor-due, disbelieving in the Hereafter, and ascribing rivals to Allah, and are called to the straight path and forgiveness.

Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba)

The idolaters are subject to treaty conditions and proclamations of freedom from obligation. Those who repent, establish worship, and pay the poor-due are to be left free, while those who break pledges or attack the religion are to be fought; those seeking protection are to be protected and conveyed safely.
