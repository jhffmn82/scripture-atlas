Zedekiah

person · 2 works · 8 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Kings, Zedekiah makes iron horns, predicts victory over the Syrians, strikes and mocks Micaiah, and is told he will hide in an inner room. In The Second Book of Chronicles, Zedekiah appears as both Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah, who falsely prophesied Ahab's victory and struck Micaiah, and a king installed by Nebuchadnezzar who reigned in Jerusalem, did evil, and rebelled.

Zedekiah in The Second Book of Chronicles

author unknown · about 424 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Zedekiah first appears

Abstract written by a model

Zedekiah appears under two figures in the records: son of Chenaanah, who made iron horns and falsely prophesied Ahab's victory over the Syrians, struck Micaiah, and was told he would hide in an inner room; and a king installed by Nebuchadnezzar in place of Jehoiachin at age twenty-one. He reigned eleven years in Jerusalem, did evil, stayed stiff-necked, refused to humble himself before Jeremiah, and rebelled despite his oath.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 18

Zedekiah son of Chenaanah made iron horns and prophesied in the LORD's name that Ahab would push the Syrians until they were consumed. He struck Micaiah on the cheek and sarcastically asked which way the LORD's Spirit went from him. Micaiah replied that Zedekiah would see on that day when he went into an inner room to hide.

Chapter 36

Zedekiah is made king by Nebuchadnezzar in place of his brother Jehoiachin and reigns eleven years from age twenty-one. He does evil, does not humble himself before Jeremiah, rebels against Nebuchadnezzar despite his oath by God, and hardens his heart against turning to the LORD.
