Chapter 13

Joakim is a great rich man of Babylon, married to Susanna, with a beautiful garden next to his house. The Jews come to him because he is more honorable than all others, and the elders-judges frequent his house. After Susanna is vindicated, he joins her parents and kindred in praising God.
