Chapter 13

The people will pass away like morning mist, dew, chaff in the whirlwind, and smoke. The LORD will lurk and tear them like a lion, leopard, and bear, send a drying east wind, and bring the sword, infant killing, and ripping open of pregnant women on Samaria.
