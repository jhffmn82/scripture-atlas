involves plunder (Amalekite livestock spared) [Saul and the people spared the best livestock instead of destroying it] Saul's failure to destroy everything as commanded is the LORD's grounds for rejecting him as king. Samuel calls rebellion like witchcraft and stubbornness like idolatry, and says that because Saul rejected the LORD's word, the LORD rejected him. Saul's confession and plea do not reverse it, and the torn robe symbolizes the kingdom being torn from him.
