Abstract written by a model

Deity Eight-Fold-Thing-Sign-Master, son of Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land, pursues birds and catches fish at Cape Miho, supports yielding the Central Land of Reed-Plains, then conveys its presentation and disappears after overturning his boat.

Sect.

Deity Eight-Fold-Thing-Sign-Master was summoned from Cape Miho and told Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land that he would respectfully present the land to the august child of the Heavenly Deity. He then disappeared after overturning his boat and clapping his heavenly departing hands in a fence of green branches.
