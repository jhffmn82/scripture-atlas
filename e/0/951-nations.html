Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, all nations and peoples are summoned to praise and extol the LORD. In Psalms, the nations and their peoples are targets of vengeance and punishment by the saints. In Psalms, the nations rage and kingdoms move, but God ends wars and is exalted among them. In Psalms, the nations are called to know God's way and salvation, sing for joy, and be governed with equity. In Psalms, the nations hear the LORD's glory and salvation proclaimed, ascribe glory and strength to him, and face his equitable judgment. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, sovereignty transfers from nation to nation, while the Lord uproots and overthrows nations. In The Book of Joel, the nations are accused of wickedness and summoned to war and judgment in the valley of Jehoshaphat. In The Book of Micah, the nations are portrayed with divided fates: some stream to the LORD's mountain, while others assemble against Zion and face judgment.

and 7 more works

In The Book of Zechariah, many nations join themselves to the LORD and become his people, while plundering nations become plunder. In The Gospel According to Matthew, all the nations are gathered before the Son of Man and separated as a shepherd separates sheep from goats. In Surah 45: Crouching (Al-Jaathiya), the nations are seen crouching and summoned to their records to be requited for their deeds. In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, the nations war against Israel, then turn against one another and fall into their own pit. In The Second Book of Nephi, nations receive promises and judgments, flow to the Lord's mountain, are summoned for battle, and are ruled by an oppressor. In The Second Book of Nephi, nations include Jews, receive God's word in writing, face judgment, and become witnesses through their written testimonies. In The Second Book of Nephi, all nations, kindreds, tongues, and people have the Lord God's work and the restoration of his people made known to them.

Abstract written by a model

The nations are a collective of peoples portrayed with divided fates. Many will stream to the LORD's mountain to learn his ways, be judged, and abandon war. Yet each walks in its own gods' name, and some assemble against Zion to defile her, not knowing the LORD's counsel. He gathers them like sheaves for threshing, Zion beating many in pieces. nations in The Second Book of Nephi

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where nations first appears

Abstract written by a model

Nations are central to promises, judgment, gathering, conflict, and Zion. They may be kept from the land of promise while the people brought from Jerusalem keep commandments, but will overrun it if those people reject the Holy One of Israel. In the last days all nations flow to the mountain of the Lord’s house, where the Lord judges and rebukes them, ending their warfare. The Lord of Hosts summons nations from far by an ensign; they come swiftly prepared for battle and carry away prey. Nations are also ruled by an oppressor, yet receive word that the Lord has founded Zion, where the poor trust.
