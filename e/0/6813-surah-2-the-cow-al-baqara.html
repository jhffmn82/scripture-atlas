Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara)

Abstract written by a model

Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara) presents Allah’s guidance, revelation, power, judgment, and commands for mankind. It distinguishes believers, disbelievers, and those who only claim belief; recounts Adam and his wife in the Garden; and follows the Children of Israel through covenant, disobedience, the Exodus, Pharaoh’s drowning, Moses’ command to sacrifice a cow, and Saul’s campaign in which David slew Goliath by Allah’s leave. Abraham’s leadership, the raising of the House with Ishmael, and prayers connected with the Inviolable Place of Worship establish worship and the qiblah. Believers are told to establish worship, pay the poor-due, give charity, fast, seek help through steadfastness and prayer, eat what is lawful, keep treaties, compete in good works, and act patiently and kindly. The text gives guidance on righteousness, pilgrimage, warfare, retaliation, marriage, divorce, nursing, waiting periods, inheritance, charity, usury, and debts. It warns against concealed revelation, inherited error, associating rivals with Allah, sin, aggression, injustice, mischief, covenant-breaking, the Fire, and rejection of faith. It ends with believers affirming faith and seeking forgiveness, mercy, and victory.
