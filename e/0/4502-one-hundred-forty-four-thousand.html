Abstract written by a model

The group is a body of 144,000 bondservants of God, sealed on their foreheads and drawn from every tribe of the children of Israel, 12,000 from each of twelve tribes. Later they stand with the Lamb on Mount Zion bearing his and his Father's name. Redeemed from the earth by Jesus, they alone can learn the new song, follow the Lamb wherever he goes, and are virgins, first fruits, blameless, with no lie in their mouth.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

The bondservants of God are sealed on their foreheads, and their number is heard to be 144,000. They are sealed out of every tribe of the children of Israel, 12,000 from each of twelve tribes.

Chapter 14

The 144,000 stand with the Lamb on Mount Zion, bearing his name and his Father's name on their foreheads. They alone can learn the new song sung before the throne, and they are described as redeemed from the earth, virgins not defiled with women, followers of the Lamb, first fruits to God and the Lamb, with no lie in their mouth and blameless.
