Abstract written by a model

The abridger condenses Jason of Cyrene's five books into one, aiming for brevity to attract readers, aid recall, and benefit all. He describes the labor as sweat and sleeplessness, likens himself to a banquet host and decorator, leaves detail to the original historian, and then begins narrating.

Chapter 2

The abridger says he will condense Jason's five books into one, to attract readers, help those who wish to recall the story, and benefit all. He describes the work as sweat and sleeplessness, compares himself to a banquet host and a house decorator, and leaves exact detail to the original historian. He closes by saying a long prologue before an abridgement would be foolish and begins the narration.
