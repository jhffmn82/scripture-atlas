Abraham's servant swears the oath, travels to the city of Nahor with ten camels, and prays at the spring for a sign, which Rebekah fulfills. He gives her gold gifts, worships the LORD, tells her family his story, secures their consent, gives gifts to Rebekah and her family, and brings her to Isaac, to whom he reports all he had done.
