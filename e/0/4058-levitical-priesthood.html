Levitical priesthood

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Many, They, men as high priests who have weakness, people who die, the Levitical priesthood, the sons of Levi, those high priests

Abstract written by a model

The Levitical priesthood is made up of the sons of Levi, who receive tithes from their brothers by law. Its priests are mortal, hindered by death, installed without an oath, and weak, offering daily sacrifices first for their own sins. It could not bring perfection, so another priest was needed, and it is shown inferior to Melchizedek's.

Chapter 7

The sons of Levi who receive the priest's office have a commandment to take tithes from their brothers according to the law, but they die and are hindered from continuing. Perfection did not come through it, so another priest was needed, and it is shown to be inferior to Melchizedek's priesthood. Its high priests have weakness and must offer sacrifices daily, first for their own sins.
