Chapter 20

They include those beheaded for the testimony of Jesus and the word of God, and those who did not worship the beast or its image or receive its mark. They sit on thrones with judgment given to them, live and reign with Christ for a thousand years, and are blessed, holy, and priests of God and Christ, with the second death having no power over them.
