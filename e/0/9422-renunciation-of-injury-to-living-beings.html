is_good good renunciation [when injury to the large class of movable beings is abandoned] Renunciation of injury to living beings is examined as a vow that may be limited to movable beings, limited by purpose or spatial boundaries, or extended to all beings. Gautama teaches that restricting it to one class cannot end injury altogether because beings change forms through rebirth.
