Abstract written by a model

Learning is presented as necessary for the proper exercise of benevolence, knowledge, sincerity, straightforwardness, boldness, and firmness. Confucius also says that the Odes can stimulate the mind, support self-contemplation, teach sociability, regulate resentment, and teach duties toward one's father and prince.
