all things in The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics

Lao-Tse · the work’s page · read it where all things first appears

Abstract written by a model

All things emerge, grow, and pass through their processes in natural development. They neither claim ownership nor expect reward. Heaven and earth deal with them as dogs of grass, while the Great Tao produces them, receives their obedience, and presides as they return to their root and disappear. If princes and kings maintained the Tao, all things would transform themselves; without desire, they would go right of their own will. The sage helps this natural development.
