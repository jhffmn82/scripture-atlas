plague of the firstborn

event · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: all the firstborn in the land of Egypt shall die, death, death of the firstborn, destruction of the firstborn, one form of death, one more plague, single stroke, yet one more plague

Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, the plague of the firstborn is the final plague on Pharaoh and Egypt, announced for midnight when the LORD goes through Egypt; it kills all Egyptian firstborn, causing a great cry and Pharaoh to thrust Israel out. In The Wisdom of Solomon, the death of the firstborn is a deadly night strike on Egypt's firstborn, delivered by God's word like a sharp-sworded warrior at midnight; it kills servant and master, commoner and king alike, with preceding dreams and fears explaining the deaths, and Egyptians confessing the people as God's children.

Abstract written by a model

A deadly night strike on Egypt's firstborn, delivered by God's word like a sharp-sworded warrior at midnight in silence. It killed servant and master, commoner and king alike; preceding dreams and fears explained the deaths, and Egyptians confessed the people as God's children.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 18

It struck in the midst of peaceful silence, when night was half spent, as God's word descended as a warrior with a sharp sword, touching the sky while standing on the earth. Servant and master, commoner and king suffered the same doom. Dreams and fears came before it so the victims would know why they were dying.
