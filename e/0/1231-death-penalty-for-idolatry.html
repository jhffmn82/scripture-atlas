Chapter 13

The chapter prescribes death for the prophet or dreamer who urges other gods, for the secret enticer who is a relative or friend, and for the inhabitants of a city that has been drawn away. The enticer is stoned with the accuser's hand first, and the city is struck by the sword and burned. The stated purposes are to remove evil from among the people and to make all Israel hear and fear.
