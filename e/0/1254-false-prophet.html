Abstract written by a model

A false prophet speaks presumptuously in the LORD's name what He did not command, or speaks in other gods' names. Such a prophet is identified by unfulfilled predictions, which show the LORD did not speak, so Israel need not fear him; the penalty is death.

Chapter 18

A prophet who speaks presumptuously in the LORD's name something the LORD has not commanded, or who speaks in the name of other gods, shall die. The test given is that if the thing he speaks does not follow or happen, the LORD did not speak it, and Israel need not fear him.
