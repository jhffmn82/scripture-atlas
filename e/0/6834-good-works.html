good works

topic · 3 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: The good deed, doeth good, good, good deeds, good things, righteous work

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), good works include obeying Allah, giving charity, worshiping, submitting to Him, and doing good voluntarily, and those who do good find their reward with their Lord. In Surah 25: The Criterion (Al-Furqaan), righteous work accompanies repentance and belief, leads to Allah's forgiveness, transforms evil deeds into good deeds, and brings promised reward and a lasting place. In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), good deeds are rewarded by Allah, and believers who do good works receive an enduring reward; good deeds can repel evil and turn enmity into friendship.
