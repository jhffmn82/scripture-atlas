Abstract written by a model

Manasseh, Joseph's firstborn son born in Egypt, is named for God making Joseph forget his toil. Jacob claims him as his own like Reuben and Simeon, foretelling he will be a great people, though his younger brother will be greater.

Chapter 48

Manasseh is Joseph's firstborn son, born in Egypt, and Jacob declares him to be his own like Reuben and Simeon. Jacob lays his left hand on Manasseh's head, and says he will become a people and be great, though his younger brother will be greater.
