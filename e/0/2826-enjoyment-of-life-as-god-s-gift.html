enjoyment of life as God’s gift

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: eat and drink, enjoy good in all his labor, his portion, his works, joy of his heart, labor, labor and enjoyment, nothing better, rejoice, the gift of God, to eat and to drink

Abstract written by a model

Enjoyment of life is presented as God's gift: eating, drinking, rejoicing, and enjoying the good of one's labor. It is called a person's proper portion, especially since no one can see what comes after him. First, the narrator questions labor's profit yet affirms rejoicing and doing good. Later, the power to enjoy riches and labor is God's gift, keeping the person joyfully occupied so he seldom broods over life's days.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The narrator questions the profit of labor but affirms that people should rejoice, do good, eat and drink, and enjoy good in all their labor, which is the gift of God. He calls rejoicing in one's works a person's portion, since no one can see what will be after him.

Chapter 5

The speaker sees it as good and proper to eat, drink, and enjoy the good of one's labor during the days of life God gives, which is one's portion. The power to eat of riches, take one's portion, and rejoice in labor is called the gift of God, who keeps the person occupied with joy so that he does not often brood over the days of his life.
