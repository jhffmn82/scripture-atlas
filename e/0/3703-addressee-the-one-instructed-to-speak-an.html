Abstract written by a model

The addressee is told to say what fits sound doctrine and to exhort the younger men and servants. He is to be an example of good works, showing integrity, seriousness, incorruptibility, and unconditionable speech, so that any opponent is ashamed. He is to speak, exhort, and reprove with all authority and let no one despise him.
