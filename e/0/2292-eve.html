In 1 Timothy, Eve is described as having been deceived and consequently falling into disobedience.

woman (Eve) in Genesis

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where woman (Eve) first appears

Abstract written by a model

Eve is the first woman, made by the LORD God from a rib taken from the man while he slept, and called 'woman' because she was taken out of Man. She is a helper comparable to him. She eats the fruit she finds desirable, gives some to her husband, and blames the serpent's deceit. She is told her pain in childbirth will be multiplied and her husband will rule over her. The man names her Eve as mother of all the living. She later bears Cain, Abel, and Seth, the last given in place of Abel, whom Cain killed.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The woman was made by the LORD God from the rib he took from the man while the man slept, and was brought to him. The man said she would be called 'woman' because she was taken out of Man; she and the man were both naked and not ashamed.

Chapter 3

The woman answers the serpent, restating God's command and adding that they must not touch the fruit. She sees that the tree is good for food, a delight to the eyes, and desirable for wisdom, eats its fruit, and gives some to her husband. She says the serpent deceived her, is told she will have greatly multiplied pain in childbirth and that her husband will rule over her, and is named Eve by the man because she will be the mother of all the living.

Chapter 4

Eve, wife of the man, conceives and gives birth to Cain, saying she has gotten a man with the LORD's help, and then to Abel. She later bears Seth, saying God has given her another child instead of Abel, whom Cain killed.
