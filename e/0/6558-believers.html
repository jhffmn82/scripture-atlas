believers

group · 8 works · 22 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: believer, the believers, the people of duty, the righteous, those who believe, those who keep from evil, those who kept their duty, whoso believeth, whoso believeth in Allah, ye who believe

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 43: Ornaments of gold (Az-Zukhruf), the believers are those who believed Allah's revelations and surrendered themselves, and they are told to enter the Garden without fear or grief. In Surah 44: The Smoke (Ad-Dukhaan), the people of duty are promised a secured place amid gardens and watersprings, with silk garments, fair companions, fruits in safety, and salvation from hell. In Surah 46: The Dunes (Al-Ahqaf), those who believe accept the revelation and follow the right path, and those who say their Lord is Allah and walk aright will have no fear or grief and will enter the Garden. In Surah 52: The Mount (At-Tur), those who kept their duty dwell in gardens and delight, happy with what their Lord has given them and because He has warded hell-fire's torment from them. In Surah 59: The Exile (Al-Hashr), the believers helped ruin the houses of the People of the Scripture, received guidance about spoil, welcomed earlier fugitives, and were the subject of prayers for forgiveness and freedom from rancour. In Surah 64: Mutual Disillusion (At-Taghaabun), believers are urged to believe in Allah, His messenger, and His revealed light, and those who believe and do right receive forgiveness and enter the Gardens. In Surah 65: Divorce (At-Talaaq), believers are exhorted to keep their duty to Allah and those who believe and do good works are brought from darkness into light and enter Gardens beneath which rivers flow. In Surah 77: The Emissaries (Al-Mursalaat), those who kept their duty are amid shade and fountains with desired fruits and are told to eat, drink, and welcome their reward.
