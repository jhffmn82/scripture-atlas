Abstract written by a model

The believers are the readers addressed in Chapter 6. They were once bondservants of sin but were baptized into Christ's death, united with him, and crucified their old man. Now dead to sin and alive to God, under grace rather than law, they obey the teaching from the heart. Freed from sin, they become servants of righteousness and of God, bearing sanctification's fruit, with eternal life as the result and a future share in resurrection.

Chapter 6

The readers died to sin, were baptized into Christ's death, and were freed from bondage to sin, having become obedient from the heart to the form of teaching delivered to them. They are told to consider themselves dead to sin and alive to God, and to present their members as servants of righteousness. Once ashamed of their former fruit, they now have the fruit of sanctification and the result of eternal life.
