Abstract written by a model

Twelve memorial stones, one per tribe, were taken by twelve men from the Jordan's middle and carried to camp, then set up by Joshua at Gilgal to answer future children's questions. Joshua also set twelve stones in the Jordan where the priests stood.

Chapter 4

Twelve men, one from each tribe, took twelve stones from the middle of the Jordan and carried them to the camp as a memorial for Israel forever, to answer the questions of future children. Joshua set them up at Gilgal. Joshua also set up twelve stones in the middle of the Jordan, where they remain to this day.
