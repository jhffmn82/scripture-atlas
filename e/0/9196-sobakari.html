Sobakari

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: man-at-arms, man-at-arms in the personal service of King Sumi-no-ye-no-naka-tsu

Abstract written by a model

Sobakari, a man-at-arms serving King Sumi-no-ye-no-naka-tsu, kills his lord with a spear at His Augustness Midzu-ha-wake’s command. Appointed Prime Minister and honored, he is ultimately killed by His Augustness Midzu-ha-wake with a concealed sword.

Sobakari is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
