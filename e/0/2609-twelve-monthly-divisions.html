Chapter 27

Twelve divisions of twenty-four thousand each came in and went out month by month through the year, each led by a named captain for its month. The captains run from Jashobeam for the first month to Heldai for the twelfth, and include Benaiah the son of Jehoiada for the third and Asahel the brother of Joab for the fourth.
