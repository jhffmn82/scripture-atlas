stillness

topic · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Stillness, being still, that stillness

Abstract written by a model

Stillness is guarded with unwearying vigour and marks things’ return to their root, reporting fulfilment of their appointed end. It rules movement, overcomes heat, and with Purity gives correct law to all under heaven. Stillness also enables the female to overcome the male and is considered a sort of abasement.
