Abstract written by a model

Apollonius, called a lord of pollutions, was sent by Antiochus with twenty-two thousand troops to kill adults and enslave women and boys. He came to Jerusalem feigning peace, waited for the Sabbath, then had armed men parade and slaughtered the onlookers and great multitudes.

Chapter 5

Apollonius, called a lord of pollutions, was sent by Antiochus with an army of twenty-two thousand to kill all those of full age and sell the women and boys as slaves. He came to Jerusalem pretending peace, waited for the Sabbath, and had his armed men parade. He put to the sword those who came out to watch and killed great multitudes in the city.
