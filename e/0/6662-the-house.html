the House

place · 5 works · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: House, House (this House), My House, My house, Thy holy House, the (holy) House, the ancient House, the holy House, this House

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), the House at Makka is a sanctuary, resort, and place of worship for mankind, and Abraham and Ishmael are commanded to purify it for those who go around it, meditate, bow, and prostrate. In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan), the House is a place of pilgrimage, contains plain memorials of Allah’s guidance and the place where Abraham stood to pray, and whoever enters it is safe. In Surah 14: Abraham (Ibrahim), the holy House is near the uncultivable valley where Abraham settles some of his posterity so that they may establish proper worship. In Surah 22: The Pilgrimage (Al-Hajj), the holy House is the place whose location Allah prepared for Abraham and which Allah commanded to be kept free of partners and purified for worshippers. In Surah 22: The Pilgrimage (Al-Hajj), the ancient House is the destination around which pilgrims go after completing their unkemptness and vows, and to which sacrificial cattle are brought. In Surah 106: Quraysh (Quraish), this House is associated with the Lord who is to be worshiped and who has fed them against hunger and made them safe from fear.
