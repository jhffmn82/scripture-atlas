Abstract written by a model

In Surah 39: The Groups (Az-Zumar), the soul is received by Allah at death and during sleep, with some souls kept and others released until an appointed term. In Surah 56: The Inevitable (Al-Waaqia), the soul of the dying person rises to the throat while people look on, with the Lord of the Worlds nearer to it than they are. In Surah 81: The Overthrowing (At-Takwir), souls are reunited, and every soul knows what it has made ready. In Surah 82: The Cleaving (Al-Infitaar), a soul knows what it sent before and left behind, and no soul has power over another on that day. In Surah 91: The Sun (Ash-Shams), the soul is perfected and inspired with conscience of right and wrong, succeeding when it is caused to grow and failing when stunted. In The Table-Talk of the Prophet Mohammad (Part 2), the soul leaves the body at death; a believer’s soul is welcomed and reaches celestial regions, while an infidel’s soul is taken by punishing angels. In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), the soul belongs to God, is known by one who knows its secrets, and departs at death while receiving a spiritual janeu of mercy, contentment, continence, and truth.
