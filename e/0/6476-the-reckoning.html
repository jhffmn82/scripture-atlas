the reckoning

event · 5 works · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Day of Reckoning, Their reckoning, a woeful reckoning, day of reckoning, reckoning, that day, the Day of Reckoning, their reckoning

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 13: The Thunder (Ar-Ra'd), the reckoning is woeful for those who answer not Allah's call, belongs to Allah, and is swiftly administered by Him. In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), the reckoning of mankind draws near while people remain heedless and turn away from reminders. In Surah 38: The letter Saad (Saad), the Day of Reckoning is the day before which disbelievers ask that their fate be hastened, is forgotten by those who wander from Allah's way, and is the day for which the righteous are promised provision. In Surah 88: The Overwhelming (Al-Ghaashiya), the reckoning belongs to Allah. In Surah 100: The Chargers (Al-Aadiyaat), on the day of reckoning the contents of the graves are poured forth, the secrets of the breasts are made known, and their Lord is perfectly informed concerning them.
