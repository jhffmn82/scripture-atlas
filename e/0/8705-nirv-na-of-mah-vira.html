Abstract written by a model

The Nirvâna of Mahâvira is the final liberation of the Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra. During the last night of the rainy season, he died in king Hastipâla's office of the writers in Pâpâ, was freed from pains, cut asunder the ties of birth, old age, and death, and became finally liberated. It became a point for counting elapsed time; Indrabhûti, Ârya Sudharman, and the Ganadharas died afterward.

The death of the Venerable Ascetic Mahâvira is used as the point from which the elapsed centuries and years are counted.

List of the Sthaviras.

The Nirvâna of Mahâvira occurred before the deaths of the Sthaviras Indrabhûti and Ârya Sudharman. The Ganadharas died after the Nirvâna of Mahâvira.
