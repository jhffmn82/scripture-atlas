superior man

person · 1 work · 14 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

The superior man follows strict rules for dress. He avoids deep purple, puce, red, and reddish colors, chooses particular garments and furs for warm weather, home, sleep, condolence, mourning, and court, and presents himself at court on the first day of the month.

CHAP. VI. 1. The superior man did not use a deep purple, or a

The superior man avoided deep purple, puce, red, and reddish colors in his clothing. He wore specified garments and furs according to the weather, setting, and occasion, including mourning, condolence visits, and court appearances.
