Abstract written by a model

Faithful householders, residing in all directions and possessing vessels, may offer ready food to ascetics; such âdhâkarmika food should not be accepted.

Ninth Lesson.

Faithful householders and their servants may decide to give ascetics all food ready for their use and prepare food for themselves afterward. Food offered in this way is considered âdhâkarmika and should not be accepted.
