Abstract written by a model

Ahimaaz, son of Zadok, is a swift-running messenger judged a good man by David. With Jonathan he waited at En Rogel, hid in a well at Bahurim, escaped Absalom's servants, and warned David that Ahithophel had counseled against him. Later he insisted on running news to David despite Joab's refusal, outran the Cushite, was recognized by his running, reported all well, but claimed ignorance about Absalom.

Chapter 17

Ahimaaz stayed with Jonathan by En Rogel to carry word to King David. Seen by a boy, they hid in a well at Bahurim, escaped Absalom's servants, and then told David that Ahithophel had counseled against him and that he must cross the water quickly.

Chapter 18

Ahimaaz son of Zadok asked to carry the king news that the LORD had avenged him, but Joab refused because the king's son was dead. He insisted on running anyway, outran the Cushite by way of the Plain, and the watchman recognized him by his running. He told David all was well and blessed the LORD, but when asked about Absalom said he saw a great tumult and did not know what it was.
