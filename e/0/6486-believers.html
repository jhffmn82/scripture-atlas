Abstract written by a model

Believers fear the Hour, trust in their Lord, shun the worst sins and indecencies, forgive when wroth, answer Allah’s call, establish worship, conduct their affairs by counsel, spend from what they have been given, and defend themselves after suffering wrong. They are promised flowering meadows of the Gardens and the better, lasting provision of Allah.
