Fourth Part. / Sixteenth Lecture, Called the Liberation.

The Sixteenth Lecture, Called the Liberation, teaches the wise to meditate on the supreme truth, abandon family bonds, acts, and attachments, and live as steadfast mendicants. It describes how such a mendicant endures insult, pain, and calamities without harming beings and ultimately becomes free from the bed of pain.
