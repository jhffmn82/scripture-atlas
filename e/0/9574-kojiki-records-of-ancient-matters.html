Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods) / Preface.

The Kojiki is presented as a record compiled from the genealogies of the emperors and the ancient words preserved by various families. It consists of Three Volumes, beginning with the separation of Heaven and Earth and concluding with the august reign at Woharida.
