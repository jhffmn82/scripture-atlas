the devil

person · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: devil, evil one, him who had the power of death, the evil one

Abstract written by a model

In Paul’s Letter to the Ephesians, the devil uses wiles against which believers must stand with the armor of God. In The Letter to the Hebrews, the devil has the power of death, which Jesus brings to nothing through death. In 1 John, the devil has been sinning from the beginning and marks those who do not do righteousness or love their brother as his children.
