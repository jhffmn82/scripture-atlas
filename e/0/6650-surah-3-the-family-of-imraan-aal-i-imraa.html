Abstract written by a model

Surah 3, The Family of Imraan, presents Allah’s sovereignty, creation, mercy, guidance, warnings, and final judgment through the contrast between belief and disbelief. It addresses Muhammad, the Scripture, earlier revelations, believers, disbelievers, the People of the Scripture, messengers, and hypocrites. It recounts the family of 'Imran, Mary, Zachariah, John, Jesus son of Mary, and the disciples, and affirms Abraham, the Torah, Gospel, Becca, and pilgrimage. It then commands believers to hold fast to Allah, obey the messenger, endure trials, seek forgiveness, and recalls Badr and Uhud, ending with paradise, the Fire, death, resurrection, and accountability.

Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan)

This unit concerns Allah, Muhammad, the Scripture, earlier revelations, and the relationship between belief, disbelief, guidance, and judgment. It recounts Allah’s creation, sovereignty, mercy, and warnings, and describes the believers, disbelievers, the People of the Scripture, and the consequences awaiting them. It recounts the family of 'Imran, including Mary, Zachariah, John, Jesus, son of Mary, and the disciples. It also affirms Abraham, the Torah, the Gospel, the House at Becca, pilgrimage, and the Day of Resurrection.

This unit concerns Allah's guidance, commands, promises, and judgments toward believers, disbelievers, the People of the Scripture, messengers, and hypocrites. It urges believers to hold fast to Allah, maintain right conduct, seek forgiveness, endure trials, obey Allah and the messenger, and trust in Allah's help. It recounts the battle at Badr and the battle at Uhud, including the believers' setbacks, testing, forgiveness, and the reward promised to those who remain steadfast. It describes paradise, the Fire, the Day of Resurrection, death, and the consequences of belief, disbelief, obedience, wrongdoing, and repentance.
