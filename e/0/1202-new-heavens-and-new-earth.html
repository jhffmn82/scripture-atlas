new heavens and new earth

place · 3 works · 8 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a new heaven and a new earth, new heaven and new earth, new heavens and a new earth, the first heaven and the first earth, the new heavens and the new earth

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, it is created by the LORD and remains before him, with inhabitants living long lives in joyful peace. In Peter’s Second Letter, it is promised by God as a place where righteousness dwells and motivates believers to strive for peace and blamelessness. In Revelation, it replaces the first heaven and earth, which pass away, and has no sea, death, mourning, crying, or pain.

new heavens and new earth in The Book of Isaiah

author unknown · 739 to 680 BC · the work’s page · read it where new heavens and new earth first appears

Abstract written by a model

A new heavens and new earth created by the LORD and remaining before him. Former things are forgotten, inhabitants live long lives with no infant dying within days, and God's people rejoice forever, alongside a peaceable holy mountain where nothing hurts or destroys.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 65

The LORD declares that he creates new heavens and a new earth, and the former things will not be remembered or come into mind. His people are told to be glad and rejoice forever in what he creates. It is paired with a peaceable holy mountain where wolf and lamb feed together and nothing hurts or destroys.

new heavens and a new earth in Peter’s Second Letter

Simon Peter · AD 64 to 68 · the work’s page · read it where new heavens and a new earth first appears

Abstract written by a model

New heavens and a new earth are promised by God, a place where righteousness dwells; believers' hope in it motivates diligence to be found in peace, without defect and blameless.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 3

According to his promise, believers look for new heavens and a new earth, in which righteousness dwells. This hope is the basis for the exhortation to be diligent to be found in peace, without defect and blameless.

new heaven and new earth in Revelation

author unknown · about AD 95 · the work’s page · read it where new heaven and new earth first appears

Abstract written by a model

The new heaven and new earth, seen by the narrator, replaces the first heaven and earth, which pass away. It has no sea, and no death, mourning, crying or pain, as God makes all things new.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 21

The narrator sees a new heaven and a new earth because the first heaven and the first earth have passed away, and the sea is no more. The first things passing away is tied to the end of death, mourning, crying, and pain, and to God making all things new.
