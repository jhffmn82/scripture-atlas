Third Part. / Fifteenth Lecture, Called the Clauses.

Gods and goddesses of the Bhavanapatis, Vyantaras, Gyotishkas, and Vimânavâsins attended Mahâvîra’s birth and performed auspicious ceremonies. They later supported his renunciation, carried or accompanied his palankin, made music, and descended and ascended when he attained Kevala.
