Abstract written by a model

is_answered_by judgment seat of Christ [all will stand before it] Judging and despising are forbidden: the one who eats must not despise the one who doesn't, and the one who doesn't must not judge the one who eats. The text asks why one would judge or despise a brother, since all will stand before Christ's judgment seat and each will give account to God. Believers are told instead to judge only that no one puts a stumbling block in a brother's way.
