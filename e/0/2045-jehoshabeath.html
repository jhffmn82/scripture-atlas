Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Jehosheba rescued Joash from the slain king's sons and hid him and his nurse, saving him from Athaliah. In The Second Book of Chronicles, Jehoshabeath secretly rescued Joash from the slain king's sons and hid him with his nurse so Athaliah could not kill him.
