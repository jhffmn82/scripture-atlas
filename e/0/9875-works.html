Abstract written by a model

Works, linked to carelessness and sinful thoughts, words, and acts, are attributed to sinners through exertion. They bring misery, worldly bondage, and suffering, especially when they cause living beings’ death; a wise man avoids injuring living beings.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Eighth Lecture, Called on Exertion.

Works are associated with carelessness, sinful thoughts, words, and acts. The exertion leading to works is attributed to sinners.

Ninth Lecture, Called the Law.

Acts that cause the death of living beings bring suffering to the person who performs them, even when relations take possession of his wealth. A wise man should avoid acts that injure living beings.
