Abstract written by a model

Cornelius is a centurion of the Italian Regiment living in Caesarea, devout and God-fearing with his whole household, generous to the needy, constantly prayerful, and well spoken of by the Jewish nation. After an angel's vision he sends men to Joppa for Peter, gathers relatives and friends, and tries to worship Peter, who refuses. He and his household hear Peter's message, receive the Holy Spirit, and are baptized. Peter later retells the angel's report.

Chapter 10

Cornelius is a centurion of the Italian Regiment in Caesarea, a devout God-fearing man who gives generously to the needy and prays always. After an angel's vision he sends two servants and a soldier to Joppa for Peter, gathers his relatives and close friends, and falls at Peter's feet in worship, which Peter refuses. He and his household hear Peter's message, receive the Holy Spirit, and are baptized.

Chapter 11

The man in Caesarea, whose house Peter entered, reported that he had seen an angel standing in his house. The angel told him to send to Joppa for Simon called Peter, who would speak words by which he and all his house would be saved. The text does not name him in this chapter.
