Chapter 2

The LORD announces that in a little while he will shake the heavens, the earth, the sea, and the dry land, and all nations, so that their treasure comes to the house. He repeats the promise to Zerubbabel, adding that he will overthrow the throne of kingdoms, destroy the strength of the nations, and bring down chariots and horsemen, who will fall by the sword of one another.
