Chapter 14

Saul adjures the people with a curse on anyone who eats before evening and before he is avenged on his enemies. It leaves the people distressed and faint, and Jonathan breaks it unknowingly by eating honey. Jonathan criticizes it as troubling the land, and the people prevent its penalty from being carried out on him.
