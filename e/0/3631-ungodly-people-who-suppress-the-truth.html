ungodly people who suppress the truth

Also called: men who suppress the truth in unrighteousness, the men, their women, them, themselves, they

Abstract written by a model

A group of people who knew God through creation and revelation yet refused to glorify or thank him. They grew vain and darkened in heart, claimed wisdom but became fools, and practiced idolatry. God gave them up to uncleanness, vile passions and a reprobate mind; they practice vices and approve others who do.

Chapter 1

These people knew God through what was revealed to them and through creation, yet did not glorify him or give thanks, became vain in their reasoning, and their senseless hearts were darkened. Claiming to be wise they became fools, and God gave them up to uncleanness, vile passions, and a reprobate mind. They not only practice the listed wrongs, knowing God's ordinance, but approve of others who do.
