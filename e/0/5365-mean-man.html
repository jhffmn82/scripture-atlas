Also called: The mean man, the mean man

Abstract written by a model

The mean man is adulatory but not affable, difficult to serve yet easy to please. He accepts pleasure through ways not accordant with right, demands that employed men be equal to everything, and has pride without dignified ease.

mean man is in one work on this site, The Analects of Confucius, Book XIII: Tsze-Lu. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
