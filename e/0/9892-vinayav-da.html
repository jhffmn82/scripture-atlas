Vinayavâda

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: upholders of Vinaya

Abstract written by a model

Heretical creed upheld by various Vinaya adherents, claiming Môksha through Vinaya; its upholders mistake truth and goodness, explaining their tenet without perceiving truth.

Vinayavâda is in one work on this site, Sutrakritanga (Sûtrakritâṅga), Book I. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
