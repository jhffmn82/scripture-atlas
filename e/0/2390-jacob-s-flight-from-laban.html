Chapter 31

After the LORD's command and the agreement of Rachel and Leah, Jacob secretly leaves with his wives, children, and livestock without telling Laban. Laban learns of it on the third day and overtakes him in Gilead after seven days. Laban accuses Jacob of stealing away his daughters and gods, the search turns up nothing, and the confrontation ends in a covenant and Laban's departure.
