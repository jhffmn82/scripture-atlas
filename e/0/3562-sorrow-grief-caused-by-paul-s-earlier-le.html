Also called: affliction and anguish of heart, excessive sorrow, grieve, many tears, sorrow

Abstract written by a model

Paul says he would not come again in sorrow, and that his letter was written not to make the recipients grieve but so they would know his love. He notes that whoever caused sorrow did so in part to them all, and that the offender risks being swallowed up by excessive sorrow.
