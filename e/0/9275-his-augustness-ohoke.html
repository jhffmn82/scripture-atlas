His Augustness Ohoke

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Prince Ohoke, his elder brother, the Prince, the two Princes

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Ohoke, elder brother of Prince Woke and Emperor Ken-Zō, was discovered with Woke by Wodate. He joined Woke in counsel against Grandee Shibi, then ceded the Empire to Woke. He later slightly excavated Emperor Yū-Riyaku’s mausoleum and, after Ken-Zō’s death, ruled the succession of Heaven’s sun as Emperor Nin-Ken.

His Augustness Ohoke is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
