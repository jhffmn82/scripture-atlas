Abstract written by a model

Isaiah's commission is a message of hardening: after volunteering when the Lord asks whom to send, he is told to tell this people to hear without understanding, making hearts fat, ears heavy, eyes shut so they do not turn and heal; it lasts until the land is utterly waste.

Chapter 6

After the Lord asks whom to send, the narrator volunteers and is told to go and tell this people that they hear but do not understand and see but do not perceive. The message is to make their hearts fat, their ears heavy and their eyes shut, lest they understand and turn and be healed. When he asks how long, he is told it lasts until the land is utterly waste.
