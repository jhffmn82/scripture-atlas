seven rules of possession

Abstract written by a model

Seven rules of possession govern how monks and nuns request, take possession of, and use dwelling-places. They begin with asking for a domicile after considering its fitness, distinguish possession and use for oneself or other mendicants, address a place ceded to others, limit asking for one’s own sake, and end with permitted use of obtained Ikkada reed and clay- or wood-paved dwellings.

Seventh Lecture, Called Regulation of Possession. / First Lesson. .. Second Lesson.

The seven rules of possession govern how monks and nuns may request, use, or refrain from using dwelling-places. They include taking possession for oneself or others, using a place already ceded to others, and using Ikkada reed or a dwelling paved with clay or wood only when those things are obtained.
