Abstract written by a model

Nebuchadnezzar's madness and humbling is an event caused by his pride in boasting of Babylon. A sky voice declares his kingdom departed; immediately he is driven from men, eats grass like oxen, and grows feather-like hair and claw-like nails. It ends when he looks to heaven and his understanding and kingdom are restored.

Chapter 4

Twelve months after the dream, as Nebuchadnezzar claims credit for building great Babylon by his own might, a voice from the sky announces that the kingdom has departed from him. The sentence is fulfilled the same hour: he is driven from men, eats grass like oxen, is wet with the dew of the sky, and grows hair like eagles' feathers and nails like birds' claws. It ends when he lifts his eyes to heaven and his understanding returns.
