TAO of inaction

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: inaction

Abstract written by a model

TAO of inaction is happiness, true pleasure, and the path to perfect happiness and preservation of life. Through non-doing and inner repose, it spontaneously produces action, changes things through Heaven and Earth, accomplishes all things, leaves nothing undone, and preserves the ruler’s energy.

TAO of inaction is in one work on this site, Chuang Tzŭ. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XIII.

Inaction enables all things to be accomplished and gives the ruler energy to spare in administering the empire. It produces action spontaneously and unconsciously when inner repose has been achieved.

CHAPTER XVIII.

Inaction is presented as true pleasure and as the only path to perfect happiness and preservation of life. Heaven and Earth do nothing, yet their inaction produces all the modifications of things.
