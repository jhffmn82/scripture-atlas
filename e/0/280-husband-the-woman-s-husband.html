husband (the woman's husband)

Abstract written by a model

The husband is a figure of authority over his wife's vows and binding oaths, and over a young woman's vows in her father's house. Silence on the day he hears establishes them; forbidding that day voids them. If he nullifies them after hearing, he bears her iniquity.

Chapter 30

The husband may establish or make void his wife's vows and binding oaths. If he says nothing on the day he hears them, they stand, and if he forbids them that day they are void. If he nullifies them after he has heard them, he bears her iniquity.
