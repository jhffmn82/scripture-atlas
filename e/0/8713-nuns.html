Abstract written by a model

Nuns during the Paggusan follow Yati rules governing residence, travel, alms, food, cleanliness, inspection, lodging, speech, disputes, and movement. They extend residence and collect alms within a Yogana and Krosa, need three lodging-places and pots, limit drinks, and cannot accept unrequested food or drink.

Rules for Yatis.

Nuns during the Paggusan are subject to rules concerning residence, travel, alms, food, bodily cleanliness, inspection, lodging, speech, disputes, and movement. They must not accept food or drink for one who has not asked them or whom they have not promised to assist.
