vengeance and judgment

topic · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Vengeance, divine judgment and vengeance, fire, judgment, my arrows, my glittering sword, my sword, vengeance

Abstract written by a model

In Deuteronomy, the LORD announces judgment through fire, hunger, beasts, venom, and the sword, declares vengeance and recompense his, and promises to avenge his servants' blood. In Psalms, vengeance belongs to the LORD, who judges, repays the proud, restores righteousness, and brings the wicked's iniquity upon them.

Abstract written by a model

Vengeance belongs to the LORD; the psalmist calls God to rise as judge and repay the proud. Judgment will return to righteousness, the upright follow it, and the LORD brings the wicked's iniquity upon them, cutting them off.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 94

Vengeance is said to belong to God, and the psalmist calls on him to rise as judge and pay back the proud what they deserve. The text states that judgment will return to righteousness and that the upright in heart will follow it. It is carried out as the LORD brings the wicked's own iniquity on them and cuts them off.
