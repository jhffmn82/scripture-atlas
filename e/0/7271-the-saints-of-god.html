the saints of God

group · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: The saints of God, saints, the saint, the saints

Abstract written by a model

The saints of God are absorbed in the Name, having destroyed pain and fear of transmigration and found God, the imperishable Being. Honoured across earth and universe, they are served by people; becoming dust of their feet is a source of happiness.

The saints of God are absorbed in God’s Name and have destroyed the pain and fear of transmigration. They have found God the imperishable Being and possess great honour throughout the earth and universe.
