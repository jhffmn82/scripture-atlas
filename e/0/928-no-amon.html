Abstract written by a model

No-Amon is a place set among rivers, with the sea as its rampart and wall, and with Cush, Egypt, Put, and Libya as strength and helpers. It serves as a comparison for Nineveh: despite these defenses, it was carried into captivity, its children dashed in pieces and its great men chained.

Chapter 3

No-Amon is presented as a comparison for Nineveh: it was situated among rivers, had the sea as its rampart and wall, and had Cush, Egypt, Put, and Libya as strength and helpers. Even so it was carried into captivity, its young children dashed in pieces at the head of the streets, lots cast for its honorable men, and its great men bound in chains.
