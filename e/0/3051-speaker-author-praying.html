Chapter 23

The speaker prays that the Lord will not abandon him, asks for scourges over his thought and a discipline of wisdom over his heart, and asks that haughty eyes, evil desire, gluttony, lust, and a shameless mind be kept from him. He then addresses his children, urging them to learn the discipline of the mouth.
