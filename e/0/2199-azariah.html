Abstract written by a model

Azariah is a chief priest of the house of Zadok, also called ruler of God's house. In Chapter 26 he leads eighty priests in resisting King Uzziah's incense offering and expelling the leprous king. In Chapter 31 he answers Hezekiah about the heaps, credits the LORD's blessing, and helps appoint overseers.

Chapter 26

Azariah the priest, called the chief priest, goes into the temple after Uzziah with eighty priests. He and the priests resist the king, tell him that burning incense belongs to the consecrated sons of Aaron, and thrust him out when they see he is leprous.

Chapter 31

Azariah, the chief priest of the house of Zadok, answered Hezekiah's question about the heaps. He said that since offerings began to be brought, they had eaten and had plenty left over because the LORD had blessed his people. He is also called the ruler of God's house, and took part in appointing the overseers.
