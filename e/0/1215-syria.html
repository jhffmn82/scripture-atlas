Syria

place · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Syrians, the Syrians

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Syria oppressed Israel under Hazael, destroyed Jehoahaz's forces, and was defeated by Israel three times after Elisha's prophecy. In The Book of Isaiah, Syria, headed by Damascus under Rezin, allied with Ephraim and plotted evil against Judah, but the LORD declared the plot would fail and its remnant would match Israel's glory.

Syria in The Second Book of Kings

author unknown · 560 to 539 BC · the work’s page · read it where Syria first appears

Abstract written by a model

Syria oppressed Israel under Hazael, and its king destroyed Jehoahaz's forces, making them like dust in threshing. Elisha foretold that Israel would strike the Syrians at Aphek, and because the king struck the ground only three times, Syria would be defeated just three times. Jehoash then struck Benhadad three times.
