Abstract written by a model

The nations are a hostile collective that, in Chapter 19, capture two young lions in turn: first trapping one in a pit and hooking him to Egypt, then netting and caging another and delivering him to Babylon's king.

Chapter 19

The nations hear of the first cub, trap him in a pit, and bring him with hooks to Egypt. They later attack the second cub on every side, spread a net over him, take him in their pit, cage him, and deliver him to the king of Babylon.
