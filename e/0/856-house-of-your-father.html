Chapter 2

The man of God recalls that the LORD revealed himself to this house in Egypt, chose it from all the tribes to be his priests, and gave it the offerings made by fire. Although the LORD had said it would walk before him forever, he now announces it will be cut off, with no old man in it, and its survivors will beg for priestly positions for bread.
