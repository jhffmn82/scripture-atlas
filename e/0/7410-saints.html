Abstract written by a model

The saints are righteous males and females, including holy people, teachers, disciples, children, and those dead, living, unborn, or yet to exist. They possess life, spirit, conscience, and intelligence, strive after ritual righteousness, and may exist within or outside a province. Associated with the best life and radiant heaven, they are protected against those causing their death. Yasna and sacrificial worship are offered to them; worship honors their Fravashis, souls, spirits, consciences, intelligences, and lives, answering all saints with salvation.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna IX.

The righteous saints are associated with the best life and radiant heaven sought from H(a)oma. H(a)oma is asked to hurl his mace at beings whose actions cause a righteous saint to perish.

Yasna XXI.

The saints are among those to whom the Yasna and sacrificial worship are offered. The text says that every saint who exists, is coming into existence, or shall exist in the future is answered with the state of salvation.

Yasna XXVI.

The saints are honored through worship of their Fravashis, souls, spirits, consciences, intelligences, and lives. The unit includes dead, living, and unborn saints, as well as holy men, women, teachers, disciples, and children.
