Brâhmana

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

A religious ascetic ceases sinful actions, possesses Samitis, exerts himself without anger or pride, subdues senses, and abandons body.

Brâhmana is in one work on this site, Sutrakritanga (Sûtrakritâṅga), Book I. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Sixteenth Lecture, Called the Song.

A Brâhmana has ceased from sinful actions and possesses the Samitis. A Brâhmana always exerts himself, is not angry, and is not proud.
