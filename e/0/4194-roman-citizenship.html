Roman citizenship

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: citizenship, lawful

Abstract written by a model

Roman citizenship is a legal status protecting against uncondemned scourging. Paul invokes it, the commanding officer admits buying his for a great price while Paul was born Roman, and the officer fears having bound him.

Chapter 22

Paul invokes his Roman status to challenge the scourging, and the centurion warns the commanding officer. The officer says he bought his citizenship at great price, while Paul says he was born a Roman, and this causes the officer fear for having bound him.
