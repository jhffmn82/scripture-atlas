Tola

Abstract written by a model

Tola is a father of six sons (Uzzi, Rephaiah, Jeriel, Jahmai, Ibsam, Shemuel), each head of a father's house and mighty men of valor; their descendants numbered 22,600 fighting men in David's days.

Chapter 7
