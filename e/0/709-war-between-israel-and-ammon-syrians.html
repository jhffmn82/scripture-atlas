Abstract written by a model

A war between Israel and Ammon with hired Syrians, caused by Hanun's humiliation of David's servants. Joab and Abishai routed the enemy at the gate; David defeated a second Syrian force under Shophach across the Jordan. Syrians made peace, abandoning Ammon; Israel won.

Chapter 19

The war began after Hanun humiliated David's servants and the Ammonites hired Syrian forces. Joab and Abishai routed the Syrians and Ammonites at the city gate, and David later defeated a second Syrian force under Shophach after crossing the Jordan. It ended in Syrian submission to David and the withdrawal of Syrian support from Ammon.
