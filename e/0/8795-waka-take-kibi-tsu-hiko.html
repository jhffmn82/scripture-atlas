Waka-take-kibi-tsu-hiko

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: His Augustness Waka-take-kibi-tsu-hiko

Abstract written by a model

Waka-take-kibi-tsu-hiko, ancestor of the Grandees of Shimo-tsu-michi in Kibi and Kasa, joined Oho-kibi-tsu-hiko to set sacred jars at the River Hi in Harima, then subdued and pacified the Land of Kibi.

Waka-take-kibi-tsu-hiko is in one work on this site, Kojiki. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Sect. LX.—Emperor Kō-Rei.
