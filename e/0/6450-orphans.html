orphans in Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where orphans first appears

Abstract written by a model

Orphans possess wealth and heritage that must be safeguarded. They are to receive their wealth when they reach marriageable age, show sound judgment, and are present at its division. People must protect them through just behavior, give them just treatment, and avoid exchanging, absorbing, squandering, or wrongfully consuming their wealth. The records begin with their wealth and inheritance, set conditions for receiving it, warn against wrongful consumption, and conclude with a command to deal justly with orphans, noting that Allah is aware of good done for them.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa) / 1 O mankind! Be careful of your duty to your Lord Who created you from a single soul and .. 10 Lo! Those who devour the wealth of orphans wrongfully, they do but swallow fire into their bellies, and they will be exposed to burning flame.

Orphans are to be given their wealth and protected from having it exchanged, absorbed, squandered, or consumed wrongfully. They are to be tested until marriageable age and given their fortune when sound judgment is found.

127 They consult thee concerning women. Say: Allah giveth you decree concerning them, and the Scripture which hath been recited unto you (giveth decree), concerning female orphans and those unto whom ye give not that which is ordained for them though ye desire to marry them, and (concerning) the weak among children, and that ye should deal justly with orphans. Whatever good ye do, lo! Allah is ever Aware of it. .. 134 Whoso desireth the reward of the world, (let him know that) with Allah is the reward of the world and the Hereafter. Allah is ever Hearer, Seer.

The text commands that people deal justly with orphans. Allah is aware of whatever good is done for them.
