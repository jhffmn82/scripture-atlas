Chapter 9

The unit gives many commands for avoiding it: do not meet a prostitute, associate with a singer, gaze at a virgin, or look around the city streets and deserted places. A man should turn his eye from a beautiful woman and not dine or drink with a married woman. The stated risks include falling into snares, losing his inheritance, incurring penalties, and sliding into destruction.
