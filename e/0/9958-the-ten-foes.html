Abstract written by a model

The ten foes are the Self, four cardinal passions, and five senses; Gautama says vanquishing one and then five vanquishes all ten.

Twenty-Third Lecture.

The ten foes consist of the Self, the four cardinal passions, and the five senses. Gautama explains that by vanquishing one and then five, he has vanquished all ten.
