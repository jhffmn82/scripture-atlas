Abstract written by a model

Surah 95: The Fig (At-Tin) concerns oaths by the fig, the olive, Mount Sinai, and a land made safe. It states that man was created in the best stature and then reduced to the lowest of the low. Those who believe and do good works receive a reward unfailing. The unit asks who will give the lie about the judgment and declares Allah the most conclusive of all judges.
