Chapter 29

The multitude of nations that fight against Ariel, her stronghold, and Mount Zion will be like fine dust and blowing chaff, vanishing in an instant. They are compared to a dream, like a hungry or thirsty man who dreams of eating or drinking and wakes still unsatisfied.
