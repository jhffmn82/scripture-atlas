Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of the Maccabees, Antiochus Eupator is set up as king after his father's death, marches against Judas, makes peace, breaks the oath, returns to Antioch, and seizes it from Philip. In The Second Book of the Maccabees, Antiochus Eupator invades Judea, orders Menelaus executed, makes peace after a failed Bethsura attack, honors the sanctuary, appoints Hegemonides, and faces displeasure at Ptolemais.

Chapter 6

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

Antiochus Eupator came against Judea with a huge force and, stirred up against Menelaus, ordered him put to death at Beroea. After the Jewish night raid and a failed attack on Bethsura, he learned Philip had become reckless in Antioch, made peace with the Jews, offered sacrifice, honored the sanctuary, and left Hegemonides as governor. He then went to Ptolemais, where he faced displeasure over the treaty.
