Abstract written by a model

The beast is a composite, blasphemous ruler who rises out of the sea with ten horns crowned with ten crowns, seven heads bearing blasphemous names, and a leopard-like body with bear's feet and a lion's mouth. The dragon gives it power, throne, and great authority, and one of its heads has a fatal wound that is healed. For forty-two months it blasphemes God, makes war on the saints and overcomes them, and holds authority over every tribe, people, language, and nation. Those who dwell on the earth, whose names are not in the book of life, worship it, and its mark is received on forehead or hand; worshippers are warned of torment and God's wrath. Later its throne is struck by the fifth bowl and its kingdom is darkened, while a frog-like spirit comes from its mouth to gather kings for war. It appears scarlet and full of blasphemous names, carrying the woman. Its heads and horns represent seven and ten kings; it is described as an eighth who is of the seven, destined to come up from the abyss and go to destruction. The ten kings give it their power and kingdom, and it joins them in burning the prostitute. Finally, gathered with the kings and armies against the rider on the horse, it is taken and thrown alive into the lake of fire.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

The first beast rises out of the sea with ten horns and seven heads, ten crowns on its horns and blasphemous names on its heads, and looks like a leopard with bear's feet and a lion's mouth. The dragon gives it power, throne, and authority, and its fatally wounded head is healed. It is worshiped, speaks great things and blasphemy against God for forty-two months, makes war on the saints and overcomes them, and receives authority over every tribe, people, language, and nation.

Chapter 14

The beast and his image are worshipped by those who receive his mark on forehead or hand. Those who worship him are warned by the third angel of torment and God's wrath.

Chapter 16

The beast's throne is struck by the fifth bowl, and his kingdom is darkened. A spirit-like frog comes out of his mouth, along with those from the dragon and the false prophet, to gather the kings for war.

Chapter 17

The beast is scarlet, full of blasphemous names, and has seven heads and ten horns, and it carries the woman. The angel says it was, is not, and is about to come up out of the abyss and go to destruction, and that it is an eighth who is of the seven. The ten kings give their power and kingdom to it, and it joins them in hating and burning the prostitute.

Chapter 19

The beast is seen gathered with the kings of the earth and their armies to make war against the rider on the horse and his army. He is taken and thrown alive into the lake of fire that burns with sulfur.
