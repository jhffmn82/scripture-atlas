Amorites

group · 2 works · 1 quoted fact · 1 connection
Also called: the Amorite, the Amorites, the two kings of the Amorites

Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the Amorites under King Sihon refuse Israel passage, fight them, and lose their land and cities to Israel, which later drives them out at Jazer. In Deuteronomy, the Amorites are named as the people whose hill country Israel is to enter, and Moses recalls their defeat of Israel in Seir as far as Hormah.
