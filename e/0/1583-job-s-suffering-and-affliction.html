Abstract written by a model

Job's affliction, described in Chapter 30, is relentless suffering: days of affliction seize him, his soul is poured out, bones are pierced and burn with heat, with no rest at night. His skin blackens and peels, terrors chase his honor, and he mourns, companion to jackals and ostriches.

Chapter 30

Job's affliction is described as days of affliction that have taken hold of him, with his soul poured out and his bones pierced and gnawed by pain that gives no rest at night. His garment is disfigured, his skin grows black and peels, and his bones burn with heat. Terrors chase his honor like the wind, and he goes mourning, a brother to jackals and a companion to ostriches.
