Abstract written by a model

The ostrich is a bird whose wings wave proudly, yet she leaves her eggs on the earth, heedless that feet or wild animals may crush them. She is harsh to her young and fearless, lacks wisdom because God deprived her, but scorns horse and rider when she lifts herself.

The ostrich waves her wings proudly but leaves her eggs on the earth and forgets that feet or wild animals may crush them. She treats her young harshly, is without fear, and lacks wisdom because God deprived her of it, yet when she lifts herself up she scorns the horse and rider.
