Hara Berezaiti

place · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Haraiti Bareza

Abstract written by a model

In Vendidad (Vendîdâd), Hara Berezaiti is the place above which the sun, moon, and stars rise to produce light for the world. In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, Hara Berezaiti is the bright mountain around which many stars revolve, free from night and darkness, where Mithra dwells and Haoma sacrifices on its highest heights.
