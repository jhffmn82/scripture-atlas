Amalek

group · 4 works · 7 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: Amalek and his people, Amalekite, Amalekites, an Amalekite, he, my master, the Amalekite, the Amalekites, the troop that came against us, this troop

Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, Amalek fights with Israel at Rephidim, is defeated by Joshua and his people with the sword, and the LORD declares he will blot out its memory and have war with it from generation to generation. In Numbers, the Amalekite dwells in the valley, and the Amalekites come down and strike the presumptuous Israelites, beating them to Hormah. In Deuteronomy, Amalek attacks Israel on the way out of Egypt, striking the feeble rearmost who are faint and weary, and Israel is commanded to remember this and, once at rest, blot out its memory. In The First Book of Samuel, the Amalekites raid the South, Judah, Caleb's South, and Ziklag, burn Ziklag, carry off the women and all who are in it without killing any, and are struck by David, with only four hundred young men on camels escaping. In The First Book of Samuel, Amalek opposes Israel on the way up out of Egypt, and Saul strikes and destroys the Amalekites with the sword except King Agag, while the Kenites living among them are warned away and spared.
