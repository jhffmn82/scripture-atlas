Also called: curse, curse them

Abstract written by a model

Balak asks Balaam to curse and defy Jacob and Israel, and tries from different places where Balaam may see only part of them. Balaam says he cannot curse whom God has not cursed, and that there is no enchantment or divination against Israel. Balak finally tells him neither to curse nor bless them.
