Bhikshu

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

A Bhikshu is a modest religious ascetic obedient to the Guru, sustaining troubles and calamities, practising prescribed conduct with a pure mind, exerting himself steadfastly, eating moderately from others, subduing senses, and abandoning body.

Sixteenth Lecture, Called the Song.

A Bhikshu is modest, obedient to the Guru, and able to sustain troubles and calamities. A Bhikshu practises prescribed conduct with a pure mind, exerts himself, is steadfast, and eats only a moderate quantity of food given by others.
