Also called: heretical opinions, these heretics, unworthy heretics

Abstract written by a model

Heretics seek truth without crossing Samsâra, yet unworthy members entertain false doctrines, oppose the Law, and become slaves of women. Some rely on gods to end misery; they revile monks and defend worldly and sexual pleasures through assertions and abuse.

Third Lecture, Called the Knowledge of Troubles / First Chapter. .. Fourth Chapter.

Heretics revile monks, question their practices, and teach that worldly pleasures and sexual enjoyment carry no sin. When unable to prove their claims, they resort to bold assertions and bad language.
