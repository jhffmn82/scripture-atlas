God, according to his great mercy, caused believers to be born again to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ. This leads to an incorruptible and undefiled inheritance that doesn't fade away, reserved in Heaven. The new birth is of incorruptible seed, through the word of God, which lives and remains forever.
