righteousness

topic · 6 works · 6 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: My righteousness, their righteousness, what is true, your righteousness

Abstract written by a model

In Tobit, righteousness results in prosperous success for those who do what is true and please God. In The Book of Job, righteousness benefits man rather than God. In The Second Book of Nephi, righteousness is the girdle of the rod and branch from Jesse’s loins and guides judgment of the poor. In The Book of Helaman, righteousness is in the Lord, and the Lamanites’ righteousness exceeds that of the Nephites. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, righteousness is from the Lord. In Mencius, Book I (Liang Hui Wang), righteousness comes before profit in Mencius’s counsel.
