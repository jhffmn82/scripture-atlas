Chapter 3

Honoring parents is said to make atonement for sins, bring joy in one's own children, answer prayer, and give length of days. Kindness to a father will not be forgotten, will be remembered in the day of affliction, and will melt away sins like fair weather upon ice. It includes serving parents as masters and not dishonoring them in one's full strength.
