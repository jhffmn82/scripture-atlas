Abstract written by a model

Ten talents of silver were left in trust by Tobit with Gabael at Rages of Media. Tobit explains the deposit to Tobias, stressing that fearing God is true wealth. Tobias worries he doesn't know the holder, so Tobit gives him the handwriting. Raphael later presents it for Tobias, Gabael hands over the sealed bags, and the money is carried back toward the wedding feast.

Chapter 4

The ten talents of silver were left in trust by Tobit with Gabael the son of Gabrias at Rages of Media. Tobit recalls it and explains it to Tobias before he dies, while telling him that true wealth lies in fearing God.

Chapter 5

Tobias worries he cannot receive the money since he does not know the man who holds it. Tobit gives him the handwriting and instructs him to go and receive it, and Anna says they should not be greedy to add money to money.

Chapter 9

The money is held by Gabael in bags with their seals, and Tobias sends Raphael to receive it for him. Raphael presents the handwriting, and Gabael brings out the sealed bags and gives them to him. The money is then carried back toward the wedding feast.
