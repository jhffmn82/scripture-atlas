Abstract written by a model

Susanna, daughter of Helkias and wife of Joakim, is a fair, delicate, devout woman from Judah, taught the law of Moses by her parents. Threatened by two elders in the garden, she refuses to sin and cries out. Falsely accused and condemned to death, she prays to God and is saved when Daniel exposes the lie.

Chapter 13

Susanna, daughter of Helkias and wife of Joakim, is a very fair woman who fears the Lord and was taught the law of Moses. When the two elders threaten her in the garden, she refuses them, preferring to fall into their hands rather than sin before the Lord, and she cries out. She is falsely accused and condemned to death, prays to God, and is saved when Daniel exposes the elders' lie.
