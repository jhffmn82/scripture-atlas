CHAPTER XXII.

Knowledge asks how TAO may be known, approached, and attained. He receives no answer from Do-nothing Say-nothing, hears that All-in-extremes has forgotten his answer, and is told by the Yellow Emperor that knowledge makes both of them wholly wrong.
