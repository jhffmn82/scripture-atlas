servants of the LORD (my servants) in The Book of Isaiah

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where servants of the LORD (my servants) first appears

Abstract written by a model

The LORD's servants are a spared remnant from Jacob and Judah, chosen to inherit his holy mountains. Unlike the rebellious wicked, they receive food, drink and joy, and are given another name. They build houses, plant vineyards, live long, and enjoy their labor's fruit without toiling in vain.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 65

The LORD spares them as one saves new wine found in a cluster, bringing offspring out of Jacob and Judah to inherit his mountains and dwell there. They will eat, drink, rejoice and sing for joy while the wicked suffer, and will be called by another name. They will build and inhabit houses, plant and eat from vineyards, enjoy long lives, and not labor in vain.
