Abstract written by a model

The altar is a square acacia-wood object, five cubits long and wide and three high, overlaid with bronze. Its horns rise from the four corners in one piece with it. It is built hollow with planks, as shown on the mountain, and has bronze vessels, a grating with four rings, and carrying poles.

Chapter 27

The altar is to be made of acacia wood, square, five cubits long and wide and three cubits high, with horns of one piece with it, overlaid with bronze. It is to have bronze vessels (pots, shovels, basins, meat hooks, fire pans), a bronze network grating with four rings, and acacia poles overlaid with bronze for carrying, and it is to be made hollow with planks as shown on the mountain.
