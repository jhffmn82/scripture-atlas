ASTVAT-ERETA in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where ASTVAT-ERETA first appears

Abstract written by a model

ASTVAT-ERETA is the future beneficent Saoshyant. Rising from Lake Kãsava as Ahura Mazda’s friend and Vîspa-taurvairi’s son, he knows victorious knowledge and looks upon Paêsis with intelligence and plenty. He makes bodily creatures rise, withstands destruction, Drug, and evil, delivers living creatures to immortality, and is joined by friends who follow the good law.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XXVI.

ASTVAT-ERETA is the name of the future beneficent one who will make bodily creatures rise up. He will stand against the destruction of bodily creatures, the Drug of the two-footed brood, and the evil done by the faithful.

XIV.

Astvat-ereta will rise up from Lake Kãsava as a friend of Ahura Mazda and a son of Vîspa-taurvairi, knowing the victorious knowledge. He will look upon the living world with the eye of plenty, deliver its creatures to immortality, and be joined by friends who follow the good law.

SAOSHYANT in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where SAOSHYANT first appears

Abstract written by a model

SAOSHYANT, the future Beneficent One, benefits and restores the bodily world with helpers, opposing destruction; the world becomes ever living and increasing, the dead rise, and life and immortality come.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XXVI.

SAOSHYANT is the name of the future Beneficent One. He will benefit the whole bodily world and stand against the destruction of bodily creatures.

XIV.

Saoshyant and his helpers will restore the world. Under his restoration, the world will become ever living and increasing, the dead will rise, and life and immortality will come.
