Abstract written by a model

A peace treaty in which the king, hearing Philip grew reckless in Antioch, swore to uphold all Jewish rights, sacrificed, and honored the sanctuary. He appointed Hegemonides governor. Ptolemais's men, indignant, wanted it annulled, but Lysias persuaded and pacified them.

Chapter 13

After hearing Philip had become reckless in Antioch, the king made an overture of peace, swore to acknowledge all the Jews' rights, offered sacrifice, and honored the sanctuary and the place. He left Hegemonides as governor from Ptolemais to the Gerrenians. The men of Ptolemais, indignant against the Jews, wanted to annul the articles, but Lysias persuaded and pacified them.
