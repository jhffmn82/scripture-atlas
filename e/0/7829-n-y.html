Nan Po Tzŭ K'uei

Nü Yü says that he has learnt TAO and describes teaching it to Pu Liang I. He explains that Pu Liang I passed beyond worldly distinctions, the external world, and awareness of his own existence, ultimately becoming able to enter where life and death are no more.
