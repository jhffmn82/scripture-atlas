Saoshyants

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Saoshyants of the provinces, holy Saoshyants, pious Saoshyants, prophets, the Saoshyants, the prophets for our help

Abstract written by a model

Saoshyants are holy prophets of the provinces, summoned for help and praised as truthful, zealous, glorious, great, and powerful. They are connected with preparation, timely prayer for blessings, sanctification, victory over foes, and benefit to souls. Speakers identify themselves as Saoshyants of the provinces and of the house. In the end, holy Saoshyants are said to serve the speakers, who perform a chant for them.

Saoshyants is in one work on this site, Visparad. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Visparad III.

The pious Saoshyants are summoned as prophets for help. They are described as correct and truthful in speech, zealous, glorious in thought, great, and powerful.

Visparad V (Sp. VI).

The Saoshyants are addressed in connection with preparation, well-timed prayer for blessings, sanctification, and the victorious smiting of foes. Their work is described as beneficial for souls and holy.

Visparad XI (Sp. XII).

The speakers say that they belong to the house as the Saoshyants of the provinces. They identify themselves as holy Saoshyants and associate their preparation with the success of prayer, victory, sanctification, and the well-being of souls.

Visparad XXII (Sp. XXV).

The holy Saoshyants are described as prophets who shall serve the speakers, and the chant is performed for them.
