Abstract written by a model

A force of twelve thousand men, one thousand from each tribe, led by Phinehas, is sent against Midian. They kill every male, the five kings, and Balaam, burn the cities, and take captives and plunder. After purifying themselves for seven days outside camp, they pay a tax on their half; officers report no losses.

Chapter 31

Twelve thousand men, a thousand from each tribe, fight Midian and kill every male, the five kings, and Balaam. They burn Midian's cities and bring back captives and plunder. They must camp outside for seven days and purify themselves, and their half of the plunder is taxed one in five hundred for the LORD. Their officers report that not one man is lacking.
