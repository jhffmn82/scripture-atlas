Abstract written by a model

In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, Glory is offered to Mithra through sacrifice and sought as a blessing for faithful man. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Glory is a blessing made by Mazda, linked with health, strength, brightness, worship, wealth, and the holy Ones, and embodied by Ashi Vanguhi. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, the awful kingly Glory, made by Mazda, conquers, gives health, protects the Aryan nations, restores the world, and belongs to Zarathustra and the Aryan nations.

Glory in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Glory first appears

Abstract written by a model

Glory is a blessing made by Mazda, borne by Verethraghna and linked with health, strength, brightness, worship, wealth, and the holy Ones. The Vârengana feather maintains a person's glory, while Verethraghna may confound a house's glory with cattle wealth. Offered and requested with Vayu, it is finally embodied by Ashi Vanguhi and given to those she attends, including Zarathustra's body.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

XIV. Bahrâm Yast.

Glory is borne by Verethraghna in his various forms and is described as made by Mazda, possessing health and strength. The feather of the Vârengana maintains a person's glory, and Verethraghna can confound the glory of a house with its wealth in cattle.

XV. Râm Yast.

Brightness and glory are repeatedly offered or requested in connection with Vayu’s worship. Vayu promises health made by Mazda and full of glory, and the concluding blessing asks that the worshipper receive brightness and glory and the blissful abode of the holy Ones.

XVII. Ashi Yast.

Glory is associated with Ashi Vanguhi and is given by her to those whom she attends. Ashi Vanguhi is described as Glory in bodily form, and Zarathustra is told that Glory is given to his body.

awful kingly Glory, made by Mazda in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where awful kingly Glory, made by Mazda first appears

Abstract written by a model

The awful kingly Glory, made by Mazda, is conquering, health-giving, wise, happy, and not forcibly seizable. It belongs to Zarathustra and the Aryan nations, cleaving first to Yima, then Mithra, Thraêtaona, Keresâspa, and king Vîstâspa. Powerful and victorious, it is sacrificed to for brightness and glory, protects the Aryan nations, restores the world, and is associated with Saoshyant and Astvat-ereta.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XIX. Zamyâd Yast.

The awful kingly Glory is made by Mazda, powerful, health-giving, victorious, and more destructive than other creatures. It passes among rulers and holy beings, escapes those who try to seize it, and helps restore the world and protect the Aryan nations.

XIV.

The awful kingly Glory made by Mazda is associated with king Vîstâspa, Saoshyant, and Astvat-ereta. It is offered sacrifice for its brightness and glory, and it cannot be forcibly seized.
