Abstract written by a model

Herod is a king troubled by news of a newborn King of the Jews. He consults priests and scribes, deceives the wise men, then, mocked by their departure, orders Bethlehem's boys aged two and under killed. He later dies, and his son Archelaus reigns in Judea.

Chapter 2

King Herod is troubled by news of the newborn King of the Jews, consults the chief priests and scribes, and secretly asks the wise men to report the child's location, claiming he wants to worship him. When mocked by the wise men's departure, he angrily has all male children two years old and under killed in Bethlehem and its surroundings, and he later dies, after which Archelaus reigns in Judea in his place.
