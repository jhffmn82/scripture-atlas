Yasna LXVII (Sp. LXVI).

Yasna LXVII (Sp. LXVI) contains sections 1-4 that refer to Y. XXIII, 1-4, with “I desire to approach with sanctity” replaced by “I offer with sanctity,” and also refer to Y. VII, 24. Its sections 5-7 refer to Y. XXXVIII, 3-5.
