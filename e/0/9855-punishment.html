Abstract written by a model

is_adequate_to bad deeds [of the sinners] Punishment in the hells includes bodily mutilation, burning, boiling, drowning, piercing, crushing, and being eaten by vermin or beasts. It continues for long periods without escape and is adequate to the sinner's deeds.
