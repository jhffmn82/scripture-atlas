transgressions

Also called: disobedience, iniquities, iniquity and sin, our iniquities, our transgressions, the iniquity of us all, the sins of many

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, iniquity and sin are the reason the servant is pierced and crushed; the LORD lays the iniquity of all on the servant, who bears the iniquities and sins of many, justifies many, and intercedes for transgressors. In The Book of Mosiah, transgressions are borne by the Lord's servant, causing the servant to be wounded and stricken; the servant bears the sins of many and intercedes for transgressors.
