Emperor Kō-Rei

person · 1 work · 17 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Heavenly Sovereign, His Augustness Oho-yamato-ne-ko-hiko-futo-ni, This Heavenly Sovereign

Abstract written by a model

Emperor Kō-Rei is a Heavenly Sovereign who dwells at the Palace of Ihodo at Kuruda and rules the Empire. He weds Princess Kuhashi, Princess Chiji-haya-ma-waka, Princess Oho-yamato-kuni-are, and Haheirodo; parents several children; lives one hundred and six years; and has a mausoleum at Umasaka at Kotawoka.
