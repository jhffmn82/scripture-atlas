Abstract written by a model

A man with a discharge is ceremonially unclean whether it runs or has stopped, and he spreads uncleanness to his bed, seat, saddle, and vessels he touches; earthen vessels are broken. Anyone touching him or these must wash and is unclean until evening. After cleansing he counts seven days, bathes in running water, and on the eighth day brings two birds to the priest for atonement.

Chapter 15

A man with a discharge is unclean whether it is running or has stopped, and he makes unclean his bed, whatever he sits on, his saddle, and vessels he touches. Anyone who touches him or these things must wash and bathe and is unclean until evening. After being cleansed he counts seven days, bathes in running water, and on the eighth day brings two birds to the priest.
