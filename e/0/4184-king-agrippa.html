Abstract written by a model

King Agrippa is a king, accompanied by Bernice and known as an expert in Jewish customs and questions. He arrives at Caesarea, greets Festus, and asks to hear Paul. He attends the hearing with great pomp, permits Paul to speak, asks if Paul is trying to make him a Christian, and says Paul might have been freed had he not appealed to Caesar.

Chapter 25

King Agrippa arrives at Caesarea with Bernice and greets Festus. After Festus tells him Paul's case, he says he would like to hear the man himself. The next day he comes with great pomp to the hearing, where Festus addresses him and hopes his examination of Paul will give something to write.

Chapter 26

King Agrippa permits Paul to speak and is described as expert in Jewish customs and questions. Paul asks whether he believes the prophets, and Agrippa responds by asking if Paul is trying to make him a Christian. Afterward he tells Festus that Paul might have been set free if he had not appealed to Caesar.
