Chapter 14

The woman of Tekoa is a wise woman brought by Joab, who coaches her to dress as a long-time mourner and speak to the king. She tells the story of her two sons, wins the king's oath, and then argues that he is guilty for not bringing home his banished one. When the king asks, she admits Joab sent her and praises the king's wisdom as that of an angel of God.
