Chapter 7

The first brother speaks for the others, declaring they are ready to die rather than transgress the laws of their ancestors. The king has his tongue cut out, scalps him, cuts off his extremities, and fries him alive in a pan while his brothers and mother watch. The family exhorts one another to die nobly, citing Moses' song that God will have compassion on his servants.
