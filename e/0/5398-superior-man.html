Also called: A superior man, The superior man, the superior man

Abstract written by a model

A superior man is a person marked by careful speech, principled conduct, and balanced character. He reserves judgment about what he does not know, uses names appropriately, and requires his words to be accurate and capable of being carried out. He is affable without adulation, easy to serve yet difficult to please through ways not accordant with right. He employs men by capacity and ends in dignified ease without pride.
