The fourth animal is an awesome, exceedingly strong beast with great iron teeth, bronze nails and ten horns, differing from all others. It devours and tramples, is interpreted as a fourth kingdom devouring the whole earth, and is finally slain, destroyed and burned with fire.

Chapter 7

The fourth animal is awesome, powerful and exceedingly strong, with great iron teeth and bronze nails, and it devours, breaks in pieces and stamps the residue with its feet. It differs from all the others and has ten horns. It is interpreted as a fourth kingdom that will devour the whole earth, and it is slain, its body destroyed and burned with fire.
