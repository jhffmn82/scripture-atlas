In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, government is exercised by a sage through abstinence from action and disciplining conditions to secure good order. In The Analects of Confucius, Book II (Wei Chang), government should be exercised through virtue, filial and brotherly duties, gravity, kindness, and the advancement of the good.

government in The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics

Lao-Tse · the work’s page · read it where government first appears

Abstract written by a model

Government is exercised by a sage through abstinence from action and disciplining conditions to secure good order. It may follow the Great Tao, avoiding boastful display. Though seeming unwise, it can supply goodness to the people; government that meddles with everything works poorly and brings disappointment.
