the people

group · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: common people, people, the People, the mass of the people, the masses, the multitude, the new people, the whole population

Abstract written by a model

In Chuang Tzŭ, the people, a social class symbolized by a sword, suffer rulers’ oppression but can escape death when a ruler rejects aggression and cultivates inward sincerity. In The Great Learning, the people are to be renovated, instructed, and made tranquil and happy; they follow rulers who lead with benevolence and righteousness, and their support determines whether the kingdom is gained or lost.
