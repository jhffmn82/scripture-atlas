Chapter 73

Asaph understands the wicked's destruction when he enters God's sanctuary and considers their latter end. God throws them down from slippery places, and they are suddenly and completely swept away with terrors, their fantasies despised like a dream on waking. Those far from God and unfaithful to him perish.
