Chapter 10

Ezra and certain heads of fathers' households sit down on the first day of the tenth month to examine the matter, and finish with all the men who had married foreign women by the first day of the first month. The results name offenders among the priests, Levites, singers, gatekeepers, and many families of Israel, ending with the note that all these had taken foreign wives and some had children by them.
