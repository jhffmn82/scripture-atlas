Chapter 21

Rizpah, daughter of Aiah and concubine of Saul, is the mother of Armoni and Mephibosheth, two of the men handed over and hanged. She spreads sackcloth on the rock and from the start of harvest until rain falls keeps birds by day and animals by night from the bodies, and her deed is reported to David.
