Israel's departure from the wilderness of Sinai, begun in the second year, second month, on the twentieth day when the cloud lifted, at the LORD's command by Moses. The tribes march in set order, Judah first and Dan, Asher and Naphtali as rear guard, with the tabernacle and sanctuary carried between, while the ark leads three days' journey.

Chapter 10

The journey begins in the second year, second month, on the twentieth day, when the cloud lifts, and Israel leaves the wilderness of Sinai according to the LORD's commandment by Moses. The tribes march in order: Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun first, then the Gershonites and Merarites with the tabernacle, then Reuben, Simeon, and Gad, the Kohathites with the sanctuary, Ephraim, Manasseh, and Benjamin, and finally Dan, Asher, and Naphtali as rear guard. The ark leads them three days' journey from the Mount of the LORD.
