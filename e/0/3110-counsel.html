counsel in The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where counsel first appears

Abstract written by a model

Counsel is a topic of advice praised by every counselor and meant to precede every action. Chapter 37 warns against seeking it from the jealous or biased, upholds one's own heart's counsel as most faithful, and urges asking the Most High to direct one's way.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 37

The text says counsel should be withheld from the jealous and not sought from those with a biased stake, such as a coward on war or a merchant on business. The counsel of one's own heart is to be made to stand as most faithful, reason and counsel should precede every work and action, and the Most High should be asked to direct one's way.
