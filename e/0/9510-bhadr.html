Abstract written by a model

Bhadrâ, king Kausalika’s daughter and the Purôhita’s wife, protects Harikêsa-Bala by stopping angry young men, warning against harming the ascetic, and leading Brahmanas to appease him.

Twelfth Lecture

Bhadrâ, the daughter of king Kausalika and the Brahmana’s wife, stopped the angry young men and identified Harikêsa as the ascetic who had refused her. She warned that harming a monk would bring ruin, urged the Brahmanas to seek protection, and helped lead them to appease him.
