Narrative, section by section written by a model

XXVI.

The men of the primitive law are worshipped as those who have been, are, or will be in houses, boroughs, towns, and countries. They are credited with obtaining houses, holiness, the Mãthra, the blessedness of the soul, and all perfections of goodness.
