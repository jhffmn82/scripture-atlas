Jacob's burial instruction

Also called: Bury me with my fathers, He instructed them, Jacob's burial request, Swear to me, bury me in their burying place, charging his sons

Abstract written by a model

Jacob's burial instruction is a charge made as his death nears: he asks Joseph to swear not to bury him in Egypt but to carry him to his ancestors' burying place. Joseph swears. Jacob then charges all his sons to bury him with his fathers in the cave of Machpelah, bought by Abraham, and dies after finishing.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 47

As his death nears, Israel asks Joseph to place his hand under his thigh and deal kindly and truly by not burying him in Egypt but carrying him to his fathers' burying place. Joseph agrees, and when Israel asks him to swear, he swears.

Chapter 49

Jacob instructs his sons to bury him with his fathers in the cave in the field of Ephron the Hittite at Machpelah, bought by Abraham as a burial place. He gives this charge because he is about to be gathered to his people, and when he finishes charging them he dies.
