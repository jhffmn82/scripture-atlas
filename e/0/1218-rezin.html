Rezin

person · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Rezin king of Syria, Rezin the king of Syria, the king of Syria

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Rezin, king of Syria, came against Jerusalem with Pekah, besieged Ahaz without overcoming him, recovered Elath for Syria, drove the Jews out, and was killed when the king of Assyria took Damascus. In The Book of Isaiah, Rezin, king of Syria, went with Pekah to war against Jerusalem but could not prevail; he was described as the head of Damascus, the head of Syria.
