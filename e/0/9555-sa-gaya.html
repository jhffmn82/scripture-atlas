Sañgaya

Also called: I am Sañgaya, my name is Sañgaya

Abstract written by a model

Sañgaya, a Gôtama, is king of Kâmpilya with numerous troops and war-chariots. After descending from Brahmalôka and having been a god in Mahâprâna heaven, he hunts in Kêsara-park, kills a deer seeking refuge, learns the Law from Gardabhâli, renounces power, becomes a monk, and knows soul and life hereafter.

Sañgaya is in one work on this site, Uttaradhyayana Sutra. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Eighteenth Lecture.

Sañgaya was king of Kâmpilya and went hunting in the Kêsara-park, where he killed a deer that had fled to a houseless ascetic. Taught the Law by the monk Gardabhâli, he gave up his kingly power and adopted the faith of the Ginas.
