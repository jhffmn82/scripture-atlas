James

Also called: James the son of Zebedee

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, James, son of Zebedee and brother of John, is mending nets in a boat with them when Jesus calls him; he immediately leaves his father and follows Jesus. In The Good News According to Mark, James, son of Zebedee and brother of John, asks Jesus for seats at his right and left in glory; Jesus says they will share his cup and baptism, but the seats are prepared.
