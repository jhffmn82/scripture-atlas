firstborn (of Egypt) in Exodus

Abstract written by a model

The firstborn of Egypt, from Pharaoh's heir to the servant's and the captive's, plus livestock, are the target of a foretold judgment. In Chapter 11 their death is announced, with Israel's people and animals spared. In Chapter 12 the LORD strikes them at midnight, leaving no Egyptian house without a dead.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

All the firstborn in Egypt are foretold to die, from Pharaoh's firstborn on the throne to the firstborn of the female servant behind the mill, and all the firstborn of livestock. Israel's people and animals will be spared.

Chapter 12

At midnight the LORD strikes all the firstborn in Egypt, from Pharaoh's firstborn on the throne to the firstborn of the captive in the dungeon, and the firstborn of livestock. Their deaths leave no house in Egypt without one dead.
