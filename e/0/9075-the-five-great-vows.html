the five great vows

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: These great vows, great vows

Abstract written by a model

The five great vows concern abstaining from killing living beings, lying speech, taking what is not given, sexual pleasures, and attachments. Each has five clauses and must be practised according to the precept. Called a place of peace, great teachers, and producers of disinterestedness, they are proclaimed as light illuminating three worlds and repelling darkness.

The Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra endowed with the highest knowledge and intuition

The five great vows concern abstinence from killing living beings, lying speech, taking what is not given, sexual pleasures, and attachments. Each vow has five clauses and is to be correctly practised, followed, executed, explained, established, and effected according to the precept.

Fourth Part. / Sixteenth Lecture, Called the Liberation.

The great vows are called the place of peace, the great teachers, and the producers of disinterestedness. They have been proclaimed in all quarters of the earth as light that illumines the three worlds and repels darkness.
