Abstract written by a model

In Surah 6: The Cattle (Al-An'aam), the punishment afflicts those who deny Allah's revelations and disobey, arriving from above, beneath, openly, or unexpectedly. In Surah 8: The Spoils of War (Al-Anfaal), punishment comes to those who oppose Allah and His messenger, disbelieve, or act wrongfully, including the torment of the Fire and angels smiting those who disbelieve. In Surah 11: Hud, punishment is an inescapable doom that may be delayed until a reckoned time, then cannot be averted, befalling those who reject Allah, commit wrongdoing, or follow corrupt commands through various forms of destruction and the Fire. In Surah 20: Taa-Haa, punishment is threatened for those who lie against Allah, deny revelation, turn away, or come to their Lord guilty; Pharaoh also threatens punishment, while Allah's punishment includes hell and the burden borne on the Day of Resurrection. In Surah 32: The Prostration (As-Sajda), the doom of immortality is tasted because of what people used to do and because they forgot the meeting of their day. In Surah 34: Sheba (Saba), punishment comes to those who challenge Allah's revelations, disbelieve, are ungrateful, or deviate from His command, including painful doom, flaming Fire, and the doom of the Fire. In Surah 40: The Forgiver (Al-Ghaafir), punishment is described as severe and comes upon disbelieving peoples, encompasses Pharaoh's family, and includes the Fire and the most awful doom. In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), the doom of the Hereafter is more shameful than worldly punishment, and those who deny Allah's revelations taste an awful and hard punishment without help.

and 2 more works

In Surah 52: The Mount (At-Tur), punishment awaits those who do wrong beyond the punishment of the day when they are thunder-stricken. In Surah 59: The Exile (Al-Hashr), punishment is assigned to the People of the Scripture who opposed Allah and His messenger, and to hypocrites and disbelievers as painful consequences, with the Hereafter's punishment being the Fire.
