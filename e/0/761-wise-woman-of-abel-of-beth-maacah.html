Chapter 20

The wise woman calls from the city to Joab and argues that Abel is a peaceable, faithful city and a mother in Israel that should not be destroyed. After Joab says he wants only Sheba, she promises his head will be thrown over the wall. She persuades the people in her wisdom, and they behead Sheba.
