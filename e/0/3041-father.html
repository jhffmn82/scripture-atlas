Chapter 3

The father speaks as the instructor, calling his children to hear him and do what they hear. He is to be honored in deed and word, and his blessing establishes the houses of his children. He is to be helped in old age and treated with patience if his understanding fails, and one who forsakes him is as a blasphemer.
