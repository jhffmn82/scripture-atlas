constant excellence

Also called: the constant excellence, the unchanging excellence, unchanging excellence

Abstract written by a model

Constant excellence is retained by one who knows his manhood's strength yet maintains female feebleness, displayed by one who knows how white attracts yet remains within black's shade, and completed by one who knows how glory shines yet loves disgrace.
