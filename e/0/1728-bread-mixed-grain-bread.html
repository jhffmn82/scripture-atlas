Chapter 4

The bread is made from wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and spelt in one vessel, eaten for 390 days at twenty shekels by weight per day. It is baked as barley cakes in the people's sight over dung, changed from human to cow's dung after the protest. It symbolizes the unclean, rationed bread of Israel and the breaking of Jerusalem's staff of bread.
