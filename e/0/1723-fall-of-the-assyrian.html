Chapter 31

The fall of the Assyrian happens because of its pride and wickedness, when the LORD delivers it to the mighty one of the nations and foreigners cut it off. Its branches fall across the land and the peoples leave its shadow. The fall causes mourning, makes the nations shake, and serves as a warning to the other trees not to exalt themselves, a fate Pharaoh is told he will share.
