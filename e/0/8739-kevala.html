Kevala

Abstract written by a model

Kevala is the highest knowledge and intuition: infinite, supreme, unobstructed, unimpeded, complete, and perfect or full. Mahâvîra obtained it under Uttaraphalgunî during his thirteenth year; Indrabhûti attained it on the night of Mahâvîra's death. Pârsva attained it under a Dhâtaki tree on the eighty-fourth day after entering houselessness. Arishtanemi attained it fifty-five days after renunciation and lived nearly seven centuries as a Kevalin. Rishabha reached it after fasting three and a half days and deep meditation.

Life of Pârsva.

Kevala was the infinite, supreme, unobstructed, unimpeded, complete, and full highest knowledge and intuition. Pârsva attained it under a Dhâtaki tree on the eighty-fourth day after entering houselessness.

Life of Arishtanemi.

Arishtanemi attained the highest knowledge and intuition called Kevala on the fifty-fifth day after his renunciation. He lived something less than seven centuries as a Kevalin.

Life of Rishabha.

Rishabha reached the highest knowledge and intuition called Kevala after fasting three and a half days, engaging in deep meditation, and attaining infinite knowledge.
