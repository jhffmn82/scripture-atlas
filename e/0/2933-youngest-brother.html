Abstract written by a model

The youngest brother is offered wealth, honor, and friendship by Antiochus if he abandons ancestral ways, but refuses. Loyal to the law given through Moses rather than the king, he warns the king he will not escape God's hands. Treated worse than the others, he dies pure, trusting the Lord and praying for the nation.

Chapter 7

The youngest brother refuses the king's oaths of enrichment, high honor, and friendship if he would abandon his ancestors' ways. Before his mother finishes speaking, he says he obeys the law given through Moses rather than the king, and he tells the king he will not escape God's hands. He gives up body and soul praying that God become gracious to the nation, is handled worse than the rest, and dies pure, trusting the Lord.
