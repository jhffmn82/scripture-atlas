Chapter 13

Amnon, David's son, loves Tamar and becomes sick over her, then follows Jonadab's plan to feign illness and get her sent to him. He rapes her, then hates her more than he had loved her and has his servant bolt her out. Two years later he goes to Absalom's sheep shearing at David's permission and is killed by Absalom's servants.
