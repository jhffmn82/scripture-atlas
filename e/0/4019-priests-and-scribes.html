Chapter 20

The priests and scribes, with the elders, challenge Jesus' authority but cannot answer his question about John's baptism for fear of the people. They seek to lay hands on him after the parable, send spies to trap him, and are warned by Jesus of greater condemnation for seeking honor and devouring widows' houses.
