Chi Ch'ê

person · 1 work · 1 quoted fact · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

teaches government of the Sage Chi Ch'ê criticizes Chiang Lü Mien's advice as ineffective and compares it to a mantis trying to stop a carriage. He explains that the government of the perfect Sage works by influencing people according to their natural dispositions, so that they become virtuous without being conscious of the influence.
