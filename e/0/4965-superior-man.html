superior man

person · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 2 connections

Abstract written by a model

The superior man values righteousness above valour and does not associate with a man guilty of doing evil. During the three years' mourning, he avoids pleasant food and music, and he does not feel at ease in comfortable lodging.
