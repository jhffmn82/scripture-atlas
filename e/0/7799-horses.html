horses

Abstract written by a model

Horses live on dry land, eat grass, drink water, have four feet, hoofs and hair for cold conditions, and show pleasure or anger physically. Poor care and unequal tasks cause injury or exhaustion. Poh Loh’s restraints, training, grooming, and whipping kill many and deprave their natural dispositions.

CHAPTER IX.

Horses naturally live on dry land, eat grass, drink water, and express pleasure or anger through simple physical actions. Poh Loh’s branding, clipping, hoof-paring, halters, shackles, stables, training, grooming, and whipping kill many horses and deprave their natural dispositions.
