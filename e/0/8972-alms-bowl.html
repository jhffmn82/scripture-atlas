alms-bowl

Abstract written by a model

An alms-bowl may be made of bottle-gourd, wood, or clay, and may be used, left off, or unwanted by others. Expensive materials or bands, food or drink, and cold water offered by the householder make it impure and unacceptable. It is inspected, emptied of living beings or seeds, wiped dry, and protected from moisture and heavy rain.

alms-bowl is in one work on this site, Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Sixth Lecture, Called Begging for a Bowl. / First Lesson. .. Second Lesson.

An alms-bowl may be made of bottle-gourd, wood, or clay and may be used, left off, or unwanted by others. Bowls made of expensive materials, containing precious-material bands, filled with food or drink, or offered with cold water are impure and unacceptable. The bowl must be inspected, emptied of living beings or seeds, wiped when dry, and protected from moisture and heavy rain.
