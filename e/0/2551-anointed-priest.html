Abstract written by a model

The anointed priest is a role whose sin can bring guilt on the people. He must offer a young bull without defect as a sin offering, performing blood rites: sprinkling seven times before the veil, applying blood to the incense altar's horns, and pouring the rest at the burnt offering altar's base. Atonement and forgiveness follow.

Chapter 4

If the anointed priest sins so as to bring guilt on the people, he offers a young bull without defect as a sin offering. He also performs the blood rites: he sprinkles blood seven times before the veil, puts blood on the horns of the incense altar, and pours out the rest at the base of the altar of burnt offering. In the congregation's case he carries out the same rites, and in the cases of the ruler and the common person he applies the blood and makes atonement.
