Chapter 9

Six men come from the way of the upper gate toward the north, each with a slaughter weapon in hand, and stand beside the bronze altar. They are ordered to follow the man in linen through the city and kill utterly without sparing or pity, defile the house and fill the courts with the slain, avoiding anyone with the mark. They begin with the old men before the house and strike in the city.
