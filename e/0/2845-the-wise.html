Abstract written by a model

The wise is a figure portrayed across Chapters 7, 8 and 10. His heart dwells in the house of mourning, and he welcomes rebuke over fools' song. Wisdom strengthens him beyond ten rulers, yet extortion can make him foolish. Wisdom makes his face shine, and his heart knows time and procedure, though he cannot find out the work under the sun. His heart is at his right hand, his words gracious, but a little folly can outweigh his honor.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

The heart of the wise is in the house of mourning, and the wise welcome rebuke over the song of fools. Extortion can make a wise man foolish, and wisdom gives the wise man more strength than ten rulers in a city.

Chapter 8

The wise man's wisdom makes his face shine and changes its hardness. His wise heart knows the time and procedure, yet even if he thinks he can comprehend the work done under the sun, he will not be able to find it.

Chapter 10

The wise man's heart is at his right hand, and the words of his mouth are gracious. His wisdom and honor can be outweighed by a little folly, and skill is said to bring success.
