Abstract written by a model

Tyre's downfall is a catastrophic ruin in the heart of the seas, caused by the east wind. Its riches, crew and wares sink; island inhabitants are astonished, kings terrified, and merchants hiss in mockery, as Tyre meets a terrible end and will be no more.

Chapter 27

The east wind breaks Tyre in the heart of the seas, and its riches, wares, crew, and company fall into the sea in the day of its ruin. The pasture lands shake at the pilots' cry, island inhabitants are astonished, kings are horribly afraid, and merchants hiss at Tyre, which has come to a terrible end and will be no more.
