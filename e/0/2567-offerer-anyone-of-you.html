Leviticus / Chapter 1

The offerer brings the offering of livestock to the door of the Tent of Meeting so that he may be accepted before the LORD. He lays his hand on the head of the burnt offering to make atonement, kills the animal, skins it, cuts it into pieces, and washes the innards and legs.
