Abstract written by a model

A close relation or friend who secretly entices others to serve other gods. The person enticed must show no consent, pity, or concealment, and must kill him by stoning, hand first, then all the people, for seeking to draw them from the LORD.

Chapter 13

A brother, son, daughter, wife, or friend as close as one's own soul who secretly urges serving other gods is not to be consented to, listened to, pitied, spared, or concealed. The person enticed must kill him, with his hand first on him and afterward the hands of all the people, by stoning, because he sought to draw them from the LORD.
