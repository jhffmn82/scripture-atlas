Abstract written by a model

The four living creatures stand in the middle of and around the throne, covered with eyes before, behind, around, and within, each with six wings. They resemble a lion, a calf, a man, and a flying eagle. In chapter 4 they ceaselessly praise God day and night, saying "Holy, holy, holy." In chapter 5 they fall before the Lamb with the elders, hold harps and golden incense bowls, sing the new song, and say "Amen."

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

The four living creatures are in the middle of and around the throne, full of eyes before, behind, around, and within, each with six wings. The first is like a lion, the second like a calf, the third has a face like a man, and the fourth is like a flying eagle. They have no rest day and night, saying "Holy, holy, holy is the Lord God, the Almighty," and they give glory, honor, and thanks to the one on the throne.

Chapter 5

The four living creatures are around the throne, with the Lamb standing in their midst. They fall down before the Lamb with the elders, each having a harp and golden bowls of incense, and they sing the new song. They say "Amen" to the praise offered by every created thing.
