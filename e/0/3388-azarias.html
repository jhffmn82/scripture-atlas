Abstract written by a model

Azarias, also called Azariah, is a companion of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego. Standing in the fire, he prays, blessing the Lord, confessing Israel's sins as just judgment, and asking deliverance; the Lord's angel then descends into the furnace with him and his fellows.

Chapter 3

Azarias stood in the midst of the fire and prayed, blessing the Lord, confessing that Israel's suffering was just judgment for its sins, and asking God not to annul his covenant but to deliver them and put their enemies to shame.
