Also called: Nan Yung

Abstract written by a model

Nan Yung Ch'u, a disciple troubled by moral dilemmas, seeks TAO and the art of preserving life. He first hears TAO only with his ears, cultivates good and eliminates evil within himself, then seeks further instruction from Kêng Sang Ch'u and Lao Tzŭ after self-cultivation leaves him confused.

CHAPTER XXIII.

Nan Yung Ch'u asked how he could attain TAO while avoiding the harms caused by ignorance, knowledge, lack of charity, charity, and duty to his neighbour. After receiving limited instruction from Kêng Sang Ch'u, he travelled to Lao Tzŭ, described his confusion, and sought the art of preserving life. He attempted self-cultivation but remained troubled until Lao Tzŭ continued his instruction.
