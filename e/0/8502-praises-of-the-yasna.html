Praises of the Yasna

Also called: Praises of the Yasna which were the production of the ancient world, all the Praises of the Yasna, the Praises of the Yasna, the Yasna Praises, the entire collection of the Praises of the Yasna

Abstract written by a model

The Praises of the Yasna are an entire collection and productions of the world of old, instituted in the world of yore. They are recollected and put in use, then learned and taught in the present ritual tradition. Worship unfolds through holding them in mind, repeating, remembering, reciting, chanting, and complete offering, which furthers the world through grace. Their careful language has reached its object; worshippers sacrifice to the collection and offer homage to beautiful forms of Ahura Mazda, the stars, and the highest of the high.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna LV (Sp. LIV).

The Praises of the Yasna are worshipped as productions of the ancient world that are recollected, learned, taught, held in mind, repeated, remembered, recited, and worshipped. Their recitation, memorised recital, chanting, and complete offering are also worshipped because they further the world through grace.

Yasna LVIII (Sp. LVII).

The Praises of the Yasna are described as having a careful structure of language that has reached its object. The worshippers sacrifice to their entire collection and offer homage to the most beautiful forms of Ahura Mazda, including the stars and the highest of the high.
