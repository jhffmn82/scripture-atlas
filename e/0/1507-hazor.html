Abstract written by a model

Hazor is a secure, gateless nation targeted by Nebuchadnezzar, who has planned against it. Its people are told to flee, its camels and livestock become plunder, and it ends as a perpetual desolation, a dwelling place of jackals.

Chapter 49

The kingdoms of Hazor are struck by Nebuchadnezzar, and its inhabitants are told to flee and dwell in the depths because he has planned against them. Hazor is described as a nation at ease without gates or bars, whose camels and livestock become plunder. It will become a dwelling place of jackals, a perpetual desolation where no one lives.
