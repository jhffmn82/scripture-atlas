Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Nebuzaradan serves as captain of the guard and burns Jerusalem, carries away its remaining people and treasures, leaves some of the poorest, and brings officials to the king at Riblah. In The Book of Jeremiah, Nebuzaradan serves as captain of the guard, burns Jerusalem, carries off its people and temple treasures, leaves some poor inhabitants as farmers, brings officials to Riblah for execution, releases Jeremiah, and later carries away 745 Jews.

Nebuzaradan in The Book of Jeremiah

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Nebuzaradan first appears

Abstract written by a model

Nebuzaradan is a captain of the guard serving Nebuchadnezzar, king of Babylon. In the fifth month of the nineteenth year he enters Jerusalem and burns the LORD's house, the king's house and the great houses. He carries off the rest of the people, including deserters, and takes the temple's gold and silver. He leaves some of the poorest as vineyard keepers and farmers, giving them fields. He brings Seraiah, Zephaniah and others to Riblah for execution. On the king's command he takes Jeremiah from the court of the guard, releases him from his chains, acknowledges that the LORD brought the evil because of sin, offers him care in Babylon or freedom, and commits him to Gedaliah with food and a present. He later carries away 745 Jews in the twenty-third year.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 39

Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard carried the rest of the people of the city, including the deserters, captive into Babylon, but left some of the poor who had nothing in Judah and gave them vineyards and fields. On Nebuchadnezzar's command, he joined other officers in taking Jeremiah out of the court of the guard and committing him to Gedaliah.

Chapter 40

Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, releases Jeremiah from his chains and tells him the LORD brought this evil on the place because of sin and disobedience. He offers Jeremiah care in Babylon or freedom to go anywhere, directs him to Gedaliah, and sends him off with food and a present.

Chapter 52

Nebuzaradan, captain of the guard, came into Jerusalem in the fifth month of Nebuchadnezzar's nineteenth year and burned the LORD's house, the king's house and the great houses. He carried away the poorest and the rest of the people, left some of the poorest as vineyard keepers and farmers, took the temple's gold and silver, and brought Seraiah, Zephaniah and others to Riblah to be put to death. He also carried away 745 Jews in the twenty-third year.
