priests (of Babylonian idols)

group · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: priests, their priests

Abstract written by a model

The priests of Babylonian idols are portrayed as exploiting the idols they serve. They take gold and silver for themselves, give some to prostitutes, sell offered sacrifices, and clothe their own wives and children in the idols' garments. They lock temples against robbers, mourn with torn clothes and shaven heads, and plan to flee disasters, leaving the idols to burn.

The Letter of Jeremiah

The priests take gold and silver from the idols to spend on themselves, give some to prostitutes, and sell the sacrifices offered to them. Their wives salt away part of the offerings and give none to the poor, and the priests also strip the idols' garments to clothe their own wives and children. They lock their temples against robbers and sit with torn clothes and shaven heads, and when war, plague, or fire comes they plan to hide or flee, leaving the idols to burn.
