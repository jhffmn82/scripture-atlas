salvation

topic · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Salvation, guarding for salvation, the state of salvation

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, salvation is a state granted by Ahura as the answer to every saint who exists, is coming into existence, or shall exist. In Yasna, salvation is a universal blessing received by humankind when foretold punishment and blessing begin, taught by Ahura Mazda in accordance with Asha through Good Mind and inspired words, belonging to those who choose the righteous way, maintain Righteousness, receive Ahura Mazda’s gifts, and worship the Fire in truth with prescribed ritual materials, and contrasting with the fate of followers of the Lie and the wicked.

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where salvation first appears

Abstract written by a model

Salvation is a universal blessing received by humankind when foretold punishment and blessing begin. Ahura Mazda teaches it in accordance with Asha, through Good Mind and inspired words. It belongs to those who choose the righteous way, maintain Righteousness, receive Ahura Mazda’s gifts, and worship the Fire in truth with prescribed ritual materials; it contrasts with the fate of followers of the Lie and the wicked.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XXX.

Salvation is promised when the foretold courses of punishment for the wicked and blessings for the righteous begin. The unit presents it as the portion of those who choose and follow the righteous way.

Yasna XLIII.

Salvation is announced for this man and for whosoever may be. It is connected with the gifts of Ahura Mazda, the maintenance of Righteousness, and the blessings granted to the righteous.

Yasna LI.

Salvation is taught through the wisdom Ahura Mazda conceived in accordance with Asha and through the inspired words of Good Mind. It is contrasted with the fate of those who follow the Lie and the wicked.

Yasna LXII (Sp. LXI).

Salvation is promised to the man who worships the Fire in verity and truth while bringing the prescribed ritual materials.
