unbelief

topic · 6 works · 4 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: an evil heart of unbelief, dwindle in unbelief, idolatry, unbelief and idolatry, veil of unbelief

Abstract written by a model

In The Letter to the Hebrews, unbelief is warned against as an evil heart that leads to falling away from the living God and kept the wilderness generation from entering God’s rest. In The First Book of Nephi, unbelief causes the seed of Lehi’s brethren to become dark, loathsome, filthy, idle, and full of abominations. (The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry) In The Second Book of Nephi, unbelief, combined with pride and works of darkness, causes many of Nephi’s people to dwindle and be destroyed. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon’s Own Record, unbelief and idolatry cause the seed of the people to become dark, filthy, and loathsome. (The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record) In The Book of Ether, unbelief causes knowledge and great things to remain hidden and requires the veil of unbelief to be rent before great and marvelous things can be made manifest. In The Book of Moroni, unbelief can cause the spiritual gifts and power of God to be done away among people.
