Abstract written by a model

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Rag Asa .. Asa Ashtapadi

Salvation is obtained through God’s Name, the Guru’s guidance, devotion, and love of God. It frees people from worldly entanglements, sin, transmigration, fear, and death, and brings them into God’s presence.

Ramkali .. Ramkali ki War I

Salvation is not obtained through outward austerities, ablutions, calculations, disguises, or religious claims alone. It is obtained through God's Name, the Guru's instruction, good works, truth, and devotion.

Bhairo

Salvation is not obtained without God's name. The unit also says that the Guru's instruction is necessary for salvation.
