His Augustness Brave-Rustic-Illuminator

person · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Brave-Rustic-Illuminator is a child of His Augustness Ame-no-hohi, one of five children born afterwards. He is identified as ancestor of rulers in Idzumo, Musashi, Upper and Lower Unakami, Izhimu, and Tohotsu-Afumi, and of the departmental suzerains of Tsu.

His Augustness Brave-Rustic-Illuminator is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
