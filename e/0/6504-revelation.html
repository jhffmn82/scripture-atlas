Abstract written by a model

In Surah 6: The Cattle (Al-An'aam), the revelation is denied and derided by disbelievers, who call it magic or fables; it gives warning, confirms earlier Scripture, and makes the way of the unrighteous manifest. In Surah 13: The Thunder (Ar-Ra'd), revelation is from Muhammad’s Lord and is the truth, revealed as a decisive utterance in Arabic; Muhammad is commanded to recite it to a nation whose members disbelieve in the Beneficent. In Surah 14: Abraham (Ibrahim), revelation is given to Moses and sent through messengers to make Allah’s message clear to their peoples, bringing mankind from darkness to light by Allah’s permission. In Surah 15: The Rock (Al-Hijr), revelation is guarded by Allah and sent in appointed measure; Muhammad is commanded to proclaim what has been revealed, and those who divide it are warned that they will be questioned. In Surah 17: The Night Journey (Al-Israa), revelation is sent by Allah to Muhammad and divided for recitation to mankind at intervals; Allah states that what He reveals is by His mercy and that He can withdraw it if He wills. In Surah 20: Taa-Haa, revelation comes from Allah and includes the Qur’an, the commands given to Moses, and the guidance sent to people, serving as a reminder, warning, and proof. In Surah 40: The Forgiver (Al-Ghaafir), the revelation of the Scripture is from Allah, the Mighty, the Knower, establishing Allah’s authority and calling people toward worship of Him alone. In Surah 53: The Star (An-Najm), revelation is an inspiration given to Muhammad by Allah through the mighty teacher, and Muhammad sees one of the greater revelations of his Lord.

and 2 more works

In Surah 57: The Iron (Al-Hadid), Allah reveals scripture with His messengers and also reveals the Balance so that mankind might observe right measure, sending clear revelations to bring people from darkness into light. In Surah 76: Man (Al-Insaan), revelation is identified with Allah’s revealing the Qur’an and presented as an admonishment through which whoever wills may choose a way unto his Lord.
