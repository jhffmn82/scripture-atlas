Chapter 4

The widow of one of the sons of the prophets cries out to Elisha because a creditor is coming to take her two children as slaves. Following Elisha's instructions, she fills borrowed containers with oil behind a closed door. Elisha then tells her to sell the oil, pay her debt, and live on the rest with her sons.
