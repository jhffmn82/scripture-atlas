the cup of trembling

Also called: cup of the LORD's wrath, the bowl of the cup of my wrath, the bowl of the cup of staggering, the cup of his wrath, the cup of staggering, the dregs of the cup of my fury

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Isaiah, the cup of trembling is the LORD's wrath that Jerusalem has drained, which the LORD takes from her hand and gives to those who afflict her. In The Second Book of Nephi, the cup of trembling contains the Lord's fury; Jerusalem drinks its dregs until the Lord takes it from her and gives it to her afflictors.
