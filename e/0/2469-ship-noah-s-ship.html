Abstract written by a model

Noah's ship is a vessel God commanded him to build from gopher wood, sealed with pitch, three hundred cubits long, with a side door, a roof, and three levels. Noah, his household, and pairs of animals and birds enter, and the LORD shuts them in. As waters rise, it floats, carrying the only survivors. It rests on Ararat on the seventeenth day of the seventh month. Noah opens its window, removes its covering, and all aboard leave at God's command.

Chapter 6

God commanded Noah to make the ship of gopher wood, with rooms, sealed inside and outside with pitch. It was to be three hundred cubits long, fifty wide, and thirty high, with a roof finished to a cubit upward, a door in its side, and lower, second, and third levels.

Chapter 7

Noah, his household, and pairs of animals and birds enter the ship, and the LORD shuts them in. As the waters increase, the ship is lifted up above the earth and floats on the surface of the waters. Those inside it are the only survivors.

Chapter 8

The ship comes to rest on Ararat's mountains on the seventeenth day of the seventh month. Noah opens its window to send out birds and later removes its covering to see dry ground. Everyone and everything aboard goes out of it at God's command.
