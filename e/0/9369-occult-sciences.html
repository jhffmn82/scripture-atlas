Abstract written by a model

Occult sciences include divination, signs, incantations, conjuring, oblations, martial arts, astrology, spells, and similar practices. Practised for worldly needs and pleasures, they are called wrong, unworthy, and mistaken, leading after death to Asuras and evildoers, then to disabilities including deafness and dumbness.

Second Lecture, Called on Activity.

Occult sciences include divination, interpretation of signs, incantations, conjuring, oblations, martial arts, astrological practices, spells, and other similar practices. Men practise them for food, drink, clothes, lodging, beds, and pleasures, but the text calls them wrong, unworthy, and mistaken sciences leading to rebirth among Asuras and evildoers and later to conditions such as deafness, dumbness, blindness, or night-blindness.
