Yearly festivals

Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Yearly festivals receive offerings for sacrifice, homage, propitiation, and praise, unfolding through Maidhyô-zaremaya, Maidhyô-shema, Patishahya, Ayâthrima, Maidhyâirya, and Hamaspathmaêdhaya. In Visparad, Yearly festivals, holy lords of ritual order, include Maidhyô-zaremaya through Hamaspathmaêdhaya.

Visparad / Visparad I.
