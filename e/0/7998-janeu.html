janeu

object · 1 work · 13 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: sacred thread, string, thread

Abstract written by a model

Janeu is described as two threads: a spiritual thread for the soul, made from mercy, contentment, continence, and truth, and a purchased cotton ritual thread. The cotton thread costs four damris, is worn by a Brahman around a man’s neck, grows old, is discarded and replaced, and falls at death. The lasting spiritual janeu remains intact, clean, unburned, and present, qualifying one for entry into God’s court.

Slok XV .. Pauri XV

The janeu is described in two forms: a lasting spiritual thread made from mercy, contentment, continence, and truth, and a purchased cotton thread put on in a ceremony. The cotton janeu becomes old, is thrown away, and falls when a person dies, while the true thread does not break or become soiled, burned, or lost.
