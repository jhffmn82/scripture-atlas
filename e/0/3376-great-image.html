Abstract written by a model

A mighty, bright, terrifying great image with a head of fine gold, silver chest and arms, bronze belly and thighs, iron legs, and feet of part iron, part clay. A stone cut without hands strikes the feet, shattering it into chaff the wind carries away, leaving no trace.

Chapter 2

The great image is mighty, bright, and terrifying, with a head of fine gold, chest and arms of silver, belly and thighs of bronze, legs of iron, and feet part iron and part clay. A stone cut without hands strikes its feet and breaks it all to pieces, which become like chaff carried away by the wind so that no place is found for them.
