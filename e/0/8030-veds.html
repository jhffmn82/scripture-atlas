Also called: four Veds, the Veds

Abstract written by a model

In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), the four Veds are described as religious compositions that Hindus consider true, yet their study reveals four different doctrines and teachings and is insufficient for salvation. In Hymns of Guru Nanak, the Veds are read by Pandits, but reading alone neither destroys sinful inclinations nor dispels ignorance without understanding.
