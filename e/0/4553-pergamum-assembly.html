Abstract written by a model

A community dwelling where Satan's throne is, it holds firmly to Jesus' name and kept its faith even when Antipas, a faithful witness, was killed. Rebuked because some follow Balaam's and the Nicolaitans' teaching, it is told to repent or face war by the sword.

Chapter 2

It dwells where Satan's throne is, holds firmly to the speaker's name, and did not deny the faith in the days of Antipas, his faithful witness who was killed among them. It is rebuked because some hold the teaching of Balaam and of the Nicolaitans, and it is told to repent or he will come quickly and war against them with the sword of his mouth.
