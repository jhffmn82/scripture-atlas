Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the fathers were the wilderness generation who tested the LORD and were barred from entering his rest. In The Letter to the Hebrews, those who heard and disobeyed were the Israelites who rebelled against God and failed to enter his rest.

those who heard and disobeyed (the Israelites) in The Letter to the Hebrews

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where those who heard and disobeyed (the Israelites) first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Israelites are the fathers who left Egypt under Moses, heard God's word, yet rebelled and tested him for forty years while seeing his deeds. Because they erred in heart and lacked faith, the good news did not profit them. God, displeased, swore in wrath that they would not enter his rest. Their bodies fell in the wilderness, a result of unbelief and disobedience.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

These are the fathers who came out of Egypt led by Moses, heard and rebelled, tested God, and saw his deeds for forty years. God was displeased with them because they erred in their heart and did not know his ways; their bodies fell in the wilderness, and they could not enter his rest because of unbelief.

Chapter 4

Those to whom the good news was preached before failed to enter God's rest because of disobedience. The word they heard did not profit them because it was not mixed with faith, and God swore in his wrath that they would not enter his rest.
