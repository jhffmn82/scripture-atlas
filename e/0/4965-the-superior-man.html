the superior man

person · 1 work · 13 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a superior man

Abstract written by a model

The superior man honors the talented and virtuous, bears with all, praises the good, and pities the incompetent. He does not carry inferior studies into remote applications, undergoes changes in outward manner while remaining firm in speech, and has faults that all can see but also changes that cause all to look up to him.
