pride

topic · 3 works · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Arrogance, Everyone who is proud in heart, Pride, arrogant spirit, proud in heart, the proud, your pride

Abstract written by a model

In Proverbs, pride precedes destruction; everyone proud in heart is an abomination to the LORD, and an arrogant spirit precedes a fall. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, pride is hated by the Lord and by men, begins when a man's heart departs from his maker, begins in sin, and pours out abomination. In The Book of Jeremiah, pride is condemned by the LORD, who says he will ruin it in Judah and Jerusalem, and the people are urged to humble themselves and give glory to him.
