physical defects disqualifying priests

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: a defect, any deformity, has a defect

Abstract written by a model

Physical defects disqualify Aaron's descendants from priestly service. Chapter 21 lists them: blindness, lameness, flat nose, deformity, injured foot or hand, hunchback, dwarfism, eye defect, itching disease, scabs, damaged testicles. Such a man may not offer the offerings or bread of God, nor approach the veil or altar, lest sanctuaries be profaned, but may still eat most holy and holy food.

Chapter 21

No descendant of Aaron who has a defect may approach to offer the offerings or the bread of God. The listed defects include blindness, lameness, a flat nose, any deformity, an injured foot or hand, being hunchbacked or a dwarf, a defect in the eye, an itching disease, scabs, or damaged testicles. Such a man may still eat the most holy and the holy food, but may not come near the veil or the altar, so that he does not profane the sanctuaries.
