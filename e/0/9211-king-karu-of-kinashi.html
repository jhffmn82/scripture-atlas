King Karu of Kinashi

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: Heir Apparent Karu, Prince Karu, captive Prince

Abstract written by a model

King Karu of Kinashi was designated to rule after Emperor in-Giyō. After debauching his younger sister, Queen So-tohoshi, he lost official and public support, fled to the Noble Oho-mahe Wo-mahe’s house, was captured and banished to Iyo, then died with Queen So-tohoshi by killing themselves together.

King Karu of Kinashi is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Sect. CXXXVII.—Emperor in-Giyō (Part I—Genealogies). .. Sect. CXLIII.—Emperor in-Giyō (Part VII.—Death of Prince Karu and Princess So-Tohoshi).

King Karu of Kinashi was designated to rule after Emperor in-Giyō but debauched his younger sister, Karu-no-oho-iratsume. The officials and people turned against him, and after taking refuge in the house of the Noble Oho-mahe Wo-mahe, he was captured and banished to the hot waters of Iyo. He later killed himself together with Queen So-tohoshi.
