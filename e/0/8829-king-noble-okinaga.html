Sect. LXII.—Emperor Kai-Kuwa.

King Noble Okinaga was the child of King Kami-me-ikadzuchi and Princess Takaki. He wedded the Princess of Takanuka in Kadzuraki and fathered Princess Okinaga-tarashi, Sora-tsu-hime, and King Prince Okinaga; he was also the ancestor of the Dukes of Homuji in Kibi and the Dukes of Aso in Harima.
