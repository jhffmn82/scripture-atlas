Abstract written by a model

A being who, while answering Knowledge, forgets his words; this forgetting places him near TAO, and he later approves the Yellow Emperor’s words.

CHAPTER XXII.

All-in-extremes begins to answer Knowledge's questions but forgets what he wants to say. He is described as near the truth because he has forgotten, and later considers the Yellow Emperor's words well spoken.
