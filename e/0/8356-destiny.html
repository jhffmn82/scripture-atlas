destiny

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: What is destined, favourable destiny, past acts, what is destined for them

Abstract written by a model

Destiny is a condition for favourable understanding and governs all creatures from the beginning. It is written on each forehead and engraved in God's court; what is destined takes place and will be obtained. The foolish do not read its lines. God alone is not subject to destiny and executes His order.

Rag Sorath .. Sorath Ashtapadi

Destiny governs all creatures from the beginning, with each animal bearing its destined record on its forehead. God alone is not subject to destiny and causes His order to be executed.

Rag Dhanasari .. Dhanasari Chhant

Destiny is written on the forehead and engraved in God's court. What is counterfeit is of no avail, and whoever is destined from the beginning to obtain something shall obtain it.
