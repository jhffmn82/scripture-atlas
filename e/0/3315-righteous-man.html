Chapter 4

The righteous man is found well-pleasing to God, loved, and transported from among sinners so that evil would not change his understanding or deceive his soul. Even if he dies before his time, he is at rest, and having been made perfect quickly, he fills a long time. Dead, he condemns the living ungodly.
