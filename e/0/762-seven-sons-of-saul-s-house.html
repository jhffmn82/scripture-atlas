Abstract written by a model

Seven sons of Saul's house: Armoni, Mephibosheth, and Merab's five sons by Adriel. Delivered to the Gibeonites, they are hanged together on the mountain before the LORD at barley harvest's start; their bones are later buried with Saul and Jonathan's.

Chapter 21

Seven men are delivered to the Gibeonites: Armoni and Mephibosheth, sons of Rizpah and Saul, and the five sons of Merab daughter of Saul, borne to Adriel. They are hanged on the mountain before the LORD at the start of barley harvest, all falling together, and their bones are later gathered and buried with those of Saul and Jonathan.
