Sacrifice is an offering and celebration for Ahura Mazda, divine beings, Fravashis, the Bountiful Immortals, Mãthra Spenta, the Mazdayasnian Religion, Yasnas, ritual lords, prayers, blessedness, and the entire holy, clean creation. It unfolds through homage, propitiation, praise, glorification, and adoration.

Visparad IX (Sp. X).

Sacrifice is presented as an offering of praise and homage to Ahura Mazda, the Bountiful Immortals, the holy and lofty Lord, blessedness, Mãthra Spenta, the Mazdayasnian Religion, the Yasnas, the lords of the ritual, well-timed prayers for blessings, and the entire creation of the holy.

Visparad XI (Sp. XII).

Sacrifice is performed for Ahura Mazda, divine beings, Fravashis, and the entire creation of the holy and clean. The offerings and celebrations are made for sacrifice together with homage, propitiation, and praise.
