Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Samuel, Nathan is a prophet and advisor to King David who delivers the LORD's messages, confronts David's sin, announces judgment, and conveys Solomon's naming as Jedidiah. In The First Book of Kings, Nathan advises Bathsheba, confirms her plea to David, and helps anoint Solomon king at Gihon. In 1 Chronicles, Nathan the prophet delivers the LORD's message and vision to David, forbidding him to build the house and promising David a house.

Nathan in The Second Book of Samuel

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Nathan first appears

Abstract written by a model

Nathan is a prophet and advisor to King David, sent by the LORD. He first tells David to follow his heart, then is corrected by the LORD's word and delivers the message about the house and promises. Later he confronts David's sin with a parable, declares "You are the man!", announces judgment, and conveys Solomon's naming as Jedidiah.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

Nathan the prophet first tells David to do all that is in his heart. That night the LORD's word comes to him, correcting this, and he is sent to deliver the message to David about the house and the promises. He speaks to David all these words according to the vision.

Chapter 12

Nathan is sent by the LORD to David and tells him the parable of the rich man and the poor man's lamb. He then declares "You are the man!", delivers the LORD's judgment, and tells David his sin is put away but the child will die. He later conveys the LORD's naming of Solomon as Jedidiah.
