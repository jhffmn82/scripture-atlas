Chapter 20

Pashhur, son of Immer the priest and chief officer in the LORD's house, heard Jeremiah prophesying, struck him, and put him in the stocks at the upper gate of Benjamin. The next day he released Jeremiah, who renamed him Magormissabib and foretold that he would become a terror to himself and his friends. Pashhur and his household were told they would go into captivity in Babylon, where he would die and be buried, having prophesied falsely to his friends.
