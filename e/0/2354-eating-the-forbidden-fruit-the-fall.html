Abstract written by a model

The Fall: persuaded by the serpent, the woman eats the fruit first and gives some to her husband Adam, who eats. Their eyes open to their nakedness; they sew fig leaves and hide, then God questions them, pronounces curses, and expels the man from the garden.

Chapter 3

The woman eats the fruit after the serpent's persuasion and gives some to her husband, who also eats. Their eyes are opened and they know they are naked, so they sew fig leaves for coverings and hide from God. The act leads God to question them, pronounce curses, and send the man out of the garden.
