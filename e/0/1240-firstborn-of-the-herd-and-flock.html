Abstract written by a model

Firstborn males of herd and flock are dedicated to the LORD and exempt from work and shearing. Each year the owner and household eat them before the LORD at the chosen place. Defective animals, lame or blind, are not sacrificed but eaten within the gates by clean and unclean alike, blood poured on the ground like water.

Chapter 15

All firstborn males of the herd and flock are to be dedicated to the LORD, not worked or shorn. They are eaten year by year before the LORD in the place he chooses, by the owner and his household. An animal with any defect, such as lameness or blindness, must not be sacrificed; it is eaten within the gates by clean and unclean alike, and the blood is poured out on the ground like water.
