mysterious excellence

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: mysterious excellence (of a governor), such mysterious excellence

Abstract written by a model

Mysterious excellence is the deep, far-reaching ability to know and follow the model and rule; it makes its possessor seem opposite to others while leading them into great conformity with the possessor.
