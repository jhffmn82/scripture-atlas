Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Ardvi Sûra Anâhita is healing, life-furthering water worshipped with sacrifice, whose mighty flow supplies the seven Karshvares and aids fertility and childbirth. In Visparad, Ardvi Sûra Anâhita is approached in praise as a holy ruler in the ritual order. In Yashts (Yasts) I-XII, Ardvi Sûra Anâhita is a holy spring, river, and water deity who preserves creation, increases life and wealth, and grants or refuses boons. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Ardvi Sura Anahita is a holy, life-giving river sustained by the Fravashis that purifies and supplies the seven Karshvares. In Nyayish, Ardvi Sûra Anâhita is a holy water-spring and river worshipped with offerings and prayers, whose waters purify and increase households and lands.

Ardvi Sûra Anâhita in Yasna

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where Ardvi Sûra Anâhita first appears

Abstract written by a model

Ardvi Sûra Anâhita is wide-flowing water, healing, efficacious against Daêvas, devoted to Ahura Mazda’s lore, and worshipped with sacrifice in the corporeal world. She furthers living things, increases herds, settlements, and wealth, and helps the Province progress. She purifies men’s seed, women’s wombs and milk; sanctifies wombs for birth; aids women in labour; and brings timely milk. Sounding from afar and equal in bulk to all earthly waters, she flows with mighty volume from Hukairya to Vouru-kasha, through a thousand tributaries and outlets. Her chief outlet reaches the seven Karshvars, bearing waters to them in every season.

She purifies the seed of male beings, sanctifies women's wombs, makes women fortunate in labour, and provides a regular and timely flow of milk. Ardvi Sûra Anâhita in Yashts (Yasts) I-XII

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Ardvi Sûra Anâhita first appears

Abstract written by a model

Ardvi Sûra Anâhita is a wide-expanding, health-giving, holy spring, river, and water. She hates the Daêvas, obeys Ahura Mazda’s laws, and preserves Ahura Mazda’s whole holy creation. Flowing from Hukairya to Vouru-Kasha and through the seven Karshvares, she is the source of their waters, with a thousand cells and channels. Her waters increase life, herds, folds, wealth, and countries; purify male seed and female wombs; and put milk into females’ breasts in the proper measure. Bright and glorious, she appears as a powerful, pure, high-girded maid, wearing a gold-embroidered mantle and beaver garments, and travels with four horses: wind, rain, cloud, and sleet. Worthy of sacrifice and prayer, she is worshipped by Ahura Mazda, Hvôvas, and Naotaras. She waits for libations and prayers, granting boons to worthy supplicants, such as Vîstâspa and Zairi-vairi, while also refusing some requests. She rescues Vafra Navâza and aids Vistauru.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

V. Âbân Yast.

Ardvi Sûra Anâhita is a wide-expanding, health-giving, holy spring and river who hates the Daêvas and obeys the laws of Ahura. Her waters increase life, herds, folds, wealth, and countries; purify male seed and female wombs; and provide milk to females. She grants many requested boons, refuses some, and appears in the shape of a strong, radiant maid to rescue Vafra Navâza and aid Vistauru.

XX.

Ardvi Sûra Anâhita is a powerful holy spring and water who protects the creation made by Ahura Mazda. She grants or refuses boons in response to sacrifices, libations, gifts, and entreaties, and is described as descending from the stars, wearing golden ornaments and beaver garments, and possessing a thousand cells and channels.

Ardvi Sûra Anâhita in Nyayish

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Ardvi Sûra Anâhita first appears

Abstract written by a model

Ardvi Sûra Anâhita is a holy water-spring, a large river, and a master of holiness. Her good waters flow over the seven Karshvares; when she runs to sea Vouru-Kasha, she boils over its shores and middle. She makes males’ seed and females’ wombs pure and increases house, borough, town, and country. Sacrifice is offered for her brightness and glory, with libations, Haoma, meat, baresma, wisdom of the tongue, holy spells, words, and deeds. Most fully worshipped, she gives boons to one who hears specified prayers and makes the good waters pure.
