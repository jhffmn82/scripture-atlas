Abstract written by a model

The Gibeonites trick Israel by posing as distant travelers in worn clothing and moldy bread. Because Israel did not consult the LORD, a covenant is made; three days later their nearby home is discovered and Joshua confronts them.

Chapter 9

The Gibeonites disguise themselves with old sacks, torn wineskins, patched sandals, old garments, and dry moldy bread, claiming to come from a far country. It succeeds, and the covenant is made, but three days later Israel learns they live nearby, and Joshua asks why they deceived him.
