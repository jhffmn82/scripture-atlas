Abstract written by a model

Capernaum in The Gospel According to Matthew

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Capernaum first appears

Abstract written by a model

Capernaum is a seaside place in the region of Zebulun and Naphtali. Jesus leaves Nazareth and settles there, fulfilling Isaiah's words. Later he addresses it as exalted to heaven yet destined to go down to Hades, warning its judgment will be more severe than Sodom's despite its mighty works.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

Leaving Nazareth, Jesus comes and lives in Capernaum, which is by the sea in the region of Zebulun and Naphtali. His settling there is presented as fulfilling what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet.

Chapter 11

Jesus addresses Capernaum as exalted to heaven and says it will go down to Hades. He says that had the mighty works done there been done in Sodom, Sodom would have remained until today, and that the land of Sodom will have a more tolerable judgment than Capernaum.

Capernaum in The Gospel According to Luke

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Capernaum first appears

Abstract written by a model

Capernaum is a Galilean city with a synagogue, where Jesus, arriving from Nazareth, teaches on the Sabbath to astonished listeners, casts out a demon, and heals Simon's mother-in-law.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

Capernaum is a city of Galilee to which Jesus comes down after leaving Nazareth. He teaches there on the Sabbath, and people are astonished at his authority. It is mentioned earlier as a place where deeds attributed to him were heard of, and there he casts out a demon and heals Simon's mother-in-law.
