The Second Book of the Maccabees / Chapter 1

It was found in the well where the fire had been hidden, and Nehemiah had it sprinkled on the wood and sacrifices, which then burst into a great blaze when the sun shone. Poured on large stones, it flamed but went out when the light from the altar shone back. The Persian king enclosed the place, gave some of the liquid as presents to those he favored, and it was named Nephthar, meaning Cleansing, though most call it Nephthai.
