religion

topic · 8 works · 10 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: all religion, religion of truth, the Religion of Truth, the Surrender, the religion, the religion of Allah, the religion of truth, their religion

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan), religion is surrender to Allah's will and guidance, and seeking another religion is rejected. In Surah 8: The Spoils of War (Al-Anfaal), religion is to be all for Allah after fighting ends persecution, and believers are recognized by their faith and striving. In Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba), the Religion of Truth is sent with Muhammad so it prevails over all religion despite idolaters' aversion. In Surah 42: Consultation (Ash-Shura), religion is ordained for people and commanded to be established without division. In Surah 48: The Victory (Al-Fath), the religion of truth is sent with Muhammad so Allah may cause it to prevail over all religion. In Surah 60: She that is to be examined (Al-Mumtahana), religion grounds the prohibition against friendship with those who war against or expel believers, while permitting kindness and justice toward peaceful nonbelievers. In Surah 61: The Ranks (As-Saff), the religion of truth is sent with Allah's messenger's guidance to conquer all religion despite idolaters' aversion. In Surah 110: Divine Support (An-Nasr), the religion of Allah is entered by mankind in troops after Allah's succour and triumph.
