Chapter 5

The LORD says he will bring a mighty, ancient nation from far away on the house of Israel, whose language they do not know or understand. Its quiver is an open tomb and all its people are mighty men. It will eat up the harvest, bread, sons' and daughters' food, flocks, herds, vines, and fig trees, and beat down the fortified cities with the sword.
