Antiochus's decree

Abstract written by a model

Antiochus's decree commanded his whole kingdom to become one people and forsake their own laws, which all nations accepted. For Jerusalem and Judah it banned offerings, Sabbaths, feasts and circumcision, ordered idol altars and unclean sacrifices, appointed overseers, and made disobedience punishable by death.

Chapter 1

Antiochus wrote to his whole kingdom that all should be one people and each forsake his own laws, and all the nations agreed. His letters to Jerusalem and Judah forbade burnt offerings, sacrifices, Sabbaths, feasts, and circumcision, and ordered altars, temples, and shrines for idols and the sacrifice of swine and unclean animals. Anyone who did not obey was to die, and he appointed overseers and ordered the cities of Judah to sacrifice.
