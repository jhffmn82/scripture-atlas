Abstract written by a model

The ungodly are a group who neglect righteousness and revolt from the Lord. In Chapter 1, their words and hands summon death, and they make a covenant with him, deeming him a friend. In Chapter 3, they are punished as their reasoning deserves; their hope is void, their toils and works useless, and their families foolish and cursed. In Chapter 4, they watch the wise man's end without grasping the Lord's plan, then become a dishonored carcass, are left waste, and their memory perishes.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Ungodly men are said to be searched out in their counsels, and the sound of their words comes to the Lord to bring their lawless deeds to conviction. By their hands and words they summon death, deem him a friend, pine away, and make a covenant with him, being worthy to belong with him.

Chapter 3

The ungodly neglected righteousness and revolted from the Lord, and they will be punished as their reasoning deserves. Their hope is void, their toils unprofitable and their works useless, and their wives are foolish, their children wicked and their descendants cursed.

Chapter 4

The ungodly watch the wise man's end without understanding the Lord's plan for him, and they despise what they see. They will become a dishonored carcass and a reproach among the dead, will be shaken from their foundations and left waste, and their memory will perish. Their long years of old age are condemned by the quickly perfected righteous.
