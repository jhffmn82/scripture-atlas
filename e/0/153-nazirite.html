Nazirite

person · 1 work · 13 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: man, man or woman, the Nazirite, woman

Abstract written by a model

The Nazirite is a person who vows separation to the LORD. Throughout the days of separation he is holy, abstains from wine and grape products, lets his hair grow, and avoids dead bodies, even those of his father, mother, brother, or sister. If suddenly defiled by a death, he shaves, brings offerings, and loses the former days. At the vow's end he is brought to the Tent of Meeting, offers a lamb, ewe lamb, ram, and bread, shaves, burns the hair under the peace offerings, and may then drink wine.

Chapter 6

The Nazirite separates himself to the LORD, avoiding wine, grape products, razors, and dead bodies, and is holy throughout the days of separation. If defiled by a sudden death, he shaves his head, brings offerings, and loses the former days. At the end of the vow he brings offerings, shaves his head at the Tent of Meeting, burns the hair, and may then drink wine.
