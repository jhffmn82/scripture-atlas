Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the Gershonites are a Levite family division responsible for transporting the tabernacle curtains, coverings, screens, hangings, and cords. In The Book of Joshua, the Gershonites receive thirteen cities by lot, including Golan in Bashan and Kedesh in Galilee as cities of refuge.

Gershonites in Numbers

author unknown · 1439 to 1399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Gershonites first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Gershonites are a Levite family division, the sons of Gershon, descending through Libni and Shimei into the Libnite and Shimeite families. They number 7,500 males from a month old and camp west behind the tabernacle under prince Eliasaph. Their duty is the tabernacle curtains, coverings, screens, hangings and cords. Later, 2,630 men aged thirty to fifty carry these under Ithamar's direction, receiving two wagons and four oxen.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The Gershonites descend from Gershon through Libni and Shimei, forming the Libnite and Shimeite families, and number seven thousand five hundred males. They camp west behind the tabernacle under prince Eliasaph son of Lael, and are responsible for the tabernacle, tent, coverings, screens, court hangings, and cords.

Chapter 4

The Gershonites are assigned to carry the tabernacle curtains, coverings, screens, court hangings, cords, and related instruments, under the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron. They number 2,630 men aged thirty to fifty.
