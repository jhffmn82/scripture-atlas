Abstract written by a model

is_a parable [spoken by Jesus] Jesus speaks the sheep fold parable in verses 1-5, and those listening do not understand it. He then explains it in two images: he is the door through which one is saved and finds pasture, and he is the good shepherd who knows his own and lays down his life, unlike the thief or the hired hand. The thief comes to steal, kill and destroy, while Jesus came that the sheep may have life abundantly.
