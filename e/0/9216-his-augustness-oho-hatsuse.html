His Augustness Oho-hatsuse

person · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: King Oho-hatsuse, Prince Oho-hatsuse, the Prince

Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Oho-hatsuse, younger brother of Emperor an-Kō and intended husband of Queen Waka-kusaka, ruled after an-Kō, slew several kings, and died with Grandee Tsubura during a siege.

He was the younger brother of Emperor an-Kō and was to wed Queen Waka-kusaka. After Emperor an-Kō’s death, he slew Kings Kurobiko and Shiro-biko, died with Grandee Tsubura during the siege of Tsubura’s house, and later slew King Ichi-no-be-no-oshiha.
