Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, it is built as a gold-overlaid acacia-wood chest and placed in the tabernacle's most holy place with the covenant inside. In Numbers, it goes before Israel for three days to seek a resting place and signals the LORD's departure and return among the people. In Deuteronomy, it is made by Moses from acacia wood to hold the new tablets and is carried by Levi's tribe as the ark of the covenant. In The Book of Joshua, it is carried before Israel across the Jordan and around Jericho during the city's procession. In The First Book of Samuel, it is captured by the Philistines, returned after seven months, and kept at Abinadab's house at Kiriath Jearim for twenty years. In The Second Book of Samuel, it is moved from Abinadab's house to David's city after Uzzah's death and a three-month stay with Obed-Edom. In The First Book of Kings, it is placed in the inner sanctuary beneath the cherubim's wings, containing only the two stone tablets Moses placed at Horeb. In 1 Chronicles, it is transported to David's city, carried by the Levites, and placed in the middle of David's tent for continual ministry.

ark of the covenant in Exodus

author unknown · about 1439 to 1399 BC · the work’s page · read it where ark of the covenant first appears

Abstract written by a model

The ark of the covenant is a chest of acacia wood, two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide and high, overlaid inside and outside with pure gold, with a gold molding, four gold rings in its feet, and gold-overlaid acacia poles that stay in the rings. It is first specified as a command, then built by Bezalel to those measures. Finally Moses puts the covenant inside, sets the poles, places the mercy seat above it, and brings it into the tabernacle's most holy place, screened by the veil as the LORD commanded.

Chapter 25

The ark is to be made of acacia wood, two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide and high, overlaid inside and outside with pure gold with a gold molding. It has four gold rings in its feet and acacia poles overlaid with gold, which must stay in the rings, and the covenant the LORD will give is to be put inside it.

Chapter 26

The ark of the covenant is to be brought in within the veil, into the most holy place. The mercy seat is to be put on top of it there.

Chapter 37

Bezalel makes the ark of acacia wood, two and a half cubits long and a cubit and a half wide and high. He overlays it with pure gold inside and outside, adds a gold molding, casts four gold rings for its feet, and makes gold-overlaid acacia poles set in the rings to bear it.

Chapter 40

Moses puts the covenant into the ark, sets the poles on it, places the mercy seat above it, and brings it into the tabernacle. He screens it with the veil, as the LORD commanded.

ark of the covenant in The Book of Joshua

author unknown · about 600 BC · the work’s page · read it where ark of the covenant first appears

Abstract written by a model

The ark of the covenant is the LORD's own covenant ark, called the ark of the Lord of all the earth and owned by the LORD. Levitical priests carry it ahead of the people, who follow it to learn the way. At the Jordan, its passing cuts off the waters; it rests on dry ground mid-river until the whole nation crosses, then crosses itself, prompting the memorial stones. Later, priests bear it in procession around Jericho, with armed men before and a rear guard after, circling once daily for six days and seven times on the seventh.

The ark of the covenant is carried by the priests ahead of the people, and the people are to follow it to learn the way. It is called the ark of the Lord of all the earth, and its passing into the Jordan is the sign that cuts off the waters. It rests in the middle of the Jordan on dry ground until all the nation has crossed.

Chapter 4

The ark was borne by priests who stood in the middle of the Jordan until the people had crossed. After all the people were over, it crossed with the priests in their presence. The waters of the Jordan were cut off before it, which is the reason given for the memorial stones.

Chapter 6

The ark of the LORD's covenant is carried by the priests in the procession around Jericho, with armed men going before the priests and a rear guard after the ark. It circles the city once daily for six days and seven times on the seventh day, returning to the camp each day.

Ark of the covenant in The First Book of Samuel

author unknown · about 930 to 721 BC · the work’s page · read it where Ark of the covenant first appears

Abstract written by a model

The ark of the covenant is the LORD's ark, belonging to the God of Israel. The elders bring it from Shiloh to the camp to save Israel, but the Philistines capture it in the second battle. They take it from Ebenezer to Ashdod, setting it beside Dagon, then to Gath and Ekron, where people are struck as it arrives. After seven months, the Philistine lords send it back on a new cart with golden jewels. At Beth Shemesh, men who looked into it die. It is then brought to Abinadab's house at Kiriath Jearim, where it stays twenty years.

Chapter 5

The ark is taken by the Philistines from Ebenezer to Ashdod and set in Dagon's house beside Dagon. It is then moved to Gath and Ekron, and the people of each place are struck as it arrives. The lords of the Philistines finally say it should be sent away to its own place.

The ark stays seven months in Philistine country, then is sent back on a new cart with a box of golden jewels beside it. It arrives at Beth Shemesh, where Levites set it and the box on the great stone. Because men looked into it, many die, and the people send word to Kiriath Jearim to come bring it up.
