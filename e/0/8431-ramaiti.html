Abstract written by a model

In Yasna, Âramaiti is the divine quality and personified Piety of the saints, an attribute and daughter of Ahura Mazda who supports righteousness, endurance, and blessings. In Yasna, Bountiful Âramaiti is personified Piety, a bountiful virtue that promotes prosperity, peace, moral Truth, and relief from cruelty. In Visparad, Âramaiti is a holy, good, bountiful being and ready mind whose wisdom is associated with the laws of the Righteous Order.

Âramaiti in Yasna

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where Âramaiti first appears

Abstract written by a model

Âramaiti is a divine quality, virtue, and personified Piety of the saints, belonging to Ahura Mazda as His attribute and also described as His daughter through good deeds. Approaching with Sovereign Power, the Good Mind, and the Righteous Order, she gives body to the spiritual creations of good and evil and embodies abiding, strenuous endurance. Within the faithful and the worshipper, she is piety, a ready mind, and pious wisdom: she questions the two spirits, seeks understanding of future matters and final states, utters saving regulations, and gives holy blessings through the Good Mind. She supports religious truth, sacred orderliness, just action, righteous beings, good rule, and the Kine’s peace, providing joyful meadows. Bountiful, holy, benevolent, beloved, and sanctifying, she increases Universal Weal, Immortality, and the Righteous Order. Yet she departs from the evil-doer who abandons her through ignorance of Good Mind. Her increase helps instruct seekers, prosper the Kine, and sanctify blessings for offspring.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

10 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XXX.

Âramaiti, the personified Piety of the saints, approached with the Sovereign Power, the Good Mind, and the Righteous Order. She gave a body to the spiritual creations of good and evil and is associated with abiding and strenuous endurance.

Yasna XXXI.

Piety belongs to Ahura Mazda and is active with the Good Mind in the faithful. It questions the two spirits and seeks understanding concerning future events, prayers, offerings, awards, and the final state.

Yasna XXXIII.

Âramaiti is identified with piety and is asked to give Zarathustra power and to show forth religious truths through the Righteous Order.

Yasna XXXIV.

Âramaiti is described as bountiful Piety and as the creatrix of righteous beings. She departs from those who abandon her through evil and ignorance, and she increases Universal Weal, Immortality, and the Righteous Order.

Yasna XXXVII.

Piety is worshipped as the ready mind within Thy folk.

Yasna XLIII.

Âramaiti is identified with Piety within the worshipper and is asked to grant holy blessings and perfect readiness of mind. She utters saving regulations and Ahura Mazda’s understanding, and she is present in the holy Realm to give blessings through the Good Mind.

Yasna XLIV.

Piety Âramaiti is asked about as a force that increases sacred orderliness and makes actions just. She is described as beloved and connected with Ahura Mazda's Sovereign Power, the Faith, and the speaker's growth in goodness.

Yasna XLV.

Piety is described as the daughter of Ahura Mazda through good deeds. Yasnas of Piety are used to praise Ahura Mazda with homage.

Yasna XLVII.

Âramaiti brings forth blessedness through her two hands and is identified with Piety as she lives within the soul and as earth. Ahura Mazda bestows her as joyful meadows of peace for the Kine, and her increase helps instruct those who seek her.

Yasna XLVIII.

Âramaiti is addressed as pious wisdom and is asked to sanctify the best blessings for offspring. She is asked to help the Kine prosper and to support good rule through deeds of good discernment.

Bountiful Âramaiti in Yasna

L.H. Mills · the work’s page · read it where Bountiful Âramaiti first appears

Abstract written by a model

Bountiful Âramaiti is personified Piety, a bountiful and perfect virtue. She increases prosperity alongside righteous ritual and moral Truth, supports peace and pastures for the Kine, and teaches those who seek her. Linked with Ahura Mazda’s Bountiful Spirit and Fire, she is expected with Righteous Order to bring homes, relief from cruel people, and blessings. Though Bendva failed to sustain her, Âramaiti endures through self-humbling praise and sacrifice, approaches when asked, speaks her truths, and is associated with Kavi Vîstâspa.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XLVII.

Piety is represented by Âramaiti and is linked with peace for the Kine, the increase of righteous Truth, and the instruction of those who seek her. Its prosperous increase accompanies the gifts given through Ahura Mazda’s Bountiful Spirit and Fire.

Yasna XLVIII.

Perfected Piety is awaited together with the Righteousness. She is expected to come with the amenities of home and pastures for the Kine and to bring relief from cruel people of evil life and faith.

Yasna XLIX.

Piety is described as bountiful and perfect, but Bendva has not maintained or strengthened it. Piety exists through Âramaiti and is associated with self-humbling praises and sacrificing zeal.

Yasna LI.

Âramaiti is identified with Piety and is asked to approach, grant blessings, and speak her truths. She is also associated with Kavi Vîstâspa, who is called a man of Âramaiti.
