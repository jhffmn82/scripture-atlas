is_characterized_by absence of hostility [no enmity, no seeking harm] Killing is unintentional when a person shoves another suddenly without hostility, hurls something without lying in wait, or casts a stone without seeing him, and was not the victim's enemy or seeking his harm. Such a killer may flee to a city of refuge and receives judgment by the congregation rather than immediate death.
