Sect.

She was another name for Divine-Princess-of-Ata and was sent by Deity-Great-Mountain-Possessor together with Princess-Long-as-the-Rocks. His Augustness Heaven's-Sun-Height-Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty kept her and wedded her for one night.

Sect.

Princess-Blossoming-Brilliantly-Like-the-Flowers-of-the-Trees tells His Augustness Prince Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty that she is pregnant and nearing delivery. She builds a hall without doors, seals its entrance with earth, and sets it on fire before giving birth.
