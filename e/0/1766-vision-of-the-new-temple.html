vision of the new temple

Abstract written by a model

A vision given to Ezekiel, under the LORD's hand, in the twenty-fifth year of captivity, fourteen years after the city was struck. On a very high mountain in Israel he sees a city-like structure: gates, courts, offering tables, priests' rooms, and porch, and is commanded to declare it to Israel.

Chapter 40

The vision is given in the twenty-fifth year of the captivity, fourteen years after the city was struck, and shows a structure like a city frame on a very high mountain. This chapter covers the gates, courts, offering tables, priests' rooms, and the house's porch. The speaker is commanded to declare all of it to the house of Israel.
