census of Israel

event · 2 works · 12 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: census, census of Israel and Judah, count Israel, count Israel and Judah, counted, number, take the number of them, that which I have done, the census of the people, the counting of the people, this thing

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Samuel, it was ordered by David at the LORD's prompting despite Joab's objection, lasted nine months and twenty days, counted 800,000 in Israel and 500,000 in Judah, and was later called sin and foolishness by David. In 1 Chronicles, it was instigated by Satan and ordered by David, carried out by Joab, and counted 1,100,000 swordsmen in Israel and 470,000 in Judah, omitting Levi and Benjamin; it displeased God, brought a strike on Israel, and led David to confess sin. In 1 Chronicles, it was begun by Joab but left unfinished, brought wrath on Israel, and was never entered in King David's chronicles; those twenty and under were uncounted because of the LORD's promise of stars-like increase.

Abstract written by a model

A census of Israel and Judah ordered by David at the LORD's prompting, despite Joab's objection. Spanning the land from across the Jordan to Beersheba, it lasted nine months and twenty days, counting 800,000 in Israel and 500,000 in Judah; David then called it sin and foolishness.

Chapter 24

The census is ordered by David at the LORD's prompting, over Joab's objection. It covers the land from across the Jordan to Dan, Sidon, Tyre and Beersheba, takes nine months and twenty days, and yields 800,000 in Israel and 500,000 in Judah. David afterward calls it a sin and a foolish act.
