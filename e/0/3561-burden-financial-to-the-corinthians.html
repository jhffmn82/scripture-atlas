burden (financial) to the Corinthians

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: I seek not your possessions, a burden to you, burden

Abstract written by a model

Paul says the Corinthians were not made inferior to other assemblies except that he was not a burden to them, and he asks forgiveness for this wrong. He affirms he will not burden them, since parents should save up for children, and he denies taking advantage of them himself or through Titus and the brother he sent.
