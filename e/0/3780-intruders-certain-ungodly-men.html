intruders (certain ungodly men)

group · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: certain men, mockers, murmurers and complainers, these, those who cause divisions, ungodly men

Abstract written by a model

These men crept in secretly, were long ago written about for condemnation, turn God's grace into indecency, and deny Jesus Christ. They defile the flesh, despise authority, slander celestial beings, speak evil of what they don't know, follow the ways of Cain, Balaam, and Korah, and are compared to hidden reefs, waterless clouds, fruitless trees, wild waves, and wandering stars. They are murmurers, mockers, and divisive, sensual people without the Spirit, and blackness of darkness is reserved for them.
