Caiaphas

person · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Caiaphas the high priest, high priest, the high priest

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, Caiaphas serves as high priest, presides over Jesus's trial, questions him about being the Christ and Son of God, and declares his answer blasphemy. In The Gospel According to John, Caiaphas serves as high priest, prophesies that Jesus will die for the nation, and receives Jesus after Annas sends him bound from Caiaphas's house to the Praetorium.

Caiaphas in The Gospel According to John

author unknown · AD 90 to 100 · the work’s page · read it where Caiaphas first appears

Abstract written by a model

Caiaphas is the high priest that year and son-in-law of Annas. He tells the council it is advantageous for one man to die for the people so the nation does not perish; the text calls this prophecy that Jesus would die for the nation and gather God's scattered children. Later, Annas sends the bound Jesus to him, and from his house Jesus is led to the Praetorium.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

Caiaphas, high priest that year, tells the council they know nothing and that it is advantageous for one man to die for the people so the whole nation does not perish. The text says he spoke as high priest, not of himself, prophesying that Jesus would die for the nation and also gather into one the scattered children of God.

Chapter 18

Caiaphas is identified as the high priest that year and father-in-law of Annas's... correction: son-in-law of Annas, and as the one who advised that it was expedient for one man to perish for the people. Annas sends the bound Jesus to him, and from his house Jesus is led to the Praetorium.
