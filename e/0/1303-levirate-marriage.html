levirate marriage

topic · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Levirate duty (duty of a husband's brother), raise up offspring for your brother, the duty of a husband’s brother

Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, Levirate duty requires Onan to raise offspring for his deceased brother by going in to Tamar, though he evades it and Judah later admits withholding Shelah was wrong. In Deuteronomy, levirate marriage requires a surviving brother to marry his deceased brother’s sonless widow and have her firstborn succeed in the dead man’s name.

levirate marriage in Deuteronomy

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where levirate marriage first appears

Abstract written by a model

Levirate marriage is a duty when brothers dwell together and one dies sonless: the surviving brother must marry the widow rather than a stranger, and her firstborn succeeds in the dead man's name, preserving it in Israel.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 25

When brothers dwell together and one dies without a son, his widow must not marry a stranger; her husband's brother is to take her as wife and perform a husband's brother's duty. Her firstborn son succeeds in the dead man's name so that it is not blotted out of Israel.
