Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Jeremiah, pestilence, sword, and famine are described as judgments that will strike the city's inhabitants and deliver survivors to Nebuchadnezzar. In The Book of Ezekiel, pestilence, sword, and famine are instruments of the LORD GOD's wrath on Israel, among four severe judgments sent on Jerusalem.

sword, famine, and pestilence in The Book of Ezekiel

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where sword, famine, and pestilence first appears

Abstract written by a model

Sword, famine, and pestilence are instruments of the Lord GOD's wrath on Israel. Chapter 6 assigns them by place: pestilence for the far off, sword for the near, famine for the besieged. Chapter 14 names four severe judgments, adding evil animals, and says the LORD will send all four on Jerusalem.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

These are the means by which Israel will fall: the far off will die of pestilence, the near will fall by the sword, and the besieged remnant in the city will die by famine. Through them the Lord says he will accomplish his wrath.

Chapter 14

The four severe judgments are the sword, the famine, the evil animals, and the pestilence. Each is described as something the LORD sends on a sinning land, and he says he will send all four on Jerusalem to cut off man and animal.
