Abstract written by a model

In the Yasna, the Zarathustrian law against the Daêvas is identified as Ahura's Faith and the Faith of Mazda, whose long descent is worshipped. In the Yasna, Mãthra Spenta is presented as a bounteous, holy, effective Zarathustrian law and revelation instituted against the Daêvas, worshipped, offered, and propitiated. In the Yasna, Mãthra-spenta is described as the bounteous word-of-reason and worshipped. In the Yasna, Zarathustrian law is described as a holy, bounteous Mazdayasnian revelation and Mãthra Spenta, worshipped as sacred law and identified with science and Mazda-made understanding. In the Visparad, Mãthra Spenta is the holy, bounteous word of reason, praised through sacrifice, homage, propitiation, glorification, and Yasna. In the Vendidad (Vendîdâd), Mãthra Spenta, the most glorious and all-glorious one, responds to Ahura Mazda's request for healing by asking how diseases are driven away.

Zarathustrian law in Yasna

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Zarathustrian law first appears

Abstract written by a model

Zarathustrian law is a holy, bounteous, effective Mazdayasnian revelation and Mãthra Spenta, pronounced and instituted against the Daêvas. It matters as a glorious sacred law and bounteous word of reason, imparting heart’s devotion to the saint. It is first named among sacred objects receiving a complete offering, then worshipped and identified with Mãthra, science, and Mazda-made understanding.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna VII.

The Zarathustrian law is named with Mãthra Spenta and is included among the sacred objects receiving a complete and sacred offering.

Yasna XXV.

The Mãthra Spenta is worshipped as verily glorious and as the law pronounced against the Daêvas, the Zarathustrian law, and its long descent. It is also identified with the heart-devoted and bounteous Mãthra, the science that is Mãthra Spenta, and Mazda-made innate and derived understanding.
