Abstract written by a model

A minister to Duke Ling of Wei, Pei Kung Shê raises voluntary contributions for bells and completes them in three months without resentment, gratitude, or injury.

CHAPTER XX.

Pei Kung Shê levies contributions to make bells and completes the work in three months. He explains that he showed no resentment or gratitude and allowed everyone to contribute what they wished, so no injury was done.
