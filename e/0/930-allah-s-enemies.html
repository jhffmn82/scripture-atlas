Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the LORD's enemies hate him and plot evil against him; his hand finds them out, he makes them like a fiery furnace, fire devours them, their descendants are destroyed from the earth, and they turn their backs when he aims drawn bows at their faces. In Psalms, enemies of God are asked to scatter like smoke and wax before fire, face destruction as God strikes their heads, and include war-loving nations and fleeing armies' kings who are scattered. In The Book of Nahum, the adversaries of the LORD are his enemies and those who plot against him; he takes vengeance, pursues them into darkness, and ends their place with a flood, cutting them down and consuming them utterly so affliction will not recur. In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), the enemies of Allah are gathered and driven toward the Fire, their ears, eyes, and skins testify against their deeds, and their lasting home is the Fire because they denied Allah's revelations. In Surah 60: She that is to be examined (Al-Mumtahana), Allah's enemies are described as people who disbelieve in the truth, drive out the messenger and believers, and seek to make believers disbelieve; if they gain the upper hand, they become foes and use their hands and tongues against believers with evil intent.

Abstract written by a model

The adversaries of the LORD are his enemies and those who plot against him. He takes vengeance, pursuing them into darkness and ending their place with a flood. Though strong and numerous, they are likened to thorns, drunkards, and dry stubble, and are cut down and consumed utterly, so affliction will not recur.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

The Book of Nahum / Chapter 1

The LORD takes vengeance on his adversaries and maintains wrath against his enemies, pursuing them into darkness and making a full end of their place with an overflowing flood. Those who plot against him are told affliction will not rise a second time, and they are likened to entangled thorns, drunkards, and dry stubble that is consumed utterly. Even at full strength and in great numbers they will be cut down and pass away, and one who devises evil and counsels wickedness against the LORD has gone out of the addressed party.
