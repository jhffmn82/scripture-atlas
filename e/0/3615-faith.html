Abstract written by a model

Faith is presented as the means by which God's righteousness reaches all who believe. In Chapter 1, the Romans' faith is proclaimed worldwide, and the Good News brings salvation through it, with Paul aiming at the obedience of faith among nations. Chapter 3 says faith justifies apart from works, excludes boasting, and covers circumcised and uncircumcised alike. Chapter 10 says it comes by hearing God's word, though not all listened. It establishes the law rather than nullifying it.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

The Romans' faith is proclaimed throughout the whole world, and Paul says he and they will be encouraged by each other's faith. Faith is the means by which the Good News brings salvation to everyone who believes, and by which the righteous shall live. Paul's apostleship aims at the obedience of faith among all nations.

Chapter 3

Faith is the means by which the righteousness of God reaches all who believe, and by which a man is justified apart from works of the law. It excludes boasting as a law of faith, and through it God justifies both circumcised and uncircumcised.

Chapter 10

The righteousness of faith says the word is near, in the mouth and heart, which is the word of faith that is preached. Faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God. Not all listened, as Isaiah asks who has believed the report.
