The righteous man is a figure in Ecclesiastes who sometimes perishes despite his righteousness. The Preacher warns against excessive righteousness, noting no one on earth is sinless; later, some righteous men suffer the wicked's deserts, called vanity.

Chapter 7

A righteous man is observed to perish in his righteousness. The Preacher warns against being overly righteous and says there is not a righteous man on earth who does good and does not sin.

Chapter 8

Some righteous men experience what would befit the work of the wicked. The narrator names this a vanity done on the earth.
