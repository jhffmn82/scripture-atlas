Abstract written by a model

David's prayer in this psalm consists of pleas that the LORD not rebuke him in anger, that he have mercy, heal, return, deliver, and save him, and that his enemies be shamed. He argues that the dead cannot remember or thank the LORD. The psalm closes with David declaring that the LORD has heard his supplication and accepts his prayer.
