complacent elites (notable men of Zion and Samaria)

Also called: the notable men of the chief of the nations, those who are at ease in Zion, those who are secure on the mountain of Samaria, who drink wine in bowls, who lie on beds of ivory, you who put far away the evil day, you who rejoice in a thing of nothing

Abstract written by a model

These are notable men of the chief of the nations, living at ease in Zion and secure in Samaria. They recline on ivory beds, eat choice lambs and calves, drink wine from bowls, anoint themselves with the best oils, and invent musical instruments like David. They are not grieved by Joseph's affliction, push away the evil day, boast of their own strength, and turn justice into poison. They are told they will go captive among the first, ending their feasting.

Chapter 6

These men are described as at ease and secure, lying on ivory beds, eating choice lambs and calves, strumming harps, inventing instruments like David, drinking wine in bowls, and anointing themselves with the best oils, while not grieving for the affliction of Joseph. They put far away the evil day, boast of taking horns by their own strength, and are told they will go captive with the first who go captive, ending the feasting and lounging.
