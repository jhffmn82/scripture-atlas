Salih

person · 3 works · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: their brother Salih

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), Salih calls Thamud to serve Allah and respect Allah’s camel, then turns from them after they reject the command, having delivered Allah’s message and counsel. In Surah 11: Hud, Salih calls Thamud to worship Allah alone, seek forgiveness, and repent; presents Allah’s camel as a token; and warns of a three-day reprieve after they hamstring it. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), Salih calls Thamud to duty and obedience, gives the she-camel as a sign with an appointed drinking arrangement, and warns them not to harm it.
