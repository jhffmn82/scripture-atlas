Chapter 23

The wife who leaves her husband and produces an heir by another man is charged with three offenses: disobeying the law of the Most High, trespassing against her husband, and committing adultery and bearing children by another man. She will be brought out into the congregation, her punishment will extend to her children, who will not take root, and she will leave a memory that is a curse with a reproach that is not blotted out.
