Abstract written by a model

Libations are ritual offerings that must be cleanly prepared, well strained, and properly offered, often with Haoma, meat, sacrifice, gifts, prayers, and entreaties. They accompany requests for boons from Ardvi Sûra Anâhita and Drvâspa, may please Mithra, and give him strength and energy. Qualified Âthravan priests may drink them after prescribed washing, strokes, and knowledge of the staota yêsnya; offerings after sunset are received by Daêvas.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

V. Âbân Yast.

Libations are to be cleanly prepared and well strained and offered with Haoma and meat. Figures who request boons give libations, gifts, and sacrifice, and Ardvi Sûra Anâhita grants or refuses their requests in that context.

XX.

Libations are offered to Ardvi Sûra Anâhita together with sacrifices, gifts, prayers, and entreaties. She says that qualified Âthravan priests may drink them, while various disqualified people may not; libations offered after sunset are received by six hundred and a thousand Daêvas.

IX. Gôs Yast.

An offering of libations accompanies the sacrifices made to Drvâspa. Those who seek boons are repeatedly said to receive them while offering libations, giving gifts, sacrificing, and entreating her.

XXIV.

Faithful worshippers give Mithra strength and energy through offered and proffered Haomas. The libations must be cleanly prepared, and those who drink them must wash and undergo prescribed strokes and know the staota yêsnya.
