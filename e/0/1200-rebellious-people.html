rebellious people

Also called: a people who provoke me to my face continually, a rebellious people, their, you, you who forsake the LORD, your own iniquities

Abstract written by a model

A group of rebellious people who walk in their own thoughts and continually provoke the LORD to his face. They sacrifice in gardens, burn incense on bricks, eat pig's meat, and claim to be holier than others. They forsake the LORD, forget his holy mountain, and prepare tables and mixed wine for Fortune and Destiny. Because they did not answer his call, they are destined for the sword, and their name will become a curse among his chosen.

Chapter 65

They are described as walking in a way that is not good, provoking the LORD to his face by sacrificing in gardens, burning incense on bricks and mountains, sitting among graves, eating pig's meat, and saying they are holier than others. They forsake the LORD and prepare a table for Fortune and Destiny, and are told they will be destined to the sword because they did not answer when he called. They will leave their name as a curse to his chosen.
