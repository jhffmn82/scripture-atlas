food in Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where food first appears

Abstract written by a model

Food is lawful for believers and those who received the Scripture. It includes cattle, all good things, and the food of those who received the Scripture; trained beasts and birds of prey may catch permitted food when Allah's name is mentioned. It excludes carrion, blood, swineflesh, divining arrows, improperly dedicated or killed animals, and animals immolated unto idols. The record moves from lawful cattle and good things to mutual lawfulness of food, then sets out forbidden categories and the condition for caught food.
