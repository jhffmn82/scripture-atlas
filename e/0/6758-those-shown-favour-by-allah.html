those shown favour by Allah

group · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: those unto whom Allah hath shown favour

Abstract written by a model

Those shown favour by Allah are the prophets, saints, martyrs, and righteous. Allah commands restoring deposits and judging justly; those who obey Allah and the messenger are with these favoured people.
