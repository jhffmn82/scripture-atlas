Abstract written by a model

The prophets are a group who, in that day, will be removed from the land with the spirit of impurity. Any who still prophesy are condemned by their own parents and stabbed. Ashamed of their visions, they drop the hairy mantle, deny being prophets, claim to be farmers, and explain their wounds as received in friends' houses.

Chapter 13

The prophets, along with the spirit of impurity, will be caused to pass out of the land. Anyone who still prophesies will be told by his father and mother that he must die for speaking lies in the LORD's name, and they will stab him. The prophets will be ashamed of their visions, will not wear a hairy mantle to deceive, and will deny being prophets, claiming instead to be tillers of the ground and bondservants since youth. When asked about wounds between his arms, one says he was wounded in the house of his friends.
