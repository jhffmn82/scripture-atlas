Chapter 13

The old prophet of Bethel learned of the man of God from his sons, rode after him on a donkey, and lied that an angel had told him to bring him home to eat and drink. When the LORD's word came at the table, he announced the judgment to the man of God. After the lion's attack he retrieved the body, buried it in his own grave, mourned him as 'my brother,' and told his sons to bury him beside the man of God, affirming that the prophecy would happen.
