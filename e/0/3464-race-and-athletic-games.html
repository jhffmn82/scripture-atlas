Chapter 9

Paul uses the race as an image: all run but one receives the prize, and he urges the readers to run so as to win. Athletes exercise self-control for a corruptible crown, whereas believers seek an incorruptible one. Paul says he runs not aimlessly and does not beat the air, but beats his body into submission lest he be disqualified.
