Abstract written by a model

A praiser priest, invoker, memoriser, reciter, and chanter who performs ritual for the Bountiful Immortals. The speaker chants sacrifice, homage, propitiation, and praise, offers body and life’s blessings, and confesses belief in Ahura Mazda.

Visparad V (Sp. VI).

The speaker comes as a praiser priest, invoker, memoriser, reciter, and chanter for the Bountiful Immortals’ sacrifice, homage, propitiation, and praise. The speaker offers the Bountiful Immortals the flesh of his body and all the blessings of his life, and confesses his belief in Ahura Mazda.
