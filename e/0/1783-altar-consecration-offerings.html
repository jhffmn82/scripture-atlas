It opens with a young bull sin offering whose blood marks the horns, ledge corners and border, the bull burned outside the sanctuary. Daily goat, young bull and ram offerings follow, without defect, with salt; on the eighth day regular burnt and peace offerings begin.

Chapter 43

The consecration begins with a young bull as a sin offering, with its blood put on the four horns, the four corners of the ledge, and the border, and the bull burned outside the sanctuary. On the second day a male goat without defect is offered, and then a young bull and a ram without defect are offered as burnt offerings with salt. For seven days a goat, a young bull, and a ram are prepared each day, and on the eighth day the regular burnt and peace offerings begin.
