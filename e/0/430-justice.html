justice in Deuteronomy

author unknown · 624 BC · the work’s page · read it where justice first appears

Abstract written by a model

Justice is a commanded standard that must be followed fully: it must not be perverted, show partiality, or accept bribes, which blind the wise and distort the righteous. Pursuing it is tied to living and inheriting the land the LORD gives.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

Justice is to be followed in full: it must not be perverted, partiality must not be shown, and bribes must not be taken because they blind the wise and pervert the words of the righteous. Following what is altogether just is tied to living and inheriting the land the LORD gives.
