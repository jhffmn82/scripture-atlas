Abstract written by a model

Solomon's daily provision is a large food supply: thirty cors of fine flour, sixty measures of meal, thirty fat and pasture cattle, one hundred sheep, plus deer, gazelles, roebucks and fattened fowl, supplied month by month by twelve officers.

Chapter 4

Solomon's provision for one day is thirty cors of fine flour, sixty measures of meal, ten fat cattle, twenty pasture-fed cattle, and one hundred sheep. It also includes deer, gazelles, roebucks, and fattened fowl. The twelve officers supply it month by month.
