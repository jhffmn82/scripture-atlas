Chapter 29

The LORD's instructions tell the exiles to build houses, plant gardens and eat their fruit, take wives, have children, arrange marriages for their sons and daughters, and multiply rather than diminish. They are also to seek the city's peace and pray for it. Shemaiah quotes these instructions in his complaint against Jeremiah.
