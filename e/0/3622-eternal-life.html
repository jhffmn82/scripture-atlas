eternal life

topic · 9 works · 19 quoted facts · 20 connections
Also called: a life which is endless, death, endless life, everlasting life, glory, honor, and incorruptibility, glory, honor, and peace, have it abundantly, life, life eternal, newness of life, that life which is eternal, that precious gift of eternal life, the Word of life, the life
and 3 more, the life, the eternal life, the promise, this life

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, eternal life is inherited by those who keep the commandments, give up possessions, follow Jesus, and leave family or lands for his name. In The Gospel According to John, eternal life is the gift of knowing God and Jesus Christ, received by believers and associated with resurrection and Jesus' words. In Paul’s Letter to the Romans, eternal life is God's free gift in Christ Jesus resulting from freedom from sin and service to God. In 1 John, eternal life is the life with the Father revealed through Jesus Christ, given by God to believers so they may know they have it. In The Second Book of Nephi, eternal life is the end of the chosen path of faith, obedience, reliance on grace, and enduring in Christ. In The Book of Enos, eternal life is a teaching from Enos's father that awakens Enos's desire for God. In The Book of Mosiah, eternal life is the endless salvation, heaven, and happiness given through Christ to the redeemed and those with good works. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, eternal life is received through repentance, faith, hope, love, and keeping God's commandments.

and 1 more work

In The Book of Helaman, eternal life is a precious heavenly gift promised to those who do good and look to the Son of God with faith and a contrite spirit.

eternal life in The Gospel According to John

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where eternal life first appears

Abstract written by a model

Eternal life is a gift received by whoever believes in Jesus, the Son, and is defined as knowing the only true God and Jesus Christ. It begins with a promise in Chapter 3 that believers will not perish, tied to the Son being lifted up. Chapter 6 adds food that remains, being raised at the last day, and Jesus' words of eternal life. Chapter 17 defines it and says Jesus gives it to those the Father gave him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Eternal life is promised to whoever believes in the Son, who will not perish. It is linked to the Son being lifted up as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness. John adds that one who believes in the Son has eternal life, while one who disobeys won't see life.

Chapter 6

Jesus says the food that remains leads to eternal life and that the Son of Man will give it. He says everyone who sees the Son and believes has eternal life and will be raised at the last day, and that eating this bread means living forever. Peter says Jesus has the words of eternal life.

Chapter 17

Eternal life is defined as knowing the only true God and Jesus Christ whom he sent. Jesus says that, having been given authority over all flesh, he will give eternal life to all whom the Father has given him.

Abstract written by a model

Eternal life is a freely chosen way received through the great Mediator. Men and children of men are instructed to look to the Mediator, obey his commandments, reconcile to the will of God, and rely on grace. The path unfolds through faithfulness, pressing forward in Christ with hope and love, feasting on his word, and enduring to the end, which is identified as eternal life.
