The dedication of Jerusalem's wall was a joyful celebration with thanksgiving and music. Levites and singers were gathered to Jerusalem; priests, Levites, people, gates and wall were purified, then processions, great sacrifices and rejoicing followed, and men were appointed over storerooms.

Chapter 12

The dedication was held with gladness, thanksgiving, and singing with cymbals, stringed instruments, and harps. Levites and singers were gathered to Jerusalem, and the priests, Levites, people, gates, and wall were purified first. It included processions, great sacrifices, and rejoicing, and on that day men were appointed over the storerooms.
