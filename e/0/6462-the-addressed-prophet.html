the addressed prophet

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 74: The Cloaked One (Al-Muddaththir), the addressed prophet is told to arise and warn, magnify his Lord, purify his raiment, shun pollution, avoid showing favour for worldly gain, and be patient for the sake of his Lord. In Surah 88: The Overwhelming (Al-Ghaashiya), the addressed prophet is told to remind them and is described as a remembrancer, not a warder over them.
