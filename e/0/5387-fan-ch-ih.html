Fan Ch'ih

person · 3 works · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Fan Hsu

Abstract written by a model

In The Analects of Confucius, Book VI (Yung Yey), Fan Ch'ih asks the Master what constitutes wisdom and perfect virtue. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XII (Yen Yuan), Fan Ch'ih rambles with the Master under the trees about the rain altars, asks how to exalt virtue, correct cherished evil, and discover delusions, asks about benevolence and knowledge, does not immediately understand the answers, and asks Tsze-hsia what the Master meant. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XIII: Tsze-Lu, Fan Ch'ih requests instruction in husbandry and gardening, is called a small man after leaving, and later asks the Master about perfect virtue.
