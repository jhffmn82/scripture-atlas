Afumi

place · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: land of Afumi

Abstract written by a model

Afumi is a place containing Kuta, Wata, and the moor of Kaya at Wata, noted for abundant boars and deer where kings built temporary hunting palaces.

Afumi is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
