Abstract written by a model

victim Tamar Amnon forces Tamar after luring her into his room under pretense of illness, ignoring her protest that it is a folly not done in Israel. He then hates her and expels her, which she calls a wrong worse than the first. The act leaves Tamar desolate, angers David, and is the stated reason for Absalom's hatred and Amnon's later death.
