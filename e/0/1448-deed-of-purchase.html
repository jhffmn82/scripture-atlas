Chapter 32

Jeremiah signs and seals the deed, with one sealed copy containing the terms and conditions and one open copy, and has it witnessed. He gives it to Baruch with the instruction to put both deeds in an earthen vessel so they will last many days, as a sign that houses, fields, and vineyards will be bought again.
