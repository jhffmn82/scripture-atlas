Abstract written by a model

The overseer is a role-holder who desires a good work and cares for God's assembly. The records stress strict qualifications: he must be without reproach, husband of one wife, temperate, gentle, and good at teaching, and not greedy. He must rule his household well, keeping obedient children, since failure there would unfit him. He must not be a new convert, lest he fall into the devil's condemnation, and must have good outside testimony.

Chapter 3

The overseer is described as someone who desires a good work. He must be without reproach, the husband of one wife, temperate, sensible, modest, hospitable, and good at teaching, and not a drinker, violent, quarrelsome, or greedy. He must rule his own house well with obedient children, since failing at that would make him unfit to care for God's assembly. He must not be a new convert, lest he be puffed up and fall into the devil's condemnation, and he must have good testimony from outsiders to avoid reproach and the devil's snare.
