the superior man

person · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a superior man

Abstract written by a model

The superior man is associated with a ruler or person who can teach the principles of propriety and music. Confucius says that a good man's solid and sincere discourse may be only an appearance of gravity rather than the reality of a superior man.
