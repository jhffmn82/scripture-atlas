hunchback Su

Abstract written by a model

Hunchback Su is a hunchback with extreme deformities, including jaws at his navel and inverted viscera. He supports a family of ten through tailoring, washing, and sifting rice. Exempt from conscription and public works because of his deformity, he also receives grain and firewood donations, preserving his life until its allotted end.
