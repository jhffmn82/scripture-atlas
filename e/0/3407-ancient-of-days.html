Chapter 7

The Ancient of Days sits on a throne of fiery flames with wheels of burning fire, his clothing white as snow and his hair like pure wool. Thousands upon thousands minister to him as he sets judgment and opens the books. The one like a son of man is brought before him, and he comes to give judgment to the saints.
