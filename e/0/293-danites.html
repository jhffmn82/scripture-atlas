Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the camp of Dan forms a three-tribe division with Asher and Naphtali, encamps on the north side under Ahiezer, totals 157,600 men, and marches last. In The Book of Joshua, the tribe of Dan receives the seventh lot, takes Leshem beyond its border, settles there, and renames it Dan. In The Book of Judges, the Danites spy out land, march with six hundred armed men, seize Micah's idols, ephod, and priest, conquer and rebuild Laish as Dan, and install the image with Jonathan and his sons as priests.

camp of Dan in Numbers

Abstract written by a model

The camp of Dan is a three-tribe division (Dan, Asher, Naphtali) encamped on the north side under prince Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai; totaling 157,600 men, it is the largest-numbered camp noted and marches last.

Chapter 2

The camp of Dan, with Asher and Naphtali, encamps on the north side, with Ahiezer son of Ammishaddai as prince of Dan. Its total is 157,600 (Dan 62,700, Asher 41,500, Naphtali 53,400), and it sets out last.

Danites in The Book of Judges

author unknown · about 1000 BC · the work’s page · read it where Danites first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Danites are a tribe of Israel descended from Dan, who had not yet received an inheritance among the tribes. They sent five valiant men to spy out land, then marched with six hundred armed men, seizing Micah's idols, ephod, and priest. They conquered and burned Laish, rebuilt it as Dan, and installed the image, with Jonathan and his sons as priests.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 18

The Danites, lacking an inheritance among the tribes, sent five men to spy out the land, then set out with six hundred armed men. They seized Micah's image, ephod, and teraphim, took his priest, and threatened Micah when he protested. They struck Laish with the sword, burned it, rebuilt it and named it Dan, and set up the engraved image, with Jonathan and his sons as priests.
