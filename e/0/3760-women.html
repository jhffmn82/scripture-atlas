Abstract written by a model

In Paul’s Letter to the Ephesians, wives are instructed to be subject to and respect their own husbands as to the Lord, with the union tied to the one-flesh passage and Christ-assembly mystery. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), women are presented chiefly in relation to marriage, inheritance, household conflict, consultation, and divine decree. In Surah 58: The Pleading Woman (Al-Mujaadila), wives are wrongfully treated as mothers when their husbands make that comparison, requiring specified expiation if retracted. In Surah 64: Mutual Disillusion (At-Taghaabun), wives may be enemies for believers, who are told to beware of them and to efface, overlook, and forgive.

women in Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where women first appears

Abstract written by a model

Women are presented chiefly in relation to marriage, inheritance, and household conflict. A wife may receive an inheritance share, money or gifts from her husband, and a strong pledge through marriage, yet she is also described as potentially weak or unable to plan. The account rejects forcibly inheriting women and constraining them to recover what husbands gave them; husbands are instructed to live with wives kindly, and gifts should not be reclaimed merely because a husband seeks another wife. It then addresses feared rebellion by wives through admonition, separation in beds, and scourging, while barring further action if they obey; disputes between spouses may involve arbiters from each family. Finally, women are subjects of consultation and divine decree, including female orphans and weak children. A wife fearing mistreatment or desertion may negotiate peaceful terms with her husband.

19 O ye who believe! It is not lawful for you forcibly to inherit the women (of your deceased kinsmen), nor (that) ye should put constraint upon them that ye may take away a part of that which ye have given them, unless they be guilty of flagrant lewdness. But consort with them in kindness, for if ye hate them it may happen that ye hate a thing wherein Allah hath placed much good. .. 28 Allah would make the burden light for you, for man was created weak.

A wife may not have her gifts taken back when a husband wishes to exchange her for another wife. The text says that spouses have gone in unto one another and taken a strong pledge.

29 O ye who believe! Squander not your wealth among yourselves in vanity, except it be a trade by mutual consent, and kill not one another. Lo! Allah is ever Merciful unto you. .. 35 And if ye fear a breach between them twain (the man and wife), appoint an arbiter from his folk and an arbiter from her folk. If they desire amendment Allah will make them of one mind. Lo! Allah is ever Knower, Aware.

Women who are wives may be considered rebellious when rebellion is feared of them. They are to be admonished, separated in beds, and scourged, but if they obey, no way may be sought against them.

127 They consult thee concerning women. Say: Allah giveth you decree concerning them, and the Scripture which hath been recited unto you (giveth decree), concerning female orphans and those unto whom ye give not that which is ordained for them though ye desire to marry them, and (concerning) the weak among children, and that ye should deal justly with orphans. Whatever good ye do, lo! Allah is ever Aware of it. .. 134 Whoso desireth the reward of the world, (let him know that) with Allah is the reward of the world and the Hereafter. Allah is ever Hearer, Seer.

Women are the subject of consultation and divine decree. A woman who fears ill treatment or desertion from her husband may make terms of peace with him.
