oath and curse (covenant)

event · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: a curse and into an oath, entered into a curse and into an oath

Abstract written by a model

A covenant sworn by the people, with their brothers and nobles, under oath and curse to walk in God's law and keep all the LORD's commandments, ordinances, and statutes. Its terms forbid intermarriage with the peoples of the land and buying on the Sabbath or holy days, require forgoing seventh year crops and debt exaction, and commit them to a yearly temple tax, wood offering, and bringing firstborn sons, livestock, and tithes. Leaders sealed it by name.

Chapter 10

The people, joined with their brothers and nobles, entered into a curse and an oath to walk in God's law and keep all the LORD's commandments, ordinances, and statutes. Its specific terms include no intermarriage, Sabbath trade restrictions, forgoing the seventh year crops and debt exaction, supporting the temple, and bringing offerings and tithes. Leaders sealed it by name.
