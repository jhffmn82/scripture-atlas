Seventh Lecture, Called Liberation. / First Lesson. .. Eighth Lesson.

Householders may approach mendicants and offer food, clothing, lodging, or other assistance, but they should not injure living beings for the mendicant’s sake. A householder who does so should be instructed not to show such obsequiousness.
