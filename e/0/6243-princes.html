Abstract written by a model

Princes are judged by virtue, power, and benevolent government: under right rule lesser princes submit to worthier ones; under bad rule the weak submit to stronger, forming leagues and seeking enrichment.

Chapter 7

Princes of little virtue or worth submit to those of greater virtue or worth when right government prevails. Under bad government, princes of small power and the weak submit to those who are stronger.

Chapter 14

Princes who did not practise benevolent government could be enriched by ministers whom Confucius rejected. Some princes were joined in leagues against other princes.
