seeking

Also called: our seeking, the seeking

Abstract written by a model

Seeking helps people obtain things within themselves when effort brings gain and neglect brings loss. It does not help obtain things outside themselves when seeking follows the proper course but what is gained is only as appointed.

Chapter 3
