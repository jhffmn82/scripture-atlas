V. Âbân Yast.

Sacrifice is repeatedly commanded for Ardvi Sûra Anâhita and is offered with prayer, propitiation, glorification, libations, Haoma, meat, baresma, holy spells, words, and deeds. Ahura Mazda and numerous human figures perform sacrifices to seek her favor and boons.

XX.

Sacrifices to Ardvi Sûra Anâhita are made with libations, gifts, prayers, spells, Haoma, meat, baresma, speech, deeds, and rightly spoken words. Through such sacrifices, worshippers request victory, prosperity, knowledge, good husbands, safe delivery, swift horses, and other favours.
