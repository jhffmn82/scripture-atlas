Wang T'ai

Also called: Master

Abstract written by a model

Wang T'ai is a mutilated man of Lu and a prophet whose disciples rival Confucius's. Though he neither preaches nor discusses, he teaches TAO through a complete mind, indifference to outward form, and seeing all things as one; visitors arrive empty and leave full through his virtue.

CHAPTER V.

Wang T'ai has had his toes cut off, yet he has as many disciples as Confucius. He does not preach or discuss, but those who come to him empty depart full because he directs his mind toward the climax of virtue and sees all things as one.
