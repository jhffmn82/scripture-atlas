Abstract written by a model

Virtue is an honourable quality used to practise benevolence. It enables a person to become sovereign, pleasing people and gaining sincere submission. A prince should esteem it and honour virtuous scholars. Shun treated it as common property and spread it through example. Though one of three acknowledged honors, it surpasses nobility and age in helping one’s generation and presiding over people.

Chapter 3

Virtue enables a person to practise benevolence and become the sovereign of the kingdom. It causes people to be pleased in their hearts and sincerely submit.

Chapter 4

A prince seeking to avoid disgrace should esteem virtue and honor virtuous scholars.

Chapter 8

Shun regarded virtue as the common property of himself and others. Practising virtue by taking example from others helps them practise it as well.

Chapter 2

Virtue is one of the three universally acknowledged honors, along with nobility and age. In helping one’s generation and presiding over the people, virtue is greater than the other two.
