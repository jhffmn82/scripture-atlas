Chapter 8

The scribes write the letter on the twenty-third day of Nisan, seal it with the king's ring, and send it by couriers and horsemen to 127 provinces in their own languages. It denounces Haman, orders the Jews allowed to use their own customs and defend themselves on the thirteenth of Adar, and calls for a day of festivity. It threatens any city or province that does not comply with destruction by spear and fire, and copies are posted publicly and published in Susa.
