Abstract written by a model

In Surah 6: The Cattle (Al-An'aam), the disbelievers deny and deride Allah's revelations, reject resurrection, ascribe rivals to Allah, demand signs, and face loss, punishment, and regret. In Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba), the disbelievers oppose Allah's guidance and obstruct His way, while the Prophet strives against them harshly and they face chastisement, humiliation, painful doom, and hell. In Surah 10: Jonas (Yunus), disbelievers reject the revelations, call them magic, follow conjecture, deny meeting Allah, and face boiling drink, painful doom, the Fire, and dreadful punishment. In Surah 25: The Criterion (Al-Furqaan), the disbelievers accuse Muhammad of inventing a lie, call the revelation fables, demand signs, and reject the Hour and the Qur'an, facing fire, hardship, and judgment. In Surah 29: The Spider (Al-Ankaboot), disbelievers deny Allah's revelations, reject meeting Him, and urge believers to follow their way, facing Allah's doom without hope of His mercy. In Surah 34: Sheba (Saba), disbelievers deny the Hour, resurrection, the Qur'an, and Allah's revelations, challenge the messengers, and face torment, the Fire, remorse, and carcans on their necks. In Surah 35: The Originator (Faatir), disbelievers face awful doom and hellfire, where their torment does not end or lessen, and they plead for release without help. In Surah 38: The letter Saad (Saad), disbelievers reject the warner, call him a wizard and charlatan, object to worshipping One Allah, claim the message is invented, and face doom, the Fire, and Hell.

and 11 more works

In Surah 40: The Forgiver (Al-Ghaafir), the disbelievers argue about Allah's revelations, reject the messengers and Scripture, ascribe partners to Allah, and are dragged through boiling waters and into the Fire. In Surah 47: Muhammad, disbelievers follow falsehood, turn people from Allah's way, oppose the messenger, and hate what Allah revealed, facing perdition and the Fire. In Surah 50: The letter Qaaf (Qaaf), the disbelievers marvel at the warner, call his message strange, deny the truth, and doubt that the dead will be raised. In Surah 53: The Star (An-Najm), the disbelievers in the Hereafter name the angels with female names without knowledge, follow guesswork, and some turn from Allah's remembrance while desiring worldly life. In Surah 56: The Inevitable (Al-Waaqia), the rejecters, who are erring, receive boiling water and roasting at hell-fire. In Surah 61: The Ranks (As-Saff), disbelievers are averse to Allah's light and the religion of truth, but cannot put out Allah's light. In Surah 64: Mutual Disillusion (At-Taghaabun), disbelievers deny resurrection, Allah's revelations, and the messengers' proofs, and abide in the Fire. In Surah 66: The Prohibition (At-Tahrim), disbelievers are told not to make excuses on the appointed day and are told they are being paid for what they did; the Prophet is commanded to strive against them and be stern. In Surah 77: The Emissaries (Al-Mursalaat), repudiators repeatedly face woe on the Day of Decision, deny the doom, offer no excuses, and do not bow when told to bow. In Surah 98: The Evidence (Al-Bayyina), those who disbelieve among the People of the Scripture and the idolaters are described as the worst of created beings and abide in hellfire. In Surah 109: The Disbelievers (Al-Kaafiroon), disbelievers are addressed as not worshipping what the speaker worships, with their religion assigned to them.
