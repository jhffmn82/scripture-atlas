hell

place · 11 works · 9 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: hell-fire, kingdom of the devil

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 13: The Thunder (Ar-Ra'd), hell is the dire abode and habitation of those who do not answer Allah's call. In Surah 32: The Prostration (As-Sajda), hell will be filled with the jinn and mankind together because of evildoing. In Surah 44: The Smoke (Ad-Dukhaan), the sinner is dragged into the midst of hell and punished with boiling water. In Surah 50: The letter Qaaf (Qaaf), hell is asked whether it is filled and replies that more may come, and the evil-doer is commanded to be hurled into its dreadful doom. In Surah 81: The Overthrowing (At-Takwir), hell is lighted. In Surah 82: The Cleaving (Al-Infitaar), hell is where the wicked will be, burning on the Day of Judgment, and they will not be absent from it. In Surah 83: Defrauding (Al-Mutaffifin), the repudiators burn in hell, where they are told that it is what they used to deny. In Surah 89: The Dawn (Al-Fajr), hell is brought near on that day, when man remembers but finds that remembrance of no avail.

and 3 more works

In Surah 102: Competition (At-Takaathur), hell-fire will be beheld and seen with sure vision. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the kingdom of the devil is inherited by those raised to endless misery. In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), hell receives deceivers and the false with blackened faces, and naked people, and appears very formidable to a naked man.
