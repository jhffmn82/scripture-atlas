Abstract written by a model

God's conditional covenant with Solomon: if he walks in integrity and uprightness like David and keeps the statutes, his throne over Israel will be established forever. If he or his children turn to other gods, Israel will be cut off from the land, the house cast out, and passersby will explain it as abandoning the LORD.

Chapter 9

The LORD promises that if Solomon walks in integrity and uprightness as David did and keeps his statutes, his throne over Israel will be established forever. If Solomon or his children turn away to serve other gods, the LORD will cut Israel off from the land and cast out the house, and passersby will say it happened because they abandoned the LORD who brought their fathers out of Egypt.
