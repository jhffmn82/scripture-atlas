Gahi

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: the Gahi, the wicked Gainis

Abstract written by a model

Gahi is a whore addicted to Yâtu, who goes a-whoring after faithful and unfaithful alike; her look and touch wither floods, plants, grass, and the faithful person’s good thoughts, words, deeds, strength, fiend-killing power, and holiness.
