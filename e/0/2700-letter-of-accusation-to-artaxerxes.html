Chapter 4

Written in Syrian by Rehum, Shimshai, and their companions, the letter says the Jews are rebuilding walls and foundations and warns the king he will lose his possession beyond the River. It urges a search of the records of the king's fathers. It succeeds in prompting Artaxerxes' order to halt the building.
