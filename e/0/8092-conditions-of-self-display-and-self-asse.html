conditions of self-display and self-assertion

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Such conditions

Abstract written by a model

From the standpoint of the Tao, self-display and self-assertion are like remnants of food or a tumour on the body, disliked by all; those pursuing the course of the Tao neither adopt nor allow them.

The conditions of self-display and self-assertion are viewed from the standpoint of the Tao as like remnants of food or a tumour on the body, which all dislike. Those who pursue the course of the Tao do not adopt or allow them.
