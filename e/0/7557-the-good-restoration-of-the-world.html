Abstract written by a model

The good restoration of the world is a restoration performed by Saoshyant and helpers, with blessed beings bringing it about. It unfolds as the dead rise, life and immortality come, and the world becomes ever-living, deathless, free from decay and death, increasing, and master of its wish.

XIX. Zamyâd Yast.

The restoration of the world occurs when the dead rise, life and immortality come, and the world becomes ever-living, increasing, and master of its wish. The Amesha-Spentas and other blessed beings are to bring it about.

XIV.

The restoration of the world occurs when Saoshyant and his helpers restore it at its wish. It makes the world deathless, free from decay and death, and accompanied by the rising of the dead.
