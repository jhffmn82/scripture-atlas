Prince Woke

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: His Augustness Woke, King Woke, the younger brother His Augustness Woke

Abstract written by a model

Prince Woke, a younger brother of Prince Ohoke and child of King Ichi-no-be-no-oshiha, fled after his father’s death, hid in Harima, and worked as a groom and cow-herd. Later discovered with Ohoke, he was to wed Ofuwo, joined the Song-Hedge, helped besiege and slay Grandee Shibi, and became King by ruling the Empire after Ohoke ceded his claim.

Prince Woke is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

He was one of King Ichi-no-be-no-oshiha’s children. After his father was slain, he fled with King Ohoke, crossed into Harima, hid his person, and worked as a groom and cow-herd.

Sect. CLXIII.—Emperor Sei-Nei (Part I.—Search for a Successor to Him). .. Sect. CLXVI.—Emperor Sei-Nei (Part IV.—Prince Ohoke Cedes the Empire to Prince Woke).

Prince Woke was one of the two princes discovered by Wodate and was about to wed Ofuwo. He joined the Song-Hedge with Grandee Shibi, helped besiege and slay him, and ruled the Empire first after receiving Prince Ohoke's cession.
