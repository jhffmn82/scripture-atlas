Tsang Wan

person · 2 works · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

In The Analects of Confucius, Book V (Kung-Ye Ch'Ang), Tsang Wan kept a large tortoise in a house with hills made on the capitals of the pillars and representations of duckweed on the small pillars above the beams supporting the rafters. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XV (Wei Ling Kung), Tsang Wan is criticized as possibly having stolen his situation because he did not procure Hui of Liu-hsia to stand with him in court.
