Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Samuel, David's enemies, including Saul and a strong foe, attack him, but he pursues and crushes them with the LORD's help. In Psalms, the enemy is David's foe who triumphs over him, and David prays that it will not rejoice when he falls. In Psalms, adversaries are David's wicked enemies who attack him with hatred, hostility, and scorn, and are destined for shame. In Psalms, evil men are David's violent, proud enemies who plot mischief, wage war, use poisoned tongues, and set traps for his feet. In Psalms, workers of iniquity are wicked people who lay snares and traps for the psalmist. In Psalms, the enemy pursues David's soul and afflicts him, while David asks the LORD to deliver him and destroy his foes. In Psalms: The wicked oppress David and surround him and those with him, closing up their callous hearts and speaking proudly with their mouths. In Psalms: David's enemies, described as strong, hateful, and too mighty for him, came on him in his day of calamity.
