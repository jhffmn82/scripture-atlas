Abstract written by a model

Raphael's wages are half of all Tobias brought back, proposed by Tobias and agreed by Tobit as due; the angel is told to take it, but Raphael gives no recorded response, instead teaching and revealing himself.

Chapter 12

Tobit says the companion must have his wages and more, and Tobias proposes half of what he brought back. Tobit agrees it is due and the angel is told to take half of all that was brought. The text records no response from Raphael to the offer, as he turns to teach and then reveals himself.
