Abstract written by a model

The offering of the first fruits sheaf is a ritual commanded for after the people enter the land and reap its harvest. They bring a sheaf to the priest, who waves it before the LORD on the day after the Sabbath. That day a year-old unblemished male lamb is burnt, with a fine flour and oil meal offering and a wine drink offering. Until it is brought, no new grain may be eaten, a statute forever.

Chapter 23

After entering the land and reaping its harvest, the people are to bring a sheaf of the first fruits to the priest, who waves it before the LORD on the day after the Sabbath. On that day a male lamb a year old without defect is offered as a burnt offering, with a meal offering of two tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil and a drink offering of a fourth of a hin of wine. No bread, roasted grain, or fresh grain may be eaten until this offering is brought, as a statute forever.
