Abiathar

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Samuel, Abiathar escaped Saul's massacre of the priests of Nob, fled to David with the ephod, reported the killings, and received David's promise of safety. In The First Book of Kings, Abiathar bore the ark before David, was removed from the priesthood and exiled to Anathoth by Solomon, and was replaced by Zadok.

Abstract written by a model

Abiathar is a priest who bore the ark before David; Solomon, though judging him deserving of death, spares him, removes him from the priesthood and exiles him to his Anathoth fields, Zadok replacing him, fulfilling the word about Eli's house.

Chapter 2

Solomon says Abiathar deserves death but spares him because he bore the ark before David and shared his afflictions. He is sent to his fields at Anathoth and removed as priest, fulfilling the LORD's word about the house of Eli, and Zadok replaces him.
