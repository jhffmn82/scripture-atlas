Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the righteous have a congregation where sinners cannot stand, and the LORD knows their way. In Proverbs, the righteous are portrayed as secure, favored, cautious, just, respectful of animal life, flourishing, protected from trouble, favored by the LORD, dwelling in the land, and following the way of life. In The Wisdom of Solomon, the righteous live forever with their reward in the Lord and the Most High’s care, receiving the royal crown and beautiful diadem from his hand and protection by his right hand and arm.

the righteous in Proverbs

author unknown · 969 to 699 BC · the work’s page · read it where the righteous first appears

Abstract written by a model

The righteous are a group portrayed as secure and favored. They hold just thoughts, are cautious in friendship, and respect their animals' lives. Their root stays unmoved and flourishing, their house stands, and they escape trouble, suffering no mischief. They obtain the LORD's favor and dwell in the land. The way of righteousness is described as life, with no death in its path.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The upright are those for whom the LORD lays up sound wisdom, and those who walk in integrity have him as a shield. The text says the upright will dwell in the land and the perfect will remain in it.

Chapter 12

The righteous have just thoughts, and their root shall not be moved and flourishes. Their house shall stand, and they come out of trouble. Their speech rescues them, and no mischief shall happen to them. A righteous man respects the life of his animal, and a righteous person is cautious in friendship. In the way of righteousness is life, with no death in its path.
