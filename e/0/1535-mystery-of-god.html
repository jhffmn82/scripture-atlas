Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Job, the mystery of God is described as beyond human knowing, with immeasurable height, depth, length, and breadth. In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, the mysteries of God are known by Nephi, who has great knowledge of God's goodness and mysteries.
