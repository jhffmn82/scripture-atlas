Abstract written by a model

The Levite is the husband of a concubine. He travels to Bethlehem Judah to win her back, stays with her father, then leaves and lodges in Gibeah, where he gives her to the city's men. Finding her fallen at the door, he takes her home, cuts her into twelve pieces, and sends them through Israel. At Mizpah he recounts the attack to the assembly and asks their counsel.

Chapter 19

The Levite goes to Bethlehem Judah to speak kindly to his concubine and bring her back, stays several days with her father, and then leaves in the evening, refusing to lodge in a foreign city and choosing Gibeah. At the old man's house he gives his concubine to the men of the city, then finds her fallen at the door, takes her home, cuts her into twelve pieces, and sends them throughout Israel.

Chapter 20

The Levite, husband of the murdered woman, tells the assembly at Mizpah that he came to Gibeah with his concubine to spend the night and was attacked there. He says he cut her in pieces and sent them throughout Israel, and asks the people for their advice and counsel.
