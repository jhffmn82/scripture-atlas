Abstract written by a model

Mount Nebo is a mountain in the Abarim range in Moab, across from Jericho, with Pisgah at its top. The LORD tells Moses to climb it, see Canaan from afar, and die there. Moses then ascends from the plains of Moab to Pisgah and is shown the whole land he will not enter.

Chapter 32

Mount Nebo, in the Abarim range in the land of Moab across from Jericho, is the place the LORD tells Moses to climb. From there Moses will see the land of Canaan from a distance. He is to die on that mountain and be gathered to his people, as Aaron was on Mount Hor.

Chapter 34

Mount Nebo is where Moses goes up from the plains of Moab, to the top of Pisgah opposite Jericho. From there the LORD shows him the whole land he will not enter.
