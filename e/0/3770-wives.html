Chapter 5

Wives are told to be subject to their own husbands as to the Lord, in everything, just as the assembly is subject to Christ. The chapter ends by saying the wife must see that she respects her husband. The husband-wife union is linked to the one-flesh passage and to the mystery concerning Christ and the assembly.
