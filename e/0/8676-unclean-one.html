Fargard IX.

The unclean one walks to the holes, is sprinkled with gômêz, cleanses the body with dust, washes with water, and remains secluded for three, six, and nine nights. After nine nights and a final washing, the unclean one may approach the fire, water, earth, cow, trees, and faithful.
