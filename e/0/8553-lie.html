Lie

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Lie-demon, harmful Lie

Abstract written by a model

The Lie is a harmful doctrinal power opposed to Righteousness, delivering its settlements to death. Followed by rejecters of truthful teaching, it is linked to evil rulers, wicked deeds, and the wicked’s eternal abode. Failing to oppose an evil wrongdoer leads there. Its demon, hate, and bondsmen are guarded against and prayed away, while the Kingdom is to smite it.

Lie is in one work on this site, Yasna. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Yasna XXXI.

The Lie opposes Righteousness and delivers its settlements to death. Its harmful doctrines are followed by those who reject the truthful teaching, and its demon is to be smitten by the holy Kingdom.

Yasna XLVI.

The Lie is associated with evil rulers, wicked deeds, and the abode where the wicked remain forever. A person who fails to oppose an evil wrongdoer is said to go to the abode of the Lie.

Yasna LIII.

The Lie-demon is opposed and guarded against. Its hate and the hate of its bondsmen are prayed away from the body, and its courses are said to destroy mental life.
