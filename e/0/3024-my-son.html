Chapter 4

The son is instructed throughout the chapter to give promptly to the poor, defend the wronged, and act as father and husband figure to the fatherless and their mother, so that he will be as a son of the Most High. He is also told to seek wisdom, avoid partiality and sinful shame, speak truth, confess his sins, and be consistent in giving and repaying.

Chapter 6

The son is instructed to gather instruction from youth, come to wisdom as one who plows and sows, and bear her yoke and bonds with all his soul.
