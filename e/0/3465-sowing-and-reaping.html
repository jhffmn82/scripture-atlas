Abstract written by a model

Sowing and reaping is a principle that whatever a man sows he reaps, so God is not mocked. Sowing to the flesh yields corruption, sowing to the Spirit yields eternal life; believers will reap in due season if they persist in doing good.

Chapter 6

Sowing and reaping is used as a principle that whatever a man sows he will also reap, so God is not mocked. Sowing to the flesh reaps corruption, while sowing to the Spirit reaps eternal life, and the brothers are told they will reap in due season if they do not give up doing good.
