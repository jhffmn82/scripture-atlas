Also called: the outer court

Abstract written by a model

The outer court is a space in a temple vision, surrounding the inner court. In Chapter 40 the guide shows the speaker a pavement all around it, with thirty rooms, lying beside the gates as the lower pavement; one hundred cubits separate the lower gate from the inner court on the east and north. In Chapter 42 the speaker is led north into it, finding rooms fifty cubits long, and priests must change garments before entering.

outer court is in one work on this site, The Book of Ezekiel. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 40

The man brings the speaker into it and shows rooms and a pavement all around, with thirty rooms on the pavement. The pavement lies beside the gates, matching their length, and is called the lower pavement. The width from the lower gate to the inner court's forefront is one hundred cubits on the east and north.

Chapter 42

The guide brings the speaker out into the outer court toward the north. The rooms there are fifty cubits long, and the entry to the rooms on the east side is reached from the outer court. Priests must not go out into it from the holy place until they have changed their garments.
