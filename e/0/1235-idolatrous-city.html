Abstract written by a model

A hypothetical city whose inhabitants are reported to have been drawn by wicked fellows to serve other gods. The people must diligently investigate; if the report is true, inhabitants and livestock are killed by sword, plunder is burned with the city as an offering to the LORD, and it remains a heap forever, never rebuilt.

Chapter 13

If it is reported that wicked fellows from among the people have drawn the inhabitants of a city to serve other gods, the people must inquire, investigate, and ask diligently. If it is true, they are to strike the inhabitants and livestock with the sword, gather the plunder into the street, and burn the city with it as an offering to the LORD. It is to remain a heap forever and never be rebuilt, and nothing of the devoted thing may be kept.
