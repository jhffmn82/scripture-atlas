Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the camp is where the LORD dwells, and it must not be defiled, so lepers, those with a discharge, and those unclean by a corpse are put outside it. In Deuteronomy, the military camp is holy because the LORD walks in its midst, and soldiers must avoid every evil thing, with the unclean staying outside until evening and waste taken outside and covered.

Chapter 23

When the Israelites camp against enemies, they must keep from every evil thing. A man made unclean by a nighttime emission must stay outside until evening, bathe, and return at sundown, and waste must be relieved outside the camp and covered with a trowel, so the camp stays holy because the LORD walks in it.
