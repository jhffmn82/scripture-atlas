Chapter 26

The husband of a good wife is called happy, and the number of his days will be doubled. He fulfills his years in peace, is delighted by her grace, and has his bones strengthened by her knowledge. By contrast, the man who takes hold of a wicked woman is compared to one grasping a scorpion.
