Shao-lien

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Shao-lien is named among the men who retired to privacy from the world. Confucius says that Shao-lien surrendered his will and submitted to taint, but that his words corresponded with reason and his actions were admirable.

Shao-lien is in one work on this site, The Analects of Confucius, Book XVIII: Wei Tsze. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
