Abstract written by a model

The eleven and their companions in Jerusalem first doubt the women's report, then say the Lord is risen. Terrified when Jesus appears, they are convinced by his hands, feet and eating. He names them witnesses of his resurrection and tells them to wait for power; after his ascension they worship and continually praise God in the temple.

Chapter 24

The eleven and others first disbelieve the women's report, then gather and say the Lord is risen and has appeared to Simon. When Jesus appears, they are terrified and think he is a spirit, but he shows them his hands and feet and eats before them. He opens their minds, names them witnesses, tells them to wait for power from on high, and after his ascension they worship him and continually praise God in the temple.
