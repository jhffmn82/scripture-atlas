the people

Abstract written by a model

The people eagerly built King Wen’s marvellous tower in less than a day, rejoicing in the tower and pond and sharing pleasure in his deer, fish, and turtles. Their response portrays them figuratively as King Wen’s children, unlike their wish for Jie’s death.

Chapter 2

The people addressed themselves to building King Wen's marvellous tower and completed it in less than a day. They rejoiced to build the tower and pond and were pleased that King Wen had deer, fish, and turtles.
