Chapter 11

The wolf will live with the lamb, and the leopard will lie down with the young goat, with the calf, young lion and fattened calf together, led by a little child. The cow and bear will graze, their young will lie down together, and the lion will eat straw like the ox. Nursing and weaned children will play safely near the cobra's hole and viper's den.
