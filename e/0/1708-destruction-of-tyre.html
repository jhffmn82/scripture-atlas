Chapter 26

The destruction is described in detail: siege works, broken walls and towers, trampled streets, slain people, plundered riches, and stones, timber, and dust thrown into the waters. The islands and princes of the sea will tremble and lament, and Tyre will become a terror that is sought but never found again.
