Emperor Yū-Riyaku

person · 1 work · 16 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: August Child of the High-Shining Sun, Heavenly Sovereign Oho-hatsuse, Heavenly Sovereign Oho-hatsuse-no-waka-take, His Augustness Oho-hatsuse-no-waka-take, Our Great Lord, the Heavenly Sovereign, the Heavenly Sovereign Oho-hatsuse

Abstract written by a model

Emperor Yū-Riyaku, also called His Augustness Oho-hatsuse-no-waka-take and the Heavenly Sovereign Oho-hatsuse, dwelt at the palace of Asakura at Hatsuse and ruled the Empire. He married Queen Wake-kusaka-be, Queen Waka-kusaka-be, and Princess Karu; he fathered Shiraka and Princess Waka-tarashi. His life includes commands concerning Akawi-ko, a wedding at Yeshinu, progresses to Yeshinu and Mount Kadzuraki, reverence for the Great Deity of Kadzuraki, and a flagon from Princess Wodo. He established clans and retainers, hunted and composed songs, lived 124 years, and had slain King Ichinobe, provoking Ken-Zō’s wish for revenge.

Emperor Yū-Riyaku is in one work on this site, Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume III (Emperor Nin-toku to Empress Sui-ko). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Emperor Yū-Riyaku, called His Augustness Oho-hatsuse-no-waka-take, dwelt in the palace of Asakura at Hatsuse and ruled the Empire. He married Queen Waka-kusaka-be and Princess Karu, had Shiraka and Princess Waka-tarashi, established clans and retainers, made progresses and hunts, composed songs, and was said to have lived one hundred and twenty-four years.

Sect. CLXVII.—Emperor Ken-Zō (Part I.—The Old Woman Oki-Me). .. Sect. CLXX.—Emperor Ken-Zō (Part IV.—His Age and Place of Burial). / Sect. CLXXI.—Emperor Nin-Ken.

Emperor Yū-Riyaku, called the Heavenly Sovereign Oho-hatsuse, had slain King Ichinobe. Emperor Ken-Zō deeply hated him and wished to take revenge on his spirit, while His Augustness Ohoke regarded him as their uncle and as a Heavenly Sovereign who had ruled the Empire.
