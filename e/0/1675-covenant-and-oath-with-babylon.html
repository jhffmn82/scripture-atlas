Chapter 17

The covenant and oath were made by the king of Babylon with a royal offspring so that the kingdom might be kept low and stand by keeping the covenant. The king despised the oath and broke the covenant by seeking help from Egypt, and the LORD calls them his own oath and covenant and says he will bring them on the king's head.
