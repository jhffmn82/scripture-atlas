the idolaters

group · 4 works · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: idolaters, partners of your make-believe, those who ascribed partners (unto Allah), ye (idolaters)

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 6: The Cattle (Al-An'aam), the idolaters associate partners, sons, and daughters with Allah, invoke beings unable to profit or harm, invent rules for cattle and crops, kill their children, demand signs, excuse idolatry as Allah’s will, follow opinion and guesswork rather than knowledge, and are declared wrongdoers. In Surah 16: The Bee (An-Nahl), the idolaters worship beside Allah, assign partners and daughters to Him, claim that Allah willed their idolatry, are warned that their inventions will fail, and will face punishment. In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), the idolaters choose gods beside Allah and worship what cannot benefit or harm them; they and what they worship are described as fuel for hell. In Surah 98: The Evidence (Al-Bayyina), the idolaters could not leave off error until the clear proof came to them, and those among them who disbelieve will abide in the fire of hell.
