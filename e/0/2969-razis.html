Razis, a Jerusalem elder, is well reported and called Father of the Jews for his good will toward countrymen, having earlier risked life for Jewish religion. When Nicanor sends five hundred soldiers to seize him, he violently kills himself, calling on the Lord of life.

Chapter 14

Razis is an elder of Jerusalem, well reported and called Father of the Jews, who had earlier risked body and life for the Jewish religion. Nicanor sends over five hundred soldiers to seize him, and he tries to fall on his sword, leaps from the wall, and tears out his bowels, calling on the Lord of life and spirit to restore them before he dies.
