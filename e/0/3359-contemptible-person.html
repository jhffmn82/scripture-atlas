Abstract written by a model

A contemptible person rises to a kingdom's throne without being granted its honor. He arrives in a time of security, gains power through flatteries, sweeps away forces and the prince of the covenant, and grows strong through deceit after a treaty. He plunders, attacks strongholds and the king of the south, then, opposed by Kittim's ships, turns hostile against the holy covenant.

Chapter 11

The contemptible person stands up in the place of the tax-collector's kingdom, though the honor of the kingdom had not been given to him. He comes in a time of security and obtains the kingdom by flatteries, sweeps away overwhelming forces and the prince of the covenant, and after a treaty works deceitfully while becoming strong with a small people. He scatters plunder, plots against strongholds, attacks the king of the south, and then directs his heart and actions against the holy covenant after ships of Kittim oppose him.
