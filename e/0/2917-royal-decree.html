royal decree

Also called: a royal decree, the law of the king

Abstract written by a model

An unalterable royal decree, proposed by Memucan and written under the laws of the Medes and Persians, bars Vashti from entering the king's presence and gives her royalty to a better woman. Sent to all provinces in their own languages, it aims to make wives honor their husbands.

Esther (Greek) / Chapter 1

On Memucan's advice, the king made a decree written according to the laws of the Medes and Persians, not to be altered, forbidding Vashti to come in to the king and giving her royalty to a better woman. It was sent through all the provinces in their own languages so that men might be feared in their own houses and wives would honor their husbands.
