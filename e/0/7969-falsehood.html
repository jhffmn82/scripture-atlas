falsehood

Also called: false

Abstract written by a model

Falsehood makes false man love falsehood and forget the Creator; it is tied to covetousness, impurity of the tongue, and dwells in the false one’s mouth. Prevailing in this age as master of the mint, it brings contempt, but cleansing and devotion remove it and wash away sin.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Slok X .. Pauri X

Falsehood causes man to love what is false and forget the Creator. It is associated with covetousness, and its removal cleanses man and washes away sin.

Slok XI .. Pauri XI

Falsehood prevails in the age and is described as master of the mint. Guru Nanak says that devotion can remove every trace of falsehood from a person.

Slok XVII .. Pauri XVII

Falsehood dwells in the mouth of the false person, who is ever despised.
