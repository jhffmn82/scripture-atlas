Abstract written by a model

A borrower who treats a loan as a windfall: submissive, kissing the lender's hands until the money arrives. When repayment is due, he delays, makes excuses, blames the season, repays only half or with cursing and disgrace, and needlessly makes an enemy of the creditor.

Chapter 29

Many borrowers treat a loan as a windfall, kissing the lender's hands and speaking submissively until they receive the money. When payment is due they delay, make excuses, and blame the season, and they may repay only half or pay with cursing, railing, and disgrace instead of honor, making an enemy of the creditor without cause.
