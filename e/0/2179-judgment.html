judgment

topic · 4 works · 1 quoted fact · 3 connections
Also called: controversies, judge, judging righteously, judgment and justice, just cause, the judgment

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Chronicles, judgment and justice are administered faithfully, courageously, and without iniquity, partiality, or bribes. In Psalms, judgment is carried out and prepared by the LORD, who judges righteously and will judge the world in righteousness. In The Second Book of Nephi, judgment is what the Lord of Hosts seeks from the house of Israel and is where he will be exalted. In The Book of Ether, judgment is executed in righteousness by Orihah and Shule.

judgment and justice in The Second Book of Chronicles

Abstract written by a model

Judgment is presented as being done for the LORD, in his fear, faithfully and with a perfect heart, free of iniquity, respect of persons, and bribes. Those judging controversies must warn people not to be guilty toward the LORD so that wrath does not come on them and their brothers, and are told to deal courageously, with Amariah the chief priest over the LORD's matters and Levites serving as officers.
