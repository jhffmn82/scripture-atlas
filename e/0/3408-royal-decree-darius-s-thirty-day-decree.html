Abstract written by a model

A royal decree signed by Darius, proposed by the presidents and governors, forbids petitioning any god or man except the king for thirty days, on penalty of the lions' den. Bound by the unalterable law of the Medes and Persians, Darius cannot revoke it, leading to Daniel's being thrown into the den.

Chapter 6

The decree, proposed by the presidents and local governors and signed by Darius, forbids anyone for thirty days to petition any god or man except the king, on penalty of being cast into the den of lions. Because it falls under the unalterable law of the Medes and Persians, Darius cannot revoke it, and it leads to Daniel's being thrown into the den.
