Part II / Chapter 1 .. Chapter 38

A sage is a teacher of a hundred generations whose example can purify the corrupt, strengthen the weak, enlarge the mean, and make the niggardly liberal. The sages' doctrines are transmitted across generations, from Yao and Shun through Tang, king Wen, and Confucius.
