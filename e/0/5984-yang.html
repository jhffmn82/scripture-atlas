Yang

person · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Yang Zhu, the philosopher Yang

Abstract written by a model

In Mencius, Book III (Teng Wen Gong), Yang Zhu's words filled the country alongside those of Mo Di, and Mencius opposes his doctrine because it does not acknowledge the claims of the sovereign. In Mencius, Book VII (Jin Xin), Yang is a philosopher who holds that each person should act for himself, refusing even a single-hair sacrifice to benefit the kingdom.
