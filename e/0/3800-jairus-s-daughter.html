Jairus's daughter

Abstract written by a model

In The Good News According to Mark, Jairus's daughter is raised by Jesus after being reported dead. In The Gospel According to Luke, Jairus' daughter is raised by Jesus after dying before he arrives.

Chapter 5

She is Jairus's little daughter, twelve years old, at the point of death and then reported dead. Jesus says she is asleep, takes her by the hand and says 'Talitha cumi,' and she immediately rises and walks. Jesus orders that she be given something to eat.
