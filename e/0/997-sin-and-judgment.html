Isaiah 1 presents sin and judgment: the people, burdened with iniquity and scarlet sins, are offered cleansing as white as snow. Yet if they refuse and rebel, the sword devours them; the LORD will avenge, purge dross, and consume those who forsake him unquenchably.

The Book of Isaiah / Chapter 1

The people are called loaded with iniquity, and their sins are said to be like scarlet and crimson, though the LORD offers to make them white as snow and wool. Refusal and rebellion will bring devouring by the sword. The LORD promises to avenge himself on adversaries, purge dross, and consume transgressors, sinners, and those who forsake him, who will burn like tinder and a spark with no one to quench them.
