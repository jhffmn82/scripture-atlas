the second beast

being · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: another beast coming up out of the earth

Abstract written by a model

The second beast rises out of the earth with two lamb-like horns yet speaks like a dragon. It wields the first beast's full authority in his presence and compels the earth's inhabitants to worship him. Through great signs, including fire from the sky, it deceives them into making an image, gives that image breath to speak, and imposes the mark on all people.

Chapter 13

The second beast comes up out of the earth with two horns like a lamb but speaks like a dragon. It exercises all the first beast's authority in its presence and makes the earth's inhabitants worship the first beast. It performs great signs, including bringing fire down from the sky, and deceives people into making an image of the first beast. It gives breath to the image and imposes the mark on all people.
