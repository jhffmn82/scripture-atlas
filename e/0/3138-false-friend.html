Abstract written by a model

A false friend is a fair-weather companion described in Chapter 6: friendly only for an occasion, a table companion who fails in affliction, or one who becomes an enemy and exposes strife to your reproach. In prosperity he acts as an equal and bold over servants; when you are brought low he turns against you and hides. The text advises separating from enemies and being wary of friends.

Chapter 6

False friends are described in several kinds: one who is a friend only for an occasion, one who turns into an enemy and exposes strife to your reproach, and one who is a table companion but does not continue in affliction. In prosperity he acts as your equal and is bold over your servants, but when you are brought low he turns against you and hides from your face. The text advises separating from enemies and being wary of friends.
