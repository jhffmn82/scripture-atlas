wrongful sacrifices

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: He who sacrifices a thing wrongfully gotten, he who brings a sacrifice from the goods of the poor, offering, sacrifice, the multitude of sacrifices, the offerings of the ungodly

Abstract written by a model

Wrongful sacrifices are offerings made from ill-gotten goods. They are declared a mockery, unacceptable to the Lord, and unable to pacify sins however numerous. The text escalates: sacrificing from the poor's goods equals killing a son before his father, and depriving the needy of bread or wages is murder.

Chapter 34

Sacrifices made from wrongfully gotten goods are declared a mockery and unacceptable, and the Most High takes no pleasure in the offerings of the ungodly or in a multitude of sacrifices for sins. A sacrifice from the goods of the poor is likened to killing a son before his father's eyes. The text adds that depriving the needy of bread or a hireling of his hire is like murder.
