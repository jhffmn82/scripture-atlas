Abstract written by a model

Naaman is a valiant captain of the Syrian king's army, credited with a victory the LORD gave Syria, yet afflicted with leprosy. He travels to Israel seeking healing, is angered by Elisha's simple instruction to wash in the Jordan, but heeds his servants, dips seven times, and is cleansed. He then confesses the God of Israel, refuses to give a gift, takes earth, vows worship of the LORD alone, and asks pardon for bowing in Rimmon's house.

Chapter 5

Naaman, captain of the Syrian army, is a great and valiant man but a leper, and he travels to Israel at his king's direction seeking healing. He is angered when Elisha only sends a messenger telling him to wash in the Jordan, but his servants persuade him, and he dips seven times and is made clean. He declares that there is no God in all the earth but in Israel, offers a gift that is refused, asks for two mules' load of earth, vows to offer sacrifice only to the LORD, and asks pardon for bowing in the house of Rimmon when accompanying his master.
