Wickedness is a woman sitting in an ephah basket; an angel names her, forces her down, seals the basket with lead, and she is carried to Shinar, to be set in her own house.

Chapter 5

The angel names the woman sitting in the ephah basket as Wickedness. He throws her down into the basket and weights its mouth with lead. She is carried off to Shinar, where a house will be built for her and she will be set in her own place.
