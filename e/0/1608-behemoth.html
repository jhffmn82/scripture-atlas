behemoth

creature · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Behemoth is a creature made by the LORD along with Job. It eats grass like an ox and has immense strength in its thighs and belly muscles, with bones like bronze tubes and limbs like iron bars. Called the chief of the ways of God, it is given its sword by its maker. It rests under lotus trees and willows, stays unafraid when a river overflows, and is fed by mountains. The text asks whether anyone could capture it.

Chapter 40

Behemoth is presented as a creature the LORD made as well as Job, eating grass like an ox, with strength in its thighs, a tail like a cedar, bones like tubes of bronze and limbs like bars of iron. It is called the chief of the ways of God, and its maker gives it its sword. It lies under the lotus trees and reeds, does not tremble when a river overflows, and the text asks whether anyone can take it on watch or pierce its nose with a snare.
