Chapter 28

The women come to see the tomb and are told by the angel not to be afraid, that Jesus has risen, and to tell his disciples to go to Galilee. They leave quickly with fear and great joy, meet Jesus on the way, worship him, and receive his instruction to tell his brothers to go to Galilee.
