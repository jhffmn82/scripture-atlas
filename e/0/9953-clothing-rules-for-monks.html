Twenty-Third Lecture.

The clothing rules differ between the two Laws: the Law of Pârsva allows an under and upper garment, while the Law of Vardhamâna forbids clothes for a monk. Gautama explains that outward marks are useful for religious life and recognition but are not the true causes of final liberation.
