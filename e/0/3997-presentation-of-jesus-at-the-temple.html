Abstract written by a model

takes_place_in Jerusalem [after purification days] When the days of purification according to the law of Moses are fulfilled, Joseph and Mary bring Jesus up to Jerusalem to present him to the Lord, as the law says every male who opens the womb shall be called holy, and to offer a pair of turtledoves or two young pigeons. Simeon takes the child in his arms, blesses God, and speaks of him as salvation and a light for the nations, and Anna gives thanks and speaks of him. The parents then return to Nazareth after accomplishing everything the law required.
