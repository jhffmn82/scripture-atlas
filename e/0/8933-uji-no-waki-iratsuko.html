Uji-no-waki-iratsuko

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Uji-no waki-iratsuko, the Prince his younger brother, the younger King

Abstract written by a model

Uji-no-waki-iratsuko, Emperor Ō-Jin’s intended successor, was designated to rule Heaven’s sun, defeated Oho-yama-mori after Ō-Jin’s death, repeatedly ceded the Empire with Oho-sazaki, then died early; Oho-sazaki succeeded him.

Uji-no-waki-iratsuko was designated to rule the succession of Heaven’s sun. After Emperor Ō-Jin’s death, he defeated the attack of His Augustness Oho-yama-mori, and he and His Augustness Oho-sazaki repeatedly ceded the Empire to each other until he died early.
