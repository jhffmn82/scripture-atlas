second-month Passover provision

Abstract written by a model

A provision for a second-month Passover, answering the complaint of men made unclean by a dead body or away on a distant journey. In every generation they keep it on the fourteenth day of the second month at evening, eating unleavened bread and bitter herbs, leaving none until morning and breaking no bone, unlike a clean man at home, who is cut off if he neglects it.

Chapter 9

The LORD establishes that anyone unclean by a dead body, or on a journey far away, in any generation, shall still keep the Passover in the second month on the fourteenth day at evening. They eat it with unleavened bread and bitter herbs, leave none until morning, and break no bone, following the full statute of the Passover. This provision answers the unclean men's complaint and is distinguished from the case of a clean man not on a journey, who is cut off if he fails to keep it.
