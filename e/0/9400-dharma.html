Dharma

Abstract written by a model

In Sutrakritanga, Book II, Dharma is presented as an existent entity that the monk should not deny exists. In Uttaradhyayana Sutra, Dharma is described as a formless thing with divisions and indivisible parts, co-extensive with the world and without beginning or end.
