God's order

topic · 3 works · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: His order, His orders, divine order, obedience, obedience to God's orders, order, steed, the steed, the will of the Commander

Abstract written by a model

In Japji, God's order produces bodies and souls, assigns worldly and spiritual conditions, manifests divine qualities, and removes egoism when understood. In Asa ki War (Asa di Var), God's order governs the world, determines spiritual fate, guides worship, and leads the obedient toward God's court. In Hymns of Guru Nanak, obedience to God's orders is sent into the world as a steed, with reflection on the Guru's instruction serving as its kettle-drums.

God's order in Japji

Max Arthur Macauliffe · the work’s page · read it where God's order first appears

Abstract written by a model

God's order produces bodies and infuses souls, assigning greatness, status, pain, pleasure, reward, and transmigration to all without exemption. In the realm of knowledge, divine light, songs, beauty, incomparable fashioning, and fashioned wisdom and power appear. Understanding God's order removes egoism.

divine order in Asa ki War (Asa di Var)

Abstract written by a model

God's order governs the world, determines people’s spiritual fate regarding mammon, and guides worshippers; obeying it makes a person acceptable and leads toward God's court.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Slok II .. Pauri II

God's order determines whom He absorbs, destroys, withdraws from mammon, or causes to remain therein.

Slok XV .. Pauri XV

God's order is the rule by which the worshipper abides and worships the Lord. Obeying God's order makes a person acceptable and leads that person toward God's court.
