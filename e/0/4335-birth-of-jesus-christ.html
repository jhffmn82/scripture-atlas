birth of Jesus Christ

event · 5 works · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a child, glad tidings of a word from him, shall bear a son, shall conceive, the birth of Jesus, the birth of Jesus Christ, the birth of the Lord, the child’s birth, the day that the Lord should be born, the night before he is born

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, the birth of Jesus Christ occurs after Mary’s engagement to Joseph, when she is found pregnant by the Holy Spirit, fulfilling the Lord’s word through the prophet, and Joseph names the child Jesus. In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan), the birth of Jesus is announced to Mary as the creation of a child without a mortal touching her. In The Second Book of Nephi, the child’s birth from a virgin fulfills the announcement, preceding the forsaking of Ahaz’s hated land by its kings. In The Book of Helaman, the birth of Jesus Christ is signaled by a night that is not dark and by a new star. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the birth of the Lord is recognized through the night without darkness and other promised signs, and the Lord declares that he will come into the world to fulfill the prophets’ words.
