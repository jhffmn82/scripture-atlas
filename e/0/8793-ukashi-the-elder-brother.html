Ukashi the Elder Brother

person · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection

Abstract written by a model

Ukashi the Elder Brother, a resident of Uda, opposes His Augustness Kamu-yamato-ihare-biko by trying to turn back the eight-foot crow and building a palace pitfall. Forced into his own trap, he dies, is cut in pieces, and is slain in the slaying of Ukashi the Elder Brother.
