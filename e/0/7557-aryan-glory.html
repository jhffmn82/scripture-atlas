Aryan Glory

object · 1 work · 17 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Glory of the Aryans, Glory of the Aryas

Abstract written by a model

The Aryan Glory, made by Mazda or Ahura Mazda, is rich in food, flocks, wealth, intelligence, money, and glory. It withstands Need and enemies, destroys named fiends and non-Aryan nations, then brings increase to mountains, vales, and fair golden-hued growing plants, with victory, intelligence, and virtuous offspring.

Aryan Glory is in one work on this site, Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

XVIII. Âstâd Yast.

The Aryan Glory is made by Mazda and is rich in food, flocks, wealth, intelligence, money, and glory. It withstands Need and enemies, destroys several fiends and the non-Aryan nations, and brings increase, victory, intelligence, and virtuous offspring.
