Abstract written by a model

In Surah 89: The Dawn (Al-Fajr), angels come rank on rank when the Lord comes on the final day. In Surah 97: The Power, Fate (Al-Qadr), angels descend during the Night of Predestination by permission of their Lord, with all decrees.
