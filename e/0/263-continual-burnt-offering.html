The continual burnt offering is two year-old unblemished male lambs offered daily with fine flour, beaten oil and a drink offering. Ordained at Mount Sinai as a pleasing aroma to the LORD, it underlies all Sabbath, monthly and festival offerings.

Chapter 28

The continual burnt offering consists of two male lambs a year old without defect, offered day by day, with fine flour mixed with beaten oil and a drink offering. It was ordained at Mount Sinai as a pleasant aroma to the LORD, and all the Sabbath, monthly and festival offerings are made in addition to it.
