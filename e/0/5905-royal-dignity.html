royal dignity

Also called: the royal dignity

Abstract written by a model

Royal dignity is a status attainable through benevolent rule. It is reached when a ruler ensures elderly people wear silk and eat flesh, the black-haired people lack neither hunger nor cold, and the ruler shares the people’s joy and sorrow. Even if a duke merely does good, a descendant in a later generation may attain it.

Chapter 3

A ruler whose state has people of seventy wearing silk and eating flesh, while black-haired people suffer neither hunger nor cold, will attain royal dignity.

Chapter 5

Royal dignity can be attained by a state with a territory only a hundred li square if its ruler dispenses benevolent government.

Chapter 4

Royal dignity is said to be attainable when a ruler rejoices in the joy of the people and grieves at their sorrow.

Chapter 14

Mencius says that if the duke does good, one of his descendants in a later generation may attain royal dignity.
