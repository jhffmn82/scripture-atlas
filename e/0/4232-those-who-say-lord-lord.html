Abstract written by a model

A group who call Jesus 'Lord, Lord' and cite prophesying, exorcisms, and mighty works, yet are excluded from the Kingdom of Heaven; Jesus rejects them as never known, workers of iniquity, since only doing the Father's will suffices.
