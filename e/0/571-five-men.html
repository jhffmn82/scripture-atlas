Chapter 18

The five men, valiant men of Dan from Zorah and Eshtaol, were sent to spy out the land and lodged at Micah's house, where they recognized the Levite and asked him for counsel. They saw Laish's people living in safety and urged their brothers to attack. On the return they told the six hundred about the ephod and images in Micah's house and went in and took them.
