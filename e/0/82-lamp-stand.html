Abstract written by a model

The lamp stand is a tabernacle furnishing of pure hammered gold, made as one piece with base, shaft, cups, buds, and flowers, weighing a talent including all accessories. It has six branches, three per side, with almond-blossom cups, and seven lamps lighting the space in front of it, plus gold snuffers and snuff dishes. It stands on the south side, opposite the table. Chapter 25 gives the instructions; Chapter 37 shows Bezalel making it accordingly.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 25

The lamp stand is to be made of pure gold in hammered work, with base, shaft, cups, buds, and flowers all of one piece. It has six branches, three on each side, with almond-blossom cups, seven lamps to give light in front of it, and pure gold snuffers and snuff dishes, all made from a talent of pure gold.

Chapter 37

Bezalel makes the lamp stand of beaten pure gold, with its base, shaft, cups, buds, and flowers all of one piece. It has six branches, three on each side, with almond-blossom cups, and he adds seven lamps, snuffers, and snuff dishes of pure gold. The whole, with all its vessels, is made from a talent of pure gold.
