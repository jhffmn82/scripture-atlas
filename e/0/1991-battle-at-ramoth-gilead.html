Abstract written by a model

The First Book of Kings records the battle at Ramoth Gilead, during which Ahab was wounded and a cry went out at sunset for every man to return to his city and country. The Second Book of Chronicles records that Ahab died at about sunset in the battle at Ramoth Gilead.

Ahab and Jehoshaphat fight at Ramoth Gilead, with Ahab in disguise and Jehoshaphat in his robes. The Syrian chariot captains pursue Jehoshaphat until they see he is not the king of Israel, then a random arrow wounds Ahab. The battle increases that day, and at sunset a cry goes out for every man to return to his city and country.
