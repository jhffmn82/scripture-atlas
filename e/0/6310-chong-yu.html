Chong Yu begged to question Mencius at Ying. He asked about the coffin because he thought its wood was too good.

Chong Yu questioned Mencius on the way after Mencius left Qi, asking about his dissatisfied appearance and recalling Mencius's teaching that the superior man does not murmur against Heaven or grudge against men.
