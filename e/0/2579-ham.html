1 Chronicles / Chapter 1

Ham's sons are given as Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan, and their descendants are listed. Cush became the father of Nimrod, who began to be a mighty one on the earth, and Canaan fathered Sidon his firstborn along with several peoples.
