Abstract written by a model

The sanctuary belongs to the LORD and is most holy, set within the holy portion offered to the LORD. It occupies a 500 by 500 square with fifty cubits of pasture around it. Chapter 45 places it by the priests' portion; chapter 48 locates it in the offering's middle, beside Judah's border and the prince's land.

Chapter 45

The sanctuary is most holy and sits within the holy portion, on a 500 by 500 square with fifty cubits of pasture lands all around. The priests' portion serves as a place for their houses and a holy place for it.

Chapter 48

The sanctuary of the LORD is placed in the middle of the offering that lies in the portion beside Judah's border. It is also in the middle of the land belonging to the prince.
