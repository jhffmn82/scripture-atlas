Athaliah

person · 2 works · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Athaliah the daughter of Omri, Athaliah the daughter of Omri king of Israel, Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Athaliah destroyed the royal offspring after Ahaziah's death, reigned six years while Joash was hidden, and was seized and slain. In The Second Book of Chronicles, Athaliah was mother and wicked counselor of Ahaziah, destroyed Judah's royal offspring, reigned six years, and was removed and killed by Jehoiada.

Athaliah in The Second Book of Chronicles

author unknown · about 424 to 399 BC · the work’s page · read it where Athaliah first appears

Abstract written by a model

Athaliah, daughter of Omri, was mother and wicked counselor of Ahaziah. After her son died, she destroyed all the royal offspring of Judah's house and reigned over the land six years. When she heard the people praising the king, she entered the LORD's house, tore her clothes, and cried 'Treason!' Jehoiada had her removed and killed at the horse gate.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 22

Athaliah, daughter of Omri, was Ahaziah's mother and his counselor in acting wickedly. When she saw her son was dead, she destroyed all the royal offspring of the house of Judah and then reigned over the land for six years.

Chapter 23

Athaliah heard the noise of the people praising the king and came into the LORD's house, where she saw the king standing by his pillar and tore her clothes, crying 'Treason! treason!' Jehoiada ordered her brought out between the ranks and not killed in the LORD's house. She was killed at the entrance of the horse gate to the king's house.
