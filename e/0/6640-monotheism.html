monotheism

topic · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: There is no Allah save Allah, There is no Allah save Me, single-minded, your Allah is One Allah

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), monotheism was affirmed through the declaration that there is no Allah save One Allah, which all messengers were inspired to proclaim while calling people to worship Allah. In Surah 37: Those drawn up in Ranks (As-Saaffaat), monotheism was stated through the declaration that there is no Allah save Allah, affirming Allah’s oneness and rejecting claims that He has offspring or partners.
