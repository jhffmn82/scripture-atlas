Chapter 10

The speaker cries out over a serious wound, a destroyed tent, broken cords, and children who are gone, with no one left to set up the curtains. He says he must bear his grief, acknowledges that a man's way is not in himself, and asks the LORD to correct him gently and not in anger lest he be reduced to nothing.
