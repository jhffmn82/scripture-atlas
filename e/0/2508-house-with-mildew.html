Abstract written by a model

A house in Canaan afflicted with spreading mildew, shown by greenish or reddish hollow streaks. The owner reports it to the priest, who has it emptied and shut up seven days. If the plague spreads, stones are removed and the house scraped and replastered. If mildew returns, it is destructive, unclean and broken down, its materials taken outside the city; if not, it is pronounced clean and cleansed with the bird ritual.

Chapter 14

A house in Canaan with a spreading mildew, shown by greenish or reddish hollow streaks deeper than the wall, is reported by its owner to the priest. The priest has it emptied, shuts it up seven days, and if the plague has spread has the infected stones removed and the house scraped and replastered. If the mildew returns, the house is destructive and unclean and is broken down, with its materials carried to an unclean place outside the city, but if it does not spread it is pronounced clean and cleansed with the bird ritual.
