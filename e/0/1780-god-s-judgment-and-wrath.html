God's judgment and wrath

Abstract written by a model

God's judgment and wrath on Jerusalem: unprecedented, never to be repeated, because of her abominations and defiled sanctuary. He shows no pity or sparing until His anger is accomplished, making her a reproach among nations and teaching that the LORD spoke in zeal.

Chapter 5

The Lord announces judgments on Jerusalem that He has never done before and will never do again, because of her abominations and the defilement of His sanctuary. His eye will not spare and He will have no pity, until His anger is accomplished and His wrath rests. Through this the people will know that the LORD has spoken in His zeal.
