Abstract written by a model

In Surah 20: Taa-Haa, good works accompany belief, lead to high stations and the Gardens of Eden, and receive undiminished reward from Allah. In Surah 29: The Spider (Al-Ankaboot), good works accompany belief, lead to remission of evil deeds and admission among the righteous, and bring lofty dwellings in the Garden.
