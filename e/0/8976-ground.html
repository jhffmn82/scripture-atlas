Abstract written by a model

Ground is unsuitable for easing nature when it is infected by eggs or living beings, prepared for a monk or nun, occupied by force, covered or treated, wet, dusty, or otherwise containing life. Suitable ground must be inspected and cleaned and must be away from specified public, cultivated, sacred, or dangerous places.
