selection of Judas's replacement

Abstract written by a model

Peter says Judas's place must be filled by a man who accompanied them from John's baptism to the ascension, as a witness of the resurrection. Two candidates, Joseph Barsabbas and Matthias, are put forward; after prayer they draw lots, and the lot falls on Matthias, who is counted with the eleven.

The Acts of the Apostles / Chapter 1

Peter said one of the men who had accompanied them from John's baptism until Jesus was received up must become a witness of the resurrection in Judas's place, citing Psalms. They put forward Joseph called Barsabbas and Matthias, prayed that the Lord who knows all hearts would show his choice, and drew lots. The lot fell on Matthias, who was counted with the eleven apostles.
