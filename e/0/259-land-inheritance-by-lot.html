land inheritance by lot

Abstract written by a model

Land inheritance by lot is the LORD's method for dividing the land among Israel's tribes. In Chapter 26 it is apportioned by number of names, with larger tribes receiving more and smaller ones less, assigned by lot according to fathers' tribal names, while Levites get none. Chapter 33 repeats the command: lot by families and tribes, larger groups larger shares, and wherever the lot falls is the man's.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 26

The LORD says the land is to be divided as an inheritance according to the number of names, giving more to the larger tribes and less to the smaller. It is also to be divided by lot, and the tribes inherit according to their fathers' tribal names. The Levites receive no inheritance among the Israelites.

Chapter 33

The LORD commands that Israel inherit the land by lot according to families and the tribes of their fathers. Larger groups receive a larger inheritance and smaller groups a smaller one, and wherever the lot falls to a man, that is his.
