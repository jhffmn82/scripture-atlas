Chapter 5

Elders who rule well are to be counted worthy of double honor, especially those who labor in the word and in teaching, supported by the sayings about not muzzling the ox and the laborer deserving wages. An accusation against an elder may be received only on the word of two or three witnesses. Those who sin are to be reproved in the sight of all so that the rest may fear.
