Abstract written by a model

The altar of incense is a most holy object made of acacia wood, one cubit long and wide (square) and two cubits high, with horns of one piece with it. Its top, sides and horns are overlaid with pure gold, with a gold molding and two gold rings under the molding holding gold-overlaid carrying poles. It stands before the veil by the mercy seat, and only incense may be offered on it: no strange incense, burnt, meal or drink offerings. It is anointed with holy oil. Aaron makes atonement on its horns once yearly with sin offering blood. Bezalel builds it.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 30

The altar is to be made of acacia wood, a cubit square and two cubits high, with horns of one piece, overlaid with pure gold, with a gold molding, two gold rings, and gold-overlaid poles. It is placed before the veil by the ark and mercy seat, and only incense is allowed on it, with no strange incense, burnt, meal, or drink offerings. It is anointed with the holy oil and is most holy.

Chapter 37

Bezalel makes the altar of incense of acacia wood, square at a cubit by a cubit and two cubits high, with horns of one piece with it. He overlays its top, sides, and horns with pure gold, adds a gold molding, and makes two gold rings under the molding on its two sides for gold-overlaid acacia carrying poles.
