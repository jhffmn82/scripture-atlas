Abstract written by a model

Faith and works is a teaching topic arguing that faith without works is dead. It begins with useless kind words to the hungry and the shuddering belief of demons, then cites Abraham, whose faith was perfected by works, concluding that people are justified by works, not faith alone.

Chapter 2

Faith without works is called dead in itself, useless like kind words that give a hungry, poorly clothed brother or sister nothing, and no better than the belief of demons who shudder. The text says faith worked with Abraham's works and was perfected by them, that a man is justified by works and not only by faith, and that faith apart from works is dead like a body apart from the spirit.
