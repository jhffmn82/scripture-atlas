Also called: great men, the great man

Abstract written by a model

A great man inhabits the world’s proper way, acts for the people when in office, and remains faithful to principle when disappointed. From public service to solitude, he resists the corrupting pull of riches and honours, poverty and mean condition, and power and force.

Chapter 2

A great man dwells in the wide house of the world, stands in its correct seat, and walks in its great path. He practices his principles for the good of the people in office and alone when disappointed, and he is not swayed by riches, poverty, or power.
