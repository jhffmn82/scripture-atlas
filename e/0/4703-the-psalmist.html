Chapter 22

The psalmist, named in the heading as David, cries day and night to a God who seems to have forsaken him, describing himself as a worm, scorned, poured out like water, and surrounded by enemies who pierced his hands and feet and divided his garments. He recalls that God has been his God since birth, pleads for deliverance, and then vows to declare God's name and pay his vows in the great assembly because God heard him.
