Chapter 32

A man wrestles Jacob at the Jabbok until daybreak and, seeing he does not prevail, touches the hollow of Jacob's thigh and strains it. He asks Jacob's name, renames him Israel because he has fought with God and men and prevailed, and blesses him. He declines to give his own name, asking why Jacob asks.
