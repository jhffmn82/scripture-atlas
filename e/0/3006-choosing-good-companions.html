Chapter 9

The man is told to know his neighbors as well as he can and to take counsel with the wise. His conversation should be with men of understanding and centered on the law of the Most High, and righteous people should be his table companions. He is also warned to keep away from a man with power to kill and not to envy or delight in sinners and the ungodly.
