daughter of Herodias

person · 2 works · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: the daughter of Herodias, the young lady

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, she danced at Herod's birthday, asked for John's head on a platter at her mother's prompting, received it, and brought it to her mother. In The Good News According to Mark, she danced for Herod and his guests, asked for John's head on a platter after consulting her mother, received it, and gave it to her mother.
