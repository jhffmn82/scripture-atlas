Chapter 23

The LORD promises to raise a righteous Branch to David who will reign as king, deal wisely, and execute justice and righteousness in the land. In his days Judah will be saved and Israel will dwell safely, and he will be called 'The LORD our righteousness.'

Chapter 33

The LORD says he will cause a Branch of righteousness to grow up to David in those days. The Branch will execute justice and righteousness in the land.
