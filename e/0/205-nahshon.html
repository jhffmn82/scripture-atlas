Chapter 7

Nahshon son of Amminadab, of the tribe of Judah, offered first, on the first day of the altar's dedication. His offering was a silver platter of 130 shekels and a silver bowl of 70 shekels full of flour and oil, a golden ladle of 10 shekels full of incense, and burnt, sin, and peace offering animals.
