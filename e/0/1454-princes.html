Chapter 36

The princes, including Elishama, Delaiah, Elnathan, Gemariah, and Zedekiah, hear Micaiah's report and have Baruch read the scroll to them. They turn in fear, question Baruch, tell him and Jeremiah to hide, and store the scroll in Elishama's room before telling the king. Elnathan, Delaiah, and Gemariah plead with the king not to burn it.
