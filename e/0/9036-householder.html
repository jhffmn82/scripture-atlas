Abstract written by a model

A householder gives and prepares lodging for mendicants; preparing, altering, cleaning, furnishing, or surrendering it may make it unsuitable or affected by sins and create occasions for attachment, approval, dislike, or sexual temptation.

Second Lecture, Called Begging for a Couch. / First Lesson. .. Third Lesson.

A householder may prepare, alter, clean, furnish, or surrender lodging for the sake of a mendicant, but such actions can make the lodging unsuitable or infected by various sins. A householder may also unknowingly create occasions for attachment, approval, dislike, or sexual temptation.
