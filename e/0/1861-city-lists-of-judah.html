Chapter 15

The chapter lists Judah's cities by region: twenty-nine in the South, fourteen and then sixteen and nine in the lowland groups, plus Ekron, Ashdod and Gaza with their towns and villages. The hill country has eleven, nine, ten, six and two cities in successive groups, and the wilderness has six. Each group is given with its villages.
