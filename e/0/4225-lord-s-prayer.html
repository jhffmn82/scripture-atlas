Abstract written by a model

The Lord's Prayer is a model prayer given as a pattern, addressed to Our Father in heaven. It asks for his name to be holy, his Kingdom to come, daily bread, forgiveness as we forgive, and deliverance from temptation and the evil one, ending with praise.

Lord's Prayer is in one work on this site, The Gospel According to Matthew. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 6

The prayer is introduced with 'Pray like this' and addresses 'Our Father in heaven.' It asks that his name be kept holy, his Kingdom come and will be done on earth as in heaven, for daily bread, forgiveness of debts as we forgive debtors, and deliverance from temptation and the evil one, ending with 'For yours is the Kingdom, the power, and the glory forever. Amen.'
