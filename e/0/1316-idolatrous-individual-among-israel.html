idolatrous individual (among Israel)

Abstract written by a model

An idolatrous individual, whether man, woman, family, or tribe, whose heart turns from the LORD to serve other gods, likened to a root producing bitter poison. Hearing the covenant curse, he blesses himself and assumes peace despite stubbornness; the LORD refuses pardon, his anger burns, and the name is blotted out.

Chapter 29

Any man, woman, family, or tribe whose heart turns to serve the gods of the nations is described as a root producing bitter poison. If he hears the curse and assures himself of peace despite his stubborn heart, the LORD will not pardon him, his anger and jealousy will smoke against him, and his name will be blotted out from under the sky.
