fighting

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Fight, Warfare, fought, warfare

Abstract written by a model

Fighting is ordained for believers but restricted by nonaggression: they must not initiate hostilities or fight in an inviolable place unless attacked. It is permitted against those who fight, while warfare in the sacred month is a grave transgression. Fighting in Allah’s way is commanded, illustrated by Saul’s steadfast small company defeating a mighty host.

Warfare is permitted against those who fight, but hostilities must not be initiated and aggression is forbidden. Warfare in the sacred month is described as a great transgression, though persecution and expulsion are described as greater offenses.

238 Be guardians of your prayers, and of the midmost prayer, and stand up with d

Fighting in Allah’s way is commanded, and the account of Saul, the river, Goliath, and David shows a small steadfast company defeating a mighty host by Allah’s leave.
