Chapter 3

Heliodorus, the king's chancellor, was sent to remove the reported money from the Jerusalem treasury and, despite Onias's explanation, declared it must be confiscated. He was struck down by a horse and two scourging young men, left speechless and helpless, then spared after Onias's sacrifice. He offered a sacrifice and vowed vows to God, returned to the king, testified to God's works, and told the king that anyone sent against that place would be scourged because of God's power there.
