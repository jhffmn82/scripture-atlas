Abstract written by a model

A household master in a parable who owns a vineyard and calls himself good. He hires laborers from early morning through the eleventh hour, then has his manager pay all a denarius, last to first, answering the complaining first-hired that he did no wrong.

Chapter 20

The master goes out early in the morning and again at the third, sixth, ninth, and eleventh hours to hire laborers for his vineyard. At evening he has his manager pay everyone a denarius, last to first, and answers the complaining first laborers that he has done no wrong, may do what he wants with what he owns, and is good.
