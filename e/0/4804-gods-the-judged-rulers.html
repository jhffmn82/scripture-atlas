Chapter 82

The gods are accused of judging unjustly and showing partiality to the wicked, and are commanded to defend and rescue the weak, poor, fatherless, and needy. They are described as not knowing or understanding and walking in darkness, while the foundations of the earth are shaken. God calls them sons of the Most High, yet declares they will die like men and fall like one of the rulers.
