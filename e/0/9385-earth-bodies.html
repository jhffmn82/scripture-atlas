Abstract written by a model

Earth-bodies are a class of living beings, including earth, gold, gravel, sand, stones, metals, minerals, and precious stones. Many living beings consume them; their associated beings have varied qualities. Resolving to meet wants through them is described as uncontrolled and sinful.

Third Lecture, Called Knowledge of Food.

Earth-bodies are consumed by many living beings. They include earth, gravel, sand, stones, metals, minerals, and precious stones, and their associated beings have bodies of manifold colours, smells, tastes, touches, forms, and arrangements.

Fourth Lecture, Called Renunciation of Activity.

Earth-bodies are one of the six classes of living beings. A sentient being may resolve to meet wants through earth-bodies generally, and such conduct is treated as uncontrolled and sinful.
