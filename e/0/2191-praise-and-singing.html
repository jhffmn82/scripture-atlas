Abstract written by a model

The Levites of the Kohathites and Korahites praise the LORD with an exceedingly loud voice, and Jehoshaphat appoints singers in holy array to go before the army saying, "Give thanks to the LORD, for his loving kindness endures forever." When they began to sing and praise, the LORD set ambushers against the enemy. Judah later returns to Jerusalem with stringed instruments, harps, and trumpets.
