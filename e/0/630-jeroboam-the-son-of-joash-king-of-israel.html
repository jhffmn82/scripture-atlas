Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Jeroboam, son of Joash, is king of Israel who reigns forty-one years, does evil in the LORD's sight, saves Israel by the LORD's will, restores its border, recovers Damascus, and is succeeded by his son Zechariah. In The Book of Amos, Jeroboam, son of Joash, is king of Israel against whose house the LORD and Amos prophesy judgment, including death by the sword.

Jeroboam the son of Joash king of Israel in The Second Book of Kings

author unknown · 560 to 539 BC · the work’s page · read it where Jeroboam the son of Joash king of Israel first appears

Abstract written by a model

Jeroboam, son of Joash, was a king of Israel who reigned forty-one years in Samaria, beginning in Amaziah's fifteenth year. He did evil in the LORD's sight, following the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat. Yet, by the LORD's will and the prophet Jonah's word, he saved Israel, restored its border from Hamath to the Arabah sea, and recovered Damascus. His son Zechariah succeeded him.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 14

Jeroboam began to reign in Samaria in the fifteenth year of Amaziah and reigned forty-one years, doing evil and not departing from the sins of Jeroboam son of Nebat. He restored Israel's border from Hamath to the sea of the Arabah according to the word spoken through the prophet Jonah son of Amittai, and recovered Damascus and Hamath. He was succeeded by his son Zechariah.
