women who prophesy (daughters of your people)

group · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: the daughters of your people, the women

Abstract written by a model

A group of women who prophesy from their own hearts, not from the LORD. They sew magic bands and make veils to hunt souls, profane the LORD for barley and bread, and use lies to kill and spare wrongly. The LORD vows to tear off their bands and veils, free the ensnared souls, and end their false visions.

Chapter 13

These women prophesy out of their own heart and sew magic bands on elbows and make veils for heads to hunt souls. They profaned the LORD among his people for handfuls of barley and pieces of bread, killing souls who should not die and saving alive those who should not live through lies. The LORD says he will tear off their bands and veils, release the souls they ensnared like birds, and end their false visions and divination.
