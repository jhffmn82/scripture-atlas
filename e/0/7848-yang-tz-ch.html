Yang Tzŭ Chü

Also called: Yang Tzü Chü

Abstract written by a model

Yang Tzŭ Chü visits Lao Tzŭ, travels south to P'ei, and is rebuked for an overbearing look. After instruction, he changes his spirit, countenance, and behavior. He also asks about wise rule and government; on returning, people at an inn treat him differently.

CHAPTER VII.

He asks Lao Tzŭ whether an ardent, courageous, knowledgeable, and tireless pursuer of TAO would be a wise ruler, and asks what wise government consists in.

CHAPTER XXVII.

Yang Tzŭ Chü went to meet Lao Tzŭ and was rebuked for having an overbearing look. After receiving Lao Tzŭ’s instruction, he changed his countenance and behavior, and the people at the inn treated him differently when he returned.
