Chapter 40

A series of pairings sets a good thing beside something better: a treasure, a blameless wife, the love of wisdom, a pleasant tongue, green shoots of grain, a wife with her husband, almsgiving, counsel, and the fear of the Lord. Each is said to be better than the two good things named before it.
