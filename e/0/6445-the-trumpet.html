the trumpet

event · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: one blast, the trumpet blast, the trumpet sounding, when the trumpet shall sound

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 69: The Reality (Al-Haaqqa), the trumpet blast sounds one blast, after which the earth and mountains are lifted and crushed and the Event befalls. In Surah 74: The Cloaked One (Al-Muddaththir), the trumpet sounding makes that day one of anguish and not of ease for disbelievers.
