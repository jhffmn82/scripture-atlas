Abstract written by a model

His Augustness Mi-ma-tsu-hiko-wake-shine, a Heavenly Sovereign, was begotten by Emperor I-Toku and Princess Futo-ma-waka, later ruled the Empire, dwelt at Waki-no-kami, reigned ninety-three years, and has an August mausoleum.

Sect. LVII.—Emperor I-Toku.

His Augustness Mi-ma-tsu-hiko-wake-shine was begotten by Emperor I-Toku and Princess Futo-ma-waka. He was later the one who ruled the Empire.

Sect. LVIII.—Emperor Ko-Sho.

Emperor Ko-Sho, identified as His Augustness Mi-ma-tsu-hiko-kawe-shine, dwelt at the palace of Waki-no-kami in Kadzuraki and ruled the Empire. His august years were ninety-three, and his August mausoleum was on Mount Hakata at Waki-no-kami.
