Paul's apostolic authority is given by the Lord to build up the Corinthians, not tear them down. He will boast of it without shame, denies intending to terrify by letters, affirms it is the same in presence and writing, and notes God-appointed limits reaching Corinth.

Chapter 10

Paul's authority was given by the Lord for building the Corinthians up and not for casting them down, and he says he will not be ashamed of boasting somewhat abundantly about it. He denies wanting to terrify them by his letters, and says his authority is the same in presence as in writing. Its boundaries are appointed by God and reach to Corinth.
