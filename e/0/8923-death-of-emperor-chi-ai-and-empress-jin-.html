Sect. XCV.—Emperor Chiū-Ai (Part I.—Genealogies). .. Sect. CIII.—Emperor Chiū-Ai (Part IX.—His Death and That of the Empress Jin-Gō).

Emperor Chiū-Ai dies after the Deities become angry when he rejects their message, and his august years are given as fifty-two. Empress Jin-Gō later dies at the august age of one hundred; Emperor Chiū-Ai is buried at Nagaye near Wega in Kafuchi, and she is buried at Tatanami in Saki.
