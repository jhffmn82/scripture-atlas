fishes

Also called: Fishes, all the fishes, all the things broad of fin and the things narrow of fin

Abstract written by a model

In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, fishes should not be taken from the deep. In Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods), fishes are gathered and asked to serve Deity Prince of Saruta and declare they will respectfully serve him, except for the bêche-de-mer.
