CHAPTER XXXII.

Poh Hun Wu Jen warns Lieh Yü K'ou that outward influence attracts people and causes reciprocal influence, disturbing natural constitution. He later departs silently when he finds Lieh Yü K'ou's courtyard filled with visitors, judging that instruction is no longer useful.
