Abstract written by a model

A locust plague in the Book of Joel, Chapter 1, described metaphorically as a strong, innumerable nation with lion's teeth. Successive waves of locust, grasshopper and caterpillar each eat what the previous left, laying waste the vine and fig tree, stripping bark and leaving branches white.

The Book of Joel / Chapter 1

Successive swarms (the swarming locust, great locust, grasshopper and caterpillar) eat whatever the previous one left. The invader is described as a strong nation without number with the teeth of a lion and the fangs of a lioness. It lays waste the vine and fig tree, stripping the bark and leaving branches white.
