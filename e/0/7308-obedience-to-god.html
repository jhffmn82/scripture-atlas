obedience to God

topic · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: By obeying Him, obedience to the commandments of God, obedient to the commandments, obeyeth God, obeying Him

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, obedience to the commandments of God is required for salvation at the last day, together with enduring to the end. In Japji, obedience to God is an activity that produces wisdom and understanding, leads to salvation, and brings honour, ecstasy, virtue, freedom from punishment and transmigration, and joy in His pure Name.

obedience to God in Japji

Max Arthur Macauliffe · the work’s page · read it where obedience to God first appears

Abstract written by a model

Obedience to God is an activity that produces wisdom and understanding and leads to salvation. It brings honour, ecstasy, virtue, freedom from punishment and transmigration, and the heart’s pleasure in His pure Name, unfolding through obedience into divine knowledge, fashioned intellect, and incomparable joy.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Japji .. 36 In the realm of knowledge the light of divine knowledge is resplendent. There are heard songs from which millions of joys and pleasures proceed. Beauty is the attribute of the realm of happiness. There things are fashioned in an incomparable manner. What is done there cannot be described. Whoever endeavoureth to describe it shall afterwards repent. There are fashioned knowledge, wisdom, intellect, and understanding; And there too is fashioned the skill of demigods and men of supernatural power.

Obeying God brings wisdom, understanding, honour, ecstasy, virtue, salvation, and freedom from punishment and transmigration. Whoever obeys God knows the pleasure of His pure Name in the heart.
