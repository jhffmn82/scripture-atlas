Sect. LXIII.—Emperor Sū-Jin (Part I.—Genealogies). .. Sect. LXVIII.—Emperor Sū-Jin (Part VI.—His Age and Place of Burial).

During his reign a great pestilence arose and was ended through religious rites. Military campaigns restored peace, tribute on men's arrow-notches and women's finger-tips was first levied, and the Pool of Yosami and the Pool of Sakawori at Karu were made.
