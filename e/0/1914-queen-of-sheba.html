Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Kings, the queen of Sheba travels to Jerusalem to test Solomon with hard questions, praises the LORD, gives Solomon gold, spices and precious stones, receives all she desired, and returns home. In The Second Book of Chronicles, the queen of Sheba travels to Jerusalem to test Solomon with hard questions, affirms the report was true, blesses the LORD, gives him gold, spices and precious stones, and returns to her land. In Surah 27: The Ant (An-Naml), the Queen of Sheba rules her people, possesses a mighty throne, consults her chieftains, sends a present to Solomon, recognizes his letter, and says that she surrenders with Solomon unto Allah.

Chapter 10

The queen of Sheba came to Jerusalem with a very great caravan to test Solomon with hard questions, and was left with no more spirit in her after seeing all he had. She declared that not even half had been told her, praised the LORD for setting Solomon on the throne, and gave him 120 talents of gold, abundant spices, and precious stones. She received all she desired and returned to her own land with her servants.
