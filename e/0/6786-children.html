children

group · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: a child, your children

Abstract written by a model

Children are addressed in Allah’s inheritance provisions. A male child receives the equivalent of two female portions. If there is one female child, her share is half; if there are more than two female children, their share is two-thirds. The provisions also set shares for parents, depending on whether the deceased has children or brethren, after legacies and debts. The passage says people do not know which parents or children are nearer in usefulness, calls the rule an injunction from Allah, and warns that disobeying Allah and His messenger brings Fire and a shameful doom.
