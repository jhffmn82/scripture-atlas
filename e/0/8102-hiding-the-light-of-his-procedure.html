Hiding the light (of his procedure)

Abstract written by a model

“Hiding the light of his procedure” is the name given to the sage’s skilful saving of every man and thing without casting anything away. Hiding the light (of his procedure) is the practice of first making an expiration before taking an inspiration, strengthening before weakening, raising up before overthrowing, and making gifts before despoiling.

Hiding the light (of his procedure) is in one work on this site, The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
