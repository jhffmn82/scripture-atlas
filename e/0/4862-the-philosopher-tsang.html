Abstract written by a model

In Chuang Tzŭ, Tsêng Shên holds office twice and demonstrates filial piety by valuing his parents' welfare and nourishing his inner man. In The Analects of Confucius, Book VIII (T'ai-po), the philosopher Tsang teaches caution, sincerity, proper conduct, and endurance. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XII (Yen Yuan), the philosopher Tsang says that cultured friendship helps the superior man's virtue. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XIX: Tsze-Chang, the philosopher Tsang teaches about virtue, mourning, filial piety, and pity for the guilty.

Abstract written by a model

Tsêng Shên, a famous disciple of Confucius and pattern of filial piety, holds office twice. He is happy with a small salary while his parents live, then sad with a large one after their death, nourishing the inner man and becoming oblivious of his body.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

CHAPTER XXVII.

Tsêng Tzŭ held office twice and experienced different emotions according to his salary. He was happy with a small salary while his parents lived, but sad with a large salary after their death; Confucius said that his salaries were to him like a heron or mosquito flying past.

The philosopher Tsang says that Tsze-chang has an imposing manner and is difficult to join in practicing virtue.
