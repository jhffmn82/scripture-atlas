Chapter 21

A beautiful woman taken captive in battle may be brought into her captor's house as his wife. She must shave her head, trim her nails, take off her captive's clothing, and mourn her father and mother a full month. If he has no delight in her he must let her go where she desires, not sell her or treat her as a slave, because he has humbled her.
