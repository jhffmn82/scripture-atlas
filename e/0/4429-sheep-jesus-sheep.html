sheep (Jesus' sheep)

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to John, Jesus' sheep are his followers who recognize his voice, are called by name, and follow him while fleeing strangers; they receive eternal life and cannot be snatched from his or the Father's hand, and other sheep will join them as one flock. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the good shepherd's sheep hearken to his voice and follow him; he calls them into his fold and commands that no ravenous wolf be allowed to enter among them. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the other sheep are Jesus’s sheep outside the twelve disciples’ fold; Jesus must bring them, they will hear his voice, and are identified as the twelve disciples. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, other sheep are outside Jerusalem and surrounding lands, have not yet heard or seen Jesus, but he will go to them so they hear his voice and are numbered among his sheep.

Abstract written by a model

Jesus' sheep are his followers who recognize his voice, are called by name, and follow him while fleeing strangers. They receive eternal life and cannot be snatched from his or the Father's hand. Other sheep will join them as one flock; unbelieving Jews are not his sheep.

Chapter 10

The sheep listen to the shepherd's voice, are called by name, and follow him but flee strangers. Jesus says his sheep hear his voice and follow him, are given eternal life, will never perish, and cannot be snatched from his or the Father's hand. He has other sheep not of this fold who will become one flock with one shepherd, and he tells the Jews they do not believe because they are not of his sheep.
