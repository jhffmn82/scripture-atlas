Abstract written by a model

Surah 21 concerns the approaching reckoning of mankind, the oneness of Allah, and the rejection of idolatry. It recounts how Allah supported messengers and prophets including Abraham, Lot, Moses, Aaron, Noah, David, Solomon, Job, Dhu'n-Nun, and Zachariah, while destroying wrong-doing peoples. It describes revelation, resurrection, the balance of justice, punishment in hell, and mercy for the righteous and believers. Muhammad is sent as a mercy for the peoples and warns that Allah is One.
