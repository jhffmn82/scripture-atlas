Rules for Yatis.

The Paggusan conduct rules require careful inspection of places and substances, permission before leaving or undertaking activities, proper beds and benches, three pots, specified lodging places, notice of intended direction, and limits on travel. They prohibit harsh words, require forgiveness and reconciliation after disputes, and regulate conduct in rain and around householders.
