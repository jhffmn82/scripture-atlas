Chapter 19

The water is made by putting ashes of the burnt sin offering into a vessel and pouring running water on them. A clean person sprinkles it with hyssop on the third and seventh days, and anyone who touches it is unclean until evening, as is the one who sprinkles it, who must wash his clothes.
