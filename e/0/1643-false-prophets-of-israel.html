false prophets of Israel

group · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: foolish prophets, prophets who see false visions, the prophets of Israel, the prophets of Israel who prophesy concerning Jerusalem, they, those who prophesy, your prophets

Abstract written by a model

The false prophets of Israel are a group who prophesy from their own heart and spirit, having seen nothing, yet claim 'The LORD says' though he has not sent them. Compared to foxes in waste places, they failed to go up into the gaps and build up the wall, instead seducing the people with 'Peace' where there is none and plastering a whitewashed wall. The LORD declares himself against them: they will be consumed when the wall falls and lose their place in the council, register, and land of Israel.

Chapter 13

The prophets of Israel follow their own spirit, have seen nothing, and claim 'The LORD says' though the LORD has not sent them. They did not go up into the gaps or build up the wall, and they seduced the people by saying 'Peace' and plastering the wall with whitewash. The LORD declares he is against them, that they will be consumed when the wall falls, and that they will lose their place in the council, the register, and the land of Israel.
