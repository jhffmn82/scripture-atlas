Sutrakritanga, Book II / First Lecture, Called the Lotus.

The second man sees the first man stuck in the mud, declares him unknowing and unskilled, and enters the lotus-pool himself. He also becomes stuck in the mud and represents the doctrine that everything consists of the five elements.
