Abstract written by a model

In Numbers, the ground splits and the earth swallows Korah's men, Dathan, Abiram, their households, and all their goods, who go down alive into Sheol before the earth closes over them. In Surah 28: The Stories (Al-Qasas), Allah causes the earth to swallow Korah and his dwelling-place.
