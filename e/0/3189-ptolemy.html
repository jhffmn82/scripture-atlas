Ptolemy

Also called: King Ptolemy, Ptolemy king of Egypt, Ptolemy the king, the king, the king of Egypt

Abstract written by a model

Ptolemy is the king of Egypt and father of Cleopatra. He first gave her in marriage to Alexander at Ptolemais, becoming his father-in-law. He then entered Syria peacefully, garrisoned cities up to Seleucia, turned on Alexander, gave Cleopatra to Demetrius, and crowned himself over Asia and Egypt. He defeated Alexander, received his head, and died three days later.

Ptolemy is in one work on this site, The First Book of the Maccabees. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 10

Ptolemy, king of Egypt, agreed to Alexander's request for his daughter in marriage. He came with Cleopatra to Ptolemais in the 162nd year, where Alexander wed her.

Chapter 11

King Ptolemy of Egypt gathered a great force, entered Syria with words of peace, and garrisoned the cities. He turned against Alexander, gave his daughter to Demetrius, and put on the crown of Asia at Antioch along with that of Egypt. He defeated Alexander, received his head, and died three days later.
