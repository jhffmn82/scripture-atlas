Abstract written by a model

Impurity and unacceptability apply to partly edible sugar-cane and pods, meat or fish with many bones, and fossil salt or sea salt offered in the specified circumstances. Such items should generally not be accepted, though the text prescribes special procedures for items accepted inadvertently.
