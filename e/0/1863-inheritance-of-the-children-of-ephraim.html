inheritance of the children of Ephraim

place · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: This is the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Ephraim, inheritance, territory of Ephraim, their inheritance

Abstract written by a model

The territory allotted to the children of Ephraim by families. Its eastern border runs from Ataroth Addar to upper Beth Horon, then westward at Michmethath, around Taanath Shiloh, past Janoah, Ataroth, Naarah and Jericho to the Jordan. Another line runs from Tappuah along the brook of Kanah to the sea. It also includes set-apart cities within Manasseh's inheritance.

Chapter 16

Its eastern border ran from Ataroth Addar to upper Beth Horon, then out westward at Michmethath, around to Taanath Shiloh, past Janoah, to Ataroth, Naarah, Jericho and the Jordan, with another line from Tappuah westward along the brook of Kanah to the sea.
