Abstract written by a model

In Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara), disbelievers remain in disbelief, are sealed from guidance, and face curses and painful doom in the Fire. In Surah 3: The Family of Imraan (Aal-i-Imraan), the disbelievers reject Allah’s revelations, oppose Him, and face punishment and Hell. In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), disbelievers reject Allah, His messengers, and revelations, oppose believers, hinder Allah’s way, and face hell and shameful doom. In Surah 5: The Table (Al-Maaida), disbelievers deny Allah’s revelations and face hell, painful doom, and punishment from which they cannot ransom themselves. In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), disbelievers deny and scorn Allah’s revelations, follow devils, and face closed gates of heaven, the Fire, and lasting torment. In Surah 8: The Spoils of War (Al-Anfaal), disbelievers oppose Allah and His messenger, hinder His way, break treaties, and are defeated and gathered into hell. In Surah 13: The Thunder (Ar-Ra'd), those who disbelieve reject Muhammad’s messengership, dispute concerning Allah, deny revelation, and face torment and the Fire. In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), the disbelievers deny revelation, mock Muhammad, question the promised punishment, and face unexpected fire.

and 8 more works

In Surah 30: The Romans (Ar-Room), disbelievers deny Allah’s revelations and the meeting of the Hereafter and are brought to doom. In Surah 33: The Clans (Al-Ahzaab), disbelievers and their allies advance against believers but are repulsed, and those who disbelieve face an everlasting flaming fire. In Surah 41: Explained in detail (Fussilat), the disbelievers reject the message, prevent others from hearing the Qur’an, and face awful doom for their deeds. In Surah 48: The Victory (Al-Fath), disbelievers are warned of painful punishment, debar access to the Inviolable Place of Worship, and are angered by believers’ strength. In Surah 67: The Sovereignty (Al-Mulk), the disbelievers deny the warnings and Allah’s revelations and face a painful doom. In Surah 76: Man (Al-Insaan), disbelievers have manacles, carcans, and a raging fire prepared for them. In Surah 83: Defrauding (Al-Mutaffifin), disbelievers are laughed at by those who believed on the Day of Judgment. In Surah 83: Defrauding (Al-Mutaffifin), the repudiators deny the Day of Judgment, have their record placed in Sijjin, and burn in hell.

disbelievers in Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara)

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where disbelievers first appears

Abstract written by a model

Disbelievers are in disbelief, do not believe whether warned or not, and are portrayed as deaf, dumb, blind, and lost from the plain road. Their hearing and hearts are sealed, with a covering over their eyes. They are wrong-doers whose patrons are false deities, bringing them from light into darkness. Those who die in disbelief are cursed by Allah, angels, and mankind, and they face a painful, shameful, grievous doom in the Fire, where they abide.

Surah 2: The Cow (Al-Baqara)

Disbelievers do not believe whether warned or not, and Allah describes their hearing and hearts as sealed with a covering over their eyes. They face the Fire and an awful, shameful, or grievous doom.

104 O ye who believe, say not (unto the Prophet): "Listen to us" but say "Look u

Disbelievers face a painful doom, and those who disbelieve and die in disbelief are under the curse of Allah, angels, and mankind. Disbelievers are likened to those who are deaf, dumb, and blind to guidance.

238 Be guardians of your prayers, and of the midmost prayer, and stand up with d

Disbelievers are described as wrong-doers whose patrons are false deities and who will abide in the Fire. They are also associated with rejecting Allah, and believers ask Allah for victory over them.
