Judith / Chapter 1

Ecbatana is the Median city of Arphaxad, fortified with hewn-stone walls seventy cubits high and fifty broad, towers a hundred cubits high, and gates seventy cubits high so the army could march out. Nebuchadnezzar takes its towers, plunders its streets, and turns its beauty into shame.
