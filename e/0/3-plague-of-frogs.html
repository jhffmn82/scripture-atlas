Chapter 8

The frogs come up from the rivers, streams, and pools at Aaron's gesture and fill houses, bedrooms, beds, ovens, and kneading troughs. The magicians also produce frogs, but at Moses' prayer the frogs die in houses, courts, and fields and are gathered into stinking heaps, after which Pharaoh hardens his heart.
