Abstract written by a model

In Vendidad (Vendîdâd), Yathâ ahû vairyô is a fiend-smiting, healing prayer recited during cleansing. In Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV, Yathâ ahû vairyô is a prayer recited and worshipped as a statement affirming the law of holiness.

Yathâ ahû vairyô in Yashts (Yasts) XIII-XXIV

James Darmesteter · the work’s page · read it where Yathâ ahû vairyô first appears

Abstract written by a model

Yathâ ahû vairyô is a prayer, recited and worshipped as a statement affirming the law of holiness. It unfolds by identifying the law of holiness with the will of the Lord, then explaining that the will of the Lord is that law.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

XXVI.

Yathâ ahû vairyô is recited with the explanation that the will of the Lord is the law of holiness.

XVIII. Âstâd Yast.

The law of holiness is identified with the will of the Lord in the Yathâ ahû vairyô. It is recited and worshipped in the unit.
