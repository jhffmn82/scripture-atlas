Abstract written by a model

The demons in the two men cry out to Jesus as Son of God and ask whether he has come to torment them before the time. They beg to be sent into the herd of pigs, and when Jesus says "Go!" they come out and enter the pigs. Demons are cast out by Jesus, whom the Pharisees say does so by Beelzebul, prince of the demons. Jesus says he does it by the Spirit of God, showing that God's Kingdom has come. He also describes an unclean spirit that leaves a man, returns, and brings seven other spirits more evil than itself.
