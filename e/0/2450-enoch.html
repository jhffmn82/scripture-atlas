Enoch

Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, Enoch fathered Methuselah at 65, walked with God for 300 years, had more children, and was taken by God at 365 without dying. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, Enoch pleased the Lord, was taken up from earth unlike any created man, and stood as an example of repentance to all generations.
