Chapter 3

The LORD promises that an adversary will overrun the land, pull down strongholds, and plunder fortresses, with only a scrap-like remnant rescued. On the day He visits Israel's transgressions, the horns of Bethel's altars will be cut off and fall, and the winter house, summer house, houses of ivory, and great houses will be struck down and come to an end.
