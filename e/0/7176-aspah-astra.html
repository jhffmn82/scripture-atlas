Aspahê-astra

Abstract written by a model

Aspahê-astra is an implement of punishment used for prescribed stripes: penalties run from five for a first Âgerepta to a thousand for broken contracts, including four hundred stripes as atonement for a man who touched a corpse.

Aspahê-astra is in one work on this site, Vendidad (Vendîdâd), Fargards I-VIII. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Fargard IV.

The Aspahê-astra is used for the prescribed stripes. Penalties range from five stripes for a first Âgerepta to two hundred stripes for a Peshôtanu, and from three hundred to a thousand stripes for broken contracts.

VIII.

The Aspahê-astra is used in the prescribed atonement of four hundred stripes when the man who touched a corpse finds water or trees on his way.
