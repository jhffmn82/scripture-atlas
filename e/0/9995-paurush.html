Abstract written by a model

Paurushî divides each day and night into four seasonally varying parts, assigning duties including study, meditation, begging-tour, and sleep cessation.

Twenty-Sixth Lecture.

The day and night are each divided into four Paurushî. Their length varies by season, and each division is assigned duties such as study, meditation, begging-tour, or leaving off sleep.
