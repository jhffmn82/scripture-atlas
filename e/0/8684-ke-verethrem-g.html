Abstract written by a model

Ke verethrem gâ is a fiend-smiting, healing cleansing prayer recited after Gâthas; through named formulas and object-specific words, it cleanses a house and drives away fiends, defilement, uncleanness, and named daêvas.

Ke verethrem gâ is in one work on this site, Vendidad (Vendîdâd). This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Fargard X.

The fiend-smiting and most-healing words are recited after the specified Gâthas. They drive away Angra Mainyu, Nasu, defilement, Indra, Sauru, Naunghaithya, Tauru, Zairi, Aêshma, Akatasha, the Varenya daêvas, and the wind-daêva.

Fargard XI.

The cleansing words are described as fiend-smiting and most-healing. They include the Ahuna-Vairya, Mazdâ ad môi, Airyama-ishyô, words for particular objects, and formulas driving away uncleanness.
