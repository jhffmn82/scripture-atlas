Also called: a householder

Abstract written by a model

Householders are said to perform the same acts as people who use cold water, eat seeds, and have intercourse with women; these acts do not distinguish Sramanas.

Sixth Lecture, Called .

Householders perform the same acts as those who use cold water, eat seeds, and have intercourse with women. Such acts therefore do not distinguish those people as Sramanas.
