Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, it served as the sin offering for Aaron's consecration, offered daily during the seven days for atonement. In Leviticus, it served as the sin offering whose blood purified the altar for atonement and whose remains were burned as prescribed.

Abstract written by a model

is_a sin offering A young bull is brought to the Tent of Meeting, Aaron and his sons lay hands on its head, and it is killed before the LORD. Its blood is put on the altar's horns and poured at the base, its fat and organs are burned on the altar, and its meat, skin, and dung are burned outside the camp as a sin offering. A bull of sin offering is offered every day of the seven days for atonement.
