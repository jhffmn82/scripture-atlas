resurrection

topic · 2 works · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: resurrection which is in Christ, the resurrection, the resurrection of the dead

Abstract written by a model

In The Gospel According to Matthew, the Sadducees deny the resurrection and challenge it with a case of seven brothers; Jesus says that in the resurrection people neither marry nor are given in marriage but are like God's angels, and that God is the God of the living. In The Book of Jacob, the Brother of Nephi, resurrection is associated with power in Christ, through which people may gain knowledge of it and the world to come; it brings those who reject warnings and the gospel before the bar of God with shame and awful guilt.
