Chapter 14

The battle begins with Jonathan and his armor bearer killing about twenty men, followed by trembling, an earthquake and confusion in the Philistine camp. Saul and his people join in, and Hebrews with the Philistines and hidden Israelites turn to help. The Philistines are struck from Michmash to Aijalon and flee, and the LORD saves Israel that day.
