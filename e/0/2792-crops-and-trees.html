Crops and trees fail together: vine, fig, pomegranate, palm, apple and field trees wither, wheat and barley harvest perish, seeds rot, granaries and barns fall into ruin, and human joy withers with them.

The Book of Joel / Chapter 1

The vine, fig tree, pomegranate, palm, apple and all the trees of the field wither, and the wheat, barley and harvest perish. Seeds rot under clods, granaries are desolate and barns broken down. Joy withers away from the sons of men with them.
