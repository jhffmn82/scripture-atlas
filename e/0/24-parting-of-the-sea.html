A miraculous event in which, at the LORD's command, Moses stretches his hand over the sea and a strong east wind all night divides it. Israel crosses on dry ground between walls of water; the Egyptians follow, and the returning waters drown them.

Chapter 14

At the LORD's command Moses stretches his hand over the sea, and the LORD causes it to go back by a strong east wind all night, making dry land with the waters divided. Israel crosses in the middle of the sea with a wall of water on the right and left. The Egyptians follow, and when Moses stretches out his hand again the waters return and drown them.
