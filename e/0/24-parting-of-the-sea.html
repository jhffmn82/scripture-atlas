parting of the sea

event · 3 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: brought the Children of Israel across the sea, did deliver you from Pharaoh's folk, it parted, the crossing of the sea, the great work which the LORD did to the Egyptians, the parting of the sea, the salvation of the LORD, the waters were a wall to them on their right hand and on their left, the waters were divided

Abstract written by a model

In Exodus, it is the miraculous event in which Moses divides the sea, Israel crosses on dry ground between walls of water, and the returning waters drown the Egyptians. In Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), it brings the Children of Israel across the sea after Allah delivers them from Pharaoh's people. In Surah 26: The Poets (Ash-Shu'araa), it saves Moses and those with him while Pharaoh's forces approach and are drowned.

parting of the sea in Exodus

author unknown · about 1439 to 1399 BC · the work’s page · read it where parting of the sea first appears

Abstract written by a model

A miraculous event in which, at the LORD's command, Moses stretches his hand over the sea and a strong east wind all night divides it. Israel crosses on dry ground between walls of water; the Egyptians follow, and the returning waters drown them.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 14

At the LORD's command Moses stretches his hand over the sea, and the LORD causes it to go back by a strong east wind all night, making dry land with the waters divided. Israel crosses in the middle of the sea with a wall of water on the right and left. The Egyptians follow, and when Moses stretches out his hand again the waters return and drown them.
