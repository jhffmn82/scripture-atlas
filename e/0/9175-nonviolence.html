nonviolence

Also called: Ceasing to use the stick (i.e. cruelty), abstaining from killing, abstinence from killing, killing no creatures, what is free from violence

Abstract written by a model

Nonviolence, the opposite of violence, is understood by examining violence and adopting its contrary. It requires not injuring living beings, including through others or support for killing, and unfolds in Mahâvîra’s careful avoidance of harm while acting and begging.

Third Lecture, Called Hot and Cold. / First Lesson. .. Fourth Lesson.

Nonviolence is known by examining violence and adopting its contrary. The doctrine of the Seer requires not injuring living beings and forbids killing or supporting killing.

Eighth Lecture, (Called) the Pillow of Righteousness. / First Lesson. .. Fourth Lesson.

Nonviolence required Mahâvîra neither to injure living beings himself nor to cause or approve injury through others. He ceased using a stick against beings, avoided creatures while begging, and carefully refrained from killing.
