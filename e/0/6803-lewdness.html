lewdness

topic · 1 work · 0 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: flagrant lewdness

Abstract written by a model

Lewdness is treated as an offense requiring four witnesses against women accused of it and punishment for the two guilty of it. Flagrant lewdness is an exception to the prohibition against constraining the women of deceased kinsmen. Lewdness is also identified with forbidden sexual conduct and results in punishment for a believing maid who commits it after marriage.
