Chapter 3

The assembly in Sardis has a reputation of being alive but is dead, and none of its works are found perfected before God. It is told to wake up, strengthen what remains, remember what it received and heard, keep it, and repent, or the speaker will come as a thief. A few names in Sardis did not defile their garments and will walk with him in white.
