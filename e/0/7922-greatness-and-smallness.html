greatness and smallness

topic · 1 work · 2 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Greatness and smallness, great, greatness, small, smallness

Abstract written by a model

Greatness and smallness are relative, without fixed extremes or limits. The wise person avoids treating either as excessive or deficient. Greatness is not secured by talk or great deeds, but by seeking and rejecting nothing, avoiding injury, and finding safety according to nature.

CHAPTER XVII.

Greatness and smallness are relative and cannot be defined as fixed extremes. The wise person does not regard the small as too little or the great as too much because dimensions have no limit.

Kuan Chung being at the point of death, Duke Huan went to see him.

Greatness is not secured by talk or by merely doing great things. It consists in seeking nothing, rejecting nothing, avoiding injury from without, and finding safety in accordance with one's nature.
