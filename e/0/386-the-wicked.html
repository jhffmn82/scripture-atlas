Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the wicked are killed by evil, and those who hate the righteous are condemned. In Proverbs, the wicked are proud, violent, devious people who desire evil, show neighbors no mercy, refuse to do right, and end in ruin. In The Book of Malachi, the proud and wicked people become stubble, are burned up, and end as ashes beneath the feet of those who fear the LORD’s name. In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, the wicked are separated from the righteous and the tree of life by God’s justice and cannot dwell in God’s kingdom.

Abstract written by a model

The wicked are proud, violent, devious people who desire evil and show neighbors no mercy. They refuse to do right, so their own violence drives them away. Their sacrifices are abominations, and they harden their faces. They end in ruin, serving as a ransom for the righteous.

Chapter 21

The wicked are described as proud, violent, devious, and desiring evil, showing no mercy to their neighbor. Their violence drives them away, their sacrifice is an abomination, and they are brought to ruin. The wicked serve as a ransom for the righteous, and a wicked man hardens his face.
