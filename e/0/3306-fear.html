is_a surrender of the help which reason offers Fear fills the lawless men through phantoms, terrifying sounds, vermin, and serpents, so that they perished trembling and refused even to look at the air. The text defines fear as a surrender of the help which reason offers, and says wickedness, condemned by conscience, is cowardly and always forecasts the worst. Even natural sounds such as wind, birds, water, rocks, and beasts paralyzed them with terror.
