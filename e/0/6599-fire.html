fire

object · 4 works · 6 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: The fire, burning flame, the fire, the fire which ye strike out

Abstract written by a model

In Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), fire is what those who wrongfully devour orphans’ wealth swallow into their bellies, leading them to burning flame. In Surah 21: The Prophets (Al-Anbiyaa), the fire is commanded to be coolness and peace for Abraham, while disbelievers cannot repel the fire of punishment. In The Believers (Al-Muminoon), the fire burns the faces of those in hell, who are glum therein. In Surah 56: The Inevitable (Al-Waaqia), fire is struck out by human beings, but the Lord of the Worlds causes its tree to grow and appoints it as a memorial and comfort for dwellers in the wilderness.
