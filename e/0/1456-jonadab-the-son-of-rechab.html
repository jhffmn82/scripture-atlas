Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Kings, Jehonadab the son of Rechab affirms that his heart is right with Jehu's, rides in Jehu's chariot to see his zeal for the LORD, and accompanies him into the house of Baal. In The Book of Jeremiah, Jonadab the son of Rechab is father of the Rechabites, commands them to live in tents and abstain from wine and agriculture, and is promised by the LORD that he will always have a man standing before him.
