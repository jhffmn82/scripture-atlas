Chapter 5

The writing appears on the plaster of the palace wall near the lamp stand, and no wise man can read it. Daniel reads it as MENE, MENE, TEKEL, UPHARSIN and interprets it as God numbering and ending the kingdom, weighing the king and finding him wanting, and dividing the kingdom to the Medes and Persians.
