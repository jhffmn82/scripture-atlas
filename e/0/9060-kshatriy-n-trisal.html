Kshatriyânî Trisalâ

person · 2 works · 11 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Kshatriyânî, Trisalâ, her, lotus-eyed queen, queen, she, the Kshatriyânî Trisalâ

Abstract written by a model

In Akaranga Sutra (Âkârâṅga Sûtra), Trisalâ receives Mahâvîra’s embryo and, after nine months and seven and a half days, gives birth to him under Uttaraphalgunî. In Kalpa Sutra, Kshatriyânî Trisalâ receives Mahâvîra’s embryo, sees fourteen auspicious dreams during pregnancy, accepts their interpretation, carries her unborn child, and gives birth to him as a perfectly healthy boy.

Abstract written by a model

Trisalâ, of the Vâsishtha gotra and wife of Siddhartha, received Mahâvîra’s embryo and, after nine months and seven and a half days, gave birth to him under Uttaraphalgunî.

Third Part. / Fifteenth Lecture, Called the Clauses.

Trisalâ belonged to the Vâsishtha gotra and was the wife of the Kshatriya Siddhartha of the Kâsyapa gotra. She received Mahâvîra’s embryo and, after nine complete months and seven and a half days, gave birth to him while the moon was in conjunction with Uttaraphalgunî.

Kshatriyânî Trisalâ in Kalpa Sutra

Bhadrabâhu · the work’s page · read it where Kshatriyânî Trisalâ first appears

Abstract written by a model

Kshatriyânî Trisalâ is a Kshatriyânî of the Vâsishtha gotra and wife of Siddhârtha. Her womb receives the embryo of the Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra after its transfer. That night, during pregnancy, she sees fourteen illustrious, auspicious great dreams and awakens joyfully. She asks Siddhârtha what they portend, accepts his and the dream interpreters’ interpretation, carefully carries her unborn child, and gives birth to Mahâvîra as a perfectly healthy boy.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Kalpa Sutra / Lives of the Ginas. / Life of Mahâvîra.

Trisalâ is a Kshatriyânî of the Vâsishtha gotra, wife of Siddhârtha. Her womb received Mahâvîra's embryo when Harinegamesi carried out Sakra's command.

In that night in which the embryo of the Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra was removed

The Kshatriyânî Trisalâ of the Vâsishtha gotra receives the embryo of the Venerable Ascetic Mahâvîra into her womb. She then sees fourteen illustrious, beautiful, lucky, blest, auspicious, fortunate, great dreams and awakens with the hair of her body bristling for joy.

When the Kshatriyânî Trisalâ, having seen these fourteen illustrious, great drea

She awakens after seeing fourteen illustrious great dreams and asks Kshatriya Siddhârtha what they portend. She accepts his interpretation and that of the interpreters of dreams, then returns to her apartments. She carries her unborn child carefully and gives birth to a perfectly healthy boy.
