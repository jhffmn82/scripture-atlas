Sabbath offering

Also called: On the Sabbath day, every Sabbath, the burnt offering of every Sabbath

Abstract written by a model

The Sabbath offering is a burnt offering made every Sabbath: two unblemished year-old male lambs, with two tenths of an ephah of oil-mixed fine flour as meal offering, plus a drink offering, presented in addition to the continual burnt offering.

Chapter 28

On the Sabbath day two male lambs a year old without defect are offered, with two tenths of an ephah of fine flour mixed with oil as a meal offering, and the drink offering. This is the burnt offering of every Sabbath, in addition to the continual burnt offering and its drink offering.
