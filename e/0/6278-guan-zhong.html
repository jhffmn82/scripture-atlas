Guan Zhong

Abstract written by a model

Guan Zhong is a minister who serves duke Huan, gains his prince’s full confidence, and directs government for a long time. He raises his prince to leadership over other princes and teaches the duke Huan, yet Mencius judges his accomplishments ultimately limited despite that influence.

Mencius, Book II (Gong Sun Chou) / Chapter 1

Guan Zhong is described as having raised his prince to leadership over the other princes. Mencius also presents him as a comparison that Zeng Xi rejected because Guan Zhong’s accomplishments were ultimately limited despite his influence and long direction of government.

Chapter 2

Guan Zhong is presented as a minister from whom the duke Huan first learned. Mencius says that if Guan Zhong could not be summoned by his prince, still less could someone who would not play Guan Zhong’s part be summoned.
