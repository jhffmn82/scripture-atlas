he who is skilful in managing life

Also called: he, he who is skilful in managing the life entrusted to him for a time, him

Abstract written by a model

A person skilled in managing life when entrusted with it. From coming forth to living, he travels land unafraid of rhinoceros or tiger and enters a host without avoiding weapons, because no place of death is in him.

50. 1. Men come forth and live; they enter (again) and die.

He who is skilful in managing life travels on the land without shunning rhinoceros or tiger and enters a host without avoiding buff coat or sharp weapon. He cannot be harmed because there is no place of death in him.
