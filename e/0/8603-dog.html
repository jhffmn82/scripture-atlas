dog

Also called: dogs, the dog

Abstract written by a model

The dog is a creature of the good spirit, valued as a protective watcher over goods, houses, and folds when of sound mind. Its right food is milk, fat, and meat; it ages quickly when not eating near eating people and receiving none of their food. It has characters likened to eight sorts of people: priest, warrior, husbandman, strolling singer, thief, wild beast, courtezan, and child. These characters describe its conduct, from guarding and fighting to singing, prowling, sleeping, and running away. Different kinds receive defined protections, duties, and penalties for injury or death.

Fargard XIII.

The dog is regarded as a protective creature that watches over people’s goods and helps guard houses and folds. It has qualities compared with eight kinds of people and should be fed milk, fat, and meat. Different kinds of dogs receive defined protections, duties, and penalties for injury or death.
