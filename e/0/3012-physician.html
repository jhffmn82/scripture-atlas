Abstract written by a model

The physician is a healer created by the Lord, honored for his skill, which wins admiration from great men and gifts from the king. Though long disease mocks him, the sick are urged to keep him near, since recovery sometimes lies in his hands, and he himself asks the Lord to prosper diagnosis and healing.

Chapter 38

The physician is to be honored for his need, since the Lord created him, and his skill lifts up his head and earns admiration from great men and a gift from the king. The sick are told to give him place and not let him leave, for there are times recovery is in his hands, and he too asks the Lord to prosper his diagnosis and healing.
