Bethlehem

place · 6 works · 6 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Bethlehem Judah, Bethlehem of Judea, David’s city, the city

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Judges, Bethlehem Judah is where the Levite takes his concubine from and where she returns to her father's house. In The Book of Ruth, Bethlehem Judah is the home of Elimelech's family and welcomes Naomi and Ruth's return. In The Second Book of Samuel, Bethlehem has a Philistine garrison and a well by the gate, and Elhanan the son of Dodo is listed among the thirty. In 1 Chronicles, Bethlehem has a Philistine garrison, and Elhanan the son of Dodo is listed as being from there. In The Gospel According to Matthew, Bethlehem of Judea is the prophesied birthplace of the Christ, where Jesus is born and Herod orders the male children killed. In The Gospel According to Luke, Bethlehem, David's city in Judea, is where Joseph enrolls, Jesus is born, and shepherds find the baby.
