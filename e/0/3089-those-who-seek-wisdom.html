Abstract written by a model

Those who seek wisdom early are filled with gladness, serve the Holy One, and are loved by the Lord. They inherit glory and security if they trust her through discipline and testing; those who stray are forsaken to their fall.

Chapter 4

Those who seek wisdom early are promised gladness, and those who hold her fast will inherit glory, judge the nations, and dwell securely. If they trust her, they and their generations will possess her, though she first disciplines and tests them. Those who go astray are forsaken and handed over to their fall.
