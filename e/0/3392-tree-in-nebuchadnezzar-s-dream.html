Abstract written by a model

A great tree in Nebuchadnezzar's dream, standing mid-earth with sky-high height, beautiful leaves, and fruit feeding all, sheltering animals and birds. A watcher orders it cut down, leaving a stump bound in iron and bronze; Daniel identifies it as Nebuchadnezzar, symbolizing his greatness and dominion.

Chapter 4

The tree stands in the middle of the earth, grows strong with its height reaching the sky and its sight to the end of the earth, with beautiful leaves and much fruit that feeds all. Animals shelter under it and birds live in its branches. A watcher orders it cut down, with the stump of its roots left in the earth bound with iron and bronze, and Daniel identifies it as Nebuchadnezzar.
