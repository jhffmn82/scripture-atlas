Abstract written by a model

CHAPTER XXVI.

Lao Lai Tzŭ tells Confucius to rid himself of dogmatism and specious knowledge. He teaches that employing goodness to gain influence through gratifying others brings everlasting shame, and that the true Sage acts without claiming personal effort for success.
