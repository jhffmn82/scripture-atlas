Shechem

Abstract written by a model

In Genesis, the city of Shechem is where Hamor and Shechem address its men before it is attacked, plundered, and its women and children are taken captive. In The Book of Joshua, Shechem is a hill-country city in Ephraim designated a city of refuge, where Joshua gathers the tribes for the covenant and Joseph's bones are buried. In The Book of Judges, Shechem is where Abimelech is made king, Gaal is driven out, and Abimelech later captures, destroys, and salts the city.

Chapter 24

Shechem is the place where Joshua gathers all the tribes and where the covenant, statute, and ordinance are made. The great stone is set up there under the oak by the LORD's sanctuary. Joseph's bones, brought up out of Egypt, are buried there in the parcel Jacob bought from the sons of Hamor for a hundred pieces of silver, and it becomes the inheritance of Joseph's children.
