Eliashib the high priest

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Eliashib, Eliashib the priest

Abstract written by a model

Eliashib is the high priest who first appears leading his fellow priests in building and sanctifying the sheep gate, with his house serving as a landmark for wall repairs by Baruch and Meremoth. Later, as the priest over the rooms of God's house and an ally of Tobiah, he prepares a great temple room, once used for offerings and vessels, for Tobiah. Nehemiah condemns this as evil.

Chapter 3

Eliashib the high priest rose up with his brothers the priests and built the sheep gate, sanctifying it and setting up its doors. His house is also named as a landmark, with Baruch repairing from the turning of the wall to its door and Meremoth repairing along the house from its door to its end.

Chapter 13

Eliashib, appointed over the rooms of God's house and allied to Tobiah, prepared a great room for Tobiah where the offerings and vessels had been stored. Nehemiah condemned this as evil. A son of Joiada, Eliashib's son, is also named as having married into Sanballat's family.
