the sun

object · 4 works · 16 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: The Sun (Ash-Shams), The sun, sun

Abstract written by a model

In Psalms, the sun is a heavenly body given a tent by God, likened to a bridegroom and a strong man rejoicing in its course, whose heat reaches everything. In The Wisdom of Jesus the Son of Sirach, or Ecclesiasticus, the sun is a marvelous instrument made by the Lord that gives light, looks at all things, rises bringing tidings, burns intensely at noon, dries the land, blinds eyes, and hastens its course at the Lord's word. In Surah 91: The Sun (Ash-Shams), The Sun is mentioned with its brightness. In The Doctrine of the Mean, the sun shines successively, illuminates wherever the sun and moon shine, and pursues its course without colliding with the moon.

Abstract written by a model

The sun is a marvelous instrument made by the Lord, which gives light and looks at all things. It rises bringing tidings, then at noon burns three times hotter than a furnace, drying the land and blinding eyes, its course hastened at the Lord's word.

The sun is described as a marvelous instrument, the work of the Most High, that brings tidings as it rises. At noon it dries up the land, burns three times hotter than a furnace, and blinds the eyes, and the Lord hastens its course at his word.
