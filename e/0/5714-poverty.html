Abstract written by a model

In The Tao Teh King, or The Tao and Its Characteristics, poverty among the people increases when prohibitive enactments multiply. In The Analects of Confucius, Book XV (Wei Ling Kung), poverty may come upon the superior man, but he is not anxious about it.
