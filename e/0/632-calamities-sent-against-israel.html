Abstract written by a model

A series of calamities the LORD declares he sent against Israel: famine, selective drought, blight, locusts, Egypt-like plagues, slaughter of young men, and a Sodom-and-Gomorrah overthrow. Escalating from hunger and withheld rain to death and destruction, it ends with survivors like a burning stick plucked from fire.

Chapter 4

The LORD lists cleanness of teeth and lack of bread, rain withheld three months before harvest and sent on one city but not another, blight and mildew, swarming locusts, plagues like Egypt's, young men slain by the sword, horses carried away, and an overthrow like Sodom and Gomorrah. Some were left like a burning stick plucked from the fire.
