Abstract written by a model

Conversion is a turning to the Lord, brought about by preaching the word and, in Abish’s case, her father’s remarkable vision. Abish has long been converted; missionaries, Lamoni, Ammon, and servants lead many others, including the king’s household, to truth. Those converted reject war, bury their weapons, and rely on God.
