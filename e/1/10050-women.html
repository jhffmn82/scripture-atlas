Thirty-Second Lecture.

Women are presented as a serious difficulty for a man seeking liberation and chastity. A Sramana should not watch, remember, praise, or long for women's forms, beauty, laughter, gestures, glances, or other attractions, and should not stay in a house inhabited by women.
