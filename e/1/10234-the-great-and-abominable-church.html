Abstract written by a model

The great and abominable church, founded by the devil and called the mother of harlots and whore of all the earth, slays, tortures, binds, and captivates saints. Desiring gold and silver, it removes plain and precious gospel parts and covenants, perverts the Lord’s ways, and gathers multitudes against the Lamb. It ultimately turns upon its own head, tumbles to dust, and suffers a great fall.

Chapter 13 .. Chapter 14

The great and abominable church is founded by the devil and slays, tortures, binds, and brings the saints of God into captivity. It takes away plain and precious parts of the gospel and many covenants, and it gathers multitudes to fight against the Lamb of God.

Chapter 22

The great and abominable church will turn upon its own head, tumble to the dust, and suffer a great fall. It is described as having perverted the right ways of the Lord.
