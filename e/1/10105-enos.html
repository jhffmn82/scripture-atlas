Enos

Abstract written by a model

Enos learns from his father, hunts in the forests, prays all day and night, receives a remission of his sins, and then prays for the Nephites and Lamanites. He obtains God's promise concerning the records of the Nephites, prophesies and testifies among the people of Nephi, and looks forward to resting with his Redeemer and putting on immortality.
