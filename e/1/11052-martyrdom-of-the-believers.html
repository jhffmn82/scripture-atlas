martyrdom of the believers

event · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: burning of the believers, the destruction of those who were consumed by fire, this martyrdom

Abstract written by a model

The martyrdom of those who believed includes believers, women, and children being burned, while Alma and Amulek witness it. Alma says the Lord permits it because of hardened hearts. Later, rulers have believers in the Lord and his power put to death, stirring anger among their brethren and causing contention in the wilderness.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 14

The people burn believing women and children and others who believe or have been taught to believe in the word of God. Alma and Amulek witness the destruction, and Alma says the Lord permits it according to the hardness of the people's hearts.

Chapter 25

The rulers who are the remnant of the children of Amulon cause all those who believe in the Lord and his power to be put to death. This martyrdom stirs many of their brethren to anger and begins contention in the wilderness.
