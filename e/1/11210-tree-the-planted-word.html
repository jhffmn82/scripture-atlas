tree (the planted word)

Also called: a tree, the tree, the tree of life, tree

Abstract written by a model

A seed sprouts into a tree. It needs great care, nourishment, faith, diligence, and patience; otherwise, without root and scorched, it withers away. Nourished, it brings forth fruit and, as the planted word in Alma's listeners, springs up unto everlasting life.

Chapter 32

The growing seed becomes a tree that must be nourished with great care, faith, diligence, and patience. If neglected, it lacks root, withers in the heat, and is cast out; if nourished, it brings forth fruit.

Chapter 33

The planted word, when nourished by faith, will become a tree springing up in Alma's listeners unto everlasting life.
