Abstract written by a model

Everlasting death is a way chosen by people according to the flesh and evil within them, rejecting God's will. This choice lets the devil captivate and bring them to hell, though God may raise people from it through the atonement.

Chapter 2

Men may choose eternal death according to the will of the flesh and the evil within it. This choice gives the spirit of the devil power to captivate them and bring them down to hell.

Chapter 10

Jacob teaches that people may choose the way of everlasting death or the way of eternal life. God may raise people from everlasting death through the power of the atonement.
