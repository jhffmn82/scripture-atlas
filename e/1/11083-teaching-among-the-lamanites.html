Abstract written by a model

For fourteen years, Ammon's brethren teach the word of God among the Lamanites. Despite being cast out, mocked, beaten, stoned, bound, and imprisoned while teaching widely, they endure with patience and are delivered by God. Their work succeeds in bringing many Lamanites to knowledge of the truth.

Chapter 17

The sons of Mosiah teach among the Lamanites for fourteen years. Their teaching has much success, and many are brought to the knowledge of the truth and before the altar of God.

Chapter 26

Preaching to the Lamanites involved teaching in their homes, streets, hills, temples, and synagogues. Ammon and his brethren were often cast out, mocked, beaten, stoned, bound, and imprisoned, but God delivered them.

Chapter 29

The speaker believes he should be content to perform the work to which he has been called.
