king Laman

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: Laman, king, the king

Abstract written by a model

King Laman is the ruler of the Lamanites. He covenants with Zeniff to yield Lehi-Nephi and Shilom, but uses cunning, lying craftiness, and fair promises to seek Zeniff’s people’s bondage or destruction. He commands his people to depart, then stirs them up to contend against Zeniff’s people. He dies, and his son reigns in his stead; a later Laman, named after his father, rules a numerous people and appoints teachers.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 9

King Laman covenants with Zeniff that Zeniff may possess the land of Lehi-Nephi and the land of Shilom, and commands his people to depart. His action is described as cunning and crafty because he intends to bring Zeniff’s people into bondage, and he later stirs up his people to contend with them.

Chapter 10

King Laman dies, and his son begins to reign in his stead. Before his death, king Laman deceived Zeniff through cunning, lying craftiness, and fair promises, leading Zeniff’s people into the land so they could be destroyed.

Chapter 24

Laman is king of the Lamanites and is named after his father. He rules over a numerous people and appoints teachers in the lands possessed by his people.
