plates of gold

object · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: plates of ore, the plates, these records, twenty-four plates

Abstract written by a model

Plates of pure gold, filled with engravings recording a destroyed people, are brought from a discovered land by Limhi’s people and delivered to king Mosiah. Though initially uninterpretable, Mosiah translates them with interpreters; the account gives his people much knowledge and causes them to mourn exceedingly.

Chapter 8

The twenty-four plates were brought back from the discovered land as evidence of what the forty-three people reported. They were filled with engravings and made of pure gold, but no one in the land could interpret them.

Chapter 28

The plates of gold were found by the people of Limhi and delivered to king Mosiah by Limhi. King Mosiah translates their records with the interpreters, and the account causes his people to mourn exceedingly while giving them much knowledge.
