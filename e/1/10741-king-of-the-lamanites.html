king of the Lamanites

person · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: Lamanite king, king, the king, the king of the Lamanites, their king

Abstract written by a model

The king of the Lamanites receives Nephite tribute and leads the Lamanites in war. He swears with Limhi that his people will not slay Limhi’s people, takes tribute, and stations guards. Later, wounded and captured while attacking Limhi’s people, he learns priests caused an abduction, is pacified, and returns peacefully.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 19

The king of the Lamanites makes an oath that his people will not slay Limhi’s people. He receives their tribute, places guards around their land, and supports the guards from the tribute.

Chapter 20

The king of the Lamanites leads his people against the people of Limhi, is wounded and captured, and learns that the priests caused the abduction. He is pacified and returns peacefully with his people.
