Zoram

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, Zoram serves as Laban’s servant and keeper of the treasury keys, leads Nephi to the plates of brass, and joins Nephi and his brethren. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, Zoram serves as a Nephite chief captain and leads the recovery of captives with his sons Lehi and Aha.

Abstract written by a model

Zoram, Laban’s servant and keeper of the treasury keys, mistakes Nephi for Laban and leads him to the plates of brass. In the wilderness, he agrees to go with Nephi’s father and swears to remain with Nephi and his brethren.

Chapter 4

Zoram is Laban’s servant and holds the keys to Laban’s treasury. Believing Nephi is Laban, he follows him to the plates of brass, then agrees to go into the wilderness and swears to remain with Nephi and his brethren.
