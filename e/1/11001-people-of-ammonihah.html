the people who were in the city of Ammonihah in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where the people who were in the city of Ammonihah first appears

Abstract written by a model

The people of Ammonihah reject, revile, and expel Alma, then grow more wicked and seek to destroy liberty. They contend with Alma, question his authority, refuse belief, and seek to imprison him. Hard-hearted, stiffnecked, lost, fallen, and influenced by Satan, they hear Alma and Amulek but many become angry and accuse Amulek. Their sin and transgression bring repeated warnings: repent or face the Lord’s anger and destruction.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 8

The people of Ammonihah reject Alma's preaching, revile him, spit upon him, and cast him out. They grow more wicked, study to destroy the liberty of the people of Nephi, and are warned to repent or face the Lord's anger and destruction.

Chapter 9

The people contend with Alma, question his authority, and refuse to believe his words. They are described as hard-hearted, stiffnecked, lost, and fallen; they become angry and seek to cast Alma into prison.

Chapter 10

The people of Ammonihah hear the testimonies of Amulek and Alma, but many become angry and accuse Amulek of reviling their laws and lawyers. They are warned that their transgression makes them ripe for destruction unless they repent.

people of Ammonihah in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where people of Ammonihah first appears

Abstract written by a model

The people of Ammonihah are a hard-hearted, stiffnecked group who forget their fathers’ traditions and the commandments of God, become angry at Alma’s description, reject repentance, and attribute Alma and Amulek’s power to Satan. They belong to the profession of Nehor and are ultimately destroyed by the Lamanites, with every living soul killed.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 9

The people of Ammonihah are called a hard-hearted and stiffnecked people because they have forgotten the traditions of their fathers and the commandments of God. They become angry when Alma uses this description.

Chapter 15

The people of Ammonihah remained hard-hearted and stiff-necked and did not repent of their sins. They attributed Alma and Amulek's power to the devil and did not believe in repentance.

Chapter 16

The people of Ammonihah were destroyed by the Lamanites, and every living soul of them was killed.
