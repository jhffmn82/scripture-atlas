Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Mosiah, children of Christ are called his sons and daughters because Christ spiritually begets them and their hearts are changed through faith on his name. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record, the children of Christ are mentioned as people whom the despisers of the works of the Lord will despise.
