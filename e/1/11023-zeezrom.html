Zeezrom

person · 1 work · 18 quoted facts · 7 connections

Abstract written by a model

Zeezrom is a man and expert lawyer with much business among the people, initially foremost in accusing Amulek and Alma. Expert in devices of the devil, he tries to induce Amulek to deny a Supreme Being and uses subtle plans and lies to deceive people. Amulek’s words make him tremble, and Alma exposes his lies to God and men. Zeezrom becomes conscious of his guilt, convinced of God’s power, and diligently asks about the kingdom of God and resurrection. He recognizes Alma and Amulek as spotless, pleads for them, and is cast out by the people. In Sidom, tribulation and sin leave him sick with a burning fever; believing Alma’s words about Christ’s redemption, he is healed through faith, baptized unto the Lord, and preaches in Sidom. He later goes with Alma among the Zoramites as a fellow laborer, to preach the word.

Zeezrom is in one work on this site, The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

Zeezrom is one of the lawyers and is foremost in accusing Amulek and Alma. He is described as one of the most expert lawyers, with much business among the people.

Chapter 11

Zeezrom questioned Amulek and offered him six onties of silver if he would deny the existence of a Supreme Being. He was described as expert in the devices of the devil, and he later trembled after hearing Amulek’s words.

Chapter 12

Zeezrom is exposed as having lied to men and God and as having used a subtle plan to deceive the people. He trembles under a consciousness of his guilt, becomes convinced of God's power, and asks Alma about the kingdom of God and the resurrection of the dead.

Chapter 14

Zeezrom becomes astonished and harrowed by consciousness of his guilt after recognizing the blindness caused by his lying words. He declares Alma and Amulek spotless before God and pleads for them, but the people revile him, spit upon him, and cast him out.

Chapter 15

Zeezrom lay sick in Sidom with a burning fever caused by the tribulations of his mind and his sins. He believed Alma's words about Christ's redemption, was healed after Alma prayed, was baptized, and began preaching to the people.

Chapter 31

Zeezrom went with Alma among the Zoramites to preach the word. Alma prayed that Zeezrom would be comforted and strengthened in Christ.
