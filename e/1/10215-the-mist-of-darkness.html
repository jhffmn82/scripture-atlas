Abstract written by a model

In Lehi’s dream, an exceedingly great mist of darkness causes people on the path to lose their way, though some pass through while clinging to the rod of iron. It represents temptations of the devil that blind eyes, harden hearts, and lead people onto broad roads where they perish and are lost.
