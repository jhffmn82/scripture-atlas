Amalekites

group · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Amalekites and by the Amulonites, an Amalekite, none who were Amalekites or Amulonites, the Amalekites, those of the Lamanites who slew so many of their brethren were Amalekites and Amulonites

Abstract written by a model

The Amalekites are a hardened Lamanite people, many following Nehors, who build Jerusalem, synagogues, and sanctuaries. An Amalekite rejects and mocks Aaron’s teachings about Christ. They repeatedly stir unconverted Lamanites and Amulonites to anger, form the greatest number who slay Anti-Nephi-Lehi’s people, and never join the people of the Lord. Angry over losses, they renew destruction and later serve as chief captains under Zerahemnah, inspiring battle.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 21

The Amalekites have built Jerusalem and synagogues after the order of the Nehors. An Amalekite contends with Aaron, rejects his teachings, and mocks him when Aaron teaches about Christ.

Chapter 24

The Amalekites stir up the unconverted Lamanites and Amulonites to anger against their brethren. They form the greatest number of those who slay the people of Anti-Nephi-Lehi, and none of them join the people of the Lord.

Chapter 27

The Amalekites were exceedingly angry because of their losses. They stirred up the Lamanites against the people of Anti-Nephi-Lehi and began again to destroy them.

Chapter 43

The Amalekites are described as more wicked and murderous than the Lamanites. They become chief captains under Zerahemnah and inspire the Lamanites in battle.
