Abstract written by a model

Worship at the holy stand is a Zoramite practice requiring a repeated public prayer, followed by silence about God until the next gathering. Alma counters the belief that exclusion from synagogues prevents worship, teaching worship and scripture study elsewhere; Nephites seek to preserve worship according to their desires.

Chapter 31

Worship at the holy stand required each Zoramite to stand on a raised place, stretch forth the hands toward heaven, and offer the same prayer. Afterward, they returned home and did not speak of God again until the next gathering.

Chapter 33

Alma's listeners mistakenly suppose that being cast out of their synagogues prevents them from worshiping God. Alma teaches that they can worship God and should search the scriptures.

Chapter 43

The Nephites seek to preserve the ability to worship God according to their desires. They know that the Lamanites would destroy those who worship God in spirit and in truth.
