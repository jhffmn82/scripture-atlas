the atonement

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: atonement, infinite atonement

Abstract written by a model

The atonement is an infinite provision for all mankind, received through the Holy Messiah. It answers the ends of the law and relates to punishment and happiness attached to human choices. Necessary for resurrection, it enables corruption to put on incorruption and prevents the first judgment from continuing endlessly. It satisfies justice for those without the law, delivers them from death and torment, raises people from everlasting death, and may receive them into God’s eternal kingdom.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The atonement answers the ends of the law and is connected with the punishment and happiness affixed to men's choices. Its benefits are received through the Holy Messiah.

Chapter 9

An infinite atonement is necessary so that corruption can put on incorruption and the first judgment does not continue endlessly. It satisfies the demands of justice for those who have not been given the law and delivers them from death, hell, the devil, and endless torment.

Chapter 10

The atonement has power to raise people from everlasting death. Through it, people may be received into the eternal kingdom of God.

Chapter 25

The atonement of Jesus Christ is infinite for all mankind. The Jews will eventually be persuaded to believe in Christ and the atonement.
