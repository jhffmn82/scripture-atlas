the curse upon the land

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: a curse upon the face of the land, curse, curse upon the land, great curse, great curse upon all the land, the curse, the curse from off the land

Abstract written by a model

The curse upon the land comes as a consequence of wickedness and destroys the people when they are ripened in iniquity. It makes tools and swords disappear by the following day, causing people to hold tightly to property and avoid borrowing or lending. The Lord begins taking it away during Emer’s reign, but prophets warn it will return without repentance.

Chapter 9

The curse upon the land is associated with the people’s iniquity and with the destruction of those who possess the land when they are ripened in iniquity. The Lord begins to take the curse away during Emer’s reign, but prophets later warn that it will return if the people do not repent.

Chapter 14

The curse upon the land caused a man's tool or sword to disappear when left on a shelf or other place. Because of it, every man held tightly to his own property and would not borrow or lend.
