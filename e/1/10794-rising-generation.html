Abstract written by a model

The rising generation, once little children during king Benjamin's speech, becomes a separate people in faith. Unbelief leaves them unable to understand the word of God; with hardened hearts, they reject their fathers' traditions, resurrection, Christ, baptism, the Lord, and church membership.

Chapter 26

The rising generation cannot understand the words of king Benjamin because they were little children when he spoke. They do not believe the traditions of their fathers, including the resurrection of the dead and the coming of Christ, and they refuse baptism and church membership.
