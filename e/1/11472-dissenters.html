Abstract written by a model

Dissenters, a group from the Nephites, go to the Lamanites and stir them to war, helping a numerous Lamanite army obtain Nephite possessions in the land southward. Later, Nephi and Lehi’s preaching confounds many dissenters; they confess sins, are baptized unto repentance, return to the Nephites, and repair their wrongs.

Chapter 4

Dissenters from the Nephites went to the Lamanites and stirred them up to war against the Nephites. With the help of a numerous Lamanite army, they obtained the Nephites' possessions in the land southward.

Chapter 5

Dissenters who had gone over from the Nephites are confounded by the preaching of Nephi and Lehi; many confess their sins, receive baptism unto repentance, and return to the Nephites to repair the wrongs they had done.
