Abstract written by a model

In The Fourth Book of Nephi, Who Is the Son of Nephi, One of the Disciples of Jesus Christ, Ammaron keeps the record after his brother Amos dies and, constrained by the Holy Ghost, hides the sacred records after three hundred and twenty years. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record, Ammaron deposits and hides sacred records and engravings at hill Shim in Antum, then instructs Mormon to retrieve Nephi’s plates at twenty-four and engrave observations about the people.
