Lachoneus

person · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Lachoneus (judge and governor), governor of the land, the judge, Lachoneus, this Lachoneus

Abstract written by a model

Lachoneus, a just chief judge and governor, receives Giddianhi’s threat without fear, gathers the people to repent, pray, and prepare, appoints Gidgiddoni to command the armies, builds fortifications, and with other leaders establishes great peace in the land.

Lachoneus is in one work on this site, The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
