Also called: Srênika, great king, king, lion of kings, ruler of Magadha, ruler of men

Abstract written by a model

King Srênika, ruler of Magadha with wealth, horses, elephants, subjects, power and command, encounters a saint. He first offers worldly protection and pleasures, then hears the teaching, understands being without protection, seeks forgiveness, and becomes a staunch believer in the Law with his household.
