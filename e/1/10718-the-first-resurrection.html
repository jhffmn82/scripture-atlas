the first resurrection

event · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: a first resurrection, resurrection of the dead

Abstract written by a model

The first resurrection is the resurrection of those who lived before, live now, and will live until Christ’s resurrection. Brought to pass through Christ’s power, sufferings, and death, it provides eternal life and includes prophets, believers, commandment keepers, the ignorant without salvation declared, and little children, but excludes wilful rebels who die in sins.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 15

The first resurrection includes all the prophets, those who believed their words, those who kept the commandments of God, those who died before Christ came in ignorance without salvation being declared to them, and little children. Those who wilfully rebel against God and die in their sins have no part in it.

Chapter 18

The resurrection of the dead was among the teachings Alma received from Abinadi and taught to the people. Those who entered the covenant were promised to be numbered with those of the first resurrection.
