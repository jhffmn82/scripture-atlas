Chapter 5

The preaching and spiritual manifestations involving Nephi and Lehi convince many Lamanites. Those convinced lay down their weapons, hatred, and traditions, and yield their lands to the Nephites.

The conversion of the Lamanites brings great joy to the people of the church. Converted Lamanites declare its manner to the Nephites and exhort them to faith and repentance.
