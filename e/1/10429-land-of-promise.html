Abstract written by a model

Land of promise is a land choice above all other lands, preserved by God for a righteous people and possessed under the Lord. Its possessors must serve the true and only God; if they do, their nation is free from bondage and captivity, but if they do not, they may be swept off in the fulness of God’s wrath. It contains fruit, grain, silks, fine linen, gold, silver, precious things, useful animals, and many cities, and it contains New Jerusalem. Jared, the brother of Jared, with families and friends, is promised leadership into it. It is restored to Omer and prospers under Emer, then repeatedly is rebuilt and populated after destruction and war, becoming rich and industrious. Yet wickedness brings dearth, famine, serpents, wars, pestilence, calamity, destruction, and warnings to repent. Finally, a great curse covers all the land with the dead; New Jerusalem is to be built upon it for the remnant of the seed of Joseph.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

The Book of Ether / Chapter 1

The Lord promised to lead Jared, the brother of Jared, their families, and their friends into a land which is choice above all the lands of the earth.

Chapter 2

The land of promise is described as choice above all other lands and preserved for a righteous people. Whoever possesses it must serve the true and only God or be swept off when the fulness of God’s wrath comes upon them.

Chapter 9

The land is restored to Omer and later prospers greatly under Emer, with abundant resources, animals, cities, and riches. It is described as choice above all other lands and later suffers wickedness, dearth, famine, and poisonous serpents.

Chapter 10

The land was rebuilt and populated after destruction and war. It contained many cities, abundant animals, ores, fertile areas, and a people who became rich and industrious; it was described as choice above all lands because the Lord had spoken it.

Chapter 11

The land suffered wars, contentions, famines, pestilences, calamity, and great destruction. Prophets warned that a curse would come upon the land unless the people repented.

Chapter 13

This land becomes a choice land above all other lands and a chosen land of the Lord. The New Jerusalem is to be built upon it for the remnant of the seed of Joseph.

Chapter 14

A great curse came upon all the land because of the iniquity of the people. The land was covered with the bodies of the dead, and the scent of the bodies troubled the people by day and by night.
