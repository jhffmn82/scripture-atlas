Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Helaman, Nephi, Lehi’s brother, receives daily revelations and prays for famine to bring the people to repentance and for its end when they repent. In The Book of Helaman, Nephi succeeds Helaman as judge, then gives up the judgment-seat to preach, prophesy, baptize, and declare repentance. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, Nephi is baptized by Jesus and baptizes all the disciples Jesus had chosen. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, Nephi keeps the records, receives authority from Jesus to baptize, and brings forth his record. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, Nephi, son of Helaman, testifies of repentance, performs miracles, ordains baptizers, and entrusts the records to his eldest son. In The Fourth Book of Nephi, Who Is the Son of Nephi, One of the Disciples of Jesus Christ, Nephi keeps the last record on the plates of Nephi until his death, after which his son Amos keeps it.

Nephi in The Book of Helaman

The Book of Mormon · the work’s page · read it where Nephi first appears

Abstract written by a model

Nephi is Helaman’s eldest son, a judge, prophet, preacher, and servant of God. After Helaman dies, he succeeds him in the judgment-seat and governs with justice and equity, keeping God’s commandments. With Lehi, he warns the Nephites about their iniquities and the consequences of refusing repentance. Wearied by the people’s wickedness, he gives the judgment-seat to Cezoram and devotes the rest of his days to preaching, prophesying, baptizing, and declaring repentance among Nephites and Lamanites. Imprisoned with Lehi, he is encircled by fire without being burned. After preaching and prophesying in the land northward, where his words are rejected, he returns to Zarahemla mourning for the Nephites. From his garden and tower, he prays to God, condemns corrupt law and secret works of darkness, and testifies of approaching judgments and destruction unless the people repent. Though accused of murdering Seezoram, he reveals Seantum as the murderer and is released after Seantum confesses. God blesses Nephi for his unwearying declaration of the word; when people seek to imprison him, the Spirit conveys him away. He continues among all the people, performing signs, wonders, miracles, prophecy, preaching, and baptism for those who confess their sins.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Nephi is Helaman's eldest son. After Helaman dies, Nephi begins to reign in his stead and fills the judgment-seat with justice and equity.

Chapter 4

Nephi, together with Lehi, preached and prophesied to the people concerning their iniquities and what would happen if they did not repent.

Chapter 5

Nephi gives up the judgment-seat to Cezoram because of the iniquity of the people and spends the remainder of his days preaching the word of God with Lehi. He preaches to the Nephites and Lamanites, is imprisoned with Lehi, and is encircled by fire without being burned.

Chapter 7

Nephi, the son of Helaman, returned to the land of Zarahemla after preaching and prophesying among the people in the land northward, who rejected his words. He mourned over the wickedness of the Nephites, called them to repentance, and testified of the judgments that would come upon them.

Chapter 8

Nephi speaks plainly against the judges, their corrupt law, and their secret works of darkness. He testifies of the judgments that will come upon the people unless they repent and reveals that the judge has been murdered.

Chapter 9

Nephi is accused by the judges of conspiring to murder Seezoram, but he denies the accusation and gives instructions that reveal Seantum as the murderer. He is bound and questioned, then released after Seantum confesses.

Chapter 10

Nephi is left alone after the people divide and goes toward his house pondering the things the Lord has shown him. God blesses him for declaring the word with unwearyingness, and Nephi declares the Lord’s warning of destruction to the people. When they try to cast him into prison, the Spirit conveys him away, and he continues declaring the word among all the people.

Chapter 16

Nephi baptized, prophesied, preached repentance, showed signs and wonders, and worked miracles. Those who believed Samuel's words went to Nephi, confessed their sins, and sought baptism.
