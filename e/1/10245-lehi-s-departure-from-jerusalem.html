Lehi's departure from Jerusalem

Abstract written by a model

Lehi’s departure is a departure from Jerusalem into the wilderness. Warned by the Lord after prophesying of the people’s iniquity and facing threats to his life, Lehi is commanded in a dream to take his family, provisions, and tents, leaving possessions behind.

Chapter 2

Lehi's departure occurs after the Lord commands him in a dream to take his family into the wilderness. He leaves his house, land of inheritance, gold, silver, and precious things, taking only his family, provisions, and tents.
