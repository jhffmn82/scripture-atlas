Abstract written by a model

Alma and Amulek preach and prophesy among the people of Ammonihah. Filled with the Holy Ghost, they receive power from the Lord against confinement and death, continuing until they are bound and cast into prison.

Chapter 8

Alma and Amulek go among the people of Ammonihah to declare the words of God and prophesy. They are filled with the Holy Ghost and receive power so they cannot be confined in dungeons or slain, until they are bound and cast into prison.
