Chapter 5

The servant of the Lord of the vineyard carries out the Lord's commands by grafting branches, nourishing the trees, and laboring in the vineyard. He asks the Lord to spare the vineyard longer and explains that the branches have overcome the good roots. He later brings other servants to labor with him.
