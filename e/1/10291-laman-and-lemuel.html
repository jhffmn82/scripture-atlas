Laman and Lemuel in The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry

Nephi · the work’s page · read it where Laman and Lemuel first appears

Abstract written by a model

Laman and Lemuel are Nephi’s brethren, portrayed as stiffnecked murmurers who resist their father, Nephi, and the Lord’s instructions. They leave Jerusalem resentfully, doubt its destruction, and refuse to hearken until confounded. They dispute, fail to inquire, suffer famine, and are chastened for anger and murmuring. They doubt Nephi can build a ship, are shaken by the Lord, bind Nephi during the voyage, then repent when a storm threatens destruction. Later they ask about the plates of brass and are taught their prophecies concern spiritual and temporal things.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Laman and Lemuel murmur against their father because he led them away from Jerusalem and their inheritance and precious things. They do not believe Jerusalem can be destroyed, refuse to hearken unto Nephi, and are confounded by Lehi when he speaks with power.

Chapter 15 .. Chapter 16

The brethren dispute over their father's words, fail to inquire of the Lord, and are rebuked by Nephi. They later humble themselves, follow the ball, suffer from the famine, and are chastened for their anger and murmuring.

Chapter 17 .. Chapter 18

Nephi’s brethren murmur that he cannot build a ship and refuse to believe that he was instructed by the Lord. They are confounded by Nephi’s words, are shaken by the Lord, later bind Nephi during the voyage, and repent when the storm threatens to destroy them.

Chapter 22

Nephi's brethren ask what the things on the plates of brass mean. They are taught that the prophecies concern both spiritual and temporal things.

Nephi's brothers in The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry

Nephi · the work’s page · read it where Nephi's brothers first appears

Abstract written by a model

Nephi’s younger brothers, outside Jerusalem, murmur over returning for Laban’s records. They try to obtain them with valuables, lose their property, flee, and angrily smite Nephi and other younger brothers. Still angry and murmuring, they follow Nephi to Jerusalem, flee from him, return when called, and depart into the wilderness.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Nephi's brothers initially murmur about returning to Jerusalem and later attempt to obtain the records from Laban with gold, silver, and precious things. After losing their property and fleeing into the wilderness, they become angry, and some of them smite Nephi and their other younger brothers with a rod.

Chapter 4

Nephi’s brethren are initially angry and continue to murmur, but they follow him to Jerusalem. Laman, Lemuel, and Sam flee when they mistake Nephi for Laban, then return after Nephi calls to them and depart with him into the wilderness.
