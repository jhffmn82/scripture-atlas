Omer

person · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 7 connections

Abstract written by a model

Omer, son of Shule, reigned as king and fathered Jared, Esrom, Coriantumr, Emer, and other children. Jared captured him, and Omer spent half his reign in captivity. Warned in a dream, he fled with his family to Ablom, later dwelt with Nimrah, returned to his inheritance, anointed Emer king, and died old.

Omer is in one work on this site, The Book of Ether. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 8

Omer reigned after his father and was Jared's father. Jared took him into captivity, where Omer spent half of his reign and begat Esrom and Coriantumr.

Chapter 9

Omer is warned by the Lord in a dream to leave the land, and he travels with his family to Ablom, except for Jared and his family. He later dwells with Nimrah and is restored to the land of his inheritance, where he anoints Emer as king before dying in old age.
