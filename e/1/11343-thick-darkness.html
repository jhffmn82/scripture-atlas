thick darkness

event · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: smoke and of darkness, the darkness, the mists of darkness, the vapor of smoke and of darkness

Abstract written by a model

Thick darkness covers the whole land after destruction, its vapor felt by surviving inhabitants. It prevents candles, torches, and fire from producing or showing light for three days, then disperses in the morning.

thick darkness is in one work on this site, The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
