Zeniff

Abstract written by a model

Zeniff, a Nephite, comes from Zarahemla seeking to inherit his fathers’ land and is made king over his people in the land of Nephi. Sent as a spy among the Lamanites, he sees good in them and opposes their destruction. His over-zealous desire for the land leads king Laman to deceive him and secure Lehi-Nephi and Shilom. Zeniff nevertheless obtains permission to occupy those lands, establishes and defends his kingdom, prepares his people for battle, and leads them against the Lamanites. In old age he teaches trust in the Lord and gives the kingdom to his son Noah.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

Zeniff came up from the land of Zarahemla to inherit the land of his fathers and was made king over the people. His over-zealousness led him to be deceived by king Laman and to yield the cities of Lehi-Nephi and Shilom.

Chapter 9

Zeniff is sent as a spy among the Lamanites but desires that they not be destroyed after seeing what is good among them. He leads people to possess the land, secures king Laman’s permission to occupy the land of Lehi-Nephi and the land of Shilom, and later leads his people in battle against the Lamanites.

Chapter 10

Zeniff establishes the kingdom, prepares weapons and guards, and organizes his people for defense. In his old age, he leads them into battle, teaches them about the Lamanites, urges them to trust in the Lord, and later confers the kingdom upon one of his sons.

Chapter 11

Zeniff confers the kingdom upon Noah, one of his sons.
