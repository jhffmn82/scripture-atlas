our people

group · 1 work · 15 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: a people like this, his people, our brethren, this people

Abstract written by a model

The people are an unrepentant, violently wicked group who have lost love for one another and continually seek blood and revenge. Without fear, order, mercy, principle, or feeling, they imprison the daughters of the Lamanites in Moriantum, violate them, murder them cruelly, and devour their flesh as bravery. They carry away provisions from Sherrizah and cause women and children suffering across the land, and are expected to perish unless they return unto God.

Chapter 9

The people do not repent, have lost their love for one another, and thirst continually after blood and revenge. They have committed abominations against prisoners, including violating, torturing, murdering, and devouring the daughters of the Lamanites. They are described as without order, mercy, principle, and feeling, and their wickedness exceeds that of the Lamanites.
