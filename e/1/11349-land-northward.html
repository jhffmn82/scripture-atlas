Abstract written by a model

The land northward suffers greater destruction than the land southward: tempests, whirlwinds, thunderings, lightnings, and quaking change its whole face, break roads, destroy cities, and slay or carry away many inhabitants.

Chapter 8

The land northward suffered a more great and terrible destruction than the land southward. Its whole face was changed, highways and level roads were broken or spoiled, cities were sunk, burned, or shaken down, and many inhabitants were slain or carried away in the whirlwind.
