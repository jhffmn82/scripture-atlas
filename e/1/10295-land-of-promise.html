Abstract written by a model

The land of promise is a land prepared by the Lord for Nephi, promised to Lehi's family, and choice above all other lands. Lehi obtains it and rejoices, while Nephi teaches that faithfulness and keeping the commandments allow people to obtain it. It becomes the setting for multitudes, wars, generations, judgments, and destruction through earthquakes, darkness, fire, sinking cities, and broken mountains and plains.
