Com

Abstract written by a model

Com, son and successor of Coriantum, first ruled half the kingdom for forty-two years, then gained the rest after fighting Amgid. Though unable to prevail against robbers, he protected fleeing prophets, received their prophecies, was blessed in his remaining days, lived to old age, and begat Shiblom.

Com is in one work on this site, The Book of Ether. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

Com was begotten by Coriantum and drew away half of the kingdom. He reigned over half of it for forty-two years, fought Amgid for many years, gained power over him and the remainder of the kingdom, and fought against robbers without prevailing over them.

Chapter 11

Com protected prophets who fled to him, and they prophesied many things to him. He was blessed for the remainder of his days, lived to a good old age, and begat Shiblom.
