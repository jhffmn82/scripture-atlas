Chapter 17

The flocks of Lamoni are driven to the water of Sebus and scattered by certain Lamanites. Ammon and the servants gather them again, protect them, water them, and return them to the king’s pasture.

Chapter 18

The king’s flocks are scattered by Lamanites at the waters of Sebus. Ammon preserves the flocks and prevents the king’s enemies from scattering them while he is present.
