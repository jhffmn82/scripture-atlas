Amalickiah in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Amalickiah first appears

Abstract written by a model

Amalickiah is a large, strong, subtle, evil Nephite by birth who desires kingship. Through flattery, cunning, fraud, and deceit, he leads people into dissension and wickedness, seeking to destroy the church of God and the foundation of liberty. Opposed by Moroni, he flees to the land of Nephi, stirs the Lamanites against the Nephites, gains command of their armies, poisons Lehonti, arranges the Lamanite king’s assassination, marries the queen, and obtains the Lamanite kingdom. As king, he hardens the Lamanites, gathers a large army, seeks dominion over the land and bondage for the Nephites, and wages war with vengeance against Moroni. His forces capture Nephite cities, including the city of Moroni. After being angered by losses and swearing to drink Moroni’s blood, he continues leading the Lamanite army until Teancum kills him with a javelin.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 46

Amalickiah is a large and strong man who desires to be king. Through cunning devices and flattering words, he leads many people into dissensions, wickedness, and an attempt to destroy the church of God and the foundation of liberty. He flees with some followers to the land of Nephi when Moroni’s people become more numerous.

Chapter 47

Amalickiah stirs the Lamanites to anger against the Nephites and plans to dethrone the king of the Lamanites. He gains command of the armies, causes Lehonti to be poisoned, arranges the king’s assassination, marries the queen, and obtains the kingdom through fraud.

Chapter 48

Amalickiah becomes king over the Lamanites, appoints men to stir them against the Nephites, gathers a large army, and seeks to rule all the land and bring the Nephites into bondage.

Chapter 49

Amalickiah did not come down to battle, and his chief captains did not attack Ammonihah because Moroni had altered the Nephite defenses. After learning of the Lamanites’ losses, Amalickiah became exceedingly angry, cursed God and Moroni, and swore to drink Moroni’s blood.

Chapter 51

Amalickiah gathers and leads a Lamanite army against the Nephites, captures many cities, and is killed by Teancum with a javelin.
