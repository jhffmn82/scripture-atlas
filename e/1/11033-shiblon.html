Abstract written by a model

Shiblon, Alma’s son, is praised for faithfulness, diligence, patience, and long-suffering among the Zoramites despite bonds and stoning for the word’s sake. From youth he looked to the Lord Jesus Christ, walked uprightly before God, kept commandments, and taught the people. He received the sacred things from Helaman, later conferred them upon Helaman’s son before his death, and died in the thirty-ninth year.

Chapter 38

Shiblon is praised for his faithfulness, diligence, patience, and long-suffering among the Zoramites. He is counseled to trust in God, continue keeping the commandments, teach the word, avoid pride and idleness, bridle his passions, and acknowledge his unworthiness before God.

Chapter 63

Shiblon took possession of the sacred things delivered to Helaman. He was described as just and upright before God, and he conferred the sacred things upon Helaman, the son of Helaman, before his death. Shiblon then died in the thirty-ninth year.
