Abstract written by a model

Orihah, Jared’s son and the people’s king, humbly walks before Lord, teaches ancestral great things, judges righteously, and begins a reign in which the people prosper and become exceedingly rich.

Orihah is in one work on this site, The Book of Ether. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
