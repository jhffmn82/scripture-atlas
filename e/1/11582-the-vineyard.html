the vineyard

place · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: all the land of my vineyard, his vineyard, my vineyard, vineyard

Abstract written by a model

The vineyard, possessed by the Lord, contains olive trees and branches producing good and bad fruit. Once corrupt, it is repeatedly pruned, dug, nourished, dunged, and grafted by the Lord’s servants. After the final labor it bears natural fruit, is no longer corrupt, and becomes one body after the bad is cast away. It is ultimately burned in fire after the good and bad are separated.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

The vineyard contains a tame olive-tree, wild olive branches, natural branches planted in different places, and trees that produce both good and bad fruit. After extensive pruning, digging, nourishing, dunging, and grafting, the vineyard again produces natural fruit and is no longer corrupt. The Lord of the vineyard says it will ultimately be burned after the good and bad are gathered.

Chapter 6

The vineyard will be nourished and pruned by the servants of the Lord. Those who labor diligently in it will be blessed, while those cast out will be cursed and sent into their own place.
