bondage

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: bondage of Limhi's people, subjection, subjection or into bondage, yoke of bondage

Abstract written by a model

Bondage is the subjection of Limhi’s people to the Lamanites, brought on by their iniquities and abominations. It burdens, afflicts, and drives them, motivating a search for Zarahemla and deliverance. Limhi teaches that God will deliver them if they turn to him and serve diligently. After humbling themselves, their burdens are eased; Gideon’s plan finally enables their departure.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

The people of Limhi were in bondage to the Lamanites because of their iniquities and abominations. Limhi taught that God would deliver them from bondage if they turned to him with full purpose of heart and served him diligently.

Chapter 8

The bondage of Limhi’s people motivated the forty-three people to seek the land of Zarahemla and appeal to their brethren for deliverance.

Chapter 21

Limhi’s people are surrounded and subjected to the Lamanites’ authority, burdens, blows, and driving. They humble themselves and submit to the yoke of bondage, while the Lord later eases their burdens without yet delivering them.

Chapter 22

The people of Limhi consult about how to deliver themselves out of bondage under the Lamanites. Gideon offers a plan that enables them to depart from it.
