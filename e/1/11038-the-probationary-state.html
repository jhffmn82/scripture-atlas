the probationary state

topic · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: a preparatory state, a probationary time, day of life, day of this life, preparatory state, probationary state, this life, this probationary state

Abstract written by a model

The probationary state is this life after death came upon mankind. It is a preparatory time for people to repent, serve God, perform their labors, meet conditions for mercy, and prepare to meet God and for eternity after the resurrection. It ends when the night of darkness comes, when no labor can be performed.

Chapter 12

This life became a probationary state after death came upon mankind. It is a time to repent and prepare to meet God and for the endless state after the resurrection.

Chapter 34

This life is the time for people to prepare to meet God and perform their labors. If people do not improve their time in this life, the night of darkness comes when no labor can be performed.

Chapter 42

The probationary state is a time granted to man to repent and serve God. It is a preparatory state in which mankind can meet the conditions required for mercy.
