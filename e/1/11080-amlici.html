Amlici

person · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 5 connections

Abstract written by a model

Amlici is a cunning, worldly-wise, wicked man who follows Nehor’s order, draws people after him, and seeks kingship. Rejected by the people, he is made king by supporters, leads them to take arms against their brethren and destroy the church of God, and is killed by Alma.

Amlici is in one work on this site, The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 2

Amlici is described as a cunning and worldly-wise man who draws many people after him and seeks to become king. After the voice of the people rejects him, his followers consecrate him as king; he commands them to fight the Nephites, and Alma kills him with the sword.
