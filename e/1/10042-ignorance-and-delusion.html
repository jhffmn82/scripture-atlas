ignorance and delusion

Abstract written by a model

Ignorance and delusion, origin of desire and Karman, are avoided for final deliverance; without delusion, misery ceases.

Thirty-Second Lecture.

Ignorance and delusion are to be avoided because delusion gives rise to desire and Karman. When delusion is absent, misery ceases.
