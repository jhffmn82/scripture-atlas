Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, Lehi is a prophet, visionary, husband, father, and leader who leaves Jerusalem, receives revelations, and teaches and prophesies to his family. In The Second Book of Nephi, Lehi is a father and prophet who teaches his family, blesses them, dies, is buried, and is redeemed from hell. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, Lehi leaves Jerusalem, brings out the plates of brass, and becomes an ancestor of the children of Lehi.

Lehi in The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry

Nephi · the work’s page · read it where Lehi first appears

Abstract written by a model

Lehi is a prophet, visionary man, faithful to the Lord, husband of Sariah, and father of Laman, Lemuel, Sam, Nephi, Jacob, and Joseph. He dwells in Jerusalem all his days until the Lord warns him to depart because he prophesies of the people’s iniquity, Jerusalem’s destruction, and things he has seen and heard; the people mock and seek to destroy him. After praying with all his heart for his people, he sees a pillar of fire, receives visions and a book, and prophesies. Obeying a dream, he leaves Jerusalem with his family, possessions, and tent, travels into the wilderness, builds an altar, gives thanks, and names the river Laman and valley of Lemuel. He sends his sons back for the plates of brass, rejoices at their return, searches them, learns his genealogy as a descendant of Joseph, and prophesies concerning his seed. His dreams and teachings include the tree, rod of iron, fountain, mists of darkness, large and spacious building, the Jews, Messiah, gospel, Gentiles, and the scattering and gathering of the house of Israel. Though he murmurs during famine and is chastened, he receives revelations about the journey and the ball, leads his family onward, receives the command to go into the ship, and has Jacob and Joseph born in the wilderness. Nephi records his father’s record, genealogy, journeyings, and prophecies.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

10 sections; each heading opens the text

The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry

Lehi is warned by the Lord to depart from Jerusalem because he prophesies concerning the people’s iniquity and they seek to destroy his life. He takes his family into the wilderness.

Chapter 1

Lehi dwelt at Jerusalem and prayed with all his heart for his people. He saw a pillar of fire, received visions and a book, read and prophesied of Jerusalem's destruction, and was mocked and threatened by the Jews.

Chapter 2

Lehi is warned by the Lord in a dream and obeys the command to leave Jerusalem with his family. He travels in the wilderness, builds an altar, offers thanks, names the river Laman and the valley of Lemuel, and speaks with power to Laman and Lemuel.

Chapter 3

Nephi's father tells Nephi that he dreamed the Lord commanded Nephi and his brothers to return to Jerusalem for the records on the plates of brass. He is exceedingly glad when Nephi agrees to obey, and he would perish if he remained in Jerusalem after being commanded to flee.

Chapter 4

Nephi and his brethren intend to return to their father in the wilderness with the plates of brass and Zoram. Zoram is promised a place with them if he goes down in the wilderness to Nephi’s father.

Chapter 5 .. Chapter 8

Lehi rejoices when his sons return and searches the plates of brass. He learns their contents, discovers his genealogy, prophesies concerning his seed, and recounts a dream in which he fears for Laman and Lemuel.

Chapter 9 .. Chapter 12

Lehi teaches his sons about the Jews, the Messiah, the gospel, the Gentiles, and the scattering and gathering of the house of Israel. He had seen the tree, the rod of iron, the fountain, the mists of darkness, and the large and spacious building in a vision.

Chapter 15 .. Chapter 16

Nephi's father receives revelations concerning the journey into the wilderness and the ball. He murmurs during the famine, is chastened by the Lord, and inquires of the Lord after Nephi's exhortation.

Chapter 17 .. Chapter 18

Lehi leads his family from the land of Jerusalem into the wilderness and receives the Lord’s command that they should arise and go down into the ship. He has two sons born in the wilderness, Jacob and Joseph.

Chapter 19

Nephi records his father’s record, genealogy, journeyings in the wilderness, and prophecies on the first plates.

Lehi in The Second Book of Nephi

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Lehi first appears

Abstract written by a model

Lehi is a father and prophet who receives a vision of Jerusalem’s destruction, beholds the Lord’s glory, and prophesies concerning the land of promise and Joseph’s seed. In his final days, he teaches his family about the Lord’s dealings, obedience, rebellion, creation, opposition, the fall, redemption, and choices before men. He urges his sons to unite in righteousness, obey Nephi, and remember the commandments, while blessing Laman’s and Lemuel’s children, Ishmael’s household, Sam, and Zoram. Having chosen the good part and sought their everlasting welfare, he grows old, dies, is buried, and is redeemed from hell.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

The Second Book of Nephi / Chapter 1

Lehi teaches his sons about the Lord’s dealings, the land of promise, obedience, rebellion, and the consequences of iniquity. Near death, he urges them to obey Nephi, unite in righteousness, and remember the Lord’s commandments; he also gives blessings to Laman, Lemuel, Sam, the sons of Ishmael, and Zoram.

Chapter 2

Lehi teaches Jacob and his sons about God, creation, opposition, the fall, redemption, and the choices before men. He says he has chosen the good part and seeks the everlasting welfare of their souls.

Chapter 4

Lehi speaks blessings to the children of Laman, the children of Lemuel, Ishmael’s household, and Sam. He teaches that obedience to the commandments brings prosperity in the land, then grows old, dies, and is buried.
