Abstract written by a model

The government is claimed by Ammoron for the Lamanites, whom he says the Nephites’ fathers robbed. It is later condemned for indifference to national freedom, great slothfulness, neglect, withholding provisions, and failing to strengthen armies; Moroni demands repentance and threatens destruction and opposition.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 54

Ammoron claimed that the government rightly belonged to those whom the Nephites' fathers had robbed of it. He offered peace if the Nephites laid down their arms and submitted to those he said had the rightful government.

Chapter 59

Moroni becomes angry with the government because of its indifference concerning the freedom of their country.

Chapter 60

The government is condemned for slothfulness, neglect, withholding provisions, and failing to send men to strengthen the armies. Moroni warns that the government must repent or face destruction and opposition from him.
