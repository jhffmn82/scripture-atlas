bad pupils

Also called: A bad pupil

Abstract written by a model

Bad pupils lack zeal, prize success, fare, comfort, and anger, and avoid begging or insults. They object, imagine difficulties, oppose superiors, evade errands, and wander as they please. Though instructed, admitted into the order, and nourished, they behave like servants of the king and disperse in all directions.

Twenty-Seventh Lecture.

Bad pupils break down through want of zeal, attach importance to success, fare, comfort, or anger, and may be averse to begging or afraid of insults. They make objections, oppose superiors, avoid errands, act disobediently, and disperse after being instructed, admitted into the order, and nourished.
