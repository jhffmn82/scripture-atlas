Abstract written by a model

Destruction is an imminent, heavy, and certain fate awaiting the Nephites unless they repent. Their murders, fornication, wickedness, oppression, abominations, and rejection of the Lord ripen them for everlasting destruction. Secret oaths, covenants, and bands of robbers help bring it about. It is foretold that their cities and lands will be taken and that they will be destroyed from the earth. It begins through robbers and the sword, grows severe through famine, and includes killings and captivity among Nephites and Lamanites.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 6

The Nephites become ripened for an everlasting destruction because of their wickedness and oppression. The secret oaths and covenants are identified as a means of bringing the people down to destruction.

Chapter 7

The destruction of the Nephites was foretold as the consequence of refusing to repent. Their cities and lands would be taken from them, and they would be destroyed from off the face of the earth.

Chapter 8

The people are ripening because of their murders, fornication, and wickedness for everlasting destruction. Nephi warns that it will come soon unless they repent.

Chapter 11

Destruction is first carried out by the secret band of robbers and by the sword, then becomes severe through famine. Later, the robbers cause great destruction among the Nephites and Lamanites, killing many and taking others captive.

Chapter 13

Destruction is promised to the Nephites and their cities because of wickedness, abominations, and rejection of the Lord. The fourth generation of their enemies will behold and visit their destruction.
