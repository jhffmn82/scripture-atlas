Abstract written by a model

Restoration of the house of Jacob brings its people to knowledge of the covenant and of Jesus Christ, then gathers the children of Israel to their inherited lands; it begins to be fulfilled as these sayings come to the Gentiles.

Chapter 5

The house of Jacob will be restored to knowledge of the covenant made with it. Its people will know Jesus Christ, the Son of God, and be gathered to their own lands.

Chapter 29

Restoration concerns the children of Israel returning to the lands of their inheritance. Its fulfillment is already beginning when these sayings come to the Gentiles.
