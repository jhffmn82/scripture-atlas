Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Mosiah, Abinadi is a prophet who delivers God’s message, teaches salvation through Christ, and is killed by fire for refusing to deny his words. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, Abinadi is a holy prophet who teaches of Jesus Christ and redemption, dies by fire for his belief in God, and whose prophecy about the priests’ seed is fulfilled.

Abinadi in The Book of Mosiah

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Abinadi first appears

Abstract written by a model

Abinadi is a prophet among Noah’s people, commanded by the Lord and empowered by God’s Spirit to deliver a message to the priests and people. He first prophesies that the Lord will visit them in anger unless they repent, and is delivered when they seek his life. He later returns in disguise, condemns their hardened hearts and iniquity, is seized and imprisoned, and boldly rebukes king Noah and the priests. Protected from immediate death, he recites God’s commandments and teaches the law of Moses, salvation, the atonement, the Messiah, resurrection, and redemption through Christ. He testifies that God will come down, suffer, die, break death’s bands, and save his people. Refusing to deny his words or God’s commandments, Abinadi is bound, scourged, and killed by fire. Afterward, Alma teaches his words, which help form Alma’s church, while others mourn Abinadi’s death and remember his warning.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

Abinadi goes among Noah's people and prophesies that the Lord will visit them in anger unless they repent. After the people and Noah seek to take his life, the Lord delivers Abinadi from their hands.

Chapter 12

Abinadi comes among the people in disguise and prophesies against them because they have hardened their hearts and failed to repent. He is seized, brought before king Noah, imprisoned, and questioned by the priests, whom he boldly confounds and rebukes.

Chapter 13

Abinadi finishes his message to king Noah and the people of king Noah after God protects him from being slain. He recites the commandments of God, condemns the people’s iniquities, and teaches about the law of Moses, salvation, the atonement, the Messiah, and the resurrection of the dead.

Chapter 15

Abinadi teaches that God shall come down among the children of men, redeem his people, suffer, die, break the bands of death, and bring salvation. He identifies the seed of the Lord, the first resurrection, and the consequences of wilfully rebelling against God.

Chapter 17

Abinadi refuses to recall the words he has spoken concerning this people and accepts suffering, even death, rather than deny them. He is bound, scourged with faggots, and put to death by fire after prophesying against those who destroy God’s people.

Chapter 18

Alma taught the words of Abinadi concerning what was to come, the resurrection of the dead, and the redemption of the people through Christ.

Chapter 20

Abinadi is remembered as having prophesied against the people because they would not hearken to the Lord's words and turn from their iniquities.

Chapter 21

Abinadi’s words are associated with the formation of Alma’s church of God. Ammon and his brethren mourn his death.

Abinadi in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

Abstract written by a model

Abinadi is a holy prophet who delivers the word of God and speaks of Jesus Christ, redemption, and the priests’ seed. Alma’s father believes his words. Abinadi dies by fire for belief in God; afterward, his words about the priests’ seed are fulfilled.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

Abinadi delivered words that Alma's father believed. He is identified as the source of words concerning Jesus Christ and the redemption of the people.

Chapter 25

Abinadi is identified as the first to suffer death by fire because of his belief in God. His words concerning the seed of the priests who caused his death are fulfilled when they are hunted, scattered, and slain.
