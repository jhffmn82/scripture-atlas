Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Mosiah, the words of Abinadi are a prophecy and testimony against king Noah and his people, warning of affliction, bondage, and the need for repentance; they are preserved by Alma and later fulfilled. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the words of Abinadi concerning the seed of the priests who caused his death by fire are brought to pass when their seed are hunted, scattered, and slain.

words of Abinadi in The Book of Mosiah

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where words of Abinadi first appears

Abstract written by a model

Abinadi’s words are a prophecy and testimony against king Noah and his people. They warn that unless the people repent and turn to the Lord, enemies will bring affliction and bondage, and the Lord will be slow to hear them. The words address their iniquity and the coming of God, are preserved by Alma, and are later fulfilled through suffering and bloodshed.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

Abinadi's prophecy declares that Noah's people must repent and turn to the Lord or be delivered into the hands of their enemies and brought into bondage. It also states that the Lord will be slow to hear their cries unless they repent in sackcloth and ashes and cry mightily to him.

Chapter 17

Abinadi’s words concern the iniquity of this people and the coming of God among the children of men. He refuses to recall them, and Alma writes all the words Abinadi had spoken.

Chapter 20

The words of Abinadi are said to be fulfilled through the people's suffering and the shedding of blood caused by their iniquities.
