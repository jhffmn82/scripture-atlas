prophets of the Lord

group · 3 works · 11 quoted facts · 11 connections
Also called: men inspired from heaven, prophets, the prophets, those who testified of the things pertaining to Christ, those who testified of these things

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Nephi, prophets are cast out, stoned, and slain by the wicked, while those who heed and preserve them will not perish. In The Book of Jarom, prophets warn the people of Nephi that transgression will lead to destruction, teach the law of Moses, testify of the Messiah, and stir the people to repentance. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, prophets of the Lord testify boldly of Christ’s redemption, resurrection, death, and sufferings, and many are unlawfully seized and killed by secret judges.
