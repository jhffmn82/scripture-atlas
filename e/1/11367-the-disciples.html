the disciples

Abstract written by a model

The disciples are twelve ministers chosen by Jesus from among the people to serve this people and the multitude. They are given authority to baptize with water, testify of Jesus because they have seen and known him, teach the scriptures and his words, and minister the gospel sacrament of bread and wine. Jesus identifies them as his disciples and as a light to a people who are a remnant of the house of Joseph, commanding them to keep his commandments and rely on the Father in his name. He gives them power through ordination to give the Holy Ghost. After baptism, they receive the Holy Ghost and fire, are purified through faith, are ministered to by angels and Jesus, and become white as Jesus. They continue praying, teaching, baptizing in Jesus's name, distributing the sacrament, preaching what they have heard and seen, fasting and praying together, writing what they see and hear, and judging according to Jesus's judgment. Their ministry is built upon the gospel and extends to as many as come unto them.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 12

The twelve are chosen from among the people to minister to them and be their servants. They receive power and authority to baptize with water, and those who believe their testimony and are baptized may receive fire, the Holy Ghost, and a remission of sins.

Chapter 13

The twelve are the people Jesus chose to minister unto this people. Jesus tells them to remember his words and not to take thought for food, drink, or clothing.

Chapter 15

Jesus tells the twelve disciples that they are his disciples and a light unto the people who are a remnant of the house of Joseph. He says they have heard his voice and seen him, and commands them to keep his commandments.

Chapter 18

The disciples bring bread and wine, administer them to the multitude, and are commanded to continue doing so in remembrance of Jesus. Jesus touches them and gives them power to give the Holy Ghost, and they bear record that he ascended into heaven.

Chapter 19

The disciples teach the multitude, pray, and minister unto the people. They are baptized, receive the Holy Ghost and fire, are ministered to by angels and Jesus, become white as Jesus, and continue praying steadfastly unto him.

Chapter 20

The disciples are commanded to pray in their hearts, arise, eat bread, and drink wine. They are also commanded to break bread and give it, and to give wine, to the multitude.

Chapter 26

The disciples whom Jesus had chosen began to baptize and teach as many as came unto them.

Chapter 27

The disciples of Jesus journey, preach what they have heard and seen, baptize in the name of Jesus, and unite in prayer and fasting. Jesus commands them to write what they see and hear and to judge the people according to his judgment.
