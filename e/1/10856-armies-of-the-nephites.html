armies of the Nephites in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where armies of the Nephites first appears

Abstract written by a model

The armies of the Nephites, composed of Nephites and commanded by Moroni, defend the lands of Jershon and Zarahemla. Equipped with protective armor, thick clothing, shields, swords, cimeters, and other weapons, they fight, drive, scatter, defeat, pursue, and surround Lamanite forces. Moroni’s army grows through liberated Nephite prisoners, refugees from Nephihah, additional men under the standard of liberty, and union with Pahoran’s forces. It captures city Gid after surrounding its Lamanite forces, captures Nephihah by lowering men over its wall, and ultimately pursues and defeats the Lamanites.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 28

The armies of the Nephites were set round about the land of Jershon and in all the borders around the land of Zarahemla. They fought the Lamanites and drove and scattered them.

Chapter 43

The Nephite armies are equipped with breastplates, arm-shields, head-plates, shields, thick clothing, swords, cimeters, and other weapons. Under Moroni and Lehi, they defeat, pursue, and surround the Lamanite armies.

Chapter 55

Moroni’s army is prepared with weapons of war and surrounds the Lamanite forces outside city Gid. It gains strength when the liberated Nephite prisoners join it.

Chapter 59

The remainder of the people of Nephihah flee and join the army of Moroni.

Chapter 62

Moroni's army gathered additional men under the standard of liberty, united with Pahoran's forces, and became stronger than the men of Pachus. It captured Nephihah by lowering men over the wall with cords and ladders and later pursued and defeated the Lamanites.
