Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Omni, they were discovered by Mosiah’s people, rejoiced at their arrival, came from Jerusalem in Zedekiah’s time, crossed the great waters, became numerous, suffered wars, lost their language and records, and united with Mosiah’s people. In The Book of Mosiah, they were descendants of Mulek who dwelt in the land, were more numerous than the descendants of Nephi, were numbered with the Nephites, and gathered with them at the temple to hear King Benjamin’s words.
