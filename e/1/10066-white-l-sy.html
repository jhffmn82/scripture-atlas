white Lêsyâ

Abstract written by a model

White Lêsyâ is a good Lêsyâ, conch-shell-colored and infinitely sweeter than dates, grapes, milk, and sugars. It develops in a calm, self-controlled, meditative, sense-subduing man who meditates on Law and truth and practises Samitis and Guptis, bringing the soul into happy courses of life.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Thirty-Fourth Lecture.

The white Lêsyâ has the colour of a conch-shell, the aṅka-stone, Kunda-flowers, flowing milk, silver, or a necklace of pearls. It develops in a person who meditates on the Law and truth, practises the Samitis and Guptis, and subdues the senses; it is one of the good Lêsyâs and brings the soul into happy courses of life.
