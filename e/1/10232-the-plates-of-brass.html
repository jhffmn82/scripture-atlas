Abstract written by a model

The plates of brass are records of great worth to Lehi’s family. They contain the genealogy of Nephi’s and Lehi’s fathers; the language of Nephi’s fathers; the law of Moses and the five books of Moses; the creation of the world; a record of the Jews from the beginning to the reign of Zedekiah; covenants of the Lord; and writings and prophecies of holy prophets, including Isaiah and Jeremiah. Nephi’s father commands his sons to obtain them so their language and the prophets’ words can be preserved for their children. Nephi obtains the plates from Laban and carries them out of Jerusalem. They testify that prophecies are true and that people must obey God’s commandments. Lehi prophesies they will go to his seed among all nations, kindreds, tongues, and people, and never perish or be dimmed by time.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The records on the plates of brass contain the genealogy of Nephi's father and the words spoken by the holy prophets through the Spirit and power of God. Nephi's father commands his sons to obtain them so they can preserve their language and the prophets' words for their children.

Chapter 4

The plates of brass contain the engravings of the law of Moses. Nephi understands that the Lord delivered Laban into his hands so he could obtain the plates, and he carries them out of Jerusalem to his brethren.

Chapter 5 .. Chapter 8

The plates of brass contain the five books of Moses, a record of the Jews, the prophecies of holy prophets including Jeremiah, and Lehi’s genealogy. Lehi prophesies that they will go forth to Lehi’s seed and never perish or be dimmed by time.

Chapter 19

The plates of brass contain writings about the Lord’s dealings in other lands and among people of old, including the books of Moses and the writings of Isaiah.

Chapter 22

The plates of brass contain writings that testify that the prophecies are true and that people must be obedient to the commandments of God.
