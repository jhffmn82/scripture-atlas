The Road of Penance

Abstract written by a model

The Road of Penance, taught by the Ginas, depends on right knowledge, faith, conduct, and austerities; it leads beings to beatitude by teaching monks to stop bad Karman influx and annihilate accumulated Karman.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Twenty-Eighth Lecture.

The road to final deliverance is taught by the Ginas and depends on four causes: right knowledge, faith, conduct, and austerities. Beings who follow it obtain beatitude.

Thirtieth Lecture.

The Road of Penance teaches the practices by which a monk stops the influx of bad Karman and annihilates Karman acquired in millions of births.
