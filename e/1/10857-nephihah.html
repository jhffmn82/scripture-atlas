Nephihah

Abstract written by a model

Nephihah, a wise elder of the church, is appointed by Alma as the second chief judge through the people’s voice. He governs from the judgment-seat with power to enact and enforce laws, serves with perfect uprightness before God, and is Pahoran’s father.

Nephihah is in one work on this site, The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 4

Nephihah was a wise man among the elders of the church. Alma gave him power according to the voice of the people, and he was appointed chief judge to sit in the judgment-seat and govern the people.
