Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Mosiah, Alma's conversion began with an angel confronting his persecution, led to repentance, spiritual rebirth and redemption, and ended with him teaching others. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, Alma’s conversion began when an angel stopped his destruction of God’s church; after three days of suffering, he called on Jesus Christ, received joy and light, was born of God, and brought souls to repentance.
