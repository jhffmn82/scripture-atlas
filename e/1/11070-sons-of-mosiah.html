Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Mosiah, the sons of Mosiah are repentant former unbelievers who repair injuries to the church, publish truth, decline the kingdom, and preach the word of God among the Lamanites. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the sons of Mosiah are missionaries who teach the word of God among the Lamanites for fourteen years despite afflictions.

sons of Mosiah in The Book of Mosiah

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where sons of Mosiah first appears

Abstract written by a model

The sons of Mosiah begin as unbelievers who secretly seek to destroy the church with Alma (son). After an angelic appearance, they suffer anguish for their iniquities, confess, repair injuries to the church, teach God’s commandments, and publish truth and good tidings, bringing many to knowledge of truth. They desire salvation for every creature, decline the kingdom, and ask king Mosiah to let them preach the word of God among the Lamanites. With selected companions, they go to the land of Nephi, hoping the Lamanites abandon hatred, rejoice in the Lord, and become friendly with the Nephites.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 27

The sons of Mosiah were numbered among the unbelievers and secretly sought to destroy the church with Alma (son). After the angelic appearance, they confessed their sins, repaired injuries done to the church, preached the word of God, and brought many to the knowledge of the truth.

Chapter 28

The sons of Mosiah ask their father to let them go with selected companions to the land of Nephi to preach the word of God to the Lamanites. They desire the salvation of every creature and hope the Lamanites will abandon their hatred, rejoice in the Lord, and become friendly with the Nephites. The Lord promises to deliver them from the Lamanites, and king Mosiah grants their request.

sons of Mosiah in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where sons of Mosiah first appears

Abstract written by a model

The sons of Mosiah are a group who once went with Alma seeking to destroy the church of God until a holy angel stopped them. They gained strong knowledge and understanding through scripture study, prayer, and fasting, receiving prophecy and revelation. Refusing the kingdom Mosiah wished to confer, they left Zarahemla and taught the word of God among the Lamanites for fourteen years despite afflictions.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 17

The sons of Mosiah have searched the scriptures, prayed, fasted, and taught with power and authority of God. They refused the kingdom, departed from the land of Zarahemla, and spent fourteen years teaching among the Lamanites despite many afflictions.

Chapter 36

The sons of Mosiah went with Alma seeking to destroy the church of God. A holy angel stopped them by the way.
