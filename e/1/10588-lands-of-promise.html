Abstract written by a model

The lands of promise are choice, precious lands of inheritance, consecrated forever to Jacob’s children and Joseph’s seed. They are lands of liberty for the Gentiles and fortified by God. Security depends on keeping commandments; believing Jews are restored, and people return from the ends of the earth.
