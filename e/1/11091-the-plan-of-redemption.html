the plan of redemption

topic · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: plan of redemption, plan of salvation, the great plan of salvation, the plan of salvation

Abstract written by a model

The plan of redemption is prepared from the foundation of the world through Christ. It includes the coming of Christ and is taught by Ammon and Aaron to king Lamoni, his servants, and all who believe on Christ’s name. God makes the plan of salvation known through mercy and angels, and it is to be declared to every soul, including the Zoramites and their children, so they may repent and come unto God. It depends on a space for repentance; it would have been frustrated if Adam had partaken of the tree of life and lived forever before repentance.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 18

The plan of redemption was prepared from the foundation of the world. Ammon expounds it to king Lamoni and his servants and teaches them about the coming of Christ and the works of the Lord.

Chapter 22

Aaron teaches that the plan of redemption was prepared from the foundation of the world through Christ for all who believe on his name.

Chapter 24

God makes the plan of salvation known to the people of Anti-Nephi-Lehi and to future generations through his mercy and visits by angels.

Chapter 29

The speaker wants to declare the plan of redemption to every soul so that people will repent and come unto God.

Chapter 39

The plan of redemption is to be made known to the people as well as to their children. It can be declared beforehand because souls are as precious to God before Christ's coming as afterward.

Chapter 42

The plan of salvation would have been frustrated if Adam had lived forever without a space for repentance. It concerns the redemption and salvation made possible through God’s purposes.
