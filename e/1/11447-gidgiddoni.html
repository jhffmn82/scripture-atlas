Gidgiddoni

person · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 4 connections

Abstract written by a model

Gidgiddoni is a Nephite chief captain, army commander, great prophet, chief judge, and leader. Appointed chief commander, he counsels preparation against robbers. He commands pursuit of Giddianhi’s forces, blocks the robbers’ retreat, and causes them to be cut off. With judge Lachoneus and other leaders, he establishes great peace in the land.

Gidgiddoni is in one work on this site, The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Gidgiddoni is appointed chief commander of the Nephite armies and is described as a great prophet and the chief judge. He counsels the people to prepare in the center of their lands and wait for the robbers to attack.

Chapter 4

Gidgiddoni commanded the Nephite armies to pursue Giddianhi’s forces and slay those who fell into their hands. He later learned of the robbers’ retreat, placed armies in their path, and caused their forces to be cut off.

Chapter 6

Gidgiddoni, judge Lachoneus, and other appointed leaders established great peace in the land.
