The forty-three people traveled through the wilderness for many days while seeking the land of Zarahemla. They were lost but remained diligent and eventually returned to Limhi’s land.

The priests of king Noah tarry in the wilderness after fleeing from Nephi. They discover the daughters of the Lamanites there and take them away.
