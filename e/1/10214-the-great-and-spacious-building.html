the great and spacious building

place · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: building, great and spacious building, large and spacious building, strange building

Abstract written by a model

A building high above the earth, filled with finely dressed people who mock those partaking of the fruit. It is the pride of the world and the vain imaginations and pride of the children of men; its mocking causes shame and falling away, before it falls in exceedingly great destruction.
