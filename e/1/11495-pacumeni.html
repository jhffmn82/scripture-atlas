Pacumeni

person · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections

Abstract written by a model

Son of Pahoran, Pacumeni fails to obtain the judgment-seat, joins the people of Nephi, becomes chief judge after Pahoran’s death, and is killed by Coriantumr at Zarahemla’s wall.

Pacumeni is in one work on this site, The Book of Helaman. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
