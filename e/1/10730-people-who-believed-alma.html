Abstract written by a model

The people who believed Alma gather at Mormon, about two hundred and four souls, to hear him. They are baptized in the waters of Mormon, filled with grace, and covenant to serve God and keep his commandments. Called the church of God and church of Christ, they support one another and leave with Alma into the wilderness. Under Alma as high priest, the church admonishes sin, receives repentant believers through baptism, rejects those who will not hear the Lord’s voice, and gains peace, prosperity, and many baptized people.

Chapter 18

The people who believed Alma went to Mormon to hear his teachings. They were baptized, became members of the church, covenanted to serve God and keep his commandments, supported one another, and departed into the wilderness with Alma.

Chapter 26

The church admonishes members who commit sin and brings them before the priests and Alma. Alma regulates its affairs according to the word of the Lord; the church then has peace, prospers, receives many, and baptizes many.
