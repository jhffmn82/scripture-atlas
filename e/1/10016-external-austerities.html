external austerities

Also called: austerities, external, internal austerities, two kinds of austerities

Abstract written by a model

External austerities are practices including fasting and abstinence, one of two austerity kinds; faithfully performing both kinds leads to release.

Thirtieth Lecture.

Austerities are of two kinds, external and internal, with six kinds in each group. Faithfully performing both kinds of austerities leads to release from the Circle of Births.
