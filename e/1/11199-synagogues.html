Abstract written by a model

Synagogues are places for worship of God, built by the poor in heart and used by Zoramites on the day of the Lord. They contain a raised Rameumptom for standing and praying, yet poor people are cast out because of apparel and poverty.

Chapter 31

The Zoramites had built synagogues and gathered in them on one day of the week called the day of the Lord. Their synagogues contained a high place for standing and praying.

Chapter 32

The synagogues are places where the people seek to worship God. The poor are cast out of them because of their apparel and poverty.
