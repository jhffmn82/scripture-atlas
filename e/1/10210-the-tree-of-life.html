the tree of life

Abstract written by a model

The tree of life stands in a large and spacious field, bearing fruit desirable to make one happy. Lehi partakes and wants his family to partake. Shown to Nephi as beautiful and white beyond comparison, it represents the love of God and connects with the fountain of living waters. Its fruit is most precious and the greatest of God’s gifts.
