Abstract written by a model

Corihor, son of Kib, rebels, settles in Nehor, draws followers, and captures Moron’s king. Shule defeats him and restores Kib. Corihor later repents, receives power in Shule’s kingdom, and has exceedingly fair sons and daughters, including Noah.
