colour

Also called: 'Colour', 'colours

Abstract written by a model

Colour is perceived by and attracts the eye. Pleasant colour causes love and unpleasant colour hatred; passionate desire or hatred brings ruin, pain, violence, dissatisfaction, greed, and misery, while dispassionate indifference frees a person from sorrow. As an aspect of development, it has five kinds: black, blue, red, yellow, and white.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Thirty-Second Lecture.

Colour attracts the eye and becomes a cause of love when pleasant and hatred when unpleasant. Passion for colour leads to ruin, violence, dissatisfaction, greed, and misery, whereas indifference to colour frees a person from sorrow.

Thirty-Sixth Lecture.

Colour is one of the five aspects of development. Things which develop with regard to colour are of five kinds: black, blue, red, yellow, and white.
