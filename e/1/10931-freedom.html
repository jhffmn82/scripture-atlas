freedom

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: a true spirit of freedom, freedom of their country, that liberty wherewith God hath made them free, the cause of our freedom, the welfare and the freedom of this people

Abstract written by a model

Freedom is the country’s cause and a privilege tied to church, Redeemer, and God. Government indifference neglects it, angering Moroni, who defends freedom and welfare over power or honor. People are urged to resist rebellion and defend it with arms against bondage; those who deny or fight it are executed according to law.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 59

Moroni is angry with the government because of its indifference concerning the freedom of their country.

Chapter 60

Freedom is the cause Moroni defends and the government is urged to support. Moroni says he seeks the freedom and welfare of his country rather than power or worldly honor.

Chapter 61

The people are urged to defend their country and freedom and to resist rebellions and dissensions. Pahoran describes freedom as a privilege connected with their church and the cause of their Redeemer and God.

Chapter 62

The people took up arms to defend their freedom and avoid bondage. Those who denied their freedom or fought against it were executed according to the law.
