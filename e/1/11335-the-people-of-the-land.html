the people of the land

group · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: all the people, all the people of the land, the people, the people who were spared alive

Abstract written by a model

The people earnestly seek a sign amid doubtings and disputations. Following destruction and darkness, they continually mourn, howl, and weep, with some regretting they had not repented. They later hear and witness the sayings and voice, experience silence, and again weep and howl for lost kindred and friends.

Chapter 8

The people looked earnestly for the sign given by prophet Samuel, the Lamanite, but doubtings and disputations arose among them. After the destruction and darkness, they mourned, howled, and wept continually, and some cried that they should have repented.

Chapter 10

They hear and witness the sayings and the voice. After hearing the voice, they weep and howl again because of the loss of their kindred and friends.
