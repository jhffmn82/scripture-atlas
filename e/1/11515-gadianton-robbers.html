Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Helaman, Gadianton’s robbers and murderers use secret signs and words, commit murders, infiltrate judgment-seats, and pursue wicked rule. In The Book of Helaman, the secret band of robbers is formed by Kishkumen and Gadianton, spreads through Nephite society, gains governmental control, oppresses the people, and is eventually destroyed. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the Gadianton robbers form a secret society that commits violence, threatens the Nephites, lays siege, and is defeated, captured, or slain. In The Fourth Book of Nephi, Who Is the Son of Nephi, One of the Disciples of Jesus Christ, the robbers of Gadianton spread throughout the land and accumulate gold and silver through trade. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon’s Own Record, the Gadianton robbers infest the land, contribute to wickedness and destruction, fight the Nephites, and later join the Lamanites in dividing the lands of inheritance. (The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record)

Gadianton’s robbers and murderers in The Book of Helaman

The Book of Mormon · the work’s page · read it where Gadianton’s robbers and murderers first appears

Abstract written by a model

Gadianton’s robbers and murderers are a band of robbers and murderers using secret signs and words to identify covenant brothers. They murder Cezoram and his son in the judgment-seat without being found. Though the Lamanites hunt and destroy the band among themselves, the Nephites build it up; it fills judgment-seats, usurps authority, abandons God’s commandments, and pursues gain, glory, and wicked rule.

secret band of robbers in The Book of Helaman

The Book of Mormon · the work’s page · read it where secret band of robbers first appears

Abstract written by a model

The secret band of robbers is a combination of murderers and plunderers, formed by Kishkumen and Gadianton, bound by secrecy, covenants, signs, and words to conceal wickedness and seek murder, robbery, and power. After Kishkumen is killed, it flees into the wilderness. It spreads among the Nephites and the more wicked Lamanites, gains governmental control, and oppresses the poor and humble. Its wickedness brings destruction and wars, until the people sweep it away, make it extinct, and hide its secret plans in the earth.

Gadianton robbers in The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman

Mormon · the work’s page · read it where Gadianton robbers first appears

Abstract written by a model

The Gadianton robbers are a numerous secret society of robbers who dwell in mountain strongholds and secret places, infest the land, and commit murders, slaughter, death, and carnage. They grow as dissenters and some Lamanites join them, lay waste to cities, and gain advantages through wickedness and divisions among the people of Nephi. Led by Giddianhi, with armies organized in secret places, they threaten the Nephites with utter destruction unless they surrender and join them. They occupy deserted Nephite lands, but their dependence on wilderness food leaves them without provisions and wild game during a siege; they can survive only by plundering, robbing, and murdering. Their armies are defeated with heavy losses and hunger. Most are captured, while the remainder are slain. Prisoners are taught the word of God; those who repent and covenant to murder no more are released to keep the peace, while others are condemned and punished.

5 sections; each heading opens the text

The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman / Chapter 1

The Gadianton robbers dwell in the mountains and infest the land. Their strongholds and secret places prevent the people from overpowering them, and they commit many murders and much slaughter; they increase as dissenters and some Lamanites join them.

Chapter 2

The Gadianton robbers become numerous, slay many people, lay waste to many cities, and spread death and carnage throughout the land. The Nephites and converted Lamanites take up arms against them; the robbers are driven into the mountains and secret places, but later gain many advantages because of the wickedness and divisions among the people of Nephi.

Chapter 3

The Gadianton robbers threaten to attack and destroy the Nephites unless they surrender and join the robbers. They are led by Giddianhi and prepare to come down against the Nephites.

Chapter 4

The robbers occupied deserted Nephite lands but lacked food and could not subsist except by plundering, robbing, and murdering. Their armies were defeated, suffered heavy losses and hunger, and many were captured while the remainder were slain.

Chapter 5

The robbers are taken prisoner, except those who are slain. Their prisoners are taught the word of God; those who repent and covenant not to murder are freed, while others are condemned and punished.
