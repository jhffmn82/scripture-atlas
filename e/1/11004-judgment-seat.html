judgment-seat

object · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: judged, judgment, seat, the judgment-seat

Abstract written by a model

The judgment-seat is the office held by the chief judge. Alma delivers it to Nephihah, who judges and governs until his death; his son Pahoran then fills it. Pahoran keeps it after the king-men fail to remove him, though rebels led by Pachus take it. Pahoran seeks it only to preserve his people’s rights and liberty, is restored after Pachus is slain, and returns after the wars.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

Alma delivered the judgment-seat to Nephihah at the commencement of the ninth year. Nephihah used it to judge and govern the people.

Chapter 50

The judgment-seat is filled by Nephihah until his death and then by his son Pahoran, who begins his reign at the end of the twenty and fourth year.

Chapter 51

Pahoran retains the judgment-seat after the people favor the freemen, while the king-men's effort to remove him fails.

Chapter 61

The judgment-seat is the position the rebels seek to take from Pahoran. Pahoran says he seeks to retain it only to preserve the rights and liberty of his people.

Chapter 62

The judgment-seat was taken from Pahoran by Pachus and his men. After Pachus was slain, Pahoran was restored to the judgment-seat and later returned to it after the wars.
