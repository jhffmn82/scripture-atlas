Chapter 28

The change wrought upon the three disciples prevents them from suffering pain or sorrow except for the sins of the world and keeps Satan from having power to tempt them. It also makes them holy and prevents the powers of the earth from holding them, until they receive a greater change at the judgment day of Christ.
