Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, land Desolation is the land northward of Bountiful, bordering it from east sea to west sea, near Hagoth’s shipbuilding place, reaching a far northern land once peopled, destroyed, and marked by discovered bones. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record, land Desolation contains the city of Desolation, where Mormon gathers Nephites for defense; their armies depart, are driven back, flee Lamanite attacks, and face impending overthrow.
