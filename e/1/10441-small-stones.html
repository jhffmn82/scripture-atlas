small stones

Also called: sixteen small stones, stones, these stones

Abstract written by a model

Sixteen small, white and clear stones are made from a rock for light in vessels or barges. The finger of the Lord touches them one by one so they shine. The brother of Jared places one in each end, where they give continual light in darkness above or under the water.

Chapter 3

The brother of Jared makes sixteen small stones from a rock. The Lord touches them one by one so they may shine in the vessels and provide light while the people cross the sea.

Chapter 6

The brother of Jared carries the prepared stones into the vessels or barges, placing one in each end. The stones shine in darkness and give continual light above or under the water.
