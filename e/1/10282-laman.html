Laman in The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry

Nephi · the work’s page · read it where Laman first appears

Abstract written by a model

Laman is Lehi’s elder son, elder brother of Nephi and brother of Lemuel, described as a mighty man who commands fifty men. Compared to the continually running river Laman, he repeatedly murmurs, refuses Nephi’s words, and risks being cut off from the Lord’s presence. Chosen to seek records from Laban, he is rejected and threatened; angered, he smites Nephi and doubts deliverance. Later he flees from Nephi disguised as Laban, rebels while traveling, desires Jerusalem, and does not partake of the fruit in Lehi’s dream. During the voyage he helps bind Nephi, then repents and helps free him when a storm threatens destruction.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Laman is Lehi's elder son and is compared to the river Laman, which continually runs into the fountain of all righteousness. He murmurs against Lehi, refuses to hearken unto Nephi's words, and is included among those who will be cut off from the presence of the Lord if he rebels.

Chapter 3

Laman is chosen by lot to enter Laban's house and request the records, but Laban rejects him, calls him a robber, and threatens to slay him. Laman later becomes angry with Nephi and smites him with a rod, then questions how the Lord could deliver Laban into their hands.

Chapter 4

Laman initially follows Nephi to Jerusalem but later flees when he sees Nephi dressed as Laban, believing that Laban has killed Nephi and is seeking their lives. He stops fleeing when Nephi calls after him.

Chapter 5 .. Chapter 8

Laman rebels with Lemuel and others while traveling with Ishmael’s family and desires to return to Jerusalem. In Lehi’s dream, he does not partake of the fruit, and Lehi fears that he may be cast off from the Lord’s presence.

Chapter 17 .. Chapter 18

Laman murmurs against Nephi and helps bind him with cords during the voyage. When the storm threatens destruction, he repents and helps loose Nephi.
