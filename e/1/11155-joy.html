Abstract written by a model

Chapter 26

Ammon's joy is full because many Lamanites have been brought to repentance and into God's fold. He says his joy carries him to boast in God and gives him reason to praise and thank God forever.

Chapter 29

The speaker’s joy comes from being an instrument in God’s hands, seeing people repent, and witnessing the success of his brethren. His joy becomes so great that his soul is carried away as it were from the body.
