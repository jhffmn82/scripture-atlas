captivity

Abstract written by a model

Captivity is a recurring condition prophesied by the brother of Jared when a king is taken captive. Kib, Kim, several descendants, Seth, Moron, and Coriantor remain captive for life or into old age. Shule is captured but freed by his sons; Levi serves forty-two years before gaining the kingdom.

Chapter 7

Kib dwells in captivity under Corihor until he becomes exceedingly old. Noah takes Shule captive and carries him into Moron, but Shule’s sons break open the prison and bring him out.

Chapter 10

Kim was brought into captivity by his brother and remained there all his days. His descendants, including Levi’s father, Heth, Aaron, Amnigaddah, and Coriantum, also lived in captivity, while Levi served in captivity for forty-two years before obtaining the kingdom.

Chapter 11

Seth and Moron dwelt in captivity for all their days, and Coriantor also dwelt in captivity all his days.
