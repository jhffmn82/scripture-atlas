Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Nephi, Lucifer seeks to ascend above God and rule from the mount of the congregation, weakens nations, destroys worlds and cities, falls, is brought to the pit, and is cast out as an abominable branch. In The Second Book of Nephi, the man (Lucifer) is considered by those who see him as the man who made the earth tremble, shook kingdoms, made the world a wilderness, destroyed cities, and did not open the house of his prisoners.
