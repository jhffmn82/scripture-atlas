redemption of the world

Also called: a redemption, the redemption of man

Abstract written by a model

The redemption of the world comes through Jesus Christ. It brings all men into the presence of the Lord through resurrection, redeeming them from temporal death. At judgment day, those found guiltless receive the presence and kingdom of God, dwell there, and sing ceaseless praises.
