Abstract written by a model

The fruit of the tree is sweet, white, and desirable above all other fruit. It is the love of God, filling the soul with exceedingly great joy. Lehi tastes it, and Nephi is shown the tree; those who partake rejoice, though some become ashamed when mocked.
