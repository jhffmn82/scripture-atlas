Limhi

person · 1 work · 19 quoted facts · 12 connections
Also called: king, king Limhi, my, the king

Abstract written by a model

Limhi is a just king, son of Noah, made king by the voice of the people and ruling the people in the land of Nephi and Shilom. After captivity and his father’s iniquities, the people confer the kingdom on him; he establishes peace, defends his people when the Lamanites prepare for war, refuses to kill their captured king, and seeks the truth about the abduction. He returns to the city of Nephi, permits battle, supports widows and children, and sends men to search for Zarahemla. When Ammon arrives, Limhi learns that their brethren in Zarahemla are alive, tells his people, receives their history, and presents plates containing his people’s record for translation. He encourages trust in God, covenants to serve God and keep his commandments, and desires baptism. Under an oath with the king of the Lamanites, he consults Ammon and his people about deliverance, accepts Gideon’s plan, sends wine as a present, and leads his people away by night. Alma later baptizes him into the church of God.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

Limhi was the son of Noah and grandson of Zeniff, and he had been made king by the voice of the people. He questioned Ammon, learned that the people of Zarahemla were alive, and encouraged his people to trust in God for deliverance.

Chapter 8

King Limhi tells his people about their brethren in the land of Zarahemla and has Ammon recount their history. He presents the plates containing the record of his people to Ammon and asks about translating the records.

Chapter 19

Limhi, a son of king Noah, is taken captive with the people and desires that his father not be destroyed, although he knows his father’s iniquities. The people confer the kingdom upon him, and he establishes the kingdom and peace among his people.

Chapter 20

Limhi discovers the Lamanites' preparations for war and leads his people in defending themselves. He refuses to kill the captured king, searches for the truth, and explains the abduction to him.

Chapter 21

Limhi returns with his people to the city of Nephi and grants their request to battle the Lamanites. He later commands support for the widows and their children, sends men to search for Zarahemla, welcomes Ammon and his brethren, and enters into a covenant with God while desiring baptism.

Chapter 22

king Limhi consults with the people about delivering themselves from bondage and listens to Gideon's plan. He sends wine and more wine as a present to the Lamanites, then leads his people in departing by night.

Chapter 25

King Limhi desired to be baptized, and Alma baptized him.
