Abstract written by a model

Moronihah, a great city, was covered by earth and replaced by a great mountain after the earth was carried up upon it, prompting people’s later grief and regret over unrepented iniquities and abominations.

Chapter 8

The earth was carried up upon the city of Moronihah, and a great mountain occupied the place where the city had been. The people later cried that their mothers, fair daughters, and children would have been spared if they had repented.
