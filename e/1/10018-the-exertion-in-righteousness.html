The Exertion in Righteousness

topic · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: exertion in righteousness

Abstract written by a model

Mahâvîra’s lecture teaches righteous exertion: longing for liberation, disregard of worldly objects, desire of the Law, obedience, and pure meditation, through which exertion destroys Karman and brings perfection, enlightenment, deliverance, and final beatitude.

The Exertion in Righteousness is in one work on this site, Uttaradhyayana Sutra. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
