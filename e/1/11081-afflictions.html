Abstract written by a model

Afflictions are sufferings such as travel, privation, rejection, mocking, beatings, imprisonment, trials, and troubles. Ammon and his brethren endure them while preaching to save souls. People are exhorted to bear all afflictions patiently, with firm hope and without reviling others; through trust in God, they can be overcome and bring deliverance and lifting up at the last day.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 26

Ammon and his brethren endured travel, privation, rejection, mocking, spitting, beatings, stoning, binding, imprisonment, and other afflictions while preaching. They bore these sufferings patiently in hopes of saving souls.

Chapter 34

The people are exhorted to bear all manner of afflictions with patience and a firm hope that they shall one day rest from them. They are warned not to revile against those who cast them out because of their poverty.

Chapter 38

Trials, troubles, and afflictions can be endured and overcome through trust in God. Those who trust in God will be delivered from them and lifted up at the last day.
