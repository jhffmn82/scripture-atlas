Abstract written by a model

The Book of Mormon record contains Mormon’s account of what he saw, heard, and observed concerning the people. Mormon made it, Moroni finished it, and it was hidden in the earth. Though imperfect in language and manner of speech, it is of great worth, written in reformed Egyptian, and will come to light by God’s power to bring knowledge of Christ and greater things to those who receive it without condemnation.

The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record / Chapter 1

The Book of Mormon record contains Mormon's account of the things he has seen and heard and his observations concerning the people.

Chapter 8

The record was made by Mormon, finished by Moroni, and hidden in the earth. It is of great worth and will come to light by the power of God, bringing greater knowledge to those who receive it without condemning its imperfections.

Chapter 9

The record was written according to the writers’ knowledge and contains imperfections because of their language and manner of speech. It was written to rid their garments of the blood of their brethren and to help restore them to the knowledge of Christ.
