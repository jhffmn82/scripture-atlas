Abstract written by a model

Jacob became king of a wicked band, opposed prophets who testified of Jesus, and ordered the band to flee north because enemies were more numerous. He was an ancestor of the house of Israel and later testified concerning a remnant of the seed of Joseph.

Jacob is in one work on this site, The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
