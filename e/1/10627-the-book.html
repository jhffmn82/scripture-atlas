Abstract written by a model

A book containing words of those who have slumbered in the dust and God’s revelation from the world’s beginning to its ending. Sealed by God and hidden from the world, it is delivered to a man, comes forth for the Gentiles, is believed by many, carried to Nephi’s seed, then sealed again unto the Lord.

Chapter 27

The book contains words of those who have slumbered in the dust and a revelation from God from the beginning of the world to its ending. It is delivered to a man, hidden from the eyes of the world, and later sealed and hidden again.

Chapter 30

The book of which Nephi has spoken will come forth, be written unto the Gentiles, and be sealed up again unto the Lord. Many will believe its words and carry them to the remnant of Nephi’s seed.
