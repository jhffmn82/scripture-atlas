Abstract written by a model

The title of liberty is a standard made from a rent part of Moroni’s coat, bearing words concerning God, religion, freedom, peace, wives, and children. Moroni raises it before the people; it is then hoisted across Nephite towers, including by king-men compelled to defend their country.

Chapter 46

The title of liberty is made from a rent part of Moroni’s coat and bears the words concerning God, religion, freedom, peace, wives, and children. Moroni raises it before the people, and later causes it to be hoisted upon every tower in the Nephite land.

Chapter 51

The remaining king-men are compelled to hoist the title of liberty upon their towers and in their cities and to take up arms in defense of their country.
