Abstract written by a model

The promised land is a land of promise and inheritance, choice above all other lands, reached by Lehi’s family after crossing the large waters. Covenanted to the seed of Nephi’s father, it is later obtained by Gentiles who prosper there; those who hearken to the Lamb may be blessed upon it forever. On arrival, the family settles, plants seeds, and finds abundance.

Chapter 13 .. Chapter 14

The promised land is the land of inheritance covenanted by the Lord God to the seed of Nephi's father. The Gentiles who go forth out of captivity prosper and obtain it, and those who hearken to the Lamb may be blessed upon it forever.

Chapter 17 .. Chapter 18

The family arrives at the promised land after Nephi guides the ship there. They pitch their tents, plant the seeds brought from the land of Jerusalem, receive abundant growth, and find many animals and ores.
