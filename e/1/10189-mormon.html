Also called: I, Mormon's father, me, my, my father, my father Mormon, my father’s name was Mormon

Abstract written by a model

In The Words of Mormon, Mormon prepares to deliver his record to Moroni and includes the plates containing the account from Jacob to King Benjamin. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, Mormon records Jesus Christ’s teachings and testifies of the three disciples. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record, Mormon serves as a record keeper, commander, witness, and leader of the Nephites. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record, Mormon makes a record, commands Moroni to finish it, and is killed with their kinsfolk in battle. In The Book of Moroni, Mormon teaches faith, hope, charity, and repentance as a prophet and Moroni’s father.

Abstract written by a model

Mormon is a disciple of Jesus Christ, a pure descendant of Lehi, writer, and record keeper called to declare his word. Named after the land of Mormon, he makes a record on plates with his own hands, drawing on earlier accounts and what he has seen. He writes a lesser part of Jesus’s teachings, ends those sayings, and later pauses his account, withholding the three disciples’ names as forbidden while testifying that he has seen them minister among all nations.

Mormon in The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record

Mormon · the work’s page · read it where Mormon first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mormon is a Nephite, descendant of Nephi, record keeper, commander, and witness of things seen and heard by the Spirit. Visited by the Lord at fifteen and knowing the goodness of Jesus through personal experience, he is forbidden to preach because of his people’s rebellion. Young and large in stature, he is appointed to lead the Nephite armies and repeatedly leads them in battle while recording their wickedness, abominations, and destruction. He loves the Nephites with all his heart and continually prays for them, yet loses hope because he knows the Lord’s judgments. He refuses for a time to command them because of their wickedness, standing as an idle witness, then returns to lead them after repenting of his oath. Following Ammaron’s words, he takes the plates from hill Shim, makes records, and writes a small abridgment for the seed of Jacob, Gentiles, and all the ends of the earth. As the Lamanites overthrow the land, he gathers the Nephites at Cumorah, hides the entrusted records, gives plates to Moroni, leads his ten thousand in battle, survives wounded, and mourns the slain.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record / Chapter 1

Mormon is a descendant of Nephi whose father is named Mormon. He records his observations, is visited by the Lord at age fifteen, and is forbidden to preach because of the people's rebellion and hard hearts.

Chapter 2

Mormon is appointed leader of the Nephite armies while still young because he is large in stature. He leads battles, urges the Nephites to defend their families and homes, records their wickedness on the plates of Nephi, and sorrows over their destruction and abominations.

Chapter 3

Mormon led the Nephites many times despite their wickedness and prayed for them, but he refused to continue as their commander because of their wickedness and abomination. He stood as an idle witness and wrote to the Gentiles, the house of Israel, and all the ends of the earth.

Chapter 4

Mormon sees that the Lamanites are about to overthrow the land. He goes to hill Shim and takes up the records that Ammaron had hidden unto the Lord.

Chapter 5

Mormon repents of his oath not to assist the Nephites and again commands their armies. He witnesses their destruction, writes a small abridgment because of a commandment, and warns the Gentiles to repent.

Chapter 6

Mormon finishes his record concerning the destruction of the Nephites, leads his people to the land of Cumorah, and writes to the king of the Lamanites to request a gathering there. He hides the entrusted records in the hill Cumorah, keeps a few plates for Moroni, survives the battle wounded, and mourns the slain.

Mormon in The Book of Moroni

Moroni · the work’s page · read it where Mormon first appears

Abstract written by a model

Mormon is a prophet and Moroni’s father, permitted by God to speak. He teaches people to discern good and evil by the light of Christ, seek every good thing, and develop faith, hope, and charity. He judges Christ’s peaceable followers by their conduct, writes and prays continually for Moroni, teaches against baptizing little children, and warns the Nephites to repent.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

Mormon teaches the peaceable followers of Christ about good and evil, faith, hope, and charity. He urges them to judge by the light of Christ, seek every good thing, and pray to be filled with the pure love of Christ.

Chapter 8

Mormon writes to Moroni, rejoicing in his calling and praying for him. He teaches against the baptism of little children and warns the Nephites to repent because of their pride, rejection of God’s power, and denial of the Holy Ghost.
