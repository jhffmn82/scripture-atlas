Abstract written by a model

War is an exceedingly great, lasting conflict throughout all the land. It begins after the brother of Shiblom rebels, continues without ceasing as Coriantumr, his household, and the people do not repent, and culminates in the armies of Shiz and Coriantumr leaving the land covered with unburied dead.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

An exceedingly great war began throughout the land after the brother of Shiblom rebelled. Wars and contentions later spread through all the land.

Chapter 13

The wars continue because Coriantumr, his household, and the people do not repent. They include battles involving Coriantumr and Shared and eventually spread across the whole land.

Chapter 14

The war between the armies of Shiz and Coriantumr was so great and lasting that the whole face of the land was covered with dead bodies. The armies moved from one shedding of blood to another, leaving men, women, and children unburied.
