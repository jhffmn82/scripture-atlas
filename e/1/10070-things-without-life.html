Things without life

Also called: The Things without life, Things without life possessing form

Abstract written by a model

Things without life are constituents of world and solely comprise Non-world; divided into formless and form-possessing things, they are described through substance, place, time, and development.

Thirty-Sixth Lecture.

Things without life, together with Living Beings, make up the world (Lôka), while the Non-world (Alôka) contains only Things without life. They are divided into formless things and things possessing form, and are described with reference to substance, place, time, and development.
