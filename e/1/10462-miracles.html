Abstract written by a model

Miracles are works performed by God, Jesus Christ, and the apostles through divine power. The record will come forth when people deny them, though faithful people are described moving mountains, shaking earth, overcoming prisons, and receiving protection. God remains a God of miracles; they cease among people through unbelief and departure from the right way.

Chapter 8

The record will come forth in a day when people say miracles are done away. The text also describes faithful people removing mountains, shaking the earth, causing prisons to tumble, and being protected from fire, beasts, and serpents.

Chapter 9

Miracles were wrought by God, Jesus Christ, and the apostles. God has not ceased to be a God of miracles, but miracles cease among people when they dwindle in unbelief and depart from the right way.
