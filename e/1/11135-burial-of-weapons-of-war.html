Abstract written by a model

The burial of weapons of war is practiced by the Anti-Nephi-Lehies and Lamanites who join them. Converted Lamanites lay down rebellion, stop fighting God and their brethren, and bury swords and other weapons deep in the earth as testimony to God and men that they will never again shed their brethren’s blood, becoming righteous.
