Abstract written by a model

Afflictions of the people arise from lost brethren, livestock, and grain destroyed by the Lamanites. Humble followers of God suffer for Christ’s sake; later Moroni, Helaman, and their men endure hunger, thirst, fatigue, while Lamanites murder people and carry women and children captive.
