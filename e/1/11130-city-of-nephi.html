city of Nephi

place · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: city, city Nephi, city of Lehi-Nephi, land of Lehi-Nephi, the city, the city of Nephi

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Mosiah, it is land once possessed by the Nephites, granted by King Laman to Zeniff and later inhabited by Limhi and his people. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, it is the chief city in the land of Nephi, taken by Amalickiah after the king is assassinated.

land of Lehi-Nephi in The Book of Mosiah

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where land of Lehi-Nephi first appears

Abstract written by a model

The land of Lehi-Nephi is land once possessed by the Nephites. Ammon and sixteen men seek it from Zarahemla, wandering forty days before arriving. King Laman grants it to Zeniff, whose people possess it. After failed battles, Limhi and his people return and dwell there, and Limhi is taken there after Ammon and his brethren are discovered.

Chapter 7

The land of Lehi-Nephi was the destination of Ammon and the sixteen men from Zarahemla. Ammon and his companions did not know the route and wandered in the wilderness for forty days before reaching the area.

Chapter 9

King Laman allows Zeniff to possess the land of Lehi-Nephi and commands his people to leave it. Zeniff and his people enter and possess the land.

Chapter 21

The city of Nephi is where Limhi and his people return and dwell. The people of Limhi return there after their failed battles, and Limhi is taken there after Ammon and his brethren are discovered.
