Abstract written by a model

The prophets are holy messengers sent by God among all people. Since the world began, they have proclaimed Christ’s coming through signs, wonders, types, and shadows, publishing peace and good tidings. Those who prophesy of the Lord without transgression are the seed of the Lord and, with obedient believers, shall come forth in the first resurrection.
