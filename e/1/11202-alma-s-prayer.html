Chapter 31

In Alma's prayer, he asked God for strength to bear the Zoramites' wickedness, comfort in Christ, success for himself and his fellow laborers, and power and wisdom to bring the Zoramites again unto God. Afterward, his companions were filled with the Holy Spirit and were strengthened to endure their afflictions.
