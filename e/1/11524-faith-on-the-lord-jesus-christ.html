faith on the Lord Jesus Christ

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Mosiah, repentance and faith on the Lord Jesus Christ are required for salvation. In The Book of Helaman, faith on the Lord Jesus Christ is identified with repentance as the means by which the people can be saved from the destruction that awaits them.
