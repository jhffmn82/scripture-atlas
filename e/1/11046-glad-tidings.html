Glad tidings of great joy are declared by angels to all nations and to people scattered across the earth. They prepare hearts to receive the word at the time of the Son's coming.

Chapter 39

Glad tidings concern the coming of Christ, who will declare salvation to his people. The son's ministry was to declare these glad tidings and prepare the people for Christ's coming.
