Abstract written by a model

Amaleki was the son of Abinadom and wrote about Mosiah, Benjamin, the people of Zarahemla, and an attempted return to the land of Nephi. Having no seed and knowing Benjamin was just before the Lord, Amaleki delivered the plates to him and exhorted people to come unto God and Christ.
