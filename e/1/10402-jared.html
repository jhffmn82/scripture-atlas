Jared

person · 1 work · 15 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: brother of Jared

Abstract written by a model

Jared is Omer’s son and brother of the brother of Jared. He first asks his brother to pray that their language not be confounded and to learn where they should go. He travels with his brother, their families, friends, and people through the wilderness, across the great waters, and into the promised land. Jared has four sons, gathers the people before dying, and agrees that they may have a king. Later, Jared rebels against Omer, gains half the kingdom, takes Omer captive, and loses the kingdom after defeat, becoming sorrowful. He is then anointed king through wickedness, gives his daughter to Akish, and is murdered by Akish while on his throne.

Jared is in one work on this site, The Book of Ether. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

The Book of Ether / Chapter 1

Jared asked his brother to cry unto the Lord so that their language would not be confounded. He later asked his brother to inquire where the Lord would lead them.

Chapter 2

Jared travels with his brother, their families, and their friends into the valley of Nimrod and then through the wilderness under the Lord’s direction. He is brought with his brethren to the great sea and dwells with them at Moriancumer for four years.

Chapter 6

Jared travels with his people across the great waters and lands in the promised land. He has four sons, dies after gathering the people, and agrees that they may have a king.

Chapter 8

Jared rebelled against Omer, gained half the kingdom by flattering people, and took Omer captive. After Esrom and Coriantumr defeated his army, Jared surrendered the kingdom and became sorrowful over its loss.

Chapter 9

Jared is anointed king over the people through wickedness and gives his daughter to Akish as a wife. Akish has Jared murdered while he sits upon his throne giving audience to his people.
