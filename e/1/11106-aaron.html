Aaron

person · 1 work · 14 quoted facts · 8 connections

Abstract written by a model

Aaron, Ammon’s brother and Alma’s fellow laborer, is a preacher of the word of God. He is led to the land of Nephi, preaches repentance, Christ, resurrection, redemption, and atonement, and is repeatedly rejected and imprisoned before being delivered. He travels with his brethren to the king who was Lamoni’s father, expounds the scriptures and the plan of redemption, administers to the king, and raises him after he falls as if dead. Protected by the king of the Lamanites, Aaron goes from city to city preaching, establishes churches, and consecrates priests and teachers. Many Lamanites later remember his and his brethren’s words, believe, and are converted. He later accompanies Alma among the Zoramites, where Alma prays for his comfort and strength in Christ.

Aaron is in one work on this site, The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 20

Aaron is in prison in the land of Middoni with Ammon’s other brethren and is delivered when Lamoni and Ammon arrive.

Chapter 21

Aaron travels to Jerusalem and preaches in an Amalekite synagogue about repentance, the coming of Christ, the resurrection of the dead, redemption, and the atonement of Christ's blood. After the people reject him, he preaches in Ani-Anti and Middoni, is imprisoned, is delivered by Lamoni and Ammon, and continues preaching.

Chapter 22

Aaron goes with his brethren to the palace of the king who was Lamoni’s father. He teaches the king from the scriptures, explains the plan of redemption, and raises the king from the earth after the king is struck as if dead.

Chapter 23

Aaron was protected by the king of the Lamanites and went from city to city establishing churches and teaching the word of God. He also consulted with the king and many priests about the name of the converted people.

Chapter 25

Aaron and his brethren preach to the Lamanites, and many later remember their words, believe in the Lord, and are converted in the wilderness.

Chapter 31

Aaron went with Alma and the others among the Zoramites to preach the word. Alma prayed that Aaron would be comforted and strengthened in Christ.
