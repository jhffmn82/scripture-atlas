tribes

group · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: every tribe, their tribes

Abstract written by a model

Tribes are family-, kindred-, and friend-based groups whose members appoint chiefs, grow exceedingly great, establish laws and government by their leaders, and maintain some peace through strict rules and agreements not to war. They are also united in hatred against those who entered a covenant to destroy the government.

tribes is in one work on this site, The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 7

The people separated into tribes according to family, kindred, and friends. The tribes became exceedingly great, established their own laws, and agreed not to go to war one with another.
