Nehor

person · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: the man, the man who slew him

Abstract written by a model

Nehor is a large, very strong man who preaches false doctrines, including universal salvation and paid priests. He gains followers, money, and costly apparel and establishes a church. After killing Gideon with a sword, he is condemned to death under Mosiah’s law and acknowledges the word of God before dying.

Nehor is in one work on this site, The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 1

Nehor preaches against the church, teaches that priests should be supported by the people, and declares that all mankind will have eternal life. He gains followers, money, and costly apparel, establishes a church, kills Gideon with a sword, acknowledges that his teachings oppose the word of God, and suffers an ignominious death.
