Zerahemnah

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: chief leader and commander

Abstract written by a model

Zerahemnah is a Lamanite chief leader and commander who appoints Amalekites and Zoramites as captains, stirs anger against the Nephites, and seeks power over both peoples. Armed for war, he leads forces that are surrounded by Moroni’s and Lehi’s armies. He refuses an oath, attacks Moroni and is wounded, urges further fighting, then promises a covenant of peace to spare surviving Lamanites.

Zerahemnah is in one work on this site, The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 43

Zerahemnah leads the Lamanite armies and appoints Amalekites and Zoramites as chief captains to preserve hatred toward the Nephites. His forces are eventually surrounded by Moroni’s and Lehi’s armies.

Chapter 44

Zerahemnah delivers his weapons but refuses to take an oath, saying that he and his children would break it. He attacks Moroni, loses his sword and scalp, stirs the remaining Lamanites to fight, and later promises a covenant of peace to spare the survivors.
