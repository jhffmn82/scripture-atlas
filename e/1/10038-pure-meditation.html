Abstract written by a model

Pure meditation, its third degree relapse-free, requires only the subtlest organ functions and finally stops mind, speech, body, and breathing, annihilating four remaining Karman before the soul rises naturally.

Twenty-Ninth Lecture.

Pure meditation is the final stage in which the soul ceases the functions of mind, speech, body, and breathing. During the final pure meditation, it annihilates the four remaining kinds of Karman and rises to its natural form.
