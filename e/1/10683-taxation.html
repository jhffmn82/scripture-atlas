taxation

Also called: a tax, tax, taxes, tribute

Abstract written by a model

Taxation is a grievous burden imposed on the people. Under Limhi, they pay the Lamanites half their crops, herds, and possessions or lose their lives. Under Noah, one fifth of possessions supports Noah, his wives and concubines, and priests, making the people labor exceedingly for iniquity.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

The people of Limhi paid the Lamanites half of their corn, barley, grain, flocks, herds, and possessions, or their lives. Limhi described this tribute as grievous to bear.

Chapter 11

Noah lays a tax of one fifth of the people's possessions, including precious metals, other materials, fatlings, and grain. The taxation supports Noah, his wives and concubines, and his priests, and causes the people to labor exceedingly to support iniquity.
