Also called: chief judges, those judges

Abstract written by a model

Judges secretly and unlawfully put prophets and witnesses of Christ to death. Accused of murder, they unite with lawyers, high priests, friends, and kindreds in a covenant to defeat justice, destroy the governor, and establish a king over liberty.

Chapter 6
