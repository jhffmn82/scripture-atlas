Alma in The Book of Mosiah

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Alma first appears

Abstract written by a model

Alma is a young descendant of Nephi, formerly a priest of king Noah, whose belief in Abinadi’s words transforms him after he pleads with the king, is cast out and pursued, and hides to write Abinadi’s words. He repents of his sins and iniquities, teaches Abinadi’s words privately, and teaches believers repentance, redemption, and faith. With authority from God, he baptizes believers in the waters of Mormon, ordains priests, and forms the church of God through God’s strength and power and faith in Abinadi’s words. When king Noah’s army threatens them, Alma leads his people into the wilderness. As their beloved high priest, he teaches liberty, rejects kingship, consecrates just priests and teachers, teaches love of neighbors and avoidance of contention, and urges trust in the Lord. He leads people from bondage, preaches repentance and faith, teaches remembrance of the Lord’s deliverance, baptizes Limhi’s people, and receives power to establish churches and ordain priests and teachers. Later, as high priest with authority over the church from king Mosiah, he asks the Lord how to judge transgressors, records the Lord’s words, judges the accused, regulates church affairs, and continues teaching and serving despite afflictions.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 17

Alma, a young descendant of Nephi, believes Abinadi’s words and pleads with king Noah not to be angry with him. Alma is cast out, pursued by servants, and hides while writing all the words Abinadi had spoken.

Chapter 18

Alma repented of his sins and iniquities after fleeing from the servants of king Noah. He taught Abinadi’s words privately, baptized believers, organized the church, ordained priests, and led the people into the wilderness when the king’s army came to destroy them.

Chapter 21

Alma forms a church of God through the strength and power of God and faith in the words spoken by Abinadi, then flees into the wilderness with his people. Ammon and his brethren mourn Alma’s departure, and Limhi’s people desire to become like Alma and his brethren.

Chapter 23

Alma leads his people away from the armies of king Noah and into the wilderness. He teaches them about liberty, rejects kingship, serves as their high priest, consecrates just priests and teachers, and encourages them to trust the Lord when the Lamanites appear.

Chapter 24

Alma is one of the king’s priests who believed the words of Abinadi and was driven out before the king. He leads his people out of bondage into the wilderness, and God tells him that He will go with him.

Chapter 25

Alma preached repentance and faith on the Lord, taught the people of Limhi and his brethren to remember the Lord's deliverance, baptized Limhi's people, and received power to establish churches and ordain priests and teachers.

Chapter 26

Alma is the high priest and has authority over the church from king Mosiah. He inquires of the Lord about judging transgressors, records the Lord’s words, judges the accused, and regulates the affairs of the church.
