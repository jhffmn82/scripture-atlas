Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Mosiah, Gideon is a strong man and enemy of king Noah who serves king Limhi, fights and pursues Noah, counsels Limhi, identifies Noah’s priests, and devises an escape from bondage. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, Gideon teaches the church of God, speaks God’s words against Nehor, helps deliver Limhi’s people from bondage, and is slain by Nehor’s sword.

Gideon in The Book of Mosiah

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Gideon first appears

Abstract written by a model

Gideon is a strong man, enemy of king Noah, captain, and servant of king Limhi. He fights and pursues Noah but spares him when the Lamanites appear. He counsels Limhi against punishing his people, identifies Noah’s priests, and devises an escape: wine for the guards and a secret pass from bondage.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 19

Gideon, a strong man and enemy of the king, fights king Noah and pursues him to the tower. He spares king Noah when the Lamanites appear, later sends men to search for him, and learns what happened to the people and their king.

Chapter 20

Gideon, the king's captain, asks Limhi not to search and punish his people. He identifies the priests of king Noah as the abductors and urges Limhi to pacify the Lamanite king.

Chapter 22

Gideon proposes using wine to make the Lamanite guards drunk and escaping through a secret pass behind the city. He offers to pay the last tribute of wine and says he will deliver the people from bondage.
