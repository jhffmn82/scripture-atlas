the gospel of the Messiah

Abstract written by a model

The gospel of the Messiah begins in purity as it goes from the Jews, but the great and abominable church removes many plain and precious parts. Its fulness later comes to the Gentiles, then from them to the remnant of the seed and to those of the house of Israel. It teaches the Redeemer, his doctrine, his rock, and salvation, enabling people to come unto him and be saved. In the end, natural branches are grafted in, know the true Messiah, and the Lord makes bare his arm among the nations.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 9 .. Chapter 12

The gospel is preached among the Jews, and its fulness is received by the Gentiles. After this, the natural branches of the house of Israel are grafted in and come to know the true Messiah.

Chapter 13 .. Chapter 14

The gospel of the Lamb goes forth from the Jews in purity, but the great and abominable church takes away many plain and precious parts. The Lamb will bring forth much of it in plain and precious form, including his rock and salvation.

Chapter 15 .. Chapter 16

The fulness of the gospel of the Messiah will come to the Gentiles and then to the remnant of the seed. It enables them to know their Redeemer and the points of his doctrine so they may come unto him and be saved.

Chapter 22

The Lord God will bring his gospel to those who are of the house of Israel as part of making bare his arm among the nations.
