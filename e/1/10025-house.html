Abstract written by a model

A pleasant painted house, secured by doors and adorned with white ceiling-cloth, garlands, and frankincense, increases desire and passion; its construction kills living beings, so a monk should neither build it nor cause it to be built.

Thirty-Fifth Lecture.

A house may increase a monk's desire and passion through its pleasant furnishings and fragrances. A monk should not build a house or cause one to be built because living beings are killed during its construction.
