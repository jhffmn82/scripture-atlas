Also called: Faith, right belief, right faith, righteousness

Abstract written by a model

Faith is a cause of final deliverance, produced by nature, instruction, command, study of the Sûtras, religious exercise, and the Law. Right belief requires acquaintance with truth, devotion to those who know it, and avoidance of schismatical and heretical tenets; it precedes right conduct. Faith may be obstructed or take right, wrong, or mixed forms, while perfected souls, orthodox opinions, and faith in the creed of the Ginas lead toward Bôdhi and escape from the Circle of Births.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Twenty-Eighth Lecture.

Faith is one of the four causes of final deliverance and is produced by ten causes, including nature, instruction, command, study of the Sûtras, comprehension of sacred lore, religious exercise, and the Law. Right belief depends on acquaintance with truth, devotion to those who know the truth, and avoidance of schismatical and heretical tenets.

Thirty-Third Lecture.

Right faith is obstructed by Darsanâvaranîya. Môhanîya also concerns faith, including right faith, wrong faith, and faith partly right and partly wrong.

B. Living Beings.

Perfected souls are developed into faith. Orthodox opinions and faith in the creed of the Ginas are associated with reaching Bôdhi and escaping the Circle of Births.
