Abstract written by a model

The wilderness is a place of travel for Lehi’s family after they leave Jerusalem, containing the valley of Lemuel, the Red Sea, and eventually the land of Bountiful. Lehi is commanded to take his family there, traveling three days before camping in a valley by a river. Nephi and his brothers repeatedly journey, flee, hide, and return through it, including with Zoram and the plates of brass. Over many days and eight years, the family travels nearly eastward toward the land of promise, enduring hunger, sufferings, afflictions, and raw meat. They follow the ball, hunt, and receive fruit, wild honey, beasts, and ore, before reaching large waters and leaving the wilderness for the promised land.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry

The wilderness is where Lehi’s family travels after leaving Jerusalem. They experience sufferings and afflictions there before reaching the large waters.

Chapter 2

The wilderness is where Lehi is commanded to take his family and where they travel after departing from Jerusalem. Lehi travels for three days before pitching his tent in a valley by a river of water.

Chapter 3

The wilderness is where Nephi's father has taken his family after being commanded to flee Jerusalem. Nephi and his brothers travel through it, flee into it after losing their property, and hide in the cavity of a rock.

Chapter 4

Nephi and his brethren leave Jerusalem and journey into the wilderness with Zoram and the plates of brass. Their father is in the wilderness, where they intend to reunite with him.

Chapter 5 .. Chapter 8

Lehi’s family travels through the wilderness toward the land of promise. Lehi’s dream includes a dark and dreary wilderness and a dark and dreary waste.

Chapter 15 .. Chapter 16

The wilderness is the destination of Nephi's family after leaving their former land. They travel through it for many days, suffer hunger, follow the ball, and obtain food by hunting.

Chapter 17 .. Chapter 18

Nephi’s family travels nearly eastward through the wilderness for eight years, suffering affliction while living upon raw meat. The wilderness provides fruit, wild honey, beasts, and ore, and the family eventually leaves it for the promised land.
