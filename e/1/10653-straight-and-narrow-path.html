Abstract written by a model

The straight and narrow path is the way entered through repentance and baptism by water. It leads to eternal life, requires receiving the Holy Ghost, pressing forward, feasting upon the word of Christ, and continuing until the end of the day of probation.

Chapter 31

The straight and narrow path leads to eternal life. People enter it through the gate of repentance and baptism by water and must press forward, feast upon the word of Christ, and endure to the end.

Chapter 32

After entering in by the way, people should receive the Holy Ghost. If they enter in by the way and receive the Holy Ghost, it will show them all things they should do.

Chapter 33

The strait path leads to life, and those who enter it must continue in it until the end of the day of probation.
