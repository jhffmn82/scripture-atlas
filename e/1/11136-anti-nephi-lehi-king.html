Anti-Nephi-Lehi (king)

person · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: Anti-Nephi-Lehi, king, the king, their king

Abstract written by a model

Anti-Nephi-Lehi is king and leader of the people of Anti-Nephi-Lehi. He commands them not to prepare for war, teaching them to bury their swords because God has forgiven them. Fearing Nephite destruction for murders and sins, he accepts slavery as repayment and will go to the Nephites if commanded by the Lord.

Anti-Nephi-Lehi (king) is in one work on this site, The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 24

Anti-Nephi-Lehi commands his people not to make preparations for war. He teaches them to retain and bury their swords, explaining that God has forgiven them and that they should not stain their swords with the blood of their brethren.

Chapter 27

The king feared that the Nephites would destroy his people because of their murders and sins. He agreed to go to the Nephites if the Lord commanded it and promised that the people would otherwise perish in the land.
