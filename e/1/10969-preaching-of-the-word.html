Abstract written by a model

Preaching of the word is a divinely guided ministry performed by Alma, Ammon, Aaron, their brethren, and others according to revelation and prophecy. It is presented as more powerful in leading people to just conduct than the sword, so Alma uses it to reclaim the people of Nephi and the Zoramites through pure testimony against pride, craftiness, and contention. Aaron, Ammon, and their brethren persist through rejection and imprisonment while preaching across cities, houses of worship, and among the Lamanites and Amalekites. Their teaching brings thousands of Lamanites to knowledge of the Lord and repentance among many Zoramites. The word is preached in synagogues, houses, and streets, delivered by angels, and given to men, women, and little children. It concludes with instruction that teaching continue diligently, temperately, boldly, humbly, lovingly, and with self-control.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

Alma devoted himself to preaching the word of God among the people of Nephi and stirring them up in remembrance of their duty. He sought to reclaim them by bearing down in pure testimony against their pride, craftiness, and contentions.

Chapter 21

Aaron, Ammon, and their brethren preach the word in Jerusalem, Ani-Anti, Middoni, and among the Lamanites and Amalekites. They continue preaching after rejection and imprisonment, and the Lord blesses their efforts.

Chapter 23

Aaron and his brethren preached and taught the word of God from city to city and from one house of worship to another. Their preaching brought thousands of Lamanites to the knowledge of the Lord and led to great success.

Chapter 31

Preaching of the word had a greater tendency to lead the people to do what was just than the sword or anything else that had happened to them. Alma therefore chose to use it among the Zoramites.

Chapter 32

The word of God is preached by Alma and others in synagogues, houses, and streets. It is also imparted by angels and given at times to men, women, and little children.

Chapter 35

Alma and his brethren preached the word unto the Zoramites, and Alma had also declared or been sent to declare the word among the people in every city.

Chapter 38

Shiblon is instructed to continue teaching the word to the people. His teaching should be diligent, temperate, bold without overbearance, and accompanied by humility, love, and self-control.
