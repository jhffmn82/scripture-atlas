Lemuel

person · 2 works · 8 quoted facts · 5 connections

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, Lemuel rebels against Lehi and Nephi, murmurs, joins violence against his younger brothers, seeks to return to Jerusalem, rejects the fruit in Lehi’s dream, helps bind Nephi, and later helps free him during a storm. In The Second Book of Nephi, Lemuel rebels against and grows angry with Nephi, while Lehi blesses his children and descendants against utter destruction.

Lemuel in The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry

Nephi · the work’s page · read it where Lemuel first appears

Abstract written by a model

Lemuel is Lehi’s elder son and Nephi’s elder brother, likened to the firm, steadfast valley of Lemuel. He murmurs against Lehi, refuses Nephi’s words, follows Laman, and joins in harsh speech and violence toward younger brothers. He rebels and seeks to return to Jerusalem, does not partake of the fruit in Lehi’s dream, and causes Lehi fear. During the voyage he murmurs and helps bind Nephi, then repents and helps loose him when a storm threatens destruction.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Lemuel is Lehi's elder son and is compared to the valley of Lemuel, which is firm, steadfast, and immovable in keeping the commandments of the Lord. He murmurs against Lehi, refuses to hearken unto Nephi's words, and is included among those who will be cut off from the presence of the Lord if he rebels.

Chapter 3

Lemuel hearkens to Laman's words, joins him in speaking hard words to their younger brothers, and smites them with a rod. After the angel departs, Lemuel continues to murmur with Laman about the possibility of overcoming Laban.

Chapter 5 .. Chapter 8

Lemuel rebels with Laman and others and seeks to return to Jerusalem. In Lehi’s dream, he does not partake of the fruit, and Lehi fears for him.

Chapter 17 .. Chapter 18

Lemuel murmurs against Nephi and helps bind him with cords during the voyage. When the storm threatens destruction, he repents and helps loose Nephi.

Lemuel in The Second Book of Nephi

Abstract written by a model

Lemuel, Lehi’s son, rebels against and later grows angry with Nephi; his sons and daughters receive Lehi’s blessing that they and their seed will not be utterly destroyed.

Chapter 4

Lemuel’s children receive from Lehi the same blessing given to the children of Laman, including that they will not be utterly destroyed and that their seed will be blessed. After Lehi’s death, Lemuel becomes angry with Nephi because of the Lord’s admonitions.
