The Book of Mosiah / Chapter 1

The gathering at the temple is commanded so the people of Zarahemla and the people of Mosiah may hear king Benjamin speak. Mosiah proclaims that the people should gather for this purpose.

Chapter 2

The gathering brings the people and their families together to hear king Benjamin and to receive his teachings. King Benjamin also causes the gathering so he can declare his final instructions and be found blameless before God.
