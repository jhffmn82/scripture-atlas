Abstract written by a model

The Zoramites are a people separated from the Nephites and led by Zoram, living in Antionum. They build synagogues and worship weekly at the holy stand, but their worship is marked by prayers meant to be heard and praised by people. They deny Christ’s coming, believe salvation is for themselves as a chosen people, and are characterized by pride, riches, fine goods, and great errors that pervert the ways of the Lord. After the word destroys their craft, they become angry, exile believers in the preached words, and refuse to hearken. A son’s conduct further damages their willingness to believe. They stir up the Lamanites against the people of Ammon and the Nephites, prepare for war, then become Lamanites and join their armies, with their leaders serving as chief captains and inspiring fierce fighting.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 30

The Zoramites were a people who had separated themselves from the Nephites and were led by Zoram. Korihor was among them when he was run upon and trodden down until he was dead.

Chapter 31

The Zoramites were dissenters from the Nephites who had fallen into great errors and perverted the ways of the Lord. They worshipped at the Rameumptom, believed they were chosen, rejected belief in Christ, and set their hearts on riches and pride.

Chapter 35

The Zoramites became angry because the word destroyed their craft and refused to hearken to it. They cast out those who favored the words of Alma and his brethren, stirred up the Lamanites against the people of Ammon and the Nephites, and prepared for war.

Chapter 38

The Zoramites are described as praying to be heard by people and praised for their wisdom. Shiblon is told not to pray as they do.

Chapter 39

The Zoramites were affected by the son's conduct, and when they saw it they would not believe the speaker's words. The son is commanded to return to them and acknowledge his faults.

Chapter 43

The Zoramites become Lamanites and join the Lamanite armies. Their leaders serve as chief captains, and they inspire the Lamanites to fight fiercely.
