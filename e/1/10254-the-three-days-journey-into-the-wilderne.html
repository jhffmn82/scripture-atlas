Abstract written by a model

A three-days’ journey into the wilderness, begun after the Lord commands Nephi’s father to depart. Lehi takes his family from Jerusalem along borders near the Red Sea; after three days he pitches his tent in a valley beside a river. Later the family gathers provisions and seed, crosses the river Laman, follows the ball, and travels many days.
