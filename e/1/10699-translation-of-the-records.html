translation of the records

Abstract written by a model

The records are translated by King Mosiah through interpreters after King Limhi desires their meaning. Their translation reveals an account of people who were destroyed, tracing from their destruction back to the great tower and the creation of Adam. It gives the people much knowledge and causes them to mourn.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 8

The translation of the records is desired by king Limhi because it may reveal the identity and destruction of the people who made them. Ammon says the king of the people in the land of Zarahemla can translate them through the interpreters.

Chapter 28

King Mosiah translates the records on the plates of gold by means of the interpreters. The translation gives an account of the people who were destroyed from the time of their destruction back to the building of the great tower and the creation of Adam, causing the people to mourn and giving them much knowledge.
