the work of the Father

event · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: a great and a marvelous work, the work, the works which shall be wrought among you hereafter, these works, work of the Father

Abstract written by a model

In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the work of the Father commences among the Gentiles and the remnant of Jacob, prepares people to come to Jesus Christ, and gathers Israel from all nations to its inheritance. In The Book of Ether, the work of the Father has commenced upon all the face of the land when the record is received.
