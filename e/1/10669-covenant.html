covenant

Abstract written by a model

The covenant with God is a righteous commitment to do his will, serve him with all one’s heart, and keep his commandments for the remainder of life. King Benjamin connects it with becoming children of Christ, and the people enter it by taking upon themselves Christ’s name. Alma’s people seek God’s fold and, through baptism, covenant to serve him and keep his commandments until death. Ammon, his brethren, Limhi, and many of Limhi’s people also enter it. God recognizes Alma’s people’s covenant, promises to covenant with his people, and deliver them from bondage.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

The covenant with God is a commitment to do his will and obey his commandments for the remainder of one’s days. King Benjamin calls it a righteous covenant and connects it with becoming children of Christ.

Chapter 6

The covenant is entered into by all the people except little children, and it concerns keeping God's commandments and taking upon themselves the name of Christ.

Chapter 18

The people desired to enter the fold of God and be called his people. Through baptism, they entered a covenant to serve God and keep his commandments until death.

Chapter 21

Ammon and his brethren have entered into a covenant with God to serve him and keep his commandments. Limhi and many of his people also enter into a covenant with God.

Chapter 24

God knows of the covenant Alma and his people have made unto Him. He promises to covenant with His people and deliver them out of bondage.
