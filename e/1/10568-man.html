Abstract written by a model

Man worships idols made by his own hands, but lofty looks and haughtiness are humbled and idols cast away in the day of the Lord. Though breath is in his nostrils and reliance on him is rejected, he becomes more precious than fine gold and Ophir’s golden wedge.
