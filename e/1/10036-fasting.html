Abstract written by a model

Fasting is an external austerity of two kinds: temporary fasting, with six forms, and maranakâla, which ends with death and is classified by bodily motion and permitted relief or departure.

Thirtieth Lecture.

Fasting is an external austerity of two kinds: temporary fasting and fasting which precedes and ends with death. Temporary fasting has six forms, while fasting before death is classified according to bodily motion and whether relief or leaving the chosen place is allowed.
