Lehi's family

Abstract written by a model

Lehi’s family is a family including Lehi, Sariah, Laman, Lemuel, Sam, and Nephi. They depart Jerusalem with provisions and tents, travel through the wilderness, rejoice and offer sacrifice after records return, journey with Ishmael’s family, gather seeds, take Ishmael’s daughters as wives, cross large waters, and reach the promised land.

The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry

Lehi’s family includes Lehi, Sariah, Laman, Lemuel, Sam, and Nephi. The family departs from Jerusalem, travels through the wilderness, takes the daughters of Ishmael as wives, crosses the large waters, and reaches the promised land.

Chapter 2

Lehi's family consists of his mother, Sariah, and his elder brothers Laman, Lemuel, and Sam. The family departs with Lehi into the wilderness, taking provisions and tents.

Chapter 5 .. Chapter 8

Lehi’s family rejoices and offers sacrifice after Nephi and his brothers return with the records. They travel in the wilderness with Ishmael’s family and gather seeds of every kind.
