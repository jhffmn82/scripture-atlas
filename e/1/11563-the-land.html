Abstract written by a model

The land extends from the sea west to the sea east. It experiences great famine, exceedingly great peace, war, dryness, destruction, and a curse because of wickedness and abominations. It has no light for three days when Jesus Christ suffers death, then receives rain, brings forth fruit and grain, and its people rebuild and spread across it.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

The land suffers war, famine, dryness, and destruction, but later receives rain and again brings forth fruit and grain. The people rebuild its waste places and spread across it from the sea west to the sea east.

Chapter 13

The land is cursed because of the people’s wickedness and abominations. Things hidden or possessed by the wicked become slippery and cannot be retained.

Chapter 14

The land shall have no light for three days when Jesus Christ suffers death. Signs and wonders shall come to pass across the land.

Chapter 16

The people questioned why Christ would not show himself in this land as well as in the land of Jerusalem.
