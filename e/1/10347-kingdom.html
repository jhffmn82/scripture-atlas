Abstract written by a model

The kingdom is sought by Jared for worldly glory. Jared gains half through flattery but loses it after defeat, prompting his daughter’s plan to restore him. It later alternates between righteous building, rebellion, war, captivity, succession, division, and repeated struggles for power under rulers from Shez through Moron.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 8

Jared gained half of the kingdom through flattering people and later lost the kingdom after Esrom and Coriantumr defeated his army. Jared's daughter sought to redeem the kingdom to him.

Chapter 10

Shez built up a righteous kingdom, while Riplakish’s rule ended in rebellion, war, death, and the expulsion of his descendants. Morianton established himself as king over the land, later rulers lost control and served in captivity, and Com eventually obtained power over the remainder of the kingdom after fighting Amgid.

Chapter 11

The kingdom passed from Com to Shiblom, Seth, Ahah, Ethem, and Moron amid rebellion, overthrow, wickedness, and captivity. Moron's challenger held half of it for many years, and another mighty man later obtained it.
