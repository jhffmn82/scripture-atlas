Abstract written by a model

Black Lêsyâ is one of the lowest Lêsyâs. Rain-cloud coloured, bitter, foul-smelling, and harsh to touch, it develops in a person ruled by the five Âsravas, lacking the three Guptis, injuring living beings, and acting cruelly and violently. It brings the soul into miserable courses of life.

Thirty-Fourth Lecture.

The black Lêsyâ has the colour of a rain-cloud, a buffalo's horn, Rishtaka, or the eye of the wagtail. It develops in a person ruled by the five Âsravas, lacking the three Guptis, injuring living beings, and acting cruelly and violently; it is one of the lowest Lêsyâs and brings the soul into miserable courses of life.
