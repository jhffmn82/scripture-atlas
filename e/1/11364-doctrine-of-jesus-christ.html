Chapter 11

The doctrine of Jesus Christ is that all people should repent and believe in him, be baptized, become as a little child, and thereby receive salvation and the kingdom of God. Jesus Christ teaches that no more or less should be declared as his doctrine, and that it is a rock against which the gates of hell shall not prevail.
