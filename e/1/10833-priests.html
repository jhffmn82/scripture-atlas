priests

Abstract written by a model

Priests are ordained servants and preachers who preside over the church. They leave labor to impart God’s word, then diligently return to it, remaining equal to hearers and learners. They teach commandments and redemption, preach repentance throughout the land, condemn many sins, and teach the coming, sufferings, death, and resurrection of the Son of God.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Priests leave their labor to impart the word of God, after which they return diligently to their labors. They do not esteem themselves above their hearers, and the preacher is described as no better than the hearer.

Chapter 13

Priests were ordained by the Lord God after the holy order of the Only Begotten Son to teach the commandments to the people and help them look forward to redemption.

Chapter 16

The priests preached against lying, deceiving, envy, strife, malice, reviling, stealing, robbing, plundering, murdering, adultery, and lasciviousness. They also taught the coming, sufferings, death, and resurrection of the Son of God.
