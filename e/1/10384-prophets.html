Abstract written by a model

Prophets warn the people to repent, forsake wickedness, and turn to the Lord or face destruction and judgment. Rejected and pursued, they seek protection, are killed or withdraw, yet continue foretelling another people possessing the land.

Chapter 11

The prophets warned of destruction unless the people repented, turned to the Lord, and forsook their murders and wickedness. They were rejected, sought for destruction, put to death, or withdrew from the people, but they continued to prophesy of judgment and another people possessing the land.
