Abstract written by a model

Deliverance from bondage is promised by the Lord if Limhi’s people turn to him and serve him. Limhi teaches that they must trust God, then the people cry mightily for relief. Their burdens are eased as Lamanite hearts soften, but they remain captive. Unable to win by the sword, they escape through a secret pass using Gideon’s plan.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

Limhi taught that deliverance from bondage was near if the people trusted in God. He said that God would deliver them according to his own will and pleasure if they turned to him with full purpose of heart and served him diligently.

Chapter 21

Limhi’s people cry mightily to God for deliverance from their afflictions and bondage. The Lord eases their burdens by softening the hearts of the Lamanites, but does not yet deliver them from bondage.

Chapter 22

Ammon, king Limhi, and the people of Limhi seek deliverance from bondage but cannot achieve it by contending with the Lamanites by the sword. Gideon’s plan uses wine and a secret pass to deliver the people through escape.
