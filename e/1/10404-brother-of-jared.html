brother of Jared

person · 1 work · 18 quoted facts · 11 connections
Also called: brother, his brother, the brother of Jared, this man

Abstract written by a model

The brother of Jared is a large and mighty man, highly favored of the Lord, with Jared’s families. He begins by crying to the Lord, who preserves the language of Jared, their friends, and families and directs their gathering and journey. After chastening because he had not called upon the Lord, he repents, builds barges with his brethren, and seeks help for breathing, steering, and light. He makes sixteen small stones; the Lord makes them shine, and he places them in the vessels for light. His exceedingly great faith leads him to see the finger of the Lord, Jesus Christ, all things beyond the veil, and great and marvelous things. He writes what he sees on plates in a language that cannot be read, receives two stones, and is commanded to seal them until the Lord shows them to the children of men. A seer with perfect knowledge of God and redemption from the fall, he teaches his people humility, gathers them before death, warns against a king, and by faith commands the mountain Zerin to move.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

The Book of Ether / Chapter 1

The brother of Jared was a large and mighty man who was highly favored of the Lord. He cried unto the Lord, and the Lord preserved the language of Jared, their friends, and their families, then directed him to gather them and go into the valley which is northward.

Chapter 2

The brother of Jared leads or joins the journey under the Lord’s direction and receives instructions from the Lord. After being chastened for not calling upon the Lord, he repents and builds barges with his brethren, then asks how they will breathe, steer, and have light in them.

Chapter 3

The brother of Jared makes sixteen small stones, asks the Lord to make them shine, and sees the finger of the Lord. Because of his faith and knowledge, he is brought into the Lord’s presence, sees Jesus Christ, and is shown the inhabitants of the earth. He is commanded to write and seal what he has seen and heard.

Chapter 4

The brother of Jared sees things greater than any others made manifest, and the Lord commands him to write them. He exercises faith, and the things he saw are promised to those who later exercise faith as he did.

Chapter 6

The brother of Jared places the prepared stones into the vessels or barges, and the stones give light. He travels with his people, teaches them to walk humbly before the Lord, gathers them before his death, and warns that having a king will lead into captivity.

Chapter 12

The brother of Jared has exceedingly great faith and sees the finger of the Lord and all things beyond the veil. His written words are described as mighty, and he commands the mountain Zerin to move, which it does because of his faith.
