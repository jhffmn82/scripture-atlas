Abstract written by a model

Lamoni’s father is a king over nearly all the land of Nephi and Lamoni’s father. At first, angered by Lamoni’s intent to help Ammon’s brethren, he orders violence against Ammon and attacks him, but yields after Ammon overcomes him, releasing the brethren and preserving Lamoni’s kingdom. He later receives Aaron and his brethren, seeks understanding of the Spirit, repentance, and Ammon’s words, believes, prays, and desires eternal life. After being raised by Aaron, he is converted with his household, ministers and pacifies his people, authorizes preaching, issues protective proclamations, and consults on a name for the converted people.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 20

The father of Lamoni becomes angry when Lamoni explains that he is going to deliver Ammon’s brethren. He commands Lamoni to slay Ammon, attacks Ammon himself, and then agrees to release Ammon’s brethren and allow Lamoni to retain his kingdom after Ammon overcomes him.

Chapter 22

The king receives Aaron and his brethren and asks about the Spirit of the Lord, repentance, and the words of Ammon. He believes Aaron’s teachings, prays to know God, offers to give away his sins, is struck as if dead, and is raised by Aaron. He ministers to the people, causes his household to be converted, pacifies the multitude, commands Aaron and his brethren to preach, and sends a proclamation throughout the land.

Chapter 23

The king of the Lamanites was converted unto the Lord along with all his household. He issued a proclamation protecting those who preached the word of God and later consulted with Aaron and the priests about a name for the converted people.
