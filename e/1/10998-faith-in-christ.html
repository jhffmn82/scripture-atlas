Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Nephi, faith in Christ is unshaken reliance on Christ to enter the straight and narrow path and rely wholly upon his merits, and Nephi’s great faith leads him to pray and expect spotless souls at Christ’s judgment-seat. In The Book of Jacob, the Brother of Nephi, Jacob has faith in Christ who should come based on revelations, visions of angels, and the voice of the Lord, and Sherem cannot shake it. In The Book of Enos, faith in Christ brings Enos forgiveness, becomes unshaken, and leads God to grant his desires and promise to preserve the records. In The Book of Mosiah, faith in God is preached by the churches throughout the churches, and the people are taught repentance and faith. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, faith in Christ is steadfast belief and hope in things not seen but true, grows through receiving and nourishing the word, and leads to repentance, redemption, eternal salvation, mighty changes of heart, remission of sins, knowledge of mysteries, preservation, and deliverance. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, faith of Christ grants Alma and Amulek power to break bonds and survive a prison’s collapse, gives the people of Ammon hope concerning death and resurrection, and is held firmly by Moroni while defending his people, rights, country, and religion. In The Book of Helaman, faith of Christ is strengthened among the more humble people through fasting and prayer, contributing to joy, consolation, purification, and sanctification.

faith in Christ in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where faith in Christ first appears

Abstract written by a model

Faith in Christ is steadfast belief and hope in things not seen but true, rather than perfect knowledge. It begins by being taught, received through words, and given place in the heart. As people experiment upon, plant, and diligently nourish the word with patience and care, faith increases. It is joined with the spirit of prophecy, repentance, good works, continual prayer, and obedience. Faith leads people to repentance, brings about redemption, supports the great and eternal plan of redemption, and offers hope unto eternal salvation; without faith unto repentance, people remain exposed to the demands of justice. It also brings mighty changes of heart and remission of sins, enables knowledge of mysteries, and can help bring many souls to repentance. Faith on Jesus Christ withstands temptation, while faith in God brings guidance and miracles. The records show it in Alma's father, the queen, Ammon, Melchizedek, the Lamanites, the righteous church, and the two thousand and sixty. It brings preservation and deliverance to those who do not doubt, stand fast, and obey, and cannot be destroyed by force.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

13 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 5

Faith in the words delivered by Abinadi led Alma's father to a mighty change in his heart. Steadfast belief in the name of Jesus Christ is connected with having sins taken away.

Chapter 7

The people are to have everlasting faith for the remission of their sins and to show faith in the Lamb of God. Their strong faith in the speaker’s teachings brings him great joy.

Chapter 13

Exceeding faith is associated with being called to the holy calling and becoming a high priest. Melchizedek exercised mighty faith before preaching repentance, and people are urged to have faith on the Lord.

Chapter 19

The queen believes Ammon despite having no witness beyond his word and the servants' word. Ammon tells her that she has exceeding faith, and Mosiah trusts Ammon to the Lord according to his faith.

Chapter 25

The law of Moses serves to strengthen the Lamanites' faith in Christ. Through faith and the spirit of prophecy, they retain a hope unto eternal salvation.

Chapter 26

Faith, together with repentance, good works, and continual prayer, is described as a way to receive knowledge of God's mysteries. Those who exercise faith can help bring thousands of souls to repentance.

Chapter 32

Faith is not a perfect knowledge of things but hope for things that are not seen and are true. Faith is exercised by giving place to the word and nourishing it with diligence, patience, and care.

Chapter 33

Faith is used to plant and nourish the word in the heart. Alma tells his listeners to begin to believe in the Son of God.

Chapter 34

Faith is to be exercised unto repentance and is necessary for the great and eternal plan of redemption. Those who have no faith unto repentance remain exposed to the whole law of the demands of justice.

Chapter 37

Faith in God enabled the Liahona to show the way and brought about miracles. Faith in Jesus Christ helps people withstand every temptation of the devil.

Chapter 44

Moroni says the Nephites have been delivered and preserved because of their faith in Christ. He says the Lamanites cannot destroy this faith and that the Nephites will be preserved while faithful.

Chapter 45

Alma blesses those in the church who stand fast in the faith from that time onward.

Chapter 57

The two thousand and sixty have exceeding faith in what they were taught about a just God. Their faith is associated with their preservation and their exact obedience to every command.

Abstract written by a model

Faith of Christ grants Alma and Amulek power to break bonds and survive a prison’s collapse. Held firmly by the people of Ammon, it gives hope concerning death and resurrection; those who die in it are happy and redeemed by the Lord Jesus Christ. Moroni also holds it firmly while defending his people, rights, country, and religion.
