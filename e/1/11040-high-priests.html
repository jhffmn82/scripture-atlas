Abstract written by a model

High priests are priests of God called by faith and ordained in the holy order to teach commandments so people may enter the Lord’s rest. Through faith, repentance, and righteousness they are sanctified by the Holy Ghost, their garments washed white through the Lamb’s blood, and they reject sin with abhorrence.
