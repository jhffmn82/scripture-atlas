king-men

group · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: rebels against Pahoran, remainder of those dissenters, the king-men, those dissenters, those king-men, those people who professed the blood of nobility, those who have risen up in rebellion against us, those who were desirous that Pahoran should be dethroned from the judgment-seat, those who were in favor of kings

Abstract written by a model

The king-men are a political faction and rebellion seeking to dethrone Pahoran, alter the law, and establish a king. They seek power and authority, refuse war against the Nephites during a Lamanite invasion, and cause bloodshed. After Moroni's army kills dissenters, imprisons leaders, and compels others to support liberty, the rebels rise again, drive out Pahoran, withhold provisions, possess Zarahemla, appoint a king, and ally with the king of the Lamanites.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 51

The king-men seek to dethrone Pahoran, alter the law, and establish a king. Moroni's army kills four thousand dissenters, imprisons leaders, and compels the remainder to support liberty.

Chapter 60

The king-men desire power and authority and caused bloodshed by taking up their swords against the people. Their actions weakened the people while they were contending with the Lamanites.

Chapter 61

The rebels rise up against Pahoran and those of his people who are freemen, drive Pahoran out, withhold provisions, and daunt the freemen. They take possession of Zarahemla and appoint a king over themselves.
