Abstract written by a model

Liahona is a compass, ball, or director prepared by God to show the course through the wilderness; it works according to faith, but its marvelous works cease when people are slothful and fail to exercise faith and diligence.

Chapter 37

The Liahona is a ball or director prepared by the Lord to show the fathers the course they should travel in the wilderness. It worked according to their faith, but its marvelous works ceased when they were slothful and forgot to exercise faith and diligence.
