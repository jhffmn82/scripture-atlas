Amlicites

group · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: The people of Amlici, the Amlicites

Abstract written by a model

The Amlicites are a people and Amlici’s followers, distinguished by his name. Armed for war and led by appointed rulers, they fight the Nephites, join the Lamanites, and are defeated and pursued into the wilderness. Fighting alongside the Lamanites, they suffer 12,532 slain, mark their foreheads red, rebel openly against God, and bring a curse upon themselves.

Amlicites is in one work on this site, The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
