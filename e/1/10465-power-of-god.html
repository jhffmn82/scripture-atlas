Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Job, God's power and ways span the realm of the dead, earth, waters, clouds, heavens, sea, Rahab, and the serpent, yet remain only the outskirts of his ways. In The Second Book of Nephi, the power of God enables Nephi to speak boldly, command obedience, and do all things through faith, while false teachers deny it. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the miraculous power of God delivers and preserves people, causes servants to fall, and enables mighty miracles. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record, the power of God brings forth the record and enables faithful people to perform mighty acts and withstand dangers. In The Book of Moroni, the power of God works through gifts according to faith, and denying it prevents perfection in Christ.

God's power and ways in The Book of Job

Abstract written by a model

God's power and ways, as Job describes them in Chapter 26: his power spans the realm of the dead, earth, waters, clouds, heavens, sea, Rahab and the serpent, yet these are merely the outskirts of his ways, a whisper, while the thunder of his power is beyond understanding.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

1 section; each heading opens the text

Chapter 26

God's power is shown over the realm of the dead, the north, the earth, the waters and clouds, the boundary of light and darkness, the pillars of heaven, the sea, Rahab, and the swift serpent. Job calls these things only the outskirts of his ways, a small whisper that is heard. The thunder of his power, he says, no one can understand.

miraculous power of God in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where miraculous power of God first appears

Abstract written by a model

The miraculous power of God delivers and preserves people, causes servants to fall, and enables mighty miracles. It comes upon Alma and Amulek, allowing them to rise, break strong cords, and leave a fallen prison unharmed. It is recognized in the falling of the king, queen, servants, and Ammon, strengthens Ammon and his brethren in their work and escape from imprisonment, brings many Lamanites to repentance, and preserves the wounded two thousand and sixty through faith and God's goodness.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 14

The power of God comes upon Alma and Amulek after the chief judge and others have finished smiting them. It enables them to rise, stand, break their cords, and come out of the fallen prison unharmed.

Chapter 19

Abish recognizes that the falling of the king, queen, servants, and Ammon is caused by the power of God. The people also marvel about the great power shown when the attacker falls dead.

Chapter 26

God's power enabled Ammon and his brethren to perform mighty miracles, endure their work, and escape imprisonment. Through the power of God's word in them, many Lamanites were released from the pains of hell and brought to repentance.

Chapter 57

The miraculous power of God is identified as the reason the wounded two thousand and sixty are preserved. Helaman connects this power with their exceeding faith and the goodness of God.
