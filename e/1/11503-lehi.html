Lehi

person · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: his brother

Abstract written by a model

Lehi, Helaman’s son and Nephi’s brother, is righteous and preaches repentance, prophecy, and God’s word among Nephites and Lamanites for the rest of his days. Driven from Jerusalem for testifying of Christ, he later commands an army that turns the Lamanites toward Zarahemla. Imprisoned with Nephi, he is surrounded by fire unharmed and shines like an angel.

Lehi is in one work on this site, The Book of Helaman. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Moronihah sends Lehi with an army to head Coriantumr’s forces before they reach the land of Bountiful. Lehi’s action causes the Lamanites to begin retreating toward Zarahemla.

Chapter 4

Lehi, together with Nephi, preached and prophesied to the people concerning their iniquities and what would happen if they did not repent.

Chapter 5

Lehi joins Nephi in preaching the word of God among the Nephites and Lamanites. He is imprisoned with Nephi and is encircled by fire without being burned; his face shines as the face of an angel.
