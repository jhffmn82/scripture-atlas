the secret band of Gadianton

group · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: secret band, secret band of Gadianton, secret combination, secret combinations, secret plans, secret plans of Gadianton, their combination

Abstract written by a model

The secret band of Gadianton is a secret combination to murder, rob, and gain power. Established by Gadianton the robber in the more settled parts of the land, it persists because government leaders do not know of it. It includes judges and a judge’s murderer, and its authors are Gadianton and the evil one.
