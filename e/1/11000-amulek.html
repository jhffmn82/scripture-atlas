Amulek

person · 1 work · 24 quoted facts · 16 connections
Also called: I, a man, me, mine, the man

Abstract written by a model

Amulek is a Nephite man of Ammonihah, son of Giddonah, a man of reputation, wealth, household, kindreds, and friends. Though called many times, he had hardened his heart until an angel of the Lord confronted him while he traveled to visit kindred. The angel directed him to receive Alma, and Amulek gave Alma food and shelter; Alma blessed his household, kindred, and possessions. Guided by the Spirit, Amulek became a prophet and preacher alongside Alma. In Ammonihah he testified against wickedness, rejected Zeezrom’s offer to deny a Supreme Being, taught one true and living God, the coming Son of God, resurrection, judgment according to works, and redemption for believers. His words silenced Zeezrom. With Alma, he was bound, imprisoned, beaten, mocked, and deprived of necessities. He grieved for women and children cast into flames, then through faith in Christ broke his bonds and survived the prison’s collapse. Rejected by former friends, father, and kindred, he left Ammonihah, forsook possessions for the word of God, and was cared for by Alma in Zarahemla. He continued with Alma and other fellow laborers, preaching repentance and the word in temples, sanctuaries, synagogues, and among the Zoramites. He taught that Christ would atone for the world’s sins and urged faith, repentance, continual prayer, care for those in need, and patience in affliction.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

10 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 8

Amulek receives Alma into his house, gives him bread and meat, and is blessed by Alma. He later preaches with Alma to the people of Ammonihah with the power of the Holy Ghost.

Chapter 9

Amulek accompanies Alma in preaching to the people who were in the city of Ammonihah. After Alma speaks, Amulek stands forth and begins to preach, though only part of his words is recorded.

Chapter 10

Amulek identifies himself as the son of Giddonah and a descendant of Nephi and Manasseh. He recounts his former rebellion, his encounter with an angel, his reception of Alma, and his testimony against the wickedness of the people, lawyers, and judges.

Chapter 11

Amulek refused Zeezrom’s offer to deny the existence of a Supreme Being. He testified that there is one true and living God, that the Son of God will come to redeem believers, and that all people will be resurrected and judged according to their works.

Chapter 12

Amulek's words silence Zeezrom, and Alma establishes and expands upon them. He had spoken concerning death, resurrection, and being brought before the bar of God to be judged according to works.

Chapter 14

Amulek is bound, imprisoned, beaten, mocked, and deprived of food, water, and clothing with Alma. He asks Alma to save the women and children from the flames, then breaks his bonds through the power granted according to faith in Christ and survives the prison's collapse.

Chapter 15

Amulek departed with Alma for Sidom and had forsaken his gold, silver, and precious things in Ammonihah for the word of God. Alma later took him to his own house in Zarahemla and administered to him in his tribulations.

Chapter 16

Amulek went with Alma preaching repentance and the word of God in temples, sanctuaries, and synagogues.

Chapter 31

Amulek went with Alma among the Zoramites to preach the word. Alma prayed that Amulek would be comforted and strengthened in Christ.

Chapter 34

Amulek teaches that Christ shall come among the children of men, take upon him the transgressions of his people, and atone for the sins of the world. He exhorts the people to exercise faith unto repentance, pray continually, care for those in need, and bear their afflictions with patience.
