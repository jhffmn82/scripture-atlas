Abstract written by a model

The commandments come from God and are given by Jesus. They call people to believe, repent, come with a broken heart and contrite spirit, and govern sacrament, prayer, worship, ministering, worthiness, and avoiding disputations; keeping them brings blessing and is required to enter the kingdom of heaven.

Chapter 12

Jesus gives the people the commandments of his Father, including believing in him, repenting, coming unto him with a broken heart and contrite spirit, and keeping his commandments. Keeping them is required to enter the kingdom of heaven.

Chapter 18

Jesus gives commandments concerning the sacrament, prayer, worship, ministering, worthiness, and avoiding disputations. Those who keep them are blessed and built upon his rock.
