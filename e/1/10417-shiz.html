Chapter 15

Shiz refused to spare the people unless Coriantumr gave himself up so that Shiz could kill him. He led his people in repeated battles against the people of Coriantumr and pursued Coriantumr after his army fled. Shiz was eventually killed by Coriantumr after fainting from loss of blood.
