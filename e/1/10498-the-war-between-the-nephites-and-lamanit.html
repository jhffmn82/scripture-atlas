Abstract written by a model

A war between the Nephites and Lamanites begins in Zarahemla, by the waters of Sidon. The Nephites first win several battles, but war soon resumes and brings repeated attacks, retreats, destruction, and a treaty dividing the lands. After ten years, the Lamanites return to battle and are defeated again; the Nephites vow vengeance for their slain brethren. Over many further years, thousands die on both sides as the Lamanites increasingly defeat and drive back the Nephites, who gain no further power over them.

The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record / Chapter 1

The war begins among the Nephites and Lamanites in the borders of Zarahemla by the waters of Sidon. The Nephites win several battles, and the Lamanites withdraw their design.

Chapter 2

War begins again between the Nephites and the Lamanites in the same year. The conflict continues through repeated attacks, retreats, battles, widespread destruction, and eventual division of the lands by treaty.

Chapter 3

The Lamanites resumed battle after ten years, and the Nephites defeated them in the three hundred and sixty-first and three hundred and sixty-second years. The Nephites then vowed to go to battle against their enemies and avenge their slain brethren.

Chapter 4

The battles continue for many years and cause thousands to be slain on both sides. The Lamanites increasingly defeat and drive back the Nephites, who gain no further power over them after the three hundred and seventy and fifth year.
