Abstract written by a model

Samudrapâla, a Srâvaka’s son, grew up comfortably in Kampâ, married Rûpinî, and learned seventy-two arts. Seeing a condemned man led him to houselessness; he practised monkhood, endured calamities, annihilated Karman, and attained Nirvâna.

Twenty-First Lecture.

Samudrapâla was born at sea to Pâlita’s wife and grew up in comfort in Kampâ. After seeing a condemned man, he became enlightened, entered the state of houselessness, practised the Law of monks, endured calamities, annihilated his Karman, and obtained exemption from transmigration.
