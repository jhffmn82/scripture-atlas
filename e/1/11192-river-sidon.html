Abstract written by a model

The river Sidon runs by the land of Zarahemla in the south wilderness, bordering the land of Manti and a wilderness. Its depths contain bones of slain Lamanites and Amlicites. In battles, Nephites and Lamanites cross it: bodies are thrown into its waters, Zoram meets armies east of it, and Nephites hold both banks to encircle fleeing Lamanites.

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The river Sidon runs by the land of Zarahemla. The Nephites cross it during the battle, and the bodies of slain Lamanites are thrown into its waters to clear a passage.

Chapter 16

The river Sidon was the place the Lamanites would cross in the south wilderness. Zoram and his sons crossed it and met the Lamanite armies on its east side.

Chapter 43

The Lamanites cross the river Sidon while fleeing Lehi and Moroni. The Nephites hold both banks and use the river to encircle the Lamanite armies.
