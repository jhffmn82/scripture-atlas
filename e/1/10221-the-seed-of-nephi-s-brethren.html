Abstract written by a model

The seed of Nephi’s brethren contends with and overpowers Nephi’s seed, then goes forth in multitudes in the land of promise amid wars. It dwindles in unbelief and becomes corrupt. Separated from, scattered, and smitten by Gentiles, it is not utterly destroyed; records come to its remnant.

Chapter 9 .. Chapter 12

The seed of Nephi’s brethren contends against Nephi’s seed and overpowers it. It later goes forth in multitudes, experiences wars, dwindles in unbelief, and becomes dark, loathsome, filthy, idle, and full of abominations.

Chapter 13 .. Chapter 14

The seed of Nephi's brethren is separated from the Gentiles by many waters, scattered, smitten, and subjected to the wrath of God. The Lord will not suffer the Gentiles to utterly destroy it, and the book of the Lamb of God and other records will come unto its remnant.
