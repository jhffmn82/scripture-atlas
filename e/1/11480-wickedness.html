Abstract written by a model

Wickedness is the great and increasing iniquity among the Nephites, recorded down to the coming of Christ. It includes pride arising from riches, oppression, violence, rejection of sacred things and prophecy, murder, robbery, adultery, contention, and secret combinations. Over successive years it grows severe, causing sorrow, affliction, weakness before the Lamanites, loss, and slaughter. The people briefly remember the Lord but forget him again, harden their hearts, reject his counsels, and strengthen in pride and evil. As they ripen in wickedness, it brings fear, curses on people, treasures, and the land, warnings to Zarahemla, Gideon, and nearby cities, and ultimately destruction.

The Book of Helaman

The wickedness and abominations of the Nephites are recorded in the Book of Helaman down to the coming of Christ.

Chapter 4

Wickedness and iniquity among the Nephites included pride, riches, oppression of the poor, withholding food and clothing, violence, mocking sacred things, denying prophecy and revelation, murder, plundering, lying, stealing, adultery, contention, and desertion to the Lamanites. Because of it, they were afflicted, smitten, driven before the Lamanites, and weakened.

Chapter 6

Wickedness increases among the Nephites through riches, secret murders, robbery, plunder, and secret combinations. The Nephites become exceedingly wicked, while the Lamanites grow in knowledge of God and uprightness.

Chapter 7

The people's wickedness had become severe within a short time, involving pride, riches, iniquity, and the influence of the Gadianton robbers. It caused Nephi great sorrow and led him to warn the people of destruction.

Chapter 11

The people's iniquity brings evil, destruction, and fear upon them. After briefly remembering the Lord, they again forget him, grow stronger in iniquity and pride, and ripen for destruction.

Chapter 12

Iniquity causes people to harden their hearts, forget the Lord, reject his counsels, and do evil. Because of iniquity, a treasure may be cursed, and a person may be cursed or cut off from the Lord's presence.

Chapter 13

Wickedness and abominations bring a curse upon the land and warnings of destruction upon Zarahemla, Gideon, and the surrounding cities.
