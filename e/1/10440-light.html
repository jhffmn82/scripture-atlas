Abstract written by a model

Light is absent from the barges before preparation by the Lord, prompting the brother of Jared’s concern about crossing the great water in darkness. The Lord rejects windows and fire; stones then provide continual light in the vessels, above or under water.

Chapter 2

The barges have no light, and the brother of Jared asks the Lord whether the people must cross the great water in darkness. The Lord says they cannot use windows or fire and asks what should be done so they may have light in the vessels.

Chapter 6

The stones provide light in the vessels or barges so that the people do not cross the great waters in darkness. They have light continually, whether above or under the water.
