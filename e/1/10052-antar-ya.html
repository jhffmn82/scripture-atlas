Antarâya

topic · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

Antarâya is Karman, one of eight kinds, preventing entry to eternal bliss by fivefold obstruction of gifts, profit, momentary and continuous enjoyment, and power.

Thirty-Third Lecture.

Antarâya prevents one's entrance on the path that leads to eternal bliss. It is fivefold as preventing gifts, profit, momentary enjoyment, continuous enjoyment, and power.
