Giddianhi

person · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 5 connections

Abstract written by a model

Giddianhi is a robber leader and governor of the secret society of Gadianton. He leads the Gadianton robbers, values their ancient secret works and unconquerable spirit, and demands that Lachoneus surrender the Nephites’ lands and possessions. When refused, he commands war against the Nephites; his armored armies are defeated, and he flees before being overtaken and slain.

Giddianhi is in one work on this site, The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 3

Giddianhi identifies himself as governor of the secret society of Gadianton and demands that Lachoneus surrender the Nephites’ lands and possessions. He threatens to destroy the people if they refuse.

Chapter 4

Giddianhi led the robbers and commanded them to battle against the Nephites because they could not otherwise subsist. His armies were defeated, and he was pursued, overtaken, and slain after much fighting.
