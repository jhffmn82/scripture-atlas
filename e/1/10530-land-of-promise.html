land of promise

Also called: a land of promise, land, the face of this land, the land, the land of my people, this land

Abstract written by a model

The land of promise is choice above all lands, consecrated and kept for those brought there. It is a land of liberty, prosperity, security, and inheritance for the righteous who serve the Lord and keep his commandments; Sam and his seed inherit it like Nephi. If iniquity abounds, it is cursed and people are removed from their possessions.

The Second Book of Nephi / Chapter 1

The land of promise is described as choice above all other lands and consecrated for those whom the Lord brings there. It will be a land of liberty, prosperity, security, and inheritance for the righteous, but iniquity will cause it to be cursed and its people to be removed from their possessions.

Chapter 4

The Lord God promises prosperity in the land to those who keep his commandments. Sam is told that he and his seed will inherit the land like Nephi.
