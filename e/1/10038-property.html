Abstract written by a model

Mrigâputra gives up his fields, house, gold, power, wealth, servants, family claims, and all other property. He renounces possessions permanently, as a snake casts off its slough. A monk should have no property and should be indifferent to dirt and gold. He should not desire gold or silver or engage in buying and selling.
