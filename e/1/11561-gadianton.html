Gadianton

person · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Gadianton the robber

Abstract written by a model

Gadianton is a leader and robber, exceedingly expert in many words and in secret murder and robbery. He leads Kishkumen’s band, promising members power and authority if they gain the judgment-seat. After Kishkumen fails to return, he causes the band’s flight through a secret wilderness way. He helps form bands of murderers and plunderers, gives secret laws, and establishes secret combinations hidden from government leaders. These works eventually cause the overthrow and near-destruction of the people of Nephi.

Gadianton is in one work on this site, The Book of Helaman. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Gadianton was exceedingly expert in many words and in the craft of carrying on the secret work of murder and robbery. He became the leader of Kishkumen's band, promised its members power and authority, and later caused the overthrow and near-destruction of the people of Nephi.

Chapter 3

Gadianton the robber establishes secret combinations in the more settled parts of the land, but those at the head of government do not know of them.

Chapter 6

Gadianton helps form the band of murderers and plunderers and gives them secret laws. Satan puts secret oaths, covenants, and works of darkness into Gadianton’s heart.
