Abstract written by a model

More than a thousand Lamanite converts, actual descendants of Laman and Lemuel, come to a knowledge of the truth and join the people of God. Brought into God’s fold, light, and salvation, they rejoice, bury their weapons from love for brethren, hate sin, and choose martyrdom rather than kill an enemy.

Chapter 24

More than a thousand Lamanites are brought to a knowledge of the truth and join the people of God. None of these converts are Amalekites, Amulonites, or of the order of Nehor; they are actual descendants of Laman and Lemuel.

Chapter 26

The Lamanite converts were brought into the fold of God and into his everlasting light and salvation. They showed sincerity through love for their brethren, willingness to sacrifice their lives, and hatred of sin.
