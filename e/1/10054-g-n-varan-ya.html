Gñânâvaranîya

Also called: Obstruction of knowledge, both Âvaranîyas, obstruction to right knowledge

Abstract written by a model

Gñânâvaranîya is a Karman that obstructs right knowledge through five forms: sacred-book, perception, supernatural, thought-reading, and highest unlimited knowledge.

Thirty-Third Lecture.

Gñânâvaranîya acts as an obstruction to right knowledge. Its obstruction is fivefold, concerning Sruta, Âbhinibôdhika, Avadhigñâna, Manahparyâya, and Kêvala knowledge.
