Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the people of Anti-Nephi-Lehi are converted Lamanites who repent of murder, bury their weapons, refuse to fight, and become the people of Ammon in Jershon. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the people of Ammon are faithful former Lamanites who refuse weapons, care for poor believers, deal with Korihor, and later leave Jershon for Melek.

people of Anti-Nephi-Lehi in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where people of Anti-Nephi-Lehi first appears

Abstract written by a model

The people of Anti-Nephi-Lehi are a people, originally Lamanites, converted unto the Lord. Their sore repentance leads them to fear sin, repent of murders, refuse arms, and bury their weapons deep in the earth as a testimony to God. They covenant not to shed their brethren’s blood, choosing to give their own lives instead; when war comes, they do not prepare or fight, and 1,005 are slain while calling on the Lord. Many Lamanites then join them as the people of God. At the Lord’s command they leave the land of Nephi, receive Jershon as an inheritance, and become known as the people of Ammon. Protected by the Nephites, they support Nephite armies with their substance. Though compassion later moves them to consider taking up arms, Helaman persuades them to keep their oath.

people of Ammon in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where people of Ammon first appears

Abstract written by a model

The people of Ammon, formerly called the people of Anti-Nephi-Lehi and formerly Lamanites, were numbered among the church of God. They were marked by zeal toward God and men, perfect honesty, uprightness, and firm faith in Christ. Established in Jershon, they abhorred shedding their brethren’s blood, refused weapons, and would suffer death rather than fight, trusting in Christ and the resurrection. They received the poor Zoramite believers, providing food, clothing, lands, and care according to need. In Jershon they also took Korihor, bound him, and brought him before Ammon, who had him carried from the land. They eventually left Jershon for Melek so Nephite armies could use the land.

Chapter 27

The people of Anti-Nephi-Lehi were called the people of Ammon after taking possession of the land of Jershon. They were distinguished for zeal toward God and men, honesty, uprightness, and firm faith in Christ.

Chapter 28

The people of Ammon were established in the land of Jershon, where they were received and kept safe.

Chapter 30

The people of Ammon were established in the land of Jershon. They took Korihor, bound him, and carried him before Ammon, who caused him to be carried out of the land.

Chapter 35

The people of Ammon refused to cast out the Zoramites who came to them and instead received and supported the poor among them. They later departed from the land of Jershon and moved to the land of Melek to make room for the Nephite armies.
