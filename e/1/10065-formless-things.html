Abstract written by a model

Formless things, a division of Things without life, comprise ten kinds: Dharma, Adharma, space and time, including their divisions and indivisible parts.

Thirty-Sixth Lecture.

Formless things are one of the two divisions of Things without life and are of ten kinds: Dharma, its divisions and indivisible parts; Adharma, its divisions and indivisible parts; space, its divisions and indivisible parts; and time.
