charity

topic · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: everlasting love, perfect love, pure love of Christ, this love

Abstract written by a model

Charity is the pure, everlasting love of Christ. It is patient, kind, humble, selfless, truthful, enduring, and unfailing, neither envious nor easily angered. It accompanies faith and hope; without it a person is nothing and cannot be saved, while those possessing it at the last day fare well.
