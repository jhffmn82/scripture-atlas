Chapter 19

The multitude disperses after Jesus ascends, then gathers again after hearing that Jesus will show himself. It is divided into twelve bodies, taught by the disciples, led to kneel and pray, follows the disciples to the water, witnesses the Holy Ghost and fire, and hears and understands the words of Jesus's prayer in their hearts.
