Shule

person · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 9 connections

Abstract written by a model

Shule, son of Kib, is king of the kingdom of Shule. Mighty in strength and judgment, he makes steel swords, defeats Corihor, restores Kib, and reigns in righteousness. After defeating Noah’s forces and being rescued from captivity, he gives Corihor power, judges those who revile prophets, rules prosperously without wars, has children, and remembers the Lord’s great deed.

Shule is in one work on this site, The Book of Ether. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 7

Shule becomes mighty in strength and judgment, makes steel swords at the hill Ephraim, defeats Corihor, and restores the kingdom to Kib. He reigns in righteousness, defeats Noah’s forces, is rescued after being taken captive, gives Corihor power, executes judgment against those who revile the prophets, and rules during a time of prosperity and no wars.
