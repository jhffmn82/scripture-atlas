Abstract written by a model

The priests of Noah are priests serving king Noah, installed after Noah replaces his father’s priests with men proud in heart. Supported by the people’s taxes, they practice laziness, idolatry, whoredoms, and lying and vain speech while claiming to teach the law of Moses and understand prophecy. They question and accuse Abinadi, advise Noah against releasing him, and help lead to his death. After fleeing into the wilderness, they abduct twenty-four daughters of the Lamanites, steal grain and precious things, and cause destruction that leads Limhi’s people to seek their punishment. Later, under Amulon, they oppress Alma’s people, bring them into iniquity, join the Lamanites, and possess and till the land of Amulon.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 11

Noah replaces the priests consecrated by his father with new priests lifted up in the pride of their hearts. Noah's priests are supported by the people's taxes, engage in laziness, idolatry, and whoredoms, and speak lying and vain words to the people.

Chapter 12

The priests question Abinadi in order to accuse him, but he withstands and confounds them. They claim to teach the law of Moses, yet Abinadi says they have not kept it, have set their hearts upon riches, committed whoredoms, and caused the people to sin.

Chapter 17

The priests advise king Noah concerning Abinadi and accuse him of reviling the king. They oppose releasing him, and Abinadi is ultimately delivered to be slain.

Chapter 20

The priests of king Noah remain in the wilderness because they fear returning to Nephi. They watch the daughters of the Lamanites and carry twenty-four of them into the wilderness.

Chapter 21

The priests of Noah have fled into the wilderness, stolen the daughters of the Lamanites, and caused great destruction. Limhi’s people seek to capture and punish them, and the priests have also stolen grain and precious things.

Chapter 23

The priests of king Noah oppress Alma’s people and bring them into iniquity. Led by Amulon, they join the Lamanites and begin to possess and till the land of Amulon.
