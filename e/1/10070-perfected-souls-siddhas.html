perfected souls (siddhas)

group · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: The perfected souls, blessed perfected souls, perfected souls, the perfected souls (siddhas)

Abstract written by a model

Perfected souls are living beings including women, men, hermaphrodites, orthodox, heterodox, and householders. On reaching perfection they go to Îshatprâgbhâra at the world's top, barred from Alôka, formless, living, knowing, faithful, and incomparably happy.
