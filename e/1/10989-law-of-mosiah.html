Abstract written by a model

The law established by Mosiah governs the people of Nephi, requiring obedience and punishing liars, thieves, robbers, and murderers. It provides judges, their wages, reckoning, and measures; directs judgment of transgressors; and requires matters such as establishing a king to be decided by the voice of the people.

Chapter 1

The law established by Mosiah is acknowledged by the people and governs their conduct. It punishes liars, thieves, robbers, and murderers, and is applied to transgressors when possible.

Chapter 2

The law requires matters such as establishing a king to be decided by the voice of the people.

Chapter 11

The law of Mosiah required judges to receive wages according to the time they labored and established the reckoning and measures used by the Nephites.
