Lêsyâ

topic · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: Lêsyâs

Abstract written by a model

Lêsyâ is a Karman-produced state of the soul. It has six types, black, blue, grey, red, yellow, and white, with specified degrees, innumerable varieties, and durations. The unit describes its qualities, results, conjunction with, and leading to new existence, including conditions governing birth.

Lêsyâ is in one work on this site, Uttaradhyayana Sutra. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Thirty-Fourth Lecture.

Lêsyâs are six in number and are named black, blue, grey, red, yellow, and white. This unit describes their qualities, degrees, varieties, durations, results, and conditions at birth.
