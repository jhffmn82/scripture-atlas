love and hatred

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: Hatred, Love, Love and hatred, attachment

Abstract written by a model

Love and hatred cause Karman, bondage, and misery; arising from attachment or aversion, monks avoid them and austerities destroy them for final deliverance.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Thirtieth Lecture.

Love and hatred are identified as the causes through which the monk acquired the Karman that austerities destroy.

Thirty-Second Lecture.

Love and hatred are to be destroyed as causes of bondage and misery. They arise through attachment or aversion to pleasant and unpleasant objects.
