Âyuhkarman

topic · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: Âyushka

Abstract written by a model

Âyuhkarman is one of the eight kinds of Karman. It determines length of life and is fourfold, referring to denizens of hell, brute creation, men, and gods. Its duration ranges from a part of a muhûrta to thirty-three Krores of Krores of Sâgarôpamâs.

Thirty-Third Lecture.

Âyuhkarman determines the length of life. It is fourfold as referring to denizens of hell, brute creation, men, and gods, with a longest duration of thirty-three Krores of Krores of Sâgarôpamâs and a shortest duration of a part of a muhûrta.
