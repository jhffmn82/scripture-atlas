Abstract written by a model

A covenant, administered by Satan, begins among robbers to keep peace and gain lands, then expands through leaders to oppose righteousness, protect murderers, destroy the governor, and establish a king.

Chapter 6

Some robbers entered into a covenant to keep the peace and were granted lands. Later, lawyers, judges, high priests, and kindreds entered into a covenant administered by the devil to oppose righteousness, free murderers from justice, destroy the governor, and establish a king.
