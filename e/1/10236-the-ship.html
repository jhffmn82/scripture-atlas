The ship is built by Nephi after his brethren rebel against him. It is used to cross the large waters.

Nephi constructs the ship from timbers according to the manner shown by the Lord rather than the manner of men. The family boards it with provisions, seeds, wives, and children, and it carries them toward the promised land.
