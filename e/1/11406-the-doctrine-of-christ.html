the doctrine of Christ

topic · 3 works · 8 quoted facts · 4 connections
Also called: doctrine of Christ, gospel, the good word of Christ, the only and true doctrine, this is the doctrine of Christ

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Nephi, the doctrine of Christ is described as the only and true doctrine of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost, unfolding through repentance, baptism by water, entering in by the way, receiving the Holy Ghost, following Jesus Christ, and enduring to the end until Christ manifests himself in the flesh. In The Book of Jacob, the Brother of Nephi, the doctrine of Christ is preached by Jacob, opposed by a man and Sherem, and defended when Jacob confounds Sherem. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the doctrine of Christ is regarded by the people as a foolish and vain thing.

the doctrine of Christ in The Second Book of Nephi

Abstract written by a model

The doctrine of Christ is the only and true doctrine of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost. It unfolds through repentance, baptism by water, entering in by the way, receiving the Holy Ghost, following Jesus Christ, and enduring to the end, until Christ manifests himself in the flesh.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 31

The doctrine of Christ includes repentance, baptism by water, receiving the Holy Ghost, following Jesus Christ, and enduring to the end. Nephi identifies it as the only and true doctrine of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost.

Chapter 32

The doctrine of Christ includes entering in by the way and receiving the Holy Ghost. No more doctrine will be given until Christ manifests himself in the flesh.
