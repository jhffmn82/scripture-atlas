Land possessed by the Nephites: King Laman grants it to Zeniff, whose people repair Shilom’s walls and cultivate it. Noah later builds there and overlooks it from towers. During Limhi’s escape, his people travel around it in the wilderness.

Chapter 9

King Laman allows Zeniff to possess the land of Shilom and commands his people to depart. Zeniff’s people build and repair the walls of the city of Shilom and cultivate the surrounding land.

Chapter 11

Noah builds many buildings in the land of Shilom and builds a tower on the hill north of it. From a tower near the temple, Noah can overlook the land of Shilom.

Chapter 22

The people of Limhi travel around the land of Shilom in the wilderness during their escape.
