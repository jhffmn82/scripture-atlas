B. Living Beings.

Denizens of hell are divided according to seven hells called Ratnâbha, Sarkarâbha, Vâlukâbha, Paṅkâbha, Dhûmâbha, Tamâ, and Tamatamâ. Their shortest and longest durations increase according to the hell in which they live.
