land northward

Abstract written by a model

The land northward, called Desolation, lies beyond a small neck separating it from the land southward. The Nephites guard it against Lamanite overrunning. It has large bodies of water, receives migrants from Zarahemla, is approached through a pass, and is supplied by ship.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 22

The land northward is separated from the land southward by a small neck of land. It includes Desolation, and the Nephites prevent the Lamanites from overrunning it.

Chapter 50

The land northward is covered with large bodies of water and is the destination Morianton plans to reach. Teancum stops the fleeing people at the pass leading into it.

Chapter 63

The land northward received a company of men and many other people from Zarahemla. Corianton went there by ship to carry provisions, and several voyages were directed there.
