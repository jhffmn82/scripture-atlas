Abstract written by a model

The contentions belong to the Nephites and are internal conflicts among the people. They occur across multiple years, including the forty-sixth through forty-eighth years, begin to cease late in the forty-eighth, and give way to continual peace in the forty-ninth; refusal to heed Nephi brings division.

The Book of Helaman

The contentions of the Nephites are recorded in the Book of Helaman.

Chapter 3

Contention occurs among the people in the forty-sixth through forty-eighth years, then begins to cease in the latter end of the forty-eighth year. Continual peace is established in the forty-ninth year.

Chapter 10

After the people refuse to hearken to Nephi’s words, contentions begin among them, and they become divided against themselves.
