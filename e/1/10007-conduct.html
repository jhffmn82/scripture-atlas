Abstract written by a model

Conduct, a cause of final deliverance, destroys all Karman through five forms: avoiding sin, novice initiation, austerity purity, reducing desire, and annihilating sinfulness under Arhat precepts.

Twenty-Eighth Lecture.

Conduct is one of the four causes of final deliverance and produces the destruction of all Karman. It is fivefold, including the avoidance of everything sinful, initiation of a novice, purity from peculiar austerities, reduction of desire, and annihilation of sinfulness according to the precepts of the Arhats.
