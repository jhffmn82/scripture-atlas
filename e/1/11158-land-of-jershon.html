land of Jershon

place · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: Jershon, land, the land of Jershon, this land Jershon

Abstract written by a model

The land of Jershon, east by the sea and joined to land Bountiful, was given by the Nephites to the people of Anti-Nephi-Lehi for an inheritance. It became a place where the people of Ammon and a church were established after the Lamanites were driven out. It received Alma, Amulek, their brethren, and cast-out Zoramites. Later it served as a gathering and guarded place for Nephite armies as Moroni met the Lamanites.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 27

The land of Jershon was given by the Nephites to the people of Anti-Nephi-Lehi for an inheritance. It was on the east by the sea and joined the land Bountiful.

Chapter 28

The land of Jershon became the place where the people of Ammon and a church were established. The armies of the Nephites were set round about it.

Chapter 30

The land of Jershon became the place where the people of Ammon were established after the Lamanites were driven out. Korihor preached there but was taken and carried before Ammon.

Chapter 35

The land of Jershon received Alma, Amulek, their brethren, and many Zoramites who had been cast out. The people of Ammon later gave place in the land of Jershon for the armies of the Nephites.

Chapter 43

Jershon is where the Nephites gather their armies and where Moroni first meets the Lamanites. Moroni leaves part of his army there to guard against a Lamanite attack.
