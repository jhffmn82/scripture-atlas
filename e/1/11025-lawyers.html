Abstract written by a model

Lawyers are a profession hired or appointed to administer law before judges, learned in arts and cunning. Seeking gain, they chiefly work through Zeezrom to accuse and trap Amulek and Alma and pervert righteous ways.

The lawyers are hired or appointed to administer the law and are learned in the arts and cunning of the people. They seek to trap Amulek and Alma, pervert the ways of the righteous, and gain wealth through their profession.
