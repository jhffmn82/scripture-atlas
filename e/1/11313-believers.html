Abstract written by a model

Believers have faith in prophets and their fathers’ tradition, remember Jesus and receive his Spirit. Sorrowful when unbelievers deny the prophecies, they steadfastly await the promised sign. Though threatened with destruction and death, they are preserved when the sign appears.

Believers become very sorrowful when unbelievers say the time for the prophecies has passed, but they watch steadfastly for the promised sign. A day is set apart for believers to be put to death unless the sign comes, and they are preserved when the sign appears.
