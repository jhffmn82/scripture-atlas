Abstract written by a model

The people reject Ether’s words, remain unrepentant, and experience widespread wickedness. Under Satan’s power, their hearts are hard and minds blind. Because of the curse upon the land, they cling to property, refuse borrowing or lending, and carry swords for defense. They divide between the armies of Shiz and Coriantumr as war, secret violence, robbery, bloodshed, deaths, displacement, and destruction spread. After many days of fighting, only a few remain.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

The people reject Ether’s words and continue in war, secret violence, robbery, and wickedness. Many are slain, and there is none to restrain the bloodshed.

Chapter 14

The people guarded their property and lives because of the curse upon the land. They joined the armies of Shiz or Coriantumr, and many people were killed, displaced, or swept away during the war.

Chapter 15

The people did not repent of their iniquity. They gathered into opposing armies, fought for many days, and were destroyed until only a few remained.
