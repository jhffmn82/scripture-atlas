Abstract written by a model

The tame olive-tree, in a nourished vineyard, grows old and decays. Its master prunes and nourishes it, removes main branches, grafts in wild branches, and plants natural branches elsewhere. With further care and grafting, its roots are preserved and natural fruit is restored.

Chapter 5

The tame olive-tree grows old and begins to decay, so the master prunes it, digs about it, and nourishes it. Its main branches are removed, wild olive branches are grafted in, and its natural branches are planted elsewhere. After further grafting and care, its natural fruit is restored.
