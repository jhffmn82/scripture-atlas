things possessing form

topic · 1 work · 10 quoted facts · 1 connection
Also called: those possessing form

Abstract written by a model

Things possessing form are without life and have four kinds: compound things, their divisions, indivisible parts, and atoms. Their development flows without beginning or end, while each individual has a beginning and end. Duration ranges from one Samaya to an immeasurable period, with interruptions from one Samaya to endless time.

Thirty-Sixth Lecture.

Things possessing form are one of the two divisions of Things without life and are of four kinds: compound things, their divisions, their indivisible parts, and atoms. Their longest duration is an immeasurable period and their shortest duration is one Samaya.
