Akish

person · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 5 connections

Abstract written by a model

Akish, son of Kimnor and friend of Omer, seeks Jared’s daughter and power. After Jared promises her for Omer’s head, he leads kindred and friends through oaths and fair promises in a secret combination, overthrows Omer, marries Jared’s daughter, murders Jared, becomes king, kills one son by starvation, and wars with his other sons.

Akish is in one work on this site, The Book of Ether. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 8

Akish was the son of Kimnor and a friend of Omer. Jared's daughter pleased him by dancing, and Akish formed a secret combination after Jared promised her to him if he brought Omer's head.

Chapter 9

Akish and his friends overthrow the kingdom of Omer. Akish marries Jared’s daughter, arranges the murder of his father-in-law through a secret society, reigns in Jared’s place, imprisons and starves one of his sons to death, and wars with his other sons.
