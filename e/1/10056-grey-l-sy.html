grey Lêsyâ

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 0 connections

Abstract written by a model

Grey Lêsyâ is one of the lowest Lêsyâs, coloured like the Atasî flower, Kôkila feathers, or pigeons’ collars, and infinitely sour. It develops in dishonest, base, deceitful, heretical, hurtful, sinful, thieving, jealous people and brings the soul into miserable courses of life.

Thirty-Fourth Lecture.

The grey Lêsyâ has the colour of the flower of Atasî, the feathers of the Kôkila, or the collar of pigeons. It develops in a dishonest, base, deceitful, heretical, hurtful, thieving, and jealous person; it is one of the lowest Lêsyâs and brings the soul into miserable courses of life.
