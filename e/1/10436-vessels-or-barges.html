Abstract written by a model

The vessels or barges are small, light upon the water, and exceedingly tight like a dish and the ark of Noah. With peaked ends, a tight-shut door, no light, and air holes in the top and bottom, they carry people, food, flocks, herds, beasts, animals, and fowls safely across the great water and sea.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

The barges are built small, light upon the water, and exceedingly tight, with peaked ends and a door that is tight when shut. They have no light, require holes in the top and bottom for air, and are intended to carry the people across the great water.

Chapter 6

The vessels or barges carry the people, food, flocks, herds, beasts, animals, and fowls across the sea. They are tight like a dish and like the ark of Noah, so water cannot hurt the people inside.
