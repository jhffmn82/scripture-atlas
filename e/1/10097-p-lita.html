Abstract written by a model

Pâlita is a merchant and Srâvaka, Mahâvîra’s disciple, who travels by boat to Pihunda for business, marries a merchant’s daughter, and heads toward Kampâ.

Twenty-First Lecture.

Pâlita was a merchant and a Srâvaka who was a disciple of the noble and venerable Mahâvîra. While doing business in Pihunda, he received a merchant’s daughter in marriage and later brought her with him toward Kampâ.
