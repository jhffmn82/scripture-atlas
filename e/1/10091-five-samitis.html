five Samitis

Also called: (five) Samitis, Samitis, Samitis.

Abstract written by a model

The five Samitis are disciplines of walking, speech, begging, handling necessary articles, and disposing bodily waste; broadly, they include the three Guptis.

Twenty-Fourth Lecture.

The Samitis are five disciplines concerning walking, speech, begging, handling necessary articles, and disposing of bodily waste. In their wider application, the term Samiti includes the three Guptis.
