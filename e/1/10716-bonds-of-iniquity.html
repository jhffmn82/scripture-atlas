bonds of iniquity

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 7 connections
Also called: iniquities, iniquity, the bands of iniquity, the bonds of iniquity

Abstract written by a model

Bonds of iniquity are a bondage from which Alma’s people are delivered. Iniquities are laid upon the Lord’s servant, bring affliction with diseases, are testified against by Abinadi, and are brought upon Alma’s people by King Noah and his priests.
