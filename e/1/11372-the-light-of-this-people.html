the light of this people

topic · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: light, the light, your light

Abstract written by a model

The people are given light by Jesus, to shine through good works before others. Like an unhidden city on a hill, it is held up by disciples to the world, following Jesus so others glorify their Father in heaven.

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 12

Jesus gives the people to be the light of this people. He teaches that a city set on a hill cannot be hid and that their light should shine so others may see their good works and glorify their Father in heaven.

Chapter 18

Jesus says that he is the light and commands the people to hold up their light to the world by following what they have seen him do.
