Also called: beatitude, deliverance

Abstract written by a model

Final deliverance is bliss attained when the soul destroys remaining Karman and rises to the highest Akâsa, accompanying perfection, enlightenment, and final beatitude through true knowledge, avoidance, and destruction.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Twenty-Ninth Lecture.

Deliverance is attained after the soul destroys the remaining Karman and rises to the highest Akâsa. It accompanies perfection, enlightenment, and final beatitude.

Thirty-Second Lecture.

Final deliverance is reached through true knowledge, avoidance of ignorance and delusion, and destruction of love and hatred. It is described as nothing but bliss.
