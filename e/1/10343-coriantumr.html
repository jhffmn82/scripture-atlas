Coriantumr

person · 1 work · 19 quoted facts · 6 connections
Also called: he, himself, his

Abstract written by a model

Coriantumr is Omer’s child and a king over all the land who possesses the kingdom through his sons. Skilled in every art of war, he initially refuses repentance despite prophecy. Captured and wounded in the thigh by Shared, he is freed by his sons, kills Shared, and cannot fight for two years. He later defeats Gilead and Lib, kills Lib at Agosh, and flees Shiz before suffering many deep wounds near the seashore and hill Comnor. After recovering, he remembers Ether’s words, mourns the destruction of nearly two million people, repents, and offers Shiz the kingdom to spare the people. He continues leading battles, kills Shiz, and falls as if lifeless.

Coriantumr is in one work on this site, The Book of Ether. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 13

Coriantumr studies the arts of war and the cunning of the world and fights those seeking to destroy him. He refuses to repent, is captured by Shared and later freed by his sons, kills Shared, and is wounded in the thigh so that he cannot fight for two years.

Chapter 14

Coriantumr fought the brother of Shared, Gilead, and Lib, defeating and pursuing them at different times. He killed Lib on the plains of Agosh, then fled before Shiz until the armies fought for three days by the seashore and later at hill Comnor. Shiz inflicted many deep wounds on him, causing him to faint and be carried away as though dead.

Chapter 15

Coriantumr recovered from his wounds, remembered Ether’s words and the prophets, sorrowed over the deaths of nearly two million people, and repented of his evil. He wrote to Shiz asking him to spare the people and offered to give up the kingdom. Coriantumr led his people in repeated battles, killed Shiz, and then fell to the earth as if lifeless.
