atonement of Christ

topic · 2 works · 4 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: atonement

Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Jacob, the Brother of Nephi, the atonement of Christ is necessary for mankind, reconciles people to God through Christ, enables resurrection, presents them as Christ’s first-fruits unto God, and is testified of by Jacob through the Holy Ghost. In The Book of Moroni, the atonement of Christ answers the law’s ends, claims those with faith in Christ, and provides hope of resurrection to eternal life.

atonement of Christ in The Book of Jacob, the Brother of Nephi

Jacob · the work’s page · read it where atonement of Christ first appears

Abstract written by a model

The atonement of Christ is necessary for mankind: without it all must be lost. It reconciles people to God through Christ, enables resurrection, and presents them as Christ’s first-fruits unto God; Jacob testifies of it through the Holy Ghost.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

The atonement of Christ is presented as the means by which people may be reconciled unto God. Through it, they may obtain a resurrection and be presented as the first-fruits of Christ unto God.

Chapter 7

Jacob declares that if no atonement were made, all mankind must be lost. His testimony of the atonement is made manifest to him by the power of the Holy Ghost.
