Abstract written by a model

The Atonement of Jesus Christ, prepared from the foundation of the world, is made by God himself through his blood. It atones for Adam’s transgression and for sins, including ignorant sin and those who died without knowing God’s will; it blesses infants and little children, brings salvation to those who endure in faith, and prevents God’s people from perishing.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The blood of Jesus Christ atones for the sins of those who died without knowing God’s will or who sinned ignorantly. Salvation, including the blessing of infants and little children, comes through his atoning blood.

Chapter 4

The atonement was prepared from the foundation of the world so that salvation might come to those who trust in the Lord, diligently keep his commandments, and continue in faith to the end of mortal life.

Chapter 13

The atonement is made by God himself for the sins and iniquities of his people. Without it, they must unavoidably perish even if they keep the law of Moses.
