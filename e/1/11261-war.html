Abstract written by a model

War is a long-running conflict involving the Nephites and the Lamanites, sometimes also marked by contentions among the Nephites. It brings thousands and tens of thousands of deaths, sending souls to the eternal world. Wars and contentions occur from the first through the fifteenth years of the reign of the judges; after years without conflict, the Lamanites invade again in the fourteenth year. The account returns to war in the eighteenth year, when the Nephites defend their lands, families, liberty, rights, and worship of God. Though reluctant to shed blood and taught to fight only to preserve life, the Nephites are compelled to continue contending for many years. Lamanites attack fortified Nephite cities, but Nephite preparations and the leadership of Moroni and Lehi bring victory. After Amalickiah's death, Ammoron continues the war, claiming rights to the government, while Moroni calls it an unjust war and seeks peace.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The wars and contentions in the fifth year of the reign of the judges include the battle between the Lamanites and Amlicites and the later Lamanite attack. Thousands and tens of thousands die and are sent to the eternal world to reap rewards according to their works and the spirit they obeyed.

Chapter 16

After many years without wars or contentions, a cry of war was heard when the Lamanites invaded the land. The Lamanites did not come again to war against the Nephites until the fourteenth year of the reign of the judges.

Chapter 28

Wars and contentions occurred among the Nephites, as well as wars between the Nephites and the Lamanites, from the first year through the fifteenth year of the reign of the judges.

Chapter 43

The account returns to the wars between the Nephites and the Lamanites in the eighteenth year of the reign of the judges. The conflict concerns the defense of lands, families, liberty, rights, and worship of God.

Chapter 48

The Nephites are taught to fight only when necessary to preserve their lives, and they are reluctant to shed blood. Despite their reluctance, they are compelled to contend with the Lamanites, and the wars continue for many years.

Chapter 49

The war in this unit involved Lamanite attacks on fortified Nephite cities. The Nephites prevailed because of their preparations and the leadership of Moroni and Lehi.

Chapter 54

The war had been waged against the Nephites and was still being continued after Amalickiah's death. Moroni said it concerned the defense of the Nephites, while Ammoron said it was to avenge wrongs and obtain and maintain rights to the government.

Chapter 55

Ammoron wages the war against the people of Nephi without a just cause, according to Moroni. The conflict continues as Moroni seeks to force the Lamanites to sue for peace.
