Abstract written by a model

The valley of Lemuel is a valley near the Red Sea, at the mouth of the river Laman. After three days in the wilderness, Lehi pitches his tent there; it becomes where Nephi's father dwells during marriages and the command to journey, and an image of steadfast commandment-keeping.
