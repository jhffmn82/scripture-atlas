Abstract written by a model

The people of Limhi are Nephites living in the land of Nephi under king Limhi. They are fewer than the Lamanites, held in bondage, and compelled to pay grievous tribute. Limhi gathers them at the temple, teaches that their iniquities brought their afflictions, and urges them to turn to God. After hearing Ammon recount the history of their brethren and the words of king Benjamin, they endure an attack arising from the Lamanites’ belief that they abducted their daughters; they defend themselves, capture the wounded king, and resolve the matter without arms. Oppression, repeated defeats, hunger, mourning, and many widows follow. Unable to contend by the sword, they humble themselves, cry to God, prosper, raise grain more abundantly, covenant to serve God, and desire baptism. Led by Ammon and his brethren, they leave by night with women, children, flocks, herds, tents, provisions, gold, silver, and precious things. After many days in the wilderness, they arrive in Zarahemla, are delivered from bondage, become subjects of Mosiah, and are baptized by Alma into the church of God.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

6 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

The people of Limhi were in bondage to the Lamanites and paid a grievous tribute. Limhi gathered them at the temple and taught that their iniquities had brought their afflictions, while urging them to turn to God.

Chapter 8

The people of king Limhi hear Ammon recount the history of their brethren and the words of king Benjamin. They possess records and artifacts brought back from a discovered land.

Chapter 20

The people of Limhi are attacked because the Lamanites think they abducted their daughters. They defend themselves, capture the wounded king, and meet the Lamanites without arms after the matter is explained.

Chapter 21

The people of Limhi suffer oppression, repeated defeats, mourning, hunger, and bondage under the Lamanites. They humble themselves, cry to God, begin to prosper, enter into a covenant with God, and desire baptism.

Chapter 22

The people of Limhi gather their flocks and herds, depart by night with their women and children, and take their provisions, gold, silver, and precious things. They travel through the wilderness to the land of Zarahemla, join Mosiah’s people, and become his subjects.

Chapter 25

The people of Limhi desired to be baptized, and Alma baptized them after the manner he had baptized his brethren in the waters of Mormon.
