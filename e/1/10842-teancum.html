Teancum

person · 1 work · 15 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: every man of Teancum

Abstract written by a model

Teancum is a Nephite military leader and commander, a valiant defender of liberty who serves under Moroni. He leads armies against Morianton, kills him, defeats his people, takes prisoners, and returns to Moroni’s camp. He repulses Amalickiah and the Lamanites at Bountiful, then enters Amalickiah’s tent at night and kills him with a javelin. He fortifies and defends his position, follows Moroni’s orders, and helps capture Mulek by drawing the Lamanites away. Later, with Lehi, he is left in charge of war and commands part of Moroni’s army. Finally, angered by Ammoron, he attacks and wounds him at night but is slain by Ammoron’s servants.

Teancum is in one work on this site, The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 50

Teancum leads Moroni's army against the people of Morianton at the narrow pass. He kills Morianton, defeats his army, takes them prisoners, and returns to Moroni's camp.

Chapter 51

Teancum repulses Amalickiah and the Lamanites at the borders of the land Bountiful. He later enters Amalickiah's tent at night and causes his death.

Chapter 52

Teancum prepares to battle the Lamanites, defends his position, and receives orders from Moroni. He leads the Lamanites away from the city of Mulek, helping Moroni capture it.

Chapter 61

Teancum is left with Lehi in charge of conducting the war in that part of the land. Pahoran sends provisions to Teancum and Lehi until Moroni can come.

Chapter 62

Teancum was given command over part of Moroni's army and later encamped with Lehi and Moroni against the Lamanites. In anger toward Ammoron, he entered the Lamanite camp and wounded Ammoron with a javelin, but Ammoron's servants pursued and slew him.
