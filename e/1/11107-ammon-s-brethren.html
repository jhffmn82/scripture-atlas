Abstract written by a model

Ammon’s brethren are a group of just, holy prophets and Ammon’s brothers who preach among the Lamanites. They separate in the borders of the land of the Lamanites and journey in the land of Nephi, laboring exceedingly despite rejection, beating, imprisonment, hunger, thirst, nakedness, binding, violence, and other afflictions. They endure patiently and are released from imprisonment in Middoni through Lamoni and Ammon. Saved by God from their sinful state, they become instruments of God in bringing Lamanites to repentance. Their preaching, records, and words convince many thousands, bring much fruit, and produce success, joy, reception, and safety in the land of Jershon.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 20

Ammon’s brethren are imprisoned in Middoni after being rejected, beaten, and driven from place to place. They suffer hunger, thirst, affliction, nakedness, and binding with strong cords, yet remain patient, and are released through Lamoni and Ammon.

Chapter 21

Ammon and his brethren separate in the borders of the land of the Lamanites to preach the word. They suffer rejection, imprisonment, and hardship, but continue preaching and bring many to a knowledge of the truth.

Chapter 26

Ammon's brethren labor with him among the Lamanites, enduring travel, privation, rejection, mocking, violence, imprisonment, and other afflictions. They rejoice because many people have been brought to repentance through their work.

Chapter 28

Ammon’s brethren are described as journeying in the land of Nephi, suffering and experiencing sorrow, affliction, incomprehensible joy, and reception and safety in the land of Jershon.

Chapter 29

The speaker rejoices in the success of his brethren who went to the land of Nephi. They labored exceedingly and brought forth much fruit.
