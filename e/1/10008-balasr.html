Abstract written by a model

Balasrî, also called Mrigâputra, is a crown-prince living in Nandana. After recalling a former birth and understanding vows and suffering, he rejects pleasures, renounces property, enters the Sramana order, practises austerities, and reaches perfection.

Nineteenth Lecture.

Balasrî is the son of King Balabhadra and Mrigâ, the crown-prince of Sugrîva. After remembering his former birth, he rejects pleasures, renounces his property, enters the Sramana order, and practises austerities until he reaches perfection.
