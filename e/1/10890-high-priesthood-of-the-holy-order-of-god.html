high priesthood of the holy order of God

Abstract written by a model

The high priesthood of the holy order of God is a holy order received through holy calling and ordinance, after the order of the Only Begotten Son and prepared from eternity. After delivering the judgment-seat to Nephihah, Alma retained it and devoted himself to testimony, revelation, and prophecy.
