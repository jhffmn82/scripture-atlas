judgment-seat

Also called: the judgment-seat, the place of the judgment-seat

Abstract written by a model

The judgment-seat is a contested place of leadership. Pahoran is appointed to it and murdered there, then Pacumeni succeeds him. Later Helaman is appointed amid contention and targeted by Kishkumen. Finally, Seezoram is found dead there, and five men are wrongly accused.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

The judgment-seat becomes the focus of contention among Pahoran, Paanchi, and Pacumeni. Pahoran is appointed to it, is murdered while sitting upon it, and is succeeded by Pacumeni.

Chapter 2

There was initially no one to fill the judgment-seat, causing contention among the people. Helaman was appointed to it, and Kishkumen sought to reach it in order to murder him.

Chapter 9

The judgment-seat is where Seezoram is found lying dead in his blood. The people gather there and wrongly identify the five men as his murderers.
