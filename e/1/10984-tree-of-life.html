tree of life

object · 1 work · 6 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: the tree of life, tree

Abstract written by a model

The tree of life is in the garden of Eden, where its fruit could provide everlasting life. After the Fall it was guarded by cherubim and a flaming sword, preventing partaking until there was a space for repentance. Those who come to God, repent, come to Christ, and nourish the word with an eye of faith may partake of its fruit and receive life forever.

Chapter 5

Those who come to God and repent may partake of the fruit of the tree of life.

Chapter 12

The tree of life could not be partaken of by the first parents after the Fall, because doing so would have prevented death and the preparatory state. Partaking of it then would have frustrated the plan of redemption.

Chapter 32

The fruit of the tree of life cannot be plucked if the word is not nourished while looking forward with an eye of faith.

Chapter 42

The tree of life was guarded by cherubim and a flaming sword so that man could not partake of its fruit and live forever without a space for repentance.
