Abstract written by a model

Joseph, Lehi's last-born son, is born in the wilderness during Lehi's greatest sorrow. Lehi blesses him forever with a conditional inheritance, teaches lineage and promises, and assures that the fruit of Joseph's loins will not be utterly destroyed.
