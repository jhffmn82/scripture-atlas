blue Lêsyâ

topic · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 0 connections
Also called: blue one

Abstract written by a model

Blue Lêsyâ is one of the lowest Lêsyâs. Blue like Asôka, the Kasha’s tail, or lapis lazuli, it tastes intensely pungent. It develops in people marked by envy, anger, ignorance, deceit, greed, hatred, carelessness, pleasure-seeking, and sinful undertakings, bringing the soul into miserable courses of life.

Thirty-Fourth Lecture.

The blue Lêsyâ has the colour of the blue Asôka, the tail of the Kasha, or lapis lazuli. It develops in a person marked by envy, anger, want of self-control, deceit, greed, hatred, carelessness, and sinful undertakings; it is one of the lowest Lêsyâs and brings the soul into miserable courses of life.
