Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Nephi, the lost tribes of Israel, led away by the Lord God, write his words, whose words join those of the Jews and Nephites. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the other tribes of the house of Israel are led away and separated because of iniquity and remain known to the Father, though unknown to Jerusalem and the twelve disciples.
