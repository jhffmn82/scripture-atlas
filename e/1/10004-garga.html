Abstract written by a model

Garga, a kind, grave, meditating learned Sthavira and Ganadhara leading a Gana, reflects on flawed things, becomes disheartened by bad pupils, leaves lazy donkeys, and practises severe austerities.

Twenty-Seventh Lecture.

Garga is a Sthavira and Ganadhara and the learned sage who makes reflections about cars, religious exercise, bad bullocks, and bad pupils. After becoming disheartened by bad pupils, he leaves the lazy donkeys and practises severe austerities.
