Abstract written by a model

Moron, son of Ethem, reigns wickedly, is challenged, regains the kingdom by overthrowing a mighty man, then is overthrown by another and lives the rest of his days in captivity; he begets Coriantor.

Moron is in one work on this site, The Book of Ether. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 11

Moron reigned after Ethem and did that which was wicked before the Lord. He was challenged by a mighty man, later overthrew him and regained the kingdom, and was then overthrown by another mighty man and spent the rest of his days in captivity.
