Chapter 21

The servant of the Lord is called from the womb, made a sharp sword and a polished shaft, and hidden in the Lord's hand and quiver. He is appointed to bring Jacob back, restore Israel, serve as a light to the Gentiles, and be given as a covenant of the people.
