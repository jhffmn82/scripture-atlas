Alma the younger

person · 1 work · 17 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: Alma (son), Alma, who was the son of Alma, he, thyself

Abstract written by a model

Alma the younger begins as an unbelieving, wicked, idolatrous man who flatters people into dissension, steals hearts from the church of God, and seeks its destruction. After an angelic rebuke, he repents, is born of God, beholds God’s marvelous light, and is redeemed despite having rejected and denied the Redeemer. Made a new creature, he preaches the word of God throughout the land despite persecution. King Mosiah gives him the plates, records, and interpreters, commanding him to preserve them, record the people, and pass them from generation to generation.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 27

Alma (son) was a wicked and idolatrous man who used flattery to lead people astray and sought to destroy the church of God. After the angel of the Lord rebuked him, he repented, was born of God and redeemed, and began preaching the word of God.

Chapter 28

Alma the younger receives the plates of brass, the plates of Nephi, the records, and the interpreters from king Mosiah. He is commanded to keep and preserve them and to keep a record of the people, handing them down from one generation to another.
