Abstract written by a model

The Sramana order demands difficult vows: impartiality, non-injury, truthfulness, non-stealing, chastity, non-possession, fasting, and endurance. It confers Sramana status through equanimity. Mrigâputra enters with parental permission; Gayaghôsha urges Vigayaghôsha to renounce attachments and enter.

Nineteenth Lecture.

The Sramana order requires difficult duties, including impartiality, abstention from injury, truthfulness, non-stealing, chastity, non-possession, fasting rules, and endurance of hardship. Mrigâputra enters the order after his parents permit him to do so.

Twenty-Fifth Lecture.

Entering the monastic order requires giving up worldly attachment and former connections and taking delight in noble words. Gayaghôsha urges Vigayaghôsha to enter it at once so that he will not continue drifting on the dreadful ocean of Samsâra.
