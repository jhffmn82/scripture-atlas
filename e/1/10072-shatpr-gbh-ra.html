Îshatprâgbhâra is a pure, white-gold place twelve Yôganas above Sarvârtha where perfected souls go, shaped like an open umbrella, vast in length and breadth, and thickest at its middle.

B. Living Beings.

Îshatprâgbhâra is the place twelve Yôganas above Sarvârtha where perfected souls go. It is shaped like an open umbrella, consists of white gold, and is forty-five hundred thousand Yôganas long and broad.
