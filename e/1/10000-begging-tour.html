Abstract written by a model

The begging-tour occurs in the third Paurushî of the day. Food and drink may be sought to prevent illness, serve the Guru, follow walking and self-control rules, save life, or meditate on the Law. The monk should contentedly continue his begging-tour whether he receives alms or not.
