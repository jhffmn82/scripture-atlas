fortifications

Also called: fortified the city Gid, forts, strongholds, the fortifications, the fortifications round about the city Gid

Abstract written by a model

Fortifications are strong Lamanite-held defenses: timber works on earth ridges, high pickets, overlooking towers, and secure places atop them. Strongholds are prepared around Nephite cities; later Lamanites shelter in them, Moroni draws them out, and fortifications at Gid and Morianton are strengthened.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 50

Strongholds are prepared around every Nephite city to protect against the Lamanites and prevent them from retaining power in their lands.

Chapter 52

The Lamanites seek protection in their fortifications, and Teancum decides not to attack them directly because of their strength. Moroni’s plan draws the Lamanites out of their strongholds.

Chapter 55

Moroni causes the Lamanite prisoners to strengthen the fortifications around city Gid. The Lamanites also fortify city Morianton until it becomes an exceedingly stronghold.
