Abstract written by a model

The sealed words remain undelivered in the book during the people’s wickedness and abominations. At the Lord’s due time, they are read on house tops by Christ’s power and reveal all things from the foundation of the world to its end.

Chapter 27

The sealed words are not delivered during the day of the wickedness and abominations of the people. They remain in the book until the Lord’s due time, when they will reveal all things from the foundation of the world to its end.
