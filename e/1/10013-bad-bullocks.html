bad bullocks

topic · 1 work · 12 quoted facts · 2 connections
Also called: A bad bullock, The cursed beast, lazy donkeys

Abstract written by a model

Bad bullocks tire out a car driver through beating, resist goads, bite or wound their mate, break rope or yoke, make for a young cow, attack furiously, go backward, stand still as if dead, then run off roaring after breaking loose. They are compared with bad pupils yoked to the car of the Law.

Twenty-Seventh Lecture.

Bad bullocks may bite or wound their mate, break the pin or yoke, leave the road, fall down, sit or lie down, jump or caper, pursue a young cow, attack, go backward, stand still, run at full speed, rend the rope, and break loose. They are compared with bad pupils yoked to the car of the Law.
