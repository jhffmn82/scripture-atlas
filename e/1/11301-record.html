Abstract written by a model

Mormon’s record, based on Nephi and written on Nephi’s plates, is a just and true small account spanning Lehi’s departure from Jerusalem to Mormon’s day, containing Mormon’s own observations though many things cannot be written.

record is in one work on this site, The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
