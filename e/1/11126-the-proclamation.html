Abstract written by a model

A proclamation sent by the king of the Lamanites throughout his land and surrounding regions. It protects Ammon, Aaron, Omner, Himni, and their preaching brethren from harm and expulsion, and grants them free access to houses, temples, and sanctuaries so they may preach as they desire.

Chapter 22

The king sends a proclamation throughout all his land and among the people in the surrounding regions, including areas bordering the seas and wilderness.

Chapter 23

The proclamation ordered the Lamanites not to harm, imprison, expel, or attack Ammon, Aaron, Omner, Himni, or their preaching brethren. It gave them free access to houses, temples, and sanctuaries so they could preach according to their desires.
