Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, Bountiful is the land southward of the line dividing it from Desolation, spanning the east sea to the west sea, where Nephites inhabit its wilderness and Hagoth builds an exceedingly large ship on its border with Desolation. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, land Bountiful is a Nephite-protected land and an exceeding stronghold that Moroni fortifies, with Lehi protecting the city and prisoners building a ditch and high timber-and-earth wall around it. In The Book of Helaman, the city of Bountiful is the intended target of Coriantumr's march as he seeks to cut through to obtain the north parts of the land, but he is headed before reaching it. In The Book of Helaman, the land of Bountiful is fortified from the west sea eastward, where Coriantumr marches toward Bountiful and Nephites under Moronihah fortify after being driven there.

land Bountiful in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where land Bountiful first appears

Abstract written by a model

Land Bountiful is a Nephite-protected land, bordered by Teancum’s and Amalickiah’s camps. Moroni fears its loss could threaten Nephite liberty; Teancum repulses approaching Lamanites at its borders. Moroni then fortifies it and secures the narrow pass, while Lehi protects the city. Lamanite prisoners build a ditch and high timber-and-earth wall around the city, making the land an exceeding stronghold where they are guarded.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 50

The land Bountiful is where Moroni and his people are located when Moroni fears that Morianton will gain possession of land and threaten Nephite liberty.

Chapter 51

The Lamanites march toward the land Bountiful but are met and repulsed by Teancum and his men at its borders.

Chapter 52

Moroni orders Teancum to fortify land Bountiful and secure the narrow pass leading into the land northward. The city Bountiful is protected by Lehi and a small army.

Chapter 53

The Lamanite prisoners are marched back into the land Bountiful and forced to dig a ditch and build a strong wall around the city. The land becomes an exceeding stronghold, and the prisoners are guarded there.
