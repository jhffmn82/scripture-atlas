records

Also called: all the records, record, the records, these records, those records

Abstract written by a model

The records are sacred plates of Nephi containing holy scriptures and the people’s history, including Laman and Lemuel’s rebellions. Helaman receives and must preserve them for a wise purpose: to enlarge memory, reveal God’s power, convince people to repent, and bring them to salvation. They remain protected if he keeps God’s commandments.

Chapter 37

The records enlarge the memory of the people, convince many of the error of their ways, and bring them to the knowledge of God and salvation. They helped Ammon and his brethren convince many thousands of the Lamanites to repent.
