Pahoran

person · 1 work · 19 quoted facts · 9 connections
Also called: chief governor, chief judge, chief judge Pahoran, governor of the land

Abstract written by a model

Pahoran, son of Nephihah, becomes chief judge and governor over the people of Nephi in the twenty and fourth year of the reign of the judges, filling his father’s judgment-seat. Bound by oath and sacred ordinance, he is charged to judge righteously, preserve peace and freedom, protect sacred worship, support the cause of God, and bring the wicked to justice. He refuses petitions to alter the law and remains in office after the people favor the freemen. As chief judge and governor, he receives Moroni’s request to gather men for Helaman’s armies. Rebels led by Pachus drive him from the judgment-seat, forcing him to Gideon, where he gathers freemen, sends provisions to Lehi and Teancum, and asks Moroni to help reclaim Zarahemla. With Moroni he defeats the rebels, is restored to office, and helps obtain the city of Nephihah.

Pahoran is in one work on this site, The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

Chapter 50

Pahoran, the son of Nephihah, is appointed chief judge and governor at the end of the twenty and fourth year. He is charged to judge righteously, preserve peace and freedom, protect sacred worship, support the cause of God, and bring the wicked to justice.

Chapter 51

Pahoran refuses to alter the law and remains chief judge after the voice of the people favors the freemen.

Chapter 59

Pahoran receives an epistle from Moroni requesting that he cause men to be gathered together to strengthen Helaman’s armies.

Chapter 60

Pahoran is the chief judge and governor over the land and is the recipient of Moroni's letter.

Chapter 61

Pahoran, the chief governor, has been driven out by rebels and fled to Gideon with as many men as possible. He gathers freemen, plans to resist the rebels, and asks Moroni to join him in reclaiming Zarahemla.

Chapter 62

Pahoran was driven from the judgment-seat by Pachus and his men, then joined Moroni in defeating them and was restored to the judgment-seat. He later marched with Moroni toward the land of Nephihah and helped obtain possession of the city.
