plates of Nephi

object · 7 works · 15 quoted facts · 16 connections
Also called: other plates, plates, plates of Nephi, which contain the records and the sayings of our fathers, plates which were called the plates of Nephi, the plates of Nephi, these plates

Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, the plates of Nephi preserve Nephi’s people’s ministry, prophecies, instruction, and a greater account of wars, contentions, and destructions, and are kept for instruction and wise purposes and handed down until further commandments of the Lord. In The Words of Mormon, the plates of Nephi provide Mormon’s abridgment down to the reign of king Benjamin and the remainder of his record. In The Book of Mosiah, the plates of Nephi contain true records and sayings of the fathers from their departure from Jerusalem onward, whose truth King Benjamin affirms and which King Mosiah preserves before conferring them upon Alma the younger. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the plates of Nephi contain records to be kept and preserved as sacred and are part of the records entrusted to Helaman. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the plates of Nephi bear Nephi’s account and most teachings of Jesus, from which Mormon makes his record. In The Fourth Book of Nephi, Who Is the Son of Nephi, One of the Disciples of Jesus Christ, the plates of Nephi hold the records kept by Nephi, Amos, and his son Amos. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record, the plates of Nephi are taken by Mormon at Ammaron’s command, and Mormon engraves his observations concerning the people on them, making a record containing a full account of their wickedness and abominations.
