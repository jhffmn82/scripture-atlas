Abstract written by a model

The words of Isaiah concern all the house of Israel: the Lord God’s dealings with the Gentiles, Israel’s scattering and gathering, the Messiah, judgments, and future events. Nephi records and likens them to his people and children to prove his words and Christ’s coming. Though difficult for some, they are plain through prophecy and bring rejoicing and latter-day worth.

Chapter 6

The words of Isaiah concern all the house of Israel and describe the Lord God’s dealings with the Gentiles, the scattering and gathering of Israel, and the coming of the Messiah.

Chapter 11

Nephi writes more of the words of Isaiah and will liken them to his people. These words are sent forth to Nephi’s children and are intended to help prove the truth of Nephi’s words and the coming of Christ.

Chapter 25

The words of Isaiah concern judgments and future events and are difficult for some to understand. They are plain to those filled with the spirit of prophecy and will be of great worth to Nephi’s people in the last days.
