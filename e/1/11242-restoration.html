restoration

topic · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: plan of restoration, restoration of those things, word restoration

Abstract written by a model

Restoration reunites soul and body, restoring every limb, joint, and hair to a proper, perfect frame and fulfilling what the prophets have spoken. Required by the justice of God, it returns all things to proper order, bringing like for like: righteousness, justice, and mercy for their corresponding qualities, but evil, carnal, and devilish things for the same. It condemns rather than justifies the sinner.

Chapter 40

Restoration brings souls and bodies back together and restores every limb, joint, and hair to its proper and perfect frame. It fulfills what the prophets have spoken.

It brings all things back to their proper order and returns evil for evil, carnal for carnal, devilish for devilish, and good for good. It condemns the sinner and does not justify him.
