Abstract written by a model

The plates of brass are written scriptures said to be fulfilled by the coming of Jesus Christ and by destructions. Readers are told to search them; Jesus expounds them, commands teaching, and wants additional scriptures written.

Chapter 10

They are said to have been fulfilled by the events. Whoever reads them is told to search them and see whether the destructions fulfill the prophecies of the holy prophets.

Chapter 23

Jesus expounds all the scriptures that the people had received and commands them to teach the things he has expounded. The scriptures include the things that had been written and the other scriptures Jesus wanted written.
