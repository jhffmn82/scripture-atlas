Lehi

person · 1 work · 13 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: our father Lehi, our father, Lehi

Abstract written by a model

Lehi is a Nephite military leader, one of Zoram’s two sons, who came from the land of Jerusalem and was a father in Alma’s line. He helps recover captives, then repeatedly serves with Moroni in major battles. He conceals an army near hill Riplah, attacks the Lamanites from behind, and drives them across the river Sidon. Moroni appoints him chief captain over the city of Noah, where the Lamanites fear him greatly. He protects Bountiful with a small army, takes command of Mulek, and later shares command of Moroni’s army and the war with Teancum. He rejoices with Moroni and mourns Teancum’s death.

Lehi is in one work on this site, The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

Lehi was one of Zoram's two sons and helped lead the Nephite armies in searching for and recovering the captives.

Chapter 43

Lehi leads an army concealed south of hill Riplah. He attacks the Lamanites from the rear, drives them across the river Sidon, and keeps his army on the eastern bank.

Chapter 49

Lehi was appointed by Moroni as chief captain over the city of Noah. The Lamanites feared Lehi exceedingly when they learned that he commanded the city.

Chapter 52

Lehi and a small army protect the city Bountiful and meet the retreating Lamanites. He presses upon their rear, causing many to surrender their weapons.

Chapter 53

Lehi goes with Moroni to the city of Mulek and receives command of it. He has been with Moroni in most of his battles, and he and Moroni rejoice in each other’s safety.

Chapter 61

Lehi is left with Teancum in charge of conducting the war in that part of the land. Pahoran sends provisions to Lehi and Teancum so they will not perish until Moroni arrives.

Chapter 62

Lehi was given command over part of Moroni's army with Teancum. He later joined Moroni in pursuing the Lamanites and was exceedingly sorrowful when Teancum died.
