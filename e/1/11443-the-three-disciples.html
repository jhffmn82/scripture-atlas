the three disciples

group · 1 work · 20 quoted facts · 11 connections
Also called: the three, the three who were to tarry, those whom the Lord hath chosen, three, three who were caught up into the heavens

Abstract written by a model

The three disciples desire to bring souls to Jesus while the world stands. They never taste death and are changed to immortality when Jesus comes in glory. In their changed state they have no pain, sorrow except for the sins of the world, and fulness of joy; they are as angels of God, see and hear unspeakable things in heaven, and are forbidden to utter them. They unite the church of Christ, receive no harm, remain hidden while among Gentiles and Jews, minister to scattered Israel and all peoples, and bring souls to Jesus. They may show themselves, work great and marvelous works, and remain until Christ’s judgment day.
