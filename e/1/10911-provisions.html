Chapter 56

Provisions are sent from the fathers of the two thousand young men, and additional provisions arrive to support the forces, their wives, and their children. Helaman’s march near Antiparah is presented as if he were carrying provisions to another city.

Chapter 60

The armies suffer and nearly perish with hunger because food is withheld. Moroni demands food for their support and says God will provide it even if it must be obtained by the sword.
