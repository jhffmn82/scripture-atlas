Lib

Abstract written by a model

Lib, son of Kish and brother of Lib, was a just king of great stature who did good in the sight of the Lord. During his reign serpents were destroyed, hunting expanded southward, and the north filled with inhabitants. A great hunter, he fathered sons and daughters including Hearthom, then gained the kingdom after Gilead’s murder and died fighting Coriantumr.

Lib is in one work on this site, The Book of Ether. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 10

Lib reigned after Kish and did what was good in the sight of the Lord. In his days the poisonous serpents were destroyed, the people hunted in the land southward, and the land northward became covered with inhabitants. Lib became a great hunter, and he lived many years and begat sons and daughters, including Hearthom.

Chapter 14

Lib was a man of great stature, more than any other man among all the people. After a secret combination murdered Gilead, Lib obtained the kingdom and fought Coriantumr. Coriantumr smote him on the plains of Agosh until he died.
