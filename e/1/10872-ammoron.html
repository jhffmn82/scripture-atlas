Abstract written by a model

Ammoron is a descendant of Zoram, brother of Amalickiah, and king and commander of the Lamanites. Appointed after Amalickiah, he orders the Lamanites to hold captured cities and leads war against the people of Nephi without a just cause. He negotiates and disputes prisoner exchanges with Moroni and Helaman, seeking advantage while declaring the war will continue until the Nephites submit or are extinguished. He supports Lamanite forces at Cumeni with provisions and an army. While with his armies in the land of Moroni, Teancum pierces him with a javelin; Ammoron's servants kill Teancum, and Ammoron dies.

Ammoron is in one work on this site, The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 52

Ammoron, the brother of Amalickiah, is appointed king over the Lamanites. He commands his people to maintain the cities they have taken and leads forces against the Nephites.

Chapter 54

Ammoron sent Moroni a request to exchange prisoners and later wrote an epistle in response to Moroni. He agreed to exchange prisoners to preserve food for his men of war, but declared that the war would continue until the Nephites were subjected to Lamanite authority or eternally extinguished.

Chapter 55

Ammoron has a perfect knowledge of the fraud concerning the war against the people of Nephi. He refuses to grant Moroni’s epistle, so Moroni refuses to exchange prisoners with him.

Chapter 57

Ammoron offers to deliver Antiparah if Helaman will surrender the prisoners of war, but refuses Helaman's proposed exchange. He later sends provisions and a numerous army to support the Lamanites at Cumeni.

Chapter 62

Ammoron, the king of the Lamanites, was with the Lamanite armies in the land of Moroni. Teancum cast a javelin at him and pierced him near the heart, and Ammoron's servants then slew Teancum before Ammoron died.
