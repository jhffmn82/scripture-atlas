Abstract written by a model

A large number of people attempted to return to the land of Nephi to possess the land of their inheritance. Their strong and stiffnecked leader caused contention, all but fifty were slain in the wilderness, and the survivors returned to the land of Zarahemla; another group later departed, including Amaleki’s brother, whose fate was unknown.
