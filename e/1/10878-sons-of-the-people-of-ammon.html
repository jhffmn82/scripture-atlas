Abstract written by a model

The sons of the people of Ammon are a military force of young descendants of Laman, initially two thousand and later two thousand and sixty. Though bound by a covenant not to take up weapons against their brethren, they enter a covenant to defend the Nephites, protect their country, and fight for liberty. They call themselves Nephites and choose Helaman as leader. Before battle they are inexperienced, yet valiant, strong, truthful, sober, obedient, and continually trusting God. Joined to Antipus’s army after prisoners are sent to Zarahemla, they help secure victory at Cumeni. Later with Helaman in Manti, they suffer many wounds but remain standing; none is slain. Their steadfastness is marked by exact obedience, remembrance of the Lord God, observance of commandments, and strong faith in prophecy.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 53

Two thousand young men of the people of Ammon, who are not bound by the oath against shedding blood, enter a covenant to fight for the liberty of the Nephites and protect the land. They are valiant, strong, active, truthful, sober, and taught to keep the commandments of God, and they choose Helaman as their leader.

Chapter 56

The stripling Ammonites are two thousand young descendants of Laman who take up weapons of war to defend their country. They follow Helaman into battle despite having never fought before, and none of them is killed.

Chapter 57

Sixty sons of the Ammonites join their two thousand brethren in Helaman's army. The two thousand and sixty fight firmly, obey commands exactly, and help secure victory at Cumeni.

Chapter 58

The sons of the people of Ammon were with Helaman in the city of Manti. Although they received many wounds, not one of them was slain, and they remained steadfast in liberty, remembered the Lord God, kept his statutes, judgments, and commandments, and had strong faith in prophecy.
