Abstract written by a model

The Lord’s wife is a once-barren, forsaken, grieved, refused wife of youth with children and seed. The Lord promises her restoration: an enlarged tent and habitations, freedom from remembered shame and widowhood’s reproach, establishment in righteousness, and safety far from oppression and terror.

Chapter 22

The woman is described as barren, forsaken, grieved in spirit, and refused, but the Lord calls her his wife. She is promised restoration, expansion, protection, and freedom from shame and reproach.
