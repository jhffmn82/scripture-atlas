the gospel of Christ

Abstract written by a model

In The Second Book of Nephi, the gospel of Jesus Christ is declared among the remnant of Nephi’s seed to restore them to knowledge of their fathers and of Jesus Christ. In The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman, the gospel of Christ is given by Jesus and preached to the people of Nephi, the Gentiles, and the remnant of Jacob. In The Fourth Book of Nephi, Who Is the Son of Nephi, One of the Disciples of Jesus Christ, the gospel of Christ is rejected and opposed by the Lamanites, Lemuelites, and Ishmaelites. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record, the gospel of Jesus Christ is known by the scriptures, set before a remnant, and preached to every creature. In The Book of Ether, the gospel of Christ is commanded to be believed, and those who believe and are baptized in Jesus Christ’s name shall be saved.

the gospel of Christ in The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman

Mormon · the work’s page · read it where the gospel of Christ first appears

Abstract written by a model

The gospel of Christ is the fulness given by Jesus and preached to the people of Nephi, the Gentiles, and the remnant of Jacob. Jesus teaches that he came to do the Father’s will, was lifted up upon the cross, and draws all men to judgment according to their works; those in the church are to do the works they have seen him do. The three disciples preach it broadly, converting those who accept it and uniting them to the church of Christ. Its fulness is manifested to the Gentiles and then brought to the house of Israel: hardened, rejecting Gentiles lose it, while repentant Gentiles are numbered among Israel. It then begins the Father’s work among the remnant of Jacob and the dispersed house of Israel.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

5 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 16

The fulness of the gospel will be manifested unto the Gentiles and brought to the house of Israel. If the Gentiles sin against it and reject its fulness, the Father will bring the fulness of the gospel from among them; if they repent and return, they will be numbered among the house of Israel.

Chapter 20

When the Gentiles have received the fulness of the gospel, they may still harden their hearts against Jesus Christ. If they do, the Father will return their iniquities upon their own heads.

Chapter 21

The gospel will be preached among the remnant of Jacob, beginning the work of the Father among that people and the dispersed house of Israel.

Chapter 27

The gospel given by Jesus is that he came into the world to do the will of the Father, was lifted up upon the cross, and draws all men to be judged according to their works. Those in the church are told to do the works they have seen Jesus do.

Chapter 28

The three disciples preach the gospel of Christ to all the people of Nephi and to all people upon the face of the land. Those who accept it are converted to the Lord and united to the church of Christ.

gospel of Jesus Christ in The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record

Mormon · the work’s page · read it where gospel of Jesus Christ first appears

Abstract written by a model

The gospel of Jesus Christ is a message known by the scriptures, available among Gentiles, and set before a remnant in records from Jews and Gentiles. Mormon urges the ends of the earth to believe it, while another witness affirms that Jesus is the Christ and God. The gospel goes from Gentiles to the seed of the people to strengthen belief, calls people to lay hold on it, and is preached to every creature; believers who are baptized are saved.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Mormon urged the ends of the earth to believe the gospel of Jesus Christ, which they would have among them. He also testified that the Jews would have another witness that Jesus was the very Christ and the very God.

Chapter 5

The gospel will go forth from the Gentiles to the seed of the people. It is intended to help that seed more fully believe.

Chapter 7

The people are told to lay hold upon the gospel of Christ. It is set before them in this record and in the record that will come unto the Gentiles from the Jews and from the Gentiles unto them.

Chapter 9

The gospel of Christ is not understood by those who deny revelations and spiritual gifts. It is to be preached to every creature, and those who believe and are baptized shall be saved.
