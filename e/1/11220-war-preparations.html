Abstract written by a model

War preparations were defensive measures during war between the Lamanites and Nephites in the eighteenth year of the reign of the judges. They centered on walls, fortifications, earthworks, timber structures, pickets, towers, and secure places. Moroni and Teancum strengthened cities, land Bountiful, and its narrow pass, while Moroni supplied armies and relieved women and children from famine and affliction.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 35

War between the Lamanites and the Nephites commenced in the eighteenth year of the reign of the judges. The armies of the Nephites contended with the armies of the Lamanites and the Zoramites.

Chapter 50

Moroni continues making preparations for war by ordering defensive earthworks, timber structures, pickets, towers, and places of security around the cities.

Chapter 52

Teancum prepares walls and places of resort to defend his men. Moroni orders the defense and strengthening of land Bountiful, its narrow pass, and nearby cities.

Chapter 53

Moroni employs his men in preparing for war and making fortifications against the Lamanites. He also provides food for the armies and delivers women and children from famine and affliction.
