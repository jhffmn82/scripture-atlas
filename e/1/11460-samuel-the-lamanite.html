Abstract written by a model

Samuel the Lamanite is a prophet and servant of Jesus who testifies to Jesus’ people. Some reject his words about a sign, though it is fulfilled. Commanded to foretell saints rising, appearing, and ministering, he gives a prophecy later fulfilled according to Jesus’s words.

Samuel the Lamanite is in one work on this site, The Third Book of Nephi, the Son of Nephi, Who Was the Son of Helaman. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.
