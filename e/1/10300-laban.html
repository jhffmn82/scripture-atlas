Laban

person · 1 work · 9 quoted facts · 5 connections

Abstract written by a model

Laban, a mighty man, possesses the records on the plates of brass. He refuses requests for them, takes property, seeks Nephi’s life, and sends servants against Nephi and his brothers. Found drunk outside his house in Jerusalem, he is delivered into Nephi’s hands and killed with his own sword; Nephi takes his sword, garments, and armor.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

Laban possesses the records on the plates of brass and refuses Laman's request for them. He rejects Nephi and his brothers when they offer their property, lusts after their wealth, sends servants to slay them, and is promised to be delivered into their hands.

Chapter 4

Laban is found drunk outside his house in Jerusalem. The Spirit says that the Lord has delivered him into Nephi’s hands, and Nephi kills him with his own sword because he had sought Nephi’s life, rejected the Lord’s commandments, and taken Nephi’s family’s property.
