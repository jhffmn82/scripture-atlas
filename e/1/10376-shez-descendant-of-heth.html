Chapter 10

Shez was a descendant of Heth, whose household had perished except for Shez. He rebuilt a broken people, built a righteous kingdom, remembered the Lord’s deliverance of Jared and his brother, walked in the Lord’s ways, built many cities, and lived to an exceedingly old age.
