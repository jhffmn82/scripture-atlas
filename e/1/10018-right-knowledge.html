Abstract written by a model

Right knowledge is a fivefold kind of knowledge, Sruta, Âbhinibôdhika, Avadhi, Manahparyâya, and Kêvala, and one of four causes of final deliverance. It requires right faith and supports virtuous conduct. True knowledge leads toward deliverance as duties remove its obstructions, while Gñânâvaranîya obstructs its forms.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Twenty-Eighth Lecture.

Right knowledge is one of the four causes of final deliverance and is fivefold: Sruta, Âbhinibôdhika, Avadhi, Manahparyâya, and Kêvala. Without right faith there is no right knowledge, and without right knowledge there is no virtuous conduct.

Thirty-Second Lecture.

True knowledge is taught as a means to final deliverance. The dispassionate person who performs all duties removes the obstructions to right knowledge.

Thirty-Third Lecture.

Right knowledge is obstructed by Gñânâvaranîya. The obstruction includes knowledge derived from the sacred books, perception, supernatural knowledge, knowledge of the thoughts of other people, and the highest, unlimited knowledge.
