Abstract written by a model

The tradition of the Lamanites' fathers is an incorrect, destructive set of teachings that contributes to Lamanite ignorance. Though some follow it while others are called Nephites for believing correct traditions and God’s commandments, the sons of Mosiah and missionaries expose its baseness, convince many, and help them learn it is not correct.

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The Lamanites follow traditions that are described as incorrect and that would prove destructive. Those who believe the correct traditions of their fathers, together with the commandments of God, are called Nephites.

Chapter 9

The people of Ammonihah have forgotten the traditions of their fathers, while the traditions of the Lamanites have contributed to their ignorance. The Lamanites will eventually learn that those traditions are incorrect.

Chapter 17

The sons of Mosiah seek to bring the Lamanites to understand the baseness of the traditions of their fathers, which were not correct.

Chapter 21

The missionaries convince many of their sins and of the traditions of their fathers that were not correct.
