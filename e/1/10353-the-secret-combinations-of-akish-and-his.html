the secret combinations of Akish and his friends

group · 1 work · 8 quoted facts · 13 connections
Also called: murderous combinations, secret combination, secret combinations, secret society, the secret plans again of old, this wicked and secret society

Abstract written by a model

Akish and his friends form a wicked, secret combination through oaths and fair promises. Seeking power, gain, and the kingdom, it overthrows Omer, spreads corruption through the people, and causes rebellion and rejection of prophets. It ultimately fights against Coriantumr, with many slain, illustrating the destructive threat such combinations pose to freedom in all lands, nations, and countries.

Akish and his kindred formed a secret combination through oaths and fair promises. Such combinations are described as abominable and wicked, built to gain power and gain, and destined to cause destruction.

The secret combinations of Akish and his friends overthrow the kingdom of Omer. The spreading of the wicked and secret society corrupts the hearts of all the people.

Chapter 11

A secret combination was built up to obtain power and gain, causing rebellion among the people. Coriantor's people also rejected the prophets because of their secret society and wicked abominations.
