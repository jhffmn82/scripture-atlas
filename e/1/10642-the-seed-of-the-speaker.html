Chapter 30

The remnant of Nephi’s seed will learn that Nephi’s people came out from Jerusalem and that they are descendants of the Jews. The gospel of Jesus Christ will restore them to knowledge of their fathers and of Jesus Christ, and they will become a pure and delightsome people.
