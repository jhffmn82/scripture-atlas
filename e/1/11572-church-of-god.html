Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Mosiah, the church of God is formed through Alma and his followers, adds baptized people by God’s authority, unites many churches, and later suffers persecution and dissension before its injuries are repaired. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the church is established under its prior order, receives repentant and baptized people, rejects the unrepentant, and is reestablished amid dissension. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the church of God is established throughout the land, serves its members, faces contention and persecution, and is later cared for and reestablished by Helaman and his brethren. In The Book of Helaman, the church of God receives many baptized souls, suffers from pride and iniquity, dwindles, and later spreads among the Nephites and Lamanites during a time of peace.

church of God in The Book of Mosiah

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where church of God first appears

Abstract written by a model

The church of God is a church formed by Alma and those with him through God’s strength, power, and faith in Abinadi’s words. Baptized people are added to it by God’s power and authority. Though composed of many churches, all are one church, preaching repentance and faith in God. It begins with baptism and formation, includes Limhi’s people waiting for authority, and later suffers persecution and dissension before Alma’s son and the sons of Mosiah repair injuries they caused.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 18

The baptized people were called the church of God from that time forward. Whoever was baptized by the power and authority of God was added to the church.

Chapter 21

The church of God is formed by Alma and the people who went with him through the strength and power of God and faith in Abinadi’s words. Limhi’s people desire to form themselves into a church but wait upon the Spirit of the Lord because no one in the land has authority to baptize.

Chapter 25

The baptized people belonged to the church of God because of their belief on the words of Alma. Although there were many churches, they were all one church, the church of God.

Chapter 27

The church of God suffered persecution and dissension from unbelievers, but king Mosiah commanded that it not be persecuted. Alma (son) and the sons of Mosiah later sought to destroy it, then worked to repair the injuries they had done to it.

Abstract written by a model

The church is established in Zarahemla under its prior order, with priests and teachers appointed throughout the land. It receives repentant, baptized people, while unrepentant members are rejected and removed. Alma establishes its order in Gideon, though Ammonihah rejects its traditions. Alma blesses the faithful, and Helaman and his brethren reestablish the church amid dissension from those who refuse their words.

church of God in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where church of God first appears

Abstract written by a model

The church of God is a church belonging to God, named after Christ because its members take upon themselves his name. It shares the word of God freely, prohibits persecution, gives to the poor, needy, sick, and afflicted without respect to persons, and remains neat without costly apparel. Though persecuted and tried by contention, it can be steadfast in faith, peaceful, and prosperous without setting its heart on riches. It is established in Zarahemla, the land of Zarahemla, the land of Mormon, and throughout the land, with high priests who care for it. Its course includes threats from Nehor and Amlici; restoration, labor, generosity, and prosperity; fuller establishment and thousands joining through baptism; then pride, envy, strife, malice, and persecution among members, which hinder its progress and become a stumbling-block to unbelievers. Alma is warned to cease seeking its destruction. Later, Helaman and his brethren care for it, maintain order, preach with power, bring many to repentance and baptism, and establish it again throughout the land.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

7 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

The church of God takes Nehor before Alma after Gideon is killed. Its members endure persecution, restore order, labor, share their substance with the needy, and become prosperous.

Chapter 2

The church of God is threatened by Amlici because he intends to destroy it. The people of the church are alarmed that Amlici might gain authority over them.

Chapter 4

The church of God was established more fully, and about three thousand five hundred souls joined and were baptized in the seventh year. In the eighth year, many church members became proud, contentious, and persecuting, causing the church to fail in its progress.

Chapter 5

The church of God was established in the land of Mormon and throughout the land. Alma speaks by command to those who belong to the church and calls them to remain faithful, humble, and watchful.

Chapter 36

Alma and the sons of Mosiah sought to destroy the church of God. The holy angel warned Alma to seek no more to destroy it.

Chapter 46

The church of God is cared for by Helaman and his brethren, who are high priests. Some members are led away by Amalickiah’s flattering words, while Helaman and the high priests later maintain order and experience peace and rejoicing in the church.

Chapter 62

Helaman and his brethren preached with power, causing many to repent and be baptized. They established the church of God again throughout the land.

church of God in The Book of Helaman

The Book of Mormon · the work’s page · read it where church of God first appears

Abstract written by a model

The church of God is a church that receives many souls baptized unto repentance and first experiences exceedingly great prosperity. Pride among some who profess to belong to it brings persecution, though the church itself is distinguished from their pride. It then dwindles because of iniquity, including denial of prophecy and revelation. Later it is established among the Lamanites and spreads throughout the land, with most Nephites and Lamanites belonging to it amid exceedingly great peace.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 3

The church of God experiences great prosperity, with thousands joining and being baptized unto repentance. Pride enters the hearts of some who profess to belong to it, leading to persecution.

Chapter 4

The church of God included people involved in wickedness, and because of their iniquity it began to dwindle. Some who professed to belong to it denied the spirit of prophecy and the spirit of revelation.

Chapter 6

The church of God is established among the Lamanites, contributing to the joy of the people of the church.

Chapter 11

The church spreads throughout the land, and most Nephites and Lamanites belong to it during a period of exceedingly great peace.
