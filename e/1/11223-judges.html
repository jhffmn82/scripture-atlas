Abstract written by a model

Judges were selected to judge crimes, ruled the people of Nephi, received wages by time labored, enforced debts, and judged by law and evidence. Some lawyers and judges stirred up disturbances to gain cases and earnings, laying a foundation for the people’s destruction.

Chapter 10

The judges are selected to judge crimes under the law. Amulek says the unrighteousness of the lawyers and judges is beginning to lay the foundation of the people’s destruction.

Chapter 11

Judges received wages for their service, enforced payment of debts, and judged people according to the law and the evidence brought against them. Some stirred up riots and disturbances to increase the number of cases and their earnings.
