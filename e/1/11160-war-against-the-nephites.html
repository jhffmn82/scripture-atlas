war against the Nephites

event · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 5 connections

Abstract written by a model

War against the Nephites is a conflict between Nephites and Lamanites, driven by anger toward Nephite religion and waged against their religion, liberty, lands, and country. Lamanites fear the fighting and many resist gathering. Amalickiah prepares a large army and leads it. Moroni demands surrender; later Moroni and Lehi defeat Jacob’s Lamanites, with heavy deaths, Moroni wounded, Jacob killed, and survivors surrendering or captured.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 44

The Lamanites came against the Nephites to make war because of their anger toward the Nephites’ religion. Moroni requires them to surrender their weapons and promise not to return to war.

Chapter 47

The battle against the Nephites is the conflict for which the king commands the Lamanites to gather. The Lamanites fear this battle, and many refuse to participate.

Chapter 51

Amalickiah prepares and leads the Lamanites in war against the Nephites after swearing to drink Moroni's blood.

Chapter 52

The battle involves Moroni’s and Lehi’s forces against the Lamanites led by Jacob. Many are slain, Moroni is wounded, Jacob is killed, and the surviving Lamanites surrender or are captured.
