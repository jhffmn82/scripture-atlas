the plates of brass

object · 1 work · 5 quoted facts · 5 connections
Also called: plates of brass, these plates

Abstract written by a model

The plates of brass contain records and commandments of God, preserved by the Lord and enabling understanding of the mysteries of God. King Benjamin says they prevent ignorance and unbelief; King Mosiah keeps and preserves them, then confers them on Alma the younger.

The Book of Mosiah / Chapter 1

The plates of brass contain records and commandments and make it possible to read and understand the mysteries of God. King Benjamin teaches that without them the fathers would have suffered in ignorance and dwindled in unbelief.

Chapter 28

King Mosiah takes the plates of brass among the records and things he has kept and preserved, and later confers them upon Alma the younger.
