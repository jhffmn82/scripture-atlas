Riplakish

Abstract written by a model

Riplakish, son of Shez, reigned unrighteously, taking many wives and concubines, imposing heavy taxes, building cities, a beautiful throne, and prisons, and exploiting prisoners through forced labor and death penalties. He afflicted the people with whoredoms and abominations until, after forty-two years, they rebelled, killed him, and drove out his descendants.

Chapter 10

Riplakish reigned after Shez and did not do what was right before the Lord. He had many wives and concubines, imposed heavy taxes, built spacious buildings and prisons, forced people to labor, caused refineries and fine workmanship to be done in prison, and afflicted the people with whoredoms and abominations. After forty-two years, the people rebelled, Riplakish was killed, and his descendants were driven from the land.
