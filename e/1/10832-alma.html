Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Mosiah, Alma is Zarahemla’s first chief judge and church high priest who keeps the commandments, judges righteously, and presides during continual peace. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, Alma is a Nephite leader, judge, governor, chief judge, high priest, preacher, teacher, and righteous man who leads armies, preaches repentance, organizes churches, teaches faith, and blesses his sons before departing from Zarahemla. In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, Alma is a leader, prophet, teacher, preacher, and high priest who gives up the judgment-seat to preach, teaches repentance and faith in Christ, survives imprisonment, confronts Korihor, ministers among the Zoramites, and charges his sons to live righteously.

Alma in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where Alma first appears

Abstract written by a model

Alma is a Nephite leader, judge, governor, chief judge, high priest, preacher, teacher, and righteous man. He begins by reigning in the judgment-seat, judging Nehor for priestcraft and killing Gideon. As chief judge and governor, he leads Nephite armies against the Amlicites and Lamanites, kills Amlici, and preserves his people. Consecrated a high priest by his father, he receives revelation and prophecy through the Spirit, preaches repentance, rebirth, righteousness, and the coming of Jesus Christ, and organizes the church by ordaining priests and elders and establishing churches in many lands. He teaches in Gideon, Melek, Ammonihah, Sidom, Zarahemla, and among the Zoramites. Rejected from Ammonihah, he returns by divine command, fasts, stays with Amulek, testifies boldly, and later helps Zeezrom, establishes a church in Sidom, and administers to Amulek. Alma rejoices with the sons of Mosiah, leads companions among the Zoramites, prays for their strength, and teaches the poor in heart that faith can begin as an experiment upon the word and grow like a seed into a tree. He has sons Helaman, Shiblon, and Corianton. Near the end, he teaches and blesses Helaman and his other sons, prophesies concerning the people of Nephi, blesses the land and church, then departs from Zarahemla; his death and burial are unknown.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

15 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Alma reigns in the judgment-seat and judges Nehor according to the law. He condemns Nehor for priestcraft and for killing Gideon.

Chapter 2

Alma is the chief judge and governor of the people of Nephi. He leads the Nephites in battle, prays for his life to be spared so he can preserve his people, kills Amlici, and defeats the guards of the king of the Lamanites.

Chapter 5

Alma delivers the word of God to the people and teaches that they must repent, be born again, and bring forth works of righteousness. He testifies that Jesus Christ will come and that these things have been made known to him by the Holy Spirit of God.

Chapter 6

Alma ordained priests and elders by laying on his hands to preside and watch over the church in the city of Zarahemla. After making regulations, he departed from Zarahemla and went to the valley of Gideon, where he declared the word of God to the church.

Chapter 8

Alma teaches in Gideon, Melek, and Ammonihah. After being rejected and expelled from Ammonihah, he returns at the command of an angel of the Lord, receives food from Amulek, and preaches with him to the people.

Chapter 9

Alma is commanded by God to take Amulek and preach again to the people who were in the city of Ammonihah. He boldly testifies of their wickedness, calls them to repentance, warns them of destruction, and is opposed when they seek to seize him.

Chapter 10

Alma is described as a holy, chosen man of God who had fasted many days because of the sins of the people. He stayed at Amulek’s house, where his presence was associated with blessings, and he testified with Amulek.

Chapter 11

Alma was opposed by those who stirred up the people for the purpose of gaining more employment and money through legal suits.

Chapter 15

Alma departed with Amulek for the land of Sidom and found people who had been cast out of Ammonihah. He asked Zeezrom whether he believed in the power of Christ unto salvation, prayed for his healing, baptized him, established a church in Sidom, and later took Amulek to Zarahemla.

Chapter 17

Alma meets the sons of Mosiah while journeying from the land of Gideon toward the land of Manti. He rejoices because they remain his brethren in the Lord and have grown strong in the knowledge of the truth.

Chapter 27

Alma met Ammon and his brethren in the wilderness, and the meeting was joyful. He conducted them to the land of Zarahemla and related his conversion to the people of Anti-Nephi-Lehi.

Chapter 31

Alma became sorrowful when he learned of the Zoramites' iniquity and separation from the Nephites. He went with companions to preach among them, was grieved by their worship and pride, and prayed for strength, comfort, wisdom, and success.

Chapter 32

Alma teaches the poor in heart on the hill Onidah. He explains humility, repentance, faith, knowledge, and the growth of the word through the comparisons of a seed and a tree.

Chapter 43

Alma goes forth with his sons to declare the word. At Moroni’s request, he inquires of the Lord and learns that the Lamanites are marching through the wilderness toward Manti.

Chapter 45

Alma teaches Helaman about the records, Jesus Christ, and keeping commandments. He prophesies concerning the future of the people of Nephi, blesses Helaman, his other sons, the land, and the church, then departs from Zarahemla and is never heard of again.

Alma in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

Abstract written by a model

Alma is a leader, prophet, teacher, preacher, and high priest over the church, consecrated by his father Alma. As chief judge and governor, he sees wickedness, inequality, and persecution in the church, becomes sorrowful, appoints Nephihah chief judge, delivers the judgment-seat to him, and devotes himself wholly to the high priesthood and preaching. He teaches repentance, faith in Christ, the Son of God, worship, prayer, the holy order, redemption, death, resurrection, judgment, and God’s rest. He explains the scriptures, knows Zeezrom’s thoughts through God’s Spirit, and calls people to humble themselves and not delay repentance. Imprisoned with Amulek, bound, beaten, mocked, and deprived, he seeks strength through faith in Christ, breaks his bonds, and survives the prison’s collapse. Believed to have prophecy, he inquires of the Lord, directs Zoram to the Lamanites, and preaches throughout the land. He confronts Korihor, testifies that church labor brought no gain, and declares that Korihor will be struck dumb. After ministering among the Zoramites and helping many repent, Alma returns to Zarahemla grieved by continuing iniquity, wars, bloodshed, contention, and hardened hearts. He has two sons and gives each a charge concerning righteousness.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

8 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 4

Alma was consecrated high priest over the people of the church by the hand of his father Alma. He saw the church’s wickedness, inequality, and persecution, became sorrowful, and selected Nephihah as chief judge while retaining the office of high priest.

Chapter 12

Alma speaks to Zeezrom and the people, establishes Amulek's words, and explains the scriptures concerning the plan of redemption, death, resurrection, judgment, repentance, and God's rest. He teaches that hardened hearts receive a lesser portion of the word and urges the people to repent.

Chapter 13

Alma teaches about the holy order, high priesthood, faith, repentance, and the rest of the Lord. He urges the people to repent, humble themselves, pray continually, and not procrastinate the day of their repentance.

Chapter 14

Alma is bound with strong cords, imprisoned, beaten, mocked, deprived of food, water, and clothing, and questioned by the chief judge and others. He refuses to answer them, then cries to the Lord for strength according to his faith in Christ; he breaks his bonds and survives the collapse of the prison.

Chapter 16

Alma was high priest over the church and was believed to have the spirit of prophecy. He inquired of the Lord, directed Zoram where to find the Lamanites, and preached repentance and the word of God.

Chapter 30

Alma told Korihor that he and his brethren did not receive gain for their labors in the church and that he had labored with his own hands for support. Alma warned Korihor that God would strike him dumb, and Korihor was struck dumb according to Alma's words.

Chapter 33

Alma teaches his listeners about worship, prayer, the scriptures, faith, and the Son of God. He urges them to plant the word in their hearts and nourish it by faith.

Chapter 35

Alma and his brethren ministered to the Zoramites who came to the land of Jershon. Alma returned to Zarahemla after helping bring many Zoramites to repentance, and he was grieved by the iniquity, wars, bloodshed, contentions, and hardening hearts among the people.
