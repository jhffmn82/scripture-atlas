hope

Abstract written by a model

Hope requires faith and charity, comes through Christ’s atonement and resurrection unto eternal life, and is filled by the Holy Ghost through meekness and lowliness of heart. Required for salvation, it contrasts with despair, which comes because of iniquity.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 7

Hope is attained through faith and comes through the atonement of Christ and the power of his resurrection. It is hope of being raised to eternal life according to the promise.

Chapter 10

Hope is required where there is faith, and without hope a person must be in despair. Despair comes because of iniquity.
