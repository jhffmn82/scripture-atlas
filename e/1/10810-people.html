Abstract written by a model

The people initially desire a king, but after Mosiah’s words relinquish that wish, choose judges by collective voice, seek equal chances throughout the land, and accept responsibility for their sins.

Chapter 29

The people first desire a king, then accept Mosiah’s arguments and choose judges. They become anxious that every person should have an equal chance and express willingness to answer for their own sins.
