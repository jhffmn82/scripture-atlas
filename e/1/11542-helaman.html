Abstract written by a model

Helaman, son of Helaman, is appointed by the people to the judgment-seat. He pursues robbers and secret murderers for lawful execution, judges with justice and equity, keeps God’s commandments, and prospers. He fathers Nephi and Lehi and teaches them to remember God’s commandments, repentance, Jesus Christ, and the Redeemer’s rock. He dies in the fifty-third year of the judges’ reign.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 2

Helaman, the son of Helaman, was appointed by the voice of the people to fill the judgment-seat. After Kishkumen was killed, Helaman sent to take the band of robbers and secret murderers so they could be executed according to the law.

Chapter 3

Helaman fills the judgment-seat with justice and equity, keeps God's commandments, and prospers in the land. He has two sons, Nephi and Lehi, and dies in the fifty-third year of the reign of the judges.

Chapter 5

Helaman taught Nephi and Lehi to remember the commandments of God, the works of their first parents, repentance, Jesus Christ, and the rock of the Redeemer.
