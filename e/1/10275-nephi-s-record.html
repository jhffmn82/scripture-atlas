Abstract written by a model

Nephi’s record, written by Nephi in his father’s language with Jewish learning and Egyptian language, abridges Lehi’s record. It recounts Lehi and his family’s travels and sufferings, shipbuilding, and crossing into the promised land.

Nephi's record is in one work on this site, The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry

Nephi’s record gives the account of Lehi and his family, their travels and sufferings, the building of the ship, and their crossing into the promised land. Nephi states that he wrote the record.

Chapter 1

Nephi makes a record in the language of his father, using the learning of the Jews and the language of the Egyptians. He makes it with his own hand and according to his knowledge, and he abridges Lehi's record.
