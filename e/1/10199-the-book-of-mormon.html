Abstract written by a model

In The First Book of Nephi, His Reign and Ministry, the book of the Lamb of God is described as a record of the Jews containing the Lord’s covenants, holy prophets’ prophecies, and the gospel’s fulness, with later records establishing its truth and restoring plain and precious things taken away. In The Book of Mormon, Mormon's Own Record, The Book of Mormon is the record Mormon calls his record of the things he has seen and heard.
