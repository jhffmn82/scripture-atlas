Abstract written by a model

The destruction of the people, prophesied by Ether, unfolds in all the land after warnings to repent. Moroni records it and Ether observes from a rock as wars, famines, pestilences, and wickedness bring unparalleled destruction, including Coriantumr’s household.

Chapter 11

The prophets warned of a great destruction among the people, such as had never been upon the face of the earth, unless they repented. The destruction came through wars, famines, pestilences, and the people's wickedness.

Chapter 13

The destruction of the people is the subject of Moroni’s record and of Ether’s observations from the cavity of a rock. Ether warns that the people and Coriantumr’s household will be destroyed if they do not repent.
