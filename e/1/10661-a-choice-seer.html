a choice seer

person · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 8 connections
Also called: a choice seer will I raise up, a seer, that seer

Abstract written by a model

A choice seer from Joseph’s loins is raised up and highly esteemed among them. Commanded only by the Lord, he is like Moses, brings forth and confirms the Lord’s word to Joseph’s descendants, and helps bring salvation through the Lord’s power. Blessed and protected from destroyers, he is named after Joseph, Lehi’s last-born son, and his father.

Chapter 3

A choice seer will be raised up from the fruit of Joseph's loins and esteemed highly among them. He will receive commandments and power to bring forth and convince Joseph's descendants of the word of the Lord, and his work will help bring them to salvation.
