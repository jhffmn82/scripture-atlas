Also called: all manner of bondage

Abstract written by a model

Bondage is the threatened state of Limhi’s people if Noah’s people refuse to repent: enemies afflict them, and only the Lord can deliver them. Their later deliverance is remembered with great joy and taught by Alma.

Chapter 11

The Lord warns through Abinadi that Noah's people will be brought into bondage and afflicted by their enemies if they do not repent. None will deliver them except the Lord the Almighty God.

Chapter 25

Those delivered from bondage were remembered with great joy, and Alma taught the people of Limhi and his brethren to remember that the Lord delivered them.
