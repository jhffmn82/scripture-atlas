the rod of iron

Also called: rod, rod of iron

Abstract written by a model

The rod of iron extends along the bank of the river and leads to the tree of life. In Lehi’s dream, those who cling to it press through the mist of darkness to partake of the tree’s fruit. It represents the word of God, leading to the fountain of living waters or the tree of life; those who hearken and hold fast will not perish or be overcome by the adversary.
