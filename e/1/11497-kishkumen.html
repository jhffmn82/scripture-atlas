Kishkumen

person · 1 work · 7 quoted facts · 5 connections

Abstract written by a model

Kishkumen is a murderer belonging to a secret band. Sent by Paanchi’s supporters, he murders Pahoran at the judgment-seat, escapes in disguise, and covenants to conceal it. He later plans to murder Helaman but is stabbed to death by Helaman’s servant. With Gadianton, he helps form and govern a band of murderers and plunderers.

Kishkumen is in one work on this site, The Book of Helaman. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

3 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Kishkumen is sent by those who supported Paanchi to murder Pahoran. He escapes pursuit in disguise and covenants with his band to conceal the murder.

Chapter 2

Kishkumen had murdered Pahoran and planned to destroy Helaman, supported by his band. Helaman's servant led him toward the judgment-seat and stabbed him to the heart, killing him without a groan.

Chapter 6

Kishkumen helps form the band of murderers and plunderers with Gadianton. He and Gadianton give laws governing the band’s secret wickedness.
