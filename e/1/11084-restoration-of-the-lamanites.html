Abstract written by a model

In The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma, the restoration of the Lamanites is brought about through God's word as many thousands are converted, repent, reject inherited traditions, believe in the Lord, and become a righteous people. In The Book of Helaman, the restoration of the Lamanites is prophesied as their return to knowledge of the truth and their Redeemer, and as their being numbered among his sheep.

conversion of the Lamanites in The Book of Alma, the Son of Alma

author unknown · the work’s page · read it where conversion of the Lamanites first appears

Abstract written by a model

The conversion of the Lamanites is a work God brings about through the power of his word, fulfilling his purpose to restore many thousands to truth. The sons of Mosiah begin by seeking their repentance and knowledge of redemption. After losses and afflictions, many remember Aaron and his brethren’s preaching, reject inherited traditions, believe in the Lord, and are converted. Thousands leave darkness for God’s light and salvation, showing change through love and rejecting violence and sin. God’s power in them will continue in future generations.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 17

The sons of Mosiah undertake their work to bring the Lamanites to repentance and to the knowledge of the truth and the plan of redemption. The text says that many are brought to the knowledge of the truth through their words.

Chapter 25

After suffering much loss and many afflictions, many Lamanites remember the words preached by Aaron and his brethren, disbelieve the traditions of their fathers, believe in the Lord, and are converted in the wilderness.

Chapter 26

The conversion of the Lamanites brought thousands from darkness and the pains of hell into God's light, salvation, and fold. The converts demonstrated their change through love for their brethren and rejection of violence and sin.

Chapter 37

The restoration of many thousands of the Lamanites to the knowledge of the truth is identified as one purpose God has fulfilled. God has shown his power in them and will continue to show it in future generations.
