Abstract written by a model

In the first year of the judges, the sons of Mosiah and selected companions leave Zarahemla with supplies for the wilderness, bound for Nephi to preach to the Lamanites and save souls. They separate at the Lamanite borders, endure privation and affliction, rely on God’s mercies, and hope to reunite after their harvest.

Chapter 17

The sons of Mosiah leave the land of Zarahemla with selected companions, weapons, and provisions for the wilderness. They journey to the land of Nephi, separate in the borders of the land of the Lamanites, and trust that they will meet again after their harvest.

Chapter 26

The journey from Zarahemla to Nephi was undertaken to preach to the Lamanites rather than to destroy them. During it, Ammon and his brethren endured privation and affliction and relied on the mercies of God.
