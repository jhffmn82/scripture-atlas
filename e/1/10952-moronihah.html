Moronihah in The Book of Helaman

The Book of Mormon · the work’s page · read it where Moronihah first appears

Abstract written by a model

Moronihah is a Nephite military leader and commander during a Lamanite invasion. He stations strong armies at the borders, sends Lehi against attackers near Bountiful, then directs a retreat and encirclement that recaptures Zarahemla and removes prisoners peacefully. Later, he preaches because of Nephite iniquity, leads recovery of half their possessions after repentance, maintains those lands, and establishes peace with the Lamanites.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

2 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Moronihah has strong armies stationed around the borders because he expects the Lamanites to attack there. After Coriantumr invades the center of the land, Moronihah sends Lehi to head the army and later heads the retreat, surrounds the Lamanites, recaptures Zarahemla, and sends the prisoners out of the land in peace.

Chapter 4

Moronihah preached to the people because of their iniquity and led the Nephites to regain half of their possessions after they repented. He later maintained the recovered lands when he could obtain no more possessions from the Lamanites.
