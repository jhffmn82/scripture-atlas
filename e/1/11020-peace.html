Abstract written by a model

Peace returns as church members remain steady, labor, and share with the needy, bringing much peace among the people of Nephi. After the Lamanites are driven out, continual peace lasts three years. It begins throughout the land of Zarahemla in the sixteenth year of the reign of the judges and continues into the seventeenth.

Chapter 1

The church begins to have continual peace again after its members remain steady, labor, and share with the needy. There is much peace among the people of Nephi until the fifth year of the reign of the judges.

Chapter 16

There had been much peace in the land before the invasion. After the Lamanites were driven out, the people of Nephi had continual peace for three years.

Chapter 30

Continual peace began throughout all the land in the sixteenth year of the reign of the judges and continued into the commencement of the seventeenth year.
