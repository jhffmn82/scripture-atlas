five great vows

topic · 1 work · 11 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: the five great vows, the great vows, vows

Abstract written by a model

The five great vows are strict, demanding duties for ordained monks, observed by Mrigâputra after entering the order. They require lifelong nonviolence, truthfulness, non-taking, chastity, and non-possession: not killing, lying, stealing, being unchaste, or holding property. Carelessness prevents complete release from fetters and failure in life’s battle.

Nineteenth Lecture.

The five great vows are among the demanding duties of a Sramana. Mrigâputra observes them after entering the order.

Twentieth Lecture.

The great vows must be strictly kept by an ordained monk. Carelessness toward them prevents his fetters from being completely cut off and causes him to fail in the battle of life.
