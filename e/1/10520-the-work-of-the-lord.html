Abstract written by a model

The Lord's work is a marvelous work and wonder undertaken among all his people. It begins as the Lord commences restoring the house of Israel and commands a choice seer to do the work given him. The Lord proceeds among the children of men, causing the wisdom of the wise to perish and the prudent's understanding to be hidden. Its purpose is to remember covenants and recover his people, including the house of Israel.
