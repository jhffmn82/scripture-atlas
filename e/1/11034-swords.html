Abstract written by a model

Swords are weapons alongside cimeters and bows, used by Nehor to kill Gideon and later surrendered by Lamanites under compulsion or covenant. Anti-Nephi-Lehi retain and bury brightened swords as testimony of ending their use. Zerahemnah delivers, receives back, and ultimately loses weapons; Moroni commands further Lamanite surrender, binding those who resist.

Narrative, section by section written by a model

4 sections; each heading opens the text

Chapter 1

Nehor draws his sword and strikes Gideon, killing him. Nehor’s attempt to enforce priestcraft by the sword is condemned by Alma.

Chapter 24

The people of Anti-Nephi-Lehi regard their swords as bright after God removes their stains. They retain and bury the swords so they will remain bright as a testimony that they have not used them again.

Chapter 44

Zerahemnah delivers his sword, cimeter, and bow to Moroni but refuses the required oath. Moroni returns the weapons, later takes the Lamanites’ weapons after their covenant, and the surrendered weapons are not retained by the departing Lamanites.

Chapter 52

Moroni commands the Lamanites to surrender their weapons of war. Many throw down their weapons, while others are taken and bound and compelled to march into the land Bountiful.
