Desolation is a city by a narrow pass in the land Desolation. Nephite armies defend it against Lamanites, who capture it; the Nephites retake it, but the Lamanites capture it again amid deadly battles and Nephite defeat.

Chapter 3

The city of Desolation was in the borders by the narrow pass leading into the land southward. The Nephites placed their armies there, and the Lamanites came down there to battle.

Chapter 4

The Lamanites take possession of city Desolation, the Nephites later retake it, and the Lamanites subsequently take it again. Battles in and around the city result in many deaths and the Nephites' defeat.
