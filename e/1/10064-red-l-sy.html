Abstract written by a model

Red Lêsyâ is a good Lêsyâ, vermilion like the rising sun or a parrot’s bill, with a pleasant taste. It develops in a humble, disciplined, law-loving person and brings the soul into happy courses of life.

Thirty-Fourth Lecture.

The red Lêsyâ has the colour of vermilion, the rising sun, or the bill of a parrot. It develops in a humble, steadfast, disciplined, restrained person who loves and keeps the Law; it is one of the good Lêsyâs and brings the soul into happy courses of life.
