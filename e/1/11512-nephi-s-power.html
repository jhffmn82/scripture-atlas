Nephi's power

object · 1 work · 4 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: power, power over this people

Abstract written by a model

A power given by God to Nephi, enabling sealing and loosing on earth and in heaven, declarations of smiting, famine, and destruction according to the Lord’s will, and escape through the Spirit from imprisonment.

Nephi's power is in one work on this site, The Book of Helaman. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Chapter 10

God gives Nephi power over the people, including power to declare that the earth, a temple, a mountain, or the people will be smitten according to the Lord’s will. Nephi also has power through the Spirit to escape when the people seek to imprison him.
