Ether

Abstract written by a model

Ether is a descendant of Coriantor, a prophet of the Lord, and a record keeper. Guided by the Spirit of the Lord, he prophesies to the people concerning Christ, this land, the New Jerusalem, Jerusalem, the house of Israel, the remnant of Joseph, and Coriantumr. The people reject and expel him. By day he hides in a cavity of a rock, observing their doings, opposing armies, and destructions. After the Lord’s words are fulfilled, he finishes and hides his record so the people of Limhi later find it. He desires the kingdom of God and salvation in it.

Ether is in one work on this site, The Book of Ether. This is the abstract a model wrote of it from that work.

Ether wrote the record and was a descendant of Coriantor.

Chapter 11

Ether was begotten by Coriantor. Coriantor died after dwelling in captivity all his days.

Chapter 13

Ether prophesies about Christ, this land, the New Jerusalem, Jerusalem, the house of Israel, and the remnant of the seed of Joseph. The people reject him and cast him out, after which he hides in the cavity of a rock and records the destructions he observes.

Chapter 15

Ether’s words were remembered by Coriantumr. Ether observed the doings of the people, saw that the Lord’s words had been fulfilled, finished his record, and hid it where the people of Limhi found it.
