our first parents

group · 1 work · 3 quoted facts · 3 connections
Also called: first parents

Abstract written by a model

Our first parents, after the Fall, were sent from the garden of Eden and cut off from the tree of life and the Lord’s presence, temporally and spiritually. They became subject to their own will; they could not partake of the tree lest they remain forever miserable without a preparatory state, requiring redemption, death, and judgment.

Chapter 12

The first parents could not partake of the tree of life after the Fall, because doing so would have left them forever miserable without a preparatory state. Their condition relates to the need for the plan of redemption and the appointment of death and judgment.

Chapter 42

Our first parents were sent out from the garden of Eden and were cut off from the tree of life. They became cut off temporally and spiritually from the presence of the Lord and became subject to their own will.
