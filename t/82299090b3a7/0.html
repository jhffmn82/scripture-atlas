Surah 113: The Dawn (Al-Falaq) was revealed at Mecca. It tells the listener to seek refuge in the Lord of the Daybreak. It names evil from created things, intense darkness, malignant witchcraft, and the envier when he envies. The passage concerns protection from these forms of evil.
