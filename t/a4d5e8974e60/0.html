Surah 101: The Calamity (Al-Qaari'a) describes the Calamity and asks what it is. On that day, mankind will be like thickly-scattered moths and the mountains will become like carded wool. Him whose scales are heavy with good works will live a pleasant life, while him whose scales are light will have a bereft and Hungry One as his mother. The unit concludes by identifying that mother as Raging Fire.
