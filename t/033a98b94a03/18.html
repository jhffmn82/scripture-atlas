This section, in brief written by a model
The unit concerns impurity, divine knowledge, God, Guru Nanak, and the True Guru. It teaches that impurity is present in everything if judged by physical contamination, but that impurity is truly rooted in greed, falsehood, coveting, slander, superstition, and worldly attachment. Guru Nanak states that divine knowledge washes away impurity and that those who know God have no impurity. The True Guru leads people to behold God's greatness, brings God's praises into the heart, removes evil, and grants the nine treasures when God is pleased.
In this section: God · True Guru · Guru Nanak · impurity · divine knowledge

Slok XVIII If the idea of impurity be admitted, there is impurity in everything. There are worms in cow-dung and in wood; There is no grain of corn without life. In the first place, there is life in water by which everything is made green. How shall we avoid impurity? It falleth on our kitchens. Saith Nanak, impurity is not thus washed away: it is washed away by divine knowledge. Guru Nanak Impurity of the heart is greed, impurity of the tongue is falsehood; Impurity of the eyes is gazing on another's wealth, his wife, and her beauty; Impurity of the ears is listening to slander. Nanak, even the pretended saint who practiseth such things, shall go bound to hell. All impurity consisteth in Superstition and attachment to worldly things. Birth and death are ordained; as it pleaseth God, we come and go. The eating and drinking which God sent as sustenance are pure. Nanak, the Pious persons who know God have no impurity, Pauri XVIII Magnify and praise the True Guru in whom there is all greatness. If the Guru cause us to meet God, we shall behold His greatness. If it please the Guru, he will cause God's praises to dwell in the heart. He putteth his hand on our foreheads; and when he giveth the order, removeth evil from within us. When God is pleased the nine treasures are obtained.
