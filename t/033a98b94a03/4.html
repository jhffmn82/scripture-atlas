This section, in brief written by a model
Guru Nanak describes fear as governing winds, rivers, fire, earth, Indar, Dharmraj, the sun, the moon, spiritual beings, stars, heroes, and the movements of people, while God, the Formless One, alone is without fear. He teaches that worldly rulers, performers, ornaments, bodies, and religious compositions do not provide divine knowledge. Divine knowledge is obtained by God's grace rather than by words, skill, or orders. The Pauri says that the true Guru is obtained through the Kind One's kindness, gives the Word to the wandering soul, removes pride, and leads people to the True One.

Slok IV Guru Nanak In fear the winds and breezes ever blow In fear flow hundreds of thousands of rivers In fear fire performeth its forced labour; In fear the earth is pressed by its burden In fear Indar moveth headlong: in fear sitteth Dharmraj at God's gate; In fear is the sun, in fear the moon; they travel millions of miles without end; in fear are the Sidhs, the Budhas, the demigods, and the Naths; in fear are the stars and the firmament; In fear are wrestlers, very mighty men and divine heroes; In fear cargoes of men come and go. God hath destined fear for every one; Nanak, the Formless One, the True, is alone without fear. Guru Nanak Nanak, the Formless One is without fear; all the Rams were dust. How many stories there are of Krishan! how many Veds and religious compositions! How many beggars dance, and fall, and again beat time! Actors enter the market-place and draw forth their appliances; Kings and queens sing and utter nonsense; They wear earrings worth hundreds of thousands, and necklaces worth hundreds of thousands. The body on which they are worn, O Nanak, shall become ashes. Divine knowledge is not sought in mere words; to speak concerning it were as hard as iron; By God's grace man obtaineth it; skill and orders are useless therefor. Pauri IV If the Kind One look with kindness, then is the true Guru obtained. The soul hath wandered through many births, and now the true Guru hath communicated the Word. There is no benefactor so great as the true Guru; hear this, all ye people. By meeting the true Guru who hath removed pride from his heart, and who preacheth the Truest of the true, The True One is obtained.
