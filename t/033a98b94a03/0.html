This section, in brief written by a model
Asa ki War (Asa di Var) is presented in a translation by Max Arthur Macauliffe in The Sikh Religion, volume 1, published in 1909. The unit states that one God is true-named, the Creator, fearless, without enmity, immortal, unborn, self-existent, great, and beneficent. Guru Har Gobind is named. The War includes sloks attributed to the first Guru, which are to be sung to the air of “Tunda As Raja.”

Asa ki War (Asa di Var), translated by Max Arthur Macauliffe (The Sikh Religion, vol. 1, 1909) There is but one God whose name is true, the Creator, devoid of fear and enmity, immortal, unborn, self-existent, great and beneficent. Guru Har Gobind This War includes sloks. The sloks also were written by the first Guru, and should be sung to the air of 'Tunda As Raja'.
