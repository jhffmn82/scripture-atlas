This section, in brief written by a model
Guru Angad teaches that a gift obtained through personal asking differs from the wonderful gift received when the Lord is pleased. He also teaches that true service is marked by absorption in the love of the master rather than freedom from fear. Nanak says that God's end and boundaries are unseen, and that God Himself creates and destroys. The unit says that God causes people to act, and that He who made the world takes care of it.
In this section: God · servant · Guru Nanak · world · love · gift · people (those with chains or horses) · care · creation · service · destruction

Slok XXIII Guru Angad What sort of gift is that which we obtain by our own asking? Nanak, wonderful is the gift we obtain when the Lord is pleased. Guru Angad What sort of service is that in which the fear of the master departeth not? Nanak, he is called a servant who is absorbed in the love of his master. Pauri XXIII Nanak, God's end is not seen, nor hath He a thither or a hither side. He Himself createth, and He Himself again destroyeth. Some have chains on their necks, and some ride on many horses. It is God who causeth to act and who acteth Himself to whom else shall we complain? Nanak, it is for Him who made the world to take care of it.
