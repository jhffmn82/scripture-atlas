This section, in brief written by a model
Tsze-kung asks the Master why Kung-wan received the title of Wan. The Master explains that Kung-wan was active and fond of learning. He also says Kung-wan was unashamed to ask and learn from his inferiors. For these reasons, Kung-wan was styled Wan.

CHAP. XIV. Tsze-kung asked, saying, 'On what ground did Kung-wan get that title of Wan?' The Master said, 'He was of an active nature and yet fond of learning, and he was not ashamed to ask and learn of his inferiors!-- On these grounds he has been styled Wan.'
