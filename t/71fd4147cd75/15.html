This section, in brief written by a model
Mencius explains that intuitive ability is the ability people possess without learning, while intuitive knowledge is knowledge they possess without thought. He says children instinctively love their parents and, as they grow, love their elder brothers. Filial affection for parents is the working of benevolence, and respect for elders is the working of righteousness. These feelings belong to all under heaven.

Chapter 15 Mencius said, 'The ability possessed by men without having been acquired by learning is intuitive ability, and the knowledge possessed by them without the exercise of thought is their intuitive knowledge. Children carried in the arms all know to love their parents, and when they are grown a little, they all know to love their elder brothers. Filial affection for parents is the working of benevolence. Respect for elders is the working of righteousness. There is no other reason for those feelings - they belong to all under heaven.'
