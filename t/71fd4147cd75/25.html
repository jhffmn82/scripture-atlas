This section, in brief written by a model
Mencius distinguishes between two ways of beginning the day. A person who rises at cock-crowing and earnestly practices virtue is described as a disciple of Shun. A person who rises at cock-crowing and earnestly pursues gain is described as a disciple of Zhi. Mencius says that the interval between the thought of gain and the thought of virtue separates Shun from Zhi.

Chapter 25 Mencius said, 'He who rises at cock-crowing and addresses himself earnestly to the practice of virtue, is a disciple of Shun. He who rises at cock-crowing, and addresses himself earnestly to the pursuit of gain, is a disciple of Zhi. If you want to know what separates Shun from Zhi, it is simply this: the interval between the thought of gain and the thought of virtue.'
