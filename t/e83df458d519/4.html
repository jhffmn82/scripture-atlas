This section, in brief written by a model
The unit states that nine centuries have elapsed since the Venerable Ascetic Mahâvira died. It adds that the eightieth year of the tenth century has been reached, though another redaction gives the ninety-third year. The Fifth Lecture ends, as does the Life of Mahâvîra.

(147) Since the time that the Venerable Ascetic Mahâvira died, &c. (all down to) freed from all pains, nine centuries have elapsed, and of the tenth century this is the eightieth year. Another redaction has ninety-third year (instead of eightieth). (148) End of the Fifth Lecture. End of the Life of Mahâvîra.
