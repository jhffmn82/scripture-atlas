This section, in brief written by a model
The duke Ching of Ch'i had a thousand teams, each consisting of four horses. On the day of his death, the people did not praise him for a single virtue. Po-i and Shu-ch'i died of hunger at the foot of the Shau-yang mountain, and the people still praise them. The saying is illustrated by this contrast.
