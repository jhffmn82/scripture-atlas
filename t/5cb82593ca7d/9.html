This section, in brief written by a model
Confucius describes four classes of people according to their possession of knowledge and their approach to learning. Those born with knowledge are the highest class. Those who readily gain knowledge through learning are next, followed by those who are dull and stupid but still achieve learning. Those who are dull and stupid and do not learn are the lowest of the people.

CHAP. IX. Confucius said, 'Those who are born with the possession of knowledge are the highest class of men. Those who learn, and so, readily, get possession of knowledge, are the next. Those who are dull and stupid, and yet compass the learning, are another class next to these. As to those who are dull and stupid and yet do not learn;-- they are the lowest of the people.'
