Mencius left Qi and dwelt in Xiu. In response to Gong Sun Chou, Mencius explained that the ancients did not generally hold office without receiving salary. Mencius had intended to leave after seeing the king in Chong and therefore declined salary, but orders for the collection of troops made it improper to ask permission to leave, although remaining in Qi was not his purpose.

Chapter 14 When Mencius left Qi, he dwelt in Xiu. There Gong Sun Chou asked him, saying, 'Was it the way of the ancients to hold office without receiving salary?' Mencius replied, 'No; when I first saw the king in Chong, it was my intention, on retiring from the interview, to go away. Because I did not wish to change this intention, I declined to receive any salary. Immediately after, there came orders for the collection of troops, when it would have been improper for me to beg permission to leave. But to remain so long in Qi was not my purpose.'
