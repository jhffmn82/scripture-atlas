Surah 102: Competition (At-Takaathur), revealed at Mecca, warns that rivalry in worldly increase distracts people until they come to the graves. It repeatedly declares that they will come to know, and says that sure knowledge would reveal what awaits them. They will behold hell-fire, see it with sure vision, and on that day be asked about pleasure.
