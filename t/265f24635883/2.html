This section, in brief written by a model
Guru Nanak teaches that there are six schools of philosophy, six teachers, and six doctrines, but the Guru of gurus is one despite having various forms. He urges preservation of the system in which the Creator is praised. He explains that, like one sun across many seasons and divisions of time, there is one God although His forms are many.

Guru Nanak, Rag Asa There are six schools of philosophy, six teachers, and six doctrines. The Guru of gurus is but one, though He hath various forms. O father, preserve the system In which the Creator is praised that will redound to thy glory. As there is one sun and many seasons, In which there are wisas, chasas, gharis, pahars, lunar and week days, and months; So, O Nanak, there is but one God, although His forms are many.
