Surah 1: The Opening (Al-Faatiha) is revealed at Mecca and concerns worship, praise, and seeking guidance from Allah. Allah is praised as the Lord of the Worlds and as Beneficent and Merciful. The text identifies the Day of Judgment and asks for guidance to the straight path. It distinguishes those who receive favor from those who earn anger or go astray.
