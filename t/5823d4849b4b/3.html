  16. XXIV. Vîstâsp Yast.
This section, in brief written by a model
This unit worships the Fravashis of many named holy men, women, kings, heroes, and peoples, and concludes with invocations of the Fravashis of the faithful and their ancestors. It concerns SAOSHYANT and ASTVAT-ERETA, whose future work will benefit and raise up the bodily world and withstand the Drug and evil. It praises Ahura Mazda, Sraosha, Zarathustra, the law, holiness, sacrifice, prayer, and the blessed destinies of holy men and women. It closes with blessings for houses, worshippers, the Fravashis, and the good powers associated with Ahura Mazda and the Amesha-Spentas.
