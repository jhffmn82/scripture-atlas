  1. Surah 82: The Cleaving (Al-Infitaar)
This section, in brief written by a model
The unit describes the signs accompanying the Cleaving, including the heaven being cleft, the planets dispersed, the seas poured forth, and the sepulchres overturned. It states that each soul will know what it sent before and left behind. Man is questioned about his carelessness toward his Lord, who created and fashioned him, while guardians record all deeds. The righteous will be in delight, the wicked in hell, and Allah's command will prevail on the Day of Judgment.

Surah 82: The Cleaving (Al-Infitaar) Revealed at Mecca 1 When the heaven is cleft asunder, 2 When the planets are dispersed, 3 When the seas are poured forth, 4 And the sepulchres are overturned, 5 A soul will know what it hath sent before (it) and what left behind. 6 O man! What hath made thee careless concerning thy Lord, the Bountiful, 7 Who created thee, then fashioned, then proportioned thee? 8 Into whatsoever form He will, He casteth thee. 9 Nay, but ye deny the Judgment. 10 Lo! there are above you guardians, 11 Generous and recording, 12 Who know (all) that ye do. 13 Lo! the righteous verily will be in delight. 14 And lo! the wicked verily will be in hell; 15 They will burn therein on the Day of Judgment, 16 And will not be absent thence. 17 Ah, what will convey unto thee what the Day of Judgment is! 18 Again, what will convey unto thee what the Day of Judgment is! 19 A day on which no soul hath power at all for any (other) soul. The (absolute) command on that day is Allah's.
