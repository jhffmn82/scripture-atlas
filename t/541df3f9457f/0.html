Surah 94: The Consolation (Ash-Sharh), revealed at Mecca, speaks to one whose bosom has been dilated and burden eased. It says that hardship is accompanied by ease, repeating this assurance twice. When relieved, the addressed person is told to continue toiling and strive to please the Lord.
