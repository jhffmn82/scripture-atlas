This section, in brief written by a model
Sze-ma Niu asks the Master about perfect virtue. The Master says that a person of perfect virtue is cautious and slow in speech. Niu questions whether this caution and slowness alone constitute perfect virtue. The Master explains that awareness of the difficulty of doing leads a person to speak cautiously and slowly.

CHAP. III. 1. Sze-ma Niu asked about perfect virtue. 2. The Master said, 'The man of perfect virtue is cautious and slow in his speech.' 3. 'Cautious and slow in his speech!' said Niu;-- 'is this what is meant by perfect virtue?' The Master said, 'When a man feels the difficulty of doing, can he be other than cautious and slow in speaking?'
