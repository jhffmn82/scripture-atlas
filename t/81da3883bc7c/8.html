This section, in brief written by a model
Bihagre ki War describes Hindus as having forgotten God and going in the wrong direction. It says they worship according to Narad’s instruction and characterizes them as blind, dumb, and ignorant fools. The unit says they take stones and worship them, questioning how a stone that sinks can carry them across.

Bihagre ki War The Hindus have forgotten God, and are going the wrong way. They worship according to the instruction of Narad. They are blind and dumb, the blindest of the blind. The ignorant fools take stones and worship them. O Hindus, how shall the stone which itself sinketh carry you across?
