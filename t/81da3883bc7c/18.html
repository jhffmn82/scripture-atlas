This section, in brief written by a model
Rag Basant praises the great month in which spring begins and urges remembrance of the Sustainer of the earth. It teaches that pride should be subdued and that meditation on God and excellent virtues bring joy and spiritual wealth. The passage describes good acts, God's name, religion, divine knowledge, attainment of God, and dispelling mental pride through a tree metaphor. Nanak teaches that the pious who remain absorbed in God shall be perennial and never wither.

← BhairoSarang ki War →
Rag Basant All hail to the great month in which spring ever beginneth. Ever and ever remember the Sustainer of the earth, and thy heart shall rejoice. O silly man, forget thy pride, Subdue thy pride and meditate on God in thy heart adopt the most excellent virtues. Good acts are the tree, God's name its branches, religion its flowers, divine knowledge its fruit, Attainment of God its leaves, and the dispelling of mental pride its dense shade. They who behold God's power with their eyes, hear it with their ears, and repeat the true Name with their tongues, Obtain the full wealth of honour and tranquilly meditate on God. The great season hath come, be careful and do good works. Nanak, the pious who continue absorbed in God shall be perennial and never wither.
