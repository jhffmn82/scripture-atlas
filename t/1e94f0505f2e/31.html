This section, in brief written by a model
The unit concerns Her Augustness the Heavenly-Alarming-Female and the Great Deity Prince of Saruta. She is charged with respectfully escorting the Great Deity Prince of Saruta back, because she had made him known. She is also charged with bearing his august name and serving the speaker. As a result, the Duchesses of Saru bear the name of the Male Deity the Prince of Saruta, and the women are called Duchesses of Saru.

Sect. XXXV.—The Duchess of Saru. So then he charged Her Augustness the Heavenly-Alarming-Female [saying]: "Do thou, who wast the one to make known this Great Deity Prince of Saruta who respectfully served as my august vanguard, respectfully escort him [back]; and do thou likewise bear the august name of that Deity, and respectfully serve me." Wherefore the Duchesses of Saru bear the name of the Male Deity the Prince of Saruta, and the women are Duchesses of Saru.
