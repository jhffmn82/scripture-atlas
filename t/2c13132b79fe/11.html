This section, in brief written by a model
Chapter 12 presents a short song of thanksgiving that the people will sing "in that day." In the first part, the speaker thanks the LORD because his anger has turned away and he now comforts them, and declares trust in God as salvation, strength, and song. The people will joyfully draw water from the wells of salvation. In the second part, they are called to give thanks to the LORD, call on his name, declare his deeds among the peoples, and sing of the excellent things he has done so it is known in all the earth. The chapter closes by urging the inhabitant of Zion to cry aloud and shout because the Holy One of Israel is great among them.
In this section: the LORD · Zion · inhabitant of Zion · salvation

Chapter 12 1 In that day you will say, “I will give thanks to you, the LORD; for though you were angry with me, your anger has turned away and you comfort me. 2 Behold, God is my salvation. I will trust, and will not be afraid; for the LORD, the LORD, is my strength and song; and he has become my salvation.” 3 Therefore with joy you will draw water out of the wells of salvation. 4 In that day you will say, “Give thanks to the LORD! Call on his name! Declare his doings among the peoples! Proclaim that his name is exalted! 5 Sing to the LORD, for he has done excellent things! Let this be known in all the earth! 6 Cry aloud and shout, you inhabitant of Zion, for the Holy One of Israel is great among you!”
