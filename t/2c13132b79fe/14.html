This section, in brief written by a model
Chapter 15 delivers a burden, or oracle, against Moab. Its cities Ar and Kir are laid waste in a single night. The people go to high places such as Bayith and Dibon to weep, shave their heads and beards, and wear sackcloth, while cries ring from Heshbon, Elealeh, Jahaz, and across Moab's borders. Nobles flee toward Zoar and Horonaim, the waters of Nimrim dry up, and survivors carry off their stored goods over the brook of the willows. The speaker laments for Moab, yet declares the waters of Dimon full of blood and promises still more on Dimon, a lion against those of Moab who escape and the remnant of the land.
In this section: Moab · mourning of Moab · destruction of Moab

Chapter 15 1 The burden of Moab. For in a night, Ar of Moab is laid waste, and brought to nothing. For in a night Kir of Moab is laid waste, and brought to nothing. 2 They have gone up to Bayith, and to Dibon, to the high places, to weep. Moab wails over Nebo and over Medeba. Baldness is on all of their heads. Every beard is cut off. 3 In their streets, they clothe themselves in sackcloth. In their streets and on their housetops, everyone wails, weeping abundantly. 4 Heshbon cries out with Elealeh. Their voice is heard even to Jahaz. Therefore the armed men of Moab cry aloud. Their souls tremble within them. 5 My heart cries out for Moab! Her nobles flee to Zoar, to Eglath Shelishiyah; for they go up by the ascent of Luhith with weeping; for on the way to Horonaim, they raise up a cry of destruction. 6 For the waters of Nimrim will be desolate; for the grass has withered away, the tender grass fails, there is no green thing. 7 Therefore they will carry away the abundance they have gotten, and that which they have stored up, over the brook of the willows. 8 For the cry has gone around the borders of Moab, its wailing to Eglaim, and its wailing to Beer Elim. 9 For the waters of Dimon are full of blood; for I will bring yet more on Dimon, a lion on those of Moab who escape, and on the remnant of the land.
