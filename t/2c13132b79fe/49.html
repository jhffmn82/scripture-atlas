This section, in brief written by a model
In Chapter 50, the LORD challenges the people by asking where the divorce certificate for their mother is and to which creditor he sold them, declaring that they were sold for their iniquities and their mother put away for their transgressions. He asks why no one answered when he came and called, insisting that his hand is not shortened and that he can redeem, as shown by his power to dry up the sea and clothe the heavens with blackness. A speaker, the servant of the Lord GOD, then describes being given the tongue of the taught to sustain the weary, and having his ear opened so that he was not rebellious. He tells of giving his back to those who beat him and not hiding his face from shame and spitting, trusting that the Lord GOD will help him and that his adversaries will wear out like a moth-eaten garment. The chapter closes with a call for those who fear the LORD to trust his name while walking in darkness, and a warning that those who kindle their own fires will lie down in sorrow.

Chapter 50 1 The LORD says, “Where is the bill of your mother’s divorce, with which I have put her away? Or to which of my creditors have I sold you? Behold, you were sold for your iniquities, and your mother was put away for your transgressions. 2 Why, when I came, was there no one? When I called, why was there no one to answer? Is my hand shortened at all, that it can’t redeem? Or have I no power to deliver? Behold, at my rebuke I dry up the sea. I make the rivers a wilderness. Their fish stink because there is no water, and die of thirst. 3 I clothe the heavens with blackness. I make sackcloth their covering.” 4 The Lord GOD has given me the tongue of those who are taught, that I may know how to sustain with words him who is weary. He awakens morning by morning, he awakens my ear to hear as those who are taught. 5 The Lord GOD has opened my ear. I was not rebellious. I have not turned back. 6 I gave my back to those who beat me, and my cheeks to those who plucked off the hair. I didn’t hide my face from shame and spitting. 7 For the Lord GOD will help me. Therefore I have not been confounded. Therefore I have set my face like a flint, and I know that I won’t be disappointed. 8 He who justifies me is near. Who will bring charges against me? Let us stand up together. Who is my adversary? Let him come near to me. 9 Behold, the Lord GOD will help me! Who is he who will condemn me? Behold, they will all grow old like a garment. The moths will eat them up. 10 Who among you fears the LORD and obeys the voice of his servant? He who walks in darkness and has no light, let him trust in the LORD’s name, and rely on his God. 11 Behold, all you who kindle a fire, who adorn yourselves with torches around yourselves, walk in the flame of your fire, and among the torches that you have kindled. You will have this from my hand: you will lie down in sorrow.
