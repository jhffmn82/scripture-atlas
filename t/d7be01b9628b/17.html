This section, in brief written by a model
In Chapter 17 the speaker declares his spirit consumed and his days extinct, with the grave ready for him, and says mockers surround him. He asks to be given a pledge, since no one will strike hands with him, and says that the one addressed has hidden his opponents' hearts from understanding. He describes being made a byword, spat upon, and dimmed in eye by sorrow, while upright men are astonished and the righteous hold to their way. He tells his opponents to come back, saying he will find no wise man among them. He ends by saying his days and plans are broken, and asks where his hope is if he makes Sheol his house, calling corruption his father and the worm his mother and sister, and whether his hope will go down to Sheol's gates and into the dust.

Chapter 17 1 “My spirit is consumed. My days are extinct and the grave is ready for me. 2 Surely there are mockers with me. My eye dwells on their provocation. 3 “Now give a pledge. Be collateral for me with yourself. Who is there who will strike hands with me? 4 For you have hidden their heart from understanding, therefore you will not exalt them. 5 He who denounces his friends for plunder, even the eyes of his children will fail. 6 “But he has made me a byword of the people. They spit in my face. 7 My eye also is dim by reason of sorrow. All my members are as a shadow. 8 Upright men will be astonished at this. The innocent will stir himself up against the godless. 9 Yet the righteous will hold to his way. He who has clean hands will grow stronger and stronger. 10 But as for you all, come back. I will not find a wise man among you. 11 My days are past. My plans are broken off, as are the thoughts of my heart. 12 They change the night into day, saying ‘The light is near’ in the presence of darkness. 13 If I look for Sheol as my house, if I have spread my couch in the darkness, 14 if I have said to corruption, ‘You are my father,’ and to the worm, ‘My mother,’ and ‘My sister,’ 15 where then is my hope? As for my hope, who will see it? 16 Shall it go down with me to the gates of Sheol, or descend together into the dust?”
