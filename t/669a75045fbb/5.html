This section, in brief written by a model
The unit describes the manner of administering the wine. The participants take the cup and ask God, the Eternal Father, in the name of Jesus Christ, to bless and sanctify the wine. The wine is intended to be consumed in remembrance of the blood of Jesus Christ, which was shed for them. Those who drink are to witness that they always remember Jesus Christ and may have his Spirit with them.
In this section: Jesus Christ · Holy Ghost · souls (of those who drink the wine) · wine

Chapter 5 1 The manner of administering the wine—Behold, they took the cup, and said: 2 O God, the Eternal Father, we ask thee, in the name of thy Son, Jesus Christ, to bless and sanctify this wine to the souls of all those who drink of it, that they may do it in remembrance of the blood of thy Son, which was shed for them; that they may witness unto thee, O God, the Eternal Father, that they do always remember him, that they may have his Spirit to be with them. Amen.
