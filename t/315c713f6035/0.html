Surah 93: The Morning Hours (Ad-Dhuhaa) concerns the Lord’s reassurance and guidance to the addressed person. It says that the Lord has not forsaken or hated the addressed person and that the latter portion will be better than the former. The Lord promises to give to the addressed person until the addressed person is content, recalling that He protected, directed, and enriched the addressed person. The unit commands that the orphan not be oppressed, the beggar not be driven away, and the bounty of the Lord be spoken of.

Surah 93: The Morning Hours (Ad-Dhuhaa) Revealed at Mecca 1 By the morning hours 2 And by the night when it is stillest, 3 Thy Lord hath not forsaken thee nor doth He hate thee, 4 And verily the latter portion will be better for thee than the former, 5 And verily thy Lord will give unto thee so that thou wilt be content. 6 Did He not find thee an orphan and protect (thee)? 7 Did He not find thee wandering and direct (thee)? 8 Did He not find thee destitute and enrich (thee)? 9 Therefor the orphan oppress not, 10 Therefor the beggar drive not away, 11 Therefor of the bounty of thy Lord be thy discourse.
