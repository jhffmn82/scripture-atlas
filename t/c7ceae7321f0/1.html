The unit concerns a man's conduct while waiting at court. He speaks freely but straightforwardly with the great officers of the lower grade, and blandly but precisely with those of the higher grade. When the ruler is present, his manner shows respectful uneasiness while remaining grave and self-possessed.

CHAP II. 1. When he was waiting at court, in speaking with the great officers of the lower grade, he spake freely, but in a straightforward manner; in speaking with those of the higher grade, he did so blandly, but precisely. 2. When the ruler was present, his manner displayed respectful uneasiness; it was grave, but self-possessed.
