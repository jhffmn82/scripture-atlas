This section, in brief written by a model
The unit concerns the unnamed attendant’s conduct when the prince sends him different gifts. He adjusts his mat, tastes cooked meat before giving it away, cooks undressed meat and offers it to his ancestors’ spirits, and keeps a living animal alive. When attending the prince at an entertainment, he tastes everything after the prince sacrifices. When ill, he receives the prince with prescribed clothing arrangements, and when summoned, he goes immediately without waiting for his carriage.

CHAP. XIII. 1. When the prince sent him a gift of cooked meat, he would adjust his mat, first taste it, and then give it away to others. When the prince sent him a gift of undressed meat, he would have it cooked, and offer it to the spirits of his ancestors. When the prince sent him a gift of a living animal, he would keep it alive. 2. When he was in attendance on the prince and joining in the entertainment, the prince only sacrificed. He first tasted everything. 3. When he was ill and the prince came to visit him, he had his head to the east, made his court robes be spread over him, and drew his girdle across them. 4. When the prince's order called him, without waiting for his carriage to be yoked, he went at once.
