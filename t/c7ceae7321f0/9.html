This section, in brief written by a model
This unit concerns the villagers and the conduct of a man during their communal activities. When the villagers were drinking together, he followed immediately after those who carried staffs went out. When the villagers were performing ceremonies to drive away pestilential influences, he put on his court robes and stood on the eastern steps.
