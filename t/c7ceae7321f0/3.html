This section, in brief written by a model
The unit concerns a person entering a palace and appearing highly respectful and uneasy in the presence of the prince. At the palace gate, the person bends his body, avoids the middle of the gate-way and threshold, and shows physical signs of anxiety when passing the prince’s vacant place. During the audience, he holds up his robe, bends his body, and restrains his breath. After leaving the audience, he relaxes somewhat but continues to show respectful uneasiness.

CHAP. IV. 1. When he entered the palace gate, he seemed to bend his body, as if it were not sufficient to admit him. 2. When he was standing, he did not occupy the middle of the gate-way; when he passed in or out, he did not tread upon the threshold. 3. When he was passing the vacant place of the prince, his countenance appeared to change, and his legs to bend under him, and his words came as if he hardly had breath to utter them. 4. He ascended the reception hall, holding up his robe with both his hands, and his body bent; holding in his breath also, as if he dared not breathe. 5. When he came out from the audience, as soon as he had descended one step, he began to relax his countenance, and had a satisfied look. When he had got to the bottom of the steps, he advanced rapidly to his place, with his arms like wings, and on occupying it, his manner still showed respectful uneasiness.
