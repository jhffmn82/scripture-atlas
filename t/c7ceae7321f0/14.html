This section, in brief written by a model
The unit concerns a man and his conduct toward friends, deceased friends, and presents. When a friend died without dependable relations to perform the necessary offices, he offered to bury him. He did not bow when a friend sent him a present, even if it was a carriage and horses, but he did bow for the flesh of sacrifice.

CHAP. XV. 1. When any of his friends died, if he had no relations who could be depended on for the necessary offices, he would say, 'I will bury him.' 2. When a friend sent him a present, though it might be a carriage and horses, he did not bow. 3. The only present for which he bowed was that of the flesh of sacrifice.
