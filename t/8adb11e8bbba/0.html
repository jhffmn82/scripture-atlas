Surah 97: The Power, Fate (Al-Qadr) concerns the Night of Predestination, which is described as better than a thousand months. The angels and the Spirit descend during that night by permission of their Lord, with all decrees. The night remains peace until the rising of the dawn.

Surah 97: The Power, Fate (Al-Qadr) Revealed at Mecca 1 Lo! We revealed it on the Night of Predestination. 2 Ah, what will convey unto thee what the Night of Power is! 3 The Night of Power is better than a thousand months. 4 The angels and the Spirit descend therein, by the permission of their Lord, with all decrees. 5 (The night is) Peace until the rising of the dawn.
