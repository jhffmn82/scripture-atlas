This section, in brief written by a model
The Master contrasts historiographer Yu and Chu Po-yu under good and bad government. When good government prevailed in his State, historiographer Yu was like an arrow, and when bad government prevailed, he was also like an arrow. Chu Po-yu is praised as a superior man who holds office under good government and keeps his principles in his breast under bad government.

CHAP. VI. 1. The Master said, 'Truly straightforward was the historiographer Yu. When good government prevailed in his State, he was like an arrow. When bad government prevailed, he was like an arrow. 2. A superior man indeed is Chu Po-yu! When good government prevails in his state, he is to be found in office. When bad government prevails, he can roll his principles up, and keep them in his breast.'
