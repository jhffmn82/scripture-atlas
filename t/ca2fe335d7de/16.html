This section, in brief written by a model
Visparad XVII (Sp. XX) concerns striving after the good thoughts, words, and deeds inculcated in the Yasna Haptanghâiti. It states that a blessing is the Right (called) the Best. It says there is weal, including weal for the man who is toward Righteousness Best and right.
