This section, in brief written by a model
Visparad XV instructs Zarathustrian Mazdayasnians to prepare themselves for lawful deeds in accordance with the sacred Order and to avoid unlawful and evil deeds contrary to the ritual. It directs them to perform good deeds that further husbandry and to render the needy rich. Sraosha is called to be present for the worship and service of Ahura Mazda and for devotion to the Yasna Haptanghâiti. The Yasna Haptanghâiti is to be memorised and recited victoriously, without addition or omission, for the pronunciation of victorious words for Ahura Mazda's Fire.

Visparad XV (Sp. XVIII). 1. Hold your feet in readiness, and your two hands, and your understandings, O ye Zarathustrian Mazdayasnians! for the well-doing of lawful deeds in accordance with the sacred Order, and for the avoidance of the unlawful and evil deeds which are contrary to the ritual. Let the good deeds for the furtherance of husbandry be done here. Render ye the needy rich. 2. Let Sraosha (Obedience) be present here for the worship of Ahura Mazda, the most helpful, and the holy, who is so desired by us in the pronunciation, and for the service, and the pondering of the Yasna Haptanghâiti, for the heart's devotion to it, for its memorisation, and its victorious and holy recital (or for the victorious saint), without addition or omission, (3) which has been intoned, and which shall yet be uttered as great, powerful, smiting with victory, separate from harmful malice, for the pronunciation of victorious words for Ahura Mazda's Fire. (4, 5 are identical with Visp. I X, 6, 7.)
