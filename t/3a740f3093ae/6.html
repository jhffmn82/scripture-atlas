This section, in brief written by a model
Chapter 7 describes the LORD's desire to heal Israel being met by the exposed wickedness of Ephraim and Samaria, where falsehood, theft, and robbery prevail. The princes and king are portrayed as adulterers and schemers, burning like an oven with plots, drunkenness, and anger, and they devour their judges while their kings fall without anyone calling on the LORD. Ephraim mingles with the nations, loses strength without realizing it, and is likened to a pancake not turned over and to a dove easily deceived that calls to Egypt and goes to Assyria. The LORD declares he will spread his net over them, chastise them, and bring woe and destruction because they wandered from him, spoke lies against him, and plot evil despite his teaching and strengthening them. The chapter ends with their return not being to the Most High, their comparison to a faulty bow, and the prediction that their princes will fall by the sword and be derided in Egypt.

Chapter 7 1 When I would heal Israel, then the iniquity of Ephraim is uncovered, also the wickedness of Samaria; for they commit falsehood, and the thief enters in, and the gang of robbers ravages outside. 2 They don’t consider in their hearts that I remember all their wickedness. Now their own deeds have engulfed them. They are before my face. 3 They make the king glad with their wickedness, and the princes with their lies. 4 They are all adulterers. They are burning like an oven that the baker stops stirring, from the kneading of the dough, until it is leavened. 5 On the day of our king, the princes made themselves sick with the heat of wine. He joined his hand with mockers. 6 For they have prepared their heart like an oven, while they lie in wait. Their anger smolders all night. In the morning it burns as a flaming fire. 7 They are all hot as an oven, and devour their judges. All their kings have fallen. There is no one among them who calls to me. 8 Ephraim mixes himself among the nations. Ephraim is a pancake not turned over. 9 Strangers have devoured his strength, and he doesn’t realize it. Indeed, gray hairs are here and there on him, and he doesn’t realize it. 10 The pride of Israel testifies to his face; yet they haven’t returned to the LORD their God, nor sought him, for all this. 11 “Ephraim is like an easily deceived dove, without understanding. They call to Egypt. They go to Assyria. 12 When they go, I will spread my net on them. I will bring them down like the birds of the sky. I will chastise them, as their congregation has heard. 13 Woe to them! For they have wandered from me. Destruction to them! For they have trespassed against me. Though I would redeem them, yet they have spoken lies against me. 14 They haven’t cried to me with their heart, but they howl on their beds. They assemble themselves for grain and new wine. They turn away from me. 15 Though I have taught and strengthened their arms, yet they plot evil against me. 16 They return, but not to the Most High. They are like a faulty bow. Their princes will fall by the sword for the rage of their tongue. This will be their derision in the land of Egypt.
