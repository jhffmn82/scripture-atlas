The LORD commands the speaker to go again and love a woman who is loved by another and is an adulteress, as the LORD loves the children of Israel even though they turn to other gods and love cakes of raisins. The speaker buys her for fifteen pieces of silver and a homer and a half of barley. He tells her she must stay with him many days, not play the prostitute, and not be with any other man, and that he will be so toward her. The text then says the children of Israel will live many days without king, prince, sacrifice, sacred stone, ephod or idols. Afterward they will return, seek the LORD their God and David their king, and come with trembling to the LORD and his blessings in the last days.

Chapter 3 1 The LORD said to me, “Go again, love a woman loved by another, and an adulteress, even as the LORD loves the children of Israel, though they turn to other gods, and love cakes of raisins.” 2 So I bought her for myself for fifteen pieces of silver and a homer and a half of barley. 3 I said to her, “You shall stay with me many days. You shall not play the prostitute, and you shall not be with any other man. I will also be so toward you.” 4 For the children of Israel shall live many days without king, without prince, without sacrifice, without sacred stone, and without ephod or idols. 5 Afterward the children of Israel shall return and seek the LORD their God, and David their king, and shall come with trembling to the LORD and to his blessings in the last days.
