This section, in brief written by a model
The speaker praises the Lord because divine anger has turned away and comfort has come. God is declared to be salvation, strength, and song, and people draw water with joy from the wells of salvation. The people are told to praise the Lord, call on his name, declare his doings, and sing because he has done excellent things. The inhabitant of Zion is told to cry out and shout because the Holy One of Israel is in its midst.

Chapter 22 1 And in that day thou shalt say: O Lord, I will praise thee; though thou wast angry with me thine anger is turned away, and thou comfortedst me. 2 Behold, God is my salvation; I will trust, and not be afraid; for the Lord JEHOVAH is my strength and my song; he also has become my salvation. 3 Therefore, with joy shall ye draw water out of the wells of salvation. 4 And in that day shall ye say: Praise the Lord, call upon his name, declare his doings among the people, make mention that his name is exalted. 5 Sing unto the Lord; for he hath done excellent things; this is known in all the earth. 6 Cry out and shout, thou inhabitant of Zion; for great is the Holy One of Israel in the midst of thee.
