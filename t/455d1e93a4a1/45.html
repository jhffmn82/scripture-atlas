This section, in brief written by a model
Chapter 45 records a message that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch the son of Neriah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, when Baruch was writing Jeremiah's words in a book. The LORD, the God of Israel, quotes Baruch's complaint that He has added sorrow to his pain and that he is weary with groaning and finds no rest. The LORD declares that He will break down what He has built and pluck up what He has planted throughout the whole land. He asks whether Baruch seeks great things for himself and tells him not to, since evil will come on all flesh. The LORD promises that Baruch will escape with his life wherever he goes.
In this section: the LORD · Jeremiah · Baruch the son of Neriah · Baruch's lament (sorrow and weariness) · coming judgment (the LORD's breaking down and plucking up)

Chapter 45 1 The message that Jeremiah the prophet spoke to Baruch the son of Neriah, when he wrote these words in a book at the mouth of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, saying, 2 “The LORD, the God of Israel, says to you, Baruch: 3 ‘You said, “Woe is me now! For the LORD has added sorrow to my pain! I am weary with my groaning, and I find no rest.”’ 4 “You shall tell him, the LORD says: ‘Behold, that which I have built, I will break down, and that which I have planted I will pluck up; and this in the whole land. 5 Do you seek great things for yourself? Don’t seek them; for, behold, I will bring evil on all flesh,’ says the LORD, ‘but I will let you escape with your life wherever you go.’”
