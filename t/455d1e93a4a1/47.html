This section, in brief written by a model
Chapter 47 records the LORD's word to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the Philistines, given before Pharaoh struck Gaza. The LORD describes waters rising out of the north that become an overflowing stream flooding the land, its cities and inhabitants, so that men cry out and all wail. The noise of the hoofs, chariots, and wheels of the invader's strong ones makes fathers too feeble to look back for their children. The day is coming to destroy all the Philistines and cut off every remaining helper from Tyre and Sidon, and Gaza and Ashkelon are described as bald and brought to nothing. The chapter ends with an address to the sword of the LORD, which asks how long until it is quiet, and is answered that it cannot rest because the LORD has commanded it against Ashkelon and the seashore.

Chapter 47 1 The LORD’s word that came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the Philistines, before Pharaoh struck Gaza. 2 The LORD says: “Behold, waters rise up out of the north, and will become an overflowing stream, and will overflow the land and all that is therein, the city and those who dwell therein. The men will cry, and all the inhabitants of the land will wail. 3 At the noise of the stamping of the hoofs of his strong ones, at the rushing of his chariots, at the rumbling of his wheels, the fathers don’t look back for their children because their hands are so feeble, 4 because of the day that comes to destroy all the Philistines, to cut off from Tyre and Sidon every helper who remains; for the LORD will destroy the Philistines, the remnant of the isle of Caphtor. 5 Baldness has come on Gaza; Ashkelon is brought to nothing. You remnant of their valley, how long will you cut yourself? 6 “‘You sword of the LORD, how long will it be before you are quiet? Put yourself back into your scabbard; rest, and be still.’ 7 “How can you be quiet, since the LORD has given you a command? Against Ashkelon, and against the seashore, there he has appointed it.”
