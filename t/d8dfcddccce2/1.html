This section, in brief written by a model
Chapter 2 instructs the addressee to speak what fits sound doctrine and to teach each group in the community. Older men are to be temperate, sensible, and sound in faith, love, and perseverance. Older women are to be reverent and good teachers who train young wives to love their husbands and children and to be chaste, kind, and workers at home. Younger men are to be sober minded, and servants are to be subject to their masters, honest, and faithful. The addressee must be an example of good works with sound speech, and the chapter grounds this conduct in the grace of God, which brings salvation, teaches godly living, and looks for the appearing of God and Savior Jesus Christ, who gave himself to redeem and purify a people zealous for good works.

Chapter 2 1 But say the things which fit sound doctrine, 2 that older men should be temperate, sensible, sober minded, sound in faith, in love, and in perseverance, 3 and that older women likewise be reverent in behavior, not slanderers nor enslaved to much wine, teachers of that which is good, 4 that they may train the young wives to love their husbands, to love their children, 5 to be sober minded, chaste, workers at home, kind, being in subjection to their own husbands, that God’s word may not be blasphemed. 6 Likewise, exhort the younger men to be sober minded. 7 In all things show yourself an example of good works. In your teaching, show integrity, seriousness, incorruptibility, 8 and soundness of speech that can’t be condemned, that he who opposes you may be ashamed, having no evil thing to say about us. 9 Exhort servants to be in subjection to their own masters and to be well-pleasing in all things, not contradicting, 10 not stealing, but showing all good fidelity, that they may adorn the doctrine of God, our Savior, in all things. 11 For the grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation to all men, 12 instructing us to the intent that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we would live soberly, righteously, and godly in this present age; 13 looking for the blessed hope and appearing of the glory of our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ, 14 who gave himself for us, that he might redeem us from all iniquity and purify for himself a people for his own possession, zealous for good works. 15 Say these things and exhort and reprove with all authority. Let no one despise you.
