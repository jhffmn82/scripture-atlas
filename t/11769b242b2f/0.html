Surah 92: The Night (Al-Lail) contrasts the night and day and declares that human effort is directed toward different ends. It describes the generous and dutiful person who believes in goodness as being eased toward ease, while the hoarding and self-sufficient person who disbelieves in goodness is eased toward adversity. It warns that the flaming Fire is endured by the most wretched person, who denies and turns away, while the righteous are kept far from it. The righteous give their wealth seeking the purpose of their Lord Most High and will be content.

Surah 92: The Night (Al-Lail) Revealed at Mecca 1 By the night enshrouding 2 And the day resplendent 3 And Him Who hath created male and female, 4 Lo! your effort is dispersed (toward divers ends). 5 As for him who giveth and is dutiful (toward Allah) 6 And believeth in goodness; 7 Surely We will ease his way unto the state of ease. 8 But as for him who hoardeth and deemeth himself independent, 9 And disbelieveth in goodness; 10 Surely We will ease his way unto adversity. 11 His riches will not save him when he perisheth. 12 Lo! Ours it is (to give) the guidance 13 And lo! unto Us belong the latter portion and the former. 14 Therefor have I warned you of the flaming Fire 15 Which only the most wretched must endure, 16 He who denieth and turneth away. 17 Far removed from it will be the righteous 18 Who giveth his wealth that he may grow (in goodness). 19 And none hath with him any favour for reward, 20 Except as seeking (to fulfil) the purpose of his Lord Most High. 21 He verily will be content.
