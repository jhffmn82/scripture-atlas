This section, in brief written by a model
The Splitting Open (Al-Inshiqaaq) describes the heaven and earth responding attentively to Allah and the final accounting of man. Those given their account in their right hand receive an easy reckoning and return joyfully to their folk, while those given it behind their back face destruction and scorching fire. The text warns that all people journey from plane to plane and that Allah knows what those who disbelieve conceal. Those who believe and do good works receive an unfailing reward.

Surah 84: The Splitting Open (Al-Inshiqaaq) Revealed at Mecca 1 When the heaven is split asunder 2 And attentive to her Lord in fear, 3 And when the earth is spread out 4 And hath cast out all that was in her, and is empty 5 And attentive to her Lord in fear! 6 Thou, verily, O man, art working toward thy Lord a work which thou wilt meet (in His presence). 7 Then whoso is given his account in his right hand 8 He truly will receive an easy reckoning 9 And will return unto his folk in joy. 10 But whoso is given his account behind his back, 11 He surely will invoke destruction 12 And be thrown to scorching fire. 13 He verily lived joyous with his folk, 14 He verily deemed that he would never return (unto Allah). 15 Nay, but lo! his Lord is ever looking on him! 16 Oh, I swear by the afterglow of sunset, 17 And by the night and all that it enshroudeth, 18 And by the moon when she is at the full, 19 That ye shall journey on from plane to plane. 20 What aileth them, then, that they believe not 21 And, when the Qur'an is recited unto them, worship not (Allah)? 22 Nay, but those who disbelieve will deny; 23 And Allah knoweth best what they are hiding. 24 So give them tidings of a painful doom, 25 Save those who believe and do good works, for theirs is a reward unfailing.
