  1. Surah 90: The City (Al-Balad)
This section, in brief written by a model
The City (Al-Balad) is presented as the place by which the speaker swears, where the addressee dwells. Man is created in an atmosphere, given eyes, a tongue, two lips, and guidance to the mountain ways, yet some think no one has power over or sees them and boast of destroying wealth. The Ascent is identified with freeing a slave, feeding someone in the day of hunger, helping an orphan near of kin or a poor wretch in misery, and believing while exhorting one another to perseverance and pity. Believers are assigned the right hand, while disbelievers who reject the revelations are assigned the left hand, with Fire as an awning over them.

Surah 90: The City (Al-Balad) Revealed at Mecca 1 Nay, I swear by this city - 2 And thou art an indweller of this city - 3 And the begetter and that which he begat, 4 We verily have created man in an atmosphere: 5 Thinketh he that none hath power over him? 6 And he saith: I have destroyed vast wealth: 7 Thinketh he that none beholdeth him? 8 Did We not assign unto him two eyes 9 And a tongue and two lips, 10 And guide him to the parting of the mountain ways? 11 But he hath not attempted the Ascent - 12 Ah, what will convey unto thee what the Ascent is! - 13 (It is) to free a slave, 14 And to feed in the day of hunger. 15 An orphan near of kin, 16 Or some poor wretch in misery, 17 And to be of those who believe and exhort one another to perseverance and exhort one another to pity. 18 Their place will be on the right hand. 19 But those who disbelieve Our revelations, their place will be on the left hand. 20 Fire will be an awning over them.
