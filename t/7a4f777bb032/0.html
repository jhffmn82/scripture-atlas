Surah 111: The Palm Fibre (Al-Masad) concerns Abu Lahab, his fate, and his wife. Abu Lahab’s power will perish, and he will perish. His wealth and gains will not exempt him from being plunged into flaming Fire. His wife, the wood-carrier, will have a halter of palm-fibre upon her neck.
