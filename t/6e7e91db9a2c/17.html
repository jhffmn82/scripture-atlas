This section, in brief written by a model
Empress Sui-Ko, also called Toyo-mike-kashiki-ya-hime, dwelt at the Palace of Wohorida. She ruled the Empire for thirty seven years. Her august mausoleum was initially on the mound of Ohonu and was later removed to the great sepulchre at Shinaga.
