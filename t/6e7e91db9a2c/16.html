This section, in brief written by a model
Emperor Su-Jun, also called the Heavenly Sovereign Hatsuse-be-no-waka-sazaki, dwelt at the Palace of Shibabaki at Kurahashi. He ruled the Empire for four years. His august mausoleum is on the mound of Kurahashi.
