This section, in brief written by a model
Tobias calls Raphael, whom he addresses as Brother Azarias, and asks him to take a servant and two camels to Gabael in Rages of Media to collect money for him and bring Gabael to the wedding feast. Tobias explains that Raguel has sworn he must not leave, and that his father counts the days and will be very grieved if he is delayed. Raphael goes, lodges with Gabael, and hands over the handwriting, and Gabael brings out the sealed bags of money and gives them to him. Raphael and Gabael rise early the next morning and travel together to the wedding feast, where Tobias blesses his wife.

Chapter 9 1 And Tobias called Raphael, and said to him, 2 “Brother Azarias, take with you a servant and two camels, and go to Rages of Media to Gabael, and receive the money for me, and bring him to the wedding feast, 3 because Raguel has sworn that I must not depart. 4 My father counts the days; and if I wait long, he will be very grieved. 5 So Raphael went on his way, and lodged with Gabael, and gave him the handwriting; so he brought forth the bags with their seals, and gave them to him. 6 Then they rose up early in the morning together, and came to the wedding feast. Tobias blessed his wife.
