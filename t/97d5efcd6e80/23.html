This section, in brief written by a model
The Fourteenth Lecture concerns a mendicant’s conduct regarding reciprocal actions that relate to himself and produce karman. He should neither be pleased with nor prohibit such actions, including wiping or rubbing each other’s feet. The whole Thirteenth Lecture is to be repeated in this context. The unit ends by stating the whole duty and marking the end of the Second Part, called the Seven Lectures.

Fourteenth Lecture. One should not be pleased with nor prohibit a reciprocal action, which relates to one's self, and produces karman. A mendicant should not be pleased with nor prohibit it, if (he and the other) wipe or rub each other's feet, &c. In this way the whole Thirteenth Lecture should be repeated here. This is the whole duty, &c. Thus I say. (1) End of the Second Part, called the Seven Lectures.
