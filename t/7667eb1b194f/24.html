This section, in brief written by a model
Mencius addresses the disciple Yue Zheng about his coming there in the train of Zi Ao. Mencius says that Yue Zheng came only because of the food and drink. He expresses disbelief that Yue Zheng, having learned the doctrine of the ancients, would act with a view to eating and drinking.

Chapter 25 Mencius, addressing the disciple Yue Zheng, said to him, 'Your coming here in the train of Zi Ao was only because of the food and the drink. I could not have thought that you, having learned the doctrine of the ancients, would have acted with a view to eating and drinking.'
