This section, in brief written by a model
Mencius states that people commonly speak of the kingdom, the State, and the family. He explains that the State is the root of the kingdom. He further explains that the family is the root of the State, and the person of its Head is the root of the family.

Chapter 5 Mencius said, 'People have this common saying, "The kingdom, the State, the family." The root of the kingdom is in the State. The root of the State is in the family. The root of the family is in the person of its Head.'
