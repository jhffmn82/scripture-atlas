This section, in brief written by a model
Chun Yu Kun asks Mencius whether males and females should avoid touching hands when giving or receiving things. Mencius says this is the general rule but explains that a man must rescue his drowning sister-in-law with his hand. Kun compares the whole kingdom to a drowning person and questions why Mencius will not rescue it. Mencius answers that a drowning kingdom must be rescued with right principles, not by hand.

Chapter 17 Chun Yu Kun said, 'Is it the rule that males and females shall not allow their hands to touch in giving or receiving anything?' Mencius replied, 'It is the rule.' Kun asked, 'If a man's sister-in-law be drowning, shall he rescue her with his hand?' Mencius said, 'He who would not so rescue the drowning woman is a wolf. For males and females not to allow their hands to touch in giving and receiving is the general rule; when a sister-in-law is drowning, to rescue her with the hand is a peculiar exigency.' Kun said, 'The whole kingdom is drowning. How strange it is that you will not rescue it!' Mencius answered, 'A drowning kingdom must be rescued with right principles, as a drowning sister-in-law has to be rescued with the hand. Do you wish me to rescue the kingdom with my hand?'
