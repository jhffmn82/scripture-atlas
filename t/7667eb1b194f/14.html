This section, in brief written by a model
Mencius teaches that the pupil of the eye is the most excellent part of a man's body. The pupil cannot hide a man's wickedness because it is bright when his breast is correct and dull when it is not. By listening to a man's words and looking at his pupil, one can learn his character.

Chapter 15 Mencius said, 'Of all the parts of a man's body there is none more excellent than the pupil of the eye. The pupil cannot be used to hide a man's wickedness. If within the breast all be correct, the pupil is bright. If within the breast all be not correct, the pupil is dull. Listen to a man's words and look at the pupil of his eye. How can a man conceal his character?'
