This section, in brief written by a model
The unit concerns Satan's rebellion against Allah and his efforts to mislead people through false promises and stirred desires. It states that those who choose Satan instead of Allah will lose and dwell in hell. It contrasts them with those who believe and do good works, who will enter Gardens beneath which rivers flow and abide there forever. It also praises Abraham for surrendering to Allah and following the upright tradition.
