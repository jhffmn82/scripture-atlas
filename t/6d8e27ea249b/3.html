This section, in brief written by a model
The LORD of Armies announces that the coming day will burn like a furnace, consuming the proud and all who work wickedness so that nothing remains of them, neither root nor branch. For those who fear his name, the sun of righteousness will rise with healing in its wings, and they will leap like calves of the stall and tread down the wicked, who will become ashes under their feet. The LORD calls the people to remember the law of Moses his servant, given at Horeb for all Israel as statutes and ordinances. He promises to send Elijah the prophet before the great and terrible day of the LORD comes. Elijah will turn the hearts of fathers to children and children to fathers, so that the LORD does not come and strike the earth with a curse.

Chapter 4 1 “For behold, the day comes, burning like a furnace, when all the proud and all who work wickedness will be stubble. The day that comes will burn them up,” says the LORD of Armies, “so that it will leave them neither root nor branch. 2 But to you who fear my name shall the sun of righteousness arise with healing in its wings. You will go out and leap like calves of the stall. 3 You shall tread down the wicked; for they will be ashes under the soles of your feet in the day that I make,” says the LORD of Armies. 4 “Remember the law of Moses my servant, which I commanded to him in Horeb for all Israel, even statutes and ordinances. 5 Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the great and terrible day of the LORD comes. 6 He will turn the hearts of the fathers to the children and the hearts of the children to their fathers, lest I come and strike the earth with a curse.”
