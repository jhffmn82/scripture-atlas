This section, in brief written by a model
Chapter 2 presents Jonah's prayer to the LORD, his God, from inside the fish's belly. Jonah recounts his affliction, how the LORD threw him into the depths of the seas where waves, weeds, and the deep surrounded him, and how he felt banished yet resolved to look again toward the LORD's holy temple. He says the LORD brought his life up from the pit, and that when his soul fainted he remembered the LORD. He contrasts those who regard vain idols with himself, vowing to sacrifice with thanksgiving and declaring that salvation belongs to the LORD. At the end, the LORD speaks to the fish, and it vomits Jonah onto dry land.

Chapter 2 1 Then Jonah prayed to the LORD, his God, out of the fish’s belly. 2 He said, “I called because of my affliction to the LORD. He answered me. Out of the belly of Sheol I cried. You heard my voice. 3 For you threw me into the depths, in the heart of the seas. The flood was all around me. All your waves and your billows passed over me. 4 I said, ‘I have been banished from your sight; yet I will look again toward your holy temple.’ 5 The waters surrounded me, even to the soul. The deep was around me. The weeds were wrapped around my head. 6 I went down to the bottoms of the mountains. The earth barred me in forever; yet you have brought my life up from the pit, LORD my God. 7 “When my soul fainted within me, I remembered the LORD. My prayer came in to you, into your holy temple. 8 Those who regard vain idols forsake their own mercy. 9 But I will sacrifice to you with the voice of thanksgiving. I will pay that which I have vowed. Salvation belongs to the LORD.” 10 Then the LORD spoke to the fish, and it vomited out Jonah on the dry land.
