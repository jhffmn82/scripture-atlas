Surah 87: The Most High (Al-A'laa) concerns praise of the Lord the Most High, who creates, measures, guides, brings forth pasturage, and turns it to stubble. It states that Allah knows what is disclosed and hidden, and that the way to ease will be made accessible. The passage urges reminding people, describing the heedful as successful and the hapless as flung into the great Fire. It teaches that the Hereafter is better and more lasting than the life of the world and says this is found in the former scrolls, the Books of Abraham and Moses.

Surah 87: The Most High (Al-A'laa) Revealed at Mecca 1 Praise the name of thy Lord the Most High, 2 Who createth, then disposeth; 3 Who measureth, then guideth; 4 Who bringeth forth the pasturage, 5 Then turneth it to russet stubble. 6 We shall make thee read (O Muhammad) so that thou shalt not forget 7 Save that which Allah willeth. Lo! He knoweth the disclosed and that which still is hidden; 8 And We shall ease thy way unto the state of ease. 9 Therefor remind (men), for of use is the reminder. 10 He will heed who feareth, 11 But the most hapless will flout it, 12 He who will be flung to the great Fire 13 Wherein he will neither die nor live. 14 He is successful who groweth, 15 And remembereth the name of his Lord, so prayeth, 16 But ye prefer the life of the world 17 Although the Hereafter is better and more lasting. 18 Lo! This is in the former scrolls. 19 The Books of Abraham and Moses.
