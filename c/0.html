Qur'an and Prophetic Teachings

a collection of 116 works · 899 entities, 198 of them in two or more works

Abstract written by a model

A collection of Qur'anic surahs and teachings attributed to the Prophet Muhammad concerning Allah's sovereignty, revelation, worship, ethical conduct, prophetic history, law, and final judgment.

A model named and described this body of work when it grouped the works; the works and their own abstracts follow.
