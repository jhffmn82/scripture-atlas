The Bible

a collection of 73 works · 4,853 entities, 614 of them in two or more works

Abstract written by a model

A sacred anthology of Jewish and Christian writings encompassing law, history, poetry, wisdom, prophecy, Gospels, letters, and apocalyptic vision centered on God’s relationship with humanity.

A model named and described this body of work when it grouped the works; the works and their own abstracts follow.
