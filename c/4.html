    This unit concerns Quraysh and their taming. It says that the caravans set forth in winter and summer for their taming. Quraysh are told to worship the Lord of this House. The Lord of this House has fed them against hunger and made them safe from fear.

  • Surah 107: Almsgiving (Al-Maa'un)

    Surah 107: Almsgiving (Al-Maa'un) was revealed at Mecca. It describes him who belieth religion as one who repelleth the orphan and urgeth not the feeding of the needy. It warns worshippers who are heedless of their prayer, seek to be seen at worship, and refuse small kindnesses.

  • Surah 108: Abundance (Al-Kawthar)

    Surah 108: Abundance (Al-Kawthar) was revealed at Mecca. It says that the addressee has been given Abundance. It instructs the addressee to pray unto the Lord and sacrifice, and says that the insulter, not the addressee, is without posterity.

  • Surah 113: The Dawn (Al-Falaq)

    Surah 113: The Dawn (Al-Falaq) was revealed at Mecca. It tells the listener to seek refuge in the Lord of the Daybreak. It names evil from created things, intense darkness, malignant witchcraft, and the envier when he envies. The passage concerns protection from these forms of evil.

  • Surah 114: Mankind (An-Naas)

    Surah 114: Mankind (An-Naas) was revealed at Mecca. It tells mankind to seek refuge in the Lord, King, and god of mankind. It warns of the evil of the sneaking whisperer, who whispers in the hearts of mankind. The sneaking whisperer is associated with the jinn and mankind.
