Abstract written by a model

Surah 99: The Earthquake (Az-Zalzala), revealed at Medina, describes Earth’s final earthquake and the events of that day. Earth is shaken, yields up her burdens, and relates her chronicles because the Lord inspires her. Mankind issues forth in scattered groups to be shown their deeds. Every person sees the result of deeds weighing an atom, whether good or ill.
