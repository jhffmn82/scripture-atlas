Abstract written by a model

Surah 93: The Morning Hours (Ad-Dhuhaa) concerns the Lord’s reassurance and guidance to the addressed person. It says that the Lord has not forsaken or hated the addressed person and that the latter portion will be better than the former. The Lord promises to give to the addressed person until the addressed person is content, recalling that He protected, directed, and enriched the addressed person. The unit commands that the orphan not be oppressed, the beggar not be driven away, and the bounty of the Lord be spoken of.
