Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns Allah’s revelation through Muhammad, the call to worship Allah, and the accountability of mankind after death. It contrasts believers, who receive guidance, mercy, good tidings, and the Garden, with disbelievers and wrongdoers, who face error, punishment, and the Fire. It recounts the experiences of Noah’s people, Moses, Pharaoh, the Children of Israel, and the folk of Jonah (Yunus) as signs of Allah’s power and judgment. The unit affirms creation and resurrection, the return to Allah, and the truth of the revelation.
