Abstract written by a model

Surah 11: Hud presents perfected, expounded Scripture from Allah, calling people to serve Him alone, seek forgiveness, repent, and prepare to return and be raised before Him. It contrasts humble believers who do good works and enter the Garden with disbelievers devoted to worldly life who face the Fire. It then recounts Noah, Hud, Salih, Abraham, Lot, Shu'eyb, and Moses: their peoples reject warnings and commit wrongdoing; Noah and believers are saved in the ship, while the rejecting peoples are destroyed. It closes with Resurrection, worship, repentance, punishment, the Fire, and the Garden.

Surah 11: Hud / 1 Alif. Lam. Ra. (This is) a Scripture the revelations whereof are perfected and then expounded. (It com body

Surah 11: Hud presents Scripture as perfected revelations from Allah, commanding people to serve Him alone, seek forgiveness, and turn repentantly to Him. It warns that people will return to Allah, be raised after death, and face the consequences of belief or disbelief. Believers who do good works and humble themselves before Allah will enter the Garden, while disbelievers and those who pursue only worldly life face the Fire. The unit contrasts those who hear and see the truth with those who reject it and lose themselves.

25 And We sent Noah unto his folk (and he said): Lo! I am a plain warner unto yo body

This unit recounts the warnings and fates of Noah, Hud, Salih, Abraham, Lot, Shu'eyb, and Moses and their peoples. Noah's folk reject his warning, the Flood destroys the disbelievers, and the ship saves Noah and the believers. A'ad, Thamud, the folk of Lot, and Midian are destroyed after rejecting their messengers and committing wrongdoing. The unit also describes the Day of Resurrection, the Fire, the Garden, worship, repentance, and the consequences of punishment.
