The unit concerns Muhammad, the Jinn, humankind, and Allah. The Jinn hear the Qur'an, believe in its guidance, affirm Allah's uniqueness, and describe their different groups and accountability. Muhammad is instructed to say that he prays only to Allah, controls neither harm nor benefit, and conveys the Truth and Allah's messages.
