Abstract written by a model

Surah 24: The Light (An-Noor) concerns Allah's revelations, legal and social guidance, the conduct of believers, and warnings to those who slander or disbelieve. It addresses accusations, privacy, modesty, marriage, worship, obedience to the messenger, and relations within the community. It describes Allah's light, the signs in the heavens and the earth, and the consequences awaiting believers and disbelievers in the world and the Hereafter.
