Abstract written by a model

Enos describes his struggle before God, receiving a remission of his sins through faith in Christ and then praying for the welfare of the Nephites and Lamanites. God promises to answer Enos according to his faith and covenants to preserve the records of the Nephites for the Lamanites in his own due time. Enos prophesies and testifies among the Nephites, whose efforts to restore the Lamanites to the true faith are unsuccessful. The chapter also describes the land, the peoples' conduct, wars between the Nephites and Lamanites, and Enos's expectation of rest with his Redeemer and of putting on immortality.
