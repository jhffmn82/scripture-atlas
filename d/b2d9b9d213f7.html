Abstract written by a model

The unit concerns Allah's signs, judgment, worship, and the fate of those who believe or disbelieve. It recounts Abraham's honoured guests, Moses and Pharaoh, A'ad, Thamud, and Noah, describing signs and punishments connected with them. It teaches that jinn and humankind were created to worship Allah, that all things were created in pairs, and that warning benefits believers. It promises gardens and watersprings for those who keep from evil and an evil day for disbelievers and wrongdoers.
