Abstract written by a model

The unit concerns the Sovereignty (Al-Mulk), declaring Allah’s power over creation, life, death, the heavens, the earth, provision, and resurrection. It describes the fate of the disbelievers in hell, where they acknowledge that they denied the warnings. Muhammad is instructed to warn people, affirm trust in Allah, and answer questions about judgment, protection, and provision. The unit urges people to observe creation, give thanks, and recognize that knowledge and sustenance belong to Allah.
