Abstract written by a model

Surah 75: The Resurrection (Al-Qiyaama) concerns the Day of Resurrection, the resurrection of the dead, and the fate of man. It describes the cosmic signs of that day, including confused sight, an eclipsed moon, and the joining of the sun and moon. Man will be shown what he sent before and left behind, while some faces will be resplendent and others despondent. The unit also warns that man will face death and be brought before his Lord, who is able to restore him after death.
