Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns God’s compassion, worship, charity, fasting, reading the Korān, honest labour, fighting for the faith, judgment, women and slaves, animals, and hospitality. It describes rewards and punishments connected with prayer, almsgiving, conduct, debt, justice, family relations, and treatment of animals. It also records teachings attributed to the Prophet about social duties, religious observance, and ethical behavior.
