Abstract written by a model

Surah 86: The Morning Star (At-Taariq) concerns the Morning Star, human creation, resurrection, and the Qur'an. It says that every human soul has a guardian and that hidden thoughts will be searched out. It declares the Qur'an to be a conclusive word and describes a plot against Muhammad. The disbelievers are to be given a respite and dealt with gently for a while.
