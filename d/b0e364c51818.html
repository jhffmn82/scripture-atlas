Abstract written by a model

Surah 104: The Traducer (Al-Humaza), revealed at Mecca, warns every slandering traducer who gathers and arranges wealth (of this world). Such a person thinks that wealth will make him immortal, but he will be flung to the Consuming One. The Consuming One is identified as the fire of Allah, kindled and leaping over the hearts of men. The fire is closed in on them in outstretched columns.
