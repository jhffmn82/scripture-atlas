Abstract written by a model

The unit concerns Allah’s revelation of Scripture through a messenger to bring mankind from darkness to light and guide them to the way of Allah. It recounts Moses’ call to his people, the rejection of messengers by earlier peoples, the fate of disbelievers in Hell, and the reward of believers in the Gardens. It presents Allah’s signs in the heavens and the earth, Abraham’s prayers concerning worship, his descendants, and the holy House, and warnings about the Day of Judgment. It concludes that the clear message teaches that Allah is One and calls people of understanding to take heed.
