Abstract written by a model

Surah 81: The Overthrowing (At-Takwir) describes signs of the Last Day and what every soul will know about its deeds. It concerns the sun, stars, hills, animals, seas, souls, the girl-child that was buried alive, the sky, hell, and the Garden. It affirms that the revelation is the word of an honoured messenger, supported by the Lord of the Throne, and that your comrade is not mad. It calls creation to remember and walk straight, while stating that none wills unless Allah willeth.
