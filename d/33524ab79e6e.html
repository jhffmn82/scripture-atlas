Abstract written by a model

Surah 61: The Ranks (As-Saff), revealed at Medina, concerns Allah, His messengers, believers, and those who disbelieve. It condemns saying what one does not do and praises those who battle for Allah's cause in solid ranks. Moses and Jesus son of Mary are presented as messengers, and Jesus announces a messenger after him whose name is the Praised One. Believers are called to faith, striving with wealth and lives, and becoming Allah's helpers, with forgiveness, Gardens, help from Allah, and present victory promised.
