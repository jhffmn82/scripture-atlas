Abstract written by a model

Surah 110: Divine Support (An-Nasr) concerns Allah's succour, the triumph, mankind, and the religion of Allah. It describes mankind entering the religion of Allah in troops when Allah's succour and the triumph come. It instructs the addressed person to hymn the praises of the Lord and seek forgiveness, because He is ever ready to show mercy.
