Abstract written by a model

Surah 44 concerns the Scripture revealed by Allah on a blessed night as a warning and mercy, and it calls people to recognize Allah as Lord and reject doubt. It describes the visible smoke, the torment, and the greater seizure awaiting those who turn away, while recounting how Pharaoh's folk opposed the noble messenger and how the Children of Israel were delivered. It contrasts the punishment of the sinner with the secured place prepared for the people of duty on the Day of Decision. Muhammad is told that the Scripture has been made easy in his language and to wait while the disbelievers also wait.
