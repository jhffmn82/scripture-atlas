Abstract written by a model

Surah 36: Yaseen (Yaseen) concerns Ya Sin, the revelation of the Qur'an, warnings to humanity, and signs of Allah's power. It describes the people of the city rejecting the messengers, while a man from the city urges them to follow those who ask no fee. It presents signs in the earth, night, sun, moon, living creatures, and human creation, and describes the resurrection, the trumpet blast, paradise, hell, and the Day of Judgment. It concludes that Allah's command brings things into being and that all will be brought back to Him.
