Abstract written by a model

The Inevitable (Al-Waaqia) describes the event that will shake the earth, grind the hills to dust, and divide people into three groups. Those brought nigh and those on the right hand receive gardens, nourishment, peace, and delight, while those on the left hand, including the erring deniers and rejecters, face scorching wind, boiling water, the tree called Zaqqum, and hell-fire. The text calls human beings to recognize that the Lord of the Worlds creates them, controls death, cultivates plants, sends water, and causes fire. It declares the noble Qur'an to be a revelation from the Lord of the Worlds, kept in a hidden Book, and calls Muhammad to praise the name of his Lord.
