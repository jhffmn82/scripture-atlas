Abstract written by a model

Surah 74: The Cloaked One (Al-Muddaththir) concerns the addressed prophet, who is commanded to rise, warn, magnify his Lord, purify his raiment, shun pollution, and remain patient. It describes the man created by Allah who rejects Allah's revelations and is threatened with the burning. It explains that the Fire has nineteen angelic wardens and presents this number as a test for believers, those given Scripture, and disbelievers. The unit warns men about the Hereafter, the Day of Judgment, and the consequences awaiting the guilty, while calling whoever wills to heed the Reminder.
