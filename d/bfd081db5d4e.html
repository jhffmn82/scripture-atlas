Abstract written by a model

Surah 89: The Dawn concerns the oath of the Dawn, ten nights, the Even and the Odd, and the departing night. It recounts how the Lord dealt with A'ad, Iram, Thamud, and Pharaoh because of their rebellion and iniquity. It describes man's mistaken judgments about honor and deprivation, and his failure to honor the orphan or feed the poor. It warns of the Day when the earth is ground to atoms, the angels come rank on rank, hell is brought near, and the soul at peace is invited to return to its Lord and enter the Garden.
