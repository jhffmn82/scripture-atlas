Abstract written by a model

Surah 78: The Announcement (An-Naba) concerns the awful tidings about which the questioners disagree. It describes signs of the Lord’s power, including the earth, the high hills, human beings, the seven strong heavens, rain, grain, plants, and gardens. It warns of the Day of Decision, when the heavens open, the hills move, hell receives the rebellious, and the deeds of all are recorded. It contrasts the torment of those who denied with the reward of the duteous and warns the disbeliever of a doom at hand.
