The Great Learning

Confucius · 1 section
The Great Learning, translated by James Legge (1893), public domain, via sacred-texts.com. Shown here as plain text, with facts drawn from it. https://sacred-texts.com/cfu/

Abstract written by a model

The Great Learning teaches that people should illustrate illustrious virtue, renovate the people, and rest in the highest excellence through ordered stages of self-cultivation and government. The Text of Confucius explains that investigating things completes knowledge, sincere thoughts rectify the heart, and cultivation of the person regulates the family and state. Chapter 10 teaches that the sovereign gains or loses the kingdom through virtue and the people, while wealth must remain subordinate to righteousness. It also describes the virtuous minister, the production and use of wealth, and the dangers of favoritism, selfishness, and improper gain.
