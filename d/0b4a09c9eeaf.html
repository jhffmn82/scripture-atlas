Surah 80: He frowned (Abasa)

Abstract written by a model

Surah 80: He frowned (Abasa) concerns a man who turns away from a blind man while attending to one who thinks himself independent. It teaches that the Admonishment is available for whoever will heed it and describes man's creation, death, burial, and return to life. It recounts how food and provision come through rain, crops, fruits, and grasses for people and cattle. It describes the Shout and the day when people flee from their closest relatives, with bright faces for some and dust-covered, darkened faces for disbelievers and the wicked.
