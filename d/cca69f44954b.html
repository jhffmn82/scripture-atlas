Abstract written by a model

Surah 9: The Repentance sets out Allah’s commands to Muhammad and the believers on treaties, idolaters, worship, alms, warfare, and allegiance. It contrasts faithful believers with idolaters, disbelievers, hypocrites, wandering Arabs, and those staying behind without excuse, describing repentance, mercy, punishment, hell, and the Hereafter. It recalls Huneyn and the cave, identifies poor-due recipients, and addresses prayer, covenant, and believers’ pledge of lives and wealth. It contrasts worship founded on duty to Allah with a mosque causing dissent, ending with promised acceptance, the Garden, Gardens of Eden, and supreme triumph for the faithful.

Surah 9: The Repentance (At-Tawba) body

This unit concerns Allah's commands to Muhammad and the believers regarding treaties, idolaters, worship, alms, warfare, and allegiance. It describes the conduct and consequences of idolaters, disbelievers, hypocrites, and believers, including their treatment of Allah's revelations and messenger. It recounts events at the day of Huneyn and in the cave, and contrasts the life of the world with the Hereafter. It also identifies recipients of the poor-due and promises believers mercy, acceptance, and the Gardens of Eden.

73 O Prophet! Strive against the disbelievers and the hypocrites! Be harsh with body

This unit concerns Allah's commands and judgments regarding the Prophet, the messenger of Allah, believers, disbelievers, hypocrites, the Muhajirin, the Ansar, the wandering Arabs, and the townspeople of Al-Madinah. It describes striving in Allah's way, excuses for staying behind, the consequences of disbelief and hypocrisy, and Allah's mercy toward those who repent. It also addresses alms, prayer, Allah's covenant, the pledge of believers' lives and wealth, and the place of worship founded upon duty to Allah versus the mosque chosen to cause dissent. The unit promises the Garden and the supreme triumph to the faithful, while warning of hell, the painful doom, and the consequences of wrongdoing.
