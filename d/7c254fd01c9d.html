Abstract written by a model

The Reality concerns the judgment to come, the destruction of Thamud, A'ad, Pharaoh, and other communities, and the events that accompany the final Event. Allah describes the trumpet blast, the lifting and crushing of the earth and mountains, the splitting of the heaven, and the exposure of all people. Those given their record in the right hand enter bliss, while those given it in the left hand are fettered and cast into hell-fire. The unit declares that the revelation is the speech of an illustrious messenger from the Lord of the Worlds and absolute truth.
