Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods)

Futo no Yasumaro · 38 sections
Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods), translated by Basil Hall Chamberlain (1882), public domain. Shown here as plain text, with facts drawn from it. https://sacred-texts.com/shi/kj/

Abstract written by a model

This is the first volume of Kojiki, the age of the gods, presented by Yasumaro as a record compiled from traditions memorized by Hiyeda no Are under the Empress’s command. It begins with the birth of the separate Heavenly Deities and the command to Male-Who-Invites and Female-Who-Invites to create the drifting land. Their first failed union is corrected, producing the Land-of-the-Eight-Great-Islands and further islands, followed by many deities. Female-Who-Invites dies after bearing the Fire Deity; Male-Who-Invites kills that child, seeks her in Hades, escapes its pollution, and purifies himself. From this purification come the Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity, Moon-Night Possessor, and Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness. Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness is expelled after violent offenses against Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity, whose withdrawal into the Heavenly Rock-Dwelling darkens the world until the deities lure her out. He later kills Princess-of-Great-Food, defeats the eight-forked serpent, discovers the Herb-Quelling Great Sword, and settles at Suga. The narrative then follows Master-of-the-Great-Land, also Great-Name-Possessor, through persecution, aid from the White Hare of Inaba, trials in the Nether-Distant-Land, marriages, descendants, and the making and consolidation of the land. Heaven eventually seeks control of the Central Land of Reed-Plains: failed envoys delay the mission, but Master-of-the-Great-Land yields it after his sons comply, in return for a great abode and respectful service. Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty descends from Heaven to rule, aided by Prince of Saruta and divine regalia. His line continues through Princess-Blossoming-Brilliantly-Like-the-Flowers-of-the-Trees, whose fiery childbirth produces Fire-Shine and Fire-Subside. Fire-Subside gains help from the Ocean-Possessor, subdues his elder brother with tide jewels, and fathers Heaven’s-Sun-Height-Prince-Wave-limit-Brave-Cormorant-Thatch-Meeting-Incompletely, ending the volume’s stated span.

Kojiki (Records of Ancient Matters), Volume I (The Age of the Gods) / Preface. body

The preface, spoken by Yasumaro, explains the origins of Heaven and Earth, the succession of rulers, and the history of the empire up to the reign at Woharida. It recounts how the Heavenly Sovereign who governed the Eight Great Islands ascended at the Great Palace of Kiyomihara at Asuka and ordered the correction and compilation of the imperial traditions. Hiyeda no Are memorized the genealogies and ancient words, and Yasumaro later recorded them under the Empress’s command. Yasumaro presents the resulting three volumes, with the First Volume covering the age from the Deity Master-of-the-August-Centre-of-Heaven to Prince-Wave-Limit-Brave-Cormorant-Thatch-Meeting-Incompletely.

Sect. body

The Beginning of Heaven and Earth concerns the birth of the first Deities. In the Plain of High Heaven, Master-of-the-August-Centre-of-Heaven, High-August-Producing-Wondrous Deity, and Divine-Producing-Wondrous-Deity were born alone and hid their persons. When the young Earth drifted about medusa-like, Pleasant-Reed-Shoot-Prince-Elder Deity and Heavenly-Eternally-Standing-Deity were born from something sprouting like a reed-shoot, and they likewise hid their persons. These five Deities are separate Heavenly Deities.

Sect. body

The Seven Divine Generations are described, beginning with the Earthly-Eternally-Standing-Deity and ending with the Female-Who-Invites. The Heavenly Deities command the Male-Who-Invites and Female-Who-Invites to make, consolidate, and give birth to the drifting land. Using a heavenly jewelled spear from the Floating Bridge of Heaven, they stir the brine until it becomes curdled, and the dripping brine forms the Island of Onogoro.

Sect. body

The Courtship of the Deities the Male-Who-Invites and the Female Who-Invites begins after they descend from Heaven onto the island and erect an heavenly august pillar and an hall of eight fathoms. Augustus Mas-Qui-Invitat and Augusta Femina-Qui-Invitat discuss their bodies and agree that his superfluous part should be inserted into her undeveloped part to procreate regions. They circle the heavenly august pillar and unite, but Augusta Femina-Qui-Invitat speaks first, which Augustus Mas-Qui-Invitat says is improper for a woman. They produce Hirudinem, whom they place in a reed boat and let float away, and then produce the Island of Aha, which is not counted among their children.

Sect. body

The two Deities learn that their first children were not good because the woman spoke first, so they consult the Heavenly Deities and repeat the ritual with the Male-Who-Invites speaking first. They then give birth to the eight islands known as the Land-of-the-Eight-Great-Islands, including the Island of Ahaji, Ho-no-sa-wake, the Island of Futa-no, the Islands of Mitsu-go, the Island of Iki, the Island of Tsu, the Island of Sado, and Great-Yamato-the-Luxuriant-Island-of-the-Dragon-Fly. Afterward, they give birth to six additional islands.

Sect. body

This unit concerns the birth of various deities after the birth of countries. Great-Male-of-the-Great-Thing is the first deity named in a sequence of ten deities, followed by deities associated with water, wind, trees, mountains, moors, valleys, food, and fire. Prince-of-Swift-Autumn and Princess-of-Swift-Autumn give birth from their separate dominions of river and sea to eight water-related deities.

Sect. body

Her Augustness the Princess-Who-Invites sickened after giving birth to the Deity-of-Fire because her august private parts were burnt, and she divinely retired. Deities were born from her vomit, faeces, and urine, including the Deity Metal-Mountain-Prince, the Deity Clay-Viscid-Prince, the Deity Mitsuhanome, and the Young-Wondrous-Producing-Deity. His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites mourned her and said that he had exchanged her for their single child. He buried her on Mount Hiba at the boundary of the Land of Idzumo and the Land of Hahaki, while his tears gave birth to the Crying-Weeping-Female-Deity.

Sect. body

His Augustness the Male-Who-invites cuts off the head of his child, the Deity Shining-Elder, with a ten-grasp sabre. Blood from the august sword gives birth to eight Deities, while the slain Deity Shining-Elder gives birth to eight Deities from different parts of his body. The sword is named Heavenly-Point-Blade-Extended and Majestic Point-Blade-Extended.

Sect. body

His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites follows Her Augustness the Female-Who-Invites to the Land of Hades and asks her to return because the lands made by the siblings are unfinished. He sees her rotting body and the eight Thunder-Deities dwelling in it, then flees while she sends pursuers after him. Three peaches help him repel the pursuers, and he blocks the Even Pass of Hades with a thousand-draught rock. Her Augustness the Female-Who-Invites and His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites exchange threats concerning death and birth, after which she is called the Great-Deity-of-Hades and the Even Pass of Hades is called the Ifuya-Pass.

Sect. body

The unit concerns the purification of the Great Deity the Male-Who-Invites after he reaches a hideous and polluted land. At a small river-mouth near Tachibana in Himuka, he removes his clothing and accessories, from which twelve Deities are born, then bathes in the middle reach of the water. His bathing produces Deities of evils, rectification, the ocean depths and surfaces, and finally the Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity, His Augustness, Moon-Night Possessor, and His Brave-Swift-impetuous-Male-Augustness. The three Ocean-Possessing Deities are ancestral Deities of the Chiefs of Adzumi, while three others are the three Great Deities of the Inlet of Sumi.

Sect. body

His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites rejoices after begetting three illustrious children and invests them with rule. He gives the Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity authority over the Plain-of-High-Heaven, Moon-Night-Possessor authority over the Dominion of the Night, and His-Brave-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness authority over the Sea-Plain. His-Brave-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness instead cries and weeps because he wishes to depart to the Nether Distant Land. His Augustness the Male-Who-Invites becomes angry and drives him out by a divine expulsion.

Sect. body

His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness ascends to Heaven after being expelled, intending to take leave of the Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity. The Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity prepares for a possible attack and questions his intentions. They establish the August Oath by swearing and producing children from a sabre and curved jewels. Several deities are born from the mist of their breath, with five attributed to each act of producing children.

Sect. body

The Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity declares that the five male Deities born last are her children, while the three female Deities born first are His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness's children. The three female Deities dwell in the inner, middle, and outer temples of Munakata and are revered by the Dukes of Munakata. His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness then claims victory, ravages the Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity's ricefields and palace, and releases a flayed heavenly piebald horse into her weaving-hall. The women weaving the heavenly garments are alarmed, injure their private parts against the shuttle's rays, and die.

Sect. body

The Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity closes and fastens the door of the Heavenly Rock-Dwelling, causing the Plain of High Heaven and the Central Land of Reed-Plains to become dark and eternal night to prevail. The myriad Deities assemble in the bed of the Tranquil River of Heaven and devise a plan involving offerings, a mirror, curved jewels, divination, singing birds, and the actions of several Deities. The Heavenly-Alarming Female performs a ritual before the door, causing the eight hundred myriad Deities to laugh and the Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity to open the door slightly. The Heavenly-Beckoning-Ancestor-Lord and Great-Jewel show her the mirror, and the Heavenly Hand-Strength-Male-Deity draws her out, restoring light to both lands.

Sect. body

The eight hundred myriad Deities impose a fine on High-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness, cut his beard, pull out his fingernails and toenails, and expel him. Afterward, he begs food from Princess-of-Great-Food, who offers dishes made from things taken from her nose, mouth, and fundament. Believing she has offered him filth, he kills Princess-of-Great-Food. From her body come silkworms, rice-seeds, millet, small beans, barley, and large beans, which Deity-Producing-Wondrous-Ancestor causes to be used as seeds.

Sect. body

His-Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness descends from Heaven to the head-waters of the River Hi and finds Foot-Stroking-Elder, Hand-Stroking Elder, and Wondrous-Inada-Princess mourning the annual devouring of their daughters by the eight-forked serpent of Koshi. He agrees to protect Wondrous-Inada-Princess and directs the preparation of a liquor trap using eight-fold refined liquor, eight gates, platforms, and vats. After the serpent becomes intoxicated, he cuts it into pieces with his ten-grasp sabre. A great sword is discovered inside the serpent's middle tail and is respectfully reported to the Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity as the Herb-Quelling Great Sword.

Sect. body

His Swift-Impetuous-Male-Augustness searched in Idzumo for a place to build a palace and arrived at Suga. He said his august heart was pure there and built the palace of Suga. When the palace was first built, clouds rose, and he made an august song about eight clouds and the eight-fold fence of Idzumo. He appointed Deity Foot-Stroking-Elder as Headman of the palace and bestowed on him the name Master-of-the-Temple-of-Inada-Eight-Eared-Deity-of-Suga.

Sect. body

The unit recounts the ancestry of the Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land. It traces a succession of marriages and births from the Deity Eight-Island Ruler through several generations. The Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land is identified by four other names, making five names in all.

Sect. body

The Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land’s eighty brethren travel to Inaba seeking to marry the Princess of Yakami, taking him along as an attendant. At Cape Keta, the eighty Deities deceive the White Hare of Inaba, but the Deity Great-Name-Possessor heals it after hearing its story. The hare foretells that the Princess of Yakami will marry the Deity Great-Name-Possessor. The eighty Deities then repeatedly attempt to kill him, but his august parent restores him and sends him to the Deity Great-House-Prince in Ki.

Sect. body

The Deity Great-Name-Possessor goes to the Nether-Distant-Land, where His Impetuous-Male-Augustness tests him with snakes, centipedes, wasps, and fire. With help from the Forward-Princess and a mouse, he survives, escapes after tying up the Great Deity, and takes the great life-sword, life-bow-and-arrows, and heavenly speaking-lute. At the Even Pass of Hades, the Great Deity commands him to pursue his half-brethren and become Master-of-the-Great-Land and Spirit-of-the-Living-Land. The Deity Great-Name-Possessor scatters the eighty Deities and begins to make the land, while the Forward-Princess bears a child called the Tree-Fork-Deity and the Deity-of-August-Wells.

Sect. body

The Deity-of-Eight-Thousand-Spears travels to the Land of Koshi to woo the Princess of Nuna-kaha, having heard that she is wise and beautiful. He sings outside her house, lamenting that the birds and cock interrupt his wooing. The Princess of Nuna-kaha answers from inside with songs expressing maidenly reserve and promising to come forth at night. They do not cohabit that night, but they cohabit augustly on the following night.

Sect. body

The unit concerns the Deity-of-Eight-Thousand-Spears and Her Augustness the Forward-Princess. Because the Deity's Chief Empress is very jealous, the Deity prepares to depart from Idzumo for the Land of Yamato. Her Augustness the Forward-Princess offers him an august liquor-cup and sings of her exclusive bond with him. They pledge each other by the cup with their hands on each other's necks, and this is called the Cup Pledge and is said to continue to the present time.

Sect. body

The unit concerns the descendants of the Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land. He fathers several children through marriages with various deities and princesses, beginning with Deity Aji-shiki-taka-hiko-ne and continuing through Deity Toho-tsu-yama-zaki-tarashi. The text identifies Deity Aji-shiki-taka-hiko-ne as the Great August Deity of Kamo. From the Deity Eight-Island-Ruler through Deity Toho-tsu-yama-zaki-tarashi, the descendants are called the Deities of seventeen generations.

Sect. body

The Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land dwelt at the august cape of Miho in Idzumo when the Little-Prince-the-Renowned-Deity arrived in a boat of heavenly Kagami dressed in skins of geese. The Crumbling Prince identified him as the child of the Deity-Producing-Wondrous-Deity, who declared that the two should become brethren and make and consolidate the land. The Little-Prince-the-Renowned-Deity and the Great-Name-Possessor then made and consolidated the land conjointly until the Little-Prince-the-Renowned-Deity crossed over to the Eternal Land.

Sect. body

Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land laments that he cannot make the land alone and asks with what Deity he can make it. Deity illuminating the sea says the land can be made together if that Deity is laid to rest well. Deity illuminating the sea requests reverent worship on Yamato's green fence, the eastern mountain's top, and is identified as the Deity who dwells on the top of Mount Mimoro.

Sect. body

The Great-Harvest-Deity weds three princesses and fathers sixteen deities, including the Swift-Mountain-Dwelling Deity. The text lists the names and alternate names of these children and identifies several of their dwelling places and functions. The Swift-Mountain-Dwelling Deity weds the Deity Princess-of-Great-Food and fathers eight deities.

Sect. body

The Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity commands that the Luxuriant-Reed-Plains-the-Land-of-Fresh-Rice-ears-of-a-Thousand-Autumns,, Of Long-Five-Hundred-Autumns be governed by Truly-Conqueror-I-Conquer-Conquering-Swift-Heavenly-Great-Great-Ears. After observing that the land is painfully uproarious, Truly-Conqueror-I-Conquer-Conquering-Swift-Heavenly-Great-Great-Ears reports back to the Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity. The High-August-Producing-Wondrous-Deity and the Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity convene the eight hundred myriad Deities and ask Deity Thought-Includer to devise a plan for subduing the violent and savage Earthly Deities. They choose Deity Ame-no-ho-hi, but he curries favour with Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land and sends no report for three years.

Sect. body

The High-August-Producing-Wondrous-Deity and the Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity send the Heavenly-Young-Prince to the Central Land of Reed-Plains after Deity Ame-no-ho-hi fails to return a report. The Heavenly-Young-Prince marries Princess Under-Shining, delays reporting for eight years, and is killed by an arrow after shooting the pheasant the Name-Crying-Female. His family builds a mourning-house, and the Deity Ajishiki-taka-hiko-ne destroys it after being mistaken for the dead prince. His younger sister sings a Song to reveal his august name.

Sect. body

Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity and the other Deities sent Brave-Awful-Possessing-Male-Deity and Deity Heavenly-Bird-Boat to ask Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land to yield the Central Land of Reed-Plains to the august child of the Heavenly Deities. Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land's sons, Deity Eight-Fold-Thing-Sign-Master and Deity Brave-August-Name-Firm, agreed to follow the command after Deity Brave-August-Name-Firm lost a trial of strength. Deity Master-of-the-Great-Land then agreed to yield up the Central Land of Reed-Plains if a great abode were established for him and his children were respectfully served. Brave-Awful-Possessing-Male-Deity re-ascended to Heaven and reported that he had subdued and pacified the Central Land of Reed-Plains.

Sect. body

Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity and the High-Integrity-Deity charge His Augustness Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty to descend from Heaven and rule the Central Land of Reed-Plains after its pacification. At the eight-forking road of Heaven, the Deity Prince of Saruta identifies himself as an Earthly Deity and offers to serve as the august child's vanguard. The deities send down the mirror, sword, and other divine attendants with His Augustness Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty and instruct him on their reverence and governance.

Sect. body

Heaven-Shining-Great-August-Deity and High-Integrating-Deity commanded His Augustness Heaven's-Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty to descend from Heaven. He descended onto the peak of Kuzhifuru, which is Takachiho in Tsu-kushi, while His Heavenly Great Wondrous Augustness and His Augustness Heaven's-Round-Eyes attended him in his august van. His Heavenly Great Wondrous Augustness declared the place exceedingly good and then established a dwelling there with stout temple-pillars and high cross-beams.

Sect. body

The unit concerns Her Augustness the Heavenly-Alarming-Female and the Great Deity Prince of Saruta. She is charged with respectfully escorting the Great Deity Prince of Saruta back, because she had made him known. She is also charged with bearing his august name and serving the speaker. As a result, the Duchesses of Saru bear the name of the Male Deity the Prince of Saruta, and the women are called Duchesses of Saru.

Sect. body

The unit concerns the Deity Prince of Saruta while he dwells at Azaka. He goes fishing, is caught by a hirabu shell-fish, and drowns in the sea, receiving three names associated with sinking, gurgling water, and bubbles. The Heavenly-Alarming-Female escorts him back and asks the fishes to serve the august son of the Heavenly Deities. All the fishes agree except the bèche-de-mer, whose mouth she slits; this explains its slit mouth and the offerings granted to the Duchesses of Saru.

Sect. body

His Augustness Heaven's-Sun-Height-Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty met Divine-Princess-of-Ata, daughter of Deity-Great-Mountain-Possessor, and sought to marry her. Deity-Great-Mountain-Possessor sent both Divine-Princess-of-Ata and Princess-Long-as-the-Rocks, but His Augustness Heaven's-Sun-Height-Prince-Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty rejected Princess-Long-as-the-Rocks because she was very hideous and kept only Princess-Blossoming-Brilliantly-Like-the-Flowers-of-the-Trees. Deity-Great-Mountain-Possessor declared that rejecting Princess-Long-as-the-Rocks caused the august offspring of the Heavenly Deity to be as frail as flowers rather than immovable like rocks. The curse explains why the august lives of Their Augustnesses the Heavenly Sovereigns are not long.

Sect. body

Princess-Blossoming-Brilliantly-Like-the-Flowers-of-the-Trees tells His Augustness Prince Rice-ear-Ruddy-Plenty that she is pregnant and near delivery. He doubts that the unborn child is his, so she tests whether the child is of a Heavenly Deity by building a sealed hall and setting it on fire during delivery. Three Deities are born in the fire: His Augustness Fire-Shine, His Augustness Fire-Climax, and His Augustness Fire-Subside, also called His Augustness Heaven's-Sun-Height-Prince-Great-Rice-ears-Lord-Ears.

Sect. body

His Augustness Fire-Shine and His Augustness Fire-Subside exchange their sea-luck and mountain-luck, but Fire-Subside loses Fire-Shine’s fish-hook and cannot replace it. Fire-Subside travels to the palace of the Ocean-Possessor, marries Luxuriant-Jewel-Princess, and obtains the original fish-hook after the Sea-Deity finds it in the throat of the tahi. The Ocean-Possessor gives Fire-Subside instructions and two jewels for controlling the tides, and a one-fathom crocodile escorts him home.

Sect. body

His Augustness Fire-Subside returns the fish-hook to his elder brother according to the Sea-Deity's instructions. The elder brother grows poorer and attacks His Augustness Fire-Subside. His Augustness Fire-Subside uses the tide-flowing jewel to drown him and the tide-ebbing jewel to save him. The elder brother then promises to guard and serve His Augustness Fire-Subside, and his drowning posturings are said to continue to the present day.

Sect. body

Her Augustness Luxuriant-Jewel-Princess, daughter of the Sea-Deity, came to His Augustness Fire-Subside while pregnant and built a parturition-hall on the seashore. During parturition, she took the native shape of a crocodile, but His Augustness Fire-Subside secretly looked at her and fled. She left their august child, closed the sea-boundary, and returned to the Sea-Plain, though she later sent a song expressing love for His Augustness Fire-Subside. Their child, His Augustness Heaven's-Sun-Height-Prince-Wave-limit-Brave-Cormorant-Thatch-Meeting-Incompletely, later married Her Augustness Jewel-Good-Princess and had four august children.
