Abstract written by a model

The unit affirms that Allah is One, Lord of the heavens, earth, and all between them, and describes the Day of Judgment, the Day of Separation, resurrection, questioning, punishment, and reward. It contrasts idolaters and those who reject monotheism with believers and single-minded slaves of Allah, who receive provision and honor in the Gardens of delight. It recounts how Allah saved Noah, Abraham, Abraham's son, Moses, Aaron, Elias, Lot, and Jonah, and how Abraham's sacrifice was replaced with a tremendous victim. The unit also condemns the tree of Zaqqum and hell, and concludes with Allah's promise that His sent servants and host will be helped and victorious.
