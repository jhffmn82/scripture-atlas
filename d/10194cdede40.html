Abstract written by a model

Surah 45: Crouching (Al-Jaathiya) concerns Allah's revelations, the portents in creation, and the responses of believers and disbelievers. It describes Allah's guidance, the clear road of Allah's commandment, and the signs in the heavens, the earth, and the sea. It warns disbelievers of hell and the Fire, and describes the Day of Resurrection when the nations are summoned and judged according to their deeds. It also teaches that the life of the world is temporary and that believers will receive mercy.
