Abstract written by a model

Surah 32: The Prostration (As-Sajda), revealed at Mecca, concerns the truth of the Scripture and Allah’s creation, guidance, and judgment. It warns the people addressed by Muhammad about resurrection, punishment, and the consequences of disbelief and wrongdoing. It describes believers who prostrate, pray, spend from what they have been given, and receive the Gardens of Retreat, while evildoers face the Fire. The unit also recounts Moses, the Scripture given to him, the Children of Israel, and Allah’s judgment on the Day of Resurrection.
