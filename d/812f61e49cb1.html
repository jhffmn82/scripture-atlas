Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns Confucius and his disciples, especially Yen Yuan, Min Tsze-ch'ien, Zan Yu, Chi Lu, Tsze-kung, Kung-hsi Hwa, and Tsang Hsi. It discusses learning, virtue, death, service to spirits, government, righteous conduct, propriety, and the appropriate mean. Confucius mourns Yen Yuan, criticizes Zan Yu's service to the Chi family, and explains that counsel should differ according to each disciple's character. In the final conversation, Tsze-lu, Zan Yu, Kung-hsi Hwa, and Tsang Hsi describe what they would do if entrusted with authority, and Confucius approves Tsang Hsi's wish to enjoy the spring season with others.
