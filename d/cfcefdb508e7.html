Abstract written by a model

This unit presents Confucius's teachings and conduct concerning learning, virtue, propriety, self-discipline, and the superior man. Confucius describes himself as a transmitter who loves antiquity, pursues knowledge, teaches others, and seeks to become virtuous without weariness. He emphasizes selecting and following good qualities, avoiding bad ones, acting with careful planning, and remaining content without unrighteous riches. The unit also records his views on the superior man, whose conduct should match his words and who is composed rather than distressed.
