Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns Allah's revelation, creation, judgment, mercy, and punishment, as well as the responses of humanity, disbelievers, Iblis, and earlier peoples. Allah describes the preservation of the Reminder, the creation of humanity and the jinn, the angels' prostration before the created mortal, and Iblis's expulsion and promise to mislead. The messengers visit Abraham with the annunciation of a wise boy and are sent to destroy the sinful people of Lot while rescuing Lot's family. The unit recounts the destruction of the dwellers in the wood and Al-Hijr, and instructs Muhammad to proclaim the revelation, worship Allah, and endure until the Inevitable comes.
