Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns Joseph, his family, and the events through which Allah raises Joseph from the pit and prison to authority in Egypt. Joseph's brethren conspire against him, but he is taken to Egypt, falsely accused, imprisoned, and later released after interpreting the king's dream. Joseph's family eventually comes before him, recognizes him, receives forgiveness, and settles in Egypt. The unit also teaches about Allah's sovereignty, patience, repentance, faith, and the Hereafter.
