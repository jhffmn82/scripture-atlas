Abstract written by a model

The unit concerns teachings and exchanges involving Tsze-chang, Tsze-hsia, the philosopher Tsang, Tsze-kung, and Chung-ni. It describes the conduct of the scholar trained for public duty, the superior man, and the ways virtue, learning, righteousness, and mourning should be pursued. Tsze-hsia discusses teaching, confidence, study, and the limits of grief, while the philosopher Tsang comments on Tsze-chang, filial piety, and criminal judgment. Tsze-kung defends Chung-ni’s attainments, explains how the doctrines of Wan and Wu are preserved, and describes the effects of a sage’s rule.
