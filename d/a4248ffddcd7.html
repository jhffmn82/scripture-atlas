Additional Sloks of Guru Nanak

Guru Nanak · 2 sections
Additional Sloks of Guru Nanak, translated by Max Arthur Macauliffe, The Sikh Religion, vol. 1 (1909), public domain, via sacred-texts.com. Shown here as plain text, with facts drawn from it. https://sacred-texts.com/skh/tsr1/

Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns Nanak’s teachings on spiritual discernment, devotion, love, faith, and the conduct of religious and social groups. It urges people to reject evil, seek the True Guru, surrender themselves to divine love, and avoid attachment to transitory things and mammon. It defines the qualities of a true Brahman and Khatri, contrasts saints with deceitful and quarrelsome people, and recounts Ram Chandar’s mourning for Sita and Lachhman. It teaches that God is independent and that whatever the Perfect One does is complete and without deficiency.
