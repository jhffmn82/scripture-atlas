Abstract written by a model

This unit records Confucius's teachings on shame, virtue, learning, government, loyalty, justice, and the conduct of the superior man. It discusses historical figures including Kwan Chung, the duke Hwan, Tsze-lu, and others in relation to virtue, political service, and propriety. Confucius says that perfect virtue is difficult, that injury should be repaid with justice, and that kindness should be repaid with kindness. He also describes the superior man as self-cultivating, modest in speech, diligent in action, and concerned with giving rest to others.
