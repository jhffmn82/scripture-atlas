Abstract written by a model

In 3 John, the elder (John) writes to Gaius, whom he loves, praising him for walking in the truth and for his faithful work toward brothers and strangers. He urges Gaius to send traveling brothers on their way in a manner worthy of God, since they went out for the sake of the Name and took nothing from the Gentiles. He criticizes Diotrephes, who loves to be first, rejects the elder's words, accuses him with wicked words, refuses to receive the brothers, and expels those who would. He commends Demetrius as having a good testimony from all and from the truth itself, and says he hopes to visit Gaius soon to speak face to face.
