Abstract written by a model

The unit describes the Overwhelming and what happens to people on that day. Many faces are downcast, toiling, weary, scorched by fire, drinking boiling water, and given only bitter thorn-fruit. Other faces are calm and glad for their past effort, dwelling in a high Garden with springs, couches, goblets, cushions, and silken carpets. The addressed figure is told to remind them, while Allah will punish those who are averse and disbelieve, and all will return to Him for reckoning.
