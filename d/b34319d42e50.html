Abstract written by a model

Paul, a prisoner of Christ Jesus, writes with Timothy to Philemon, to Apphia, to Archippus, and to the assembly in Philemon's house, greeting them and thanking God for Philemon's love and faith. Paul says he could command Philemon but instead appeals out of love on behalf of Onesimus, whom he became father to in his chains. He is sending Onesimus back, asking Philemon to receive him no longer as a slave but as a beloved brother, and offers to repay anything Onesimus owes. Paul expresses confidence in Philemon's obedience, asks him to prepare a guest room in hope of being restored to him through prayer, and relays greetings from Epaphras, Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, and Luke before closing with a blessing.
