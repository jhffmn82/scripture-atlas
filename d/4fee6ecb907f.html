Abstract written by a model

Obadiah's vision records the LORD's judgment against Edom, also called Esau, whose pride in its high rocky dwelling leads it to think no one can bring it down. The LORD says he will make Edom small, bring it down from its heights, and destroy its wise men and mighty men, including those of Teman. Edom is condemned for violence against its brother Jacob: it stood aside while strangers plundered Jerusalem, and it rejoiced, seized wealth, and cut off escapees. The day of the LORD is near for all the nations, and what they have done will return on their own heads. Mount Zion will have those who escape, the house of Jacob will be a fire consuming the house of Esau, and the possessions of Esau and other lands will be taken, ending with the kingdom belonging to the LORD.
