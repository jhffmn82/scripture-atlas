Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns Allah’s revelations, the Qur'an, and the guidance given to believers, while disbelievers reject the Hereafter and face punishment. It recounts Moses receiving signs, and Solomon ruling over people, jinn, birds, and ants before receiving the Queen of Sheba and her people’s submission to Allah. It recalls the destruction of wrongdoers among Pharaoh’s people, Thamud, and Lot’s folk, and describes Allah’s signs in the heavens and the earth. It warns of resurrection, Judgment Day, the Trumpet, the beast of the earth, and the consequences of good and evil deeds.
