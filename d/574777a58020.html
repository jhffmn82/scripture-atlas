Abstract written by a model

Surah 50: The letter Qaaf (Qaaf) concerns the Glorious Qur'an, the warning brought by a warner, and the disbelievers' denial and doubt. It describes Allah's creation, knowledge of man, recording of deeds, death, resurrection, judgment, hell, and the Garden. It recalls destroyed peoples and calls Muhammad to bear their words, praise his Lord, and warn by the Qur'an those who fear the threat.
