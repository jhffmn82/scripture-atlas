Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns the Qur'an's warning to disbelievers and recounts the fate of earlier peoples who denied messengers. It describes Allah's gifts, trials, forgiveness, and judgments involving David, Solomon, Job, and other chosen servants. It contrasts believers and the righteous with transgressors, describing Gardens of Eden for the former and the Fire and Hell for the latter. It recounts the angels' prostration before the created mortal, Iblis's refusal and punishment, and the warning that all peoples will come to know the truth.
