Abstract written by a model

The unit concerns Allah's creation, mercy, sovereignty, and judgment over mankind. It warns mankind against the devil, worldly deception, disbelief, and denial of messengers, while promising forgiveness, reward, and Gardens of Eden to believers who do good works. It describes signs in the heavens, the earth, seas, winds, rain, plants, animals, and human life as evidence of Allah's power. It teaches that the Resurrection, judgment, and accountability are certain, and that Allah delays punishment until an appointed term.
