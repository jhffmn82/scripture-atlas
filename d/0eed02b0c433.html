Abstract written by a model

Surah 109: The Disbelievers (Al-Kaafiroon) was revealed at Mecca. It concerns the speaker and the disbelievers. The speaker declares that the speaker will not worship what the disbelievers worship, and that the disbelievers do not worship what the speaker worships. The unit ends by assigning the speaker's religion to the speaker and the disbelievers' religion to the disbelievers.
