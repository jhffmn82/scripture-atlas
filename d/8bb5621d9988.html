Abstract written by a model

In 2 John 1, the elder writes to the chosen lady and her children, whom he loves in truth along with all who know the truth. He wishes them grace, mercy, and peace from God the Father and Jesus Christ, and rejoices that some of her children walk in truth. He urges them to love one another and to walk according to the commandment they have had from the beginning. He warns that many deceivers, who deny that Jesus Christ came in the flesh, have gone out into the world, and that those who do not remain in the teaching of Christ do not have God. He tells her not to welcome anyone who does not bring this teaching, and says he hopes to visit face to face, closing with greetings from the children of her chosen sister.
