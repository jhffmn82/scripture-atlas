Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns the hypocrites and their claims before Muhammad, whom Allah identifies as speaking falsely despite acknowledging him as His messenger. The hypocrites use their faith as a pretext to turn people from Allah's way, resist calls for forgiveness, and seek to drive away those with Allah's messenger. They say the mightier will drive out the weaker from Medina, while Allah, His messenger, and the believers possess might. The unit also warns the believers not to let wealth or children distract them from remembrance of Allah and urges them to spend before death arrives.
