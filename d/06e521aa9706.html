Abstract written by a model

The unit describes the qualities and reward of the believers, including humility in prayer, avoidance of vain talk, charity, modesty, keeping pledges, and attentiveness to prayer. It recounts Allah's creation of man, provision of water, gardens, cattle, and ships, and the missions and rejection of Noah, Moses, Aaron, and other messengers. It contrasts the believers with disbelievers who deny resurrection, reject the revelations, divide the religion into sects, and face punishment, hell, and the fire. It affirms Allah's oneness, the coming Day of Resurrection, the weighing of deeds, and the triumph of steadfast slaves.
