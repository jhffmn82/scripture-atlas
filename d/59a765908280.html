Abstract written by a model

Surah 57: The Iron concerns Allah’s sovereignty, knowledge, power, and mercy over creation. It calls people to believe in Allah and Allah's messenger, spend from what they have been entrusted with, and seek forgiveness and the Gardens of Paradise. It contrasts the believers, whose light and rewards are promised, with the hypocritical men and the hypocritical women and the disbelievers, who face separation, loss, and the Fire. It also discusses the sending of messengers, the revelation of scripture and the Balance, iron, the temporary life of the world, and Allah's infinite bounty.
