Abstract written by a model

This unit presents Confucius's judgments on propriety, ritual, music, governance, and moral conduct. He criticizes abuses of rank and ceremony, stresses humanity, reverence, sorrow, and proper observance, and discusses how princes and ministers should relate. The unit also records teachings about music, archery, sacrifices, and the traditions of past dynasties. Confucius is portrayed as valuing faithful ritual practice while condemning empty or excessive observance.
