Abstract written by a model

Surah 31, Luqman, concerns guidance, faith, worship, gratitude, and the consequences of belief or disbelief. Allah describes the wise Scripture, the creation of the heavens and the earth, and signs showing His power and wisdom. Luqman is given wisdom and instructs his son to avoid associating partners with Allah, honor parents, establish worship, enjoin kindness, forbid iniquity, persevere, and remain humble. The unit warns mankind about the Hereafter, the life of the world, the Day of judgment, and matters known only to Allah.
