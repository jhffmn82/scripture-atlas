Abstract written by a model

Jarom writes a brief record on small plates according to the commandment of his father, Enos, for the benefit of the Lamanites. The Nephites grow strong, keep the law of Moses and the commandments, defend themselves against the Lamanites, and become prosperous in the land. Prophets, priests, and teachers warn the people, teach the law of Moses, and persuade them to look forward to the Messiah, helping prevent their destruction. Jarom records years of wars, contentions, and dissensions before delivering the plates to his son Omni.
