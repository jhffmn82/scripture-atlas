Abstract written by a model

Surah 6, The Cattle, presents Allah as Creator, Sustainer, sovereign judge, and sole object of worship, whose signs appear in creation and revelation. Muhammad is commanded to warn, follow revelation, reject idolatry, teach worship, justice, lawful food, and the straight path. Disbelievers reject revelations, while messengers, including Abraham, who argues against worshiping created things, are denied yet supported by Allah. The unit culminates in judgment: the Day of Resurrection, the Hereafter, the Fire, and consequences for disbelief, wrongdoing, division, associating partners with Allah, and neglected obligations.

Surah 6: The Cattle (Al-An'aam) body

This unit concerns Allah’s creation, sovereignty, knowledge, mercy, judgment, and punishment, and the rejection of His revelations by the disbelievers. Muhammad is instructed to warn people, reject idolatry, follow revelation, and call them to Allah’s guidance and worship. The messengers, including Abraham, are presented as guides who were denied but supported by Allah, while Abraham argues against worshiping created things. The unit describes the Day of Resurrection, the Fire, the Hereafter, and the fate of those who deny the truth, associate partners with Allah, or neglect their obligations.

It describes Allah as the Creator, Sustainer, and sole object of worship, and presents His revelations as guidance through signs in nature and scripture.
