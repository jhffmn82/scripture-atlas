Abstract written by a model

The unit concerns the sun, moon, day, night, heaven, earth, and soul as signs of creation. It teaches that the soul is successful when caused to grow and fails when stunted. It recounts how (The tribe of) Thamud denied the truth, and how the basest of them hamstrung the she-camel of Allah after the messenger of Allah told them to let her drink. Allah doomed them for their sin and razed their dwellings.
