Abstract written by a model

Surah 76: Man (Al-Insaan) concerns man, his creation, guidance, choices, and ultimate consequences. Allah creates man from a drop of thickened fluid, shows him the way, and prepares differing outcomes for the grateful, the righteous, and the disbelievers. The righteous receive a Garden, fine clothing, pure drink, and acceptance of their endeavour, while disbelievers and evil-doers face punishment. The unit also concerns the Qur'an as a revelation and instructs submission, remembrance, and worship of Allah.
