Surah 83: Defrauding (Al-Mutaffifin) body

The unit condemns the defrauders, who demand full measure for themselves but cause others loss. It warns that mankind will be raised on the Awful Day to stand before Allah, the Lord of the Worlds, and that the repudiators of the Day of Judgment will face punishment in hell. It contrasts the guilty, who mock those who believed, with the righteous, who enjoy delight and drink from Tasnim. On that day, those who believed will have the laugh of disbelievers.
