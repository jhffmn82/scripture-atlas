Abstract written by a model

Jude, a servant of Jesus Christ and brother of James, writes to those who are called, urging them to contend earnestly for the faith once delivered to the saints. He warns that certain ungodly men have crept in secretly, turning God's grace into indecency and denying Jesus Christ. Jude cites examples of judgment: the people saved out of Egypt who didn't believe, angels who deserted their dwelling, Sodom and Gomorrah, and Cain, Balaam, and Korah, and he quotes Enoch's prophecy of the Lord's coming to judge the ungodly. He calls the intruders murmurers, mockers, and causers of divisions, as the apostles foretold. He then exhorts the beloved to build themselves up in faith, pray in the Holy Spirit, keep themselves in God's love, show varied compassion to others, and closes with praise to God who is able to keep them from stumbling.
