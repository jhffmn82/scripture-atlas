Abstract written by a model

Surah 77: The Emissaries (Al-Mursalaat), revealed at Mecca, concerns the promised event and the Day of Decision. It describes emissary winds and other forces that revive vegetation, winnow, bring down the Reminder, and excuse or warn. The unit warns the repudiators and guilty of punishment, including the flame and doom they denied. It describes the reward of those who kept their duty in shade, fountains, and desired fruits.
