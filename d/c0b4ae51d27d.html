Abstract written by a model

Omni, Amaron, Chemish, Abinadom, and Amaleki successively keep the plates and record warfare, judgments, transfers of the records, and teachings. Mosiah leads those who heed the Lord from the land of Nephi through the wilderness to the land of Zarahemla, where they discover and unite with the people of Zarahemla. The Lord preserves the righteous, gives Mosiah power to interpret engravings, and allows the Nephites to gain advantage over the Lamanites. Amaleki gives the plates to Benjamin and describes an unsuccessful attempt to return to the land of Nephi.
