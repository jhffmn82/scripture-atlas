Abstract written by a model

This unit concerns Noah's warning to his people and his call to serve Allah, keep their duty, and obey him. Noah says that his people resist his warnings, reject repentance, and follow leaders whose wealth and children bring ruin. He recounts Allah's signs in creation and promises of forgiveness, rain, wealth, sons, gardens, and rivers. Noah says that the people were drowned because of their sins and then entered a Fire, and he asks Allah to leave no disbelievers in the land and to forgive him, his parents, and believers.
