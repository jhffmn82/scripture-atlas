Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa)

Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), from The Meaning of the Glorious Koran, translated by Mohammed Marmaduke Pickthall (1930), public domain in the United States, text via Tanzil.net. Shown here as plain text, with facts drawn from it. https://tanzil.net/

Abstract written by a model

This document summarizes Surah 4, The Women (An-Nisaa), revealed at Medina. It addresses mankind and believers with commands from Allah concerning faith, justice, family life, wealth, worship, conflict, and the Last Day. It begins with duty to Allah, protection of orphans and their property, and detailed inheritance shares for children, parents, spouses, siblings, and distant kindred. It regulates marriage, dowries, prohibited relations, treatment of wives, female orphans, divorce or reconciliation, and arbitration between spouses. It condemns wrongful wealth-taking, killing, lewdness, treachery, false accusation, pride, hoarding, and injustice, while emphasizing repentance and Allah’s forgiveness under stated conditions. Believers are commanded to serve Allah alone, show kindness to relatives, neighbors, travellers, and dependents, return deposits, judge fairly, and stand firmly for justice even against themselves or kin. The text gives directions for prayer, purification, travel, and worship amid danger. It addresses obedience to Allah, the messenger, and authority, the handling of disputes, migration, military precautions, fighting for oppressed people, and penalties for mistaken or intentional killing. Hypocrites and disbelievers are portrayed as deceptive, wavering, obstructive, or hostile; believers are warned against choosing disbelievers over believers. The People of the Scripture are reproached for demands, covenant-breaking, distortion, and disbelief, while Moses, Jesus son of Mary, Abraham, Muhammad, and other messengers are mentioned. The surah repeatedly contrasts Fire, painful doom, and hell for disbelief, transgression, hypocrisy, and ascribing partners to Allah with eternal Gardens, reward, mercy, pure companions, and shade for those who believe, obey, repent, and do good works. It closes by affirming Allah’s oneness, knowledge, authority over heaven and earth, the messenger’s truth, and final inheritance guidance.

Surah 4: The Women (An-Nisaa), revealed at Medina, addresses mankind concerning duty toward Allah and the treatment of orphans, women, and family wealth. It commands that orphans receive their wealth and that inheritance be divided into legal shares for men and women. It discusses marriage, marriage portions, fairness, and justice. It warns that wrongfully consuming orphans' wealth results in fire and burning flame.

Allah's provisions for inheritance concern children, spouses, parents, mothers, sons, daughters, and siblings. Male children receive the equivalent of the portions of two female children, while other shares depend on the presence of children, parents, and heirs. These rules are described as an injunction and commandment from Allah, who is Knower, Wise, and Indulgent. Those who obey Allah and His messenger enter Gardens, while those who disobey and transgress Allah's limits enter Fire.

The unit concerns women and others guilty of lewdness or evil deeds, and sets out consequences, repentance, and forgiveness. Women accused of lewdness are to be confined to their houses if four witnesses testify against them, until death takes them or Allah appoints a way. The two guilty of the offense are to be punished, but they are to be left alone if they repent and improve. Forgiveness is for those who do evil in ignorance and repent quickly, but not for those who repent only when death attends them or who die as disbelievers.

19 O ye who believe! But consort with them in kindness, for if ye hate them it may happen that ye hate a thing wherein Allah hath placed much good. .. 28 Allah would make the burden light for you, for man was created weak. body

The unit addresses believers about marriage, inheritance, prohibited relations, dowries, and proper conduct toward women. It forbids forcibly inheriting the women of deceased kinsmen, mistreating wives to reclaim gifts, marrying certain relatives, and marrying married women except captives whom the right hands possess. It permits honest wedlock with lawful women and believing maids under specified conditions, while prescribing reduced punishment for believing maids who commit lewdness after marriage. Allah explains guidance, promises mercy, warns against vain desires, and makes the burden light because man was created weak.

The unit addresses believers regarding wealth, trade, unlawful killing, prohibited great sins, envy, inheritance, and family obligations. It states that Allah forbids squandering wealth in vanity, permits trade by mutual consent, and warns against aggression and injustice. It describes men as responsible for women and gives instructions concerning women from whom rebellion is feared. It also addresses marital breach by appointing arbiters from both families, with amendment depending on their desire for reconciliation.

The unit commands people to serve Allah alone and show kindness to parents, relatives, orphans, the needy, neighbors, fellow-travellers, wayfarers, and those whom their right hands possess. It condemns pride, boastfulness, hoarding wealth, encouraging avarice, concealing Allah’s bounty, and spending wealth to be seen by people. Allah does not wrong even by the weight of an ant, doubles good deeds, and gives immense reward. On the Day when every people has a witness, disbelievers and those who disobeyed the messenger will wish to be level with the ground and will be unable to hide anything from Allah.

This unit concerns Allah's commands, judgments, and promises regarding believers, those given Scripture, Jews, mankind, and those who disbelieve. Believers are instructed about prayer, purification, and seeking clean soil when water is unavailable. The text describes how some Jews distort Scripture and seek to turn others from the right way, while Allah condemns disbelief and ascribing partners to Him. Allah promises punishment in the Fire for disbelievers and eternal Gardens, pure companions, and shade for those who believe and do good works.

The text condemns those who seek judgment from false deities and describes the hypocrites turning away from Allah's revelation and the messenger. Those who obey Allah and the messenger are promised company with those shown favour by Allah and an immense reward.

Disbelievers and hypocritical people are described as fearful, obstructive, or spreading unverified tidings. The unit affirms the truth of the Qur'an, the certainty of the Day of Resurrection, and Allah's complete knowledge, power, and judgment.

The unit concerns hypocrites, believers, and others in relation to disbelief, migration, war, peace, and killing. It states that Allah condemns intentional killing of a believer and prescribes penalties for mistaken killing, including freeing a believing slave, blood-money, or fasting. It describes different ranks and rewards for believers who strive in Allah’s way and those who remain behind, with exceptions for those disabled. It also describes migration, the treatment of the oppressed and helpless, and Allah’s forgiveness and mercy.

After worship, they are told to remember Allah and observe worship at fixed times, while pursuing the enemy without relenting. Muhammad is told that Allah reveals the Scripture with the truth so that he may judge between mankind, avoid defending the treacherous, and seek forgiveness.

Allah forgives those who seek His pardon, while assigning partners to Him is not pardoned except for whom He wills. The unit also describes Allah's grace toward Muhammad, the revelation of the Scripture and wisdom, and the consequences of opposing the messenger and following another way.

It teaches that Allah owns all things, is able to replace people, and gives the reward of the world and the Hereafter.
