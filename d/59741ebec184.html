Surah 53: The Star (An-Najm) body

This unit concerns Muhammad, the revelation given to him, and Allah’s signs, power, and judgment. It describes the mighty teacher showing Muhammad the revelation and states that Muhammad’s heart did not lie about what he saw. It rejects the idols and the claims of those who disbelieve in the Hereafter, while affirming that Allah owns the afterlife and the former life. It teaches that each person receives according to human effort and calls people to prostrate themselves before Allah and serve Him.
