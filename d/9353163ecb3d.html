Abstract written by a model

Surah 29: The Spider (Al-Ankaboot), revealed at Mecca, concerns belief, testing, worship, and accountability before Allah. It recounts how Allah tested earlier peoples, including Noah, Abraham, Lot, Midian, A'ad, Thamud, Korah, Pharaoh, and Haman, and describes the consequences of their deeds. It teaches that believers who persevere, trust Allah, and do good works will receive mercy and dwell in the Garden, while disbelievers and wrong-doers face Allah's doom. It urges mankind to worship Allah alone, remember Him, seek provision from Him, and follow His guidance.
