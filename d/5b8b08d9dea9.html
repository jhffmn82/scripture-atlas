Abstract written by a model

Surah 28: The Stories (Al-Qasas) recounts Moses’ life, from his infancy and escape from Pharaoh to his call as a messenger and confrontation with Pharaoh. It describes Pharaoh’s oppression, the destruction of Pharaoh and his hosts through drowning in the sea, and Allah’s guidance and warnings to Muhammad and the people. The unit also tells of Korah’s wealth, arrogance, and swallowing by the earth, and contrasts worldly comfort with the Hereafter. It affirms Allah’s sole authority, the truth of the Qur'an, accountability on the Day of Resurrection, and the consequences of corruption.
