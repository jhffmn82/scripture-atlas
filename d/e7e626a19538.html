Surah 7, The Heights, revealed at Mecca, calls Muhammad and believers to follow Allah’s revelations and warns of judgment for disbelievers. It tells of creation, Adam and his wife, Iblis’s defiance, Satan’s temptation, and the contrasting fates of the Garden, Fire, and Heights. It recounts Noah, Hud, Salih, Lot, and Shu'eyb, whose peoples face rescue or destruction. Moses then confronts Pharaoh with Allah’s tokens, overcomes the wizards, frees the Children of Israel, and receives the tablets; Pharaoh’s people are punished and drowned, while the Children of Israel are tested through provision, idolatry, and disobedience.

Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf) body

Surah 7: The Heights (Al-A'raaf), revealed at Mecca, warns Muhammad and believers to follow Allah's revelations and recounts the consequences faced by disbelievers. Allah describes creation, judgment, the prostration before Adam, Iblis's defiance, and Satan's temptation of Adam and his wife, followed by their expulsion from the Garden. The unit contrasts the dwellers of the Garden, the dwellers of the Fire, and the dwellers on the Heights, emphasizing guidance, justice, mercy, and accountability. It recounts the messengers Noah, Hud, Salih, Lot, and Shu'eyb, and the destruction or rescue of their peoples, including Noah's people, A'ad, Thamud, Lot's household, and Midian.

103 Then, after them, We sent Moses with our tokens unto Pharaoh and his chiefs, body

This unit concerns Moses, Pharaoh, Pharaoh's people, the wizards, Aaron, and the Children of Israel. Moses presents Allah's tokens, defeats the wizards' display, leads the Children of Israel from Pharaoh's oppression, and receives the tablets after forty nights. Pharaoh's people are punished through signs and drowning, while the Children of Israel cross the sea, receive provision, and are tested by idolatry and disobedience. The unit also teaches about Allah's guidance and mercy, the messenger Muhammad, the Qur'an, the Hereafter, and the coming Hour.
