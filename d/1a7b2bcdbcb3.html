Abstract written by a model

The Master teaches that learning, friendship, virtue, filial conduct, sincerity, propriety, and self-examination are central to good character and government. The philosopher Yu explains that filial piety and fraternal submission are roots of benevolent actions, while ease in propriety must remain regulated by propriety itself. Tsang, Tsze-hsia, Tsze-kung, and Tsze-ch'in discuss self-examination, conduct toward parents and friends, learning, and the Master’s methods. The unit concludes that people should seek to understand others and correct their own faults rather than be distressed when others do not understand them.
