Surah 16: The Bee (An-Nahl)

Surah 16: The Bee (An-Nahl), from The Meaning of the Glorious Koran, translated by Mohammed Marmaduke Pickthall (1930), public domain in the United States, text via Tanzil.net. Shown here as plain text, with facts drawn from it. https://tanzil.net/

Abstract written by a model

Surah 16: The Bee (An-Nahl) concerns Allah’s signs, commands, revelation, and judgment, and calls mankind to worship Him alone, give thanks, and act justly. It describes the creation and service of the heavens, the earth, water, the sea, cattle, the bee, and other creatures as signs for those who reflect. It contrasts those who believe and do good with those who disbelieve, the idolaters, and the proud, describing their respective rewards and punishments in the Hereafter. Muhammad is instructed to convey the message, call with wisdom, endure patiently, and follow the religion of Abraham.
